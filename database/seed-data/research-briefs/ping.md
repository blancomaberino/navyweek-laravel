# PING — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **new PING G440 driver (~$450 MSRP)** bought from an authorized dealer, plus a worked example of a full G440 iron set.

## Headline finding

**PING does not give a point-of-sale military discount — it runs a mail-in-style "Military Rebate Program" instead.** You buy new PING clubs from an **authorized US dealer** (PING sells through dealers, not a discounted direct checkout), then verify your service via **ID.me** and submit each club's **serial number + sales receipt** to claim a **per-club virtual prepaid rebate card**. Current rebate period (Jan 30 – Aug 1, 2026) pays **$30 per driver, $20 per fairway, $15 per hybrid, $15 per putter, $10 per iron, $10 per wedge** on specific eligible models. It's real money back for those who buy new PING anyway, but the amounts are modest, it's **online (rebate) only**, **not combinable with any other offer**, and the card must be cashed within six months. Archetype: **premium hard-goods brand sold through a dealer network with a post-purchase rebate, not a shelf discount** — so the biggest savings usually come from where/how you buy (fitting deals, trade-in, prior-gen/used) rather than the rebate itself.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + program terms)
1. **The rebate "may not be combined with any other offers"** (PING T&Cs) — you can't stack it with another PING promotion.
2. **It attaches to the purchase, then pays back** — you still pay the dealer's full price up front; the rebate is a virtual prepaid card mailed 6–8 weeks later.
3. **Only authorized US dealers (and US-base shops / APO-FPO) qualify** — **auction-site purchases are explicitly excluded**, and duplicate serial numbers are rejected.
4. **One rebate card per new product type per email address** per rebate period (Driver, Iron, Fairway, Hybrid, Wedge, Putter), within the per-type quantity limits.
5. **Card expires if not cashed within 6 months** of the card date.

### How To Redeem (Online Rebate)
1. Confirm you meet the service requirements and that your clubs are **eligible models** in the current rebate period.
2. Buy the new clubs from an **authorized PING dealer** in the US (or an on-base shop / to an APO-FPO address). Keep the receipt.
3. Click **Request Rebate** and **verify via ID.me** (create an account if needed).
4. Complete the rebate form with a **valid PING serial number** and a **receipt image** for each club.
5. Submit by the deadline (**Sep 1, 2026** this cycle). Allow **6–8 weeks** for the virtual prepaid card; cash it within **6 months**.

### How Verification Works
PING verifies military status through **ID.me** at the rebate step. Verification is per-claim; there's no permanent account discount. After ID.me confirms you, you're routed to the rebate submission form. Claim status is tracked at rapid-rebates.com or 800-619-4703.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Auction-site purchases not eligible**; duplicate serial numbers not honored.
- **US only** (authorized dealers, US bases, APO/FPO).
- **Not combinable with any other offer.**
- One rebate card per new product type per email address per period; per-type limits apply.
- Card expires if not cashed within **6 months**.

### Best Ways To Save More

### FAQ

#### Does PING offer a military discount?
Not a percentage discount — PING offers a **Military Rebate Program**. You buy new eligible clubs from an authorized dealer, verify via ID.me, and claim a fixed-dollar prepaid rebate card per club.

#### How much is the PING military rebate?
For Jan 30 – Aug 1, 2026: **$30 driver, $20 per fairway wood, $15 per hybrid, $15 putter, $10 per iron, $10 per wedge**, on specific eligible models, with per-type limits.

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Yes — Active, Active Reserve, Retirees **and spouses**, and honorably discharged veterans across all six services (including Space Force) qualify.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **ID.me** when you click **Request Rebate**, then you complete the rebate form with each club's serial number and receipt.

#### Does PING use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**ID.me**. There's no GovX or SheerID path for the PING rebate.

#### Can I use it in a pro shop or only online?
The **rebate is claimed online**, but the **purchase** must be from an authorized US dealer, an on-base shop, or shipped to an APO/FPO address. Auction sites don't count.

#### Can I combine it with other offers?
No — the rebate "may not be combined with any other offers." It does sit on top of a dealer's own price, though.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy PING?
If buying **new**, claim the rebate. If you have clubs to trade, **Trade-In** usually beats it. If you're flexible on model, **prior-gen or certified-used** saves the most. On base, check for a **tax-free** authorized-dealer buy.
