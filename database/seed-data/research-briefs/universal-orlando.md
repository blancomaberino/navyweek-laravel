# Universal Orlando Resort Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Scope: **Universal Orlando Resort (Florida)** — Universal Studios Florida, Universal Islands of Adventure, Universal Volcano Bay, and Universal Epic Universe. This is **distinct from Universal Studios Hollywood (California)**, which runs a separate military offer not covered here.
> Access date for all sources: **June 24, 2026.**

## Executive Summary

Universal Orlando Resort offers eligible U.S. military members a discounted multi-day pass called the **2026 Military Freedom Pass** — a year-long, unlimited-admission ticket (annual-pass-style, but explicitly **not** an Annual Pass) sold through on-base military ticket offices, not at the park gate or on Universal's website. The 2-Park pass (Universal Studios Florida + Islands of Adventure) is **$220 adult / $215 child (ages 3-9)**; the 3-Park pass (adds Volcano Bay) is **$255 adult / $250 child**. It is valid **November 13, 2025 through December 18, 2026** with blockout dates. Eligibility is limited to Active Duty, Reservists, National Guard, Retired members of all U.S. military branches, 100% Disabled Veterans, their spouses, and Department of War (formerly DoD) Civilian personnel — each with a valid **DEERS-issued photo ID**. Verification is done by the DEERS ID at purchase, at voucher exchange/activation in-park, and on first park entry; there is no ID.me/SheerID/GovX involved. **Most important caveat:** non-retired, non-100%-disabled veterans do **not** qualify, and the pass cannot be bought at Universal's front gate or online — it must be purchased at an authorized base ITT/LTS office. Activated 2026 Freedom Pass holders may also buy specially priced **1-Day Epic Universe add-on tickets** (price varies by date).

### Intro
Universal Orlando Resort thanks U.S. service members with the **2026 Military Freedom Pass** — a year-long, unlimited-admission ticket sold at a deep discount through on-base military ticket offices. The 2-Park pass covers Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure; the 3-Park pass adds Volcano Bay. Activated passholders can also buy specially priced single-day Epic Universe tickets.

Eligibility runs through your **DEERS-issued military ID**: Active Duty, Reservists, National Guard, Retirees, 100% Disabled Veterans, their spouses, and Department of War (formerly DoD) Civilians qualify. Importantly, non-retired and non-100%-disabled veterans do **not** qualify, and the pass can't be bought at the Universal gate or on Universal's website — only at an authorized base ITT/LTS office (or a few that sell online or by phone).

### Who Qualifies
- Active Duty (all branches incl. Coast Guard, National Guard, Reservists) — **eligible** (with DEERS ID)
- Retired U.S. military (all branches) — **eligible**
- 100% Disabled Veterans — **eligible**
- Reservists / National Guard — **eligible**
- Spouses of eligible members — **eligible** (spouse or member may use the benefit; member need not be present after activation)
- Department of War (formerly DoD) Civilian personnel — **eligible**
- Veterans who are NOT retired and NOT 100% disabled — **not eligible**
- Child dependents (as the purchasing/eligible member), divorced spouses, contractors, foreign affiliates — **not eligible**
- NOAA, U.S. Public Health Service, Department of Homeland Security — **not eligible**
- DoW Civilian (Retired) — **not eligible**
- First responders, teachers, nurses, students (as such) — **not stated / not part of this offer**

### How To Redeem (Purchase + Activate)
1. Confirm you hold a valid, qualifying **DEERS-issued military photo ID** and that your category is eligible (see above).
2. Visit your local base **Information, Tickets & Tours (ITT)** or **Leisure Travel Services (LTS)** office. No local base? Order online from Shades of Green or Patrick SFB ITT, or by phone from select offices (e.g., Camp Pendleton ITT).
3. Buy the 2-Park or 3-Park 2026 Military Freedom Pass (max 6 per eligible ID; one must be used by the member or spouse). You receive **vouchers**, not finished tickets — save your receipt and photograph the vouchers.
4. At Universal Orlando, take your vouchers and DEERS ID to the **front gate ticket window, Guest Services, or a Destination Universal ticket desk** in a UO/partner hotel to exchange and activate. The eligible member (or spouse) must be present with the DEERS ID for the whole order.
5. On **first park entry**, the member or spouse must again show the DEERS ID. Entry uses a biometric scan; once activated, each pass is tied to one person and is non-transferable.
6. (Optional) After activation, buy a 1-Day **Epic Universe** add-on ticket — date-specific, price varies, max 1 per day per pass.

### How To Redeem In Store / At Gate
You **cannot** buy this pass at Universal's front gate or on UniversalOrlando.com. The only purchase channel is an authorized base ITT/LTS office (a few sell online or by phone). The Universal gate is only for **exchanging/activating** the vouchers you already bought on base.

### How Verification Works
Verification is entirely via your **DEERS-issued military photo ID** — there is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or online account verification for this ticket. You must present the DEERS ID (1) when purchasing at the base office, (2) when exchanging vouchers for activated tickets at Universal, and (3) on the first use to enter the parks. The eligible member or spouse must be present for purchase and activation; after first entry, activated passes function like regular tickets for the assigned individual. Universal uses a biometric (facial) scan at park entry, and activated passes are non-transferable.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Not sold at the Universal Orlando front gate or online — base ticket offices only.
- Non-refundable and non-transferable; must be used by the same person on all days once activated.
- Excludes separately ticketed events (e.g., Halloween Horror Nights), parking, and discounts on food or merchandise. (Universal sometimes runs *separate* military offers for hotels and hard-ticket events — check the official military page.)
- The Freedom Pass does **not** include Epic Universe; Epic Universe is a separate paid 1-Day add-on requiring an activated pass.
- Blockout dates apply (see Verified Facts).
- Maximum 6 tickets per eligible DEERS ID; additional tickets must be bought from the original selling office.
- Explicitly **not** a Universal Annual Pass and does not include Annual Passholder perks (lounges, passholder store) — *Annual-Pass-perk exclusion is per a secondary source (TouringPlans), not confirmed in Universal's primary terms; flag as Medium.*
- Some base ITT offices require all 6 tickets to be purchased at once — this may be a base policy, not Universal's. **Confirm with your selling office.**

### FAQ

#### Does Universal Orlando offer a military discount?
Yes. Universal Orlando Resort offers the **2026 Military Freedom Pass**, a discounted year-long unlimited-admission ticket for eligible U.S. military members, plus discounted Epic Universe add-on tickets and military hotel rates. (Source: Universal press release, Nov 13, 2025.)

#### How much is the Universal Orlando military discount?
The 2-Park 2026 Military Freedom Pass is **$220 adult / $215 child (3-9)**; the 3-Park pass (adds Volcano Bay) is **$255 adult / $250 child**. (Source: Shades of Green ticket page; Universal press release.)

#### Do veterans qualify for the Universal Orlando military discount?
Only **retired** veterans and **100% disabled** veterans qualify. Veterans who are not retired and not 100% service-disabled are **explicitly not eligible**. (Source: Shades of Green official terms.)

#### Do spouses and dependents qualify?
**Spouses** of eligible members qualify and can use the benefit even without the member present (after activation). **Child dependents** are not eligible to purchase, and divorced spouses are excluded. (Source: Shades of Green official terms.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
With a valid **DEERS-issued military photo ID** — shown at purchase (base office), at voucher exchange/activation at Universal, and on first park entry. There is no online verification step. (Source: Shades of Green terms; press release.)

#### Does Universal Orlando use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No. The 2026 Military Freedom Pass is verified by **DEERS-issued military ID** at military ticket offices and at the park — not through ID.me, GovX, or SheerID. (Source: Universal/Shades of Green purchase and terms documentation.)

#### Can I buy the pass at the Universal Orlando gate or online?
No. It must be purchased at an **authorized base ITT/LTS office** (some sell online, e.g., Shades of Green and Patrick SFB ITT, or by phone). The Universal gate only **exchanges and activates** vouchers. (Source: Press release; TouringPlans; Universal base-office list.)

#### What's excluded?
Separately ticketed events, parking, and food/merchandise discounts are excluded; Epic Universe is a separate paid add-on; the pass is non-refundable and non-transferable; blockout dates apply; and the pass is not an Annual Pass. (Source: Shades of Green official terms.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other discounts?
The Freedom Pass is its own promotional product purchased through base offices; it isn't a code stacked at Universal checkout. Universal may run separate military offers for hotels and hard-ticket events, but those are distinct. (Source: Press release; TouringPlans.) *Stacking with other ticket promos: not stated in official source.*

#### Does Universal Orlando offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
**Not stated in the official military source.** This brief covers the military offer only; any first responder/teacher/nurse/student discount would need separate verification and is not part of the Military Freedom Pass.
