# Best Buy Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Best Buy does not offer a military or veteran discount.** Best Buy's own Q&A "Memberships Expert Team" states plainly: "While we don't offer a military discount, we work hard to stay competitive with our prices and deals. We also offer a Price Match Guarantee to help you save even more." There is no nationwide program, no ID.me/SheerID/GovX verification flow, and the military benefit is **not** tied to a My Best Buy Plus/Total membership perk. The single most important caveat: any "10% military discount" you see advertised is **not an official Best Buy offer** — it originates from coupon aggregators and from anecdotal reports that individual store managers *may* grant a courtesy discount at their sole discretion, which Best Buy does not publish, guarantee, or honor as policy. The honest, accurate page tells veterans the truth (no official discount) and routes them to Best Buy's real savings levers — Price Match Guarantee, My Best Buy memberships/rewards, the My Best Buy Credit Card, outlet/open-box, and manufacturer-direct military discounts (e.g., Samsung, LG, Apple via their own programs).

> Accuracy note: Aggregator and "military discount" listicle pages widely claim a **"10% Best Buy military discount."** That figure is **not confirmed by any official Best Buy source.** Best Buy's official answers say it does **not** offer a military discount. The only "10%" tied to Best Buy that appears on official surfaces is the **10% back in rewards on first-day purchases when approved for a My Best Buy Credit Card** — which is available to all approved applicants, not a military benefit. Do not present a 10% military discount as fact.

### FAQ

#### Does Best Buy offer a military discount?
No. Best Buy states on its own site: "While we don't offer a military discount, we work hard to stay competitive with our prices and deals. We also offer a Price Match Guarantee to help you save even more." There is no nationwide military or veteran discount.

#### How much is the Best Buy military discount?
There isn't one. Any "10% military discount" advertised by coupon sites is not an official Best Buy offer. The only Best Buy-related "10%" is the new-cardmember credit-card bonus (10% back in rewards on first-day purchases upon approval), which is open to everyone, not just military.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
No — Best Buy does not offer a military discount to any group, including active duty, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, or dependents.

#### Will a Best Buy store manager give me a military discount?
Maybe, but it's not guaranteed and it's not policy. Some shoppers report that individual managers *can* apply a courtesy discount at their discretion, but Best Buy does not publish or promise this. Don't rely on it, and expect it to be unavailable on price-controlled items.

#### Does Best Buy use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute?
No. Because Best Buy has no military discount, there is no military verification provider or checkout button to use. Be wary of any third-party site claiming to "verify" you for a Best Buy military discount.

#### Does Best Buy have a Veterans Day military discount?
Not confirmed on any official Best Buy source. Best Buy runs frequent sales and has supported military causes, but there is no verified, official Veterans Day military discount published by Best Buy. Treat third-party claims as unverified.

#### How can military members and veterans still save at Best Buy?
Use the **Price Match Guarantee**, join **My Best Buy** for rewards/free shipping, consider the **My Best Buy Credit Card** bonus, shop **Best Buy Outlet/Open-Box**, and check whether the manufacturer (Samsung, LG, Apple, Dell) offers its **own** verified military discount on the same product.

#### Does Best Buy offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No consumer first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount is confirmed on Best Buy's official site. (Best Buy Education serves schools/businesses, not individual student/teacher shoppers.)
