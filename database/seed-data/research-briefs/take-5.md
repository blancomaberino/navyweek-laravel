# Take 5 Oil Change Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Take 5 Oil Change offers a **year-round 25% discount on any oil change** to military personnel who are **active, reserve, or retired**, with **proof of service or a valid government-issued military ID card** shown in person at a shop. This is confirmed by two Take 5 primary sources: a newsroom press release (Oct. 30, 2024) and the live Take 5 coupons page, which lists the offer as "25% Off Any Oil Change — Veterans & Military — Must show proof of service or military ID." Take 5 has no e-commerce checkout; the discount is redeemed **in person at the shop** only. The single most important caveat: the coupons page states coupons are "valid only if emailed or texted — requires enrollment in marketing emails or texts from Take 5," and coupons "typically cannot be combined with other offers," so the military discount cannot be stacked. Eligibility wording is narrow — the official sources name only active/reserve/retired military and do **not** mention veterans-who-separated, spouses, dependents, or first responders by name (the coupon tile is labeled "Veterans & Military," and the press release headline references veterans, but the body's enumerated groups are "active, reserve, or retired"). Participation and exact mechanics can vary by location.

### FAQ

#### Does Take 5 Oil Change offer a military discount?
Yes. Take 5 offers a year-round 25% discount on any oil change for active, reserve, and retired military, per its newsroom announcement and coupons page. (Source: Take 5, accessed June 24, 2026.)

#### How much is the Take 5 military discount?
25% off any oil change. Take 5 states this is a year-round discount. (Source: Take 5 newsroom + coupons page.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Take 5's coupon is labeled "Veterans & Military," and the press release headline honors veterans, but the explicitly listed eligible groups are "active, reserve, or retired." Spouses and dependents are not mentioned in the official source. Confirm broader eligibility with your local shop.

#### How do I verify my military status at Take 5?
In person at the shop. Show proof of service or a valid government-issued military ID card to the technician. No online verification step is published.

#### Does Take 5 use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No verification provider is named on Take 5's official pages. Verification is in-person ID. Claims that some locations use ID.me appear only on third-party sites and are unverified by Take 5.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
The discount is an in-shop/drive-up discount only — Take 5 has no online oil-change checkout. Show your ID and the texted/emailed coupon at the shop.

#### What is excluded or required?
Coupons are valid only if emailed or texted to you (requires opting into Take 5 marketing), have no cash value, and typically can't be combined with other offers. Coupon instances may have expiration dates listed on the offer.

#### Can I combine it with other promo codes or sale items?
Generally no. Take 5 states coupons typically cannot be combined; pick the single coupon that saves the most.

#### Does Take 5 offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student oil-change discount is published on Take 5's official pages. Take 5 does publish a separate 15% Rideshare Drivers discount. (Take 5 has also donated to scholarships for families of fallen/disabled service members and first responders, but that is charitable giving, not a customer discount.)

#### Does the military discount vary by location?
Possibly. Take 5 operates 1,000+ company-owned and franchised shops, and participation/mechanics can vary. Confirm with your local shop before you go.
