# Beautyrest — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one queen Beautyrest mattress (≈$1,299 typical online price; Beautyrest Black lines run higher).

## Headline finding

**Beautyrest does not offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** Its own help center says so directly: "While we do appreciate your service, at this time we do not offer a Military or First Responder discount." (help.beautyrest.com, accessed July 18, 2026). Beautyrest is a **mattress manufacturer** (owned by Serta Simmons Bedding) that sells direct at beautyrest.com and through third-party retailers — so the real military savings come from **who you buy the Beautyrest through, not from Beautyrest itself**. The two paths that matter: (1) the **military Exchange** (shopmyexchange.com / mynavyexchange.com), which sells Beautyrest tax-free to eligible shoppers — usually the best deal on **Beautyrest Black**, and (2) **Mattress Firm's 10–20% ID.me military discount**, which covers many Beautyrest models but **explicitly excludes Beautyrest Black**. Beautyrest's own best lever for everyone is its holiday sale calendar (up to ~$700–$1,000 off Black lines) plus a price-adjustment courtesy within the 100-night trial. Archetype: **manufacturer-sold-through-retailers** (no first-party discount; savings live in the retail channel and the exchange).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Beautyrest.com sales cannot be combined** — its promotions page states offers "cannot combine with other promotions" and exclude previous purchases. There is no military code to stack because none exists.
2. **Mattress Firm's ID.me military discount is a retailer program, not Beautyrest's.** It applies to eligible Beautyrest models but **not Beautyrest Black** (Black is on Mattress Firm's exclusion list alongside Tempur-Pedic, Stearns & Foster, etc.). Mattress Firm markets the discount as "10–20% off," typically stackable on its own sale prices — confirm the final code and eligible model at checkout via ID.me.
3. **Exchange pricing needs no code and is tax-free** — the saving is the avoided state sales tax plus Star Card free shipping, not a stacked coupon.
4. **Beautyrest's 100-night price adjustment** is a post-purchase courtesy (email support while the sale is active), not a stackable discount.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, dependents** — Not eligible for any Beautyrest discount (none exists). Eligible for the Exchange (per Exchange rules: honorably discharged veterans, active, Reserve, Guard, retirees, family) and for Mattress Firm's ID.me military program.
- **First responders / nurses / teachers / students / seniors** — No Beautyrest program. Mattress Firm's ID.me marketplace runs separate offers for some of these groups; verify there.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**There is no Beautyrest military code to redeem.** Instead:

*Via the military Exchange (tax-free):*
1. Sign in at shopmyexchange.com (Army/Air Force) or mynavyexchange.com (Navy) with your verified account.
2. Search "Beautyrest" — Black Series, World Class, BR800, and Regal models appear.
3. Check out tax-free; free shipping with a Military Star Card or on orders $49+.

*Via Mattress Firm (ID.me, non-Black Beautyrest):*
1. On mattressfirm.com, add an eligible Beautyrest model to the cart.
2. Choose "Verify with ID.me" and confirm your military status.
3. Apply the ID.me code at checkout (10–20% off; Beautyrest Black is excluded).

### How Verification Works
Beautyrest itself verifies nothing — it has no program. For the retailer path, **Mattress Firm uses ID.me**: you create or sign into an ID.me account, it confirms your service record, and it issues a discount code. For the Exchange, eligibility is your existing military/veteran shopping authorization (veterans verify once via the Exchange's veteran verification). No claim here is more permanent than those third-party programs state.

### FAQ
#### Does Beautyrest offer a military discount?
No. Beautyrest's help center states: "at this time we do not offer a Military or First Responder discount."

#### How much is the Beautyrest military discount?
There isn't one. Beautyrest publishes no military, veteran, or first-responder discount and no military promo code.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Not for a Beautyrest discount — none exists for any group. Veterans and family may still buy Beautyrest tax-free through the military Exchange if they're eligible to shop there.

#### How do I verify my status?
There's nothing to verify at Beautyrest. Mattress Firm uses ID.me for its own military discount; the Exchange uses your military/veteran shopping eligibility.

#### Does Beautyrest use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. Beautyrest uses none of these because it has no military program. Mattress Firm, which sells Beautyrest, uses **ID.me** for its retailer discount.

#### Can I use a military discount in stores or online at Beautyrest?
No — neither channel has one. Use the Exchange (online/in-store, tax-free) or Mattress Firm's ID.me discount instead.

#### What is excluded?
Everything, in the sense that no Beautyrest military discount exists. On the retailer path, Mattress Firm's military discount excludes **Beautyrest Black**.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Beautyrest sales can't be combined with other promotions. Retailer/exchange savings are separate programs, not stacked on a Beautyrest military code (there is none).

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Beautyrest?
Beautyrest **Black** → the military Exchange (tax-free), since retailer military discounts exclude Black. Non-Black Beautyrest → Mattress Firm's 10–20% ID.me discount. Compare final tax-included totals.

#### Does Beautyrest offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No. Beautyrest offers none of these. Mattress Firm's ID.me marketplace runs separate offers for some of these groups.

#### Does Beautyrest run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Beautyrest runs public holiday sales (Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Presidents Day) open to everyone — none are military-specific.

#### Can I buy Beautyrest at the Exchange?
Yes. shopmyexchange.com and mynavyexchange.com carry Beautyrest Black, World Class, BR800, and Regal models, sold tax-free with free shipping via the Military Star Card or on orders $49+.
