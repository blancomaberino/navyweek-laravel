Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**, and the offer can be redeemed through the dates stated by Google/ID.me (ID.me terms: redeemable through December 31, 2026; Google Help: available until January 10, 2027 — see Open Questions). There is also a separate **Student Plan at $119** (add RedZone for $10) verified through **SheerID** — a different program, not part of the ID.me military/community offer. The single most important caveat: the discount applies only to the **YouTube Primetime Channels** version of NFL Sunday Ticket (watchable afterward on YouTube or YouTube TV), it covers **out-of-market Sunday afternoon games only**, it is **non-refundable**, and the military/community offer **auto-renews** at the eligible price.

### How To Redeem Online
**Military / Veteran / First Responder / Medical / Teacher (ID.me):**
1. Make sure you're signed in to the Google account you want NFL Sunday Ticket attached to (consider which account is logged in on your TV).
2. Go to the ID.me link for your group: Military or Veteran, First Responder, Medical Community, or Teacher (linked from the official YouTube Help page).
3. Click the green "Verify now" button and complete ID.me verification.
4. You'll be directed to a YouTube purchase page.
5. Choose 1 payment or installments, optionally add NFL RedZone for $42, and check out.
6. **Returning buyers who used ID.me last season:** go to youtube.com/nfl, click **Renew membership**, and the eligible $198 price should apply automatically when signed in with the same Google account.

**Student (SheerID):**
1. Go to youtube.com and open the NFL channel page; select **Get NFL Sunday Ticket** (sign in to your Google account — a personal email like @gmail.com is recommended over a restricted .edu account).
2. Below the package options, select **Eligible students can save with a student plan**.
3. If not verified in the last 11 months, complete instant SheerID verification (name, email, date of birth, institution).
4. If instant verification fails, upload proof of enrollment (school ID with expiration, class schedule, or tuition receipt); U.S. students typically get a decision by email within ~20 minutes.
5. Optionally add NFL RedZone for $10 and complete your purchase.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. NFL Sunday Ticket is a digital subscription with no physical retail location. There is no in-store redemption — all purchases are completed online at youtube.com.

### How Verification Works
- **Military & community offer:** Verification is handled by **ID.me**. You verify your status (Military/Veteran, First Responder, Medical Community, or Teacher) before being routed to the YouTube checkout. A Google account is required (you'll be prompted to create one if you don't have one). For returning ID.me buyers signed in with the same Google account, the eligible price applies automatically at renewal — and existing subscribers will **auto-renew at the $198 eligible price** unless they cancel. Verification issues go to ID.me support (help.id.me) or YouTube TV support at +1 833-389-2054.
- **Student plan:** Verification is handled by **SheerID**. Students must re-verify if they haven't verified in the last 11 months, and must verify again to purchase in future seasons. A .edu account is not required.
- **Brand Account note:** Purchasing through ID.me is not available while signed in to a YouTube Brand Account; switch to a non-Brand account first.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Discount applies only to NFL Sunday Ticket via **YouTube Primetime Channels** (watchable afterward on YouTube or YouTube TV).
- Covers **out-of-market Sunday afternoon regular-season games only**. Locally broadcast Fox/CBS games, Sunday Night Football (NBC), Monday Night Football (ESPN), and digital-only games are excluded. Preseason and postseason games are not included.
- **Non-refundable.** No refunds for partial seasons; no free trial.
- Offer **cannot be combined with other coupon-based offers** (ID.me terms). Student Plans are not eligible for any discounts/coupons.
- Military/community offer **auto-renews** at the then-current eligible price unless cancelled before the renewal date; existing subscribers auto-renew at $198.
- Student Plan is a **one-time purchase that does not auto-renew**; includes only one signed-in device / one concurrent stream and no family sharing.
- Must be **18 or older**. United States only; some U.S. territories excluded. Commercial use excluded.
- Google reserves the right to change or cancel the offer at any time.

### FAQ

#### Does NFL Sunday Ticket offer a military discount?
Yes. For the 2026 season, eligible U.S. Military Members and Veterans (plus First Responders, Medical Community, and Teachers) can buy NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube Primetime Channels for $198, verified through ID.me. (Source: YouTube Help.)

#### How much is the NFL Sunday Ticket military discount?
$198 for the 2026 season on YouTube Primetime Channels, with NFL RedZone available for $42 more. (Source: YouTube Help; ID.me offer page.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify under ID.me's "Military or Veteran" category. ID.me's NFL Sunday Ticket store page indicates active-duty, veterans, reservists, and spouses/dependents 18+ can qualify; the official Google Help page lists "Military & Veterans" as a group without itemizing spouses/dependents. Verify your specific status through ID.me. (Confidence: Medium for spouses/dependents.)

#### How do I verify my status?
Click the ID.me link for your group from the YouTube Help page, click "Verify now," and complete ID.me verification while signed in to the Google account you want to use. Then finish checkout on YouTube. (Source: YouTube Help; ID.me offer page.)

#### Does NFL Sunday Ticket use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
It uses **ID.me** for the military/veteran/first-responder/medical/teacher offer, and **SheerID** for the separate Student Plan. (Source: YouTube Help.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. NFL Sunday Ticket is a digital subscription; the discount is online only, on youtube.com via YouTube Primetime Channels. (Source: YouTube Help.)

#### What is excluded?
Out-of-market Sunday afternoon games only; local Fox/CBS, Sunday Night Football, Monday Night Football, and digital-only games are excluded, as are preseason/postseason. The offer is non-refundable and applies only to the YouTube Primetime Channels version. (Source: ID.me offer terms.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military/community offer cannot be combined with other coupon-based offers, and Student Plans are not eligible for any discounts or coupons. (Source: ID.me offer terms; YouTube Help.)

#### Is there a student discount for NFL Sunday Ticket?
Yes — a separate Student Plan at $119 for the 2025–26 season (add NFL RedZone for $10), verified by SheerID. You must be 18+, enrolled at an accredited college/university, and located in the U.S. (Source: YouTube Help.)

#### Does NFL Sunday Ticket offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or medical discount?
Yes. First Responders, Teachers, and the Medical Community get the same $198 2026-season price via their own ID.me verification links. (Source: YouTube Help.)
