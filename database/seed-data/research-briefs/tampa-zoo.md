## Headline finding

**ZooTampa is currently running a genuinely strong active-duty offer: FREE summer admission for active-duty military plus up to three dependents, May 25 – September 7, 2026,** by showing a valid military ID at the front gate — part of the Zoo's "America's 250th" salute (which also gives free admission to Hillsborough County government employees, City of Tampa team members, and Hillsborough County School teachers, plus **25% off general admission for up to four guests**). Rack GA is **$51.95 adult / $41.95 child** at the gate; buying online saves **$5/guest** for everyone. Archetype: **attraction/venue with an ID-at-the-gate military discount plus a limited-time free-admission promo** — where the timing (summer 2026) makes it a standout deal.

### FAQ

#### Does ZooTampa offer a military discount?
Yes. Through **September 7, 2026**, active-duty military get **free** admission plus up to three dependents. Year-round, active duty/retirees/veterans get a reported **$4 off per ticket** (up to four) at the gate with a valid military ID.

#### How much is the ZooTampa military discount?
This summer it's **100% off (free)** for active-duty members and up to 3 dependents. Otherwise it's a reported **$4 off per ticket**, verified at the ticket window.

#### Do veterans and dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify for the year-round **$4-off** gate discount. The **summer free-admission** offer is for **active-duty** members plus up to three dependents.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person — show a **valid military ID at the front-gate ticket windows**. ZooTampa does not use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID.

#### Can I get the military discount online?
No. The military benefit is redeemed at the gate. Everyone can buy tickets online to save $5 per guest, but there's no online military rate.

#### What's the cheapest way for a military family to visit?
If you're active-duty, **visit before September 7, 2026 — it's free** for you and up to three dependents. Otherwise use the military gate discount and buy any additional tickets online.

#### Does ZooTampa offer a teacher or first-responder discount?
This summer, **Hillsborough County/City of Tampa employees and Hillsborough County School teachers get in free** (+25% off up to 4 guests) through Sept 7, 2026. No dedicated first-responder discount is published.
