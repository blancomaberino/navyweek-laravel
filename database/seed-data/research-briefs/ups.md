# The UPS Store — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $100 walk-in order at The UPS Store (e.g., ~$60 printing + ~$40 packing/shipping/notary), continental US.

> **Important disambiguation:** "The UPS Store" (theupsstore.com — the independently owned retail print/ship/notary franchise) is a **different business** from **UPS the shipping carrier** (ups.com). The keyword *"ups military discount"* is searched by people meaning either one. This brief covers **The UPS Store** primarily and clears up the carrier confusion, because nearly every aggregator conflates the two.

## Headline finding

**The UPS Store publishes no national military or veteran customer discount.** Its official membership-discount page lists only **AAA** and **AARP** — no military program, no ID.me, no SheerID/GovX. Because every location is **independently owned and operated**, a *local* store owner may grant a courtesy military discount at their discretion, but there is nothing corporate to cite, so treat it as strictly YMMV. Archetype: **franchise services business with no national discount + heavy aggregator fabrication** (the "$75 off," "50% off with ID.me," and "40% military" claims are invented or copied from the separate carrier). The genuine best paths are the **public 20%-off printing coupon** and **AAA/AARP member rates** for in-store services; for the *shipping* portion, the biggest save is to **skip The UPS Store's retail markup entirely** and ship via **ups.com** or **USPS APO/FPO military rates**.

Separately, **UPS the carrier** historically ran a **"VETERAN" promo code** (20% off Ground, 40% off Air) on ups.com, but its published terms expired **12/31/2021** and the code no longer appears on ups.com; independent checks through late 2025 found no active veteran shipping discount. Treat that offer as **historical/discontinued**, not current.

### FAQ
#### Does The UPS Store offer a military discount?
No national one. The UPS Store's official membership program lists only AAA and AARP. Because stores are independently owned, a local owner may offer a courtesy military discount, but there's no corporate policy to rely on.

#### How much is The UPS Store military discount?
There is no published military discount percentage. Any specific figure you see on coupon sites ("$75 off," "50% off," "40% military") is unsupported — the official page does not list a military offer.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
No corporate military discount applies to any of these groups. AAA/AARP membership rates are available to anyone who holds those memberships.

#### How do I verify my military status?
You can't — The UPS Store has no military verification because it has no military program. It does not use ID.me or SheerID for a military discount.

#### Does The UPS Store use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. The UPS Store is not listed as a military-discount partner on ID.me or the other verification providers. Claims that you can "verify with ID.me for The UPS Store military savings" are not supported.

#### Can I use a discount in stores or online?
Membership (AAA/AARP) rates and the public print coupon are used in store at participating locations. There is no online military discount.

#### What's excluded?
The 20% print coupon needs a $10 minimum, is one-per-customer, can't combine with other offers, and expires Dec 31, 2026. Membership rates apply only to eligible products/services.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the print coupon cannot be combined with other offers, and membership rates are standalone. There's no military discount to stack.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from The UPS Store?
For printing, use the 20% coupon or an AAA membership rate. For shipping, don't pay the store's retail markup — ship directly on ups.com or via USPS (APO/FPO military rates). Then ask your local owner for a courtesy military discount.

#### Does The UPS Store offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No published national program for any of these groups. Only AAA and AARP membership discounts are documented.

#### Is the "UPS military discount" (20% off shipping / 40% off air) real?
That was **UPS the carrier** (ups.com), not The UPS Store, via a "VETERAN" promo code. Its published terms expired 12/31/2021 and the code no longer appears on ups.com. Treat it as discontinued.

#### Can I buy at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
Not applicable — The UPS Store is a local print/ship/notary services franchise, not a product brand sold through the Exchange or ExpertVoice.
