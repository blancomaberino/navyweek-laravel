# Ripley's Aquarium — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult (age 12+) general-admission ticket to Ripley's Aquarium of Myrtle Beach at **$39.99** (published adult rate; some third-party listings show $42.99 — see Open Questions). Effective prices are before sales tax; the military rate is a box-office price, not an online cart.

## Headline finding

Ripley's US aquariums (Myrtle Beach, SC and Gatlinburg/"of the Smokies," TN) run a **real, evergreen 30% military & first-responder discount** on single-attraction admission — for the qualifying person **plus up to four additional guests** — redeemed **in person at the box office with valid ID**. It is **not combinable** with any other promotion or online code, and it is not an online-checkout discount. Archetype: **discretionary/gated in-person attraction discount (box-office identity offer)** — no verification provider (ID.me/GovX), no code, just an ID at the window. The one thing to know: because the 30% is capped at admission and can't stack, a **public combo/online promo code (e.g. ~25% off a combo pass)** can occasionally rival it for groups visiting multiple attractions — but for a straight single-aquarium adult ticket, the **30% military rate is the best single path**. Two important nuances: (1) **Ripley's Aquarium of Canada (Toronto) is a different program** — it honors the **CFOne card at Guest Services**, with no published percentage; and (2) the Myrtle Beach cluster has historically run a **July 3–4 "free admission for veterans/military" promo** (family 50% off), which beats 30% on those two days only.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 30% military/first-responder rate is "not valid with any other promotions or discounts"** — it does not combine with online promo codes, combo-pass discounts, membership pricing, or coupons.
2. **It is box-office / in-person only** — there is no online checkout code; you can't apply it on ripleys.com or third-party ticket sites.
3. **Public online promo codes** (e.g. ~25% combo, 20% member offers) apply **only to the public price online** and cannot be layered onto the military rate — you pick one path.
4. **Toronto CFOne is a separate, closed program** — the US 30% wording does not apply there; the CFOne benefit is redeemed at Guest Services and its exact value is not published online.
5. **No verification provider** (ID.me/GovX/SheerID) is in the loop, so there is no cashback/portal layer to add — the only "stack" is choosing the single best path.

### How To Redeem In Person
**US aquariums (Myrtle Beach / Gatlinburg):**
1. Go to the box office / ticket window at the aquarium.
2. Present a **valid military or first-responder ID** at the time of purchase.
3. The 30% applies to your admission and up to four additional guests bought in the same transaction. Children with an eligible adult don't need their own ID.

**Toronto (Ripley's Aquarium of Canada):**
1. Bring your **CFOne card**.
2. Present it at **Guest Services on arrival** to receive the CFOne member rate (ask for the current percentage — it isn't published online).

*(There is no online military discount — do not expect a code at ripleys.com checkout.)*

### How Verification Works
Ripley's uses a **human ID check at the box office** — no third-party verification service (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute), no account, and no code. Bring a valid military ID (or first-responder credential such as a badge/department ID). Because there's no digital pre-verification, expect the cashier to check your ID and possibly a brief line at busy times. In Toronto the "credential" is the **CFOne card**.

### FAQ

#### Does Ripley's Aquarium offer a military discount?
Yes. Ripley's US aquariums (Myrtle Beach and Gatlinburg's Aquarium of the Smokies) give **30% off** single-attraction admission to active and retired military and first responders, plus up to four guests, with valid ID at the box office.

#### How much is the Ripley's Aquarium military discount?
**30% off** admission, redeemed in person. On a ~$39.99 adult ticket that's about **$27.99**. Toronto's Ripley's Aquarium of Canada uses a separate CFOne program whose percentage isn't published.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Active and retired military and first responders qualify, and **up to four additional guests** get the same 30% when bought together. Separated veterans should confirm eligibility at the window — the evergreen wording emphasizes "active and retired."

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a **valid military or first-responder ID at the box office** at the time of purchase. There's no online verification, account, or code.

#### Does Ripley's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. The US military discount is a **manual box-office ID check** with no third-party verification provider and no online code. (Toronto uses the **CFOne** card at Guest Services.)

#### Can I use it online or only in stores?
**In person only.** There is no online military discount at ripleys.com; public promo codes online are a separate, non-combinable path.

#### What is excluded?
It's not valid online, not valid with any other promotion/code/combo/membership, and is limited to single-attraction admission plus up to four guests.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or combo passes?
No — the 30% "is not valid with any other promotions or discounts." Pick either the 30% military rate or a public online code/combo, whichever is cheaper for your trip.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Ripley's Aquarium tickets?
For a single aquarium ticket: the **30% box-office military rate**. For a multi-attraction trip: compare a **combo pass + public online code** against 30% per ticket. On **July 3–4** at Myrtle Beach, the historical **free-admission** promo wins.

#### Does Ripley's offer a first responder discount?
Yes — the **same 30%** program covers first responders (police, fire, EMT/paramedic) with valid ID, in person.

#### Does Ripley's run a Veterans Day or July 4th military offer?
The 30% is year-round. Historically the **Myrtle Beach** attractions have offered **free admission to veterans/military on July 3–4** (family 50% off); confirm it before relying on it.
