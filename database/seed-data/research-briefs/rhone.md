# Rhone — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic $200 full-price Rhone apparel order (e.g. a pair of Commuter pants at ~$128 + a Reign or Element tee at ~$68).

## Headline finding

Yes — Rhone offers a **15% off** discount for **military** (and separately for veterans/families, first responders, medical providers, nurses, hospital employees, teachers, and students), verified through **ID.me**, on **full-price** merchandise, online and in participating stores. This is confirmed on the ID.me provider catalog for Rhone. The single best path depends on timing: **when Rhone is not running a sitewide sale, the 15% ID.me discount is the best evergreen deal**; but Rhone runs frequent public sitewide promos (commonly 25–40% off around Black Friday, semi-annual/warehouse sales, and holidays) that beat 15% and generally **do not stack** with the ID.me code — during those windows the public sale wins. Rhone's archetype is a **DTC premium men's activewear brand with a flat verified-discount program plus aggressive seasonal sales**; the money move is to check whether a live public sale exceeds 15% before verifying.

## Stacking Rules (verified where possible; flagged where not)
1. **ID.me discount is a single-use code applied at checkout** — like nearly all ID.me apparel offers, it does not stack with other promo codes or with already-reduced sale items. *(Standard ID.me apparel behavior; Rhone's exact exclusion wording not read verbatim this session — flag.)*
2. **Public sitewide sales replace, not add to, the 15% code** — you pick the lower price, not both. Confirm at checkout.
3. **Cashback portals (ID.me Shop cashback, Rakuten) generally track on top** of the final checkout price because they are a rebate, not a code — but cashback is often void when another discount code is used, so verify the portal's terms before relying on it. *(Flag — portal-specific.)*
4. **GovX** is a separate verification ecosystem; its Rhone discount is redeemed through GovX, not combined with an ID.me code.

Yes. Rhone offers 15% off full-price items for military members, verified through ID.me, online and at participating stores.

Both — on rhone.com, the Rhone app, and participating retail stores.

Full-price items only; sale/final-sale items and gift cards are typically excluded, and the code doesn't combine with other promos. (Exact exclusion wording pending a direct read of Rhone's terms.)
