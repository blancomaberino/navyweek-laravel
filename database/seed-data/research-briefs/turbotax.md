# TurboTax (Intuit) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

TurboTax offers a **Free Military Discount**: eligible **enlisted active-duty and reserve service members, ranks E-1 through E-9, who have a W-2 from DFAS** can file both their **federal and state** returns for **$0** using any standard TurboTax **Online** product (Free Edition, Deluxe, or Premium). There is no promo code — you enter your military W-2, verify your rank when prompted, and the discount is applied at filing. The single most important caveat: the offer is for **enlisted ranks only**. Commissioned officers, warrant officers, veterans/retirees, and National Guard are **not** eligible per the official military page, and the discount does **not** apply to TurboTax Experts (Live/expert-assisted), TurboTax Desktop, or add-on bundles (PLUS/MAX). One source inconsistency to note (flagged below): the military-edition page says "Excludes National Guard members," while a TurboTax blog quote lists "National Guard E-1–E-9" as eligible — treat National Guard eligibility as unresolved and lean on the official page's exclusion.

This is also separate from two other free options military filers often confuse it with: **MilTax** (free filing via DoD/Military OneSource) and **IRS Free File** — noted under "Best Ways To Save More."

### Intro
TurboTax offers free federal and state tax filing for eligible **enlisted** active-duty and reserve service members. If your rank is **E-1 through E-9** and you have a **W-2 from DFAS**, you can file both returns for **$0** using TurboTax Online's Free Edition, Deluxe, or Premium products. There's no coupon code to hunt for — you enter your military W-2, verify your rank when prompted, and the discount is applied when you're ready to file.

The offer is limited to enlisted ranks. According to TurboTax's official military page, commissioned officers, warrant officers, veterans, retirees, and National Guard members do **not** qualify, and the discount does not apply to TurboTax Experts (expert-assisted), TurboTax Desktop, or add-on bundles. A military spouse can be included on a joint return even if the spouse is not in the military.

- Reserve (enlisted E-1–E-9, DFAS W-2) — **eligible** (per official source)
- Military spouses — **may be included on the eligible member's joint return** (not an independent qualification)
- Commissioned officers — **not eligible** (per official source)
- Warrant officers — **not eligible** (per official source)
- Veterans (no longer on active duty) — **not eligible** (per official source)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to TurboTax Online (turbotax.intuit.com) and start a return using a standard Online product (Free Edition, Deluxe, or Premium). Do **not** use TurboTax Desktop or a TurboTax Experts/Live product — the discount does not apply there.
2. Enter your military **W-2 from DFAS** when prompted.
3. Verify your **military status and pay grade (E-1–E-9)** when prompted.
4. TurboTax detects eligibility and automatically applies the military discount, reducing your cost to **$0 for both federal and state** returns. The discount shows up when you're ready to file (not in the prices listed before filing).

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. The military discount is for **TurboTax Online only**. There is no in-store military discount; TurboTax Desktop (CD/download) products and in-person TurboTax expert offices are explicitly excluded.

### How Verification Works
Verification happens **inside the TurboTax Online product** while you prepare your return — there is no separate signup with a third-party verifier like ID.me, SheerID, or GovX, and no promo code. You enter your **DFAS-issued military W-2** and confirm your **rank/pay grade (E-1–E-9)**; TurboTax applies the discount automatically at filing. An account is required to use TurboTax Online. The official source does not state a re-verification cadence; in practice you verify each tax year when you file. If the discount does not apply, the likely cause is rank/product ineligibility (officer, warrant officer, Guard, or use of Desktop/Experts) — contact TurboTax Support for help.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Enlisted only.** Offer valid for active duty and reservists of an enlisted rank **E-1 through E-9** with a W-2 from DFAS. "Offer not valid for officers." (per military-edition page)
- **Excludes National Guard members** (per military-edition page; note conflicting blog statement — see Open Questions).
- **Excludes veterans and retirees** (per blog; military-edition FAQ also lists veterans, retirees, commissioned and warrant officers as not qualifying).
- **Online products only.** Valid on TurboTax Online Free Edition, Deluxe, and Premium. **Not valid for TurboTax Experts** (Live/expert-assisted) or **TurboTax Desktop** products.
- **No discount on add-on bundles**, including TurboTax PLUS or MAX.
- Discounts are **not reflected in the prices shown before filing** and apply when you file.
- Intuit "reserves the right to modify or terminate any offer at any time for any reason in its sole discretion," and offers are generally not combinable with other TurboTax offers (per the page's offer-terms footer).
- Per-account/return caps, dependent eligibility, and surviving-spouse rules: Not stated in official source.

### Best Ways To Save More

Worth flagging for officers, Guard, and recently separated members who don't qualify for the TurboTax military discount. *(Verify current MilTax eligibility on militaryonesource.mil before publishing as a claim.)*

- **Competitor comparison:** TurboTax's own page emphasizes that "unlike other tax companies, your military **state** taxes are free." Worth comparing against H&R Block and TaxSlayer military offers in a side panel. *(Verify each competitor's current terms separately.)*
- **Common checkout mistake:** Starting in TurboTax **Desktop** or a **TurboTax Experts/Live** product — the discount won't apply. Start a standard **Online** return and enter your DFAS W-2.

### FAQ

#### Does TurboTax offer a military discount?
Yes. TurboTax offers free federal and state tax filing on TurboTax Online for eligible enlisted active-duty and reserve service members (ranks E-1–E-9) with a W-2 from DFAS. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### How much is the TurboTax military discount?
It makes eligible enlisted members' filing **$0 for both federal and state** returns on TurboTax Online (Free Edition, Deluxe, or Premium). It is not a percentage off — it zeroes out the price at filing. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### Who is eligible — do officers qualify?
Only **enlisted** ranks E-1–E-9 (active duty and reserve) with a DFAS W-2. **Commissioned officers and warrant officers do not qualify**, nor do veterans or retirees. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### Do veterans, retirees, or National Guard qualify?
Per TurboTax's official military page, **veterans, retirees, and National Guard members do not qualify** for the free TurboTax Online military discount. (Note: a separate TurboTax blog quote lists National Guard E-1–E-9 as eligible — the official military page's exclusion is the more authoritative statement; confirm in-product.) *(Sources: TurboTax military-edition.jsp; TurboTax Blog, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### Can my spouse use it?
An eligible service member can include a spouse on a **joint return**, even if the spouse is not in the military. The spouse does not qualify independently. *(Source: TurboTax Blog, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### How do I verify my military status?
Inside TurboTax Online, enter your **DFAS-issued military W-2** and verify your **rank/pay grade (E-1–E-9)** when prompted. There is no promo code and no third-party verification step. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### Does TurboTax use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. TurboTax's military discount is verified **in-product** by entering your DFAS W-2 and confirming your rank — not through ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### Does the discount work on TurboTax Desktop or TurboTax Live/Experts?
No. The military discount is **TurboTax Online only**. There is **no** military discount on TurboTax Experts (Live/expert-assisted), TurboTax Desktop products, or add-on bundles like PLUS or MAX. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### Is there a promo code, and when is the discount applied?
There is **no promo code**. The discount is applied automatically **when you're ready to file** and is **not reflected in the prices shown beforehand**. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*

#### Does TurboTax offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount is stated on TurboTax's official military/discount pages. The free military offer is specific to enlisted service members. *(Source: TurboTax military-edition.jsp, accessed June 23, 2026.)*
