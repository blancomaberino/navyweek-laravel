# Under Armour Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Under Armour offers **20% off all purchases** to verified military members — Active Duty, Retirees, Veterans, Military Spouses, and Military Family Members — plus the same 20% to First Responders, Healthcare Workers, and teachers/educators. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**, selected at checkout on UA.com or in the UA App. The 20% rate also applies at **UA Brand House** stores, while **UA Factory House (outlet) stores get 10%**. The offer is evergreen, but Under Armour's own page states the rate **"may be greater during promotional timeframes"** (e.g., Veterans Day, when it has run at 40%). The single most important caveat: the discount **cannot be combined with other promotional codes, sitewide promotions, or gift card purchases** — it does not stack.

> Accuracy note: Several aggregator pages advertise a **40% Under Armour military discount as if it were the standing rate, and describe it as "stackable on top of most sales."** That is **not the everyday offer.** Under Armour's official FAQ states the standing rate is **20%** (10% at Factory House) and that it **cannot be combined** with other promotions. The 40% figure has appeared only during limited Veterans Day promotions. Only the 20%/10% non-stackable terms are confirmed on Under Armour's official page as of June 23, 2026.

### FAQ

#### Does Under Armour offer a military discount?
Yes. Under Armour offers 20% off all purchases to verified military members through ID.me — online and at UA Brand House stores (10% at UA Factory House outlets).

#### How much is the Under Armour military discount?
20% off on UA.com, the UA App, and at UA Brand House stores; 10% at UA Factory House outlets. The rate may be higher during limited promotions like Veterans Day, but 20% is the everyday rate.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family members qualify?
Yes. Under Armour's official list names Active Duty, Retirees, Veterans, Military Spouses, and Military Family Members — all eligible at 20%.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a free ID.me account, then at checkout choose "Military and First Responder Discount" and press "Verify with ID.me." Once verified, your discount applies immediately.

#### Does Under Armour use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Under Armour's official discount page uses **ID.me**. (Under Armour also appears on GovX and WeSalute marketplaces, but the official UA.com route is ID.me.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes — 20% at UA Brand House stores and 10% at UA Factory House outlets, with verified eligibility. Under Armour's FAQ confirms the in-store rates but doesn't publish the exact in-store verification steps, so confirm the current process at the store.

#### What is excluded?
The discount cannot be combined with other promotional codes, sitewide promotions, or gift card purchases. Under Armour does not publish per-order or annual caps.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Under Armour states the discount cannot be combined with other promotional codes or sitewide promotions. Use it on its own rather than on top of a sale.

#### Does Under Armour offer a first responder, healthcare, teacher, or student discount?
Yes — 20% applies to first responders (Police, Fire, EMT), healthcare workers (Nurses, Doctors), and teachers/educators, all verified through ID.me. Students have a **separate** discount through UNiDAYS, not the ID.me military program.
