# Bose Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Bose offers a military and veteran discount through **ID.me**: **10% off your total cart value, with a maximum savings of $100**, applied in-cart after ID.me verification. The same 10% applies to all eligible ID.me groups (military, veterans, students, nurses, first responders, medical personnel, hospital employees, teachers) — there is no higher, military-specific rate on Bose.com. The offer is **online only at Bose.com (U.S.)**; verification happens in the cart via a "Verify with ID.me" button and applies automatically. The single most important caveat: **aviation headsets are excluded for all groups except Military**, and consumer aviation headsets bought through the standard cart still cap the discount at $100 — the dedicated Bose Military Aviation program (email Military_Headsets@Bose.com) is a separate channel.

Bose gives active-duty military, veterans, and military families 10% off their total cart at Bose.com, with maximum savings of $100, through the third-party verification service ID.me. The same 10% discount also covers students, nurses, first responders, medical personnel, hospital employees, and teachers. You verify your status in your shopping cart and the discount applies automatically.

The one military-specific advantage: aviation headsets, which are excluded for every other group, are eligible for military members.

This is an independent guide, not affiliated with Bose. Bose controls the offer and can change or end it at any time; always confirm current terms in your cart before buying.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Bose.com (U.S.) and add eligible products to your cart.
2. In your cart, select the "Verify with ID.me" button.
3. Sign in to ID.me or create an account, and verify your eligible group (e.g., military).
4. After verification, the 10% discount (max $100) is applied automatically to your cart.
5. Complete checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not available. The Bose ID.me discount is documented only for the online Bose.com cart. The official source describes no in-store redemption path. Show-at-register steps cannot be provided because no official in-store offer is stated.

(Aviation note: To buy aviation headsets through the Bose Military Aviation program, email Military_Headsets@Bose.com — this is a separate channel, not the standard ID.me cart discount.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**, a third-party identity and group-affiliation service. You verify in your Bose cart using the "Verify with ID.me" button; the discount applies automatically once verified. An ID.me account is required (sign in or create one during checkout). Per ID.me's own help center, once you verify a group you generally do not need to re-verify unless you lose account access or go more than a year without using the account, and ID.me may periodically ask you to re-verify identity for security. Do not assume verification is permanent — re-verification can be required. For verification problems, use ID.me support; for order/discount issues at checkout, use Bose customer support.

### FAQ

#### Does Bose offer a military discount?
Yes. Bose offers 10% off your total cart (maximum savings $100) for military personnel and veterans through ID.me, applied in your cart after verification. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### How much is the Bose military discount?
10% off your total cart value, with a maximum savings of $100. All eligible ID.me groups get the same 10%. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family qualify?
Veterans are explicitly listed as eligible. Active-duty, Guard/Reserve, retirees, and military family typically qualify under ID.me's military verification group. Bose's page does not itemize each military subgroup, so verify your specific status through ID.me. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page; ID.me military verification.)

#### How do I verify my status?
Add products to your cart on Bose.com, click "Verify with ID.me" in the cart, sign in or create an ID.me account, and verify your group. The discount applies automatically after verification. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### Does Bose use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Bose uses **ID.me**. There is no official Bose military discount documented through GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Not per Bose's official source. The ID.me discount is documented only for the Bose.com online cart; no in-store redemption is described. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### What is excluded?
Aviation headsets are excluded for all eligible groups except Military. The discount is capped at $100 maximum savings. Other standard exclusions (e.g., gift cards, services) are not specifically addressed on the ID.me program page. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Bose states verified ID.me members see a 10% deduction off total cart value but does not confirm stacking with other promo codes. Treat the ID.me discount as likely not combinable with other coupons; combinability is not stated in the official source. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### Does Bose offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — Bose's ID.me program covers first responders, teachers, nurses, medical personnel, hospital employees, and students, all at the same 10% (max $100). There is no separately documented government-employee discount. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)

#### Are aviation headsets included in the military discount?
For Military members, yes — aviation headsets are included (other groups are excluded), with $100 savings applied in the cart. To purchase aviation headsets through the Bose Military Aviation program specifically, email Military_Headsets@Bose.com. (Source: Bose ID.me Program page.)
