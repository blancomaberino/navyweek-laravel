# 2nd Swing Golf — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$500 used-club order** (e.g., a pre-owned driver + wedge) on 2ndswing.com.

## Headline finding

**2nd Swing Golf has no evergreen, storewide military discount.** ID.me's own page states: *"We're not aware that 2nd Swing Golf offers Military discounts,"* and there's no GovX/SheerID service tier on 2ndswing.com. What the brand's **`/military-discount` page actually promotes is the PING Military Rebate** — a **PING manufacturer** mail-in rebate ($10–$30 per eligible PING club) that 2nd Swing merely facilitates, not a 2nd Swing store discount. Separately, 2nd Swing runs **seasonal, form-gated "extra 10% off used clubs" promos around military holidays** (e.g., the Memorial Day 2026 offer that began **May 21, 2026**, excluding new and custom-order clubs). The real, year-round savings engine at 2nd Swing isn't a military badge at all — it's the **used/pre-owned inventory** (routinely 30–70% off retail) plus its **trade-in program**, which dwarfs any 10% promo. Archetype: **used-goods specialty retailer — no service discount, deep pre-owned pricing + a manufacturer rebate + occasional holiday promos**.

### FAQ

#### Does 2nd Swing Golf offer a military discount?
Not a year-round storewide one. ID.me confirms it's unaware of a 2nd Swing military discount. What exists: the **PING Military Rebate** (a manufacturer rebate) and **seasonal "10% off used clubs"** promos around military holidays.

#### How much is the 2nd Swing military discount?
There's no flat storewide percentage. The **PING rebate** is $10–$30 per eligible PING club; the **seasonal promo** is 10% off used clubs (when live).

#### Do veterans qualify?
Veterans are named in the seasonal used-club promo. The **PING rebate is limited to active duty, active reserves, and military retirees** — not all veterans.

#### How do I verify my status?
No ID.me/GovX. The PING rebate uses your **military ID + mail-in form**; the seasonal promo uses a **sign-up form** that emails a code.

#### Does 2nd Swing use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Not for a military discount. 2nd Swing appears on the ID.me **Shop** portal for cashback (open to all members), but ID.me says it's unaware of a 2nd Swing military discount.

#### Can I combine the offers with promo codes?
Generally no — the PING rebate is "not combinable with other offers," and the seasonal 10% code excludes new/custom clubs. Trade-in credit is the most reliable stackable saver.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from 2nd Swing?
Buy **pre-owned** and use the **trade-in program** (the real savings), file the **PING rebate** if buying eligible new PING, and grab the **10% used-club code** if a military-holiday promo is live.

#### Does 2nd Swing offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
