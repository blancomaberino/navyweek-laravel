# Dyson Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Researched and verified against primary sources on **June 23, 2026**. All factual claims below are sourced; where a source is silent, this brief says "Not stated in official source."

## Executive Summary

**Dyson does not operate its own military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** As of June 23, 2026, there is no military/veteran offer on Dyson.com, and ID.me explicitly states it is "not aware of Dyson offering Military community discounts" (and states the same for First Responders). Dyson's only first-party everyday savings program is **Owner Rewards** — 20% off one selected qualifying floorcare or air-treatment machine via a one-time code, available to Dyson machine owners regardless of military status — alongside its rotating **Deals** and **Certified Refurbished (Outlet)** pricing. Service members and veterans *can* find Dyson products at member/verified prices through **third-party marketplaces** — **GOVX** (military/government-gated reseller, also powering the Veterans Canteen Service store) and **WeSalute** (paid membership; routes to a BenefitHub marketplace) — but these are reseller programs, not a Dyson-run discount, and neither publishes an exact percentage to the public. The single most important caveat: anyone claiming "Dyson 20% military discount via ID.me" is wrong; that 20% figure is the unrelated, non-military Owner Rewards offer.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no Dyson military discount to redeem online. To use Dyson's everyday **Owner Rewards** savings (open to owners, not military-gated):
1. Register your Dyson machine, or call 1-866-693-9766 to set up Owner Rewards and get a one-time-use code.
2. Add a qualifying floorcare or air-treatment machine to your basket at Dyson.com.
3. Enter the one-time code in the payment section at checkout, or sign in and the discount applies automatically.

To check third-party military pricing:
1. **GOVX:** Create a GOVX account and verify your military/government status; Dyson pricing displays only after verification.
2. **WeSalute:** Log in or join WeSalute (paid membership, with a stated 30-day trial), then open the Dyson offer to be routed to the BenefitHub marketplace.

### How To Redeem In Store
There is no Dyson military discount at the register. For everyday Owner Rewards savings in person:
1. Visit a Dyson Demo Store (find one at dyson.com/demo-stores) or call 1-866-693-9766.
2. Ask a Dyson Expert about Owner Rewards and confirm your qualifying machine.
3. Apply your one-time Owner Rewards code to your purchase. (GOVX and WeSalute are online-only marketplaces; no in-store Dyson military redemption exists.)
