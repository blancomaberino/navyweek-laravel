# Dorney Park — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **single-day adult admission** to Dorney Park & Wildwater Kingdom (Allentown, PA), gate price ~$80, compared against every discounted path a service member can use.

## Headline finding

**Dorney Park has a genuine, year-round military discount: a single-day ticket for about $39 (vs. an ~$80 gate price), verified through ID.me**, for active and retired military, veterans, and first responders — plus the ability to buy up to six discounted tickets for family/friends in the same order. On top of that, Dorney historically runs **"Military Days" with FREE admission** during set windows (typically around Memorial Day and again near July 4). Dorney Park is now operated by **Six Flags Entertainment** (post 2024 Cedar Fair–Six Flags merger), so dorneypark.com redirects to sixflags.com/dorneypark. Archetype: **theme park with a real, verified military ticket discount plus recurring free-admission days** — here the official offer genuinely wins; the only better price is $0 during Military Days. The discount is **online-only and can't be combined with other offers or sales.**

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The military discount cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales** — it's a standalone, ID.me-gated ticket price.

4. **Military Days free admission is a separate, time-boxed offer** — you don't stack it with the ~$39 ticket; you use whichever applies to your visit date.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **sixflags.com/dorneypark/military-discounts**.

Dorney Park uses **ID.me**. After you select tickets you'll get an ID.me login prompt; verify your military/first-responder status once, then check out. Keep your ID.me login for future visits.
