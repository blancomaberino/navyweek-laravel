# SIG Sauer — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **SIG SAUER P365 (Nitron, 9mm)** — SIG's best-selling everyday-carry pistol, **MSRP $519.99**, typical street price **~$499** pre-tax (sigsauer.com product page; cross-checked at Palmetto State Armory / gun.deals). Firearms ship to an FFL, not to your door — that constraint shapes every path below.

## Headline finding

SIG SAUER (sigsauer.com) runs a **real, brand-operated discount program for those who serve — but it is split into three distinct pieces, and the biggest one does NOT cover firearms.** The everyday online offer is **15% off all non-sale items on the SIG webstore, verified through VerifyPass** — and it **explicitly excludes firearms, suppressors, and sale items**. To save on an actual gun you use the separate **OFF-DUTY program** (launched January 2025), which gives **special program pricing on select SIG firearms** and is redeemed **through participating OFF-DUTY / SIG Law-Enforcement & Military dealers** (an FFL), not shipped to your home. The deepest tier — **up to ~30% off** under the **SIG Professional Program (SPP)** — exists but is aimed at *armed professionals* (sworn LE, agencies, instructors) and was extended to honorably discharged veterans in 2023; it requires an application and credentials.

The honest bottom line: **there is no single "30% off everything for all veterans online" deal** — that aggregator line conflates the three programs. For a full-price SIG **firearm**, the best standing route is **OFF-DUTY special pricing at a participating dealer** (e.g. a standard P365 at ~**$449.99** vs. ~$499 street; a P320 Carry Optic-Ready at **$499** vs. ~$650 retail). For **accessories, optics, apparel, mags, and ammo bought online**, it's the **15% VerifyPass webstore code**. A strong wildcard for the firearm is the **military Exchange (shopmyexchange.com / NEX)** — it stocks SIG P365/P320 pistols and sells them **tax-free with free shipping** to an Exchange Firearm Shop. Verification is **VerifyPass** — **not ID.me, not SheerID, not GovX-verification**; GovX only sells SIG *accessories/optics* (third-party gear), never the firearms. Archetype: **B — multi-tier identity discount with a hard product carve-out** (firearms fenced off from the flat online %), with a secondary **exchange/pro-deal** flavor.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 15% VerifyPass code excludes firearms, suppressors, and sale items** — it will not apply to a pistol, and it won't stack with a sale price (sale items are carved out entirely).
2. **VerifyPass codes are one-time-use and carry no balance** — one code, one order; treat as a single order-level discount.
3. **OFF-DUTY firearm pricing is a special program price, not a stackable coupon** — you don't add the 15% code or a portal on top; it's the dealer's program price.
4. **SIG Professional Program (SPP) is application-gated** — separate from the online code; credentials/documentation required, redeemed through dealers.
5. **GovX is a closed marketplace** — its member price on SIG *accessories* is the price; no SIG code or cashback stacks, and it carries **no SIG firearms**.
6. **Exchange purchases are tax-free** but do **not** take SIG's OFF-DUTY/VerifyPass codes — it's a substitute channel, not a stack.
7. **Firearms always transfer through an FFL** — online or Exchange firearm orders ship to a dealer/Exchange Firearm Shop; you complete a background check in person. Budget a transfer fee ($25–$50 typical) on out-of-state online firearm buys.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible for OFF-DUTY firearm pricing and the 15% webstore code.
- **Honorably discharged veterans** — Eligible (DD214 or veteran designation on a state-issued ID); named for OFF-DUTY and the 15% webstore code, and added to SPP in 2023.
- **Retired military / reservists / National Guard** — Eligible with military ID.
- **Sworn law enforcement (active/retired)** — Eligible; the core SPP/OFF-DUTY audience (retired needs "retired" credentials).
- **Corrections, court officers, coroners** — Named eligible for OFF-DUTY/SPP with agency credentials or documentation.
- **First responders / EMT / firefighters (incl. volunteer) / paramedics** — Eligible with current credentials.
- **Security professionals** — Named for OFF-DUTY firearm pricing.
- **Government employees** — Named on the **15% VerifyPass webstore** offer (verify your category at checkout).
- **Spouses / dependents / Gold Star / caregivers** — **Not separately named** by SIG for these programs; do not assume eligibility — verify with VerifyPass or the dealer.

### How To Redeem — 15% webstore code (online, sigsauer.com)
1. On sigsauer.com, start the discount and **verify your eligibility through VerifyPass**.
2. Receive your **one-time coupon code** (single use, no balance).
3. Apply it at checkout to **non-sale, non-firearm/suppressor** items (accessories, optics, apparel, mags, ammo, parts).
4. It won't apply to a firearm, suppressor, or a sale item — use a different path for those.

### How To Redeem — OFF-DUTY firearm pricing (dealer / FFL)
1. Find a **participating OFF-DUTY dealer** or a **SIG Law-Enforcement/Military dealer** (SIG dealer locator).
2. Bring **approved purchaser ID** — military ID, DD214/veteran state-ID designation, or agency credentials.
3. Buy the firearm at the **OFF-DUTY special program price**; complete the background check and FFL transfer in person.
4. For the full portfolio (suppressors, ammunition, electro-optics at program pricing), go through a **SIG LE/Military dealer**.

### How To Redeem — SIG Professional Program (armed professionals)
1. Apply at **thesigapp.com** and submit required credentials/documentation.
2. Once approved, purchase at **up to ~30% off** through Master/Elite/LE dealers — personal-use, credential-gated.

### How Verification Works
SIG's own discounts verify through **VerifyPass**: you confirm eligibility once and receive a **one-time code** for the webstore. In-person firearm buys verify with your **ID/credentials at the dealer**. The SPP tier requires a **separate application** at thesigapp.com. There is **no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX-verification** for SIG's direct program. SIG and its dealers can re-verify eligibility and change or end any program at any time.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The **15% webstore code excludes firearms, suppressors, and all sale items** — accessories/optics/apparel/mags/ammo only.
- VerifyPass codes are **one-time-use, no balance** — one code per order, non-combinable.

- **All firearms transfer through an FFL** — online/Exchange firearm orders ship to a dealer; expect a transfer fee ($25–$50) on out-of-state online buys and a background check.

### FAQ

#### Does SIG Sauer offer a military discount?
Yes — three ways. **15% off non-sale webstore items** (via VerifyPass, firearms excluded), **OFF-DUTY special pricing on select firearms** at participating dealers, and the **SIG Professional Program (up to ~30%)** for armed professionals and, since 2023, honorably discharged veterans.

#### How much is the SIG Sauer military discount?
Online, **15% off non-sale accessories/optics/apparel/ammo** (not firearms). On firearms, **OFF-DUTY special program pricing** (e.g. a P365 around **$449.99** vs. ~$499 street). Armed professionals can reach **up to ~30%** through SPP. There's no flat "30% off everything online."

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
**Honorably discharged veterans** qualify (DD214 or veteran state-ID designation), and were added to SPP in 2023. SIG does **not separately name spouses or dependents** for these programs — verify your category with VerifyPass or the dealer.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online, through **VerifyPass** — you get a **one-time code**. For firearms, you show **ID/credentials at a participating dealer**. SPP requires an **application at thesigapp.com**.

#### Does SIG Sauer use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No — SIG's own verification is **VerifyPass**. **GovX** sells SIG *accessories/optics* (and third-party gear) to verified members but **doesn't verify SIG's direct discount and doesn't sell SIG firearms**.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or online?
Both, but split: the **15% code is online** (non-firearm gear); **firearm OFF-DUTY pricing is at a dealer** (in person, FFL). SPP is dealer-based.

#### What is excluded?
The 15% webstore code excludes **firearms, suppressors, and all sale items**. Firearm discounts are separate (OFF-DUTY/SPP) and require credentials and an FFL transfer.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale items?
No — the 15% code is one-time-use, non-combinable, and doesn't touch sale items or firearms. OFF-DUTY is a set program price, not a stackable coupon.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a SIG?
For a **firearm**: OFF-DUTY special pricing at a participating dealer (SPP up to ~30% if you're an armed professional). If you're **Exchange-eligible**, price it **tax-free** at shopmyexchange.com. For **gear/accessories online**: the **15% VerifyPass code** or a public rebate.

#### Does SIG offer a first responder, law enforcement, or government discount?
Yes — sworn LE, corrections/court officers, coroners, first responders, EMTs, firefighters, and paramedics are core eligible groups for OFF-DUTY/SPP; **government employees** are named on the **15% VerifyPass webstore** offer.

#### Can I buy SIG at the Exchange?
Yes — shopmyexchange.com and the Navy Exchange stock SIG P365/P320 pistols **tax-free with free shipping** to an Exchange Firearm Shop (background check and transfer completed in person). Online Exchange shopping is open to all honorably discharged veterans; in-store base access has its own eligibility rules.
