## Headline finding

**Peak Design offers a real, official 10% military discount** on peakdesign.com via **ID.me**, open to US & Canadian active duty, veterans/retirees, spouses, and immediate family (the same 10% extends to students, teachers, medical workers, and first responders). But this is a classic **archetype C brand (real discount, the sale usually beats it)** with a **K secondary (active ExpertVoice store)**: the 10% "may not be valid during a site-wide sale, bundles or on already discounted (Sale) items," and Peak Design's Black Friday 2025 sale discounted everything up to 30%. During any sitewide sale, skip the military discount and buy the public price through a cashback portal; if you hold an ExpertVoice-eligible profile, check its Peak Design store before paying retail anywhere.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 10% does not stack on sales, bundles, or already-discounted items** — Peak Design's own MSFR page: "Additional discounts may not be valid during a site-wide sale, bundles or on already discounted (Sale) items."
2. **Sale + cashback is the portal-safe stack** — no code is entered, so standard portal terms are satisfied; an ID.me-gated checkout, by contrast, frequently breaks portal tracking, so don't count on cashback on top of the military 10%.
3. **ExpertVoice is a closed substitute** — no cashback, no codes, personal-use and no-resale terms; compare its net price against the public paths.
4. **One identity discount per order** — the military/student/teacher/responder discounts are the same 10% program; they don't combine with each other.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to peakdesign.com/pages/msfr-discount (or /pages/idme).
2. Click "Verify With ID.ME" and complete verification (free ID.me account).
3. Return to the store; the 10% applies to qualifying full-price purchases at checkout.
4. If a sitewide sale is live, price both ways — the sale price usually wins and the 10% likely won't apply on sale items.

Peak Design uses ID.me. You verify once with your ID.me login before checkout; Peak Design's page doesn't state a re-verification frequency, so don't assume it's permanent. Eligibility questions route to help.id.me.

### FAQ
#### Does Peak Design offer a military discount?
Yes — 10% off at peakdesign.com for verified US & Canadian military, via ID.me.
#### How much is the Peak Design military discount?
10%. The same rate applies to students, teachers, medical providers, hospital workers, and first responders.
#### Do veterans and military spouses qualify?
Yes — Peak Design's page lists active duty, veterans/retirees, military spouses, and immediate family members.
#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me: click "Verify With ID.ME" on Peak Design's discount page and sign in or create a free ID.me account.
#### Can I use it on sale items or during Black Friday?
Generally no — the brand says additional discounts may not be valid during sitewide sales, on bundles, or on Sale items. During those windows the public sale price is the better deal anyway.
#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Peak Design?
During a sitewide sale (Black Friday 2025 reached 30% off), buy the public sale price through a cashback portal. Outside sales, use the ID.me 10%. ExpertVoice-eligible buyers should compare its store price too.
#### Does Peak Design use GovX or SheerID?
No — verification is ID.me on the brand site. There's no Peak Design GovX storefront; Provengo and ExpertVoice are the third-party channels.
#### Can I buy Peak Design through ExpertVoice?
Peak Design runs an ExpertVoice brand store. Eligibility and pricing sit behind the ExpertVoice login — confirm your group qualifies for this store before counting on it; purchases are personal-use only.
