# Origin USA (Origin Maine) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $200 order of regular-priced Origin apparel/gear (e.g., jeans + a boot or gi component), shipped to a US address.

## Headline finding

Origin (originusa.com — the Maine-made apparel/BJJ/boots/workwear brand founded by Jocko Willink and Pete Roberts) offers a military/veteran discount **through GovX only**. Origin's site embeds GovX's verification script (`id-shop.govx.com/…/govx.js`), and both Origin's GovX page and the VA's veteran-discount roundup confirm the partnership: *"we've partnered with GovX to offer a discount on all regular priced items."* GovX gates the exact live percentage behind verification, so no fixed public % is published; the VA roundup has described it as discounted pricing on individual items / around 5% on the full order, but treat the exact figure as GovX-gated. There is **no ID.me path** and **no on-site "VETERAN" code** today — the old `VETERAN2020`-style codes are historical and not verified live. Best path for eligible shoppers: verify with GovX for the regular-price discount; for everyone, Origin's own **20% Off** collection and seasonal sales are the public levers.

## Stacking Rules (verified)

3. **Exact GovX % is revealed after verification** — the public description ("a discount on all regular priced items") is qualitative; the VA roundup's ~5% figure is indicative, not guaranteed.
4. **Free shipping over $75** is automatic and independent of the discount.
