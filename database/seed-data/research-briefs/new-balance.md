# New Balance Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

New Balance offers a **15% off online discount** to verified military members (and to first responders, nurses, medical providers, hospital employees, teachers, government employees, and seniors 60+). Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**; once verified you receive a **unique, one-time-use discount code** to enter at checkout on newbalance.com. The offer is **online-only and applies to full-price purchases only** — it cannot be combined with other offers, is limited to **one code per customer per 30 days**, and an extensive list of styles is excluded. The single most important caveat: the ID.me store listing caps the offer at **"15% off orders up to $400 plus free shipping,"** and a long Sale Exclusions list (many popular 990, 1080, 550, 327, 9060, etc. styles) means many in-demand shoes are **not** eligible. New Balance does **not** offer this discount in physical stores.

> Accuracy note: Several aggregators show **25% off** (worthepenny) or **20% promo codes** (marieclaire/coupon pages) for New Balance. Those are **not** the verified military discount. Only **15%** is confirmed on New Balance's official idme.html page and on the ID.me store page as of June 23, 2026. One web summary surfaced a stale "10%" figure — also not supported by the current official page.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to New Balance's special discounts page (newbalance.com/idme.html) and select **Verify with ID.me**.
2. Sign in to ID.me or create a free account, and verify your eligible community (e.g., Military).
3. Confirm your email if prompted; ID.me issues you a **unique discount code**.
4. Shop newbalance.com and add **eligible, full-price** items to your bag.
5. Enter your unique code at checkout to apply the **15%** discount (one-time use; one code per 30 days).

### How To Redeem In Store

### How Verification Works
New Balance uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You create a free ID.me account, verify your community (Military, First Responder, Nurse, Medical Provider, Hospital Employee, Teacher, Government Employee, or Senior 60+), and receive a **unique, one-time-use discount code**. You enter that code at checkout on newbalance.com. New Balance states you will not receive marketing from ID.me or New Balance unless you opt in. Because each code is single-use with a one-per-30-days limit, you re-verify/request a new code through ID.me for future orders. For ID.me account issues, New Balance directs shoppers to help.id.me.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Online only:** "Discount valid only at newbalance.com." Not valid in stores.
- **Full-price only:** does not apply to sale/clearance items.
- **One code per customer per 30 days; each code valid for one-time use only.**
- **Cannot be combined with other offers** (no stacking with promo codes or sales).
- **Published style exclusions:** New Balance's Sale Exclusions page lists specific excluded styles across Running, Lifestyle, Tennis, Golf, Baseball/Softball, Football, Skate, Kids, Apparel, and Accessories — including flagship models such as 990, 1080, 860, 1540, 550, 327, 530, 574, 9060, 1906, 2002, and the COCO tennis line. **Gift cards and eGift cards are excluded.** This list changes; always check sale-exclusions.html for the current styles.
- **Order cap:** ID.me's store listing states "15% off orders **up to $400**" — larger orders may not receive the full benefit beyond that threshold. *(Flag: the dollar cap appears on the ID.me store page, not verbatim on newbalance.com/idme.html; verify at checkout.)*

### FAQ

#### Does New Balance offer a military discount?
Yes. New Balance offers verified military members **15% off** full-price online orders at newbalance.com, with eligibility confirmed through ID.me.

#### How much is the New Balance military discount?
**15% off** full-price purchases online. (Coupon sites showing 25% or 20% are not the verified military offer; an older "10%" figure is also out of date.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes. New Balance's own discount page lists Military eligibility as "active duty, veterans, retirees, **spouses, dependents,** and more."

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify through **ID.me** on newbalance.com/idme.html. After verifying, you receive a unique discount code to enter at checkout.

#### Does New Balance use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
New Balance uses **ID.me**. (It does not use SheerID for this program.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. The discount is **online only** at newbalance.com and is not redeemable in physical New Balance stores.

#### What is excluded?
The discount is full-price online only, can't be combined with other offers, and is limited to one code per customer per 30 days (one-time use). New Balance also publishes a long list of excluded styles (including many 990, 1080, 550, 327, and 9060 models) and excludes gift cards. Check New Balance's Sale Exclusions page for the current list.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The discount applies to full-price items only and "cannot be combined with other offers," so it won't stack with promo codes or sale prices.

#### Does New Balance offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or senior discount?
Yes — the same **15%** extends to first responders, nurses, medical providers, hospital employees, teachers, government employees, and seniors 60+, each verified through ID.me. Students are not listed among New Balance's eligible communities on its discount page.
