# Liberty Safe — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one mid-size Liberty gun safe at ~$1,500 (e.g. a Fatboy-class model), before tax/delivery.

## Headline finding

Liberty Safe runs an **official mail-in "Police / Fire / Military / EMS" rebate** (marketed in blog copy as a "Heroes" appreciation program) for active military, veterans, and first responders. It is **not** an instant online code and **not** an ID.me/GovX-gated checkout discount — it is a **rebate you claim by mail after purchase**, with a copy of your receipt and proof of service. Reported rebate value is **tiered by how much you spend on a qualifying safe, roughly $25–$250** (some Liberty marketing says "save up to $300"). Because Liberty safes are also sold through authorized dealers who run their own stacking sales, the genuine maximum-savings path is usually **a dealer sale price + the mail-in hero rebate**, not the rebate alone.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + dealer terms)
1. The hero rebate is a **manufacturer mail-in rebate** based on your receipt — it generally **stacks on top of a dealer's sale price**, since the rebate is calculated from what you actually paid for the safe.
2. Rebate value **excludes tax and delivery** and is based on the safe (plus qualifying accessories) purchase price.
3. You must **register the safe** and **mail the completed form with a receipt copy and proof of service** by the program's postmark deadline; allow ~6–8 weeks for the rebate.
4. Program runs in defined windows (e.g. a stated purchase period and postmark cutoff) — confirm current-year dates on the official page.

### How To Redeem (mail-in)
1. Buy a qualifying Liberty branded safe (and any qualifying accessories) within the program period.
2. Register your safe at libertysafe.com.
3. Complete the Police/Fire/Military/EMS rebate form; attach a copy of your receipt and proof of service.

5. Allow ~6–8 weeks for the rebate check.

### FAQ

#### Does Liberty Safe offer a military discount?
Yes — an official mail-in rebate for active military, veterans, and first responders, claimed after purchase.

#### How much is the Liberty Safe military rebate?
Reported around $25–$250, tiered by how much you spend on the safe (Liberty marketing cites "up to $300"). Confirm the current tier chart on the official page.

#### Do veterans and first responders qualify?
Yes — active military, veterans, police, fire, and EMS are all listed as eligible.

#### How do I verify my status?
By mailing proof of service plus your receipt with the rebate form — it's a manual mail-in process, not an instant online code.

Tax and delivery are excluded from the rebate basis. Non-qualifying models or purchases outside the program window don't count — check the current model list and dates.

#### Can I combine it with a sale?
Yes — because the rebate is based on your receipt, buying on a dealer sale and then filing the rebate captures both. This is the best path.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Liberty safe?
Buy on a dealer sale, then mail in the hero rebate. Compare dealer prices and GovX pricing first.
