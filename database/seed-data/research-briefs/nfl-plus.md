# NFL+ — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **NFL+ Premium Annual** subscription — regular price **$99.99/season** (basic **NFL+ Annual** is $49.99/season; both priced pre-any-applicable-state-tax; no tax-free channel exists for this product).

## Headline finding

NFL+ (the league's own streaming subscription) **does run a Military / First Responder discount, verified through ID.me**, with **per-season promo codes** you apply at checkout. The catch that matters for a page: **the exact military percentage is not published anywhere public** — NFL reveals the code/price only *after* you verify with ID.me, and it resets the codes every season. Adjacent ID.me-verified groups (nurses, first responders, hospital staff) have historically shown **25% off** on the same store, so that's the best available proxy — but treat the military number as **unverified until you see it in-account**. The bigger money lesson: NFL+ runs a **public 20%-off annual promo every preseason (open to everyone, no verification)** and cuts prices far deeper in the off-season (historically as low as **$19.99/year**), so the ID.me discount frequently **does not beat the public sale**, and the two **do not stack** (one promo code per order). Archetype: **subscription/OTT streaming brand** with a discretionary, verification-gated, seasonally-reset discount layered under aggressive public promo pricing. Do not confuse NFL+ with **NFL Sunday Ticket** — the widely-cited "$198 military" deal is a *different product* (Sunday Ticket on YouTube), not NFL+.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **One promo code per order.** The ID.me military/first-responder code and the public 20%-off code **cannot be combined** — you enter a single code at checkout. Choose the lower one.
2. **Annual-plan promos generally exclude monthly plans.** The public 20% and off-season deals are advertised on **annual** subscriptions; monthly ($6.99 / $14.99) is typically full price.
3. **The ID.me discount requires ID.me verification** completed before/at checkout; codes are **season-specific** and prior-season codes are voided (per NFL support).
4. **Bundle pricing (Disney+/Hulu) is its own track** — you can't layer the standalone NFL+ promo codes onto a bundle subscription.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — eligible (ID.me Military).
- **National Guard / Reserve** — eligible (ID.me Military).
- **Veterans / retirees** — eligible per standard ID.me military verification *(NFL's exact list is gated; medium confidence)*.
- **Military spouses / dependents** — typically eligible via ID.me military group *(gated; medium confidence)*.
- **First responders** (police, firefighters, EMTs/paramedics) — eligible (NFL's discount is explicitly "Military/**First Responder**").
- **Nurses / hospital employees** — **not** part of the military line, but the NFL+ ID.me store historically lists a separate **25% off** for these groups.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the NFL+ Military/First Responder activation flow (linked from NFL support) or the NFL+ ID.me store page.
2. Select your category (Military or First Responder) and **verify with ID.me**.
3. Copy the **season promo code** ID.me/NFL provides after verification.
4. At NFL+ checkout, choose your plan, click **Redeem Coupon**, paste the code, and click **Redeem**.
5. Before paying, **compare** that price to the current public promo (e.g., 20% off annual) and use whichever is lower — they don't stack.

*(In-store redemption: not applicable — NFL+ is a digital subscription.)*

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**. You create/sign in to an ID.me account and confirm military or first-responder status. Codes are **season-specific**: NFL issues new promo codes each season and voids prior-season codes, so re-verify/re-pull a fresh code each year. No indication the discount is permanent or auto-renews at the discounted rate — treat it as a per-season code, not a lifetime price.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **One promo code per order** — no stacking with public sales.
- Promo codes are **season-specific**; expired/prior codes won't redeem.
- Public **annual** promos generally **exclude monthly** plans.
- Exact discount percentage for military is **not disclosed** until after verification — the figure is gated, so any specific "%" quoted by coupon sites is unverified.
- US only.

### FAQ

#### Does NFL+ offer a military discount?
Yes. NFL+ offers a Military and First Responder discount verified through ID.me, applied as a promo code at checkout on NFL+ and NFL+ Premium plans (US only).

#### How much is the NFL+ military discount?
The exact percentage isn't published — NFL reveals your discounted price only after you verify with ID.me, and codes reset each season. Adjacent ID.me-verified groups (nurses, first responders, hospital staff) have historically seen **25% off** on the same store, which is the best available proxy. Compare your revealed code against the public 20%-off annual promo and use the lower one.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify under standard ID.me military verification, and military spouses/dependents are typically covered by ID.me's military group. NFL's exact eligible list is gated behind the offer, so confirm during verification (medium confidence).

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **ID.me**: sign in, confirm military or first-responder status, then apply the code ID.me/NFL provides at NFL+ checkout.

#### Does NFL+ use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**ID.me.** NFL+'s military/first-responder discount is ID.me-verified. GovX covers NFL *tickets/merch*, not the streaming subscription. NFL Shop merchandise uses ID.me separately (about 15% off). SheerID/WeSalute/VerifyPass are not used for NFL+.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Online only — NFL+ is a digital subscription redeemed at nfl.com/plus.

#### What is excluded?
Monthly plans are usually excluded from annual promos, codes are season-specific, and you can only use one code per order (no stacking with public sales).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. It's one promo code per order — the military code and the public 20%-off sale code can't be combined. Pick whichever is cheaper.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy NFL+?
Verify with ID.me, then compare your military code to the current public 20%-off annual price and take the lower. If you're flexible on timing, an off-season or Black Friday flash sale (historically $19.99–$29.99/year) usually beats both.

#### Does NFL+ offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
First responders qualify alongside military. The NFL+ ID.me store has historically listed a separate **25% off** for nurses and hospital employees. No confirmed dedicated teacher/student NFL+ rate.

#### Is the NFL+ military discount the same as the NFL Sunday Ticket "$198 military" deal?
No. Those are different products. The **$198 military** price is **NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube**; **NFL+** is a separate streaming subscription with its own ID.me discount.
