# Brilliant Earth — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$200 jewelry order (enough to clear the $150 gift-with-purchase threshold), with a secondary look at a ~$3,000 engagement-ring purchase.

## Headline finding

Brilliant Earth's "military discount" is **not a percentage off** — it's a **gift with purchase: a free pair of silver stud earrings on any order over $150**, unlocked by verifying through **ID.me** (a promo code is issued to use at cart). The **same offer** extends to **nurses/medical providers, first responders, and teachers**; **students** get the same free studs at a **$250** minimum via **Student Beans**. That's the whole military offer — a modest gift, **not** a discount on the ring or the diamond. It **can't be combined** with other Brilliant Earth offers.

Because the "military perk" is a fixed low-dollar gift, **it is almost never the best way for a service member to save on a real Brilliant Earth purchase.** On a $3,000 engagement ring, a free pair of silver studs is a rounding error. The genuine max-savings paths are: **(1) the live public promo code** (e.g., a lab-diamond studs gift on $1,000+ orders under code STUDS, "ends soon"), **(2) a cashback portal** (Rakuten has listed Brilliant Earth up to ~5% — date-check it), and **(3) choosing lab-grown over natural diamonds**, which is the single biggest lever on total cost at Brilliant Earth. Archetype: **E-ish "identity gift-with-purchase" but effectively closer to J/B** — the identity perk exists but is small, so the honest page routes buyers to the promo/cashback/lab-diamond levers. Watch the stacking landmine: an **ID.me-gated promo code and a cashback portal usually don't both pay** — pick one.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The ID.me free-studs offer "cannot be applied or combined with other offers available on site"** — so it won't stack with the public promo code or another gift.
2. **ID.me-gated promo codes typically break cashback tracking** — Rakuten and similar portals commonly void cash back on coupon/identity-gated orders, so **"ID.me studs + Rakuten" usually does NOT both pay.** Choose the higher-value single path.
3. **Student (Student Beans) and service-professional (ID.me) offers are the same gift** at different thresholds — you use one, not both.
4. **Lab-grown vs natural is a product choice, not a discount** — it's independent of every code, so it layers "for free" with whichever single savings path you pick.
5. **Free shipping/returns apply to everyone** — not a stack you need a code for.
