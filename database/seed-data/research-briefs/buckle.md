# Buckle — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of BKE/Buckle denim — **$99.95**. Illustrative sales tax at 7% ≈ $7.00 unless noted.

## Headline finding

Buckle offers a **genuine 10% military discount** verified through **SheerID**, and it's unusually good on two counts most retailers won't match: it works **both online and in store**, and it applies to your **entire merchandise purchase — regular *and* sale**. Eligibility is broad (active duty **and** veterans, National Guard, Reserves, plus spouses and dependents). You verify **once** via the "Military Discount" link on the shopping-bag page and it's tied to your Buckle.com account. Because the 10% covers sale items — where most brands cut you off — the counterintuitive "skip your discount during a sale" trap doesn't apply here; the military 10% layers on top of Buckle's already-marked-down Sale section (up to ~75% off). The open question is stacking with the **Buckle Credit Card** first-purchase 10% and **Buckle Rewards** — treat those as unconfirmed until verified against Buckle's terms. Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount)** leaning **D-adjacent** because the 10% explicitly applies to sale merchandise (rare and valuable).
