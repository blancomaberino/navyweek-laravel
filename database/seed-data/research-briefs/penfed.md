# PenFed (Pentagon Federal Credit Union) — Credit Cards Military Benefits & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 19, 2026 · **Baseline "cart" for all math:** one **PenFed Pathfinder® Rewards Visa Signature® ($95 annual fee)** plus **$20,000/year of everyday spend on a PenFed Power Cash Rewards card** — the two cards where military status changes the price.

> This is a **membership-benefit page, not a coupon page** — but it is also *not* a statutory SCRA/MLA page like Amex or Chase. PenFed's military benefit is **Honors Advantage**, a program PenFed created and controls: military members (including **veterans and retirees**) automatically get the Pathfinder's **$95 annual fee waived**, **4X travel / 3X dining** instead of 3X/2X, and **2% flat cash back** on Power Cash instead of 1.5%. That's the distinctive story: statutory waivers (Amex, Chase) end when active duty ends — **PenFed's fee waiver and rate boosts follow veterans and retirees for life**, because they're membership perks, not federal-law compliance.

## Headline finding

PenFed has **no coupon-style military discount**, and since **January 1, 2019** (its merger with Progressive Credit Union created an open charter) **anyone can join** — PenFed's own site says "Everyone is eligible to apply." The military benefit lives in the **Honors Advantage** program on two of its four credit cards: qualify by being "in an active military service status, a member of the Reserves or National Guard, an honorably discharged U.S. Military Veteran or retired from such service" — **or** by holding a qualifying PenFed checking account. Honors Advantage waives the **Pathfinder's $95 annual fee**, boosts Pathfinder earning to **4X travel / 3X dining**, and bumps **Power Cash to a flat 2%** cash back. Separately, PenFed honors **SCRA** at the statutory **6% cap** on pre-service balances (Navy Federal voluntarily goes to 4%). Archetype: **military-heritage issuer open to everyone** — closest to **multi-tier identity program (E)**, with a **statutory-relief (Amex-style) secondary** for SCRA; the page's job is to explain who actually gets what, and that veterans/retirees are the uniquely-served group.

## Stacking Rules (verified)

1. **Honors Advantage benefits stack with each other and with card bonuses** — the fee waiver, the 4X/3X boost, and the 2% rate are program features of the account, and welcome bonuses (50K Pathfinder / $100 Power Cash / 15K Platinum) pay normally alongside them.
2. **Military route and checking route are alternative qualifiers, not additive** — qualifying both ways earns nothing extra; the military route simply has no maintenance condition.
3. **Honors Advantage does not touch Platinum Rewards or Gold** — the compare table marks both "Honor Advantage Member Benefits: No." Military status changes nothing on those two cards.
4. **SCRA and Honors Advantage are independent and can coexist** — Honors Advantage is a rewards/fee program; SCRA is statutory rate relief on pre-service balances. One does not preclude the other.
5. **The waiver is conditional and revocable for checking-route members** — "If at any time, you fail to maintain the eligibility requirements, you will no longer be eligible for the waiver" (advance notice given). Military-route qualifiers don't have a balance condition to lose.
6. **No coupon layer exists** — PenFed products have no promo-code field; nothing external stacks.

PenFed — Pentagon Federal Credit Union — doesn't have a "military discount," and despite the name it isn't military-only: anyone in the U.S. has been able to join since 2019. ### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty servicemembers** — Honors Advantage, automatic; plus SCRA (6% cap, waived late/over-limit fees) on pre-service PenFed balances.
- **Reserve / National Guard members** — Honors Advantage, automatic (activation not required by the published terms).
- **Honorably discharged veterans** — Honors Advantage, automatic. This is PenFed's signature edge — statutory waivers at other issuers exclude veterans.
- **Military retirees** — Honors Advantage, automatic.
- **Military spouses / dependents** — **not listed** in the military route; qualify via the checking-account route instead.
- **Civilians** — full membership open to everyone; Honors Advantage via Access America Checking, or Free Checking with a $500 minimum daily balance or $500+/month direct deposit.

### How To Get It (Redeem)
1. **Join PenFed** (open to everyone): open a share savings account in the online join flow (small opening deposit — historically about $5).
2. **Apply for the card** — Pathfinder (travel) or Power Cash (flat cash back). Non-members can apply; membership is created during the application.
3. **Qualify for Honors Advantage** — military status (active, Guard/Reserve, honorably discharged veteran, retiree) qualifies you at application with nothing else to do; otherwise be/become the primary owner of a qualifying PenFed checking account.
4. If you qualify **at application**, the Pathfinder fee is waived immediately; if you qualify **later**, the fee is waived at your next account anniversary.
5. **SCRA (separate, pre-service accounts only):** gather activation orders and upload to PenFed.org/secureupload — during active duty or within 180 days after it ends.

### How Verification Works
PenFed verifies eligibility itself inside membership and card applications — there is no ID.me/SheerID/GovX step. The published terms do not spell out which documents prove veteran/retiree status for Honors Advantage (expect service details; a DD-214 may be requested). Checking-route eligibility is monitored on an ongoing basis: fail the $500 balance/direct-deposit condition and PenFed ends the waiver with advance notice.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Honors Advantage boosts only **Pathfinder** and **Power Cash** — Platinum Rewards and Gold get nothing from military status.
- The military route requires an **honorable** discharge for veterans ("honorably discharged U.S. Military Veteran or retired from such service").
- Spouses/dependents are not in the published military-route language.
- Checking-route waiver is conditional (maintain the account and, for Free Checking, the $500 balance or direct deposit).
- Pathfinder/Platinum points expire after 5 years; Power Cash rewards don't expire while the account is open.
- Priority Pass Select membership is complimentary but **lounge visit fees apply**; Global Entry/TSA credit recurs every 4 years, not annually.
- SCRA covers pre-service accounts only, must be requested (orders required), and PenFed's published cap is the statutory 6% — not the 4% some issuers offer.
- Rates/offers "current as of July 2026 and subject to change."

### FAQ

#### Does PenFed offer a military discount?
Not a coupon or percent-off. PenFed's military benefit is the **Honors Advantage** program: the Pathfinder card's $95 annual fee is waived, its travel/dining earning rises to 4X/3X, and the Power Cash card pays a flat 2% instead of 1.5% — automatically for qualifying military members.

#### Is PenFed only for the military?
No. Since its January 1, 2019 merger with Progressive Credit Union gave it an open charter, anyone in the U.S. can join — PenFed's site says "Everyone is eligible to apply." The military connection now shows up in better card terms, not the membership door.

#### Do veterans and retirees qualify?
Yes — this is PenFed's distinctive edge. The published terms cover anyone "in an active military service status, a member of the Reserves or National Guard, an honorably discharged U.S. Military Veteran or retired from such service." Unlike SCRA/MLA fee waivers at other issuers, it doesn't end when you leave service.

#### Do military spouses qualify?
Not through the military route — spouses and dependents aren't listed. A spouse can get identical benefits through the checking-account route (Access America Checking, or Free Checking with a $500 balance or $500+ monthly direct deposit).

#### How much is the PenFed Pathfinder annual fee, and how is it waived?
$95. It's waived if you qualify for Honors Advantage at application (military status or qualifying checking account); qualify later and it's waived at your next anniversary. Lose eligibility (checking route) and the waiver ends with advance notice.

#### What does Honors Advantage do on the Power Cash card?
Bumps cash back from 1.5% to a flat **2% on all purchases**, with no caps and a $0 annual fee. PenFed's rewards terms state it in writing: "If you qualify for Honors Advantage program, you will earn 2% cash rewards on net new qualified purchases."

#### Does PenFed use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
No. PenFed verifies military status itself within its membership and card applications.

#### What are PenFed's SCRA benefits?
For accounts opened before active duty: a **6% interest-rate cap** and **no late or over-limit fees**, effective from your active-duty start date. You must request it — upload activation orders to PenFed.org/secureupload during active duty or within 180 days after. Note: Navy Federal voluntarily caps at 4%; PenFed publishes the statutory 6%.

#### Which PenFed cards get no military benefit?
Platinum Rewards (5X gas/EV, 3X supermarkets) and the Gold Visa (low-APR card) — PenFed's comparison table marks both "Honors Advantage Member Benefits: No." Military status doesn't change their terms.

#### What's actually the best move for a servicemember?
Active duty: open premium cards under MLA at issuers like Amex ($0 on an $895 Platinum) for the biggest fee waivers, and use PenFed's 2% Power Cash/4X Pathfinder as keepers that stay boosted after you separate. Veterans/retirees: PenFed is one of the only issuers still giving you a fee-waived card — take it.

#### Is PenFed membership really open to anyone? How do I join?
Yes — open a share savings account in the online join flow (small opening deposit, reported around $5); you can also start a card application as a non-member and PenFed creates the membership during the process.
