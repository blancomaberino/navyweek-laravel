## Headline finding

Nuna (nunababy.com — premium baby gear, car seats, strollers) publishes **no military, veteran, or first-responder discount** on its own site, and there is **no ID.me / SheerID / GovX verification program** for the brand. Nuna is a strict **Minimum Advertised Price (MAP)** brand: it does not run public sitewide sales or coupon codes on core car seats and strollers, and its own support pages describe consumer service and contact routes — not a discount program. The honest headline: *there is no direct Nuna military discount to claim; the real savings paths are (1) authorized retailers' loyalty/rewards and periodic events (Nordstrom, Bloomingdale's, Dillard's, Albee Baby, buybuy-type stores), (2) tax-free/registry completion perks, and (3) occasional brand-run gestures around holidays — none of which is a standing, verifiable military rate.* This is a **"no first-party discount" finding (MAP-enforced premium brand)** — and documenting that honestly is the useful result. Archetype: **honest "no discount" page that redirects to the genuine save paths.**
