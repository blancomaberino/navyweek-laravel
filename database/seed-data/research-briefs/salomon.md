# Salomon Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Salomon (US) offers a **20% military discount**, but not as a typical instant ID.me/SheerID checkout offer. Salomon routes military members, veterans, law enforcement, and firefighters through the **Amer Sports Pro Club** (amersportsproclub.com), a membership program Amer Sports runs across its brands (Salomon, Wilson, Atomic, Armada, Peak Performance, Arc'teryx). The "20% OFF MILITARY" figure is stated directly on Salomon's own Promotions & Discounts page, which links to the Amer Sports Pro Club to redeem. Membership is "by invitation or application, based on country-specific eligibility criteria"; applicants without an invitation code select a job category (the relevant one is "SECURITY & DEFENSE PRO") and must submit a supporting document proving eligibility. Approval is not instant, and the program carries a yearly spending allowance per online store. The single most important caveat: this is **not** a one-click coupon — it requires creating and getting approved for a separate Pro Club account, and joining the Pro Club cancels access to Salomon's S/PLUS loyalty program (unless you use a different email). Salomon does not state a self-service military discount on salomon.com checkout itself, and the discount does not apply in Salomon outlet or retail stores.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Salomon's Promotions & Discounts page (salomon.com/en-us/lp/g/promotions-discounts).
2. Click "20% Off Military" (it links to the Amer Sports Pro Club / "Log In").
3. On amersportsproclub.com, choose your shipping country (United States) and create an account: select "I don't have an invitation code," then choose the profile "Security & Defense Pro."
4. Submit the required supporting document proving eligibility (proof of employment/qualification/service).
5. Wait for your application to be reviewed and approved (not instant).
6. Once approved, log in at amersportsproclub.com; your account synchronizes with salomon.com and preferred pricing is applied. Shop within the yearly spending allowance.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not available. The Amer Sports Pro Club program and Salomon promo codes do not apply at the Salomon outlet or Salomon retail stores. The military discount is online only through the Pro Club.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by Salomon/Amer Sports through the Amer Sports Pro Club, not by a third-party instant-verification service like ID.me or GovX. You register either with an invitation code or by applying with a supporting document that proves your eligibility (proof of employment/qualification). Approval is a manual review and is not instant. Membership privileges are valid for one year; you must re-confirm eligibility to renew, and renewal instructions are sent about one month before expiration. Note: signing up for the Pro Club cancels your access to Salomon's S/PLUS loyalty program unless you register the Pro Club with a different email address. For issues, use the Amer Sports Pro Club Contact page or Salomon customer service (USA +1-833-986-0382).

### FAQ

#### Does Salomon offer a military discount?
Yes. Salomon offers a 20% military discount in the US, redeemed through the Amer Sports Pro Club rather than directly at salomon.com checkout. Salomon's support article confirms it offers discounts to military, law enforcement, and firefighters via the Amer Sports Pro Club.

#### How much is the Salomon military discount?
Salomon's Promotions & Discounts page lists "20% Off Military," linking to the Amer Sports Pro Club to redeem.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Salomon's support article lists "military" (commonly including veterans). Eligibility for spouses, dependents, retirees, and Guard/Reserve is not stated in Salomon's official sources — eligibility is confirmed during the Pro Club application based on the document you submit.

#### How do I verify my military status?
You apply to the Amer Sports Pro Club (or use an invitation code), select the "Security & Defense Pro" profile, and submit a supporting document proving eligibility. A manual review follows; approval is not instant.

#### Does Salomon use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For the military discount, no — verification runs through Salomon/Amer Sports' own Pro Club application flow. SheerID is used only for Salomon's separate 15% student discount, not the military offer.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. The military discount works online only through the Amer Sports Pro Club. Promo codes and the Pro Club do not apply at the Salomon outlet or Salomon retail stores.

#### What is excluded?
Outlet and retail-store purchases, gift cards, certain product categories (e.g., listed as Intersections, Advanced, Hardgoods), and out-of-stock/pre-order items. A yearly spending cap applies, and items revert to retail price once it's reached.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Salomon's promo codes can't be combined with other offers, promo codes, discounts, sales, or coupons. Pro Club preferred pricing is a separate program with its own spending limits.

#### Will joining affect my Salomon S/PLUS account?
Yes. Signing up for the Amer Sports Pro Club cancels access to the Salomon S/PLUS loyalty program. To keep both, register the Pro Club with a different email address.

#### Does Salomon offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Law enforcement and firefighters qualify through the same Pro Club path as military. Students get a separate 15% discount via SheerID. Teachers, nurses, EMS, and government employees are not addressed as distinct offers in Salomon's official sources.
