# Motel 6 — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Last verified:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 1-night stay at a $70 nightly rate (typical Motel 6 economy rate).

## Headline finding

Motel 6 (and its sister brand Studio 6, both under G6 Hospitality) publishes a real, evergreen **Military Rate** — the official page states "**Up to 10% off**" for active-duty members, veterans, and their families, booked by choosing the **Military Rate** under "Special Rates" and showing a valid military ID at check-in. It is a self-selected room rate, **not** an ID.me/SheerID-style pre-verification — the check-in ID is the gate, so acceptance can vary by (largely franchised) property. The genuine best path for most travelers is simply the **lowest-of: Military Rate vs. the public advance-purchase / My6 member rate**, because the 10% Military Rate does not stack with other discounts and is frequently beaten by a sale rate. The one way to get a bigger, blackout-free number is a **WeSalute+ (paid) membership**, which unlocks a **15% off nationwide, no blackout dates** rate and extends eligibility to Veterans, National Guard, and Reservists. Archetype: **discretionary/self-select lodging rate** (thin, non-stacking percentage; franchise variance) with a secondary **paid-membership upsell (WeSalute+)** path.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal terms)
1. **The Military Rate does not stack.** Official terms: "cannot be combined with other promotions or memberships"; "no cash value." One rate per reservation.
2. **WeSalute+ 15% replaces, not stacks** — it is an alternative negotiated rate, not an add-on to the 10% or to public sales.
3. **My6 loyalty points** can still be earned/redeemed on stays per program rules, but that is a separate loyalty mechanic, not a stackable discount on the rate.
4. **ID at check-in is the real gate.** Because there is no online pre-verification, the front desk (often franchisee-operated) validates eligibility; a self-selected Military Rate can be denied if ID isn't accepted — always carry a military ID card or DD-214.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Motel6.com (or open the My6 App) and enter your location and dates.
2. Open the **"Special Rates"** menu and select **"Military Rate."**
3. The discounted rate applies automatically to eligible room types.
4. Bring a **valid military ID** (military ID card or DD-214) to present at check-in.

### How To Redeem By Phone
1. Call **800-4-MOTEL6 (800-466-8356)**.
2. Tell the agent you want the Military Rate and mention your service status.
3. Present valid military ID at check-in.

### How Verification Works
There is **no online verification provider** (no ID.me/SheerID/GovX) on the official Motel 6 Military Rate — you self-select the rate and the **front desk validates a military ID at check-in.** Suggested proof: military ID card or DD Form 214 (reports indicate similar proof of service is accepted). For the enhanced **15%** rate you must hold a paid **WeSalute+** membership and show your WeSalute+ ID at check-in. Because most properties are franchised, confirm acceptance with the specific location before relying on the rate.

### FAQ
#### Does Motel 6 offer a military discount?
Yes. Motel 6 advertises a year-round **Military Rate** of "**up to 10% off**" for active-duty members, veterans, and their families.

#### How much is the Motel 6 military discount?
Up to **10%** off via the official Military Rate. A paid **WeSalute+** membership unlocks **15%** off nationwide with no blackout dates.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — the official Military Rate covers active-duty, veterans, and their families. National Guard and Reservists are covered through the WeSalute+ path.

#### How do I verify my military status?
There's no online verification. You select the Military Rate at booking and show a **valid military ID (or DD-214) at check-in.**

#### Does Motel 6 use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The official Motel 6 Military Rate uses **none** of these — just ID at check-in. Separately, **WeSalute** offers a 15% Motel 6 rate to its paid **WeSalute+** members.

#### Can I use it online and in stores?
It's a lodging rate — book it online (Motel6.com/My6 App) or by phone; there's no "in-store" checkout beyond the front desk validating your ID.

#### What is excluded?
It can't be combined with other promotions or memberships, is subject to availability, and is reported to be valid only in a country you've served in; acceptance varies by (franchised) location.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale rates?
No. One rate per booking, and the Military Rate isn't stackable. Compare it against the prepaid/member rate and book the lower one.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to stay at Motel 6?
Compare the **Military Rate** against the **prepaid/advance-purchase or My6 member rate** and book whichever is lower; if you hold **WeSalute+**, use the **15%** rate.

#### Does Motel 6 offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not published as a distinct rate on the official Military Rate page (as of July 15, 2026). WeSalute serves the military/veteran community specifically.
