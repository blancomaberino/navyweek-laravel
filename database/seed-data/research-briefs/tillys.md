# Tillys — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$100 full-price apparel order** on tillys.com (e.g., a ~$60 pair of RSQ jeans + two ~$20 graphic tees), pre-tax. Tillys is a mall/streetwear retailer with no single flagship SKU, so a round $100 apparel cart is the realistic unit.

## Headline finding

**Tillys publishes no military or veteran discount.** There is no military/veteran offer on tillys.com, and the online verification provider that would run one — **ID.me — states plainly that it "is not aware of Tillys offering Military community discounts."** The aggregator claims of a "20% military discount" or "25% in-store with valid ID" are **not confirmed by any primary source**; one of the more careful coupon sites (WorthEPenny) even admits outright, "There is no official Tillys military program." Do not assert a Tillys military discount, an ID.me military code, or a GovX/SheerID military route — none is verified. (Note the trap: **tilley.com's "GovX ID Military Discount" belongs to Tilley, the Canadian hat brand — a different company**, not Tillys the action-sports retailer.)

What Tillys *does* offer, verified: a **10% student discount via UNiDAYS** (online only, full-price eligible items), a free **Tillys Rewards** loyalty program (1 point per $1; 150 points = $5 off), a **10% off first order in the app**, and a heavy, near-constant **public sale/multi-buy** calendar (up to 40% off online, clearance up to 60%, 2-for/4-for tee and top deals). For a service member the genuine best path is **stack the public sale price + a cashback portal (Rakuten 2% as of July 10, 2026) + Tillys Rewards points** — that beats any claimed "military discount," which doesn't exist. Archetype: **no-official-discount promo-driven mall apparel retailer** (primary) with a secondary **student-verified-identity** flavor (the only verified identity discount runs through UNiDAYS, for students, not military).

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **The UNiDAYS student 10% does not combine with sale items or other discounts,** and excludes named brands (Nike, adidas, Converse, Levi's, UGG, and others). It's online-only. So on already-discounted merch, the sale usually wins and the code adds nothing.
2. **App 10% and email/SMS welcome are first-order/new-customer offers** — treat as single, non-combinable order-level codes. Pick the deepest one; they don't layer on each other.
3. **Cashback portals (Rakuten 2%, as of Jul 10 2026) stack on top of any on-site price** including sale prices, because they pay on your final checkout total — no brand code required. (Excludes Free People, Rainbow Sandals, gift cards.)
4. **Tillys Rewards points accrue on net spend** and redeem later (150 pts = $5) — they layer with sales and cashback; they are not an upfront discount.
5. **WeSalute/BenefitHub "military offers" are just the public Tillys sales** re-surfaced to members; there's no verified members-only % beyond what's public, and WeSalute membership carries a fee — don't treat it as a Tillys military discount.
6. **No "military discount option at checkout" exists** — any site describing one is fabricating it.
