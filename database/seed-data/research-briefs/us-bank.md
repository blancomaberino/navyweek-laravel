# U.S. Bank — Military Benefits & Fee-Waiver Brief (issuer-wide page)

**Researched:** July 19, 2026 · **Baseline "cart" for all math:** one grandfathered U.S. Bank Altitude® Reserve Visa Infinite® with a **$400** annual fee (the card the "us bank military fee waiver" searcher historically meant) **plus** the **$12/month** Bank Smartly® Checking maintenance fee (the benefit that includes veterans).

> **This is a statutory fee-waiver / regulated-benefit page, not a retail discount — with a freshness debunk at its core.** U.S. Bank runs no "military discount." It honors the **Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA)** — and publishes that policy plainly on usbank.com: **6% rate cap + waiver of all fees** on pre-service accounts — and, by consistent reporting, waives annual fees to **$0** under the **Military Lending Act (MLA)** on personal cards opened during covered service. The catch that most ranking pages haven't caught up with: **the $400 Altitude Reserve closed to new applicants on November 11, 2024**, and the rest of U.S. Bank's open flagship consumer lineup (Smartly Visa, Shield, Altitude Go, Altitude Connect, Cash+) is **already $0-annual-fee** — so for a *new* applicant there is almost nothing left for the MLA waiver to waive. Editorial pages still telling military readers to grab a fee-waived Altitude Reserve are recommending a card they cannot get.

> **A candor note (same family as Chase):** U.S. Bank **publishes its SCRA policy** (6% + all fees waived) on usbank.com — better disclosure than Chase — but it does **not** publish the MLA $0-annual-fee practice anywhere on usbank.com. That figure comes from MLA benefits letters and consistent military-finance reporting (Military Money Manual and others). Unlike Chase and Capital One, U.S. Bank advertises **no below-6% SCRA rate** — its published benefit is the statutory 6% cap plus fee waiver. Label confidence accordingly; page copy must not imply the $0-fee number appears on usbank.com.

## Headline finding

There is **no promo code and no percent-off**. U.S. Bank provides two statutory tracks plus one genuinely veteran-inclusive banking benefit:

1. **SCRA (accounts opened *before* active duty) — published policy:** "a cap of 6% on the interest rate and a waiver of all fees while you are on active military service" on mortgages, loans, lines of credit, and credit cards obtained prior to being called to active duty. It is **requested, not automatic** — via the U.S. Bank Military Service Center (**800-934-9555**; overseas collect **513-277-5899**; email 24hrmilitaryservicecenter.shared@usbank.com) or by uploading active-duty orders inside online banking/the app ("Call to active duty orders" under Manage cards).
2. **MLA (cards opened *during* covered service) — reported practice:** U.S. Bank checks applicants' SSNs against the DoD MLA database at application and reduces annual fees to **$0** for covered borrowers (active duty; Guard/Reserve on 30+ day orders; spouses/dependents), mailing an MLA benefits letter — often before the card itself arrives. **But the practice is now largely moot for new applicants:** the only premium card it famously zeroed (Altitude Reserve, $400) has been **closed to new applications since November 11, 2024**, and every current flagship U.S. Bank consumer card is $0-annual-fee anyway. Annual fees survive mainly on the Korean Air SKYPASS co-brands.
3. **Military checking (includes veterans):** the **$12 monthly maintenance fee on Bank Smartly® Checking is waived for "active duty military and veterans"** — eligibility wording is "current or former member of the United States Armed Forces," by **self-disclosure** (no ID.me/DD-214 upload step published) — plus $0 U.S. Bank ATM fees, the first four non-U.S. Bank ATM transactions per statement period fee-free with the first surcharge reimbursed, and free cashier's checks, personal money orders, and stop payments.

**Veterans and retirees not on active duty get no card fee relief** — the benefit that explicitly includes them is the checking package ($144/year + ATM and service-fee savings). Grandfathered Altitude Reserve holders who opened while MLA-covered keep the reported $0 fee (and absorbed significant benefit devaluations on December 15, 2025).

Archetype: **regulated-benefit / statutory-relief brand** (same family as American Express, Chase, Capital One, Citi). The make-or-break variable is the **account-open date** (before active duty → SCRA, by request; during covered service → MLA, automatic) — with the U.S. Bank twist that the MLA side now has almost no fee left to waive for new applicants.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **SCRA and MLA never stack on one account** — the account-open date picks exactly one. SCRA reaches obligations incurred before service (50 U.S.C. § 3937); MLA governs credit extended to an already-covered borrower (32 CFR 232).
2. **SCRA must be requested; MLA is automatic.** U.S. Bank says so itself for SCRA ("you're not automatically covered") and provides upload/mail/fax channels; the MLA check is U.S. Bank querying the DoD database at application (reported). Never tell readers SCRA self-applies or that the MLA waiver needs a request.
3. **The waiver covers fees, not spending** — no percent-off at merchants, nothing to stack with portals, promo codes, or shopping benefits.
4. **Card rewards are unaffected** — welcome bonuses and rewards earn normally on fee-waived accounts (editorial consensus; no U.S. Bank statement to the contrary).
5. **Business cards: no MLA fee waiver** (consumer credit only under 10 U.S.C. 987). A *pre-service* business account may still be claimable under statutory SCRA — U.S. Bank doesn't publish product scope; route readers to 800-934-9555.
6. **Spouses are covered borrowers in their own right under the MLA** (32 CFR 232.3), so a spouse's own personal card gets its own waiver where a fee exists — but U.S. Bank-specific spouse data points are thin (see Community).
7. **The checking waiver stacks with everything** — it's an account-fee waiver by self-disclosure, independent of SCRA/MLA status.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty members** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Space Force) — SCRA on pre-service accounts; MLA on cards opened during service; checking fee waiver.
- **National Guard / Reserve on 30+ day active orders** — MLA covered borrowers while activated; SCRA on accounts predating the orders.
- **Military spouses and dependents** — MLA covered borrowers in their own right (32 CFR 232.3); U.S. Bank checks the applicant's SSN against DoD records. (U.S. Bank-specific spouse reports are thin — the law is solid, the mechanics less documented.)
- **Veterans / retirees** — "current or former member of the United States Armed Forces" qualifies for the **military checking benefits** (self-disclosure). No ongoing card fee relief; statutory SCRA claims for a past active-duty period close 180 days after release.
- **Business cards** — excluded from the MLA fee waiver (consumer credit only).

### How To Redeem — SCRA (account opened before active duty)
1. Fastest: log in to usbank.com or the app → Accounts → **Manage cards** → select the card → **"Call to active duty orders"** → upload your orders (up to 5 documents, 5MB each; PDF/JPG/PNG/DOC/XLS accepted).
2. Or call the **Military Service Center: 800-934-9555** (Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–midnight ET; Sat 8 a.m.–8:30 p.m. ET); overseas, call collect **513-277-5899**; or email 24hrmilitaryservicecenter.shared@usbank.com.
3. Or mail/fax for card accounts: ATTN: SCRA Cardmember Service, PO Box 6352, Fargo, ND 58125-6354 · fax **866-568-7729**. Include **all** your account numbers.
4. U.S. Bank reviews and sends the outcome by letter. Published result: **6% cap + waiver of all fees** while on active military service; the statutory floor (excess interest forgiven) applies regardless.

### How To Redeem — MLA (card opened while serving)
1. Apply for any **personal** U.S. Bank card as normal — there is no military application page and no code. (Unsure of your status? Check the DoD database at mla.dmdc.osd.mil; spouses are in the same system via DEERS.)
2. U.S. Bank checks your SSN against DoD records at account opening. Covered borrowers' annual fees are **reported waived to $0 automatically**, with an MLA benefits letter by mail — sometimes the fee posts first and is credited back days later.
3. Practical note: on today's open lineup, only the SKYPASS co-brands still carry a fee for this to act on. Questions → **800-934-9555**.

### How Verification Works
- **MLA:** automatic — U.S. Bank queries DoD records; no third-party verifier (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute). The mailed letter is your proof. Not published on usbank.com — confirmed via letters and consistent reporting.
- **SCRA:** manual — you submit orders through the in-app upload, phone, email, mail, or fax; the Military Service Center reviews and responds by letter.
- **Military checking:** **self-disclosure** — tell U.S. Bank you're a current or former member of the Armed Forces when opening or servicing the account; no document-upload step is published.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No coupon, code, or retail percent-off exists — anything claiming one is wrong.
- One relief per account, decided by the open date; SCRA and MLA never stack.
- **You can no longer apply for the Altitude Reserve** (closed November 11, 2024) or FlexPerks Gold Amex — ignore pages that still route you to them.
- Business/corporate cards: no MLA fee waiver.
- Veterans/retirees outside active duty: standard card pricing; the 180-day statutory SCRA window covers only the past active-duty period. The checking waiver is the benefit that includes you.

#### Does U.S. Bank offer a military discount?
Not a retail discount. U.S. Bank honors the SCRA — a published **6% interest cap plus waiver of all fees** on accounts opened before active duty — and is reported to waive annual fees to **$0** under the MLA on personal cards opened during covered service. No promo code exists.

#### Does U.S. Bank waive credit card annual fees for military?
Two ways. Pre-service accounts: yes, by **SCRA request** — U.S. Bank's published policy waives all fees while you're on active service. Cards opened while serving: **reported** waived to $0 automatically under the MLA, confirmed by a mailed benefits letter — though usbank.com doesn't publish that policy, and today's open flagship cards have no annual fee to begin with.

#### Can I still get the Altitude Reserve with the military fee waiver?
No. U.S. Bank **stopped accepting Altitude Reserve applications on November 11, 2024**. Existing cardholders keep the card (reported fee-waived if opened while MLA-covered), but new applicants can't get it — despite what older military-finance pages still say. The card's benefits were also cut on December 15, 2025.

#### Which U.S. Bank cards does the MLA waiver actually matter for now?
Almost none. Smartly Visa, Shield, Altitude Go, Altitude Connect, and Cash+ are all $0-annual-fee for everyone. Annual fees survive mainly on the Korean Air SKYPASS co-brands — where the reported waiver logic would apply for covered borrowers.

#### What is U.S. Bank's SCRA benefit?
For U.S. Bank mortgages, loans, lines of credit, and credit cards obtained **before** you were called to active duty: a **6% rate cap and a waiver of all fees** while on active military service — U.S. Bank publishes this on usbank.com. Federal law adds that interest above 6% is forgiven, not deferred. It must be requested.

#### How do I request SCRA benefits from U.S. Bank?
Upload your active-duty orders in online banking or the app (Manage cards → "Call to active duty orders"), call **800-934-9555** (overseas collect 513-277-5899), email 24hrmilitaryservicecenter.shared@usbank.com, or mail/fax card documents to SCRA Cardmember Service, PO Box 6352, Fargo, ND 58125-6354 / 866-568-7729.

#### Do military spouses qualify?
Under the MLA, yes — spouses and dependents are covered borrowers in their own right, screened against DoD records at application. U.S. Bank-specific spouse data points are thinner than at Amex or Chase, so treat the mechanics as reported and confirm via the benefits letter.

#### Do veterans get anything at U.S. Bank?
Yes — the best veteran benefit here is banking, not cards: the **$12 monthly Bank Smartly Checking fee is waived for "active duty military and veterans"** ("current or former member of the United States Armed Forces," by self-disclosure), plus ATM fee waivers and free cashier's checks, money orders, and stop payments. There's no ongoing card fee relief for veterans.

#### MLA vs. SCRA — which applies to me?
The **account-open date** decides. Opened before active duty → SCRA (request it). Opened while covered → MLA (automatic). They never stack on one account.

#### Does U.S. Bank use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
No. MLA verification is U.S. Bank checking Department of Defense records; SCRA is direct document review by the Military Service Center; the checking waiver is self-disclosure.

#### What's actually the best move for a servicemember?
If you held any U.S. Bank account before activating, **file SCRA** — it's published policy and covers fees and interest across the relationship. If you want a fee-waived premium travel card while serving, U.S. Bank no longer offers one — use the MLA route at Amex or Chase and keep U.S. Bank for its genuinely free checking (which you'll keep as a veteran).
