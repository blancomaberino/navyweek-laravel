# Burlington — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic $120 in-store Burlington basket (apparel + home).

## Headline finding

**Burlington (Burlington Stores, formerly Burlington Coat Factory) publishes no military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** Nothing on Burlington's Help Center, Pricing Policy, Loyalty, or Deals pages mentions a military program, and Burlington uses no military-verification provider (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass). This is a classic **no-discount off-price retailer**: the model is everyday low "comparable value" pricing plus clearance, not coupons or ID-gated discounts. The genuine best path for a service member is ordinary Burlington value-shopping — hit clearance, and if you shop there often, the **Burlington Credit Card** (5% back in Rewards, plus a periodic "% off first purchase" opener) is the only structural price lever. Aggregator claims of a "30% off military promo code" or an in-store military-ID discount are **unverified/fabricated** and should not be trusted.
