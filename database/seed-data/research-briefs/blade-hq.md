# Blade HQ — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $200 name-brand folding knife order (e.g., a MAP-protected premium folder), shipped to a US address.

## Headline finding

Blade HQ has **no flat, published military discount percentage** and **no verified self-serve verification portal** (ID.me / GovX / SheerID). What it actually offers is a **manual, item-by-item Military & Law-Enforcement discount**: you email `questions@bladehq.com` or call 1-888-252-3347, prove your status, and they quote a discount **that varies by item and excludes many products entirely** — notably the premium MAP-protected brands (Chris Reeve, and similar) that most buyers actually want. Archetype: **MAP-constrained specialty retailer** — manufacturer minimum-advertised-price rules, not Blade HQ's generosity, cap what can be discounted. For most shoppers the **single best reliable path is the free Blade Club loyalty program** (points worth ~2.5%–3.5% back + free domestic shipping + a 1,000-point signup bonus), which is self-serve, stacks with sales, and beats a military "discount" that is often $0 on the exact knives people come for. The coupon-site claims of "10%–25% military" or "up to 75%–85% off" are **fabrications** with no primary basis.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military/LE discount does not stack with promo codes or other offers** — one discount per order (consistent across sources). It is applied as a **custom quoted price**, not a stackable code.
2. **Blade Club points earn on top of sale prices** — you still accrue points on discounted orders, so Blade Club effectively "stacks" with a sale in value terms (points are a rebate, not a checkout code).
3. **Only one checkout code per order** — you cannot combine a coupon code with a military quote.
4. **MAP is the real ceiling** — manufacturer minimum-advertised-price rules, not Blade HQ policy, are why premium brands show no discount on any path. No portal overrides MAP.

### FAQ

#### Does Blade HQ offer a military discount?
Yes — a **Military and Law-Enforcement discount**, but it's **item-by-item and quoted manually**, not a flat percentage or automatic code. Many items, especially premium name-brand knives, are excluded.

#### How much is the Blade HQ military discount?
Blade HQ **publishes no percentage.** The amount **depends on the item**, and many items get no discount at all. Coupon-site figures like "10%–25%" are **not from Blade HQ** and shouldn't be trusted.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The confirmed groups are **active-duty military and law enforcement.** Broader veteran/family eligibility is **not confirmed** by a Blade HQ source — email `questions@bladehq.com` to ask.

#### How do I verify my status / get the discount?
**Manually** — email `questions@bladehq.com` or call **1-888-252-3347**, provide proof of status, and get a per-item quote. There's no instant checkout code.

#### Does Blade HQ use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No verified integration.** Aggregators name all three inconsistently, but there's **no primary evidence** Blade HQ uses a self-serve verifier. Verification is manual.

#### Can I use it online and in stores?
The quoted discount applies to `bladehq.com` / the Blade HQ app orders after verification. In-store handling at physical locations is not documented — ask staff.

#### What's excluded?
Premium **MAP-protected brands** (e.g., Chris Reeve and similar) and various other items. The discount "varies from item to item and excludes some items completely."

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
**No.** One discount per order — the military quote does not stack with coupon codes. Blade Club **points**, however, still accrue on any order.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Blade HQ?
For most knives (MAP-locked), **join the free Blade Club** — ~2.5%–3.5% back in points, free shipping, and a signup bonus, all automatic. For **non-MAP / exclusive** items, **email for a military quote** and compare to the current sale price; take the lower.

#### Does Blade HQ offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
The manual discount extends to **law enforcement**; no published program was found for firefighters/EMS, nurses, teachers, or students.

#### Does Blade HQ do anything special for Veterans Day?
Yes — the **Continue Mission fundraiser**, a veteran charity raffle/donation drive (not a storewide discount). Blade HQ also sells **veteran-designed knives** that route royalties to the designers.
