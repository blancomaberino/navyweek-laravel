# TuxMat — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **~$189 TuxMat front floor-mat set** (single vehicle, US site pricing; TuxMat prices vary by model).

## Headline finding

**Yes — TuxMat offers a genuine, official 20% military and veteran discount.** TuxMat's own FAQ states plainly: *"Yes, we're proud to support those who serve and protect, with 20% off upon proof of service,"* redeemed by submitting proof on TuxMat's **Military and Veterans Form** (a manual, TuxMat-run review — **not** ID.me, GovX, or SheerID). On the baseline set this is the single best legitimate path: ~$37.80 off a $189 set, beating TuxMat's only other standing offer (a **$20 returning-customer credit**). TuxMat is a **direct-to-consumer Shopify brand** — there is no cashback portal that reliably survives its checkout, no military exchange channel, and no GovX storefront — so the official 20% is both the best and effectively the only military-relevant lever. Archetype: **DTC single-brand retailer with a real, manually-verified military discount and thin stacking surface.** Aggregator claims of stackable "35% off" codes (e.g. "Custom35") are unverified promotional noise, not a military benefit.
