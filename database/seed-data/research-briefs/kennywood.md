# Kennywood — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult single-day admission to Kennywood (West Mifflin, PA), gate list price **$74.99**.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Kennywood offers a real, year-round military discount:** a **Military Admission ticket at $36.99** (up to ~50% off the $74.99 gate price), buyable online, that covers the service member **and up to 4 guests**, with affiliation verification required at purchase. On top of that, Kennywood runs a **free-admission Military Days** promotion each summer (**July 1–5, 2026** this year) for active, retired, veteran, and reservist military with a valid military ID at the gate, plus up to four discounted guest passes at $34.99. Kennywood is a **Palace Entertainment** park, and the online military ticket is gated by an affiliation-verification step (reported as **SheerID**). Archetype: **regional amusement park with a genuine gated military ticket** — the discount is legitimate and usually the best price, but a public "kids' price" flash sale ($39.99) and the free Military Days window sometimes beat or match it.
