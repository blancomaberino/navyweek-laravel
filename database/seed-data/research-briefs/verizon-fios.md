# Verizon Fios Home Internet — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one Fios Home Internet plan (e.g. **300 Mbps**, standard promo price **$49.99/mo** with Auto Pay; or **1 Gig** at **$89.99/mo**). Fios is address-gated to 9 states + DC.

## Headline finding

Verizon Fios home internet has its **own dedicated military discount** — a real, separate program from the well-known **mobile** military discount, and both are gated by **ID.me**. Verizon's official Fios Military & Veterans FAQ confirms eligible military members and veterans "can get a monthly discount on Verizon Fios home internet service when they have or get an eligible monthly plan" (**300 Mbps, 500 Mbps, 1 Gig, or 2 Gig**), and the **$99 setup fee is waived** when you order online. Critically: **the wireless/mobile military discount ($10/$25/$20 per account, or 15% off) does NOT automatically flow to Fios home internet** — they are two distinct discounts you register for separately, each through ID.me.

The catch worth being honest about: Verizon's own **`/military/` marketing page shows Fios "military discount" prices that are identical to the standard Auto Pay + Mobile + Home bundle prices** anyone gets ($44.99 / $64.99 / $74.99 / $94.99 for 300 Mbps / 500 Mbps / 1 Gig / 2 Gig). Verizon does **not publish a separate military-specific dollar/percent figure** for Fios on its pricing tables — the FAQ confirms a discount "exists" and is ID.me-gated, but the exact incremental amount over the everyday promo price isn't posted. So the standout, clearly-documented Fios military benefit is the **waived $99 setup fee for online orders**, plus the ID.me-verified monthly discount whose exact value you should confirm at checkout after verifying. Archetype: **home-service subscription — the program is real and ID.me-gated, but the posted "military" price mirrors the standard bundle promo, so verify the true delta live.**

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Fios military discount and mobile military discount are separate registrations** — verifying one does not apply the other. Register each through ID.me. *(Fios & Mobile Military FAQs)*

3. **Military discount stacks with Auto Pay + paper-free billing and with the Mobile + Home Discount** — Verizon explicitly names these as combinable. *(Mobile Military FAQ)*
4. **Only one "Those Who Serve"–type discount per account** — you can't combine the military discount with first responder, nurse, teacher, Student, or **Deals at Work** discounts. Pick one. *(Fios Military FAQ)*
5. **$5/mo Digital Voice / Home Device Advisor add-on** is a separate military perk on those specific services. *(Fios Military FAQ)*
6. **Mobile + Home Rewards** is an additional bundle program (discounts + device dollars) layered on when you have both Fios and a Verizon mobile plan. *(Fios Military FAQ)*

### How To Redeem
**If you're already a Fios customer:**
1. Make sure you're on an eligible plan (300 Mbps, 500 Mbps, 1 Gig, or 2 Gig) — change plans in My Verizon if needed.
2. Go to Verizon's military discount page and choose **Check eligibility**; verify your military status with **ID.me**.
3. Choose **Already with Verizon** and follow the prompts to apply the discount.
4. It appears on your Fios bill in **1–2 billing cycles**.

**If you're new to Fios:**
1. Go to the military discount page and choose **Check Eligibility** to confirm Fios is available at your address; verify with **ID.me**.
2. Pick an eligible plan, choose **New to Verizon**, and complete the order **online** to get the **$99 setup fee waived**.
3. The discount shows on your bill in 1–2 billing cycles.

### How Verification Works
Verizon uses **ID.me**, a third-party digital identity network, to confirm military status. During registration ID.me may ask you to **upload a document** validating your service. ID.me works in all major browsers except Internet Explorer. Verizon states the information is used only to validate status. The Fios discount lasts as long as you keep a qualifying plan and verified status — **no annual renewal**, though Verizon may occasionally re-verify. Note: your Fios ID.me verification and your **mobile** ID.me verification are registered separately.

### FAQ

#### Does Verizon Fios have a military discount?
Yes. Fios home internet has its own military discount, separate from Verizon's mobile military discount. You verify with ID.me, get a monthly discount on an eligible plan (300 Mbps–2 Gig), and the $99 setup fee is waived when you order online.

#### Is the Fios military discount the same as the Verizon mobile military discount?
No. They're two separate programs with separate ID.me registrations. Verifying for the mobile discount does not apply a discount to your Fios bill, and vice versa.

#### How much is the Verizon Fios military discount?
Verizon confirms a discount exists but **doesn't post a distinct military-only dollar or percent figure** — on its military page the Fios "military" prices match the standard Auto Pay + Mobile + Home bundle prices. The clearly military-specific wins are the **waived $99 setup fee (online)** and a **$5/mo** discount on Digital Voice or Home Device Advisor. Confirm your exact price at checkout after ID.me.

#### Which Fios plans qualify?
300 Mbps, 500 Mbps, 1 Gig, and 2 Gig.

#### How do I verify my military status for Fios?
Through **ID.me** on Verizon's military discount page. ID.me may ask you to upload a document. It works in all major browsers except Internet Explorer.

#### Do veterans and Guard/Reserve qualify, or only active duty?
All qualify: US active duty, retirees, veterans, current or former Guard or Reserve, and Gold Star family members.

#### Can I stack the Fios military discount with other discounts?
It stacks with Auto Pay + paper-free billing and the Mobile + Home Discount. It does **not** combine with Deals at Work, and you can only have one "serve" discount (military/first responder/nurse/teacher) per account.

#### Does the $99 setup fee waiver always apply?
It's waived for qualifying Fios orders completed **online**. Order online to capture it.

#### Where is Fios available?
Key metro areas of NJ, NY, MD, DE, MA, PA, RI, VA, and Washington DC.

#### When will the discount show on my bill?
In 1–2 billing cycles after ID.me verification.
