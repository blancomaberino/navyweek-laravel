# iFIT (NordicTrack / ProForm) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **NordicTrack Commercial-class treadmill at $1,499** (representative mid-range iFIT-enabled machine), plus a note on the separate **iFIT app membership ($39/mo or $396/yr)**.

## Headline finding

There is **no single "iFIT military discount."** iFIT is the software/subscription layer; the hardware is sold under **NordicTrack, ProForm, and FreeMotion.** Only **NordicTrack** publishes a military discount — **5% off your entire order**, for **current and former U.S. military**, verified through **GovX ID**, and it "applies to all NordicTrack products." **ProForm does not offer a military discount** (ID.me confirms it is "not aware" ProForm offers one, and ProForm is not a listed GovX partner). The **iFIT app subscription itself has no military discount.** Because NordicTrack machines carry deep, frequent seasonal markdowns (often **$200–$700 off** sticker), the **public sale almost always beats the 5% GovX discount**, and the two generally cannot be combined. Archetype: **big-ticket durable-goods brand with a small flat verified discount that is routinely out-earned by its own sale calendar** — plus a **subscription** (iFIT) with no military pricing.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal)
1. **GovX single-use codes generally exclude already-discounted/sale items** — NordicTrack's deep seasonal markdowns typically can't take the extra 5%. Confirm in-cart before assuming a stack.
2. **One GovX discount code per user** (and one per day on the GovX side) — no multi-code stacking.
3. **The iFIT app subscription is billed separately** and has no military pricing; the NordicTrack 5% is an equipment-order discount, not a subscription discount.
4. **ProForm has no military discount to stack at all.**

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible for NordicTrack 5% via GovX.
- **Veterans** — Eligible ("current & former U.S. military").
- **Retirees / Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible as current or former military via GovX.
- **Military spouses / dependents** — Not stated as separately eligible; GovX verifies the member.
- **First responders / medical / teachers** — No military tier, but NordicTrack runs **separate Medical Professional and Educator** discount programs (not military).
- **ProForm / iFIT-app-only buyers** — No discount available.

### How To Redeem Online (NordicTrack)
1. Go to **nordictrack.com/military-discount**.
2. Click to claim and **verify with GovX ID** (real-time; log in if you already have an account).
3. Copy your **single-use discount code**.
4. Apply it at checkout on a full-price NordicTrack order (it generally won't apply to sale items).

### How Verification Works
NordicTrack uses **GovX ID** to confirm current/former military status. Verification is instant; you receive a single-use code, and you can log back into GovX for a new code on future orders (one code per user/limit one per day). There is no ID.me or SheerID path, and no verification path for ProForm or the iFIT app because neither offers a military discount.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **5% generally excludes already-discounted/sale items** — verify in-cart.
- **One discount code per user.**
- **ProForm and the iFIT app subscription are excluded** — no military pricing.
- Applies to **NordicTrack.com (US)**.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Buy during a seasonal sale** (New Year, Memorial Day, July 4, Veterans Day, Black Friday) — usually beats 5%.
- **Check certified refurbished / open-box** NordicTrack stock.
- **Use 0% APR financing** when offered to spread the cost.
- **iFIT Pro membership is bundled free** for a period with most new machines — factor that before paying for the app.
- **Consider a ProForm equivalent** — no military %, but often a lower list price for comparable iFIT features.

### FAQ

#### Does iFIT offer a military discount?
Not as "iFIT." iFIT is the subscription/app; the military discount belongs to **NordicTrack** (5% off via GovX). The iFIT app subscription itself has no military price.

#### How much is the NordicTrack military discount?
**5% off your entire order**, verified through GovX ID, for current and former U.S. military. (Aggregators saying "10%" are wrong; the official page says 5%.)

#### Does ProForm have a military discount?
No. ProForm publishes no military discount, and ID.me confirms it is "not aware" of one.

#### Do veterans and retirees qualify?
Yes — NordicTrack's offer covers "current & former U.S. military," so veterans, retirees, Reserve, and Guard qualify via GovX.

#### Does NordicTrack use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**GovX ID.** Not ID.me or SheerID.

#### Can I stack the 5% with a sale price?
Usually no — the GovX code generally excludes already-discounted items, and NordicTrack's sales run deep. Compare the sale price against 5% off full price and take the lower.

#### What's actually the cheapest way to buy a NordicTrack machine?
Buy during a seasonal sale, or check refurbished/open-box. The 5% GovX code only wins when nothing is on sale.

#### Is there a discount on the iFIT app subscription?
No military discount is published for the iFIT membership. New machines typically include a free iFIT period.
