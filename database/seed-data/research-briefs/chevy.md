# Chevrolet — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new Chevrolet Silverado 1500 (illustrative ~$45,000 MSRP; GM's benefit is a preferred **price**, not a fixed dollar figure, so the baseline sets context only).

## Headline finding

Chevrolet's military discount is the **GM Military Appreciation Program** (gmmilitaryappreciation.com), a real, official, first-party manufacturer program covering Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, and Cadillac. It gives eligible members a **special below-MSRP "program price"** on eligible new GM vehicles — **GM does not publish a fixed percentage or dollar amount**, and the value varies by model and month, so any specific "% off" you see on an aggregator is unverified. The mechanics are precise and worth getting right: verify once through **ID.me**, pull an **authorization number** (two per calendar year, each valid 90 days), and bring it to a participating dealer before delivery. The headline strength is **stacking** — the program price **combines with most current GM offers and incentives**, which is what makes it more than a token discount. The hard walls: it can't be combined with GM's other appreciation/discount programs (First Responder, Educator, College, Family First, Supplier, Employee, Executive Referral), and surviving spouses/family members do **not** qualify (though an eligible member **can sponsor their spouse**). Archetype: **manufacturer preferred-pricing program with a strong stacking angle** (compare directly to Subaru's flat $500 bonus-cash model).

## Stacking Rules (verified)

1. **Program price combines with most current GM offers/incentives** — the core value. *(gmmilitaryappreciation.com FAQs)*
2. **Cannot combine with** GM First Responder, Educator, College, Family First, Supplier, Dealership Employee, Supplier-for-Friends, or Executive Referral/Employee Purchase programs — strictly either/or. *(FAQs)*
3. **Can combine with GM Mobility** reimbursements. *(FAQs)*
4. **Financing is open** — you don't have to finance through GM Financial, though some special APR offers are GM-Financial-exclusive. *(FAQs)*
5. **Dealer-installed options, protection plans, upfit packages are excluded** from the program price (priced separately). *(Rules & Guidelines)*
6. **Not retroactive** — can't be applied to a purchase/lease you already completed. *(FAQs)*
7. **Authorization number is mandatory before delivery** — no auth number in the dealer's hands, no program price. *(Rules & Guidelines)*

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Eligible.
- **Reservists / National Guard** — Eligible.
- **Veterans** — Eligible (all veterans; **no** 24-month limit, unlike Subaru).
- **Retirees** — Eligible.
- **Sponsored spouse** — Eligible when sponsored by an eligible member.
- **Surviving spouses / other family** — **Not** eligible.
- **Branches** — Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard (per the official eligibility list).

### How To Redeem
1. Create a **GM My Account**, then verify military status through **ID.me** (USAA, .mil email, service records, or documents).
2. Click **Get Authorization Number** on gmmilitaryappreciation.com and request one (two per calendar year; valid 90 days).
3. Take the authorization number — plus a copy of your driver's license with the number redacted — to a **participating GM dealer** before delivery.
4. The dealer applies the **program price**; stack most current incentives on top.

### How Verification Works
GM uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility (via USAA account, .mil email, military service records, or uploaded documents like an LES within 30 days, DD214, or retiree statements). A **CAC/military ID card is not accepted** due to Title 18 restrictions. Once verified, you no longer show proof at the dealer — but you must still bring the authorization number and a redacted driver's license.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **No published percentage** — the value is a model-specific program price.
- **Cannot combine** with GM First Responder, Educator, College, Family First, Supplier, Dealership Employee, Supplier-for-Friends, or Executive Referral/Employee programs.
- **Two vehicles per calendar year**; **6-month retention**; personal, noncommercial use; new/unused vehicles (demos under 7,500 miles may qualify).
- **Surviving spouses/family** don't qualify; only a member's **sponsored spouse**.
- **Not retroactive**; dealer participation is voluntary/by vehicle line; **max $75 doc fee** (state exceptions).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Stack it** — the program price plus most current national/dealer incentives is the whole strategy.
- **Time the purchase** to a strong national cash/APR month.
- **Compare against Supplier/Employee/First-Responder pricing** if you have it — those can't combine, so take the single lowest total.
- **Use both authorization numbers** wisely (up to two vehicles/year).
- **Overseas?** You can arrange the offer online and take **delivery in the U.S.**

### FAQ

#### Does Chevrolet offer a military discount?
Yes — through the **GM Military Appreciation Program**, which gives eligible members a special below-MSRP program price on eligible new Chevrolet (and Buick/GMC/Cadillac) vehicles.

#### How much is the Chevy/GM military discount?
GM **doesn't publish a fixed percentage or dollar amount** — it's a model-specific program price that stacks with most current incentives. Any specific "% off" from a coupon site is unverified.

#### Who qualifies?
Active Duty, Reservists, National Guard, all Veterans and Retirees of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, and Coast Guard — plus a **sponsored spouse**. Surviving spouses and other family members do not qualify.

#### Do veterans qualify, and is there a time limit?
Yes, all veterans qualify — **no** 24-month cutoff (that's Subaru's rule, not GM's).

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **ID.me** — using a USAA account, .mil email, service records, or uploaded documents. A CAC card is not accepted.

#### How many vehicles can I buy, and how long must I keep them?
Up to **two per calendar year** (two authorization numbers, each valid 90 days); you must **retain each for 6 months**.

#### Can I combine the GM military offer with other discounts?
Yes with **most current GM offers/incentives** (and GM Mobility). **No** with GM First Responder, Educator, College, Family First, Supplier, Employee, or Executive Referral programs.

#### Can I use it on a certified pre-owned Chevy?
GM's terms specify **new and unused** vehicles (demo units under 7,500 miles may qualify). Confirm on GM's live Eligible Vehicles list — don't rely on aggregator claims about CPO.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Chevy?
Verify on ID.me, pull the authorization number, and stack the program price on the deepest current incentives. If you also have Supplier/Employee/First-Responder access, compare all totals and take the single lowest — those don't combine with Military.
