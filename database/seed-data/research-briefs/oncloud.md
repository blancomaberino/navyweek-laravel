So the honest headline: *On gives military 15% off online via ID.me — the best everyday path for a single pair, but because it can't stack with sales, you should compare it against On's own end-of-season markdowns and third-party sale prices before you buy.* Archetype: **standing ID.me % discount, non-stackable, online-only.**

### How To Redeem
1. Go to On's Community Discounts page and click the **ID.me verification link**.

4. Shop On.com or the On app, add eligible items (current-season, under $575 total), and enter the code at checkout.

### How Verification Works
On uses **ID.me** exclusively. ID.me confirms your military (or nurse/responder/teacher/student) status and issues a one-time code back to you. On never sees your ID documents — ID.me handles the check. There is no in-store verification path; the discount is online only.

- Applies only to orders totaling **$575 USD / $800 CAD or less** before the discount.
- **Online only** — valid on On.com or the On app, not in On retail stores.

### FAQ

#### Does On (On Cloud) offer a military discount?
Yes. On offers a **15%** Community Discount for U.S. and Canadian military, verified through **ID.me**, redeemable online on On.com or the On app.

#### How much is the On military discount?
**15% off** eligible items. It's a flat rate — the "up to 50%" figures on coupon sites are clearance noise, not a military rate.

#### How do I verify my military status with On?
Through **ID.me**. Verify once, get a single-use code, and apply it at checkout. On doesn't see your documents.

#### Can I use the On military discount in stores?
No. The single-use code works **only online** — On.com or the On app — not in On retail stores.

#### Does the On military discount stack with sales?
No. It **can't be combined** with other promotions, and it **excludes Last Season and Limited Edition** items. If a Last Season pair is marked down more than 15%, buy that instead (the code won't apply to it).

#### Is there an order limit?
Yes — the code applies only to orders of **$575 USD ($800 CAD) or less** before discount.

#### Do veterans qualify, or only active duty?
Both. ID.me verifies active duty, Reserve/Guard, and veteran/retired status.

#### Can nurses, teachers, or first responders get the On discount too?
Yes — On offers the same **15%** to nurses, medical providers, first responders, teachers, and students via their ID.me group.
