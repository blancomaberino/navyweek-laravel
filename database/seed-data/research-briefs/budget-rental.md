# Budget Rent a Car — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **3-day midsize leisure rental, ~$60/day base ($180 base + taxes/fees)**, comparing every military-linked path against public Budget coupons and rival rental brands.

## Headline finding

**Budget does not have a free "flash your military ID" discount — its headline military rate runs through WeSalute+, a paid membership.** Budget's official military page advertises **up to 35% off PAY NOW (prepaid) rates** but only "simply by being a **WeSalute+ subscriber**" (WeSalute is the rebrand of Veterans Advantage). There's also a lower **up to 25% off leisure rate** tied to the Veterans Advantage/WeSalute program (aggregators cite BCD code **X612500** — treat the exact code as unverified). Budget is part of **Avis Budget Group**, so **Avis offers the identical up-to-35% WeSalute+ deal** — they're the same program. The honest maximum-savings read: the "35%" is a **member rate that requires paying for WeSalute+ and prepaying a nonrefundable PAY NOW booking**, and it **excludes** taxes, fees, LDW, and car group X. For many renters, a **public Budget coupon (Costco Travel, USAA, AARP, or a sitewide BCD)** or **Enterprise's free 5% military rate (no membership)** nets a lower out-the-door price than paying for WeSalute+. Archetype: **travel brand whose "military discount" is a paid-membership affinity rate — verify the code, price it against public coupons, and don't assume 35% is free.**

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + program terms)
1. **Only one discount code per booking** — Budget states the military/affinity rate "cannot be combined with any other BCD numbers, promotions, or offers." You choose the military BCD *or* a public coupon, not both.
2. **35% is a PAY NOW (prepaid) rate** — deeper discount, but the booking is **prepaid and typically nonrefundable**; the ~25% leisure rate is the pay-at-counter alternative.
3. **The discount is on time & mileage only** — taxes, concession/facility fees, LDW, and refueling are never discounted and are added on top.
4. **WeSalute+ is a paid membership** — factor its cost; the "35%" is not free. Car group X (specialty vehicles) is excluded.
5. **GovX is a closed marketplace substitute** — you can't add a Budget BCD or a cashback portal on top of a GovX booking; compare its net price standalone.

### FAQ

#### Does Budget offer a military discount?
Yes, but it's not a free ID-based discount — it runs through **WeSalute+**, a paid membership. Members save up to 35% on prepaid PAY NOW rates or ~25% on leisure rates.

#### How much is the Budget military discount?
Up to **35% off** PAY NOW (prepaid) rates, or up to **25%** off leisure rates, on time and mileage only — via WeSalute+.

#### Do veterans and family members qualify?
Yes — veterans, active-duty, National Guard, Reservists, and their immediate family, through WeSalute+.

#### How do I verify my status?
You verify when you join **WeSalute+** (formerly Veterans Advantage), then apply its BCD code on budget.com. GovX is a free-account alternative marketplace.

#### Does Budget use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Budget's headline military rate is via **WeSalute+**. **GovX** also books Budget through its rental-car marketplace. Budget does not gate this rate through ID.me or SheerID.

#### Can I combine it with a coupon?
No — one discount code per booking. You choose the military/WeSalute+ BCD or a public coupon, not both.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to rent from Budget?
If you already pay for WeSalute+ and can prepay, the **35% PAY NOW** rate. If not, a **public Budget coupon** (Costco/USAA/AARP) or **Enterprise's free 5%** often beats the net cost of buying a membership. Always price both.

#### How does Budget compare to Avis and Enterprise for military?
Avis offers the **identical up-to-35% WeSalute+** deal (same parent company). Enterprise offers a **free ~5%** military/government rate with no membership. Budget/Avis go deeper *if* you hold WeSalute+; Enterprise is easier and refundable.
