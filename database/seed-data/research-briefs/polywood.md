## Headline finding

POLYWOOD (polywood.com) has a **real but modest 5% military discount, verified through SheerID** — stated on POLYWOOD's own Help Center. It's a **one-time-use code, one per eligible person per calendar year**, good only for orders placed on **polywood.com** (not showrooms or other retailers). It covers active-duty military, veterans, reservists, National Guard, and military families, and it is **not combinable** with any other coupon or sale.

Here's the honest part: **5% is the weakest military route to a POLYWOOD chair.** Because POLYWOOD enforces uniform MAP pricing, the exact same chair sells for the same $199 at **Home Depot and Lowe's — both of which give eligible service members 10%** (Home Depot via SheerID, capped at $400/yr; Lowe's via ID.me). So the smarter military play is usually to **buy the identical POLYWOOD item from Home Depot or Lowe's and take 10%, not POLYWOOD's own 5%.** And POLYWOOD runs **deep, frequent public sales** (Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Black Friday, Winter — historically up to ~30% on select collections) that beat every military route outright and **don't stack** with the 5%. Archetype: **C — real discount, but the sale (and here, the retailer substitute) usually beats it**, with a secondary **E — single-tier identity discount, no coupon/cashback stacking game.** Do **not** repeat the aggregator claim of "**10% off POLYWOOD via ID.me**" — that figure is a confusion of the *retailers'* 10%, not POLYWOOD's own offer, which is **5% via SheerID**. POLYWOOD has **no** separate first-responder, teacher, or nurse discount.

### FAQ

#### Does POLYWOOD offer a military discount?
Yes. POLYWOOD offers a **5% military discount** on polywood.com, verified through **SheerID**, for active-duty service members, veterans, reservists, National Guard, and military families. It's a one-time code, one per calendar year.

#### How much is the POLYWOOD military discount?
**5% off** on polywood.com. That's the only figure POLYWOOD publishes — coupon sites advertising "10% via ID.me" or "up to 40% military" are wrong (that 10% is the *retailers'* discount, not POLYWOOD's).

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — POLYWOOD's SheerID offer names active duty, veterans, reservists, National Guard, and military dependents/families. (At Home Depot, spouses qualify for 10% but non-spouse dependents don't — verify per seller.)

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a free POLYWOOD account, log in, and verify through **SheerID** at polywood.com/pages/military-discount-verification. You'll get a one-time 5% code.

#### Does POLYWOOD use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute?
POLYWOOD uses **SheerID**. There is **no ID.me route on polywood.com** — ID.me is *Lowe's* verification partner. There is **no GovX or WeSalute** program for POLYWOOD.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or only online?
The 5% is **online only, on polywood.com** — it's not valid at POLYWOOD showrooms or other retailers. For an in-store or in-app military discount on POLYWOOD, buy it at Home Depot or Lowe's under their 10% programs.

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards, cushions, pillows, umbrellas, and the PW Designer Series. The 5% is non-combinable, single-use, one per calendar year, and discounted orders aren't eligible for future price adjustments.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale items?
No. The 5% won't stack with any sale or coupon. Because POLYWOOD runs deep public sales, the sale price often beats 5% — and you can't have both.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy POLYWOOD?
If it's full price and you qualify, **buy the identical item at Home Depot or Lowe's for 10%** (about **$179 on the $199 Classic Adirondack Chair**) instead of POLYWOOD's 5% ($189). If a real POLYWOOD sale is live and includes your item, buy the sale price — it beats every military route.

#### Does POLYWOOD offer a first responder, teacher, or nurse discount?
No. POLYWOOD's only verified identity program is the military 5%. First responders, teachers, and nurses may qualify at Home Depot or Lowe's under those retailers' separate programs, but not on polywood.com.

#### Does POLYWOOD run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No dedicated extra military-holiday promo is documented. POLYWOOD runs big public sales around Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, and Black Friday (up to ~30% on select collections) — those beat the 5% but are open to everyone.
