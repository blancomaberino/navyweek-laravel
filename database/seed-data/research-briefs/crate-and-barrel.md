# Crate & Barrel — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 7, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** $500 full-price home-goods cart (e.g., dinnerware set + bedding), pre-tax. No tax-free channel appears in the table, so all effective prices are pre-tax.

## Headline finding

Crate & Barrel has **no military or veteran discount** — not online, not in store, not through ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute. The brand's own coupons page lists every discount program it runs (registry completion, Trade Program, new-mover, refer-a-friend) and a military discount is not among them; Military.com and The Military Wallet both confirm the old 10% in-store discount was discontinued. The genuine best path for a service member is **buying during Crate & Barrel's deep seasonal sales (up to 60% off) and paying with the Crate & Barrel Visa Signature Card (10% back in Reward Dollars)** — and for anyone mid-PCS, the **15% new-mover discount** is the closest thing to a "military" discount the brand offers. Archetype: **B — no military route at all**, with an **H-style myth to debunk** (aggregators still publish a 10% military discount that no primary source confirms).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **There is no military discount to stack.** Any "stack the military code with X" advice for this brand is built on a discount that doesn't exist.
2. **Card Reward Dollars accrue on sale purchases** — rewards are "calculated based on your net purchases," so paying a sale price with the Crate card is a legitimate layer (crateandbarrel.com/rewards, accessed July 7, 2026). Exception stated by the brand: special-financing purchases earn 0% financing *instead of* the 10% back — it's either/or.
3. **New-mover 15% + sale:** the offer page states no terms about combining with sale prices — **not verified either way; do not assert stacking.**
4. **Registry completion 15%** applies in-store and online with unlimited redemptions for 3 months; the coupons page does not state whether it applies to sale items — flag as unstated.
5. **Cashback portals:** Rakuten currently lists Crate & Barrel as "No Cash Back" (as of July 7, 2026), so the portal layer is effectively dead there; TopCashback's live rate must be checked at purchase time. Standard portal caveat applies: non-portal coupon codes can void cashback.
6. **Trade Program 20% is full-price merchandise only** — it does not combine with sale pricing.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
No military discount exists, so there is nothing to redeem. The paths that do work:

**Sale + card (online or in store):**
1. Shop during a major sale window (Memorial Day, July 4, Black Friday, or the clearance/outlet section).
2. Pay with the Crate & Barrel Visa Signature Card to earn 10% back in Reward Dollars (redeemable at Crate & Barrel, CB2, and Crate & Kids).

**New-mover 15% (online):**
1. Go to the New Home Furnishings page at crateandbarrel.com/furniture/new-home-furnishings/1.
2. Complete the "15% off new mover discount" sign-up form.
3. Apply the offer you receive to your order.

### FAQ
#### Does Crate & Barrel offer a military discount?
No. Crate & Barrel has no military or veteran discount online or in store, and no partnership with ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute (verified July 7, 2026).

#### Didn't Crate & Barrel used to have a 10% military discount?
Yes — an in-store 10% discount with a valid military ID existed in the past, but it was discontinued. The Military Wallet and Military.com both confirm it is no longer offered.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for any discount?
Not for a military-specific one. Any household can use the 15% new-mover discount, the registry completion discount, or seasonal sales.

#### Does Crate & Barrel use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. There is no Crate & Barrel offer on any of these platforms. Coupon-site instructions to "verify with ID.me in your cart" are wrong.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Crate & Barrel?
Buy during a major sale (up to 60% off) and pay with the Crate & Barrel Visa for 10% back in Reward Dollars. On a $500 cart that's roughly a $185 combined benefit versus paying full price.

#### I'm PCSing — does that help?
Yes. Anyone who has recently moved can claim the 15% new-mover discount via the sign-up form on the New Home Furnishings page — no military status required.

#### Can I combine discounts with promo codes or sale items?
There are no active sitewide codes (per the brand, July 7, 2026). Card Reward Dollars accrue on sale purchases; whether the new-mover or registry discounts apply to sale items is not stated by the brand.

#### Does Crate & Barrel run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Memorial Day brings a large public sale (up to 60% off in 2026). There is no verified military-specific Veterans Day offer in recent years — the old 10% ID discount is gone.

#### Can I buy Crate & Barrel at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
No. Crate & Barrel is its own retailer — it is not sold through AAFES/NEX, and no ExpertVoice store exists.

#### Does Crate & Barrel offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No. The brand publishes no identity-based discount for any group.
