# Celebrity Cruises — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **7-night Caribbean sailing, 2 guests in a Veranda stateroom**, measured against every discount path.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Celebrity Cruises (Royal Caribbean Group) runs an official, year-round Military Savings offer**, but it is **smaller than aggregators claim.** The verified offer is a **per-person price reduction: $25 per person for Inside/Ocean View, $100 per person for Veranda, Concierge, AquaClass, and The Retreat suites**, applied to the **first two guests** (max **$200 off per stateroom**) — **not** the "$200–$400 per person onboard credit" that travel-agency sites advertise (those larger OBC figures are the **agencies' own added perks**, not Celebrity's offer). You book **direct with Celebrity** using promo code **"MILITARY"**, and the offer is **combinable with most Celebrity promotions** (excluding Exciting Deals, Interline, Loyalty, Net, Travel Agent, and Employee rates). Proof of service is shown at **check-in**. Archetype: **cruise line with a modest official military discount that stacks with public promos** — the smart play is to combine it with a live Celebrity sale, then compare a **military-specialist travel agency's added OBC** against booking direct.

### FAQ

#### Does Celebrity Cruises offer a military discount?
Yes — an official **Military Savings** offer: **$25 per person** (Inside/Ocean View) or **$100 per person** (Veranda and above), first two guests, **max $200 per stateroom**, booked direct with code **"MILITARY."**

#### How much is the Celebrity military discount?
**$25–$100 per person** depending on cabin, capped at **$200 per stateroom**. The bigger "$200–$400 onboard credit" figures are **travel-agency add-ons**, not Celebrity's offer.

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Yes. Veterans with an **Honorable Discharge (2+ years, or 6 months in a war zone)** qualify; **spouses of actively deployed or deceased** members qualify if occupying the stateroom.

#### How do I verify my military status?
You show a **military ID or DD-214 (with photo)** at **check-in / embarkation** — not at booking. Book truthfully and bring valid proof.

#### Does Celebrity use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No. Celebrity verifies with a **military ID/DD-214 at check-in**, and you book direct with code **"MILITARY."** No ID.me/GovX/SheerID gate.

#### Can I combine it with other Celebrity offers?
Yes — it's **combinable with most Celebrity promotions**, excluding Exciting Deals, Interline, Loyalty, Net, Travel Agent, and Employee rates.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Celebrity?
Book **direct with code "MILITARY" and stack a live public promo**, or **compare a military-specialist agency's added OBC** against that direct total and book whichever nets more.

#### Does Celebrity run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day cruise sale?
No separate military-holiday cruise promo is documented — the military offer is **evergreen**. Stack it with whatever public sale is live (Wave Season, Jan–Mar, is usually deepest).
