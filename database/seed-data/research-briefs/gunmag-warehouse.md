# GunMag Warehouse — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $120 order of pistol/rifle magazines at gunmagwarehouse.com.

## Headline finding

GunMag Warehouse does **not** publish a standing, verified, always-on military or first-responder discount. Its own FAQ does not document an identity-verified program (no GovX ID, ID.me, or SheerID integration is advertised on-site), and the "up to 16% off military discount" and various "$7.99 off," "30% off with code GunMag" claims circulating on coupon-aggregator sites are **not verified** GunMag Warehouse offers. The brand instead runs public promotions — a 10% newsletter welcome offer (order over $100), frequent sitewide/clearance sales up to ~70% off, free shipping on items flagged "Free Shipping" (and a spend threshold around $250), and bulk/wholesale quote pricing. So the genuine best-savings path for a service member is stacking a public sale with the newsletter code and card/portal cashback — not a military program.
