# Cobra Golf — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Cobra DARKSPEED driver (~$449 new)** — a flagship big-ticket club where a discount matters most.

## Headline finding

**Cobra Golf (cobragolf.com, owned by PUMA) does not offer an official military or veteran discount.** ID.me reports it is "not aware that Cobra Golf offers Military discounts," and `cobragolf.com/pages/id-me` returns a **404** — there is no ID.me/GovX military gate at Cobra Golf checkout. The widely-repeated "Cobra 10% military via ID.me" claim is a **brand mix-up**: that offer belongs to **Cobra Electronics (cobra.com — radios/dash cams/radar detectors)**, an unrelated company. For a golfer in the military, the genuine best path is **ExpertVoice** — Cobra Golf runs an ExpertVoice pro store where verified members (military is an ExpertVoice-eligible community) see member pricing reported around **~25% off**, though **current-season sale prices are frequently already lower than the ExpertVoice price** (Cobra's own checkout even flags "on sale now — lower than your ExpertVoice discount"). Second-best for everyone: Cobra's frequent public **promo codes and price drops** (often 25–45% off older-generation clubs). Archetype: **OEM golf-equipment brand with no first-party military discount** but an **ExpertVoice pro program** plus deep public/last-gen markdowns that usually beat any single military rate.

### FAQ

#### Does Cobra Golf offer a military discount?
No. ID.me is unaware of any Cobra Golf military discount, and cobragolf.com has no ID.me/GovX military gate (its ID.me URL 404s). The "10% ID.me" you may have seen is Cobra *Electronics*, a different company.

#### How much is the Cobra Golf military discount?
There isn't one. The closest thing is **ExpertVoice** (reported ~25% off full-price items for eligible members) — but Cobra's own sale prices often beat it.

#### Do veterans qualify for anything?
Not through a brand discount. Eligibility for **ExpertVoice** follows ExpertVoice's rules (active military is a recognized community); it isn't an automatic veteran discount.

#### Does Cobra Golf use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No. There's no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID military discount at Cobra Golf. ExpertVoice (a separate pro platform) is the only military-relevant channel.

#### Can I buy Cobra clubs on GovX or at the Exchange?
Cobra clubs aren't listed on the GovX marketplace as of July 15, 2026. Base Exchange pro shops occasionally stock golf gear tax-free, but selection is limited and not guaranteed — check locally.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Cobra?
Compare the **live public sale/price-drop** against your **ExpertVoice** price for the same club and take the lower. On sale items the public price wins (ExpertVoice excludes them). Prior-generation clubs are cheapest of all.

#### Does Cobra Golf run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
It runs public seasonal deals around those windows (open to everyone), but none is verified as military-gated. The deepest cuts come at Black Friday and new-model launches.
