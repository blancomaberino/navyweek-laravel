# Vivid Seats — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$300 order (two ~$150 concert tickets, before fees)** — a typical Vivid Seats purchase.

## Headline finding

Vivid Seats (a large secondary-market ticket marketplace) offers a **military discount of 10% off, capped at $30 per order, delivered through ID.me verification**. The offer is listed on the ID.me storefront (the primary artifact) and extends to the full ID.me community — **military, nurses, first responders, teachers, medical providers, students, and hospital employees** all get the same 10%/$30-cap terms. The discount **applies to the ticket price only — not to Vivid Seats' service fees, delivery/shipping charges, or taxes** — and **can't be combined** with other promo codes or cash-back offers. Because the cap bites at $300 (10% of $300 = $30, exactly the ceiling), the discount tops out fast; on larger orders the effective rate falls below 10%. Archetype: *marketplace ID.me discount with a hard cap*. The real optimization is comparing the capped $30 military discount against Vivid Seats' **public promo codes** (often "$X off first order"), the **Vivid Seats Rewards** program (buy 10 get 1 credit), and **cashback portals** — and, for military-specific ticket deals, **GovX Tickets** as a separate channel.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Ticket price only.** The 10% applies to the ticket subtotal — **not** Vivid Seats service fees, delivery charges, or taxes. Those are a large share of the total, so effective savings are lower than "10% off the order" implies.
2. **Hard $30 cap.** 10% is capped at **$30 per order** — the discount maxes at a $300 ticket subtotal; larger orders get a smaller effective rate.
3. **Non-combinable.** The ID.me military code **can't be combined** with other promo codes or cash-back offers — it's the military code OR a public code, not both.
4. **Rewards runs underneath.** Vivid Seats Rewards (buy-10-get-1) generally accrues on purchases and is the one loyalty lever that isn't a competing code.
5. **Cashback on public codes only.** A cashback portal can layer on a *public* promo code, not on the non-combinable ID.me code.
