# Citi (Citibank Credit Cards) — Military Benefits & Fee-Waiver Brief

**Researched:** July 19, 2026 · **Baseline "cart" for all math:** one Citi Strata Elite℠ Card with a **$595** annual fee (Citi's flagship premium card and the case the "citi military" search is increasingly about).

> **This is a fee-waiver / statutory-relief page, not a retail discount.** Citi does not run a "military discount" — no code, no percent-off. What it does is honor two federal laws: the **Military Lending Act (MLA)** for card accounts opened *while* you're covered (in practice: **$0 annual fees**, via an "MLA FEE/INT CREDIT" refund) and the **Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA)** for accounts opened *before* active duty (a 6% statutory interest cap that Citi is widely reported to beat with **0% APR** — reported, not published). Frame the page as "how Citi's military fee waiver works," never "what percent do I get off."

## Headline finding

There is **no retail discount and no promo code.** Citi provides two statutory benefits, split by **when the account was opened**:

1. **MLA (accounts opened *during* covered service, and only on/after October 3, 2017):** Citi refunds **annual fees to $0** on consumer cards for MLA covered borrowers — active duty, Guard/Reserve on 30+ day active orders, and their spouses/dependents. On the **Citi Strata Elite ($595)**, military-finance editors report the waiver is now **automatic**: the fee posts on the statement, then an **"MLA FEE/INT CREDIT"** for the full amount appears — no documentation, no email. The **$75 authorized-user fee** is also reported waived. On older/legacy cards (Strata Premier, AAdvantage Platinum Select, AAdvantage Executive), cardholders who don't see the credit email **militaryorders@citi.com** with their MLA-database certificate. Community reports on legacy cards add a quirk: the statement had to close with a **small balance (> $0 but under fee ÷ 0.36)** in the fee month to trigger the credit.
2. **SCRA (accounts opened *before* active duty):** Citi's official page promises "reduced interest rates and more" and lets you request benefits **any time during active duty or within one year of release** — a full year, more generous than the statute's 180-day floor. Citi does not publish the rate; editorial sources consistently report Citi applies **0% APR and waives fees** on eligible pre-service accounts (the law only requires 6%). Enroll online, or via the **Military Response Unit at 877-804-1082**, fax 866-533-0503, or mail (St. Louis). Benefits apply within **2–3 billing cycles**.

Archetype: **regulated-benefit / statutory-relief brand** (same as American Express) — the "discount" is compliance with federal law; eligibility is legally defined, not a marketing choice; the make-or-break variable is the **account-open date**. Distinctive Citi wrinkles vs. Amex: the SCRA request window is **one year** (Amex: 180 days), the reported SCRA rate is **0%** (Amex: 6%), the MLA waiver on legacy cards historically required a **manual email + a statement-balance trigger** (Amex: fully automatic), and **cards opened before October 3, 2017 can never get MLA relief** (the MLA's credit-card provisions only took effect that day).

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **SCRA and MLA never stack on one account** — the account-open date picks exactly one. Pre-service = SCRA; during-service = MLA. *(50 U.S.C. 3937; 32 CFR 232)*
2. **Pre-Oct-3-2017 card accounts are excluded from MLA entirely** — the regulation didn't cover credit cards until that date. *(32 CFR 232.13(c)(1))*
3. **The fee waiver never touches card earnings** — welcome bonus, ThankYou points/AAdvantage miles, credits, and lounge access work normally.
4. **Nothing here combines with promos or portals** — there's no code and no percent-off to stack; the benefit is the fee/interest relief itself.
5. **Per-borrower coverage:** a servicemember and a DEERS-enrolled spouse are each covered borrowers — each can hold their own fee-waived Citi cards (community-consistent, mirror of the Amex two-spouse play; label as reported).
6. **A balance transfer during active duty creates new post-service debt** — editorial sources warn it isn't protected like the original pre-service balance; don't imply SCRA covers transferred-in balances.

### Who Qualifies

### FAQ

#### Does Citi offer a military discount?
Not a retail discount. Citi provides **statutory relief**: annual fees refunded to **$0** under the MLA for cards opened during covered service, and SCRA interest/fee relief (6% statutory cap; **0% reported**) on pre-service accounts. No code exists.

#### Does Citi waive annual fees for military?
Yes — for **MLA covered borrowers** on consumer cards opened while covered (and after October 3, 2017). On the Strata Elite the $595 fee is reported to be refunded automatically as an "MLA FEE/INT CREDIT"; on other cards email **militaryorders@citi.com** with your MLA certificate if the credit doesn't post.

#### Is the Citi Strata Elite free for military?
Effectively, yes, while you're covered: the $595 annual fee posts and is reported refunded in full the same statement cycle for MLA covered borrowers, and the $75 authorized-user fee is reported waived too. Benefits (4 Admirals Club passes, $300 hotel credit, $200 Splurge Credit, Priority Pass Select) are unaffected.

#### Do military spouses qualify?
Yes — DEERS-enrolled spouses (and dependents receiving more than half support) are MLA covered borrowers on their **own** accounts, and Citi's SCRA eligibility list includes spouses and dependents.

#### Do veterans or retirees get Citi benefits?
No ongoing benefit. The honest exception: SCRA requests are accepted **up to one year after leaving active duty**, covering the active-duty period.

#### What interest rate does Citi charge under SCRA — 6% or 0%?
Citi's official page says only "reduced interest rates and more." The law requires no more than **6%** (fees count as interest, excess forgiven). Cardholders and military-finance editors consistently report Citi applies **0% and waives fees** — treat that as reported policy and confirm via your SCRA confirmation letter.

#### Why isn't my older Citi card getting the MLA waiver?
Two common reasons: the account was opened **before October 3, 2017** (the MLA didn't cover credit cards until then — no waiver is possible), or you weren't a covered borrower when the account was opened. Shoppers also report legacy cards needed a small statement balance in the fee month to trigger the credit.

#### Does Citi use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
No. MLA status comes from the **DoD's MLA database**; SCRA runs on military documents reviewed by Citi's Military Response Unit.

#### Is this the same as the Citi government travel card?
No. The **Government Travel Charge Card (GTCC)** is a DoD-issued travel card program administered by Citi — separate from personal Citi cards and from MLA/SCRA relief.

#### What's actually the best move for a servicemember?
Serving now (or a military spouse)? Open the Citi consumer card you want **while covered** — the fee is refunded under MLA (automatic on the Strata Elite). Had the card before activating? **Enroll in SCRA** — reported 0% APR plus fee relief, retroactive to your duty start, with a year after separation to file.
