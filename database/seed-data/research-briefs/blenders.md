# Blenders Eyewear — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $59 pair of Blenders polarized sunglasses (full price, before tax/shipping).

## Headline finding

Blenders Eyewear runs a real, official **20% military discount, verified instantly through ID.me** on blenderseyewear.com. It covers active-duty, reserves/National Guard, veterans, and retirees, and the same ID.me gateway also serves first responders, nurses, and teachers. There is also a **GovX storefront** for Blenders (a marketplace alternative). The main trade-off is stacking: the 20% ID.me discount generally cannot be combined with other promo codes or sitewide sale prices, so during Blenders' frequent public sales (often 30–50% off), the public sale can beat the military 20% — you take whichever is larger, not both.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online (ID.me):**
1. Add sunglasses to your cart at blenderseyewear.com.
2. Select the Military & First Responders (ID.me) option in the cart.
3. Verify (or log in) with your ID.me account.
4. The 20% shows on qualifying items before payment.

**GovX:** Shop the Blenders storefront on govx.com and verify with your GovX ID; pricing is set within GovX and is separate from the on-site ID.me offer.

### How Verification Works
On-site verification uses ID.me. Create or log into an ID.me account, verify your eligibility group, and the discount applies at cart. ID.me support runs 8 AM–midnight ET, 7 days a week, at 866-775-4363. GovX uses its own separate verification.
