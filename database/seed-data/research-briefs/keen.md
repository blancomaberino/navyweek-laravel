## Headline finding

KEEN (KEEN, Inc. / KEEN Footwear) runs a **real, official military discount — 15% off eligible online orders on keenfootwear.com**, verified through **ID.me** and branded as the **"KEEN Pro"** program. The same 15% extends to **first responders, government employees, medical providers/nurses, teachers and students**. That's the easy, free, everyday route. But the discount is **modest and non-combinable** — KEEN's own terms say discounts **"cannot be combined with any other promotion,"** and Pro/ID.me orders are **not eligible for the promotions on the regular site** — while KEEN is a **chronic deep discounter** that runs a standing **"50% Off" collection** and sale/clearance up to **50% off select styles**. So during a markdown the **public sale price routinely beats the 15% code**, and the two don't stack — check both and buy whichever is lower. For those who qualify, **ExpertVoice (KEEN pro deal)** and the **GovX** KEEN storefront are separate gated routes that can go deeper than 15%. Archetype: **C (real discount, but the frequent public sale usually beats it)** layered on **B/E (on-site ID.me identity discount)**, with secondary **A/K (GovX marketplace + ExpertVoice pro-deal routes)**.
