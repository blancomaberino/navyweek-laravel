# Hers (forhers.com) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **compounded semaglutide GLP-1 weight-loss subscription** (the category the discount actually covers), benchmarked against Hers' standard GLP-1 pricing.

## Headline finding

Hers does **not** run a general, sitewide military discount across its telehealth categories (hair, skin, mental health, sexual health, etc.). What exists is a **narrow, product-specific** offer: the **Hims & Hers "Service Appreciation Initiative,"** announced **September 18, 2024**, which gives **U.S. military, veterans, teachers, nurses, and first responders up to 50% off compounded semaglutide GLP-1 weight-loss subscriptions — as low as $99/month**. It applies to the GLP-1 weight-loss line only, requires eligibility verification, and does not touch Hers' other prescription categories. Because compounded semaglutide is a regulatory moving target, the offer's availability and exact terms are **volatile** and must be re-verified at hims.com/appreciation and forhers.com/appreciation before relying on them.
