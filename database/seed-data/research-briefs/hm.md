## Headline finding

**H&M does not offer a military discount.** There is no official H&M military, veteran, first-responder, or age-based discount program — a point confirmed on H&M's behalf by **ID.me Shop**, which explicitly states it is "not aware that H&M offers Military community discount or cash back," and echoed across independent guides. The 10–35% "H&M military discount" figures on coupon sites are **fabricated or conflated with public promo codes** and should be treated as false. The real savings paths at H&M are the **free H&M Member (Loyalty) program**, its perpetual **sale/clearance**, **student verification** (UNiDAYS/Student Beans-style, not military), and **cashback portals**. Archetype: **no discount published — steer to loyalty + sale + student + cashback.** For a service member, the honest answer is: no military rate exists, so stack the free membership, a public code, sale prices, and cashback instead.
