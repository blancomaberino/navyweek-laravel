# Sky Zone — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** **two 60-minute jump passes ≈ $40 total** (a typical parent + child or two-person visit), before tax.

## Headline finding

Sky Zone (the trampoline / indoor-adventure park chain) has **no chain-wide, nationally published military discount**. Sky Zone is a **franchise system, and any military offer is set park-by-park**: some individual locations run a standing 10%-off-with-military-ID deal, some host recurring "Military Night" events (e.g., discounted weekday passes), and **many locations offer nothing at all**. There is **no single official corporate military-discount page or verifiable national percentage** — the location URLs surfaced in search (e.g., Bowie MD, Colorado Springs) returned 404s at access time, underscoring how transient and local these offers are. Archetype: *franchise, location-dependent perk with no national program*. The honest guidance is: **do not assume a Sky Zone military discount exists; call your specific park**, and lean on the real, reliable savings levers — memberships, weekday/off-peak "Jump Around" pricing, coupon codes, and Groupon — which usually save more than any location's 10%.
