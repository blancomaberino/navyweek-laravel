# Dolly Parton's Stampede — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adult tickets to the Pigeon Forge dinner show, adult base price ~$60 each (~$120 before tax/fees).

## Headline finding

Dolly Parton's Stampede (Pigeon Forge, TN and Branson, MO — a Herschend/Dolly-branded dinner attraction) offers a **real but phone-only military rate**. The venue's own FAQ states it is "proud to offer a special military rate as a thank-you for your service," and the discount **cannot be booked online** — you must **call the box office** (Pigeon Forge 865-453-4400, Branson 417-336-3000) or buy at the gate with ID. Reported savings are around **$10 off an adult ticket and ~$4 off a child ticket**, though the venue publishes the rate by phone rather than a fixed public percentage. The single best path: **call the location directly** with your military ID ready; if traveling during a promotion window, compare against Pigeon Forge/Branson "Celebrate Freedom" area military-appreciation offers. Do not rely on aggregator "promo codes" — the military rate is verified in person or by phone, not by a web coupon.
