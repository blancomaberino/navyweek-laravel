# Rally House — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $100 team-apparel order (e.g., a licensed jersey + cap), pre-tax.

## Headline finding

**Rally House does not offer a military or veteran discount.** Its own Help Center carries a dedicated article titled "Do you offer a military or veterans discount?" and the answer is no — there is no year-round military, veteran, first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student program, and Rally House is not on GovX, ID.me, SheerID, or WeSalute. Multiple coupon aggregators fabricate a "20% off in-store with a military ID" claim; **this is not a real Rally House policy** and should be treated as invented. Because no military channel exists, the genuine best path for a service member is the same as for any shopper: **stack a public sitewide promo/email-signup offer + a cashback portal (Rakuten) + the free Rally Rewards program, and time the order to a major sale event (Memorial Day, Black Friday/Cyber Monday, Labor Day).** Archetype: **licensed-sports-apparel retailer with no service discount** — savings come from promo-stacking and sale timing, not from status verification.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Cashback (Rakuten) stacks with everything** — it's a rebate paid by the portal, not a Rally House discount, so a sale price + a promo code + Rakuten cashback all apply to the same order (as long as you enter through the Rakuten link and don't break tracking with other extensions).
2. **Public promo codes generally do not stack on already-marked-down clearance** at apparel retailers; assume one code per order and that "sale" items may exclude further codes — treat this as the norm unless the code's terms say otherwise. *(Flagged: Rally House's per-code exclusions were not readable from a primary source at access — verify on the code's own terms.)*

4. **No military verification layer exists to stack** — there is nothing to combine on the status side.
