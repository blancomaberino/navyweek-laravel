# Easton — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one dozen **Easton 4mm FMJ arrow shafts (~$180)** on eastonarchery.com (archery is the primary brand for this page; Easton baseball/softball is covered as a secondary).

## Headline finding

There is **no published military or veteran discount** at Easton's direct-to-consumer sites — neither **Easton Archery** (eastonarchery.com, Easton Technical Products) nor **Easton baseball/softball** (easton.com, Easton Diamond Sports). Easton archery historically appeared on **ExpertVoice**, but that is a **verified-pro/industry program, not a military discount**, and customers report Easton is **no longer listed there**. Neither site uses GovX, ID.me, or SheerID for a military offer. The genuine best-savings paths are **retailer military discounts** (e.g., Lancaster Archery Supply honors military on many brands, including Easton products), **Easton's own rebates/vouchers**, deep **third-party retailer sales** (OpticsPlanet), and — for baseball gear — the **military Exchange (AAFES/NEX)** where carried. Aggregator "25% Easton military discount" claims are fabricated.
