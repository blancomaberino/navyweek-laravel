# Red Robin — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$40 dine-in lunch for two** (two entrées + drinks, pre-tax/tip) at a company-owned Red Robin. Menu dollar values below are **illustrative** — Red Robin does not publish a national price list and prices vary by location/franchise; they are used only to size each path, not asserted as sourced facts.

## Headline finding

Red Robin has **no everyday, year-round percentage military discount** — anyone quoting a flat "10% / 15% / 20% off every day for veterans" is wrong. What Red Robin actually runs is a **long-running, annual Veterans Day free-burger promotion** (a free **Red's Big Tavern Burger + a bottomless side**, dine-in only, Nov 11) plus a **free Red Robin Royalty loyalty program** whose real everyday value is points and member deals — not a military rate. A **15% military discount during the month of July** is widely reported by military editorial sites (Military.com) as a Royalty-member, in-store perk, but it is **not documented on Red Robin's own site or newsroom**, so treat it as editorially reported (Medium confidence), not brand-confirmed. Because most Red Robins are **franchised**, every offer is "at participating locations" and terms can vary — this is a **discretionary/YMMV** brand by location. Archetype: **H (holiday-only military benefit + a loyalty wallet, not a flat everyday %)**, secondary **J (franchise/participating-location discretion — confirm with your local restaurant)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **Veterans Day free burger does NOT stack** — Red Robin's terms explicitly say it is **"not valid with other offers or Red Robin Royalty rewards."** You also can't earn Royalty points on a comped item or modify the burger.
2. **July 15% (reported)** — described as an in-store check discount for Royalty members; assume **either/or** with any coupon or public deal (pick the lower). Unverified whether it stacks with anything; confirm at the location.
3. **Royalty points earn on paid items only** — a free/comped Veterans Day burger won't earn points; welcome and birthday perks are **one-time**.
4. **Public coupons/app deals** are one-per-check and generally **not combinable** with each other or with the military perks.
5. **Franchise variance is the master caveat** — every offer is "at participating locations"; a franchisee can decline or alter any promo. Confirm locally.

### FAQ

#### Does Red Robin offer a military discount?
Not an everyday one. Red Robin's confirmed military benefit is an **annual Veterans Day free burger** (a Red's Big Tavern Burger + bottomless side, dine-in, Nov 11, proof of service). There is **no flat year-round percentage** military discount.

#### How much is the Red Robin military discount?
There's no standing percentage. The Veterans Day benefit is a **free burger + bottomless side** (worth roughly $13). Military-editorial sites also report a **15% discount in July** for Royalty members, but Red Robin doesn't publish this itself — confirm at your location.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
**Veterans and active-duty** qualify for the Veterans Day free burger with proof of service. **Spouses and dependents are not** included in that offer. Anyone can join Red Robin Royalty.

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a **valid military/veteran ID (proof of service)** to your server, in the restaurant, on Veterans Day. There's no online verification.

#### Does Red Robin use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Red Robin verifies military status **in person** with a valid ID. It uses no third-party online verification partner, and it isn't sold through GovX or the military exchange.

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?
Red Robin is a **dine-in restaurant** — the Veterans Day offer and reported July 15% are **in-restaurant**. There is no online military discount.

#### What is excluded?
On Veterans Day: **modifications, other Tavern Burger styles, and combining with Royalty or other offers** are all excluded; substitutions/premium sides cost extra; dine-in and participating locations only.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or Royalty rewards?
**No.** Red Robin's terms state the Veterans Day burger is **not valid with other offers, including Red Robin Royalty rewards**. Treat the reported July 15% as either/or with any coupon too.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at Red Robin?
**Veterans Day (Nov 11)** for the free burger. Every other day, **join Red Robin Royalty** (free) and stack the welcome appetizer, birthday treat, points, and current app deals — there's no everyday military rate, so the loyalty program plus public deals is the real play.

#### Does Red Robin offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
**No published discount** for first responders, nurses, or students. Royalty signup reportedly includes a teacher designation, but no specific teacher discount is confirmed.

#### Does Red Robin run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
**Veterans Day, yes** — the free-burger promotion runs annually (item and dates re-verified each fall). **Memorial Day / May: no brand-confirmed military promo** was found this pass.
