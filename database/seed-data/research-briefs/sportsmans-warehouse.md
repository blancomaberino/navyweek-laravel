# Sportsman's Warehouse — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one full-price item cart of $200 (e.g., a jacket + boots), used to illustrate the 5% mechanic; **verify live at checkout**.

## Headline finding

Sportsman's Warehouse offers a clear, standing **5% military discount on most full-price items, online and in-store**, verified through **ID.me**. Eligibility is broad on the service side (all branches, including Space Force, plus National Guard) and **extends to the registered service member's spouse** — but **dishonorably discharged veterans are excluded**. The discount **cannot be applied to gift cards, service plans, warranties, licenses, or clearance items**, and (like most retailer ID.me programs) generally doesn't stack with other promos. So the honest headline: *a modest but genuine 5% on full-price gear; the real optimization is knowing that clearance and firearms-adjacent categories (licenses/service plans) are carved out, and that a live sale or clearance price will usually beat 5% off full price.* Archetype: **standing verified % — modest, with meaningful category carve-outs.**
