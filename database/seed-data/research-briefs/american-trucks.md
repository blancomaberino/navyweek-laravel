# AmericanTrucks — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$400 AmericanTrucks parts order (e.g. a leveling kit + accessories for an F-150 or Jeep).

## Headline finding

AmericanTrucks (a Turn5 brand) runs an official **Military & First Responder Cash Rewards Program**, but it's **store credit, not an instant discount**: verified members earn **5% AT Cash Rewards sitewide**, **10% on brands owned by Turn5/affiliates**, or **20% on Open Box items**. Verification is through **SheerID** (~90 seconds). The critical mechanic: rewards are **not taken off your current order** — they post to your account within 24 hours of shipment and must be spent on a **future order within 90 days** or they expire. So on a single one-time purchase the effective value is delayed and capped, but for repeat buyers building a truck/Jeep over months it's real recurring value. Because AT frequently runs sitewide public sale/promo codes and free shipping, the smart move is to **stack a public promo code with the AT Cash earn** (rewards are calculated on the post-coupon price). This is the auto-parts archetype: no instant percent, delayed store-credit rewards, SheerID-gated, best for the repeat modifier.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **AT Cash stacks with promo codes** — rewards are calculated on "the price paid after any coupons or discounts are applied," so a public code and the reward earn combine.
2. **Rewards exclude shipping, fees, and taxes** from the earn basis.
3. **Deferred + expiring** — credit posts within 24 hrs of shipment and expires **90 days** from issuance; it's spendable only on future orders.
4. **No cash value / no cash back** — you can't cash it out.
5. **Return clawback** — if you return an item after redeeming rewards elsewhere, the refund is reduced by the redeemed AT Cash amount.
6. **Exclusions** — gift-card purchases earn nothing; some products are ineligible (check the product page).
