# Ford Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Ford Motor Company runs a standing **Military Recognition Program** that gives an **Exclusive Cash Reward** toward the purchase or lease of an eligible new Ford or Lincoln vehicle. The reward amount **varies by market and is not published as a single national figure** on Ford's program site — buyers see their amount only after entering a ZIP code at fordrecognizesu.com/military (dealer/editorial sources commonly cite ~$500 as the base reward, and Ford periodically runs higher limited-time "Military Appreciation Bonus Cash" promotions, e.g. $1,000 around holidays). Eligibility covers active duty (including National Guard, Space Force, reservists on active-duty orders, and DEP members), retirees, honorably discharged **veterans within 24 months of separation** (with possible regional incentives regardless of separation date), and their **spouses, surviving spouses, and household members** who are U.S. residents. Status is verified online through **ID.me**, which issues a Certificate that you bring to a Ford/Lincoln dealer to redeem. The single most important caveat: there is **no fixed national discount amount**, the reward changes by region and by program period, and certain high-performance models are excluded — confirm your exact offer and eligible vehicle at the dealer.

### Intro
Ford Motor Company thanks those who serve through its **Military Recognition Program**, which provides an Exclusive Cash Reward toward the purchase or lease of an eligible new Ford or Lincoln vehicle. You confirm your status online through Ford's verification partner, **ID.me**, then bring your emailed Certificate to a Ford or Lincoln dealer to apply the reward to your deal.

This is an independent guide to Ford's program. It is not affiliated with Ford Motor Company. Ford sets and controls all program terms, amounts, and eligible vehicles, and can change them at any time — always confirm your exact offer at fordrecognizesu.com/military and with your dealer.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Eligible. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, plus National Guard, Space Force, reservists serving on active duty, and Delayed Entry/Enlistment Program (DEP) members.
- **Veterans** — Eligible. Honorably discharged veterans **within 24 months of separation**; veterans past that window may still qualify for a special regional incentive (enter ZIP to check).
- **Retirees** — Eligible. Members entitled to retirement (typically 20 years of service), including medical retirement, regular/reserve/early (TERA) retirement.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — National Guard eligible under current branch of service. Reservists must be on orders to deploy, or qualify as a veteran (within 24 mo) or retiree. Drilling Selected Reserve / Active Duty Reserve who have not yet completed active service are **not** eligible on that basis alone.
- **Inactive Ready Reserve (IRR) / Standby Reserve** — Not eligible on that basis; may qualify as a veteran or retiree.
- **State Guard / State Defense Forces** — **Not eligible** (not part of U.S. Armed Forces).
- **Military spouses** — Eligible (U.S. residents).
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star** — Eligible; surviving spouses may submit a Casualty Report (DD Form 1300).
- **Household members** — Eligible (U.S. residents).
- **First responders** — Ford separately runs first-responder appreciation offers (see Ford.com support), but the Military Recognition Program above is the military/veteran track. Confirm current first-responder terms at the dealer.
- **Dependents (beyond household)** — Not separately stated; eligibility is framed around "members within the same household."

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **fordrecognizesu.com/military**.
2. **Step 1 – Find My Offer:** enter your ZIP code to see the reward available in your market.
3. **Step 2 – Confirm My ID:** complete identity/military verification through **ID.me** (a quick process; may take up to ~4 minutes if uploading documents). An email address is required.
4. Receive your **confirmation email with a unique Certificate ID** (proof of eligibility).
5. Take or forward that Certificate confirmation to an authorized Ford Dealer / Lincoln Retailer to apply the reward to your purchase or lease.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify online first (above) and have your **Certificate confirmation email** ready.
2. Visit an authorized **Ford Dealer or Lincoln Retailer**.
3. Choose an **eligible new 2025/2026/2027 Ford or Lincoln** vehicle (confirm the model is eligible — some performance models are excluded).
4. Present your Certificate confirmation; the dealer applies the Exclusive Cash Reward to the deal.
5. Complete the purchase/lease by the program deadline; limit of 5 eligible vehicles per household per calendar year.

### How Verification Works
Ford uses **ID.me** as its verification partner. You verify through fordrecognizesu.com/military (Step 1 ZIP, Step 2 Confirm My ID). ID.me may require your SSN and may ask you to upload military documentation (e.g., LES/ERB/ORB or enlistment contract for active duty; DD214, NGB Form 22, VHIC/VIC, or a VETERAN-endorsed driver's license for veterans; eRAS or DD214 showing 20 years/retirement for retirees; any of the above plus DD Form 1300 for surviving spouses). A valid email is required to receive the Certificate. If verification stalls or is denied, contact ID.me (help.id.me, 24/7) or Ford Program Headquarters at (888) 322-5862 / candihelp@fordprogramhq.com. The brief does not assert how long a Certificate stays valid beyond the stated program window — confirm currency at redemption.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Applies to eligible **new** 2025/2026/2027 Ford or Lincoln vehicles only; **vehicle eligibility may change at any time**.
- Certain high-performance/specialty models are commonly **excluded** — dealer and editorial sources list Mustang Dark Horse, Mustang GTD, F-150 Raptor, Ranger Raptor, Bronco Raptor, and Ford GT. *(Flag: exclusion list seen on dealer/editorial pages, not on the fetched Ford program pages — confirm the current excluded-model list at the dealer.)*
- Reward **amount varies by market**; there is no single published national figure.
- **Limit of 5** purchases/leases per household per calendar year.
- U.S. residents only; buyers stationed overseas use Military AutoSource (militaryautosource.com) instead.
- Combinability with other Ford offers is **not guaranteed** — "See your authorized Ford Dealer or Lincoln Retailer for more details regarding program compatibility." Some dealer sources note it may not combine with other Ford private offers. *(Flag: confirm stacking at dealer.)*
- Any specific limited-time bonus-cash amount and end date must be confirmed against Ford's current program; do not assume the $1,000 figure is live.

### FAQ

#### Does Ford offer a military discount?
Yes. Ford's Military Recognition Program gives eligible service members, veterans, retirees, and their families an Exclusive Cash Reward toward an eligible new Ford or Lincoln vehicle, verified through ID.me at fordrecognizesu.com/military.

#### How much is the Ford military discount?
Ford does not publish one national amount — the Exclusive Cash Reward **varies by market**, and you see your figure after entering your ZIP code and verifying. Dealer and editorial sources commonly cite around $500 as the base reward, with periodic limited-time promotions (e.g., $1,000) around holidays. Confirm your exact amount at the official site and dealer.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and household members qualify?
Yes. Honorably discharged veterans within 24 months of separation qualify (and may qualify for a regional incentive beyond that window). Spouses, surviving spouses, and other U.S.-resident household members are also eligible.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Online at fordrecognizesu.com/military: enter your ZIP (Step 1), then confirm your identity through ID.me (Step 2). You'll get a Certificate confirmation email to bring to the dealer.

#### Does Ford use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Ford uses **ID.me**. The other services are not Ford's stated military verification partner for this program.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / at the dealer?
Yes — that's the only redemption channel. You verify online first, then present your Certificate confirmation at an authorized Ford Dealer or Lincoln Retailer to apply the reward.

#### What vehicles and what's excluded?
Eligible new 2025/2026/2027 Ford and Lincoln vehicles. Certain high-performance models (commonly Mustang Dark Horse, Mustang GTD, F-150 Raptor, Ranger Raptor, Bronco Raptor, Ford GT) are excluded — confirm the current list at the dealer, as eligibility can change.

#### Can I combine it with other offers or sale incentives?
Maybe — Ford says to check program compatibility with your dealer, and some sources note it may not combine with other Ford private offers. Confirm before you sign.

#### Is there a limit on how many vehicles I can buy with it?
Yes — up to 5 eligible new vehicle purchases or leases per household per calendar year.

#### What if I'm stationed overseas?
Use Ford's overseas military car-buying program through Military AutoSource (militaryautosource.com) instead of the domestic program.

#### Does Ford offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Ford runs a separate **first-responder** appreciation offer (see Ford.com support). The brief found no Ford teacher, nurse, or student vehicle-purchase program; confirm any such offer directly with Ford.
