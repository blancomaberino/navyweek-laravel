# Stanley (Stanley 1913) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> **Brand disambiguation:** This brief covers **Stanley 1913** — the drinkware / Quencher tumbler brand at **stanley1913.com**, a brand of PMI (Pacific Market International / PMI WW Brands, LLC). It is **NOT** Stanley Black & Decker tools (stanleytools.com), which runs a separate program. Do not merge the two.

## Executive Summary

Stanley 1913 offers a **20% discount** to verified Military community members (and to Veterans within that community) on stanley1913.com, with the same 20% extended to Nurses, First Responders, Medical Providers, Hospital Employees, Teachers, Government Employees, and Students. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**: you click "Verify with ID.me," and after verification a **one-time discount code is automatically applied at checkout**; for future orders you sign in to ID.me again at checkout. The single most important caveat: the code **does not apply to customized, sale, or clearance items, taxes, or shipping, cannot be combined with other offers, and select products may be excluded** — and Stanley's own ID.me page does not print the exact percentage (it sits behind the ID.me flow), so the 20% figure is confirmed via the ID.me Shop store page, the authoritative provider source.

> **Accuracy note:** Aggregators and even some news write-ups advertise a **"30% off"** or **"up to 40% off"** Stanley military discount. Those figures are **not supported by the primary sources** as of June 23, 2026 — the ID.me Shop store page for Stanley 1913 lists **20% off** for Military and every other community. A separate **GOVX** channel also exists at **20%** but **excludes Quenchers and accessories**. Treat 30%/40% as **unverified/stale** and do not state them as fact.

### FAQ

#### Does Stanley offer a military discount?
Yes. Stanley 1913 offers **20% off** to verified Military community members (including veterans) through **ID.me**, applied as a one-time code at checkout on stanley1913.com.

#### How much is the Stanley military discount?
**20%**, per the ID.me Shop store page for Stanley 1913. (Note: coupon sites and some articles claim "30%" or "up to 40%" — those figures aren't supported by the current primary sources and should be treated as outdated.)

#### Is this Stanley the tumbler brand or Stanley tools?
This is **Stanley 1913** — the drinkware/Quencher brand at stanley1913.com (a brand of PMI). Stanley Black & Decker (tools, stanleytools.com) is a different company with its own separate program.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify through the ID.me **Military** community. Stanley does not state on its official page whether military **spouses or dependents** are eligible, so check directly through ID.me before assuming.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to Stanley's ID.me page, click **Verify with ID.me**, sign in or create a free ID.me account, and verify your community. After verification a one-time code auto-applies at checkout; for future orders, sign in to ID.me at checkout again.

#### Does Stanley use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Stanley's official discount page uses **ID.me**. Stanley **also** appears on the **GOVX** marketplace at 20% (with Quenchers and accessories excluded there), but the offer on Stanley's own site is the ID.me route.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Stanley's official page documents only the **online** ID.me checkout flow. In-store redemption is **not confirmed** by Stanley's primary source — don't count on it without confirming with the store.

#### What is excluded?
The code doesn't apply to **customized, sale, or clearance items, taxes, or shipping**, can't be combined with other offers, and **"select products may be excluded."** Stanley doesn't publish a full exclusion list, so confirm at checkout.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Stanley's terms state the code **cannot be combined with any other offers** and does not apply to sale or clearance items.

#### Does Stanley offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, student, or government discount?
Yes — the same **20%** applies to Nurses, First Responders, Medical Providers, Hospital Employees, Teachers, Government Employees, and Students, each verified as a separate community through ID.me.
