# Free People — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$150 Free People apparel cart** (pre-tax; representative of one dress or a top-plus-accessory order on freepeople.com).

## Headline finding

Free People (a **URBN / Urban Outfitters, Inc.** brand, sister to Urban Outfitters and Anthropologie) has **no military or veteran discount** — not on freepeople.com and not through ID.me. ID.me's own Free People brand pages explicitly state, in plain language, that they're **"not aware that Free People offers Military discounts,"** and the same for **Students** and **First Responders**. So the aggregator claims of a "Free People military discount" (a fabricated **20% code**, or "use INSTAGRAM for 30% off") are false as a *military* offer — those are public codes anyone can try, not a verified military program. The only identity discount Free People runs is a **student discount via UNiDAYS / Student Beans** (third-party student verification, **not** ID.me and **not** military). The genuine best path for a service member is therefore **not** a Free People discount at all: it's the **military Exchange (shopmyexchange.com / mynavyexchange.com)**, which stocks Free People and runs **military-exclusive markdowns (Exchange social posts show "30% less than MSRP," and a past event at up to "40% off MSRP") completely tax-free** — plus, for everyone, Free People's frequent public sales (**FP Mart / clearance up to ~70% off**), the **10% first-order SMS code**, and **~2% cashback** (Rakuten, as of July 2026). Archetype: **B (no military route on the brand's own site)** + **L (exchange-carried brand where the tax-free path wins)**, with the only on-brand identity offer being a non-military **student** discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Free People offer a military discount?
**No.** Free People does not offer a military or veteran discount on freepeople.com, and ID.me confirms it is "not aware that Free People offers Military discounts." Any "Free People military discount code" on coupon sites is a public promo, not a military benefit.

#### How much is the Free People military discount?
There isn't one. The best *military* value is shopping **Free People at the military Exchange**, which carries it with military-exclusive markdowns (Exchange posts have shown "30% off MSRP") completely **tax-free** — but that's an Exchange benefit, not a Free People discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents get a Free People discount?
No — Free People has no veteran, spouse, or dependent discount. Veterans and authorized dependents can, however, shop Free People **at the Exchange** (all honorably discharged veterans can shop the Exchange online, tax-free, for life).

#### Does Free People use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute for military?
No. Free People's ID.me pages state no military, first-responder, or student offer runs through ID.me, and no Free People GovX/WeSalute military storefront was found. Its only identity discount (student) runs through **UNiDAYS / Student Beans**.

#### Does Free People offer a student discount?
**Yes** — through **UNiDAYS** or **Student Beans** (verified students only). This is the one on-brand identity discount; the percentage varies and isn't posted publicly, so verify at the provider. Note it's a *student* discount, not military.

#### Is the "20% military code" or "INSTAGRAM 30% off" real for military?
No. Those are generic public promo codes (that come and go), not a military program. Treat them as ordinary coupons, not a verified military benefit.

#### Can I use a discount in stores or only online?
The public 10% first-order code is **online/app only, not in-store**. Sales/FP Mart pricing applies where marked. The Exchange path is its own tax-free channel (online at shopmyexchange.com / in authorized Exchange stores).

#### Can I combine codes with sale items?
No. The 10% SMS code and student codes exclude sale/FP Mart items and don't stack with each other. On a no-code full-price order you can still add ~2% cashback.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Free People?
If you can shop the **Exchange**, buy Free People there — military-exclusive markdown **plus** no sales tax beats freepeople.com. If not, buy from **FP Mart / a seasonal sale** (up to ~70% off) and add ~2% cashback, or use the **10% first-order code** on a full-price piece.

#### Does Free People run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Free People runs standard **public seasonal sales** around those windows (open to everyone), but no documented *extra* military tier on freepeople.com. The military-exclusive Free People events are run by the **Exchange**, not by Free People.
