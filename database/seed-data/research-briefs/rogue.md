## Headline finding

Rogue Fitness does **not** publish a standing everyday military/veteran discount on its own site. Its official Military page is a **charitable-support + APO-shipping** page, not a discount page — it states Rogue's stance is to "keep our prices as low as possible so that all of our customers can enjoy the benefit of quality equipment at a competitive cost." The one concrete, brand-run military offer is the **annual Veterans Day sale (November): exclusive pricing available only to logged-in, verified veterans / active US military**, with verification handled inside your Rogue account. Separately, Rogue lists on **GovX** (closed government/military marketplace) for a members-only rate. So the honest headline: *no everyday % off; the real savings are (1) the once-a-year Veterans Day verified-member sale, (2) the GovX channel, and (3) Rogue's aggressive free-shipping promos that often dwarf any coupon on heavy iron.* Archetype: **no-standing-discount brand — the calendar and shipping mechanics matter more than a code.**

### FAQ

#### Does Rogue Fitness offer a military discount?
Not an everyday one. Rogue's Military page is about charity and APO shipping, and the brand keeps everyday prices low instead of issuing a code. The one military-specific offer is the annual **Veterans Day sale** (November) with exclusive pricing for verified veterans and active military.

#### How much is the Rogue Fitness military discount?
There's no published everyday percentage. Veterans Day pricing is member-exclusive and shows only after you verify your status in your account. GovX offers a separate members-only rate — verify it live.

#### Do veterans qualify, or only active duty?
Both. The Veterans Day sale covers "verified Veterans or active US Military." GovX also covers veterans, retirees, reserves, and first responders.

#### How do I verify my military status?
For the Veterans Day sale, log in and verify on your Rogue account dashboard. For the GovX channel, verify with GovX ID.

#### Does Rogue use ID.me or GovX?
Rogue lists on **GovX** (GovX ID verification). Its own Veterans Day sale verifies status inside your Rogue account rather than through ID.me.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Rogue?
Time a big buy to the **Veterans Day sale**, verify ahead of time, and stack a **free-shipping promo**. Off-season, compare **GovX** vs roguefitness.com and always factor freight.

#### Is there a first responder or student discount?
First responders and law enforcement are covered on **GovX**. Rogue publishes no student discount on its own site.

#### Can Rogue ship to APO/FPO?
Yes — use Rogue's dedicated **APO store** for APO/FPO addresses.
