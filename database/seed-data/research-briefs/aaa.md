# AAA Membership Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> **Research note (read first):** AAA (American Automobile Association) is **not a single company** — it is a federation of ~30 independently operated regional clubs (e.g., AAA Club Alliance, AAA – The Auto Club Group, AAA Mountain West Group, AAA Northeast, AAA Carolinas, AAA Minneapolis, etc.). **There is no single, corporate-wide AAA military discount.** Offer existence, amount, eligibility, enrollment channel, and verification method **all vary by which club serves your ZIP code.** This brief documents the offers that specific AAA clubs publish, attributes each to its club, and flags the regional variance prominently. Do not generalize any one club's offer as "the AAA military discount."

> **Access/method caveat:** Direct page fetch (`web_fetch`) was rate-limited for the entire research session, so AAA's club pages (which are JS-heavy and often render terms in expandable fine-print modules) were read via search-engine snippets of the official `*.aaa.com` domains plus AAA press releases — not via full raw-HTML capture. Treat exact dollar figures below as **Medium confidence** until re-verified by loading the live club page for the user's region. All amounts should be re-checked against the live page before publish. Accessed date for all sources: **June 23, 2026.**

## Executive Summary

Several AAA clubs offer a military/veteran membership discount, but **terms are club-specific, not national.** The most broadly documented offer is a **first-year membership discount** plus member perks (e.g., a free Classic→Plus upgrade, 20% off AAA-branded merchandise, 10% off future renewals) for new members who are active duty, retired military, or veterans (honorable/general-under-honorable discharge), with at least one eligible person on the membership. Two distinct enrollment/verification paths exist: (1) **online at aaa.com/military**, where status is verified through **ID.me** (used by AAA Club Alliance and the national-facing offer), and (2) **branch- or phone-only enrollment** (e.g., AAA Mountain West Group's offer is explicitly *not valid online*). A separate **GovX**-verified "up to 50% off a new AAA Membership" offer runs through GovX.com for most states (excluding OR, WA, MT, ID, WY, NV, AZ). AAA Travel separately gives active military, veterans, and families ~10% off vacation packages (no blackout dates) and 10% off the rail portion of rail vacations. **The single most important caveat: a service member must confirm their own local club's offer — the discount, channel, and verification method that apply depend entirely on region.**

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Yes (includes Reserves and National Guard), per AAA MWG/Club Alliance.
- **Veterans** — Yes (honorable discharge, general discharge under honorable conditions, medical separation, or separation for convenience of the government), per AAA MWG.
- **Retirees** — Yes (entitled to retirement or retainer pay), per AAA MWG.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Yes, per AAA MWG/Club Alliance.
- **Military spouses** — Verifiable via ID.me as part of the military community; **whether the AAA membership offer extends to spouses is club-specific** — Not clearly stated as universally eligible in official offer terms.
- **Dependents / immediate family** — Same as spouses: verifiable via ID.me, but offer-level eligibility is **club-specific / Not stated** as universal.
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not stated in official AAA offer sources.
- **First responders** — Some travel offers and club promotions include first responders (e.g., AAA "Military, Veteran & First Responder Safety Week"); the membership military offer itself is military-focused — confirm by club.
- **Medical workers** — Some AAA Travel vacation-package language includes healthcare professionals; not part of the core military membership offer — Not stated for membership.
- **Teachers / Government employees / Students** — Not stated in official military-offer sources.
- **Explicitly excluded** — **Current AAA members (and, on the MWG offer, anyone who held a AAA membership in the previous 90 days)** are not eligible for the new-member military offer.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Confirm your local AAA club participates and offers an online path (some clubs are branch/phone only).
2. Go to **aaa.com/military** (national/ID.me path) — or to the AAA listing on **GovX.com** if using the GovX channel.
3. Verify your military status: with **ID.me** at aaa.com/military, or with **GovX ID** on GovX.com.
4. Select your membership level (the offer is for **new members**; some clubs require enrolling in **automatic renewal**).
5. Apply the verified offer and complete enrollment; first-year discount applies, with perks (e.g., free Plus upgrade, 20% merchandise) added per the club's terms.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Locate your nearest AAA branch (or call **800-922-8228**) — required for clubs whose military offer is **not valid online** (e.g., AAA Mountain West Group).
2. Bring proof of eligibility — typically a **valid Military ID** or veteran/retiree documentation.
3. Tell the representative you're enrolling under the military membership offer and provide your service documentation.
4. Choose your membership tier; add an eligible household/associate member if the offer includes a free first-year associate.
5. Complete enrollment; the first-year dues discount and any club perks are applied at sign-up.

### How Verification Works
Verification depends on the channel. On the **aaa.com/military** online path, AAA uses **ID.me**, a third-party identity network that confirms military status for active duty, Reserves/National Guard, veterans, and (for the broader community) spouses and family members. On the **GovX** path, AAA uses **GovX ID**, which verifies current and former service members of the Air Force, Army, Coast Guard, Marines, Navy, Space Force, or National Guard. **In branch or by phone**, you show a **Military ID** or equivalent documentation. ID.me and GovX may require creating an account and can re-verify status periodically — do not assume a one-time verification is permanent. If automatic verification fails, both providers offer a document-upload/manual-review fallback and support; AAA branch staff can also verify in person.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **New members only.** Current AAA members are generally not eligible; the MWG offer requires no AAA membership in the **previous 90 days**.
- **Automatic renewal often required** to receive the offer; the discount typically applies to **first-year dues only**, and membership renews at the standard rate (plus any recurring percentage perk where offered).
- **Cannot be combined** with other offers or promotions (Club Alliance).
- **7-day waiting period** for some Plus-level services after upgrading (Club Alliance).

- **Travel discounts** require advance reservation through AAA Travel; savings may vary by departure date; package terms set by AAA Travel and suppliers.
- **Anything not listed here is "Not stated in official source"** — full benefits, exclusions, and restrictions are published at AAA.com/Benefits and on each club's page; confirm for your region.

### FAQ

#### Does AAA offer a military discount?
Yes, but not as a single national program. Many AAA clubs offer a military/veteran membership discount, and AAA promotes an online military offer at **aaa.com/military**. Because AAA clubs operate independently, the exact discount, enrollment method, and verification differ by region — confirm with the club that serves your ZIP code.

#### How much is the AAA military discount?
It varies by club and channel. Common forms include a **discount off first-year membership dues** plus perks (free Classic→Plus upgrade, 20% off AAA merchandise, ~10% recurring membership discount), a **flat amount** at some clubs (e.g., $15 off the basic portion at AAA South Jersey), or **up to 50% off a new membership** through the GovX channel in eligible states. AAA does not publish one universal percentage.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
**Veterans** qualify (honorable or general-under-honorable discharge, medical separation, or separation for convenience of the government), as do **active duty, Reserves, National Guard, and retirees**. **Spouses and dependents** can verify as part of the military community via ID.me, but whether the AAA membership offer itself extends to them is **club-specific** — confirm locally.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Online at aaa.com/military, AAA verifies through **ID.me**. Through GovX.com, AAA uses **GovX ID**. In a branch or by phone (800-922-8228), you show a **Military ID** or equivalent documentation.

#### Does AAA use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
AAA uses **ID.me** (aaa.com/military path) and **GovX ID** (GovX.com path). SheerID and WeSalute are not the verification providers for AAA's military membership offer in the sources reviewed.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / at a branch?
Yes — you can enroll at an AAA branch or by phone at **800-922-8228**, and **some clubs offer the military rate only in branch or by phone, not online** (e.g., AAA Mountain West Group). Bring a valid Military ID or veteran/retiree documentation.

#### What is excluded?
Current AAA members are generally excluded (the MWG offer excludes anyone with a membership in the prior 90 days). The discount usually applies to **first-year dues only**, often **requires auto-renewal**, **can't be combined** with other offers, and may carry a **7-day waiting period** for some Plus services. The GovX channel excludes OR, WA, MT, ID, WY, NV, and AZ.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Generally no — AAA Club Alliance states the military offer **cannot be combined with any other offer or promotion**. Travel discounts have their own terms and require advance AAA Travel booking.

#### Does AAA offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
AAA runs periodic promotions that include **first responders** (e.g., "Military, Veteran & First Responder Safety Week"), and some **AAA Travel** vacation-package language includes healthcare professionals and first responders. A standing teacher, nurse, government-employee, or student membership discount is **not stated** in the official military-offer sources — check your local club.
