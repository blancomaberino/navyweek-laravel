### FAQ

#### Does BJ's Wholesale Club offer a military discount?
Yes — a **discounted membership** for active duty, veterans, and retirees, verified via **ID.me** at BJs.com/Military. It's a discount on the club fee, not on your purchases. (Source: [BJ's Help Center](https://help.bjs.com/hc/en-us/articles/45564733744276-Military-Membership-Offer), accessed July 6, 2026.)

#### How much is the BJ's military membership?
The last-published deal: **$25 for a 1-year Club Card + a $10 reward** (regularly $60), or **$50 for Club+ + a $20 reward**. BJ's dated that promo "Expires 01/31/26," so confirm the current figures at BJs.com/Military.

#### Do veterans and retirees qualify?
Yes — BJ's names **active duty, veterans, and retired military**. Verification via ID.me accepts an active-duty ID, veteran status on a state-issued ID, or other proof of service.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through **ID.me** at BJs.com/Military. It's an online check — no in-club paperwork needed to get the discounted rate.

#### Does BJ's use ID.me or SheerID?
**ID.me.** BJ's help article explicitly names ID.me. (Some third-party pages say SheerID — that's incorrect for this offer.)

#### Is the discount on my groceries or just the membership?
**Just the membership fee** (plus a one-time welcome reward). Military members then get the same everyday member savings as everyone else — there's no extra military percentage on merchandise.

#### Can existing members get the military rate?
The join offer is **new members only**. However, on **Easy Renewal**, a military membership renews at the discounted rate going forward.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to join BJ's?
The **Military Club Card** (~$15 net first year after the welcome reward) is cheapest. Heavy shoppers who'll use 2% rewards, gas savings, and free curbside come out ahead with **Military Club+** despite the higher fee.

#### Does BJ's offer first responder, teacher, or student membership discounts?
Yes — BJ's has separate discounted memberships for **first responders, teachers/educators, medical professionals, government employees, students, and EBT/SNAP recipients**, each via its own enrollment page.
