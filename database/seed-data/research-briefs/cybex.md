# CYBEX — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ≈$500 CYBEX stroller or convertible car seat (e.g., a Callisto/Gazelle-class stroller or a premium convertible seat).

## Headline finding

CYBEX (cybex-online.com, US) does **not** publish an official military, veteran, or first-responder discount, and there is no ID.me or SheerID verification gate on its US store. What actually saves money are CYBEX's own **public seasonal sales** (currently "Summer Savings — up to 25% off select strollers and car seats"), the free **CYBEX Club** loyalty program (points, free shipping, birthday rewards), and **authorized retailers** — some of which (e.g., certain baby specialty stores) run their own military discounts on CYBEX products. Treat coupon-site "military 25% off with code LUCKY" claims as **public codes, not a military benefit**. The honest answer: **no official military discount**; buy during a CYBEX sale, join the Club, and check retailer military programs.
