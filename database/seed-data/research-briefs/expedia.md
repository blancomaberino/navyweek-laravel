# Expedia Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Expedia offers a military/veteran (and broader "Shared community") discount on **select stand-alone, prepaid hotel bookings**, gated through **ID.me** verification. Expedia's own marketing landing pages (the ID.me welcome page and the "Expedia Travel for Veterans" page) advertise **"10% off select hotels"** for verified Military, First Responders, Nurses, Medical Providers, Government Employees, Teachers, and Students. However, the only official Expedia coupon **terms-and-conditions page** located states the coupon value as **8%** (not 10%), restricts it to stand-alone prepaid hotel bookings made on Expedia.com (no flight+hotel packages, no pay-at-destination, first room only, no multi-room), excludes taxes/fees, and carried a redemption window of **Jan 1–Dec 31, 2025** with travel by **Mar 31, 2026** — meaning that specific documented coupon code has lapsed even though the marketing pages still promote the offer. The single most important caveat: **the exact current percentage (8% vs 10%) and the live coupon validity cannot be fully reconciled from Expedia's own pages as of June 23, 2026** — the marketing pages say 10% and route through ID.me, but the only terms page found says 8% and shows a 2025 redemption period. The discount is redeemed by entering an ID.me-issued coupon code at checkout; it does not apply automatically. This is a U.S.-only offer (legal U.S. residents 18+).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Expedia's ID.me page (expedia.com/lp/b/idme) or the Travel for Veterans page and click the ID.me "Verify" button.
2. Sign in to or create an ID.me account and verify your eligible status (e.g., military/veteran).
3. After verification, copy the coupon code ID.me provides.
4. On Expedia.com, search for a **stand-alone hotel** for dates within the offer's travel period and select a prepaid (pay-now) rate.
5. On the payment/checkout page, click **"Use a coupon, credit, or promotion code"** (the "Enter coupon code" link).
6. Enter the code and click **Apply**. The discount is taken off the pre-tax hotel price (first room only) if the booking is eligible.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Expedia is an online travel agency with no physical stores; the discount is online-only on Expedia.com. (Booking by phone/agent is not documented as an eligible channel for this coupon.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**. You create or sign in to an ID.me account, prove your eligible status (military, veteran, first responder, nurse, medical, government, teacher, or student), and ID.me issues a coupon code for use on Expedia.com. Account creation with ID.me is required. The Expedia terms require that the transaction "go through the ID.me link for the use of coupon codes to be valid" (per ID.me Shop restrictions). Re-verification frequency, document requirements, and the failure/support path are governed by ID.me's standard verification process and are **not stated on Expedia's offer pages** — direct verification issues to ID.me's help center. Do not assume one-time verification is permanent; ID.me may re-verify per its own policies.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Applies only to **stand-alone hotel bookings** — not hotel-in-a-package (flight+hotel, flight+hotel+car).
- **Prepaid (pay-now) bookings only**; cannot be redeemed on bookings paid at the destination.
- Coupon applies to the **first room only** on a multi-room booking; **does not apply to multi-room bookings** per the "how to use" section.
- Discount is calculated on the travel reservation price **before taxes, supplier fees, cancellation/change fees, administrative fees, or other charges**.
- **Not combinable** with other coupons, promotions, or special offers.
- No cash value, no refunds/credit for unused value; voided if booking is changed or cancelled.
- **U.S. legal residents aged 18+ only**; void where prohibited. Governed by Washington State law.
- ID.me Shop lists a **"Maximum $40 savings"** and separate cash-back caps (cash back not available on car transactions over $5,000; using coupons/promo codes may void cash back).

### FAQ

#### Does Expedia offer a military discount?
Yes. Expedia advertises a discount on select stand-alone hotel bookings for verified military and veteran community members, redeemed through ID.me. As of June 23, 2026, Expedia's dedicated "Limited Time Military Travel Deals" page also states there are no separate military-only package deals currently available, so the active offer is the ID.me hotel coupon.

#### How much is the Expedia military discount?
Expedia's landing pages advertise "10% off select hotels." The coupon terms page we located states the value as 8% off the pre-tax stand-alone hotel price. Because of this discrepancy, confirm the exact live percentage inside the ID.me flow before booking. ID.me Shop also notes a maximum $40 savings.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify (Expedia has a dedicated "Travel for Veterans" page and uses ID.me's Military category, which covers active duty, veterans, retirees, reserve, and National Guard). Expedia's offer pages do not explicitly itemize spouses or dependents — eligibility for those groups is governed by ID.me's Military verification rules and is not stated on Expedia's pages.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify through ID.me. Click the ID.me "Verify" button on Expedia's ID.me or Veterans page, sign in to or create an ID.me account, prove your eligible status, then copy the coupon code ID.me issues and apply it at Expedia checkout.

#### Does Expedia use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Expedia uses **ID.me**. There is no indication on Expedia's pages of GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for this offer.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. Expedia has no physical stores. The discount is online-only at Expedia.com on stand-alone, prepaid hotel bookings.

#### What is excluded?
Flight+hotel and flight+hotel+car packages, pay-at-destination bookings, multi-room bookings (first room only), taxes and fees, and combining with other coupons/offers. U.S. residents 18+ only.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The coupon "may not be used or combined with other coupons, promotions or special offers" per Expedia's terms. Using a coupon may also void ID.me cash back.

#### Does Expedia offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes — through ID.me, Expedia advertises "10% off select hotels" for first responders, nurses, medical providers, government employees, teachers, and students, each as a separate ID.me offer with the same checkout coupon mechanic.
