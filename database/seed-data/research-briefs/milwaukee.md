On the $199 baseline kit that's **$19.90 off → $179.10**. Layer that on top of Milwaukee's public **E-Rebates** and seasonal "free tool with kit" promos, and you beat any aggregator's fictional "Milwaukee military discount." GovX also lists Milwaukee tools for verified military/government as an alternative marketplace.
