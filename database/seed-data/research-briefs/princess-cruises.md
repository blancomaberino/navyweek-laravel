# Princess Cruises — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 7-day Alaska cruise, two adults (both qualifying veterans) in one interior stateroom, illustrative fare **$1,798 total** ($899/person — fares vary by sailing; the benefit amounts below are fixed regardless of fare). No tax-free channel applies to cruise fares; taxes, fees & port expenses are identical on every path and excluded from the math.

## Headline finding

Princess Cruises runs a genuine, official, year-round **Military Cruise Benefit Program**: free onboard spending money of **$50 / $100 / $250 per qualified passenger** (by cruise length: ≤6 days / 7–13 days / 14+ days), for active, retired, veteran, and disabled members of the U.S. Armed Forces, National Guard, Reserves, and Canadian National Defense — verified **once** (SheerID online for U.S. personnel), then attached to **every future booking automatically**. Unlike Carnival's military *fare*, this is **onboard credit, not a rate** — and Princess's own terms say it is **"combinable with other Onboard Credit offers"** and **"combinable with most cruise fares."** That makes this the rare **combinable archetype (D, within travel/loyalty I)**: the winning play is to book the *cheapest public/sale fare* you can find, then layer the military OBC on top — and, if you hold 100+ Carnival Corp (CCL) shares, stack the **shareholder OBC** ($50–$250 per stateroom) on the same booking. Up to two military members per booking each get the credit, so a qualifying couple on a 7-day sailing collects **$200**, plus $100 more as shareholders — $300 in onboard money on any fare.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal terms)
1. **Military OBC stacks on fares, not instead of them.** Princess's terms: *"This offer is combinable with most cruise fares. Some restrictions apply"* — so sale fares, package fares (Plus/Premier), and standard promos generally keep the benefit. The stated exception: *"Offer excludes Travel Agent fares"* (exact scope of "Travel Agent fares" not defined — see Open Questions).
2. **Military OBC + shareholder OBC currently combine.** Princess's military terms say the offer *"is combinable with other Onboard Credit offers,"* and Carnival Corp's shareholder terms don't exclude it. **History warning:** in June 2023 Princess announced the two would stop combining for voyages after September 12, 2023, then reversed course (Cruise Critic threads, below) — this rule has flipped before; re-verify quarterly.
3. **Two military members per booking, full credit each** — a qualifying couple doubles the OBC. A third qualifying member in the same booking gets nothing (cap is two).
4. **The OBC can't touch the casino** — *"cannot be used for casino credits/charges"* — and it's **non-refundable** (use it or lose it) and **non-transferable**. It's spending money (spa, specialty dining, excursions, shops), not a fare or deposit reduction.
5. **Cashback portals (TopCashback 3% / Rakuten 2%, as of July 13, 2026)** track the online booking itself; the military OBC is applied through a separate post-booking verification, so it shouldn't break tracking — but neither portal guarantees cruise tracking, and payout waits until after you sail. Treat as a bonus, never the plan.
6. **The 14-day application deadline is the way people lose this** — verify/apply at least 14 days before departure; processing takes up to 7 days. Verified status then persists for all future bookings (visible in Cruise Personalizer / My Princess account).

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard — eligible
- National Guard / Reserves — eligible
- Retirees — eligible with 20+ years' service, medical retirement, or 100% disabled status
- Veterans — eligible with an honorable discharge **and** 2+ years' service or 6 months in an active war zone (or educational discharge within 90 days of normal separation)
- Canadian National Defense (active or retired) — eligible
- Spouses / dependents — **not eligible** for their own credit (the benefit is per qualified military passenger; max two per booking)
- First responders / Gold Star families — not included in this program

### How To Redeem (U.S. — online)
1. Book any eligible Princess cruise (a sale fare is fine — the credit layers on top).
2. Register for a free Captain's Circle number on princess.com if you don't have one.
3. Verify once at Princess's SheerID military portal (linked from the Military Benefit Program page), at least **14 days before departure**.
4. Approval takes up to 7 days; the credit then appears in Cruise Personalizer on current bookings and attaches to all future ones automatically.

### How To Redeem (Canada / by mail)
1. Download the Military Personnel Benefit request form from princess.com.
2. Send it with proof (SSN crossed out) to militarybenefit@princesscruises.com, by fax (1-661-753-1394), or by mail — no later than 14 days pre-departure.

### How Verification Works
U.S. personnel verify through **SheerID** — a one-time check tied to your Captain's Circle account; once approved you never resubmit. Accepted proof: DD214 (or WD AGO / NAVPERS), a military verification certificate for active duty, plus current photo ID for veterans. Canadian members use the CF1FC Silver Card or VAC Health Identification Card via the paper/email process. Questions: 1-800-774-6237.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Apply **≥14 days before departure** or the sailing won't get the credit.
- Maximum **two military personnel per booking**; the offer is non-transferable and the credit is non-refundable (unused OBC is forfeited).
- **No casino use** — the credit cannot be applied to casino credits/charges.
- *"Combinable with most cruise fares. Some restrictions apply"*; the page states the offer **excludes Travel Agent fares**.
- It's spending money, not a fare discount — it doesn't reduce the cruise fare, taxes, fees, or port expenses.
- Credit is issued in the ship's operational currency (e.g., AUD on Australia-based sailings).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Book the cheapest public fare** — sales, package promos, and last-minute deals all still take the military OBC. Never pay more for a "military" fare somewhere else.
- **Stack the Carnival Corp shareholder benefit** — 100+ CCL shares adds $50–$250 per stateroom (claim via the Stockperks app ≥3 weeks out; currently combinable, re-check before relying on it).
- **Go through TopCashback (3%) or Rakuten (2%)** when booking online direct (rates as of July 13, 2026; pays after you sail).
- **Favor longer itineraries** when comparing: 14+ days pays $250 per qualified passenger vs $100 on a 7-day.
- **Captain's Circle loyalty** accrues on every sailing regardless — the military benefit doesn't affect it.

### FAQ

#### Does Princess Cruises offer a military discount?
Yes — an official, year-round Military Cruise Benefit Program: free onboard spending money of $50–$250 per qualified passenger on any cruise, rather than a percentage off the fare.

#### How much is the Princess Cruises military benefit?
$50 on cruises of 6 days or less, $100 on 7–13 days, and $250 on 14+ days — per qualified military passenger, up to two per booking.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Yes, with an honorable discharge and either 2+ years of service or 6 months in an active war zone (DD214 plus photo ID). Retirees qualify with 20 years' service, medical retirement, or 100% disabled status.

#### Do spouses or dependents get the credit?
No — the credit goes only to the qualified military passenger (maximum two per booking). Family in the same stateroom shares the spending money in practice, since it posts to the folio.

#### Does Princess use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
**SheerID** handles U.S. online verification, tied to your free Captain's Circle account. It's one-time — once verified, the benefit attaches to every future booking. Canadian members use a mail/email form instead.

#### Can I combine the military credit with a sale fare or other onboard credit?
Yes — Princess states the offer is combinable with most cruise fares and with other onboard-credit offers, including (currently) the Carnival Corp shareholder credit. It excludes Travel Agent fares and can't be used for casino charges.

#### Does the military onboard credit stack with the shareholder benefit?
Currently yes — Princess's terms allow combining with other OBC offers, and shoppers consistently report both posting to one booking. Princess briefly announced ending this in June 2023 before reversing, so re-confirm when you book.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to cruise Princess?
Verify once via SheerID, book the lowest public fare for your sailing through a cashback portal, and add the shareholder credit if anyone in the cabin holds 100+ CCL shares — fare savings, $200/couple military OBC (7-day), $100 shareholder OBC, and ~3% cashback all on one booking.

#### What can't I spend the credit on?
Casino credits/charges. It's also non-refundable — spend it onboard (spa, specialty dining, excursions, shops) or lose it.

#### Is there a deadline to apply?
Yes — no later than 14 days before your departure date; processing takes up to 7 days.
