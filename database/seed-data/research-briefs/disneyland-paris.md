# Disneyland Paris — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult 2-Day / 2-Park dated ticket for a mid-demand date (full price ~$248; ~$124 with the military rate).

## Headline finding

**Disneyland Paris (Marne-la-Vallée, France) runs a genuine, official US-military ticket discount** — roughly **50% off** the standard **2-Day / 2-Park dated ticket** for both adults and children — bookable directly on the **official Disneyland Paris website/app** ("special rates" / offres spéciales) and redeemed by showing a **valid United States Uniformed Services (military) ID card** at the gate. It also honors **UK and French** military with their respective government-issued service proof. This is a real Disney offer, not an aggregator invention.

Two things make this different from a US-parks military deal: **(1) it's France-based** — pricing is set in euros and shown in your local currency at checkout, and **there is no Shades of Green / US base ticket-office bundle for Disneyland Paris**; you buy it yourself on the official DLP site. **(2) It's a ticket-only rate that does not combine with Disney-hotel packages** — Disney's own hotel packages bundle their own tickets, so to use the military ticket price you book your room separately (a non-Disney/third-party hotel, or a room-only Disney rate where available) and buy the military tickets on their own. Archetype: **travel/attraction official discount (a real rate, redeemed with in-person ID)** — the honest job of the page is to state the eligibility precisely (it's an **active-duty + retiree Uniformed Services ID** offer, not an all-veterans offer) and to flag the hotel-package incompatibility that trips people up.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military ticket rate is a standalone special rate** — it **does not combine** with Disney-hotel packages (packages bundle their own tickets). Book lodging separately to use it.

4. **US-only mechanic caveat:** there's **no US base ticket-office or Shades of Green path** — the official DLP site is the channel; don't buy "military DLP tickets" from third-party resellers.

### Who Qualifies
- **US active-duty service members** — yes, with a valid Uniformed Services ID.
- **US military retirees** — yes; retirees hold a Uniformed Services ID.
- **US veterans without a Uniformed Services ID** — **generally not**, since the offer is verified by that ID at the gate; confirm on the official terms.
- **Dependents / family in the party** — covered as part of the member's ticket purchase (per the group limit); the member must be present with ID.
- **UK & French military** — yes, with their respective government service proof.

### How To Redeem
1. On the **official Disneyland Paris site/app**, open the **special-rates ("offres spéciales") / military** ticket section.
2. Choose your **dates** and buy the **2-Day / 2-Park dated** military tickets in advance (up to the current per-order limit).
3. If you want a hotel, **book the room separately** — a Disney-hotel package would bundle its own tickets and void the military ticket rate.
4. On day 1, present your **e-ticket + US Uniformed Services ID** at entry; use the tickets on **2 consecutive days**.

### How Verification Works
Verification is **in person at the park gate** — you show your e-ticket and a valid **US Uniformed Services ID card** (or UK/French military proof). There is **no online ID.me/SheerID step** and **no US base ticket-office channel** — the official Disneyland Paris website is the place to buy.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Applies to the **2-Day / 2-Park dated** ticket specifically; **used on 2 consecutive days.**
- **Does not combine** with Disney-hotel packages.
- **Uniformed Services ID** offer (active/retiree) — not a blanket all-veterans discount.
- **Euro pricing** — US buyers face FX and possible foreign-transaction fees.
- Per-order ticket cap varies by source — **confirm on the official terms** before buying for a large group.

### FAQ

#### Does Disneyland Paris offer a military discount?
Yes. Disneyland Paris offers a real US military ticket rate — about 50% off the 2-Day / 2-Park dated ticket for adults and children — bookable on the official Disneyland Paris website/app and redeemed with a US Uniformed Services ID at the gate.

#### How much is the Disneyland Paris military discount?
Roughly 50% off the 2-Day / 2-Park dated ticket; the exact price depends on your dates (for example, around $124 vs ~$248 for a mid-demand date). Aggregators claiming a flat "20% off" understate the real offer.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and family qualify?
Active-duty and retired US service members qualify with a Uniformed Services ID. Family in your party is covered by the member's purchase (up to the per-order limit). Veterans without a Uniformed Services ID generally can't redeem, since the ID is checked at the gate — confirm on the official terms.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person at the park entrance: show your e-ticket plus a valid US Uniformed Services (military) ID card. UK and French military use their own government service proof. There's no online verification.

#### Does Disneyland Paris use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No. Verification is done in person at the gate with your military ID — there's no third-party online verification partner.

#### Can I use the military ticket rate with a Disney hotel package?
No. Disney-hotel packages bundle their own tickets, so the military ticket rate won't apply. Book your room separately to use the discount.

#### Can I buy these tickets through a US base ticket office or Shades of Green?
No. There's no US base ticket-office or Shades of Green route for Disneyland Paris — buy directly on the official Disneyland Paris website/app.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to visit Disneyland Paris?
Buy the official military 2-Day / 2-Park rate on the DLP site, book lodging separately (non-Disney hotel or room-only), use flexible dates for lower ticket pricing, and pay with a no-foreign-transaction-fee card.
