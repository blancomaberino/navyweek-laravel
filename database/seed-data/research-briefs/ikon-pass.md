### How To Redeem Online
1. Choose the pass product you want and add it to your cart at IkonPass.com.
2. During the **Assign Passes** step of checkout, select the pass holder name for the eligible product.
3. Click the **Add Military Discount** link below the pass holder name.
4. Select your military status — Active, Reservist or Guard, or Military Retiree. If buying for an adult dependent, select **Spouses/Dependents of Active/Retiree**.
5. Enter your email address (it must match the email on your Ikon Pass account).
6. Select your branch of service and complete SheerID verification — **only when you're ready to buy**, since repeated attempts lock your account.

### How To Redeem In Store (On Base)
1. Visit a participating U.S. military installation's Rec/ITT (or ticket) office. (Ikon lists "Participating Military Installations — Coming soon!" with states including AK, AZ, CA, CO, CT, FL, HI, LA, ME, MD, MA, MI, NE, NV, NJ, NY, NC, OK, PA, TX, UT, VA, WA, Washington DC, and WY.)
2. Present a valid military ID (or valid dependent military ID for spouses/dependents).
3. Purchase the pass in person at the location's special program pricing.

### How Verification Works
For online purchases, Ikon uses **SheerID**, a third-party verification service, during checkout. SheerID checks the name, branch of service, and current military status you enter against government-issued documentation you may be asked to upload (if not instantly verified). If your last name changed since your military document was issued, SheerID asks for an additional government ID showing your current name. SheerID states it does not sell, rent, or store uploaded documents. There is no offline/phone verification path for the online offer; if automatic verification fails you use SheerID's information/feedback form (response within 24 business hours). **Re-verification frequency is not stated in the official source** — do not assume it is one-time. Critically, do not run verification until you're ready to purchase: multiple attempts lock your Ikon account, and you must call 888-365-IKON to unlock it. (Sources: ikonpass.com/en/discounts; verify.sheerid.com/ikon-pass-military/faq.html — accessed June 24, 2026.)

### FAQ

#### Does Ikon Pass offer a military discount?
Yes. Ikon Pass runs a 26/27 Military Sales Program with discounted "Military Base Pricing" for eligible service members and dependents, available on base and online at IkonPass.com. (Source: ikonpass.com/en/military.)

#### How much is the Ikon Pass military discount?
Military pricing is a flat rate: **Ikon Pass $899, Ikon Base Pass $589, Ikon Session 4-Day Pass $389 (USD)** for the 26/27 season. Because standard prices are tiered and change over the season, Ikon does not publish a fixed percentage off. (Source: ikonpass.com/en/military.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Active duty, retired (the on-base program specifies 20+ years of service), and reserve/guard personnel qualify, along with spouses and dependents. General separated veterans without a qualifying status are not clearly addressed — verify your eligibility through SheerID at checkout. (Sources: ikonpass.com/en/military; ikonpass.com/en/discounts; SheerID FAQ.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
Online, you click **Add Military Discount** during the Assign Passes step of checkout, choose your status, enter your account email and branch, and complete SheerID verification. On base, you present a valid military ID. Verify only when ready to buy — repeat attempts lock your account. (Source: ikonpass.com/en/discounts.)

#### Does Ikon Pass use SheerID, GovX, or ID.me?
Online verification is handled by **SheerID**. The on-base Military Sales Program lists **MWR Military Ticket Program & GOVX** as partners. ID.me is not used. (Sources: ikonpass.com/en/discounts; ikonpass.com/en/military.)

#### Can I buy the military pass in person?
Yes. You can purchase in person at a participating U.S. military installation with a valid military ID; Ikon says a full list of participating installations is "coming soon." (Source: ikonpass.com/en/military.)

#### What is excluded or restricted?
Your verification email must match your Ikon account email; multiple verification attempts lock your account (call 888-365-IKON); Canadian military members can't buy online and must email the Contact Center with a CFOne card. Eligible products are the 26/27 Ikon Pass, Base Pass, Session Pass, and select local passes. (Source: ikonpass.com/en/discounts.)

#### Can I combine the military discount with other promotions or sale prices?
Ikon's own pages reviewed do not state a stacking rule. Some third-party sites claim it can't be combined with other offers, but we could not confirm that on Ikon's site — confirm at checkout. (Source: ikonpass.com/en/discounts; aggregator claim flagged unverified.)

#### Does Ikon Pass offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Ikon offers separate **College** (enrolled students) and **Nurse** (active licensed nurses) discounts, both verified via SheerID. No first-responder, teacher, or government-employee program was found on Ikon's pages. (Source: ikonpass.com/en/discounts.)
