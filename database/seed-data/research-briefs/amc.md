# AMC Theatres Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

AMC Theatres offers a **military discount of "at least 10% off an evening ticket"** to service members who **show a valid photo military ID at the box office**. Per AMC's own discounts page, the savings are **available at the box office only** (not online), at **participating theatres only**, and apply to the **standard evening price** — premium formats (IMAX, RealD 3D, Dolby) and special events carry surcharges that the discount does not cover. There is **no official online verification path** (no ID.me/GovX/SheerID route stated on AMC's own page) and AMC does **not publish a full eligibility list, a participating-theatre list, or an exact percentage above the 10% floor**. The single most important caveat: this is a **manager-/location-dependent, in-person box-office discount**, so the exact amount and even availability vary by theatre, and shoppers should confirm at their local AMC before relying on it.

> Accuracy note: A widely repeated figure — "AMC offers a **$1.50** discount on peak ticket pricing to all servicemembers" — comes from **military.com (a third-party aggregator)**, **not** from AMC's official page. AMC's own discounts page states "at least 10% on an evening ticket" and does not publish a $1.50 figure. Treat the $1.50 number as **unverified against the primary source** and do not assert it as fact.

### FAQ

#### Does AMC Theatres offer a military discount?
Yes. AMC's official discounts page states service members can **show a valid photo military ID at the box office and save at least 10% on an evening ticket**, at participating theatres.

#### How much is the AMC military discount?
AMC says **"at least 10%"** off an evening ticket — a floor, not a fixed figure. The exact amount can vary by location, so confirm at your local AMC. (A "$1.50" figure circulates on third-party sites but is not stated on AMC's official page.)

#### Can I get the AMC military discount online?
No. AMC states the savings are **available at the box office only** — there's no official online code or verification path. Third-party sites like GovX or WeSalute sell discounted AMC tickets separately, but those aren't AMC's official box-office discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
AMC's official wording is broad — "no matter how you served" — but it does **not** separately list veterans, spouses, retirees, or dependents. Eligibility for those groups isn't confirmed on AMC's page, so **ask at the box office** before relying on it.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person: **show a valid photo military ID at the box office**. AMC does not use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID for this discount on its official page.

#### Does AMC use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not for its official box-office military discount — AMC's discounts page lists **no third-party verification provider**. ID.me, GovX, and WeSalute do feature AMC tickets through their own marketplaces, but those are separate programs with their own terms.

#### What is excluded?
The discount applies to the **standard evening price at participating theatres only**, at the **box office only**. **Premium formats (IMAX, RealD 3D, Dolby) and special events carry surcharges** the discount doesn't cover. Caps and combinability aren't stated.

#### Can I combine it with AMC Stubs, Discount Tuesday, or promo codes?
AMC does not state that the military discount combines with AMC Stubs deals, Discount Tuesdays, or promo codes. Assume it can't be combined unless an associate confirms otherwise. On Tuesdays/Wednesdays, AMC Stubs' own 50%-off offer may save more.

#### Does AMC offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
AMC offers a separate **student** box-office discount (show student ID) and **senior** pricing (ages 60+). First responder, teacher, nurse, and government discounts are **not listed** among AMC's official box-office discounts.
