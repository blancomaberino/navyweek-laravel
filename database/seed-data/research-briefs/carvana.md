# Carvana — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$25,000 used vehicle purchased online from carvana.com.

## Headline finding

**Carvana does not offer a military or veteran purchase discount.** There is no ID.me/GovX/SheerID code, no price reduction, and no service-member checkout benefit. Carvana's model is no-haggle, fixed pricing — the listed price is the same for everyone. The "Carvana veterans" page that circulates is a **careers/hiring** landing page (it redirects to carvana.com/careers), not a customer offer. For service members the real savings levers are structural: no-haggle pricing itself, the 7-day return window, occasional public promotions, military **Power of Attorney** support for remote/deployed buyers, and — most importantly — financing through a **military-friendly lender** (Navy Federal, USAA, PenFed) rather than any Carvana "military rate."

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. There is **no military discount to stack** — Carvana pricing is identical for all buyers.
