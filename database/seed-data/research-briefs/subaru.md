# Subaru — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new 2026 Subaru Forester, ~$32,000 negotiated out-the-door before incentives (illustrative; Subaru's benefit is a flat bonus, so the baseline vehicle price only sets context).

## Headline finding

Subaru runs a **real, official, first-party military benefit**: the **Subaru VIP Military Program**, worth a flat **$500 off the purchase or lease of a new Subaru**, verified through **ID.me**. It is manufacturer bonus cash, not a coupon or a below-invoice "employee price" — you first negotiate your best price with the retailer, then the $500 comes off **on top of any current zone or regional cash incentives** and any other retailer/customer incentives. Eligibility is broad on the front end (active duty and reserves, **all** military retirees) but tightly time-boxed for veterans: **only veterans within 24 months of their separation date** qualify. The one thing it can't stack with is **another Subaru VIP program offer** (e.g., the Subaru VIP Healthcare or College Grad offers). Archetype: **manufacturer bonus-cash program** (a close cousin of the GM/Chevy model, but a fixed $500 rather than preferred pricing). The real game is timing — stack the $500 on the deepest-incentive month, because the incentive stack, not the $500 alone, is what moves the deal.

## Stacking Rules (verified)

1. **$500 stacks with zone/regional cash incentives** — Subaru states the $500 applies "plus any current zone or regional cash incentives in effect at the time of purchase." *(subaru.com/military.html)*
2. **$500 may be combined with other retailer or customer incentives** — explicit in the fine print: "May be combined with any other retailer or customer incentives when purchasing." *(sportsubaru.com)*
3. **Cannot be combined with other Subaru VIP program offers** — the one hard wall. If you also qualify for VIP Healthcare or the College Grad VIP, you pick one. *(sportsubaru.com fine print)*
4. **Not an online code** — there's no cashback-portal or promo-code layer on a dealer car purchase; the $500 is applied in the retailer transaction after ID.me verification.
5. **Affinity car-buying programs (Costco Auto, USAA) are substitutes, not stacks** — compare totals; don't assume they add to the $500.

### FAQ

#### Does Subaru offer a military discount?
Yes. The **Subaru VIP Military Program** gives **$500 off** the purchase or lease of a new Subaru, verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the Subaru military discount?
A flat **$500** — not a percentage. It's applied after you negotiate your price, on top of current zone/regional cash incentives.

#### Who qualifies for the Subaru military discount?
Those on active duty and in the reserves, **all** military retirees, and **veterans within 24 months of their separation date**.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Yes, but **only within 24 months of separation**. After that window, veterans are not eligible for the VIP Military Program.

#### Do spouses or dependents qualify?
Subaru's official page doesn't name spouses or dependents (unlike GM's program, which allows spouse sponsorship). Contact VIP Program HQ (vipprogram@subaru.com) before assuming.

#### How do I verify my military status for Subaru?
Through **ID.me** during the online request at subarunet.com/vip/customer/military.

#### Can I combine the $500 with other Subaru offers and incentives?
Yes with zone/regional cash and retailer/customer incentives; **no** with other Subaru VIP program offers.

#### Does Subaru use GovX or offer a military discount at the Exchange?
No — new cars aren't sold through GovX or AAFES/NEX storefronts. The VIP Military Program (ID.me) is the route.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Subaru?
Claim the $500 through ID.me, then time your purchase to a deep-incentive month and negotiate the pre-incentive price hard. Compare Costco Auto / USAA Car Buying prices against negotiate-plus-$500 and take the lower.

#### Does Subaru offer a first responder or healthcare discount?
Subaru runs a separate **VIP Healthcare Program**; first-responder eligibility isn't part of the Military Program. These VIP offers can't be combined with each other.
