# RTIC Outdoors Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

RTIC Outdoors offers a **10% discount on all orders** for **Active Military, Active Military Spouses, and First Responders** (Law Enforcement Officers, Firefighters, and Emergency Medical Technicians), verified through **ID.me**. The discount is applied automatically at online checkout after you click "Verify with ID.me" and complete verification. The single most important caveat: **RTIC's own pages do NOT list veterans (or retirees) as eligible** — they name only *active* military, their spouses, and first responders. ID.me's marketplace listing for RTIC separately advertises a "10% Off for Active Military & Veterans" offer, creating a conflict between sources; veteran eligibility should be treated as unconfirmed against RTIC's primary statements. The discount is online-only at rticoutdoors.com (US), cannot be shared, and per aggregator reporting does not stack with other promotions — though RTIC does not state stacking rules on its own pages.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add the items you want to your cart at rticoutdoors.com.
2. Proceed to Checkout.
3. Click the **"Verify with ID.me"** button.
4. Log in to your ID.me account (or create one and verify your military/first-responder status with ID.me).
5. After ID.me validates you, you're returned to RTIC and the **10% discount is applied automatically** in checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
RTIC does not state any in-store military discount. The military/first-responder discount is described only for online checkout at rticoutdoors.com via ID.me. RTIC products are also sold through authorized retailers ([rticoutdoors.com/authorized-retailers](https://rticoutdoors.com/authorized-retailers)), but RTIC does not state that the 10% discount applies at those retailers. Not stated in official source for in-store redemption.

### How Verification Works
RTIC uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You verify at online checkout by clicking "Verify with ID.me" and logging in to (or creating) an ID.me account. ID.me handles the document/status checks; RTIC links to ID.me's guides for [verifying military status](https://help.id.me/hc/en-us/sections/200566514-Verifying-My-Military-Status) and [verifying first responder status](https://help.id.me/hc/en-us/sections/200566524-Verifying-My-First-Responder-Status). For verification problems, RTIC directs users to ID.me's [Help Center](https://help.id.me/hc/en-us) and [Customer Support form](https://help.id.me/hc/en-us/requests/new). RTIC does not state how often ID.me re-verifies; ID.me may require periodic re-verification, so do not assume a one-time check is permanent.

### FAQ

#### Does RTIC offer a military discount?
Yes. RTIC Outdoors offers a 10% discount on all orders for active military, active military spouses, and first responders, verified through ID.me. (Source: RTIC Military & First Responder Discount FAQ.)

#### How much is the RTIC military discount?
10% off all orders, applied automatically at online checkout after ID.me verification. (Source: RTIC FAQ.)

#### Do veterans qualify for the RTIC discount?
RTIC's own pages list only active military, active military spouses, and first responders — they do not name veterans. ID.me's RTIC store page separately advertises a "10% Off for Active Military & Veterans" offer, so the sources conflict. Veterans should try ID.me verification at checkout and confirm with RTIC support before relying on it. (Sources: RTIC FAQ; ID.me Shop – RTIC.)

#### Do military spouses qualify?
Yes. Active military spouses are explicitly listed as eligible. (Source: RTIC FAQ.)

#### How do I verify my military or first responder status?
At rticoutdoors.com checkout, click "Verify with ID.me" and log in to (or create) an ID.me account. ID.me confirms your status using its military and first-responder verification flows. (Source: RTIC FAQ.)

#### Does RTIC use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
RTIC uses **ID.me**. There is no indication RTIC uses GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for this discount. (Source: RTIC FAQ.)

#### Can I use the RTIC military discount in stores?
RTIC describes the discount only for online checkout at rticoutdoors.com via ID.me. No in-store military discount is stated. (Source: RTIC FAQ; not stated for in-store.)

#### What is excluded, and can I combine it with sales or promo codes?
RTIC states the discount cannot be shared. It does not state on its own pages whether it stacks with sales or promo codes; ID.me notes "additional restrictions may apply." RTIC also says it does not distribute coupons through deal websites. (Sources: RTIC FAQ; RTIC Coupons page; ID.me Shop – RTIC.)

#### Does RTIC offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount?
First responders (law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs) qualify for the 10% discount. RTIC does not state a nurse, medical, teacher, government, or student discount, and ID.me's RTIC page does not list active offers for those groups. (Sources: RTIC FAQ; ID.me Shop – RTIC.)

#### Is there a cap on how much I can save?
No per-order, annual, or per-account cap is stated in RTIC's official source. (Source: RTIC FAQ — absence.)
