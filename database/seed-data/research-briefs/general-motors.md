# General Motors (GM) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new 2026 Chevrolet Silverado 1500 LT (illustrative MSRP ~$50,000). GM publishes no percentage or dollar figure for this program, so all savings below are mechanism-based and clearly labeled where illustrative.

## Headline finding

Yes — GM runs a real, official first-party discount: the **GM Military Appreciation Program**, a set **"program price" below MSRP** on new **Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, and Cadillac** vehicles, verified through **ID.me**. But GM does **not publish a percentage or dollar amount** — the savings are a fixed, no-haggle price that changes model-by-model, and its official terms only say it is "not available with some other offers." Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount)** with a **big-ticket / negotiate-vs-fixed-price** twist. The real maximum-savings move is not "take the program price and stop" — it's to (1) get your ID.me authorization number, (2) confirm which **current national GM cash incentives** stack on your specific model, and (3) sanity-check the program price against a hard-negotiated deal + those same public incentives, because on high-incentive models a well-negotiated price can match or beat the program figure. The program's most concrete, guaranteed wins are the **$75 dealer documentation-fee cap** and protection from being talked up above a set price.

## Stacking Rules (verified where possible; flagged where not)
1. **Official line:** the program is "not available with some other offers," and **GM publishes no list of what it combines with** (GM Financial). Any "it stacks with Customer Cash / Loyalty Cash" claim is **model-specific and must be confirmed at the dealer on your VIN** — not a blanket truth.
2. **It does not combine with GM's other identity programs** — Supplier/Employee, First Responder, Educator, College/Recent-Grad, Family First, Executive Referral. You choose **one** identity program per purchase (widely reported; Medium confidence). Compare quotes and pick the lowest.
3. **GM Card / GM Rewards Earnings** are commonly reported (dealer FAQs citing GM rules) to apply **in addition** to the program — verify on your deal (Medium confidence).
4. **Sequence matters:** you must present the program authorization from the start of the deal. You cannot negotiate a low price and then add the program on top; dealers treat it as an either/or against a negotiated figure (community-reported; Medium confidence).
5. **No cashback-portal or coupon-code path exists** for a new-car purchase — this is a dealer transaction, not an e-commerce checkout. Ignore any "GM military promo code" claim.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard) — eligible.
- **Reservists & National Guard** — eligible.
- **Retirees** — eligible.
- **Veterans** — eligible per current GM Financial materials (no separation-date limit stated); historically limited to within one year of separation. **ID.me approval is the real gate** — if ID.me validates you, you're in.
- **Sponsored spouse** — eligible only as a sponsored purchaser under an eligible member (co-titling allowed).
- **Dependents / children** — **not listed** as eligible for this program.

### How To Redeem (in dealership)
1. Create/log in to **ID.me** via gmmilitaryappreciation.com and validate your military status.
2. Request an **Authorization Number** (you get up to 2 per year; each is valid 90 days).
3. Bring the authorization number and a copy of your driver's license to a **participating GM dealer** and present it **at the start** of the deal.
4. Ask the dealer to show every **current national incentive** that applies to your specific model, and confirm what stacks.
5. Confirm the **$75 documentation-fee cap** is applied.

*(There is no online-checkout redemption — this is an in-dealership vehicle-purchase program.)*

### How Verification Works
GM uses **ID.me**. You register once, validate with a .mil email, USAA/service documents, or records, then request a time-limited authorization number. Authorizations expire **90 days** after issue and you may request **two per year**. GM makes the final eligibility decision and may audit (request military ID, marriage license for a sponsored spouse, or title/registration). Verification is per-authorization, not a permanent standing status.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No published discount amount; the price is model-specific and can change without notice.
- "Not available with some other offers" — GM does not publish which incentives combine.
- Cash/loan buyers must **retain the vehicle ≥ 6 months** from delivery.
- Vehicle must be for **personal, non-commercial use**; U.S. residency required.
- Eligible-vehicle list changes over time; some models/trims (e.g., certain Corvette, high-demand EV, and select Cadillac trims in past cycles) have been **excluded** — verify current list.
- Cannot be combined with GM's other identity programs (Supplier, First Responder, Educator, College, Family First, Executive Referral).

### FAQ

#### Does GM offer a military discount?
Yes. The **GM Military Appreciation Program** gives eligible service members and veterans a set "program price" below MSRP on new Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, and Cadillac vehicles, verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the GM military discount?
GM **does not publish a percentage or dollar amount.** It's a fixed, model-by-model program price below MSRP. Anyone quoting a flat "$500" or a fixed percentage is citing an unverified figure — get your model's actual program price from a participating dealer.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify (current GM materials state no separation-date limit; ID.me approval is the gate). A **sponsored spouse** qualifies as a sponsored purchaser under an eligible member. **Dependents/children are not listed** as eligible.

#### How do I verify my status?
Register at gmmilitaryappreciation.com and validate through **ID.me**, then request an authorization number valid for 90 days (two per year).

#### Does GM use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**ID.me.** GM does not use GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass for this program.

#### Can I use it online or only at a dealer?
It's redeemed **in person at a participating GM dealer.** There's no online-checkout version — it's a vehicle-purchase program, so ignore any "GM military promo code."

#### What's excluded?
No published amount; "not available with some other offers"; 6-month retention for cash/loan buyers; personal-use only; U.S. residency; and a rotating eligible-vehicle list that has excluded certain high-demand models/trims in past cycles.

#### Can I combine it with other GM offers or incentives?
Sometimes — but GM publishes **no combinable list** and says "not available with some other offers." Whether current Customer/Bonus/Loyalty Cash stacks is **model-specific**; confirm on your VIN. It **cannot** combine with GM's other identity programs (Supplier, First Responder, Educator, College).

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from GM?
Lock your ID.me authorization first and treat the program price as your ceiling. On models with heavy public incentives, get a hard-negotiated quote with those same incentives and take the lower. Apply GM Card Earnings if you have them, benchmark against Exchange New Car Sales, and insist on the $75 doc-fee cap.

#### Does GM offer a first responder, educator, or college discount?
Yes — GM runs separate **First Responder, Educator, and College/Recent-Grad** Appreciation programs with the same below-MSRP structure. You can only use **one** identity program per purchase.

#### Does GM run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day military sale?
The program is **year-round**, not holiday-gated. Historically GM temporarily opened eligibility to all veterans during **Military Appreciation Month in 2016**; there's no consistently documented recurring military-specific holiday discount beyond the standing program and GM's normal public seasonal sales.
