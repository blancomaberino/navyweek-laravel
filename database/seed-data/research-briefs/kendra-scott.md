# Kendra Scott — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $120 fashion-jewelry order (e.g., an Elisa Pendant Necklace ~$65 + Elisa Stud Earrings ~$55) — clears the $85 free-shipping threshold and is all discount-eligible fashion jewelry.

## Headline finding

Yes — Kendra Scott runs a genuine, **standing (everyday) 15% discount** for military, first responders, healthcare workers, and teachers, verified through **ID.me** online and by **valid photo ID in a Kendra Scott store**. This is a first-party brand offer (page: kendrascott.com/id-me.html), not a limited event, and it applies year-round — the brand simply promotes it harder around Veterans Day. The single best everyday path for a repeat military shopper is that **15% ID.me discount**, because it works both online and in-store and covers the full range of eligible fashion jewelry.

But the 15% is frequently **not the cheapest one-time buy.** Kendra Scott behaves like a promo/loyalty-driven fashion brand: the **new-subscriber 15% email code**, the **"Share the Love" 20%-off referral for new customers**, and — for Kendra Scott Insiders during their **birthday month** — **50% off one fashion-jewelry item** each match or beat the verified 15%. None of these stack with the ID.me discount (one code per order). Archetype: **verified-identity everyday discounter** (ID.me standing 15%) with a strong secondary **promo/loyalty apparel-style** layer where public offers can win outright.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The ID.me 15% cannot be combined** with any other offer, promo code, email/referral code, or a **Kendra Gives Back** charity event — one code per order (kendrascott.com/id-me.html). So the 15%, the 20% referral, the 15% email code, and the birthday offer are **mutually exclusive** — pick the biggest single one for that order.
2. **Excluded merchandise never gets the 15%,** regardless of channel (see Exclusions). The discount is effectively a **fashion-jewelry** discount, not a fine/sterling/vermeil discount.
3. **Cashback portals don't reliably stack.** Rakuten (~3% as of May 2026) commonly voids or reduces cash back when a non-portal coupon code is used, and ID.me identity-gated checkouts frequently break portal tracking. Treat cashback as an **either/or** with any code — use it only on a full-price, no-code order.
4. **GOVX is a closed marketplace** — you can't add a brand code or a portal on top; compare its net price against brand-site-minus-15% and factor its separate returns.

### FAQ

#### Does Kendra Scott offer a military discount?
Yes. Kendra Scott offers a **15% military discount every day**, verified online through ID.me or with a valid photo ID in stores. It's a standing offer, not a Veterans-Day-only event.

#### How much is the Kendra Scott military discount?
**15% off** eligible fashion jewelry. It excludes fine jewelry, sterling, vermeil, watches, collaborations, and sale/other-offer items, and it can't be combined with other codes.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family members qualify?
Yes. Kendra Scott verifies military eligibility through ID.me, whose military group covers active duty, National Guard, Reserves, veterans, retirees, military spouses, and immediate family members.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online, create a free ID.me account, verify your status, and choose "Verify with ID.me" at checkout to get a coupon code. In store, just bring a valid photo ID — no ID.me account needed.

#### Does Kendra Scott use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Online verification uses **ID.me**. A separate GOVX marketplace storefront also exists, but that's a different retailer with its own pricing and selection — the brand's own discount is the ID.me 15%.

#### Can I use the discount in stores and online?
Yes — both. Online through ID.me at kendrascott.com, and in any Kendra Scott store with a valid photo ID (the eligible person must be present).

#### What's excluded from the discount?
Fine jewelry, sterling, vermeil, watches and bands, pre-orders, gift sets, layered sets, anniversary items, engagement rings, lab-grown diamonds, fragrance, apparel, eyewear, tech/bag accessories, gift cards, and rotating collaborations (Disney, Wrangler, Dallas Cowboys, LoveShackFancy, and others). It also excludes tax and shipping.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or a sale?
No. The military discount can't be combined with other offers, promo codes, or Kendra Gives Back events — one code per order. If a public sale or the 20% referral is deeper, use that instead (but not on top of the 15%).

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Kendra Scott?
For a first order, a **20% referral link** or the **15% email code** matches or beats the military 15%. If it's your birthday month and you're an Insider, **50% off one fashion item** wins. For repeat buying, the standing **15% ID.me** discount is the reliable everyday path.

#### Does Kendra Scott offer a first responder, nurse, or teacher discount?
Yes — the same **15%** applies to first responders, healthcare workers/nurses, and teachers, verified through ID.me or with a photo ID in store. A student discount is **not** listed on the official page.

#### Does Kendra Scott run a Veterans Day sale?
The 15% military discount runs year-round, so there's no separate "Veterans Day rate." Kendra Scott does hold in-store **Kendra Gives Back** charity events around Veterans Day that donate a share of sales to military nonprofits — those are separate from, and don't stack with, the discount.
