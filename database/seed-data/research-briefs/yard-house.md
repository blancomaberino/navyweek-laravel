# Yard House — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic dine-in visit for two adults — ~$70 tab including one shared appetizer (~$16) plus two entrees (pre-tax, pre-tip).

## Headline finding

**Yard House has no everyday, company-wide military or veteran discount.** The only *official* military offer is the annual **Veterans Day free appetizer** — one complimentary appetizer per person, dine-in only, on November 11 only, no purchase necessary — which Yard House has run at least 2023–2025 (yardhouse.com). Yard House is a Darden Restaurants brand (Olive Garden, LongHorn, Capital Grille, etc.), and Darden runs no unified everyday military program either. The single best *everyday* lever for a service member is not military-specific at all: Yard House's **daily Happy Hour** (50% off select appetizers and all pizzas, dine-in), which beats any claimed military percentage most days of the year. Archetype: **restaurant / event-driven appreciation offer** (annual freebie + discretionary local goodwill), *not* a percentage-discount brand. Widely repeated aggregator claims of a "10%–25% Yard House military discount" are **unverified and appear fabricated** — no official Yard House or Darden source states any such rate.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The **Veterans Day free appetizer** is a single-item comp ("one complimentary appetizer per person") — treat it as **not combinable** with Happy Hour half-price appetizers or other promos; no source indicates stacking.
2. **Happy Hour** is dine-in only and does not apply to takeout/delivery/online orders; it is a menu-pricing promo, not a code, so it can't be "stacked" with a coupon.
3. **Gift-card discounts stack with everything** — a below-face Darden gift card is just the payment method, so it layers on top of Happy Hour pricing or a Veterans Day comp (you still pay the reduced remaining tab with the discounted card).
4. **ID.me cash back (if applicable)** attaches to the *gift-card purchase*, not the restaurant check; don't expect an in-restaurant percentage.
5. No loyalty program exists to stack.

### How To Redeem (Veterans Day, in store)
1. Dine in at a participating Yard House on **November 11**.
2. Tell your server you're a veteran or active duty service member.
3. Present military ID / proof of service if asked.
4. Receive one complimentary appetizer per eligible person. No purchase necessary.

### How To Save Everyday (in store)
1. Arrive during **Happy Hour** (typically ~3–6pm; Mon–Fri, or Wednesday-only at some CA/VA/MD/MA locations — confirm your location).
2. Order from the half-price appetizer + pizza menu, dine-in.
3. Pay with a **discounted Darden gift card** (warehouse club or reputable reseller) to shave ~10–15% off the whole tab.

### FAQ

#### Does Yard House offer a military discount?
Not as an everyday, company-wide policy. The only official military offer is a **free appetizer on Veterans Day (November 11)** for veterans and active duty, dine-in only.

#### How much is the Yard House military discount?
There is **no published military percentage**. Coupon sites claiming "10%–25% off" are not backed by any official Yard House or Darden source. The verified perk is a complimentary appetizer on Veterans Day.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The Veterans Day offer covers **veterans and active duty military**. Yard House does not confirm eligibility for spouses or dependents, despite some coupon-site claims of "immediate families."

#### How do I verify my status?
For the Veterans Day appetizer, just tell your server and show a military ID or proof of service if asked. There is no online verification step.

#### Does Yard House use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No — none of these gate a Yard House military offer. (Darden gift-card purchases may earn cash back via ID.me Shop, but that's a gift-card portal perk, not an in-restaurant discount, and the rate isn't disclosed on the landing page.)

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The Veterans Day appetizer and Happy Hour are **dine-in only**. Neither applies to takeout, delivery, or online orders.

#### What is excluded?
Happy Hour excludes certain appetizers and applies only during set hours/days that vary by location. The Veterans Day offer is limited to one appetizer per person on Nov 11.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The Veterans Day comp isn't combinable with other Yard House offers. But paying with a **discounted Darden gift card** works on top of any check, including a Happy Hour tab.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Yard House?
On Veterans Day, take the free appetizer. Any other day, go at **Happy Hour** and pay with a **discounted Darden gift card** — together that beats the imaginary "military percentage" most of the year.

#### Does Yard House offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No published discount for these groups.

#### Does Yard House run a Memorial Day military deal?
No history of a dedicated Memorial Day or May military offer was found. Its military appreciation is concentrated on **Veterans Day**.
