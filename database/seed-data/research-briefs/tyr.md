# TYR Sport — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $200 full-price TYR swimwear/triathlon order (goggles, tech suit, training gear).

## Headline finding

TYR Sport runs a genuine, official military discount: **20% off full-price items** for verified service members, first responders, teachers, nurses and the broader medical community, gated through **ID.me**. It is a standing program (not a one-off holiday promo). The key nuance is stacking: the 20% code can't be combined with other promo codes, but it can be applied on top of TYR's frequent sitewide sales and clearance, so the best realistic outcome is well above 20% off during a sale window. For most buyers the ID.me 20% is the single best path; during a big public sale, stacking 20% on sale prices is the max.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. Only **one promo code per checkout** — the 20% ID.me code cannot be combined with any other coupon code.
2. The 20% is scoped to **full-price items** in its base terms, but shoppers report it applying on top of sitewide sale/clearance pricing (one code still per order). This is the make-or-break lever; verify live before assuming.
3. GovX pricing is a **separate marketplace channel** — you buy on GovX, not on tyr.com, so it does not stack with the ID.me code.
