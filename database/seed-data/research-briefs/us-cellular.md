# UScellular — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 11, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one single line of unlimited wireless service, monthly cost (before device financing).

## Headline finding

UScellular's old **15% military/veteran plan discount is effectively defunct.** T-Mobile closed its acquisition of UScellular's **wireless business** on **August 1, 2025** (FCC approved July 11, 2025; ~$4.3–4.4B deal), and UScellular's own military-discount page (`uscellular.com/military`) now returns **404**. New customers are redirected to T-Mobile; existing UScellular subscribers keep their current plans for now, but self-serve account changes (adding lines, plan changes, upgrades) end **May 1, 2026** and accounts migrate to T-Mobile through summer 2026. **The real military savings path today is T-Mobile's** Experience plan "Military Savings" tier — verified in-house (not ID.me), roughly **$70/mo for one line with AutoPay** (as of July 11, 2026). Archetype: an **acquired/transitioning telecom (successor-brand)** — a formerly real multi-tier identity discount (archetype E) that has been retired by an M&A event, so the page's whole value is correcting the record and routing readers to the live offer.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Carrier plan discounts don't stack across carriers** — this is a "which carrier" choice, not a coupon stack.
2. **UScellular's historical 15%** applied only to **calling-plan charges** (not devices, taxes, or fees) and was **in-store**; it is not combinable with the T-Mobile offer because the two are now the same company mid-migration and the UScellular offer is withdrawn.
3. **T-Mobile plan discount vs. device/trade-in promos** are separate levers — a "Military Savings" plan does not block trade-in credits, but those credits have their own eligibility/terms; verify per promo.
4. **T-Mobile 45-day verification is a hard gate** — miss it and the plan silently reverts to Magenta at up to **+$20/line/mo**. This is the make-or-break term, not a coupon.
5. **No cashback portal / no ID.me code** applies to carrier plan pricing — ignore any aggregator claim of a "UScellular military promo code."

### How Verification Works
UScellular's military verification path is gone. The successor, **T-Mobile, verifies in-house** — through the T-Life app (Settings → Profile → Military Verification), your my.t-mobile.com profile, in-store, or by phone. It does **not** use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX. You must verify **within 45 days** of activation, or the plan reverts to Magenta at up to **+$20/line per month**. The service member/veteran generally must be the primary account holder.

### FAQ

#### Does UScellular offer a military discount?
Not to new customers anymore. UScellular historically gave 15% off plan charges, but T-Mobile acquired UScellular's wireless business (closed August 1, 2025), the UScellular military page now 404s, and new sign-ups are redirected to T-Mobile.

#### How much was the UScellular military discount?
Historically **15%** off calling-plan charges on single line and/or Family plans, for Active Duty, Retirees, Veterans, and Reserve/Guard — redeemed in-store. This is no longer obtainable for new customers.

#### I'm a current UScellular customer — do I keep my discount?
You keep your current plan for now. Self-serve changes end May 1, 2026 and your account migrates to T-Mobile through summer 2026; after that you'd choose a T-Mobile plan and apply its Military Savings tier.

#### What's the military discount now that T-Mobile owns UScellular?
T-Mobile's **Experience plans with Military Savings** — about $70/month for one line with AutoPay, scaling to ~$720/year savings at 4 lines. Verify within 45 days or the plan reverts and costs up to $20/line more.

#### Does UScellular or T-Mobile use ID.me?
No. **T-Mobile verifies in-house** (T-Life app, my.t-mobile.com profile, store, or phone) — not ID.me, SheerID, or GovX. (AT&T and Verizon do use ID.me/SheerID.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
For T-Mobile: Active Duty, Reserve, Guard, Veterans, Retirees, and Gold Star family qualify. Spouses of living members and DoD civilians do **not** (unless Gold Star).

#### Can I still sign up for a UScellular plan?
No — new wireless sign-ups are directed to T-Mobile. UScellular's corporate remainder (renamed Array Digital Infrastructure) keeps towers and spectrum, not the retail wireless business.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to get wireless in the UScellular footprint?
Compare by coverage: **AT&T's 25%** gives the deepest single-line price (~$37.50/mo); **T-Mobile Military Savings** (~$70/mo, 1 line) is the natural path for migrating UScellular customers; **Verizon** (~$55/mo single line) if you prefer its network. Multi-line drops all three sharply.

#### Does UScellular offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No current UScellular program is verifiable post-acquisition. T-Mobile, AT&T, and Verizon each run first-responder and other discount tiers — check the successor carrier you choose.
