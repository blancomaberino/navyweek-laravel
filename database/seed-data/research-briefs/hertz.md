# Hertz Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Hertz runs **several distinct, officially documented military/veteran rental programs**, and the most important thing to get right is that they are not one "military discount %." (1) A **military/government leisure program**: book through Hertz's Government & Military page and a **Hertz Rental Car Discount Code (CDP) is pre-populated** for you — it delivers "discounted rates," space-available upgrades, and a complimentary Hertz Gold Plus Rewards membership, but Hertz **does not publish a single percentage** on that page. (2) A **Military Brands Everyday Savings** offer: **save up to 20% off the pay-later base rate** at neighborhood and airport locations with **CDP 2306779**. (3) **WeSalute+ (formerly Veterans Advantage) members get up to 25% off everyday base rates** with **CDP 1264103**. (4) A separate **official government TDY program** (not a leisure discount) where the CDP is pre-populated and the **Government Administrative Rate Supplement (GARS) of $5/day per vehicle** is added per the U.S. Government Car Rental Agreement. The single most important caveat: every published percentage is "up to," applies to the **pay-later base rate only** (time + mileage; taxes/fees/options excluded), **cannot be combined** with other discounts/promotions, and the discount varies by date, location, car class and length of rental — so no flat "X% off your total" claim is accurate.

> Accuracy note: Coupon/aggregator and military-news pages variously cite "10% off" (legacy Veterans Advantage), "20% off," "25% off," and a seasonal "Veterans Day 20% off base rate." These are inconsistent and partly stale. Only the figures tied to a Hertz CDP page are treated as fact here: **up to 20%** (Military Brands, CDP 2306779) and **up to 25%** (WeSalute+, CDP 1264103). The Government & Military leisure program's exact percentage is **not published by Hertz** — do not assert one.

### Intro
Hertz doesn't have one flat "military discount" — it has a few official programs, and which one saves you the most depends on who you are and how you book. Active-duty service members, National Guard, Reservists, and (where applicable) military spouses can book through Hertz's **Government & Military** page, where a **Hertz discount code (CDP) is automatically applied** to give discounted rates, space-available upgrades, and a free Hertz Gold Plus Rewards membership. Separately, Hertz's **Military Brands** offer gives **up to 20% off the base rate** with CDP **2306779**, and **WeSalute+ members** (the program formerly known as Veterans Advantage) get **up to 25% off** with CDP **1264103**.

A few things to know before you book: every published percentage is an "up to" figure that applies to the **pay-later base rate only** (the time-and-mileage charge — taxes, fees, and add-ons are extra), the discount **can't be combined** with other promotions, and the exact savings vary by date, location, car class, and rental length. You may also be asked to show **military or government ID at pickup**. For official-duty (TDY) travel, Hertz runs a completely separate government program where the **$5/day GARS fee** applies — that's official-travel pricing, not the leisure discount.

This is an independent guide to help you understand and use these offers. We're not affiliated with Hertz, and Hertz controls the terms and can change them at any time. Always confirm current rates and codes on Hertz's official pages before you book.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Hertz's **Government & Military** page (or the Military Brands / WeSalute+ offer page) and choose the matching booking link — the **CDP discount code is pre-populated** for you.
2. Enter your pickup location, dates and times, and drop-off details (book at least **24 hours** ahead).
3. Select **pay-later** to keep the discounted base rate eligible (the discount applies to the pay-later base rate).
4. Choose your vehicle and review the rate; the CDP-linked discount is reflected in the base rate.
5. Complete the reservation. Bring **military/government ID** in case proof of eligibility is requested at pickup.

### How To Redeem In Store / At Counter
1. Have a reservation booked under the correct CDP (military leisure, Military Brands 2306779, or WeSalute+ 1264103), or ask the counter/phone agent to apply the appropriate military CDP.
2. Present your **military or government ID** if asked to verify eligibility.
3. Collect your vehicle at the discounted base rate; any taxes, fees, and add-ons are charged on top.

### How Verification Works
Hertz's military/government program pages do **not** use a third-party verification service (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX gate) for the standard military leisure CDP — instead, the **discount code (CDP) is automatically applied** through the program booking link, and Hertz states **"you may be asked to provide proof that you qualify for the Military CDP code when you collect your rental"** (e.g., official government or military ID). The **WeSalute+** route is different: it requires an active **WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage) membership**, and the up-to-25% discount books under **CDP 1264103**. There is no stated re-verification cadence beyond presenting ID at pickup when requested.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Discounts apply to the **pay-later base rate only** — base rate is **time and mileage charges**; **taxes, fees, and options are excluded.**
- **"Up to"** figures: actual discount **varies by date, location, car class, length of rental and other factors.**
- **Not combinable:** "may not be used with other discounts, promotions or special rates."
- **24-hour advance reservation required** (Military Brands and WeSalute+ terms).
- **Age, driver, credit, and qualifying-rate restrictions** for the renting location apply.
- Rate may change / extra fees may apply on reservation modification, early/late return, or returning to a different location.
- **Official TDY travel** carries the **$5/day GARS** fee — this is official-travel pricing, not part of the leisure discount.
- **Veterans:** Hertz's military page says only "some veterans" qualify on the standard program — *flag: do not state a universal veteran rate outside WeSalute+.*
- Caps (per-order/annual), gift-card rules, and full international terms are **not stated** on the US program pages.

### FAQ

#### Does Hertz offer a military discount?
Yes. Hertz has a Government & Military program that auto-applies a discount code (CDP) for discounted leisure rates, plus a Military Brands offer (up to 20% off the base rate, CDP 2306779) and a WeSalute+ route for veterans (up to 25% off, CDP 1264103). There's also a separate official-duty (TDY) government program.

#### How much is the Hertz military discount?
It depends on the program. Hertz publishes **up to 20%** off the base rate for its Military Brands code and **up to 25%** for WeSalute+ members. The standard Government & Military leisure program gives "discounted rates" but Hertz doesn't publish a single percentage for it. All percentages apply to the pay-later base rate only and vary by date, location, and car class.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Hertz's military page lists active-duty, National Guard, Reservists, **some veterans**, and military spouses "where applicable." The clearest veteran path is **WeSalute+** membership (up to 25%). Other dependents and surviving spouses aren't specified on Hertz's official pages.

#### How do I verify my status / does Hertz use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Hertz's own military program pages don't gate the standard discount behind ID.me, SheerID, or GovX — the CDP is auto-applied, and you may be asked for **military/government ID at pickup**. The veteran route uses **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage) membership for the up-to-25% code.

#### What is the Hertz military discount code (CDP)?
Hertz surfaces more than one: the Government & Military leisure booking link, **Military Brands CDP 2306779**, and **WeSalute+ CDP 1264103**. The code is normally pre-populated when you use the official booking link, so you usually don't type it in manually.

#### What's the difference between the leisure discount and official government travel?
The leisure discount gives military members a reduced personal-travel rate. **Official government travel (TDY)** is a separate contracted program where the CDP is pre-populated and a **$5/day GARS fee** is added per the U.S. Government Car Rental Agreement — that's duty-travel pricing, not a personal discount.

#### Can I combine it with other promo codes or sale rates?
No. Hertz's terms state the offer "may not be used with other discounts, promotions or special rates." Pick the single best offer rather than stacking.

#### What's excluded?
The discount applies to the pay-later **base rate** (time + mileage) only; taxes, fees, and add-ons are excluded. Discounts are "up to" and vary, a 24-hour advance booking is required, and age/driver/credit restrictions apply.

#### Does Hertz offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Those groups aren't listed in Hertz's military/government programs. Hertz's documented audience-based programs here cover military, government (TDY), and veterans via WeSalute+.
