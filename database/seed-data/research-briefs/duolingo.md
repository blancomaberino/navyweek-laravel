# Duolingo — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one year of Super Duolingo (individual, ~$95.99/yr list; ~$12.99/mo).

## Headline finding

**Duolingo does not offer a military or veteran discount** — no standing program, no ID.me/GovX/SheerID verification path. But the honest lede is that **Duolingo's core app is free**: any service member, veteran, or family member can learn a language at $0, and the paid tiers (Super/Max) are conveniences, not requirements. If someone wants Super Duolingo cheaper, the real levers are the **Family Plan** (~$119.99/yr for up to 6, the lowest per-person cost), the **annual vs monthly** switch, the **free trial**, and the recurring **"New Year, New You" ~50–60% off annual promo** (late Dec–mid Jan). For fully-free structured learning, military-connected users can use **Mango Languages free through DoD MWR Libraries** — a genuine benefit Duolingo itself doesn't provide.
