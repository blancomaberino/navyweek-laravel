# Tractor Supply Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Tractor Supply has **no flat, everyday percentage military discount** — the widely-copied "10% off every day" claim is a myth, and so is "up to 25% military." What Tractor Supply actually runs is the structured **Hometown Heroes program** (launched 2024) with two real, verified components: (1) a **10% discount** on the recognition days — **National First Responders Day (Oct 28), National Hometown Heroes Day (the first Saturday of November, ~Nov 1), and Veterans Day (Nov 11)** — historically in-store, covering **military, veterans, and first responders plus dependents**; and (2) a free, year-round **Neighbor's Club "Hometown Heroes" wallet** of rotating brand/category offers (Victor, Purina Dog Chow, Nutrena ProForce, Standlee, plus TSC Towing and seasonal items), unlocked once via **ID.me**. The honest best-savings play is two-fold: **time big-ticket purchases** to the late-October → Veterans Day window for the 10%, and — year-round — **enroll free in Neighbor's Club and verify via ID.me** to capture the wallet offers on the feed and pet brands rural shoppers buy most. Because sale/clearance is generally excluded from the 10%, the holiday rate is **pick-the-lower, not a stack**.

> Accuracy note: Veterans Day and July 4 were **15%** in prior years (e.g. 2023); the latest (2025–26) announcements **standardize the rate at 10%**. Don't rely on the older 15% figure. Tractor Supply re-announces the program each year, so always confirm the current-year %, dates, and exclusions on the official Hometown Heroes page before purchasing.
