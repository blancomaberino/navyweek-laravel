# SimpliSafe — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new SimpliSafe system listed at **$300** (hardware only, before state sales tax). Percentages apply the same way to any system list price.

## Headline finding

SimpliSafe runs a **real, official, always-on** discount: **50% off any new system** for military, veterans and dependents (plus first responders, medical professionals, and teachers) through its **Protectors Program**, verified by **VerifyPass** at SimpliSafe.com/Protectors. But the honest catch — and the reason this page beats a coupon page — is that SimpliSafe **runs a public "up to 50% off" sale most of the year**, and the Protectors discount **cannot be combined** with any other promotion. So for much of the year the 50% Protectors code delivers **no extra savings over the public sale** (which often adds a free camera on top), and it can be *beaten* by the Black Friday sale (historically up to 70% off + free camera). Archetype: **verified discount at parity with the public sale, non-stacking** — with a **subscription-upsell** overlay (bundle freebies require opting into monitoring). Bottom line: take **whichever single offer is highest at purchase time** — usually the live public 50% + free-camera bundle, the 50% Protectors code only when no equal-or-better public sale is running, and **wait for Black Friday (up to 70%)** if you can.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The Protectors 50% cannot be combined with any other discount or promotion**, and cannot be applied retroactively — SimpliSafe Support states this verbatim. It is an **either/or** against the public sale, never a stack.
2. **VerifyPass** only issues the coupon after status verification; the coupon is **one-time** and applied in-cart online. VerifyPass's offer page confirms the same 50%/new-system terms and adds no stacking allowance.
3. Because public sales routinely hit the same 50%, the Protectors code's marginal value is **$0 during an equal public sale** — its worth is as a floor when no public sale is running.
4. **Bundle freebies (free camera / free month of monitoring) are tied to opting into a monitoring plan**, and ride on the *public* promotion — not confirmed to attach to the Protectors code.

### FAQ

#### Does SimpliSafe offer a military discount?
Yes. Through the **Protectors Program**, verified military members, veterans and dependents get **50% off a new SimpliSafe system**.

#### How much is the SimpliSafe military discount?
**50% off** the purchase of a new system. Ignore aggregator claims of "15% off" — that figure is wrong.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans and dependents are explicitly named as eligible, along with active-duty members and the broader "military and armed forces."

#### How do I verify my status?
At **SimpliSafe.com/Protectors**: pick your status, choose "Receive 50% Off," and verify through **VerifyPass** (a free account may be required). You then get a one-time in-cart coupon.

#### Does SimpliSafe use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
The official Protectors Program uses **VerifyPass**. It does **not** use GovX or SheerID for this discount. ID.me lists SimpliSafe as a cash-back store under ID.me's own program — that is separate from and not the official Protectors discount.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
**Online only**, direct from SimpliSafe — a one-time coupon applied in your cart.

#### What is excluded?
It applies to **new systems only** (not individual devices) and **can't be combined** with other discounts or promotions, and isn't retroactive.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
**No.** The Protectors offer cannot be combined with any other discount or promotion. Since the public sale is often the same 50%, the code frequently adds nothing extra — so compare and take the higher single offer.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from SimpliSafe?
Take **whichever single offer is highest when you buy**: the live public 50% + free-camera bundle (usual winner), the Protectors 50% (when no equal public sale is running), or — best of all — **Black Friday's up to 70% off**. Refurbished systems (up to ~60% off) are the lowest hardware cost.

#### Does SimpliSafe offer a first responder, teacher, medical, or government discount?
Yes — the same **50% Protectors** discount covers first responders (police, fire, EMT), medical professionals, teachers/educators, and (per VerifyPass) government employees. No student tier was found.

#### Does SimpliSafe run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
There's no distinct military-holiday uplift — the Protectors 50% is always on. SimpliSafe's biggest public sale is **Black Friday/Cyber Monday (historically up to 70% off)**.

#### Can I buy SimpliSafe at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
No verified AAFES/NEX Exchange stocking or ExpertVoice/pro-deal path was found; buy direct from SimpliSafe.
