# American Airlines Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

American Airlines does **not publish a percentage discount on its own airfare** for military members. Its military program is built on **policy perks, not a fare discount**: free checked bags (up to 5 bags at 100 lbs each for active-duty U.S. military and dependents traveling **on orders**; up to 3 bags at 50 lbs each for active-duty on **personal/leisure** travel), **Group 1 priority boarding** for active-duty with ID, and **complimentary Admirals Club access** for U.S. military traveling **in uniform** on a same-day American-operated flight. The bag and boarding perks require **active-duty status** — American's own definition **excludes Civilian Military ID holders, U.S. Public Health Service, Military Reserves and National Guard** for the bag benefit. The only published discount that touches a price is **10% off the land portion of American Airlines Vacations packages** (hotels, rental cars, theme park passes, tours — **not airfare**), available only to **WeSalute+** subscribers booking through WeSalute's private engine. The single most important caveat: the widely repeated aggregator claim of "10% off American Airlines flights" is **misleading** — the 10% applies to AA Vacations **land** components, and AA describes its own military fares only as "government or military fares in some markets, call Reservations."

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator pages advertise "**up to 10% off American Airlines flights/airfare**" for military via WeSalute/Veterans Advantage. WeSalute's **own official offer page** states the 10% is on "**the land portion**" of AA Vacations packages (hotels, rental cars, theme parks, tours), not on airfare. Do not state a 10% airfare discount. Verified June 23, 2026.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Eligible (free bags, Group 1 boarding, in-uniform lounge access).
- **Dependents traveling on orders** — Eligible for the same bag privileges with **Military Dependent ID + a copy of active orders**.
- **Veterans** — **Not stated** as eligible for the free-bag/boarding perks on American's page. Eligible for the WeSalute+ AA Vacations 10% (WeSalute lists active duty, retiree, veteran, current/former Guard & Reserve, and immediate family).
- **Retirees** — **Not stated** for free bags/boarding on American's page. Eligible for the WeSalute+ vacations 10%.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — **Explicitly excluded** from the free-bag benefit by American's definition of "active U.S. military personnel." Eligible for the WeSalute+ vacations 10%.
- **Military spouses / dependents (not on orders)** — **Not stated** for the bag perk outside of "on orders"; dependents qualify only when traveling on orders with Dependent ID + orders. Lounge guest access: an eligible member may bring immediate family or up to 2 guests.
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not stated in official source.
- **First responders / Teachers / Nurses / Government / Students** — Not listed among American's military benefits.

### How To Redeem (Free Checked Bags)
1. Book and travel on an itinerary **marketed and operated by American Airlines** (not a codeshare partner).
2. If you're on a military fare, you **cannot** pay for bags online — handle bags at the airport.
3. At the **check-in counter**, present your **active-duty U.S. military ID** (dependents: **Military Dependent ID + a copy of active orders**).
4. The agent applies your free-bag allowance: up to 5 bags/100 lbs each **on orders**, or up to 3 bags/50 lbs each on **personal/leisure** travel.

### How To Redeem (Boarding & Lounge)
1. **Boarding:** At the gate, show your **military ID**; active-duty members board with **Group 1**.
2. **Lounge:** Arrive at an **Admirals Club** in **uniform** with your **military ID** and a **same-day boarding pass on an American-operated flight**; access is subject to lounge capacity (excludes Airspace Lounge SAN and JAL Sakura Lounge HNL).

### How To Redeem (WeSalute+ 10% on Vacations)
1. Enroll in **WeSalute+** (paid membership; verifies military/veteran status).
2. Log in and open the **American Airlines Vacations** offer through **WeSalute.com**'s private booking engine — accessing aavacations.com any other way **won't** apply the discount.
3. Book a **round-trip** package originating from one of the 50 U.S. states; the **10% off the land portion** is already reflected at checkout.

### How Verification Works
For the **core perks** (bags, boarding, lounge) there is **no third-party verification provider and no code** — you present your **physical military ID** at the airport (and, for dependents, a **Military Dependent ID plus a copy of active orders**). American's definition of eligible "active U.S. military personnel" is members of the Air Force, Army, Coast Guard, Marines, Navy and Space Force in active status (cadets included), and it **excludes** Civilian Military ID holders, U.S. Public Health Service, Military Reserves and National Guard for the bag benefit. The separate **WeSalute+** vacations discount requires a **paid WeSalute+ subscription** that verifies your status; that membership re-bills per WeSalute's own terms.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Free checked bags apply **only on American marketed and operated itineraries** — **not** codeshare flights operated by partner airlines.
- The free-bag benefit's "active U.S. military" definition **excludes Reserves, National Guard, Civilian Military ID, and U.S. Public Health Service**. *(Flag: a veteran or Guard/Reserve member should not expect the free-bag perk based on American's stated definition; confirm at the counter.)*
- On personal/leisure travel the active-duty allowance is **3 bags at 50 lbs**, not the 5-bag/100-lb "on orders" allowance.
- American publishes **no percentage discount on its airfare**; "government or military fares in some markets" requires calling Reservations and the amount is **not published**.
- WeSalute+ **10% applies to the land portion only** (hotels, cars, theme parks, tours), **not airfare**; **online only** through WeSalute's engine, **round-trip**, max **9 passengers** per reservation, must originate from a U.S. state. Changes made by phone reps forfeit the discount.
- Admirals Club access is **capacity-based** and excludes Airspace Lounge (SAN) and JAL Sakura Lounge (HNL).

### FAQ

#### Does American Airlines offer a military discount?
American Airlines offers military **benefits** rather than a flat fare discount: free checked bags, Group 1 priority boarding, and complimentary Admirals Club access for active-duty members. It does not publish a percentage off its own airfare.

#### How much is the American Airlines military discount on flights?
American does not publish a percentage discount on airfare. It mentions "government or military fares in some markets" and directs you to call Reservations. The often-cited "10%" applies to the **land portion of American Airlines Vacations packages** through WeSalute+, not to flights.

#### How many free checked bags do military members get?
Active U.S. military and dependents traveling **on orders** get up to **5 bags at 100 lbs each free**; active-duty on **personal/leisure** travel get up to **3 bags at 50 lbs each free**, on American-operated itineraries.

#### Do dependents, veterans, or National Guard qualify?
**Dependents on orders** qualify with a Military Dependent ID plus a copy of active orders. For the **free-bag benefit**, American's definition **excludes** Reserves, National Guard, Civilian Military ID, and U.S. Public Health Service. **Veterans** aren't listed for the bag/boarding perks but can use the WeSalute+ AA Vacations 10%.

#### How do I verify my military status?
For bags, boarding, and the lounge there's no code or third-party verifier — show your **military ID** at the airport (dependents also show a Dependent ID and orders; the lounge requires being in **uniform**). The vacations 10% requires a **WeSalute+** membership.

#### Does American Airlines use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For its on-airport perks, American uses **no verification provider** — just your military ID. For the **10% AA Vacations** discount it uses **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage).

#### Can active-duty military use the Admirals Club for free?
Yes — U.S. military **in uniform** with a military ID and a same-day American-operated boarding pass can enter most Admirals Club locations (capacity permitting; excludes Airspace Lounge SAN and JAL Sakura Lounge HNL) and may bring immediate family or up to 2 guests.

#### Is the discount good on partner/codeshare flights?
No. Free checked bags apply only on **American marketed and operated** itineraries, not on codeshare flights operated by partner airlines.

#### Does American Airlines offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
American's military benefits page lists only military programs. First responder, nurse, teacher, and student discounts are **not listed** among American's offers.
