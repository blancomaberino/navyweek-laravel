# Hyatt — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **1-night stay at a U.S. Hyatt Place at a $200/night "Hyatt Standard Rate"** (pre-tax; representative select-service rate — hotel rates vary wildly by market/date, so treat percentages, not dollars, as the durable output).

## Headline finding

Hyatt runs a **real, official Veteran and Military Rate** bookable directly on hyatt.com: **10–15% off the Hyatt Standard Rate** at participating U.S. hotels, booked with **Special Offer Code `MILVET`**, verified by **a valid U.S. Military/Veteran ID or DD-214 at check-in** (no ID.me/SheerID — it's an honor-style rate confirmed at the front desk). Crucially, it is a **flexible/refundable rate that still earns World of Hyatt points and elite-night credit** — so it behaves like the standard rate, just cheaper. Separately, Hyatt offers a **Government rate** (per-diem-style) for people **traveling on official government business** (government employees + U.S. military), confirmed with a **government ID at check-in**, bookable up to **two rooms** (distinct ID per room). This is a classic **Archetype I (travel/loyalty — status and points stack, but the rates are mutually exclusive)**: you don't stack MILVET against the AAA/member/advance-purchase rates — you **price-shop them and book the lowest**, then the single highest-leverage move is to layer **Rakuten cashback (3% base, as of Dec 2025; up to 15% in past promos)** on top of whichever cash rate you book, because that's the one thing that genuinely stacks. The overlooked long-game play is the **Chase World of Hyatt card's annual free night (Cat 1–4)** with its **$95 fee waived for active duty/spouses under the MLA** — often worth more than any single-stay percentage.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The discounted rates are mutually exclusive.** MILVET is "not valid in conjunction with… other rates, promotions or offers." You **cannot** combine MILVET with the Government rate, AAA rate, Member rate, or Advance Purchase — you pick **one** rate per booking. Price-shop, don't stack. *(Archetype I: rates don't stack.)*
2. **Points and elite status DO stack on top of the rate.** MILVET, Government, Member, AAA, and Senior are all **Eligible Rates** that earn World of Hyatt points + elite-night credit. Your loyalty status (and its perks) applies to whatever eligible rate you book. *(Archetype I: status stacks.)*
3. **Rakuten cashback genuinely stacks** with the booked cash rate — it's a portal rebate on the transaction, not a coupon code, so it doesn't violate the "no other offers" rate rule, and (unlike an ID.me-gated checkout) it does **not** break tracking. **3% as of Dec 2025**; **cash stays only** — no cashback on any portion paid with points; must originate the click at Rakuten. Re-stamp the rate before relying on it.
4. **Elite-night credit caps at one room** even when you book the 2-room maximum on the MILVET/Government rate.
5. **The Chase World of Hyatt free-night certificate is a separate benefit**, not a stack — it replaces the room charge entirely (Cat 1–4). The **MLA fee waiver** (active duty/spouse) is the value unlock.
6. **The Inclusive Collection (all-inclusive) offer is a separate program** with its own code/terms — not the same as the `MILVET` hotel rate; don't assume one code works at the other.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military (any U.S. branch, in good standing)** — Eligible for the MILVET rate; also the Government rate when on official business.
- **Veterans** — Eligible for the MILVET rate (leisure or otherwise) with a Veteran ID or DD-214.
- **Military retirees** — Eligible under the veteran/active-service umbrella (retiree ID / DD-214).
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible as members of an official U.S. armed-services branch (present valid ID).
- **Immediate family** — Eligible on the MILVET rate: **parents, children/legal wards, spouses, domestic partners** (rate is non-transferable beyond immediate family).
- **Government employees (federal/state/county/local)** — Eligible for the **Government rate** when traveling on government business.
- **State-funded higher-ed & health-care employees** — Listed as eligible for the **Government rate** on official travel.
- **First responders / nurses / teachers / students** — **Not** covered by Hyatt's military or government rate; no separate Hyatt program found this pass.

### How To Redeem — Veteran & Military Rate (online)
1. Go to **hyatt.com**, enter your hotel, dates, and rooms.
2. Open **Special Rates** and enter Special Offer Code **`MILVET`** (or call the Global Contact Center and ask for the Veteran and Military Rate).
3. Book the room (up to **2 rooms** per stay). The rate is **refundable/flexible** per the rate rules shown — confirm the cancellation policy on your specific booking.
4. At **check-in**, present a **valid U.S. Military/Veteran ID or DD-214** (or other documented proof of service). Immediate family traveling with — or on behalf of — the service member/veteran may use it.
5. For cashback, start at **Rakuten → World of Hyatt** before you book to earn **3%** (as of Dec 2025); you still earn full World of Hyatt points and elite credit.

### How To Redeem — Government Rate (online, official travel)
1. On **hyatt.com**, open **Special Rates** and select **"Government."**
2. Book up to **2 rooms** under one name (a **distinct valid government ID is required for each room** at check-in).
3. At check-in, present a **valid government/military ID**; the rate is for travel on **government work-related business**.

### How Verification Works

### FAQ

#### Does Hyatt offer a military discount?
Yes. Hyatt offers a **Veteran and Military Rate of 10–15% off the Hyatt Standard Rate** at participating U.S. hotels, booked on hyatt.com with Special Offer Code **`MILVET`** and verified with a **Military/Veteran ID or DD-214 at check-in**.

#### How much is the Hyatt military discount?
**10% to 15% off** the Hyatt Standard Rate — the exact figure varies by property and date. It's a flexible, refundable rate that still earns World of Hyatt points and elite-night credit.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. It covers **veterans, active-duty members of any U.S. branch, and their immediate family** — defined as parents, children/legal wards, spouses, and domestic partners. You don't have to be on active orders to book it.

#### What's the difference between the Hyatt Government rate and the Military rate?
The **Government rate** is a per-diem-style rate for people **traveling on official government business** (government employees and military), selected via "Government" at booking. The **Veteran & Military (MILVET) rate** is for veterans/active members/family for **any** travel, including leisure. They're separate rates — book whichever fits, not both.

#### How do I verify my status — does Hyatt use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For the **core hotel MILVET rate, none** — you book with the code and show a **Military/Veteran ID or DD-214 at check-in**. ID.me/WeSalute listings you see for "Hyatt" are for the **separate Hyatt Inclusive Collection** all-inclusive resorts.

#### Is the military rate refundable, and does it earn points?
Yes on both — it's a **flexible/refundable** rate (confirm the exact policy on your booking) and it **earns World of Hyatt points and elite-night credit**, so your status and perks still apply.

#### Can I combine the military rate with AAA, member rates, or promo codes?
No. Hyatt's discounted rates are **mutually exclusive** — MILVET can't combine with the AAA, Member, Government, or Advance Purchase rates. Compare them and book the lowest. The one thing that stacks is **Rakuten cashback (3%)** on the cash booking.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Hyatt?
If you're **on official orders**, the **Government rate** is usually deepest. For leisure, **compare MILVET, AAA, and the Member Advance Purchase rate** on your dates and book the lowest, always **through Rakuten** for 3% back. For recurring value, the **Chase World of Hyatt free annual night** (fee $0 for active duty via MLA) beats most single-stay discounts.

#### Does Hyatt run a special Veterans Day or Memorial Day discount?
Not that we found — the **MILVET rate is evergreen (year-round)**, not a holiday promo. Hyatt launched it around Veterans Day 2018; we found no documented *extra* holiday discount on top of it. Re-check before each holiday.

#### Does Hyatt offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No dedicated Hyatt program for those groups was found this pass. The Government rate can cover state-funded higher-ed and health-care employees **on official travel**, but that's not a general first-responder/teacher/nurse discount.
