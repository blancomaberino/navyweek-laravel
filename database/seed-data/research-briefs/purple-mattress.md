# Purple — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Purple Mattress® (Original), Queen — $1,499 MSRP**, pre-tax (Purple's flagship "mattress in a box," the most-searched model; price per purple.com, cross-checked at retailers).

## Headline finding

Purple (purple.com) runs a **real, brand-endorsed 10% military discount, verified through SheerID** — stated on Purple's own Military Discount page. It covers **active-duty and reservist military, veterans, retirees, military spouses, and registered dependents**, and Purple runs **parallel 10% programs** for **first responders, healthcare professionals, students & teachers, and truck drivers** — each verified the same way. You verify once, get a **single-use promo code**, and apply it at checkout. That's the honest everyday answer.

But 10% is **rarely the best price**, and here's why: the code is **full-price-only and "cannot be combined with other offers,"** while Purple is a mattress brand that **discounts constantly** — Memorial Day 2026 ran **~25% off** select models, and holiday events routinely beat 10%. So the counterintuitive verdict is that during a real sale a verified service member should usually **skip the military code and buy the public sale price** (then add ~2% Rakuten cash back on the auto-applied sale, which is portal-safe where the SheerID code is not). Veterans buying a big-ticket bed should also **price the AAFES Exchange** (shopmyexchange.com stocks Purple) for the **tax-free** angle. Archetype: **C — real discount, but the sale usually beats it**, with a secondary **L — exchange-carried tax-free path**. Do **not** repeat the aggregator claim that Purple uses **ID.me** (it's **SheerID**) or that there's a flat **"20% off" military code** (that's a public sale figure, not a military rate).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 10% is non-combinable and full-price-only.** Purple states it "**cannot be combined with other offers**" and makes "**no price adjustments on previous purchases**." It won't layer on a sitewide sale, another code, or a past order — so during any real sale you **pick one**, and the sale usually wins.
2. **The military/first-responder/healthcare/teacher/trucker programs are mutually exclusive single codes** — you verify one eligibility and get one single-use code; they don't stack on each other.
3. **Rakuten only credits cash back on Rakuten-found codes.** A **SheerID single-use military code is not a Rakuten code**, and identity-gated redemptions typically break portal tracking — so **"10% military + Rakuten" should not be assumed to stack.** A **public auto-applied sale** (no code) *is* portal-safe, so **sale + 2% Rakuten** is the combinable play. (Rate as of Jul 10, 2026 — re-check.)
4. **The AAFES Exchange is a closed channel** — its price is the price; no purple.com code, sale, or portal stacks on top. Its value is **no sales tax + free shipping**, offset by an **exchange-only** return policy (no cash refund; comfort-level exchange within 120 days).
5. **The 10% covers merchandise only** — not shipping & handling, taxes, or packaging.

### FAQ

#### Does Purple offer a military discount?
Yes. Purple offers a **10% military discount** on purple.com, verified through **SheerID**, for active-duty and reservist military, veterans, retirees, spouses, and registered dependents.

#### How much is the Purple military discount?
**10% off** any Purple order, on **full-price merchandise only**. It's the only military figure Purple publishes — the "20% off" and "$500 off" numbers on coupon sites are public **sales**, not a military rate.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Purple names "active and reservist military, retirees, veterans, military spouses and registered dependents" as eligible via SheerID.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify through **SheerID** on Purple's discount page — click **Verify Eligibility**; most people are approved instantly, otherwise by email within about 20 minutes. You then get a single-use code.

#### Does Purple use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The named partner is **SheerID**. There is **no confirmed ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute** route for Purple — treat those aggregator claims as unverified.

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?
It works online at purple.com and, per Purple's terms, at **participating retail stores** with your eligibility card presented at checkout. Confirm in-store participation with the specific store.

#### What is excluded?
It's a single, **non-combinable, full-price-only** discount — no sale items, no retroactive adjustments, and it applies to merchandise only (not shipping, tax, or packaging).

#### Can I combine it with a promo code or a sale?
No. Purple states it "**cannot be combined with other offers**" and applies to full-price items only. Because Purple sales are frequent and often exceed 10%, the **public sale usually beats the military code** — use whichever is lower.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Purple mattress?
During a sale (almost always near a holiday), buy the **public sale price + ~2% Rakuten** — it beats the 10% and the two can't combine. In a rare no-sale window, use the **SheerID 10%** (about **$1,349 on the $1,499 Original Queen**). If you're an eligible veteran buying big-ticket, price the **tax-free AAFES Exchange**.

#### Does Purple offer a first responder, healthcare, teacher, or trucker discount?
Yes — Purple runs separate **10%** programs for first responders, healthcare professionals, students & teachers, and truck drivers, each verified through SheerID.

#### Does Purple run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Purple runs public holiday sales (Memorial Day 2026 was ~25% off select models; July 4 up to $800 off a mattress+base), but no documented **military-only** holiday promo. Watch the public sales — they usually beat the 10% code.

#### Can I buy Purple at the Exchange?
Yes — Purple is listed on **shopmyexchange.com** (AAFES), which is **tax-free with free shipping** for eligible shoppers. Note the Exchange's return policy is **exchange-only** (no cash refund) under a 120-day comfort guarantee.
