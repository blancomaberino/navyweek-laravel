# Ashley (Ashley Furniture / Ashley HomeStore) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Ashley offers an everyday **5% off** military discount, **online only** through www.ashley.com, applied via a promo code issued after you verify your status with **SheerID**. The offer is valid for U.S. **Active Duty Military, Military Family, Reservist or National Guard Members, and Military Veterans or Retirees**. The single most important caveat: it is **online-only at the corporate level and cannot be combined with any other discount or code**, with broad exclusions (premium mattress brands, Ashley Luxe, clearance/closeouts, floor models, protection plans, sales tax, shipping, and In-Home Delivery). Note that Ashley HomeStore locations are largely **independently owned and operated by licensees**, so any in-store military pricing is set by individual stores and is not governed by this corporate online offer — verify locally. Ashley runs parallel SheerID-verified programs for **first responders** and **healthcare/medical professionals** as well.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Ashley's Military Discount page at www.ashleyfurniture.com/c/deals/discount-programs/military-discount/.
2. Start the SheerID verification and confirm your military status.
3. If registration issues occur, use sheerid.com/help/contact.
4. Receive the promo code provided upon successful validation.
5. Shop at www.ashley.com and apply the promo code at checkout (it cannot be combined with any other discount or code).

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Ashley's corporate military offer is online-only; an in-store version is not stated in the official offer.
2. Most Ashley HomeStore showrooms are independently owned and operated by licensees, and pricing varies by store.
3. Contact your local Ashley store before visiting to ask whether they honor any military discount and what proof they require.

### How Verification Works
Ashley verifies military status through **SheerID**. After you complete SheerID verification on Ashley's military discount page, you receive a single-use promo code to apply at checkout. Per Ashley's terms, "Additional codes granted 30 days after previous validation," meaning re-verification is needed to obtain another code after that window. For registration problems, Ashley directs users to www.sheerid.com/help/contact. Ashley's broader discount programs are run "in collaboration with SheerID." (Note: some third-party/aggregator pages reference ID.me for Ashley — the official military page names SheerID, not ID.me.)

### Exclusions And Fine Print

### FAQ

#### Does Ashley Furniture offer a military discount?
Yes. Ashley offers an everyday 5% off military discount online at www.ashley.com after you verify your status through SheerID.

#### How much is the Ashley military discount?
5% off upon validation, applied via a promo code at checkout on www.ashley.com. Aggregator claims of 10% or more are not supported by Ashley's official military page.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and military families qualify?
Yes. Ashley's offer is valid for U.S. Active Duty Military, Military Family, Reservists or National Guard members, and Military Veterans or Retirees. Ashley lists "Military Family" but does not spell out which family members qualify; SheerID confirms eligibility during verification.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through SheerID on Ashley's military discount page. After verification you receive a promo code. For registration issues, Ashley points to sheerid.com/help/contact.

#### Does Ashley use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Ashley's official military page uses **SheerID**. Some third-party coupon and shopping sites list Ashley under ID.me or WeSalute, but the brand's own page names SheerID for the online military offer.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Ashley's corporate military offer is online-only. Most Ashley HomeStore showrooms are independently owned and operated, so any in-store military pricing is set by the individual store — call your local store to ask.

#### What is excluded?
The discount excludes Ashley Luxe, Event Featured items, premium mattress brands (Nectar, Beautyrest Black, Purple, Tempur-Pedic, Stearns & Foster, certain Sealy and Serta lines), clearance/closeouts, floor models, protection plans, warranty, service charges, sales tax, shipping, In-Home Delivery, and handling. Previous purchases are excluded.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Ashley states the military discount cannot be combined with any other discount or code.

#### How often can I get a code?
Ashley grants additional codes 30 days after your previous validation, so you re-verify to get a new code after that window.

#### Does Ashley offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Ashley runs separate SheerID-verified programs for first responders and for healthcare/medical professionals. A teacher, government, or student discount is not stated on Ashley's military page; check Ashley's discount-programs hub for current offers.
