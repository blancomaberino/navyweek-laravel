# WeatherTech Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**WeatherTech does not offer a military or veteran discount.** Its official FAQ states plainly: *"Do you offer a military discount? We do not currently offer a military discount."* WeatherTech also explicitly disclaims third-party coupons: *"WeatherTech does not offer or honor any coupons or discounts through any third-party coupon/discount sites."* There is **no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute** military program — ID.me's own page confirms, *"We're not aware that Weathertech offers Military discounts."* The honest, citable finding for this page is the **absence** of a discount, plus a warning that aggregator codes (e.g., "SAVEMORE," "15% off," "25% off," "first responder 50% off") are **not honored by WeatherTech** and should not be relied on. The single most important caveat: any site claiming a live WeatherTech military code is contradicted by WeatherTech's own published policy.

> Accuracy note: Coupon aggregators (WorthEPenny, ValueCom, Goodshop, DontPayFull, TenereTeam, etc.) advertise "WeatherTech military discounts," promo codes like **SAVEMORE**, and percentages from 15% to 75% off. **None are confirmed by WeatherTech, and WeatherTech's FAQ states it does not honor third-party coupon-site codes at all.** Treat every such claim as unverified/false. Do not publish any code or percentage as a working WeatherTech military offer.

> Confusion trap (flag): GovX lists discounted **event tickets** to **"WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca"** — that is a stadium *naming-rights sponsorship*, not a discount on WeatherTech-brand auto products. Separately, **"3Wliners"** is a *different* aftermarket floor-liner brand that runs its own GovX/first-responder program; it is **not** WeatherTech. Do not conflate either with a WeatherTech product discount.
