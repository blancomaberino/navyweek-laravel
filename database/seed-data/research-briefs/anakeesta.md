# Anakeesta — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **family-of-four single-day general admission** at Anakeesta (Gatlinburg, TN) — **2 Adults + 2 Children**. Official advance (online) price = 2 × $39.99 + 2 × $27.99 = **$135.96** pre-tax (anakeesta.com/buy-now). A single **Adult general admission = $39.99 advance** is the per-person unit.

## Headline finding

Anakeesta has a **real, officially published military discount: 10% off single-day general admission for the ID holder and immediate family** — but it is **in-person only, at the Guest Services ticket booth (576 Parkway, Gatlinburg), and explicitly cannot be used online**. Anakeesta states this on its own FAQ. There is **no verification provider** — you show a valid active/retired military ID at the booth; there is no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute checkout for this brand. The discount is **not combinable** with any other discount (AAA, Local, promo codes) and does not apply to annual passes, adventure add-ons, or in-park purchases.

The honest twist for savings: because Anakeesta already **discounts advance online tickets by about $2/ticket** and the **10% military discount only exists at the gate**, the military discount's real edge over just booking online is **small** — roughly $6 on a family of four. And for many families the **cheapest verified path isn't the military discount at all** — authorized third-party resellers (Tripster, Undercover Tourist, Ticketing For Less) frequently sell the same e-tickets **below** Anakeesta's own online price (e.g. ~$34.98/adult via Tripster's ANAKEESTA10, as of July 10, 2026). Archetype: **E — single-tier identity discount, no stacking game** (the published discount basically *is* the direct-site savings path), with a strong **G-flavor trap** (in-person-only redemption — buying online forfeits it) and an **H/J debunk job** (aggregators invent flat "20–75% off military" codes and an online ID.me code that Anakeesta's policy contradicts). The best move depends entirely on whether you value the small gate discount + skipping-the-line-versus-not, or the lower reseller price.

5. **AAA (10%) and Sevier County Locals (20%)** are separate on-site discounts under the same "one discount only" rule — a local resident, not the military discount, is the deepest standing on-site option if eligible.
