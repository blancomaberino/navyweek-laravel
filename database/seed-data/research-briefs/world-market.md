# Cost Plus World Market — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $150 home/decor order (pre-tax).

## Headline finding

Cost Plus World Market has **no year-round, everyday military or veteran discount** — there is no ID.me/SheerID checkout gate and no standing "show your ID for X% off" program. What it *does* run is a **seasonal Veterans Day promotion**, historically **~25% off an entire in-store purchase** for active duty, veterans, Reserve, and military spouses (with military ID, typically a ~4-day window around Nov 10–13, **alcohol excluded, in-store only**), and in some years a **20% off veteran offer**. Outside that window, the best savings come from the **free World Market Rewards program** (15% welcome offer, member coupons, 10% off store pickup), the **World Market credit card**, and frequent **public sales**. Archetype: **holiday-only military promo + strong free loyalty program.** The honest verdict: don't wait at the register expecting an everyday discount — join Rewards for the standing 15%-class savings, and plan bigger buys around the Veterans Day event or a public sale.

### FAQ
#### Does Cost Plus World Market offer a military discount?
Not year-round. It runs a **seasonal Veterans Day** in-store promotion (historically ~25% off) and, some years, a ~20% veteran offer. There's no everyday military discount.

#### How much is the World Market military discount?
Historically about **25% off an entire in-store purchase** during the Veterans Day event (alcohol excluded), and ~20% in some veteran-specific offers. Verify each year — it changes.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
During the Veterans Day promo, **active duty, veterans, Reserve, and military spouses** qualify with military ID.

#### How do I verify my status?
By showing a **physical military ID at the register** during the event — there's no online verification.

#### Does World Market use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No — World Market has no online military verification. The military benefit is an in-store, ID-checked seasonal event.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The Veterans Day military promo is historically **in-store only**. World Market Rewards works both online and in store year-round.

#### What is excluded?
The Veterans Day promo **excludes alcohol** and is in-store only; it's not combinable with other coupons. Terms change annually.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The military promo is generally **one coupon per transaction** and not stackable with other codes. Rewards coupons can apply to sale items per their terms.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from World Market?
If it's the **Veterans Day window**, shop in store with your ID. Otherwise, join **free World Market Rewards** and stack a **member coupon on a sale**.

#### Does World Market offer a first responder or teacher discount?
World Market has referenced separate promos for **first responders, healthcare workers, and K-12 teachers** in some campaigns — check when live; these are not the military event.
