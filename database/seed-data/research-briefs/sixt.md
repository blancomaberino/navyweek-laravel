# SIXT rent a car Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

SIXT rent a car offers a military and veteran car rental discount of **up to 15% off** the daily base rate, available to active members and veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces. Eligibility is verified by **GoCertify** (not ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute) at `secure.gocertify.me/at/sixt/military`; reservations must be made through that GoCertify-linked flow for the discount to apply, and SIXT states no coupon code is needed because the discount is applied automatically once you book through the offer page. Eligible groups are active duty and veterans across the Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard, and the Army/Air National Guard Reserve. The discount is delivered online only (rental cars are not a "checkout coupon" — there is no in-store register redemption), and the single most important caveat is that the offer applies to the **daily base rate only**, with taxes, fees, surcharges, extras (protection packages, GPS, child seats), one-way fees, and blackout periods excluded. SIXT's own military offer-terms page also explicitly warns that an incorrect discount is occasionally applied due to a website issue and instructs renters to contact SIXT directly to resolve it.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to SIXT's military page (sixt.com/rental-services/military-car-rental/) and select "Book discount."
2. You'll be taken to GoCertify (secure.gocertify.me/at/sixt/military). Verify your military or veteran employment/status there.
3. After verifying, continue to SIXT's booking flow from the offer page, choose your pickup location and dates (rental length 3–27 days), and pick your vehicle category.
4. The discounted daily base rate is applied automatically — no coupon code is entered. Confirmed pricing already includes the discount.
5. At pickup, bring your accepted military/veteran ID and meet SIXT's age, driver, and credit requirements.

### How To Redeem In Store
SIXT does not offer a register/in-store redemption for this discount. The offer is booked online through the GoCertify-linked SIXT flow. At the rental counter you present your valid driver's license, accepted military/veteran ID, and payment to collect a reservation already booked at the discounted rate. (No in-store coupon mechanic is stated in the official source.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by GoCertify, an independent verification service. You verify your status before booking, on the GoCertify offer page. SIXT's page does not state whether a SIXT account is required, nor does it state a re-verification frequency, so do not assume "verify once forever" — GoCertify-style services commonly re-verify per session or periodically. Accepted IDs for the verification/pickup include: a U.S. Armed Forces ID (Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard) or an Army/Air National Guard Reserve ID; for veterans, a driver's license with veteran designation, a state veterans ID card, or the VA Veteran Health Identification Card (VIC). If verification fails or you have questions about eligibility, GoCertify provides a Support request form (secure.gocertify.me/at/sixt/military/support_requests/new). For questions about which IDs are accepted, SIXT advises contacting the specific military branch before booking. If you notice an incorrect discount was applied (SIXT's offer-terms page flags this as an occasional website issue), contact SIXT directly to resolve it.

### FAQ

#### Does SIXT offer a military discount?
Yes. SIXT rent a car offers a military and veteran discount of up to 15% off the daily base rate, verified through GoCertify. (Source: sixt.com/rental-services/military-car-rental/, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the SIXT military discount?

#### Do veterans qualify, and what ID is accepted?
Yes, veterans qualify. SIXT accepts a driver's license with a veteran designation, a state veterans ID card, or the VA Veteran Health Identification Card (VIC). (Source: sixt.com military page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Do spouses or dependents qualify?
Not stated in SIXT's official source. The stated eligibility is active members of the U.S. Armed Forces and veterans, plus National Guard Reserve. Spouses, dependents, retirees, and surviving spouses are not addressed. (Source: GoCertify SIXT military FAQs, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How do I verify my status?
You verify through GoCertify on the SIXT military offer page before booking, using a U.S. Armed Forces or National Guard Reserve ID (or a veteran ID for veterans). GoCertify offers a Support form if verification fails. (Source: secure.gocertify.me/at/sixt/military and /faqs, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does SIXT use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. SIXT uses GoCertify for military/veteran verification. (Source: sixt.com military page → secure.gocertify.me/at/sixt/military, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I use the SIXT military discount in stores or at the counter?
There is no in-store/counter coupon for this offer. You book online through the GoCertify-linked SIXT flow, where the discounted rate is applied automatically, then present your ID at pickup. (Source: sixt.com military page; GoCertify offer terms, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### What's excluded from the SIXT military discount?
It applies to the daily base rate only — taxes, fees, surcharges, and extras (protection packages, GPS, child seats) are excluded. Rentals must be 3–27 days, blackout periods may apply, and it can't be combined with other promotions or corporate rates. #### Can I combine the discount with other promo codes or corporate rates?
No. The SIXT/GoCertify program terms state the offer may not be used with other promotions or corporate rates. (Source: SIXT First Responder GoCertify campaign T&C as program-standard wording; confirm on SIXT's post-verification terms, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does SIXT offer a first responder, medical, senior, or student discount?
Yes — SIXT runs separate offers for First Responders (up to 15%, via GoCertify), medical workers, and seniors, plus a young-driver/student page. Each has its own dedicated SIXT page and terms. (Source: sixt.com military page footer links; sixt.com/campaign/15-gocertify-firstresponders/, accessed June 23, 2026.)
