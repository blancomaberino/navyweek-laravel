# LongHorn Steakhouse — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$60 dinner for two** (two entrées + one shared appetizer), dine-in, pre-tax — a realistic LongHorn check that every savings path is measured against.

## Headline finding

LongHorn Steakhouse (a **Darden Restaurants, Inc.** brand) publishes **no official, company-wide everyday military or veteran discount**. Darden's own verification partner, **ID.me, explicitly states it is "not aware that LongHorn Steakhouse offers Military discounts,"** and Darden has no all-locations military policy. The widely-copied **"10% (or 10–15%) military discount"** on coupon and even reputable military-listing sites is **location/manager discretion at company-owned restaurants — reported, not brand-confirmed** — so it is a "your mileage may vary" benefit you must confirm at your specific LongHorn, never a guarantee. The **real, recurring, documented** military offer is LongHorn's **annual Veterans Day promotion** (historically **10% off the meal + a free appetizer or dessert** for the table on Nov 11, with proof of service, dine-in/call-in). For everyday value that anyone can actually count on, the **LongHorn eClub free-appetizer welcome coupon** and the **seasonal Darden gift-card bonus (buy $500, get a $50 bonus card ≈ 9% off)** beat chasing an unverified register discount. Archetype: **H (the "myth" brand — aggregators assert a flat everyday % the brand never confirms; the real value is a holiday promo + a loyalty/eClub wallet)**, with a secondary **J (unofficial / manager's-discretion in-store discount)**.

### FAQ

#### Does LongHorn Steakhouse offer a military discount?
Not as an official, company-wide everyday policy. Darden's discount partner ID.me states it isn't aware of a LongHorn military discount. Some individual locations may give roughly 10% off at manager discretion — ask first. The one recurring, documented military offer is LongHorn's **Veterans Day** promotion.

#### How much is the LongHorn military discount?
There is **no published everyday percentage**. Aggregators cite ~10% (some say up to 15%), but that's a location courtesy, not a brand rate. On **Veterans Day**, LongHorn has recently offered **10% off the meal plus a free appetizer or dessert** for the table.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and active duty qualify?
For the **Veterans Day** offer, yes — active duty, military retirees, and veterans, with proof of service (military ID, VA card, or DD214). There's no confirmed everyday discount for any group.

#### How do I verify my military status?
There's no online verification partner. On Veterans Day (or for any location courtesy discount), you simply **show proof of service** — a military ID, VA card, or DD214 — at the restaurant.

#### Does LongHorn use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. LongHorn/Darden uses none of these for dine-in discounts, and ID.me itself notes it isn't aware of a LongHorn military discount. Any "military code" for LongHorn online is not real.

#### Can I use a military discount online or only in the restaurant?
Any military-related offer is **in restaurant** (dine-in or call-in). There is no online military discount or code for LongHorn.

#### What's excluded?
Any everyday discount is discretionary and can be declined; the Veterans Day offer is dine-in/call-in, proof-of-service, typically not combinable with other coupons, and usually excludes alcohol. eClub welcome coupons are one-per-email with an entrée purchase.

#### Can I combine a military discount with coupons or the free-appetizer offer?
No — treat them as either/or. A discretionary or Veterans Day discount generally won't stack with other coupons or the eClub free-appetizer voucher; use whichever saves more.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at LongHorn?
Join the **eClub** for a free appetizer (works any day), **ask your location** about a military courtesy discount, and save the **Veterans Day** promo (10% + free appetizer/dessert) for Nov 11. If you buy Darden gift cards, time a **$500→$50 bonus** event for ~9% off.

#### Does LongHorn offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No documented program for any of these groups. Watch for eClub and public seasonal offers instead.

#### Does LongHorn run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day deal?
**Veterans Day, yes** — recently 10% off + a free appetizer or dessert with proof of service (dine-in/call-in; varies by location). No LongHorn-specific Memorial Day military deal is documented — verify each year.
