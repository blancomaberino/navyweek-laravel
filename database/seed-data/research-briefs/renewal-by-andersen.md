# Renewal by Andersen — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic whole-home replacement project — ~8 windows at roughly $12,000 before any discount (Renewal by Andersen sells installed, consultation-quoted projects, not a fixed cart, so treat this as an illustrative order).

## Headline finding

Yes — Renewal by Andersen (RbA, the full-service replacement-window-and-door division of Andersen) runs a real, official, **evergreen $300 off your entire purchase** for military, first responders, and teachers, verified through **SheerID** (or by showing proof of service at your in-home consultation). Critically, RbA's own page states the military discount **"applies after all other discounts, no minimum purchase required"** — so it **stacks on top of** whatever seasonal windows/doors promotion is running. That makes the archetype clear: this is a **big-ticket, consultation-quoted home-improvement lead-gen** brand. The $300 is a guaranteed flat add-on, but the real money is in (a) timing your project to a live seasonal promo (frequently "40%-off + additional dollars off with 4+ units"), and (b) getting competing quotes to negotiate the base price — the single largest lever on a five-figure job. There is no cashback portal, no military exchange channel, and no online checkout to hunt; GovX merely re-lists the same $300 offer as a lead form.
