# Navy Federal Credit Union — Military Credit Card Benefits & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 19, 2026 · **Baseline "cart" for all math:** one **Visa Signature® Flagship Rewards card held for one year ($49 annual fee)** — the only NFCU personal card with a fee, and the card the "is the fee waived for military?" question is really about — with the $0-fee lineup priced alongside.

> This is a **military-focused-issuer page, not a percent-off discount page.** Navy Federal doesn't run a "military discount" — the whole credit union is membership-gated to the military community, and its cards compete on structurally low costs (six of seven personal cards at $0 annual fee, no foreign-transaction or balance-transfer fees on personal cards, APRs that "currently don't exceed 18%"). The page's job is the **honest map**: what NFCU actually gives military members — and what it does NOT. The big "not": **NFCU does not waive credit card annual fees under the MLA for cards opened during active duty**, unlike Amex, Chase, Citi, U.S. Bank, and Bank of America. Its SCRA relief (a 4% cap, better than the federal 6%) applies **only to accounts opened before active duty**.

## Headline finding

Navy Federal is the **USAA pattern applied to credit cards: membership IS the discount.** Anyone in the military community — all branches (including Guard and Space Force), **all veterans regardless of service length**, DoD civilians/contractors/retirees, and immediate family down to grandchildren — can join (15.3 million members, $197.1B in assets as of December 31, 2025), and every member gets the same low-cost card lineup: $0-fee cards earning up to 2–3x, an 18% APR ceiling, and no foreign-transaction fees anywhere. The one fee in the lineup, the Flagship's $49, is **self-liquidating** right now via the Amazon Prime statement credit ($139/yr value) plus a $120 Global Entry/TSA PreCheck credit every 4 years. But the honest caveat this page must own: **active-duty members opening cards during service get no MLA fee waiver** — NFCU's military relief is SCRA-only (pre-service accounts, capped at 4% with account fees waived, on request). Archetype: **multi-tier identity program (E) — the membership gate is the product — with a statutory-relief (SCRA) secondary** like the Amex page, except NFCU's statutory story is the inverse of Amex's (better-than-required SCRA, nothing extra under MLA).

### FAQ

#### Does Navy Federal offer a military discount on credit cards?
Not in the coupon sense — membership itself is gated to the military community, and the value is structural: $0 annual fees on six of seven personal cards, no foreign-transaction fees, and APRs that currently don't exceed 18%.

#### Does Navy Federal waive credit card annual fees for active duty (MLA)?
**No.** Unlike Amex, Chase, Citi, U.S. Bank, and Bank of America, Navy Federal does not adjust annual fees under the Military Lending Act for cards opened during service. The $49 Flagship fee stands — though the card's Amazon Prime credit ($139/yr) more than covers it.

#### What SCRA benefits does Navy Federal offer?
For accounts opened **before** active duty: interest capped at **4%** (below the federal 6% requirement) and account fees waived. You must request it — eMessage, fax, mail, or branch — no later than 180 days after active duty ends.

#### Who can join Navy Federal?
All branches of the military (including National Guard and Space Force), all veterans regardless of service length, DoD employees/contractors/retirees/annuitants, and immediate family: spouses, siblings, parents, children, grandparents, and grandchildren. A $5 minimum savings balance establishes membership; there's no fee.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Yes — NFCU's join page says "no matter how long you served, you're in!" and publishes no discharge-type gate (unlike USAA's Honorable/General-Under-Honorable requirement).

#### Which Navy Federal card is best for military members?
For everyday spend: More Rewards Amex (3x supermarkets, gas, transit, restaurants; $0 fee) or cashRewards Plus (flat 2% with a $5,000+ limit; $0 fee). For travel: the $49 Flagship (3x travel, Prime and Global Entry credits). For carrying a balance: Platinum (10.24%–18.00%, $0 fee, no balance-transfer fees).

#### What is the Flagship card's Amazon Prime benefit?
A statement credit covering the Amazon Prime **annual** membership ($139 value) when you pay it with the Flagship card — limit one credit per card account per year, posting in 6–8 weeks. It alone outweighs the $49 fee.

#### What is the Senior Enlisted Reward Program?
An E-7–E-9 (Active Duty/Guard/Reserve) program: a 1% rate discount on many consumer loans, a $0 intro annual fee on the Flagship for the first year ($49 after), 0% intro APR offers, and a $250 loyalty card on a first mortgage or equity close. Retirees, warrant officers, and spouses are excluded. Enroll at 1-877-720-7121.

#### Does Navy Federal use ID.me or SheerID?
No. NFCU verifies military/DoD/family eligibility itself when you join, and SCRA requests are documented directly with NFCU.

#### Is there a Navy Federal promo code?
No. Credit-union products have no coupon field; every "Navy Federal promo code" published by coupon sites is fabricated.

#### What other military banking perks does Navy Federal offer?
Free Active Duty Checking (military pay one business day early, up to $20/statement in ATM rebates, free military checks), a 0.25% APR discount on select loans for active-duty and retired members with direct deposit, VA and Military Choice home loans with 100% financing options, and emergency relief lending (over $350 million in shutdown loans to 82,000 members in 2025).

#### What if I'm not eligible for Navy Federal?
PenFed Credit Union serves everyone (no military tie required), and the Amex/Chase MLA fee waivers are tied to active-duty status rather than any credit union's field of membership.
