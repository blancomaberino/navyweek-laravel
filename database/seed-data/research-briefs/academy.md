# Academy Sports + Outdoors Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Academy Sports + Outdoors offers a **10% military, veteran, and first-responder discount on your entire purchase**, but it is **seasonal/event-based, not a standing everyday offer**. The current promotion runs **April 26, 2026 – July 4, 2026**, online and in store. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me** (online, applied automatically at checkout after sign-in to a myAcademy account; in store, by presenting a valid Military or First Responder ID, or an ID.me-generated barcode). Academy's own help page states plainly that "Academy doesn't run a promotion for all of the segments the entire year" — so outside an active window, verified shoppers will see no discount. The single most important caveat: this is a **limited-time offer with broad exclusions** (YETI products, firearms-adjacent items per terms, clearance, Hot Deals, gift cards, and more), it **cannot be combined** with other offers or a price match, and it can change or end at Academy's sole discretion.

> Accuracy note: Some aggregators describe Academy's discount as a permanent/standing "10% military discount" and at least one claims SheerID verification. Both are inaccurate per Academy's official source: the discount is **seasonal** (Academy explicitly says it does not run year-round), and Academy's official pages name **ID.me** as the verification provider, not SheerID. Coupon sites advertising "25% off" codes are unrelated promo codes, not the military discount. Verified against academy.com as of June 23, 2026.

If you've verified but don't see a discount, the most likely reason per Academy's own help page is that **no promotion is currently running** for your segment — the discount is seasonal, not year-round. ### FAQ

#### Does Academy Sports offer a military discount?
Yes, but it's seasonal — not a year-round discount. Academy offers 10% off your entire purchase during set promotional windows. The current window runs April 26 – July 4, 2026, online and in store.

#### How much is the Academy military discount?
10% off your entire purchase during an active promotional window, on eligible items.

#### Is the Academy military discount available all year?
No. Academy's own help page states it "doesn't run a promotion for all of the segments the entire year." If you're verified but see no discount, it's most likely because no promotion is currently active.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Retired and discharged veterans (honorable, under honorable condition, or general discharge) qualify; dishonorably discharged do not. Immediate family — spouses and dependent children up to 18 — are also eligible.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me. Online, sign in to a myAcademy account and verify with ID.me; the discount applies automatically at checkout. In store, verify with ID.me for a barcode and present it with a valid Military or First Responder ID.

#### Does Academy use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Academy's official pages name **ID.me**. (Some third-party sites mention SheerID — that is not supported by Academy's official source.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Present your ID.me barcode and a valid Military or First Responder ID at checkout, and the 10% applies to eligible items.

#### What is excluded?
YETI products, deer corn, clearance, Hot Deals, licenses, services, gift cards, sales tax, shipping & handling, exchanges, store credit, and previous purchases. Additional exclusions and restrictions may apply, and it can't be combined with other offers or a price match.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Academy's terms state it cannot be combined with a price match or with other offers, discounts, or coupons, and it doesn't apply to clearance or Hot Deals.

#### Does Academy offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
First responders (active police, fire, EMT) are covered by the same seasonal military offer. Teachers and school staff have a separate Academy seasonal discount. Nurses, students, and government employees are not named under these offers.
