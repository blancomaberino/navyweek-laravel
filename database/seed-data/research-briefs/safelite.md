# Safelite AutoGlass Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Safelite AutoGlass offers a **10% military/veteran discount** via promo code **`10THANKYOU10`**, confirmed in Safelite's own help center. The same code and discount also covers AAA members, AARP members, and first responders — it is a shared "thank you" discount, not a military-exclusive program. Eligibility is **self-applied at booking** (enter the code when scheduling on safelite.com) and **verified at the time of your appointment**; Safelite does not publish which documents satisfy verification, and it does **not** use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID for this offer. The discount is valid on any product or service **except recalibration**, and **cannot be applied to insurance claims or deductibles**. The single most important caveat: most windshield work is covered by auto insurance, and this discount does **not** apply to insurance jobs — so it mainly benefits customers paying out of pocket.

- **Military spouses / dependents / surviving spouses** — Not stated in official source.
- **First responders** — eligible (named by Safelite). (Source: safelite.com/help-center/cost)

- **AARP members** — eligible (named by Safelite).
- **Medical workers / teachers / government employees / students** — Not stated in official source (no dedicated discount published).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to safelite.com and start a quote / schedule a service for your vehicle.
2. Enter promo code **`10THANKYOU10`** when prompted (or use Safelite's direct link: safelite.com/schedule-service?start_type=fmg&promo=10THANKYOU10).
3. Confirm the 10% discount appears on your quote before booking. The code can't be combined with other Safelite promo codes.
4. Be ready to **verify your eligibility at the time of your appointment** (bring proof of your military/AAA/AARP/first-responder status).

### How To Redeem In Store
Safelite does not publish separate in-store/phone redemption steps for this discount. The official instruction is to book with the code on safelite.com and verify eligibility at the appointment. If you booked without the code or need help, Safelite says you can call **800-800-2727** or ask your technician at service completion for assistance. (Source: safelite.com/help-center/cost) — *In-store-only application is not confirmed by the official source; flag as unverified.*

### How Verification Works
You apply the code yourself at booking; **Safelite verifies your eligibility at the time of your appointment.** Safelite does **not** name a verification provider and does **not** list which documents it accepts. It does not use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID for this discount (ID.me's own page states it is not aware Safelite offers a military discount and lists only cash back). Bring whatever proof of status you have to your appointment, and contact Safelite directly if you're unsure what qualifies. (Source: safelite.com/help-center/cost; shop.id.me/discounts/safelite/military)

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Recalibration** is excluded — the discount is valid on any product or service *other than* recalibration. (Source: safelite.com/help-center/cost)
- **Insurance claims and deductibles** are excluded — the discount cannot be applied to either. (Source: safelite.com/help-center/cost)
- **No stacking** — multiple promotional discounts cannot be added to one order, so `10THANKYOU10` won't combine with the general $45/$15 coupons. (Source: safelite.com/coupons)
- **Eligibility must be verified at the appointment.** (Source: safelite.com/help-center/cost)
- **Caps, expiration, and commercial/fleet rules:** Safelite's coupon terms note general offers aren't valid on commercial/fleet services; the help-center military entry does not state an expiration or a per-order/annual cap — Not stated in official source.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **General public coupons:** $45 off replacement (`WEBSL45`) or $15 off repair (`WEB15SL`) when scheduling on safelite.com. If one of these saves more than 10%, it may beat the military code — but you can't use both. (Source: safelite.com/coupons)
- **Insurance:** Safelite notes windshield repair/replacement is often fully covered by insurance and they file the claim for you. Out-of-pocket is where the military discount helps; insured jobs may cost you little or nothing regardless. (Source: safelite.com/help-center/cost)
- **ID.me cash back (separate from the discount):** ID.me Shop lists cash back on Safelite (up to ~$6 on replacements, up to ~$1 on repairs) for ID.me members generally — this is affiliate cash back, not a military discount. (Source: shop.id.me/stores/2784-safelite-autoglass)
- **Competitor note:** Glass.com and many local/independent auto-glass shops also serve military customers; terms vary by shop. Verify any competitor claim with the shop directly.
