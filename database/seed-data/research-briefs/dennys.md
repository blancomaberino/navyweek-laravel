# Denny's — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** breakfast for two at a sit-down Denny's — two **Original Grand Slam** entrées (~$11 each) + two coffees (~$3 each) = **~$28 pre-tax** at standard menu price. (Denny's does not publish national prices; entrée prices vary by franchise/market, so treat this as a representative diner check, not a fixed number.)

## Headline finding

Denny's has **no published, chain-wide everyday military discount.** Denny's is heavily franchised, and any everyday "military discount" is **discretionary at the individual location** — some franchises grant roughly **10–20% off** if you show a military ID, many grant nothing, and the amount is never guaranteed. Aggregator claims of a flat "10% (or 20%) off for military, always" are **not an official Denny's policy** — treat them as YMMV, and call the specific restaurant ahead. What Denny's *does* run officially and reliably is a **long-standing Veterans Day free-meal promotion**: on **November 11, 2025**, active-duty and retired military got a **free Original Grand Slam**, dine-in only, 5 a.m.–noon, at participating locations, with a valid military ID or DD 214 (documented history back through 2024/2023 — **treat as historical, never as a promised future offer**). The **real everyday savings for everyone** — including service members on the other 364 days — are the **$5 Slams value menu** ("5 Slams starting at $5, never over $10") and **Denny's Rewards** (Booth Rewards / BoothBucks, a free birthday Everyday Value Slam, and a welcome offer). Archetype: **J (unofficial / franchisee-discretion in-store discount)** dominant, **+ H (the "myth" brand — the everyday value is the loyalty wallet + value menu, not a blanket military %)**, with a genuine recurring **Veterans Day free-meal promo** on top.

## Stacking Rules (verified where stated)
1. **Veterans Day free meal "cannot be combined with any other offers"** (Denny's release) — it's standalone, one free Grand Slam per eligible diner, dine-in only.
2. **A discretionary in-store military discount does not combine** with the free Veterans Day meal, coupons, or Rewards redemptions — treat it as an either/or, and it only exists where the franchise chooses to offer it.
3. **Denny's Rewards** BoothBucks accrue on spend; a redeemed Booth Reward / birthday Slam is a free item and won't also earn a discount on the same item. Value-menu pricing is menu pricing (no ID needed) and isn't a "discount" that stacks.
4. **No portal/marketplace stacking exists** — dine-in restaurant meals have no GovX storefront, no cashback portal, no exchange/ExpertVoice channel to layer.

### FAQ

#### Does Denny's offer a military discount?
Not as an official, chain-wide program. Denny's is largely franchised, so **any everyday military discount is up to the individual location** — some offer roughly 10–20% off with a military ID, many offer nothing. The one official military offer is the **Veterans Day free meal**. Call your local Denny's to confirm an everyday discount.

#### How much is the Denny's military discount?
There is **no published amount**. Aggregators cite 10–20%, but that's franchise-discretionary and not guaranteed. Denny's itself publishes no everyday military percentage.

#### Does Denny's give a Veterans Day free meal?
**Yes — historically, every year.** On Veterans Day 2025 (Nov 11), active-duty and retired military could get a **free Original Grand Slam**, dine-in only, 5 a.m.–noon, at participating locations, with a valid military ID or DD 214. It's an annual tradition (38,000+ meals in 2024) but can change — check the current-year announcement and confirm your local restaurant participates.

#### Who qualifies for the Veterans Day free meal, and what ID do I need?
Active-duty and retired military. Bring a **valid military ID or DD 214** (some locations also accept a VA card or a veteran-designated driver's license). Dine-in only; non-military guests pay for their own meals.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify for the Veterans Day meal with proper ID. **Spouse and dependent eligibility is not stated** in Denny's Veterans Day offer — don't assume it; ask your location.

#### Does Denny's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Denny's has no first-party verification partner. WeSalute lists Denny's but explicitly says it's **not** in its partner coalition and **can't guarantee** the offer — verification is just showing a military ID in person, at the franchise's discretion.

#### Can I use a military discount online or only in the restaurant?
**In restaurant only.** There's no online or delivery military discount, and the Veterans Day free meal is dine-in only.

#### Can I combine the military discount with coupons, the value menu, or Rewards?
No. The **Veterans Day free meal can't be combined with any other offer.** A discretionary in-store military discount also won't stack with coupons or Rewards redemptions — it's one or the other.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at Denny's?
On a normal day, order the **$5 Slams value menu** (meals from $5, never over $10) and use **Denny's Rewards** (birthday Everyday Value Slam + BoothBucks) — that beats an uncertain discretionary discount. On **Veterans Day**, the **free Original Grand Slam** is the best deal.

#### Does Denny's offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
**No** documented program for any of these. Not stated in any official Denny's source.
