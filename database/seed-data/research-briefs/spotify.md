# Spotify Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Spotify does not offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** As of June 23, 2026, Spotify's official Premium plans page lists only four paid tiers — Individual ($12.99/mo), Duo ($18.99/mo), Family ($21.99/mo), and Student ($6.99/mo) — plus a free ad-supported tier. The only identity-verified discount Spotify runs is **Premium Student**, which uses **SheerID** to confirm enrollment at an accredited US higher-education institution; military status is not an eligibility path. Spotify's own community forum confirms it has repeatedly **closed** military/veteran/first-responder discount requests as out of scope ("no pricing, subscription, or content related requests"). The honest, useful finding for service members: there is no military rate, but real ways to pay less are the **free tier**, the **3-month $0 intro** on Premium Individual (new customers), splitting **Duo/Family** with household members, **Student** pricing if you're enrolled, and bundled value (Student includes Hulu).

> Accuracy note: Coupon/aggregator and "military discount guide" pages (WorthEPenny, OperationMilitaryKids, usmilitary.com, etc.) rank for "Spotify military discount" but **none point to an official Spotify military offer** — most explicitly conclude none exists. Treat any "Spotify military promo code" as invalid. Even ID.me hosts a "Does Spotify Have Military Discounts?" page, which is a marketing landing page, **not** evidence of a Spotify–ID.me partnership.

### How Verification Works
Spotify does not verify military status because it has no military offer. For its **Student** plan, Spotify uses a third-party service, **SheerID**, to confirm you're enrolled at an eligible US Title IV accredited college or university and are 18 or older. Student status must be re-verified every 12 months (up to 3 additional 12-month periods). There is no equivalent path that accepts a military ID, DD‑214, ID.me Military community, or GovX/WeSalute verification — those services are not used by Spotify for any service-based discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Spotify offer a military discount?
No. As of June 2026, Spotify has no military, veteran, or first-responder discount. Its plans page lists only Individual, Duo, Family, and Student tiers, plus a free ad-supported tier.

#### How much is the Spotify military discount?
There isn't one. Service members pay standard pricing: Premium Individual is $12.99/month in the US.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
No. Spotify offers no service-based discount, so veterans, spouses, dependents, and Gold Star families all pay standard rates.

#### Does Spotify offer a first responder, nurse, or teacher discount?
No. Spotify has confirmed in its community forum that it offers no first-responder or similar discount and has closed those requests. The only verified discount is Premium Student.

#### Does Spotify use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for military verification?
No. Spotify uses **SheerID** only for the **Student** plan (enrollment verification). It does not use ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for any military discount. ID.me's "Spotify military" landing page does not reflect an actual Spotify military offer.

#### Is there a Spotify military promo code?
No. Any "Spotify military discount code" from a coupon site is invalid — Spotify runs no such program.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to get Spotify Premium?
New customers can get Premium Individual free for 3 months (then $12.99/mo; offer ends July 6, 2026). Otherwise, splitting Premium Family (up to 6 accounts for $21.99/mo) is the lowest per-person cost, and enrolled students qualify for Premium Student at $6.99/month.

#### Will Spotify add a military discount?
There's no announced plan. Spotify has repeatedly closed community requests for a military discount as outside its Idea Exchange scope, so don't count on one.
