# Burton — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Burton Custom snowboard (2026)** — **$679.95 MSRP**, pre-tax (Burton's flagship all-mountain board; the price shown on burton.com and matched at retailers like evo).

## Headline finding

Burton (burton.com) runs a **real, first-party 20% military discount, verified through SheerID** — stated on Burton's own military-discount page. It covers **active-duty, veteran, and reservist personnel of the U.S. & Canadian Armed Forces** (Military.com's Burton listing also names **retirees and Reserve/National Guard**). You verify once through SheerID and get a **one-time-use code** you can re-request every **3 months**. It works on **burton.com only** — **not** in Burton Flagship or Outlet stores — is **not valid on previous purchases**, and **cannot be combined with other percent-off discounts**. That's the honest everyday answer, and for a current, full-price board it's the best standing route.

The catch is that Burton is a **seasonal-sale brand that discounts hard**: an **End-of-Season sale up to 40%** and a **Black Friday sale up to 50%**, plus a standing **Clearance/Outlet** channel, are routine. Because the 20% military code is **full-price-only and non-combinable**, the counterintuitive truth is that **during any sale of 20%+ you should skip your own code and buy the public sale price** — and since a plain sale order carries no coupon, a **cashback portal (Rakuten ~2% as of July 10, 2026)** stays intact on top, where it would likely void on a SheerID-code order. Archetype: **C — real discount, but the sale usually beats it** (non-combinable, full-price-only, deep frequent sales), with a secondary **E — multi-tier SheerID identity discount** (military, student, teacher, first responder, and healthcare all sit at the same flat 20%, with no code/cashback stacking game). Do **not** repeat the aggregator claims of "30%, 40%, even 50% military codes," the "up to 10%–25%" range, or that the code works in the **mobile app or retail stores** — the only verified military figure is **20%, online at burton.com**, via **SheerID** (not GovX, not ID.me).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military 20% is one discount** — Burton states it **"cannot be combined with other percent-off discounts"** and is **"not valid on previous purchases."** Treat it as a single order-level code.
2. **Military 20% does NOT stack on sale/clearance** — it's full-price-only in practice (non-combinable with other percent-off), so a live 20%+ sale and the code are **mutually exclusive**; pick the lower price.
3. **Sale price + cashback DOES work** — a public sale carries **no coupon code**, so **Rakuten (~2%)** tracks normally. This is the portal-safe stack. A **SheerID-code** order, by contrast, is exactly the kind of coupon/identity-gated checkout that commonly **voids or reduces** cashback — don't assume "military code + Rakuten."
4. **burton.com only** — the code is invalid at **Flagship and Outlet** stores and (per Burton's terms) not the retail/app channel some aggregators claim.
5. **One code per verification** — single-use, re-requestable every **3 months**; not a permanent account discount.
6. **The five SheerID tiers don't stack with each other** — military, student, teacher, first responder, and healthcare are separate 20% offers; you use one.
