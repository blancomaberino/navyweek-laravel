**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair Altra Lone Peak trail runners — **$140 MSRP** (representative current-line price), pre-tax.

## Headline finding

Altra Running (a division of **VF Outdoor, LLC**) has **no ID.me or SheerID "military discount" on altrarunning.com** — but it does route the military community to a real, brand-integrated program: **ExpertVoice**. Verified ExpertVoice members (which includes active military, veterans, and spouses under ExpertVoice's military eligibility) see their discount **applied automatically at checkout on altrarunning.com** with no promo code — and Altra's ExpertVoice group is listed as **"Altra VIP (40% off MSRP)."** A second official route is the **GovX marketplace** ("Altra" storefront + an "Altra Open Box" storefront); community reports put GovX Altra pricing around **25% off** for verified members. The everyday public offer is a **10% email-signup welcome code** (full-price, one-time). Archetype: **K (ExpertVoice pro-deal is the official military route)** + **A (GovX marketplace substitute)**. **Critical disambiguation:** the "20% GovX military/first-responder discount" widely indexed for "Altra" belongs to **Altra Arrows** — an unrelated **archery-arrow** company (The Outdoor Group) — **not** Altra Running shoes.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **ExpertVoice does not stack with anything** — Altra's Pro Program T&C: "Discount is not valid with any other offer, discount, coupon, promotion/promo code, or on prior purchases." It's an either/or vs. the public sale.
2. **If the public sale price is lower than your ExpertVoice price, you pay the sale price** — the on-site widget states this ("On sale now — lower than your ExpertVoice discount").
3. **ExpertVoice is personal + immediate-family use only, capped at $2,000/year, and all EV orders are final sale** (no returns/exchanges) — plan accordingly.
4. **GovX is a closed marketplace** — its code works only on GovX; no cashback or brand code stacks on top.
5. **The 10% welcome code is full-price, one-time, non-combinable** — it won't stack on sale items or with EV/GovX.
6. **Cashback on altrarunning.com** — not verified this pass; if a portal lists it, standard rules apply (off-portal/verified-gated codes typically void cashback). Verify the live rate before relying on it.

### FAQ

#### Does Altra Running offer a military discount?
Yes — through **ExpertVoice**, not an on-site ID.me/SheerID code. Verified military, veterans, and spouses get a discount **applied at checkout on altrarunning.com** (Altra's ExpertVoice group is listed at up to 40% off MSRP). There's also a **GovX "Altra"** marketplace.

#### How much is the Altra military discount?
Altra's ExpertVoice group is named **"Altra VIP (40% off MSRP)"** — up to ~40%, visible after verification. GovX pricing is gated; community reports ~25%. Exact depth depends on the item and your verification.

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Yes — ExpertVoice verifies veterans and includes spouses; Altra's EV terms permit purchases for the member's spouse and children. GovX also verifies veterans.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create an **ExpertVoice** account (military/veteran/spouse) or a **GovX** account. There's no verification step on altrarunning.com itself — the ExpertVoice discount applies once you're logged in and connected.

#### Does Altra use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or ExpertVoice?
**ExpertVoice** (the on-site route) and **GovX** (marketplace). No ID.me or SheerID military checkout on altrarunning.com.

#### Can I combine the discount with sales or promo codes?
No. The ExpertVoice discount **can't be combined** with any other offer, and if a public sale price is lower, the site charges you the sale price. The 10% welcome code is also non-combinable.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Altra?
Usually **ExpertVoice (up to ~40% off MSRP)** on a current, full-price model. But during a **30%+ public sale/clearance**, the public price can win — check both, since they don't stack. GovX Open Box is worth a look for returned pairs.

#### Isn't there a 20% Altra military discount via GovX?
That "20%" is **Altra Arrows** — an **archery-arrow** brand — not Altra Running shoes. For the shoe brand, the routes are ExpertVoice and the GovX "Altra"/"Altra Open Box" storefronts.
