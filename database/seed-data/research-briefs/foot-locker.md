## Headline finding

Foot Locker runs a **real, official, evergreen 10% military discount** on most online and in-store purchases, verified by **SheerID** — with hard caps: **$100 maximum discount per verification, four SheerID verifications per rolling 30 days**, no combining with other offers. But 10% is the floor, not the ceiling: Foot Locker's own **20% off $99+ email/SMS welcome code** beats it outright on discount-eligible items (one-time use), and Black Friday/Cyber Monday markdowns have historically run far deeper than 10%. Archetype: **C — real discount, but the sale/public code often beats it**, with a secondary **L — exchange-carried brand** angle (AAFES stocks Nike core styles tax-free). Foot Locker itself is also the classic *retailer-substitute* route for brands with no military discount of their own (e.g., Hoka, Jordan, Nike).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **The military 10% cannot be combined with other offers** — no promo codes, no welcome code, no student code on the same order (Foot Locker Military Discount FAQ, accessed July 4, 2026). Pick one per order.
2. **Nothing discounts new releases/launch products.** Foot Locker's release FAQ: "Can I use discount codes or coupons on new releases? — Nope, you can't." This covers the military discount too.
3. **Sale items + military 10% is NOT addressed in official terms.** A markdown is not an "offer/code," and Foot Locker's exclusion mechanism is item-level ("excluded from discount" on the product page) — but no official source states sale items are eligible. Present as "the checkout will show you"; never promise it. *(Not stated in official source.)*
4. **FLX layers on everything** — free standard shipping (no minimum, logged in) and 100 XPoints/$1 accrue regardless of which discount you use. But per Rakuten's terms, the **portion paid with FLX Cash earns no cashback**.
5. **Rakuten 2% + military discount is unreliable.** Rakuten's Foot Locker terms exclude marquee styles (Jordan Retro, Nike LeBron/Kyrie/Foamposite/Kobe/PG, NB 550) and portals commonly devalue/void tracking on orders using non-portal codes or identity-gated checkouts. Portal-safe play: no code at all, sale price only.
6. **Gift cards can't be bought with any discount code** (coupons-page restrictions). Whether the military discount can be *paid for* with gift cards: not stated in official source.
7. **Caps are per verification, not per year:** $100 max discount each time (= $1,000 of eligible spend at 10%), up to 4 verifications per rolling 30 days — so a big kit-out can capture up to $400/month in military savings by splitting orders.

### Who Qualifies

- **Veterans** — eligible (Foot Locker help center).
- **National Guard / Reservists** — eligible (Foot Locker help center).
- **Retirees** — SheerID's Foot Locker FAQ covers "Active and Retired (receiving retirement benefits) service members"; Foot Locker's own list names retirees only via their dependents. Verifiable in practice per SheerID.
- **Registered dependents of active-duty or retiree service members** — eligible (Foot Locker help center).
- **Military spouses** — not named as a standalone group; may fall under "registered dependents." *Not stated in official source.*
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — *not stated in official source.*
- **First responders, nurses, teachers, government employees** — not eligible; the program is military-only.
- **Students** — separate 20% off $99+ student discount (any current student, not military-specific).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add items to your cart and start checkout at footlocker.com.
2. In the **Payment** section, select **"military discount"** (Use Military Discount link) and complete the short SheerID form.
3. If prompted, upload official government-issued documentation showing your full name, branch of service, and current military status (.bmp, .gif, .jpg, .png, or .pdf).
4. SheerID verifies your status — up to about 20 minutes; watch your inbox.
5. Once approved, choose "military discount" at checkout and pay. The 10% applies up to the $100-per-verification cap.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Bring a valid photo/military ID.
2. Show it to the store associate at checkout; they apply the 10% to eligible items.

### How Verification Works
Foot Locker uses **SheerID** (not ID.me, not GovX). SheerID states you "only need to be successfully verified once," but Foot Locker's offer terms meter usage at four verifications per rolling 30 days — in practice, expect a quick SheerID pass per redemption window. If document verification fails, contact customerservice@sheerid.com; for order issues, Foot Locker customer service is 24/7 (800-991-6815).

- "Some product exclusions apply" — Foot Locker publishes **no itemized list**; excluded items are flagged "excluded from discount" on their product pages. Third-party SKU lists (specific Jordans, Kobes, etc.) are unconfirmed — don't rely on them.
- **New releases/launch products:** no discount codes or coupons, period.
- **Cannot be combined with other offers; not valid on previous purchases; not for gift-card purchases (per general coupon restrictions).**

### FAQ

#### Does Foot Locker offer a military discount?
Yes — 10% off most online and in-store purchases for verified military members, powered by SheerID.

#### How much is the Foot Locker military discount?
10% off most purchases, capped at a $100 maximum discount per verification (that's up to $1,000 in eligible spend), with up to four SheerID verifications per rolling 30 days.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify explicitly. Registered dependents of active-duty or retiree service members qualify. Spouses aren't named as a standalone group — many will qualify as registered dependents; SheerID makes the call.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online: select "military discount" in the Payment field at checkout and complete the SheerID form (you may upload a government-issued document; verification takes up to ~20 minutes). In store: show a valid photo/military ID at the register.

#### Does Foot Locker use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
SheerID only. Claims that you can "verify through GovX" are incorrect, and there is no Foot Locker GovX storefront.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — both. Online goes through SheerID at checkout; in store you show a valid photo/military ID.

#### What is excluded?
"Some product exclusions apply" (flagged per product page as "excluded from discount"), plus new releases/launch products, previous purchases, and gift cards. Foot Locker publishes no full SKU list.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Promo codes: no — it can't be combined with other offers. Sale items: Foot Locker's terms don't say; checkout will show whether the 10% applies to a marked-down item.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Foot Locker?
First order: the 20% off $99+ email/SMS welcome code beats the 10% (on discount-eligible items). Repeat orders at full price: the military 10%. Late November: Black Friday/Cyber Monday markdowns (historically 50%+) crush both.

#### Does Foot Locker offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No first responder/teacher/nurse program. Students get a separate 20% off $99+ discount.

#### Does the military discount work at Kids Foot Locker or Champs Sports?
Each sister banner runs its **own** identical 10% SheerID program (documented in kidsfootlocker.com's and champssports.com's help centers). Eastbay no longer exists — it closed January 13, 2023.

#### Can I buy the same sneakers tax-free at the Exchange?
AAFES's shopmyexchange.com carries core Nike styles including the Air Force 1 '07, tax-free for authorized patrons. All honorably discharged veterans can shop the Exchange online; in-store shopping requires broader base privileges.
