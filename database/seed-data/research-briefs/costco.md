# Costco Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Costco does not offer a military or veteran discount.** Membership fees and product prices are identical for everyone — Costco's own customer service has long stated it does not give military discounts on memberships or purchases, and there is no ID.me/SheerID/GovX-style military verification gate on Costco.com. Costco's official "My Life Military Family Savings" page is a **content/tips page** ("12 Ways to Save as a Military Family at Costco"), not a discount offer. What military shoppers can use is the **same new-member sign-up promotion available to the general public**: as of June 23, 2026, join as a new **Executive Member ($130/yr)** and get a **$40 Digital Costco Shop Card**, or a new **Gold Star Member ($65/yr)** and get a **$20 Digital Costco Shop Card**, after enrolling in auto-renewal on a Visa credit/debit or Mastercard debit card. The single most important caveat: **this promo is not military-specific** — it's open to any new member (or anyone who hasn't been a member in the last 18 months), so framing it as a "military discount" would be inaccurate.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator and "military discount" pages (military.com, WeSalute, GovX, sempersave, operationmilitarykids, etc.) list a "Costco military discount" and sometimes specific figures ("$20/$30/$40 Shop Card," "save $60"). The dollar figures track Costco's **general** new-member promo, which is **not gated by military status**. Treating the Shop Card as a *military* benefit is the recurring SERP error this page should correct. Only the general new-member promo is confirmed on Costco's official pages as of June 23, 2026.

### FAQ

#### Does Costco offer a military discount?
No. Costco does not offer a military or veteran discount on membership or on products. Military members pay the same prices as everyone else.

#### How much is the Costco military discount?
There isn't one. The dollar figures you may see ($20/$40 Shop Cards) come from Costco's general new-member promotion, which is open to all new members — not a military discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a Costco discount?
There is no Costco military discount for any group, including veterans, spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families. The new-member Shop Card promo is available to them only on the same terms as the general public.

#### Does Costco use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Costco.com does not use any military verification provider, because it has no military discount to gate. Third-party sites like GovX or WeSalute may list "Costco" but do not provide a discount on Costco membership itself.

#### What is the Costco new-member Shop Card offer?
As of June 23, 2026, new Executive members get a $40 Digital Costco Shop Card and new Gold Star members get a $20 card, after enrolling in auto-renewal on a Visa credit/debit or Mastercard debit card. It's open to any new member (or anyone not a member in the last 18 months) — not just military.

#### Can I use a Costco discount in the warehouse?
No military discount applies in-store or online. The new-member promo is available both in-warehouse and online when you sign up.

#### Is "Gold Star Member" a military Gold Star family benefit?
No. "Gold Star" is just the name of Costco's basic membership tier. It is unrelated to Gold Star military families and carries no military benefit.

#### Does Costco offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No. Costco does not publish a discount for first responders, teachers, nurses, government employees, or students either.

#### Where can military members get true warehouse-style savings?
On-base commissaries and military exchanges (AAFES, NEX, MCX) offer military-only pricing. Off base, the honest move at Costco is to take the general new-member Shop Card promo and, for heavy spenders, weigh the Executive 2% reward.
