# McDonald's — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one typical lunch order — a mid-tier sandwich combo (sandwich + medium fries + medium drink), ~$9–$11 à la carte at a U.S. non-base location.

## Headline finding

**McDonald's has no national military or veteran discount.** McDonald's corporate confirms only that "certain McDonald's locations *may* offer" a military/veteran discount and directs guests to contact individual stores — because 90%+ of U.S. restaurants are independently owned franchises, any discount is set by the local owner, varies store to store, and is not guaranteed. There is **no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass** program, no national code, and nothing in the McDonald's app for military. The one recurring, real benefit is **Veterans Day free meals**, but even those are run by regional franchise co-ops (e.g., Greater Houston), not corporate — participation and items differ by market and year. Archetype: **franchise-discretion QSR** (like most fast food). For a service member day to day, the genuine best path is **not a military discount at all — it's the McDonald's app: McValue bundles ($5 Meal Deal, under-$3 menu, $4 breakfast), Free Fries Friday, and MyMcDonald's Rewards (100 pts/$1)**, which beat the informal "10% if they honor it" a franchise might give.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **No national military discount exists to stack.** There is no code, no provider, no portal — so the only "stack" question is whether a *local* informal discount combines with app deals, and register-level courtesy discounts generally **do not** combine with McValue/app-priced items.
2. **Veterans Day free meals do not combine with the app** and are **dine-in or drive-thru only** (the Houston 2025 co-op offer explicitly excluded the app). It's the free meal *or* an app deal, not both.
3. **MyMcDonald's Rewards points accrue on paid orders**, including McValue purchases — so the real "stack" is *deal price now + points toward a future free item*. A $0 Veterans Day meal earns no points if given free.
4. **On-base / AAFES:** McDonald's is **not listed among AAFES's operated quick-serve concessions** (AAFES names Taco Bell, Burger King, Popeyes, Charleys, etc.). Where a McDonald's operates on an installation it is a franchise; whether it is sales-tax-exempt or applies exchange pricing is **unverified** and store-specific — do not promise tax-free McDonald's on base.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — eligible only where a local franchise chooses to offer a discount, or for Veterans Day meals at participating stores.
- **Veterans** — same: local/Veterans Day only; documentation (Military ID, VIC, DD‑214, VA card, or veteran-designated license) is up to the individual store.
- **Spouses / dependents** — no stated eligibility; entirely franchise discretion.
- **Nationally: no one qualifies** — because there is no national program.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online / app:** There is **no military discount online or in the app.** For everyday savings, open the McDonald's app → add McValue deals ($5 Meal Deal, under-$3 items, $4 breakfast) → order pickup or drive-thru → earn 100 points per $1.

**In store (any informal military courtesy):**
1. Order at the counter or drive-thru.
2. Mention you're military/a veteran and show your ID **before** you pay.
3. The store applies a discount only if that location offers one — many don't.

**In store (Veterans Day):**
1. Confirm your local store is participating (call ahead — it's regional).
2. On Nov 11, go in person (dine-in or drive-thru, not the app).
3. Show a valid Military/Veteran ID and claim the free meal offered at that location.

### How Verification Works
There is **no national verification.** McDonald's uses no third-party verifier (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass) for military status. Any check happens at the register, at the cashier's discretion, using whatever ID that store accepts. Do not expect an online account, a re-usable code, or a permanent status flag.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No national discount; availability, amount, and terms vary by location and time of year (McDonald's FAQ).
- Informal discounts and Veterans Day meals are **in-person only** — excluded from the app, McDelivery, and online ordering.
- Veterans Day free meals do not combine with app/McValue deals.
- Franchisees can start, stop, or change any offer without notice.
- On-base pricing/tax treatment is store-specific and unverified — not a stated benefit.

### FAQ

#### Does McDonald's offer a military discount?
Not nationally. McDonald's corporate says only that *some* locations *may* offer a military, veteran, senior, or teacher discount, and directs you to ask your local store. Because 90%+ of restaurants are independently owned franchises, there's no company-wide program.

#### How much is the McDonald's military discount?
There is no set amount. Where a franchise chooses to offer one, community reports mention around 10% off or a free drink/coffee — but it's unadvertised, inconsistent, and not a published McDonald's rate. Don't assume any specific percentage.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Only where a local store offers a discount, or for Veterans Day meals at participating locations. There's no national eligibility list, and documentation requirements are set by each store.

#### How do I verify my military status?
You don't — there's no national verification. Any check happens at the register with whatever ID the store accepts. McDonald's has no online military verification.

#### Does McDonald's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. McDonald's uses none of these for military discounts. Any offer is handled in person, store by store.

#### Can I use a military discount in stores or online?
In stores only, and only where offered. There is **no** military discount in the McDonald's app, on the website, or through McDelivery.

#### What is excluded?
Everything online/app; app/McValue deals don't combine with an in-store military courtesy; Veterans Day meals exclude the app and are dine-in/drive-thru only. Franchisees can change or drop offers anytime.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Generally no. A register-level military courtesy doesn't stack with McValue/app pricing, and a free Veterans Day meal can't be combined with app deals.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from McDonald's?
The **McDonald's app**: McValue's $5 Meal Deal, the under-$3 menu, $4 breakfast, Free Fries Friday, and banking MyMcDonald's Rewards points. For most orders this beats any informal military discount — and it's guaranteed nationwide.

#### Does McDonald's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No national program for any of these. The same FAQ lumps teachers and seniors in with military as "some locations may offer" — all franchise discretion, none guaranteed.

#### Does McDonald's run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Veterans Day, yes — regionally. Franchise co-ops (e.g., Greater Houston in 2025) have offered free breakfast or a free Extra Value Meal with a military ID on Nov 11, in person. It's market-by-market, not corporate, so confirm your local store each year. No national Memorial Day military offer is on record.

#### Can I buy McDonald's at the Exchange (AAFES) tax-free?
Not as a verified benefit. AAFES operates many on-base quick-serve brands but McDonald's isn't listed among them, and where a McDonald's sits on an installation it's a franchise — tax and pricing are store-specific and unconfirmed. Don't count on tax-free McDonald's on base.
