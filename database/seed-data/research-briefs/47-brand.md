# '47 (47 Brand) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $100 '47 headwear/apparel cart (two caps or a cap + tee) at list price.

## Headline finding

'47 does **not** run a brand-direct military discount. ID.me explicitly states it is "not aware that '47 offers Military discounts," and there's no ID.me/SheerID/VerifyPass program on 47brand.com. The real military path is a **marketplace one: '47 caps and apparel are sold through GovX**, the military/first-responder-gated retailer, at member pricing. On the brand's own site, the everyday levers are the **15% first-order email code** and the **'47 Family loyalty program** (free shipping on all orders, early sale access). So: no on-site military rate, but a legitimate GovX channel plus stackable public savings.
