# Ace Hardware Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Ace Hardware does **not** publish or guarantee a flat corporate military or veteran discount. Ace stores are **independently owned and operated**, so any percentage-off military discount (commonly cited as 10%, sometimes up to 20% near bases) is a **store-by-store decision, not a company-wide policy** — and it is **not stated on any official Ace corporate source**. The one genuinely Ace-supported, corporate-documented military benefit is the **Military Double Points Program**: participating Ace retailers can give active, reserve, retired, or disabled military personnel **double Ace Rewards points** on purchases, verified by a service ID card at the register. Participation in that program is also **optional per store**. The single most important caveat: there is **no official, uniform "Ace Hardware military discount" you can rely on anywhere** — you must confirm with your specific local store, and any percentage figure is in-store only (never on acehardware.com or the app).

> Accuracy note: Aggregator and editorial pages widely state a flat "10% off" (and "up to 20%") military/veteran discount. **No Ace corporate source confirms any specific percentage.** Treat all percentage figures as unverified, store-dependent claims — do not present them as an official Ace program. What IS corporately documented is the **Military Double Points Program** (double Ace Rewards points), via Ace's retailer support materials (Paladin POS knowledge base), not a percentage discount.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Call your local Ace ahead and ask whether they offer a military discount and/or the **Military Double Points** program, and what qualifies.
2. Bring valid proof of service — a Common Access Card (CAC), Veteran ID Card, or a state driver's license with a veteran designation.
3. If you're an Ace Rewards member, have your Rewards card or phone number ready (required for double points).
4. Show your military/veteran ID to the cashier at checkout and ask for the military benefit.
5. The cashier applies whatever the store offers (a percentage discount and/or double Ace Rewards points).

### How Verification Works
For the corporate-supported **Military Double Points Program**, Ace's retailer instructions are: the cashier asks for the customer's **service ID card** to verify active/reserve/retired/disabled status, scans the customer's Ace Rewards account, and scans a Military Double Points barcode to add the points. There is **no corporate online verification provider (ID.me / SheerID / GovX) mandated** by Ace for an in-store military discount — verification is a physical ID shown at the register, and exact accepted IDs are at the store's discretion. Because the program is optional per store, the only reliable "verification" that a discount exists at all is calling the store.

### FAQ

#### Does Ace Hardware offer a military discount?
There is no single company-wide Ace Hardware military discount. Ace stores are independently owned, so each store decides whether to offer one. Many do — commonly a percentage off in store and/or double Ace Rewards points — but it's not guaranteed. Call your local Ace to confirm.

#### How much is the Ace Hardware military discount?
Ace does not publish a percentage. Coupon sites often quote "10% off" (and "up to 20%" near bases), but **no official Ace source confirms any figure** — it's set store-by-store. The one corporately documented benefit is double Ace Rewards points (not a percentage), where the store participates.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
It depends on the store. Ace's corporate Military Double Points materials name active, reserve, retired, and disabled military; veterans, spouses, and dependents are commonly served at participating/veteran-owned stores but are **not stated** in the official corporate program — confirm locally.

#### How do I verify my status?
In store, show a military or veteran ID to the cashier — a CAC, Veteran ID Card, or a driver's license with a veteran designation. There's no official online verification (no ID.me/GovX/SheerID requirement set by Ace corporate) for an in-store discount.

#### Does Ace Hardware use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not as an official corporate program. Verification for the in-store benefit is a physical ID shown at the register. An ID.me Ace Hardware marketplace page exists, but its specific terms aren't confirmed and shouldn't be treated as an official Ace online discount.

#### Can I use the discount online or in the app?
No. Any military discount or double-points benefit is **in store only** at participating locations. It does not apply on acehardware.com or the Ace mobile app.

#### What is excluded?
Ace publishes no central exclusion list. Where a store offers a percentage discount, it's commonly limited to regular-priced, in-stock items and excludes sale/clearance — but that's a typical store practice, not an official Ace rule. Confirm with your store.

#### What is the Military Double Points Program?
It's an Ace-supported option that participating stores can offer: active, reserve, retired, or disabled military earn double Ace Rewards points (about 20 points per $1 instead of 10). The cashier verifies your service ID, scans your Ace Rewards account, and applies the double points.

#### Does Ace Hardware offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or senior discount?
Not as official corporate programs. Some independently owned stores offer first responder or other discounts at their own discretion, but Ace does not publish these centrally. Check with your local store.
