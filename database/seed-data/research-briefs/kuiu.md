> Research conducted June 24, 2026. All facts verified against primary sources (KUIU's own pages and GovX, its verification provider). ## Executive Summary

KUIU offers a **10% discount** to U.S. military, retirees, veterans, and active first responders, available **every day, online only at KUIU.com**. Eligibility is verified through **GovX** (GOVX ID), which the shopper completes by signing in to or creating a GovX account from KUIU's cart/checkout (or the "Military/First Responders" link in the account menu). Verification is tied to the KUIU account, so the discount applies on each subsequent login. The single most important caveat: the discount **cannot be combined with any other promotion or discount**, and it **excludes select New Releases, 3rd-party gear, outlet items, previous purchases, and Gift Certificates**. KUIU's own pages state the offer is **U.S. only, online at KUIU.com** — KUIU does not confirm in-store redemption, despite a third-party aggregator claiming otherwise.

KUIU runs a year-round Military/First Responders discount of **10% off**, available to U.S. active-duty military, retirees, veterans, and active first responders. ### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to KUIU.com and create an account or log in.
2. In the account menu, click the "Military/First Responders" link (or proceed to Cart/Checkout, where the GovX verification option appears).
3. Sign in to your GovX (GOVX ID) account, or create one if you don't have it, and complete verification.
4. Once verified, the 10% discount is applied to your KUIU account and applies each time you log in. Confirm the discount is reflected before placing your order.

### How To Redeem In Store
KUIU operates physical retail stores (Dallas, Denver, Dixon, Houston, San Antonio), but KUIU's official military-program page states the discount is "valid in US only **online at KUIU.com**." KUIU does **not** confirm that the GovX military/first-responder discount can be used in its stores. One third-party aggregator (Military.com) lists "Online & in store," but this is not confirmed by KUIU. Do not present in-store redemption as fact until KUIU states it.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **GovX (GOVX ID)**. You start it from KUIU's cart/checkout or the "Military/First Responders" link in your account menu, then sign in to or create a GovX account to get instantly verified before placing your order. GovX stores the verification on the GOVX ID account, and the discount is tied to your KUIU account so it applies on future logins. KUIU does not publish a stated re-verification interval; do not promise "verify once, forever." If automatic verification fails, use GovX's support (support.govxinc.com) or KUIU's Help Center (kuiu.com/pages/get-support).

### FAQ

#### Does KUIU offer a military discount?
Yes. KUIU offers a 10% discount to U.S. military, retirees, veterans, and active first responders, available every day, online at KUIU.com. (KUIU)

#### How much is the KUIU military discount?
10% off eligible items. (KUIU help article and military-program page)

#### Do veterans, retirees, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify per KUIU's pages. KUIU's GovX store listing also names "their dependents," and GovX verifies military spouses and children (18–24); KUIU's own help article names active-duty, veterans, and first responders specifically. Confirm your status during GovX verification at checkout.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through GovX (GOVX ID). Start from KUIU's cart/checkout or the "Military/First Responders" link in your account menu, then sign in to or create a GovX account to get verified. (KUIU)

#### Does KUIU use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
KUIU's stated military/first-responder verification partner is **GovX**. (KUIU help article) A KUIU store page also exists on ID.me Shop, but KUIU's own help center names GovX as the verification method for the 10% program.

#### Can I use the discount in KUIU stores?
KUIU's official page states the discount is valid in the U.S. only online at KUIU.com. KUIU does not confirm in-store use of the military discount. (KUIU)

#### What is excluded?
Select New Releases by KUIU, 3rd-party gear, outlet items, previous purchases, and Gift Certificates. The discount also cannot be combined with other promotions or discounts. (KUIU)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. KUIU states the discount may not be used in conjunction with any other promotions or discounts. (KUIU)

#### Is the KUIU military discount available outside the U.S.?
No. It is for U.S. customers only; international users are not eligible. (KUIU)

#### Does KUIU offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
First responders are included in KUIU's 10% Military/First Responders program. KUIU's help center does not describe separate teacher, nurse, or government discounts under this program; ID.me Shop lists separate KUIU nurse/medical/teacher/government landing pages, but offer specifics there are not confirmed on KUIU's own pages.
