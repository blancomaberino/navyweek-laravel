# SOREL — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of SOREL Caribou boots at a ~$160 list price (the brand's signature winter boot, the everyday unit most readers compare).

## Headline finding

**SOREL (US) has no first-party military or veteran discount.** SOREL's own US Help Center answers the question "Do you offer military discounts?", and **ID.me confirms it is not aware of any SOREL military offer** ("We're not aware that Sorel offers Military discounts"). There is no SOREL military checkout on sorel.com, no ID.me military tier, and no GovX SOREL military storefront. Archetype: **B (no military route on the brand's own site) — the honest job is to route shoppers to real savings that DO exist.**

Two traps to correct: (1) A **"15% off new collection + extra 10% off reduced items"** SOREL offer does circulate via GoCertify/SheerID — but its accepted IDs are **"R.A.F, Army, Navy or Armed Forces Dependents,"** i.e., it's a **UK offer, not a US one.** US shoppers should not expect it. (2) SOREL's **parent company, Columbia Sportswear, does run a US military discount (10% via ID.me) on columbia.com** — but that's the Columbia brand's site, not sorel.com, and doesn't apply to SOREL products.

So the genuine best-savings path for SOREL boots in the US is **retailer substitution + sale-stacking**: buy SOREL at a retailer that *does* offer a verified military discount (REI Co-op, and others via GovX-style verification), or ride SOREL's own **deep seasonal sales** (30%+ end-of-winter markdowns) plus a public signup code and a cashback portal.
