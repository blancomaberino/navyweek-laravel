# Hotels.com — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 3-night US leisure hotel stay at $150/night = **$450** (pre-tax room total).

## Headline finding

**Hotels.com (an Expedia Group brand) runs a real military discount through ID.me**: as of July 14, 2026, ID.me Shop's Hotels.com storefront lists **"10% off" for Military** (and identically for nurses, first responders, teachers, government employees, medical providers, and students). It is a **coupon-code discount on select hotel bookings**, unlocked by ID.me verification at redemption — not an automatic rate. Two honesty flags: (1) older sources — including Military.com and the sibling Expedia coupon's own terms page — put the value at **8%**, so the live percentage must be confirmed inside the ID.me flow; (2) like all Expedia Group coupons it applies to **select stand-alone stays**, and using the coupon **voids Rakuten cashback** (up to 3% as of July 14, 2026). Because Hotels.com's **One Key member prices** (10%+ on select hotels) are portal-safe and open to any free account, the member-price + cashback path frequently ties or beats the military coupon. Archetype: **I (travel/loyalty)** with **C flavor** (the public/member deal often beats the identity coupon, and they don't combine).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military coupon and cashback don't stack.** Rakuten's Hotels.com terms void cashback when a code not listed on Rakuten is used — the ID.me military coupon is exactly that. Pick coupon OR cashback.
2. **One coupon per booking; coupon vs member/promo pricing is effectively either/or.** Expedia Group coupon terms (documented on the sibling Expedia offer) restrict to select stand-alone prepaid bookings, first room only, not combinable with other offers — treat Hotels.com's flow the same and confirm in-cart.
3. **Complete the booking in the redemption session.** ID.me guidance: click through and finish the booking at code-redemption time, or the discount may not apply.
4. **One Key member prices are portal-safe** — they're logged-in site pricing, not codes, so Rakuten tracking survives; that's why member price + 3% often beats the coupon.
5. **OTA bookings usually forfeit hotel-chain loyalty points, elite credit, and chain military rates** — for chain hotels, compare booking direct before using any Hotels.com path.
6. **Taxes/fees are excluded from the discount** — Expedia Group coupons apply to the pre-tax room price.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Sign in / create your free Hotels.com (One Key) account — you'll want it anyway for member prices.
2. Reach the offer via ID.me Shop's Hotels.com page (or the "Verify with ID.me" prompt) and complete military verification.
3. Unlock the coupon and note the exact live percentage and terms shown.
4. **Complete your booking in that same session** — ID.me's guidance is to book at code-redemption time.
5. Before paying, compare: is the Member Price or a mobile-app deal on your hotel already ≥ the coupon? If so, book that through Rakuten instead and keep the cashback.

### How Verification Works
Hotels.com uses **ID.me**. You verify once (service-record lookup, .mil email, or document upload — most verifications clear in minutes) and your ID.me login then unlocks this and hundreds of other offers. The coupon itself is issued per redemption; expect to re-click through ID.me for each new booking.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Select properties only** — not every hotel accepts Expedia Group coupons; the eligible-property rule appears at checkout.
- Applies to the **pre-tax room price**; taxes and fees are excluded.
- Expedia Group's parallel coupon terms (documented June 2026) restricted to stand-alone prepaid bookings, first room only, with a dated redemption window and a dollar cap (8%, $40 max on Expedia) — **confirm Hotels.com's live terms in the flow; don't assume no cap**.
- Using the coupon voids portal cashback; one coupon per booking.
- OTA bookings generally don't earn hotel-chain points or honor chain military rates.

### FAQ
#### Does Hotels.com offer a military discount?
Yes — a coupon for verified military members through ID.me, listed at 10% off select hotel bookings on ID.me Shop (July 14, 2026).

#### How much is the Hotels.com military discount?
ID.me Shop lists 10% off. Historically the documented value was 8% (and Expedia's parallel coupon terms said 8% with a $40 cap), so confirm the exact live percentage and any cap when the coupon appears in your verification flow.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans yes, via ID.me. Family members are typically covered by ID.me's military community policy — confirm during verification, as Hotels.com's public pages don't spell it out.

#### Does Hotels.com use ID.me or SheerID?
ID.me. One verified ID.me account also unlocks the sibling Expedia offer and hundreds of other military discounts.

#### Can I combine it with cashback or other codes?
No. The coupon voids Rakuten cashback (per Rakuten's Hotels.com terms) and Expedia Group coupons don't combine with other offers. Pick the single deepest discount.

#### Why would I skip the military coupon?
When your hotel's One Key Member Price or a mobile-app deal is already 10%+ — those are portal-safe, so booking them through Rakuten (up to 3%) nets more than the coupon.

#### Is it better to book a chain hotel direct with a military rate?
Often, yes. Chain military rates booked direct earn points and elite credit that Hotels.com bookings forfeit — compare totals plus points value for Hilton/Marriott/IHG/Choice stays.

#### Do nurses, teachers, first responders, or students get the same Hotels.com discount?
Yes — ID.me Shop lists the same offer for nurses, first responders, teachers, government employees, medical providers, and students.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book on Hotels.com?
On a $450 stay: military coupon ≈ $405–$414; Member Price + Rakuten ≈ $392; an app-exclusive 25% deal ≈ $338. Check all three; book the lowest — they never combine.
