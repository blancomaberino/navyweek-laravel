## Headline finding

There is **no promo code and no retail discount**. Chase provides two statutory benefit tracks:

1. **MLA (accounts opened *during* covered service):** Chase checks applicants against the Department of Defense MLA database at account opening and, for covered borrowers — active duty, Guard/Reserve on 30+ day orders, and their **spouses/dependents** — **adjusts the annual fee to $0 on personal credit cards**, including the $795 Sapphire Reserve and its co-brand lineup (Southwest, United, Marriott, Hyatt, IHG, and more). It's **automatic**; Chase mails a benefits letter confirming the waiver. Business cards are excluded. This applies to credit-card accounts opened on or after **October 3, 2017** (when credit cards came under the MLA rule).
2. **SCRA (accounts opened *before* active duty):** federal law caps interest on pre-service obligations at **6%** with the excess **forgiven**; multiple military-finance outlets consistently report Chase voluntarily goes further — **4% APR on eligible balances, plus waived/credited fees (including annual fees), during active duty and for up to one year after** it ends. SCRA relief is **requested**, not automatic: secure message (SCRA dropdown), mail, or **Chase Military Services 1-877-469-0110** (1-318-340-3308 overseas).

**Veterans and retirees not on active duty get no ongoing card fee waiver** — community reports say Chase re-checks MLA status after separation and fees return, typically within a year or two. The one benefit that explicitly *does* include veterans, on chase.com itself: the **$25 monthly service fee on Chase Premier Plus Checking is waived** for "current servicemembers and veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces."

Archetype: **regulated-benefit / statutory-relief brand** (same family as American Express, USAA, Navy Federal). The "discount" is compliance with — and voluntary extension of — federal law; eligibility is legally defined; the make-or-break variable is the **account-open date** (before vs. during covered service → SCRA vs. MLA).

**Verdict logic:**

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **SCRA and MLA never stack on one account** — the account-open date picks exactly one. SCRA reaches obligations "incurred… before" service (50 U.S.C. § 3937); MLA governs credit extended to an already-covered borrower.
2. **MLA is automatic; SCRA must be requested.** Chase's own SCRA page documents a request process; the MLA check is run by Chase at account opening. Never tell readers SCRA self-applies, and never tell them the MLA waiver needs a request.
3. **The waiver covers fees, not spending** — no percent-off at merchants, nothing to stack with portals, promos, or shopping codes.
4. **Card earnings are unaffected** — welcome bonus, Ultimate Rewards points, travel credits and lounge access work normally on a fee-waived card (editorial consensus; no Chase statement to the contrary).
5. **Business cards get no MLA fee waiver** (consumer credit only). A pre-service business account may still be claimable under statutory SCRA — Chase doesn't publish product scope; route readers to 1-877-469-0110.
6. **Per-borrower coverage:** spouses are covered borrowers in their own right, so a military couple can each hold their own fee-waived personal cards (reported; see Community).

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty members** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Space Force) — MLA on cards opened during service; SCRA on pre-service accounts.
- **National Guard / Reserve on active orders of 30+ days** — MLA covered borrowers while activated; Chase's SCRA page names "a member of the National Guard or reserve… called to active duty."
- **Military spouses and dependents** (DEERS-listed) — MLA-covered on their **own** personal cards; Chase screens by SSN against the DoD database.
- **Veterans / retirees not on active duty** — no ongoing card fee waiver; statutory SCRA claims close 180 days after release. Premier Plus Checking's $25/mo fee waiver **does** include veterans (with DD-214/VA ID or similar proof).
- **Business cards** — excluded from the MLA fee waiver.

### How To Redeem — MLA (new card while serving)
1. Apply for any **personal** Chase card as normal — there is no military application page and no code.
2. Chase checks your SSN against the Department of Defense MLA database at account opening (self-check at mla.dmdc.osd.mil if unsure; spouses are in the same system via DEERS).
3. If you're a covered borrower, the annual fee is **adjusted to $0 automatically** and Chase **mails a benefits letter** confirming it. Questions → Chase Military Services **1-877-469-0110**.

### How To Redeem — SCRA (card opened before active duty)
1. Log in at chase.com/securemessages → New Message → choose **"Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA)"** from the dropdown, and attach electronic copies of your active-duty orders (photos are discouraged as unclear).
2. Or mail: Chase, Attn: SCRA Request, PO Box 183240, Columbus, OH 43218-3240. Or call **1-877-469-0110** (M–F 8am–9pm ET; **1-318-340-3308** overseas).
3. Reported result: APR reduced to **4%** on eligible balances and fees (including annual fees) waived/credited, through active duty and up to one year after.
4. Statutory floor regardless of Chase's practice: 6% cap with excess interest **forgiven**; request within active duty or **180 days** after release.

### How Verification Works
- **MLA:** automatic — Chase queries DoD records; no third-party verifier (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute). The mailed letter is your proof.
- **SCRA:** manual — you submit orders/documentation; Chase Military Services reviews and applies relief. Coverage tracks DoD records; community reports say Chase re-checks after separation and fees resume — don't treat the waiver as permanent.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No coupon, code, or retail percent-off exists — anything claiming one is wrong.
- One relief per account, decided by the open date; they never stack.
- Business/corporate cards: no MLA fee waiver.
- Veterans/retirees outside active duty: standard card pricing; the 180-day SCRA window covers only the past active-duty period.
- The **4%, fee-waiver, and +1-year figures are Chase practice as reported, not published chase.com terms** — Chase can change them without notice; confirm on 1-877-469-0110.
- Card rewards, bonuses, and travel credits are unaffected by the waiver.

### FAQ
#### Does Chase offer a military discount?
Not a retail discount. Chase honors the MLA and SCRA: **$0 annual fees on personal cards** for active-duty members and spouses who open accounts while covered, and reported **4% APR plus fee relief** on accounts opened before active duty. No promo code exists.

#### Does Chase waive annual fees for military?
Yes — on **personal** cards opened while you're MLA-covered (active duty, Guard/Reserve on 30+ day orders, spouses/dependents), the annual fee is adjusted to **$0 automatically** and Chase mails a confirmation letter. Business cards are excluded. Chase doesn't publish this on chase.com; it's confirmed via the letters and consistent reporting.

#### Which Chase cards does the waiver cover?
Any personal Chase card, per consistent reporting — Sapphire Reserve ($795) and Sapphire Preferred, plus Southwest, United, Marriott, Hyatt, IHG and other co-brands, and reported authorized-user fees too. There's no published card list; the policy is card-agnostic across the personal lineup.

#### Do military spouses qualify?
Yes. Spouses (and dependents) are MLA covered borrowers in their own right — Chase checks the applicant's SSN against DoD records, so a spouse's own card gets its own $0 fee.

#### Do veterans or retirees qualify?
No ongoing card fee waiver — once you're no longer covered, fees return (community reports: within about 1–2 years as Chase re-checks records). Veterans **do** keep the Premier Plus Checking $25/mo fee waiver, and can claim statutory SCRA for their active-duty period within 180 days of release.

#### What is Chase's SCRA benefit?
Federal law caps pre-service interest at 6% with the excess forgiven. Chase is consistently reported to reduce eligible balances to **4% APR** and waive/credit fees, through active duty and up to one year after. Request it — it is not automatic — via secure message, mail, or 1-877-469-0110.

#### MLA vs. SCRA — which applies to me?
The **account-open date** decides. Opened before active duty → SCRA (request it). Opened while covered → MLA (automatic). They never stack on one account.

#### Does Chase use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
No. MLA verification is Chase checking Department of Defense records; SCRA is direct document review by Chase Military Services.

#### How do I contact Chase Military Services?
**1-877-469-0110** (Mon–Fri, 8am–9pm ET) or **1-318-340-3308** from overseas. SCRA documents: secure message (SCRA dropdown) or Chase, Attn: SCRA Request, PO Box 183240, Columbus, OH 43218-3240.

#### What's actually the best move for a servicemember?
If you're serving (or a military spouse) and want a premium travel card, **apply while covered** — the fee is $0 automatically for as long as you stay covered. If you held Chase cards before activating, **file SCRA** and let the reported 4% + fee relief work on the old accounts.
