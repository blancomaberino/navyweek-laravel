# CVS / CVS Pharmacy — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $40 front-store (over-the-counter / health & beauty) cart of full-price, non-excluded items on CVS.com. Effective prices exclude sales tax; membership costs are called out separately because they change the ranking.

## Headline finding

CVS has **no free, standing military discount at the register.** The only "CVS military discount" is a **20% off full-price items at CVS.com** offer, plus free shipping — and it is **gated behind a paid WeSalute+ membership** (formerly Veterans Advantage / VetRewards). The 20% is real and first-party (CVS publishes the terms on cvs.com), but it applies **online only, to full-price items only,** and excludes prescriptions, gift cards, BOGO, and sale-price items. Because so much of a drugstore run is prescriptions, sale items, and BOGOs, and because WeSalute+ costs roughly **$59.95 the first year (renewing ~$119.98/yr)**, the 20% pays for itself as a *CVS-only* play only for someone who buys a lot of **full-price** merchandise online at CVS.com each year. Archetype: **paid-membership / third-party-gated discount** (like a warehouse-club or subscription discount) layered over a conventional **drugstore loyalty** program. For almost every service member, the genuine best everyday path is the **free ExtraCare program** (2% ExtraBucks + digital coupons + weekly ad + ExtraBucks Rewards deals) — the same tools every CVS shopper uses — and to treat the WeSalute+ 20% as a bonus only if you already carry WeSalute+ for its broader benefits or you spend heavily on full-price CVS.com orders.

**Verdict logic:**
- **Most service members, everyday →** free **ExtraCare** + digital coupons + weekly ad. No cost, no membership, works in store and online.
- **You buy a lot of full-price stuff on CVS.com (vitamins, supplies, beauty) →** the **WeSalute+ 20%** can beat ExtraCare, *if* you clear the ~$300/yr full-price spend break-even or already hold WeSalute+ for other retailers.
- **The item is on sale or BOGO →** skip the 20% (it's excluded) and use the **sale + digital coupons/ExtraBucks**.
- **You buy CVS-brand goods often →** **ExtraCare Plus** ($48/yr, 20% off CVS Health brand + $10/mo reward) may out-save WeSalute+ for less money — and it's not military-gated.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **WeSalute+ 20% requires a PAID WeSalute+ membership** — the free WeSalute (basic) tier does not unlock it. This is the make-or-break cost fact.
2. **20% is full-price, online-only** — excludes prescriptions, gift cards, BOGO, sale-price items, same-day delivery charges, CVSphoto, and CVS Optical. It does not apply to a walk-in register purchase under current terms.
3. **WeSalute+ 20% + ExtraCare loyalty DO stack** — CVS states the 20% "can be combined with rewards" via ExtraCare/ExtraCare Plus. So the ExtraCare 2% ExtraBucks and eligible digital coupons can layer on the discounted order. *(Confirm exact eligible-coupon interaction at checkout — Medium confidence.)*
4. **ExtraCare Plus 20% is CVS-Health-brand only** and is a separate paid program from WeSalute+ — don't conflate the two "20% off" figures.
5. **Base commissary/exchange is a closed substitute channel** — no CVS code stacks there; the value is tax-free OTC/HBA, not a CVS discount.
6. **Cashback portals** occasionally list CVS.com; if used, place a no-coupon order and verify the current rate and that tracking isn't broken by the WeSalute-gated checkout. Rate not asserted here (verify live).

CVS does not give veterans a free discount at the register. The one "CVS military discount" that exists is **20% off full-price items at CVS.com** (plus free shipping) — and it's unlocked only through a **paid WeSalute+ membership** (formerly Veterans Advantage). ### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military — eligible (via WeSalute+)
- Veterans / retirees (honorably discharged) — eligible (via WeSalute+)
- National Guard / Reserve — eligible (via WeSalute+)
- Service academy cadets — eligible (via WeSalute+)
- Immediate family (spouse, parent, sibling, child) — eligible (via WeSalute+)
- Anyone (free ExtraCare) — the free loyalty program is open to all, no military status needed

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online (CVS.com) — the WeSalute+ 20%:**
1. Join and verify with **WeSalute**, then enroll in the paid **WeSalute+** tier.
2. From your WeSalute account, open the CVS offer and click the **activation link**.
3. Shop full-price items on CVS.com — 20% comes off eligible items and standard shipping is free. You can choose in-store pickup for the online order.

**In store (walk-in register):**
- There is **no confirmed standing 20% register discount** today. Use free **ExtraCare** in store: scan your ExtraCare card/app, load digital coupons, and let ExtraBucks Rewards and the weekly ad do the work.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage), a third-party service. You verify your service status with WeSalute once, then subscribe to the paid **WeSalute+** tier; CVS never receives a copy of your military ID. The CVS 20% is tied to your WeSalute+ membership and an offer activation link — it is only active while your paid membership is current. The free "WeSalute" basic tier does **not** unlock the CVS 20%.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Applies to **full-price items on CVS.com only**.
- Excludes **prescriptions, gift cards, BOGO offers, sale-price items, same-day delivery charges, CVSphoto.com, and CVS Pharmacy Optical**.
- Tax is calculated on the pre-discount price.
- Requires an **active paid WeSalute+ membership** (~$59.95 first year; renews higher).
- A reported "10% off MinuteClinic visits" appears on WeSalute's listing but is **not confirmed** on CVS's own pages — treat as unverified.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Free ExtraCare** — 2% back in ExtraBucks, personalized deals, $3 birthday reward, and pharmacy rewards; the backbone of CVS savings for everyone.
- **Stack digital coupons + weekly ad + ExtraBucks deals** — on sale/BOGO items this routinely beats a flat 20% (and those items are excluded from the 20% anyway).
- **ExtraCare Plus ($5/mo or $48/yr)** — 20% off CVS Health brand products + a $10 monthly reward + free delivery; not military-gated, and the monthly reward can out-save WeSalute+ for less money.
- **Base commissary / exchange** — if you have base access, over-the-counter and health-&-beauty items are **tax-free** there, a clean substitute for a CVS front-store run.
- **WeSalute+ as a bundle** — only pays as a CVS play if you buy heavily full-price on CVS.com; if you already carry WeSalute+ for other retailers, the CVS 20% is a free bonus.

### FAQ

#### Does CVS offer a military discount?
Sort of. CVS has no free register discount, but it offers **20% off full-price items at CVS.com** to **paid WeSalute+ members** (active duty, veterans, Guard/Reserve, cadets, and immediate family).

#### How much is the CVS military discount?
20% off full-price items on CVS.com, plus free shipping — but only through a paid WeSalute+ membership, and it excludes prescriptions, gift cards, BOGO, and sale items.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — honorably discharged veterans/retirees, Guard/Reserve, service academy cadets, and immediate family (spouse, parent, sibling, child) qualify through WeSalute+.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage). You verify once, subscribe to paid WeSalute+, and activate the CVS offer via a link in your WeSalute account.

#### Does CVS use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
CVS's military offer runs through **WeSalute** (WeSalute+). We found no CVS ID.me, SheerID, or GovX military storefront.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
**Online at CVS.com only.** You can pick up the online order in store, but the 20% does not apply to a walk-in register purchase under current terms.

#### What is excluded?
Prescriptions, gift cards, BOGO offers, sale-price items, same-day delivery charges, CVSphoto, and CVS Optical. Full-price items only.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No sale/BOGO items, but CVS says the 20% **can combine with ExtraCare rewards**, so your 2% ExtraBucks and eligible coupons can layer on a full-price order.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from CVS?
For most people, **free ExtraCare + digital coupons + the weekly ad** — no membership, works in store. The WeSalute+ 20% only wins if you buy a lot of full-price merchandise on CVS.com each year.

#### Does CVS offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No CVS-specific first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount was found. The WeSalute channel is military/veteran-focused; everyone can use ExtraCare.

#### Does CVS run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No confirmed CVS in-store Veterans Day military discount. The WeSalute+ 20% is a year-round (membership-gated) benefit, not a holiday event; CVS's public weekly deals run all year.
