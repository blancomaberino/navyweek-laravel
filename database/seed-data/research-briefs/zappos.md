# Zappos Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

As of June 23, 2026, Zappos does not offer a standing military or veteran discount that can be verified on a current Zappos primary source. Zappos previously ran a SheerID-gated "Programs" discount (10% off online for verified military, students, and teachers) launched in 2019, but it was discontinued — The Military Wallet (updated September 24, 2025) states Zappos "no longer offers a military discount," ID.me Shop (2026) says it is "not aware that Zappos offers Military discounts," and Zappos' former policy page that documented the programs (`/truth-about-zappos-coupons`) now returns a "no results" error. Zappos' live coupon and promotion pages list only general savings (a free Zappos VIP rewards program, a one-time 10%-off code for new email subscribers, clearance/sale markdowns, and limited-time sitewide codes such as SAVEBIG20). Most important caveat: no military-specific discount, code, or verification flow is confirmable on Zappos.com today; service members get the same free shipping, free 365-day returns, and price-adjustment terms as all customers.

### FAQ

#### Does Zappos offer a military discount?
Not currently in a form we can verify. Zappos previously ran a SheerID-gated 10% online discount for military, students, and teachers, but independent sources (The Military Wallet, updated September 24, 2025; ID.me Shop, 2026) report it has been discontinued, and no military offer appears on Zappos' live coupon or promotion pages as of June 23, 2026.

#### How much was the Zappos military discount?
When the program was active, it was 10% off online orders, applied after status verification and good for up to one year before re-verification. That figure reflects the historic program and is not a currently confirmable offer.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
There is no active military discount to qualify for. The former program had included active duty, reservists, National Guard, veterans, retirees, and immediate family with a valid dependent ID, but it is no longer confirmable as live.

#### How do I verify my military status at Zappos?
There is no active verification flow. The discontinued program used SheerID through a Zappos account login. If Zappos relaunches the offer, verify the current provider and steps on Zappos.com directly.

#### Does Zappos use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The historic program used SheerID. ID.me Shop states it is not aware of any current Zappos military discount. No GovX or WeSalute partnership for Zappos was found.

#### Can I use a Zappos discount in stores?
Zappos has no physical stores; it is online-only. All purchases and any savings happen on Zappos.com or the Zappos app.

#### What is excluded from Zappos promo codes?
General codes like SAVEBIG20 exclude taxes, shipping, gift cards/eGift cards, and previous purchases, can't be combined with certain other offers, and apply only to qualifying products while supplies last.

#### Can I combine offers or sale items with a code?
Zappos states promo codes may not be combined with certain other offers or discounts, and exclusions apply. Stacking is generally not supported.

#### Does Zappos offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
The former SheerID student and teacher discounts have been discontinued, and a Zappos healthcare (medical/nurse) discount was reported ended October 8, 2025 (third-party reporting; not confirmable on a Zappos primary source). No first-responder or government-employee discount was found. Treat all of these as not currently available unless Zappos republishes them.
