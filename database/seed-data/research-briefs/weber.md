# Weber — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$500 gas grill** bought new (Spirit-class; illustrative sticker — pin the live model price before publishing dollar examples). Sales tax (~7% illustrative) is included where the tax-free exchange channel competes.

## Headline finding

**Weber runs a real, official military discount on weber.com: 10% off, verified through ID.me at checkout** — confirmed on ID.me Shop's Weber storefront ("10% off for Military") and administered as the "Weber.com Military Program." It's weber.com-only (retail stores set their own policies, which Weber explicitly says it doesn't control), and per ID.me-ecosystem terms it doesn't combine with promo codes — so during Weber's own 20%-off promos on select grills, the public sale beats the military 10%. Two channels can beat weber.com outright on big-ticket carts: **AAFES/Exchange stocks Weber grills tax-free** (Spirit II and Genesis models listed on shopmyexchange.com), and **Lowe's / The Home Depot both carry Weber with their own everyday 10% military programs**. Archetype: **E (identity discount via ID.me)** with a strong **L secondary (exchange-carried, tax-free)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military 10% and promo codes don't combine** on weber.com — ID.me-ecosystem guidance says compare and pick one; Weber's 20%-off select-grill promos are the case in point.
2. **Cashback + military checkout is unreliable** — ID.me-gated checkouts commonly break portal tracking, and unlisted codes void Rakuten cashback. Treat military-10% orders as no-cashback; sale-price no-code orders as portal-safe (2.5% as of July 14, 2026).
3. **Retailer military discounts are the retailer's, not Weber's** — Weber says it doesn't control in-store discounts. Home Depot's 10% applies to full-priced merchandise (with reported annual caps — see the Home Depot brief); Lowe's runs its own 10% program. One program per register — they don't stack with each other or with Weber's.
4. **Exchange pricing is a substitute channel** — no weber.com codes or portals apply; the win is **no sales tax**, exchange price-matching policies, and MILITARY STAR card perks.
5. **Exchange eligibility is precise** — active duty, retirees, dependents, plus **all honorably discharged veterans online** (Veterans Online Shopping Benefit, 2017); in-store shopping requires the narrower 2021-expansion groups (service-connected disabled veterans, Purple Heart, former POWs, caregivers). Don't overpromise base access.

### FAQ
#### Does Weber offer a military discount?
Yes — 10% off at weber.com for verified military members, using ID.me at checkout (listed on ID.me Shop's Weber storefront, verified July 14, 2026).

#### How much is the Weber military discount?
10%. Claims of "up to 25% military" conflate Weber's public select-model promos (open to everyone) with the military program.

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Veterans verify through ID.me's standard military policy. Spouse/dependent eligibility isn't stated on Weber's readable pages — attempt verification in the ID.me flow to confirm.

#### Does Weber use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
ID.me, at weber.com checkout. There is no Weber grills storefront on GovX (GovX's "Weber Tactical" is a different company), and no SheerID program.

#### Can I use the military discount in stores like Lowe's or Home Depot?
No — Weber's program is weber.com-only. But Lowe's and The Home Depot run their own everyday 10% military discounts that apply to Weber grills they sell.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sales?
No. The military 10% doesn't stack with codes or promotional pricing. When Weber runs 20% off your model, take the sale instead.

#### Can I buy Weber tax-free at the Exchange?
Yes — ShopMyExchange lists Weber Spirit II and Genesis grills tax-free with free shipping. Active duty, retirees, and dependents shop in-store; honorably discharged veterans can use the online benefit.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Weber?
On a $500 grill: a live 20% public promo (+ cashback) ≈ $418 out the door; the military 10% ≈ $481; the Exchange at sticker parity ≈ $500 flat (no tax). Sale first, then compare 10%-plus-tax vs Exchange-tax-free.

#### Does Weber ship to APO/FPO / military bases?
Military editorial sources report Weber honors full warranty service on grills shipped to U.S. bases worldwide — confirm shipping options with Weber support for your address before ordering.
