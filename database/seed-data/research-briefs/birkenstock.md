# Birkenstock Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Birkenstock (US) offers **10% off full-price styles** to verified Military members (and several other communities) through **ID.me** verification at checkout on www.birkenstock.com/us. The offer is evergreen, online-only, and verified per ID.me's community-verification rules (active duty, veterans, retirees, Reserve/National Guard, spouses, surviving spouses, and adult immediate family/dependents all qualify under ID.me's military community). The single most important caveat: it covers **full-price styles only** — **1774 and Last Chance (sale/clearance) styles are excluded**, and it cannot be combined with any other Birkenstock code or offer. There is no published in-store military discount and no exact discount stated for Birkenstock retail stores.

### Intro
Birkenstock offers **10% off full-price styles** to verified members of the military community when you verify with **ID.me** at checkout on the US Birkenstock site. The same 10% offer also covers students, teachers, nurses, medical providers, and first responders. You verify once through ID.me and the discount applies automatically at checkout.

### Who Qualifies
Birkenstock lists the eligible audience simply as "Military"; the specific sub-groups are governed by ID.me's military community definition:
- Active duty — eligible (per ID.me)
- National Guard — eligible (per ID.me, counted under active duty)
- Reserves — eligible (per ID.me, counted under active duty)
- Veterans — eligible (per ID.me)
- Retirees — eligible (per ID.me)
- Military spouses — eligible (per ID.me)
- Surviving spouses — eligible (per ID.me)
- Dependents / immediate family (adult, 18+) — eligible (per ID.me "Military family")
- First responders — eligible (separate ID.me community Birkenstock also serves; 10%)
- Nurses / medical providers — eligible (separate ID.me communities; 10%)
- Teachers — eligible (separate ID.me community; 10%)
- Students — eligible (separate ID.me community; 10%)
- Government employees — Not stated as a Birkenstock ID.me community (not listed)
- Civilian DoD employees — not eligible for the military tier (per ID.me exclusions)
- Less-than-honorable discharge — not eligible (per ID.me exclusions)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add full-price item(s) to your cart at www.birkenstock.com/us (avoid 1774 and Last Chance styles).
2. Proceed to checkout and select the ID.me verification option.
3. Sign in to your existing ID.me account, or create one if you're new to ID.me.
4. Verify your military status through ID.me (upload documents if prompted).
5. After successful verification, the 10% discount applies automatically at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not stated in official source. Birkenstock's published military/ID.me offer is for online redemption only ("can only be redeemed at www.birkenstock.com/us"). There is no official in-store military discount documented. Verify with the specific Birkenstock store before relying on any in-store benefit.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me** at checkout. You select the ID.me button, then sign in (existing account) or create an account and confirm your email (new users), then follow ID.me's steps to verify your identity and military status — which can require uploading documents (e.g., military ID, DD214, or other proof, depending on your group). Birkenstock notes that if your ID.me community verification expires, you must reverify through ID.me; community verifications are not permanent and can expire. For verification problems, use the ID.me Help Center (help.id.me). Do not state or imply that a single verification lasts forever — ID.me re-verifies community status periodically.

### FAQ

#### Does Birkenstock offer a military discount?
Yes. Birkenstock's US site offers 10% off full-price styles to verified Military members through ID.me at checkout. (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the Birkenstock military discount?
10% off full-price styles. 1774 and Last Chance styles are excluded. (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.) Note: third-party coupon sites listing "25%" or "40%" military discounts are not supported by Birkenstock's official offer.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Birkenstock lists the audience as "Military," and ID.me's military community covers active duty, Guard/Reserve, veterans, retirees, military spouses, surviving spouses, and adult immediate family/dependents. (Sources: birkenstock.com/us/idme/; help.id.me/hc/en-us/articles/360010305173, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
At checkout on www.birkenstock.com/us, select the ID.me option and sign in or create an ID.me account, then verify your military status (documents may be required). The discount applies automatically after verification. (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does Birkenstock use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Birkenstock's official military/community offer uses **ID.me**. (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.) A Birkenstock listing also appears on GovX, but the brand's own published offer and terms reference ID.me only; treat any non-ID.me channel as unverified against Birkenstock's official terms.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Not per Birkenstock's published terms — the offer "can only be redeemed at www.birkenstock.com/us" (online). No official in-store military discount is documented. (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### What is excluded?
1774 styles, Last Chance (clearance) styles, previous orders, and anything that isn't a full-price style. It also can't be combined with other codes or offers, and Birkenstock employees aren't eligible. (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The discount is "not combinable with other Birkenstock discount codes or offers" and applies to full-price styles only (sale/Last Chance excluded). (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does Birkenstock offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — the same 10% off full-price styles applies to verified First Responders, Teachers, Nurses, Medical Providers, and Students via ID.me. (Source: birkenstock.com/us/idme/, accessed June 23, 2026.) A government-employee discount is not listed.

#### Does the discount expire or need renewal?
The Birkenstock offer is ongoing, but your ID.me community verification can expire and require reverification through ID.me. (Sources: birkenstock.com/us/idme/; ID.me Help Center, accessed June 23, 2026.)
