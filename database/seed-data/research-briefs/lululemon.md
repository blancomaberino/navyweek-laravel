# lululemon Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

lululemon offers a **15% discount** to verified military members and first responders, redeemable both online and in store. Eligibility is verified through **SheerID**, and the military community is defined broadly: **active, reservist, veteran, and retired** personnel all qualify, plus the **spouses and dependents of active personnel from the same household** (who must verify separately, with limits on the number of dependents). Once verified, the discount is an "always-on" benefit with **no limit on number of uses**, and the verified status is **good for four years** before you must re-verify. The single most important caveat: the discount is **not stackable** — it cannot be combined with any other promotion, promo code, sale/markdown, gift card (online), Klarna, Afterpay, or PayPal, and it excludes the lululemon Like New resale site, gift-card purchases, co-branded product, and product sold through third parties.

> Accuracy note: The discount applies to lululemon gear **for yourself only**. Using it for friends/family, gifting, or resale is prohibited and can lead to removal from the program. Several coupon sites describe the offer loosely as a flat "save 15%" without these limits — the exclusions above are confirmed in lululemon's own FAQ and Terms & Conditions as of June 23, 2026.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to lululemon's Military & First Responder page and click **Get Verified**.
2. Sign in to your lululemon account (or create one), then verify your military/first-responder status with **SheerID**; upload additional documentation if requested.
3. After you receive your SheerID confirmation email, sign in with **the same account** on lululemon.com or the iOS app.
4. At checkout, make sure the **15% off toggle is turned on** (and that there are no markdown items in your cart).
5. Pay with a **credit card or Apple Pay** — gift cards, Klarna, Afterpay, and PayPal can't be used with the discount online.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Complete the online **SheerID** verification in advance (in-store redemption requires you to have verified online first).
2. Visit a lululemon store and bring a **valid ID**.
3. Ask a lululemon **educator** to apply your 15% off at checkout.
4. (Gift cards may be used in combination with the discount for **in-store** purchases.)

### How Verification Works
lululemon uses **SheerID** to confirm eligibility. You click **Get Verified**, sign in to (or create) a lululemon account, and submit your information and military or first-responder status. Verification may be instantaneous (up to about 20 minutes); if it can't be confirmed automatically, SheerID compares your uploaded documents against your form entries, so enter your name exactly as it appears on your documents. You'll get a confirmation email from SheerID, after which the discount is active on your account. Your verified status is **good for four years** — after that, lululemon asks you to verify again. Spouses and dependents of active personnel must each complete their own verification. For verification problems (including denials and renewals), lululemon directs members to SheerID support; for other questions, to lululemon's contact page.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **For yourself only.** Cannot be used to buy for friends, family, or others, for gifting, or for resale; misuse can mean removal from the program.
- **Not stackable.** "Cannot be combined with any other offers, promotional codes, discounts or coupons." Turn the toggle off if buying for others, and note **markdown items in the cart can block the online toggle**.
- **Excluded channels/products:** lululemon **Like New** resale site; **gift cards and e-gift cards**; **co-branded product**; lululemon product **sold through third parties**; **phone orders**.
- **Online payment limits:** No gift cards, Klarna, Afterpay, or PayPal online (gift cards OK **in store**); credit card or Apple Pay only online.
- **Caps:** No published dollar cap, but lululemon reserves the right to "restrict transactions and spending limits, at its sole discretion and without notice," and to "exempt certain products or categories of products."
- **Returns:** Standard lululemon return policy applies (30 days; We Made Too Much sales are final).
- **Region/legal:** Program governed by laws of British Columbia, Canada; covers US and Canada shoppers via the SheerID form for the country on their ID. "Additional exclusions may apply."

### FAQ

#### Does lululemon offer a military discount?
Yes. lululemon offers 15% off to verified military members and first responders, both online and in store, with eligibility confirmed through SheerID.

#### How much is the lululemon military discount?
15% off. It applies to regular-price gear plus We Made Too Much, lululemon lab, and selfcare items, for your own purchases only.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify, along with active and reservist members. Spouses and dependents qualify **only for active personnel** in the same household, and each must verify separately (limits on number of dependents may apply).

#### How do I verify my status?
Click "Get Verified" on lululemon's Military & First Responder page, sign in to or create a lululemon account, and verify through SheerID. You'll get a confirmation email, usually within minutes.

#### Does lululemon use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
lululemon uses **SheerID** for military and first-responder verification.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Verify online first, then bring a valid ID to a lululemon store and ask an educator to apply your 15% at checkout.

#### What is excluded?
The Like New resale site, gift cards and e-gift cards, co-branded product, third-party-sold product, and phone orders. It also can't be combined with any other offer, promo code, sale, or markdown item.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The 15% can't be combined with other offers, promo codes, or discounts, and markdown items in your cart can prevent the online toggle from applying.

#### How often do I have to re-verify?
Your verified status is good for four years; after that, lululemon asks you to verify again. There's no limit on how many times you use the discount in between.

#### Does lululemon offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
First responders get the same 15% (firefighters, law enforcement, search & rescue, EMTs, medical doctors, and licensed nurses). Teachers, students, and government employees are not listed for this program. Separately, fitness instructors, trainers, gym owners, coaches, and athletes can apply for the unrelated **Sweat Collective** program (25% off).
