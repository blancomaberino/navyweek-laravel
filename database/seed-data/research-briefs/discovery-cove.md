# Discovery Cove — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult **Discovery Cove Day Resort Admission** (dynamic pricing; roughly **$200–$280 incl. tax & fees** off-peak/mid-season, higher on summer weekends and holidays). Dolphin-swim carts run higher (~$245+ base, up to $420+ peak).

## Headline finding

Discovery Cove **does** participate in SeaWorld/United Parks' **Waves of Honor** military program — but unlike its sister parks, **Discovery Cove offers only a *discounted* rate, not free admission.** This is the single most important, most-misunderstood fact: active-duty members get **free** one-day admission to SeaWorld, Busch Gardens, Aquatica and Sesame Place, but at Discovery Cove they get a **discount** on the Day Resort Admission and Signature Dolphin Swim packages, for the member/veteran plus up to **3 dependents/guests**. Verification is through **ID.me** (TroopID). Crucially, **Discovery Cove does not publish the discount percentage or price** anywhere public — the discounted rate is only revealed after you log in and verify with ID.me, so no honest page can quote a fixed "%." Brand archetype: **gated-verification experience brand** (all-inclusive reservation park; dynamic pricing; discount hidden behind an ID.me wall) with a **cross-sell** wrinkle (every Discovery Cove package already bundles 14 days of SeaWorld/Aquatica). Best path for most: **verify via Waves of Honor / ID.me and compare that rate against Discovery Cove's own seasonal public promotions** (the park regularly runs 30%-off + free-open-bar sitewide deals that anyone can book) and against **base ITT/MWR ticket offices** — whichever is lowest for your date wins, because the military rate does **not** stack with public promos.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The Waves of Honor discounted rate is standalone.** It cannot be combined with public promo codes, seasonal sitewide sales, AAA, Florida-resident, or reseller pricing — you pick one path per booking. (Discovery Cove military pages present it as a single verified offer, not a stackable code.)
2. **No cashback-portal stack.** Discovery Cove bookings run through an ID.me-gated flow; identity-gated checkouts routinely break cashback-portal tracking. Do not promise a Rakuten/TopCashback stack.
3. **Guest cap is hard:** active duty + up to 3 dependents; veteran + up to 3 guests (**limit 4 total**), and dependents/guests must be booked **under the member's/veteran's own ID.me account**. The veteran must be **physically present with guests** at redemption.
4. **The 14-day SeaWorld/Aquatica inclusion is not a stack or a military perk** — it comes with every Discovery Cove package regardless of status.
5. **In-park ID still required** — ID.me verification does not remove the need to show a valid military/veteran ID at reception.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty U.S. military, activated or drilling reservists, National Guard** — eligible, plus up to 3 direct dependents.
- **Veterans and military retirees** — eligible, plus up to 3 guests (limit 4 total); the veteran must be present with guests at redemption.
- **Dependents/spouses** — eligible only when booked **under the service member's or veteran's own ID.me account**.
- **Ineligible for the active-duty tier:** inactive, standby and retired reserve members, U.S. Merchant Marine, and civilian Department of Defense employees.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Discovery Cove's Waves of Honor / military-discount page (active-duty or veteran tier).
2. Click to verify through **ID.me** — log in or create a free account and confirm your status.
3. View your **personalized discounted rate** (this is where the price is revealed) and select your date and package.
4. Add dependents/guests **under your own account**, up to the limit (3 dependents active-duty; 3 guests / 4 total for veterans).
5. Complete the booking; bring a **valid military or veteran ID** plus your reservation to reception on arrival.

### How Verification Works
Discovery Cove uses **ID.me** (also branded TroopID) for a free, one-time online verification of active-duty, reserve/Guard, veteran or retiree status. Verification is done online — not at the gate — but you must **still present a valid physical military/veteran ID at park reception**. Re-verification cadence is set by ID.me, not by Discovery Cove; the park can terminate or change the program at any time.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Not free admission** — Discovery Cove offers only a discounted rate (unlike free SeaWorld/Busch Gardens/Aquatica/Sesame Place active-duty admission).
- **No published discount amount** — the rate is only visible after ID.me verification; treat any fixed "%" quoted elsewhere as unverified.
- **Hard party cap:** 3 dependents (active duty) or 3 guests / 4 total (veterans); guests booked under the member's account.
- **Not combinable** with public promo codes, seasonal sales, AAA, Florida-resident, annual-pass, or reseller pricing.
- **Veteran offer currently valid through December 31, 2026**; program subject to change or termination.
- Active-duty tier excludes inactive/standby/retired reserve, U.S. Merchant Marine, and civilian DoD workers.

### FAQ

#### Does Discovery Cove offer a military discount?
Yes. Through the **Waves of Honor** program, active-duty service members, activated/drilling reservists, National Guard, veterans and retirees get a **discounted rate** on Discovery Cove's Day Resort Admission and Signature Dolphin Swim packages, for themselves plus up to 3 dependents or guests. It is a discount, not free admission.

#### How much is the Discovery Cove military discount?
Discovery Cove **does not publish the amount.** The discounted rate is only shown after you verify your military or veteran status through ID.me and log in. Any specific percentage you see on coupon sites is unverified — check your real rate in the ID.me-gated booking flow.

#### Is Discovery Cove free for military like SeaWorld?
No. This is the biggest point of confusion. Active-duty members get **free** one-day admission to SeaWorld, Busch Gardens, Aquatica and Sesame Place through Waves of Honor, but **Discovery Cove offers only a discount**, never free admission.

#### Do veterans qualify for the Discovery Cove discount?
Yes. Veterans and military retirees get a discounted rate plus up to 3 guests (limit 4 total), and the offer is currently valid through **December 31, 2026**. The veteran must be present with their guests at the park for redemption, and guests are booked under the veteran's ID.me account.

#### Do spouses and dependents qualify?
Yes, within the limits — up to 3 direct dependents (active-duty tier) or 3 guests (veteran tier, 4 total). Dependents and spouses must be booked **under the service member's or veteran's own ID.me account**, and the qualifying member must be present.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Discovery Cove uses **ID.me (TroopID)**. Create a free ID.me account or log in on the Discovery Cove military page, verify your status online, and your discounted rate appears. You must also bring a valid physical military or veteran ID to park reception.

#### Does Discovery Cove use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Discovery Cove uses **ID.me** (TroopID) for Waves of Honor. It is **not** sold through GovX, and the discount is not available via the military exchange (AAFES/NEX) webstores.

#### Can I combine the military discount with a sale or promo code?
No. The Waves of Honor rate is standalone and cannot be stacked with public promo codes, seasonal sitewide sales, AAA, Florida-resident, annual-pass, or reseller pricing. Because it doesn't stack, compare it against the current public promo and book whichever is cheaper.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Discovery Cove?
Verify with ID.me to see your military rate, then compare it against (1) any live Discovery Cove public sitewide sale — which often matches or beats it and covers your whole party — and (2) your base **ITT/MWR ticket office**. Book the single lowest option for your travel date; they can't be combined.

#### Does Discovery Cove run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No confirmed Discovery Cove military-holiday promo exists separate from the year-round Waves of Honor rate. Its deeper deals tend to be public sitewide seasonal sales open to everyone, not military-holiday-specific.
