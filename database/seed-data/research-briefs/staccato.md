# Staccato — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Staccato C2 pistol (MSRP $2,299), bought direct from Staccato2011.com.

## Headline finding

Yes — Staccato (the premium 2011® pistol maker, formerly STI International) runs a **real, official** service discount through its **Heroes Program**: **10% off Staccato firearms, magazines, and optic plates** for military professionals, veterans, retired/non‑active‑duty law enforcement, EMS, and firefighters, redeemed online at Staccato2011.com after eligibility verification (via **VerifyPass**). A separate **Blue Line Program** gives *active‑duty law enforcement* a much deeper deal — **$800 off HD P4 & P4.5 models and 20% off** the standard version of all other firearms, magazines, and optic plates. Archetype: **manufacturer-direct, verification-gated single-use discount** — no cashback, no exchange channel, no stacking. The catch for the *military/veteran* audience: 10% is off **MSRP**, and Staccato pistols routinely sell **below MSRP at FFL dealers**, so the genuine best move is to price the Heroes 10%-off-MSRP against the cheapest street price at a dealer and take whichever is lower (plus FFL transfer + tax). Active-duty LEO should always use Blue Line — nothing beats it.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Staccato2011.com and open the **Heroes Program** (or **Blue Line Program** for active LEO) page.
2. Submit the application/verification form via **VerifyPass**, uploading proof of eligibility.

4. Place your order at Staccato2011.com and apply the code at checkout **before** paying.
5. The firearm ships to your chosen **FFL dealer** for pickup (a transfer fee may apply).

### How Verification Works
Eligibility is confirmed through **VerifyPass**. You submit documentation proving your service or first-responder status; on approval, Staccato emails a single-use discount code (typically within one business day). Contact **heroes@staccato2011.com** or **(512) 686-3613** with questions. Re-verification isn't described as automatic — treat each issued code as tied to a single order.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Handgun customization is not discounted** under either program.
- Discount applies to **direct Staccato2011.com orders only** — not to third-party dealers.
- Codes are **single-use and non-stackable**.
- Firearm purchases are subject to federal law: the gun ships to an **FFL**, and **transfer fees + state sales/excise tax** are additional.
- Blue Line's 20%/$800 is limited to **active-duty law enforcement**; "some restrictions apply" per Staccato.

### Best Ways To Save More

### FAQ

#### Does Staccato offer a military discount?
Yes. Through the **Heroes Program**, military professionals and veterans get **10% off** Staccato firearms, magazines, and optic plates when buying direct at Staccato2011.com.

#### How much is the Staccato military discount?
**10%** for military and veterans (Heroes Program). Active-duty law enforcement get more via **Blue Line**: **$800 off** the HD P4/P4.5 and **20% off** the standard version of other models.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Veterans qualify** (Heroes, 10%). Staccato does **not publish** eligibility for spouses or dependents — treat that as unconfirmed and contact heroes@staccato2011.com to check.

#### How do I verify my status?
Submit your credentials through **VerifyPass** on the Heroes/Blue Line page. Staccato emails your discount code, usually **within 1 business day**.

#### Does Staccato use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
It uses **VerifyPass**. Staccato pistols are **not** sold on GovX (only third-party Staccato holsters/accessories appear there).

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
**Online, direct at Staccato2011.com.** There's no published consumer in-store discount; the firearm ships to your FFL for pickup.

#### What is excluded?
**Handgun customization** is excluded, the code is **single-use and non-stackable**, and it applies only to direct Staccato orders — not third-party dealers.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — Staccato's discount codes **don't stack** with each other or with dealer pricing, since the discount only applies on a direct Staccato2011.com order.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Staccato?
**Active LEO:** Blue Line (20% / $800). **Military/veteran/first responder:** get the Heroes 10% code, then compare it to the lowest **FFL-dealer street price** — because Staccato often sells below MSRP, and 10% is off MSRP.

#### Does Staccato offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
**First responders (EMS, firefighters) qualify** for the Heroes Program (10%). There is **no published** teacher, nurse, government-employee, or student discount on firearms.

#### Can I buy Staccato at the Exchange or through GovX?
No. Staccato firearms aren't carried by AAFES/NEX exchanges or GovX — buy direct through the Heroes/Blue Line programs.
