# Peacock Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Peacock (Peacock TV streaming) offers verified military members **Peacock Premium at $6.99/month for 12 months**, versus the $10.99/month retail price — roughly a 36% saving for a year. Eligibility is verified through **SheerID** at [peacocktv.com/military](https://www.peacocktv.com/military): you verify your status, receive a unique one-time voucher code, then redeem it on a new or existing Peacock account. Eligible groups are **active-duty service members, Reservists, National Guard, veterans, and retirees** who are 18+, reside in the U.S. (including territories), and provide a valid payment method. The single most important caveat: this is a **price-lock promo, not a permanent rate** — after the 12-month Access Period it **auto-renews at $10.99/mo. (or the then-current retail price) plus tax** unless you cancel, and Peacock requires **annual re-verification through SheerID** to keep the discounted rate.

> Accuracy note: Several coupon/aggregator pages still advertise Peacock military pricing of **$3.99/mo, $2.99/mo, or "$4/mo savings."** Those figures are **outdated** — they reflect older promotional pricing. Peacock's own military page and offer terms state **$6.99/month for 12 months (a $10.99/month retail value)** as of June 23, 2026. Use only $6.99.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **peacocktv.com/military** and select **Get Started**.
2. Verify your military status with **SheerID** (you'll share first name, last name, DOB, and branch of service).
3. After successful verification, copy the **unique one-time promo code** you're given (valid 30 days).
4. Click **Redeem Now** to create a Peacock account — or, if you already have one, sign in and apply the code on your **Account → "Plans & Payments"** page.
5. Add a valid payment method (credit card or PayPal). Your **$6.99/month** rate starts and runs for 12 months.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Peacock is a streaming service with **no physical stores** — the military discount is redeemed online only, at peacocktv.com/military.

### How Verification Works
Peacock uses **SheerID** to confirm military eligibility. At peacocktv.com/military you're redirected to SheerID and asked for your first name, last name, date of birth, and branch of service; SheerID checks this against authoritative military data. On success you receive a one-time promo code (valid 30 days) to apply to a new or existing Peacock account. **Peacock requires annual verification** — to keep the discounted rate beyond 12 months you must re-verify your eligibility through SheerID, otherwise the plan renews at the full $10.99/month (or then-current) rate. For verification problems, Peacock links to SheerID's military FAQ (verify.sheerid.com/military-faq-b/).

### FAQ

#### Does Peacock offer a military discount?
Yes. Verified military members get **Peacock Premium for $6.99/month for 12 months** (versus $10.99/month retail), verified through SheerID at peacocktv.com/military.

#### How much is the Peacock military discount?
**$6.99/month for 12 months.** (Older coupon pages showing $3.99, $2.99, or "$4/mo savings" are out of date — Peacock's current military price is $6.99.) After 12 months it renews at $10.99/mo or the then-current rate unless you cancel.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Veterans and retirees qualify.** Peacock's official terms do **not** list military spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families as eligible, so don't assume family coverage — only service members, Reservists, Guard, veterans, and retirees are named.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to peacocktv.com/military, verify with **SheerID** (you provide first/last name, DOB, and branch of service), then apply the one-time code SheerID gives you to a new or existing Peacock account. Verification must be repeated **annually** to keep the discounted rate.

#### Does Peacock use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Peacock's **official military offer uses SheerID.** (Peacock also appears on third-party marketplaces like ID.me Shop and WeSalute, but the page Peacock links to for the military offer is SheerID.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. Peacock is streaming-only; the discount is redeemed **online** at peacocktv.com/military. There are no physical stores.

#### What is excluded?
It's **one-time use, one code per person/email**, the code **expires 30 days** after verification, it **can't be combined** with other offers, and the price reverts to **$10.99/mo (or then-current) after 12 months.** A valid payment method is required and existing paid subscribers may need to cancel first.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Peacock's terms state the code **"cannot be combined with any other offer"** and is **one-time use only.**

#### Does Peacock offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — separate offers exist: **first responders and medical professionals** ($6.99/mo, peacocktv.com/medical-first-responder), **teachers** ($6.99/mo, peacocktv.com/teacher), and **students** ($5.99/mo, peacocktv.com/student), each verified through SheerID. These are distinct from the military offer.
