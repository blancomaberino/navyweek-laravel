# Panda Express — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Panda Express Bigger Plate** (1 side + 3 entrées) — **$12.90 pre-tax** (representative "~$12 plate meal"; the standard Panda Plate is ~$11.10). All figures dine-in/carry-out, one U.S. location; local sales tax applies to every row and does not change the ranking, so it's omitted for clarity.

## Headline finding

Panda Express (a **Panda Restaurant Group, Inc.** brand) has **no official, published, company-wide military or veteran discount.** It isn't in Panda's FAQ, rewards terms, or any corporate page, and Panda Express does **not** appear on the major Veterans Day free-meal roundups the way Chipotle, Applebee's, and others do. What actually exists is a **discretionary, location-by-location "military courtesy" discount** — commonly reported around **10%** (some report 5–10%, a few 15%) — granted at the manager's discretion, **in-store only, with a valid ID, and never guaranteed.** Treat it as a YMMV perk to *ask* about, not a benefit to count on. The single best *documented, everyday* savings path for a service member is the free **Panda Rewards** program: a **25%-off (up to $10) welcome gift** on your first order, then **10 points per $1**, redemptions starting at **200 points**, a birthday gift, and a monthly "Good Fortune Gift." Archetype: **J (unofficial / manager's-discretion)** — the reliable savings is the loyalty program and coupons, not the unadvertised counter discount — with a secondary **H (aggregators invent a flat everyday % that doesn't exist)** flavor to debunk.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?

#### How do I verify my status?

No. Panda Express has no discount-verification partner because it has no formal military program.

#### Can I use a military discount online or in the app?
No. Any courtesy discount is **in-store only** and can't be verified digitally. For online/app savings, use **Panda Rewards** (the 25% welcome gift and points) instead.

#### Is there a "10MILITARY" or "20% off military" promo code?
No. Those are **unverified aggregator claims**. Panda Express doesn't publish a military promo code, and there's no online military discount.

#### Does Panda Express offer a first responder, teacher, or student discount?
