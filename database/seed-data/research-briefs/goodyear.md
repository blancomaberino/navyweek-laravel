# Goodyear — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one set of **4 mid-tier Goodyear tires, ~$800 installed** (the order most readers price).

## Headline finding

**Goodyear publishes no permanent, year-round national military discount percentage.** What actually exists is three separate things people conflate: (1) a **recurring Veterans Day promo** — historically **10% off tires and services instantly** at company-owned Goodyear Auto Service and Just Tires stores, valid for military, veterans, and first responders with ID, and notably **combinable with current tire/service offers**; (2) an **ID.me online cash-back offer** on goodyear.com for verified Military/First Responder/Nurse/Teacher shoppers (recently **up to ~$80 back on a set of 4 select tires**, cash back only — not on installation); and (3) the everyday lever that beats both — **Goodyear's public mail-in/online rebates** (recently **up to $180 back on 4 tires with the Goodyear Credit Card**), which any customer gets.

The single best path is usually **not** the military angle at all: it's a **public Goodyear rebate stacked at a retailer that gives a real, combinable military discount**. **Discount Tire sells Goodyear tires and honors a verified 5% ID.me military discount that explicitly stacks with manufacturer rebates** — so a service member typically nets more buying Goodyear tires *through Discount Tire* than chasing Goodyear's own discretionary in-store discount. Archetype: **tire/auto-service retailer** (rebate-driven, in-store-discretionary discount, ID.me cash-back layer) with a **recurring Veterans Day** secondary. Ignore aggregator pages promising a flat "Goodyear military discount code" online — no such standing percentage code exists.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Public Goodyear rebates are open to everyone** and are the base layer — a service member's "discount" is usually *rebate + a combinable retailer discount*, not a special Goodyear military %.
2. **The Veterans Day 10% is explicitly combinable** with current tire/service offers (per Goodyear's release) — the rare stack that genuinely works, but only in the promo window at company-owned stores.
3. **ID.me online cash back does NOT stack with a partner exclusive promo code** and is **not paid on installation labor** — tires only.

5. **In-store discretionary discounts are not guaranteed and generally won't combine with a rebate** at franchise Goodyear stores — confirm at the counter.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Veterans Day in-store promo (ID) and ID.me online cash back.
- **Veterans** — same; verified via ID.me online or military ID in store.
- **First responders** — included in the Veterans Day promo and ID.me store.
- **Nurses / teachers / students** — eligible for ID.me online offers where the ID.me store lists them; no in-store tire discount specific to these groups.
- **Family/dependents** — no stated corporate military-family tire discount; store discretion only.

### How To Redeem Online (ID.me cash back)
1. Go to the Goodyear tire offer via ID.me Shop and verify your status with ID.me.
2. Shop qualifying tires on goodyear.com through the ID.me link.
3. Cash back posts to your ID.me account after purchase — remember it excludes installation and won't combine with a partner exclusive promo code.

### How To Redeem In Store (Veterans Day)
1. During the Veterans Day window (historically Nov 10–14), visit a company-owned Goodyear Auto Service or Just Tires store.
2. Show a valid military/veteran/first-responder ID.
3. Get 10% off tires and services instantly — and ask them to also apply any live rebate.

### How To Save The Most (everyone)
1. Check the **current Goodyear rebate** (goodyear.com/en-us/offers) — often the biggest single saving (up to ~$180 on 4).
2. Price the same tires at **Discount Tire**, apply its **5% ID.me military discount**, and stack the manufacturer rebate.
3. Consider the **Goodyear Credit Card** only if the higher rebate tier/financing beats paying cash.

### How Verification Works
Online, verification is handled by **ID.me** — create/verify an ID.me account, then shop through the linked offer. In store on Veterans Day, a store associate simply checks your **military or first-responder ID**. There is no SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute military program with Goodyear.

- Veterans Day terms (dates, eligible stores) change year to year — confirm before Nov 11.

### FAQ

#### Does Goodyear offer a military discount?
Not as a permanent year-round national program. Goodyear runs a **recurring Veterans Day** promo (historically 10% off tires and services instantly at company-owned Goodyear Auto Service and Just Tires stores) and an **ID.me online cash-back** offer for verified military and first responders. Day to day, the bigger savings come from public rebates open to everyone.

#### How much is the Goodyear military discount?
There's no set standing percentage. The Veterans Day promo has historically been 10% instant; the ID.me online offer has recently been up to about $80 back on a set of 4 tires (cash back, not on installation).

#### Do veterans and first responders qualify?
Yes for the Veterans Day promo (military, veterans, first responders with ID) and for ID.me online offers. Verify online through ID.me or show ID in store.

#### Does Goodyear use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Goodyear's online military/first-responder cash back runs through **ID.me**. There's no GovX or WeSalute military program. In-store Veterans Day discounts are applied by an associate checking your ID.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
Both, but they're different offers: ID.me cash back is **online** at goodyear.com; the Veterans Day 10% is **in store** at company-owned locations.

#### What is excluded?
There's no permanent discount, so nothing is guaranteed outside the promo windows. ID.me cash back excludes installation and won't stack with a partner exclusive promo code.

#### Can I combine a discount with rebates or promo codes?
The Veterans Day 10% is combinable with current offers. ID.me cash back won't combine with a partner exclusive promo code. Public rebates are open to everyone and are the main thing to stack.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Goodyear tires?
Usually: buy the same Goodyear tires **through Discount Tire**, use its **verified 5% ID.me military discount** (which stacks with manufacturer rebates), and apply the current Goodyear rebate. That typically beats Goodyear's own discretionary in-store discount.

#### Does Goodyear run a Veterans Day sale?
Historically yes — 10% off tires and services instantly (combinable with current offers), plus a free car-care check, at company-owned stores for military, veterans, and first responders. Confirm the current-year window before Nov 11.
