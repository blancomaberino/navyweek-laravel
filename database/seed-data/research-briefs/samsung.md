# Samsung Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Samsung runs an official **Military Offer Program** that gives verified shoppers **up to 30% off** select Samsung products (Galaxy phones and tablets, TVs, laptops, monitors, and home appliances) when buying online at Samsung.com. It is not a single flat percentage — it is program pricing that varies by product, capped at "up to 30%." Active-duty members verify instantly with a **.mil email** (or .gov/.edu/.company email for related programs); everyone else verifies their status through **ID.me** using a personal email. Samsung's help center confirms **veterans, National Guard/Reserve, retirees, and immediate family members** can also get a military discount — but for those groups Samsung routes you to its partner **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage), which requires a **paid WeSalute+ membership** to unlock the Samsung store. The single most important caveat: the discount is **online only** (not in Samsung retail/Experience stores or third-party retailers), there is a **two-products-per-category, per-year purchase limit**, and Samsung's own program terms say the pricing **"cannot be combined with any other offer"** — which conflicts with third-party claims that it stacks, so treat it as non-combinable unless Samsung's checkout shows otherwise.

> Accuracy note: Some aggregator and partner pages state the Samsung military discount "can be combined with public and seasonal offers." Samsung's own Offer Program terms (samsung.com/us/shop/offer-program/military) state program prices "cannot be combined with any other offer." Where the brand's primary terms and a third party disagree, this brief follows Samsung's own terms and flags the conflict. Verify at checkout.

### How To Redeem Online (Active Duty — Samsung Offer Program)
1. Go to Samsung's Military Offer Program page (samsung.com/us/shop/offer-program/military/).
2. Create or sign in to a **Samsung Account** using your **.mil** email (auto-approved). If you don't have a .mil email, use a personal email and continue to step 3.
3. If using a personal email, you'll be redirected to **ID.me** to verify your military status instantly.
4. Add eligible products to your cart and proceed to checkout.
5. Your program pricing (up to 30% off) is shown at checkout once you're signed in and verified — no code needed.

### How To Redeem Online (Veterans / Family — WeSalute)
1. Create a free WeSalute account and get your military eligibility verified.
2. Upgrade to a **paid WeSalute+** plan (required to unlock the Samsung store).
3. Log in to the WeSalute Marketplace and open the Samsung offer.
4. Follow WeSalute's redemption steps to create or sign in to a Samsung account linked to the private WeSalute member store.
5. Your up to 30% off pricing is automatically applied in your Samsung cart at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not available. The Samsung Military Offer Program pricing is **online only**. Samsung's terms state these prices "are not available at retail locations or online sites other than this site," and asking a retailer to price-match the program price can get a user removed from the program. There is no in-store military discount through this program.

### How Verification Works
Samsung verifies eligibility two ways. **Automatic email approval:** a .gov, .mil, .edu, or .company email is auto-approved when you create your Samsung Account, and you're signed straight into the Offer Program. **ID.me:** if you use a personal email, Samsung redirects you to ID.me to confirm your affiliation to a qualified group (Military, Government, First Responder, Medical, Nurse, Student, or Teacher). For **veterans, Guard/Reserve, retirees, and family**, Samsung also points to **WeSalute**, which verifies your service when you create a free account and requires a **paid WeSalute+ membership** to access the Samsung store. Samsung notes the discount available through the Offer Program (ID.me) and through WeSalute **may differ**. Re-verification frequency is not stated in Samsung's official source; ID.me and WeSalute apply their own periodic re-verification per their policies. For problems, Samsung's order help center (order-help.us.samsung.com) and ID.me/WeSalute support are the paths.

### FAQ

#### Does Samsung offer a military discount?
Yes. Samsung runs an official Military Offer Program with up to 30% off select products (Galaxy devices, TVs, laptops, monitors, and appliances) when you shop online at Samsung.com and verify your military status.

#### How much is the Samsung military discount?
Up to 30% off, but it's program pricing that varies by product — not a single flat percentage. Some items will be discounted less than 30%. Check the price at checkout after verifying.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes. Samsung's help center confirms veterans, National Guard/Reserve, retirees, and immediate family members qualify. Veterans and family typically verify through Samsung's partner WeSalute (which requires a paid WeSalute+ membership), or through ID.me in the Offer Program. Spouses and dependents fall under "immediate family member"; confirm at verification.

#### How do I verify my status?
Active-duty members can use a .mil email for instant approval. Everyone else verifies a personal email through ID.me in the Samsung Offer Program, or signs up through WeSalute. Samsung notes the discount through the two programs may differ.

#### Does Samsung use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Samsung uses **ID.me** (and direct .mil/.gov/.edu/.company email approval) for its Offer Program, and partners with **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage) for veterans and military families. It does not use GovX or SheerID for this program per Samsung's official pages.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. The Military Offer Program is online only. Samsung's terms say the pricing isn't available at retail locations, and price-matching requests can get you removed from the program.

#### What is excluded, and is there a purchase limit?
Yes — there's a limit of two products per category, per email/shipping address, per calendar year. The Galaxy Fit3 is excluded on the WeSalute offer. The discount is online-only and, per Samsung's terms, "cannot be combined with any other offer."

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Samsung's program terms say the pricing cannot be combined with any other offer. (Some third-party pages claim it stacks with public/seasonal sales — that's not confirmed by Samsung's own terms, so don't count on it.) Trade-in credit and financing are applied alongside program pricing per Samsung's terms.

#### Does Samsung offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount?
Yes — those are separate Samsung Offer Programs (First Responder, Medical, Nurse, Teacher, Government, Student, Employee), each verified through ID.me, not part of the Military program.
