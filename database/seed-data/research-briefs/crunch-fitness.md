# Crunch Fitness Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Crunch Fitness runs a national military/veteran/first-responder offer in partnership with ID.me. Per Crunch's own page, eligible members get a 7-day free trial plus the "Peak Results Military Plan": **$0 enrollment and $3 off the standard recurring monthly dues**. Verification is done through ID.me — either online during checkout or in club at the front desk (scan the QR to verify, then show ID.me proof; the barcode does not scan, so staff enter the discount manually). Crunch states the offer is **only available at participating locations**, and because Crunch is heavily franchise-operated, exact participation and pricing vary by club. The single most important caveat: there is **no single percentage** — the corporate-stated benefit is the flat "$0 enrollment + $3 off dues" structure, and anything framed as "10%/25%/50% off" comes only from aggregators and is unverified. Always confirm with the local club.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the Crunch/ID.me verification link (hosted-pages.id.me/offers/crunch-fitness) and verify your military or first-responder status with ID.me.
2. Start your membership purchase on crunch.com (at a participating location).
3. In the online checkout flow, choose "verify with ID.me" and enter your ID.me credentials.
4. Once confirmed, the discount is applied automatically in checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. If not yet verified: scan the QR code at the club's front desk to reach ID.me's verification portal and verify.
2. If already verified: show your ID.me credentials/proof (digital or print) to the front-desk associate.
3. Note that the barcode on your ID.me verification will not scan — the associate enters the discount manually.
4. Ask about the 7-day free trial and the Peak Results Military Plan ($0 enrollment, $3 off recurring dues) when signing up.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by ID.me. You can verify ahead of time online or on the spot at a club by scanning the front-desk QR code. ID.me confirms your eligible community status (military/first responder). Crunch's page states each person can request a one-time code once a month via ID.me. ID.me may periodically require re-verification per its own policies; do not assume verification is permanent. If automatic verification fails, ID.me provides a support/help path (help.id.me), and the club front desk can assist with applying the discount manually.

Crunch states the benefit as $0 enrollment plus $3 off the standard recurring monthly dues on the Peak Results Military Plan, plus a 7-day free trial. Crunch does not publish a flat percentage. Any "10%/25%/50% off" figures come from third-party sites and are not confirmed by Crunch.

#### Can I use the discount in stores (in club)?
Yes. Verify with ID.me at the front desk (or in advance online) and staff apply the discount to your membership sale, at participating locations.

The offer is limited to participating locations, the benefit applies to the Peak Results Military Plan, and codes are limited to one per person per month. Franchise locations may differ. No published percentage applies.
