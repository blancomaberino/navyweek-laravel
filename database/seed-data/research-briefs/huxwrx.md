HUXWRX is also on **ExpertVoice** and is sold through **GovX**, giving service members two additional gated channels to compare. Archetype: **tactical/pro-deal brand where the gated "pro program" (ID.me) is the primary military save, with ExpertVoice and GovX as parallel verified channels** — the win is Pro/pro-deal pricing, not a coupon.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Pro pricing is gated, not stacked** — you verify via ID.me and get member prices; there's **no public code to add**, and **sharing/price-matching program pricing is prohibited** (account termination for misuse).
2. **The 3-suppressor/year limit resets annually**, and **special-sale suppressors don't count** toward it — so member sales are effectively "extra."
3. **Do not return Pro-priced product to retailers** — HUXWRX states returns void the benefit and can't be recovered.
4. **ExpertVoice and GovX are separate channels** — you don't stack them on HUXWRX.com; you pick the channel with the best net item price. ExpertVoice pro deals are **personal-use only, capped, no resale**.
5. **The NFA process is unavoidable** — Form 4, $200 stamp, background check, and dealer transfer apply on every channel; the discount is on the sticker, not the paperwork.
