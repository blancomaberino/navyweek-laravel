# Applebee's Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Research date / sources accessed: **June 23, 2026**. Primary source = Applebee's own site (applebees.com / Dine Brands). Everyday-discount claims are aggregator-only and flagged accordingly.

## Executive Summary

Applebee's does **not publish a corporate, everyday (year-round) military or veteran discount.** The only military offer Applebee's documents on its own website is its **annual Veterans Day free meal** (every November 11): Veterans, Active Duty, Reserves, and National Guard who **dine in** at participating U.S. locations get a **free full-size entrée** from a limited Veterans Day menu, plus a **$5 "Bounce Back" Card** for a later visit, with proof of service. The widely repeated "10% off everyday" figure does **not** appear on applebees.com — it originates from third-party sites (WeSalute, coupon aggregators) and is, per WeSalute's own disclaimer, an **unguaranteed, franchise-by-location courtesy that "varies by location."** Applebee's is franchised (1,514+ U.S. restaurants run by independent operators), so any everyday discount is at each restaurant's discretion. The single most important caveat: treat the Veterans Day free meal as the real, verifiable offer; treat any everyday percentage as "call your local restaurant to confirm — not a corporate program."

### Intro
Applebee's is best known among the military community for its **Veterans Day free meal** — a long-running tradition (its 18th year in 2025) in which Veterans, Active Duty, Reserve, and National Guard members get a free entrée when they dine in on November 11. There is **no nationwide everyday military discount published by Applebee's**; because the brand is franchised, some individual restaurants may extend a courtesy discount, but that is a local decision, not a corporate program.

To redeem the Veterans Day meal, you simply dine in at a participating U.S. Applebee's on November 11 and show proof of service — no app, code, or third-party verification service required. If you want an everyday discount, call your nearest Applebee's first and ask whether they offer one; do not assume the "10% off" figure that circulates on coupon sites is in effect at your location.

This is an independent guide. NavyWeek is not affiliated with Applebee's. Applebee's controls its offers and can change them at any time; verify current terms on Applebee's official site before you go.

### How To Redeem Online
There is **no online military discount.** The Veterans Day free meal is dine-in only and cannot be claimed online. (The separate $5 Bounce Back Card given on Veterans Day can later be redeemed online via applebees.com or the Applebee's app, within its stated redemption window.)

### How To Redeem In Store (Veterans Day, November 11)
1. Find a participating U.S. Applebee's at applebees.com/restaurants (call ahead to confirm participation).
2. Dine in on November 11.
3. Tell your server you'd like the Veterans Day free meal and choose an entrée from that year's exclusive Veterans Day menu.
4. Show proof of military service (see "How Verification Works").
5. Receive your free entrée (limit one per qualifying guest) and your $5 Bounce Back Card for a future visit.

*Everyday discount (if any): call your local Applebee's, confirm they offer a military discount, then present a valid military ID/proof of service at the table. This is not guaranteed and varies by location.*

### How Verification Works
There is **no third-party verification service** (no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID) and no account requirement. On Veterans Day, you verify in person by showing **proof of military service**. Applebee's 2025 terms list acceptable proof as: U.S. Uniformed Services ID Card; U.S. Uniformed Services Retired ID Card; current Leave and Earnings Statement (LES); a Veterans organization card (e.g., American Legion, VFW); a photograph of yourself in uniform; wearing your uniform; or a DD214 with citation or commendation. For any everyday/location discount, the restaurant decides what proof it accepts — ask when you call.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
*(Veterans Day offer, per Applebee's official 2025 terms)*

- Everyday/location discounts (where offered) carry their own restaurant-set terms; Applebee's publishes none corporately.

### FAQ

#### Does Applebee's offer a military discount?
Applebee's does not publish a nationwide, everyday military discount. Its one official military offer is the **annual Veterans Day free meal** on November 11 for Veterans and Active Duty Military who dine in. Some individual franchise locations may offer a courtesy discount, but that's a local decision — call ahead to confirm. [Source: Applebee's press release, Oct 27, 2025.]

#### How much is the Applebee's military discount?
On Veterans Day (Nov 11), the offer is a **free full-size entrée** (from a limited menu) plus a **$5 Bounce Back Card** — not a percentage. The "10% off everyday" figure seen on some coupon sites is **not published by Applebee's** and, per WeSalute, varies by location and isn't guaranteed. [Sources: Applebee's press release; WeSalute.]

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
For the Veterans Day free meal, **Veterans, Active Duty, Reserves, and National Guard** qualify. Applebee's official terms do **not** list military spouses or dependents as eligible for the free meal. [Source: Applebee's press release, Oct 27, 2025.]

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person at the restaurant by showing proof of service — e.g., a U.S. Uniformed Services (or Retired) ID Card, current LES, a Veterans organization card (American Legion, VFW), a DD214 with citation/commendation, or a photo of yourself in uniform. No online verification or account is needed. [Source: Applebee's press release, Oct 27, 2025.]

#### Does Applebee's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Applebee's does not use any third-party verification service for its military offer. Verification is done in person by showing proof of service. (WeSalute lists Applebee's only as a community deal, not an official partner offer.) [Sources: Applebee's press release; WeSalute disclaimer.]

#### Can I use the discount in stores / dine-in?
Yes — and only in store. The Veterans Day free meal is **dine-in only** at participating U.S. locations. It cannot be redeemed for takeout or delivery. [Source: Applebee's press release, Oct 27, 2025.]

#### What is excluded?
For the Veterans Day meal: beverages and gratuity aren't included, it's dine-in only, it can't be combined with other coupons or discounts, and there's a limit of one free meal per qualifying guest. The free entrée must come from that year's Veterans Day menu. [Source: Applebee's press release, Oct 27, 2025.]

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Applebee's states the Veterans Day offer is **not valid with other coupons or discounts**, and the $5 Bounce Back Card is likewise not valid with other offers. [Source: Applebee's press release, Oct 27, 2025.]

#### Does Applebee's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Applebee's does not publish any corporate first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount. As with the military discount, individual franchise locations may offer something at their discretion — call your local restaurant to ask. [Source: Not stated in official source; absence confirmed across applebees.com.]

#### Is the Applebee's Veterans Day free meal happening this year?
Applebee's has run the Veterans Day free-meal tradition for 18 consecutive years (through 2025) and confirms it annually each fall via a press release. Check applebees.com/veterans-day-free-meals or Applebee's newsroom in late October for that year's confirmation and menu. [Source: Applebee's press release, Oct 27, 2025.]
