# Nordstrom Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Nordstrom does not offer a standing military or veteran discount.** As of June 23, 2026, there is no dedicated military/veteran discount on Nordstrom's official site, and Nordstrom does not publish any military offer, percentage, code, or verification flow (ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute) on its own pages. Claims of "20% off," "15% off," or "50% off" for service members circulate only on coupon-aggregator and affiliate sites; none trace to a Nordstrom primary source, they contradict each other, and they should be treated as unverified. The same applies to Nordstrom Rack (the off-price division), which also has no published year-round military discount. The genuine, verifiable ways for military shoppers to save at Nordstrom are the ones open to everyone: the free **Nordy Club** rewards program (1 point per $1, 1,000 points = a $10 Nordstrom Note), seasonal sales and clearance (including the **Nordstrom Anniversary Sale**), and the **Nordstrom Rack** off-price channel. The single most important caveat: do not promise a military discount — none is confirmed by Nordstrom.
