# Choice Hotels Military, Veteran & Government Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Choice Hotels runs a **real, bookable military program** in **two parts**: (1) a special **military/veteran leisure rate** (it varies by property and date, and — unlike many discount rates — **still earns points**), and (2) free **limited-time Gold Elite** status **plus 2,500 bonus points** in Choice Privileges. Because hotels are rate-based, the "discount" is a variable gap (the public Best Available Rate minus the military rate), **never a flat percentage** — so this page does **not publish a single "Choice military discount %."** The headline value is the **status**, not the nightly rate: active duty, reserve, retired, National Guard, and Coast Guard can claim free **limited-time Gold Elite** + 2,500 bonus points by adding military status to a Choice Privileges account. While it's active, that status **stacks on every future booking**; the rate discounts (military leisure rate, WeSalute 15%, Pay Now & Save) are **mutually exclusive** — price-check and book the lowest. Two under-reported facts: the military rate still earns points, and **you must be a Choice Privileges member and book direct** (OTA bookings forfeit both the rate and the status/points).

> Status correction (critical): Choice has **replaced the former lifetime status with a limited-time benefit**. The status is **limited-time Gold Elite** — claim it **while the offer lasts**. Do **not** describe it as lifetime, permanent, or forever; the lifetime promo expired. (This record was corrected on June 23, 2026 to reflect the downgrade.)

### FAQ

#### Does Choice Hotels have a military discount?
Yes — in two parts. Choice Hotels offers a special military/veteran leisure rate (it varies by property and date and still earns points), and free limited-time Gold Elite status plus 2,500 bonus points in Choice Privileges for active duty, reserve, retired, National Guard, and Coast Guard members. Because hotels are rate-based, there is no flat "military discount percentage" — the reliable wins are the free limited-time Gold status and, for members, the WeSalute 15%.

#### What is Choice Privileges limited-time Gold Elite for military, and how do I claim it?
It's free, limited-time Gold Elite status (bonus points, late checkout, and more) plus 2,500 bonus points for active duty, reserve, retired, National Guard, and Coast Guard members. To claim it, sign in to your Choice Privileges account and add your military status under your account/military settings — about two minutes. It's status, not a rate, so while it's active it layers on top of every booking regardless of which rate you pay. Choice has replaced the former lifetime version with this limited-time benefit, so claim it while the offer lasts.

#### How much is the Choice Hotels military rate — what percentage?
There isn't a fixed percentage. Hotels are rate-based, so the military/veteran leisure rate is a discounted rate that varies by property and date and is availability-capped — on a $360 / 3-night baseline it often lands around $300–325 (illustrative, as of June 16, 2026). Don't trust any site quoting a single "Choice military discount %." Instead, price-check the military rate against WeSalute 15% and Pay Now & Save and book the lowest.

#### Do Comfort Inn, Quality Inn, and other Choice brands offer the military rate?
Yes — the program runs across the Choice umbrella, which includes Comfort Inn and Comfort Suites, Quality Inn, Sleep Inn, Clarion, Cambria, MainStay Suites, Suburban, Econo Lodge, Rodeway Inn, the Ascend Hotel Collection, and WoodSpring/Everhome Suites. The military/veteran leisure rate, the government/official rate, and limited-time Gold Elite are tied to Choice Privileges, so they apply when you book a participating brand direct — availability of the rate still varies by individual property and date.

#### Does the Choice military rate still earn points?
Yes — and that's an under-reported advantage. Unlike many discount rates that strip loyalty earning, Choice's military/veteran leisure rate still earns Choice Privileges points and airline rewards. Combined with Gold Elite (which boosts your earning), that makes the military rate more valuable than its nightly sticker alone. WeSalute's 15% rate also still earns points.

#### What's the difference between the military leisure rate and the government rate?
The military/veteran leisure rate is for personal travel (active duty, veterans, dependents, and certain military associations — an eligible person must occupy the room). The government/military official rate is for official-business travel by government and military employees and contractors, often at or near the federal per-diem. They're on the same Government & Military Rates page — use the one that matches your trip, and bring proof of service for check-in.

#### Can I book the Choice military discount through Expedia or another travel site?
No. You must be a Choice Privileges member and book direct on choicehotels.com or the Choice app. Third-party OTA bookings (Expedia, Booking.com, etc.) forfeit both the military rate and your points/status — it's the most common way travelers accidentally lose the benefit. Book direct, sign in, and show proof of service at check-in.

#### Is the WeSalute 15% worth it at Choice Hotels?
It can be — for members or heavy travelers. WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage) gives a flat 15% off at Choice and still earns points; on a $360 stay that's about $306. But it needs a paid WeSalute+ membership (~$10/mo) and can't combine with the non-refundable Pay Now & Save rate. It doesn't stack with the military leisure rate, so compare the two for your stay and book whichever is lower after membership.
