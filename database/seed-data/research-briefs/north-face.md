# The North Face Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

The North Face offers a **10% military discount** to all active, reservist, veteran, and retired U.S. military personnel, plus spouses and dependents of active personnel. Eligibility is verified through **SheerID** (not ID.me). After you verify, SheerID issues a **single-use promo code** good for one discounted purchase on thenorthface.com; in store, you instead show a valid military ID at the register. The discount works at both full-price stores and outlets and is **United States only**. The single most important caveat: it is **not a permanent saved discount** — each code is single-use and you may verify **once every 30 days** to get a new code, so plan one qualifying order per code. The North Face's separate **XPLR Pass** loyalty program is free to join but is **not required** for the military discount.

> Accuracy note: Some aggregator and editorial pages say verification runs through "SheerID or ID.me." The North Face's own military discount page names **SheerID only** as its verification partner, and the "Verify Military Status" button points to a SheerID URL. Treat **SheerID** as the verified provider; ID.me is not confirmed by the official source.

### How To Redeem In Store

### How Verification Works
The North Face uses **SheerID** as its verification partner. Online, you click "Verify Military Status," then submit your name, branch of service, current affiliation, and discharge date (if applicable). If SheerID can't instantly confirm you, it may ask for documents — e.g., a recent LES or current orders (active/reservist); DD-214, VA ID, VA benefits letter, discharge certificate, or a veteran-designated driver's license (veteran); DD-214 or retirement orders (retiree); or DEERS/TRICARE documentation (military family). The North Face notes SheerID will not contact you outside of requests tied to your verification. A successful online verification produces a **single-use promo code**; for in-store purchases, verification is done at the register by showing a valid military ID. For verification problems, The North Face points to SheerID support (support.sheerid.com).

Yes. The North Face offers a 10% military discount to active, reservist, veteran, and retired U.S. military personnel, plus spouses and dependents of active personnel, verified through SheerID.
