# Phoenix Zoo — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult daytime general admission (~$34.95 at the gate; day-of price varies by date/season — verify before quoting).

## Headline finding

The Phoenix Zoo offers an **everyday $5 off general admission** to military and veterans (ages 18+) with valid photo ID — **at the gate only, not on online/advance tickets, and not combinable** with other offers. The bigger wins are the **holiday programs**: **free admission on Veterans Day and on Memorial Day** for active-duty and veterans (with military ID or DD214), and Memorial Day additionally gives **$10 off** for up to four guests in the party. Verification is simple **in-person ID**, not a digital provider. The Phoenix Zoo is a nonprofit (Arizona Center for Nature Conservation), so terms are set locally and can change each year.
