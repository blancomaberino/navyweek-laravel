## Headline finding

There is **no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID** military discount on 1st Phorm supplements. Any site advertising a "1st Phorm military discount code," "first responder 70% off," or "10% off coupon" is promoting something 1st Phorm says is **not legitimate**. The only genuine 1st Phorm savings are structural: the **110% money-back guarantee**, **free shipping over $150**, a free-gift reward tier, and occasional brand-run sale events — all open to everyone, not gated to the military. Archetype: **flat-price DTC brand with a stated no-coupon policy and no military program.**
