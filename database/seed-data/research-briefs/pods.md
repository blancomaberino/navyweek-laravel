# PODS Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

PODS offers service members **up to 10% off a move** with promo code **SERV10**, claimed online with **ID.me** verification, at checkout, or by phone. It is one of the more flexible moving discounts because PODS extends it to **inactive service members too** — so **veterans and retirees qualify alongside active duty**, not just members with current orders. On a **local move**, the 10% comes off the **initial local delivery and your first month's rental**; on a **long-distance move between PODS company-owned and/or franchise locations**, it can also apply to **first month's storage and the transportation cost** (usually the biggest line on a container move). The discount is **"up to" 10%** — the exact amount can vary by current promotion and participating location — and it **cannot be combined** with any other PODS offer or promo code. A separate path exists for **USAA members via USAA Perks** (an alliance for savings on the move and monthly storage) — compare it against SERV10 rather than stack the two.

> Accuracy note: This is a live, confirmed program (audit verdict CONFIRMED, June 21, 2026). Always use the official **ID.me** flow to verify status — **never email or send a photocopy of a military ID or CAC**, which is not how legitimate verification works. The code value and terms rotate, so confirm the current code/terms on PODS' official military page before booking.

### How To Redeem Online
1. **Start your PODS quote** — build your move or storage quote at pods.com with your dates and container size.
2. **Verify with ID.me** — look for the "Verify with ID.me" option in your cart and confirm your military status (a free ID.me account takes a few minutes).
3. **Or apply promo code SERV10** — enter SERV10 at checkout, or mention it when ordering by phone, to apply up to 10% off.
4. **Confirm the discount before you pay** — check that the military discount shows on your order summary, since it cannot be combined with other offers.

### How To Redeem By Phone
1. **Call PODS to book by phone** — tell the agent you are military and want promo code SERV10 applied.
2. **Verify your service** — complete ID.me verification online, or follow the agent's instructions to confirm military status — **never email or send a photocopy of your military ID or CAC**.
3. **Schedule delivery and storage** — set your container delivery and (if needed) Storage Center dates with the military discount applied.

### How Verification Works
PODS verifies military status through **ID.me**, the third-party verifier — look for "Verify with ID.me" in your cart and confirm with a free ID.me account (a few minutes). Alternatively, apply **promo code SERV10** at checkout, or have a phone agent apply it. ID.me is the cleanest path because it confirms your status without you ever handing over a copy of your ID. **Never email or send a photocopy of a military ID or CAC** — that is not how legitimate verification works.

### FAQ

#### Does PODS offer a military discount?
Yes. PODS gives service members up to **10% off** a move with promo code **SERV10**, applied online with ID.me verification, at checkout, or by phone.

#### How much is the PODS military discount?
**Up to 10% off.** On a local move that is 10% off the initial delivery and first month's rental; on a long-distance move between PODS locations it can also apply to storage and transportation. The exact amount can vary by promotion.

#### Can veterans get the PODS discount, or only active duty?
Veterans and retirees qualify too — PODS extends the offer to **inactive service members**, not just active duty.

#### How do I claim the PODS military discount?
Use promo code **SERV10** at checkout, verify your military status with the "Verify with ID.me" option in your cart, or call PODS and ask an agent to apply it.

#### What does the 10% apply to?
For local moves, the initial local delivery and your first month's rental. For long-distance moves between PODS company-owned and/or franchise locations, it can also apply to first month's storage and the transportation cost.

#### Can I combine the military discount with other PODS offers?
No. The military discount cannot be combined with any other PODS offer or code, and it does not apply to extended delivery fees. Compare it against any general promotion running at the time.

#### Is there a discount for USAA members?
Yes, separately. USAA Perks has an alliance with PODS for savings on the move and monthly storage. It is a different path from SERV10 — compare the two rather than trying to stack them.
