# Bear Creek Arsenal — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $199.99 BCA complete AR-15 upper receiver (non-MAP, non-sale) shipped.

## Headline finding

Bear Creek Arsenal (BCA) runs a real, official **5% Service Member Discount**. It is a manual, email-verified program — you send proof of service to `servicemembers@bearcreekarsenal.com` and get an approval/code back — not an ID.me/GovX instant checkout. The discount is small and, critically, **does not apply to sale-priced items, MAP-priced items, FFL items, or non-BCA (third-party) products**, and it does not stack with other promotions. For most buyers the bigger lever is BCA's own frequent sitewide sales and clearance "blem"/overstock pricing, which routinely beat 5% — but you generally can't have both, so the decision is "5% off a full-price BCA part" vs. "a deeper public sale you can't stack the discount onto."

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The 5% discount **does not apply to sale items** — so you cannot "sale + military" stack. It is one or the other.
2. **MAP-priced and FFL products are excluded** — manufacturer/legal price floors override the discount.
3. **Non-BCA (third-party brand) products are excluded** — the discount is BCA-house-brand only.
4. Credit-card cashback and shopping-portal cashback are independent of BCA's terms and apply on top of whatever price you pay.

### FAQ
#### Does Bear Creek Arsenal offer a military discount?
Yes — an official 5% Service Member Discount for verified military, veterans, and first responders.

#### How much is the Bear Creek Arsenal military discount?
5% off eligible BCA products. It does not apply to sale, MAP, FFL, or non-BCA items.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify with proof of service. Spouse/dependent eligibility is not stated on BCA's page — do not assume it.

#### How do I verify my status?
Email proof of service to `servicemembers@bearcreekarsenal.com` and wait for an approval notification.

#### Does Bear Creek Arsenal use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Verification is a manual email review by BCA, not a third-party provider.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — on bearcreekarsenal.com, the BCA mobile app, and participating retail locations once approved.

#### What is excluded?
Sale-priced items, MAP-priced items, FFL/serialized firearms, and non-BCA (third-party) products.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The 5% does not stack and does not apply to sale items.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Bear Creek Arsenal?
Usually a live public sitewide sale or clearance/"blem" price — these typically beat 5% and are open to all. Use the 5% only on full-price items when no sale is running, and always pay with a rewards card.

#### Does Bear Creek Arsenal offer a first responder or law-enforcement discount?
Yes — the Service Member program language includes police, paramedics, and other first responders alongside military.
