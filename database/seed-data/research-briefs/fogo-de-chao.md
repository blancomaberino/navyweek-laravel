# Fogo de Chão — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **dinner for a veteran + one guest**, both ordering the **Full Churrasco** at **~$79/person = $158 food subtotal** (pre-tax, pre-beverage, pre-tip; Full Churrasco dinner runs roughly $69–$79/person and is location-priced — Fogo publishes no universal price).

## Headline finding

Fogo de Chão has **no everyday military or veteran discount.** What it runs is a **holiday-only appreciation offer**: on **Veterans Day** (and again on **Memorial Day**), active-duty service members, veterans, and military retirees get **50% off their own Full or Indulgent Churrasco**, and **up to three accompanying guests get 10% off** — **dine-in only, at participating locations, with a valid military ID** shown at the table. There is no promo code, no online redemption, and no ID.me/GovX/SheerID verification — you just present your ID. Archetype **H (the "myth" brand)**: aggregators invent a flat "10–25% everyday military discount" that does not exist; the real everyday value is Fogo's **loyalty wallet (Fogo Rewards)**.

The counterintuitive part: for a **two-person meal**, Fogo Rewards' **2nd-visit "complimentary Full Churrasco with the purchase of one at full price"** (a free entrée, available any day of the year) can **beat the Veterans Day 50%-off** — because a free full meal for the guest ($79 off) saves more than "50% off yours + 10% off theirs." So a veteran dining as a couple often does better with the free Rewards churrasco than with the holiday discount, and only the **solo** veteran or a **larger party** clearly wins with the 50% Veterans Day offer. Discounted **gift cards** (warehouse clubs periodically sell Fogo gift cards below face value) sit *under* any of these as a payment layer. Ignore the coupon sites: the "up to 25% military discount" and "15% promo code" lines are not real Fogo military offers.

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **The holiday 50% offer is a single dine-in discount** applied to the service member's own Full/Indulgent Churrasco; **guests get a separate 10%** (up to 3). No source says it combines with Rewards, birthday, or prix-fixe offers — treat it as **one offer per meal**; confirm with the restaurant.
2. **Fogo Rewards' 2nd-visit complimentary churrasco requires the paired meal at full price** — so you can't also take 50% off that paid churrasco. It's a BOGO, not a stack. Pick it *or* the holiday 50%.
3. **The $25 birthday reward excludes Market Table only, one Churrasco Cut, and Brazilian Cuts menu** — it applies to a Full Churrasco/Entrée and is one reward, not stackable with the BOGO.
4. **Discounted gift cards are a payment method, not a coupon** — they reduce out-of-pocket **under any of the above** without conflicting with them. (Verify current warehouse-club availability/rate; it's intermittent.)
5. **No promo code exists** — any "Fogo military code" from a coupon site is fabricated. Redemption is a **military ID at the table**, in person, participating locations.

### FAQ

#### Does Fogo de Chão offer a military discount?
Yes, but **only on Veterans Day and Memorial Day** — there is **no everyday military discount**. On those holidays, active-duty members, veterans, and retirees get **50% off** their Full/Indulgent Churrasco and up to **3 guests get 10% off**, dine-in with a military ID.

#### How much is the Fogo de Chão military discount?
**50% off** your own Full or Indulgent Churrasco, plus **10% off** for up to three guests — on the holiday windows only. Coupon-site claims of a flat "10–25% everyday military discount" are not real.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Active duty, **veterans, and military retirees** get the 50%. **Spouses and dependents** dining with them get the **10% guest rate** (up to 3 guests), not the 50%.

#### How do I verify my military status at Fogo?
Show a **valid military ID to your server** at a participating restaurant. There's **no online verification and no promo code**.

#### Does Fogo de Chão use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Fogo uses none of these — verification is a **military ID shown in person**.

#### Can I use the military discount online or for takeout?
**No — dine-in only**, at participating locations.

#### What's excluded?
Non-participating locations, takeout/delivery/online orders, and non-churrasco items for the 50%. The offer is one per meal and doesn't combine with Fogo Rewards freebies.

#### Can I combine it with Fogo Rewards or a promo code?
No — it's **one offer per meal**. There's no military code, and the holiday 50% doesn't stack with the Rewards complimentary churrasco. You can, however, **pay with discounted gift cards** under any offer.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at Fogo?
**Solo on Veterans/Memorial Day:** the 50% off (~$39.50 on a $79 Full Churrasco). **As a couple any day:** the Fogo Rewards **2nd-visit complimentary churrasco** (two eat for ~$79) usually beats the holiday deal.

#### Does Fogo offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
**No documented discount** for those groups — don't assume one. The verified offer is military-only, holiday-only.

#### Does Fogo run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day meal deal every year?
It has run both most years (50% + 10% for guests). The **exact date/window changes yearly** (2025 Veterans Day ran Nov 7–11), so confirm ~2 weeks ahead with your location. Never assume a future date is locked.
