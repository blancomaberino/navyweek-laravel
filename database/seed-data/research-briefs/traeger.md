## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **The ID.me discount is single-use per purchase and does not combine** with other promotions or discounts ("Restrictions may apply when combining the ID.me discount with other discounts or promotions") — treat it as **either/or** vs. any sale.
2. **Seasonal/retailer sale pricing generally beats and blocks the ID.me %** — when Home Depot or traeger.com marks a model down $100–$300, the ID.me code won't layer on top; buy whichever is lower.
3. **The military exchange is a separate channel** — its tax-free price is not an ID.me stack; you use one or the other. The exchange advantage is **no sales tax + free shipping + MILITARY STAR**, not a percentage code.
4. **Retailer military discounts (Home Depot ~10%, Ace ~10%) do not stack with Traeger's ID.me discount** (different sellers) and frequently **exclude Traeger** entirely — verify at the store.
5. **MEATER products and most sauces/rubs are excluded** from the ID.me discount regardless of channel.
6. **No public Traeger promo code** applies alongside the ID.me discount; it's applied automatically in-cart after verification.
7. **Cashback:** ID.me-gated checkouts typically break cashback-portal tracking; not verified this pass — don't rely on a portal stacking with the ID.me discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Traeger offer a military discount?
Yes. Traeger offers a first-party military discount on **traeger.com**, verified through **ID.me** — **up to 20% off select grills and 15% off select accessories** — for active duty, veterans, reservists, retirees, and their spouses/dependents.

#### How much is the Traeger military discount?
**Up to 20% off select grills and 15% off select accessories.** The "up to 20%" means only certain models get the full 20% — verify the exact grill in your cart after signing in with ID.me.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — Traeger's discount covers active duty, veterans, reservists, retirees, and their spouses and dependents, verified through ID.me. Honorably discharged veterans can also shop the exchanges tax-free online.

#### How do I verify my status?
On traeger.com, click **"Verify with ID.me"** at the bottom of your cart in checkout, sign in or create a free ID.me account, and verify once. Qualifying items then show discounted automatically.

#### Does Traeger use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?

#### Can I use the discount in stores or only online?
It's **online only** on traeger.com and limited to **US and Canada residents** — it's not valid in Traeger stores or at dealers.

#### What is excluded?
**MEATER products** are excluded, only limited accessories/sauces/rubs are included, and the discount is single-use and **won't combine** with sales or promo codes.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale items?
No. The ID.me discount is single-use and doesn't stack on sales or other codes. Because Traeger runs frequent $100–$300 seasonal markdowns, a public sale often beats the ID.me discount — buy whichever is lower.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Traeger?

#### Does Traeger offer a first responder, nurse, or teacher discount?
Yes — Traeger extends the same up-to-20%-grills / 15%-accessories offer to **nurses, first responders, and teachers** through ID.me.

#### Can I buy Traeger at the Exchange?
Yes — AAFES (shopmyexchange.com) and the Navy Exchange (mynavyexchange.com) stock Traeger models **tax-free with free shipping** for eligible shoppers. That tax savings can beat a small ID.me percentage.

#### Does Traeger run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Traeger and its retailers run frequent seasonal sales (Memorial Day, July 4, Black Friday), but no documented *extra* military-holiday Traeger promo was found this pass. Watch for standard markdowns around those windows.
