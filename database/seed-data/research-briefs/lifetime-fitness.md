# Life Time (Lifetime Fitness) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** joining one Life Time athletic country club — the **one-time initiation/enrollment fee** (Life Time does not publish standard initiation or monthly dues; they vary by club and membership tier, so figures below are structural, not fixed dollar amounts).

## Headline finding

Life Time announced a **standing, nationwide military rate in 2019: 50% off standard initiation fees** for **all active and retired military and all veterans (including Reserve and National Guard)**, extended to **military personnel and family members in their immediate household**, and to Canadian clubs/armed forces. The benefit is **verified in club with a valid military ID or DD Form 214** — **not** through ID.me (ID.me's own page confirms it is "not aware that Lifetime Fitness offers Military discounts" via ID.me, so the aggregator "authenticate through ID.me" claim is wrong). The important caveats: this is a **premium club** that **does not publish initiation fees or dues** (rates vary widely by location and tier), Life Time **no longer maintains a live "military discount" web page** (the old URL 404s), and the discount is on the **initiation/enrollment fee — not monthly dues** — so the real dollar value depends entirely on your club's current enrollment fee. Archetype: **E (identity discount) with J-style club-level discretion** — a real, documented rate, but confirm it and the current fee **at your specific club**.

#### Does Life Time (Lifetime Fitness) offer a military discount?
Yes — a standing **military rate of 50% off the standard initiation fee** for active, retired, and veteran military (including Reserve and National Guard), extended to immediate-household family. It's verified in club.

#### How much is the Life Time military discount?
**50% off the initiation/enrollment fee** — not monthly dues. Because Life Time doesn't publish its fees (they vary by club and tier), ask your local club for the current enrollment fee to know the exact dollar value.

#### Do veterans, Guard/Reserve, and family qualify?
Yes — all active and retired military and all veterans, including Reserve and National Guard, plus military personnel and family members in their immediate household.

**In club**, with a valid military ID or DD Form 214. There is no online verification.

No — it's **50% off the initiation (enrollment) fee**. Monthly dues are not discounted under the documented rate.

The rate was announced as nationwide (US and Canada), but pricing and current availability are **club-specific** — confirm with your location.
