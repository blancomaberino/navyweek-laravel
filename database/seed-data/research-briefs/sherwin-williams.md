# Sherwin-Williams — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $300 in-store paint order (e.g., 4 gallons of premium interior paint + rollers/supplies) at a Sherwin-Williams retail store.

## Headline finding

Sherwin-Williams offers a strong, clearly published, evergreen **15% off Military Discount on paints, stains, and painting supplies** — **in-store only** at Sherwin-Williams retail paint stores in the US, Puerto Rico, and Canada, applied by showing a **valid military ID** (no ID.me/GovX/SheerID needed). It extends to **spouses**. Each **May (Military Appreciation Month)** the brand historically stacks **an extra 10% off paints & stains** on top. The key fine print: it's **not combinable** with other coupons/promos, and **sale pricing that yields greater savings supersedes it** (you get the deeper of the two). Archetype: **G-adjacent (real in-store service/retail discount, holiday boost)** — but simpler than auto-service: no membership tier, no tax-free kicker, just a clean 15% at the register.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. The 15% **cannot be combined** with any other offer, percentage-off coupon, promotion, discount, or special-price program.

4. **Preferred Customers (PaintPerks)** get a standard 10% — a separate everyday tier, not additive to the 15%.
5. Discount is taken off **list price**.

### Who Qualifies
- Currently serving, reservists, or veterans of: US Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, National Guard, ROTC; Royal Canadian Navy, Canadian Army, Royal Canadian Air Force — eligible.
- **Spouses** of eligible members — eligible (must present a distinct military ID).
- Must show an ID validating military service at time of purchase.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop paints, stains, and painting supplies at a Sherwin-Williams retail paint store.
2. At checkout, present your valid military ID (spouses present their distinct military ID).
3. The 15% is applied to list price on eligible items.
4. In May, ask about the additional 10% off paints & stains for Military Appreciation Month.

### How Verification Works
Verification is a simple **military ID check at the register** — there's no third-party portal (ID.me/GovX/SheerID), no account, and no code. This makes it low-friction but **in-store only**; there's no confirmed online military-discount path.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **In-store only**; retail (personal) purchases only.
- Not combinable with any other offer, % coupon, promotion, discount, or special-price program.
- A sale/offer that yields greater savings **supersedes** the 15%.
- Excludes: previous purchases; gift cards; Multi-Purpose primers (US); wallcovering; window treatments; floorcovering; spray equipment; installations; HGTV HOME™ by Sherwin-Williams Ovation®.
- Discount taken off list price. Other exclusions may apply — see store for details.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Buy paint in May** to stack the extra 10% on paints & stains.
- **Compare to live sales** — if a public promo is deeper than 15%, it supersedes and saves you more.
- **PaintPerks** membership adds coupons/rewards over time (non-military 10% base tier).
- For **pro/contractor volume**, ask your store rep about pro/contractor pricing (separate from the retail military 15%).

### FAQ

#### Does Sherwin-Williams offer a military discount?
Yes — 15% off paints, stains, and painting supplies, in-store, for military members, reservists, veterans, and spouses.

#### How much is the Sherwin-Williams military discount?
15% off (off list price), on eligible paints, stains, and supplies.

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Yes. Currently serving, reservists, and veterans qualify, and the discount extends to their spouses (with a distinct military ID).

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a valid military ID at the register. There's no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID step.

#### Can I use it online?
No — the terms specify Sherwin-Williams retail paint stores only. There's no verified online military-discount path.

#### Can I combine it with coupons or sales?
No. It's not combinable, but if a sale is deeper than 15%, that sale supersedes it — you always get the bigger discount.

#### Does Sherwin-Williams run a Military Appreciation (May) promo?
Historically yes — an additional 10% off paints & stains during May, on top of the year-round 15%. Verify dates each May.

#### What's excluded?
Primers (US Multi-Purpose), wallcovering, window treatments, floorcovering, spray equipment, installations, gift cards, previous purchases, and HGTV HOME™ Ovation®.

#### What's the everyday 10% I've seen mentioned?
That's the PaintPerks Preferred Customer rate (non-military). The military benefit is the higher 15%.
