# B&H Photo Video — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$1,999 camera/lens purchase (a big-ticket order where B&H's real savings lever — sales tax — actually moves the needle).

## Headline finding

**B&H Photo Video has no military or veteran discount — there is no official B&H military program, no percentage-off military code, and no ID.me/GovX/SheerID/WeSalute partner.** Aggregator pages advertising "B&H military discount 25% with code `COUPONDISCOUNT`" or "23% off military" are fabricated; B&H sells most gear at manufacturer-set (MAP) prices and does not issue blanket percentage-off coupons.

The single biggest way a service member (or anyone) saves at B&H is **not a discount at all — it's the B&H Payboo credit card, which instantly reimburses the sales tax on your order.** On a $1,999 camera in an ~8.5% tax state that's about **$170 back, immediately**, which beats any realistic "military %" a competitor might offer and is available to any approved applicant (not military-specific). Layer that with B&H's standing perks — **free expedited shipping over $49, price matching, the B&H Rewards points program, and the B&H Used department** — and you have the honest maximum-savings path. Archetype: **B (no military route)** where the real lever is a **tax-reimbursement store card** open to everyone. B&H also runs a charitable **Veterans Portrait Project** (free portraits) — a goodwill event, not a discount.
