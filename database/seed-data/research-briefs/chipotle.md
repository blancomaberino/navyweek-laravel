# Chipotle Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Chipotle Mexican Grill does **not** offer an everyday (year-round) military or veteran discount. There is no permanent percentage off, no ID.me/SheerID/GovX/WeSalute partnership, and no standing in-store discount confirmed by any primary source — ID.me's own Chipotle page states it is "not aware that Chipotle offers Military discounts." What Chipotle does offer is a **limited-time Veterans Day promotion**: on **Tuesday, November 11, 2025, 4:00–8:00 p.m. local time**, guests who order an entrée in-restaurant and present a valid government-issued military ID receive a **second entrée free (BOGO)** at participating U.S. locations. The single most important caveat: this is in-restaurant only (no mobile, online, delivery, or catering), valid only during that 4-hour window, and Chipotle runs it at its discretion year to year — it did **not** run a Veterans Day BOGO in 2024. Anyone seeking everyday savings should use Chipotle Rewards instead.

### How To Redeem Online
Not available. The Veterans Day offer is explicitly **not valid for mobile, online, or delivery orders**. There is no online or app-based military discount to redeem. (For everyday online ordering, the only official savings channel is earning/redeeming Chipotle Rewards points.)

### How To Redeem In Store (Veterans Day offer, when running)
1. Go to a participating U.S. Chipotle restaurant on Veterans Day, between 4 p.m. and 8 p.m. local time.
2. Order an entrée in-restaurant (bowl, burrito, salad, or tacos).
3. Present your valid government-issued military ID at the register.
4. Receive a second entrée of equal or lesser value free (up to five free entrées per check, each requiring a paid entrée of equal or greater value).
5. Note kids' meals do not count as a qualifying entrée purchase.

### How Verification Works
There is no third-party verification service. For the Veterans Day offer, verification is done in person at the register by presenting a valid government-issued military ID. The free entrée may be collected only by the customer holding that ID. There is no account creation, no annual re-verification, and no online flow — because there is no standing online discount. If a location declines the offer or it isn't honored, the only recourse is to confirm with that store; Chipotle notes the offer is at "participating U.S. locations" and "subject to availability."

### FAQ

#### Does Chipotle offer a military discount?
Not on an everyday basis. Chipotle has no year-round military or veteran discount. It runs a limited-time Veterans Day promotion in some years — most recently a buy-one-get-one-free entrée offer on November 11, 2025, in-restaurant only.

#### How much is the Chipotle military discount?
There is no everyday discount, so there is no standing percentage or dollar amount. The Veterans Day 2025 offer was effectively one free entrée (BOGO) with a paid entrée of equal or greater value, up to five free entrées per check.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
For the Veterans Day 2025 offer, Chipotle defined eligibility as "all guests with a valid military ID." It did not separately list spouses or dependents. If you hold a valid government-issued military ID, you qualify; for spouses/dependents specifically, the official source does not address it — confirm with the location.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person only. Show a valid government-issued military ID at the register during the offer window. There is no online or app verification.

#### Does Chipotle use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Chipotle is not partnered with any of these for a military discount. ID.me's own Chipotle page states it is "not aware that Chipotle offers Military discounts."

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
The Veterans Day offer is in-restaurant only — that's the only channel where any military offer applies. It is not valid for mobile, online, delivery, or catering orders. There is no everyday in-store discount.

#### What is excluded?
For the Veterans Day offer: anything outside the 4–8 p.m. local window on Nov 11, 2025; catering, mobile, online, and delivery orders; more than five free entrées per check; and kids' meals (which don't count as a qualifying entrée). It generally can't be combined with other coupons or promotions.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The offer "may not otherwise be combined with other coupons, promotions, or special offers." However, purchased entrées do still earn Chipotle Rewards points.

#### Does Chipotle offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No such discounts are stated in any official Chipotle source. The only military-community offer found is the limited-time Veterans Day promotion; the everyday savings option for everyone is Chipotle Rewards.

#### Does Chipotle run the Veterans Day deal every year?
Not guaranteed. Chipotle offered the BOGO in 2025 and in several prior years, but did not run a Veterans Day military offer in 2024. Always check Chipotle's newsroom or app for the current year before planning a visit.
