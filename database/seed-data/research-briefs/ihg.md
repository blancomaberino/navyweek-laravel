# IHG — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 2-night leisure stay at a $150/night Best Flexible Rate = **$300 pre-tax** (representative mid-tier Holiday Inn / Holiday Inn Express nightly rate, as of July 12, 2026). Effective prices exclude occupancy/sales tax except where noted.

## Headline finding

IHG (Holiday Inn, Holiday Inn Express, Crowne Plaza, Kimpton, InterContinental, Staybridge, Candlewood, Hotel Indigo, etc.) runs **two distinct** first-party programs, and conflating them is the #1 mistake:

1. **Military Leisure Rate** — a genuine, evergreen **leisure** discount, "a minimum discount of 5% off Best Flexible Rate" (varies by hotel, availability-limited), open to **US and Canadian active duty, veterans, retired military, and family members with valid ID**. This is the one veterans can use for a vacation. Valid military/veteran ID required at check-in.
2. **Government Rate** — an **official-travel** rate (a hotel-controlled percentage off Best Flex, often aligned toward the GSA per-diem cap) for government employees, military personnel on orders, defense/CRC contractors, and state-funded universities/healthcare. Requires **official ID or work orders at check-in** — it is not a general veteran leisure discount.

This is the classic **hotel-loyalty archetype**: status and points layer on almost any rate, but the *rates themselves are mutually exclusive* — the military leisure rate cannot be combined with other offers, promo codes, or reward nights. Because the military minimum is only ~5%, IHG's own **advance-purchase / member rates frequently beat it outright** (they routinely run 10–20% under Best Flex). Best everyday leisure play: **compare the military leisure rate against the member advance-purchase rate on your exact dates and book the lower one** — while still earning IHG One Rewards points either way. Separately, **IHG Army Hotels** (on-base lodging) is a different animal entirely and the leisure rate does *not* apply there.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The Military Leisure Rate cannot be combined with any other offer, discount, or promotion** — including promo codes and **reward (points) nights** (per IHG's military-leisure-rate terms). It's a rate, not a stackable coupon.
2. **The Government Rate is "not applicable with other discounted-rate promotions" and "not applicable to group bookings"** (per IHG's government-rate terms). Blackout dates apply; certain room types excluded.
3. **Rates are mutually exclusive; status and points are not.** You book exactly one rate per reservation, but **IHG One Rewards points still accrue** on qualifying military/government cash rates, and elite status benefits still apply — this is the hotel-loyalty pattern (status layers, rates don't).
4. **No cashback-portal stack worth banking.** Hotel bookings made direct on IHG.com are the reliable path; there's no verified Rakuten/portal + IHG-military-rate combo that survives IHG's "no other offer" rate terms. Treat any such claim as unverified.
5. **IHG Army Hotels is a closed on-base channel** — the public military leisure rate does not apply there, and access requires base entry + military ID.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military (US/Canadian) — eligible for Military Leisure Rate
- Veterans — eligible for Military Leisure Rate (with valid veteran ID)
- Retired military — eligible for Military Leisure Rate
- Military family members — eligible ("family members with valid ID")
- Government employees (federal/state/county/local) — eligible for **Government Rate** (official travel; ID/orders)
- Military on official orders / defense & CRC contractors — eligible for **Government Rate**
- State-funded universities & healthcare facilities — eligible for **Government Rate**
- IHG Army Hotels guests — must have base access + military ID (separate on-base program; leisure rate does not apply)

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online (IHG.com or IHG app):**
1. Search your hotel and dates on IHG.com or the IHG app.
2. In the rate/room list, choose the **"Government/Military Rate"** (select the veteran/retiree option where shown via the "i" info icon).
3. Book direct. Bring your **valid military or veteran ID** to present at check-in.

**By phone:**
- Military leisure booking help: 1-877-834-3613. Government rate: 1-877-424-2449. (Per-diem/official-orders stays are often easiest to confirm by phone.)

**At check-in (required):**
- Military Leisure Rate: show valid military or veteran ID.
- Government Rate: show official government/military ID **or work orders**. Without proper ID, the discount cannot be given.

### How Verification Works
IHG does **not** use an ID.me/SheerID-style online pre-verification for these rates. You self-select the Government/Military rate at booking, and eligibility is **verified in person at the front desk** by ID (military/veteran ID for the leisure rate; official ID or work orders for the government rate). The reservation should be in the name of the eligible ID holder. There is no re-verification cycle — it's checked per stay at check-in.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Military Leisure Rate is **leisure travel only**, minimum 5% off Best Flex, **not available at IHG Army Hotels**, blackout dates apply, rooms limited/subject to prior sale.
- Government Rate is **not applicable with other discounted-rate promotions** and **not applicable to group bookings**; certain room types may be excluded; blackout dates apply.
- Neither rate combines with other offers, promo codes, or reward (points) nights.
- Taxes/occupancy fees are additional; the discount applies to the room rate.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Compare the member advance-purchase (non-refundable) rate** — it routinely runs 10–20% under Best Flex and often beats the 5% military minimum on locked-in dates.
- **Join IHG One Rewards (free)** — points earn on qualifying military/government cash rates; watch for member flash/"Cyber" sales that beat the military rate.
- **IHG Army Hotels** — if you have base access, on-base lodging at ~39 Army installations is typically ~50% cheaper than local hotels.
- **Use the Government Rate only if you truly qualify** (official travel/orders) — it's often the deepest, but ID/work orders are enforced at check-in.
- **Price your exact dates both ways** — the military minimum is small, so the "best" path is trip-specific, not a fixed answer.

### FAQ

#### Does IHG offer a military discount?
Yes. IHG's **Military Leisure Rate** gives US/Canadian active-duty, veterans, retired military, and their families a minimum of 5% off the Best Flexible Rate (more at some hotels), for leisure travel, with valid military/veteran ID required at check-in.

#### How much is the IHG military discount?
A **minimum of 5% off the Best Flexible Rate**, varying by hotel and subject to availability. Some properties offer more; the exact amount shows when you select the Government/Military rate for your dates.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and family members qualify?
Yes — the Military Leisure Rate explicitly covers US/Canadian active duty, **veterans**, retired military, and **family members with valid ID**. You show that ID at check-in.

#### What's the difference between the Military Leisure Rate and the Government Rate?
The **Military Leisure Rate** is for vacation travel (veteran/military ID). The **Government Rate** is for **official travel** — government employees, military on orders, and contractors — and requires official ID or **work orders** at check-in. Don't book the government rate for a personal vacation unless you qualify.

#### How do I verify my military status?
There's no online ID.me/SheerID step. You select the Government/Military rate when booking and **prove eligibility with your ID at the front desk** at check-in. The booking should be in the eligible person's name.

#### Does IHG use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. IHG verifies **in person at check-in** rather than through a third-party online verifier, and there's no IHG storefront on GovX/WeSalute that beats the first-party rate. Book direct on IHG.com/app.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, points, or sale rates?
No. The military and government rates **cannot be combined** with other offers, promo codes, or reward nights — they're standalone rates. You do still **earn IHG One Rewards points** on qualifying cash bookings.

#### Is the military discount actually the cheapest option?
Not always. Because the military minimum is ~5%, IHG's **member advance-purchase rate often beats it** on fixed dates. Compare both for your trip and book the lower one — and if you have Army base access, IHG Army Hotels is usually cheapest of all.

#### Does IHG offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
IHG's **Government Rate** covers government employees and state-funded universities/healthcare (official travel). There's no separate public first-responder/teacher/nurse leisure rate confirmed on IHG's own pages.

#### What are IHG Army Hotels?
On-base lodging (Candlewood Suites, Staybridge Suites, Holiday Inn Express) at roughly 39 US Army installations, for guests with base access and military ID. It's a separate program — the public military leisure rate does not apply there.
