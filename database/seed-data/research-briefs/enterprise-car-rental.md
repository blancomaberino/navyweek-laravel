**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **midsize** car, **7-day (weekly)** rental (rates are dynamic by location/date — **verify live at checkout**; illustrative base rate ≈ $350/week used only to show relative math).

## Headline finding

Enterprise's military "discount" is really two different government-rate programs, not a consumer veteran percentage. On its own pages Enterprise publishes exactly one number: **Government Leisure Travel — 5% off base rates.** For **Official Government Travel** (on-duty/TDY), it offers **contracted government rates** (unlimited CONUS/Canada mileage, damage + liability protection, full tank of gas) whose dollar amount is **not published** — it's the negotiated U.S. Government Car Rental Agreement rate you must price live. Eligibility for the leisure 5% is broad and stated: **current federal employees, active-duty members, retired employees, and veterans.** Verification is **document-based at the counter** (military ID, official orders, or a government-issued credit card) — Enterprise uses **no ID.me, no SheerID, and no GovX** for these rates. So the honest headline: *there is no flashy "20% off" veteran discount; there's a modest, verified 5% off leisure rates for the whole military/gov community, plus a separate official-travel contract rate — and the real win is usually price-comparing that 5% path against Enterprise Plus loyalty rates, USAA member rates, and whatever public/weekend sale is live on your dates.* Archetype: **II (rate-code brand — the code and the channel matter more than a headline %).**

## Stacking Rules (verified / reasoned)
1. **One discount per reservation.** Rental rate codes (the gov leisure 5%, USAA, a public promo) generally **do not stack** — you enter one Customer/Corporate Number. Choose the single best rate. *(Standard Enterprise rate-code behavior; verify at checkout.)*
2. **Official vs. Leisure are mutually exclusive per trip** — official-travel contract rates require orders/gov credit card and apply only to on-duty travel; leisure 5% is for personal travel. *(gov-rates FAQ)*
3. **USAA is a separate program**, not a stack on the gov 5% — pick whichever nets lower. *(deals page USAA note)*
4. **Enterprise Plus loyalty** earns points/benefits and can be attached to a profile, but a member *rate* is an alternative to the gov code, not a guaranteed stack — compare. *(reasoned)*
5. **No membership required** for the military/gov discounts, and **no ID.me/SheerID/GovX** — verification is a physical military/gov ID, official orders, or a government-issued credit card at pickup. *(deals & gov-rates pages)*
6. **Credit-card rental benefits** (e.g., primary/secondary CDW from a travel card) are independent of the rate and can reduce the need to buy Enterprise's coverage on leisure rentals — a real saving lever, though not an Enterprise offer. *(reasoned; verify your card's terms)*

### FAQ

#### Does Enterprise offer a military discount?
Yes — but as a government rate. **Government Leisure Travel gets 5% off base rates**, open to current federal employees, active-duty members, retired employees, and veterans. Separately, official-travel renters use the contracted U.S. Government rate.

#### How much is the Enterprise military discount?
**5% off base rates** for personal (leisure) travel. Official-government-travel rates are contracted and **not published as a percentage** — you price them live; they bundle unlimited CONUS/Canada mileage, damage + liability protection, and a full tank of gas.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Yes. Enterprise lists **veterans** among those eligible for the Government Leisure discount. There is no separate, larger veteran-only percentage — veterans use the same 5% leisure rate.

#### Do I need ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
No. Enterprise verifies with **documents at the counter** — a military/government ID, your official orders, or a government-issued credit card. There's no third-party verification service and no membership requirement.

#### What's the difference between Government Leisure and Official Travel?
**Leisure** is off-duty/personal travel (5% off base rates). **Official** is on-duty/TDY travel on the U.S. Government Car Rental Agreement, requiring orders or a gov credit card, and it bundles mileage and coverage benefits.

#### Can active-duty members use the leisure discount?
Yes — Enterprise states it "provides discounted leisure rates to active-duty military personnel." You must be 21+ to book leisure.

#### Is the discount available everywhere?
Yes — **all Enterprise locations worldwide** accept the military/government discounts.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to rent?
On orders, book the **official contract rate**. Off-duty, take the **5% leisure rate** but compare it to a **USAA** rate, your **Enterprise Plus** rate, and any **live public sale** — a public weekend deal can beat 5%.

#### Is there a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Enterprise's military pages cover military, government, and veterans. It doesn't advertise a standing first-responder/nurse/teacher/student rate on these pages; check the general Deals & Coupons page and partner programs.
