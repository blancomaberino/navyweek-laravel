### How To Redeem Online
1. Add full-priced item(s) to your cart at bootbarn.com.
2. In the cart, click the "Verify with ID.me" option.
3. Sign in to (or create) your ID.me account and verify your military or first responder status.
4. Once verified, the 10% discount is applied to eligible full-priced items at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop at any Boot Barn store.
2. At the register, tell the associate you qualify for the military/first responder discount.
3. Present valid proof of service or a qualifying ID.
4. The 10% discount is applied to eligible full-priced items at checkout.

### How Verification Works
Boot Barn uses **ID.me** for online verification. You verify in the cart before checkout; an ID.me account is required. ID.me lets members verify their community status once and reuse it across participating retailers without re-verifying each time, though ID.me may re-verify or require re-authentication at its discretion — do not assume verification is permanent. For verification problems, contact ID.me support through the ID.me Help Center. For in-store purchases, eligibility is confirmed by showing proof of service/ID at the register rather than through ID.me.

### FAQ

#### Does Boot Barn offer a military discount?
Yes. Boot Barn offers 10% off all full-priced styles to military members (and first responders), online and in stores.

#### How much is the Boot Barn military discount?
10% off full-priced styles. It cannot be combined with other offers and does not apply to gift cards or previous purchases.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Boot Barn/ID.me confirm "all active and retired personnel." Veterans, spouses, and immediate military family are likely eligible under ID.me's standard military groups but are not explicitly named on Boot Barn's page — verify your specific status through ID.me to confirm.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online, verify with ID.me in your cart before checkout (an ID.me account is required). In store, show valid proof of service or a qualifying ID at the register.

#### Does Boot Barn use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Boot Barn uses **ID.me** for online military and first responder verification.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. The 10% discount applies in Boot Barn stores; present proof of service/ID at the register.

#### What is excluded?
Full-priced items only; gift cards and previous purchases are excluded; it can't be combined with other promotions. Boot Barn notes additional exclusions "see cart for details."

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The discount cannot be combined with any other offer or promotion and applies only to full-priced styles.

#### Does Boot Barn offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders: yes (10%, via ID.me). Teachers, nurses/medical, government employees, and students: no Boot Barn discount is listed through ID.me for these groups.
