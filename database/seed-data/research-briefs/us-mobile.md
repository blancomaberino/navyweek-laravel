# US Mobile — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 12 months of one US Mobile Unlimited line (the everyday unit a single service member compares against a big-carrier "military plan").

## Headline finding

**US Mobile publishes no military, veteran, or first-responder discount, and uses no verification partner** (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute anywhere on its site or help center). That's not a gap in the research — US Mobile is a low-cost MVNO whose whole pitch is that the *public* price already undercuts the big carriers, so it doesn't run an identity-gated military rate the way Verizon/AT&T/T-Mobile do.

Because there's no military discount to chase, the honest best path for a service member is the same as for everyone: **prepay annually** (US Mobile's annual plans are its biggest standing markdown vs. paying monthly), **pick the smallest plan that fits** (the entry "Light" plan starts around $8/mo), **stack multi-line/family pricing** if you have dependents, and **use referral credits + first-cycle promo codes**. Archetype: **no-discount, price-leader brand** — the everyday retail price beats most carrier "military" rates, so there's nothing to verify and little to gain from one. Ignore coupon-aggregator pages advertising a "US Mobile military discount 50% off" — no such program or code exists.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **There is no military discount to stack** — the baseline is simply the public price.
2. **Referral credits auto-apply to your bill and promo codes add at checkout** — these two work together (per Referrals page).
3. **Annual prepay is a plan-selection choice, not a code** — you get the annual rate by choosing annual billing; a first-cycle promo can still apply to the first term.
4. **The "Light" and "Custom" plans (and certain promos) are excluded from referral-qualifying spend** — a referred line on Light won't unlock the referrer bonus.
5. Promo codes are generally **new-customer / first-billing-cycle** only — not a recurring monthly discount.
