# Kohl's Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Kohl's offers a **15% "Military Mondays" discount, in-store only, every Monday year-round** to active-duty military, veterans, retirees, and their immediate family members on qualifying purchases. Shoppers must present proper identification at checkout — a military ID card, a state-issued ID indicating veteran status, or Form DD 214. The discount **cannot be used online** and Kohl's does not use a third-party verifier (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX); eligibility is checked in person at the register. It **cannot be combined with other percent-off coupons** (including age-specific senior discounts) but **can be stacked with Kohl's Cash and dollar-off coupons**, which are applied before the 15% is taken. Separately, Kohl's runs an enhanced **30% Veterans Day** offer around Nov 10–11 each year (most recently confirmed for Nov 10–11, 2025); treat that as a recurring seasonal promo, not a year-round term. The single most important caveat: this is a **Monday-only, in-store-only** discount — it does not apply on other days or to Kohls.com orders.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop at any Kohl's store **on a Monday** (the discount applies only on Mondays year-round).
2. Bring your merchandise to the register and tell the cashier you're using the military discount.
3. Present **proper identification**: a military ID card, a state-issued ID indicating veteran status, or **Form DD 214**. (Immediate family members redeem in store with qualifying identification — confirm the exact ID accepted with the store.)
4. If you're paying with **Kohl's Cash or dollar-off coupons**, the cashier applies those **first**, then takes **15% off** the remaining qualifying balance.

### How To Redeem Online
- **Not available.** The Kohl's military discount is **in-store only** and is not valid on Kohls.com. Kohl's does not offer an online military-verification path (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX). *(Per official terms; if Kohl's adds an online option in the future, this guide will be updated.)*

### How Verification Works
There is **no online verifier and no account requirement**. Verification happens **in person at checkout**: you show a military ID card, a state-issued ID marked with veteran status, or Form DD 214. Because there's no third-party service (ID.me/SheerID/GovX) involved, there's nothing to enroll in ahead of time — but you must have acceptable physical ID with you each time you redeem. If your ID isn't accepted or you have an eligibility question, ask a store associate or contact Kohl's customer service.

### FAQ

#### Does Kohl's offer a military discount?
Yes. Kohl's offers a **15% "Military Mondays" discount** in store every Monday of the year to active-duty military, veterans, retirees, and their immediate family members.

#### How much is the Kohl's military discount?
**15% off** qualifying purchases on Mondays. Around Veterans Day, Kohl's typically increases it to **30%** for a short in-store window (Nov 10–11, 2025 most recently).

#### Do veterans, retirees, and family members qualify?
Yes. Official terms list active-duty military, veterans, retirees, and immediate family members as eligible. Reserve/National Guard and surviving-spouse/Gold Star eligibility are not separately spelled out in the official source.

#### How do I verify my military status at Kohl's?
At the register, present a **military ID card**, a **state-issued ID indicating veteran status**, or **Form DD 214**. There's no online verification or pre-enrollment.

#### Does Kohl's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Kohl's verifies eligibility **in store with ID** and does not use a third-party online verification service. There is no online military discount.

#### Can I use the Kohl's military discount online?
No. The discount is **in-store only** and is not valid on Kohls.com.

#### What days can I use the Kohl's military discount?
**Mondays only**, year-round (plus the special Veterans Day window). It does not apply on other days.

#### What is excluded from the Kohl's military discount?
It can't be combined with other percent-off coupons (including senior discounts), and it excludes gift cards, taxes, shipping/handling, price adjustments, Kohl's credit card payments, Kohl's Cares® charitable items, Sephora at Kohl's, and other coupon-ineligible brands/categories.

#### Can I combine it with Kohl's Cash or other coupons?
You **can stack Kohl's Cash® and dollar-off coupons** (they're applied before the 15%), but you **cannot** combine it with another **percent-off** coupon.

#### Does Kohl's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not stated in Kohl's official source — no separate first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount is published. Kohl's does offer a separate age-based senior discount (which can't be combined with the military discount).
