# Carowinds — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 7, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adults, one single-day visit to Carowinds + Carolina Harbor on a summer Saturday in July 2026 (all online prices "plus applicable taxes & fees," which are identical across the online paths compared; no tax-free channel is priced in).

## Headline finding

Carowinds (a Six Flags park, Charlotte NC) runs an **official, published military & first responder ticket program**: discounted single-day tickets **starting from $37** (vs an $80 gate price) plus a **2026 Military Gold Pass at $105** (vs $110 for the public Gold Pass online, $160 gate), both verified through **ID.me** and **redeemable online only**. Eligibility is broad — active, retired, veterans, Reserves, National Guard, plus first responders — and a verified member can buy **up to 6 tickets for family and friends**. The honest catch: Carowinds' own advance-purchase public ticket starts at **$39**, so the everyday military ticket saves only ~$2/person over just buying early — the real prizes are **Military Days (May 22–25, 2026: FREE admission at the gate with military ID)** and the pass-level savings. The discount **"cannot be combined with any other offers, including sales"** (brand's own terms), and no cashback path exists (Rakuten lists Six Flags as "No Cash Back," as of July 7, 2026). Archetype: **E — multi-tier identity discount, no stacking game**, with a strong **H-style holiday calendar** (the free-admission windows are worth more than the everyday %).

## Stacking Rules (verified)

1. **Nothing stacks.** The brand's own terms: "Military, Veteran, and First Responder Discounts **cannot be combined with any other offers, including sales**." When a public promo (like the $39 2-Day Ticket) is cheaper, take the public promo instead. *(carowinds.com/military-discounts)*
2. **Online only.** "All offers must be redeemed online" — there is **no military discount at the ticket window** on regular days. Walking up costs $80/person. The one gate exception is the Military Days free ticket (May 22–25, 2026), which is redeemed **at the turnstile with a valid U.S. Military ID**, while companion tickets must still be bought online in advance. *(carowinds.com/military-discounts; /events/military-days)*
3. **No cashback layer.** Rakuten lists Six Flags as "No Cash Back" (as of July 7, 2026); no TopCashback listing surfaced. There is no portal rebate to lose or gain. *(rakuten.com/shop/six-flags)*
4. **Prices float.** "During Pass and One-Day Ticket sales, product offerings will periodically change" — the $37/$105 figures are date-based starting prices, not fixed rates. Quote them as "from," and re-check before publish cycles. *(carowinds.com/military-discounts)*
5. **Season-pass perks still apply to passholders** — the Military Gold Pass is a Gold Pass (10% food & merch, bring-a-friend discounts, free parking) at $5 off the public online price; those built-in perks aren't a "stack," they're pass benefits. *(carowinds.com/season-passes; season-pass terms)*

### FAQ

#### Does Carowinds offer a military discount?
Yes — an official, published program: single-day military & first responder tickets **from $37** (gate price $80) and a **$105 2026 Military Gold Pass**, verified through ID.me and sold online at carowinds.com.

#### How much is the Carowinds military discount?
Carowinds doesn't publish a percentage — it sells **fixed discounted prices** that float by date. On July 7, 2026: single-day from $37 (vs $39 public advance / $80 gate) and the Military Gold Pass at $105 (vs $110 public Gold online).

#### Do veterans qualify, or only active duty?
Veterans qualify. The brand's own list: **Active U.S. Military, Retired U.S. Military, Veterans, Reserves, and National Guard**.

#### Do military spouses or dependents qualify?
Not as independent purchasers, per the published copy — but a verified member can buy **up to 6 discounted tickets for family and friends** in the same order, and the brand extends the program to "immediate family members" through that mechanic.

#### How do I verify — ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**ID.me.** Select your tickets at carowinds.com and an ID.me login prompt appears before checkout. (GovX hosts Carowinds ticket venue pages too, but that's a separate marketplace with login-gated pricing — the brand-site program is ID.me.)

#### Can I get the military discount at the gate?
No — "all offers must be redeemed online." The one exception: during **Military Days (May 22–25, 2026)**, eligible members get **free admission at the front gate** with a valid U.S. Military ID.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sales?
No. Carowinds states the military/veteran/first-responder discounts "**cannot be combined with any other offers, including sales**." When a public deal (like the $39 2-Day Ticket) is cheaper, buy that instead.

#### When does Carowinds give military members free admission?
**May 22–25, 2026** (Memorial Day weekend Military Days), free at the turnstile with a valid U.S. Military ID. In 2025 the same event ran May 23–26 with companion tickets at $39.99. Local coverage says the park has historically added military days near July 4 and Veterans Day, but Carowinds has published nothing beyond May for 2026.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit Carowinds?
Free, if you go during Military Days (May 22–25, 2026). Otherwise: the ID.me ticket (~$37) for a one-off day, the public $39 2-Day Ticket if you want two days before July 31, 2026, or the $105 Military Gold Pass if you'll visit twice or more.

#### Does Carowinds offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
**First responders: yes** — same ticket program and prices as military, via ID.me. No teacher, nurse, government, or student offer was verified this pass.

#### Can I buy Carowinds tickets at my base ITT office or through GovX?
Possibly. Several MWR/ITT ticket offices list Carowinds tickets (pricing varies by installation — check yours), and GovX hosts Carowinds ticket venue pages with member-only pricing. Compare both against the ~$37 ID.me price.
