# SKIMS — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$100 full-price SKIMS cart** (e.g. a Fits Everybody T-Shirt Bra plus a couple of pairs of underwear, or one Soft Lounge slip dress) bought at skims.com, pre-tax.

## Headline finding

**SKIMS has no military or veteran discount.** There is no military checkout on skims.com, and SKIMS is not partnered with any verification provider (ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass) for a military offer. ID.me itself states plainly: *"We're not aware that SKIMS offers Military discounts."* There is likewise no first-responder, nurse, or teacher discount, and no confirmed student program on SKIMS's own site. This is a valid, useful finding — do not publish the invented "15–20% off military/first-order" codes the coupon aggregators list.

Because there is no identity discount, the honest maximum-savings answer is a **stack of public paths**: (1) join **SKIMS Rewards** free (an access/perks program — early sale access, waived return fees, and free shipping at the ONYX tier — not a % off); (2) route the purchase through a **cashback portal** — for the military audience specifically, **USAA MemberShop (~1.5%, as of May 26, 2026)** is the relevant one, though a public portal has run higher (~2%); and (3) the real money is only in **shopping the SKIMS Sale section during public sale events** (Black Friday, seasonal drops). SKIMS is a premium DTC brand that rarely issues sitewide % codes and offers **no welcome/first-order discount** on its own signup page — so on a full-price cart, cashback + rewards perks is the ceiling, and a genuine Sale markdown is the only thing that beats it. Archetype: **B — no military route at all**, with a secondary **H "myth brand"** flavor (aggregators invent a flat military/first-order % that SKIMS does not confirm).

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **SKIMS allows one promo code per order** and codes are **not combinable** with other codes or sitewide offers. (Promo Terms — read via SERP snippet; verify verbatim on the JS-rendered page.)
2. **Sale-section, final-sale, and bundle items are excluded from promo codes** — you cannot stack a code on a marked-down style. Gift cards, brand collaborations, taxes, and shipping are also excluded.
3. **There is no military code to stack** — the question is moot for the identity angle; SKIMS publishes no such code.
4. **Cashback portals sit on top of the purchase price** and generally still track on full-price SKIMS orders (no identity/verification checkout to break tracking) — but portal cashback does **not** combine with a Sale price to create a bigger % off; it just returns its rate on whatever you pay.
5. **SKIMS Rewards perks (free shipping, waived return fees) are not a discount** and apply regardless of code — treat them as separate, always-on value.
6. **Amex Offers are card-linked** and apply on top of the SKIMS price for direct purchases only (not via resellers/third parties) — independent of any code.

#### Does SKIMS offer a military discount?
No. SKIMS does not offer a military or veteran discount, and it isn't partnered with ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass for a military offer. ID.me confirms it isn't aware of any SKIMS military discount.

#### How much is the SKIMS military discount?
There isn't one. Any "15–20% military" or "first responder" SKIMS code you see on coupon sites is not verified — SKIMS publishes no military discount and no welcome/first-order code on its own signup page.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
No — SKIMS has no military program for any of these groups. The savings paths (SKIMS Rewards, cashback, Sale section) are open to everyone.

#### Does SKIMS use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?

No confirmed first-responder, nurse, or teacher discount. A student discount is inconsistently reported — SKIMS's own site shows no active student program, though some third-party sites claim a Student Beans/UNiDAYS code; verify before relying on it.

#### Is there a SKIMS first-order or newsletter discount?
Not from SKIMS. Signing up for SKIMS email/SMS gives early access to drops and sales — no discount code. Coupon sites claiming "15–20% off your first order" are not supported by the brand.

No. SKIMS allows one code per order, codes aren't combinable, and Sale/final-sale items are excluded from promo codes. Cashback portals return their rate on whatever you pay but don't create a bigger discount on a Sale price.

#### Does SKIMS run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?

#### Can I buy SKIMS at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
