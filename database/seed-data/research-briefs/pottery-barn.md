# Pottery Barn Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

As of June 23, 2026, **Pottery Barn does not offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** Pottery Barn (a Williams-Sonoma, Inc. brand) publishes no military-offer page, lists no military discount in its customer-service Help Topics, and does not partner with ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for any active military offer. ID.me Shop's Pottery Barn page explicitly states it is "not aware that Pottery Barn offers Military discounts," and Military.com lists "No discounts currently available from this merchant." Aggregator pages still circulating a "15% military discount" are referencing a legacy in-store program that was discontinued (widely reported as ending around 2021) and is no longer confirmable on any primary source. The most important caveat: anyone seeing a "15% off for military" claim for Pottery Barn is looking at outdated or unverified information. The realistic ways for service members to save are Pottery Barn's free **Key Rewards** loyalty program, frequent sitewide sales (currently up to 60% off), and the Pottery Barn credit card — none of which require military status.
