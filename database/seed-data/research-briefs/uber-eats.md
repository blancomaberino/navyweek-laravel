# Uber Eats — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$30 restaurant delivery order** on Uber Eats (pre-tax, pre-tip), which on a non-member account typically carries roughly **$3–$8 delivery fee + a service fee (~15% of subtotal) + a small-order fee if under the minimum ≈ $8–$12 in fees** on top of the $30 subtotal (fees vary by market, merchant, distance, and demand; tip excluded).

## Headline finding

**Uber Eats has no first-party military or veteran discount.** ID.me states plainly it is "not aware of Uber Eats offering Military community discounts," and there is no ID.me/SheerID **military** verification at Uber Eats checkout for a percentage-off order. Unlike DoorDash — which sells a discounted DashPass through GovX — **no verified GovX (or any provider) military deal on Uber One was found this pass**; GovX does not appear to carry an Uber One offer. So the genuine "military" play at Uber Eats is **not a military discount at all** — it's getting the **Uber One** membership (which waives delivery fees and gives up to 10% off orders) as **cheap or free as possible**: eligible **Amex Platinum/Centurion** cardholders get **up to $120/yr in statement credits** that fully cover Uber One's **$96/year**, verified **students** get Uber One at **$4.99/mo (50% off) via SheerID**, participating **federal employees** get complimentary Uber One through the **GSA Rideshare** program, and any first-time member gets a **4-week free trial**. Archetype: **A/B (no first-party military checkout — the "military discount" everyone searches for does not exist)** with an **F-adjacent (subscription-is-the-savings)** twist, but — unlike DoorDash — **without a GovX subscription discount**, so the win is card/student/employer-provided Uber One, not a GovX deal.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both Uber + partner terms)
1. **You hold one Uber One membership at a time** — student, standard, Amex-credited, and employer-provided are **sources for the same membership**, not stackable add-ons. Pick the cheapest single source.
2. **Uber One "savings" are fee waivers + up to 10% off, not a military discount** — $0 delivery fee on eligible orders over the minimum subtotal, up to 10% off eligible delivery/pickup, and 6% back on rides. It does not zero out the service fee, tax, or tip.
3. **Amex $120 Uber One credit only applies to the amount actually charged to the eligible Amex card**, paid **directly through Uber** — Uber Cash, promos, or third-party payment (e.g. Apple Pay) don't earn the credit. The $15/mo Uber Cash is a **separate** Platinum perk that layers on top (usable on the order itself).
4. **Amex Uber Cash stacks on any order** (it's a payment credit, not a discount) — the one genuinely additive value; add the eligible card to your Uber account to receive it.
5. **No verification-gated military code exists** at Uber Eats checkout — there is nothing for a portal, coupon, or "military code" to stack onto.
6. **In-app "Offers"/promo codes are merchant- or Uber-funded and open to everyone**; a first-order promo can pair with the free trial but is one-time and not a military tier. Cashback portals rarely track gated/subscription flows reliably — verify a live rate before counting on one.

#### Does Uber Eats offer a military discount?
No. Uber Eats has **no first-party military or veteran discount**, and ID.me confirms it isn't aware of one. There's no military code at checkout. Service members save by making an **Uber One** membership cheap or free (Amex card, student plan, federal GSA program, or annual billing).

#### How much is the Uber Eats military discount?
There isn't one — no military percentage off. The relevant savings is **Uber One** ($9.99/mo or $96/yr), which can be **free** with an eligible **Amex Platinum/Centurion** card, **$4.99/mo** for verified students, or complimentary for participating federal employees.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify for a discount?
There's no military-specific Uber Eats discount for any of these groups. They save the same way everyone does via Uber One (plus the student/Amex/GSA routes if they also fit those).

#### How do I verify my military status for Uber Eats?
You don't — Uber Eats runs **no military verification** (no ID.me or SheerID military step at checkout, and no GovX Uber One deal). The only verification in play is **student** status for the $4.99/mo student plan.

#### Does Uber Eats use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for military discounts?

#### Does Uber Eats offer a first responder, government, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
