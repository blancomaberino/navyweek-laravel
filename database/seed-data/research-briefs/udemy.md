# Udemy — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one popular Udemy course (sticker **$99.99–$199.99**; realistic sale price **$9.99–$14.99**), compared against the **Personal Plan** annual subscription.

## Headline finding

**Udemy publishes no current military or veteran discount.** ID.me — the very verification partner Udemy once used — now states plainly on its Udemy page: *"We're not aware that Udemy offers Military discounts."* The often-cited **"lifelong 50% military discount"** traces to a **2014 ID.me + Udemy partnership** (announced November 11–12, 2014) that is **no longer live**; aggregators recycling "30% off" or "up to 85% off military" codes are quoting either that dead program or Udemy's ordinary public sale prices, not a real military entitlement. The genuine savings story is that **Udemy barely needs a discount**: it runs **sitewide sales almost every week**, dropping $99.99–$199.99 courses to **$9.99–$14.99**, so the sticker price is marketing theatre. On top of that, there are two real, verified military-community paths that beat any coupon: **military spouses get 5,000+ Udemy courses FREE** through the War Department's **SpouseWorks (formerly SECO)** program, and service members/families can often reach Udemy-style content **free via base MWR Digital Libraries** and credentialing/education channels. Archetype: **documented "no military discount" — the constant sale cycle plus free base/spouse education channels are the actual savings.**

### FAQ

#### Does Udemy offer a military discount?
No. Udemy does not publish a military or veteran discount, and ID.me — its former verification partner — states it is "not aware that Udemy offers Military discounts" (verified July 15, 2026).

#### How much is the Udemy military discount?
There isn't one. Claims of "30%," "50% lifelong," or "up to 85% off military" come from a defunct 2014 ID.me/Udemy program or from ordinary public sale prices — not a current military entitlement.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for anything?
Veterans get no Udemy discount. **Military spouses** do qualify for **free** access to 5,000+ Udemy courses through the War Department's SpouseWorks (formerly SECO) program.

#### How do I verify my military status for Udemy?
You can't — there's no discount to verify. The spouse benefit is confirmed through the SpouseWorks/SECO portal, not at Udemy checkout.

#### Does Udemy use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass for a military discount?
No active military program uses any of these. Udemy partnered with **ID.me back in 2014**, but that offer is gone; ID.me's Udemy page now lists only public promos and confirms no military discount.

#### Can I use a discount in the app or online?
There's no military discount to use. The best price for anyone is Udemy's **public sitewide sale** (~$9.99–$14.99), applied automatically at udemy.com during a sale — no code.

#### What is excluded?
Everything, in the sense that no military discount applies to any course. The spouse free-access catalog (5,000+) is a subset of Udemy, and the Personal Plan excludes some courses.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
There's nothing to combine. Udemy's sale price is already the floor for a single course; the spouse program simply provides courses free.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Udemy?
If you're a **spouse**, SpouseWorks (free) wins. Otherwise, **buy the course you want during Udemy's near-weekly sale at ~$10** — that beats any advertised "military" percentage. For career/credential topics, check **base MWR libraries and Navy COOL** first; they're often free.

#### Does Udemy offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Udemy has no dedicated verified discount for these groups either. The ID.me Shop surfaces general public promos across audiences, but they are not group-gated military-style discounts.

#### Does Udemy run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Udemy runs frequent **public** sitewide sales, including around holidays, but no distinct *military* holiday promotion. Any holiday price is the same public sale available to everyone.
