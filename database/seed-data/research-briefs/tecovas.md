### Intro
Tecovas, the Austin-based Western boot and apparel brand, offers a **10% discount to Active Duty Military, Military Retirees, and Veterans**. It runs as a standing, everyday offer rather than a limited-time promotion, and it's available both online at tecovas.com and at Tecovas retail stores.

To claim it, you verify your military status through **ID.me**. Once verified, a unique discount code is applied at checkout, or you can use the ID.me button in the Tecovas checkout to log in and apply it. The discount can't be combined with any other promotion or offer.

This is an independent guide. NavyWeek is not affiliated with Tecovas. Tecovas controls the offer and its terms and can change them at any time — always confirm the current details on Tecovas' official military discount page before you buy.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to ID.me and create an account (or sign in), then verify your military status. ID.me offers a variety of verification methods.
2. Shop at tecovas.com and add eligible item(s) to your cart.
3. At checkout, use the ID.me button to log in and apply the discount — once verified, a unique discount code is applied at checkout.
4. Confirm the 10% discount appears before completing your order.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify your military status with ID.me before or at the time of purchase. (Tecovas states the discount is available at retail store locations but does not publish the exact in-store presentation steps — see Open Questions.)
2. At a Tecovas retail store, tell the associate you qualify for the military discount.
3. Provide your ID.me-verified discount/proof as directed by the store associate.
4. The 10% discount is applied at the register.

### How Verification Works
Tecovas uses **ID.me** to verify military eligibility. You create an ID.me account and verify your military status; ID.me offers a variety of verification methods. After verification, a unique discount code is applied at Tecovas checkout, or you can use the ID.me button in the checkout flow to log in and apply it. ID.me lets members verify their status once and reuse it across participating retailers, but ID.me may re-verify or require re-authentication at its discretion — do not assume verification is permanent. For verification problems, Tecovas directs customers to ID.me support (help.id.me); if issues persist, Tecovas asks customers to email howdy@tecovas.com for assistance. (The "contact ID.me support, then email howdy@tecovas.com" support path is confirmed verbatim on Tecovas' student discount page, which uses the identical ID.me program structure; Tecovas' military page lists a parallel "I am having problems verifying" FAQ but its expanded answer was not captured in a static fetch — see Open Questions.)

### FAQ

#### Does Tecovas offer a military discount?
Yes. Tecovas offers a 10% discount to Active Duty Military, Military Retirees, and Veterans, verified through ID.me, online and in stores.

#### How much is the Tecovas military discount?
10% off. It cannot be combined with any other promotions or offers. (Around Veterans Day, Tecovas has historically offered 20% for a single day — a separate limited-time promo, not the everyday rate.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify — they're named explicitly alongside Active Duty Military and Military Retirees. Tecovas does not list military spouses or dependents on its military discount page, so eligibility for those groups is not stated; verify your specific status through ID.me.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create an ID.me account and verify your military status (ID.me offers several verification methods). Once verified, a unique discount code applies at checkout, or you can use the ID.me button in Tecovas' checkout to log in and apply it.

#### Does Tecovas use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Tecovas uses **ID.me** for both its military and student discount verification.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Tecovas states the discount is available at its retail store locations as well as online. Tecovas does not publish the exact in-store presentation steps; verify with ID.me and ask a store associate.

#### What is excluded?
The everyday offer cannot be combined with any other promotions or offers. Tecovas does not publish a detailed everyday-offer exclusion list. (The expired 2024 Veterans Day promo additionally excluded gift cards, wholesale-partner purchases, and limited shipping to the continental U.S. — those are promo-specific terms, not confirmed for the everyday offer.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The discount cannot be combined with any other promotions or offers.

#### Does Tecovas offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount?
Students: yes — a separate 10% discount for enrolled students, via ID.me. First responders, nurses/medical, teachers, and government employees: no standing Tecovas discount is listed on Tecovas' page or its ID.me store page for these groups.
