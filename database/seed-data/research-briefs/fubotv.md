# Fubo (fuboTV) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one month of **Fubo Pro** at the standard **$84.99/month** (the plan WeSalute quotes), viewed over a realistic first-2-month horizon.

## Headline finding

Fubo has **no first-party military discount page** — it delivers military savings exclusively through **third-party verification marketplaces**, and the terms differ by provider. The deepest verified path is **GovX: $30 off each of your first two months** ($60 total). **WeSalute** gives **25% off the first month of Fubo Pro** (~$21) plus a 5-day free trial, and **ID.me** offers **$30 off the first two months** plus small cashback. Every version is **new-customers-only and first-month(s)-only** — after the intro window, you pay full price. There is no ongoing military rate.

Claims of a permanent percentage off the monthly bill are false.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **One marketplace offer at a time** — you redeem through a single provider's verified link; you can't combine GovX + WeSalute + ID.me on one account.
2. **New customers only** (WeSalute states this explicitly; GovX/ID.me intro offers behave the same) — existing/returning subscribers don't qualify.
3. **One redemption per customer** (WeSalute).
4. **Add-ons are not included** — the discount applies to the base plan, not premium add-ons; channel availability varies by plan and region.
5. **Intro window only** — after the discounted month(s), billing reverts to full price; there's no stacking a coupon on the ongoing rate.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty, retirees, veterans, reserve/National Guard** — all four via GovX.
- **Immediate military family** — accepted through WeSalute.
- **Nurses, first responders, teachers, government, medical, seniors 55+** — via ID.me's broader audience list.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Pick a marketplace (GovX, ID.me, or WeSalute) and open its Fubo offer.
2. Create/sign in to the marketplace account and verify your military status (usually minutes).
3. Follow the offer link to Fubo and start service — the intro discount applies automatically at checkout.
4. Note your renewal date: after the discounted month(s), you pay the full plan price.

### How Verification Works
Each provider verifies eligibility independently — GovX and ID.me typically confirm military status in minutes with a service email or document; WeSalute uses its membership. The discount attaches to the offer link, not permanently to your Fubo account.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- New customers only; one redemption per person.
- Add-ons not included; channel availability varies by plan and region.
- Intro window only — reverts to full price afterward.
- Regional Sports Network fees may apply on top in many markets.

### Best Ways To Save More
- Compare the **GovX $30×2** offer against Fubo's **public first-month promo** and take whichever is deeper.
- Use **WeSalute's 5-day free trial** to test before paying.
- Choose the **plan tier you'll actually watch** — the discount is a fixed dollar/percent, so a lower base plan costs less overall.
- If you leave after the intro window, you avoid the full-price months entirely.

### FAQ
#### Does Fubo offer a military discount?
Not directly — it's offered through GovX, ID.me, and WeSalute. GovX and ID.me give $30 off each of your first two months; WeSalute gives 25% off the first month of Fubo Pro.

#### How much is the Fubo military discount?
Up to **$60 total** (GovX/ID.me: $30 off two months), or ~$21 off month one with WeSalute plus a free trial.

#### Do veterans and family qualify?
Veterans qualify through all three. Immediate family is accepted via WeSalute.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through GovX, ID.me, or WeSalute — usually a quick online check, then you use their Fubo offer link.

#### Does Fubo use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
All three of **GovX, ID.me, and WeSalute** — not SheerID.

#### Can I use it if I'm already a subscriber?
No — these are new-customer, intro offers.

#### What's excluded?
Add-ons, existing customers, and anything beyond the first month or two; RSN fees still apply.

#### Can I combine it with other promo codes?
No — one marketplace offer per account, no stacking.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to subscribe to Fubo?
If you'll keep it 2+ months, use **GovX or ID.me** ($30 off × 2). If you just want to try it, use **WeSalute's free trial + 25% off**.

#### Does Fubo run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Fubo runs public first-month promos around holidays, but these aren't a separate military-verified rate — compare them to the GovX offer.
