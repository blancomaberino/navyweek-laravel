## Headline finding

Palmetto State Armory publishes **no military or veteran discount** — no discount page, no mention on its official Coupon Code Deals page or Help Center, and no verification partner (no ID.me, no GovX ID, no SheerID checkout exists on the site). What does exist is a **store-level, discretionary in-store discount** that veterans report works with ID at PSA retail locations but is not published, not guaranteed, and never available online. ID.me formerly listed a Palmetto State Armory store; that page now **redirects to the ID.me Shop homepage** (deactivated), and the surviving "PSA Outdoors" ID.me listing is affiliate-only (up to 2.5% cash back for everyone — not a military offer). The genuine best path for a service member is public: **Daily Deals/sale pricing + a cashback portal** (BeFrugal 7% as of July 7, 2026) — no code, no verification, portal-safe. Archetype: **J — unofficial/manager's-discretion** (dominant) + **B — no online military route; sale-stack is the real path** (secondary).

Not online. PSA publishes no military discount and has no verification program. Veterans report a discretionary in-store discount at PSA retail stores with ID — unpublished and store-by-store.

#### Do PSA sale prices work with cashback?
Yes — sale pricing is automatic, not a code, so portal tracking survives. Entering an off-portal coupon code is what risks voiding cashback.

#### Does PSA offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No verified program in any channel. Aggregator pages claiming a PSA first-responder discount are unverified.

#### Does PSA run Veterans Day or Memorial Day sales?
