# Guns.com — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $500 certified used handgun + $50 box of ammo = **$550 subtotal** (before FFL transfer fee, shipping, and tax).

## Headline finding

Guns.com runs a real, **official** "Heroes" discount: **5% off** for verified military and first responders, delivered through **ID.me** and applied automatically as a coupon at checkout. It covers new firearms, *select* used guns, accessories, ammo, and merch — but **not auction purchases, sale/clearance items, taxes, fees, or shipping**. The 5% is modest and firearms are low-margin, so the discount rarely moves the needle much; on this category the bigger levers are the price on the specific used gun, avoiding a high FFL transfer fee, and Guns.com's own "Certified Used" deal flow. The brand occasionally lets verified ID.me members "nearly double" the discount during special promotions — that is the window worth waiting for.

**Verdict logic:**
- Buying a Buy-Now gun/ammo/accessory + already ID.me-verified → take the **5% Heroes discount**; it's free and automatic.
- Non-urgent purchase → **wait for a "nearly double" promo** and stack it on the same order.
- Item is an **auction** listing → the discount will not apply; decide purely on the bid math and fees.
- Every purchase → shop the local **FFL transfer fee** separately; a $15 swing there beats the 5% on a $300 gun.
- On-page chooser: "Auction or Buy-Now?" → Auction = no discount; Buy-Now = verify with ID.me for 5%.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The 5% is delivered as an **auto-applied coupon** once ID.me verification is confirmed at checkout — you don't hand-enter a code.
2. **Does not stack** onto sale or clearance products, and **not on auctions** — the two most common ways Guns.com prices are already cut.
3. Discount is calculated on the **product subtotal only** — never on taxes, FFL/handling fees, or shipping.
4. The "nearly double" boost is a **replacement** promotional rate for verified members, not a second coupon stacked on the standard 5% (treat as one enhanced rate during the window).

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty & Reserve service members — eligible
- Retirees & veterans — eligible
- Military spouses and family members/dependents — eligible
- First responders with EMT certification or professional AED/defibrillator certification — eligible
- General public — not eligible for the Heroes discount

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
Online (only channel):
1. Add eligible items (new/select used guns, ammo, accessories, merch — not auctions) to your cart.
2. At checkout, click the ID.me **Verify** button.
3. Log in to ID.me or create an account and confirm your information.
4. The 5% coupon applies automatically to the eligible subtotal.
5. Complete purchase; arrange delivery to your local FFL for firearms.

### How Verification Works
Verification is through **ID.me**, the same third-party identity service used across many military-discount programs. You verify once and confirm your info at checkout; ID.me may require periodic re-verification per its own policy. Support for the discount itself runs through Guns.com customer service; identity issues route to ID.me support.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No discount on **auction** purchases.
- No discount on **sale or clearance** products.
- Discount does not apply to **taxes, fees, or shipping**.
- Applies to "select" used guns — not guaranteed on every used listing (flagged: Guns.com does not publish the exact list of eligible used inventory).

### Best Ways To Save More
- Watch Guns.com email/news for the **"nearly double"** ID.me promotional window.
- Compare the **used-gun listing price** first — a well-priced Certified Used unit beats 5% off an overpriced one.
- Shop your **local FFL transfer fee** (often $25–$50); it's outside Guns.com and not discounted.
- Public holiday sales (Memorial Day, July 4, Black Friday) cut sticker prices — but those sale items are excluded from the 5% stack, so compare final numbers.

### FAQ

#### Does Guns.com offer a military discount?
Yes. Guns.com offers a 5% "Heroes" discount for verified military members and first responders through ID.me.

#### How much is the Guns.com military discount?
5% off eligible products, applied automatically at checkout. During special promotions, verified ID.me members can "nearly double" it.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — active/Reserve, retirees, veterans, spouses, and military family members/dependents all qualify, as do first responders with EMT or AED certification.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me at checkout: click Verify, log in or create an account, confirm your info, and the coupon applies.

#### Does Guns.com use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
ID.me. There is no verified GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute program for Guns.com.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Online only — Guns.com is an online marketplace.

#### What is excluded?
Auction purchases, sale and clearance products, and taxes/fees/shipping. It applies only to "select" used guns.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — it doesn't stack on sale/clearance or auctions, and it's calculated on the product subtotal only.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Guns.com?
Take the 5% on a Buy-Now item (ideally during a "nearly double" promo), pick the sharpest-priced used listing, and minimize your local FFL transfer fee — that fee often matters more than the 5%.

#### Does Guns.com offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
First responders (EMT/AED-certified) are explicitly eligible for the Heroes discount. No teacher/nurse/student program is published.
