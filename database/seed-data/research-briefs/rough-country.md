# Rough Country Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Rough Country does not currently offer a verified military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** As of June 24, 2026, Rough Country's own website (Help & FAQ, customer-service footer, and Sale pages) lists no military/veteran discount program, and its primary candidate verification provider, ID.me, explicitly states it is "not aware that Rough Country offers Military discounts" (and the same for First Responders). Various coupon aggregators claim a Rough Country military discount of 5%, 10%, or 30%, but these conflict with one another, cite no official source, and could not be confirmed against any primary source; they are flagged Low and should not be presented as live. Best current ways for service members to save are Rough Country's own Sale section and free sitewide shipping, or buying Rough Country-branded parts through a third-party military-discount retailer (e.g., GovX storefront sellers). This brief documents the absence.
