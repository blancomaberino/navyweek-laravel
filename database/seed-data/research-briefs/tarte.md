# Tarte Cosmetics — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $120 Tarte order (e.g., Shape Tape Concealer + Maracuja Juicy Lip + a face palette), online at tarte.com.

## Headline finding

Tarte Cosmetics runs a **real, official, and unusually deep 40% off military discount**, verified through **SheerID** (not ID.me or GovX) and redeemed **online only at tarte.com**. It covers active duty, veterans, retirees, reservists, National Guard, and active-duty and Gold Star military families. At 40%, this is one of the deepest identity discounts in prestige beauty — it **beats essentially every public Tarte sale**, so the strategy here is simple: verify once, then use your monthly code. The one catch is that it is strictly non-combinable (no stacking with promo codes, sale offers, e-gift cards, loyalty rewards, or cashback portals) and is capped at **$500 of eligible items** per order. Archetype: **multi-tier identity discount (E)** — Tarte offers the same 40% to first responders, medical professionals, K-12 teachers, and pro makeup artists, 20% to students, and there is no stacking game to play. Debunk up front: the widely repeated claim that Tarte's military discount is "in stores only, show your military ID at the register" is **false** — Tarte's own terms make it an online-only, SheerID-verified offer at tarte.com.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 40% code is fully standalone.** Tarte's FAQ and Promotional Disclaimers state it "cannot be combined with any other promo codes / offers / discounts," and the FAQ adds e-gift cards and loyalty rewards to that non-combinable list. You use exactly one code at checkout.
2. **Because it doesn't stack, pick the single best code.** With 40% being deeper than Tarte's typical public sales (~25–30%), the military code is the winner for verified shoppers on virtually any eligible cart.
3. **Cashback won't stack with the military code.** Rakuten's terms void or reduce cash back when a non-Rakuten code is used, and identity-gated checkouts often break portal tracking. Use Rakuten's ~4% only on a plain full-price order, not on top of the 40% code — and 40% beats 4% anyway.
4. **Cap and exclusions:** valid on eligible items up to **$500** per order, **in-stock only**, **not** on previous purchases. **Custom Kit** and **eGift cards** are excluded, and "select items" may be excluded (flagged on the product page or at checkout).
5. **Monthly reuse:** verified members receive a fresh one-time-use code by email at the **start of each month** — one code per purchase, once a month.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Tarte's **Special Offers** page (tartecosmetics.com/pages/offers) and choose the Military Personnel offer.
2. Click **Apply Now** and complete verification through **SheerID** (military status).
3. Once approved, check your email for your one-time-use discount code (re-issued at the start of each month for verified members).
4. Shop tarte.com, add in-stock eligible items (up to $500), and enter the code at checkout. Only one code applies — the 40% won't stack with anything else.

### How To Redeem In Store
There is no in-store Tarte military discount. The offer is **online only at tarte.com**. Ignore any source that tells you to present a military ID at a register for a Tarte discount.

### How Verification Works
Verification runs through **SheerID**, a third-party identity service. You submit your details on Tarte's Special Offers page; SheerID confirms eligibility (it may ask for documentation if it can't auto-verify). Approved members receive a unique, **one-time-use** code, and a fresh code is emailed at the **start of each month**. Verification is per Tarte's/SheerID's terms and can change; it is not a permanent account discount.
