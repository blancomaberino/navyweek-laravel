# Secretlab — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Secretlab TITAN Evo chair at a typical ~$549 price.

## Headline finding

**Secretlab offers a small, fixed military discount — reportedly $20 off via ID.me for active duty, retirees, and veterans — but it is not special: the same $20 is available to anyone through Secretlab's referral program or newsletter signup.** On a ~$549 chair, $20 is under 4%, and because Secretlab is a premium brand that rarely runs deep percentage sales, the honest advice is to **treat the military discount, the referral $20, and the newsletter first-order credit as interchangeable $20-ish options — take whichever you can stack-free — and time your purchase to Secretlab's seasonal sales (Black Friday / anniversary), which usually beat any of them.**

Archetype: **E (small identity discount via ID.me)** on a **premium, MAP-tight, rarely-deeply-discounted** brand — closest in spirit to a "the discount barely moves the needle" case. There is **no GovX storefront, no student program, and no first-responder-only tier** that's larger than the general $20. Aggregator claims of "$150 off" or "60% off Secretlab military" are fabricated — those numbers are scraped from unrelated sitewide-sale headlines, not a military offer.
