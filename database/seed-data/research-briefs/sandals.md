# Sandals Resorts — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 7, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 5-night all-inclusive stay for two adults at a Sandals resort, booked at a published promotional rate of $700/night = **$3,500** (Sandals online quotes are all-inclusive package prices; no tax-free channel competes here, so prices are as quoted). Beaches Resorts (the family brand) runs the identical program and is covered below, clearly labeled.

## Headline finding

Sandals (and sister brand Beaches) runs a real, official, year-round **10% "Service Personnel Discount"** for US and Canadian military — and it is the rare **combinable** kind: both the brand page and SheerID describe it as an **additional** 10% applied on top of Sandals' already-discounted special/promotional rates, so the right move is sale rate first, then the 10% on top. It is verified through SheerID inside a Sandals account (not a promo code) and applied to the reservation before final payment. Around Veterans Day the brand has historically boosted military savings to **15%** (2024 and 2025), which is the best-known booking window. Archetype: **D — combinable discount (stacking is the strategy)**, secondary **I — travel/loyalty**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **The 10% stacks on public promotions — this is confirmed and is the headline.** The brand's own specials page calls it "an additional 10% savings year round" and lists it alongside its public sales; SheerID says "an additional 10% off their special packages"; Sandals' Best Price Guarantee states the booking engine "will automatically apply all combinable offers." (All accessed July 7, 2026.)
2. **It is not a code — it's an account-level discount.** Verify via SheerID under Profile → Service Personnel Discount, then apply it to the reservation in the Trips tab. It must be attached **before final payment** (reported by Military.com; the brand page says applied to a "new reservation" — apply early).
3. **The $100 book-online offer and other individual credits:** the engine auto-applies "combinable" offers, but Sandals does not publish which offers are excluded from combining with the military 10%. Not asserted — let the engine's quote decide.
4. **Rakuten/portal cashback + the military discount is unverified.** Because the discount is applied in-account rather than by coupon code, tracking may survive, but neither Rakuten's nor Sandals' terms confirm payout on such bookings. Do not promise the 2%.
5. **WeSalute 10% is a substitute, not a stack** — it runs through American Airlines Vacations' private engine, is online-only via the WeSalute link, and is forfeited if a phone rep touches the booking (WeSalute terms, accessed July 7, 2026).
6. **Airfare:** the 10% applies to the resort/package portion; editorial sources say flights are excluded — treat flight components as full price.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Price your stay at sandals.com or beaches.com during a public sale and book (reservations can be held from $49/person deposit).
2. Sign in to (or create) your free Sandals account.
3. In your account Profile, open the **Service Personnel Discount** section and complete SheerID verification (name, date of birth, branch of service).
4. Once verified, open your reservation under the **Trips** tab (use "Find A Reservation" if it isn't linked) and apply the discount to the booking.
5. Do this **before you pay the final balance** — the discount attaches to the reservation, not the receipt.
6. Bring your military ID or DD214 to check-in (editorial sources report it's required and the difference is charged if you can't show it).

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable — Sandals has no retail checkout. Phone (1-888-SANDALS) and travel-agent bookings can carry the discount, but the verification still happens in your Sandals account.

### How Verification Works
SheerID, once, inside your Sandals account. You verify after booking — not at a checkout gate — so it never blocks a sale price. If instant verification fails, SheerID requests documents (its standard flow); support runs through SheerID's help center and Sandals at 1-888-SANDALS. The brand does not state how long verification remains valid; re-verify if your account status lapses.

### FAQ
#### Does Sandals offer a military discount?
Yes — an official, year-round additional 10% for US and Canadian military, verified through SheerID in your Sandals account. Beaches Resorts runs the identical program.
#### How much is the Sandals military discount?
10% off the resort portion, applied on top of current promotional rates. Around Veterans Day 2024 and 2025 Sandals boosted it to 15% for active and retired military (historical, not guaranteed).
#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
US veterans, retirees, reserves, and National Guard are eligible per SheerID. SheerID's offer page also lists spouses; the brand page doesn't mention them, so expect verification to decide. Dependents are not stated.
#### How do I verify my status?
Book first, then sign in to your Sandals account, open Profile → Service Personnel Discount, and complete the SheerID check (name, birthday, branch). Apply the discount to your trip before final payment.
#### Does Sandals use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
SheerID, inside your Sandals account. GovX merely refers members to the same program. WeSalute offers a separate 10% through American Airlines Vacations that requires paid membership — no better than the free direct path.
#### Is there a Sandals military promo code?
No. The discount is account-applied after verification. Any site advertising a "Sandals military coupon code" is publishing a fake.
#### Can I combine it with sales and other deals?
Yes — that's the strength of this program. Both Sandals and SheerID describe it as an *additional* 10% on top of special packages, and the booking engine auto-applies combinable offers. Airfare is reported excluded.
#### What is excluded?
Flights (reported), more than one room per member (reported), and any rate where you can't show military ID or a DD214 at check-in — the difference gets charged back.
#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Sandals?
Book direct online during a public sale, apply the verified 10% before final payment, and if it's early November, check for the historical 15% Veterans Day boost first.
#### Does Sandals offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Police, firefighters, EMTs, and search-and-rescue members get a parallel 10%, as do active FEMA members. No nurse, teacher, or student program is published.
#### Does Sandals run a Veterans Day sale?
Historically yes — 15% for active and retired military (including Reserve and National Guard) in November 2024 (book by November 16, 2024; travel through December 31, 2025) and again through November 11, 2025.
