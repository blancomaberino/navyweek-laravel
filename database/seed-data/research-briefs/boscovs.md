# Boscov's — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $200 apparel order at Boscovs.com (apparel sits in the full 15% tier).

## Headline finding

Boscov's runs a **real, evergreen, self-verified military discount** — **up to 15% off**, tiered by department (15% on apparel and most goods, 10% on jewelry/shoes/home, 5% on cosmetics/fragrances/candy/small appliances). Its standout feature: the discount **applies even during sale events** — it layers on top of already-marked-down prices — which is unusual for a department store and is the whole reason it wins. The code is **good for a full year**, works **in-store and online**, and covers **service members, veterans, and (per Boscov's help content) registered spouses and dependents 16+**. The one hard limit: it **cannot be combined with any other coupon/promo code** (one discount per order), so it's an either/or against Boscov's frequent public 15%-off codes. Archetype: **department-store loyalty/verified-discount** — the best play for an eligible shopper is simply the military code on clearance/sale merchandise, since no public coupon touches those items. There is no GovX, exchange (AAFES/NEX), or ExpertVoice channel for Boscov's; the competition is the military code vs. a public sitewide code vs. a thin 2% cashback layer.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **Stacks on sale prices — yes.** The military discount applies "even during sales events," i.e., on top of already-reduced prices. This is the program's biggest edge over typical retailer military discounts that exclude sale items.
2. **Stacks with other coupon/promo codes — no.** Boscov's allows one discount per order; the military code cannot be combined with any other discount or promo code. So it's military code **or** a public code, never both.
3. **Rewards points — layer separately.** Boscov's credit-card / rewards points accrue on the purchase regardless of the code used, but the military discount **cannot reduce a Boscov's card Payment on Account**.
4. **Cashback (Rakuten 2%)** — unverified whether it tracks on a discount-code / verified checkout; identity-gated or coupon-applied orders commonly break portal tracking. Treat as a possible small add-on, not a guaranteed stack. Re-check monthly.
5. **Excluded from the discount entirely:** toys, BOGO-free promotions, beauty salon, DYBT items, Optical Center, Travel Center, gift cards.

### FAQ

#### Does Boscov's offer a military discount?
Yes. Boscov's offers up to 15% off for eligible military shoppers, valid in-store and online for one full year per enrollment.

#### How much is the Boscov's military discount?
Up to 15%, tiered by department: 15% on apparel and most goods, 10% on jewelry, shoes, glassware/chinaware, housewares, mattresses, rugs and home/patio furniture, 5% on cosmetics, fragrances, candy and small appliances.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans, retirees and reserve/guard are eligible. Per Boscov's help content, registered spouses and dependent children 16+ also qualify and each must enroll for their own code (confirm at enrollment).

#### How do I verify my status?
Enroll at boscovs.com/military with your details; you get an email confirmation and a code valid for one year. It's a Boscov's-hosted form, not ID.me.

#### Does Boscov's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
Boscov's does **not** use ID.me. Its official verification page cites "Yes Marketing"; some third-party guides say SheerID. There is no Boscov's GovX marketplace.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — both. Apply the code at Boscovs.com checkout, or show/print the coupon or barcode in store.

#### What is excluded?
Toys, BOGO-free promos, beauty salon, DYBT items, Optical Center, Travel Center, gift cards; reduced 5% tier on cosmetics/fragrances/candy/small appliances; can't reduce a Boscov's card Payment on Account.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
It stacks on **sale/clearance prices** (a big plus) but **cannot be combined with other coupon or promo codes** — one discount per order.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Boscov's?
Use the military code on sale/clearance merchandise — it's the only discount that layers on the marked-down price. On full-price goods, compare the military tier against any live sitewide code and pick the larger.

#### Does Boscov's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not verified — the published program is the military discount. Check current Boscov's promotions for public codes anyone can use.

#### Does Boscov's run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
The military discount is evergreen (year-round), not a holiday event. Boscov's runs public seasonal sales (Memorial Day, July 4, Veterans Day, Black Friday) that the military code stacks on.
