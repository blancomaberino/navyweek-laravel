# Dollar Rent A Car — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 5-day midsize/intermediate car rental (illustrative base rate ≈ $50/day → ≈ $250 base + taxes/fees; **rates are dynamic and location/date-specific — verify live at checkout**).

## Headline finding

Dollar Rent A Car (a **Hertz Global Holdings** brand, alongside Hertz and Thrifty) offers military/veteran savings through **two distinct, verified lanes**: (1) a **5% VetRewards discount** delivered via a **Veterans Advantage® / VetRewards CDP code** you enter at booking, and (2) an **"Military and First Responder Discount"** surfaced on **ID.me Shop** (marketed as "up to 25% off") that verifies status through **ID.me / SheerID** at checkout. For **active-duty on official orders**, Dollar also honors **U.S. Government car-rental agreement rates**. Dollar publishes **no single flat military percentage on dollar.com** — the 5% figure is tied to the Veterans Advantage program, and the "up to 25%" is an ID.me marketplace headline, not a guaranteed rate. Archetype: **contract-code / marketplace-verified rate — the channel and code matter more than one number, and the military rate is not automatically the cheapest.** The smart move is to price the VetRewards/ID.me rate against a **prepaid "Pay Now"** rate and a **Costco/USAA/AAA** channel on the same car and date, and take the lowest.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **VetRewards 5% applies to the pay-later base rate only** — taxes and fees are excluded, and it will not layer on top of a separate contract/CDP discount (one CDP code per reservation).
2. **You cannot stack two rate codes** — a Veterans Advantage CDP, an ID.me military code, and a Costco/USAA/AAA rate are mutually exclusive rate buckets; the booking engine takes one.
3. **Prepaid "Pay Now" rates** are their own bucket and often undercut any percentage discount on a flexible rate — always price both.
4. **Government-agreement (official travel) rates** require orders or a government travel card at pickup; they are not for leisure trips.

### FAQ
#### Does Dollar Rent A Car offer a military discount?
Yes. Dollar offers a **5% VetRewards discount** (via Veterans Advantage, applied with a CDP code), an **"up to 25%" Military & First Responder rate** through ID.me Shop, and **U.S. Government agreement rates** for active duty on official travel.

#### How much is the Dollar military discount?
The published figure is **5%** on the pay-later base rate through VetRewards. ID.me markets **"up to 25% off,"** which varies by date and location and isn't guaranteed.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, and **family members** qualify through Veterans Advantage; veterans also qualify for the ID.me military rate.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **Veterans Advantage/VetRewards enrollment** (for the CDP code) or **ID.me / SheerID** online, and by showing a **military/government ID** at the counter.

#### Does Dollar use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Dollar's online military rate is verified via **ID.me / SheerID**. The VetRewards lane uses **Veterans Advantage**. GovX and WeSalute are not the primary Dollar mechanic.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — apply the CDP/ID.me rate online, or present ID at the counter. Official-travel rates require orders/a government card at pickup.

#### What is excluded?
Taxes and fees are excluded from the 5% base discount, and you **cannot stack** multiple rate codes. Government rates require official travel documentation.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or a Costco/USAA rate?
No. The booking engine accepts **one rate code per reservation** — military, Costco, USAA, and AAA rates are mutually exclusive.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to rent from Dollar?
Run the **VetRewards/ID.me rate**, a **prepaid "Pay Now"** rate, and your **Costco/USAA/AAA** rate on the same car and date, and book the lowest — the military lane is not automatically cheapest.

#### Does Dollar offer a first responder discount?
Yes — the ID.me "**Military and First Responder Discount**" covers first responders as a separate eligible audience.
