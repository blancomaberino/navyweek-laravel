# Olive Garden Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Olive Garden (a Darden Restaurants brand) does **not** offer an everyday or year-round military/veteran discount. Its official FAQ states plainly that it does not give a discount to "one particular group of guests." **There is no everyday 10% military discount** — the only "10%" on Olive Garden's site is an unrelated bulk gift-card purchase discount ($1,000+). The single confirmed military offer is a **limited-time Veterans Day free entrée**: on Veterans Day (most recently November 11, 2025), dine-in veterans and active-duty service members get one free entrée from a special menu by showing proof of military service. Dine-in only; beverages and gratuity not included; family members do not get free meals. There is no third-party verification provider (ID.me/SheerID/GovX) — eligibility is confirmed in-restaurant by showing a military ID or other proof of service. The most important caveat: outside of Veterans Day, the page must not promise any military price break, because Olive Garden's own policy is that it has none.

### Intro
Olive Garden does not offer an everyday military or veteran discount. Its parent company, Darden Restaurants, has a stated policy of not giving any one group of guests a special price, so there is no year-round percentage off for active-duty members, veterans, or retirees — and no senior, first-responder, or teacher discount either.

What Olive Garden does offer is a **Veterans Day free meal**. On Veterans Day each year, veterans and active-duty service members who dine in can choose a free entrée from a special menu by showing proof of military service. It is dine-in only and does not include drinks or gratuity.

This is an independent guide, not affiliated with Olive Garden. Olive Garden controls the terms and can change or end the Veterans Day offer at any time; confirm current details with your local restaurant before visiting.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no online military discount. Olive Garden's Veterans Day offer is dine-in only and cannot be redeemed for online/To Go orders. To save online at other times, sign up for the Olive Garden eClub or check the Specials page for general (non-military) coupons.

### How To Redeem In Store (Veterans Day)
1. Go to a participating Olive Garden on Veterans Day (most recently November 11) during business hours; consider joining the online waitlist first, as locations get busy.
2. Dine in — the offer is not valid for takeout/To Go.
3. Tell your server you are redeeming the Veterans Day military offer and choose one entrée from the special Veterans Day menu.
4. Show proof of military service (e.g., military ID, veteran ID card, DD-214, or veteran-designated driver's license).
5. Pay for any beverages and items outside the offer, and tip on the full pre-discount value of the meal.

### How Verification Works
Olive Garden does not use a third-party verification service (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute) and no online account is required. For the Veterans Day offer, staff confirm eligibility in person when you show proof of military service. Because acceptable proof can vary by location and manager discretion, call your local restaurant ahead if you are unsure your ID will be accepted.

### FAQ

#### Does Olive Garden offer a military discount?
Not on an everyday basis. Olive Garden's official FAQ says it does not offer a discount to any one group of guests. The only military-specific offer is a free entrée on Veterans Day for dine-in veterans and active-duty service members.

#### How much is the Olive Garden military discount?
There is no everyday percentage discount. On Veterans Day, the offer is one free entrée from a special menu (a 100% discount on that single dish), dine-in only, excluding drinks and gratuity. Olive Garden does not publish an everyday "10% off" military discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and active-duty service members qualify for the Veterans Day free meal. The offer does **not** extend to spouses, dependents, family, or friends dining with them.

#### How do I verify my status?
Show proof of military service in person at the restaurant on Veterans Day — for example a military ID, veteran ID card, DD-214, or veteran-designated driver's license. There is no online verification step.

#### Does Olive Garden use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Olive Garden does not use any third-party military verification service. Eligibility is checked in-restaurant by showing proof of service.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / dine-in?
The Veterans Day offer is dine-in only at participating locations. It cannot be used for takeout or To Go orders, and there is no in-restaurant discount on other days.

#### What is excluded?
Beverages and gratuity are not included, the offer is limited to one entrée from a special menu, it is dine-in only, and it does not cover family or friends.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Not stated in official source. The Veterans Day free entrée is a standalone dine-in offer; assume it does not stack with other coupons unless a local restaurant confirms otherwise.

#### Does Olive Garden offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No. Under the same policy that rules out a military discount, Olive Garden does not offer standardized first-responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discounts.

#### Does Olive Garden have a senior discount or rewards program?
No senior discount and no rewards program (per the official FAQ). You can join the free eClub for email coupons, and guests get a complimentary dessert on their birthday when dining in.
