# GM (General Motors) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

General Motors runs the **GM Military Appreciation Program** (marketed by GM Financial as the **"GM Military Offer"**), which lets eligible service members and their sponsored spouse buy or lease an eligible **new Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, or Cadillac** at a special offer. Eligibility — **Active Duty, Reservists, National Guard, Veterans, and Retirees** of the U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard — is validated through **ID.me**. You register at **gmmilitaryappreciation.com**, validate your status, and request an **Authorization Number** (you get **two per year**, each valid **90 days**), then bring it plus a copy of your driver's license to a participating GM dealer before delivery. The offer is delivered as **division-specific special pricing / bonus cash rather than one flat percentage** — the only figure GM publishes on a brand site is **Chevrolet's "$500 total cash allowance on most models."** Buick, GMC, and Cadillac do not publish a dollar figure, and the official GM Financial terms state the offer is **"not available with some other offers,"** so aggregator claims of a fixed "$X off across all brands" or specific incentive stacks should not be stated as fact. Take **new retail delivery by 1/4/2027** for the current cycle.

> **Accuracy note.** As of this pass (July 2, 2026), Chevrolet.com does officially state a **$500 total cash allowance on most models** for the military offer — so the "$500" figure that aggregators cite is confirmed **for Chevrolet only**. Do **not** generalize it to Buick/GMC/Cadillac (no dollar figure is published for those), and do **not** assert specific stacks (Customer Cash, Loyalty Cash) or specific model eligibility (e.g., Corvette) — GM's terms say "not available with some other offers" and the eligible-vehicle list "may change at any time." Direct shoppers to the live eligible-vehicle list and their dealer for the real out-the-door number.

Additional verified detail (from the official Rules PDF, Rev. 1/2/26, unless noted):
- **Required documentation at dealer:** a copy of the eligible purchaser's driver's license (license number redacted) showing the **ZIP Code and Date of Birth used to obtain the Authorization Number**, which must match the license.
- **Vehicle retention:** if buying with cash or a loan, you must **own and retain the vehicle for at least six (6) months** from delivery.
- **Titling:** vehicle must be for **personal, non-commercial use**; cannot be titled to a business; may be titled to a living trust in the purchaser's name or with a co-buyer under stated conditions.
- **Documentation fees:** dealers may charge a **maximum $75** doc fee (exceptions where state law/uniform dealer policy applies).
- **Eligible-vehicle list is dynamic:** "Vehicle eligibility may change at any time without notice"; some models may have limited availability — list lives at gmmilitaryappreciation.com/vehicles.
- **Dealer participation is voluntary** — not every GM dealer honors the program.
- **Authorization access route:** register/validate via ID.me at gmmilitaryappreciation.com (the entry portal gmmilitarydiscount.com redirects into the same program), then request the Authorization Number.
- **Sanctions:** selling or misusing an Authorization Number can mean up to a **5-year** suspension plus repayment of incentive monies; other violations carry a 2-year suspension per violation.

> **Discrepancy flagged.** An older GMC marketing page (gmc.com/retail/military-discount, 2021 imagery) limits veterans to **"within 3 years of discharge date"** and mentions **"designated household members."** GMC's **current 2026** discount hub (gmc.com/discount-programs) and the governing **Rules PDF (Rev. 1/2/26)** say **all veterans** qualify and name only a **spouse** as the sponsored purchaser. Treat the current hub + Rules PDF as authoritative: **all veterans are eligible; sponsored purchaser = spouse.** Do not publish the stale "within 3 years" restriction.

- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible.
- **Military spouses** — Eligible **only as a "Sponsored Purchaser" sponsored by the eligible participant** (not independently).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **gmmilitaryappreciation.com** (the gmmilitarydiscount.com entry point leads here).
2. Create an account and **validate your military status through ID.me**.
3. Request your **Authorization Number**. GM emails an electronic confirmation once it's issued.
4. Browse the current **Eligible Vehicles** list (gmmilitaryappreciation.com/vehicles) — it changes over time.
5. You may request up to **two Authorization Numbers per year**, each valid **90 days**.

### How To Redeem In Store (at the dealer)
1. Confirm your chosen **GM dealer participates** in the program (participation is voluntary — call ahead).
2. Bring your **Authorization Number** to the dealer **before completing the purchase or lease** (the dealer must have it prior to delivery).
3. Present a **copy of your driver's license** (license number redacted) showing the **ZIP Code and Date of Birth** used to obtain the Authorization Number — these must match.
4. The dealer applies the **program offer** to your eligible new vehicle.
5. If buying with cash/loan, plan to **keep the vehicle at least six months** (retention requirement).

### How Verification Works
GM validates eligibility through **ID.me**. You create/log in to an ID.me account at gmmilitaryappreciation.com and confirm your military status; once validated, you can request your Authorization Number. (For details on the ID.me process, GM points to ID.me/about.) The Authorization Number — not your ID.me login itself — is what the dealer needs, and it expires **90 days** after issuance. GM also conducts **periodic audits** and may request documentation (military ID, marriage license for a sponsored spouse, title/registration) to confirm eligibility, so keep your supporting records.

### FAQ

#### Does GM offer a military discount?
Yes. The **GM Military Appreciation Program** gives eligible service members and a sponsored spouse a special offer on eligible new Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, and Cadillac vehicles, validated through ID.me.

#### How much is the GM military discount?
It's set by brand and model, not one flat percentage. The only figure GM publishes on a brand site is **Chevrolet's "$500 total cash allowance on most models."** Buick, GMC, and Cadillac don't publish a dollar amount. Confirm the actual figure with a participating dealer — and be skeptical of third-party pages quoting a single "$ off" for every brand.

#### Which brands and vehicles are eligible?
New **Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, and Cadillac** vehicles on GM's current eligible-vehicle list, which changes over time and can have limited availability. Check gmmilitaryappreciation.com/vehicles for the live list.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**All veterans and retirees qualify** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard), along with Active Duty, Reservists, and National Guard. A **spouse** qualifies only as a sponsored purchaser under the eligible member. Dependents/children are **not** listed for this program.

#### How do I verify my status and get the discount?
Register at **gmmilitaryappreciation.com**, validate through **ID.me**, then request an **Authorization Number**. Bring that number plus a copy of your driver's license to a participating GM dealer before purchase.

#### Does GM use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
GM's program uses **ID.me** to validate military status.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / how does the dealer step work?
Yes — the offer is redeemed **in person at a participating GM dealer**. Give the dealer your Authorization Number before delivery and show a copy of your driver's license whose ZIP and date of birth match what you used to get the number.

#### Can I combine the military discount with other offers?
GM's official terms say the offer is **"not available with some other offers,"** and GM does not publish a combinable list. Don't assume it stacks with Customer Cash, loyalty, or financing offers — ask the dealer what specifically applies to your deal.

#### How many vehicles can I buy, and is there a deadline?
You can request **two Authorization Numbers per year**, each valid **90 days**. The current program cycle requires taking **new retail delivery by 1/4/2027** (dates change each cycle).

#### Does GM offer a first responder, teacher, or supplier discount too?
Yes — as **separate** programs: **First Responder Appreciation**, **Educator Appreciation** (Chevrolet/Cadillac), **GM Supplier Discount**, **GM Family First** (employees/family), and **Dealership Employee Discount**. Each has its own site and terms and is generally not combinable with the Military program.
