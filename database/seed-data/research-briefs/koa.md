# KOA (Kampgrounds of America) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 11, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 2-night RV/tent stay at a standard KOA daily rate of ~$50/night = **$100** (representative national mid-range rate as of July 11, 2026).

## Headline finding

KOA has **no single national military discount**. KOA is a **franchise system — every campground is independently owned and operated**, so each location decides whether to offer a military/veteran discount, at what rate, and for which season. Where offered, it is **most commonly 10% off the daily registration rate, with some locations at up to 20%**, verified by a valid military/state/federal service ID shown **at check-in** (KOA's national deals page describes it as "ID verification at arrival or via text the day of arrival"). It is a **manager/location-set discount, not an ID.me/SheerID/GovX program**, and at most participating campgrounds it **cannot be combined with KOA Rewards or any other offer**.

Do not confuse two different things: the **military discount** (location-set, free to use with an ID) versus **KOA Rewards** (the paid $39/yr loyalty program — formerly "Value Kard Rewards" — that gives **anyone** 10% off daily rates; not a military benefit). They are separate and, per KOA's terms, the military discount generally does not stack on Rewards. The genuine best public deal for a flexible camper is usually the **Mid-Week Savings 20% off (Sun–Thu)**, which KOA states Rewards members can layer an **additional 10%** onto — beating the 10% military rate outright. For readers with base access, an on-base **military Famcamp** is frequently cheaper still (often ~$20–35/night) and tax-advantaged. Archetype: **I (travel/loyalty — status/loyalty layers, rates don't stack)** with a strong **J (unofficial/location-discretion)** overlay.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + location terms)
1. **The military discount does not stack with KOA Rewards or other offers** at participating campgrounds — it's an either/or (koa.com/blog/koa-discounts/; KOA Rewards terms: "can not be combined with other offers").
2. **KOA Rewards 10% excludes weekly, monthly, and seasonal rates** (koa.com/rewards/) — the long-stay rate is already the discounted rate.
3. **Mid-Week Savings 20% DOES stack with the KOA Rewards 10%** — KOA's own deals page states Rewards members "receive an additional 10% discount." This is the one reliably combinable public path.
4. **No promo code + military discount combo exists** — the military discount is applied at check-in by a location, not via a stackable code.
5. **Terms are set per campground.** Because each KOA is independently owned, the only authoritative combinability answer is the specific campground's — confirm at booking.

### How To Redeem (In Person / At Booking)
1. Find your campground page on koa.com and open its **Deals / Hot Deals** tab, or **call the campground** to confirm it offers a military discount and at what rate for your dates.
2. Book your site (online or by phone). The military rate is generally applied by the campground, not via an online code.
3. At **check-in / arrival**, present a valid **military, state, or federal service ID** to have the discount applied (some locations accept ID by text the day of arrival).
4. Confirm at booking whether the rate can combine with anything else — usually it cannot.

### How Verification Works
KOA uses **no third-party verifier** (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute) for the military discount. Verification is **manual at the campground**: you show a valid service ID at arrival (or, per KOA's deals page, via text the day of arrival). Because each campground is independently operated, the exact ID accepted and the eligible groups are set locally — call ahead if you're unsure.

### FAQ

#### Does KOA offer a military discount?
Yes, but not as one national program. KOA campgrounds are independently owned, and **participating locations** offer a military discount — commonly **10% off the daily registration rate**, with some at up to 20%. Availability and rate vary by campground.

#### How much is the KOA military discount?
Most commonly **10% off the daily rate**; some locations go up to **20%**. There is no single fixed national percentage, and no promo code — the rate is set by each campground.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and active/retired military are named at participating campgrounds. Spouse and dependent eligibility is **not stated nationally** — confirm with the specific KOA.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Show a valid **military, state, or federal service ID at check-in** (some locations accept it by text the day of arrival). There is no online verification step.

#### Does KOA use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. KOA's military discount is verified **manually at the campground** with a service ID — no third-party verifier and no online code.

#### Can I use the military discount online or only in person?
The discount is applied by the campground, generally at **check-in/arrival**, not through an online promo code. Confirm the rate when you book online or by phone.

#### What's excluded, and can I combine it with other deals?
At most campgrounds the military discount **cannot be combined** with KOA Rewards or other offers. KOA also doesn't honor AAA, AARP, or Good Sam. Seasonal promos have blackout dates.

#### Is KOA Rewards the same as the military discount?
No. **KOA Rewards** is a paid loyalty program ($39/yr, formerly "Value Kard Rewards") giving **anyone** 10% off daily rates. It's separate from the military discount and generally can't be stacked with it.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to camp at KOA?
If your dates flex to **Sun–Thu, the Mid-Week 20%** beats the 10% military rate — and Rewards members stack an extra 10% (~28% off). For a one-off weekend trip, use the location's **military 10–20%** with your ID. If you camp KOA often, buy **KOA Rewards**. If you have base access, price a **military Famcamp** first — it's frequently cheaper.

#### Does KOA offer a first responder or EMS discount?
Some campgrounds do — KOA lists a "Military & EMS" discount, and its blog notes ~10% for firefighters, police, and EMTs at participating locations. It's location-set like the military discount.
