## Headline finding

HEYDUDE (a Crocs, Inc. brand) offers a **20% military/first-responder discount on full-price footwear and accessories**, verified through **ID.me** on heydude.com, and it also lists the brand on **GovX** as a second verification channel. It's confirmed via HEYDUDE's own ID.me landing page (heydude.com/pages/idme) and the ID.me HEYDUDE storefront. The honest headline: *20% off is a solid standing discount, but it applies only to **full-price** styles (sale and collab styles are excluded), and HEYDUDE runs frequent public sales — so a live sale can easily beat the 20%, and the two don't stack.* This is a **standard retail-verification archetype (ID.me / GovX gate, full-price only)**. Best move: verify via ID.me for the standing 20%, but check the Sale section first — on discounted styles, the public sale price often wins.
