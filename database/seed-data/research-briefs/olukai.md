During a genuine 20–30%+ public event, the sale beats the 10%, and you can only pick one. Archetype: **A (GovX-partner identity discount)** with a strong **C flavor** (real discount, but a deep sale often beats it). Ignore the aggregator claims of **"20% off via ID.me,"** **"15–25%,"** and **"50% off"** military codes — none is verified. OluKai uses **GovX, not ID.me**, and the only figure OluKai itself publishes is **10%**.
