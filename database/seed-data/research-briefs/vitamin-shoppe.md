# The Vitamin Shoppe — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$100 supplements order** (protein + a few bottles), the realistic Vitamin Shoppe basket.

## Headline finding

The Vitamin Shoppe's military discount is a **standing, in-store program called "Military Mondays": 20% off your entire purchase, every Monday, year-round, for all veterans and active-duty Armed Forces members**, redeemed by presenting a **valid military ID** at the register. The important honest finding: this is **in-store only, and The Vitamin Shoppe does not use ID.me (or any online military verification)** — there is **no military discount at checkout on vitaminshoppe.com**. So the online savings path is different from the in-store one: online shoppers lean on the free **Healthy Awards** loyalty program (1 point/$1; 125 points = a $5 Cash Back Award), frequent sitewide promo codes/sales, Auto-Delivery, and ~**2% cashback via Rakuten** (as of June 2026). Historically the brand deepens the offer around Veterans Day (**25% in 2024**, in-store on Nov 11). Archetype: **in-store identity discount (a real Military Mondays program)** with **no online military route** — the online game is loyalty + sale-stacking.
