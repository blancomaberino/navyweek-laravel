# Ray-Ban Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Ray-Ban participates in **ID.me Shop** as a partner store, and ID.me confirms that Ray-Ban offers savings to verified **Military** community members (and to nurses, first responders, teachers, government, and medical groups) redeemed online by verifying at checkout. The single most important caveat: **the exact discount percentage is NOT published on any primary source.** Ray-Ban's own website (ray-ban.com) never mentions a military or veteran discount, ID.me, or a percentage anywhere — not on its Exclusive Offers page, Terms of Sale, or FAQ. The ID.me Ray-Ban store page renders only boilerplate ("Yes, Ray-Ban has a Military discount and/or cash back offer") with no rate. The widely-cited **"15%" figure is from coupon aggregators only and could not be verified against a primary source as of June 23, 2026.** Two further verified facts shape the page: **ID.me explicitly states there is NO Ray-Ban Meta military discount**, and Ray-Ban's Terms of Sale **exclude Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses (incl. Gen2) and gift cards** from promotions/promocodes, with discounts "not combinable."

> Accuracy note: Numerous aggregators advertise a flat "15% Ray-Ban military discount," some "up to 30%," and several claim "15% off Ray-Ban Meta." None of these are confirmed by Ray-Ban or by ID.me's rendered offer terms. ID.me's own Ray-Ban Meta discount page states the opposite — that it is **not aware of any Ray-Ban Meta military discount.** Treat every percentage as **unverified** until confirmed at ID.me checkout.

### FAQ

#### Does Ray-Ban offer a military discount?
Ray-Ban partners with ID.me, and ID.me confirms Ray-Ban offers savings to verified Military community members, redeemed by verifying through ID.me at checkout. However, Ray-Ban's own website does not advertise or document the offer, and the exact discount percentage is not published on any official source.

#### How much is the Ray-Ban military discount?
The exact percentage is **not published** by Ray-Ban or on ID.me's rendered offer terms. Coupon sites commonly cite "15%" (and some "up to 30%"), but those figures are unverified. Confirm the live rate at ray-ban.com checkout via ID.me.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans are covered under the ID.me Military community. Ray-Ban and ID.me do not state whether military spouses or dependents qualify for the Ray-Ban offer, so check directly through ID.me.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a free ID.me account, verify your community (e.g., Military), then choose the ID.me verification option at ray-ban.com checkout. Accepted documents are set by ID.me.

#### Does Ray-Ban use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Ray-Ban's military savings surface through **ID.me** (the route tied to ray-ban.com checkout). Ray-Ban also appears on **GovX** and **WeSalute** marketplaces, but those are third-party platforms with separately gated terms. Ray-Ban's **student** discount uses **UNiDAYS**, not ID.me.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
In-store redemption is **not documented** by Ray-Ban or ID.me. Treat the military discount as online-only unless a store confirms otherwise.

#### Is there a Ray-Ban Meta (AI glasses) military discount?
No. ID.me states it is not aware of a Ray-Ban Meta military discount, and Ray-Ban's Terms of Sale exclude Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses (including Gen2) from promotions and promocodes.

#### What is excluded?
Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses and gift cards are excluded from promotions/promocodes, and discounts cannot be combined with other coupons, sales, or insurance. Other caps and exclusions are not stated.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Ray-Ban's terms state discounts cannot be combined with other coupons, promotions, sales, or previous purchases.

#### Does Ray-Ban offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
ID.me lists Ray-Ban under Nurse, First Responder, Teacher, Medical, and Government communities (verified the same way as Military, with percentages unpublished). Students have a separate official route via UNiDAYS on ray-ban.com.
