# Columbia Sportswear Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Columbia Sportswear offers a **10% military discount** on Columbia.com, verified through **ID.me** at checkout. The same 10% tier is also offered to first responders, teachers, and students. Eligible military groups (per Columbia's own page) are "active duty, veterans, retirees, spouses, dependents, and more." Verification happens **in the cart/checkout** via the "Apply Promo Code Or ID.Me Discount" drawer — shoppers select "Verify with ID.me," verify once through ID.me, then the discount applies to the order. The single most important caveat: the discount **cannot be combined with any other offer or discount, does not apply to sale exclusions/gift cards/taxes/shipping, and purchases do not earn Columbia Greater Rewards.** Note: the exact 10% figure is confirmed on the **ID.me Shop** store page; Columbia.com's own discount page describes the program and eligibility but does **not** publish the percentage (it is revealed only after ID.me verification at checkout).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Shop Columbia.com and add the items you want to your cart.
2. In the cart/checkout, find the "Apply Promo Code Or ID.Me Discount" drawer.
3. Select the "Verify with ID.me" prompt.
4. Choose the verification link that matches your group (military, first responder, teacher, or student) and follow ID.me's instructions to create or sign in to your ID.me account and verify your status.
5. Confirm your email address if prompted.
6. Your 10% discount is applied to the eligible items in your order.

### How To Redeem In Store
Columbia's official discount-programs page and the ID.me Shop store page describe the **online ID.me checkout flow only**. An in-store 10% military discount is reported by third-party aggregators but is **not confirmed in any official Columbia source** as of June 23, 2026. Recommend shoppers contact their local Columbia retail/outlet store to confirm whether the ID.me/military discount is honored in person and what ID is required. Do not assert an in-store discount as fact on the page.

### How Verification Works
Columbia uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. Verification happens at checkout via the "Verify with ID.me" link in the cart. You create or sign in to a free ID.me account and prove your status (military verification options include linking a USAA account, entering service details checked against records, using a .mil email, or uploading documents). ID.me — not Columbia — handles your identity documents. Re-verification frequency is not stated by Columbia; ID.me generally maintains a verified status on your account, but a group/offer may require re-confirmation over time — do not promise "verify once forever." If automatic verification fails, follow ID.me's on-screen support and troubleshooting path.

### FAQ

#### Does Columbia offer a military discount?
Yes. Columbia offers a military discount on Columbia.com, verified through ID.me at checkout. (Source: Columbia.com discount-programs page; ID.me Shop.)

#### How much is the Columbia military discount?
It is **10% off** per the ID.me Shop store page. Columbia's own discount page does not publish the percentage — it is shown after you verify with ID.me at checkout. (Source: ID.me Shop — Columbia.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Columbia lists "active duty, veterans, retirees, spouses, dependents, and more." ID.me's military definition adds National Guard, Reserves, and surviving spouses. (Source: Columbia.com; ID.me Help.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
At checkout, open the "Apply Promo Code Or ID.Me Discount" drawer, choose "Verify with ID.me," and follow ID.me's steps (USAA link, service-record check, .mil email, or document upload). (Source: Columbia.com; ID.me.)

#### Does Columbia use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Columbia uses **ID.me**. (Source: Columbia.com discount-programs page; ID.me Shop.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Official Columbia sources describe only the online ID.me checkout flow. An in-store discount is not confirmed in official sources — contact your local Columbia store to confirm. (Source: Columbia.com; ID.me Shop.)

#### What is excluded?
The offer can't be combined with other offers/discounts, used on prior purchases, gift cards, taxes, or shipping, and purchases don't earn Greater Rewards. Columbia can change or cancel the offer anytime. (Source: ID.me Shop restrictions.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The offer "may not be applied to prior purchases, combined with any other offer or discount." (Source: ID.me Shop restrictions.)

#### Does Columbia offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders, teachers, and students each get 10% via ID.me. A nurse/medical or government-employee program is **not stated** as a Columbia offer in official sources. (Source: Columbia.com; ID.me Shop.)

#### Is there a Veterans Day or larger seasonal military discount?
The everyday verified rate is 10%. A higher (20%) Veterans-period promotion has been reported by third-party sites in past years and is not currently confirmed in official sources; check Columbia.com around Veterans Day for any active promo. (Source: aggregator reports — Low confidence.)
