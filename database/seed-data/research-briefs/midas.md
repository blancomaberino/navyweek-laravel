# Midas — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $500 brake-pad-and-rotor repair at a participating U.S. Midas shop (military % applies to "tire and auto repair services").

## Headline finding

Midas has **no standardized, nationwide everyday military discount.** Midas is a franchise system, and any military/veteran discount is **left to individual franchise owners' discretion** — some shops grant roughly 10% off, some year-round, many none, and terms vary shop to shop. What Midas corporate *has* run are **labeled, time-boxed holiday promotions**: historically **15% off tire and auto-repair services Nov 1–30 (Veterans Day 2024)** and **10% off during Military Appreciation Month (May)**, both only "at participating U.S. Midas shops." This is a franchise-discretion / in-store-service archetype (brand-archetype G/J blend): there is no ID.me, GovX, or online military code. For most service members the **honest best path is Midas's public coupons/offers** (oil-change and brake specials), which frequently beat a discretionary ~10% — so **call the specific shop first, get their military price, then compare it against the current public Midas coupon and pick the lower.** The aggregator claim of a "20% off Midas military promo code" is **fabricated** — no such national code exists.

### FAQ

#### Does Midas offer a military discount?
Not as a single national program. Midas shops are franchises, so any military/veteran discount is at the individual shop's discretion. Midas corporate has run seasonal promos (10% in May, 15% for Veterans Day) at participating shops.

#### How much is the Midas military discount?
There's no published national percentage. Where a shop offers one, shoppers commonly report around 10%. The documented corporate promos were 10% (May) and 15% (Veterans Day, Nov 1–30, 2024).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The Veterans Day promo named Active Duty, Retirees, and Veterans. Everyday eligibility (including spouses/dependents) is set per shop — ask before your visit.

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a valid military ID in store. Midas uses no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or online military code.

#### Does Midas use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. There is no third-party verification provider and no GovX storefront for Midas.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
In store only, at participating shops. There is no online military discount or code.

#### What is excluded?
It applies at participating shops only, generally can't be combined with other Midas coupons, and the amount/eligibility vary by location.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Generally no — Midas coupons are typically one per service. Compare the shop's military price against the current public coupon and use the lower one.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Midas?
Call your local shop for their military price, then check midas.com/coupons.aspx for a public coupon on the same service, and book whichever is cheaper. A live brake or oil-change coupon often beats a discretionary 10%.

#### Does Midas offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Nothing is published nationally. Any such discount would be at the individual franchise's discretion.

#### Does Midas run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Historically yes for Veterans Day — 15% off tire and auto-repair services, Nov 1–30, 2024, at participating shops. Treat as historical; confirm with your shop each year.
