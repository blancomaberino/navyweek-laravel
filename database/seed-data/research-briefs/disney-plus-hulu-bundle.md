## Headline finding

**There is no military discount on the Disney+/Hulu Bundle itself.** The Disney Bundle is *explicitly excluded* from both the Disney+ and the Hulu military offers — this is stated in the fine print of each offer, not an oversight. What exists instead are two separate, Exchange-gated (AAFES / ShopMyExchange.com) military discounts on the **individual** services: **25% off the annual Disney+ Premium (No Ads) plan** and **25% off the monthly Hulu (With Ads) plan**. You cannot combine either with the bundle, and you cannot get both discounts *and* the bundled price — it's an either/or choice.

The maximum-savings answer is counter-intuitive: **for most households the standard Disney Bundle is cheaper than stacking the two military discounts**, because the bundle already carries a built-in ~45% discount off standalone pricing. The military discount only pulls ahead in one narrow case — you specifically want **ad-free Disney+**, you're fine with **ads on Hulu**, you're an **authorized Exchange shopper**, and you buy the two services **separately** through ShopMyExchange.com. Archetype: **Exchange-gated subscription offer** (secondary: **bundle-beats-the-discount**) — the official military price is real but narrow and channel-locked, and the retailer's own bundle math frequently wins.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military discount and the Disney Bundle are mutually exclusive.** Hulu's Exchange terms state the discount "cannot be combined with … any other offers, coupons, discounts, promotional offers or pricing (**including the Disney Bundle**)." Disney+'s offer likewise applies only to the standalone annual Premium plan, "not … any Disney Bundle." There is no path to get bundled pricing *and* military pricing together.
2. **The two individual military discounts do not "stack" into a bundle** — they are two separate subscriptions billed separately (Disney+ annual via Exchange; Hulu monthly, Hulu-billed via Exchange). You manage and pay for them independently; there is no combined bill or extra bundle savings.
3. **Disney+ military discount is annual-Premium-only.** It does **not** apply to the Disney+ ad tier or the monthly plan, so there is no "cheap ad-supported Disney+ military" option — the only discounted Disney+ is the No-Ads annual at 25% off.
4. **Hulu military discount is Hulu-billed, With-Ads-only.** Third-party billing (Apple/Roku/Amazon), the No-Ads tier, and Hulu + Live TV are excluded; a plan switch after redemption "may result in forfeiture of discount pricing."
5. **No promo-code or free-trial stacking** on either offer; one discount per ShopMyExchange.com account holder for each.

**Disney+ (25% off annual Premium, No Ads):**
1. Confirm you're an authorized Exchange shopper with a valid ShopMyExchange.com account.

**Hulu (25% off monthly, With Ads):**
1. From the Hulu offer page on ShopMyExchange.com, start the Hulu sign-up.

3. 25% applies and auto-renews monthly until you cancel.

**Do not** subscribe to any "Disney Bundle" SKU if you want the military discount — bundle SKUs void it.

### FAQ

#### Does the Disney+/Hulu Bundle offer a military discount?
No. The Disney Bundle is explicitly excluded from both the Disney+ and Hulu military offers. Military discounts apply only to the **individual** services purchased separately through The Exchange.

#### What is the Disney+/Hulu Bundle military discount price?
There isn't one — no military price exists for the bundle. Separately, military members can get Disney+ Premium annual for **$119.99/yr** (25% off) and Hulu (With Ads) for **~$8.99/mo** (25% off) via The Exchange. The standard Duo bundle is **~$12.99/mo** for everyone (as of July 13, 2026).

#### Can I add the military discount to my existing Disney Bundle?
No. Any bundle SKU voids military pricing. To use the 25% offers you must subscribe to the individual services separately through ShopMyExchange.com.

#### Which is cheaper — the bundle or the two military discounts?
For ad-supported service, the **standard Duo bundle (~$12.99/mo)** is cheaper. The military route only wins if you specifically want **ad-free Disney+** and are OK with ads on Hulu — then buying separately (~$227.90/yr) slightly beats the no-ads bundle (~$239.88/yr).

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through **The Exchange (ShopMyExchange.com)** — not ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute. You must be an authorized Exchange shopper.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Honorably discharged veterans qualify via the Veterans Online Shopping Benefit. Spouses/dependents qualify only if they have their own Exchange privileges. Verify at ShopMyExchange.com.

#### Does the offer work on the ESPN (Trio) bundle?
No. All Disney Bundle tiers (Duo and Trio, ads or no-ads) are excluded from military pricing. The discounts apply only to standalone Disney+ (annual Premium) and standalone Hulu (With Ads).

#### Can I use it in stores or through ID.me/GovX?
No. Both are online-only, Exchange-gated offers. No in-store redemption; no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute path.

#### Does Disney or Hulu run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day bundle sale?
No military-specific bundle sale is on record. The standing 25% Exchange offers on the individual services are the mechanism year-round; public bundle intro promos (open to everyone) appear around major sale events.

#### Can I buy the bundle at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
The Exchange hosts the individual Disney+ and Hulu military offers — but **not** a discounted bundle. There is no ExpertVoice/pro-deal channel for these streaming subscriptions.
