# Helly Hansen — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Helly Hansen jacket (~$250, e.g. a shell/rain jacket).

## Headline finding

Helly Hansen has **two overlapping military paths**, and the aggregator "35% off" figure is **not confirmed by any primary source**. The clean, verifiable everyday offer is **20% off** for US/Canada military via **SheerID**, promoted through Helly Hansen's email-verification flow. Separately, HH runs an **application-based Military "Pro/Member" program** whose exact discount is **discretionary and variable** ("Discounts vary between individuals and professional groups… find your discount on My Account") — HH does not publish a fixed % for it, and the 35% claim can't be verified. Both are **online-only, personal-use-only**. Because HH runs deep seasonal sales, a big public markdown can beat 20% (non-combinable). Archetype: **multi-tier identity discount (SheerID) + a discretionary application membership** — publish the verified 20%, flag the 35% as unconfirmed.
