# Liberty University (Online/Military Tuition) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 3-credit-hour online course (the standard unit students compare) at the military vs. standard per-credit-hour rate.

## Headline finding

**Liberty University's "military discount" is a published, deeply-reduced military tuition rate for online (LUO) programs — one of the largest verifiable education discounts in this batch.** For the 2026–2027 academic year the military rate is **$250/credit hour undergraduate, $310/credit hour master's, and $395/credit hour doctoral** — versus standard online rates of roughly **$390–455 (UG), $580–645 (master's), and $610–675 (doctoral)**. That's about **36% off undergraduate, ~47% off master's, and ~35% off doctoral** tuition. The undergraduate $250 rate is deliberately set to **match the federal Tuition Assistance (TA) cap**, so an active-duty student using TA can often cover undergraduate tuition dollar-for-dollar.

Eligibility is broad — **active duty, Reserve/National Guard, veterans/retirees, spouses of service members and veterans/retirees, and current DoD civilian employees** — and **military children get 15% off** the standard rate. The real maximum-savings path isn't just the rate: it's **the military rate stacked with your education benefit (TA or GI Bill/Yellow Ribbon) plus free ACE credit for military training and a waived application fee**, which together can drive net out-of-pocket to near zero for many service members. Archetype: **education identity-rate (E-like)** — a real, verified, brand-run rate with no coupon game; the "stacking" that matters is with federal benefits, not promo codes.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + benefit terms)
1. **The military rate stacks with federal education benefits** (TA, GI Bill, Yellow Ribbon) — that's the entire strategy; there are no promo codes here.
2. **The UG $250/cr rate = the TA cap**, so TA can cover undergraduate tuition in full for active duty using it.
3. **Military-training credit (ACE) reduces the number of credits you pay for** — evaluate it before enrolling; up to 75% of a degree may transfer, but ≥25% must be completed at Liberty.
4. **Application fee is waived** for service members/veterans (a real, if small, saving).
5. **Military children get 15% off standard**, a separate benefit from the flat military rates — a dependent generally uses one or the other, not both.
6. **Rates are annual** and may change; the figures above are 2026–27.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — eligible; qualifies after basic training is completed.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — eligible.
- **Veterans & retirees** — eligible with Honorable, Under Honorable Conditions, or General discharge.
- **Spouses** of service members and veterans/retirees — eligible (per Liberty's spouse definition).
- **Current DoD civilian employees** — eligible.
- **Military children** — 15% off standard tuition (separate from the flat rate).

### How To Redeem / Enroll
1. Apply to a Liberty online program — the **application fee is waived** for service members/veterans.
2. Submit military documentation (orders, DD-214, and/or your **Joint Services / military transcript**) to Liberty's **Office of Military Affairs** to verify status and apply the military rate.
3. Request a **free ACE evaluation** of military training for transfer credit.
4. Coordinate **TA** (active duty) or **GI Bill/Yellow Ribbon** (veterans/dependents) with the military benefits office to cover tuition.

### How Verification Works
Liberty verifies military status through its **Office of Military Affairs** using your service documentation (orders, DD-214, military transcript) — not a consumer verifier like ID.me. The military rate then applies to eligible LUO tuition. Rates are set per academic year and may change annually.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Rates apply to **online (LUO)** programs; verify residential-program terms separately.
- Doctoral $395 rate generally applies to those continuing from a graduate degree within a set window (about 2 academic years) — confirm your program.
- The military rate is **not** a promo code and isn't combinable with unrelated tuition promotions; it stacks with federal education benefits instead.
- ≥25% of the degree must be completed at Liberty (up to 75% transfer credit).
- Spouse eligibility follows Liberty's stated definition; children use the 15% benefit, not the flat rate.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Stack TA / GI Bill / Yellow Ribbon** with the military rate — the biggest lever (UG can reach $0).
- **Free ACE military-training credit evaluation** — pay for fewer credits.
- **Waived application fee** for military/veterans.
- **Inclusive Access** electronic course materials at no cost for online undergrads.

### FAQ

#### Does Liberty University offer a military discount?
Yes — a reduced military tuition rate for online programs: $250/credit hour undergraduate, $310 master's, and $395 doctoral for 2026–27, plus 15% off standard tuition for military children.

#### How much is the Liberty military tuition discount?
Roughly 36% off undergraduate, ~47% off master's, and ~35% off doctoral versus standard online rates — the UG rate matches the federal TA cap.

#### Who qualifies for the military rate?
Active duty, Reserve/National Guard, veterans/retirees, their spouses, and current DoD employees. Military children get 15% off standard tuition.

#### Does the military rate stack with Tuition Assistance or the GI Bill?
Yes — the rate is designed to work with TA (UG rate matches the TA cap) and with Post-9/11 GI Bill/Yellow Ribbon benefits.

Through Liberty's Office of Military Affairs using orders, DD-214, and/or your military transcript — not a consumer verifier.

Spouses qualify for the flat military rate (per Liberty's definition); military children get 15% off standard tuition.

The military rate plus your education benefit: active-duty UG students using TA often pay near $0; veterans/dependents can use GI Bill/Yellow Ribbon. Always get the free military-training credit evaluation first.

#### Does the rate ever change?
Yes — rates are set per academic year and may change annually. The figures here are 2026–27.
