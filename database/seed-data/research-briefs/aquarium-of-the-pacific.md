# Aquarium of the Pacific — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **family-of-four single-day general admission** at the Aquarium of the Pacific (Long Beach, CA) — **2 Adults (12–61) + 2 Children (3–11)**. Official online price = 2 × $49.95 + 2 × $34.95 = **$169.80**. California does **not** charge sales tax on admissions, so every path is tax-neutral (no tax-free channel competes).

## Headline finding

The Aquarium of the Pacific has **no online first-party military discount code** — there is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute checkout on aquariumofpacific.org, and the aggregator claims of a "10%–25% military discount" or a "SheerID unique code at checkout" are **not supported by the Aquarium's own pages**. What the Aquarium actually offers military shoppers is two real, verified things: (1) **free admission for veterans and active military (with service ID) on Veterans Day** — advance online reservation required, limited capacity — and (2) an everyday route where **"discounted tickets may be purchased in advance at any MWR/ITT office on base"** (the only military line on the Aquarium's official Discounts page). Best savings path depends entirely on timing and base access: **Veterans Day = free** (unbeatable, one day a year, ID holder only); the rest of the year, **MWR/ITT advance tickets** are the standing military route, and for anyone without base access the **AAA "visit on Wednesday for up to 30% off" online offer** or an **authorized reseller/Go City bundle** usually beats every other option — because the Aquarium has no everyday online military code to use.

Archetype: primarily **B (no first-party online military route)** layered with a strong **annual free-day** program and an **MWR/ITT-driven everyday route**, plus an **H/J debunk job** — aggregators invent a flat online military % and a SheerID code the Aquarium's own site does not confirm.

**Blue Star Museums:** The Aquarium of the Pacific does **not appear on the National Endowment for the Arts' Blue Star Museums participant list** for 2024–2026, and its own site does not mention Blue Star Museums (it runs its own Veterans Day free program and MWR/ITT discount instead). Treat Blue Star Museums participation as **not applicable / unconfirmed** — verify the current list at arts.gov/BlueStarMuseums before relying on it.

1. **Nothing stacks — every route is a substitute.** MWR/ITT is a separate off-base storefront; Veterans Day free admission is its own reservation; AAA, resellers, Go City, and GovX are each independent prices. You pick one.

5. **Combo tickets (LA Zoo, Queen Mary)** are their own bundle price — no additional discount applies on top.
