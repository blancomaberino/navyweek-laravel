# Great Clips — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult haircut at Great Clips (typical **~$18–$23** depending on market; menu prices are set per franchise).

## Headline finding

Great Clips's flagship military benefit is **free, not a percentage**: the **Thank a Veteran** program gives **veterans and active service members a free haircut (or a free-haircut card for a future visit) on Veterans Day, November 11**, at any U.S. salon. There is **no company-wide everyday military discount** — Great Clips is a **franchise**, so any day-to-day military/first-responder discount is **set by the individual salon owner** and, where offered, is typically a small flat amount (community reports commonly cite **~$2–$5 off**), never a guaranteed uniform rate. So the honest page: **Veterans Day = free haircut (official, nationwide); every other day = ask your local salon, discount is discretionary.** Archetype: **H (holiday program, not a flat everyday %)** + **J (discretionary, franchise-level everyday discount)**.

### FAQ

#### Does Great Clips offer a military discount?
Yes — the headline benefit is a **free haircut on Veterans Day (Nov 11)** for veterans and active service members through the **Thank a Veteran** program. There is no guaranteed everyday company-wide military discount; that's set per franchise.

#### How much is the Great Clips military discount?
On **Veterans Day it's free**. Any everyday military discount is **decided by the individual salon** — commonly a small flat amount (shoppers report ~$2–$5) where offered, but not guaranteed anywhere.

#### Do veterans and active-duty both qualify?
Yes — both veterans and active service members qualify for the free Veterans Day haircut.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In the salon — show a military ID or proof of service. There's no online verification (no ID.me/SheerID/GovX).

#### Does Great Clips give a free haircut on Veterans Day?
Yes. On **November 11**, any U.S. Great Clips salon gives veterans and active members a free haircut or a free-haircut card for later.

#### Is there an everyday military discount at Great Clips?
Not company-wide. Because Great Clips salons are independently owned franchises, an everyday military or first-responder discount exists **only where the local owner offers it** — ask your salon.

#### Can I combine it with a coupon?
The free Veterans Day haircut is a standalone benefit. Everyday franchise discounts may not stack with public coupons — take whichever saves more.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to get a cut at Great Clips?
Time it for **Veterans Day** (free). Any other day, ask your local salon for a military discount and compare it to whatever public coupon is live at greatclips.com/promotions.

#### Does Great Clips offer a first responder, teacher, or student discount?
Any first-responder discount is franchise-discretionary like the military one. Great Clips does publish **senior** and **Back-to-School** promotions separately.

#### Does the Veterans Day offer apply in Canada?
Yes, at participating salons on **Remembrance Day (Nov 11)** — contact your local Canadian salon to confirm.
