# Priority Tire Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Priority Tire (prioritytire.com) offers a **5% discount on all orders** to active, retired, and veteran members of the US military, verified through **ID.me**. The same 5% rate extends to first responders, healthcare professionals (doctors, nurses, medical staff, hospital employees), and educational workers (teachers and school staff). Verification happens online via ID.me before checkout; once verified, the 5% is **applied to the shopping cart automatically**. The single most important caveat: the discount **cannot be combined with other promotions or discounts** — if an ID.me discount is applied, no additional coupon code can be used on that order. Priority Tire is an online-only tire retailer (no physical stores), so there is no in-store redemption path. The offer is presented as evergreen on Priority Tire's own pages and on ID.me Shop, with no published expiration date or order cap.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Priority Tire's Military Discount page (prioritytire.com/military-discount) or Special Discounts page.
2. Click **Verify with ID.me**.
3. Sign in to ID.me or create an account, and complete identity/affiliation verification for your group (military, first responder, nurse, medical, hospital employee, or teacher).
4. After verification succeeds, return to prioritytire.com; the 5% discount is applied to your shopping cart automatically.
5. Add tires to your cart and check out. Do not apply a separate coupon code — the 5% can't be combined with another discount.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Priority Tire is an online-only retailer with no physical storefronts; the discount is available only at prioritytire.com.

### How Verification Works
Priority Tire uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You verify your status online before checkout; once verification is complete, the 5% is applied to your cart automatically. Priority Tire does not publish a re-verification frequency — ID.me manages its own affiliation checks and may require you to re-confirm periodically, so treat verification as managed by ID.me rather than a one-time permanent step. For verification problems, Priority Tire directs users to the [ID.me support page](https://help.id.me/hc/en-us); for order questions, Priority Tire support is 866-440-0177 or info@prioritytire.com.

### FAQ

#### Does Priority Tire offer a military discount?
Yes. Priority Tire offers 5% off all orders to active, retired, and veteran members of the US military, verified through ID.me. (Source: prioritytire.com/military-discount.)

#### How much is the Priority Tire military discount?
5% off the price of your order. (Source: prioritytire.com/military-discount.)

#### Do veterans, retirees, and reservists qualify?
Yes. Priority Tire states the discount is for "active, retired, or veteran members of the US military," and its Special Discounts page says "active duty, reserves, and veterans get instant access." Veterans qualify "for life." Military spouses and dependents are not mentioned in the official source. (Source: prioritytire.com/military-discount; prioritytire.com/special-discounts.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
Click "Verify with ID.me" on Priority Tire's military or special-discounts page and complete ID.me verification. Once verified, the 5% is applied to your cart automatically. For verification help, use the ID.me support page. (Source: prioritytire.com/military-discount.)

#### Does Priority Tire use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Priority Tire uses **ID.me**. There is no indication it uses GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. (Source: prioritytire.com/military-discount; shop.id.me/stores/13451-priority-tire.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. Priority Tire is an online-only retailer with no physical stores; the discount applies only at prioritytire.com. (Source: prioritytire.com — no store locations listed; online checkout only.)

#### What is excluded, and can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The 5% cannot be combined with other promotions or discounts. If an ID.me discount is applied, no additional coupon code can be used on that order. Priority Tire does not list excluded products for the discount itself. (Source: prioritytire.com/military-discount; shop.id.me/stores/13451-priority-tire.)

#### Does Priority Tire offer a first responder, nurse, medical, or teacher discount?
Yes — all at 5%, the same rate as the military discount, via ID.me. Eligible groups include first responders (police, firefighters, EMS), healthcare professionals (doctors, nurses, medical staff, hospital employees), and educational workers (teachers and school staff). (Source: prioritytire.com/special-discounts; shop.id.me/stores/13451-priority-tire.)

#### Does Priority Tire offer a government employee or student discount?
Not stated as offered. Priority Tire's official discount pages and its ID.me verification scope cover military, first responders, medical/nurse/hospital staff, and teachers — government employees and students are not listed. (Source: prioritytire.com/special-discounts; ID.me verify scope on prioritytire.com.)

#### How long does the Priority Tire military discount last?
Priority Tire presents the 5% discount as an ongoing offer with no published end date. Terms can change at any time, so verify before ordering. (Source: prioritytire.com/military-discount.)
