# Hooters — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one dine-in lunch for a service member — one entrée (~$13–15, e.g. 10 wings or the Hooters Cheeseburger) plus one beverage (~$3–4), pre-tax subtotal ~$16–19.

## Headline finding

Hooters' only **company-published** military offer is its annual **Veterans Day free-meal** promotion: on November 11 every year, veterans and active-duty/retired military get **one free entrée from a limited special menu, dine-in only, with a beverage purchase and a valid military ID or proof of service** (no coupon needed). That is verified on Hooters' own `/military-and-veterans/` page and the official Hooters Inc. press release. The widely-repeated **"10% off daily / 20% off every Tuesday" everyday military discount is NOT published by Hooters corporate** — editorial sources explicitly tie it to "participating locations only," making it a **location-discretionary (franchise/manager) perk**, not a chain-wide policy. There is **no online discount, no verification portal (no ID.me / SheerID / GovX / WeSalute), and no veteran code** — everything is in-restaurant with an ID. Archetype: **restaurant "Veterans Day free-meal"** brand with a **location-discretionary everyday discount** layered on top. Best path: eat on **Veterans Day (free)**; otherwise **ask your local Hooters** whether it honors the Tuesday 20% before ordering.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Veterans Day free meal is NOT combinable** — Hooters' terms say "Not valid with any other discounts," "One offer per person," and require a **beverage purchase** (the drink is never free). No portal is involved, so there are no portal terms.
2. **Dine-in only** — the free meal cannot be redeemed on Hooters To Go / online orders / delivery; it requires being seated in-restaurant with a valid military ID or proof of service.
3. **Everyday military discount, where offered, is applied at the register by the location** — because it is not a corporate program there are no published stacking rules; assume it does not stack with the Veterans Day free meal or other promos.
4. **No verification account / code** — nothing to link; eligibility is checked visually via military ID or proof of service at the table.

### Who Qualifies
- **Veterans** — eligible for the Veterans Day free meal (ID or proof of service).
- **Active-duty military** — eligible for the Veterans Day free meal.
- **Retired military** — eligible ("Active and Retired Military," per Hooters' page).
- **Spouses / dependents / Gold Star family** — **not stated** by Hooters; do not assume eligibility.
- **First responders / teachers / nurses / students** — no Hooters program found.

### How To Redeem In Store (Veterans Day)
1. Go to a participating Hooters on **November 11** and ask to dine in (offer is not valid for takeout/online).
2. Tell your server you're redeeming the Veterans Day military offer and **show a valid military ID or proof of service**.
3. **Choose one free entrée** from that year's special menu (2025: Cheeseburger, 10 Original Wings, Buffalo Chicken Sandwich, or Chicken Caesar Salad).
4. **Purchase a beverage** (required). One offer per person; not combinable with other discounts.

### How To Redeem (everyday, if offered)
1. There is **no online or account-based redemption**. Ask your local Hooters whether it offers a military discount and on which days.
2. If it does, **present a valid military ID** at the table; the discount is applied at the register at the location's discretion.

### How Verification Works
Hooters uses **no third-party verification service and no online account**. Eligibility is confirmed **in person** by showing a **valid military ID or proof of service** to staff. Because the everyday discount is location-run, acceptance and re-verification are entirely up to each restaurant.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Veterans Day offer: **dine-in only**, **beverage purchase required**, **one offer per person**, **not valid with any other discounts**, **participating locations only**, **menu items may vary by location**; additional charges apply for modifications/add-ons.
- Everyday military % is **not a published corporate policy** — availability, amount, and eligible days vary by location and may not exist at yours.
- Spouse/dependent eligibility is **not stated**; don't assume it.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Time your visit to November 11** — the free entrée is the single biggest saving.
- **Ask your local Hooters** whether it honors a military discount and whether Tuesday is the 20% day.
- **Use public food specials** open to everyone (weekday lunch deals, Monday burger combo) when no military discount applies — no ID needed.
- **Join HootClub Rewards** (free loyalty program) for ongoing points/perks regardless of military status.

### FAQ

#### Does Hooters offer a military discount?
Yes, but the only offer Hooters publishes company-wide is the **Veterans Day free meal** on November 11. An everyday military discount exists at some locations but is **not a corporate program**.

#### How much is the Hooters military discount?
On **Veterans Day** it's a **free entrée** (you buy a beverage). Day-to-day, editorial sources report roughly **10% daily / 20% on Tuesdays at participating locations**, but Hooters corporate publishes **no everyday percentage** — treat it as location-dependent.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Veterans and active-duty/retired military** qualify for the Veterans Day meal with an ID or proof of service. Hooters does **not** state that spouses or dependents qualify.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person only — **show a valid military ID or proof of service** to staff. There's no online verification and **no coupon is required**.

#### Does Hooters use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Hooters uses none of these. Eligibility is checked in-restaurant by staff.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
**Dine-in only.** The Veterans Day free meal is **not valid on Hooters To Go, online orders, or delivery.**

#### What is excluded?
The beverage is never free, the free meal can't be combined with other discounts, it's one per person, and it's only at participating locations with menu items that vary.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the Veterans Day meal is **"not valid with any other discounts."**

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Hooters?
Dine in on **November 11** for the free entrée. On other days, **ask whether your location honors the Tuesday 20%**; if not, the **public lunch/daily specials** are usually the cheapest option.

#### Does Hooters offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No such program was found on Hooters' site.

#### Does Hooters run a Veterans Day sale?
Yes — annually on **November 11**: a free entrée from a special menu, dine-in, with a beverage and valid military ID/proof of service.
