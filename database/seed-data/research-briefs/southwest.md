# Southwest Airlines Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Southwest Airlines offers **unpublished "Military fares"** plus benefit policies for active-duty service members — but there is **no online discount and no published percentage**, and the fare discount does **not** extend to veterans or retirees. Military fares must be booked by phone (1-800-I-FLY-SWA / 1-800-435-9792) or at a Southwest ticket counter; they are not available on Southwest.com, and Rapid Rewards points cannot be used to buy them. Eligibility for Military fares is limited to **U.S. military on active duty** (including reservists, National Guard, and Coast Guard members with active orders, and the four federal service-academy cadets/midshipmen) **and their authorized dependents** — a valid Uniformed Services ID must be shown at check-in. Beyond fares, Southwest gives active-duty members two distinct, verifiable benefits: an **active-duty checked-baggage fee exemption** (no excess/oversize/overweight fees, within 100 lb / 80 in limits) and **Priority boarding just before Group 1** (for flights departing on or after January 27, 2026). The single most important caveat: any "10%," "10–25%," or similar figure circulating on coupon and affiliate sites is **not stated by Southwest** — the airline does not publish a discount amount.

### How To Redeem Online
1. There is no online redemption for Military fares. Southwest states Military fares are not available on Southwest.com.
2. To compare standard pricing, you can still search and book regular fares at southwest.com, but a Military fare must be booked by phone or at a ticket counter (see below).

### How To Redeem In Store (Phone / Ticket Counter)
1. Call 1-800-I-FLY-SWA (1-800-435-9792) or go to a Southwest ticket counter at the airport.
2. Tell the agent you want to book a Military fare and provide your travel details.
3. Confirm you (or the traveler) are active-duty / an authorized dependent as defined by Southwest.
4. Present a valid Uniformed Services ID at check-in. Dependents 12+ must present a card showing active-duty-dependent status; dependents 2–11 must travel with an eligible military or military-dependent customer 12+.
5. For the active-duty baggage exemption, present the valid Uniformed Services ID at check-in (no fee for additional/excess/oversize/overweight bags within 100 lb / 80 in limits, up to 20 pieces).
6. For priority boarding (flights departing on/after Jan 27, 2026), show your valid Uniformed Services ID at the gate to board just before Group 1.

### How Verification Works
Southwest does not use a third-party verification service (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute gating). Verification is manual: eligibility is established when you book the Military fare by phone or at a ticket counter, and a valid United States Uniformed Services ID must be presented at the time of check-in. Military-dependent customers aged 12+ must carry and present a card showing their status as an active-duty military dependent. Because there is no online account flow, there is no "verify once" convenience — each trip requires the ID at check-in. For booking help, call 1-800-435-9792.

### FAQ

#### Does Southwest offer a military discount?
Yes — Southwest offers unpublished "Military fares" for active-duty U.S. service members and authorized dependents, plus an active-duty baggage-fee exemption and priority boarding. Military fares must be booked by phone (1-800-435-9792) or at a ticket counter; they are not available on Southwest.com.

#### How much is the Southwest military discount?
Southwest does not publish a percentage or dollar amount for Military fares. Figures such as "10%" or "10–25%" appear only on third-party coupon and affiliate sites and are not confirmed by Southwest. Call 1-800-435-9792 for a quote and compare it to the best standard fare.

#### Do veterans qualify for the Southwest military fare?
Southwest's official Military-fare eligibility list names active-duty members and their authorized dependents; veterans and retirees are not named. Veterans can book Southwest's standard fares like any traveler. (This reflects what the official page states — confirm directly when booking.)

#### Do spouses and dependents qualify?
Authorized dependents of active-duty members qualify for Military fares. Dependents aged 2–11 must travel with a military or military-dependent customer aged 12+; dependents 12+ must present a card showing active-duty-dependent status. Dependents traveling alone pay normal baggage fees.

#### How do I verify my military status with Southwest?
There is no online verification service. You book the Military fare by phone or at a ticket counter and present a valid United States Uniformed Services ID at check-in (and, for dependents 12+, a card showing dependent status).

#### Does Southwest use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Southwest's official Military Travel page does not use any third-party verification provider; eligibility is verified manually with a Uniformed Services ID at booking and check-in.

#### Can I book the military fare online?
No. Southwest states Military (and Government) fares are not available on Southwest.com. Book by calling 1-800-I-FLY-SWA (1-800-435-9792) or at a Southwest ticket counter.

#### What military baggage benefit does Southwest offer?
Active-duty customers are exempt from excess, oversize, and overweight checked-bag fees, provided no piece exceeds 100 lb or 80 in (L+W+H), up to 20 pieces per flight, with a valid Uniformed Services ID shown at check-in. Dependents traveling without the active-duty member pay normal fees.

#### Can I use Rapid Rewards points for a military fare?
No. Southwest states Rapid Rewards points cannot be redeemed to purchase Government or Military fares.

#### Does Southwest offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not stated in official source. Southwest's Military Travel page covers military and government travelers only; no first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount is published there.
