# CLEAR (CLEAR Plus) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one annual CLEAR Plus membership (standard $219/year).

## Headline finding

Yes — CLEAR offers a real, standing military/government rate on **CLEAR Plus** (its paid airport-lane identity membership): **$125/year vs. the $219 standard**, verified on CLEAR's own CLEAR Plus page. But for most service members it is **not the best deal**. If you hold an American Express Platinum, Amex Green, Hilton Honors Aspire, or Centurion card, the card **reimburses the full CLEAR Plus fee (up to $219/year)**, making the membership effectively **free** — which beats the $125 military rate. CLEAR is a *subscription/membership brand whose real economics run through credit-card statement credits and airline-loyalty tiers*, not a coupon code. Separately — and most important for active-duty readers — **CLEAR Plus is not TSA PreCheck**: active-duty military and DoD civilians already get **free TSA PreCheck** using their DoD ID as a Known Traveler Number, so don't pay for PreCheck at all. The widely repeated aggregator claim that the CLEAR military rate is redeemed online via **ID.me** is **not confirmed by CLEAR** — the official instruction is to enroll in person at a CLEAR airport location.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military rate and airline-partner rates are mutually exclusive** — you enroll through one channel or the other, not both. Pick the lower number for your situation.
2. **Credit-card CLEAR statement credits reimburse whatever you pay**, so a card credit *does* effectively "stack" on top of any enrollment rate: pay $125 (military) or $219 (standard) and the card refunds it up to its cap ($219 on Platinum/Green/Aspire). Because the cap already covers the full standard fee, the military rate saves you nothing extra once a qualifying card is in play — it only matters if you have no such card.
3. **CLEAR Plus ≠ TSA PreCheck.** The free active-duty PreCheck benefit and the $25 spouse / free Gold Star PreCheck rates apply to **TSA PreCheck enrollment**, not to the CLEAR Plus lane membership. They don't discount CLEAR Plus.
4. **No public coupon codes reduce CLEAR Plus below these program rates.** Aggregator "promo codes" for CLEAR are typically free-trial or referral links, not stackable dollar-off codes on the military rate.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military & government employees** — eligible for the $125 CLEAR Plus rate (CLEAR's stated groups).
- **Veterans** — commonly reported as eligible, but CLEAR's page names only "military service members and government employees." Bring a Veteran ID / DD-214 and confirm at the airport; do not assume.
- **Active-duty & DoD civilians (TSA PreCheck)** — free TSA PreCheck via DoD ID; separate from CLEAR Plus.
- **Military spouses (TSA PreCheck)** — $25 enrollment discount on TSA PreCheck through CLEAR.
- **Gold Star families (TSA PreCheck)** — free TSA PreCheck through CLEAR.
- **Seniors** — no discounted rate (CLEAR states this explicitly).

### How To Redeem (In Person)
1. Head to a CLEAR enrollment center at a participating airport (no appointment needed — you can enroll the next time you fly).
2. Tell the CLEAR Ambassador you want the military/government rate.
3. Present a valid U.S. Military ID (or government ID); bring a Veteran ID / DD-214 if enrolling as a veteran.
4. Complete identity verification and pay $125 for the annual membership.

*(If you hold an Amex Platinum/Green, Hilton Aspire, or Centurion card, enroll or pay with that card so the CLEAR statement credit reimburses the fee.)*

### How Verification Works
CLEAR verifies your identity biometrically at enrollment (matching your ID to a live selfie). For the military/government rate, verification of status happens **in person with your military or government ID** at the enrollment center — CLEAR does not publish a self-serve online military verification (e.g. ID.me) for this rate as of July 14, 2026. Membership is annual and renews yearly. Support is via CLEAR's "Chat with us" / Halo assistant.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The $125 rate applies to **CLEAR Plus**, not to TSA PreCheck pricing.
- CLEAR does not offer senior-citizen discounts.
- Family members added to your account are $125 each (up to 3 adults); children under 18 are free.
- Veteran eligibility is not explicitly stated by CLEAR — confirm at enrollment.
- Card-credit reimbursement depends on your card's current terms and enrolling/paying with that card.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Use a card that reimburses CLEAR** (Amex Platinum, Amex Green, Hilton Honors Aspire — up to $219/yr; Centurion up to $438/yr): net $0, beats the military rate.
- **Airline status/loyalty:** Delta 360°/United Global Services = free CLEAR; Delta Diamond/United Premier 1K = $129; airline members = $199–$209.
- **Don't pay for TSA PreCheck if active-duty/DoD** — it's free via your DoD ID.
- **Family:** kids under 18 ride free on your membership; add adults at $125 each.

### FAQ

#### Does CLEAR offer a military discount?
Yes. CLEAR offers active military and government employees CLEAR Plus at **$125/year** instead of the $219 standard rate.

#### How much is the CLEAR military discount?
$125/year for CLEAR Plus — a $94 saving off the $219 standard price.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
CLEAR's page names "military service members and government employees" for the $125 CLEAR Plus rate; veterans are commonly reported as eligible but not explicitly confirmed by CLEAR — bring a Veteran ID/DD-214 and confirm at enrollment. Separately, **military spouses get $25 off TSA PreCheck** and **Gold Star families get free TSA PreCheck** through CLEAR (these are PreCheck benefits, not CLEAR Plus).

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person at a CLEAR airport enrollment center with your U.S. Military/government ID (or Veteran ID/DD-214). CLEAR does not publish an online military verification for this rate.

#### Does CLEAR use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not for the CLEAR Plus military rate, based on CLEAR's own pages — verification is in person with ID. Aggregators claiming an ID.me "Get Offer" flow are unverified.

#### Can I use the discount online or only in person?
CLEAR instructs military members to enroll **in person** at an airport location for the $125 rate.

#### What's excluded?
The $125 rate is for CLEAR Plus only, not TSA PreCheck; no senior discount; veteran eligibility unconfirmed by CLEAR.

#### Can I combine it with a promo code or card credit?
No stackable coupon codes lower it further, and it can't combine with airline-partner rates. But a qualifying **Amex/Hilton card statement credit reimburses whatever you pay** — pay the $125 (or standard $219) on that card and the credit can make it free.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy CLEAR?
If you hold an Amex Platinum/Green, Hilton Aspire, or Centurion card, it's **free** via the card's CLEAR credit — better than $125. Otherwise, the **$125 military rate** is the cheapest out-of-pocket. And if you only need to skip security screening, your **TSA PreCheck is already free** as active-duty/DoD.

#### Do active-duty members get free TSA PreCheck?
Yes — service members of all branches and service-academy students get TSA PreCheck automatically using their 10-digit DoD ID as their Known Traveler Number. This is separate from CLEAR Plus.

#### Does CLEAR offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
CLEAR publishes no dedicated first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student CLEAR Plus rate; the standing discount groups are military and government employees. Students at armed-forces service academies get the automatic free TSA PreCheck benefit.
