Budget Truck Rental offers **20% off** for **U.S. military personnel traveling with orders** with promo code **USMIO**. You **book online at budgettruck.com**, enter **USMIO**, and the discount comes off the **time and mileage of "The Best Available Rate"** (Budget Truck's lowest publicly available online rate).
