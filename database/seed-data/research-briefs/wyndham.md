# Wyndham Hotels & Resorts — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 2-night stay at a $120/night Standard Rate = **$240** (pre-tax) at a participating brand (e.g., La Quinta, Baymont, Days Inn).

## Headline finding

Wyndham runs a **real, official military discount** — actually two overlapping paths. (1) The **Military Appreciation Rate**: **at least 15% off a property's Standard Rate**, booked with **rate code SML** on Wyndham's direct channels, verified simply by **showing a valid military ID at check-in** (no Rewards membership required). (2) A **Wyndham Rewards military benefits** path (via the "Confirm Military Status" flow, powered by ID.me) that unlocks an **automatic GOLD tier upgrade**, **3,000 bonus points** after a qualifying stay, and an **ID.me hosted rate of up to 20% off the Best Available Rate**. For most travelers the **SML rate is the reliable everyday win**; verify with ID.me if you want the tier/points and the shot at up to 20%.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The Military Appreciation Rate and the ID.me rate **cannot be combined with other discounts or promotions** — pick the lower final rate, don't stack them.
2. Both require **Direct Booking Channels** (Wyndham sites/app/brand sites/toll-free/onsite/traditional travel agents) — **not** third-party OTAs, and the ID.me rate isn't valid when calling hotels directly.
3. **New bookings only; not group bookings.** You can still **earn Wyndham Rewards points** on a qualifying military rate stay (member), which effectively stacks loyalty value on top of the discounted rate.
4. Auto-GOLD and the 3,000-point bonus require completing the **Confirm Military Status (ID.me)** step; the bonus has a booking/stay-by window (booked direct by Jun 1, completed by Sep 30, 2026).

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty, reserve, National Guard, retired, and veterans — eligible.
- Spouses — eligible.
- Children 18+ of military members — eligible for the bonus-points offer.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Book the SML rate:** 1) Go to a Wyndham/brand site or app (or call toll-free). 2) Select **rate code SML** (Military Appreciation Rate). 3) Complete a direct booking. 4) Present a **valid military ID at check-in**.
**Unlock perks (ID.me):** 1) Use the **Confirm Military Status** flow and verify via ID.me. 2) Get **auto-GOLD** + eligibility for **3,000 bonus points** after a qualifying stay. 3) Book via the ID.me link/toll-free for the **up to 20% off BAR** rate where offered.

### How Verification Works
The **SML rate** needs only a **military ID at check-in**. The **Rewards perks and ID.me rate** require completing Wyndham's **"Confirm Military Status"** step (ID.me). A dedicated military member-services line (855-464-5739) supports the process.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **New bookings only; not group bookings.** Direct channels only — not OTAs.
- Not combinable with other discounts/promotions; **blackout dates, room-type exclusions, and property variation** apply.
- The 20% figure is a **ceiling** ("may be less than 20% off" at some properties); the guaranteed floor is **15% off Standard** via SML.
- Bonus-point offer has a booking/stay-by window (booked direct by Jun 1, completed by Sep 30, 2026).

### FAQ
#### Does Wyndham offer a military discount?
Yes — the Military Appreciation Rate (≥15% off Standard, code SML) plus an ID.me path up to 20% off Best Available Rate.
#### How much is the Wyndham military discount?
At least 15% off the Standard Rate via SML; up to 20% off the Best Available Rate via ID.me (property-dependent).
#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — active, reserve, veteran, retired, and spouses; 18+ children qualify for the bonus-points offer.
#### How do I verify my status?
Show a military ID at check-in for the SML rate; complete Wyndham's "Confirm Military Status" (ID.me) for tier/points and the ID.me rate.
