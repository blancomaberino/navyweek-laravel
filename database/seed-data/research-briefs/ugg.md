# UGG Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

UGG offers verified military members **10% off full-priced styles year-round** through its **Community Heroes** program, verified by **SheerID**. The same 10% extends to educators, medical professionals, and first responders; college students get the same 10% via UNiDAYS. There is also a separate, **limited-time Veterans Day benefit**: on Veteran's Day, military personnel get **10% off all orders, including sale items**. Verification is online-only at UGG.com — you confirm your status with SheerID (uploading documents may be required; confirmation usually within 24 hours), then redeem on the Promotions & Discounts page. The single most important caveat: the everyday 10% applies to **full-priced styles only** — sale/clearance items are excluded except during the Veteran's Day window. UGG does not publish whether spouses/dependents qualify or whether the discount is in-store, so those are flagged as not stated.

> Accuracy note: An older UGG help-center article (article 207407915 / 212508725) reportedly answers "No, we are not offering any kind of military or professional discount at this time." That page is **stale/contradictory** — UGG's current "Does UGG Have Sales?" story page (ugg.com) and SheerID's live UGG Community Heroes offer both confirm the active 10% military/community-heroes discount as of June 23, 2026. Treat the live story page + SheerID as authoritative; treat the old help article as outdated.

### FAQ

#### Does UGG offer a military discount?
Yes. Verified military members get 10% off full-priced styles year-round through UGG's Community Heroes program, verified by SheerID. On Veteran's Day, military get 10% off all orders, including sale items.

#### How much is the UGG military discount?
10% off full-priced styles year-round. On Veteran's Day specifically, it's 10% off all orders including sale items.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify through the SheerID military category. UGG does not state on its official pages whether military spouses or dependents are eligible, so check directly through SheerID.

#### How do I verify my status?
Choose your group on UGG's Promotions & Discounts page and verify through SheerID. You may need to upload documentation; UGG says verification is usually confirmed within 24 hours.

#### Does UGG use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
UGG uses **SheerID** for the Community Heroes (military/first responder/medical/educator) discount, and **UNiDAYS** for the student discount. It does not use ID.me for this offer.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Not stated. UGG's published process is online verification at UGG.com. For in-person savings, UGG directs shoppers to its sale page and UGG Outlet Stores instead.

#### What is excluded?
The everyday 10% applies to full-priced styles only — sale and clearance are excluded (except on Veteran's Day for military). UGG doesn't publish a full exclusion list, so confirm at checkout.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
UGG does not state that the discount stacks with promo codes, Rewards coupons, or sale items. Assume it can't be combined, and note the everyday offer excludes sale items entirely.

#### Does UGG offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes. First responders, educators, and medical professionals get the same 10% through SheerID (Community Heroes). College students get 10% off full-priced styles verified through UNiDAYS. Government employees are not listed.
