# Ancestry — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Ancestry **All Access** subscription (month-to-month $54.99/mo; six-month upfront ~$39.83/mo effective).

## Headline finding

Ancestry runs an **official military discount of 20% off the All Access plan** for verified service members and their families, verified through **SheerID**. All Access bundles Ancestry, Newspapers.com, and Fold3 (military records). The discount is a recurring subscription price cut — it renews every six months at the discounted rate until cancelled — and it **cannot be combined** with other promotions. Separately, Ancestry runs **free-access windows to U.S. military records** (Fold3 / the "Honor" hub) around Memorial Day and Veterans Day that cost nothing at all. For most service members the winning move is: verify once for 20% off All Access, and use the free-access windows for one-off record lookups.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty service members — eligible.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Ancestry's military offer page (`ancestry.com/c/offers/military`).

3. Select the All Access plan; the 20% discount applies to your subscription.

### How Verification Works
Ancestry uses **SheerID**, which checks your details against DoD records — usually instant. Existing members typically must cancel and rejoin under the military offer at the end of their current term.

- Cannot be combined with other promotions or offers.
- Not applied retroactively; online purchases through Ancestry's website only.
- Auto-renews every six months at the discounted price until cancelled.

### Best Ways To Save More

### FAQ

#### Does Ancestry offer a military discount?
Yes — 20% off the All Access plan for verified military members and their families.

#### How much is the Ancestry military discount?
20% off All Access. On the six-month upfront rate (~$39.83/mo) that's roughly $31.86/mo; month-to-month ($54.99) becomes about $43.99/mo.

#### Do veterans, Guard, reservists, and families qualify?
Yes — active duty, veterans, National Guard, reservists, and families are all listed as eligible.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through SheerID on Ancestry's military offer page; verification is usually instant against DoD records.

#### Does Ancestry use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
SheerID.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Online only, directly through ancestry.com.

#### What is excluded?
It can't be combined with other promotions, isn't retroactive, and auto-renews at the discounted rate every six months.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the military discount cannot be combined with other offers. Compare it against a public intro sale and take the better one for your first term.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Ancestry?
For record lookups, use the free military-records windows. For ongoing access, verify for 20% off All Access (or take a deeper new-member intro sale for term one).

#### Does Ancestry run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day offer?
Historically yes — free access to U.S. military records around both, via the Fold3 / "Honor" hub. Historical pattern, not a guarantee.
