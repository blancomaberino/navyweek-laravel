**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one federal + one state tax return for a service member with a moderately complex situation (PCS move, W-2 military pay, some investment income) — the kind of return that costs money at most DIY tiers.

## Headline finding

H&R Block publishes **no percentage "military discount" on its consumer online/DIY tax software** — ID.me's own H&R Block page states plainly: *"We're not aware that H&R Block offers Military discounts."* The real military value is two specific, verified programs, and the best one costs **$0**: (1) **MilTax**, the free tax software **powered by H&R Block** and distributed through the DoD's **Military OneSource**, free for active-duty, Guard/Reserve, eligible family, survivors, and recent veterans (≤365 days from separation) — federal plus multiple state returns, plus free military-trained tax consultants; and (2) **H&R Block at the Exchange**, which gives all authorized Exchange shoppers (including honorably discharged veterans and DoD/Coast Guard civilians) **10% off in-office tax prep** at select Exchange locations. Archetype: **service/franchise brand with no national retail military discount, but a strong government-partnership free channel.** Aggregator claims of a "10%–25% online military discount" are false — they conflate general public promo codes with a military benefit. Bottom line: if you qualify for MilTax, use it — nothing H&R Block sells beats free.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + program terms)
1. **MilTax is not a discount you stack — it's a fully free, separate product.** It is not combinable with H&R Block retail promo codes because you never pay in the first place; access is only through the Military OneSource portal, not hrblock.com.
2. **No online military discount exists to stack.** Public promo codes on hrblock.com are not military offers; you cannot layer a "military discount" on top because there isn't one.
3. **The Exchange 10% applies to in-office tax-prep fees**, redeemed at H&R Block offices located in/through the Exchange — a location-based benefit for authorized shoppers, not an online-software code; don't assume it combines with online promo codes.
4. **Eligibility does not overreach:** MilTax's recent-veteran window is strictly ≤365 days from separation/retirement; veterans past that lose MilTax but keep Exchange access (as honorably discharged veterans with Exchange privileges).

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty service members** — MilTax: yes (free). Exchange 10%: yes.
- **National Guard & Reserve** — MilTax: yes, regardless of activation status. Exchange: yes (authorized shoppers).
- **Military spouses / eligible family / dependents** — MilTax: yes (eligible family members). Exchange: yes with privileges.
- **Survivors** — MilTax: yes.
- **Recently separated veterans (≤365 days)** — MilTax: yes.
- **Veterans >365 days from separation** — MilTax: no; but honorably discharged veterans keep **Exchange** privileges → 10% in-office and can use public H&R Block Free Online / codes.
- **DoD & Coast Guard civilians** — Exchange 10%: yes. MilTax: only if otherwise eligible.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**MilTax (free software):**
1. Go to Military OneSource (militaryonesource.mil) — not hrblock.com.
2. Log in / verify eligibility (DEERS-based).
3. Access the MilTax filing software from the portal.
4. File federal + state returns for free; call a MilTax consultant for military-specific questions if needed.

**H&R Block at the Exchange (10% in-office, filing season):**
1. Find a participating office via ShopMyExchange.com/hrblock or the Exchange Community Hub.
2. Bring your Exchange-authorized ID (military ID, veteran/DoD/Coast Guard-civilian credential).
3. The 10% is applied to the in-office tax-prep fee; ask about the free second-look review of prior returns.

### How Verification Works
- **MilTax:** eligibility is tied to your military status via DEERS and your Military OneSource account — there's no ID.me/SheerID retail-style code. If your status lapses (e.g., >365 days post-separation), MilTax access ends.
- **Exchange:** verification is your existing Exchange shopping authorization; no separate provider enrollment.
- No permanent "membership" — both are re-checked against your current status each season.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- MilTax free filing does **not** extend to veterans more than 365 days past separation/retirement.
- The MilTax state-return count is limited (Military OneSource has cited "up to three" historically; a 2026 page listed "up to five") — **confirm the current-season cap**.
- The Exchange 10% applies to **in-office** tax-prep service fees at participating Exchange locations only; it is not an online-software discount and the offer window is seasonal (TY2025 ran through March 19, 2026).
- H&R Block's public DIY promo codes are **not** military offers and are not asserted here as such.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **File free with MilTax if you qualify** — the single biggest saver; skip paid software entirely.
- **If not MilTax-eligible but your return is simple**, use H&R Block **Free Online** or **IRS Free File / Direct File** — all $0.
- **If you want in-person help**, use **H&R Block at the Exchange** for 10% off plus the free second-look review (during filing season).
- **If you must buy paid DIY**, apply whatever current public promo code is live — there is no military-specific code, so a general one is your best lever.

### FAQ

#### Does H&R Block offer a military discount?
Not as a percentage on its online tax software — ID.me's H&R Block page states it isn't aware of any military discount there. Instead, eligible service members file **free** through MilTax (powered by H&R Block, via Military OneSource), and Exchange shoppers get **10% off in-office** tax prep during filing season.

#### How much is the H&R Block military discount?
The best "discount" is **100% off** — MilTax is completely free for eligible military filers. Separately, **H&R Block at the Exchange** offers **10% off** in-office tax preparation to authorized Exchange shoppers.

#### Is H&R Block free for military?
Yes, through **MilTax** — for active-duty, Guard/Reserve, eligible family, survivors, and veterans within 365 days of separation. You access it through the Military OneSource portal, not the hrblock.com free-file link.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Spouses/eligible family and survivors qualify for **MilTax**. Veterans qualify for MilTax only if separated within the last 365 days; beyond that, honorably discharged veterans still get the **10% Exchange** in-office discount.

#### How do I verify my status?
MilTax eligibility is confirmed through **DEERS / your Military OneSource login**. The Exchange discount uses your existing **Exchange shopping authorization**. There is no ID.me or SheerID code for H&R Block.

#### Does H&R Block use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No verified military discount runs through any of these for H&R Block. ID.me explicitly lists **no** H&R Block military discount. The military benefits come through **Military OneSource (MilTax)** and the **Exchange**, not a retail verification provider.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
MilTax is **online** (via Military OneSource). The **10% discount is in-office only** at H&R Block at the Exchange locations. There is no online military percentage discount on hrblock.com.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
There's nothing to combine — MilTax is already free, and no online military code exists. Public DIY promo codes are separate, non-military, and apply only to paid hrblock.com products.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from H&R Block?
If you're MilTax-eligible: **use MilTax and pay $0**. If not, use H&R Block Free Online (simple returns) or IRS Free File; for in-person help with Exchange access, take the 10% Exchange discount in filing season.

#### Does H&R Block offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No verified standalone military-style discount for these groups on the DIY software; ID.me markets "up to 40% off for Military, Veterans, Nurses, Teachers, and more" via its browser extension, but the underlying H&R Block military page shows no offer — treat non-military claims as unverified.
