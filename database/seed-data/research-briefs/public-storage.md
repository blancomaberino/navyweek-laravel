# Public Storage — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **10×10 self-storage unit at ~$150/mo standard rent** (typical climate-controlled mid-size unit), plus the one-time **~$29 admin fee** and a required lock (~$20). Promo math is measured on the **first month + admin fee**, since self-storage is month-to-month.

## Headline finding

**Public Storage publishes no first-party military or veteran discount.** Its own "Military Storage" page is a *service* description (storage for deployments/PCS), not an offer, and its "Celebrates Our Veterans" blog is employee stories plus the same **$1-first-month / up-to-50%-off-first-month** promo that is open to **every** new customer. The "Public Storage military discount" that aggregators and **WeSalute** advertise ("up to 50% off monthly rent") is a **third-party WeSalute+ / BenefitHub member-marketplace listing** gated behind a **paid WeSalute+ membership (~$9.99/mo)** — and it largely mirrors the public promo, so it is **not exclusive savings**. Archetype: **service brand with no first-party discount → "military discount" is a verification-portal/marketplace repackaging of a public promo, plus statutory rights.** The genuinely military-specific value here is **not a discount at all — it is the law**: under the **Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA)**, a self-storage operator **may not auction or foreclose a servicemember's stored property during military service and for 90 days after without a court order** (50 U.S.C. § 3958), and PCS/deployment orders can terminate a term lease with no penalty (50 U.S.C. § 3955). Best path for a member: **book the public $1-first-month / online web price directly at publicstorage.com** (don't pay for WeSalute+ just for this), and **know your SCRA lien and lease-termination rights** if you deploy or PCS.

### Who Qualifies
- **Public $1-first-month / 50%-off promo** — any new customer, military or not; no verification.
- **WeSalute listing** — active duty, retirees, veterans, current/former guard & reserve, and immediate family (requires paid WeSalute+ membership).

- **SCRA § 3955 lease termination** — servicemembers who receive PCS orders or deployment orders of **90+ days** after signing a term lease (can't use orders already in hand when you signed).

### How To Redeem The Deal (Online)
1. Go to **publicstorage.com** and search your ZIP and unit size.
2. Choose a unit showing **$1 first month** or **up to 50% off first month** (availability varies by location/size).
3. **Book online** to lock the web price (up to ~50% cheaper than walk-in).
4. Budget for the **one-time ~$29 admin fee** and a lock — these are **not** waived.
5. No membership or military verification is needed for the public promo.

### How To Use Your SCRA Rights (Deployment / PCS)
1. **Notify the facility in writing** as soon as you have orders; attach a copy of your PCS or deployment orders.
2. For a **term lease**, invoke **50 U.S.C. § 3955**: the lease terminates 30 days after the next rent due date; the facility **cannot charge an early-termination penalty**.
3. If you can't pay and worry about a **lien sale**, cite **50 U.S.C. § 3958**: the facility **cannot auction or foreclose your unit without a court order** during your service and for **90 days after**.
4. If the facility resists, contact your installation's **Legal Assistance Office / JAG** — these are free services and SCRA violations carry criminal penalties.

### FAQ

#### Does Public Storage offer a military discount?
Not a first-party one. Public Storage publishes **no military or veteran discount**. The "up to 50% off" you'll see advertised is its **public** first-month promo (open to everyone), and the WeSalute "military" version sits behind a **paid membership**.

#### How much is the Public Storage military discount?
There is no dedicated military rate. The best deal — for anyone — is the public **$1 first month** or **up to 50% off first month**, booked online. The **~$29 admin fee is not waived** for military customers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify for anything special?
Not for a Public Storage discount specifically. For the WeSalute listing, active duty, retirees, veterans, guard/reserve, and immediate family qualify (paid membership required). For **SCRA protections**, active-duty servicemembers — and their property/leases — are covered, with certain dependent protections in related provisions.

#### How do I verify my status?
You don't need to for the public promo. For WeSalute, verify through a paid **WeSalute+** account. For **SCRA rights**, you provide a **copy of your military orders** in writing to the facility.

#### Does Public Storage use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
Only a **WeSalute** marketplace listing exists, and it's membership-gated. We found **no** ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or VerifyPass military program at Public Storage.

#### Can I use it online or in person?
Book the public promo **online** at publicstorage.com for the web price (cheaper than walk-in). SCRA notices should be delivered **in writing** to the facility with your orders attached.

#### What is excluded?
Promos cover **rent only** — not the admin fee or lock. They're one-per-household, vary by location/size, and aren't stackable with each other or the WeSalute listing.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other offers?
No. The first-month promos are **not stackable**, and the WeSalute listing is the same promo behind a paywall — you don't get "50% off *plus* $1 first month."

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Public Storage?
**Book the public $1-first-month deal online** — it beats the membership-gated "military" offer and needs no verification. Then downsize the unit if you can.

#### Does Public Storage offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No published first-party discount for any of these groups was found — the same public online promos are the best available deal.

#### What are my SCRA rights if I deploy or PCS with a storage unit?
Two big ones: (1) **50 U.S.C. § 3958** — the facility **cannot auction or foreclose your stored belongings without a court order** during your service and for **90 days after**; and (2) **50 U.S.C. § 3955** — you can **terminate a term lease with no penalty** using PCS or 90+ day deployment orders (written notice + a copy of your orders). Contact your **JAG/Legal Assistance Office** if the facility resists.
