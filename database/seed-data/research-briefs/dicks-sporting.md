# DICK'S Sporting Goods — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic $150 order (e.g., a jacket or a pair of running shoes) at DICK'S Sporting Goods.

## Headline finding

**DICK'S Sporting Goods has no official, company-wide military or veteran discount** — not online and not as a published in-store policy. DICK'S own ID.me verification partner states plainly: *"We are not aware of DICK'S Sporting Goods offering Military community discounts."* The only ID.me offer DICK'S runs is its **"Community MVPs"** program — **10% off one eligible product** for **nurses, medical providers, and hospital employees only** (a healthcare benefit, not a military one). Service members occasionally report a **discretionary ~10% at a store manager's discretion**, but that is unofficial, unpublished, in-store only, and never guaranteed. This is a classic **"no official discount, save through the mechanics instead"** brand: the genuine best path for a service member is **ScoreCard rewards + text/email sign-up offers + price match + timing a public sale**, which routinely beats a hypothetical 10%. Ignore aggregator claims that DICK'S gives a "guaranteed 25% military discount" — those confuse DICK'S with **DSG Outerwear** (dsgouterwear.com), a completely separate women's hunting-apparel company.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **There is no military discount to stack** at DICK'S — the question is moot for service members without a second qualifying status.

### How To Redeem (Community MVPs — healthcare only, not military)
1. Go to DICK'S Community MVPs page and verify your healthcare status with **ID.me**.

### How To Save In Store (military shoppers)
1. Bring a valid military ID.
2. Politely **ask a manager** (not floor staff) whether they can extend a military courtesy discount — some locations grant ~10%, many don't.
3. Regardless of the answer, **scan your ScoreCard** to earn rewards and ask about any current in-store or text-signup offer.

### FAQ

#### Does DICK'S Sporting Goods offer a military discount?
No. DICK'S has no official company-wide military or veteran discount, online or in store. Its ID.me partner confirms it is "not aware of DICK'S Sporting Goods offering Military community discounts."

#### How much is the DICK'S military discount?
There isn't one. The only ID.me offer DICK'S runs is 10% off one item for nurses, medical providers, and hospital employees — not the military. Some stores may give ~10% at a manager's discretion, but that's unofficial and not guaranteed.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
No — there's no verified DICK'S discount for veterans, spouses, or dependents. Your best savings come from ScoreCard, sign-up offers, sales, and price match.

#### How do I verify my military status at DICK'S?
You can't verify for a military discount at DICK'S because none exists. ID.me at DICK'S only verifies healthcare workers for the Community MVPs offer.

#### Does DICK'S use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
DICK'S uses ID.me — but only for its healthcare-focused Community MVPs program, not for military. GovX/VerifyPass "DSG" military offers belong to the separate DSG Outerwear company, not DICK'S.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The Community MVPs healthcare offer is online/app only. For military shoppers, there's nothing to apply online; in store you can only ask a manager for a discretionary courtesy.

#### What is excluded?
Everything, in the sense that no military discount applies. The healthcare Community MVPs offer is limited to one eligible product with a non-transferable code.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
There's no military discount to combine. ScoreCard points, however, earn on any purchase including sale items, so you always benefit from being a member.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from DICK'S?
Join ScoreCard, grab a text/email sign-up offer (often 5–30% off), time a public sale, and use price match. If you're eligible, the Exchange (AAFES/NEX/MCX) is often cheapest of all because it's tax-free.

#### Does DICK'S offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Only nurses, medical providers, and hospital employees qualify (Community MVPs, 10% off one item via ID.me). First responders, teachers, and students are not listed.

#### Is the "25% DICK'S military discount" real?
No. That 25% belongs to **DSG Outerwear (dsgouterwear.com)**, a separate women's hunting-apparel company verified through VerifyPass. It is not redeemable at DICK'S Sporting Goods.
