# Angel Studios — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Angel Guild Premium** annual membership — **$179/year** regular list price on angel.com/guild/pricing (the membership military shoppers actually search a discount for; note that most of Angel's library streams **free with ads** on a $0 account, so "watching" has a $0 floor).

## Headline finding

**Angel Studios publishes no military or veteran discount.** Its own support page states plainly that "Angel does not offer specific senior or military discounts." There is **no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass** verification on Angel's site, and no military rate at checkout. The widely repeated aggregator claim of "**15% off via ID.me for military**" is **fabricated** — it contradicts Angel's own documentation.

What actually saves a service member here is not a discount but Angel's **model**: (1) most of the streaming library is **free to watch with ads** on a free account; (2) **Pay It Forward** lets *anyone* — not just military — claim a **free theatrical ticket** to Angel films currently in theaters (subject to a demand waitlist); (3) the **Angel Guild Premium** membership itself bundles **2 free theater tickets to every Angel release + 20% off merch**, which is the real value if you're a regular viewer; and (4) Angel runs **frequent public promos** (e.g. a historical Veterans Day "**4 months free** on annual Premium," and promotional annual pricing seen around **$134**) that are open to everyone and are the cheapest way onto a paid membership. Archetype: **B — no military route at all**, with a heavy **H — "myth" brand** flavor (debunking the invented ID.me 15%) and a subscription (**F**) context. Angel has historically run **film-specific** free-ticket campaigns aimed at troops/first responders (Sound of Freedom, 2023) — those were one-off promotions, not a standing program.
