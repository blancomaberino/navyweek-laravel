# Maurices — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $100 women's apparel cart (before tax).

## Headline finding

Maurices has **no centrally published, corporate-wide military discount program**. ID.me's own Maurices page states plainly: *"We're not aware that Maurices offers Military discounts."* However, a **10% everyday in-store military discount is widely attested at the store level** — individual Maurices locations advertise it through mall store directories and store social accounts, redeemed by showing a valid military ID at checkout. Treat it as a **discretionary, participating-store, in-store-only** benefit (YMMV), not a guaranteed nationwide offer, and **not** available online through any verified portal. The single most reliable savings path for most shoppers is stacking a Maurices **maurices VIP/mymaurices loyalty** offer with a public promo/clearance rather than relying on the military discount online.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The in-store military discount is **store-discretionary** — there are no published corporate terms confirming whether it stacks with coupons or sale prices; assume it does **not** stack and applies to regular-price items only unless a store says otherwise.
2. There is **no online military mechanic** to stack — no ID.me/SheerID/WeSalute code exists for Maurices.
3. Public promo codes generally apply to regular + some sale items and **cannot** be combined with each other; clearance is usually the deepest single lever.

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty — typically honored in store with ID (per store listings; not corporate-confirmed).
- Veterans / retirees — commonly honored in store with ID/DD214 (YMMV, not corporate-confirmed).
- Spouses/dependents — not documented; ask the store.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**In store:** 1) Shop your local Maurices. 2) At checkout, tell the associate you'd like the military discount and present a valid military ID (CAC, VHIC, DD214, or veteran-designated ID). 3) The ~10% is applied at the register if the store participates.
**Online:** There is no verified military discount online — apply the best public promo code and shop clearance instead.

### How Verification Works
There is **no third-party verification** (no ID.me, SheerID, WeSalute, or GovX) for a Maurices military discount. In store, the associate simply checks your military ID.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No published corporate terms exist; treat availability, eligibility, and exclusions as **store-discretionary**.
- Assume the in-store discount applies to regular-price items and does not stack — confirm locally.

### Best Ways To Save More
- Shop **clearance** (the deepest single lever) and layer a current public promo code.
- Join **mymaurices** rewards for points, birthday offers, and members-only prices.
- Consider the **maurices VIP credit card** if you shop often (cardholder discounts/rewards).
- Watch major public sale windows (Black Friday, seasonal, July 4) — these beat the 10% for everyone.
