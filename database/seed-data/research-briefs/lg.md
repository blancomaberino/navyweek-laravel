# LG Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

LG Electronics (LG.com) offers active-duty military and veterans a verified discount through the **LG Appreciation Program**, redeemed on the **LG Partner Store** after identity verification with **ID.me**. The offer is a **guaranteed year-round minimum of an additional 10% off** LG-branded appliances and electronics — or an **additional 15% off LG STUDIO and LG SIGNATURE appliances** — compared to LG.com sale prices, plus free standard shipping and free installation on select categories. It is online-only at LG.com (no in-store mechanic), U.S. only, and capped at **100 items per year per member** with category limits. The single most important caveat: the percentage is a *minimum guarantee versus LG.com sale price*, not a flat coupon, and LG does not publish a maximum or per-product figure.

LG Electronics gives active-duty service members and veterans a verified discount through its **LG Appreciation Program**. ### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the LG Appreciation Program page (lg.com/us/appreciation-program) or the LG Partner Store (lg.com/us/lg-partner-store).
2. Click the ID.me verification button and select **Military** as your group affiliation.
3. Follow ID.me's prompts to provide the required information/documentation and verify your status (free).
4. Once verified, register/create your LG Partner Store account.
5. Shop the Partner Store — the guaranteed discounted prices are shown once you're signed in; select installation, delivery, and haul-away options at checkout as needed.

### How To Redeem In Store
LG does not operate consumer retail stores for this program. The LG Appreciation Program / Partner Store discount is **online-only at LG.com**. There is no in-store redemption mechanic stated in the official source. (For LG products bought at third-party retailers, this program does not apply.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**. You select the military group affiliation and complete ID.me's prompts; verification is **free** with no fees. After your status is confirmed, you register for the LG Store. LG states that **returning members can skip verification and sign in directly** at lg.com/partnerstore — but LG does not publish how long a verification lasts before re-verification is required, so do not assume it is permanent. If verification fails or you have trouble, LG directs users to the ID.me Help Center (help.id.me). Partner Store access/eligibility issues can be emailed to orders@lgsupport.com.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The discount is a **minimum guaranteed savings** of an additional 10% (15% for LG STUDIO/LG SIGNATURE) **compared to LG.com sale prices** — it floats with LG.com pricing rather than being a fixed coupon.
- **Purchase limit: up to 100 items per year per member; category limits apply.**
- **Free installation** applies to select LG OLED TVs, and all refrigerators, laundry, and cooking ranges (including LG STUDIO/LG SIGNATURE) — but **excludes the LG STUDIO Refrigerator SRSXB2622S**; you must select "Installation Service" at checkout.
- Free TV wall mounting (Angi) is offer-specific, up to a stated value, and voided if qualifying items are removed/returned/canceled.
- **Returns:** full-refund window of 14 days for most products, 30 days for laptops and vacuums; a 15% restocking fee applies to most products returned 15–30 days after delivery. Subject to LG's Terms of Purchase.
- Warranty: 1-year limited warranty on LG and LG SIGNATURE products; 2 years on LG STUDIO products (Partner Store benefit).
- Stacking with promo codes, sale items, gift cards, or rewards — Not stated in official source. (Prices already reflect LG.com sale pricing plus the guaranteed minimum.)
- Whether the military discount can be combined with the LG Referral Program (5% off) or MyLG Rewards — Not stated in official source.

### FAQ

#### Does LG offer a military discount?
Yes. Through the LG Appreciation Program, verified active military and veterans get a guaranteed minimum of an additional 10% off LG-branded products (15% off LG STUDIO/LG SIGNATURE appliances) versus LG.com sale prices, shopped on the LG Partner Store. Source: [LG Appreciation Program](https://www.lg.com/us/appreciation-program).

#### How much is the LG military discount?
LG guarantees a **minimum** of an additional **10% off** LG-branded appliances and electronics, or an additional **15% off** LG STUDIO and LG SIGNATURE appliances, compared to LG.com sale prices. LG does not publish a maximum percentage; actual savings can be higher when promos stack. Source: [LG Partner Store](https://www.lg.com/us/lg-partner-store).

#### Do veterans qualify? What about spouses and dependents?
Veterans qualify — the LG Partner Store names "Active Duty, Veterans" and eligibility runs through ID.me's military verification. Eligibility for military **spouses, dependents, retirees, Reserve, or National Guard** is not separately spelled out in LG's official copy; ID.me's military category determines who passes. Verify your specific status at the ID.me step. Source: [LG Partner Store](https://www.lg.com/us/lg-partner-store); [ID.me Help Center](https://help.id.me/hc/en-us).

#### How do I verify my military status?
Click the ID.me button on the LG Appreciation Program or Partner Store page, select **Military**, and follow the prompts to submit required documentation. It's free. Once verified, you register for the LG Store. Source: [LG Appreciation Program](https://www.lg.com/us/appreciation-program).

#### Does LG use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
LG's official Appreciation Program and Partner Store use **ID.me**. (LG products also appear on GovX, but LG's own program is ID.me-based.) Source: [LG Appreciation Program](https://www.lg.com/us/appreciation-program).

#### Can I use the LG military discount in stores?
No. The program is **online-only at LG.com** via the Partner Store. There is no in-store redemption mechanic in the official source. Source: [LG Appreciation Program](https://www.lg.com/us/appreciation-program).

#### What is excluded, and is there a purchase limit?
Members may buy **up to 100 items per year**, with category limits. Pricing is tied to LG.com sale prices. The free-installation benefit excludes the LG STUDIO Refrigerator SRSXB2622S. Source: [LG Partner Store](https://www.lg.com/us/lg-partner-store).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
LG does not state in its official source whether the Partner Store discount stacks with separate promo codes, gift cards, or rewards. Partner Store prices already reflect LG.com sale pricing plus the guaranteed minimum savings, and bundle promos appear inside the store. Source: [LG Partner Store](https://www.lg.com/us/lg-partner-store).

#### Does LG offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes — the same LG Appreciation Program covers first responders, teachers, nurses/medical providers, government employees, and students, each verified through their own ID.me group, at the same minimum 10% (15% for STUDIO/SIGNATURE) guarantee. Source: [LG Appreciation Program](https://www.lg.com/us/appreciation-program).
