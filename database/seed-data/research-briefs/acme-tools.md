**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $400 DEWALT power-tool order (a brand in Rakuten's 2.5% Acme Tools tier), shipped (order qualifies for free shipping at $199+).

## Headline finding

Acme Tools does **not** offer an official, published military or veteran discount. Its ID.me store page confirms ID.me is "not aware that Acme Tools offers Military discounts," there is no first-responder offer, Acme has no military-discount page on its own site, and it does not sell through the GovX marketplace. The genuine best path for a service member is therefore a **cashback portal stacked with an Acme sale price and Acme's own coupon/deal** — most valuably Rakuten's up-to-2.5% cashback on eligible brands (DEWALT, Milwaukee, Metabo HPT, Stihl) — plus Acme's 30-day **price-match guarantee** when a lower authorized-dealer price exists elsewhere. Archetype: **specialty/independent retailer with no verification-gated military program** (secondary: cashback-and-coupon store). Treat any "Acme Tools military promo code" from coupon aggregators as unverified.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Cashback portals are independent of Acme** — Rakuten/TopCashback pay a % for routing the sale; this stacks with any Acme on-site sale price and Acme coupon because Acme's checkout is unaware of the portal. (Rakuten: no cashback on gift-card purchase/redemption.)
2. **Price-match does NOT stack** with clearance, closeouts, special promotions, or discount offers, per Acme's price-match terms — you take the matched price OR the promo, not both.
