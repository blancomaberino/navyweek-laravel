# Tesla Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Tesla **does** offer a military/veteran discount on new vehicles, contrary to the long-standing assumption that it offers none. Under its "Military, First Responder, Healthcare, Teacher and Student Purchase Program" (marketed as "Made in America for Everyday Heroes," landing page `tesla.com/american-heroes`), Tesla gives **$500 off a new Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y, or Cybertruck** to verified U.S. military members — including veterans, retirees, active-duty personnel, their spouses, and surviving spouses — alongside first responders, medical providers/nurses, students, and teachers. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me before the order is placed**; once verified, the buyer is redirected to Tesla's Design Studio and the adjustment is applied to the vehicle purchase price (pre-tax) before order placement. The single most important caveat: **the discount must be applied before delivery and cannot be applied retroactively**, used vehicles are excluded, and the amount/program can change or end without notice (Tesla cut it from $1,000 to $500 in October 2025). Only one affiliation per order — it does not stack between hero groups — but it does stack with Tesla's current finance offers and Refer & Earn.

### Intro
Tesla offers verified U.S. military members — veterans, retirees, active-duty personnel, their spouses, and surviving spouses — **$500 off a new Tesla** through its Military, First Responder, Healthcare, Teacher and Student Purchase Program (the "Everyday Heroes" program). ### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military (including National Guard and Reserves) — eligible (per ID.me)
- Veterans (discharged under conditions other than dishonorable) — eligible
- Retirees — eligible
- Military spouses — eligible
- Surviving spouses — eligible
- Military family / dependents (immediate family, age 18+) — eligible per ID.me's military community definition
- First responders — eligible
- Medical providers / nurses — eligible
- Teachers — eligible
- Students — eligible
- Government employees (general) — not stated as a separate eligible group in Tesla's source
- Civilian DoD employees, Coast Guard Auxiliary, less-than-honorable discharges, non-U.S. military — not eligible (per ID.me)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Tesla's American Heroes page (`tesla.com/american-heroes`).
2. Verify your eligibility through ID.me (sign in or create an ID.me account and confirm your military/eligible status).
3. After successful verification you are redirected to Tesla's Design Studio.
4. Configure your new Model S, 3, X, Y, or Cybertruck; the $500 adjustment appears on the purchase price (pre-tax) before you place the order.
5. Confirm the discount is applied, then place your order. (The purchaser must match the ID.me account holder.)

### How To Redeem In Store
Tesla's program is verification-gated and applied online before order placement; Tesla does not publish a separate in-store/register redemption process for this discount. Verification through ID.me must be completed before you order and before delivery. (Not stated in official source: any in-store-only redemption path.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**. You must verify your eligible status **before placing the order** — Tesla states ID.me verification is required and that, if verified, the adjustment appears before you order. An ID.me account is required, and the **purchaser must match the ID.me account holder**. Tesla does not publish a re-verification frequency for this discount; ID.me's own status verification governs how/when your status is confirmed (documents that prove military status are listed in ID.me's help center). If automatic verification fails, ID.me's support/contact path is the route to resolve it (Tesla directs eligibility questions to ID.me). Do not assume verification is permanent — it is governed by ID.me, not stated as one-time by Tesla.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Used vehicles are not eligible** — new vehicles only.
- Benefit is deducted **pre-tax** from the vehicle purchase price.
- Must verify and apply **before delivery**; **cannot be applied retroactively** (except orders placed and delivered the same day, where verification must still occur first).
- **Not transferable; not redeemable for cash.**
- **Only one affiliation per order** — does not stack between hero groups.
- **Stackable** with Tesla's current finance offers and Refer & Earn.
- Offer value is stated in local currency; Tesla does not guarantee exchange rates.
- **Offer is subject to change or end at any time without notice** (e.g., reduced from $1,000 to $500 in October 2025).
- Purchaser must match the ID.me account holder.

### FAQ

#### Does Tesla offer a military discount?
Yes. Tesla offers verified U.S. military members $500 off a new Model S, Model 3, Model X, Model Y, or Cybertruck through its Military, First Responder, Healthcare, Teacher and Student Purchase Program. (Source: tesla.com/american-heroes.)

#### How much is the Tesla military discount?
$500 off a new Tesla vehicle, applied to the purchase price before tax. (Older sources cite $1,000 — that was the launch amount and was reduced to $500 in October 2025.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and surviving spouses qualify?
Yes. Tesla's program covers verified veterans, retirees, active-duty personnel, their spouses, and surviving spouses. Immediate military family members (18+) also qualify under ID.me's military community definition.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through ID.me on Tesla's American Heroes page, before you place your order. Once verified, you're redirected to Tesla's Design Studio and the discount is applied before you order. The purchaser must match the ID.me account holder.

#### Does Tesla use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
ID.me. Tesla's American Heroes program verifies military and other eligible groups exclusively through ID.me; GovX and SheerID are not used for this program (not stated in official source).

#### Can I use the Tesla military discount in stores?
The discount is verified and applied online before order placement; Tesla does not publish a separate in-store/register process. Complete ID.me verification before ordering and before delivery.

#### What is excluded?
Used vehicles. The discount is also not retroactive, not transferable, and not redeemable for cash, and only one affiliation is allowed per order.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, referrals, or finance offers?
It stacks with Tesla's current finance offers and Refer & Earn. It does not stack between hero groups (one affiliation per order).

#### Does Tesla offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — the same $500 program covers first responders, medical providers, nurses, teachers, and students, all verified through ID.me. A separate general "government employee" discount is not stated in Tesla's source.

#### Does the Tesla military discount apply to the Cybertruck?
Yes. As of Tesla's current pages, the program lists Cybertruck among eligible new models (Model S, 3, X, Y, and Cybertruck). Some 2025-era third-party sources said Cybertruck was excluded — that is outdated; confirm on tesla.com/american-heroes.
