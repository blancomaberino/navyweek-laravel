# CAVA — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one build-your-own CAVA bowl (~**$13** before tax, typical US price).

## Headline finding

**CAVA publishes no military or veteran discount.** There is no chain-wide program, no percentage, and no third-party verification (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute). CAVA's own customer-facing program is **CAVA Rewards** — a free points-based loyalty program open to everyone, including service members — plus a **birthday reward** (free drink, pita chips, or dessert at the entry "SEA" tier). CAVA is the classic **fast-casual, loyalty-driven, no-official-discount** archetype: the real savings come from the rewards program and occasional national promo days (e.g. National Pita Day giveaways), **not** a military line item. Any discount a service member gets is **discretionary and location-dependent** — some shoppers report a franchise/company store honoring a military or first-responder discount at the register, but it is not policy and cannot be promised. Coupon blogs claiming a fixed "10%" or "20%" CAVA military discount are **fabricated and unsourced** — treat them as false.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **No military discount to stack** — there is no official military offer, so there is nothing to combine with Rewards.
2. **CAVA Rewards earns on paid orders** and redemptions are applied as credit toward items; a redeemed reward and a fresh earn happen on the same account, but CAVA sets the earn thresholds and can change them.
3. **Birthday reward is member-only, once per year**, and the credit typically **expires ~30 days** after it's applied.
4. **Any register-level military/first-responder discount is discretionary** and not documented as stackable with a Rewards redemption — treat it as a single, location-specific courtesy, not a combinable program.
