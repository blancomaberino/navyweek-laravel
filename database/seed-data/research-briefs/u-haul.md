# U-Haul Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

U-Haul does **not** offer a percentage- or dollar-based military or veteran discount. U-Haul states this plainly on its own site: "While we don't have a specific military discount." There is no promo code, no online military-status verification (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute), and no veteran rate on truck, trailer, U-Box, or moving-supply rentals. The "10% / 15% off military" figures circulating on coupon-aggregator sites are not supported by any U-Haul source and should be treated as fabricated. What U-Haul *does* offer for the military community is a real, verifiable benefit set centered on **one month of free storage** — automatically with a one-way truck or trailer rental, or by showing a **DD Form 2278** at a participating location even without a rental — plus **24 free furniture pads** with every U-Box reservation and the moving-supply buy-back/free-shipping perks available to all customers. The single most important caveat: this is a moving-relocation benefit aimed at active-duty PCS/PPM moves, not a standing "veteran discount"; post-service veterans making a civilian move get no special rate beyond what any customer gets.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty service members — eligible for the free-month storage and PPM/PCS benefits (per uhaul.com/Military, "All service members").
- Service members doing a PPM/DITY move with a DD Form 2278 — eligible for one free month of storage even without a one-way rental.
- Anyone renting a one-way truck or trailer — eligible for one free month of storage at participating locations (not military-specific).
- Veterans making a post-service civilian move — Not stated as eligible for any special veteran rate; no veteran discount in official source.
- Military spouses / dependents / retirees / Reserve / National Guard / surviving spouses — Not separately addressed in official source for any pricing discount.
- First responders / medical workers / teachers / government employees / students — Not stated (no such discount; Collegeboxes student shipping savings exist but are not a military program).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to uhaul.com and reserve a one-way truck or trailer rental (different pickup and drop-off locations).
2. Complete your reservation; the one free month of storage at your destination is tied to the one-way rental at participating locations.
3. To check destination storage options, use uhaul.com/Storage/ or U-Haul's participating-locations guide.
4. Note: there is no military code or military-verification step to enter online — the benefit is attached to the one-way rental, not to a status check.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit a participating U-Haul or affiliate location.
2. Bring your **DD Form 2278** (Do-It-Yourself Move and Counseling Checklist) if you want the free month of storage *without* a one-way rental.
3. Present the DD Form 2278 to staff and request the first month of self-storage (U-Box or traditional) free of charge — available at origin or destination.
4. For furniture pads, reserve a U-Box container; 24 furniture pads are included per reservation.
5. Keep all rental receipts and weight tickets for your PPM reimbursement through your transportation office.

### How Verification Works
There is no third-party military-status verification (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute) and no account-based eligibility gate. Eligibility for the free storage month is established one of two ways: (1) automatically by booking a one-way truck or trailer rental, or (2) in person by presenting a **DD Form 2278** at a participating location. Because DD Form 2278 is issued during the PPM/PCS process, the in-person path is effectively limited to service members executing a move. For questions, U-Haul directs military movers to email militarymove@uhaul.com or call 1-800-GO-U-HAUL (1-800-468-4285).

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The free storage month is the **first month** only; standard rates apply thereafter.
- The automatic free-month-storage path requires a **one-way** truck or trailer rental ("Free month of storage available at U-Haul and participating affiliate locations with one-way truck or trailer rentals").
- Benefit is available at **participating** U-Haul and affiliate locations — not guaranteed at every location.
- Free furniture pads (24) are tied to a **U-Box container reservation**.
- No percentage/dollar discount exists, so there is nothing to "stack" with sales, promo codes, or rewards on a military basis. (Flag: official source does not state any stacking rules because no military discount exists.)
- Post-service civilian veteran moves: no special rate stated.
- General promotions (e.g., $0-to-start storage, free standard shipping over the order threshold, box buy-back) are open to all customers and are not military benefits.

### FAQ

#### Does U-Haul offer a military discount?
No. U-Haul states on its own site, "While we don't have a specific military discount." Instead it offers relocation benefits — chiefly one free month of storage — for service members doing a move.

#### How much is the U-Haul military discount?
There isn't one. There is no percentage or dollar amount off for military or veterans. Any "10%/15% military discount" you see on coupon sites is not supported by U-Haul and should be disregarded.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
For the free-month storage benefit, U-Haul references "all service members" and ties the in-person path to DD Form 2278 (active PPM/PCS moves). Veterans making a civilian move, spouses, and dependents are not stated as eligible for any special pricing in U-Haul's official sources.

#### How do I verify my military status?
You don't verify status through any portal. The free storage month is granted automatically with a one-way truck or trailer rental, or in person by showing your DD Form 2278 at a participating location.

#### Does U-Haul use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. U-Haul uses none of these. There is no online military-verification step.

#### Can I use the benefit in stores?
Yes — the DD Form 2278 free-storage path is handled in person at a participating U-Haul or affiliate location. The one-way-rental storage benefit applies whether you book online or in person.

#### What is excluded?
The free storage covers the first month only; the automatic path requires a one-way rental; it's available only at participating locations. Furniture-pad freebies are tied to a U-Box reservation. There's no military discount to combine with other offers.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
There is no military discount to combine. General U-Haul promotions (storage start fees, free shipping, buy-back) apply per their own terms regardless of military status.

#### Does U-Haul offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No first-responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount is published. U-Haul's Collegeboxes service offers students discounted shipping/storage, but that is a general student-moving service, not a military or first-responder program.

#### Do competitors offer a military discount U-Haul doesn't?
Yes — Penske and Budget publish military discounts on truck rentals. Service members and veterans should price-compare, since U-Haul's value is in storage and low base rates rather than a military rate.
