# SPANX — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $68 SPANX order (one pair of leggings/shaping piece), before tax.

## Headline finding

**SPANX does not offer a military or veteran discount.** It has no ID.me military gate and no SheerID military offer — both verification platforms confirm SPANX does not participate for military. SPANX also does not run a standing first-responder discount, and its student discount is intermittent (occasionally via UNiDAYS, not a military benefit). SPANX is a **promo-and-sale-driven DTC brand**: the genuine maximum-savings path for a service member is a **public sitewide sale or promo code stacked with the first-order email/SMS sign-up offer**, plus the brand's "On the Sly" clearance section — not a military discount.
