# Titanic Museum Attraction — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult general-admission ticket at the Pigeon Forge, TN location, gate price ~$39.99 (online advance ~$38.87–$38.99). Child (ages 5–12) reference price ~$14.99; ages 4 and under free. Branson, MO pricing is comparable. All effective-price math below uses the ~$39.99 adult ticket unless noted.

## Headline finding
Yes — the Titanic Museum Attraction (Pigeon Forge, TN and Branson, MO) recognizes military and veterans, but the **biggest savings are the recognition-day free-admission programs, not an everyday percentage.** Two documented annual programs run: **"Titanic Honors Veterans" in November** (free admission with military ID during Veterans Week, one comp per ID; the rest of the party gets the regular military discount) and **"Celebrate Freedom" in August** (military personnel and first responders free with ID, historically Pigeon Forge). Outside those windows there is a modest **everyday box-office military discount** (Explore Branson's DMO lists ~$2–$3 off for active/retired military and family; a larger ~$5 adult / ~$3 child figure circulates on aggregators but is **not confirmed on the museum's own pages**), plus a **GOVX** online military-verified purchase path for Pigeon Forge. Archetype: **attraction / ticketed venue behaving as Archetype H "holiday/recognition-day free admission"** (dominant), with a secondary **small everyday box-office military discount + third-party GOVX online path**. Best move: **go free in August or during Veterans Week in November**; otherwise show military ID at the box office (or buy via GOVX for Pigeon Forge).

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Recognition-day free admission is per eligible person, one comp per ID** — it does not multiply across a family; accompanying guests pay (during Veterans Week they get the "regular military discount," not free).
2. **The everyday military discount is redeemed at the box office with ID** and is not combinable with third-party coupon-site "promo codes" (those are not museum-issued).
3. **GOVX is an either/or online channel** for Pigeon Forge — you use GOVX *instead of* the box-office discount, not on top of it.
4. **Free-admission programs are not stackable with paid group rates** — a free comp is already the floor.
5. **Online-advance pricing is available to everyone** and is the fallback when no military program/ID applies; it is not stacked with the military discount.
6. Terms, exact amounts, and program dates **vary by location (Pigeon Forge vs. Branson)** and by year — confirm at the specific location.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty** service members (all branches) with a valid CAC or military ID.
- **Veterans** with a VA-issued Veteran Health ID Card (VHIC), a state driver's license/ID with a veteran designation, or discharge papers (DD-214).
- **Retirees and reserve/guard** with a valid military retiree/reserve ID.
- **First responders** — included in the **August "Celebrate Freedom"** free-admission program (historically Pigeon Forge).
- **Family/party members** — receive the regular military discount alongside the eligible person, not free admission.

### How To Redeem Online / At the Box Office
**At the box office (primary):**
1. Bring valid military or veteran ID (CAC, VHIC, retiree ID, or veteran-designated driver's license).
2. Tell the ticketing agent you're military/veteran and ask for the current military discount or, in August/November, the free-admission program.
3. Present ID for each eligible person (one comp ticket per ID during free-admission programs).
4. Pay any balance for accompanying non-eligible guests.

**Online (Pigeon Forge, via GOVX):**
1. Create/sign in to a free **GOVX** account and complete military verification.
2. Open the Titanic Museum Attraction (Pigeon Forge) GOVX offer.
3. Purchase the verified-price tickets and present them at the gate with your ID.

**Online advance (everyone):**
1. Go to booking.titanicattraction.com, choose Pigeon Forge or Branson, pick date/time, and buy — online advance is slightly below gate.

### How Verification Works
Verification is **ID-based**. The museum processes military discounts **at the box office** on presentation of a valid military/veteran ID; there is no museum-issued online promo code for the everyday box-office discount. For online purchase, **GOVX** performs the military verification and issues the discounted tickets. Bring physical ID even if you verified online — the gate may ask to see it.

### Exclusions And Fine Print

- Program dates change yearly; the August and November programs are annual but confirm current-year dates.

### FAQ
**Does the Titanic Museum offer a military discount?** Yes. There's a modest everyday military discount at the box office with valid ID, plus free-admission programs in August and during Veterans Week in November.

**How much is the military discount?** Off-program it's small — Explore Branson's DMO lists about **$2–$3 off** for Branson; Pigeon Forge's exact everyday amount should be confirmed at the box office or via GOVX. During the August and November programs, eligible military get **in free**.

**Do veterans, retirees, and family qualify?** Veterans and retirees qualify with valid ID. During Veterans Week the veteran gets a **free comp ticket (one per ID)**; accompanying family/party members get the regular military discount, not free admission.

**How do I prove military status?** Show a valid military/veteran ID at the box office — CAC (active duty), VA Veteran Health ID Card, retiree ID, or a driver's license with a veteran designation. For online at Pigeon Forge, verify through **GOVX**.

**Is there free admission on Veterans Day / Memorial Day?** Yes for **Veterans Week in November** ("Titanic Honors Veterans") and for **August** ("Celebrate Freedom," military + first responders). We found no documented Memorial Day free-admission program — confirm on the site near the date.

**Does the discount apply at both Pigeon Forge and Branson?** Both locations honor military and run the recognition programs, but exact everyday amounts and program dates can differ — confirm with your specific location.

**Can I combine it with other discounts or AAA?** No. The military discount isn't stackable with coupon-site promo codes, and free-admission comps are one per ID. We found no verified AAA discount for the museum.

**What's the cheapest way for a service member to visit?** Visit in **August or during November Veterans Week** and get in **free** with ID. Off-program, use the box-office military discount or the GOVX online price at Pigeon Forge.

**Is there a first responder, teacher, or student discount?** First responders are included in the **August "Celebrate Freedom"** free-admission program. We found **no** documented teacher or student discount — check the site or ask at the box office.
