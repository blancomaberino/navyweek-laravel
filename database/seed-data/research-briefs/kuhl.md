## Executive Summary

As of June 23, 2026, **KÜHL does not publish or advertise a standing military or veteran discount** on its own website. KÜHL's full customer FAQ (Account & Orders, Shipping, Returns, Payments, Products, Story) contains no military/veteran discount entry, and the site shows no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX military-verification flow. KÜHL operates a separate, login-gated **"Pro Sales" (pro deal) program** and has a presence on **ExpertVoice** — both are industry/professional programs with non-public terms, not a consumer military discount, and neither states military eligibility in public-facing text. KÜHL has **no GovX storefront** (`govx.com/kuhl` returns a 404 "page not found"). The most reliable verified savings path for the military community is buying KÜHL through the **Army & Air Force Exchange Service (ShopMyExchange / AAFES)**, where eligible, verified patrons shop KÜHL tax-free (and during Exchange promotions, at a reduced price). Widely circulated third-party claims of a "40% KÜHL military discount," a "30% code DOESDA," "25% off," or "75% first responder" offers are **not corroborated by any KÜHL primary source**, are mutually contradictory, and in at least one case reference an implausible/spam code — all flagged Low and should not be stated as fact.
