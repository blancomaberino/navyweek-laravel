# Cold Stone Creamery — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one "Love It" size Signature Creation (~$7 before tax).

## Headline finding

Cold Stone Creamery has **no corporate, chain-wide military discount**. Every location is franchisee-owned and operated, and — per company policy repeated across military-discount references — **each franchise owner sets its own discounts, sales, and promotions**. In practice that means military benefits are **location-dependent and not guaranteed**: some stores offer a standing ~10% military courtesy, third-party surveys of store social pages have seen anywhere from 10% up to ~50% at individual locations, and a number of stores offer a **free scoop or discount on Veterans Day** — but none of this is a promise you can rely on at any given store. The single most reliable savings path is Cold Stone's own **My Cold Stone Club rewards program** (sign-up bonus + points on every dollar), and, if you want a military rate, **calling or asking your specific store first**. WeSalute lists a Cold Stone "up to 10%" offer but explicitly notes it's outside its Partner Coalition and can't be guaranteed.

Not corporately. Individual franchises may — commonly ~10% where offered — but it's not guaranteed. Ask your local store.

Where a store offers a discount, active duty, retirees, veterans, guard/reserve, and immediate family typically qualify with a military ID — but it varies by location.

In store, with a military ID. There's no online verification.

No — any military courtesy is in-store only.

Maybe not — ask the store. My Cold Stone Club points generally still apply.
