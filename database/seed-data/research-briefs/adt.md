# ADT — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new ADT professionally-monitored home security system on a **new 3-year monitoring agreement** (homeowner), standard professional installation **$199**, monitoring from ~$24.99–$59.99/mo depending on plan/equipment.

## Headline finding

**ADT LLC (the corporate brand) publishes no first-party military or veteran discount** — none appears on ADT's own deals page, and WeSalute's terms explicitly say the offer does **not** apply to "purchases made directly from ADT LLC." What people call the "ADT military discount" is a **third-party ADT Authorized Dealer offer**, brokered through **WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage), an ADT verification partner since 2009**, and also surfaced via **GOVX** and dealers such as Secure24/S24. The verified WeSalute offer: **$99 installation** (vs a normal $199), a **free starter equipment package** (~$125 value), **eligibility for a $100 Visa reward gift card**, and a bundled year of WeSalute+ — marketed as "up to **$914.90**" in total setup savings. That is a legitimate, verified path, but the "$914.90" is a best-case marketing aggregate, not a guaranteed number. Archetype: **service/subscription brand with no first-party discount → offer is dealer/verification-portal-gated** (no tax-free exchange or pro-deal channel exists). Best path: if you are a homeowner ready to sign a 3-year monitoring agreement, redeem through **WeSalute or GOVX**; if you are a renter, want no long contract, or mainly want cameras, ADT's **public deals** (or a competitor with its own military discount) often beat it.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The WeSalute military offer cannot be combined** with other ADT offers or promotions (WeSalute terms + The Military Wallet dealer page both state this). Treat every path as either/or.

4. **AARP 5% monitoring** is a separate, age-based discount; combinability with a dealer new-customer offer is **not verified** — assume not combinable until confirmed with the dealer.

### FAQ

#### Does ADT offer a military discount?
Not directly. ADT LLC publishes no first-party military/veteran discount. The "ADT military discount" is an **ADT Authorized Dealer** offer delivered through **WeSalute (Veterans Advantage)** — ADT's verification partner since 2009 — and **GOVX**.

#### How much is the ADT military discount?
The verified WeSalute offer is **$99 installation** (vs a normal $199), a **free starter equipment package** (~$125 value), and eligibility for a **$100 Visa reward gift card** — marketed as up to **$914.90** in total setup savings (a best-case aggregate, not a guaranteed number).

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — active duty, retirees, veterans, current/former guard & reserve, and **immediate family members** qualify via WeSalute. You must also be a **homeowner** and a **new** customer.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage) or **GOVX**. Create an account, verify service, then redeem the ADT dealer offer via the toll-free number in the steps.

#### Does ADT use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
For this offer, **WeSalute** and **GOVX**. We found no evidence ADT uses ID.me or SheerID for the military offer.

#### Can I use it online or do I have to call?
You redeem by **calling the dealer's toll-free number** in the WeSalute/GOVX steps — not by checking out directly on ADT.com. Buying direct from ADT forfeits the offer terms.

#### What is excluded?
Renters, existing ADT customers, anyone unwilling to sign a 3-year monitoring agreement or who doesn't pass the credit check. The offer also can't be combined with other ADT promotions.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military dealer offer is **not combinable** with other ADT offers. Note the $100 Visa gift card also appears as a public promo on $349+ systems — you get it once, not stacked.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from ADT?
If you're a homeowner signing a 3-year plan, the **WeSalute/GOVX dealer offer** ($99 install + free kit + $100 Visa card). If you want cameras or DIY, the **public ADT deals** can win. If you don't want a long contract, a **no-contract competitor with its own military discount** may be cheaper overall.

#### Does ADT offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
WeSalute verifies these groups broadly, and GOVX serves first responders. Confirm at redemption whether the specific ADT dealer offer extends to your group — we verified the military/veteran/family path.

#### Does ADT run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No ADT-specific military-holiday promo is verified — the dealer/WeSalute military offer runs **year-round**. Public seasonal promos peak around **Black Friday/Cyber Monday**.

#### Can I buy ADT at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
No. ADT is a monitored-service subscription — there's no AAFES/NEX exchange or ExpertVoice/pro-deal channel.
