## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The GovX discount is a **single-use code, one per day** — it is **one** discount, not a stack. (safariland.com/pages/govx-discount)
2. Safariland promo/affinity codes **cannot be combined with other promotions or codes**, and are **not valid on clearance/sale items**. So GovX 15% does **not** stack on top of a Veterans Day sale or clearance price — you take whichever single discount is larger. *(General Safariland code terms; exact verbatim clearance/stacking language on the GovX page could not be re-fetched — see Open Questions.)*
3. **Loyalty points cannot be combined with other discounts** (loyalty terms) — so points don't stack on a GovX order.
4. ExpertVoice pro pricing is its **own channel**; it is not applied on top of a GovX code, and specific products are flagged "Not eligible for ExpertVoice discount."
5. Third-party retailers (OpticsPlanet, Copquest) and the Exchange are **separate sellers** — you can't apply a Safariland.com code there, but their everyday/tax-free price often lands below the 15%-off direct price on its own.

### FAQ

#### Does Safariland offer a military discount?
Yes — 15% off orders at Safariland.com for verified military and first responders, through GovX ID.

#### How much is the Safariland military discount?
15% off, delivered as a single-use code (one per day) after GovX ID verification.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. GovX ID covers active and former military, plus military spouses and dependents.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through GovX ID at safariland.com/pages/govx-discount — free, usually real-time (DoD database, email, or document upload).

#### Does Safariland use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**GovX ID.** Not ID.me — some coupon sites get this wrong.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The GovX code is for **Safariland.com online** checkout. In person, authorized patrons can buy Safariland tax-free at the military Exchange instead.

#### What is excluded?
Clearance/sale items, and it can't be combined with other codes or with loyalty points. One code per day.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. It's one discount only — not stackable with other codes, sales, clearance, or loyalty points.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Safariland?
It depends: if you qualify for **ExpertVoice**, that's usually deepest; otherwise compare a **third-party dealer (~20% off, no verification)** and the **tax-free Exchange** against the official **15%** — the winner varies by item and stock.

#### Does Safariland offer a first responder, government, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
First responders (LE/fire/EMS) and government employees: yes, same 15% via GovX. Nurses/EMS fall under GovX medical services. No separate teacher/student program is published.

#### Can I buy Safariland at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
Yes to both, if eligible — the Exchange (tax-free, authorized patrons) and ExpertVoice (verified pros/affiliates) are separate channels from the GovX code.
