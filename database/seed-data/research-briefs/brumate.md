## Headline finding

BrüMate runs an **official "Frontline" discount: 10% off**, verified through **GovX**, for active military and veterans, first responders (law enforcement, paramedics, EMTs, nurses, firefighters), and educators (K-12 and university teachers/staff). It is a real, brand-advertised program on brumate.com. The important correction: multiple aggregators claim "15% off via ID.me" — that is **outdated/incorrect**. The verified current benefit is **10% via GovX**, single-use, non-combinable, with a meaningful exclusion list (coolers, Collabs, Paragon hydration gear, gift cards, sale items, bundles/sets). Best path: use the 10% on full-price drinkware, or skip it and buy a public "discount code" promo when one beats 10% (they don't stack).
