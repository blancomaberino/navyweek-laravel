# Apple TV (Apple TV+) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Scope: This brief covers the **Apple TV streaming service** (the subscription formerly branded "Apple TV+"), not Apple hardware. Apple's hardware/accessory military program is documented separately in `apple.md`.

## Executive Summary

**Apple does not offer a military or veteran discount on the Apple TV streaming subscription.** As of June 23, 2026, Apple TV (rebranded from "Apple TV+") costs **$12.99/month after a 7-day free trial**, with no reduced rate for active-duty service members, veterans, or military families on the streaming service itself. Apple's only published military benefit — the **Veterans and Military Purchase Program**, a 10% discount verified through **ID.me** — applies explicitly to **Apple products and accessories (hardware)**, not subscriptions or services. Service members can still reduce or avoid Apple TV cost through Apple's standard, non-military offers: the 7-day free trial, 3 months free with a qualifying new Apple device, free Apple TV access included with an Apple Music Student plan, and bundling via Apple One. For an actual military discount on streaming, competitors such as Paramount+ and Hulu publish dedicated offers (context only; verify on their own pages).

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Not eligible for any streaming discount (none exists). Eligible for Apple's 10% hardware discount.
- **Veterans** — Not eligible for any streaming discount. Eligible for the 10% hardware discount.
- **Retirees** — Not stated separately; the hardware program covers current and Veteran members of the U.S. Military, National Guard, and Reserve.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Not eligible for streaming discount. Eligible for the 10% hardware discount.
- **Military spouses / immediate family** — Not eligible for streaming discount. For hardware, immediate family members residing in the same household are eligible.
- **Dependents** — Same as immediate family above (hardware only).
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not stated in official source.
- **First responders / medical workers / teachers / government employees / students** — No military-style streaming discount. Apple does run a separate Education store and a Student offer (Apple Music Student plan includes free Apple TV access for a limited time).

### How To Redeem Online
There is no military discount to redeem on the Apple TV streaming service. To subscribe at the standard rate or start a free trial:
1. Go to tv.apple.com or open the Apple TV app on a supported device.
2. Select **Accept Free Trial** (7 days free) or sign in / sign up.
3. Sign in with the Apple Account you use for purchases (or create one) and confirm a payment method.
4. After the 7-day trial, you are billed $12.99/month until you cancel.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Apple TV is a digital subscription; there is no in-store military discount for the streaming service. (Apple's in-store/hardware military pricing is handled through the ID.me-verified online Veterans and Military store and applies to devices, not subscriptions.)

### How Verification Works
There is no verification path for an Apple TV streaming military discount because no such discount exists. Apple's **hardware** military program uses **ID.me**: you verify your veteran or active-military status with an existing or new ID.me account to shop the special Veterans and Military storefront, where the 10% discount applies to eligible products and accessories. That storefront's pricing does not extend to the Apple TV subscription or other Apple services.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Apple's 10% Veterans and Military discount applies to **Apple products and accessories**; the Apple TV subscription and other Apple services are not part of the discounted storefront pricing (source states discounted pricing is available only at the special storefront, which sells hardware/accessories).
- The Apple TV subscription **does not include** third-party-branded subscription services or content available for rental or purchase in the Apple TV app.
- The 3-months-free offer applies to new/eligible subscribers who buy a qualifying Apple device; it may not be available if you currently or previously subscribed.
- Apple TV availability and features vary by country/region.
- ZEISS Optical Inserts–Prescription are excluded from Apple discount programs (hardware note; listed for completeness).

### Best Ways To Save More
Official, non-military ways to reduce or avoid Apple TV cost:
- **7-day free trial** — Try Apple TV free for one week before paying.
- **3 months free with a new Apple device** — If you buy a qualifying Apple device and are eligible, the offer appears automatically in the Apple TV app when signed in with the associated Apple Account.
- **Apple Music Student plan** — Includes free access to Apple TV content for a limited time for eligible students.
- **Apple One bundle** — Combines Apple TV with iCloud+, Apple Music, Apple Arcade (and Fitness+/News+ on Premier). Individual $19.95/mo, Family $25.95/mo, Premier $37.95/mo; new subscriptions are free for the first month.
- **Family Sharing** — One Apple TV subscription can be shared with up to five family members at no extra cost.
- **Competitor alternatives with real military discounts (context — verify on their pages):** Paramount+ and Hulu publish dedicated military discounts verified through SheerID. Reported figures (secondary sources, not Apple): Paramount+ ~50% off; Hulu ~40% off the ad-supported plan. Confirm current terms directly with those services.

### FAQ

#### Does Apple TV offer a military discount?
No. As of June 2026, Apple does not offer a military or veteran discount on the Apple TV streaming subscription. Apple TV is $12.99/month after a 7-day free trial for everyone. Apple's 10% military discount applies only to hardware and accessories.

#### How much is the Apple TV military discount?
There isn't one for the streaming service. Apple TV costs $12.99/month after a free 7-day trial regardless of military status.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount on Apple TV streaming?
No — there is no military discount on the Apple TV subscription for any group. Veterans, active-duty members, and immediate household family members do qualify for Apple's separate **10% hardware discount** on Apple products and accessories.

#### How do I verify my military status for Apple TV?
You don't, because there's no streaming discount to verify for. Apple's hardware military program uses ID.me; that verification only unlocks discounted pricing on devices and accessories, not the Apple TV subscription.

#### Does Apple use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for Apple TV?
Apple uses **ID.me** for its Veterans and Military Purchase Program — but that program is for **hardware**, not the Apple TV streaming service. There is no verification provider tied to an Apple TV subscription discount because no such discount exists.

#### Can I get Apple TV for free as a service member?
Not through a military offer, but anyone (including service members) can use Apple's standard free options: a 7-day free trial, 3 months free with a qualifying new Apple device, free Apple TV access with an Apple Music Student plan, or a first month free when starting Apple One.

#### Is the Apple TV subscription included in Apple's 10% military discount?
No. Apple's discounted military storefront covers products and accessories. The Apple TV subscription is not part of that discounted pricing.

#### Can I combine an Apple discount with the Apple TV subscription price?
There is no Apple TV subscription discount to combine. You can lower your effective cost by bundling Apple TV into Apple One or sharing one subscription with up to five family members via Family Sharing.

#### Do other streaming services offer a military discount if Apple doesn't?
Yes. Paramount+ and Hulu publish dedicated military discounts (verified via SheerID). These figures come from secondary sources — confirm current terms on each service's official page. NavyWeek maintains separate guides for those services.
