# Mattress Firm — Military & Veteran Discount Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $1,200 queen mattress purchase.

## Headline finding

Mattress Firm offers a **real, official military discount of 10%–20%**, verified through **ID.me** and honored **online and in-store**. Mattress Firm's own military page (mattressfirm.com/military-discount.html) confirms "Save **10–20%** off top mattress and bedding brands for **active and reservist military, veterans, military spouses and more**." The key nuance most pages miss: the **20% is not universal** — **Purple** brand is fixed at **10%**, and several premium brands (Tempur-Pedic, Stearns & Foster, Beautyrest Black, Nectar, etc.) plus clearance, floor models, and adjustable bases are **excluded**. Because Mattress Firm runs **deep, frequent public sales** and the military discount **applies only to the listed sale price and generally won't stack with other promos**, a big seasonal sale can rival or beat the military 20% on some carts — compare before you buy. Archetype: **real identity discount (E) with a strong sale calendar (C)** — verify, but price-check against the current public sale.

### FAQ

#### Does Mattress Firm offer a military discount?
Yes — **10–20% off** top mattress and bedding brands for active and reservist military, veterans, military spouses and more, verified via **ID.me**, online or in store. (Source: [Mattress Firm – Military Discount](https://www.mattressfirm.com/military-discount.html), accessed July 6, 2026.)

#### How much is the Mattress Firm military discount?
It's a **range: 10–20%**, not a flat 20%. The exact amount depends on brand/category — for example, **Purple is capped at 10%**, while other eligible brands can reach 20%. Premium brands, clearance, floor models, and adjustable bases are excluded.

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Yes — Mattress Firm explicitly names **active and reservist military, veterans, and military spouses** (plus "more," which secondary sources describe as including retirees and family/dependents).

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through **ID.me** on Mattress Firm's military page: verify once, receive a discount code, and use it online or in store.

#### Does Mattress Firm use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**ID.me.** Mattress Firm's military discount is verified through ID.me.

#### Can I use it online and in store?
**Both.** Verify with ID.me, then apply the code at mattressfirm.com/app checkout, or present your verified status in a participating store.

#### What's excluded?
Reported exclusions include Purple being only 10%, plus premium brands (Tempur-Pedic, Stearns & Foster, Beautyrest Black, Nectar and others), clearance, floor models, final markdowns, and adjustable bases. Confirm the current list on the ID.me storefront.

#### Can I combine it with a sale or promo code?
Generally **no** — the military discount applies to the listed sale price but **doesn't stack** with other promotions, and the code is single-use. That's why you should compare the military 20% against a big public sale before buying.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Mattress Firm?
On an eligible, non-excluded brand at listed price, **verify with ID.me and take 20%**. For **Purple** (10%) or during a **deep seasonal sale**, run the numbers — the public sale can win, and you can't stack. In store, it's also worth **negotiating**.
