# Santa's Village — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **family-of-four summer day visit** (1 service member/veteran + 1 spouse + 2 kids ages 4+), priced separately per park because **"Santa's Village" is two unrelated US parks** — Jefferson, New Hampshire and East Dundee, Illinois (plus an unrelated third in Bracebridge, Ontario, Canada).

## Headline finding

Both US Santa's Villages publish real military offers, but they are wildly different — and the page's first job is disambiguation. **Santa's Village Azoosment & Water Park (East Dundee, IL)** has the far stronger offer: **active-duty military and veterans get FREE admission with ID, plus up to four family tickets at $29.99 each** (vs. $42.99+tax regular) — **gate-only**. **Santa's Village (Jefferson, NH)** offers a modest published military rate: **$46 vs. $50 regular admission** (same as its senior rate) on **Summertime and Holly Jolly Halloween tickets only** — purchased online, ID shown at the gate — and explicitly **no discount** on FEASTival, Christmastime, or New Year's Eve tickets. Neither park uses a verification platform (no ID.me/GovX/SheerID); both are physical-ID-at-the-gate programs, and neither allows combining discounts. Archetype: **E (in-person identity discount is the whole game)** — no stacking layer exists at either park.

### Intro
First, make sure you're looking at the right park — there are **two** Santa's Villages in the US, and their military offers are nothing alike. **Santa's Village Amusement & Water Park in East Dundee, Illinois** (near Chicago) gives **active-duty military and veterans free admission** with a valid ID — Military ID, DD214, Veteran ID, or a driver's license with veteran designation — **plus up to four family tickets at $29.99 each**, sold only at the gate. **Santa's Village in Jefferson, New Hampshire** offers a smaller published discount: **$46 instead of $50** on Summertime and Holly Jolly Halloween admission, bought online with your military ID shown at the entrance — and no military discount at all on Christmastime, FEASTival, or New Year's Eve tickets.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Eligible at both parks (ID required).
- **Veterans** — Explicitly eligible at the **IL park** (DD214, Veteran ID, or vet-designated license accepted). At the **NH park**, only "Military (with ID)" is stated — whether a veteran ID qualifies is not published; call ahead.
- **Family members** — IL: up to 4 discounted $29.99 tickets alongside the free service member/veteran. NH: not stated whether the $46 rate extends beyond the ID holder.
- **First responders** — IL's page title names them, but the published offer text does not; **not verified** at either park.

### How To Redeem
**Illinois (East Dundee) — at the gate**
1. Bring valid proof: Military ID, DD214, Veteran ID, or driver's license/state ID with veteran designation.
2. Go to the ticket gate (this offer is not sold online).
3. Service member/veteran enters **free**; buy up to 4 family tickets at **$29.99 each** (+tax).

**New Hampshire (Jefferson) — online, then gate**
1. Buy the **Military (with ID) $46** admission online at santasvillage.com for a Summertime or Holly Jolly Halloween date.
2. Bring your military ID to show at the park entrance — no ID, no discounted entry.
3. Don't look for a military rate on Christmastime, FEASTival, or New Year's Eve tickets — none exists.

### How Verification Works
Both parks check a **physical ID at the entrance/gate** — no verification platform, no account, no pre-registration. The IL park accepts the broadest document set (including DD214 and vet-designated licenses); the NH park says only "military ID." Re-verification is per-visit by nature.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **NH:** no military discount on FEASTival, Christmastime, or New Year's Eve admission; no coupon codes exist for the park, period; military rate equals the senior rate ($46, incl. $1 fee).
- **IL:** offer available **only at the gate**; discounts can't combine with any other discount/promotion; ticket prices are plus tax; "rates may vary."
- **IL first responders:** in the page title, absent from the offer text — unverified.
- Kids: under 4 free at NH; under 36" free at IL.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Pick the right park's channel**: gate for IL, online-in-advance for NH (NH sells date-specific tickets; summer dates can sell out).
- **NH: time your visit** to Summertime or Holly Jolly Halloween — the only military-discount windows.

### FAQ
#### Does Santa's Village have a military discount?
Yes — both US parks do, but they're different businesses: free admission for active military and veterans (plus $29.99 family tickets) at Santa's Village in East Dundee, Illinois; $4 off ($46 vs $50) Summertime and Halloween tickets at Santa's Village in Jefferson, New Hampshire.
#### Is Santa's Village free for veterans?
At the **Illinois** park, yes — active-duty military and veterans get free admission with a Military ID, DD214, Veteran ID, or vet-designated driver's license, at the gate only. The **New Hampshire** park does not offer free admission; its published military rate is $46.
#### How much is the Santa's Village NH military discount?
$46 per person (including the $1 fee) versus the regular $50 — on Summertime and Holly Jolly Halloween admission only, purchased online with military ID shown at the entrance.
#### Does the military discount work at Christmastime?
Not in New Hampshire — the park explicitly excludes Christmastime, FEASTival, and New Year's Eve tickets from the military discount. (The IL park's season is summer-focused; check its calendar.)
#### Do family members get the discount?
Illinois: yes — up to four additional tickets at $29.99 each alongside the free service member/veteran. New Hampshire: not stated; only the "Military (with ID)" rate is published.
#### Does Santa's Village use ID.me or GovX?
No — both parks verify with a physical ID at the gate. There's no online verification platform.
#### Do first responders get a discount at Santa's Village?
Unverified. The Illinois park's page is titled "Military and First Responders," but its published offer covers only active military and veterans — call the park before counting on it. New Hampshire publishes no first-responder rate.
#### Can I combine the military discount with coupons or other offers?
No. Illinois prohibits combining discounts outright, and New Hampshire states it has no coupon codes for admission at all.
