# IHOP Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

IHOP does **not** advertise a corporate everyday (year-round) military or veteran discount. The only military offer IHOP promotes on its own website is an annual **Veterans Day** promotion: on November 11, veterans and active-duty military get a **free** order of Red, White & Blueberry Pancakes (or, at select locations, a pancake combo) for **dine-in only**, at **participating U.S. restaurants**, with a limit of **one per qualified guest** and valid proof of military service required. Outside Veterans Day, any everyday "military discount" (commonly cited online as ~10–20% off) is **not a corporate program** — IHOP restaurants are independently owned and operated, so any standing discount is offered at each location's discretion and is not stated on any IHOP primary source. The single most important caveat: confirm with your local IHOP before relying on any everyday discount, and note that the Veterans Day free meal is dine-in only at participating locations. (IHOP Veterans Day page, ihop.com/en/veterans-day, accessed June 23, 2026; IHOP Products/Values FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

### Intro
IHOP does not publish a corporate everyday military or veteran discount. Because nearly all IHOP restaurants are independently owned and operated, any year-round discount for service members is set by the individual location, not by IHOP corporate — so it varies and is not guaranteed. Your best bet is to call your local IHOP and ask before you go.

What IHOP does promote nationally is its annual **Veterans Day** offer: on November 11, veterans and active-duty military can get free Red, White & Blueberry Pancakes (or a pancake combo at select locations), dine-in only, at participating U.S. restaurants, with proof of service.

This is an independent guide from NavyWeek. We are not affiliated with IHOP. IHOP controls its offers and can change or end them at any time; confirm current terms with IHOP or your local restaurant.

### Who Qualifies
- Veterans — eligible for the Veterans Day offer (per IHOP).
- Active-duty military — eligible for the Veterans Day offer (per IHOP).
- Retirees, Reserve/National Guard, spouses, dependents, surviving spouses/Gold Star families — Not stated in official source for the Veterans Day offer (IHOP states "veterans and active duty military"). Check with your local IHOP.
- First responders, medical workers, teachers, government employees, students — Not stated in official source (no IHOP corporate program).

### How To Redeem Online
IHOP does not offer a military discount or the Veterans Day free meal online, via the app, or for delivery. This offer is dine-in only. (IHOP Veterans Day page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Confirm your local IHOP is participating (call ahead, especially on Veterans Day) — offers vary by location.
2. Dine in on Veterans Day (Nov 11), within the stated hours (historically 7 a.m.–7 p.m. local; re-confirm each year).
3. Tell your server you are a veteran or active-duty service member and present valid proof of service.
4. Order the Red, White & Blueberry Pancakes (or the pancake combo where offered); limit one per qualified guest.
5. For any everyday (non–Veterans Day) discount: ask your server before ordering whether the location offers a military discount, and show your military ID — availability is at the location's discretion.

### How Verification Works
There is no online verification provider (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute integration for IHOP). Verification happens in person: you show proof of military service to restaurant staff. IHOP's Veterans Day promotion has historically listed acceptable proof as a U.S. Uniformed Services ID card, U.S. Uniformed Services Retired ID card, current Leave and Earnings Statement (LES), a veterans organization card (e.g., American Legion, VFW), a photograph of yourself in uniform, wearing your uniform, a DD-214, military dog tags, or a citation/commendation. (IHOP news release, ihop.com/news, accessed June 23, 2026.) Acceptable proof can vary by year and location — confirm with your restaurant.

### Exclusions And Fine Print

### FAQ

#### Does IHOP offer a military discount?
IHOP does not advertise a corporate everyday military or veteran discount. It promotes an annual Veterans Day free meal for veterans and active-duty military. Any year-round discount is at the individual restaurant's discretion, since most IHOPs are independently owned. (IHOP Products/Values FAQ; IHOP Veterans Day page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the IHOP military discount?
There is no published corporate discount percentage. The Veterans Day offer is a **free** item (Red, White & Blueberry Pancakes or a pancake combo at select locations). Everyday discount amounts cited on coupon and third-party sites (e.g., 10–20%) are **not confirmed by any IHOP primary source** and would only apply at participating franchises' discretion. (IHOP Veterans Day page; IHOP Products/Values FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
IHOP's Veterans Day offer is stated for **veterans and active-duty military**. Eligibility for spouses, dependents, retirees, or Reserve/Guard is **not stated in IHOP's official source** — ask your local restaurant. (IHOP Veterans Day page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How do I verify my status?
In person at the restaurant. You show proof of service to staff (e.g., Uniformed Services ID, DD-214, LES, veterans organization card, dog tags, or a photo of yourself in uniform). There is no online verification step. (IHOP news release, ihop.com/news, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does IHOP use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. IHOP does not use any third-party online military verification provider. Verification is in-restaurant only. (IHOP Veterans Day page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
The Veterans Day free meal is **dine-in only** at participating restaurants. Any everyday discount, if a location offers one, is also in store only. Neither is available online or for delivery. (IHOP Veterans Day page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### What is excluded?
The Veterans Day offer excludes online/app/delivery orders, applies only at participating U.S. locations, is limited to one per qualified guest, and requires valid proof of service. Stacking with other offers is not stated. (IHOP Veterans Day page; IHOP news release, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
IHOP does not state whether the Veterans Day free meal can be combined with coupons, rewards, or other offers — treat it as not combinable unless your restaurant confirms otherwise. (Not stated in official source.)

#### Does IHOP offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
IHOP does not publish any corporate first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount. Any such offer would be at an individual restaurant's discretion. (Not stated in official source; IHOP Products/Values FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### When is the IHOP Veterans Day free meal?
On Veterans Day, November 11, historically 7 a.m.–7 p.m. local time at participating U.S. locations. Veterans Day 2025 has passed; the offer has recurred annually, but confirm 2026 details closer to the date. (IHOP Veterans Day page; IHOP news release, accessed June 23, 2026.)
