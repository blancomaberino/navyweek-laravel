# Champs Sports — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **$150 sneaker/apparel cart** at champssports.com, comparing the 10% military discount against sales, codes, and the Foot Locker family of brands.

## Headline finding

**Champs Sports has a genuine, official 10% military discount — verified through SheerID online, or a military ID in store.** It covers active-duty, veterans, National Guard, reservists, and **registered dependents of active-duty or retiree service members**. Two limits matter: **"some product exclusions apply"** (marquee sneaker launches and select brands are routinely excluded) and a hard cap of **four SheerID verifications per rolling 30 days with a $100 maximum discount per verification**. Champs is a **Foot Locker, Inc.** banner, so the identical 10% SheerID program runs at Foot Locker and Kids Foot Locker — same discount, same verification, same exclusions. The maximum-savings catch: the 10% **can't be combined with other offers**, so during Champs' frequent 20–40% sales and public code events, the **public sale beats the military discount outright** — and the military 10% won't apply to excluded launch products at all. Note also that **Champs does not use ID.me** (ID.me itself says it isn't aware Champs offers a discount). Archetype: **athletic-apparel retailer with a modest, capped, exclusion-heavy military % that a good sale usually beats.**

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **10% does not combine with other offers** — you use the military discount *or* a sale code/promo, whichever is deeper. It also doesn't apply to previous purchases.
2. **$100 maximum discount per verification; 4 verifications per rolling 30 days** — a hard ceiling on how much a big cart can save (a $1,000 cart still only nets $100 off, not $100+).
3. **"Some product exclusions apply"** — expect marquee launches and select premium brands to be excluded; the discount silently won't apply to those items.
4. **Online = SheerID; in-store = military ID** — separate mechanics, same 10%.
5. **No ID.me / no cashback-stack claim** — ID.me doesn't carry Champs, and identity-gated SheerID checkouts typically break cashback-portal tracking, so don't promise a portal stack.
