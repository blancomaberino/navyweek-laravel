# Frontier Airlines Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Frontier Airlines states plainly on its own help pages that it **does not offer a military discount** on airfare. What it does offer active-duty service members is a **baggage-fee waiver**: with a valid, unexpired **Common Access Card (CAC)** or an **Armed Forces of the United States Identification Card**, an active member — and the spouse and children traveling with them on the same itinerary — get **one free personal item, one free carry-on, and two free checked bags**, with oversize/overweight fees waived on those two checked bags, on all fare types. This is verified on Frontier's official Knowledge Base (last modified Feb–Mar 2026).

The single most important caveats: (1) the waiver is **active-duty only** as documented — Frontier's own definition of eligible "service members" is "current members of the military who are serving or may be called to serve," which explicitly includes National Guard and Reserves but does **not** list separated veterans or retirees; (2) it is verified **at the airport check-in counter or gate**, not online — there is no promo code and no ID.me/SheerID/GovX path. Because Frontier is an ultra-low-cost carrier that unbundles bags, the waiver has real dollar value (two checked bags can run **$40–$99+ each** depending on route and when purchased). Separately, Frontier has historically run a **Veterans Day promo (code VETS, 50% off base fares)** open to active and retired military and their loved ones — a limited-time offer, labeled historical below, not an everyday discount.

> Accuracy note: Coupon/aggregator pages describe a "Frontier military discount." Frontier's own page title is literally "Frontier Military Discount" but the body says **"Frontier Airlines does not offer military discounts"** — the benefit is a baggage waiver, not a fare discount. Any specific "% off fares" as an everyday benefit is **not** on Frontier's pages. The only fare percentage Frontier has published is the **historical** Veterans Day VETS code (50% off base fare), which had specific dates, blackout dates, and route exclusions.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Space Force, Coast Guard) — Eligible for the bag waiver.
- **National Guard** — Eligible; Frontier's definition explicitly includes the National Guard.
- **U.S. Military Reserves** — Eligible; explicitly included.
- **Academy cadets** (Air Force Academy, West Point, Naval, Coast Guard, Merchant Marine) — Eligible; explicitly included.
- **PHS Commissioned Corps / Ready Reserve Corps; NOAA Officer Corps** — Eligible; explicitly included.
- **Military spouses and children** — Eligible for the waiver **when traveling with** the active member on the same itinerary.
- **Veterans (separated, not retired)** — **Not listed** for the everyday bag waiver (they were eligible for the historical Veterans Day fare promo). Do not promise everyday eligibility.
- **Retirees** — **Not listed** for the everyday bag waiver on Frontier's page (eligible for the historical VETS fare promo). Flag.
- **First responders / nurses / teachers / students** — Not covered.

### How To Redeem (Bag Waiver)
1. Book your Frontier flight as usual (online or app) — no military code exists, and you do not pre-add the free bags online.
2. At the airport, go to the **check-in counter** (or the **gate**).
3. Present your valid/unexpired **CAC** (uniformed-services or academy affiliation) or **Armed Forces of the United States ID card** (active duty, reserve, guard, or academy affiliation).
4. The agent adds your free personal item, carry-on, and two checked bags — with no Agent Assistance Fee. Accompanying spouse/children on the same itinerary are covered too.
5. Bags must still meet standard size/weight acceptance limits.

### How Verification Works
Frontier verifies military status **in person only** — there's no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute path on Frontier's own pages, and no online promo code for the everyday benefit. You show a physical, unexpired **CAC** or **Armed Forces of the United States ID card** to a Frontier agent at the check-in counter or gate. Because the benefit is an eligibility-based exception, if you can't show a qualifying ID, standard bag fees apply.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **No everyday fare discount** — full stop. The benefit is a baggage waiver only.
- Applies to the active member **plus accompanying spouse/children on the same itinerary**; the member must be present and traveling.
- **Separated veterans and retirees are not listed** as eligible for the everyday bag waiver (they were named in the historical Veterans Day fare promo).
- Bags must comply with standard **size and weight** acceptance limits; the two-checked-bag waiver covers oversize/overweight fees only for those two bags.
- The historical **VETS 50%-off promo** carried a booking window, travel window, blackout dates (e.g., Nov 26–27 & 30, Dec 1–2, 2024), route exclusions, round-trip and 7-day advance-purchase requirements, and applied to **base fare only** (not taxes/fees). Treat any current "50% off" claim as unverified unless Frontier re-publishes it.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **The bag waiver is the win.** With Frontier bags running roughly **$40–$99+ each** (route/timing dependent) and no free checked bag on basic fares, an active member checking two bags can save well over **$100 round trip** — usually more than a small fare percentage.
- **Watch for the Veterans Day VETS promo** each fall — historically 50% off base fares for active and retired military and loved ones. It's limited-time; read the terms each year.
- **Frontier Airlines World Mastercard** gives two free checked bags to the cardholder and companions on the same reservation (not a military benefit, but stackable everyday value).
- **Bundles (The PERKS / The WORKS)** or **Discount Den** membership can beat paying à la carte for non-eligible travelers in your party.
- **Compare active-duty bag policies** across carriers — Delta, United, American, Southwest and Alaska publish their own; on short trips a legacy carrier's bundled fare may total less than a Frontier basic fare plus paid extras for family members not covered by the waiver.

### FAQ

#### Does Frontier offer a military discount?
Not on airfare. Frontier's own help page states it "does not offer military discounts." Active-duty members get a **baggage-fee waiver** instead: one personal item, one carry-on, and two checked bags free (oversize/overweight waived on the two checked bags), on all fare types.

#### How much does the Frontier military bag waiver save me?
It waives fees on two checked bags plus a carry-on. Frontier doesn't publish one flat bag price — it varies by route and when you buy, roughly **$40–$99+ per bag** — so two checked bags each way can be worth **$100+** round trip.

#### Do veterans or retirees get the Frontier bag waiver?
Frontier's page lists **active** members (including National Guard and Reserves) and academy cadets, plus their accompanying spouse/children. **Separated veterans and retirees are not listed** for the everyday bag waiver. They were eligible for Frontier's historical Veterans Day fare promo.

#### How do I verify my military status with Frontier?
Show a valid, unexpired **Common Access Card (CAC)** or **Armed Forces of the United States ID card** at the **airport check-in counter or gate**. There's no ID.me step and no online code.

#### Does Frontier use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No — Frontier verifies in person at the airport. There is no third-party verification path on Frontier's own military pages.

#### Can my family use the bag waiver?
Yes — your **spouse and children** are covered when they travel with you on the same itinerary and you (the active member) are present.

#### Is there a Frontier military fare discount code?
Not as an everyday benefit. Frontier has run a **limited-time** Veterans Day code (VETS, 50% off base fares) in the past. It had specific dates, blackout dates, and route exclusions — watch for it each November rather than assuming it's always live.

#### Does Frontier offer a first responder, nurse, or teacher discount?
Not listed on Frontier's pages.
