# Top of the Rock — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** **two adult general-admission tickets** to the Top of the Rock observation deck (adult from **$49** each = **$98** online, before tax/fees).

## Headline finding

**Top of the Rock (Rockefeller Center) publishes no military discount for online tickets.** The official buy-tickets flow shows standard timed-admission pricing — **Adult from $49, Child (6–12) from $43, Senior (62+) from $47** — with no military rate, no ID.me/GovX gate, and no "military" option on the site. The only official discounts listed are **group sales (10+)**, a **weekday kids' 50%-off** promo, and **bundle passes** (All-In Pass, Express Pass, and third-party city passes). A **box-office military rate has been widely reported by military-discount aggregators** (roughly **$17–$20 + tax per ticket**, up to ~5 tickets, for active-duty/veteran/retired/Guard/Reserve/dependents with valid military ID) — but this is **not confirmed on any Rockefeller Center primary page**, and the most-cited aggregator (JustForMilitary) is now a dead/parked domain. Treat the in-person rate as **unverified, call-ahead-only.** Archetype: **NYC attraction with no published online military discount — save via in-person inquiry, bundle passes, and off-peak/timed pricing.**

### FAQ

#### Does Top of the Rock offer a military discount?
Not online. The official site publishes standard pricing (adult from $49) with no military rate. A **box-office military rate (~$17–$20 + tax) is widely reported** but unconfirmed by Rockefeller Center — verify in person.

#### How much is the Top of the Rock military discount?
Aggregators report **about $17–$20 + tax** per ticket at the box office, up to ~5 tickets — but figures conflict and it's not officially published. Call ahead.

#### Do veterans and dependents qualify?
The reported box-office rate is said to cover active-duty, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, and dependents with valid military ID — confirm eligibility at the window.

#### Can I get the military discount online?
No. There's no ID.me, GovX, or military option in the online booking flow.

#### Does Top of the Rock use ID.me or GovX?
No. Neither is used for Top of the Rock tickets.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit?
If the box-office military rate is honored, that's the cheapest single-visit option — **call to confirm first**. If you're touring multiple NYC attractions, a **city pass** usually beats single tickets. Otherwise book **off-peak** online.

#### Is there a discount for kids or groups?
Yes — a seasonal **weekday kids 50%** promo (with a paired adult ticket) and **group sales for 10+**. Neither is military-specific.
