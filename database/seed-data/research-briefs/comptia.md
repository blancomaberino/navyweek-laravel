# CompTIA — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) exam voucher — **$439** official CompTIA retail price (as of June 1, 2026).

## Headline finding

**CompTIA does not publish a consumer-facing military or veteran percentage discount.** Its own "how to save" documentation lists five paths — bundles, a student discount (via SheerID), 529 plans, employer reimbursement, and flash sales — and names no military/veteran rate. What CompTIA *does* offer service members is (a) a **government/DoD discount** for people buying through a linked government-agency or contractor account (quote-based, not self-serve), and (b) military **career + funding resources** that point you to programs that pay for the exam. The single best path for almost every eligible service member is **not a discount at all — it's funding**: active-duty **Army/Air Force Credentialing Assistance or Navy COOL** typically covers the exam in full ($0 out of pocket); veterans use **GI Bill / VET TEC**; spouses use **MyCAA**. If you must pay cash and don't qualify for funding, the reliable universal play is an **authorized-reseller voucher (~10–15% off)** — or, if you're currently enrolled in school (which many veterans are, on the GI Bill), the **SheerID student discount (~40% off vouchers)**. Archetype: **certification/credential brand with no retail military discount but heavy third-party funding** — the money comes from DoD/VA programs and authorized resellers, not from a CompTIA coupon.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The SheerID student discount does not stack with anything.** CompTIA help: "No additional discounts can be applied in addition to your verified SheerID discount." So no bundle code, promo code, or reseller price on top of student pricing.
2. **One funding source per voucher.** If CA/COOL/GI Bill/VET TEC/MyCAA pays for the exam, you don't also apply a personal student or reseller discount — the program purchases/reimburses the voucher directly, so a self-serve discount is moot (and would just reduce the amount the program pays, not your out-of-pocket, which is already $0).
3. **Reseller vouchers are already discounted** and are bought outside the CompTIA Store — you can't add a CompTIA store promo on top.
4. **Bundle 10%** applies only inside the CompTIA Store to the à-la-carte total; whether bundle + student stack is moot given rule #1 (student pricing blocks additional discounts). Flag: exact bundle mechanics vary by product.
5. **Government/DoD account pricing** is a separate purchasing channel (quote/PO based); it is not combined with consumer discounts.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty (and typically drilling reserve/Guard)** — funded via Army/Air Force Credentialing Assistance or Navy COOL (eligible roles). Not a discount; the program pays the exam.
- **Veterans** — GI Bill / VET TEC can cover or reimburse approved CompTIA exams. No CompTIA "veteran" discount.
- **Military spouses** — MyCAA up to $4,000 through an approved school. No CompTIA spouse discount.
- **Government/DoD employees & contractors** — account-linked government pricing (quote/PO based).
- **Enrolled students (incl. veterans in school on the GI Bill)** — ~40% off vouchers via SheerID, as a *student*, not as military.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Funding (active duty / veteran / spouse) — the best path:**
1. Confirm CompTIA cert eligibility in your service's system: Army/AF Credentialing Assistance portal (ArmyIgnitED), Navy COOL, or VA GI Bill/VET TEC / MyCAA.
2. Submit the funding/voucher request **before** paying (some programs, e.g. Navy COOL, may require pre-approval or reimburse rather than prepay).
3. Receive the funded voucher (or pay and get reimbursed) and schedule the exam at Pearson VUE.

**Student discount (CompTIA Store):**
1. Create/log in to a CompTIA account and add the voucher to your cart.
2. At checkout, click "Student? See if you qualify for a discount."
3. Verify enrollment through SheerID (school-issued doc: dated student ID, schedule, transcript, tuition/registration receipt, or enrollment letter).
4. Approved pricing applies automatically. No other discount can be added.

**Authorized-reseller voucher (cash, no funding):**
1. Buy from a CompTIA-authorized reseller (Dion Training, Professor Messer, Total Seminars, GetCertified4Less).
2. Redeem the voucher code (valid ~12 months) when scheduling at Pearson VUE.

### How Verification Works
CompTIA uses **SheerID** to verify **student** status (not military status, and not ID.me). You submit a school-issued document showing your name, the school, and a date in the current term. Government/DoD pricing is not self-verified — your CompTIA account must be linked to the government entity, and quotes/forms go through a CompTIA Help Request. Funding eligibility (CA/COOL/GI Bill/VET TEC/MyCAA) is determined by your service branch or the VA, not by CompTIA.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No stacking on the SheerID student discount (CompTIA states this explicitly).
- Student discount requires **active enrollment** at a degree-granting institution — prior/other military status alone does not qualify.
- Funding caps and approval rules vary by branch (Army/AF CA, Navy COOL) and by benefit (GI Bill/VET TEC/MyCAA); some reimburse after the fact rather than prepay. Confirm your cap and process before paying.
- CEU renewal fees are separate from exam vouchers; a voucher discount does not cover CEU renewal.
- Prices rose June 1, 2026 (Security+ $425 → $439); verify current pricing at purchase.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Use funding first** — if you're active duty, a veteran with benefits, or an eligible spouse, the exam is usually $0. A discount is a worse deal than free.
- **Authorized resellers** for ~10–15% off when paying cash.
- **Student (SheerID) rate** ~40% off if you're enrolled — including veterans in school.
- **Bundle** (voucher + CertMaster) for 10% off the combined total *if you need the training anyway*.
- **Watch flash sales** on the CompTIA Store for occasional public promo pricing.
- **Buy before announced price increases** (resellers sometimes sell pre-increase stock cheaper).

### FAQ
#### Does CompTIA offer a military discount?
No. CompTIA does not publish a consumer military or veteran discount on exam vouchers. It offers government/DoD account pricing and a student discount, and it points military members to funding programs (CA/COOL, GI Bill, VET TEC, MyCAA) that typically pay for the exam.

#### How much is the CompTIA military discount?
There is no set military percentage. Claims of "~50% off for military via ID.me" are from coupon sites and are not confirmed by CompTIA. The ~40%-off figure is the **student** discount (SheerID), and it requires current enrollment, not military status.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
Not for a CompTIA "veteran" or "spouse" discount — those don't exist. Veterans can use the GI Bill/VET TEC to fund the exam; spouses can use MyCAA; and anyone currently enrolled in school (including veterans on the GI Bill) can use the student discount.

#### How do I verify my status?
For the student discount, CompTIA verifies enrollment through **SheerID** with a school document. Funding eligibility is verified by your branch or the VA, not CompTIA. There is no CompTIA military verification via ID.me.

#### Does CompTIA use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
CompTIA uses **SheerID** — for **student** verification only. We found no CompTIA program using ID.me, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass for a military discount.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
CompTIA sells vouchers online (CompTIA Store) and through authorized online resellers; exams are taken at Pearson VUE test centers or online-proctored. There's no in-store retail channel.

#### What is excluded?
The student discount can't be combined with any other discount, requires active enrollment, and doesn't cover CEU renewal fees. Funding caps and rules vary by program.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — CompTIA explicitly states no additional discounts stack on the verified SheerID student price, and funding programs are one-per-voucher.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from CompTIA?
Funding: **Credentialing Assistance/Navy COOL (active duty), GI Bill/VET TEC (veterans), or MyCAA (spouses)** — usually $0. If you can't use funding: an **authorized-reseller voucher** (~10–15% off) or the **student rate** (~40% off) if you're enrolled.

#### Does CompTIA offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Government/DoD (account-based) and **student** (SheerID) discounts exist. We found no dedicated first-responder, teacher, or nurse discount.

#### Can I get a CompTIA exam paid for through the military?
Yes — that's the main event. Active-duty Army/Air Force **Credentialing Assistance** and **Navy COOL**, veteran **GI Bill/VET TEC**, and spouse **MyCAA** can fund CompTIA exams, often fully. Rules, caps, and prepay-vs-reimburse vary by program; get approval before you pay.
