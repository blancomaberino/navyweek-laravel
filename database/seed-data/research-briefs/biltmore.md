# Biltmore Estate — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult daytime house-admission ticket to Biltmore (~$85 advance, before tax/fees; exact price varies by date).

## Headline finding

Biltmore Estate (Asheville, NC) offers an **official U.S. military discount, currently $25 off per ticket on regular adult daytime admission** — an increase Biltmore made "in honor of America's 250th anniversary." It's valid for **active military, veterans, and their guests**, redeemed by showing a **Military ID in person** at the Reception & Ticketing Sales Center, or by purchasing in advance by phone at **800-411-3812**. On top of admission, there's a **10% military discount at estate restaurants and shops (wine excluded)**. For a family visit, the winning play is to buy the $25-off adult tickets in advance by phone (and check whether the elevated $25 rate is time-limited — it's framed as running through Labor Day).

> **Time-sensitive:** The **$25** figure is an elevated, America's-250th promotion described as extended **through Labor Day**. Historically Biltmore's standing military discount was smaller (military.com references a **$10** off daytime admission). After the promo window, the discount may revert — re-verify the current dollar amount before build and before each visit.
