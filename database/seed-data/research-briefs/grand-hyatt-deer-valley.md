# Grand Hyatt Deer Valley — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **1-night winter stay at Grand Hyatt Deer Valley + one adult 1-day lift ticket**. Hotel rates float wildly by date, so percentages are the durable output.

> **Scope note:** This brief covers the **Grand Hyatt Deer Valley hotel** and its property-specific **"Salute to Service"** program. ## Headline finding

**Yes — Grand Hyatt Deer Valley runs a genuine, property-specific military program called "Salute to Service," and it's unusually generous.** Eligible service members book **discounted lodging through a dedicated Salute to Service online portal** and also get **spa and food-and-beverage discounts** (excluding alcohol). The headline perk is for lodging guests: **service members staying at the property can buy Deer Valley lift tickets at up to 75% off the window rate** — far deeper than Deer Valley's own ~25% resort military rate. Eligibility for the Salute to Service portal is broad: **Active Duty, Reserve or National Guard, Retired Military, 100% Disabled Veterans, and Actively Serving Department of Defense Civilians**, for eligible stays from Nov 20, 2024 onward. On top of that, because this is a Hyatt hotel, the **chain-wide Veteran & Military Rate (code `MILVET`, 10–15% off the standard rate)** is also bookable here and still earns World of Hyatt points and elite-night credit. Archetype: **luxury ski hotel with a deep property-run military program (Archetype I travel/loyalty) layered over a resort ticket partnership** — the play is *book Salute to Service lodging, then buy tickets at the property for up to 75% off; compare the portal rate against the `MILVET` rate and book the lower.*

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + partner terms)

3. **The up-to-75%-off ticket benefit requires a Grand Hyatt lodging stay** — it's tied to being a guest, not a standalone ticket deal.

6. **`MILVET` is verified at check-in** (Military/Veteran ID or DD-214), not via ID.me/SheerID; the Salute to Service portal has its own eligibility gate.

### How To Redeem — Salute to Service (lodging + tickets)
1. Go to the **Salute to Service portal** (hyatt.com/en-US/info/deer-valley-salute-to-service) or email **SaluteToService@hyatt.com**.

### How To Redeem — Hyatt `MILVET` Rate (alternative)
1. On **hyatt.com**, enter Grand Hyatt Deer Valley and your dates.
2. Open **Special Rates** and enter Special Offer Code **`MILVET`** (10–15% off the standard rate; up to 2 rooms).
3. At check-in, present a **Military/Veteran ID or DD-214**. The rate is refundable and earns World of Hyatt points + elite-night credit.

### How Verification Works

Because the two rates can't combine, verify under whichever path gives the lower total and matches your eligibility.

### FAQ

#### Does Grand Hyatt Deer Valley offer a military discount?
Yes — the property's **Salute to Service** program gives eligible service members discounted lodging, spa and F&B discounts (no alcohol), and — for lodging guests — Deer Valley lift tickets at **up to 75% off the window rate**. The chain-wide Hyatt `MILVET` rate (10–15% off) is also bookable here.

#### How much is the Grand Hyatt Deer Valley military discount?
Lodging is discounted via the Salute to Service portal (rate varies by date), and lodging guests can buy lift tickets at **up to 75% off the window rate**. Separately, the Hyatt `MILVET` rate is **10–15% off** the standard room rate.

#### Who qualifies for Salute to Service?
Active Duty, Reserve or National Guard, Retired Military, 100% Disabled Veterans, and Actively Serving DoD Civilians, for eligible stays from Nov 20, 2024 onward.

#### How do I book — does Grand Hyatt use ID.me or SheerID?
Salute to Service uses its own **portal** (hyatt.com/en-US/info/deer-valley-salute-to-service; email SaluteToService@hyatt.com). The `MILVET` rate is booked on hyatt.com and verified with a **Military/Veteran ID or DD-214 at check-in** — not ID.me or SheerID.

#### Is the 75% off on the room or the tickets?
On **lift tickets** for lodging guests — up to 75% off the window rate. It is **not** a 75% room discount, and it's separate from Deer Valley Resort's own ~25% ticket rate.

#### Can I combine Salute to Service with the `MILVET` rate?
No — Hyatt's discounted rates are mutually exclusive. Compare the Salute to Service portal rate against `MILVET` (and Member/Advance) and book the lowest. Your World of Hyatt points and status still apply.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to ski Deer Valley from this hotel?
Book **Salute to Service** lodging and buy lift tickets at the property (up to 75% off the window rate) — the deepest total-trip value — after rate-shopping the room against `MILVET`.

#### Is this different from Deer Valley Resort's military discount?
Yes. Grand Hyatt's Salute to Service is a hotel program (lodging + up-to-75%-off tickets for guests). Deer Valley Resort separately offers ~25% off lift tickets/passes to eligible military even without a hotel stay.
