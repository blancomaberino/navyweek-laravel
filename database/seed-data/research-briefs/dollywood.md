# Dollywood Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Dollywood (theme park, Pigeon Forge, TN) offers a verified military discount on day tickets, multi-day tickets, and Season Passes for both Dollywood and Dollywood's Splash Country, plus a separate **5% discount on resort/cabin stays**. Eligibility covers U.S. active or retired military, veterans, military reservists, and their spouses and dependents with valid military IDs. Online purchases require identity verification through **ID.me**; in-person purchases are checked by a host at any Dollywood or Splash Country ticket window. Dollywood publishes the discount as **fixed promotional ticket/pass prices, not a stated percentage off** — it does not publish a "% off" figure, and aggregator claims of a flat percentage (5%, 26%, 30% have all appeared) are not supported by the official source. The single most important caveat: the military discount on **stays** must be booked by phone (1-800-Dollywood for the resort; 1-866-857-2123 for cabins) in the military member's name, and the ID is presented at check-in. The military program is sponsored by Humana.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Confirm you are eligible (U.S. active/retired military, veteran, reservist, spouse, or dependent with a valid military/dependent ID).
2. Verify your identity through the secure ID.me system when prompted on Dollywood.com.
3. Sign in to or create your My Dollywood online account and purchase your military ticket or Season Pass at the special price.
4. For day tickets: you receive a confirmation email with barcoded tickets you can scan at the front gate — no ticket-window stop needed. For Season Passes: bring your confirmation email to a Dollywood Front Gate ticket window to get your photo taken and receive your physical pass card.
5. Enjoy your visit.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Confirm you qualify and bring your valid military ID (and dependent ID if applicable).
2. Go to any ticket window at Dollywood or Dollywood's Splash Country.
3. A host checks your military ID(s) at the window.
4. Purchase your military-rate day ticket(s) or Season Pass on the spot. (Buying in advance is recommended because park capacity is limited and a purchased ticket serves as your reservation.)

For a stay: call 1-800-Dollywood for Dollywood's DreamMore Resort and Spa, or 1-866-857-2123 for the Smoky Mountain Cabins, to book the 5% military rate. The reservation must be in the military member's name, and the military ID is presented at check-in. The lodging discount is subject to availability.

### How Verification Works
Online, Dollywood uses **ID.me** to confirm military status before you can buy discounted tickets or passes; you must also sign in to or create a My Dollywood account to complete the purchase. In person, a Dollywood/Splash Country ticket-window host checks your physical military ID instead of using ID.me. For stays, no ID.me step applies — you present your military ID at check-in. Dollywood does not state an ID.me re-verification frequency on its military page; ID.me's own policy governs how often you re-verify.

### FAQ

#### Does Dollywood offer a military discount?
Yes. Dollywood offers special military pricing on day tickets, multi-day tickets, and Season Passes for both Dollywood and Dollywood's Splash Country, plus a 5% discount on resort and cabin stays. The program is sponsored by Humana.

#### How much is the Dollywood military discount?
Dollywood publishes the discount as **fixed military prices** rather than a percentage — for example, 2026 military day tickets started around $66.49 (+ tax) for a 1-day theme park ticket. Stays get a flat **5% off**. Dollywood does not publish a single "% off" figure for tickets, so treat any specific percentage you see on coupon sites with caution and check the official page for current prices.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. The discount covers U.S. active or retired military, veterans, and military reservists, plus their spouses and dependents, with valid military/dependent IDs.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Online, you verify through ID.me before purchasing. In person, a host at any Dollywood or Splash Country ticket window checks your physical military ID. For a stay, you present your military ID at check-in.

#### Does Dollywood use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Dollywood uses **ID.me** for online military verification. Dollywood tickets also appear on **GovX**, but the official Dollywood checkout path uses ID.me. There is no indication Dollywood uses SheerID for this offer.

#### Can I use the discount in person at the park?
Yes. You can buy military-rate day tickets or Season Passes at any Dollywood or Splash Country ticket window, where a host checks your military ID. Buying in advance is recommended because park capacity is limited and a purchased ticket counts as your reservation.

#### How do I get the military discount on a hotel or cabin stay?
Call 1-800-Dollywood for Dollywood's DreamMore Resort and Spa or 1-866-857-2123 for the Smoky Mountain Cabins to book the 5% military rate. The reservation must be in the military member's name, the offer is subject to availability, and you present the military ID at check-in.

#### What is excluded or limited?
The 5% stay discount is subject to availability and must be booked in the military member's name. Diamond Season Passes are limited. Dollywood does not state whether the military rate combines with other promotions or whether there are purchase caps.

#### Does Dollywood offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
Those groups are not part of the military offer, but Dollywood runs a separate "Public Employee Appreciation Days" program that has included certain first responders and educators. Verify current eligibility and dates on Dollywood's special-offers pages.
