# SilencerCo — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **SilencerCo Omega 36M** modular multi-caliber suppressor (SilencerCo MSRP **$1,187**; typical dealer/street price **~$993** at the exclusive SPEQ distributor Silencer Shop).

## Headline finding

SilencerCo runs a **genuine, first-party military/LE discount** — the **SPEQ program** ("Service Proven Equipment") — but it is **not a percentage you can look up**. SPEQ pricing is **confidential and flat-rate**, set by SilencerCo and standardized across authorized dealers; you only see your price after your credentials are verified (by email, phone, or a secure purchase link). It is **not an ID.me / SheerID / GovX self-serve checkout** — it is a **brand-run credential verification** you complete through a participating SPEQ dealer or the SilencerCo SPEQ portal (speq.silencerco.com). Eligibility is broad: active/reserve/retired military and **honorably-discharged veterans (DD-214)**, law enforcement, federal flight deck officers, fire/EMS, and armed private security. **Archetype: E — brand-run, multi-tier identity discount with unpublished/confidential pricing (like Aimpoint), with a heavy NFA regulatory overlay (a Type-L path) that governs redemption.**

The regulatory context is the whole story in 2026, and it changes the math: a suppressor is an **NFA item**. As of **January 1, 2026** the **federal $200 tax stamp dropped to $0** (H.R.1, signed July 2025) — but you still file an **ATF Form 4**, pass a background check, get fingerprinted, transfer through an **FFL/SOT dealer**, and **wait for ATF approval** before you can take possession. So no SilencerCo "military discount" is ever an instant online cart-to-doorstep transaction, and channels like GovX marketplace, AAFES/NEX exchanges, and cashback portals **do not sell suppressors to consumers** — they're off the table here. **Verdict: if you qualify, SPEQ is the best price on a new SilencerCo can and is worth requesting; everyone (verified or not) should also weigh the current public promo — a free $250 SilencerCo webstore credit on a qualifying suppressor bought and Form-4'd by Aug 15, 2026 — and the fact that the $200 stamp is now free.** Do **not** trust the aggregator claim of a flat "**5% military discount**" — that number is fabricated; SilencerCo publishes no percentage.

### FAQ
#### Does SilencerCo offer a military discount?
Yes — the **SPEQ program** ("Service Proven Equipment") is SilencerCo's first-party discount for active/reserve/retired military, honorably-discharged veterans (DD-214), law enforcement, federal flight deck officers, fire/EMS, and armed private security.

#### How much is the SilencerCo military discount?
**SilencerCo does not publish the amount.** SPEQ pricing is confidential and flat-rate, set by SilencerCo and standardized across dealers; you see your price only after your credentials are verified. Any "5% off" you see on a coupon site is fabricated. (Shoppers report substantial savings — e.g. forum reports of an Omega around $782 vs a ~$993 street price — but that's unverified signal, not a published figure.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Veterans** with a DD-214 showing an honorable discharge qualify, as do disabled veterans with a medically-retired ID. **Spouses and dependents are not listed** as eligible — SPEQ is service-member/professional-based.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through a **SilencerCo SPEQ dealer** or the SPEQ portal: create an account and submit proof of status (ID, DD-214, or agency credentials). Federal employees can verify from a **.gov/.mil** email or agency letterhead with details redacted. A dealer reviews it before releasing your price.

#### Does SilencerCo use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** SilencerCo runs **its own SPEQ credential verification** through dealers/the SPEQ portal. Claims that it "partners with GovX ID or ID.me" are unverified boilerplate — and GovX does not sell NFA suppressors to consumers.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Neither is an instant checkout. You order **through a SPEQ dealer** (in person or via their site) after verification, then complete the ATF Form 4 transfer. A suppressor is an NFA item — it ships to a licensed dealer, not your door, and only after ATF approval.

#### What is excluded?
There's no published percentage, no stacking (no codes/cashback on top of SPEQ), and it isn't a self-serve online discount. Unarmed private security doesn't qualify. The NFA process (Form 4, fingerprints, wait) applies no matter what.

#### Can I combine SPEQ with the current $250 promo or a code?
**Not confirmed** — SPEQ is a confidential pre-set price and generally doesn't stack. If you want the public **$250 webstore-credit** promo (qualifying suppressor bought + Form 4 by Aug 15, 2026), ask your dealer whether it can apply to a SPEQ order or only to a standard-price order, then compare the two totals.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from SilencerCo?
If you qualify, **request SPEQ pricing** — it's the lowest new-can price. Then check whether the current **$250 webstore-credit** promo stacks; if not, compare SPEQ vs (street price − $250). Either way, the **$200 federal tax stamp is now $0** in 2026, which is the single biggest cost change for every buyer.

#### Does SilencerCo offer a first responder or law enforcement discount?
Yes — the **same SPEQ program** covers law enforcement (active/retired), federal flight deck officers, fire rescue/EMT, and armed private security.

#### Do I still pay the $200 NFA tax stamp?
**No** — the federal suppressor tax stamp dropped to **$0 on January 1, 2026** (H.R.1). You still file a **Form 4**, get fingerprinted, pass a background check, transfer through a dealer, and wait for ATF approval before taking possession.
