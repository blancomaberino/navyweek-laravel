# Altar'd State — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $150 women's apparel order (e.g., a dress + top, full-price) on altardstate.com or in-store.

## Headline finding

Altar'd State runs a **standing 15% military discount** — not a seasonal promo — on full-price purchases, redeemable **in-store by showing a military ID** and **online via ID.me verification**. Multiple sources (the brand's own military help page, mall store directories reproducing the brand's submitted copy, and military-discount trackers) consistently report **15%**, active-duty + veterans + military family. The discount is a **persistent** benefit once verified, which makes it the default best path for a returning shopper buying at full price. But because Altar'd State is a **markdown-driven boutique apparel** brand, a deep seasonal **sale or clearance (frequently 40–60% off)** routinely beats the flat 15%, and the military % almost certainly does **not** stack on already-reduced items (standard apparel-brand terms, and Altar'd State validates only one promotional benefit per order). No GovX marketplace, no military-exchange channel, and no pro-deal program apply to this brand. **Note:** the brand's own military page (altardstate.com/help/?page=military-discount-page) is bot-blocked (HTTP 403) and could not be read directly this session — the 15% figure is corroborated across several secondary sources but should be re-verified against the live brand page before publish.

## Stacking Rules (verified where possible, flagged where not)
1. **Military 15% does not stack with promo codes.** Sources and standard apparel terms indicate Altar'd State validates **one promotional benefit per order**. *(Not confirmed on the brand's own terms page this session — flagged.)*
2. **Military 15% almost certainly excludes sale/clearance items.** Apparel brands overwhelmingly restrict the military % to full-price merchandise; treat sale items as ineligible unless the live brand page says otherwise. *(Flagged — verify on brand page.)*
3. **Cashback + ID.me is fragile.** Identity-gated checkouts (ID.me "verify in cart") frequently break cashback-portal tracking, and portals commonly void cashback on coupon/verified orders. Do **not** promise "Rakuten + military 15% stacks." A safer stack is **cashback on a plain full-price order** without invoking ID.me.
4. **Loyalty (Stand Out Rewards) is separate.** Birthday reward / sign-up perks are their own promotional benefit and, per the one-benefit-per-order pattern, won't combine with the military 15%.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**In store**
1. Bring your items to checkout at any Altar'd State location.
2. Present a valid military ID.
3. The associate applies 15% off eligible (full-price) items.

**Online**
1. Add full-price items to your cart at altardstate.com.
2. At checkout, use the **"Verify with ID.me"** option and sign in / create a free ID.me account.
3. Once verified, the 15% applies to eligible items. *(Confirm provider on live checkout — could not be verified on the brand page this session.)*

### How Verification Works
Online verification is reported to run through **ID.me** — a free account that confirms your military status against authoritative records, then unlocks the discount at checkout. In-store, a physical military ID is sufficient. Do not assume verification is permanent; ID.me may require periodic re-authentication, and the brand can change providers.

### FAQ
#### Does Altar'd State offer a military discount?
Yes. Altar'd State offers a standing **15% military discount** on full-price purchases, in-store and online.

#### How much is the Altar'd State military discount?
**15% off.** Reports of "10%–25%" from some coupon sites are unreliable; the consistent figure is 15%.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Sources say the discount covers **active-duty, veterans, and military family members**. Reservists/retirees/Guard are likely included but not explicitly listed — confirm at checkout.

#### How do I verify my status?
**In store:** show a valid military ID. **Online:** verify with **ID.me** at checkout (free account).

#### Does Altar'd State use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
Online verification is reported to use **ID.me**. Altar'd State is **not** on GovX, and no exchange/pro-deal channel exists. (Provider should be confirmed on the live checkout.)

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — both. In-store with a military ID; online via ID.me on altardstate.com and the app.

#### What is excluded?
Typically **sale/clearance items** and combination with **promo codes** (one benefit per order). Verify current exclusions on the brand page.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Generally **no**. The military 15% doesn't stack with codes and usually excludes already-reduced items — which is why a deep public sale often beats it.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Altar'd State?
**Full-price item → military 15%.** **On-sale item → take the sale** (it won't stack, and the sale is usually deeper). **Single big item in your birthday month → Stand Out Rewards 50% off one item.**

#### Does Altar'd State offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No dedicated program was found for first responders, teachers, nurses, or students. Use sales and Stand Out Rewards instead.

#### Does Altar'd State run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No documented military-specific holiday boost — the **15% is year-round**. Public seasonal sales run around major holidays and often beat 15%.

#### Can I buy Altar'd State at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
No. Altar'd State isn't carried by military exchanges and has no ExpertVoice/pro-deal store.
