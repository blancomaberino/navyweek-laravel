# Sur La Table — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $300 full-price cookware cart of non-excluded brands (e.g., Sur La Table private-label + mid-tier cookware), bought at a physical Sur La Table store or online.

## Headline finding

Sur La Table **does** offer a real, official **15% military discount** — active duty, retired military, veterans, and their immediate family — but it is **in-store only**, requires a valid military ID at checkout, and cannot be combined with other offers. There is **no online military discount**: ID.me itself states it is "not aware that Sur La Table offers Military discounts," so any coupon site advertising a 20%/50%/$25 "military code" for surlatable.com is fabricated. Best path depends on channel: **in a store on full-price, non-excluded goods, the 15% ID discount wins**; **online, where the identity discount does not exist, you save most by stacking a public sale/promo code with a cashback portal (Rakuten ~2% as of July 13, 2026) and SLT Perks points.** Archetype: **in-store, ID-verified retailer with an online cashback/sale gap** — the official discount is genuine but channel-locked, and clearance/sale pricing frequently beats it.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 15% military discount is in-store only and cannot be combined** with other promotions, coupons, or offers (Sur La Table FAQ). It applies before tax and excludes select brands, gift cards, shipping, and taxes.
2. **Military and culinary-pro discounts don't stack** with each other (each is standalone; take the higher — 15%).
3. **Rakuten cashback (~2%) is an online-channel rebate** paid by the portal, not by Sur La Table. It stacks on top of public sale prices and public promo codes used at surlatable.com — but it **cannot stack with the in-store military discount** (different channel; the discount is in-store, cashback is online). Cashback excludes gift cards, registry items, and gift wrap.
4. **SLT Perks points** accrue on purchases regardless of channel; whether points earn on discounted/ID-discounted transactions is **not confirmed** from a primary source — flag, don't assert.

### FAQ

#### Does Sur La Table offer a military discount?
Yes. Sur La Table extends a **15% discount** to active duty, retired military, veterans, and their immediate family — **in stores only**, with a valid military ID at checkout.

#### How much is the Sur La Table military discount?
**15% off** eligible in-store purchases. It excludes select brands, gift cards, shipping, and taxes, and can't be combined with other offers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — the policy covers active duty, retired military, veterans, and **immediate family members**. Show the qualifying military ID in store.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person: present a valid military ID to the cashier. There's no online verification for Sur La Table's site.

#### Does Sur La Table use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Sur La Table runs **no online military offer** through any verification provider. ID.me explicitly states it is "not aware that Sur La Table offers Military discounts." The 15% is in-store only.

#### Can I use the military discount online?
No. The military discount is **in-store only**. Online, there is no military discount or military code — save by stacking a public sale + cashback + SLT Perks instead.

#### What's excluded from the discount?
Select brands, gift cards, shipping fees, and taxes; it also can't be combined with other promotions.

#### Can I combine the military discount with promo codes or sale items?
No — it's **not combinable** with other offers. Online, cashback (Rakuten) and public promo codes can stack with each other, but not with the in-store military discount.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Sur La Table?
In a store, on full-price non-excluded goods, the **15% military discount** is best. Online (or for excluded/premium brands), stack a **public sale/promo + Rakuten cashback + SLT Perks**, and check **clearance** (often 50–70% off) — these frequently beat 15%.

#### Does Sur La Table offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No dedicated first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount is published. There is a separate **10% culinary-professional** in-store discount for chefs, bakers, caterers, sommeliers, restaurant owners, and culinary students.

#### Can I buy Sur La Table at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
No — Sur La Table is not sold through the military exchanges (AAFES/NEX) or ExpertVoice, and it's not on the GovX marketplace.
