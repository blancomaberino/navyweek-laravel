# Brooks Running Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Brooks Running offers a **25% discount on eligible full-price items** through its **Community Heroes Ambassador Program**, a free membership for verified **military, nurses, and first responders**. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me** (hosted-pages.id.me/offers/brooks); once verified you receive **up to three single-use 25% promo codes per rolling 12-month period**, which you enter at checkout. The discount is **online only at brooksrunning.com** — it cannot be used in store, at the Trailhead, or with retail partners. The single most important caveat: it **excludes sale items, select lifestyle collections, and runDisney® products**, applies to **full-price items only**, and members pay their own shipping. Brooks separately lists a **40% discount for Sports Medicine Professionals** on its ID.me store page.

> Accuracy note: Multiple aggregators claim military **spouses, dependents, and "families"** qualify, that GovX is an official route, and that codes "reset January 1." Brooks' official page does **not** state any of these. Brooks lists only three eligible categories (Military, Nurse, First responder), verifies via **ID.me**, and states codes run on a **rolling 12-month period from the issue date of the first code** — not a calendar reset. Treat spouse/dependent eligibility as **not stated** and flag it.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Brooks' Community Heroes page and start verification (or go directly to **hosted-pages.id.me/offers/brooks**).
2. Sign in to **ID.me** (or create a free account) and verify your status as Military, Nurse, or First responder.
3. Once verified, **copy and save your single-use 25% promo code**.
4. Add eligible full-price items to your cart at **brooksrunning.com**.
5. **Enter your promo code** in your shopping cart or at checkout, then complete your order. (Up to three single-use codes per rolling 12-month period.)

### How To Redeem In Store
- **Not available.** Brooks states the discount "do[es] not apply for use in retail stores, including the Trailhead," and "can't be used in store or with retail partners." The Community Heroes 25% is **online-only at brooksrunning.com**.

### How Verification Works
Brooks uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You sign in or create a free ID.me account and verify your status in one of the eligible categories (Military, Nurse, or First responder). After verification, ID.me issues a **single-use promo code** that you enter at checkout on brooksrunning.com. Members receive **up to three of these codes per rolling 12-month period**, starting from the issue date of the first code. For problems with verification, Brooks directs shoppers to **ID.me's help center** (help.id.me).

### FAQ

#### Does Brooks Running offer a military discount?
Yes. Through the Community Heroes Ambassador Program, Brooks offers verified military members **25% off eligible full-price items** at brooksrunning.com, verified via ID.me.

#### How much is the Brooks Running military discount?
**25%** off eligible full-price items, online only. (Sports Medicine Professionals get a separate 40% offer.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify through the ID.me **Military** community (which covers active duty, veterans, retirees, Guard, and Reserve). Brooks does **not** state on its official page whether military **spouses or dependents** are eligible, so check directly through ID.me before assuming.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify through **ID.me** at hosted-pages.id.me/offers/brooks. Once verified, copy your single-use 25% promo code and enter it at checkout on brooksrunning.com.

#### Does Brooks Running use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Brooks' official Community Heroes program uses **ID.me**. (Brooks also appears on the GovX marketplace, but the official program page specifies ID.me.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. The 25% is **online only at brooksrunning.com**. Brooks states it does not apply in retail stores, including the Trailhead, or with retail partners.

#### What is excluded?
The discount is for **full-price items** and excludes **sale items, select lifestyle collections, and runDisney® products**. It can't be combined with other offers, and members pay their own shipping.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Brooks' program terms state the code can't be combined with other offers, discounts, or coupons, and it doesn't apply to sale items.

#### How many times can I use it?
Members receive **up to three single-use 25% codes per rolling 12-month period**, starting from the issue date of the first code. Each code can be used once.

#### Does Brooks offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
First responders and nurses qualify for the same 25% Community Heroes program. Sports Medicine Professionals get a separate 40% offer. Teachers, students, and government employees are **not** listed among Brooks' eligible categories.
