## Headline finding

On its official "Service Member Benefits" page, Kane states: "To thank you for your service, KANE has partnered with GovX to offer a 20% discount on our store," eligible for **current & former U.S. military, first responders (law enforcement, fire, EMS), teachers, and healthcare workers.** Verification is via **GovX ID**, you get a **single-use code** (limit one per day), and 20% off the $80 Revive brings it to **$64**. **ID.me does NOT carry Kane's military discount** — through ID.me you only get a small (~5%) shopping-portal **cashback**, not the 20% off. Kane also markets its shoes as **HSA/FSA-eligible**, a separate pre-tax savings angle. Because Kane rarely deep-discounts its core $80 Revive publicly, the **GovX 20% is usually the best single lever** for eligible shoppers. Archetype: **DTC recovery-footwear brand with a strong, clearly-published GovX discount (20%) — where the official offer genuinely wins.**

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal)
1. **GovX gives a single-use code, limit one per day** — it does not stack with another public promo code.
2. **ID.me is cashback, not the 20% discount** — and portal cashback typically **voids on coupon-code orders**, so you can't add ID.me cashback on top of a GovX-coded checkout.
3. **Free shipping kicks in at $100+** — a single $64 (post-discount) pair may still incur shipping; buying two clears it.
4. **HSA/FSA is a payment method, not a discount** — you generally can't apply a GovX code *and* claim the same dollars pre-tax; treat them as competing paths and compare.
