# YouTube Premium Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Slug: `youtube-premium` · Accessed/researched: June 23, 2026 · Country scope: United States

## Executive Summary

**YouTube Premium does not offer a military or veteran discount.** As of June 23, 2026, Google's own help documentation lists only one verified discounted YouTube Premium / YouTube Music Premium membership tier — the **student membership**, verified through **SheerID** — and provides no military, veteran, first-responder, medical, or teacher discount for YouTube Premium. Active-duty members and veterans pay the standard YouTube Premium price like any other subscriber.

The military-discount confusion comes from a **different Google product: YouTube TV** (the live-TV streaming service, not the ad-free YouTube Premium membership). YouTube TV runs an **ID.me-verified discount** for Military/Veterans, First Responders, the Medical Community, and Teachers ($67.99/mo for the first 12 months of the Base Plan vs. the standard $82.99/mo, redeemable through June 30, 2026). A separate ID.me "Heroes" offer exists for NFL Sunday Ticket. These are distinct subscriptions from YouTube Premium and should be presented as alternatives, not as a YouTube Premium discount.

**Most important caveat:** Do not conflate YouTube Premium, YouTube TV, and NFL Sunday Ticket. The military discount applies to YouTube TV / Sunday Ticket only — never to YouTube Premium.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no military/veteran redemption path for YouTube Premium. The only discounted-membership path (student) is:
1. On a computer or mobile web browser, go to `youtube.com/premium/student/inapp` (or `youtube.com/musicpremium/student/inapp` for Music Premium).
2. Select **Get Premium**.
3. Complete SheerID verification. If your school is recognized, you're verified instantly; otherwise you may upload documents for manual review (status email within ~20 minutes in the U.S.).
4. Once verified, select or add a payment method.
5. Click **Buy** to complete the transaction. View your membership any time at `youtube.com/purchases`.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable — YouTube Premium is a digital subscription with no in-store channel.

### How Verification Works
For the **student** membership, YouTube uses **SheerID**. You must be enrolled at a SheerID-approved higher-education institution in an eligible country. Verification grants up to 4 consecutive years of student membership, and **you must re-verify every year** — it is not a one-time check. For help, SheerID support is at customerservice@sheerid.com. There is no military verification flow (e.g., ID.me) for YouTube Premium; the ID.me flow applies only to YouTube TV and NFL Sunday Ticket.

### FAQ

#### Does YouTube Premium offer a military discount?
No. As of June 23, 2026, YouTube's official help pages do not list any military or veteran discount for YouTube Premium. The only discounted membership tier is the student plan (verified by SheerID). [Source: Google/YouTube Help]

#### How much is the YouTube Premium military discount?
There is no YouTube Premium military discount, so there is no military price. Military members and veterans pay the standard YouTube Premium rate. (For a military deal on the separate **YouTube TV** product, see the YouTube TV question below.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a YouTube Premium discount?
No. No military-affiliated group qualifies for a YouTube Premium discount. The only eligibility-based discount is for enrolled students.

#### How do I verify military status for YouTube Premium?
You can't — there is no military verification path for YouTube Premium. YouTube Premium's only verified discount uses **SheerID for students**, not ID.me for military.

#### Does YouTube Premium use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
YouTube Premium uses **SheerID** for student verification only. ID.me is used for **YouTube TV** and **NFL Sunday Ticket** military/responder/teacher offers — separate products from YouTube Premium. GovX and WeSalute are not used.

#### Is there a YouTube TV military discount instead?
Yes — but YouTube TV is a different product (live TV), not the YouTube Premium membership. YouTube TV offers an ID.me-verified discount for Military, Veterans, First Responders, the Medical Community, and Teachers: $67.99/mo for the first 12 months of the Base Plan, then the standard rate (currently $82.99/mo), redeemable through June 30, 2026. Confirm current terms at checkout. [Source: ID.me YouTube TV promotion page]

#### Can I use a YouTube Premium discount in stores?
No. YouTube Premium is a digital subscription with no in-store option. The student plan is redeemed online only.

#### Can I combine a YouTube Premium discount with promo codes or other offers?
There is no military discount to combine. The student plan is a standalone discounted membership; the YouTube TV ID.me offer cannot be combined with other coupon-based offers per its terms.

#### Does YouTube Premium offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Only a **student** discount (via SheerID). First responders, teachers, nurses/medical, and government employees do not get a YouTube Premium discount — though first responders, teachers, and the medical community are eligible for the separate **YouTube TV** ID.me offer.

#### Why do I see "YouTube military discount" online if Premium doesn't have one?
Those results almost always refer to **YouTube TV** or **NFL Sunday Ticket**, which do run ID.me military offers, or to coupon-aggregator pages that conflate the products. The YouTube Premium membership itself has no military discount.
