# TaylorMade Golf Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

TaylorMade Golf offers a verified everyday discount of **15% off TaylorMade products online** for eligible military, first responders, nurses, and teachers, verified through **ID.me** at checkout on taylormadegolf.com. Eligible military groups include Active Duty Service Members, Veterans, Retirees, Military Spouses, Military Dependents, and Surviving Spouses. The discount is applied in cart/checkout after ID.me verification; it is **online only** (no stated in-store program), **cannot be combined with promotional codes**, and orders placed with the ID.me discount are **not eligible to earn loyalty points**. Some exclusions may apply. The single most important caveat: this is an online checkout-verified discount, not a code, and it does not stack with promo codes or loyalty earning.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add TaylorMade gear to your cart at taylormadegolf.com.
2. Proceed to checkout and select/click the ID.me button in the checkout section.
3. Verify your community status (military, first responder, nurse, or teacher) through ID.me (free ID.me account required; sign in or create one).
4. Once verified, the 15% discount appears in your order summary in checkout. Complete your purchase.

### How To Redeem In Store
TaylorMade does not publish an in-store military discount program for its own retail. The discount is described as an online benefit applied at taylormadegolf.com checkout. (Not stated in official source for in-store/register redemption.) Verified members who prefer a phone/email path may contact TaylorMade customer service, though this is not described as an official discount channel on the ID.me page.

### How Verification Works
TaylorMade uses **ID.me** to verify eligibility. Verification happens during checkout — you click the ID.me button and confirm your status. A free ID.me account is required (sign in or create one). The official page does not state the re-verification frequency; ID.me typically re-checks status periodically, so do not assume verification is permanent. If automatic verification fails, follow ID.me's in-flow support, or contact TaylorMade customer service at help.taylormadegolf.com or 1-877-860-8624.

### FAQ

#### Does TaylorMade offer a military discount?
Yes. TaylorMade offers 15% off TaylorMade products online for verified military members, verified through ID.me at checkout on taylormadegolf.com.

#### How much is the TaylorMade military discount?
15% off, applied in your order summary at checkout after ID.me verification. It cannot be combined with promotional codes.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Eligible military groups include Active Duty Service Members, Veterans, Retirees, Military Spouses, Military Dependents, and Surviving Spouses.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Click the ID.me button in the checkout section at taylormadegolf.com and verify through ID.me. A free ID.me account is required. Once verified, the 15% discount is applied in cart.

#### Does TaylorMade use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
TaylorMade's official online military discount uses **ID.me**. The official ID.me page does not reference GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute as the verification method for this discount.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
TaylorMade describes the discount as an online benefit applied at taylormadegolf.com checkout. No in-store/register military discount program is published on the official page.

#### What is excluded?
The discount cannot be combined with promo codes, and ID.me-discounted orders do not earn loyalty points. TaylorMade notes "some exclusions may apply" but does not list specific excluded products on the official page.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
It cannot be combined with promotional codes. The official page does not state whether it applies to sale-priced items; verify at checkout.

#### Does TaylorMade offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders (law enforcement, certified firefighters, EMT/paramedic), teachers (PreK-12 and college/university), and nurses (APRN, CNA, LPN, LVN, public health nurse, RN) qualify for the same 15% off via ID.me. Government and student discounts are not stated in TaylorMade's official source.

#### Do I earn loyalty points on a discounted order?
No. Per TaylorMade's official FAQ, ID.me members who use their discount are not eligible to earn loyalty points on those discounted purchases.
