# PUMA Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

PUMA (U.S.) offers a **20% off** "Service Discount" to qualified service members — including **military personnel**, doctors, nurses, first responders, government employees, and teachers — verified through **ID.me**. The offer is **online only at PUMA.com** and is applied via a unique discount code at checkout after verification. PUMA's official Service Discount page does not state separate eligibility lines for active duty vs. veterans (it groups them under "military personnel"), and it does not publish an annual or per-order spend cap. The single most important caveat: the discount **excludes** a long list of categories — new releases, Select/Partnership collections, Icons, Nitro performance running, MB collection, FENTY x PUMA, Teamsport, new licensed/replica jerseys, and Golf — and is not redeemable in PUMA stores.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to PUMA's Service Discount page on PUMA.com.
2. Select "Verify with ID.me" and sign in to (or create) a free ID.me account.
3. Verify your eligible status (e.g., military) by submitting the documentation ID.me requests.
4. On successful verification, receive your PUMA.com discount.
5. Shop PUMA.com and apply the discount at checkout (single-use code; confirm it appears before paying).

### How To Redeem In Store

### How Verification Works

### FAQ

#### Does PUMA offer a military discount?
Yes. PUMA's U.S. "Service Discount" gives military personnel (and other qualified groups) 20% off on PUMA.com, verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the PUMA military discount?
20% off your order, per PUMA's official Service Discount page. (Note: some coupon sites claim 10% — PUMA's own page states 20%.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
PUMA lists "military personnel" and verifies via ID.me's military category. Veterans are generally covered under that category. PUMA's page does not separately confirm spouses or dependents — eligibility for those groups is not stated in the official source.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me. Create or sign in to a free ID.me account from PUMA's Service Discount page, verify your eligible group, and PUMA issues a discount to use at checkout.

#### Does PUMA use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
PUMA's Service Discount uses **ID.me**. (PUMA's separate Student Discount uses Student Beans.) Third-party listings on WeSalute or GovX are not PUMA's stated official channel for this offer.

#### Can I use the discount in PUMA stores?
No. PUMA states the Service Discount is "only available on PUMA.com." It is online only.

#### What is excluded from the PUMA service discount?
Per PUMA's FAQ: new releases, Select & Partnership collections, Icons, Nitro performance running, the MB collection, FENTY x PUMA, Teamsport, new licensed/replica jerseys, and Golf — and the list is "not limited to" those items.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
PUMA's official Service Discount source does not state that it stacks with other promo codes, sale/clearance, or gift cards. Assume it cannot be combined unless PUMA confirms otherwise at checkout.

#### Does PUMA offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes for first responders, teachers, nurses, medical professionals, and government employees — all 20% under the same ID.me Service Discount. Students get a separate 10% discount via Student Beans.
