# Hollister Co. Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Hollister Co. does not offer an official military or veteran discount.** As of June 23, 2026, there is no military/veteran offer on Hollister's own Promotions page, and ID.me's brand page for Hollister explicitly states ID.me is "not aware that Hollister offers Military discounts." Hollister has no verification partner (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX) for a military audience. The only "10%–15% military discount" claims come from individual shopping-mall directory listings (CBL Properties tenant pages), which describe an informal, in-store, manager-discretion offer that "varies by location" and is never available online — these are aggregator/third-party sources, not Hollister, and should be flagged Low confidence and never asserted as a guaranteed offer. Hollister DOES run an official **student discount** (verified through a third-party service) plus its free **Hollister House Rewards** program and frequent sitewide sales, which are the realistic ways a service member or veteran actually saves here. The single most important caveat: do not state a Hollister military discount percentage as fact — none is published by the brand.

### FAQ

#### Does Hollister offer a military discount?
No. As of June 23, 2026, Hollister Co. does not offer an official military or veteran discount. Its promotions page lists no military offer, and ID.me confirms it is "not aware that Hollister offers Military discounts."

#### How much is the Hollister military discount?
Hollister does not publish a military discount, so there is no official percentage. Some mall directory pages cite "10%–15%" at select stores, but that is unofficial, in-store only, varies by location, and is not guaranteed by Hollister.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
There is no Hollister military program, so no eligibility groups are defined. Veterans, spouses, and dependents have no published Hollister military discount to claim.

#### How do I verify my military status at Hollister?
You can't — Hollister uses no military verification service. There is no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX military gate at checkout or in stores.

#### Does Hollister use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID for military discounts?
No. Hollister has no military verification partner. ID.me's own Hollister page states it is not aware of any Hollister military discount. (Hollister does use third-party verification for its separate student discount.)

#### Can I use a military discount in Hollister stores?
Not officially. A few mall directories claim some stores give about 10%–15% off with a military ID at the associate's discretion, but this is unconfirmed by Hollister and not available everywhere. Ask your local store before counting on it.

#### What is excluded?
Since no military offer exists, no military exclusions are published. Hollister's general promo terms exclude gift cards, gift wrapping, and shipping; bar combining with other offers; and sales usually exclude clearance.

#### Can I combine a discount with promo codes or sale items?
Hollister's standard terms state offers cannot be combined with other offers or promo codes on the same items. There is no military discount to stack in the first place.

#### Does Hollister offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Hollister offers an official **student discount** (verified through a third-party service). It does not publish first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discounts.

#### What's the best way for a service member to save at Hollister?
Join Hollister House Rewards (free), shop during the brand's frequent 25%–50%-off sales, watch for app-only offers, and use the student discount if you're enrolled in school.
