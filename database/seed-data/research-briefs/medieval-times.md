# Medieval Times Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Medieval Times Dinner & Tournament offers discounted tickets to the U.S. military community, redeemable two ways: (1) at participating U.S. military bases — which include a **free Royalty upgrade** exclusive to base purchases — and (2) online for all U.S. Castles via verification through **ID.me** (Troop ID). Canadian Forces members have a separate verification path. Eligible groups, per ID.me's Troop ID, include active duty, veterans, military spouses, and family members. **The official Medieval Times page does not publish an exact discount percentage or dollar amount** — the discounted rate is revealed only after ID.me verification and varies; any specific percentage seen on coupon sites is unverified and conflicting (ranges of "10–25%" and "up to 35%" appear across aggregators). "Restrictions Apply," and certain premium shows marked with an asterisk exclude coupons. The single most important caveat: do not promise a specific percentage — state that the discount is verified and revealed at checkout via ID.me.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the official Medieval Times military page: medievaltimes.com/promotion/military-discounts.
2. Click the **US Military** (ID.me / Troop ID) button. (If the page isn't loading, Medieval Times advises clearing your cache and trying again.)
3. Sign in to or create your ID.me account and verify your military affiliation through Troop ID.
4. After verification, you're directed to the discounted military ticket purchase flow; select your Castle, date, and show.
5. Review the discounted rate (shown after verification) and complete checkout. Note: some asterisk-marked shows exclude coupons/offers.

(Canadian Forces members: click the **CAF** button instead to use the Canadian Forces verification path.)

### How To Redeem In Store / On Base
1. Visit a participating U.S. military base ticket office (e.g., ITT/MWR/leisure-travel office). Availability varies by base.
2. Present your valid military ID as required by the base office.
3. Purchase your Medieval Times tickets through the base — base purchases include a **free Royalty upgrade**.
4. Bring your tickets/confirmation to the Castle on show day.

### How Verification Works
U.S. military verification is handled by **ID.me** via **Troop ID**, a digital ID card ID.me issues to military and veterans, spouses, and family members. You sign in to or create an ID.me account and verify your affiliation; the discounted rate is then available online the same way it would be offline. ID.me's platform supports 200+ partners (the official page cites USAA and the Department of Veterans Affairs as examples). The official page does not state re-verification frequency for this offer; ID.me generally re-checks status periodically — confirm current requirements in your ID.me account. If the page won't load, Medieval Times advises clearing your browser cache and retrying. For verification problems, use ID.me's help center; for ticket questions, use Medieval Times' Contact page.

### FAQ

#### Does Medieval Times offer a military discount?
Yes. Medieval Times offers discounted tickets to the military community at all Castles, redeemable through a participating U.S. military base or online via ID.me (Troop ID). Canadian Forces members have a separate verification path. (Source: official military page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the Medieval Times military discount?
Medieval Times does not publish an exact percentage or dollar amount on its official military page. The discounted rate is revealed after you verify through ID.me and can vary by Castle and show. Percentages quoted on coupon sites are unverified and conflict with each other, so treat them with caution.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family members qualify?
Yes. The official page describes ID.me Troop ID as serving "military and veterans, spouses, and family members." Retirees and Reserve/Guard are not separately named on the page but are typically covered within ID.me's military category — verify your specific status in ID.me.

#### How do I verify my military status?
U.S. military members verify through ID.me's Troop ID by signing in to or creating an ID.me account from the Medieval Times military page. Canadian Forces members use the separate CAF link.

#### Does Medieval Times use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Medieval Times uses **ID.me** (Troop ID) for U.S. military verification, plus a **Canadian Forces (CAF)** path. The official page does not mention GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute.

#### Can I use the discount on base, and what's the free Royalty upgrade?
Yes — you can buy military tickets at participating U.S. military bases, and base purchases include a **free Royalty upgrade** (an upgraded ticket tier). This upgrade is exclusive to base purchases and is not part of the online ID.me path.

#### What is excluded?
"Restrictions Apply," and some premium shows marked with an asterisk exclude coupons/offers. The free Royalty upgrade is base-only. Caps and combinability are not stated in the official source.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The official military page does not state whether the military rate can be combined with other coupons, promo codes, group rates, or sales. Do not assume it can; coupon-site claims about combinability are not from an official source.

#### Does Medieval Times offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
The official Medieval Times military page covers the military community only. ID.me Shop's brand page lists FAQ links asking whether Medieval Times offers Nurse, Responder, Teacher, Government, Medical, Student, and Alumni discounts, but it does not confirm an active offer for those groups beyond the military program. Verify in ID.me before assuming eligibility.
