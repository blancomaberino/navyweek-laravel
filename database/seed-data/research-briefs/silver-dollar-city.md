# Silver Dollar City Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Silver Dollar City (theme park, Branson, MO, with sister parks White Water and Silver Dollar City's Showboat) offers a **year-round discounted-admission program** for U.S. military, plus **limited-time 2026 promotions tied to America's 250th**. The standing offer gives eligible military a discounted single-day Silver Dollar City ticket of **$69 + tax** (White Water $36 + tax; Showboat from $49–$62 + tax), available to U.S. active or retired military, veterans, and reservists, plus their spouse and dependent children ages 4–17 living in the household. For 2026 specifically, Silver Dollar City is offering **free 2026 season passports to active-duty military** (registration online only, activation required in person by 7/31/26 with a Common Access Card), **discounted 2026 season passports** for veterans/retirees/spouses/dependents (add-ons from $12.90/mo), and **Military Appreciation Days (May 1–29, 2026)** with a $59 single-day Silver Dollar City ticket valid for the eligible member plus up to 5 guests. **All offers are online-only** (not at the gate or by phone) and require verification through **ID.me**. The single most important caveat: most of these are dated, limited-time 2026 offers — terms, prices, and dates will change for future seasons and must be re-verified.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the Silver Dollar City military offer page (silverdollarcity.com/tickets/special-offers/military/) or the 2026 Military Passport page.
2. Select the offer you want (single-day military tickets, free active-duty passport, or discounted passport).
3. Click the "Verify with ID.me" button in the offer.
4. Follow the ID.me prompts to verify your military affiliation (or log in if you already have an ID.me account).
5. Once verified, the discounted pricing unlocks; complete your purchase online. (Active-duty free passport: register online, then activate in person at either park by 7/31/26 with your valid Common Access Card.)

### How To Redeem In Store
Not available. Silver Dollar City states these military offers are an online exclusive — not available at the gate, ticket booths, or by phone. (Exception: active-duty free 2026 passports are registered online but must be *activated* in person at either park by 7/31/26 with a valid CAC.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by ID.me. Click "Verify with ID.me" in the offer and follow the prompts; if you already have an ID.me account you log in to apply discounts to future orders. Silver Dollar City does not state on these pages how often ID.me re-verifies military status — ID.me sets its own re-verification cadence, so do not assume one-time-forever. For verification problems, ID.me support is reachable via help@id.me or the chat option in any ID.me screen. Silver Dollar City notes you will not receive ID.me marketing unless you opt in. Active-duty free-passport claimants additionally must present a valid Common Access Card (CAC) in person to activate.

### FAQ

#### Does Silver Dollar City offer a military discount?
Yes. Silver Dollar City offers discounted single-day admission to U.S. active or retired military, veterans, and reservists (and their spouses and dependent children ages 4–17), plus limited-time 2026 deals including a free active-duty season passport and discounted season passports. All are online-only and verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the Silver Dollar City military discount?
For the everyday military offer: Silver Dollar City 1-Day is $69 + tax, White Water 1-Day is $36 + tax, and the Showboat cruise/meal/show starts at $62 (ages 12+) or $49 (ages 4–11), plus tax. For 2026: active-duty members can get a free season passport; veterans/retirees/families get discounted passports with add-ons from $12.90/month; and Military Appreciation Days (May 1–29, 2026) offer a $59 + tax single-day ticket for the eligible member plus up to 5 guests.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. The military discount covers U.S. veterans and retirees plus active and retired members' spouses and dependent children (ages 4–17 living in the household). The free 2026 passport is limited to active-duty personnel with a Common Access Card, but spouses and dependents can get discounted passports.

#### How do I verify my status?
Click the "Verify with ID.me" button in the offer on silverdollarcity.com and follow the prompts. If you already have an ID.me account, log in. Active-duty members claiming the free passport must also present a valid Common Access Card (CAC) in person at either park to activate by July 31, 2026.

#### Does Silver Dollar City use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Silver Dollar City uses **ID.me** for military verification.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or at the gate?
No. Silver Dollar City states these military offers are online exclusives — not available at the gate, ticket booths, or by phone. (The free active-duty passport is registered online but activated in person with a CAC.)

#### What is excluded?
Prices are plus tax and subject to change. The free active-duty passport excludes any Bring-A-Friend tickets and added benefits and must be activated in person by 7/31/26 with a CAC. The discounted passport is limited to 6. Other blackout/refund/transfer terms are not stated on the official offer pages.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Not stated in the official source. Silver Dollar City's military pages do not describe stacking with other promotions; treat the military price as the final discounted price unless the official page says otherwise.

#### Does Silver Dollar City offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
Yes — but separately from the military offer. Silver Dollar City's Community Heroes program offers discounted tickets (reported ~$59 for 2026) to teachers, healthcare workers, first responders, military veterans, and public employees, online and verified through ID.me. Confirm current price and dates on the official Community Heroes page.
