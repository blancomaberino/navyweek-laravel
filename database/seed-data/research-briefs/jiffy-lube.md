# Jiffy Lube Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Jiffy Lube has **no single, corporate-wide military or veteran discount**. Jiffy Lube is overwhelmingly franchise-operated ("Most Jiffy Lube locations... are owned and operated by independent franchisees" — jiffylube.com), so military pricing is set location-by-location and varies. The one **currently-documented, verifiable everyday offer** is through the brand's **WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage) partnership: 15% off in-store preventive-maintenance services at 2,000+ locations**, redeemed by showing a **WeSalute+ ID Card** at the register. WeSalute states eligibility as U.S. veterans, active-duty military, first responders, nurses, and immediate family members enrolled in WeSalute+. Two other figures circulate widely but cannot be confirmed as current: a **Team Car Care 15%** program (cited by Military.com, but its primary Jiffy Lube URL now redirects to the homepage — likely discontinued or moved) and a **Heartland Automotive Services 25%** program (sourced to a **November 6, 2015 press release** — over a decade old, not re-confirmed). Most-important caveat: there is no nationwide rate; a customer must verify the exact discount with their specific local store before relying on it.

### Intro
Jiffy Lube does not run one nationwide military discount. Because nearly all Jiffy Lube service centers are independently owned franchises, the discount you can get — and whether one exists at all — depends on the specific location. The most reliable everyday option is Jiffy Lube's partnership with WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage): **15% off in-store preventive-maintenance services at more than 2,000 locations** when you show a WeSalute+ ID Card.

If you don't have a WeSalute+ membership, it's still worth asking your local Jiffy Lube directly — many franchises offer their own military, veteran, or first-responder pricing, and some run Veterans Day specials (free or steeply discounted oil changes) that change each year.

This is an independent guide. We are not affiliated with Jiffy Lube. Jiffy Lube and its franchisees control all offers and can change or end them at any time; confirm current terms with your local store before you rely on them.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no online military discount. The WeSalute+ Jiffy Lube offer is explicitly for **in-person purchases** only. Jiffy Lube services are performed in store, so there is no e-commerce checkout path for the discount.

### How To Redeem In Store
For the WeSalute+ 15% offer:
1. Enroll in WeSalute+ and obtain your WeSalute+ ID Card (paid membership; see wesalute.com).
2. Visit any participating Jiffy Lube (2,000+ locations). Confirm participation by checking jiffylube.com/locations or calling ahead.
3. At the register, tell the cashier you are eligible for the WeSalute discount.
4. Present your WeSalute+ ID Card at checkout to receive 15% off eligible preventive-maintenance services.

For a franchise's own (non-WeSalute) discount:
1. Call your local Jiffy Lube before your visit and ask whether they offer a military/veteran/first-responder discount and at what rate.
2. Bring acceptable proof of service (e.g., military ID) — the accepted documents are set by the store.
3. Mention the discount before service and present your proof at the register.

### How Verification Works
The documented program uses the **WeSalute+ ID Card**. WeSalute is a paid membership service (founded as Veterans Advantage) that verifies service status so you don't have to share your military ID or other documents at the point of sale. Eligibility to enroll, per WeSalute: active duty, retiree, veteran, current or former guard & reserve, or an immediate family member. WeSalute membership renews on its own schedule — it is not a one-time, permanent verification — and the discount is honored only while your membership is active. For franchise-run discounts that don't use WeSalute, verification is whatever the individual store accepts (commonly a military ID); there is no central standard. Support for the WeSalute offer runs through WeSalute (help.wesalute.com); support for store-level pricing runs through the individual Jiffy Lube location.

### FAQ

#### Does Jiffy Lube offer a military discount?
There is no single nationwide Jiffy Lube military discount. Discounts are set by individual franchise locations and vary. The one currently documented brand-level offer is a 15% in-store discount through Jiffy Lube's WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage) partnership at 2,000+ locations.

#### How much is the Jiffy Lube military discount?
Through WeSalute+, it's 15% off eligible in-store preventive-maintenance services. Other amounts are reported (a Team Car Care 15% program and an older Heartland 25% program), but those are not currently confirmed on Jiffy Lube's own site, so the rate at your specific store may be different — or there may be none. Ask your local store.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
For the WeSalute+ offer, veterans, active duty, reserve, retired members, and immediate family members enrolled in WeSalute+ qualify, along with first responders and nurses (per WeSalute's offer copy). For franchise-run discounts, eligibility is set by each store and isn't centrally published.

#### How do I verify my status?
The documented program uses a WeSalute+ ID Card, which you present at the register. WeSalute verifies your service status when you enroll. Franchise-run discounts may simply ask to see a military ID — it depends on the location.

#### Does Jiffy Lube use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The verification provider named in Jiffy Lube's documented offer is **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage). There is no evidence in primary sources that Jiffy Lube uses ID.me, GovX, or SheerID for a military discount.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes — in store is the only way. Jiffy Lube services are performed on-site and the WeSalute offer is for in-person purchases at the register. There is no online discount.

#### What is excluded?
Under the WeSalute terms: gift cards, tires, batteries, taxes, inspections, and regulatory/environmental/governmental fees; it can't be combined with another offer for the same service and is non-transferable. A separately reported program (Team Car Care, via Military.com) also excludes brakes, alignment, and diagnostics.

#### Can I combine it with coupons or promo codes?
The WeSalute+ offer is not valid with any other offer for the same service. Franchise-run discounts set their own stacking rules, so confirm with the store.

#### Does Jiffy Lube offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
WeSalute's Jiffy Lube offer copy names first responders and nurses (and immediate family) among those who can use the 15% in-store discount via WeSalute+. Teacher, government-employee, and student discounts are not stated in official Jiffy Lube sources. Individual franchises may offer their own first-responder pricing — ask locally.

#### Is the Jiffy Lube military discount 25%?
A 25% year-round discount was announced by Heartland Automotive Services (a large Jiffy Lube franchisee) — but that announcement dates to **November 6, 2015** and has not been re-confirmed as still active. Treat 25% as historical/unverified unless your local Heartland-operated store confirms it today.
