# San Antonio Zoo — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Standard (Any Day) adult admission** to San Antonio Zoo (gate price is dynamic/date-based, generally ~$30; verify live at sazoo.org/buy-tickets).

## Headline finding

San Antonio Zoo offers a **real, everyday military discount: $5 off admission, year-round, every day**, for military members and veterans who show a **valid Military ID at entry** — plus two seasonal upgrades that are more generous than the everyday $5: **(1) Month of the Military Child (April 6–30):** military children ages 3–11 get **FREE admission**, and immediate family (up to three/four, per the zoo's wording) get **50% off** Standard/Any Day admission on the visit date; and **(2) Zoo Salutes (November):** the zoo has offered **FREE admission to all military members and veterans** during a November window (documented for November 2024; treat as a recurring-but-confirm-annually event). Verification is **at the gate with a Military ID** — there is no online code, no ID.me/GovX. **Blue Star Museums:** San Antonio Zoo is **not confirmed** as a participating Blue Star venue this pass — do not assert it. Archetype: **local attraction with a genuine modest everyday discount + seasonal free-admission events** (a real discount, honestly bounded).
