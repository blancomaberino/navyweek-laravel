# Build-A-Bear Workshop — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 11, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one in-store make-your-own order — a **~$30 furry friend + a ~$12 outfit = ~$42 pre-tax** (typical Build-A-Bear ticket; prices as of July 11, 2026).

## Headline finding

Build-A-Bear Workshop publishes **no standing military or veteran discount** on any of its own pages as of July 11, 2026 — there is no military/hero offer page (buildabear.com/military returns 404), no ID.me or SheerID checkout, and no military/hero option in its Customer Service help topics, Current Promotions, Sale page, or Bonus Club FAQ. The only official military offer on record is a **historical** one: a 2010 Military Appreciation Month event that gave 20% off in stores (May 3–June 7, 2010). The widely repeated "20% off via SheerID" claim is **fabricated** — the specific SheerID verification link aggregators attach to Build-A-Bear (`.../verify/6585ce78159bec3b7479972c/`) actually belongs to **Clinique (Canadian Armed Forces)**, a different brand and country. The genuine everyday savings at Build-A-Bear are **not military-specific**: the free **Bonus Club** loyalty wallet (earn $10 in rewards per 100 points ≈ 10% back), the **Pay Your Age / Count Your Candles** birthday deal (a child pays their age in dollars for a Birthday Treat Bear during their birth month), and Build-A-Bear's frequent public promos (BOGO-for-$15, 2-for-$30 charms, "$10 gift card for $5"). **Archetype: H (the "myth" brand — a flat % aggregators invent that the brand never confirms; the real value is the loyalty wallet) + B (no first-party military route: no brand military checkout, and Build-A-Bear is not on GovX or the ID.me/SheerID military directories).**

#### How much is the Build-A-Bear military discount?

For a birthday child (1–14) in their birth month, Pay Your Age makes the Birthday Treat Bear cost their age in dollars. Otherwise, join the free Bonus Club (~10% back) and layer whatever public promo (BOGO-for-$15, 2-for deals) is live.

#### Does Build-A-Bear offer a first responder, teacher, or nurse discount?
No dedicated first-responder, teacher, or nurse discount is published on Build-A-Bear's site as of July 11, 2026.

#### Does Build-A-Bear run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Build-A-Bear runs public seasonal sales, but no verified military-only Veterans Day or Memorial Day offer. Its one documented military event was in May 2010 (historical).

#### What is the Bonus Club and is it worth it?
It's free, earns 1 point per $1, and gives $10 in rewards for every 100 points (about 10% back) plus a birthday reward and early access — the most reliable everyday saver at Build-A-Bear.
