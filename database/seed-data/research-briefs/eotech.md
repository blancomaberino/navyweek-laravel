# EOTECH — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one EOTECH EXPS3 holographic weapon sight (MSRP ~$729); a HWS 512 (~$519) is the secondary reference.

## Headline finding

**EOTECH has no first-party "military discount" checkout on eotechinc.com — it routes those who serve to two verified programs, and one of them is dramatically better than the other.** The straightforward path is **GovX** (current/former military, first responders, and law enforcement), typically a **modest ~10–20% off** that varies by product. The bigger win, for those eligible, is **ExpertVoice**, where EOTECH offers **up to 60% off MSRP** — and members of all U.S. Armed Service branches, **including veterans and spouses**, get access to ExpertVoice's military program. ExpertVoice pricing comes with **personal-use, purchase-cap, and no-resale** terms.

The verdict: **if you qualify for and are willing to verify through ExpertVoice, it crushes every other route (up to 60% off MSRP)**; **if you want a simple, no-cap discount, use GovX (~10–20%)**; and because EOTECH enforces MAP pricing, watch **retailer sales** (OpticsPlanet has run "up to 30% off," plus dealers like EuroOptic) as an occasional substitute. Archetype: **A (GovX-only first-party route) + K (ExpertVoice pro deal that beats the public military %).** There is **no public "EOTECH military promo code"** — those on coupon sites are fabricated.

### FAQ

#### Does EOTECH offer a military discount?
Yes, through verified partners rather than an on-site code. **GovX** gives current/former military, first responders, and law enforcement a modest ~10–20% (varies by product), and **ExpertVoice** offers eligible military (all branches, incl. veterans and spouses) up to **60% off MSRP** for personal use.

#### How much is the EOTECH military discount?
It depends on the route: **~10–20% via GovX**, or **up to 60% off MSRP via ExpertVoice** (with purchase caps and personal-use/no-resale terms).

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Yes — ExpertVoice's military program extends to all U.S. Armed Service branches including veterans and spouses; GovX covers former military as well.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a free **GovX** account, or verify military membership through **ExpertVoice** for the deeper pricing.

#### Does EOTECH use GovX or ExpertVoice?
Both. GovX is the simple, no-cap route; ExpertVoice is the deeper, capped/personal-use route. EOTECH has no self-serve military code.

#### Is there an EOTECH military promo code?
No. Coupon-site "military codes" are fabricated or expired public codes. Use GovX or ExpertVoice.

#### Can I combine the discount with a sale or cashback?
No — GovX and ExpertVoice pricing replaces public codes, and there's no cashback stacking. Compare each net price directly.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy EOTECH?
If you're eligible for **ExpertVoice** and buying for yourself, that's the cheapest (up to 60% off MSRP, with caps). If you want no caps, use **GovX** (~10–20%). Check a dealer sale or the exchange (tax-free) as a substitute.

#### Can I buy EOTECH at the Exchange or through the Industry program?
The exchange (AAFES/NEX) may carry EOTECH tax-free — verify stock first. The **Industry Discount Program** is application-based and limited to qualified professionals, not open to all veterans.
