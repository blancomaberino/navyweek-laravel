# Crocs Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Crocs offers a **15% military discount** to verified service members and veterans, redeemed online at crocs.com after you verify your status through a third-party provider and receive a single-use coupon code for checkout. Crocs' own military landing page confirms the **15%** rate (page title and meta description), and Crocs' Veterans Day "military donation" page confirms the verify-each-time, instant-coupon-code mechanic and that the offer applies to **full-priced styles** with **"Exclusions Apply."** The single most important caveat: Crocs runs a **higher seasonal 25% military offer around Veterans Day** (tied to its Hope for the Warriors donation), so the rate you see depends on timing — 15% is the everyday rate; 25% is a limited-time promo, not the baseline. Verification is handled by **SheerID** (linked from Crocs' own donation page) and Crocs also maintains an **ID.me** storefront; the discount is **online**, and most terms (eligibility breakdown, caps, in-store availability) are **not enumerated on Crocs' official pages.**

> Accuracy note: Crocs' evergreen pages (military.html, first-responder.html, healthcare.html, teachers.html) are JavaScript-rendered — the offer body did not load on fetch, but each page's **title tag and meta description state the rate verbatim** (all four say **15% off**), and these were used as the primary source. The **25%** figure is confirmed **only** on the seasonal `military-donation.html` page as a Veterans Day promo, not as the everyday rate. Aggregators that headline a flat "25% off" as the standing Crocs military discount are conflating the seasonal promo with the evergreen offer — treat 15% as the baseline.

### How Verification Works
Crocs uses a third-party verification service to confirm your status before issuing a discount code. Crocs' own military donation page links to **SheerID** for verification, and Crocs separately maintains an **ID.me** storefront for its military community — so you may encounter either provider depending on the entry point. You complete the provider's secure form (never email your military ID directly to the retailer), and on success Crocs issues an instant, single-use coupon code. Because Crocs states it "will verify your eligibility" *each time you shop*, expect to re-verify or request a new code per order rather than relying on one permanent code. For problems, use the verification provider's support (SheerID or ID.me) and Crocs customer service.

### FAQ

#### Does Crocs offer a military discount?
Yes. Crocs offers a **15%** military discount online for verified service members and veterans, with a larger **25%** promo around Veterans Day.

#### How much is the Crocs military discount?
**15%** is the everyday rate (per Crocs' military discount page). Around Veterans Day, Crocs has offered **25% off all full-priced styles** as a seasonal promotion.

#### Why do some sites say 25% and others say 15%?
Both are real Crocs figures, but for different times. **15%** is the standing everyday rate; **25%** is a limited-time Veterans Day offer tied to Crocs' Hope for the Warriors donation. Coupon sites often blur the two.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify (Crocs' verification storefront names active-duty members and veterans). Crocs does **not** state on its official pages whether military spouses or dependents are eligible, so verify directly with the provider before assuming.

#### How do I verify my status?
Start on Crocs' military page, complete the secure verification (SheerID via Crocs' own page; Crocs also has an ID.me storefront), and you'll get an instant coupon code to use at checkout. Re-verify for each new order.

#### Does Crocs use SheerID, ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute?
Crocs' official military donation page links to **SheerID**, and Crocs maintains an **ID.me** storefront. Crocs also appears on third-party marketplaces like **GovX** and **WeSalute**, but those are not Crocs' own pages.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Crocs' official pages describe an **online** verify-and-code flow only and do not confirm in-store use. Check with a Crocs store or customer service before counting on it in person.

#### What is excluded?
The discount applies to **full-priced styles**, and Crocs notes **"Exclusions Apply."** Sale and clearance items are not confirmed eligible, and the code is not stated to combine with other offers.

#### Does Crocs offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Yes — Crocs runs separate **15%** programs for first responders, healthcare workers/nurses, teachers, and students, each verified the same way.
