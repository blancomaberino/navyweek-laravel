# Seattle Aquarium — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **adult general-admission ticket** (regular ~**$33.95**; the Aquarium uses demand-based "plan-ahead" pricing that ranges roughly **$23–$51** for adults).

## Headline finding

**The Seattle Aquarium offers a modest year-round military discount — $2 off admission — for active, retired, and veteran U.S. military**, redeemable **only at the Aquarium's admission gates** (not online), for the next available time slot. That's the evergreen offer. **The far bigger military benefit is seasonal and free: Blue Star Museums.** Each summer the Seattle Aquarium has historically participated in **Blue Star Museums** (National Endowment for the Arts + Blue Star Families), which gives **free admission to active-duty/Reserve/Guard service members and up to five family members** from **Armed Forces Day (May 16, 2026) through Labor Day (September 7, 2026)** — but **participation is confirmed year-by-year on the official NEA registry**, and the free program covers *currently serving* members (not veterans). Archetype: **attraction/venue with a small flat military discount plus a valuable, seasonal, third-party free-admission program** — the summer Blue Star window is the real save.

## Stacking Rules (verified/logical)

2. **Blue Star free admission is not a discount to stack** — it replaces a paid ticket entirely during the summer window.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — $2 off year-round; **free** via Blue Star (May–Sept) with up to 5 family.
- **National Guard / Reserve** — Included in Blue Star (currently serving); $2 gate discount with ID.
- **Veterans** — **$2 gate discount** year-round. **Not** covered by Blue Star (that program is currently-serving only).
- **Retirees** — $2 gate discount year-round.
- **Military family members** — Up to **5 family members free** with the service member during the Blue Star window; otherwise standard pricing.
- **First responders / nurses / teachers / students** — Not addressed by the military offer; check the Aquarium's general discounts page for any separate programs.

### How To Redeem In Person (only channel)
1. Go to the **Seattle Aquarium admission gate** (the discount can't be booked online).
2. Show valid **U.S. military ID** (active, retired, or veteran).
3. Receive **$2 off** admission for the next available time slot.

*For Blue Star (summer):* confirm the Aquarium is on the **arts.gov/BlueStarMuseums** 2026 registry, then present your **CAC / DD-1173 / Next-Gen USID** at the gate for **free** admission for you and up to five family members.

### How Verification Works
For the **$2 discount**, verification is a simple **military ID check at the gate** — no online account or third-party provider. For **Blue Star Museums**, eligibility is confirmed by presenting a valid **military ID (CAC, DD Form 1173, DD Form 1173-1, or Next Generation USID)** at the gate during the program window; it covers **currently serving** members and up to five accompanying family members.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **$2 discount is gate-only** — not applied to online/advance tickets.
- **Blue Star is seasonal** (May 16–Sept 7, 2026) and covers **currently serving** members (not veterans/retirees), with the service member present.
- **Aquarium admission uses demand-based pricing** (~$23–$51 adult); the $2 is off that day's gate price.
- **Blue Star participation is confirmed annually** on the NEA registry — verify before relying on it.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Currently serving, May–Sept:** use **Blue Star Museums** for free admission (you + up to 5 family).

- **Multiple attractions:** compare a **Seattle CityPASS/C3** bundle.

### FAQ

#### Does the Seattle Aquarium offer a military discount?
Yes — **$2 off admission** for active, retired, and veteran U.S. military, redeemable **at the gate only**. In summer, currently serving members can also get **free admission via Blue Star Museums**.

#### How much is the Seattle Aquarium military discount?
**$2 off** the day's gate admission price year-round. During Blue Star Museums (May 16–Sept 7, 2026), currently serving members and up to five family members get in **free**.

#### Do veterans and retirees qualify?
Yes for the **$2 gate discount** (active, retired, and veteran are all named). **Blue Star free admission is for currently serving members only** — veterans and retirees aren't covered by that program.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Show a valid **U.S. military ID at the admission gate**. For Blue Star, present a CAC, DD-1173/1173-1, or Next-Generation USID. There's no online/third-party verification.

#### Does the Seattle Aquarium use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No — the military discount is verified by a simple **ID check at the gate**, not through ID.me, GovX, or SheerID.

#### Can I use the military discount online?
No. The **$2 discount is gate-only** and can't be applied to online/advance tickets.

#### When is admission free for military families?
During **Blue Star Museums**, **May 16, 2026 (Armed Forces Day) through September 7, 2026 (Labor Day)** — free for currently serving members and up to five family members, if the Aquarium is registered for 2026.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a military family to visit?
If you're **currently serving in summer**, **Blue Star Museums (free)** wins. Otherwise, combine the **$2 gate discount** with a **low-demand "plan-ahead" date**, or use a **CityPASS** if visiting several attractions.
