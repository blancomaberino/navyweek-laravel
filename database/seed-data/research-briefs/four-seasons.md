# Four Seasons Hotels & Resorts — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **3-night leisure stay** at a Four Seasons property (assume ~$900/night = ~$2,700 room subtotal before tax/resort fees).

## Headline finding

**Four Seasons Hotels & Resorts (the luxury chain, fourseasons.com) has no dedicated military or veteran discount program.** There is no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute gate on fourseasons.com, and no published "military rate." The **only** military-labeled benefit connected to the Four Seasons name is a **third-party package deal — 10% off vacation packages booked through American Airlines Vacations**, surfaced via **WeSalute** (the verification partner). That is an **AA Vacations package discount**, not a Four Seasons room-rate program, and it applies to bundled air+hotel packages rather than a standalone room booking. Critically, the "**Lodge of Four Seasons**" (4seasonsresort.com, Lake Ozark, Missouri) that advertises an "Exclusive Military Discount" is a **completely separate, unaffiliated resort** — not part of Four Seasons Hotels & Resorts. For a real Four Seasons stay, service members save more through **Amex Fine Hotels + Resorts / Virtuoso perks**, **American Forces Travel**, or simply the **best available rate**, than through any "military discount." Archetype: **ultra-luxury hotel brand with no direct military rate — value comes from status/perk programs and package channels, not a service-based discount.**

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **No Four Seasons military rate exists to stack** — status/perk programs (FHR, Virtuoso) book the standard rate and add *perks*, they don't discount the room %.
2. **FHR/Virtuoso perks are mutually exclusive** — you book through one channel; you can't double the 4th-night-free/credits across both on the same stay.
3. **The AA Vacations 10%** applies to **packages**, is verified by **WeSalute**, and is separate from any Four Seasons direct booking — you can't add it to a room-only fourseasons.com reservation.
4. **Government rate ≠ military discount** — a government rate (if offered) is for qualified official travel with its own rules; it's not a leisure veteran discount.
5. **Amex Platinum/Centurion cardholder benefits** (credits, elite-night-like perks) attach to the card, not to military status — a legitimate, separate lever.
