## Headline finding

**Skylight has no official military or veteran discount.** There is no service-verified offer at checkout on myskylight.com, no ID.me / GovX / SheerID / WeSalute program, and no published percentage for troops, veterans, spouses, or dependents. Coupon aggregators claiming Skylight gives "military-specific discounts at checkout during major holidays" are **fabricated** — Skylight's checkout has no military-verification step. The genuine best path for a service member is the same as for any shopper: **buy during a public seasonal sale (Black Friday / holiday events) with the current site-wide code, or through a retail partner (Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Target, Sam's Club) during a markdown, and layer credit-card cashback on top.** Archetype: **no-military-program direct-to-consumer hardware brand** (secondary: subscription-attach — the $79/yr Skylight Plus is a recurring cost, not a device discount).
