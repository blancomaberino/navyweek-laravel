# DISH Network — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new DISH TV subscription — **America's Top 120** at **$89.99/mo** on a **2-year agreement** (the entry package the military offer is advertised against), before taxes/surcharges.

## Headline finding

**DISH Network runs a real, first-party Military & First Responder offer** — it's on DISH's own site (dish.com/offers/military-offer), so this is a genuine corporate program, not a dealer workaround. But it is **not a percentage off your bill**. The verified benefit is a **bundle of free add-ons** stacked on a standard DISH plan: the **Stars & Stripes channel pack free ($240 value over two years)**, your **first On Demand movie rental free every month**, a **Hopper DVR upgrade**, and DISH's **2-year price guarantee (no hidden/sports/HD fees)** — marketed as **"more than $500 in value over two years."** Eligibility (active duty, veterans, reservists, retirees, their spouses/dependents, and first responders) is verified online through **SheerID or ID.me** at checkout, or by showing a **military ID in a DISH store**. Archetype: **service/subscription brand with a first-party discount that is a value-bundle, not a rate cut** — there's no tax-free exchange, GovX marketplace price, cashback stack, or ExpertVoice channel to compare against; the offer itself is the best path. Two cautions: the widely repeated **"$10 off for 25 months"** and **"10%–25% off"** figures are **third-party retailer/aggregator claims not stated on DISH's own military page** — treat them as unverified; and DISH's **Pause/seasonal-hold** feature ($5/mo) is a general account tool useful during deployment, not part of the military offer.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military offer is itself the "stack."** It layers free perks (Stars & Stripes Pack, monthly movie, Hopper upgrade, price lock) onto a normal DISH plan — you don't combine it with a separate percentage discount.
2. **Premium/add-on channels, DISH Protect, taxes, surcharges, and transactional fees are excluded** from the 2-year price guarantee and can change — the "free bundle" does not make these free.

4. **DISH Pause/seasonal hold is a separate account feature** ($5/mo), not part of the military offer and not a discount on active service.
5. The **"$10 off for 25 months"** claim is **not** verified on DISH's own military page — do not assume it stacks with the bundle; treat it as unconfirmed retailer framing.

### FAQ

#### Does DISH offer a military discount?
Yes. DISH runs a first-party **Military & First Responder offer** on its own site. It's a **free value bundle** — Stars & Stripes channel pack, a monthly free movie rental, a Hopper DVR upgrade, and a 2-year price guarantee — layered on a standard plan, marketed as "more than $500 over two years." It is not a straight percentage off your bill.

#### How much is the DISH military discount?
DISH frames it as **"more than $500 in value over two years,"** anchored by the **Stars & Stripes Pack ($240 value over two years)** plus a free monthly movie rental, the Hopper upgrade, and a 2-year price lock. Coupon sites cite "$10 off for 25 months" or "10–25% off," but those figures are **not** on DISH's official military page.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — active duty, veterans, reservists, retirees, and their **spouses and dependents** qualify, along with first responders. Confirm dependent eligibility during verification.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online, select "Military and First Responder Discount" at checkout and verify through **SheerID** or **ID.me**. In a DISH store, show a valid **military ID**.

#### Does DISH use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
DISH uses **SheerID** and **ID.me** for online verification. We found no evidence it uses GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass for this offer.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
Both — online at dish.com or the DISH app, or in person at a participating DISH retail location.

#### What is excluded?
Taxes and surcharges, add-on/premium channels, DISH Protect, and transactional fees are **not** covered by the 2-year price guarantee. A 2-year commitment applies, with a $20-per-month-remaining early termination fee.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The offer **is** the bundle — you don't stack a separate percentage discount on it. DISH Authorized Retailers may add their own signup gift card; confirm combinability at order time.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from DISH?
Take the **first-party Military & First Responder offer** (free bundle + 2-year price lock). If you're deploying, also set up **DISH Pause ($5/mo)** to avoid the full bill while away. Check Authorized Retailers for an added signup gift card.

#### Does DISH offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
The same offer covers **first responders** (firefighters, EMTs, police). We found no separate DISH teacher, nurse, government, or student discount.

#### Can I pause my DISH service during a deployment?
DISH offers a **Pause / seasonal hold** (~$5/mo) that suspends service for roughly one to nine months while you keep your equipment — useful during deployments or long absences. It's a general account feature, separate from the military offer; call DISH to set it up.
