# Lowe's Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Lowe's offers a **10% everyday military and veteran discount** on eligible items, with **no annual cap**, verified for free through **ID.me** and tied to a free, personal **MyLowe's Rewards account**. Eligibility covers **active-duty members (all branches, plus commissioned officers of NOAA and the U.S. Public Health Service), reserve and National Guard, retirees, honorably discharged veterans, and their spouses and household dependents**. You enroll in MyLowe's Rewards, verify once through ID.me on Lowe's military validation page, and the 10% applies to qualifying items online (while signed in) and in store (via your rewards lookup at the register). Verified members also receive free **MyLowe's Rewards Silver Key status** (free standard shipping on eligible orders). The discount is for **personal, non-business use** — Lowe's Pro and business accounts are not eligible. **First responders do not qualify for the everyday 10%**; Lowe's runs a separate, non-percentage first-responder program (free Silver Key status) at Lowes.com/firstresponders.

Lowe's runs one of the most generous everyday military discount programs in home improvement. Eligible active-duty members, reservists, National Guard, retirees, veterans, and their families save **10%** on qualifying purchases — **every day, with no annual spending cap** — at lowes.com and Lowe's stores.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (all branches: Navy, Marine Corps, Army, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard; plus commissioned officers of NOAA and the U.S. Public Health Service).
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible.
- **Military retirees** — Eligible.
- **Veterans** — Eligible if discharged under honorable, general (under honorable conditions), or under-honorable-conditions terms.
- **Spouses & household dependents** — Eligible (confirmed through ID.me).
- **First responders** — Not eligible for the 10%. Separate non-percentage program (free Silver Key status) at Lowes.com/firstresponders.
- **Pro / business accounts** — Not eligible (personal, non-business use only).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Create or sign in to a free, **personal MyLowe's Rewards** account at lowes.com (Pro/business accounts do not qualify).
2. Go to Lowe's military validation page and confirm your eligibility through **ID.me** (a one-time setup reused across ID.me partners).
3. Add eligible items to your cart while signed in to your verified MyLowe's Rewards account.
4. Once verified, the **10%** military discount applies to qualifying items at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Complete the same free ID.me military verification and link it to your MyLowe's Rewards account.
2. At the register, give your MyLowe's Rewards phone number or scan your member ID. Bring a valid military or government ID if asked.
3. The verified **10%** discount applies to eligible in-store purchases.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Major appliances and appliance parts are excluded.
- Commodity building products — including electrical cable and wire, dimensional lumber, plywood, and OSB — do not qualify.
- Services, installation, and assembly charges are excluded.
- Gift cards, sale, clearance, and some promotional items are excluded.
- Cannot be combined with other coupons or promotional offers, and cannot be stacked with the 5% Lowe's credit card discount.
- For personal, non-business use only — Lowe's Pro and business accounts are not eligible.

### FAQ

#### Does Lowe's offer a military discount?
Yes. Lowe's offers a **10% military discount** on eligible items every day, with no annual cap. It covers active-duty members, reservists, National Guard, retirees, honorably discharged veterans, and their spouses and dependents. You verify for free through ID.me and link the discount to a personal MyLowe's Rewards account. Confirm the current terms on Lowe's official military page.

#### How do I verify my military status for the Lowe's discount?
Create or sign in to a free, personal MyLowe's Rewards account, then complete a one-time ID.me verification on Lowe's military validation page. ID.me confirms your eligibility and ties the 10% discount to your account for online and in-store purchases. The same ID.me verification is reused across other partners.

#### Do veterans get the Lowe's discount, or only active duty?
Veterans qualify. Lowe's extends the discount to honorably discharged veterans and military retirees in addition to active-duty, reserve, and National Guard members, plus their spouses and household dependents, as long as ID.me can verify eligibility.

#### Does Lowe's offer a first responder discount?
Not in the form of the everyday 10% off — that standing discount is for the military and veteran community only, so first responders do not qualify for it. What Lowe's does offer first responders is a **separate program (not a percentage discount)**: a free upgrade to MyLowe's Rewards Silver Key status, which includes free standard shipping on eligible orders, after verifying at Lowes.com/firstresponders through ID.me. Around National First Responders Day each October, Lowe's has occasionally added limited-time perks.

#### Is there an annual limit on the Lowe's military discount?
No. Unlike some programs that cap yearly savings, Lowe's applies the 10% military discount with **no annual spending limit**, so it can add up across larger projects on eligible purchases.

#### What is excluded from the Lowe's military discount?
Major appliances and parts, certain building commodities (electrical cable and wire, dimensional lumber, plywood, and OSB), services and installation, gift cards, and sale or clearance items are typically excluded. It also cannot be combined with other coupons or the 5% Lowe's credit card discount. Check Lowe's terms for the current list.

#### Can I use the Lowe's military discount on a Pro or business account?
No. The military discount is for personal, non-business purchases tied to a free MyLowe's Rewards account. Lowe's Pro and business accounts are not eligible.

#### Does Lowe's use ID.me or SheerID for its military discount?
Lowe's verifies the military discount through **ID.me**. Some older guides reference other providers, but the current program uses ID.me — follow the verification option shown on Lowe's official military validation page.
