# Las Vegas Strip Hotels — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **2-night leisure stay in a standard room at a Strip hotel** — **$300 pre-tax room subtotal** (2 nights × ~$150 standard rate). A daily **resort fee (~$45–$55/night)** and **Clark County room tax (~13.38% on the Strip)** are added on top of every path and are **not** discounted by any military rate. All room-rate math below is on the $300 room subtotal.

## Headline finding

"Las Vegas hotels with military discounts" has **no single answer** — the Strip is a set of independent operators, and only some publish a military rate:

1. **Caesars Entertainment** publishes the deepest verified offer: **up to 25% off the lowest available rate**, gated through **ID.me**, at its Vegas hotels (Caesars Palace, Flamingo, Harrah's, Horseshoe, NOBU, Paris, Planet Hollywood, The LINQ). This is usually the best *published* military rate on the Strip.
2. **MGM Resorts** runs a first-party **Military & Veterans Program (MVP)**: **10% off standard room rates** at ~13 Vegas resorts (Bellagio, ARIA, MGM Grand, Mandalay Bay, Luxor, Excalibur, New York-New York, Park MGM, etc.), verified **in person / at check-in** (no ID.me), plus a valuable **auto-upgrade to MGM Rewards Pearl status** (free self-parking), 25% spa, and 10% food/beverage.
3. **Wynn / Encore** and **The Venetian / Palazzo** publish **no military discount at all** — only public sales (Wynn "up to 30%," Venetian "up to 33%") and their own loyalty rates.

**The honest verdict:** the biggest lever is often *not* the military rate. Because hotel rates don't stack, a **live public Strip sale (Wynn/Venetian 30–33% off)** frequently beats both Caesars' 25% and MGM's 10% — and it's available to everyone. So the play is: **price-check the current public sale against the military rate and book the single lowest direct**, keep your loyalty status/points, and — if traveling **on official orders** — book the **government per-diem rate** instead. Archetype: **I — travel/loyalty (status stacks, rates don't)**, secondary **C** (the official military % is thin and routinely beaten by a public sale).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both operator + portal terms)
1. **Hotel rates never stack with each other.** A property's military rate, its public sale rate, its advance-purchase rate, and its loyalty rate are **mutually exclusive** — book the single lowest. Only **loyalty status/points** layer on top, and only when you **book direct** (OTAs forfeit both).
2. **Caesars' rate is ID.me-gated.** Identity-gated checkouts frequently **break cashback-portal tracking**, so "Caesars military rate + Rakuten" often does **not** stack. MGM's MVP rate, by contrast, is a plain online-booked rate and is generally portal-safe (Rakuten ~3%, as of Jun 2026 per prior MGM research).
3. **Military rates don't beat a deep public sale.** Because they can't combine, when Wynn or Venetian run "up to 30–33% off," that public price routinely undercuts Caesars' 25% and MGM's 10% — and anyone can book it.
4. **Resort fees and room tax are never discounted.** The military/veteran rate applies to the **room rate only**; the daily resort fee (~$45–$55) and ~13.38% Clark County room tax are added on top of every path.
5. **The per-diem rate is not a discount and not for leisure.** GSA per diem is the reimbursement ceiling for DoD/federal travelers on orders; leisure travelers can't book it.
6. **Caesars excludes packages and Rewards-member offers**; the discount applies to standard room rates, not to promotions bundling show tickets, food, or resort credits.

### FAQ

#### Do Las Vegas hotels offer a military discount?
Some do, but not all. **Caesars Entertainment** offers up to **25% off the lowest available rate** via ID.me at its Vegas hotels (Caesars Palace, Paris, Planet Hollywood, Flamingo, Harrah's, Horseshoe, NOBU, The LINQ). **MGM Resorts** offers **10% off standard room rates** at about 13 resorts through its Military & Veterans Program. **Wynn, Encore, The Venetian and The Palazzo publish no military discount** — you'd use their public sales instead.

#### Which Las Vegas hotel has the best military discount?
By published percentage, **Caesars (up to 25% off)** is the deepest — usually better than MGM's flat 10%. But because hotel rates don't stack, a live **public sale** (Wynn/Venetian often run 30–33% off) can beat any military rate, so always price-check the public rate before booking.

#### How much is the MGM military discount?
**10% off standard room rates** at ~13 MGM Las Vegas resorts, plus 25% off spa/salon, 10% off select food and beverage, and an automatic upgrade to **MGM Rewards Pearl status** (free self-parking, bonus Slot Dollars). Verify with a military ID, VA ID, DD-214, or a driver's license with veteran designation.

#### Does Caesars use ID.me for its military discount?
Yes. You verify as Military through **ID.me** on Caesars' military-discounts page, then book the discounted rate online. MGM, by contrast, does **not** use ID.me — it verifies with a military/veteran ID in person.

#### Do veterans and spouses qualify?
Yes at both operators. Caesars lists active duty, veterans, spouses/dependents, guard, reserves, retirees, and surviving spouses. MGM covers active duty, veterans, guard, reserve, retired military, and spouses of eligible personnel.

#### Does the military discount apply to resort fees and taxes?
No. The military/veteran rate applies to the **room rate only**. The daily resort fee (~$45–$55) and the ~13.38% Clark County room tax are added on top of every rate and are never discounted.

#### Can I combine a military rate with a sale or promo code?
No. Las Vegas hotel rates are mutually exclusive — you book the single lowest of the military, public-sale, advance-purchase, or loyalty rate. Only your loyalty status and points layer on, and only when you book direct.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to stay on the Strip?
Compare three things and book the lowest direct: (1) the current **public sale**, (2) **Caesars' up to 25% ID.me rate**, and (3) **MGM's 10% + Pearl status**. If you're traveling on official orders, book the **government per-diem rate** (Clark County FY2026: $126/night, or $159 Jan–Mar) instead.

#### Do Wynn or The Venetian have a military discount?
No. Neither Wynn/Encore nor The Venetian/Palazzo publishes a military or veteran rate — their offers pages list only public sales (Wynn up to 30%, Venetian up to 33%) and their own loyalty-member pricing. Book the public sale direct.

#### Do Las Vegas hotels run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day military sale?
No Strip-wide branded military sale turned up — Caesars' and MGM's military rates are **evergreen (year-round)**, not holiday promos. The deepest seasonal savings come from the **public** summer sales, which anyone can book.
