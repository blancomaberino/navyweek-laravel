# Skylight — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Skylight Calendar 2 (15")** at $279.99 (secondary look: the 27" Calendar Max at $599.99, and the Skylight Frame ~$159–$179). Note the separate **Skylight Plus** subscription ($79/yr) that unlocks some features.

## Headline finding

**Skylight has no military discount.** ID.me — the verification partner Skylight would use if it had one — explicitly says on its Skylight page: **"We're not aware that Skylight offers Military discounts."** There is no ID.me/GovX/SheerID/WeSalute military program, no year-round veteran percentage, and no standing military code. Any "Skylight military discount 30% with code OBSESSED" claim on a coupon site is an aggregator generalizing a public code — **not a verified military offer.**

Because there's no identity discount, a service member saves exactly the way everyone else does, and the paths are real and decent: **(1) the newsletter/first-order welcome discount** (Skylight offers a **$10-off welcome** for email signup), **(2) the referral program** (give $20 off, get a $20 credit), and **(3) seasonal sitewide sales** — Skylight runs meaningful markdowns around Black Friday/Cyber Monday, Mother's/Father's Day, and other holidays, and has historically surfaced **military-appreciation-timed public promos** (e.g., around Armed Forces Day/Veterans Day) that are open to everyone. Archetype: **B (no military route at all)** — the page's job is to correct the myth cleanly and hand readers the genuine public-savings playbook, plus the **watch-out on Skylight Plus** ($79/yr) so buyers know the true cost of ownership. Skylight isn't on GovX or the exchanges, so those paths don't apply.

### FAQ

#### Does Skylight offer a military discount?
No. Skylight has no military, veteran, or first-responder discount. ID.me — which Skylight would use to verify military status if it had a program — confirms it's "not aware that Skylight offers Military discounts."

#### Is there a Skylight military discount code?
No verified one. Codes like "OBSESSED" that aggregators label "military" are **public codes anyone can try**, not a military offer — and they may not be active. Use the newsletter, referral, and seasonal-sale paths instead.

#### Do veterans or first responders qualify for a Skylight discount?
There's no dedicated program for military, veterans, first responders, nurses, teachers, or students. Everyone uses the same public offers.

#### How do I verify my military status with Skylight?
You don't — there's no verification because there's no military discount. Skylight has no ID.me/GovX/SheerID/WeSalute military program.

No. Skylight has no military-verification partner, and it isn't sold on GovX.

Buy during a seasonal sale (Black Friday/Cyber Monday are deepest); otherwise use the $10 email welcome or a $20 referral (take the larger). Budget for Skylight Plus ($79/yr) if you want the gated features.
