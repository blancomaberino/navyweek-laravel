# Caesars Entertainment — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **2-night leisure stay in a standard room at a Las Vegas Caesars Rewards property** — **$300 pre-tax room subtotal** (2 nights × ~$150 standard rate). A daily resort fee (~$45–$55/night) and Clark County room tax (~13.38%) are added on top and are **not** discounted by any military rate. All room-rate math below is on the $300 room subtotal.

## Headline finding

Caesars Entertainment (caesars.com) runs a real, first-party **military hotel discount verified through ID.me** — no GovX, no in-cart coupon-hunting. The **standing, year-round rate is up to 15% off the lowest available room rate**, plus an **extra 10% off sale rates** for verified military (and other verified groups). Right now, tied to **America's 250th birthday**, Caesars has temporarily **boosted the headline to "up to 25% off lowest available hotel rate"** — the brand's own military page states *"In honor of America's 250th birthday get up to 25% off lowest available rate with ID.me verification"* (promo window reported as **July 1 – December 29, 2026**). Eligible groups are broad: active duty, veterans, military spouses/dependents, National Guard, Reserves, retirees, and surviving spouses (US and Canadian military). The discount applies to the **room rate only** and excludes Caesars Rewards loyalty-member offers and packages with add-ons (show tickets, food/resort credits).

Archetype: **I — travel/loyalty (status stacks, rates don't)**, with a strong **calendar/promotional flavor** (the 25% is a limited 2026 window on top of the 15% base). The two genuinely durable plays are (1) the **free, in-person Caesars Rewards Salute Card**, which grants **automatic Platinum status** (free self + valet parking at many destinations — often worth more than a thin room %) plus a standing **10% online military rate all year**, and (2) simply **booking the up-to-25% ID.me rate while the 250th promo runs**. Cashback portals are largely a dead end here: **Rakuten shows "No Cash Back" for Caesars as of June 2026**, and the ID.me-gated checkout typically breaks portal tracking anyway. Do **not** repeat the aggregator "**up to 35% off**" framing as a single military room rate — that number is a marketing ceiling that blends the base 15%, the extra-10%-off-sale-rates layer, and other verified audiences; the brand's own current headline is **25%** (promo) / **15%** (standing).

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal terms)
1. **Discounted room rates are mutually exclusive** — you book **one** rate (the ID.me military rate, the Salute 10% rate, a public sale rate, or a Rewards member offer), not several stacked room discounts. Pick the lowest.
2. **The military rate excludes Caesars Rewards loyalty-member offers and packages with add-ons** (show tickets, food/resort credits) — verified on the brand's military page.
3. **Discount is room-rate only** — resort fees and room tax are **never** discounted.
4. **The "extra 10% off sale rates" layer** applies on top of a flagged sale rate for verified groups (per ID.me shop) — this is the one built-in "stack," and it's the source of the aggregators' inflated ceiling numbers.
5. **Cashback generally does NOT stack** — Rakuten shows no Caesars cash back (as of Jun 2026), and the ID.me identity gate typically breaks portal tracking where a rate exists. Don't promise a cashback double-dip.
6. **Platinum status (via Salute Card) stacks on everything** — it's a loyalty tier, not a rate. Free self/valet parking and presale access apply on top of whatever rate you book. This is the durable win.
7. **Book direct** — OTAs forfeit Caesars Rewards credit, Salute/Platinum perks, and best-rate/cancellation terms.
