# Blackstone Products — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Blackstone 28" Original Griddle with Hard Cover, **$349 MSRP** on blackstoneproducts.com (the direct site's own price).

## Headline finding

Blackstone Products (outdoor griddles/cookware, blackstoneproducts.com) **does** run an official, first-party military discount: **5% off**, verified through **GovX ID**, delivered as a **single-use code** you apply at checkout on the brand's own site. Eligible groups are broad — current and former U.S. military, military spouses and dependents, first responders (fire, law enforcement, EMS), and federal/state/local government employees — but the discount is **small, direct-site-only, and does not stack** with sale/clearance items or other promos.

The honest maximum-savings story is that **the 5% direct-site discount is usually the *worst* way for a service member to buy a Blackstone.** Blackstone is a mass-market brand sold everywhere, and third-party retailers (Walmart, Costco, Amazon, Lowe's, Best Buy) routinely price the same griddles **well below** blackstoneproducts.com's MSRP — often $50–$125 cheaper than the direct price *before* the 5% is even applied. And Blackstone is stocked by the **military exchanges** (AAFES `shopmyexchange.com` / Navy Exchange `mynavyexchange.com`), where eligible shoppers pay **no sales tax** and can use price-match. Archetype: **C (real discount, but public retail beats it) + L (exchange-carried, tax-free path wins)**. Take the 5% only when you're buying a direct-only item at full price and no retailer or exchange has it cheaper.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 5% GovX ID code does not stack** with sale/clearance items, gift cards, or any other promotional offer — Blackstone's checkout takes **one code per order** and auto-applied "Deal of the Week"/bundle pricing can't be combined with an input code.
2. **GovX ID code ≠ cashback stack.** The GovX code is an identity-gated, single-use code; cashback portals (Rakuten ~2.5% as of Jul 14, 2026) commonly **void or reduce** cashback on coupon-code orders, and identity-gated checkouts frequently break portal tracking. Do **not** assume "5% code + Rakuten." Pick one — the 5% code beats 2.5% cashback anyway.
3. **GovX marketplace is not a second discount.** GovX's Blackstone listings redeem the offer **on blackstoneproducts.com** — it's the same 5% path, not an additional or deeper cut.
4. **Retailer and exchange prices are substitutes, not stacks** — you can't add the direct-site 5% to a Walmart or Exchange purchase; you choose the cheapest channel.

Yes. Blackstone Products offers **5% off** on blackstoneproducts.com for verified military, first responders, and government employees, through GovX ID.

#### How much is the Blackstone military discount?
**5% off** direct-site orders. Coupon sites claiming 10%, 20%, or more are inflating it — the official GovX ID military discount is 5%.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The 5% is **online only**, on blackstoneproducts.com. There's no documented in-store first-party military discount. If you're exchange-eligible, buy tax-free at the military Exchange instead.

#### What is excluded?
Sale/clearance items, gift cards, and taxes. The code can't be combined with other promos, and it's one code per order.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Blackstone's checkout takes one code per order, and the military code excludes sale/clearance and other promotional offers.

#### Does Blackstone offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes for **first responders** (fire, law enforcement, EMS) and **government employees** (federal/state/local) — each verifies via GovX ID for its own 5% code. Teachers, nurses, and students are **not** listed as eligible groups for Blackstone's offer.

#### Can I buy Blackstone at the Exchange?
Yes — AAFES (`shopmyexchange.com`) and the Navy Exchange (`mynavyexchange.com`) stock Blackstone griddles. It's tax-free for eligible shoppers and often the best-value path.
