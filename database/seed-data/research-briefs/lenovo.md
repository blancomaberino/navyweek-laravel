# Lenovo Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Lenovo (US) runs an everyday military and veteran discount delivered through **ID.me** verification, applied in the shopping cart on lenovo.com. Lenovo's own discount pages confirm the **eligible groups** — "First Responders, active U.S. military members, reservists, veterans, and immediate family" — and confirm the program is **ID.me-verified, applied in cart, sitewide, excluding doorbusters and select clearance products.** The single most important caveat: **Lenovo does not publish the exact military discount percentage on a crawlable (non-JavaScript) page.** Lenovo's static discount-program content lists the *healthcare, first-responder, and senior* tiers at "an extra 5% off sitewide," and the page's meta description caps savings at "up to an additional 7% off," but the **military-specific percentage is not confirmable from a primary source.** Third parties (Veteran.com, ID.me Shop editorial) state 5% off, but this is not primary-verified and is flagged accordingly. The page should present the offer mechanics and eligibility with full confidence, and present the percentage as "verify in cart to see your exact savings" rather than asserting a number Lenovo does not publish.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to lenovo.com (US) and add the eligible PC, laptop, or accessory to your cart.
2. In the cart, choose the Military (or First Responder/Student/Senior) discount option and click **Verify with ID.me**.
3. Sign in to or create your ID.me account and verify your military/veteran status.
4. Once verified, the discount is applied to eligible products in your cart. (Some flows generate a promo code to paste into the cart; follow the on-screen prompt.)
5. Review the discounted total, confirm exclusions (doorbusters/select clearance won't discount), and check out.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Lenovo's military/veteran discount is an online program on lenovo.com; Lenovo does not operate consumer retail stores for this offer, and no in-store redemption path is stated in the official source.

### How Verification Works
Lenovo uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. Verification happens **in the shopping cart** — you select your group (Military/First Responder/Student/Senior), then verify with ID.me. An ID.me account is required (you can create one during checkout). ID.me may ask for documentation such as a military ID or service records to confirm status; the exact documents are determined by ID.me, not Lenovo. Lenovo's pages do not state a re-verification interval for the military program; note that Lenovo's *education* success message references benefits "for a full year," which implies ID.me verifications can be time-limited — do not promise permanent or one-time-only verification. If automatic verification fails, ID.me's support handles status issues; Lenovo customer support can help with applying the discount in cart.

### FAQ

#### Does Lenovo offer a military discount?
Yes. Lenovo offers an everyday discount to active-duty military, reservists, veterans, and their immediate family (and to first responders) on lenovo.com, verified through ID.me in the shopping cart. (Source: Lenovo discount-programs pages.)

#### How much is the Lenovo military discount?
Lenovo does not publish the exact military percentage on its public, crawlable discount page, and the rate can change with current promotions. Lenovo's related healthcare, first-responder, and senior tiers are shown at "an extra 5% off sitewide," and the program is advertised as "up to an additional 7% off." Third-party sites report 5% off for military, but that figure is not confirmed by Lenovo directly — the reliable way to see your rate is to verify with ID.me in your cart.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family members qualify?
Yes. Lenovo's discount page names "active U.S. military members, reservists, veterans, and immediate family" as eligible. Dependents/spouses qualify to the extent they are immediate family; surviving spouses and Gold Star families are not specifically addressed.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Add an item to your cart on lenovo.com, select the Military discount, and click Verify with ID.me. You'll sign in to or create an ID.me account and confirm your status; ID.me determines acceptable documentation (e.g., military ID or service records).

#### Does Lenovo use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Lenovo uses **ID.me** for in-cart verification of its military, first-responder, healthcare, senior, and education discounts. Lenovo has also been listed as a **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage) verification partner since 2018 for WeSalute+ members, a separate membership program.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. This is an online discount applied on lenovo.com. There is no in-store redemption path stated by Lenovo.

#### What is excluded?
Doorbusters and select clearance products are excluded. Pricing also excludes tax, shipping/handling, and recycling fees. A per-customer quantity limit and resale prohibition are reported by third parties — confirm in Lenovo's live cart terms.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Lenovo indicates verified discounts apply on top of instant savings on eligible products, but combinability with separate promo codes is not clearly stated. Doorbusters and select clearance are excluded. Confirm in-cart before purchase.

#### Does Lenovo offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes for first responders, nurses/healthcare workers, teachers, students, and seniors 55+ — each runs through ID.me verification in the cart, with related tiers shown at an extra 5% off sitewide. A dedicated government-employee discount is not stated.
