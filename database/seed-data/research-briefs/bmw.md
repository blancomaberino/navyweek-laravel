# BMW Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

BMW of North America runs the **BMW Military and First Responder Incentive** (marketed as the "BMW Military Program"): **$500–$5,000 toward a new vehicle lease or purchase**, with the exact amount varying by model. It is open to Military, Veterans, Military Family Members, and First Responders. Eligibility is confirmed online through **ID.me**; once verified, the customer submits a lead form and receives a unique Customer ID and offer code that an authorized BMW Center applies toward a new lease or purchase. The single most important caveat: this is **not a fixed percentage and not a self-serve coupon** — the incentive amount is per-model (e.g., the 3 Series shows up to $2,500; the iX up to $5,000), it is redeemed only through a BMW Center, and BMW does not publish a master per-model table; amounts shown are examples pulled from the official offer page on the access date and change over time. A separate **USAA member vehicle offer** (commonly cited at up to $2,000) exists but is documented primarily on BMW dealer pages, not on the core bmwusa.com program page, and is treated here as a distinct, lower-confidence item.

### Intro
BMW of North America offers the **BMW Military and First Responder Incentive** — $500 to $5,000 toward the lease or purchase of a new BMW, with the exact amount depending on the model you choose. It is open to active-duty service members, National Guard and Reserves, veterans, retirees, military spouses, surviving spouses, immediate military family members, and first responders.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the BMW Military Incentive page (bmwusa.com/special-offers/military-incentive.html).
2. Select your vehicle of interest and click "Verify Eligibility & Claim Offer" / "Claim Offer."
3. Complete the ID.me verification for your eligible segment (active duty, veteran, spouse, first responder, etc.).
4. Once verified, submit the lead form.
5. Receive a confirmation email with a unique Customer ID and offer code.
6. Provide the Customer ID and offer code to your authorized BMW Center, which applies the incentive toward your new lease or purchase.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Complete ID.me verification and the lead form online first (the incentive is generated digitally, not issued at the counter).
2. Bring your confirmation email with the unique Customer ID and offer code to an authorized BMW Center.
3. The BMW Center applies the military and first responder incentive toward your new vehicle lease or purchase.
(Note: BMW does not describe an in-person-only path; verification happens online before the dealer step.)

### How Verification Works
BMW uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You verify before claiming, choosing the segment that matches your status (Active Duty, National Guard, Reserves, Veterans, Retirees, Military Spouses, Surviving Spouses, Immediate Military Family Members, or First Responders). Verification typically takes a few minutes; if it stalls, BMW directs users to ID.me customer support (help.id.me). Account creation with ID.me is required to complete verification. BMW does not state a re-verification frequency on the program page; ID.me may require re-authentication per its own policies, so do not assume a one-time-forever check.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Available on **new** BMW vehicles (lease or purchase); the program page does not extend it to pre-owned/CPO.
- "Most BMW models including high-performance models and SUVs" are eligible, but **some more exclusive models are excluded** — the page does not publish the exact excluded list and directs buyers to their BMW Center.
- The BMW i4, i5, i7, and iX (all-electric) are confirmed currently eligible.
- **Cannot be combined with the BMW Corporate Sales Program**; can be combined with most other offers/campaigns.
- Amount is **per-model** within the $500–$5,000 range; BMW does not publish a master per-model table, and amounts change over time.
- Geographic scope: U.S. (personnel and families "stationed in the United States").
- A separate **USAA member vehicle offer** (reported up to $2,000) appears on BMW dealer pages but is not detailed on the core bmwusa.com program page — verify terms directly with USAA and a BMW Center before relying on it.

Yes. BMW of North America runs the BMW Military and First Responder Incentive, offering $500–$5,000 toward a new BMW lease or purchase depending on the model.

It ranges from $500 to $5,000 and varies by model. #### Can I combine it with other offers or promo codes?
