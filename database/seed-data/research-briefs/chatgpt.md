**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ChatGPT Plus subscription at **$20/month ($240/year)** — the single realistic "cart" for a consumer AI subscription. Prices are subscription fees (no sales tax modeled; digital-service tax varies by state).

## Headline finding

Contrary to the common assumption that OpenAI has no military benefit, **there IS one real, official offer — but it is narrow.** On **November 10, 2025** (ahead of Veterans Day) OpenAI launched **one free year of ChatGPT Plus** (a $240 value) for **transitioning U.S. servicemembers and recently-separated veterans only** — specifically active-duty members within 12 months of separation/retirement and veterans within their first ~12 months post-service — verified through **SheerID** at **chatgpt.com/veterans-claim**. This is a **transition-assistance giveaway**, not an ongoing percentage discount: there is **no standing military discount on ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Go, Team/Business, or Enterprise**, no discount for the broader active-duty or veteran population outside the transition window, and no annual-billing discount on Plus (Plus is month-to-month only). The brand archetype is closest to **E (identity-verified benefit via SheerID)**, but delivered as a **limited-window free year rather than a recurring discount**. The best (and only) path for an eligible transitioning member is to **claim the free year**; everyone else's best "save" is the **capable free tier** — the "ChatGPT Plus military discount codes" circulating on coupon sites are **fake** (OpenAI's billing does not accept generic coupon codes).

### FAQ

#### Does ChatGPT (OpenAI) offer a military discount?
Not a standing discount. OpenAI offers **one free year of ChatGPT Plus** for **transitioning servicemembers and recently-separated veterans** (active-duty within 12 months of separation, or veterans within ~12 months of leaving service), verified via SheerID at chatgpt.com/veterans-claim. There's no ongoing percentage discount for the general military population.

#### How much is the ChatGPT military discount?
For eligible transitioning members it's effectively **100% off for 12 months** — a $240 value (Plus is normally $20/month). For everyone else, there is no military price.

#### Do all veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
No. The program targets members **within about 12 months of separation** and veterans **within their first ~12 months out**. Veterans separated longer ago, and spouses/dependents, are not stated as eligible — verify in SheerID before assuming coverage.

#### How do I verify my status?
At **chatgpt.com/veterans-claim** through **SheerID** — upload your DD-214 (separated) or pending-separation orders (active-duty). It takes about 2–3 minutes; you then get a code for the free year.

#### Does ChatGPT use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**SheerID** is the verification provider for the veterans offer. There is no ChatGPT GovX storefront, no ID.me/WeSalute military discount, and no cashback portal — it's a first-party subscription.

#### Can I use a military discount code on ChatGPT Plus?
No. **OpenAI's billing does not accept generic coupon/promo codes.** Any "ChatGPT Plus military discount code" on a coupon or Reddit page is fake or expired. The only legitimate benefit is the SheerID free-year for eligible transitioning members.

#### What is excluded?
Everything outside the transition offer: no discount on Pro, Go, Team, or Enterprise; no annual-billing discount on Plus; no benefit for veterans outside the ~12-month window. Post-free-year renewal terms aren't published.

#### Can I combine it with other offers or an annual plan?
No. The free year is standalone, no codes stack, and ChatGPT Plus has no annual plan to discount — it's billed monthly at $20.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to use ChatGPT?
If you're transitioning, the **free year** (chatgpt.com/veterans-claim). If not, the **free tier** — it's capable enough for most transition and everyday tasks; step up to Go or pay standard Plus only if you need more.

#### Does OpenAI offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No dedicated consumer military-adjacent discount was found. OpenAI has run separate limited-time student promotions in the past, but that's not a military/first-responder program.
