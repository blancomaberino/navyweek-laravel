## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **There is no military discount to stack.** Any "military code" you find for pacsun.com is invented; entering a scraped code that isn't portal-listed also **voids TopCashback** ("use of a coupon code not displayed on TopCashback"). *(topcashback.com/pacsun/, accessed July 7, 2026)*
2. **Rewards layer on everything.** Points accrue on qualifying purchases in-store, online, and in-app, including sale purchases; the $5 signup reward is spendable online, in-store, or in-app. *(pacsun.com/styleguide-pacsun-rewards-guide.html)*
3. **UNiDAYS 10% is an "extra" discount with brand exclusions** (Fear of God, John Galt, Free People, Crocs, etc.) and **does not apply to taxes**; whether it combines with promo codes is **unstated** — assume one code field, test at checkout, never assert the stack. *(myunidays.com PacSun terms)*
4. **`APP20` is app-only and select-styles** — "Valid in app only… Valid on select styles and brands. Savings not applicable to taxes." Using it (off-portal code) forfeits cashback on that order. *(pacsun.com/promotions/)*
5. **Cashback math:** TopCashback pays 1% on item price only (excl. tax/shipping), excludes Fear of God, John Galt, and PS Reserve products, and requires starting with an empty cart. Rakuten's PacSun rate was unconfirmed this pass — link, don't quote a number. *(topcashback.com/pacsun/)*
6. **APO/FPO trap:** PacSun's own fine print — free-shipping offers **exclude military APO/FPO addresses** (and AK/HI/PR). Deployed or OCONUS readers should expect to pay shipping even during "free shipping" events. *(pacsun.com/promotions/)*

### FAQ

#### Does PacSun offer a military discount?
No. PacSun has no military discount for any group, online or in stores. ID.me states it isn't aware of any PacSun military offer, and pacsun.com has no military program page.

#### Is the "PacSun 10%–25% military discount" real?
No — that claim comes from coupon aggregators, one of which admits on the same page that "there is no official PacSun military program." There is no military code or verification gate at pacsun.com.

#### Do veterans or military spouses get a PacSun discount?
No. No military-affiliated group has a PacSun discount. College-enrolled military dependents can use the UNiDAYS student 10%.

#### Does PacSun use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. PacSun's only identity-verification discount is the **UNiDAYS student 10%**. ID.me lists PacSun only for everyone-eligible affiliate cashback, not a verified discount.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to shop PacSun?
Buy during a sitewide sale (up to 50% off; markdowns to 70%), with PacSun Rewards active (~4% back + $5 signup), and use the app-only `APP20` extra 20% when your items qualify.

#### Does PacSun give a student discount?
Yes — an extra 10% online through UNiDAYS verification, with brand exclusions (Fear of God, John Galt, Free People, Crocs, and others) and no application to taxes.

#### Can I stack PacSun promo codes with cashback?
Usually not — TopCashback voids cashback on any code not listed on its site. Use cashback on code-free sale orders.

#### Does PacSun run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No military-specific promotion was found in PacSun's history. Its public seasonal sales (July 4, Black Friday) often overlap holiday weekends and are open to everyone.

#### Does free shipping work for APO/FPO addresses?
No — PacSun's free-shipping terms exclude shipments to military APO/FPO addresses (and AK, HI, PR).
