# Royal Caribbean Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Royal Caribbean offers a **reduced "Military Rate" — a lower cruise fare on select sailings**, not a flat percentage off everything. The rate is applied **per stateroom** at booking: at least one guest in the stateroom must be eligible (active duty, retiree, veteran with an Honorable Discharge, or the spouse of a deployed/deceased service member), and that eligible person must occupy the stateroom. There is **no instant online coupon code on Royal Caribbean's own site** — the rate appears as a special fare and **eligibility is verified by document check at check-in** (military ID, DD-214, etc.), not before booking. The single most important caveat: Royal Caribbean does **not publish a guaranteed percentage** — military fares vary by sailing and may not always beat the best public promo, and **noncompliance triggers a re-price to the lowest available fare plus the difference, or denied boarding**.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregators advertise **"up to 50% off published fares"** and tell shoppers to book price code **"MIL," "PFD," "MILITARY," or "MILITARY NRD."** Royal Caribbean's **official FAQ does not state any percentage or any price code.** Those figures and codes are **trade/aggregator-sourced and unverified on RoyalCaribbean.com** — do not assert them as Royal Caribbean's published terms. Separately, the verification partner **GOVX** markets a "60% off the second guest" framing; that is GOVX's offer presentation, not a Royal Caribbean FAQ term. Treat the percentage and codes as **how the trade describes availability**, flagged, not as official fact.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Eligible (US Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, National Guard, Reserves; Canadian National Defense).
- **Veterans** — Eligible with **Honorable Discharge**, minimum 2 years' service (or 6 months in an active war zone); show DD 214 + photo.
- **Retirees** — Eligible (defined as 20+ years' service, medically retired, or 100% disabled).
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible (named among active divisions).
- **Military spouses** — Eligible **only** as the spouse of *actively deployed* personnel booking one stateroom they occupy (Pink/Tan Dependent ID). Otherwise a spouse is not independently eligible without the service member in the room.
- **Surviving spouses** — Eligible (spouses of deceased military personnel; Pink/Tan Dependent ID).
- **Dependents / children / parents / in-laws / friends** — **Not** eligible on their own; they sail at the military rate only when sharing the eligible person's stateroom.
- **First responders (police/fire/EMS)** — Eligible under a **separate** police/fire rate, not the military rate (active or retired law enforcement, fire, or EMS in US/Canada; badge/ID + second ID).
- **Teachers / Students / Government employees / Medical workers** — Not listed for the military rate (medical workers not covered; teachers/students/government not stated).

### How Verification Works
Royal Caribbean verifies the military rate by **inspecting eligibility documents at check-in**, not through an online verification provider on its own website. Each category has a specific accepted ID (see Who Qualifies). Royal Caribbean also runs **random audits at any time throughout the year**, and proof of eligibility must be in your possession when requested. If documentation is missing or invalid, the fare can revert to full retail and boarding may be denied. Some shoppers reach the rate through **GOVX**, a third-party government/military-verification marketplace that markets Royal Caribbean military fares and applies a code at booking — but that is a separate channel, and the at-check-in document requirement still governs.

### FAQ

#### Does Royal Caribbean offer a military discount?
Yes. Royal Caribbean offers a reduced **military rate** — a special, lower cruise fare on **select sailings** — for eligible active duty, retirees, Honorable-Discharge veterans, and qualifying spouses.

#### How much is the Royal Caribbean military discount?
Royal Caribbean does **not publish a fixed percentage**. The military rate is a special fare that varies by ship and sailing. (Aggregator claims of "up to 50% off" are not stated on Royal Caribbean's official FAQ.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify with an **Honorable Discharge** (2 years' service, or 6 months in a war zone) and must show a **DD 214 plus a current photo**. Spouses qualify only as the spouse of *actively deployed* or *deceased* military personnel, booking one stateroom they occupy. Other dependents, children, parents, and friends qualify **only** when sharing the eligible person's stateroom.

#### How do I verify my military status?
You don't verify in advance on Royal Caribbean's own site — you **show your eligibility documents at check-in** (military ID, DD 214, or dependent ID by category). Random audits can occur at any time, so keep your proof on hand.

#### Does Royal Caribbean use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Royal Caribbean's **official FAQ relies on a document check at check-in**, not an online provider. **GOVX** is a third-party government/military marketplace that markets Royal Caribbean military fares and applies a code at booking, but it isn't named as the verifier in RC's FAQ. ID.me, SheerID, and WeSalute are not stated.

#### How do I book the military rate?
Search your sailing on RoyalCaribbean.com, by phone (866-562-7625), or through a travel advisor; select the military rate if it's available for that sailing, with the eligible person as a stateroom occupant. (Price codes like "MIL"/"PFD" cited on aggregator sites are not published on RC's own FAQ.)

#### What is excluded?
The rate covers **one stateroom** the eligible person occupies; no extra rooms qualify, and family/friends don't qualify without the eligible person present. If the room doesn't comply, Royal Caribbean re-prices it to the lowest available fare (you pay the difference) or denies boarding.

#### Can I combine it with other offers or sale fares?
Per Royal Caribbean's promotions terms, the military rate (a "restricted discount") **is combinable** with Promo Code Offers, Crown & Anchor discounts, and Shareholder Benefits, but **not** with Standard Group, Travel Agent, Net, Interline, casino, or NextCruise offers.

#### Does Royal Caribbean offer a first responder, teacher, or government discount?
Yes for **first responders** — police, fire, and EMS (active, retired, or volunteer, US/Canada) have a **separate** restricted rate on select sailings, shown with a badge/ID plus a second ID. Teacher, student, government, and medical-worker rates are **not** listed.
