# Verizon Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Verizon offers a **per-account monthly plan discount** to verified military members and veterans — not a flat percentage. On its current Unlimited plans the discount is **$10/mo off 1 phone line, $25/mo off 2-3 lines, or $20/mo off 4+ lines** (a per-account dollar amount, not per-line); most other advertised plans instead get **15% off the plan's account access charges**. Verizon adds **25% off accessories** via My Verizon (**10%** in-store, Apple excluded) and a separate monthly discount on **Fios Home Internet** in its limited Fios footprint. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me** and covers **US active duty, retirees, veterans, current or former Guard/Reserve, and Gold Star family members/next of kin**. The single most important caveat: the discount applies to **up to 4 lines per account**, it **cannot be combined** with Deals at Work, Student, or other "Those Who Serve" (first responder/nurse/teacher) discounts, and **prepaid and promotional plans are excluded**. Spouses and dependents are **not listed** as eligible on their own (only Gold Star next of kin) — flag this.

> Accuracy note: Many coupon/aggregator pages describe the Verizon military discount as a simple "15% off" or quote outdated per-line figures. The current structure is the **$10 / $25 / $20 per-account** tiered amount on current Unlimited plans, with **15%** applying only to "most other advertised plans." Both figures are confirmed on Verizon's own support page as of June 23, 2026. Dollar amounts on Verizon's marketing pages assume **Auto Pay + paper-free billing** and exclude taxes/fees.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **verizon.com/military**.
2. Click **Verify military status** to start the **ID.me** verification (you'll create or sign in to an ID.me account; ID.me may ask you to upload a document such as a military ID or DD-214).
3. Once validated, you're routed to the Discounts by Verizon page.
4. Choose **New to Verizon** or **Already with Verizon** and follow the prompts to apply the discount.
5. The discount appears on your bill within **1-2 billing cycles**. (For Fios, follow the same flow from the military page or your My Verizon Plans page; the $99 setup fee is waived when you order online.)

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit a **Verizon Company Store** (find one at verizon.com/stores).
2. Tell the associate you want to register for the military discount.
3. Verify your eligibility (military, veteran, or first responder).
4. The associate helps apply the discount to your eligible plan/account.

### How Verification Works
Verizon uses **ID.me** to confirm military eligibility. You create a free ID.me account and verify your status; ID.me may require a validating document (e.g., military ID, orders, or DD-214 for Gold Star next of kin). The person registering must be both the military/Gold Star member **and** the Verizon Account Owner or Account Manager. There's **no annual renewal** — Verizon states eligible members generally keep the discount, though Verizon may occasionally re-verify status, and the Fios discount lasts as long as you keep a qualifying plan and verified ID.me status. Verizon notes it will never contact you unprompted to ask for sensitive account information. For help, call 1-800-VERIZON (1-800-837-4966).

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Prepaid plans and promotional plans are not eligible** for the discount program.

### FAQ

#### Does Verizon offer a military discount?
Yes. Verizon offers monthly military discounts on wireless plans, accessories, and Fios home internet to verified service members and veterans, verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the Verizon military discount?
On Verizon's current Unlimited plans it's a per-account amount: **$10/mo off 1 line, $25/mo off 2-3 lines, or $20/mo off 4+ lines**. Most other advertised plans get **15% off the plan's account access charges** instead. Accessories are 25% off via My Verizon (10% in store, Apple excluded).

#### Is the Verizon military discount a percentage or a dollar amount?
Both, depending on the plan. Current Unlimited plans use the fixed $10/$25/$20 per-account amounts; "most other advertised plans" use 15% off account access charges.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify. So do active duty, retirees, current or former Guard/Reserve, and Gold Star family members/next of kin. Verizon's military FAQ does **not** list military spouses or dependents as independently eligible for the discount, so verify directly through ID.me if that's your situation.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to verizon.com/military, click **Verify military status**, and complete **ID.me** verification (you may need to upload a document). The person registering must also be the Verizon Account Owner or Account Manager.

#### Does Verizon use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Verizon's official military registration uses **ID.me**. (Verizon also partners with WeSalute as a supporting organization, but the discount is verified through ID.me.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Military members, veterans and first responders can register and apply the discount at a Verizon Company Store, in addition to online.

#### Is there a Fios home internet military discount?
Yes — a monthly discount on eligible Fios plans (300 Mbps, 500 Mbps, 1 Gig, 2 Gig), plus the $99 setup fee waived when you order online. Fios is only available in parts of NJ, NY, MD, DE, MA, PA, RI, VA and Washington DC.

#### What is excluded, and can I combine it with other discounts?
Prepaid and promotional plans are excluded, Apple accessories are excluded, and the mobile discount covers up to 4 lines. It can't be combined with Deals at Work, Student, or other Those Who Serve discounts — but it can stack with Auto Pay + paper-free billing and the Mobile + Home discount.

#### Do I have to renew the discount every year?
No. Verizon states eligible members generally keep the discount with no annual renewal, though Verizon may occasionally re-verify your status.
