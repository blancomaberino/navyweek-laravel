## Executive Summary

Budget Rent A Car offers a military/veteran car rental discount of **up to 35% off "Pay Now" rates** in the contiguous U.S. (with special rates in Canada), delivered through its long-running partnership with **WeSalute** (the verification service founded as Veterans Advantage). Eligibility covers active-duty, retirees, veterans, current/former National Guard and Reserve, and their immediate family members — but the discount is gated: you must be an enrolled **WeSalute+** (paid) subscriber to receive Budget's BCD (Budget Customer Discount) number and apply it to a reservation. There is no free, publicly posted military percentage on budget.com; the savings flow through the WeSalute+ membership. The single most important caveat: the headline is "up to" 35%, applies only to time-and-mileage on Pay Now rates (not taxes, fees, LDW, or car group X), and is subject to blackout dates and participating-location limits.

### Intro
Budget Rent A Car thanks U.S. service members and veterans with a car rental discount of up to 35% off "Pay Now" rates, offered through its partnership with WeSalute (the verification organization founded as Veterans Advantage). The discount is open to active-duty, retirees, veterans, current and former National Guard and Reserve, and their immediate family members.

There's a catch worth knowing up front: the savings are delivered through a paid **WeSalute+** membership. Once you enroll and verify your service, WeSalute+ provides Budget's BCD (Budget Customer Discount) number, which you add to your reservation on budget.com to unlock the rate. "Up to 35%" is a ceiling, not a guarantee — it applies only to time-and-mileage on Pay Now rates and excludes taxes, fees, and add-ons.

This is an independent guide, not affiliated with Budget. Budget and WeSalute control the terms and can change them at any time; verify current details on the official pages linked below before booking.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Confirm you qualify, then enroll in **WeSalute+** at wesalute.com and complete service verification to obtain Budget's BCD number.
2. Go to budget.com and start a reservation — select your destination and rental dates.
3. Add your Budget **BCD number** to the reservation (the offer/discount code field).
4. After clicking "Select My Car," confirm the discount applied via the drop-down under "Lower Rates / This BCD."
5. If you see "Your offer code is invalid," click "Learn Why?"; the rate may be blocked by a blackout date or a non-participating location.

### How To Redeem In Store
Budget's military offer is applied at the time of booking (online or by phone), not as a walk-up code at the counter. Budget's own terms state the discount "can only be applied at the time of booking and is not available retroactively." At pickup:
1. Have your reservation/confirmation that already includes the BCD number.
2. Present a valid driver's license and a credit card meeting Budget's rental requirements.
3. Be prepared to show WeSalute+ ID/credentials if requested; family/eligibility verification may apply.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **WeSalute** (founded as Veterans Advantage), not by ID.me, GovX, or SheerID. You enroll in the paid **WeSalute+** tier, verify your military status (WeSalute issues a secure WeSalute+ ID Card so you don't share your military ID directly with each merchant), and WeSalute+ provides the Budget BCD number used to apply the rate. Treat WeSalute+ as an ongoing paid membership — eligibility/benefits persist while the membership is active; confirm current membership cost and any re-verification requirements on WeSalute's site before relying on it.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- "Up to 35%" applies to Budget **Pay Now** rates and only to **time-and-mileage** charges.
- Does **not** apply to **car group X** (specialty/premium group).
- Taxes, concession recovery fees, vehicle license recovery fee, and customer facility charges are extra.
- Optional products (LDW $29.99/day or less, refueling) are extra.
- U.S. and Canadian residents only; rentals at participating U.S./Canada locations.
- **May not be combined with any other BCD number, promotion, or offer** (per Budget terms). Note: the WeSalute offer page states the discount can be combined with certain Budget *public* promotions (dollars off, free upgrades) but **not** with private corporate coupon codes — treat combinability cautiously and verify at checkout.
- Weekly rates require a minimum 5-day rental; weekend rate rules and possible Saturday-night-keep apply.
- Holiday and other blackout periods may apply; subject to vehicle availability; offer subject to change without notice.
- Renter must meet Budget age, driver, and credit requirements; under-25 surcharge may apply.

### FAQ

#### Does Budget offer a military discount?
Yes. Budget offers a military/veteran car rental discount of up to 35% off "Pay Now" rates through its WeSalute (Veterans Advantage) partnership, available to enrolled WeSalute+ members.

#### How much is the Budget military discount?
Up to 35% off Budget "Pay Now" rates (time-and-mileage only), and up to 25% off daily/weekly retail rates per the WeSalute offer page. "Up to" is a ceiling — actual savings vary by location, dates, and car class.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Eligibility includes active-duty, retirees, veterans, current/former Guard and Reserve, and immediate family members, verified through WeSalute+.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through WeSalute (founded as Veterans Advantage). Enroll in WeSalute+, verify your military service, and use the Budget BCD number WeSalute+ provides when you book.

#### Does Budget use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
WeSalute. Budget's military offer is delivered through WeSalute (Veterans Advantage), not ID.me, GovX, or SheerID.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / at the counter?
The discount is applied when you book (online or by phone), not as a walk-up counter code. Budget says it can only be applied at booking and is not retroactive. Bring your reservation and WeSalute+ credentials to pickup.

#### What is excluded?
Car group X, taxes, concession/vehicle-license-recovery fees, customer facility charges, LDW, and refueling. Blackout dates and non-participating locations apply.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Budget's terms say it can't be combined with another BCD number, promotion, or offer. The WeSalute page indicates it may combine with some Budget public promotions but not private corporate coupons. Verify at checkout — combinability is inconsistent across sources.

#### Does Budget offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount?
Nurses/medical professionals have a separate Budget offer through WeSalute. First responders, teachers, government/civil servants, and students are listed by WeSalute as "Coming Soon" and are not currently covered by the military track.

#### Is there a separate BCD code I can just type in for free?
Budget does not publish a free public military BCD code. Aggregators cite "X612500," and Budget's offer-application link references that code, but the discount is gated behind WeSalute+ enrollment — do not rely on a free code from coupon sites.
