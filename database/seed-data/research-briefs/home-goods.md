# HomeGoods — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$150 home-decor cart** at HomeGoods markdown prices (mix of decor + a small furniture/kitchen item).

## Headline finding

**HomeGoods does not offer a military or veteran discount** — and neither does any store in the TJX family (T.J. Maxx, Marshalls, Homesense, Sierra). There is no service-member percentage, no military promo code, and no military verification (ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute) on homegoods.com or in stores; HomeGoods is not listed as an ID.me military partner, and WorthEPenny states plainly there is **"no official HomeGoods military program."** Because HomeGoods is already an off-price retailer, the genuine savings paths are open to everyone: the **TJX Rewards credit card** (5% back in Rewards across the TJX family; $10 certificate per 1,000 points, plus 10% off your first purchase), fresh-markdown timing, and — for online orders — **free shipping on $119+** (a public threshold with code `SHIP119`, **not** a military perk). Cash-back portals only thinly cover homegoods.com (~1.5% where listed). Archetype: **B — no military route at all**, off-price + store card.
