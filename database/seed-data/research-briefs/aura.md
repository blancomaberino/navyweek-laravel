# Aura — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **annual Aura Family plan** (standard introductory **~$300/year, i.e. $30/mo**), compared against every discounted path a service member can actually use.

> **Scope note — which "Aura" this is:** This page covers **Aura (aura.com)** — the **digital security / identity-theft & credit-protection** service. It is **not** Aura Frames (auraframes.com, digital photo frames), which is a separate company. The dominant "Aura military discount" search intent and the actual published military offer both belong to aura.com, so that is what this brief documents.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Aura publishes a real, standing military discount: up to 52% off its protection plans, plus a two-week free trial**, for the full military community (active duty, retirees, veterans, reservists, National Guard, and their dependents). The offer runs through Aura's **partnership with Military.com**, redeemed at **buy.aura.com/military** — there is **no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX gate**; you confirm military status through the Military.com offer flow. One wrinkle to verify at checkout: Aura's own help center says **"up to 52% off,"** its `buy.aura.com/military` landing page currently advertises **"up to 68% off,"** and its 2022 launch announcement described **50% off the Family plan** — treat the exact discounted price as *display-at-checkout* until confirmed, because the military landing page shows pricing placeholders to automated fetches. Archetype: **subscription service with a genuine, verification-light military tier** — the discount is real, but the single deepest published deal for the military community is actually the **WeSalute partner offer (up to 75% off)**, not the direct 52%. For everyone, annual billing (already ~30% under monthly) plus the universal 14-day free trial and 60-day money-back guarantee are the baseline saves.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The military tier is not stacked on the standard price — it replaces it.** You buy through buy.aura.com/military; there's no "military code on top of a normal cart."
2. **The direct offer and the WeSalute offer are mutually exclusive** — they're two separate discounted routes to the same plans; pick the cheaper net path, don't try to combine.
3. **The 14-day free trial precedes any paid plan** (including the military tier); it's a trial, not an extra discount layered on the annual price.
4. **60-day money-back guarantee** applies to annual plans purchased on aura.com (not Amazon) — an effective safety net that layers under whichever path you choose.
5. **No cashback-portal or coupon-code stack is verified** on the identity-verified military checkout; identity-gated checkouts routinely break portal tracking, so don't promise a Rakuten-on-top stack.
