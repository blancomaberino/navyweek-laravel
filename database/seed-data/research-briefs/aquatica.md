# Aquatica — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **family of 4** (service member/veteran + 3 guests), **one-day Aquatica Orlando admission**. Representative online single-day price **~$60/ticket = $240 pre-tax** (Aquatica Orlando dynamic online pricing runs roughly **$50–$75**; gate/at-park is higher). Figures are illustrative — Aquatica uses dynamic pricing that shifts by date.

## Headline finding

Aquatica (SeaWorld/United Parks & Resorts' water-park brand — **Aquatica Orlando** and **Aquatica San Antonio**) is part of the **Waves of Honor** military program, verified through **ID.me** ("Troop ID"), but its offer is **weaker than SeaWorld's**: **Aquatica does NOT give free admission.** Where SeaWorld, Busch Gardens, and Sesame Place give active-duty members a free annual admission, **Aquatica caps the active-duty benefit at 50% off a single-park ticket (limit of 4 per person, per year, to one park)**. The best value is the **Military Silver Annual Pass at $139** (reg. **$234**) — unlimited admission for 12 months, no blockout dates, free general parking, 3 free guest tickets, and up to 20% off in-park — available to active duty **and** veterans, with active duty able to buy up to 6 passes. Eligibility for the 50%-off single-day ticket is **active-duty / activated or drilling reservist / National Guard only**; **military retirees and DoD civilians are excluded** from that tier. Verify once via ID.me; buy online (not sold at the park); the 50%-off single-day offer **expires December 31, 2026**. Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount via ID.me — the identity offer *is* the savings path; no coupon/cashback stacking game)**. There is no GovX/cashback/sale-stack angle — the first-party 50%/Silver-Pass offer is the answer.

## Stacking Rules (verified, first-party terms)
1. **The 50%-off single-day offer is standalone — "not valid with any other discounts."** No coupon, cashback, or public sale layers on top; the discounted ticket *is* the price.
2. **50%-off single-day tickets and the Silver Pass are alternatives**, not a stack — you buy the discounted day ticket **or** the pass, not both toward one visit.
3. **The Silver Pass includes 3 free guest tickets separately** — these are a pass perk, not a stack; guest tickets exclude parking and are one-time single-day admissions, and the pass member must accompany guests.
4. **Aquatica is excluded from SeaWorld's free complimentary admission** — you cannot redeem a "free SeaWorld admission" at Aquatica.
5. **No third-party marketplace beats this.** GovX/cashback/discount-gift-card plays are irrelevant against a first-party 50%-off ticket or a half-price annual pass — do not manufacture a stack.

### FAQ

#### Does Aquatica offer a military discount?
Yes, through **Waves of Honor** (verified via **ID.me**), but not free admission. **Active-duty U.S. military, activated/drilling reservists, and National Guard get 50% off a single-park ticket** (limit 4/year to one park). Active duty and veterans can also buy a **Military Silver Annual Pass for $139** (regularly $234).

#### How much is the Aquatica military discount?
For active duty it's **50% off** a single-park ticket (up to 4 per year). The **Military Silver Pass is $139** (reg. $234) for unlimited 12-month admission with free parking and in-park perks — the best value for repeat visits and the confirmed veteran benefit.

#### Does Aquatica offer free admission for military like SeaWorld?
**No.** SeaWorld, Busch Gardens, and Sesame Place give active duty a free annual admission, but **Aquatica is excluded** from that free comp. Aquatica's benefit is **50% off** or the discounted **$139 Silver Pass**.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
**Veterans** qualify for the **$139 Silver Pass**. **Spouses/dependents** access offers under the service member's or veteran's ID.me account. **Retirees, DoD civilians, and inactive/standby/retired reserve are excluded** from the 50%-off single-day tier (retirees can still buy the veteran-tier Silver Pass).

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a free **ID.me** account ("Troop ID" for military), verify once on Aquatica's military-discount page, and buy online — discounts aren't sold at the park.

#### Does Aquatica use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Aquatica's pages use **ID.me / Troop ID**.

#### Can I use it in the park or only online?
**Online only** — "discounts are not available at the parks." Buy ahead on aquatica.com.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale prices?
No — the 50%-off offer is **"not valid with any other discounts,"** and it doesn't apply to separately ticketed events like AquaGlow. Each Waves of Honor offer is standalone.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit Aquatica?
**One visit:** active-duty **50% off** (up to 4 tickets) ≈ half price. **Two or more visits (active duty or veteran):** the **$139 Military Silver Pass** — unlimited admission, free parking, 3 free guest tickets, 20% in-park.

#### Does Aquatica run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day free-admission event?
Not that we could verify. SeaWorld's May Military Appreciation Month veteran free-admission event applies to **SeaWorld** parks, not Aquatica; we found no documented Aquatica free window.
