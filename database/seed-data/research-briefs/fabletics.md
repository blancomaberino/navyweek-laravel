# Fabletics — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **two-piece activewear set** — Define PowerHold® High-Waisted Legging (**$84.95 non-member/MSRP**) + a matching sports bra (**~$49.95 non-member/MSRP**) = **~$134.90 pre-tax at non-member (full) pricing**. Fabletics' listed "regular" prices are non-member prices; VIP prices are much lower — that gap is the whole story here.

## Headline finding

Fabletics has a **real, official discount for the military and first-responder community**, verified through **ID.me**. Fabletics' own help page states **15% off** for active military, veterans, spouses and dependents, plus first responders (law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs, 911 dispatchers) — redeemable **online (Fabletics.com / app) and in retail stores** (show valid ID), and it is offered to **all US customers**, so **you do not need a VIP membership to use it**. Important conflict to flag: the **ID.me Shop store page for Fabletics currently displays 10% off** (military / first responder / student), not 15% — so the exact live percentage should be confirmed at checkout. Either way, the bigger truth is that Fabletics is a **membership-pricing brand**: the 15% (or 10%) applies to Fabletics' **deliberately high non-member prices**, while the brand's genuinely low prices (2 leggings for $24, ~$49–59 leggings, monthly credits) live behind the **VIP membership** — a **$69.95/month negative-option subscription** you must actively **"Skip the Month" between the 1st and 5th** every month or be charged. The deepest savings path is the **new-VIP intro offer**, but it enrolls you in that subscription (with a well-documented history of cancellation friction, an FTC action against parent TechStyle, and a 2026 class action). Archetype: **membership/subscription brand where the subscription IS the pricing story** (template F-adjacent), with a genuine **multi-group ID.me identity discount** layered on top (template E) — and effectively **no coupon-stacking game**.
