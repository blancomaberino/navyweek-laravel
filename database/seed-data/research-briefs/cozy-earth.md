## Headline finding

Cozy Earth (cozyearth.com) runs a **real, brand-published 20% discount** for **Military (active duty, veterans, and retirees), Healthcare Professionals, First Responders (police, fire, EMT), and Teachers — including their family members** — stated verbatim on Cozy Earth's own **Cozy Cares** page. There are **two redemption routes**: a self-serve **Cozy Cares email form** (pick your category, get a code emailed) and a dedicated **GovX ID Military Discount** page that verifies through the **GovX ID** widget. Verify **through GovX**, not ID.me — **ID.me itself confirms it does not offer a Cozy Earth military discount** ("We're not aware that Cozy Earth offers Military discounts"), so any "verify with ID.me" claim is wrong.

The honest catch: **20% is often not the best price.** Cozy Earth discounts constantly — its Black Friday 2025 event ran **30–45% off sitewide with up to ~60% doorbusters** — and it runs an **everyday public "buy 3+ items, 20% off" bundle that needs no verification at all**. So during any real sale, or on a 3+ item order, a service member frequently matches or beats the 20% military code **without verifying**. Archetype: **C — a genuine identity discount, but the frequent sitewide sale usually beats it** (secondary **E — multi-audience identity discount with essentially no stacking game**). One caveat worth stating up front: Cozy Earth **does not publish any exclusions or "cannot be combined" language** on its discount pages, so the exact stacking rules are **not stated by the brand** — treat single-code checkout (Shopify allows one discount code per order) as the practical limit, not a published term.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The brand publishes NO stacking or exclusion terms.** Neither the Cozy Cares page nor the GovX page states "cannot be combined," excludes sale items, or excludes categories. So any "it doesn't stack" claim is an **inference from Shopify's one-code-per-order checkout**, not a Cozy Earth-published rule. State it as such; re-verify at checkout.
2. **The 20% military code and the public "buy 3+ = 20%" bundle land at the same 20%** — the bundle needs no verification, so on a 3+ order the military code adds nothing.
3. **A public sitewide sale is auto-applied (no code)**, so it is **portal-safe for Rakuten**; a GovX/Cozy Cares single-use code is not a Rakuten code and identity-gated redemptions typically break portal tracking — so **"20% code + Rakuten" should not be assumed to stack**, while **sale + 2% Rakuten** is the combinable play (rate as of Jul 18, 2026).
4. **The GovX marketplace storefront is a closed channel** — its member price is the price; no cozyearth.com code, sale, or portal stacks on top.

Yes. Cozy Earth offers **20% off** for military (active duty, veterans, retirees) and their families, verified through **GovX ID** or claimed via the **Cozy Cares** email form.

**20% off.** That's the only military figure Cozy Earth publishes — the "10%–25%" ranges and "40%/50%/60% off" numbers on coupon sites are aggregator guesses or public **sales**, not a military rate.

Cozy Earth is an online brand — redeem the code at checkout on **cozyearth.com**.

#### What is excluded?
Cozy Earth doesn't publish any exclusions or "cannot be combined" terms. Assume **one code per order** at checkout and compare against the live sale.

#### Can I combine it with a promo code or a sale?
The brand doesn't say. Because Shopify takes one code per order and Cozy Earth's sales are frequent and often exceed 20%, the **public sale (or the no-verification "buy 3+ = 20%" bundle) usually matches or beats the code** — use whichever is lower.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Cozy Earth?

#### Does Cozy Earth offer a first responder, healthcare, teacher, or student discount?
Yes — the same **20%** covers first responders (police/fire/EMT), healthcare/medical staff, teachers, and students (a Cozy Cares form category), each claimed the same way.

#### Does Cozy Earth run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No documented **military-only** holiday promo, but Cozy Earth runs deep **public** seasonal sales (Black Friday 30–45%+). Watch the public sale — it usually beats the 20% code.
