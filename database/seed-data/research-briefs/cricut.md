# Cricut — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Cricut Maker 4 machine (~$399 list) + a $100 materials/accessories cart** (a realistic "buy the machine and stock up" order).

## Headline finding

**Yes — Cricut runs a genuine, standing U.S. military & veteran discount, and it's two offers in one:** (1) an **everyday discounted machine price** on four popular cutting machines — **$149 Cricut Joy Xtra, $249 Cricut Explore 4, $379 Cricut Maker 4, $599 Cricut Venture** — and (2) an **additional 15% off Cricut materials & accessories + free shipping on your entire order.** Verification is through **gocertify** (a certification service — **not** ID.me, despite aggregator claims), and the discount code is emailed after you certify and is applied in cart. Eligibility is **U.S. military & veterans, personal use only.** The catch on the 15%: it **excludes machines, extensions, bulk, bundles, Protection Plans, and any product launched in the last 90 days**, and it's **not combinable with other offers** — so the machine discount and the 15%-off-materials discount are effectively parallel programs, not a single stack. Archetype: **verified-discount brand with a real, gated military offer** where the machine price is the headline value and the materials 15% is the recurring one. The one thing to weigh: during Cricut's big seasonal sales (Black Friday, Prime-style events), the *public* machine price sometimes dips to or below the "military everyday price," and public sale prices can beat 15% on materials — so check the live public price before assuming the military gate wins.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The military offer is "not combinable with any other offers."** You cannot layer a public promo code, sale price, or second coupon on top of the military code.
2. **The machine discount and the 15%-materials discount are parallel, not additive** — the 15% **excludes machines**; machines get their own everyday discounted price. You benefit from both in one order, but not by stacking one percentage on the other.
3. **90-day-new products, bundles, bulk, and Protection Plans are excluded from the 15%** — newest releases won't take the materials discount.
4. **Cashback portals are risky with the military code** — the code is emailed/applied in cart and the checkout is discount-gated; a non-portal coupon commonly voids cashback. Use a portal only on a *public-price* purchase, not the military code.
5. **U.S. orders, personal use, non-transferable** — no reselling the code or using it for a business/bulk order.
