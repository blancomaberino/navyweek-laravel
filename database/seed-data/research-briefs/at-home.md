# At Home — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$200 home-décor cart** (throw pillows, wall art, a rug — full-price, non-clearance) bought **in a physical At Home store**, where the military discount actually applies.

## Headline finding

At Home (athome.com) **does publish a real, first-party military discount: 10% off, for active and retired service members and their families, present a Military card to the cashier** — but it is **in-store only** and **cannot be redeemed online or on Buy Online, Pick Up In Store (BOPIS) orders.** This is one of the few home-retailer brands in this category with a genuine everyday military discount rather than a "no program" finding. The catch is the channel: if you shop athome.com online, the 10% does not exist, so the best online path is **Rakuten cash back (4% on athome.com, as of July 8, 2026) plus sale/clearance**, and for repeat in-store shoppers the **At Home Insider Perks credit card** (5 points per $1; 15% off your first purchase on a new account) can out-earn the one-time 10% over the first cart. At Home has **no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute** verification — the discount is a manual show-your-card transaction at the register. Archetype: a **real in-store service discount (G-adjacent, minus the tax-free kicker)** layered over a **loyalty-wallet / store-card brand (H)**; there is no exchange or pro-deal route.

## Stacking Rules (verified where possible)
1. **The 10% is in-store only** — it does **not** apply to athome.com or BOPIS orders (explicit). So it cannot stack with an online cash-back portal, because they operate in different channels.
2. **Whether the 10% combines with coupons, sale/clearance, or the Insider Perks card is *not stated* on At Home's page.** Aggregators claim it can't combine; At Home doesn't confirm that. Ask at the register; don't assume either way.
3. **Rakuten 4% (online) follows standard portal rules** — entering a non-Rakuten promo code on the order can reduce or void the cash back. A plain sitewide/newsletter code is usually portal-safe; a scraped "military code" (none exists) would not be.
4. **Insider Perks card rewards accrue on both channels** (5 pts/$1 in-store or on athome.com); the 15%-off-first-purchase is a one-time new-account offer.
5. **No verification stack** — there is no ID.me/SheerID account to layer; eligibility is judged visually at the register from your Military card.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop a full-price cart in any At Home store.
2. At checkout, **before paying**, tell the cashier you'd like the military discount and **present your Military card**.
3. The 10% comes off your total at the register. It can't be added after you've paid.

### How To Redeem Online
There is **no military discount online.** To save on athome.com instead: (1) activate **Rakuten** and click through for 4% cash back (as of July 8, 2026); (2) shop the **Sale/clearance** section; (3) skip entering scraped "military codes" — none exist, and a bogus code can void your cash back.

### How Verification Works
There is no digital verification. At Home judges eligibility **visually at the register** from your Military card — there is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute account to create, and no online gate. That also means the discount is only as reliable as the individual store's practice.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **In-store only** — no athome.com or BOPIS redemption (explicit).
- **Not retroactive** — must be applied before payment.
- **Exclusions and stacking are not published.** At Home's page does not list excluded categories (clearance, gift cards, special order) or an anti-combine rule; other sites do, but At Home doesn't confirm them — verify at the register.

### Best Ways To Save More

- **Buy online through Rakuten** (4% as of July 8, 2026) when the 10% doesn't apply.
- **Shop clearance** — an item already 30–70% off usually beats a 10% discount on full price.

### FAQ

#### Does At Home offer a military discount?
Yes — **10% off for active and retired service members and their families**, redeemed **in store** by showing a Military card to the cashier before you pay. It is not available online.

#### How much is the At Home military discount?
**10%.** That figure comes straight from At Home's own Help Center. Any site quoting a different number is not citing At Home.

#### Can I use the At Home military discount online?
No. At Home states the discount is "only available for in-store purchases and cannot be redeemed for online or Buy Online, Pick Up In Store purchases." Online, use Rakuten cash back and the Sale section instead.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
At Home names "**active and retired service members and their families**." Retirees and family members are covered; **non-retired veterans are not named**, so ask in store. Which family/dependent IDs are accepted is left to the cashier.

#### Does At Home use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute?
No. There's no digital verification — it's a show-your-Military-card discount at the register.

#### What's excluded from the At Home military discount?
At Home's page lists only two rules: in-store only, and not applied after payment. It does **not** publish a clearance/gift-card exclusion or an anti-stacking clause, so we don't assert one — confirm at the register.

#### Can I combine the 10% with coupons, sales, or the Insider Perks card?
At Home doesn't say. It's plausible the card's points still accrue on a discounted purchase, but the page is silent on combining the 10% with coupons or sale items — ask the cashier before assuming.

#### What's actually the cheapest way to buy from At Home?
In store: the 10% (or the 15%-off-first-purchase card on your opening cart). Online: Rakuten 4% (as of July 8, 2026) plus clearance — and on an already-marked-down item, the public price often beats the 10%.

#### Does At Home offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No. At Home publishes only the military (active/retired + families) 10%. No first responder, medical, teacher, or student program was found.

#### Does At Home run a Veterans Day sale?
We found no At Home-documented military-holiday promotion. Its public seasonal sales (Memorial Day, July 4, Black Friday) are open to everyone; the 10% military discount runs year-round in stores.
