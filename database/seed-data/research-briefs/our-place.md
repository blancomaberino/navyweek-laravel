# Our Place — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$200 Our Place order (clears the mandatory $100 ID.me minimum — e.g., a Titanium Always Pan Pro at ~$179 plus a small accessory).

## Headline finding

**Our Place does publish a native military discount: a flat 10% off through ID.me, on orders of $100 or more.** It's on the brand's own site (`fromourplace.com/pages/idme`) and covers military members, first responders, government, nurses, medical providers, teachers, and students who verify through ID.me. The catch — and the whole story for this brand — is that the ID.me discount **"cannot be combined with other offers,"** and Our Place runs frequent site-wide sales of **25–40% off**. Because the 10% doesn't stack on a sale, the honest best move for most service members is usually **the public sale, not the military discount** — you take whichever single offer is deeper.

Archetype: **DTC cookware brand with a native, non-stacking single-digit-plus ID.me discount that its own public sales routinely beat.** (Contrast with Caraway, whose partner 10% *stacks* on sales — Our Place's does not.) No GovX, Exchange, or pro-deal channel was found. Verified reality: **10% via ID.me on $100+, non-combinable**; the frequently false aggregator claims of "20% military," "up to 40–45% for military," or a "25% off military code" are debunked below.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The ID.me 10% cannot be combined with any other offer** — brand program terms state it verbatim: "Cannot be combined with other offers." So you cannot layer it on a public sale, a promo code, or referral credit. This is the make-or-break fact.
2. **$100 minimum** is hard — carts under $100 get nothing from ID.me.
3. **Dirty Dishes Club points** "can't be combined with other discount codes" and need a $50+ order — so points don't stack on the ID.me discount either.
4. **Referral/welcome $20** is likewise a single non-combinable offer — you pick 10% OR $20, not both.
5. **No GovX/Exchange path** exists to route around the non-combinable rule.

### FAQ

#### Does Our Place offer a military discount?
Yes. Our Place offers **10% off orders of $100+** to verified military members (and veterans) through ID.me, published on its own site.

#### How much is the Our Place military discount?
**10% off**, with a **$100 minimum** order. Claims of 20% or 40%+ "military" discounts are wrong — those are public sales or referral offers, not the military discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify through ID.me's Military verification. Spouses/dependents qualify where ID.me's Military group allows — confirm during verification.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to fromourplace.com/pages/idme, verify through ID.me (every visit), and the 10% auto-applies at checkout on a $100+ order.

#### Does Our Place use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**ID.me** — that's the only verification provider Our Place uses. No GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute listing was found.

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?
Online only, at fromourplace.com. Our Place is direct-to-consumer.

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards, previous purchases, orders under $100, and anything outside the US. It's one per customer and non-transferable.

#### Can I combine the 10% with a sale or promo code?
**No.** The ID.me discount cannot be combined with other offers, sales, or codes. You take one or the other — usually whichever is deeper.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Our Place?
Check whether a site-wide sale over 10% is running. If yes (often the case), **buy the sale**. If not, **verify ID.me for 10%** on a $100+ cart. You can't stack them, so take the bigger single discount.

#### Does Our Place offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — the same 10%/$100-minimum ID.me offer covers first responders, government employees, nurses, medical providers, teachers, and students.

#### Does Our Place run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Historically Our Place runs public site-wide sales around major holidays (open to everyone). The military 10% is standing year-round but can't stack on those sales — so the holiday value is the public sale itself.
