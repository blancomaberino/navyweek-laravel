### How Verification Works
Ulta does not run any military, veteran, first-responder, or government-employee verification. ID.me's brand pages for Ulta state, for each group, "We are not aware of ULTA Beauty offering [Military / First Responders / Government Employees] community discounts."

The only consumer verification Ulta operates is for **students**, through SheerID — a completely separate program. Students who are Ulta Beauty Rewards members (ages 16+, currently enrolled) confirm their name, email, school, and date of birth with SheerID; a successful verification lasts one year, but a coupon code is only issued when a limited-time student offer is live. This student path does not extend to military status.

### FAQ

#### Does Ulta Beauty offer a military discount?
No. As of June 23, 2026, Ulta Beauty does not offer a military or veteran discount in stores, online, or in its app. ID.me, which many brands use to run military offers, states it is "not aware of ULTA Beauty offering Military community discounts."

#### How much is the Ulta military discount?
There is no Ulta military discount, so there is no percentage or dollar amount. Any specific "Ulta military discount %" promoted on coupon-aggregator sites is unverified and not backed by Ulta or any verification provider.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for an Ulta discount?
No. Ulta does not offer a discount based on military status for veterans, active duty, retirees, Reserve/National Guard, spouses, or dependents.

#### How do I verify my military status at Ulta?
You cannot — Ulta does not run military verification through ID.me, GovX, WeSalute, or any provider. Ulta's only consumer verification (via SheerID) is for its separate student program.

#### Does Ulta use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for military discounts?
No. Ulta uses SheerID, but only to verify students for a separate student program. It does not use ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for a military discount, and ID.me reports no known Ulta military offer.

#### Can I use a military discount in Ulta stores?
No. Because Ulta has no military discount, cashiers cannot apply one. You can still use Ulta Beauty Rewards and current public coupons, which are available to everyone.

#### What is excluded from Ulta discounts?
There is no military offer to have exclusions. Ulta's general public coupons commonly exclude prestige and fragrance brands, CHANEL, Dyson, salon/brow/piercing services, Beauty Steals, clearance, and gift cards, and are not valid at Ulta at Target or third-party marketplaces.

#### Can I combine offers with promo codes or sale items?
Not relevant to a military discount, since none exists. For Ulta's general coupons: single-item coupons follow the best-available-deal rule and cannot be combined with other Ulta single-item coupons; transaction-level coupons cannot be combined with other transaction-level coupons.

#### Does Ulta offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responder and government-employee discounts: no (per ID.me's Ulta pages). Teacher/nurse discounts: none found in any official source. Students: yes — Ulta runs a separate student program verified by SheerID, which issues limited-time student coupon codes (not a permanent discount, and unrelated to military status).
