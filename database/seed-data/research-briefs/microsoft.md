# Microsoft Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Microsoft (Microsoft Store) **does** offer a military and veteran discount: **up to 10% off select physical consumer products** (primarily consumer Surface devices and accessories) for active, former, and retired military members and their immediate families in the United States. The discount is verified through Microsoft's own account-based eligibility check (sign in with a personal Microsoft account at microsoft.com/store/r/student-discounts) — **not** ID.me, GovX, or SheerID — and once approved, the better of the discount or any sale price is applied automatically in the cart. The single most important caveat: the offer **excludes Xbox consoles, games, and accessories; digital games/apps; Microsoft 365, Game Pass, and other subscriptions; Office software; Windows licenses; gift cards; and Surface for Business** — so the everyday 10% effectively applies to consumer Surface hardware and accessories, not to most software or Xbox. Microsoft 365 Family has a **separate** 30%-off "Military Appreciation Edition" ($99.99/yr) sold **only through military Exchanges** (AAFES/NEX/etc.), not through Microsoft Store.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty service members — eligible (per Microsoft)
- Veterans / former military — eligible ("active, former and retired military members")
- Retirees — eligible
- Reserve / National Guard — eligible (named in Microsoft's military page copy)
- Immediate family members — eligible ("their immediate families")
- Military spouses / dependents — eligible insofar as they are "immediate family"; Microsoft does not separately enumerate spouse vs. dependent
- Surviving spouses / Gold Star families — Not stated in official source
- First responders — Not stated in official source (no first-responder discount published on these pages)
- Public/government entities, nonprofits, educational institutions, enterprise/business/commercial purchasers — explicitly **excluded** ("do not qualify")

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Microsoft Store's eligibility page (microsoft.com/en-us/store/r/student-discounts) and sign in with the email tied to your **personal** Microsoft account (not a work or school account).
2. Microsoft checks your eligibility; if eligible, you're approved right away.
3. Browse eligible physical products (e.g., consumer Surface devices and accessories) while signed in — discounted prices show as you shop.
4. Add an eligible item to your cart. The better price (your military discount or a current sale price) is automatically selected and shown in the cart.
5. Complete checkout. The discounted price appears at purchase.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Microsoft's military page states the everyday discount is "Available only at Microsoft Store online in the United States," so the standard redemption channel is online.
2. The military page also invites shoppers to "visit one of our stores or chat with an agent online" for help choosing a qualifying device; in-store availability depends on whether a Microsoft Experience Center operates in your market.
3. Not stated in official source: a specific register-level in-store discount process. Treat in-store as assistance/availability-dependent and confirm with Microsoft directly.

### How Verification Works
Eligibility is handled by Microsoft directly through your **personal Microsoft account** (the email/password you use with Outlook.com, Hotmail, Office, OneDrive, Skype, Xbox, or Windows) — not a third-party verifier such as ID.me, GovX, or SheerID. Enter your email on Microsoft's eligibility page; if you're eligible, you're approved immediately and discounted prices appear automatically while signed in. Microsoft states it "reserves the right to determine eligibility in its discretion," that false representations void the offer (and Microsoft may collect the full price), and that work/school accounts should not be used. Microsoft does not publish a re-verification interval on these pages, so do not assume approval is permanent. Support path: Microsoft Store Sales and Support / chat with an agent online.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Excluded products:** Xbox consoles, games, and accessories; Surface for Business devices; personalized or customized products; digital games, digital apps, in-app content and subscriptions, and associated content; Office software & apps; Windows 11 and Windows 10; gift cards; services/subscriptions (e.g., Microsoft 365, Game Pass, Skype).
- **Not combinable:** "Not combinable with other sales or promotions." If a sale is deeper, the sale price applies instead; you don't stack them.
- **Personal use / no resale:** Commercial purchases not eligible; bulk orders not valid and may be cancelled; cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash or promo codes.
- **Quantity limits:** "Number of products per purchase may be subject to limitations." Exact numeric cap not stated in official source.
- **Pricing details:** Discount does not include taxes, shipping, or other fees. Refunds account for the discount.
- **Eligibility enforcement:** False representations void the offer; Microsoft may charge full price and determines eligibility at its discretion.
- **Region:** Microsoft Store online, United States only. Microsoft reserves the right to modify or discontinue offers at any time.

### FAQ

#### Does Microsoft offer a military discount?
Yes. Microsoft Store offers active, former, and retired military and their immediate families up to 10% off select physical consumer products (primarily consumer Surface devices and accessories) in the United States. Source: Microsoft Store military page and Student & Military Discount Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### How much is the Microsoft military discount?
Up to 10% off eligible physical consumer products. The exact savings depend on the product and on whether a current sale price is deeper (the better price applies automatically). Source: Microsoft Store military page, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans ("former" military) and retirees qualify, as do active-duty, Reserve, and National Guard members, plus their immediate families. Microsoft says "immediate families" rather than separately listing spouses and dependents, and does not address surviving spouses/Gold Star families. Source: Microsoft Store military page and Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### How do I verify my military status with Microsoft?
Sign in with your personal Microsoft account on Microsoft Store's eligibility page; Microsoft checks eligibility and, if approved, applies discounted prices automatically while you're signed in. Use a personal account, not a work or school account. Source: Student & Military Discount Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Does Microsoft use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not stated in Microsoft's official pages. Microsoft handles eligibility through its own personal-Microsoft-account sign-in, with no third-party verifier named on the military or terms pages. Source: Student & Military Discount Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Can I use the Microsoft military discount in stores?
Microsoft's terms state the discount is "available only at Microsoft Store online in the United States." The military page invites shoppers to visit a store or chat for help, but a specific in-store register discount process is not stated. Confirm in-store availability with Microsoft. Source: Microsoft Store military page and Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### What is excluded from the Microsoft military discount?
Xbox consoles, games, and accessories; Surface for Business; personalized/customized products; digital games, apps, and in-app content; Office software & apps; Windows 11/10; gift cards; and services/subscriptions like Microsoft 365, Game Pass, and Skype. Source: Student & Military Discount Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale items?
No. It is not combinable with other sales or promotions. If a sale is deeper than your military discount, the sale price is applied automatically instead — you don't stack the two. Source: Student & Military Discount Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Is there a Microsoft 365 / Office military discount?
Microsoft 365 (and Office software generally) is excluded from the Microsoft Store 10% discount. However, military families can buy a Microsoft 365 Family "Military Appreciation Edition" for $99.99/year (about 30% off) — but only through a U.S. military Exchange (AAFES, Navy Exchange, etc.), not Microsoft Store. Source: Microsoft 365 Military page and Microsoft Store Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Does Microsoft offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
A student/education discount (up to 10%, same mechanic as military) is offered to students, parents of students, and faculty working in education. First responder, nurse, and government-employee discounts are not stated in Microsoft's official store discount pages; government entities and commercial purchasers are explicitly excluded. Source: Student & Military Discount Terms, accessed June 23, 2026.
