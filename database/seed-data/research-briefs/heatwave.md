## Headline finding

Heat Wave Visual runs a **genuine, brand-operated 20% military/first-responder/government discount** verified through **VerifyPass** on its own site (heatwavevisual.com) — this is confirmed on both the brand's own offer page and VerifyPass's brand page, so it is a High-confidence primary fact. The 20% is a **single flat tier** covering active duty, veterans, retirees, police/fire/EMT, and federal/local government employees. Because Heat Wave Visual is **not on cashback portals** and publishes no standing public code larger than the ~10% email-signup offer, **the 20% VerifyPass discount is the best everyday path** and effectively *is* the savings strategy (archetype **E — multi-tier identity discount, no stacking game**, with a secondary **A — GovX marketplace route**). The one time it loses: a sitewide sale of 25%+ (Black Friday / seasonal), when the public sale price can beat 20% off full price — verify whether the sale stacks (it likely does not). There is **no medical/nurse/teacher/student tier** — VerifyPass lists only military/veteran and first-responder/government groups.
