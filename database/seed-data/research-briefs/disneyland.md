# Disneyland Resort Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Scope: **Disneyland Resort (Anaheim, California)** — Disneyland Park + Disney California Adventure. This is distinct from Walt Disney World (Florida); do not mix the two parks' prices or terms. All facts below are from Disneyland's official offer pages.

## Executive Summary

Disneyland Resort currently runs a 2026 "Disney Military Promotional Tickets" offer (its version of the Armed Forces Salute) for eligible U.S. service members. Disney does **not** publish a percentage discount — it publishes flat promotional ticket prices: a 1-Day 1-Park ticket ($104 adult / $98 child), plus 3-Day ($314) and 4-Day ($369) Promotional Park Hopper tickets (Lightning Lane Multi Pass versions cost more: $416 and $505). Eligibility covers active and retired U.S. military — including National Guard, Reservists, Coast Guard, Space Force, PHS and NOAA commissioned corps — and a spouse may purchase on the member's behalf (but not both). Tickets are sold at participating U.S. military base ticket offices and online via **GovX** (military-status verification through GovX); valid military ID is required for purchase and use. There is a separate offer for discounted rates on select Disneyland Resort hotel rooms (no published percentage; book by phone). The single most important caveat: the 3-Day/4-Day tickets are limited to **6 per eligible member or spouse**, one of which must be used by the member or spouse, and the offer **cannot be combined with other ticket discounts or promotions**. The current offer is valid for 2026 and Disney can pause or change it at any time.

### How To Redeem Online (GovX)
1. Go to GovX and create a GovX account.
2. Verify your U.S. military status with GovX.
3. Locate the Disneyland Resort Military Promotional Ticket offer and choose your ticket type.
4. Purchase; have valid military ID ready, as it is required for use.
5. Make a park reservation for each day/park in the Disneyland app, and link your ticket to your MyDisney account.

### How To Redeem In Store (Military base ticket office)
1. Visit a participating U.S. military base ticket office (sales outlet). Confirm participation via Disney's base list / contact the office first.
2. Present valid military identification (eligible member or spouse).
3. Purchase up to 6 promotional 3-Day/4-Day tickets (one must be used by the member or spouse), or 1-Day tickets.
4. Make park reservations in the Disneyland app for each day and park you plan to visit.
5. At the park, present your ticket and reservation; valid military ID is required for use of 3-Day/4-Day tickets.

### How Verification Works
Eligibility is verified either in person at a participating U.S. military base ticket office or online through GovX, which checks military status during account setup. A valid military ID is required both to purchase and to use the 3-Day/4-Day tickets (and to purchase 1-Day tickets). Buying through GovX requires a GovX account. Disney does not state on its page how often GovX re-verifies status; treat verification as per GovX's own policy, not as permanent. For purchase questions, Disney directs guests to their participating U.S. military base ticket office.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- May not be combined with other ticket discounts or promotions.
- 3-Day/4-Day tickets: max 6 per eligible member or spouse (combined); 1 must be used by the member or spouse; max 5 if member/spouse holds a Magic Key.
- Each promotional ticket must be used by the same person on all days.
- Tickets may not be sold or transferred for commercial purposes.
- Park reservation + valid ticket required for the same date and park; reservations are limited and not guaranteed.
- Parking is not included.
- Sales may be paused or terminated at any time; subject to change without notice.
- Hotel offer: subject to limited room allocation; excludes select Concierge rooms, Signature/Premium suites, and 2–3 Bedroom Villas; limit 2 rooms per reservation; valid military ID required at check-in or non-discounted rate is charged; not combinable with other hotel offers; nontransferable; member/spouse must stay in the room.
- Exact percentage savings vs. standard gate prices: not published by Disney (flag — do not state a percentage).

### Best Ways To Save More
- Buy the multi-day Park Hopper promotional tickets, generally Disney's lowest per-day pricing.
- Stack the ticket offer with the separate 2026 military hotel-room rate (call (866) 309-9737) — note these are two separate offers, each with its own terms; the "no combining" rule applies within ticket discounts, not between the ticket and hotel offers.
- Compare base ticket office vs. GovX: Disney notes actual prices at individual base ticket offices may be **less** than the listed prices.
- Magic Key passholders in the household can enter on their own pass without buying a promotional ticket to accompany guests.
- Competitor context: Walt Disney World (Florida) runs a parallel but separately-priced Armed Forces Salute; Universal Studios Hollywood and other parks periodically run their own military offers — verify each separately.

### FAQ

#### Does Disneyland offer a military discount?
Yes. For 2026, Disneyland Resort offers Disney Military Promotional Tickets — flat promotional prices on 1-Day, 3-Day, and 4-Day Park Hopper tickets — plus a separate discounted rate on select resort hotel rooms. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### How much is the Disneyland military discount?
Disney sets flat prices rather than a percentage. For 2026: 1-Day 1-Park $104 adult/$98 child; 3-Day Park Hopper $314 ($416 with Lightning Lane Multi Pass); 4-Day Park Hopper $369 ($505 with LL Multi Pass). Disney does not publish a percentage savings figure. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Disney's eligible-member definition is "active or retired" U.S. military (including Guard, Reserves, Coast Guard, Space Force, PHS, NOAA). A spouse may purchase in the member's place. Once purchased, tickets can be used by family and friends, but the member or spouse must do the buying and use one ticket. General veterans (not retired) are not listed as a separate category on Disney's official page. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer — Important Details](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person with valid military ID at a participating U.S. military base ticket office, or online by creating a GovX account and verifying through GovX. Valid military ID is required for purchase and use. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### Does Disneyland use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Disney's official page names **GovX** as the online purchase/verification channel, alongside U.S. military base ticket offices. ID.me, SheerID, and WeSalute are not referenced for this offer. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### How many tickets can I buy?
For 3-Day or 4-Day promotional tickets, no more than 6 per eligible member or spouse (combined), and at least one must be used by the member or spouse. The cap is 5 if the member or spouse is a Magic Key passholder. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer — Important Details](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### Are there blockout dates?
Disney's 2026 ticket page does not list blockout dates; 3-Day/4-Day tickets are valid Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026. Park reservations are still required and are limited. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other ticket discounts?
No. The promotional tickets may not be combined with other ticket discounts or promotions. ([Disney 2026 ticket offer — Important Details](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-tickets-discounts-2026/))

#### Is there a military discount on Disneyland hotels?
Yes — a separate 2026 offer gives eligible members discounted rates on select Disneyland Resort hotel rooms (travel Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026; book by Dec 27, 2026). Disney does not publish a percentage; book by calling (866) 309-9737. Valid military ID is required at check-in. ([Disney 2026 hotel offer](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/offers-discounts/military-resort-discount-2026/))

#### Does Disneyland offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not stated. Disney's official military offer pages cover U.S. military members only; no first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student category is published for this offer.

Disneyland's official page defines eligible members as "active or retired" and does not name disabled veterans as a separate category. Do not assert disabled-veteran eligibility on the page until confirmed against an official Disney source or base ticket office.
- **Exact percentage savings:** Disney never publishes a % vs. standard gate prices. If the page wants to show "save X%," compute it from current official standard ticket prices at publish time and label it as an estimate — never present a fabricated percentage.
- **GovX verification cadence:** Disney's page does not state how/often GovX re-verifies. Confirm on GovX directly; do not imply one-time-forever verification.
- **GovX page content:** The GovX brand page is JS-rendered and did not return readable terms via fetch. Verify the live GovX listing (ticket types, prices, any GovX-specific limits) in a browser before quoting GovX-specific details.
- **1-Day Park Hopper:** The 1-Day Park Hopper variant is noted as available only at participating military base ticket offices (not listed in the online price block); confirm current price at the base office.
