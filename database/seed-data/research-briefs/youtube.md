# YouTube — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one year (12 months) of the **YouTube TV Base Plan** at the standard price of **$82.99/month = $995.88/year** (tv.youtube.com, as of July 12, 2026). ## Headline finding

Two different YouTube products, two different answers. **YouTube TV (live-TV service) DOES have a real military-adjacent discount** — a promotional **$67.99/month for the first 12 months** of the Base Plan ($15/mo off the $82.99 standard price, ~$180 over the year), open to **military, veterans, first responders, medical community, and teachers verified through ID.me**, plus a parallel **NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube military offer at $198/season** for the same groups. **YouTube Premium and YouTube Music (the ad-free video/music subscriptions) have NO military or veteran discount** — the only identity discount there is the **student plan ($8.99/mo via SheerID)**; there is also a Family plan ($26.99/mo, up to 6 people) that lowers the per-person cost. Archetype: **B (identity-discount via third-party verifier) for YouTube TV, and "no military discount, student-only" for Premium.** Two important caveats: the YouTube TV promo is an *introductory* rate (year 2 reverts to full $82.99), and the ID.me landing page in-session showed a **redemption deadline of June 30, 2026** — which has already passed as of July 12, 2026, so the exact current window must be re-checked at build (these Google promos roll over/renew frequently). This is not a first-responder-only or veteran-only carve-out; it is Google's standard ID.me "community" promotion.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **YouTube TV ID.me offer cannot be combined with other coupon-based offers** — one promo per subscription; you pick the ID.me rate OR a public new-customer promo, not both (per the ID.me offer terms).
2. **The military rate is introductory, not permanent** — $67.99/mo applies for the **first 12 months only**, then auto-renews at the standard $82.99/mo (subject to change). Cancel before renewal to avoid the full rate.
3. **NFL Sunday Ticket military offer is a separate purchase** — $198/season stands on its own; existing subscribers auto-renew at $198. It does not stack on the Base Plan promo (different product).
4. **YouTube Premium has no stackable military layer at all** — no ID.me/GovX/SheerID military tier. Student ($8.99, SheerID) and Family ($26.99) are the only price reductions and they are mutually exclusive (you're on one plan).
5. **No cashback/portal/exchange stack** — subscriptions aren't sold via Rakuten/ID.me Shop cashback, the Exchange, or refurb channels in any material way; ignore those paths for this brand.

### How Verification Works
YouTube TV's military/community offer uses **ID.me** — you verify once against your eligible group (military, veteran, first responder, medical, or teacher) while signed into the Google account tied to YouTube, and the promo rate applies at signup. The **YouTube Premium student plan uses SheerID** (a different verifier) and requires **annual re-verification**; miss the re-verify window and the plan reverts to the full $15.99/month. Neither verifier makes the price permanent — YouTube TV's rate is time-boxed to 12 months, and the student rate is capped at 4 years of enrollment.

### FAQ

#### Does YouTube offer a military discount?
YouTube TV does — a promotional $67.99/month for your first 12 months of the Base Plan (about $15/month off), verified through ID.me for military, veterans, first responders, medical workers, and teachers. YouTube Premium (the ad-free subscription) does not offer a military discount.

#### How much is the YouTube TV military discount?
$67.99/month for the first 12 months, versus the standard $82.99/month — roughly $180 in the first year. After 12 months it reverts to the standard rate.

#### Do veterans and first responders qualify for YouTube TV?
Yes. The ID.me offer covers verified military members, veterans, first responders, the medical community, and teachers. Family/dependent eligibility isn't separately spelled out — verify your own status in ID.me.

#### Does YouTube Premium have a military discount?
No. There is no military or veteran discount for YouTube Premium or YouTube Music. The only identity discount is the student plan ($8.99/month via SheerID).

#### How do I verify my status?
For YouTube TV, verify through ID.me at the YouTube TV promotion page while signed into the Google account tied to YouTube. For the Premium student plan, verify enrollment through SheerID and re-verify every 12 months.

#### Does YouTube use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
YouTube TV's military/community offer uses **ID.me**. The YouTube Premium student plan uses **SheerID**. There is no YouTube GovX storefront.

#### Can I combine it with other promo codes?
No — the YouTube TV offer cannot be combined with other coupon-based offers, and it's a single introductory rate per subscription.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to get ad-free YouTube?
Since Premium has no military discount: if you're a student, the $8.99 student plan; otherwise split the Family plan ($26.99 for up to 6, ≈$4.50/person), or prepay the annual individual plan ($159.99) to save ~15%.

#### Does YouTube TV offer an NFL Sunday Ticket military deal?
Yes — $198 for the 2026 season via ID.me for military, veterans, first responders, medical workers, and teachers, for both new and existing subscribers, redeemable through December 31, 2026.

#### Does YouTube offer a first responder, teacher, or student discount?
The YouTube TV ID.me offer includes first responders, the medical community, and teachers at the same $67.99 rate. YouTube Premium offers a student plan ($8.99) but no first responder or teacher tier.
