# Mazda — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new Mazda CX-5 at a ~$30,000 MSRP (the brand's best-selling model; the Military Appreciation Bonus Cash is a flat $500 regardless of model).

## Headline finding

**Mazda runs a genuine, factory-backed "Mazda Military Appreciation Bonus Cash" program: $500 toward the purchase or lease of a new 2023–2026 Mazda.** Its defining feature — and what makes it more valuable than a typical retail discount — is that it is **combinable**: it stacks with public offers (lease deals, customer cash back, APR incentives) **and** with Mazda's loyalty/lease-loyalty rewards. Archetype: **D (combinable — stacking is the strategy), delivered as a manufacturer incentive rather than a checkout %.**

The eligibility is narrower than aggregators imply and is the #1 thing to get right: it covers **active-duty members, reserves/National Guard, retirees (20+ years), disabled veterans, and veterans within 2 years of separation** with an honorable discharge — plus **spouse and children at the same address**. It is **not** an "any veteran, any time" offer; a veteran separated more than 2 years ago (and not a retiree or disabled veteran) generally does not qualify. Limit **two** per person per year. Because it's a fixed $500 and stacks on top of whatever public incentives are running, the winning move is: **negotiate the best public deal first, then add the $500 on top** — and, if eligible, layer college-grad or loyalty programs too. These bonus-cash programs **refresh periodically**, so confirm the current terms and end date on MazdaUSA.com or with the dealer before signing.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both program terms)
1. **Bonus Cash combines with public offers** — lease offers, customer cash back, and APR incentives — so you don't have to choose between the $500 and a live promotion.
2. **Bonus Cash combines with Mazda loyalty** — Mazda Financial Services Lease Loyalty Reward or Mazda Loyalty Reward can stack with it.
3. **Limit two Bonus Cash offers per person, per year** — you can't apply it endlessly.
4. **It's transferable within the household only** — spouse/children at the same address.
5. **Other Mazda programs (college grad, etc.)** may also stack, but each program has its own terms — confirm the specific combination with the dealer before signing.
6. **Proof is required at the dealer** (LES / DEP / DD-214 + driver's license); this is an at-dealership incentive, not an online checkout code.

### FAQ

#### Does Mazda offer a military discount?
Yes — **$500 Military Appreciation Bonus Cash** toward a new 2023–2026 Mazda, and it combines with public offers and Mazda loyalty.

#### How much is the Mazda military discount?
A flat **$500** (not a percentage), applied on top of your negotiated price and any public incentives.

#### Who is eligible?
Active duty, reserves/Guard, retirees, disabled veterans, and veterans **within 2 years of separation** (honorable discharge), plus spouse and children at the same address.

#### I'm a veteran who separated 5 years ago — do I qualify?
Generally no, unless you're a retiree (20+ years) or a disabled veteran. The standard window is **within 2 years of separation**. Confirm with the dealer.

#### Can I combine it with other Mazda offers?
Yes — it combines with public offers (customer cash, APR, lease) and with Mazda loyalty/lease-loyalty rewards. Limit two per person per year.

#### How do I prove eligibility?
Bring an **LES, DEP form, or DD-214** plus your current driver's license to the dealer. There's no online code — the dealer verifies and applies the rebate.

#### Does Mazda use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No — this is a **dealer-verified factory incentive**, not an online identity-verified checkout discount.

#### Does the $500 come from Mazda or the dealer?
It's a **factory (manufacturer) rebate**, so it stacks on top of the price you negotiate with the dealer — it isn't dealer margin.

#### What's the smartest way to use it?
Negotiate the best public deal first, then add the $500 (and Mazda loyalty if you qualify). Timing a model-year closeout maximizes the public incentives it rides on.
