# ESPN / ESPN+ — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **MLB.TV All-Teams annual subscription** (regular 2026 season price **$149.99**), now sold through the ESPN app — plus a secondary look at a stand-alone **ESPN Select** (formerly ESPN+) streaming subscription (~$11.99/mo).

## Headline finding

**The ESPN streaming app itself — ESPN Select and ESPN Unlimited (ESPN+ was rebranded/folded into ESPN Select at the August 2025 DTC launch) — has no military or veteran discount.** ID.me states plainly: "We are not aware of ESPN+ offering Military community discounts," and there is no verified ESPN military offer at GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass. The "50% off with code ESPNHALF for military" and "40% off military" lines on coupon sites are **aggregator claims, not an ESPN military program** — do not repeat them as fact.

**The one genuine military discount you can get *through ESPN* is on MLB.TV.** ESPN took over MLB.TV distribution in 2026 (a three-year deal), so new MLB.TV subscribers now register in the ESPN app — and MLB.TV carries an official military discount of **up to 35% off** the regular price (about **$49 off** the all-teams annual package), verified through **GovX or SheerID**. Archetype: **digital subscription with no native discount, riding a partner's (MLB's) verified offer.** Best path: buy MLB.TV via the GovX/SheerID military offer — but watch MLB's periodic *public* in-season price drops (2026 dropped the all-teams season pass to **$94.99** for everyone), which can undercut the military price.

**MLB.TV military discount (online only):**
1. Go to the MLB.TV military page (mlb.com/live-stream-games/subscribe/military) or the ESPN Fan Support "MLB.TV Military Discount" article.

**ESPN Select / Unlimited app:** no military step exists — you subscribe at standard price in the ESPN app or via the Disney Bundle.

MLB.TV's discount uses **GovX** or **SheerID**. You create a free account, confirm eligibility with a military ID, DD-214, VA letter, or equivalent, and the discount applies at checkout. ### FAQ

#### Does ESPN offer a military discount?
The ESPN streaming app (ESPN Select / ESPN Unlimited, formerly ESPN+) does not offer a military or veteran discount. ID.me confirms it isn't aware of any ESPN military program, and none exists at GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass. The one military discount reachable through ESPN is on **MLB.TV**, which ESPN began distributing in 2026.

#### How much is the ESPN / ESPN+ military discount?
There is no ESPN-app military discount. On **MLB.TV** (sold via ESPN), the military discount is **up to 35% off** — about $49 off the all-teams annual package.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Not for the ESPN app (no discount exists). For **MLB.TV**, veterans with an honorable discharge qualify; family/dependents are eligible on some verification platforms — confirm in the GovX/SheerID flow.

#### How do I verify my status?
Only for MLB.TV: create a free **GovX** or **SheerID** account and confirm with a military ID, DD-214, or VA letter. The ESPN app has no verification because it has no military offer.

#### Does ESPN use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass for a military discount?
No — not for the ESPN app. ID.me only lists general cash back for ESPN (all members). **MLB.TV** uses **GovX** and **SheerID** for its military discount.

#### Is the "ESPNHALF" 50%-off military code real?
No. That's an aggregator/coupon-site claim, not an ESPN military program. Where such a code works at all, it's a general promo, not a service-member benefit.

#### Can I use a military discount in the ESPN app?
No. There's no military rate in the ESPN app. MLB.TV's military discount is applied online via GovX/SheerID before purchase.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy MLB.TV through ESPN?
Compare two prices: the **GovX/SheerID military discount** (~$100 on the all-teams annual) and MLB's **public in-season price drop** (e.g., $94.99 in 2026, open to everyone). Take the lower one — they don't stack.

#### Does ESPN offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No verified first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount exists for the ESPN app. MLB.TV's GovX/SheerID program is military/government-focused.

#### Does ESPN run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
The ESPN app has no military-holiday promo on record. MLB.TV's military discount runs year-round (season-based), and MLB's public price drops historically land mid-season (watch June–July), not on military holidays.
