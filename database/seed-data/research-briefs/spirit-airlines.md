**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one round-trip domestic ticket for an active-duty service member checking 2 bags + 1 carry-on (historically the exact fees Spirit's military benefit waived).

> **CRITICAL — READ FIRST (as of July 12, 2026):** Spirit Airlines **ceased all flight operations at ~3:00 a.m. ET on Saturday, May 2, 2026** and entered an orderly wind-down (liquidation) inside its second Chapter 11 case. It is **not selling tickets, not operating flights, and its customer-service and booking systems are shut down.** Every military "benefit" described below is **historical** — it no longer exists because the airline no longer exists. This page's real job for a service member in 2026 is: **do not try to book Spirit; here is what the benefit was, and here are the operating airlines with equal-or-better military baggage benefits to rebook on.**

## Headline finding

Spirit Airlines is **defunct** — it shut down May 2, 2026 and is liquidating, so there is currently **no Spirit flight to discount.** Historically Spirit **never offered a fare discount** for military (no percentage off tickets, no promo codes — ever). What it offered, and only to **active-duty** U.S. service members (plus, from May 24, 2024, an accompanying spouse and children traveling with them), was a **baggage-and-boarding benefit**: two free checked bags, one free carry-on (on top of the always-free personal item), complimentary priority boarding, and fee-free changes/cancellation-for-credit — verified via **ID.me** at booking and a military ID at the gate. **Veterans and retirees were never eligible** unless on active orders. This is Archetype I (travel/loyalty) with a decisive override: the brand has exited the market. The genuine best path today is a **substitute** — rebook on American, Delta, United, Southwest, JetBlue, or Frontier, several of which give active-duty **more** free bags than Spirit did and extend some benefits to leisure travel.
