# 5.11 Tactical — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$200 regular-price** apparel/gear order (e.g., a pair of Defender-Flex pants + a Taclite shirt).

## Headline finding

5.11 Tactical runs a real, **verification-gated professional discount** — but the exact percentage rotates and is not published as a flat number on the brand's own visible pages. As of **July 13, 2026**, the verified live offer for verified ID.me communities (Military, First Responder, Nurse/Medical, Teacher, Government, Student, Alumni) is **15% off regular-price items + an extra 25% off clearance**, redeemed by verifying **ID.me at 511tactical.com checkout**. Separately, 5.11 sells a limited catalog through the **GovX** marketplace at member-only per-item prices (aggregators say "up to 30%," but it's item-specific, not a flat rate). **Archetype: E — multi-tier identity discount via ID.me**, with a secondary **A/K GovX pro-deal marketplace** channel and an **L exchange (AAFES/NEX) tax-free** channel. The single best path depends on the item: **ID.me 15% for current full-price gear; the extra 25% off clearance for deal-hunting; GovX only when a specific SKU is listed cheaper there; the Exchange when you have base access and want tax-free.** Do not trust the "20% / 25% / 30% always-on" numbers the coupon sites publish — those are stale or conflated promos.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **ID.me discount = one code at checkout.** It does **not** stack with the public email 15%-off code or other promo codes — it's a single applied discount. *(Standard ID.me single-code behavior; the brand's exact "not combinable" line was not readable from its JS-rendered terms — flagged.)*
2. **Regular-price vs. clearance are separate offers.** The 15% applies to regular-price goods; the "extra 25% off" applies to clearance SKUs — you don't get 15%+25% on the same item.
3. **GovX is a substitute, not a stack.** It's a separate marketplace with its own account and checkout at member pricing — you cannot layer it onto a 511tactical.com order, and store-side cashback portals generally do **not** track GovX or ID.me-gated checkouts.
4. **Exchange is its own channel** (tax-free), independent of ID.me/GovX; no stacking across channels.

### FAQ

#### Does 5.11 Tactical offer a military discount?
Yes. 5.11 offers a verified discount to military, veterans and other communities through **ID.me** at its own checkout, plus a separate **GovX** marketplace. It's identity-verified, not a public code.

#### How much is the 5.11 Tactical military discount?
As of July 13, 2026, verified ID.me members get **15% off regular-price items and an extra 25% off clearance**. The percentage **rotates** (it was 20% in a March 2026 promo), so confirm the live figure at checkout.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify via ID.me and GovX. GovX allows eligible military family members; ID.me eligibility depends on the specific community — verify per account.

#### How do I verify my status?
Click the **ID.me** button on the 511tactical.com cart/checkout page and complete verification, or create a **GovX** account and verify separately for the marketplace.

#### Does 5.11 Tactical use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**ID.me** (at 511tactical.com checkout) and **GovX** (marketplace). No verified SheerID/WeSalute/VerifyPass program was found.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The ID.me discount is an **online** checkout benefit at 511tactical.com. GovX is online-only. Tax-free 5.11 is available in-store and online at military **Exchanges**.

#### What is excluded?
The 15% applies to regular-price goods; clearance has its own extra-25% offer. The discount is a single code — it won't combine with other promo codes.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the ID.me discount applies as a single discount and does not stack with the public email code or other coupons. Compare it against the current public sale and take whichever is lower.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from 5.11?
Take the **extra 25% off clearance** for the deepest markdown, the **15%** on current gear, price-check **GovX** on specific items, or buy **tax-free at the Exchange** if you have access.

#### Does 5.11 offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes — each is a separate **ID.me community** (responder, teacher, nurse, medical, government, student, alumni) with the same verify-at-checkout flow.

#### Does 5.11 run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Historically 5.11 elevates its community offer in spring (a 20% ID.me promo ran Mar 13–31, 2026) and shoppers report Veterans Day bumps. These are **not guaranteed** annual promos.

#### Can I buy 5.11 at the Exchange or through GovX?
Yes — 5.11 is carried at AAFES/NEX (tax-free, with shopping privileges) and sold on the GovX marketplace at member pricing.
