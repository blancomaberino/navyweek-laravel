# Mission BBQ — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$12 sandwich (Mission BBQ does not do percentage discounts — the benefit is a free sandwich on qualifying days).

## Headline finding

Mission BBQ — a veteran-and-first-responder-founded chain — does **not** offer an everyday percentage military discount. Instead it runs two well-documented **free-meal events**: a **free sandwich (plus a slice of cake at many locations) on Veterans Day, Nov 11**, and a **Military Appreciation Week in May** with **branch-specific free-sandwich days**. It also runs a **Veterans VIP Club** you can join in-store, and every location pauses at **noon daily to play the National Anthem**. The single best "savings path" here is showing up on your qualifying day with an ID — the meal is free, not discounted.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The free-sandwich offers are **in-store, dine-in/redeem-in-person**, one per eligible guest — not stackable with anything and generally not for online/delivery orders.
2. There is **no third-party portal** (no ID.me/GovX/SheerID/WeSalute) — eligibility is by military ID or staff discretion in store.
3. Veterans VIP Club offers are separate and member-specific; don't assume they combine with event freebies.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**In store (only):** 1) Visit a Mission BBQ on Veterans Day or your branch's May day. 2) Tell the team you're active/retired/veteran and present a military ID if asked. 3) Receive your free sandwich (and cake where offered). To join the **Veterans VIP Club**, sign up in-store.
**Online/delivery:** Free-meal events are in-store; don't expect them on app/delivery orders.

No third-party verification. In store, eligibility is confirmed by a **military ID** or staff discretion. The Veterans VIP Club is a simple in-store sign-up.

Not an everyday percentage discount. It offers **free sandwiches** on Veterans Day and during May's Military Appreciation Week.

There's no percentage — the benefit is a **free sandwich** on qualifying days.

Yes, on the event days, with a military ID.
