# EōS Fitness — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one year of the mid-tier **"Will Crush" membership at $24.99/month** (~$300/year before fees).

## Headline finding

EōS Fitness has **no published military or veteran discount**. Its website markets three membership tiers ($9.99 / $24.99 / $29.99 per month) and a "**$1 to join + 30 days free**" promo, with no military, veteran, or first-responder program anywhere on the site, and **no GovX, ID.me, SheerID, or WeSalute** partnership. The honest best-savings path for a service member is the **standing $1-to-join + 30-days-free promo** plus choosing the right tier — and, for anyone stationed near a base, the **free on-base MWR / installation fitness centers** beat any commercial gym on price. Aggregator "50% off" and "military discount" claims for EōS are unverified promo codes, not a service-member program.
