# goodr — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $60 goodr.com order (e.g., two pairs of OG polarized sunglasses at ~$30 each, or one premium PHG pair) — also the free-shipping threshold.

## Headline finding

**goodr runs no military or veteran discount on goodr.com.** There is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or verification step at goodr's checkout, and no dedicated military/first-responder/nurse/teacher/student program — this is corroborated by the absence of any offer on goodr's own help center and by multiple aggregators that concede "no official goodr military program." The aggregator claims of a "25% / 30% / 15% / 50% goodr military discount" are **fabricated** — they are generic scraped sitewide codes relabeled as "military."

Because goodr is a value brand (OGs ~$25–30), the real savings story is three honest paths: (1) **ExpertVoice** — goodr has an active pro store, and active duty, veterans, military spouses, and first responders are all eligible ExpertVoice affiliations; this is the only identity-verified route and typically the deepest discount; (2) **goodr's own frequent sitewide sales** — 20–25% off auto-applied around Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, and Black Friday, no verification needed, so during a sale the public price beats any "military code" for everyone; and (3) **Scheels**, which stocks goodr and gives verified military a **5% everyday** storewide discount via ID.me — a small but always-on route between goodr's sales.

Archetype: **B (no first-party military route)** dominant, with **K (ExpertVoice pro-deal)** as the real verified path and a strong **C (frequent sitewide sales beat any code)** flavor.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **goodr's own promos do not combine.** goodr's promo terms state the discount "cannot be combined with any other promotions or codes." So a newsletter/scraped code and the live auto-sale are mutually exclusive — you get one, not both.
2. **ExpertVoice is a substitute, not a stack.** Pro-deal pricing is its own closed checkout — no goodr codes, no cashback portal, no rewards points on top. Personal use only, purchase caps, no resale.
3. **Scheels 5% is a separate retailer's program**, not a goodr stack. It applies to goodr items at Scheels via ID.me; Scheels' own sale/exclusion rules govern.
4. **Cashback portals (Rakuten/TopCashback):** if goodr is listed, portal cashback commonly **voids on coupon-code or auto-discount orders** and on identity-gated checkouts — do not assume "sale + cashback" stacks; verify the live rate and terms. 5. **goodr F'ing Rewards points** accrue on a paid order and redeem later ($20 at 200 pts); the $20 redemption itself is a code, so it likely won't combine with a live sitewide promo — use it on a non-sale order.
