# Patagonia Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Patagonia does not offer a military or veteran discount.** As of June 23, 2026, no Patagonia-owned page (the coupons/promo-codes page, the Pro Program page, or the Help Center) advertises a percentage-off discount for active duty, veterans, retirees, reserves/Guard, spouses, or dependents — and the company does not publish any ID.me, GovX, or SheerID military verification flow. Patagonia's coupons page explicitly tells shoppers to "disregard any sites that claim to offer Patagonia coupons or discount codes." The widely repeated "10% Patagonia military discount via ID.me" and "10% Veterans Day discount" claims appear only on third-party coupon/affiliate sites and are **not corroborated by any primary Patagonia source** — they are flagged Low confidence and should be presented on the page as unverified/likely inaccurate, not as a live offer. The honest, useful answer: Patagonia rarely discounts at all, but offers everyone the same year-round savings through **Web Specials** (no code, up to 50% off past-season/irregular items) and **Worn Wear** (used gear). Separately, the **Pro Program** is a profession-based (not military-status-based) membership for outdoor professionals; its eligible categories include some roles a service member could hold (e.g., SAR Individual, Wildland Fire Crews), but it is not a military discount and is currently not accepting applications for additional categories.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no military discount to redeem. To get Patagonia's standard year-round savings (available to everyone):
1. Go to patagonia.com and open **Web Specials** (from the top navigation or patagonia.com/shop/web-specials).
2. Add discounted past-season, special make-up, or irregular items to your cart — no promo code is required; the lower price is already applied.
3. Orders over $99 qualify for free ground shipping.

### How To Redeem In Store
There is no in-store military discount.
1. Visit a Patagonia retail store (find one at patagonia.com/store-locator).
2. Ask staff about in-store Web Specials / sale items, which carry the same already-reduced pricing as online.
3. No military ID or verification provides any additional discount.

### How Verification Works
Patagonia does not use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or any military-status verification service for a consumer discount, because no such discount exists. The only application-based "membership" Patagonia runs is the Pro Program, which verifies **occupation** (outdoor professional, environmental grant partner, outdoor industry), not military status — and it is currently only accepting applications for a limited list of categories. Use a work-issued email to apply. Pro questions: see the Pro Program FAQ in the Patagonia Help Center, or call 1-800-638-6464.

### FAQ

#### Does Patagonia offer a military discount?
No. As of June 23, 2026, Patagonia does not offer a military or veteran discount on its website or in stores. Its coupons page lists only Web Specials, free shipping, and Worn Wear as ways to save, with no military discount.

#### How much is the Patagonia military discount?
There is no Patagonia military discount, so there is no percentage to claim. Third-party sites listing "10% off" are not confirmed by any Patagonia source, and Patagonia advises ignoring sites claiming to offer its discount codes.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
No. No group — active duty, veterans, retirees, reserves/Guard, spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families — qualifies for a military discount, because Patagonia does not offer one.

#### How do I verify my military status with Patagonia?
You can't, because there is no military discount to unlock. Patagonia does not run a military-status verification flow for a consumer discount.

#### Does Patagonia use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Patagonia does not publish any ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute military verification for a discount. (By contrast, The North Face uses SheerID for its military discount.)

#### Is there a Patagonia Veterans Day military discount?
Not according to any Patagonia primary source. A "Veterans Day 10%" claim circulates on coupon/affiliate sites, but we could not verify it on patagonia.com. Treat it as unconfirmed and check Patagonia's site directly around that date.

#### Can military members get the discount in stores?
There is no military discount online or in stores. In-store Web Specials offer the same already-reduced pricing available to all customers.

#### Can I combine a military discount with promo codes or sale items?
Not applicable — there is no military discount. Patagonia rarely issues promo codes at all; Web Specials prices are applied automatically with no code.

#### Does the Patagonia Pro Program count as a military discount?
No. The Pro Program is a profession-based membership for qualified outdoor professionals, environmental grant recipients, and outdoor industry partners — eligibility is by job, not military service. Its category list does not include "military" or "veteran." Some roles a service member might hold (e.g., SAR, wildland fire) appear as professional categories, but the program is currently restricting new applications and is not a military discount.

#### Does Patagonia offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Patagonia does not publish a first-responder, teacher, nurse, government-employee, or student discount. The only application-based program is the occupation-based Pro Program described above.
