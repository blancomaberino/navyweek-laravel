**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a 1,000 kWh/month Texas household electricity plan (~14¢/kWh all-in ≈ **$140/month, ~$1,680/year**) — the unit that matters for an energy retailer is ¢/kWh, not a cart.

## Headline finding

**Direct Energy's military program is in limbo, and no discount amount is published anywhere.** SheerID's shopper site still lists a "Direct Energy: Military — Save on Home Services" offer ("an exclusive rate on your home energy needs" for "members of the US military and their registered dependents"), but **Direct Energy's own military landing page — directenergy.com/military — returned 404 on July 14, 2026**, and current editorial roundups of utility military discounts (ChooseEnergy, updated May 27, 2025) don't list Direct Energy at all. Historically (May 2017 press release) Direct Energy ran reduced military rates in 12 states + D.C. plus a year-round program at that now-dead URL. This is a **"myth"-leaning subscription brand (archetype H, with F mechanics)**: treat any quoted percentage as fabricated, and treat the offer itself as unverifiable until Direct Energy restores its page. In deregulated markets the honest play is comparison-shopping the all-in ¢/kWh rate — a 1¢/kWh difference (~$120/yr on this baseline) beats every token military discount in the category — plus the named competitors that DO publish military rates (BKV Energy 5%, Reliant's Hero plan).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + program terms)
1. **A "military rate" is a plan, not a coupon** — you enroll in it *instead of* a public plan. It never stacks with promotional new-customer rates; compare the two totals and pick one.
2. **BKV's 5% applies to the energy charge only**, not TDU delivery fees — the headline % overstates the bill impact (~3–4% of the total bill).
3. **Bill credits ≠ rate discounts** (Reliant Hero): credits are fixed; on a padded base rate they can be worth less than a cheaper public plan. Always compute the all-in annual cost.
4. **No cashback portals, GovX, or code-stacking exist for energy supply** — the category's only stack is a deposit waiver layered on whatever plan you choose.
5. **SCRA/PCS note:** service members should ask any provider about deposit waivers and early-termination handling on PCS orders before signing a fixed-term contract; terms vary by provider and state.
