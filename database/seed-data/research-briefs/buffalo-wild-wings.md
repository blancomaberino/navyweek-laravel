# Buffalo Wild Wings — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 4, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$25 pre-tax dine-in/takeout order — 10 Boneless Wings + Fries bundle (~$17 at typical locations; BWW prices vary by location) + a soft drink (~$3.50) + a shareable side. All effective prices below are pre-tax (no tax-free channel exists for a restaurant meal).

## Headline finding

Buffalo Wild Wings has **no official, corporate-wide everyday military discount**. The widely repeated "10% military discount" is **franchise-location discretion** — WeSalute itself lists it as a non-partner "community deal" that "varies by location," ID.me says it is "not aware that Buffalo Wild Wings offers Military discounts," and BWW's own corporate FAQ never mentions one. The one corporate-official military offer is the **Veterans Day free meal**: on November 11, 2025 BWW ran "Wings for Heroes" — 10 free boneless wings + regular fries for veterans and active-duty service members with valid proof of service, dine-in (or walk-in counter order at BWW GO) only. The rest of the year, the best savings paths are public, not military: Boneless Thursday BOGO, Wing Tuesday BOGO-50%, BWW Rewards points, and the seasonal gift-card bonus promo. Archetype: **J (unofficial/manager's-discretion) dominant, with an H-style holiday-only official offer (Veterans Day)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **BOGO deals do not combine with anything.** BWW's published legal terms: "Offers may not be combined with other discounts or offers. Limit 1." So no military 10% on top of a BOGO order, and third-party delivery (DoorDash/Uber Eats) is excluded from BOGOs entirely. Source: BWW BOGO Days legal terms, accessed July 4, 2026.
2. **One promo code per order** — BWW FAQ. There is no military promo code to enter anyway.
3. **BWW Rewards is the only true layer.** Corporate FAQ: "Rewards members will earn points and can redeem reward certificates on the same order as a promo code." Points earn on eligible purchases whatever else you're doing — this stacks under BOGOs and (where granted) the local military 10%, subject to each offer's own terms.
4. **The Veterans Day meal is a fixed free item** — no substitutions or add-ons; drinks and gratuity are on you (as reported for the 2025 offer). Whether the free meal still earns Rewards points is not stated in official sources.
5. **Gift-card bonus coupons** can't be used for gift cards or alcohol, can't be combined with an employee discount, and can't be redeemed via third-party delivery. Whether a bonus coupon can be used on a BOGO order is not stated in official sources — the BOGO "no combining" language suggests not; don't claim it.
6. **No cashback-portal question exists** — BWW is a restaurant; Rakuten-style portals don't apply to in-store dining, and no BWW portal listing was found for online food orders as of July 4, 2026.

Buffalo Wild Wings doesn't run an official, chain-wide military discount. Some franchise locations choose to give roughly 10% off to service members and veterans who show ID — but it's a store-by-store courtesy, not a corporate program, and neither ID.me nor any other verification service partners with BWW.

What Buffalo Wild Wings *does* do, reliably, is Veterans Day: every year we've checked (2019, 2021, 2024, 2025), veterans and active-duty service members got a free order of boneless wings and fries on November 11 — dine-in, with proof of service. The rest of the year, the honest best deal is public: Boneless Thursday BOGO through the BWW app beats a maybe-10% every time.

This is an independent guide. We're not affiliated with Buffalo Wild Wings, and BWW (and its franchisees) control these offers and can change them at any time.

### Who Qualifies
- Veterans — Veterans Day meal: yes, with proof of service (2025 offer, Military.com)
- Active duty — Veterans Day meal: yes (2025 offer)
- Retirees — described as included in 2019/2021 corporate PR ("active and retired military personnel"); 2025 copy said "veterans and active-duty" — retirees qualify as veterans in practice; Not stated more precisely in official source
- Reserve/National Guard — Not stated in official source (listing sites report reservists included; unverified)
- Military spouses / dependents — Not stated in official source; Veterans Day meal is for the service member/veteran only, one per person
- First responders, teachers, nurses, students, government employees — No offer found in any official source

### How To Redeem — Veterans Day (in restaurant)
1. Go to a participating Buffalo Wild Wings on November 11 (dine-in), or a Buffalo Wild Wings GO and order at the counter.
2. Tell your server/cashier you're there for the Veterans Day offer before ordering.
3. Show proof of service: military ID, VA card, driver's license with veteran designation, or a veterans-organization membership card (accepted forms per the 2024–2025 offers).
4. Receive 10 free boneless wings + regular fries. No substitutions or add-ons; drinks and tip are separate. One per person.
5. Delivery and third-party apps (DoorDash, Uber Eats) are excluded.

### How To Redeem — Everyday 10% (if your location honors it)
1. Ask your server or the register staff whether the location offers a military discount — before you pay.
2. Show a valid military/veteran ID.
3. If granted, it's applied at the register. There is no online field, no code, and no way to use it on app orders.

### How Verification Works
There is no digital verification. BWW has no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute integration — everything is physical ID shown in person, and for the everyday discount, acceptance is up to the individual franchise. A WeSalute+ card *may* be accepted as proof at stores that honor a discount (WeSalute's own suggestion), but no store is required to take it.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Veterans Day meal: dine-in / GO counter only; no delivery or third-party apps; no substitutions or add-ons; excludes drinks and gratuity; one per person (2025 terms as reported).
- BOGO deals: "may not be combined with other discounts or offers," size exclusions, limit 1, third-party delivery excluded (BWW legal terms).
- Promo codes: one per order (BWW FAQ).
- Gift-card bonus coupons: food/non-alcoholic only; not with employee discount; no third-party delivery; time-boxed windows.
- Franchise participation varies for everything — about half of US locations are franchised.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Boneless Thursday BOGO** — buy any boneless order via the BWW app/site (takeout/delivery) or dine-in, get one of equal/lesser value free.
- **Wing Tuesday** — second traditional-wing order 50% off (BWW's FAQ copy says free for dine-in Rewards members).
- **BWW Rewards** — free; 10 points/$1 (12 after $250/year); points expire after 1 year; stacks with promo codes.
- **Gift-card bonus windows** — most recently $10 bonus per $50 in gift cards (April 27 – June 21, 2026); watch for the next window.
- **Sam's Club / Costco gift-card multipacks** — rotate in and out of stock at a discount; check current pricing.
- **Competitor cross-shop:** many chains run true Veterans Day free meals (Applebee's, Chili's, etc.); for everyday military discounts, some competing wing/sports-bar chains also leave it to franchise discretion — no clearly better everyday competitor program was verified in this pass.

### FAQ
#### Does Buffalo Wild Wings offer a military discount?
Not officially. There's no corporate program, and BWW's own FAQ and site never mention one. Some franchise locations voluntarily give about 10% off with military ID — always ask first. The one company-promoted offer is the annual Veterans Day free meal.

#### How much is the Buffalo Wild Wings military discount?
Where a franchise honors one, shoppers and listing sites report about 10%. Buffalo Wild Wings publishes no amount, so treat any number as location-specific.

#### What does Buffalo Wild Wings give away on Veterans Day?
In 2025 ("Wings for Heroes," November 11, 2025): 10 free boneless wings and regular fries for veterans and active-duty service members with proof of service — dine-in or BWW GO counter orders only, no substitutions, one per person. BWW has run essentially this offer every year we checked since 2019.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The Veterans Day meal covers veterans and active-duty service members; it's one meal per eligible person, and family members aren't included. For the everyday franchise 10%, who qualifies is up to the location.

#### Does Buffalo Wild Wings use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute?
No. ID.me states it isn't aware of any BWW military discount, and WeSalute lists BWW only as a non-partner "community deal" it can't guarantee. Verification is a physical ID at the register.

#### Can I get the military discount online or in the app?
No. There's no military field, code, or verification online. Everything military at BWW happens in person.

#### Can I combine a military discount with BOGO deals or promo codes?
No. BWW's BOGO terms say offers "may not be combined with other discounts or offers," and the FAQ limits orders to one promo code. Only BWW Rewards points layer on top of other offers.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at Buffalo Wild Wings?
On November 11: the free Veterans Day meal. Any other week: order boneless wings on a Thursday (BOGO free) through the BWW app with a free Rewards account — that's roughly 50% off the wings, versus $2.50 off a $25 order from a maybe-10%.

#### Does Buffalo Wild Wings offer first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discounts?
No official program was found for any of these groups. Individual franchises may make their own gestures; ask locally.

#### Does Buffalo Wild Wings run a Memorial Day or Military Appreciation Month deal?
No corporate offer was found for May 2024–2026. Some individual locations honor a 10% military discount in May (local-press reports); that's franchise discretion, not a chain promotion.
