# Hobby Lobby Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Hobby Lobby does not offer a military or veteran discount.** As of June 23, 2026, Hobby Lobby's official website lists no military, veteran, first-responder, teacher, nurse, student, or government discount of any kind. The company's own customer-service FAQ has a dedicated "Sales, product pricing, & discounts" section that addresses competitor price matching, bulk discounts, and rain checks — but mentions no military discount. Hobby Lobby's only public acknowledgment of the military is a 2017 newsroom article ("Honoring Veterans") that praises veterans as employees but offers no shopper discount. Online claims of a "10% military discount" or "50% military discount" appear only on coupon aggregators and unsourced posts; none is confirmed by Hobby Lobby, and they should be treated as false. The most important caveat for shoppers: the famous single-item 40%-off coupon was discontinued (reported around 2021, partly tied to a furniture-pricing class-action settlement) and no longer exists. Today everyone — military or not — saves the same way: through always-on category pricing (e.g., "Always 40% Off The Marked Price") in the weekly ad, plus clearance.

### FAQ

#### Does Hobby Lobby offer a military discount?
No. As of June 23, 2026, Hobby Lobby does not offer a military or veteran discount. Its customer-service FAQ has a discounts section that lists no military discount, and there is no military offer anywhere on hobbylobby.com.

#### How much is the Hobby Lobby military discount?
There is no Hobby Lobby military discount, so there is no percentage. Online claims of "10% off" or "50% off" for military come from coupon aggregators and unverified posts, not Hobby Lobby, and are not confirmed.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
No. Hobby Lobby publishes no discount for veterans, active duty, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families.

#### How do I verify my military status with Hobby Lobby?
You don't — there is nothing to verify. Hobby Lobby has no military-verification step in store or online.

#### Does Hobby Lobby use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Hobby Lobby does not use any military-verification provider, because it offers no military discount to gate.

#### Can I use a military discount in Hobby Lobby stores?
No. There is no military discount in stores or online. All shoppers pay the same advertised and marked prices.

#### Does Hobby Lobby still have the 40% off coupon?
No. The single-item 40%-off coupon was discontinued (reported around 2021, partly tied to a furniture-pricing class-action settlement). Hobby Lobby now uses always-on category pricing — for example, "Always 40% Off The Marked Price" on many departments — instead of a one-item coupon.

Hobby Lobby's weekly ad excludes the Seasonal Department and items labeled Kitchen, Bath, Closet, Gift, or "Your Price." No coupons or other discounts apply to "Your Price" items, and ads don't apply to pre-reduced items.

#### Does Hobby Lobby offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No. Hobby Lobby publishes no audience-based discount for first responders, teachers, nurses, government employees, or students. Classroom decor and dorm essentials do appear in general weekly-ad sales available to everyone.

#### How can military families save at Hobby Lobby?
Use the same savings everyone gets: the weekly ad's everyday category markdowns (often 40%, some 50%), clearance, the email list, and competitor price matching on custom framing. For audience-specific craft-store discounts, check Michaels and JOANN directly.
