# Silencer Shop — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **SilencerCo Omega 36M** suppressor, listed at **$993.00** at Silencer Shop, plus the NFA tax stamp — which is **$0** in 2026 (was $200 through 2025).

## Headline finding

Silencer Shop **does** run an official military/first-responder program — the **Sound-Off Program** — open to active-duty military, veterans, law enforcement, firefighters, and EMS. But it is a **call-for-pricing** discount: after Silencer Shop verifies your credentials (typically within a day), you phone **512-931-4556** and a rep quotes "best pricing" on select silencers and accessories. **No percentage or dollar amount is published anywhere on Silencer Shop's site**, and there is **no online self-serve code, GovX, or ID.me checkout**. A separate manufacturer program, **SilencerCo SPEQ** ("Service Proven Equipment"), gives qualified professionals a flat, SilencerCo-set discount on SilencerCo suppressors — also confidential pricing, redeemed through a SPEQ dealer. Archetype: **J (unofficial / manager's-discretion, call-for-pricing)** with a strong **K (pro-deal)** secondary via SPEQ. The single biggest 2026 cost change for *everyone*, military or not, is the **$200 NFA tax stamp dropping to $0** (H.R.1, effective Jan 1, 2026) — that dwarfs any percentage discount. Because suppressors are NFA items, the usual money-savers (GovX marketplace, cashback portals, the tax-free exchange) **do not apply**.

### FAQ

#### Does Silencer Shop offer a military discount?
Yes — the **Sound-Off Program** for verified active-duty military, veterans, law enforcement, firefighters, and EMS. It's quoted by phone after verification, not a posted percentage.

#### How much is the Silencer Shop military discount?
Silencer Shop does **not publish a percentage or dollar amount**. You verify, then call 512-931-4556 for pricing. Sites advertising a flat "15% off" are not citing an official Silencer Shop figure (an old forum reported ~15%, unverified).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify (SilencerCo SPEQ specifies honorable discharge with a DD-214). **Spouses and dependents are not stated as eligible** — treat as unconfirmed.

#### How do I verify my status?
Submit "a range of IDs" through the Sound-Off page for manual review (usually approved within a day). SPEQ verification is handled via SilencerCo/the SPEQ dealer.

#### Does Silencer Shop use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**No** — none is named on the Sound-Off page. Verification is manual and redemption is by phone. NFA suppressors also aren't sold through the GovX marketplace.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Sound-Off pricing is arranged by phone with Silencer Shop; SPEQ goes through a SilencerCo SPEQ dealer. Neither is a self-serve online code.

#### What is excluded?
Only "select" silencers/accessories are covered (list not published), SPEQ is SilencerCo-only, and all purchases remain full NFA transfers.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Sound-Off and SPEQ aren't additive — take the better single quote. Whether either combines with the current manufacturer credits (e.g. $250 SilencerCo) is **not published**; ask the rep.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Silencer Shop?
Get **both** a Sound-Off and (for a SilencerCo can) a **SPEQ** quote, take the lower, and ask whether the live manufacturer credit stacks. Everyone also now saves **$200** because the tax stamp is **$0** in 2026.

#### Does Silencer Shop offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders (LE, fire, EMS) qualify for Sound-Off/SPEQ. **No** teacher, nurse, or student program is published. Agencies can use **Sound-Off Agency** / SPEQ for department purchases.

#### Does Silencer Shop run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No dedicated military-holiday percentage is published — Sound-Off runs year-round, and public savings come from ongoing manufacturer rebates rather than holiday sales.

#### Can I buy through the Exchange or a pro-deal program?
The military **exchanges don't sell NFA suppressors**. The closest "pro deal" is **SilencerCo SPEQ**, the qualified-professional pricing program described above.
