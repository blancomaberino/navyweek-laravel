# Fred Meyer — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one third-Thursday trip with **$100 of *eligible* items** (Fred Meyer / Kroger / Simple Truth store-brand groceries + general merchandise like apparel, home, toys, most electronics). The military discount only touches eligible items, so the math is measured on those, not on a full mixed cart.

## Headline finding

**Fred Meyer has a real, first-party, published military discount — but it's day-boxed and category-boxed.** On the **third Thursday of every month**, enrolled current military members, their families, and veterans (with ID) get an **extra 10% off select items** — in store or on Pickup/Delivery orders placed that day. The 10% covers **private-brand groceries** (Fred Meyer, Kroger, Simple Truth and other store brands), apparel/shoes/accessories, all home/toys/sporting goods/auto/garden, most electronics, and Fred Meyer Jewelers. Crucially, it **excludes name-brand (non–private-label) groceries**, produce/deli/bakery/meat sold by the pound, pharmacy, alcohol, tobacco, fuel, gift cards, and more — so it is *not* "10% off your whole grocery bill."

This is the honest correction of the widespread **"Kroger 10% military discount" reputation**: Kroger's corporate "Honoring Our Heroes" page is a hiring/USO **PR page with no storewide discount**. The recurring 10% program lives at the **banner/division level** — and **Fred Meyer (a Kroger division serving the Pacific Northwest) is one of the banners that actually runs it**, on the third-Thursday schedule. Don't cite a flat everyday Kroger-wide 10% — that's the myth.

Archetype: a rare **real first-party discount that's time-restricted (day-boxed)**, sitting on top of a **loyalty-wallet of everyday value** (Fred Meyer Rewards: sale prices, digital coupons, fuel points, price match — archetype **H**-adjacent) — with the **commissary (DeCA)** as the tax-free grocery alternative for eligible shoppers (archetype **L**-adjacent). The best *routine* grocery savings for most readers is the free Rewards card + fuel points; the military 10% is a monthly bonus best aimed at **store-brand stock-ups and general-merchandise/jewelry buys** on the right Thursday.

**Verdict logic:**
- **Best for a store-brand stock-up or a general-merchandise/jewelry buy →** enroll once, then shop the **third Thursday** for the 10%.
- **Best for everyday name-brand groceries →** the free **Fred Meyer Rewards** card (sale prices + digital coupons + fuel points) — the 10% doesn't cover those items at all.
- **Best for a commissary-eligible shopper's main grocery run →** the **commissary (DeCA)** tax-free at-cost pricing usually beats Fred Meyer even with the 10%, especially on name brands.
- On-page 1–2 question chooser: *"Is it the third Thursday and are you enrolled?"* → use the 10% on eligible items. *"Are you commissary-eligible?"* → price your name-brand grocery run there; use Fred Meyer for convenience + fuel points the rest of the month.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 10% only ever applies to *eligible* items** — name-brand groceries, produce/deli/bakery/meat by the pound, pharmacy, alcohol, tobacco, fuel, and gift cards are excluded, so it is never "10% off the whole cart." This is the single most misunderstood fact.
2. **Card sale prices are the base the 10% comes off of** — you shop with your enrolled Fred Meyer Rewards account, and eligible items show the extra 10% at order time. The discount is **account-linked**, not a code.
3. **Fuel Stop purchases are excluded** from the 10% — but you still **earn** fuel points on qualifying grocery spend and redeem them separately at the pump. Loyalty fuel points are a *parallel* saving, not a stack on the 10%.
4. **No cashback portal or GovX layer exists** for a Fred Meyer grocery cart — there's nothing to stack on top; ignore aggregator "stack Rakuten" advice for this brand.
5. **"Combine with coupons" is promo-specific, not the standing rule** — the monthly page doesn't promise coupon-stacking; only certain holiday promos (e.g. a past Veterans Day post) said so. Digital coupons may apply to items the 10% excludes, which is where they matter most.
6. **Boost membership stacks structurally** (it changes fuel-point earn rate and delivery fees) but is a paid add-on, not a discount on the cart.

1. Sign in to your **Fred Meyer Rewards** account (create a free one if needed).

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Enroll/verify your military or veteran status in advance (or bring a valid military/veteran **ID** to the customer-service desk).
2. Shop on the **third Thursday** of the month.
3. Use your enrolled Fred Meyer Rewards account at checkout; the extra 10% applies to eligible items.

Editorial sources report the online enrollment uses **SheerID** to verify military/veteran status against records; in store, staff can verify with a valid military or veteran **ID** at the customer-service desk. The discount is tied to your Fred Meyer Rewards account, so once enrolled you don't re-verify every trip. Fred Meyer's page does not publish the exact re-verification cadence — confirm at enrollment.

### FAQ

#### Does Fred Meyer offer a military discount?
Yes. Fred Meyer gives an extra **10% off select items on the third Thursday of every month** to enrolled current military members, their families, and veterans with ID, in store or on Pickup/Delivery orders placed that day.

#### How much is the Fred Meyer military discount?
**10% off eligible items** — but only on the third Thursday, and only on qualifying items (store-brand groceries, apparel, home, most electronics, jewelry). It does not apply to name-brand groceries, produce/meat by the pound, pharmacy, alcohol, tobacco, fuel, or gift cards.

#### Is there really a "Kroger 10% military discount" every day?
No. Kroger's corporate "Honoring Our Heroes" page is a veteran-hiring and USO-partnership page with **no storewide percentage**. The recurring 10% is a **banner-level program on one day a month**, and Fred Meyer (a Kroger division in the Pacific Northwest) is one of the banners that runs it.

#### Do veterans, Guard/Reserve, and family members qualify?
Fred Meyer's page names current military members, their families, and veterans with ID; editorial coverage adds National Guard and Reserves. Bring a valid military or veteran ID.

#### How do I verify my status?
Enroll in the Military Discount through your Fred Meyer Rewards account (reported to use **SheerID** online), or show a valid military/veteran ID at the store's customer-service desk. The discount is linked to your account once enrolled.

#### Does Fred Meyer use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Editorial sources report Fred Meyer's online enrollment runs through **SheerID**; the primary page confirms an enrollment step but doesn't name the provider. There's no GovX storefront and no WeSalute requirement for this offer.

#### Can I use it online or only in stores?
Both — in store or on Pickup/Delivery orders placed on the third Thursday, once you're enrolled.

#### What's excluded?
Name-brand (non–private-label) groceries, produce/deli/bakery/meat sold by the pound, pharmacy, alcohol, tobacco, fuel, gift cards, cosmetics, Carhartt, game consoles, and various services/fees. The 10% covers store brands and general merchandise, not your whole grocery bill.

#### Can I combine it with coupons or fuel points?
The discount is account-linked and applies to eligible items; it's not a stackable code. You still **earn fuel points** on qualifying spend (redeemed separately at the pump), and **digital coupons** often apply to the very items the 10% excludes — so use both. "Combine with coupons" language only appeared in past **holiday** promos, not the standing monthly rule.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Fred Meyer?
Get the free **Fred Meyer Rewards** card for everyday sale prices, digital coupons, and fuel points; use the **military 10% on the third Thursday** for store-brand stock-ups and general-merchandise/jewelry buys; and if you're **commissary-eligible**, run your name-brand groceries through the tax-free commissary, which usually beats the 10%.

#### Does Fred Meyer offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Fred Meyer's military-discount page does **not** state a first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student tier. Don't assume one exists — confirm with the store.

#### Am I eligible for the commissary as a cheaper grocery option?
Commissary (DeCA) access covers active duty, Guard/Reserve, retirees, and their dependents, plus — since Jan 1, 2020 — **all service-connected disabled veterans (any rating), Purple Heart recipients, former POWs, and eligible VA-program caregivers**. Commissaries sell at cost plus a 5% surcharge, tax-free, and usually beat Fred Meyer's 10% on name-brand groceries.
