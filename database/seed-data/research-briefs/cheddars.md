# Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic two-person dinner at Cheddar's — roughly a $45 dine-in check before tax and tip (two entrées ~$14 each + one shared appetizer ~$8 + two drinks ~$5).

## Headline finding

Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen (a Darden Restaurants brand) publishes **no year-round, company-wide military or veteran discount** — its own FAQ lists none, and like sister brands Olive Garden and LongHorn, Darden runs *occasion-based* military recognition rather than an everyday percentage off. The one consistent, brand-confirmed offer is a **free appetizer or dessert on Veterans Day (November 11) for veterans and active-duty military, dine-in, with proof of service** — run in both 2024 and 2025. Outside that single day, the best a service member can do is stack ordinary savings anyone can get: the **eClub free Chips & Homemade Queso** on signup, weekday **lunch specials from $8.79**, and **2% cash back on gift cards via Rakuten** (or up to 1% via ID.me Shop). Archetype: **casual-dining chain with no national discount — occasional-holiday freebie + loyalty/gift-card play**. Ignore the coupon-site "10–15% military discount" and "20% promo code" claims; they are unverified and do not match any official Cheddar's source.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **No promo codes at all for dining.** Cheddar's dine-in checks have no online coupon field; "20% off codes" on aggregator sites do not apply to a restaurant bill. Nothing stacks onto the check itself except a paper/eClub offer the server applies.
2. **eClub freebie and Veterans Day freebie are both server-applied comps**, not percentage discounts — you can't "stack" two free-item offers on one check as a rule; use whichever is worth more (Veterans Day app/dessert on Nov 11, eClub queso otherwise).
3. **Gift-card cash back is the only true stack.** Rakuten (2%) and ID.me (1%) pay cash back on **gift-card purchases**, per their own terms ("Cash Back is only available on gift cards") — not on the meal. You buy the gift card through the portal, then pay your check with it. You cannot double-dip Rakuten *and* ID.me on the same purchase.
4. **Veterans Day terms** are dine-in, proof-of-service, participation-may-vary; beverages/tax/tip not included by analogy to Darden sister-brand terms — **call the local restaurant to confirm** before relying on it.

### FAQ

#### Does Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen offer a military discount?
Not as a year-round program. Cheddar's publishes no standing military or veteran discount. Its main military offer is a **free appetizer or dessert on Veterans Day** for veterans and active-duty who dine in with proof of service.

#### How much is the Cheddar's military discount?
There is no set percentage. Off Veterans Day, there is no confirmed discount at all. On November 11, the benefit is a **free appetizer or dessert** (roughly $6–$10 in value), not a percent off the check.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and active-duty qualify for the Veterans Day offer with proof of service. Cheddar's has **not** published any offer for spouses or dependents.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person — show a valid military ID or other proof of service to your server on Veterans Day. There is no online verification step.

#### Does Cheddar's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No — none of these gate a Cheddar's military discount. ID.me Shop only offers up to **1% cash back on Cheddar's gift cards**, which is a shopping-portal perk, not a military discount.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The Veterans Day offer is **dine-in only**. Cheddar's has no online ordering discount and no promo-code field for a military discount.

#### What is excluded?
No everyday discount exists to exclude. For the Veterans Day comp, expect standard limits (dine-in, proof of service, participation varies; likely excludes alcohol/tax/tip) — confirm with your restaurant.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Cheddar's dine-in checks don't take promo codes, and comped items (eClub queso, Veterans Day app/dessert) generally don't stack with each other. The only real stack is buying a **gift card through Rakuten (2%)** and paying your check with it.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Cheddar's?
On **Veterans Day**, dine in with your ID for the free appetizer or dessert. Any other day, **join the eClub** for the free queso, eat at **lunch (from $8.79)**, and pre-buy **gift cards through Rakuten** for 2% back.

#### Does Cheddar's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No — none of these programs are published by Cheddar's.

#### Does Cheddar's run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
**Veterans Day, yes** (historically) — a free appetizer or dessert. **Memorial Day** — no brand-confirmed offer; aggregator mentions are unverified.
