## Headline finding

COBB Tuning runs a **real, official military discount: 15% off a single purchase at cobbtuning.com**, verified through **SheerID**, for **"active and veteran U.S. military personnel."** You get a **unique, single-use coupon** and can **generate a new one once every 30 days**. It only loses when COBB itself runs a public sale deeper than 15% (rare; typical COBB sales are ~10%), in which case the greater discount auto-applies instead. No GovX, ExpertVoice/pro-deal, or military-exchange channel for COBB was found.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The military 15% **cannot be combined** with any other COBB promotional discount — COBB's exact wording: "cannot be stacked onto any existing promotional discounts offered and the greater discount will be applied." So there is no "military + sale" combo; the system uses whichever single discount is larger.
2. The coupon is **unique and single-use** (one purchase per code). A new code can be generated **once every 30 days** — so it's not a standing account-level discount you can reuse freely.
3. SheerID verifies eligibility and issues the code; it does not change COBB's stacking rule. The discount lives entirely on cobbtuning.com and is **not redeemable through authorized dealers** (MAPerformance, SubiSpeed, etc.), who set their own MAP-based pricing.
