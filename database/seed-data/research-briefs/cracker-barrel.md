# Cracker Barrel Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Cracker Barrel Old Country Store offers a **10% in-store military discount** to Cracker Barrel Rewards members who are active-duty, reserve, retired, or veteran members of the U.S. Armed Forces. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**, which must be linked to the member's Cracker Barrel Rewards account; once verified, the discount attaches automatically and is applied when the member gives their Rewards account info to the cashier. The single most important caveat: **the discount is in-store only** — it is not valid on online, catering, delivery, or takeout orders, and excludes alcohol, Meals for Two, Take Home Meals, gift cards, taxes, delivery fees, and gratuities. Separately, Cracker Barrel has run a one-day Veterans Day free-meal promotion (a complimentary Sunrise Pancake Special on Nov 11, 2025, dine-in, proof of service required); that is a limited-time annual promo, not the everyday discount, and must be re-verified each year.

### How To Redeem Online
The everyday 10% military discount is **not available online**. It is valid only on in-store purchases. (Source: ID.me restrictions text; Cracker Barrel Rewards military-discount page.)

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Enroll in Cracker Barrel Rewards (free) if you are not already a member.
2. Log in to your Rewards account and verify your military/veteran status through ID.me, using the same email address on ID.me as on your Rewards account.
3. Once verified, the discount attaches to your account automatically for future purchases.
4. At checkout in any participating Cracker Barrel, give the cashier your Cracker Barrel Rewards account information; the 10% discount is applied automatically to qualifying items.
5. Be prepared to present a valid military or veteran ID or your ID.me verification at the time of purchase.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me** and linked to your Cracker Barrel Rewards account. Use the same email on both accounts. After successful verification, the benefit attaches to your Rewards account so you do not re-verify on every visit. The official source does not state a re-verification interval for the everyday discount, so do not promise it is permanent — ID.me may require periodic re-verification per its own policies. If automatic verification fails, follow ID.me's support flow and Cracker Barrel Rewards support; the official page does not detail a separate manual fallback. (Source: Cracker Barrel Rewards military-discount page; ID.me.)

### FAQ

#### Does Cracker Barrel offer a military discount?
Yes. Cracker Barrel offers a 10% discount on qualifying in-store restaurant and retail purchases to Cracker Barrel Rewards members who are active-duty, reserve, retired, or veteran members of the U.S. Armed Forces, with eligibility verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the Cracker Barrel military discount?
It is 10% off qualifying in-store transactions for verified Rewards members. (Source: ID.me restrictions text.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify, along with active-duty, reserve, and retired members. The official restrictions text does not list military spouses or dependents as eligible for the everyday discount — their eligibility is not stated in the official source.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Log in to your Cracker Barrel Rewards account and verify your service through ID.me, using the same email on both. Once verified, the discount attaches to your account automatically.

#### Does Cracker Barrel use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Cracker Barrel uses **ID.me** for the everyday military discount, linked to a Rewards account. (Third-party sites like WeSalute may list Cracker Barrel offers, but the official verification path is ID.me via Cracker Barrel Rewards.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes — the discount is in-store only. It is not valid on online, catering, delivery, or takeout orders.

#### What is excluded?
Alcoholic beverages, Meals for Two, Take Home Meals, gift cards, taxes, delivery fees, and gratuities are excluded, and the discount is not valid on online/catering/delivery/takeout orders.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The official source does not state whether the military discount stacks with other promos, sales, or rewards offers. Do not assume it combines; ask staff at the register.

#### Does Cracker Barrel offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Not through its ID.me program. As of the access date, ID.me lists only a Military offer for Cracker Barrel — no first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount is shown.

#### Is the Cracker Barrel Veterans Day free meal still available?
Cracker Barrel offered a free Sunrise Pancake Special on Veterans Day (Nov 11, 2025), dine-in only, with proof of service. This is an annual limited-time promotion; confirm availability and terms each year, as the specific offer can change.
