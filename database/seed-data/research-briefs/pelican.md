A separate **GovX "Pro Deal" storefront** (govx.com/m/116/pelican) exists as an alternate verified channel with gated member pricing worth price-checking. Archetype: **E — single-tier identity discount** (the ID.me 15% basically *is* the first-party savings path; no meaningful public-code or cashback game), with a strong secondary **L — exchange-carried tax-free path**, plus a GovX pro-deal marketplace (A-flavor) as an alternate. Do **not** repeat aggregator claims of "30–40% off" sitewide codes — none is a verified Pelican military discount.

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **ID.me 15% is one order-level discount with restricted combining** — Pelican states "restrictions may apply when combining ID.me discount with other discounts or promotions." Treat it as **non-combinable** with promo codes and sale events; confirm in the cart order summary.
2. **ID.me 15% vs. a public Pelican sale = pick one.** When the sale price beats 15% off MSRP, buy the sale.
3. **The GovX "Pro Deal" storefront is a separate, closed marketplace** — its member price is the price; no brand code, ID.me discount, or cashback stacks on top. It's a *substitute* for the pelican.com route, not an addition.
4. **Exchange (NEX/AAFES) is a separate tax-free channel** — you don't stack the ID.me discount on an exchange purchase; you choose one channel. The exchange's value is no sales tax + its own pricing.
5. **Scope limits:** ID.me discount is **domestic orders only**, **personal use only**, and **not for federal government purchases**.
6. **Cashback:** unverified this pass; an ID.me/identity-gated checkout typically breaks portal tracking, so don't assume a portal rate survives the ID.me discount.
