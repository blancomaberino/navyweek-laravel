**Researched:** July 5, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adult 1-day general admissions (standard adult admission is dynamically priced — recently ≈ $77 + tax each; **verify live at checkout**).

## Headline finding

Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex (operated for NASA by **Delaware North**, not government-funded) gives the military community two distinct things. Year-round, its FAQ confirms an official **Military and Military Child admission discount** — but the exact dollar amount is **not published** on the official site (third parties report roughly $5 off; treat as unverified). The far bigger win is the annual **Armed Forces Week (mid-May): free admission for service members — current and former — plus 50% off up to four guests and 20% off retail.** So the honest headline: *if you can time your visit to mid-May, go free; otherwise the year-round military discount is modest and you should price-compare it against the GovX military/gov't ticket channel and any live seasonal sale (Veterans Day in November).* Archetype: **I (attraction/travel — the calendar and the channel matter more than a single %)**.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (year-round discount; free during Armed Forces Week).
- **Reserves / National Guard** — Eligible for Armed Forces Week (stated: "active duty, reserves, or National Guard"); year-round via military ID.
- **Veterans / former service members** — Eligible for Armed Forces Week (verify with DD214, Choose VA card, or driver's license with veteran designation).
- **Retired military** — Eligible (retired military ID accepted).
- **Military children / dependents** — A **Military Child** admission discount is offered year-round; guest coverage during Armed Forces Week is the 50%-off-up-to-4-guests term.
- **Spouses** — Not separately named; covered under the guest term during Armed Forces Week. Verify year-round eligibility on-site.
- **Seniors** — Separate year-round Senior discount (not military).

### How To Redeem
**Armed Forces Week (mid-May):**
1. Bring proof of service — active-duty/retired military ID, Choose VA card, a driver's license with veteran designation, or DD214.
2. Buy on-site at **Will Call or Information**, at the kiosks in front of the park, or online during the promo window.
3. Online/kiosk purchases require **ID.me** verification before checkout.
4. Free service-member ticket + 50% off up to four guests. Limit one complimentary ticket per person per day; bring your ID with your ticket.

**Year-round military discount:**
1. At the **Ticket Plaza**, present a valid military photo ID and ask for the Military (or Military Child) admission rate.
2. Or buy discounted advance tickets online with **ID.me** verification.
3. Compare that price on your date against the **GovX** listing and any live seasonal sale before purchasing.

### How Verification Works
On-site, KSC accepts a physical military/veteran credential (active-duty or retired military ID, Choose VA card, driver's license with veteran designation, or DD214). Online and at the front-of-park kiosks, verification runs through **ID.me**. A veteran who isn't set up with ID.me can still redeem **in person** with a qualifying document.

### FAQ

#### Does Kennedy Space Center offer a military discount?
Yes. There's a year-round Military (and Military Child) admission discount, and each May during **Armed Forces Week**, service members — current and former — get in **free**, with 50% off up to four guests.

#### How much is the Kennedy Space Center military discount?
KSC confirms a year-round military discount but **doesn't publish the exact amount** online — confirm it at the Ticket Plaza or via ID.me. The clearly documented offer is Armed Forces Week: **free** service-member admission, 50% off up to four guests, and 20% off retail.

#### Do veterans qualify, or only active duty?
Both. Armed Forces Week explicitly covers those who "currently serve or formerly served" — verify with a DD214, Choose VA card, or a driver's license with veteran designation. Year-round, bring a valid military/veteran ID.

#### How do I verify my military status?
On-site: a military/veteran ID, Choose VA card, driver's license with veteran designation, or DD214 at Will Call or Information. Online or at the front-of-park kiosks: **ID.me**.

#### Does Kennedy Space Center use ID.me or GovX?
Both exist as channels: **ID.me** verifies online/kiosk military ticket purchases, and KSC military/government tickets are also sold on **GovX**. Compare prices before buying.

#### When is Armed Forces Week 2026?
**May 11–17, 2026.** (In 2025 it ran May 12–18 — it recurs each May.)

#### Can I buy the military tickets online or only in person?
Both. Buy on-site at Will Call/Information or the kiosks, or online with ID.me verification. Physical-ID redemption is in person.

#### What's excluded?
During Armed Forces Week: one comp ticket per person per day and a four-guest cap on the 50% discount. Year-round, the exact discount isn't posted, add-on experiences may be excluded, and prices are dynamic — confirm at checkout.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit?
Go during **Armed Forces Week (mid-May)** — you're free and up to four guests are half price. Any other time, compare the on-site/ID.me military rate, the GovX price, and any live seasonal sale on your date, and buy the lowest.

#### Is there a first responder, teacher, or student discount?
KSC's FAQ names Senior, Military, and Military Child year-round admission discounts; it does not advertise standing first-responder/teacher/student rates. Check GovX (first responders) and seasonal promos.
