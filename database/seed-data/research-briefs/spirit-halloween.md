## Headline finding

Spirit Halloween runs a **seasonal 10% military & first-responder discount** during the Halloween shopping window (roughly late Aug–early Nov). Online you select **"Military and First Responder Discount"** at checkout and verify eligibility (SheerID is the most likely provider — see the debunk below), which returns a one-time 10% code; in a physical store you simply show a valid military/first-responder ID at the register. Archetype: **seasonal identity discount** — a modest flat percentage that appears and disappears with the season, with **no year-round program** and no premium (holiday) military spike beyond the standard 10%. The honest maximum-savings truth: **10% is small and Spirit Halloween's public promos routinely beat it** — sitewide coupon codes (commonly 20%+ off, deeper on clearance) and end-of-season markdowns typically save more than the identity discount, and a **cashback portal (Rakuten ~2%) layered on a plain public-sale order** is often the smarter play. Use the 10% only on full-price items when no better public code is live.
