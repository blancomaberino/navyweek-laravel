## Headline finding

Chêne Gear (premium waterfowl waders, chenegear.com) has **no percentage-off military discount and no checkout verification (no ID.me/SheerID/GovX)** — instead, active-duty military members and veterans (plus current law enforcement, fire, and EMT/paramedic personnel) can **apply to the Chêne Pro Program**, a brand-run, application-only program with **unpublished, confidential member pricing**. Verification is manual: military applicants upload a **DD Form 214 or LES**. Membership is not guaranteed, is personal-use-only, and can be revoked. Because Chêne runs essentially no public sales or coupon channel, the Pro Program is the only real savings path — archetype **K (pro-deal brand)** with **E** tendencies (the identity program IS the savings path; no stacking game exists).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to chenegear.com → Pro Program and start the application with your email.
2. Select **Military / First Responder** as your group type.
3. Upload proof: **DD Form 214 or LES** (military) or a paystub within 60 days / tax document (first responders). Black out SSN/banking details first; the file must show your name and a current date.
4. Complete contact and sizing details, accept the terms (personal use only, no resale, pricing confidential, stay email-subscribed).
5. Wait for review — some applications auto-approve, others are reviewed manually.
6. Once approved, log in at chenegear.com; Pro pricing appears in your account. It works nowhere else.

### How Verification Works
Chêne verifies in-house — there is **no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX**. You upload documentation with the application, and Chêne's team reviews it. Approval isn't guaranteed, and membership can be revoked at any time for terms violations. The page doesn't state a re-verification cadence for military members; first-responder proof must be recent (≤60 days) at application.

Yes, through its Pro Program: active-duty military, veterans, and current law enforcement/fire/EMT personnel can apply for member pricing. It's an application, not an automatic checkout discount.

No. Verification is Chêne's own document-review application. There's also no GovX storefront for the brand.

No — Pro pricing is valid only at chenegear.com through your approved account.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sales?
There's nothing to combine — Chêne publishes no public promo codes, and Pro pricing is its own account-based price.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Chêne waders?
Get approved for the Pro Program first, then buy through your account at chenegear.com. There is no verified cheaper route.

#### Does Chêne offer first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discounts?
Law enforcement, fire, and EMT/paramedic personnel qualify under the same Military/First Responder tier. Teachers, nurses, students, and government employees are not listed.
