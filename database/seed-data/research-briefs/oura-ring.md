### How To Redeem Online (Student / Educator / Healthcare / First Responder — 10% via ID.me)
1. Go to Oura's Preferred Pricing page (ouraring.com/oura-preferred-pricing).
2. Select "Verify With ID.me" and complete ID.me verification of your identity and professional/student status.
3. Once verified, the 10% discount applies automatically at checkout on Oura Ring 4 and Oura Ring 4 Ceramic.

### How To Redeem Online (Military / Veteran / First Responder — via GOVX)
1. Confirm your eligibility on GOVX (govx.com/t/eligible).
2. Create or log in to your GOVX account; you'll receive a GOVX ID that verifies your status instantly across partner sites — no document upload needed.
3. Open the Oura page on GOVX and purchase at the member-discounted rate (Oura's announcement describes up to 25% off; confirm the live price in your GOVX account).

### How To Redeem In Store
- Not applicable. Both Oura discount programs are online-only; Oura Ring is sold direct online and through authorized resellers, and the verified discounts run through ouraring.com (ID.me) and govx.com (GOVX). Not stated in official source that any in-store military discount exists.

### How Verification Works
**ID.me path:** Eligibility is confirmed through Oura's third-party verifier, ID.me. You complete a quick verification of identity and professional/student status; once verified, the discount applies automatically at checkout. Help with verification steps, documents, or eligibility goes through the ID.me Help Center. Oura does not state how long verification lasts or whether re-verification is required — assume ID.me's standard re-verification rules apply.

**GOVX path:** You verify military/first-responder status once to obtain a GOVX ID, which then unlocks discounts across GOVX partner brands. GOVX states no document upload is required for most members. Oura does not publish the GOVX re-verification frequency.

### FAQ

#### Does Oura offer a military discount?
Yes. Active-duty military, veterans, and first responders can claim a dedicated Oura discount through GOVX after verifying status with a free GOVX ID. Oura's announcement describes savings of up to 25%.

#### How much is the Oura military discount?
Oura's own announcement says "up to 25% off" through GOVX (originally framed for the Horizon and Heritage styles). The exact current percentage and eligible models on GOVX are set by Oura/GOVX and shown after you log in to GOVX; we couldn't confirm a single fixed number from a primary public source.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. GOVX eligibility (as listed by Oura) includes honorably discharged veterans, military spouses, and dependents aged 18–24, plus active-duty members of all branches.

#### How do I verify my status?
Two paths: military/veteran/first responder via a free **GOVX ID** at govx.com; student/educator/healthcare/first responder via **ID.me** on Oura's Preferred Pricing page. Both verify your status before the discount applies.

#### Does Oura use ID.me, GOVX, or SheerID?
Oura uses **ID.me** for its on-site 10% Preferred Pricing (students, educators, healthcare, first responders) and **GOVX** for its military/veteran/first-responder program. Oura does not use SheerID for these programs, per its official pages.

#### Is there an Oura student discount?
Yes — students enrolled at accredited colleges or universities can save 10% on Oura Ring 4 and Oura Ring 4 Ceramic via ID.me on Oura's Preferred Pricing page.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. Both programs are online-only, redeemed at ouraring.com (ID.me) or govx.com (GOVX). Oura does not state any in-store military discount.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Discounts can't be stacked — only one per order. If a site-wide sale (like Black Friday) beats the discount, it overrides it; Oura auto-applies the highest eligible discount.

#### Does Oura offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, healthcare, or government discount?
Yes. First responders, teachers/educators, and healthcare professionals get 10% via ID.me. First responders and full-time government employees also qualify through GOVX.

#### Which Oura Ring models does the discount cover?
The ID.me 10% applies to Oura Ring 4 and Oura Ring 4 Ceramic. Oura's support page says partner discounts apply to Oura Ring 4 models unless otherwise specified; the GOVX announcement referenced the older Horizon and Heritage styles, so confirm covered models in your GOVX account.
