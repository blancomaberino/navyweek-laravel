## Headline finding

Chewy has **no military, veteran, or senior discount** — and it says so itself. Chewy's own FAQ states plainly, *"We do not offer any additional discounts for veterans or senior citizens,"* and **ID.me** confirms *"We are not aware of Chewy offering Military community discounts."* There is no standing ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute identity discount that beats what every shopper already gets. This is the classic **"no dedicated program, public-promo-driven" archetype**: the real savings are the offers open to everyone — **up to 40% off your first Autoship order (commonly capped ~$20), a $20 WELCOME eGift on your first $49+ order, free shipping over $49, and an extra 5% off recurring Autoship on select brands** — optionally layered with **Rakuten 2% cashback** on the initial Autoship order, or the paid **Chewy+** membership for heavy buyers. The single best play for a service member is therefore the public **first-Autoship discount + Rakuten 2%** — no military status required — plus the commissary/exchange pet aisle for tax-free staples. Aggregator and partner-portal pages that dangle a "Chewy military discount" are almost always re-labeling that same public first-Autoship offer behind a verification wall, with **no incremental value**.

#### Does Chewy offer a military discount?
No. Chewy's FAQ states it does not offer additional discounts for veterans or seniors, and ID.me confirms it is not aware of any Chewy military community discount.

#### How much is the Chewy military discount?
There isn't one. The "35–40% off" you'll see advertised as a "military discount" is Chewy's public first-Autoship offer (commonly capped ~$20) that every new customer gets.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
There is no Chewy military or veteran program, so no service-based group gets a special rate. Everyone gets the same public offers.

#### How do I verify my military status with Chewy?
You don't — Chewy has no identity-verified military discount to unlock. Any portal asking you to verify is gating a public deal.

#### Does Chewy use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Chewy has no confirmed standing identity discount through any of them. ID.me lists no Chewy military discount; a GovX affiliate URL exists but no verified in-marketplace Chewy rate; WeSalute/other pages re-list the public first-Autoship offer, not a military rate.

#### Can I use a discount in stores?
Chewy is online-only, so there's no in-store discount. For a tax-free physical option, use the base commissary/exchange pet aisle.

#### What is excluded?
There's no military discount to exclude anything from. On the public offers: the first-Autoship discount is one-time and commonly capped ~$20 on eligible items; Rakuten 2% excludes prescriptions, flea & tick, and gift cards.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The first-Autoship discount is one-time and doesn't co-apply with the 5% recurring rate. Whether `WELCOME` combines with the first-Autoship discount is unverified — confirm at checkout.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Chewy?
Set up a first Autoship for up to 40% off (usually ~$20 max), start it through Rakuten for 2% cashback, and add the `WELCOME` $20 eGift on a $49+ first order. For bulk food/litter, compare the tax-free commissary.

#### Does Chewy offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No dedicated Chewy program was found for any of these groups. The public Autoship and welcome offers apply to everyone.

#### Does Chewy run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No military-specific sale. Around military holidays a "Chewy military offer" sometimes surfaces via ID.me/GovX/WeSalute, but it's the same public Autoship deal behind a verification step.
