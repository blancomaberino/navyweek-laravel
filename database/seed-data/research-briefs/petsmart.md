# PetSmart Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

PetSmart does **not** offer a sitewide military or veteran discount on merchandise (pet food, supplies, toys, etc.) — online or in store. What PetSmart *does* offer are **two service-only discounts, both 10% and both verified in person at the location, not online**: (1) **Grooming Salon — 10% off on Tuesdays only** for eligible military/armed-forces members and seniors 65+; and (2) **PetsHotel — 10% off any overnight stay** for military members, first responders, and seniors 65+. Both are confirmed on PetSmart's own Pet Services pages. Eligibility for the grooming discount is unusually well-defined: Reserves, National Guard, active members of the five U.S. armed services (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard), plus retired members and spouses; PetSmart accepts an expired military ID or a DD214 as proof. Verification is **at the salon/hotel desk by showing ID** — there is **no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID flow, and no online redemption**. The single most important caveat: aggregator pages claiming "10% off PetSmart.com purchases" or "verify with ID.me at checkout" are **inaccurate** — no such offer exists on any PetSmart-controlled page, and ID.me itself states it is not aware of any PetSmart military discount.

> Accuracy note: Multiple coupon/aggregator sites (sempersave, worthepenny, mymilitarybenefits, militarybridge, military.com) describe a "10%–25% military discount" on PetSmart orders via petsmart.com, the app, or stores, "verified through ID.me." This is **not supported by any PetSmart primary source**. PetSmart's promotional-terms page and discounts/online-codes help page make no mention of a military merchandise discount or ID.me. ID.me's own Shop page for PetSmart explicitly says: "We're not aware that PetSmart offers Military discounts." The only verified military offers are the **Grooming (Tuesdays)** and **PetsHotel** service discounts, both in-person ID verification, both 10%. Verified against petsmart.com / services.petsmart.com / shop.id.me as of June 23, 2026.

### How To Redeem Online
PetSmart's military/service discounts are **not available online.** There is no military offer on petsmart.com or the PetSmart app, no promo code, and no ID.me checkout step. Online discounts are limited to general promotions and Treats Rewards (see "Best Ways To Save More").

### How To Redeem In Store
**Grooming Salon (10% off, Tuesdays only):**
1. Make sure you have a free **Treats Rewards** membership (join at petsmart.com/treats-rewards or in store).
2. Book a grooming appointment **for a Tuesday** (book at services.petsmart.com/grooming or call your salon).
3. At check-in/checkout, tell the salon associate you're claiming the military (or senior) discount.
4. Show proof of service — a military ID (expired is accepted) or a **DD214**. Seniors show ID verifying age 65+.
5. The 10% is applied to eligible grooming services and add-ons (not merchandise).

**PetsHotel (10% off any overnight stay):**
1. Book an overnight stay at PetsHotel (services.petsmart.com/petshotel).
2. At the PetsHotel desk, tell the Services associate you're claiming the military / first-responder / senior discount.
3. Show **government-issued ID** verifying military service (or first-responder/age-65+ validation).
4. The associate provides a discount coupon for 10% off the overnight stay (one discount per stay; excludes add-ons, Doggie Day Camp, and Bundle Packages).

### How Verification Works
Verification is **in person at the point of service**, not through any third-party platform. PetSmart does **not** use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for these discounts — ID.me's own site confirms it is not aware of a PetSmart military discount. For grooming, you show a military ID (expired accepted) or a DD214 to a salon associate; for PetsHotel, you show government-issued ID to a Services associate. A free **Treats Rewards** membership is required for the grooming discount. There is no annual re-verification because there is no account-linked status — you simply present ID each time you use the offer. If an associate is unfamiliar with the discount, ask them to check the current Pet Services monthly offers; terms and availability vary by store.

### FAQ

#### Does PetSmart offer a military discount?
Partly. PetSmart does **not** offer a military discount on merchandise (pet food, supplies, toys). It offers two **service** discounts: 10% off the Grooming Salon on **Tuesdays**, and 10% off any **PetsHotel** overnight stay. Both are redeemed in person with ID. (Source: services.petsmart.com monthly services offers / PetsHotel special offers.)

#### How much is the PetSmart military discount?
**10%** in both cases — 10% off grooming (Tuesdays only) and 10% off PetsHotel overnight stays. There is no percentage discount on merchandise. Aggregator claims of "up to 25%" are not supported by any PetSmart source.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
For **grooming**, PetSmart explicitly includes Reserves, National Guard, active members of all five armed services, **any retired member, and spouses** — and accepts an expired military ID or **DD214**. Separated veterans aren't named by the word "veteran," but the DD214 acceptance points to practical eligibility; confirm at the salon. Dependents/children are not stated. (Source: services.petsmart.com monthly services offers.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person. Show a military ID (expired is accepted for grooming) or a **DD214** to a salon associate, or a government-issued ID to a PetsHotel associate. There is no online verification and no third-party platform.

#### Does PetSmart use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** PetSmart verifies in person at the salon/hotel desk. ID.me's own Shop page states it is not aware of any PetSmart military discount, so any "verify with ID.me at PetSmart checkout" claim is inaccurate. (Source: shop.id.me/discounts/7364-petsmart/military.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes — that's the **only** place these discounts work. The grooming discount is at the in-store Grooming Salon (Tuesdays); the PetsHotel discount is at the in-store PetsHotel desk. Neither applies on petsmart.com or the app.

#### What is excluded?
Grooming: merchandise and all non-grooming services; only Tuesdays; Treats membership required. PetsHotel: all add-ons, Doggie Day Camp, PetsHotel Bundle Packages, and merchandise; one discount per stay. The PetsHotel first-responder tier excludes **federal** law enforcement.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
PetSmart's general promotional terms state offers "may not be combinable with other promotions, offers, or discounts." The service discounts are not stated to stack with other service promos; treat them as non-combinable and confirm with the associate. (Source: petsmart.com/help/promotional-terms.)

#### Does PetSmart offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
**First responders:** yes, but **only at PetsHotel** (state/local firefighters, EMT/EMS, local law enforcement — federal excluded). **Seniors 65+:** yes, for both grooming (Tuesdays) and PetsHotel. **Teachers, nurses, government employees, students:** not stated in any official PetSmart source.

#### Why do coupon sites say PetSmart gives 10% off everything with ID.me?
Those pages are inaccurate. No PetSmart-controlled page describes a merchandise discount or an ID.me program, and ID.me itself disclaims any PetSmart partnership. The only real offers are the in-store grooming and PetsHotel service discounts.
