# Garage (Garage Clothing) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $80 online apparel order (typical Garage cart), tax excluded.

## Headline finding

Garage (Garage Clothing) does **not** offer a military, veteran, first-responder, nurse, or teacher discount. ID.me's own brand directory states plainly, "We're not aware that Garage Clothing offers Military discounts," and repeats the same for first responders, nurses, and students — Garage is not an ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute partner. Because there is no service-member offer, the genuine best path for military shoppers is the same one open to everyone: join the free **Garage Collectif** loyalty program for the ~10% welcome code, buy from the sale rack, and route the purchase through a cash-back portal (Rakuten, 2% as of July 13, 2026). Garage is a young-women's fast-fashion apparel brand (Groupe Dynamite) whose savings live in loyalty + sale-stacking + cash back, not in an identity discount.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **One discount code per order.** Garage typically limits an order to a single promo/loyalty code — a welcome code generally cannot stack on top of a sitewide sale or another code. *(Reported behavior consistent with the promo-details "cannot be combined with other promotions" language; not confirmed on a standalone terms page — flag.)*
2. **Rakuten cash back stacks on everything.** It's a post-purchase portal rebate on the final paid amount, so it layers on top of sale prices and any code — **except gift cards or purchases made with gift cards.**
3. **Free shipping is threshold-based, not a code** — $75+ after discounts, before tax; it does not consume the one-code slot.
4. **No identity-discount stack exists** — there is nothing military/veteran/first-responder to combine.
