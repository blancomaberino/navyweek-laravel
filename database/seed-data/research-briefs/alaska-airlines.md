# Alaska Airlines Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Alaska Airlines does **not** offer a flat percentage-off "veteran discount" on fares. Its military program is benefit-based and centered on **active-duty service members** ("Current Service Members"). Verified active-duty benefits: a phone-only **special military fare** (no published percentage), **up to 5 free checked bags when traveling on orders** (2 free for personal travel), **15% off inflight food purchases**, **priority boarding**, a **discounted Alaska Lounge day pass ($35; complimentary on SATO government-sponsored travel)**, an **Alaska Air Cargo pet-shipping discount (PCS moves only)**, and **fee-free changes/cancellations when military orders disrupt travel**. **Veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve (not on active duty), and family members** generally do **not** qualify for the special military fare or the active-duty travel perks; their main fare savings path is the third-party **WeSalute+ (formerly Veterans Advantage) program, which gives 5% off Alaska/Hawaiian base fares** through a paid membership and a private booking portal. The single most important caveat: the active-duty special fare is **only bookable by phone**, eligibility is narrow (active duty / excused absence / discharged within 7 days), and it explicitly **excludes retired personnel and temporary-duty (TDY) travel**.

### Intro
Alaska Airlines runs a "Salute to Service" program built around **active-duty** travel rather than a single percentage-off code. Active-duty service members and their accompanying spouse and dependent children get a phone-only special military fare, free checked bags (up to 5 on orders), 15% off inflight food, priority boarding, a discounted lounge day pass, and flexible changes when orders disrupt travel.

Your main fare-savings path is **WeSalute+** (formerly Veterans Advantage), a paid third-party membership that unlocks **5% off Alaska and Hawaiian base fares** through a private booking portal.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty U.S. military (with valid military ID)** — eligible for all benefits, including the special military fare. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares, salute-to-service)
- **Accompanying spouse and dependent children of active duty** — eligible for the special fare (with dependent ID/documentation), free bags, and 15% inflight food. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares, salute-to-service)
- **Military reserves on active duty** — eligible for the special fare. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares)
- **Recently discharged personnel** — eligible for the special fare only if all travel is completed within 7 days of discharge (present separation papers). (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares)
- **Veterans (general)** — Not eligible for the active-duty special fare; eligible for 5% off via WeSalute+. (Source: alaskaair.com salute-to-service; wesalute.com)
- **Retirees** — Explicitly excluded from the special military fare; eligible for WeSalute+ 5%. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares)
- **National Guard / Reserve not on active duty** — Not stated as eligible for the active-duty fare; eligible via WeSalute+ if they enroll. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares; wesalute.com)
- **First responders, medical workers, teachers, government employees, students** — Not stated in official source (no dedicated Alaska discount for these groups).

### How To Redeem Online
1. There is **no online special military fare**. Alaska's active-duty military fare is available **by phone only** through Alaska Airlines or Hawaiian Airlines Reservations.
2. For the **WeSalute+ 5% fare discount**, enroll in or sign in to WeSalute+ at WeSalute.com.
3. Book your Alaska/Hawaiian flight through WeSalute's private booking portal; the 5% is automatically applied to eligible base fares (valid for up to 6 people booked together).

### How To Redeem In Store (Airport / Onboard)
1. **Free checked bags:** At the airport check-in counter, tell the Alaska agent you're an active-duty service member (or accompanying dependent) and present a valid military ID — first 2 bags free for personal travel, up to 5 free when traveling on orders.
2. **Priority boarding:** Listen at the gate; the gate agent invites Current Service Members to board early.
3. **15% inflight food discount:** Show your military ID to the flight attendant when purchasing food onboard.
4. **Lounge day pass:** Present a valid military ID at an Alaska Lounge — complimentary if on SATO government-sponsored travel (flight within 3 hours), otherwise $35 for active duty (vs. the standard $65 single-entry pass).

### How Verification Works
There is no online verification portal for Alaska's active-duty benefits — verification is **in person with a valid military ID** (airport counter, gate, onboard, lounge) or **by phone** for the special fare. Dependents 10+ must present a white Uniformed Services Dependent ID marked "active"; additional documentation may be required if a dependent's name differs from the service member's. For pet cargo discounts, a common access card and PCS orders within 60 days of report date are required at tender. The **WeSalute+ 5% fare** is gated by **WeSalute+ membership**, a paid third-party service (founded as Veterans Advantage) open to active duty, retirees, Guard, Reserve, veterans, and family; you redeem through WeSalute's booking portal, not alaskaair.com.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Special military fare **does not apply to retired personnel** or to **temporary-duty (TDY) travel** to/from temporary duty stations. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares)
- Special fare is **new tickets only**, **phone-booked**, with **instant ticketing required**; all standard fare rules and fees (including change and name-transfer fees) apply. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares)
- Discharged personnel must complete all travel **within 7 days** of discharge. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares)
- Special fare valid on flights operated by Alaska, Hawaiian, and on behalf of Alaska by Horizon and SkyWest. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares)
- **WeSalute 5% fare**: valid on base-fare levels published at alaskaair.com between all Alaska cities **except Prudhoe Bay, AK**; only on Alaska-operated and Horizon/SkyWest-on-behalf flights; **not valid on codeshares**; **does not apply to taxes/airport fees**; **cannot be combined with other promotions or award (Mileage Plan/Atmos) travel**; not retroactive; **not applicable to all fare classes — e.g., Saver fares and select First Class fares are excluded**. (Source: wesalute.com/get-alaska-airlines)
- Lounge day passes are non-refundable and valid only at the location of purchase (with stated exceptions). (Source: alaskaair.com day-pass)
- Note: WeSalute+ is a **paid membership** — factor the membership cost against the 5% fare savings.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Atmos Rewards** (Alaska's loyalty program, formerly Mileage Plan): earn and redeem miles on paid travel; donate miles via the Hero Points / Hero Miles program for injured service members. (Source: alaskaair.com salute-to-service)
- **Free bags on orders** are often the highest-value active-duty benefit (up to 5 bags free vs. standard checked-bag fees).
- **WeSalute+** also bundles 1,400+ non-airline military offers (e.g., United 5%, Avis up to 25%, Verizon) — relevant if the membership pays off across multiple brands. (Source: wesalute.com)
- **Honor Flight Network**: complimentary travel and reduced fares for veterans on planned Washington, D.C. honor tours (not a self-service discount). (Source: alaskaair.com salute-to-service)
- **Competitor context**: Delta, United, American, and Southwest also waive checked-bag fees for active-duty on orders; United and JetBlue likewise offer 5% via WeSalute. None publishes a broad flat veteran fare discount, so Alaska is competitive but not uniquely generous on fares.

### FAQ

#### Does Alaska Airlines offer a military discount?
Yes, but it's benefit-based, not a single percent-off code. Active-duty service members get a phone-only special military fare plus free bags, priority boarding, 15% off inflight food, a discounted lounge pass, and a pet cargo discount. Veterans and others can get 5% off base fares through the third-party WeSalute+ program. (Source: alaskaair.com salute-to-service; wesalute.com)

#### How much is the Alaska Airlines military discount?
Alaska does **not publish a percentage** for its active-duty special fare — it's quoted by phone. The clearest dollar figures are: up to **5 free checked bags** on orders, **15% off inflight food**, a **$35 lounge day pass** (vs. $65), and **5% off base fares via WeSalute+**. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares, salute-to-service, day-pass; wesalute.com)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Active-duty **spouses and dependent children** traveling with the service member qualify for the special fare and most perks. **Veterans and retirees** do **not** qualify for the active-duty special fare; their fare-savings route is WeSalute+ (5%). (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares; wesalute.com)

#### How do I verify my military status?
For active-duty benefits, present a valid military ID in person (airport counter, gate, onboard, lounge) or by phone for the special fare. For the WeSalute 5% fare, you verify by holding a WeSalute+ membership and booking through their portal. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares; wesalute.com)

#### Does Alaska Airlines use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Alaska uses **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage) for its 5%-off fare offer. Its active-duty perks are verified **in person with a military ID**, not through ID.me, GovX, or SheerID. (Source: alaskaair.com salute-to-service; wesalute.com)

#### Can I book the military fare online?
No. Alaska states the special military fare is **only available when purchasing your ticket by phone** through Alaska or Hawaiian Reservations. The WeSalute 5% fare is booked online through WeSalute's portal. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares; wesalute.com)

#### How many bags can active-duty members check for free?
Current Service Members and their accompanying spouse/dependent children get the **first 2 bags free** for personal travel and the **first 5 bags free when traveling on orders**. (Source: alaskaair.com salute-to-service)

#### What is excluded?
The special fare excludes **retirees** and **TDY travel**, requires phone booking and instant ticketing, and applies standard fees. The WeSalute 5% excludes **Saver fares, select First Class, codeshares, taxes/fees, award travel, and Prudhoe Bay (AK)**, and can't be combined with other promotions. (Source: alaskaair.com military-fares; wesalute.com)

#### Can I combine the discount with sales or promo codes?
The WeSalute 5% **can be applied to Alaska's sale prices** but **cannot be combined with other promotions or award travel**. The active-duty special fare follows all standard fare rules. (Source: wesalute.com; alaskaair.com military-fares)

#### Does Alaska Airlines offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Not stated in official source. Alaska's published program covers active-duty military and (via WeSalute) the broader military/veteran community; no dedicated first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student fare discount is documented. (Source: alaskaair.com salute-to-service)
