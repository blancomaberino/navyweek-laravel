# Great Wolf Lodge Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Great Wolf Lodge runs an active, evergreen military/hero discount called the **Howlin' Heroes Offer**: **up to 40% off the room rate** on overnight stays, verified through **ID.me**. Eligible groups are broad and explicitly stated by the brand: military (active, retired, and veteran), teachers, fire, EMS personnel, medical providers, nurses, 911 dispatchers, police, government employees, and correctional officers. Booking is **online only** — you select a U.S. lodge, click the ID.me button, verify your status, and the promo code **HEROES** is applied to the reservation. The single most important caveat: the discount **applies only to the room-rate portion** of the booking (not taxes, fees, or incidentals), the exact percentage **varies by date**, and the offer is **not valid during holiday/blackout periods or combined with any other offer**. Stay dates currently run "Today until 12/31/2028" per the brand's lodge pages (accessed June 23, 2026).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the Howlin' Heroes offer page at greatwolf.com/deals/heroes.
2. Scroll down and select your desired lodge (this loads that lodge's Howling Heroes page with offer code HEROES).
3. On the lodge page, find the ID.me button and verify your eligible status (sign in to or create an ID.me account).
4. After verification, book your stay; the HEROES discount is applied to the room rate.
5. Review the rate carefully — the percentage off varies by your dates and suite type.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Great Wolf Lodge's official source describes online booking through the lodge page + ID.me only; no in-store or at-the-register redemption is stated. At check-in, you may be asked to show proof of eligibility (e.g., a Military/Veteran ID), but the discount itself is applied online during booking. (Proof-at-check-in is widely reported by editorial sources; the brand's offer page does not state a documents-at-check-in requirement — confirm with the lodge.)

### How Verification Works
Great Wolf Lodge verifies eligibility through **ID.me** ("Verified With ID.me" on each lodge's Howling Heroes page). You verify on the lodge page by clicking the ID.me button, then sign in to or create an ID.me account and confirm your status in your eligible group. ID.me is a third-party identity service; its verification and any re-verification cadence are governed by ID.me, not Great Wolf Lodge. The brand's page does not state how often you must re-verify. If automatic verification fails, follow ID.me's support flow (help.id.me) or contact Great Wolf Lodge customer service (the per-lodge pages list a resort phone number, e.g., 800.913.9653).

### Exclusions And Fine Print
Summarized from Great Wolf Lodge's official Terms & Conditions (greatwolf.com/mason/deals/howling-heroes, accessed June 23, 2026):
- Valid only on the dates listed on the offer; must book and stay within those dates.
- May not be valid during holiday and blackout periods, and cannot be combined with any other discount or promotional offer.
- Discount may vary by date; length-of-stay requirements may apply.
- Promotion code (HEROES) must be mentioned at time of reservation; based on 2026 standard rates.
- Valid on standard suite styles; offer varies on themed and premium suites. Limited number of rooms per date.
- U.S. rate is per room, per night, based on two guests per room; a $20 additional-person fee applies per extra guest (the general /deals/heroes page states two guests; the ID.me restriction text states four guests per room — see Open Questions).
- One person 21+ must stay in each room (18+ at Wisconsin Dells).
- Not valid at Great Wolf Lodge Niagara, ON. (ID.me's restriction text also lists Great Wolf Lodge Mashantucket, CT as excluded.)
- Applies only to the room-rate portion of a reservation; taxes apply regardless, and incidental charges are not covered.
- Offer may be terminated at any time without notice; not redeemable for cash.

### FAQ

#### Does Great Wolf Lodge offer a military discount?
Yes. Great Wolf Lodge offers the Howlin' Heroes Offer, up to 40% off the room rate for military members (active, retired, and veteran) and other eligible groups, verified through ID.me. (Source: greatwolf.com/deals/heroes, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the Great Wolf Lodge military discount?
Up to 40% off the room-rate portion of an overnight stay. The exact percentage varies by date, lodge, and suite type, so "up to 40%" is a ceiling rather than a guaranteed rate. Taxes, fees, and incidentals are not discounted. (Source: greatwolf.com/mason/deals/howling-heroes, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify — the brand lists "military (active, retired and veteran)." Military spouses and dependents are not mentioned in Great Wolf Lodge's official eligibility list, so their eligibility is not confirmed. Verify your specific status through ID.me or contact the lodge. (Source: greatwolf.com/deals/heroes, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How do I verify my status?
On the offer page, select your lodge, click the ID.me button, and sign in to or create an ID.me account to confirm you belong to an eligible group. The discount is then applied to your booking. (Source: greatwolf.com/deals/heroes, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does Great Wolf Lodge use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Great Wolf Lodge uses ID.me, shown as "Verified With ID.me" on each lodge's Howling Heroes page and listed on the ID.me Shop store page. A GovX listing for Great Wolf Lodge also exists, but the brand's own offer pages route verification through ID.me. (Sources: greatwolf.com/mason/deals/howling-heroes; shop.id.me/stores/8599-great-wolf-lodge, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores or at the front desk?
The discount is applied online during booking through the lodge page and ID.me. Great Wolf Lodge's offer page does not describe an in-person/front-desk redemption path. You may be asked to show eligibility ID at check-in. (Source: greatwolf.com/deals/heroes, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### What is excluded?
The offer applies to the room rate only — taxes, fees, and incidentals are excluded. It may not be valid on holidays/blackout dates, can't be combined with other offers, varies by suite type (standard vs. themed/premium), and is not valid at Great Wolf Lodge Niagara, ON (ID.me terms also list Mashantucket, CT). (Source: greatwolf.com/mason/deals/howling-heroes, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The terms state the offer "may not be... combined with any other discount or promotional offers." If a lower public rate exists for your dates, book that instead — or ask a Pack Member to compare codes and apply the better rate. (Source: greatwolf.com/mason/deals/howling-heroes, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does Great Wolf Lodge offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
Yes — all are covered by the same Howlin' Heroes Offer (up to 40% off, ID.me-verified): fire, EMS, 911 dispatchers, police, correctional officers, teachers, nurses, medical providers, and government employees. A student discount is not confirmed by the brand. (Sources: greatwolf.com/deals/heroes; shop.id.me/stores/8599-great-wolf-lodge, accessed June 23, 2026.)

## Open Questions
- **Guests-per-room discrepancy:** The general /deals/heroes terms say "based on two guests per room" while the ID.me restriction text says "four guests per room, unless otherwise stated." Per-lodge terms (Mason) say two guests. Confirm the authoritative figure per lodge.
- **Excluded-lodges discrepancy:** Mason's per-lodge terms exclude only Niagara, ON. ID.me's restriction text excludes both Niagara, ON and Mashantucket, CT. Verify whether Mashantucket is excluded at the booking step.
- **Spouses / dependents / Gold Star / Reserve / National Guard:** Not addressed in the brand's eligibility list. ID.me's military category may cover some; confirm before asserting eligibility.
- **Re-verification cadence:** Neither the brand nor the offer page states how often ID.me re-verification is required.
- **Proof at check-in:** Widely reported by editorial/aggregator sources but not stated on the brand's offer page; confirm whether the lodge requires ID at check-in.
- **Canada (Niagara):** Terms note "Discount and other date requirements may vary at Great Wolf Lodge Niagara, ON" on the national page, but the Mason per-lodge terms say the offer is not valid there. Treat Niagara as excluded for U.S. military offer purposes.
