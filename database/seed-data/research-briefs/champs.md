# Champs Sports Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Champs Sports (a Foot Locker, Inc. banner) offers a **10% military discount** on most online and in-store purchases. Eligibility — per the Champs Sports help center — covers active-duty members, veterans, National Guard members, reservists, and registered dependents of active-duty or retiree service members. Status is verified by **SheerID** (not ID.me); online shoppers select "military discount" in the Payment field at checkout and complete a quick SheerID form, while in-store shoppers show a valid photo/military ID at the register. The single most important caveat: the offer is capped — **limited to four SheerID verifications on a rolling 30-day basis and a $100 maximum discount per verification** — cannot be combined with other offers, cannot apply to previous purchases, and some product exclusions apply. Note an eligibility wording discrepancy between Champs' help center (broader: includes veterans, Guard, reservists) and the older Champs-branded SheerID FAQ page (narrower: "Active and Retired... and their eligible dependents") — flagged below.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add items to your bag and proceed to checkout.
2. When filling out payment information, select "military discount" in the Payment field and complete the quick form.
3. Complete SheerID verification — you may need to upload official government-issued documentation showing your full name, branch of service, and current military status (.bmp, .gif, .jpg, .png, or .pdf). SheerID verification can take up to 20 minutes.
4. Check your inbox for approval.
5. If approved, choose "military discount" at checkout.
6. Pay — the 10% discount applies (subject to the $100-per-verification cap and exclusions).

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop in a participating Champs Sports retail location.
2. At checkout, show a store associate a valid photo/military ID.
3. The associate applies the 10% military discount (subject to exclusions).

### How Verification Works
Champs Sports verifies eligibility through SheerID, a third-party service. The Champs-branded SheerID FAQ states you'll "only need to be successfully verified once, then the military discount can be applied to future purchases." Documentation is optional but practically required — without acceptable proof, verification cannot complete. SheerID states it does not sell, rent, or store the uploaded documents/images. The overall offer is still capped at four SheerID verifications per rolling 30-day window. For verification problems, SheerID directs users to customerservice@sheerid.com (response within 24 business hours). Note: verification is online-only — there is no phone verification path.

### FAQ

#### Does Champs Sports offer a military discount?
Yes. Champs Sports offers 10% off most online and in-store purchases for eligible military shoppers, verified through SheerID. (Source: Champs Sports help center.)

#### How much is the Champs Sports military discount?
10% off most purchases, with a $100 maximum discount per SheerID verification and a limit of four verifications per rolling 30-day period. (Source: Champs Sports help center.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Per the Champs Sports help center, eligibility includes active-duty members, veterans, National Guard, reservists, and registered dependents of active-duty or retiree service members. Spouses are not named separately and may fall under "registered dependents." The older Champs-branded SheerID FAQ uses narrower language ("Active and Retired... and their eligible dependents") — see the official pages to confirm your specific status.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online, select "military discount" in the Payment field at checkout and complete the SheerID form, uploading government-issued documentation if prompted (verification can take up to 20 minutes). In store, show a valid photo/military ID. (Source: Champs Sports help center.)

#### Does Champs Sports use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Champs Sports uses **SheerID**. We found no evidence Champs uses ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for its military discount; third-party "ID.me Champs" listings do not reflect Champs' own verification flow. (Source: Champs Sports help center; Champs SheerID FAQ.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Show a valid photo/military ID to a store associate at checkout in a participating Champs Sports location. (Source: Champs Sports help center.)

#### What is excluded?
"Some product exclusions apply" (not itemized publicly). It also can't be combined with other offers or applied to previous purchases, and the $100-per-verification cap applies. (Source: Champs Sports help center.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military discount cannot be combined with other offers. (Source: Champs Sports help center.)

#### Does Champs Sports offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
We found no official Champs Sports first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount program on Champs' own channels (third-party aggregators list these, but they are not confirmed by Champs). A separate student discount — 20% off $99+ — is available via Student Beans, a third-party verification service. (Source: Student Beans; Champs official channels for absence of others.)

The help center is more current and authoritative, but the actual SheerID gate at checkout determines who passes. Treat the help-center list as primary; flag that veterans/Guard/reservists should confirm via the live SheerID form.
- **Product exclusions:** Champs does not publicly enumerate which products are excluded ("some product exclusions apply").
- **Spouses:** Not named separately; unclear whether spouses qualify outside the "registered dependents" category.
- **In-store ID standard:** Help center says "we recommend showing a... valid photo/military ID" — the accepted ID types in-store are not strictly defined.
- **Re-verification durability:** SheerID FAQ says "verified once... applied to future purchases," but the 4-verifications-per-30-days cap implies re-verification can recur; do not promise permanent one-time verification.
