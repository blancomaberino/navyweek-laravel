# American Eagle (AE & Aerie) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Brand: American Eagle Outfitters, Inc. (AE & Aerie) — ae.com
> Audience: U.S. military / veterans
> Researched & sources checked: June 23, 2026
> Bottom line: **No official, brand-published military or veteran discount.** A store-level **in-store 10% off with military ID** is documented only on third-party (mall landlord) listings, not on AE's own site. No online military discount exists; ID.me confirms no AE partnership.

## Executive Summary

American Eagle Outfitters (the parent of both **AE** and **Aerie**) does **not** publish a military or veteran discount on its own website, and its official "Promotion and Discount Information" help page lists no such offer (accessed June 23, 2026). ID.me's American Eagle page explicitly states it is "not aware that American Eagle offers Military discounts," meaning there is **no online military discount** and **no ID.me / SheerID checkout verification** for service members. The only military offer with any independent corroboration is a **10% off in-store discount when you present a valid military ID at the register** — but this appears to be a **store-level / location-managed promotion** (documented on a Tanger Outlets mall landlord listing, marked "See store for details"), **not a nationwide AE corporate policy**. Because AE itself never states the discount, the percentage, eligible groups, and participation should all be treated as **unverified at the corporate level and store-dependent**. The single most important caveat: **call your local AE/Aerie store before relying on this — it is not guaranteed and not honored online.**
