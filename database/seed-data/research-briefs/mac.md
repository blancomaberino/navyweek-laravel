# MAC Cosmetics — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $100 MAC.com order (e.g., Studio Fix Fluid foundation + one lipstick), U.S. market.

> **Brand identity (disambiguation):** This page is about **MAC Cosmetics** (M·A·C), the professional makeup brand at **maccosmetics.com**, owned by **The Estée Lauder Companies**. It is **not** Apple "Mac" computers. The keyword "mac military discount" is treated here as the MAC Cosmetics makeup discount.

## Headline finding

MAC Cosmetics runs a **real, official 15% military discount** for active-duty, reserve/guard, and **veterans** ("currently be — or at one time have been — working for the selected Nation's Armed Services"), verified through **SheerID** and redeemable **online only at maccosmetics.com**. It is part of MAC's broader "Appreciation Discount" program (Medical 25%; Students, Teachers, Military & First Responders 15%). The discount is usable **once every 30 days** with a fresh promo code and **does not stack** with any other offer, sale, or code. Brand archetype: **SheerID-gated appreciation-discount brand** (like Crocs, Levi's, YETI). The single most important caveat: because it can't stack, MAC's periodic **sitewide Friends & Family sales (typically ~25–30% off)** can beat the 15% military code outright — so the best path is situational, not "always use the military discount."

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 15% code stacks with nothing.** MAC's T&Cs: "may not be used in combination with any other offer or discount." So it cannot be combined with promo codes, sale prices, or seasonal sitewide events. (Source: maccosmetics.com/appreciation-discount.)
2. **You get a new code each cycle** — reusable once every 30 days, but each redemption needs a fresh SheerID-issued code, not the same code re-entered.
3. **Cashback portals are a separate layer, not a "stack" in MAC's terms** — a portal pays you back independent of the cart price, but portals commonly **exclude orders that used a discount/appreciation code**. Whether Rakuten/TopCashback tracks on a code order is **not guaranteed** — verify the portal's MAC terms at purchase time.
4. **Loyalty (MAC Lover) is free and layers on top** of whatever price you pay; enrolling never costs a stack.

MAC uses **SheerID**, a third-party verifier. You confirm eligibility once; SheerID may ask for document upload if it can't verify instantly. Approval issues a single-use promo code. This is **not a permanent account discount** — each redemption cycle (every 30 days) requires a fresh code. Verification questions go to SheerID customer service (sheerid.com), not MAC.

### FAQ

#### Does MAC Cosmetics offer a military discount?
Yes. MAC offers **15% off** to military members and veterans, verified through SheerID and redeemable online at maccosmetics.com.

#### How much is the MAC military discount?
**15% off** your online order. (Ignore coupon sites claiming "35%" — that figure is not from MAC.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Veterans qualify** — MAC's terms cover anyone who is "or at one time [has] been" in the Armed Services. **Spouses and dependents are not mentioned** in MAC's terms; don't assume they're covered.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **SheerID** on the MAC Appreciation Discount page. Enter your details to match your service documents; upload documentation if prompted. Approval issues a one-time promo code.

#### Does MAC use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**SheerID.** Not ID.me (despite some aggregator claims), not GovX.

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?
**Online only**, at maccosmetics.com (US). It is not accepted at MAC stores, department-store counters, Ulta, or Sephora.

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards, Viva Glam, Minis, Value/Custom Kits, Good Buys, select limited-edition and new products, select pro tools, and Makeup Services. It also doesn't apply to sales tax or shipping.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
**No.** The military code can't be combined with any other offer, sale price, or code. If a sitewide sale is bigger than 15%, use the sale instead.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from MAC?
Normally the **15% military code**. But during a sitewide sale of 25–30%, take the **sale** (it beats the code and they can't stack). Always enroll in free MAC Lover loyalty and check a cashback portal.

#### Does MAC offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — **medical/nurses get 25%**; **students, teachers, and first responders (Police/Fire/EMT) get 15%**, all via SheerID.
