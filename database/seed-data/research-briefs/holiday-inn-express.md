# Holiday Inn Express — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **1-night leisure stay** at a Holiday Inn Express whose **Best Flexible Rate is $130/night** (pre-tax; a representative select-service Holiday Inn Express nightly rate). All effective prices are pre-tax unless noted.

## Headline finding

Holiday Inn Express is a **budget/select-service brand of IHG (InterContinental Hotels Group)** and rides on IHG's two real, first-party booking rates you select on **ihg.com** — there is no Holiday-Inn-Express-only military program. The first is the **Military Appreciation (leisure) Rate**: genuine but **variable**, with IHG publishing only a floor of **"minimum discount of 5% off the Best Flexible Rate,"** the actual amount varying by hotel, date, and availability (properties often run higher). It's for **leisure travel only**, open to **US and Canadian active duty, veterans, retired military, and family members with valid ID**, requires a **military or veteran ID at check-in**, **earns IHG One Rewards points** (Holiday Inn Express earns ~10 pts/$1), and **cannot be combined with any other offer, discount, or promotion**. The second is the **Government Rate** for **official government/military travel** (federal/state/local employees, military on orders, Defense/Cost-Reimbursable Contractors, state-funded universities/healthcare), described as a **percentage off the Best Flex Rate that varies by hotel** and verified with an **official ID or work orders** at check-in. The honest travel-loyalty truth applies: **rates don't stack, but points and status do** — so the real long-game prize is a **free IHG One Rewards account** (book direct, earn on every stay), and on any given night you should **price-check the military rate against the Best Flexible, member, and Advance Purchase rates and book whichever is lowest** — the 5% military floor is frequently *not* the cheapest. **Archetype: I (travel/loyalty — status stacks, rates don't).**

## Stacking Rules (verified, IHG terms)
1. **Rates are mutually exclusive.** The military, government, and Advance Purchase rates each state they **"cannot be used in conjunction with any other offer, discount or promotion."** You choose one rate per booking — you cannot layer the military rate onto a sale rate or a promo code.
2. **Points and status DO layer on top.** Both the military and government rates **"qualify for IHG One Rewards points,"** and qualifying nights count toward status — so the loyalty benefit stacks on whichever paid rate you book. This is the travel-archetype rule: **status stacks, rates don't.**
3. **Book direct to earn.** IHG One Rewards points and member rates require booking through IHG (ihg.com / app) or IHG channels; third-party/OTA bookings generally forfeit points and elite benefits.
4. **Cancellation is not guaranteed flexible.** The military rate's deposit/cancellation follows **the individual hotel's policy** — it may be less flexible than the standard Best Flexible Rate. Advance Purchase is **final/non-refundable**.
5. **No coupon-code stack.** There is no IHG "military promo code"; the discount is a bookable rate, so scraped coupon codes do not apply to it.
6. **Blackout dates and participating-hotel limits** apply to every one of these rates.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible for the Military Appreciation (leisure) rate with valid military ID.
- **Veterans** — Eligible with a valid veteran ID.
- **Retired military** — Eligible with valid ID.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Covered under active-duty/military ID (verify your ID is accepted at check-in).
- **Military spouses / dependents / family** — Eligible with a **valid ID**; the reservation must be in the **eligible ID holder's name**, and that person must **check in** (a family member can't book the room for a non-eligible person).
- **Government employees & contractors** (federal/state/county/local, CRCs, state universities/healthcare) — Eligible for the **Government Rate** on **official travel** with official ID/orders.
- **Not eligible for the leisure military rate:** the general public.

### How To Redeem — Military Appreciation (leisure) Rate, online
1. Go to **ihg.com** (or the IHG One Rewards app) and enter your Holiday Inn Express hotel, dates, and guests.
2. On the rate/room selection screen, choose the **Government/Military** rate and select the **Military Appreciation (leisure)** rate.
3. **Compare it** to the Best Flexible, member, and Advance Purchase rates shown — book whichever is lowest for your dates.
4. Complete the booking (sign in to your free IHG One Rewards account first, so the stay earns points).
5. At check-in, present a **valid military or veteran ID** (or family-member ID). Without accepted ID the hotel can deny the rate.

### How To Redeem — Government Rate (official travel), online or phone
1. On **ihg.com**, select the **Government** rate at room selection (or book by phone).
2. Confirm the rate is **at or under your GSA per-diem** for the destination.
3. At check-in, present your **official government ID and/or work orders** (or use your government travel card).

### How Verification Works
There's **no online verification portal or code** for the Holiday Inn Express military rate — you simply **select the rate at booking** and **prove eligibility with your ID at check-in** (military/veteran/family ID for the leisure rate; official ID or work orders for the government rate). The hotel can **deny the discount at the desk** if your ID can't be verified. There is no re-verification cycle — it's checked per stay. IHG controls the program and can change or end it at any time.

### FAQ

#### Does Holiday Inn Express offer a military discount?
Yes. Holiday Inn Express (an IHG brand) participates in IHG's **Military Appreciation Rate** bookable on ihg.com — a **minimum of 5% off the Best Flexible Rate**, varying by hotel and date — for US and Canadian active-duty, veteran, and retired service members and their families, for **leisure travel**, with **military/veteran ID required at check-in**.

#### How much is the Holiday Inn Express military discount?
IHG publishes only a **floor of 5% off the Best Flexible Rate**; the actual discount **varies by hotel, date, and availability**, and some properties run higher. There is no single fixed percentage — the exact number appears at booking.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — **veterans, retired military, and family members with valid ID** all qualify for the leisure rate. A family member must book **in their own name** and **check in themselves**.

#### How do I verify my status?
There's **no online code or portal**. You **select the Military/Government rate at booking** and show a **valid military or veteran (or family) ID at check-in**. Government-rate travelers show **official ID or work orders**.

#### Does Holiday Inn Express use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For the bookable IHG military/government rates, **no** — verification is **ID shown at check-in**, not an online provider. (ID.me lists a Holiday Inn Express storefront, but no specific ID.me-verified Holiday Inn Express room rate was confirmed this pass — the real route is the IHG rate + check-in ID.)

#### Can I use the military rate for any trip, or only leisure?
The **Military Appreciation Rate is for leisure travel only**. For **official government or military travel**, use the separate **Government Rate**, which is priced at or under the federal per diem.

#### Can I combine the military rate with promo codes or IHG points?
**No** — the rate "cannot be combined with any other offer, discount or promotion," and hotel rates are **mutually exclusive** (pick one). However, the booking **still earns IHG One Rewards points**, and status benefits apply — so points and status **do** layer on top of the rate.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Holiday Inn Express?
Compare the **Military Appreciation Rate** against the **Best Flexible, member, and Advance Purchase** rates for your hotel and dates, and **book the lowest** — the 5% military floor is often beaten by the **10–15% Advance Purchase** rate. Then earn points by booking direct through IHG One Rewards.

#### Does Holiday Inn Express offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
IHG's **Government Rate** covers government employees, military on orders, contractors, and state-funded universities/healthcare (official travel). There's no separate public first-responder, teacher, or nurse leisure rate confirmed on IHG's own pages this pass.

#### Does Holiday Inn Express run a special Veterans Day or Memorial Day rate?
No **extra** military-holiday rate was documented this pass — the **year-round Military Appreciation Rate is the standing offer**. Watch for standard IHG point-bonus and seasonal (Advance Purchase, "Cyber"/flash) sales around those windows, which often beat the 5% floor.
