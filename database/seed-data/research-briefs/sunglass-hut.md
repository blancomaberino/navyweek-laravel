## Executive Summary

Sunglass Hut offers a **15% military discount** to verified members of the U.S. military community — **Active Duty, Retirees, Veterans, Military Spouses, and Military Family Members** (U.S. only). Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta AI glasses are eligible **online only** on sunglasshut.com US, not in stores.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add eligible sunglasses to your cart at sunglasshut.com (U.S. site).
2. At the cart page, choose to verify your eligibility with ID.me.
3. Sign in to or create your free ID.me account and complete military verification.
4. Once verified, the 15% discount is applied to eligible items before checkout.
5. Complete your purchase. (Note: do not also try to apply another promo code — the discount cannot be combined.)

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify your military status with ID.me using the in-store link Sunglass Hut provides on its special-offers page ("To use your ID.me discount in store click this link").
2. Present the resulting ID.me confirmation to the store associate.
3. The associate applies the 15% discount to eligible, non-excluded items at the register.

*Exact in-store presentation steps (digital code vs. confirmation screen) are not fully detailed in the official source — see Open Questions.*

### How Verification Works
Verification is through **ID.me**, a third-party identity-verification service. You need a free ID.me account. Online, verification happens at the cart page; in store, a dedicated ID.me link is used. The official source does not state how long a verification lasts or whether re-verification is required for repeat purchases — ID.me generally maintains your verified status in your wallet, but do not assume a one-time, permanent verification beyond what ID.me's own terms state. For ID.me wallet issues, Sunglass Hut directs users to ID.me Support: email help@id.me, live chat via the "Need help? Contact us!" tab on any ID.me page, or phone 866-775-4363.

### FAQ

#### Does Sunglass Hut offer a military discount?
Yes. Sunglass Hut offers 15% off to verified members of the U.S. military community, redeemable online and in store via ID.me.

#### How much is the Sunglass Hut military discount?
15% off eligible sunglasses, per Sunglass Hut's official special-offers page.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family members qualify?
Yes. The eligible military groups named by Sunglass Hut are Active Duty, Retirees, Veterans, Military Spouses, and Military Family Members. Reservists, National Guard, and dependents are not named individually; ID.me verification will determine eligibility.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me. Online, verify at the cart page; in store, use the ID.me link on Sunglass Hut's special-offers page. A free ID.me account is required.

#### Does Sunglass Hut use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Sunglass Hut's official site uses **ID.me** for its military and community discounts. (Third-party sites and listings may reference other providers, but the brand's own page specifies ID.me.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Sunglass Hut provides a dedicated ID.me link to verify and use the discount in store, in addition to the online cart-page flow. Note that Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta AI glasses are online-only for this discount.

#### What is excluded?
It can't be combined with other codes, markdowns, new launches, limited editions, or gift cards. Excluded brands include Chanel, Dior, Oliver Peoples, Alain Mikli, Maui Jim, Prada, Prada Linea Rossa, Miu Miu, Cartier, Brunello Cucinelli, Persol, and Moncler, plus selected limited editions, exclusive styles, and special collaborations.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military discount cannot be combined with other promotional codes, other markdowns, or sale promotions. Compare which single offer saves you more on the item you want.

#### Does Sunglass Hut offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes. Through ID.me, Sunglass Hut offers 15% to First Responders (Active Police, Fire, EMT), Teachers, Government employees (Federal/Local/State), and medical/nurse communities. Students get 15% via UNiDAYS online or ID.me in store. These are separate from the military offer.
