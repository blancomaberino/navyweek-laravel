# FOX One — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** **one year of FOX One** — $199.99 on the annual plan (vs. $19.99/mo = $239.88/yr on month-to-month). All figures pre-tax; streaming is taxed in some states — see the tax note in the Decision Table.

> **Which "Fox One" this is:** This brief covers **FOX One**, Fox Corporation's direct-to-consumer streaming app that **launched August 21, 2025** (live FOX News, FOX Business, FOX Weather, FOX Sports/FS1/FS2, FOX Deportes, B1G, FOX local stations and the FOX broadcast network), at **fox.com**. It is **not** Fox Racing (apparel/MTB) and **not** Fox Rent A Car. It is also **not** *Fox Nation* — a separate, older Fox subscription (opinion/documentary/entertainment) that *does* run a well-known military offer. Because "fox one military discount" search traffic largely collides with the long-running **Fox Nation** military free-year offer, this brief leads with FOX One (the dominant "Fox One" product) and treats Fox Nation as the adjacent/sibling path a searcher probably also wants.

## Headline finding

**FOX One has no military, veteran, first-responder, or student discount.** As of July 10, 2026, Fox publishes standing FOX One pricing of **$19.99/month or $199.99/year** with a short free trial and rotating public promos — but **no verified discount** for any service-affiliated group on the FOX One product itself, and **no ID.me / SheerID / GovX / VerifyPass military gate** at FOX One checkout. The real "Fox + military" discount belongs to the **sibling product, Fox Nation**, which gives **active military and veterans their first year free, then 50% off annual renewals**, verified through **SheerID** — a different app with different (news-opinion/documentary) content, though FOX One and Fox Nation now bundle. For a service member who specifically wants **FOX One's** live news/sports, the genuine best paths are non-military: **pay-TV authentication (free if your cable/satellite/vMVPD carries FOX)**, the **Verizon FOX One perk** (as low as free-for-3-months or a $10/mo credit), the **annual plan** ($199.99 ≈ $16.67/mo, ~$40/yr cheaper than monthly), and the current **"buy 1 month, get 1 free"** launch promo. Archetype: **no-discount subscription service (Archetype "no official discount")** whose best savings are **bundle/carrier-perk/annual-prepay**, with a **sibling-brand military offer (Fox Nation, SheerID free-year)** as the adjacent play.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **No FOX One military discount exists**, so there is nothing to stack a military benefit onto at FOX One checkout.
2. **The "buy 1 month, get 1 free" promo is "not combinable with other offers,"** one redemption per subscriber (fox.com). It cannot be layered on the annual plan or a Verizon perk.
3. **Verizon perk vs. paying Fox directly is either/or** — you get FOX One *through* Verizon (perk pricing/credit) **or** you subscribe on fox.com; you don't stack the carrier credit onto a fox.com annual plan.
4. **Pay-TV authentication is a substitute, not a stack** — if you authenticate free through a TV provider, you don't also pay Fox.
5. **Fox Nation's military and first-responder offers cannot be combined** with each other ("you may not combine them") and are Fox Nation-only — they do **not** apply to FOX One or to the FOX One + Fox Nation bundle price.
6. **No cashback / GovX / ID.me path** for FOX One was found this pass; do not assume portal cashback on a digital subscription.

### FAQ

#### Does FOX One offer a military discount?
**No.** FOX One — Fox's live news/sports streaming app launched in August 2025 — has no military, veteran, first-responder, or student discount as of July 10, 2026. There's no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX military verification at its checkout.

#### Isn't there a "free year of Fox for veterans"?
Yes, but that's **Fox Nation**, a **separate** Fox subscription (opinion/documentary/entertainment), not FOX One. Fox Nation gives active military and veterans their **first year free, then 50% off** annual renewals, verified through SheerID.

#### How much is FOX One?
**$19.99/month or $199.99/year.** A FOX One + Fox Nation bundle is $24.99/month or $239.88/year. There's a short free trial and rotating public promos.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents get a FOX One discount?
No — FOX One has no service-affiliated discount for anyone. Veterans (and, per Fox's list, Reserve/National Guard) can get the **Fox Nation** free year via SheerID; verify spouse/dependent eligibility at Fox Nation checkout.

#### Does FOX One use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or VerifyPass?
No, not for FOX One. **SheerID** is used only for the separate **Fox Nation** military/first-responder/student offers.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to get FOX One?
**Pay-TV authentication** (free if your TV package carries FOX) or the **Verizon FOX One perk** (3 months free, or a $10/mo credit). If neither applies, the **$199.99 annual plan** is the best direct price.

#### Can I combine FOX One promos or use a military code?
No. There is no FOX One military code, and the public "buy 1 month, get 1 free" promo is one-time and "not combinable with other offers."

#### Do first responders get a FOX One discount?
No FOX One discount. Active police, firefighters, and EMTs can get **Fox Nation's** free first year through a separate SheerID path (not combinable with the military offer).

#### Can I watch FOX One free if I have cable?
Often yes — "pay TV subscribers receive access for free." Sign in to FOX One with your TV provider's credentials if it carries FOX.

#### Does FOX One run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day military sale?
No FOX One-specific military-holiday promo is documented. Fox Nation's free year is evergreen (not a holiday spike). Watch for public, non-military FOX One promos seasonally.
