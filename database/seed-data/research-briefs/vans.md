# Vans Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**As of June 23, 2026, Vans does not confirm a military or veteran discount on any of its own (vans.com) pages.** The official Vans US "Current Offers" page lists a Vans Family welcome offer (15% off first order), a 15%-off SMS sign-up, free-shipping thresholds, a price-adjustment policy, and Final Sale — but **no military, veteran, or first-responder discount**, and **no SheerID or ID.me verification flow for military shoppers**. A dedicated Vans military-discount-program URL that previously existed now returns a **404**. Historically Vans did run a SheerID-verified ~10% military offer, but that cannot be verified on any Vans-owned source today. The single most important caveat: **do not state a live "10% Vans military discount" as fact** — the page must say the offer is not currently confirmed by Vans, point shoppers to official alternatives, and flag this for re-verification.
