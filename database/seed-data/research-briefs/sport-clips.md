# Sport Clips — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one standard Sport Clips haircut (the "Varsity" / MVP-tier cut, roughly $23–$35 depending on market — service business, not e-commerce).

## Headline finding

Sport Clips has **no single national, published military discount percentage.** What exists is real but two-track: (1) a **free haircut on Veterans Day (November 11)** for veterans and active-duty service members at *participating* locations, and (2) an **ongoing military discount that varies by franchise** — each store is independently owned, so the amount (community-reported figures cluster around 10%) and even whether it's offered at all are set locally. Redemption is **in-store only**; there is no online store, no promo-code checkout, no GovX/ID.me/SheerID flow. The genuine best path is simple: **get the free cut on Veterans Day, and the rest of the year ask your local store before you sit down.** Archetype: **local-franchise / discretionary-discount service brand** — the "official" answer lives at the individual store, not corporate.

### FAQ

#### Does Sport Clips offer a military discount?
Yes, but not one uniform national one. Sport Clips gives **free haircuts on Veterans Day (Nov 11)** to veterans and active-duty at participating stores, and many individual franchises offer an everyday military rate — but the amount and availability are set store-by-store.

#### How much is the Sport Clips military discount?
Corporate publishes no fixed percentage — it **varies by location.** Shoppers commonly report around **10%** at stores that offer one, but you must confirm at your local Sport Clips. The Veterans Day haircut is **free.**

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and active-duty qualify for the Veterans Day free haircut. **Spouses and dependents are not covered by any corporate rule** — only if a specific franchise chooses to extend its everyday discount to them.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person, by showing a **VFW membership card or Military ID** at the store. There's no online verification step.

#### Does Sport Clips use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. None of these are used. Verification is done in person at the store.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
**In stores only.** Sport Clips is an in-person haircut service — there is no online checkout to apply a discount to.

#### What is excluded?
Non-participating locations, and (typically) add-on services beyond the haircut on Veterans Day. The everyday rate generally can't be combined with other coupons.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale coupons?
Generally no — take whichever is lower, the military rate or the current public coupon. They don't stack.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to get a Sport Clips cut?
Go on **Veterans Day (Nov 11)** for a free haircut at a participating store. The rest of the year, call ahead to confirm your store's military rate and bring a VFW card or Military ID.

#### Does Sport Clips offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No chain-wide program for these groups was found. Any such offer would be a **local franchise** decision — ask your store.

#### Does Sport Clips run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
**Veterans Day (Nov 11)** yes — free haircuts at participating locations, its biggest veteran event of the year. No comparable chain-wide Memorial Day offer was found.
