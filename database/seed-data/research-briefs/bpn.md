## Headline finding

BPN has a **real, official, published 15% military discount** — sitewide, verified through **ID.me**, open to US and Canadian **active-duty members, veterans, and dependents** (a parallel 15% covers law enforcement, firefighters, and EMT/EMS). But BPN's own help center is explicit that **it never stacks**: one discount per order, no use on subscription items, and it's **disabled outright during major sales**. That matters this exact week — BPN's **Summer Sale 2026 (30% off all products, "our deepest discount ever") began July 13, 2026 at 10:00 AM CST**, and the current **25%-off-all-new-subscriptions** promo also beats 15%. Classic **archetype C (real discount, but the sale beats it)**, with an E-flavored twist (identity discount is the only lever on quiet weeks — no GovX storefront, no cashback portal, no pro deal exists).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **One discount per order, full stop.** BPN: "BPN does not allow discount stacking. Currently, our site only accepts one applied discount per order." Any "military + code" or "military + sale" combo is impossible by policy.
2. **Military 15% is blocked on subscription carts** — "the discount cannot be used if any item in your cart is a subscription item." Military pricing applies to one-time purchases only; the subscription discount is its own separate lever.
3. **Big sales disable extra discounts** — July 4 and Black Friday named by BPN; Black Friday 2023 explicitly turned off "military and first responder discounts till after the sale" because 15% was lower than the sale's 20%. Expect the same during Summer Sale 2026.
4. **No portal layer exists** — BPN has no cashback rate at any monitored portal (as of July 13, 2026), so there is no Rakuten/TopCashback stack to protect or void.
5. **Subscription promo pricing persists by its terms** — "If a subscription discount is offered at the time of purchase, the discount shall continue to be applied for the duration of your subscription until you cancel." Historically the standard recurring rate is 10%; treat 25%-forever as terms-supported but confirm at checkout/renewal.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online (bareperformancenutrition.com) — the only channel:**
1. First check for a live sitewide sale — if 20–30% off is running, buy at the public price; the military discount will be lower and is typically disabled anyway.
2. Otherwise, go to BPN's **Military Discount** page (or First Responder page) and click **Apply Discount**.
3. Sign in / verify with **ID.me**.
4. A **one-time discount code auto-applies at checkout** for 15% off.
5. Keep every cart item one-time (not Subscribe & Save) — a subscription item in the cart voids the military discount.
6. On future orders, sign in to ID.me at checkout again to re-apply.

**In store:** BPN has no in-store military program. Its products sell through retailers (Scheels, Vitamin Shoppe, The Feed, Amazon and others) — those stores' own discounts (e.g., Scheels' everyday 5% military discount) apply there, not BPN's 15%.

BPN partners with **ID.me** ("meets the highest federal standards for online identity proofing and authentication" — BPN help center). Verification is free; after the first verification the discount arrives as a one-time checkout code, and repeat customers re-sign-in to ID.me at checkout per order. If verification fails, ID.me's support handles document review. No GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute route exists for BPN.

### FAQ

#### Does BPN offer a military discount?
Yes. Bare Performance Nutrition gives verified US and Canadian active-duty members, veterans, and dependents 15% off sitewide, verified through ID.me. The brand is veteran-owned (founder Nick Bare served in the Army).

#### How much is the BPN military discount?
Exactly 15%. Aggregator claims of "25–35% off military" are BPN's public sale and subscription promos mislabeled — the military rate itself is 15%.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and dependents are explicitly eligible. Spouses, retirees, and Guard/Reserve aren't named in BPN's published list — ID.me verification will settle eligibility case-by-case.

#### How do I verify my status with BPN?
On BPN's military-discount page, click Apply Discount and sign in with ID.me. A one-time 15% code applies at checkout; re-sign-in on future orders.

#### Does BPN use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
ID.me only. There is no BPN GovX storefront, SheerID flow, or WeSalute offer.

#### Can I use it in stores?
No — BPN's 15% is online-only at bareperformancenutrition.com. At retailers that carry BPN (Scheels, Vitamin Shoppe, The Feed), the retailer's own policies apply.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, sales, or Subscribe & Save?
No. BPN allows one discount per order, blocks the military discount when a subscription item is in the cart, and disables it entirely during major sales.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy BPN?
During a sitewide sale (like the 30% Summer Sale that started July 13, 2026): the public sale price. If you reorder regularly: the 25% new-subscription promo. Otherwise: the ID.me 15%.

#### Does BPN offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
First responders (law enforcement, firefighters, EMT/EMS) get the same 15% via ID.me. No nurse, teacher, government, or student offer is listed as of July 13, 2026.

#### Does BPN run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No dedicated military-holiday promo was found. BPN's big public windows are July 4, a summer sale, and Black Friday — during which the military discount is typically disabled because the sale is deeper.
