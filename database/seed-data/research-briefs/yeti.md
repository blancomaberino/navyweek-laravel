# YETI Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

YETI offers a **20% military and veteran discount** on eligible items, verified for free through **ID.me**. The program covers **active-duty service members (all branches), veterans, retirees, reserve and National Guard members, and military spouses/dependents** where ID.me can confirm the affiliation, plus parallel ID.me communities for **first responders, medical providers, teachers, and government employees** — typically at the same 20% rate. Verification is a one-time ID.me setup reused across other ID.me partners; you select "Verify with ID.me" in your bag or at checkout on yeti.com, confirm eligibility, and the discount applies to qualifying items. The discount is also honored at participating YETI retail stores, where availability and the exact process vary by location.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (all branches: Navy, Marine Corps, Army, Air Force, Space Force, Coast Guard).
- **Veterans / military retirees** — Eligible with a verifiable service history.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible.
- **Military spouses & dependents** — Eligible where ID.me can confirm the affiliation (eligibility set by ID.me rules — flag).
- **First responders** — Police, fire, EMT/paramedic. Same ID.me program, typically 20%.
- **Medical providers** — Nurses, doctors, and staff. Typically 20%.
- **Teachers** — K-12 and college faculty. Typically 20%.
- **Government employees** — Typically 20%.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Shop yeti.com and add eligible items (coolers, drinkware, bags, or other qualifying products) to your bag.
2. In your bag or at checkout, choose the **"Verify with ID.me"** military or community verification option.
3. Sign in to — or create — a free **ID.me** account and confirm your eligibility (a one-time setup reused across ID.me partners).
4. Once verified, the discount is applied to qualifying items in your order.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit a participating YETI store and bring a valid government or military ID.
2. Where supported, complete **ID.me verification** (often on your phone) at the register.
3. Eligible in-store purchases receive the verified discount. Availability and process vary by location.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Limited-edition colors and collaboration releases are usually excluded.
- Customized or personalized products (such as Custom Shop engraving) typically do not qualify.
- Gift cards, sale or clearance items, and some new releases may be excluded.
- An annual discount cap may apply (YETI has used a roughly $10,000-per-year limit).
- The offer is generally valid on U.S. orders only.
- Usually cannot be combined with other promo codes or offers.

### FAQ

#### Does YETI offer a military discount?
Yes. YETI offers a military and veteran discount — typically **20% off** eligible items — verified for free through ID.me. It covers active-duty members, veterans, retirees, and reserve and National Guard personnel, with parallel discounts for first responders, medical workers, teachers, and government employees. Confirm the current rate on YETI's official ID.me page.

#### How do I verify my military status for the YETI discount?
At checkout on yeti.com, choose the **"Verify with ID.me"** option and sign in to (or create) a free ID.me account. ID.me confirms your eligibility — usually within moments — and the discount applies to qualifying items. Verification is a one-time setup that is reused across other ID.me partners.

#### Do veterans get the YETI discount, or only active duty?
Veterans qualify. YETI's program covers veterans and military retirees in addition to active-duty, reserve, and National Guard members, as long as ID.me can verify your service history.

#### Does YETI use GovX or ID.me for its discount?
YETI's current military and community discount is verified through **ID.me**. If you previously used a different provider, you will complete the discount through ID.me at checkout now. Always follow the verification option shown on yeti.com.

#### What is excluded from the YETI military discount?
Limited-edition colors, collaborations, customized or personalized items, gift cards, and some new releases are typically excluded, and an annual discount cap plus a U.S.-only restriction may apply. Check YETI's terms for the current exclusion list.

#### Does YETI offer a first responder discount?
Yes. YETI gives first responders — including police officers, firefighters, EMTs, and paramedics — the same discount as the military community, typically **20% off** eligible items, verified for free through ID.me. Add gear to your bag on yeti.com, choose "Verify with ID.me," and confirm your first-responder status once to unlock the savings.

#### Can nurses, teachers, and government employees get the YETI discount too?
Yes. Through the same ID.me program, YETI extends its discount to medical providers (including nurses and doctors), teachers, and government employees, alongside the military, veteran, and first-responder communities.

#### Is the YETI military discount available in stores?
In addition to yeti.com, participating YETI retail stores can honor the verified discount. Bring a valid ID and be ready to complete ID.me verification. Availability and the exact process vary by location.
