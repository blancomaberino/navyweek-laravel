# Amazon Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Amazon does not offer a military or veteran discount** — not on Prime membership and not on general purchases. There is no standing active-duty, veteran, retiree, Guard/Reserve, spouse, or dependent discount, and Amazon's pages do not use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for any military offer. The only discounted Prime tier, **Prime Access at $6.99/month (half the standard $14.99/month)**, is gated to **government-assistance recipients (SNAP EBT, Medicaid, WIC, SSI, etc.) or income-verified customers at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Guideline** — military status is not a qualifying path. The single most important caveat: any "Amazon military discount" advertised on coupon and military-info sites refers either to **Amazon's keyword search page for products literally tagged "military discount" by third-party sellers** (not an Amazon program), or to a **one-time November 2019 Veterans Day Prime promotion that has not returned**. A service member's real options are Prime Access (only if they independently qualify on income/assistance grounds), Prime for Young Adults, Amazon Warehouse, and Veterans-Day-period product deals — none of which is a military discount.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator and military-info pages frame Amazon as having a "military discount" or recurring "Veterans Day Prime discount." That is **not confirmed by Amazon**. The 2019 $40-off Prime promo was a one-time event; no annual military Prime discount exists as of June 23, 2026. Treat all such claims as unverified/historical.

### How To Redeem Online
There is **no online military discount to redeem at Amazon.** If you separately qualify for Prime Access on income or assistance grounds:
1. Go to **amazon.com/getprimeaccess**.
2. Verify your income, or upload a document confirming participation in an eligible government assistance program (SNAP EBT, Medicaid, WIC, SSI, etc.).
3. Enter payment and billing info; new members get a 30-day free trial before the $6.99/month charge begins.
4. To re-verify later, you'll get an Amazon email reminder every 12 months.

### How To Redeem In Store

### FAQ

#### Does Amazon offer a military discount?
No. As of June 23, 2026, Amazon does not offer a military or veteran discount on Amazon Prime or on purchases, and it doesn't run a military offer through ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute.

#### Is there an Amazon Prime military discount?
No. There is no military price for Amazon Prime. The only discounted Prime tier, Prime Access ($6.99/month), is for government-assistance recipients or income-verified customers — not military members.

#### What about the "$40 off Prime for Veterans Day" I've seen?
That was a one-time promotion in November 2019. It has not returned as an annual or current offer, so don't count on it.

#### How much is Amazon Prime, and is there a cheaper version?
Standard Prime is $14.99/month. The discounted Prime Access tier is $6.99/month, but it requires government-assistance enrollment or income verification (household income at or below 200% of the federal poverty line) — not military status.

#### Can a veteran or active-duty member get Prime Access?
Only if they separately qualify on income or assistance grounds (e.g., they receive SNAP EBT, Medicaid, WIC, SSI). Being a veteran or service member is not, by itself, a qualifying path.

#### Does Amazon use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for a military discount?
No. Amazon has no military offer with any of these verification services.

#### What is the "Amazon Military Discount" page on Amazon.com?
It's a keyword search results page for products that third-party sellers have tagged with "military discount." It is not an Amazon discount program, and prices are set by sellers.

#### Does Amazon offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or government employee discount?
No. Amazon does not offer discounts based on those professions. (Government-assistance *recipients* may qualify for Prime Access, but that's about income/assistance status, not employment.)

#### What are veterans' best ways to save on Amazon?
Prime Access if you income/assistance-qualify, Prime Student or Prime for Young Adults if eligible, Amazon Warehouse for open-box deals, and watching product deals around Veterans Day and Prime Day. None of these is a military discount.
