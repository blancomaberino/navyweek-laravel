# Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two single-day Water & Theme Park admissions (advertised $45 at gate, each) for a service member and one guest = **$90 pre-tax** for a one-day visit.

## Headline finding

**Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park (Wisconsin Dells, WI) does not publish or advertise any military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** None of its official pages — Specials, Park Tickets, FAQs — mention one, and the only military reference on the entire site is that a **military ID is accepted as valid identification** for renting mobility scooters/strollers/wheelchairs (accessibility page), not as a discount. The single best savings path is the park's own **bundled resort package**, where an overnight stay at a Mt. Olympus resort includes **free water & theme park admission** for the length of the stay (rooms advertised from **$59/night, park tickets & taxes included**), which beats paying gate admission per person. Archetype: **destination attraction / resort-bundle brand** — the discount lives in the room package, not a military program. A widely repeated claim that a promo code "**WELCOME**" gives military members **35% off** is an aggregator fabrication (WorthEPenny) and is **not verified on any official Mt. Olympus page** — do not rely on it.
