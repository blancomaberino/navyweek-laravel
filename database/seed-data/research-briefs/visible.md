# Visible — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one line, 12 months of the base **Visible** plan at $25/mo standard = **$300/year** (Visible is prepaid, no device financing in the baseline; taxes/fees are included in Visible's advertised price).

## Headline finding

Visible (Verizon's app-based prepaid brand) **does** offer a real, official military discount: **$5/month off any Visible plan**, for as long as you maintain service, verified through **ID.me** at [visible.com/v/idme](https://www.visible.com/v/idme). That drops the $25/mo base plan to **$20/mo ($240/year)**. The same $5/mo offer extends to nurses, teachers, first responders, and hospital employees through ID.me. Archetype: **subscription/telecom with an evergreen ID.me group discount** — the discount is genuine and durable, but it is a flat dollar amount that does **not** stack with Visible's new-member promo codes, so the real question is *military discount vs. a limited-time port-in promo*, not *which coupon to stack*. For a switcher, the **SWITCH26** port-in code ($6/mo off base for 26 months = $19/mo) beats the military discount for the first ~2 years; after that the evergreen military $20/mo wins. **Debunk:** the ID.me *Shop* cashback pages (shop.id.me) state "We're not aware that Visible Wireless offers Military discounts" — that is the separate ID.me Shop cashback *marketplace*, not the ID.me *group-verification* offer Visible actually runs; the $5/mo discount is real and lives at visible.com/v/idme.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Visible service promos do not stack with each other.** Visible's own terms on the referral offer state a promo "may not be combined with other service promotions or Bring a Friend credits." The $5/mo ID.me discount is applied through Visible's dedicated ID.me landing (visible.com/v/idme) as a service promotion, so it is **very unlikely to combine** with FRESHSTART / SWITCH26 / 5OFF5 / SUMMER. **[Flag: Visible does not publish an explicit "ID.me + port-in code" combinability statement; treat as non-stacking until confirmed at checkout.]**
2. **ID.me is verification, not a coupon portal here.** Unlike ID.me Shop (cashback), the Visible offer uses ID.me *group verification* — you verify once, the $5/mo attaches to the account, and there is no separate cashback layer to add on top.
3. **One discount per line.** The math above assumes a single service discount per line; you choose military **or** a port-in promo, not both.
4. **Annual plans** are a separate pricing SKU; whether the $5/mo ID.me discount applies to an annual prepay is **not stated on the offer page** — verify before prepaying a year. **[Flag: unverified.]**

### How Verification Works
Visible uses **ID.me group verification** (not the ID.me Shop cashback marketplace). You verify your eligibility once through ID.me; the $5/month credit then applies to your account for as long as you keep service. Re-verification may be requested periodically by ID.me per its standard policy. Support runs through Visible's in-app chat and the Visible community. Do not expect the discount to be more permanent than "while you maintain active service" — it is tied to keeping the line active.

### FAQ

#### Does Visible offer a military discount?
Yes. Visible offers **$5/month off any plan** to verified military members, veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents through ID.me at visible.com/v/idme. It is evergreen while you keep service.

#### How much is the Visible military discount?
A flat **$5 per month**, on every plan tier. On the $25/month base plan that's $20/month; on Visible+ ($35) it's $30; on Visible+ Pro ($45) it's $40.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. ID.me's military community for this offer includes active duty, National Guard, Reserves, honorably discharged veterans, retirees, military spouses, surviving spouses, and dependents.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to visible.com/v/idme, click **Verify with ID.me**, create or sign in to an ID.me account, and upload proof (military ID, DD-214, etc.). Verification is available 24/7.

#### Does Visible use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**ID.me** — specifically ID.me *group verification*. Note that the ID.me *Shop* cashback listing for Visible is inactive and incorrectly says no military discount exists; that's a different product. GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, and VerifyPass are not used.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Online / in the Visible app only. Visible has no retail stores.

#### What is excluded?
It's a flat $5/mo (not a percentage), it doesn't combine with promo codes or other service promotions, it's one discount per line, and its applicability to annual prepaid plans is unconfirmed.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Visible's service promotions don't stack, so you can't layer the military $5/mo on top of FRESHSTART, SWITCH26, 5OFF5, or seasonal codes. Pick the single best path for your situation.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Visible?
If you're keeping Visible long-term or already a customer, the **ID.me military $5/mo** ($20/mo base, forever) is best. If you're **porting a number in** and staying under ~2 years, **SWITCH26** ($19/mo for 26 months) is cheaper. You can't do both.

#### Does Visible offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — nurses, teachers, first responders, and hospital employees get the same **$5/month** via their own ID.me verification. (A separate general student discount is not published.)

#### Does Visible run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Visible's military discount is **evergreen**, so there's no military-specific holiday sale to wait for. Its seasonal deals (e.g. annual-plan promos around July 4 / Black Friday) are public and open to everyone — but they don't stack with the military discount.
