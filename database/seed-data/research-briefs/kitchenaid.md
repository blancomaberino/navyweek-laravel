# KitchenAid — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **KitchenAid major appliance — a $1,299 French-door refrigerator** (representative major-appliance order; math scales with cart size). A separate note covers the famous **stand mixer**, which is a *countertop* appliance and may fall outside the discount.

## Headline finding

KitchenAid runs a **genuine, official military & veteran discount** on kitchenaid.com, verified through **SheerID**: you "create your KitchenAid account and sign in, then verify your account through SheerID to **shop sitewide on all major appliances**." Eligible groups are **active-duty military, their families, and veterans**. The catch that matters most: KitchenAid **does not publish the discount percentage on the page** — the savings appear only after you sign in and verify (the field literally renders as a dynamic "Order Discount"). Editorial and cross-brand reporting put the Whirlpool-family appliance discount at **~15%**, but that number is **not confirmed on KitchenAid's own page**, so we flag it rather than assert it. The discount is scoped to **major appliances** (refrigerators, ranges, cooktops, wall ovens, microwaves, dishwashers, hoods, beverage & wine centers, ice makers, compactors — excludes refurbished), and combines only with KitchenAid's **Delivery & Haul-Away** offer. Because KitchenAid (a **Whirlpool Corp.** brand) runs frequent, deep **major-appliance sales**, the public sale often rivals or beats the identity discount — and the two typically don't combine. A parallel **WeSalute "Whirlpool Inside Pass"** route (covers KitchenAid, Maytag, Gladiator) exists for WeSalute members. Archetype: **E (SheerID identity discount)** with a strong **C (deep sales frequently beat it)** overlay.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Combines only with Delivery & Haul-Away** — KitchenAid's own terms: "Offer can only be combined with the Delivery and Haul Away offer." Everything else is off the table.
2. **Does not stack with public sale pricing** — the military discount is a separate account-gated price; you take one or the other. On deep sales the public price frequently wins.
3. **Free delivery ($399+) applies regardless** and is not a stack conflict — it's a shipping benefit, not a discount.
4. **Refurbished excluded** — the discount is for new orders on kitchenaid.com; refurbished/open-box models don't qualify.
5. **WeSalute Inside Pass is its own channel** — a separate offer for paying WeSalute members, not something you add on top of the SheerID discount.

### FAQ

#### Does KitchenAid offer a military discount?
Yes. KitchenAid offers an official military and veteran discount on major appliances at kitchenaid.com, verified through **SheerID** after you create an account and sign in.

#### How much is the KitchenAid military discount?
KitchenAid **doesn't publish the percentage** — it appears only after you sign in and verify. Cross-brand reporting suggests around **15%** on major appliances, but that isn't confirmed on KitchenAid's own page, so verify your actual "Order Discount" at checkout.

#### What does the discount apply to?
**Major appliances** — refrigerators, ranges, cooktops, wall ovens, microwaves, dishwashers, hoods, beverage & wine centers, ice makers, and compactors. Refurbished models are excluded, and countertop appliances like stand mixers aren't confirmed as included.

#### Do veterans and military families qualify?
Yes — KitchenAid names **active-duty military, their families, and veterans**. First responders qualify through a separate toggle.

#### Does KitchenAid use SheerID, ID.me, or GovX?
**SheerID**, through your KitchenAid account. There's also a separate **WeSalute "Whirlpool Inside Pass"** for WeSalute members covering KitchenAid, Maytag, and Gladiator.

#### Can I combine the military discount with a sale or promo code?
Generally no — it combines **only** with the Delivery & Haul-Away offer, not with public sale pricing. When a deep appliance sale is live, compare and take the lower price.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to buy KitchenAid?
Verify via SheerID and price it against the current public sale (and retailer prices at Lowe's/Best Buy/Abt). On a big appliance event the public sale often wins; otherwise the verified discount plus free delivery is your floor.

#### Does KitchenAid offer a first responder, teacher, or nurse discount?
First responders: yes, via SheerID. Teachers/nurses: no dedicated KitchenAid appliance program confirmed (Whirlpool markets some broader "eligible groups" — verify).
