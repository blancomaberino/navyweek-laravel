# Nike Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Nike offers a **10% discount on most items — online and in stores — to active, reservist, veteran, and retired US military personnel, and to the spouses and dependents of active personnel.** This is confirmed verbatim in the meta description of Nike's official Military Discount help page. Eligibility is verified through **SheerID** (Nike's third-party verification provider); you verify once, receive a single-use promo code, and apply it at checkout while signed in to your Nike Member account. The single most important caveat: the online discount is a **single-use promo code with a short life and a re-request waiting period** (widely reported as a new code every 7 days, expiring ~2 weeks after issue), and it **excludes a meaningful slice of Nike's catalog** — SNKRS launches, Nike By You custom orders, select launch styles, Apple products, and gift cards are commonly listed as ineligible. The percentage, eligible groups, channels, and SheerID provider are all confirmed against Nike's own pages; the granular code/exclusion mechanics come from secondary guides and are flagged as Medium confidence pending direct confirmation of Nike's rendered terms.

> Accuracy note: Nike's military discount help page renders its body text via JavaScript, so the full on-page terms (code reuse window, itemized exclusion list, in-store ID requirements) could not be read directly through fetch. The **10% figure, eligible groups, online + in-store channels, and SheerID provider are confirmed** from Nike's own page metadata and the official Nike promo-code page. Code-mechanics and exclusion specifics below are corroborated across multiple military-focused guides but should be re-confirmed on Nike's live page before any are stated as absolute on the published page.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Nike's **Military Discount** help page (nike.com/help/a/military-discount) and choose to verify your eligibility.
2. You're taken to **SheerID** — select your military status (active, reservist, veteran/retiree, or registered dependent) and branch, then enter your name, date of birth, and email.
3. Once verified, SheerID issues a **single-use promo code** (also sent to your Nike Member email).
4. **Sign in to your Nike Member account**, add eligible items to your cart, and enter the code at checkout.
5. The 10% comes off the eligible items in your order. *(Reported: you can request a new code roughly every 7 days, and each code expires about two weeks after issue — confirm current limits on Nike's page.)*

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop at a participating Nike (and reportedly Converse or Hurley) US store.
2. At the register, tell the associate you're shopping with the military discount.
3. Present a **valid military ID** when asked.
4. The associate applies the 10% to eligible items. *(In-store mechanics are reported by secondary guides; confirm acceptable ID and participating banners with the specific store.)*

### How Verification Works
Nike uses **SheerID** to confirm military eligibility. You complete a short SheerID form (status, branch, name, date of birth, email); SheerID checks it against authoritative records and, when it can't auto-verify, may ask you to upload documentation. After verification you receive a promo code tied to your Nike Member account. This is a **per-code** model rather than a permanent account flag: online, you generally request a fresh code for each purchase window (reported as every ~7 days, with codes expiring ~2 weeks after issue), so don't describe it as a one-and-done lifetime discount. For verification problems, SheerID provides a shopper help center (support.sheerid.com); Nike directs discount questions through its standard Help/Contact Us channels.

### FAQ

#### Does Nike offer a military discount?
Yes. Nike offers 10% off most items, online and in stores, to active, reservist, veteran, and retired US military personnel, plus spouses and dependents of active members — verified through SheerID.

#### How much is the Nike military discount?
10% off most items. The same 10% rate also applies to Nike's separate student, teacher, first responder, medical professional, and birthday offers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify. Nike's official page extends the discount to spouses and dependents **of active-duty personnel**; it does not state that spouses/dependents of veterans or retirees qualify.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through SheerID. Start on Nike's Military Discount help page, verify your status and branch with SheerID, and you'll receive a single-use promo code to use while signed in to your Nike Member account.

#### Does Nike use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**SheerID.** Nike's verification flow runs at offers.sheerid.com/nike/military — not ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Nike's page confirms the discount applies online and in stores; in store you present a valid military ID at the register (reported to include Nike, Converse, and Hurley US stores).

#### What is excluded?
The discount covers "most items." Secondary guides consistently list SNKRS launches, Nike By You custom orders, select launch styles, Apple products, and gift cards as excluded — confirm the current list on Nike's page before assuming an item qualifies.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Generally no. The military code is reported as not combinable with other promo codes, with free-shipping codes the usual exception. You can still buy already-reduced sale items, but you typically can't add the 10% on top of another code.

#### Does Nike offer a first responder, teacher, medical, or student discount?
Yes — Nike runs separate 10% discounts for students, teachers, first responders, and medical professionals, each verified through SheerID on its own help page, plus a 10% birthday-month offer for Nike Members.
