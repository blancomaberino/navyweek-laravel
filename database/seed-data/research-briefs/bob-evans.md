# Bob Evans — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one dine-in breakfast/lunch entrée (~$14 before tax) for one service member.

## Headline finding

Bob Evans' one **reliable, brand-published** military offer is its annual **Veterans Day free meal** (November 11) for veterans and active-duty/Guard/Reserve members — a choice of a free entrée from a special menu, dine-in only, proof of service required, family members not eligible. Bob Evans does **not** publish a standing year-round military discount in any terms page; a 2020-era **official Bob Evans Facebook post announced "a 10% Military Discount at all locations for active & retired military,"** and many locations still honor ~10% off, but this is best treated as a **discretionary, location-by-location courtesy (YMMV)** rather than a documented company policy. Archetype: **restaurant chain / holiday-freebie brand** — the value is the once-a-year free meal plus an at-the-register discretionary discount; there is no online verification portal, no GovX/exchange path. Best play: eat free on Veterans Day; the rest of the year, politely ask your server for the military discount and show ID.

### Who Qualifies
- **Veterans** — eligible (Veterans Day free meal; everyday discount where honored).
- **Active-duty, National Guard, Reserve** — eligible.
- **Military retirees** — eligible (proof: retired Uniformed Services ID).
- **Military family members / dependents / spouses** — **not eligible for the Veterans Day free meal**; no separate published family discount.

### How To Redeem In Store
**Veterans Day (Nov 11):**
1. Dine in at a participating Bob Evans.
2. Tell your server you're claiming the Veterans Day offer.
3. Show proof of service (military ID, LES, VA card, DD-214, veteran-org card, or wear your uniform).
4. Choose one free meal from the special menu (beverage not included).

**Any other day (everyday ~10%):**
1. Ask your server whether the location offers a military discount.
2. Show your military ID.
3. The ~10% is applied at the manager's discretion — it may vary or not be offered at your location.

### How Verification Works
There is **no online verification** (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX). Verification is entirely in-restaurant: you show a valid military/veteran ID or accepted proof of service to your server. This means there is no re-verification cycle and no account to create.

- Veterans Day free meal is **dine-in only**, **beverage not included**, and **not valid with other offers, discounts, or coupons**.
- Offer valid at **participating locations** — confirm your local restaurant.
- Everyday discount is **not a published policy** — availability and amount vary by location; do not assume it applies.

### FAQ

#### Does Bob Evans offer a military discount?
Yes, but with a caveat. Bob Evans reliably offers a **free Veterans Day meal** on November 11 for veterans and active-duty/Guard/Reserve members. It does not publish a formal year-round discount, though an official Bob Evans Facebook post advertised a **10% military discount at all locations**, and many restaurants honor ~10% off at their discretion.

#### How much is the Bob Evans military discount?
The Veterans Day offer is a **free entrée** (dine-in, beverage not included). The everyday discount is commonly **about 10%**, but it's location-discretionary and not a guaranteed published policy.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and active-duty, National Guard, and Reserve members qualify for the Veterans Day free meal. **Military family members, spouses, and dependents are not eligible** for the free meal, and there's no separate published family discount.

#### How do I verify my status?
In-restaurant only. Show your server a Uniformed Services ID (current or retired), LES, VA card, DD-214, driver's license with a veteran designation, or a veteran-organization card (American Legion/VFW). Wearing a military uniform on Veterans Day also qualifies.

#### Does Bob Evans use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Bob Evans has **no online military-verification portal and no online military code**. Any claim that you "verify through ID.me or SheerID on the Bob Evans website" is inaccurate — verification is simply showing ID at the restaurant.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
It's **dine-in only** at participating restaurants. There's no online/delivery military discount.

#### What is excluded?
The Veterans Day free meal excludes beverages, is dine-in only, and can't be combined with other offers, discounts, or coupons. Military family members are excluded from the free meal.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The Veterans Day meal is not valid with other offers. The everyday discount, where honored, shouldn't be assumed to stack with coupons or promos.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at Bob Evans?
**Dine in on Veterans Day (Nov 11)** for a free entrée. Any other day, ask your server for the military discount and show your ID — expect roughly 10% where it's offered.

#### Does Bob Evans offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount is published by Bob Evans. Its documented appreciation offer is military/veteran-focused (the Veterans Day free meal).

#### Does Bob Evans run a Veterans Day sale?
Yes — the **Veterans Day free meal on November 11** is its long-running annual offer, and the most dependable way for veterans and service members to save.
