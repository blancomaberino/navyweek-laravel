**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one men's **Original 4-Button Polo — $98** pre-tax (johnnie-O's signature item; $98 on johnnie-o.com).

## Headline finding

johnnie-O (johnnie-o.com) has a **real, brand-run 15% discount, verified through GovX** — stated on its own "Service Discounts" page. It covers **military, first responders, healthcare workers, government employees, and teachers**. You verify your status once through **GovX**, and once approved the 15% applies to your johnnie-O order. That's the honest everyday answer, and for a **full-price** item it's the single best standing route.

Two honest caveats. **First**, the only military figure johnnie-O publishes is **15% via GovX** — do not repeat the aggregator "up to 50% off" or "15% code" claims as a *military* discount; those map to public sales and generic signup codes. johnnie-O is **not** on **ID.me** for a military discount — ID.me's own johnnie-O page states verbatim: *"We're not aware that johnnie-O offers Military discounts."* (ID.me lists johnnie-O only for member cash back and public promos.) **Second**, johnnie-O runs **frequent seasonal sales** (an "End of Season Sale" runs up to ~50% off select styles). GovX codes are generally single-use and typically don't combine with other promos, so during a deep sale the **public sale price can beat the 15%** — buy the lower of the two. Archetype: **E — multi-tier identity discount** (flat 15% across military/first-responder/healthcare/government/teacher via GovX), with a secondary **C** flavor (frequent public sales that can beat the identity discount and generally won't stack).
