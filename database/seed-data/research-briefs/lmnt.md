# LMNT — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 11, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 30-count box of LMNT Recharge (Citrus Salt), $45 one-time price on drinklmnt.com as of July 11, 2026.

## Headline finding

**LMNT does not offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount, and it has no GovX (or ID.me / SheerID) verified storefront.** LMNT's own site publishes no military checkout and no verification-gated offer; the brand's savings model is the same for everyone — **Subscribe & Save** (single box drops from $45 to about $39, and bulk bundles fall to roughly $1.13/stick or under $1/stick on subscription) plus a standing **free 8-flavor Sample Pack with any drink-mix purchase** through partner/podcast links. The task hypothesis that LMNT runs a "GovX-verified military/first-responder discount" is **not confirmed by any primary source** — LMNT is absent from GovX while competitor electrolyte brands (Ryno Power, ATH LYTES, SALTT) do have GovX storefronts. Archetype: **B (no military route at all)**, with a strong **H (myth brand)** overlay — aggregators fabricate flat "15% / 30% / 35% / 50% military" figures that LMNT does not offer. The genuine best path for a service member is identical to the best path for anyone: **bundle + Subscribe & Save, and stack the free Sample Pack via a partner link.**
