# SiriusXM — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 12 months of **SiriusXM All Access** (the top streaming + in‑car plan) at the standard published rate of **$25.99/mo → $311.88/year, pre‑tax and pre‑fees** (rate shown on siriusxm.com/plans; the military 25% is explicitly "our top plan at 25% off," so All Access is the correct baseline).

## Headline finding

SiriusXM has a **real, first‑party military discount: 25% off the then‑current rate of its top plan, for the life of the subscription**, stated on SiriusXM's own military offer page. It's open to a broad group — **veterans, retirees, active duty, reserve, plus military spouses and dependents** — and eligibility is confirmed through a third‑party check (**SheerID** is the named partner; SheerID hosts SiriusXM's "Exclusive Military Offer"). On the $25.99/mo All Access baseline that's about **$19.49/mo (~$233.88/year)**, guaranteed to stay 25% below whatever the standard rate is. That is the honest everyday answer and, for a set‑and‑forget subscriber, the best *permanent* route.

The important catch: **25% off is 25% off SiriusXM's full "then‑current" rate — a price very few long‑term subscribers actually pay.** SiriusXM is famous for **retention/promotional pricing** (call to cancel and they routinely offer things like "5–6 months for ~$25–30," i.e. roughly $5/mo), and for **cheaper plan tiers** (App‑Only or All Music at $11.99/mo standard). A shopper willing to re‑negotiate every 6–12 months usually pays **less than the 25% military rate**; the trade‑off is that retention deals are temporary and must be chased. Archetype: **C (a genuine discount that promos frequently beat)** with a strong **F (time‑variable subscription pricing)** flavor. Debunk the aggregator noise: there is **no verified "75% off" or "$1‑for‑life" military code**, the **teacher discount has been discontinued**, and **ID.me is not the confirmed military verifier** — SiriusXM's named partner is SheerID.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The military 25% cannot be combined with any other offer** — SiriusXM states this verbatim ("this offer cannot be combined with any other"). It is either/or against the $1‑intro, retention promos, and student pricing.
2. **It's 25% off the *then‑current* rate, not a locked dollar amount** — if SiriusXM raises its standard rates (it did on Feb 24, 2026), your 25%‑off price rises with them. The discount is durable; the base price is not.
3. **Retention promos beat it but replace it** — a negotiated retention rate is a *different, temporary* deal; you can't layer it on the military 25%. Pick whichever is lower for your horizon.
4. **Student pricing is a separate program** (SheerID, streaming‑only, annual re‑verify) — not stackable with the military discount; a household picks one path per subscription.
5. **GovX is a substitute route, not a stack** — verifying/redeeming via GovX doesn't add savings on top of the 25%; it's another door to the same class of offer.
6. **Cashback isn't a reliable stack** — any portal cashback is tied to new sign‑ups and can be voided by identity‑gated checkout; don't rely on it combining with the military rate.
