# Reebok Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Reebok offers a **30% military and veteran discount** in the United States, delivered through its "Hero Discount" program and verified via **ID.me** at checkout. Per Reebok's own Current Offer Terms, "All Military Members, Reservists & Veterans are entitled to 30% off their purchase," verified at checkout, with the discount "applied automatically at checkout." The offer is online only (www.reebok.com), valid to US customers, **excludes clearance clothing styles**, excludes delivery charges, is not valid on previous purchases, and may not be combined with other offers. The single most important caveat: this is an **online-only** offer tied to ID.me identity verification, and the percentage and terms can change without notice. Note: Reebok's terms page states "30% off their purchase," while its marketing collection page describes "30% off select products" — see Open Questions.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Browse Reebok's ID.me-eligible products and add items to your cart at www.reebok.com.
2. Go to checkout and choose the **ID.me** verification option.
3. Verify your affiliation as military/reservist/veteran through ID.me (sign in or create a free ID.me account; you may be asked for documents if automatic verification can't confirm your status).
4. Once verified, the 30% discount applies automatically at checkout (Reebok also notes members "receive their promo code at checkout").

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Reebok's terms state the offer is "valid to US customers purchasing directly from www.reebok.com." There is no stated in-store military discount at Reebok-operated retail. (Not stated in official source for any physical-store redemption.)

### How Verification Works
Reebok uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility at checkout. You sign in to (or create) a free ID.me account and verify your military status; ID.me may request documents proving your status if it can't verify you automatically. Verification is handled by ID.me, not Reebok — Reebok's page directs you to choose ID.me at checkout and says the discount then applies automatically. ID.me may require re-verification per its own policies; Reebok does not publish a re-verification frequency, so do not assume "verify once forever." For verification problems, use ID.me's support (help.id.me); for order/discount issues, use Reebok customer service.

### FAQ

#### Does Reebok offer a military discount?
Yes. Reebok offers 30% off for military members, reservists, and veterans in the US, verified through ID.me at checkout (Reebok Current Offer Terms; Reebok Hero Discount page).

#### How much is the Reebok military discount?
30% off. Reebok's terms state "All Military Members, Reservists & Veterans are entitled to 30% off their purchase." (Some third-party/coupon sites claim up to 50% — this is not supported by Reebok's official pages, which state 30%.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans are named directly in Reebok's terms. Spouses and dependents qualify under ID.me's military eligibility rules (which Reebok uses for verification), covering spouses, surviving spouses, and immediate family members over 18.

#### How do I verify my status?
Choose ID.me at checkout on reebok.com and verify your military affiliation. You may be asked to upload documents if ID.me can't confirm your status automatically. Verification is run by ID.me.

#### Does Reebok use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Reebok uses **ID.me**. There is no official indication Reebok uses GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for this program.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No in-store redemption is stated. Reebok's terms limit the offer to US customers purchasing directly from www.reebok.com.

#### What is excluded?
Clearance clothing styles, delivery charges, and previous purchases are excluded, and the offer can't be combined with other promotions. It's US-only and online-only.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Reebok's terms say the offer "may not be combined with other offers and promotions unless otherwise stated," and clearance clothing is excluded.

#### Does Reebok offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes — Reebok runs separate 30% ID.me offers for first responders, nurses/medical providers, hospital employees, teachers, government employees, and students, all verified the same way at checkout.

## Open Questions
- **"Their purchase" vs. "select products":** Reebok's Current Offer Terms say 30% off "their purchase," but the Hero Discount collection page markets "30% off **select products**" and curates an "ID.me Eligible Products" collection. Exact product scope (full cart vs. an eligible-items subset) is ambiguous; recommend page copy says "30% off (on eligible products; clearance clothing excluded)."
- **Re-verification frequency:** Reebok does not publish how often ID.me re-verification is required. Don't claim "verify once."
- **Spouses/dependents wording:** Reebok's military terms list only "Military Members, Reservists & Veterans"; spouse/dependent eligibility is inferred from ID.me's military eligibility rules. Confirm whether Reebok's checkout flow actually accepts the spouse/dependent ID.me groups for this specific offer.
- **Caps:** No per-order or annual cap stated.
- **50% claims:** Multiple aggregators (and one ID.me-adjacent snippet) reference up to 50% off. Contradicted by Reebok's own 30% terms — treat as Low confidence / do not publish.
