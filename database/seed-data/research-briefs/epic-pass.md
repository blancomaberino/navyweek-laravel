**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 2026/27 season ski pass for a single eligible skier (compared against the full-price 2026/27 Epic Pass at ~$1,089–$1,119).

## Headline finding

Yes — this is a real, deep, official discount, and one of the best in the military-benefits world. Vail Resorts sells the **Epic Military Pass**, a full unlimited, unrestricted season pass to its owned/operated resorts (Vail, Breckenridge, Park City, Keystone, and more) for a fraction of the ~$1,089+ retail Epic Pass. For the 2026/27 season the spring "best price of the year" was **$215 for active-duty, Guard, Reserve, and retired military plus their registered dependents**, and **$620 for veterans** (with veteran child pricing at **$331**). Eligibility is verified through **SheerID** inside the checkout flow. Archetype: **deep official military program with third-party verification** — there is no GovX, exchange, or cashback path that beats it; the official pass IS the max-savings path. Note: the spring low price ended **May 25, 2026**; current (post-May-25) pricing runs modestly higher and should be confirmed at checkout.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The Epic Military Pass **is itself the discount** — it is not a code stacked on the regular pass. You buy a distinct military-priced product after SheerID verification; you cannot also apply the young-adult 20% or other pass discounts on top.
2. **Epic Mountain Rewards (20% off food/lodging/lessons/rentals)** and the **10 Epic Friend Tickets (up to 50% off lift tickets)** come bundled with the military pass automatically — those are perks, not stackable coupons.
3. **Each pass is verified individually**, including every spouse and dependent — one verification does not cover the family.
4. **No partner-resort access** on any military pass tier (owned/operated resorts only), unlike some full Epic Pass benefits.
5. **Auto-renew is not available** for active-military or dependent categories; it is available for retired and veteran categories.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** (Title 10, 30+ days) — eligible, $215 tier.
- **Guard / Reserve** (Title 32; excludes Individual Ready Reserve) — eligible, $215 tier.
- **Retired** (entitled to retired/retainer pay) — eligible, $215 tier.
- **Veterans** (served on active duty, discharged other than dishonorable) — eligible, $620 tier.
- **Registered dependents** (spouse + child, per DOD definitions) — eligible under the active/retired product.
- **Gold Star Families** (surviving spouse/child/parent/sibling) — qualify for the Retired Dependent Pass.
- **Australian & Canadian forces** — eligible for the Epic Military Pass (Liberty Pass is U.S. only).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the Epic Pass Military Passes page on epicpass.com.
2. Choose your tier (Active/Retired + dependents, Veteran, or Liberty).
3. Complete SheerID verification in the checkout flow — auto-verify or upload documents (DD214, orders, LES within 90 days, VA service-connected card, NGB Form 22, retiree account statement).
4. Verify each family member's pass individually.
5. Purchase; passes are non-refundable except via Epic Coverage.

### How Verification Works
Verification is powered by **SheerID** and runs inside the purchase flow. Depending on status it may confirm automatically before purchase, or route you to upload documents at verify.sheerid.com/vail-military-upload. Approval is per person and per season. **Military ID cards are not accepted** (Title 18, U.S. Code); use a DD214, orders, LES, VA card, or similar.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Owned/operated resorts only — **no partner-resort access** on military passes.
- Season passes are **non-transferable and non-refundable** except where Epic Coverage applies (Epic Coverage does cover deployment, revoked leave, and reassignment).
- **Auto-renew** is not offered for active or dependent categories (only retired/veteran).
- Each pass, including spouses/dependents, must be verified separately.
- Spring "best price" ends ~May 25; prices rise afterward.

### Best Ways To Save More
- Buy in **early spring** (March–May) before the ~May 25 price increase.
- Spring buyers get **free summer lift access** plus 20% off summer bike/golf/food/lodging.
- Use the bundled **Epic Mountain Rewards** (20% off on-mountain) and **10 Epic Friend Tickets** (up to 50% off lift tickets for friends/family).
- If a family member is 13–30 and doesn't qualify for the military tier, the regular Epic Pass has a **young-adult 20% discount** ($869).
- **Epic Coverage** is included — no need to buy separate pass insurance.

### FAQ

#### Does Epic Pass offer a military discount?
Yes. The Epic Military Pass is a full unlimited season pass sold at a deep military discount — $215 for active/retired (spring 2026/27) versus ~$1,089 retail.

#### How much is the Epic Pass military discount?
For 2026/27, the spring price was $215 for active-duty, Guard/Reserve, retired members and their dependents, and $620 for veterans (veteran child 5–17: $331). Prices rise after ~May 25.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Veterans have their own tier ($620). Active/retired members' registered spouse and child dependents qualify under the active/retired product; Gold Star Families qualify for the Retired Dependent Pass.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through SheerID inside checkout — either automatic or by uploading a DD214, orders, LES (within 90 days), VA service-connected card, or similar. Military ID cards are not accepted.

#### Does Epic Pass use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
SheerID. Not ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
The pass is bought online at epicpass.com; there is no in-store or exchange purchase path.

#### What is excluded?
Partner resorts (owned/operated only), refunds (non-refundable except via Epic Coverage), and auto-renew for active/dependent categories.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the military pass is itself the discounted product. Perks (Mountain Rewards, Friend Tickets) come bundled but aren't stackable coupons.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy an Epic Pass?
The Epic Military Pass itself, bought in early spring before the May 25 increase. No GovX, exchange, or cashback route beats it.

#### Does Epic Pass run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day promo?
Not a holiday sale — but the annual lowest price ends ~May 25 (Memorial Day week), so that's the deadline to buy cheapest.
