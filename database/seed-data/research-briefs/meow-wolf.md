# Meow Wolf — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult General Admission timed-entry ticket to Meow Wolf Denver's *Convergence Station*. Meow Wolf uses **dynamic timed pricing**, so the exact regular price varies by date/time (recent adult GA has run roughly **$45–$59**; treat that band as directional, not a verified fixed price). The verified fact is that a **military discount is applied to GA and the discounted price is shown at checkout after you pick a date/time** — Meow Wolf does not publish a fixed dollar amount or percentage for the everyday military discount.

## Headline finding

**Meow Wolf DOES offer an official military/veteran discount**, and it is a genuine per-attraction identity discount — not a mere recognition-day gimmick. It applies to **standard General Admission timed-entry tickets** for active-duty and veteran military personnel and is **available at all five open exhibitions**: Santa Fe (House of Eternal Return), Denver (Convergence Station), Las Vegas (Omega Mart), Grapevine TX (The Real Unreal), and Houston (Radio Tave). You **buy the discounted ticket online (or at the door) and show a valid military ID at entry** — there is **no ID.me or SheerID gate**; verification is a physical ID check at the door. GovX is a **secondary** verified-marketplace route for at least the Las Vegas Omega Mart tickets.

**Single best path:** For most service members, the best everyday move is to **select the military/discounted ticket type directly on tickets.meowwolf.com for your date/time and show military ID at entry.** The one clearly-quantified, beat-everything path is **seasonal: Veterans Day (11/11)**, when Meow Wolf has run **50% off military admission** (historically Santa Fe, promo code `VETERANSDAY`) — if your visit is flexible, go on Veterans Day.

**Archetype:** *Ticketed-attraction identity discount* (dominant) with a *recognition-day free/half-price event* as the secondary archetype (Veterans Day 50% off).

**Important accuracy flags (verified July 15, 2026):**
1. **No published amount.** No official Meow Wolf page states the everyday military discount's exact dollar or percentage value — it is revealed at checkout. Do NOT assert a specific everyday percentage.
2. **The dedicated promo landing pages currently display "This offer is currently unavailable. Check back for updates."** for Denver, Las Vegas, and Grapevine (and the same template for Houston) as of July 15, 2026. The descriptive discount copy and FAQs remain on those pages, and the general FAQ still lists military as an active discount category shown at checkout — but this "currently unavailable" banner is a live, load-bearing caveat: availability may be intermittent or gated to specific dates/times. Verify at checkout before promising it.
3. **Eligibility wording differs by location** (see below): Denver's page explicitly enumerates dependents/retirees/Guard/Reserve; Las Vegas/Grapevine/Houston pages say only "active and veteran military personnel." The general FAQ confirms **dependents** are eligible brand-wide.

## Stacking Rules (verified)

1. **The military discount is a distinct ticket type — you select it instead of a standard adult ticket. It does not stack with other discounted ticket types** (resident, child, senior). Choose the single best-priced type you qualify for.
2. **It applies only to standard General Admission timed-entry tickets.** It does **not** apply to special events, concerts, the annual **Portal Pass**, or add-ons (verified, Denver FAQ).
3. **No promo code needed for the everyday discount** — the military/discounted price appears at checkout once you select a date and time. (The `VETERANSDAY` code is specific to the Veterans Day event.)
4. **ID is mandatory at entry.** Buying a discounted ticket without valid military ID at the door means being **charged full price** on arrival.
5. **GovX is a separate channel** with its own verification and terms — not stacked with Meow Wolf's own military ticket; use one or the other.
6. **Dynamic timed pricing is orthogonal:** the underlying GA price still varies by date/time, so the "cheapest slot" lever combines with the military ticket type (you're picking a cheaper base to discount), but that's slot selection, not coupon stacking.

### FAQ

**Does Meow Wolf offer a military discount?**
Yes. Meow Wolf offers a discount on standard General Admission timed tickets for active-duty and veteran military personnel at all five exhibitions (Santa Fe, Denver, Las Vegas, Grapevine, and Houston). You show a valid military ID at entry.

**How much is the Meow Wolf military discount, and does it vary by location?**
Meow Wolf does not publish a fixed percentage or dollar amount for the everyday military discount — the discounted price is shown at checkout after you pick a date and time, and it varies with Meow Wolf's dynamic timed pricing. The one quantified figure Meow Wolf has published is **50% off on Veterans Day**.

**Do veterans and their families/dependents qualify?**
Veterans qualify at all locations. Dependents are eligible brand-wide per Meow Wolf's FAQ, but each discounted ticket needs valid ID at entry. Denver's page also specifically lists reservists, National Guard, and retirees; the Las Vegas/Grapevine/Houston pages mention only "active and veteran military personnel," so confirm dependent tickets at checkout there.

**How do I verify or prove my status?**
Show a valid military ID at entry. Denver also accepts a VA card or DD Form 214. If you buy a discounted ticket but can't show valid ID at the door, you're charged full price.

**Does Meow Wolf use ID.me or GovX?**
Not ID.me (or SheerID) — verification is a physical ID check at entry. **GovX** does sell Meow Wolf military/government discount tickets (including Omega Mart Las Vegas) through its own verified marketplace as a separate option.

**Can I use the discount online or only at the box office?**
Both. You can buy the military/discounted GA ticket online at tickets.meowwolf.com or at the door — either way, bring your military ID to show at entry.

**Which locations offer it — Santa Fe, Denver, Las Vegas, Grapevine, Houston?**
All five. Each exhibition has its own military-discount page and applies the discount to General Admission timed tickets.

**Can I combine it with other discounts?**
No. The military ticket type doesn't stack with resident, child, or senior pricing, and it only applies to standard GA timed tickets — not the Portal Pass, special events, or add-ons. Pick the single best-priced ticket type you qualify for.

**What's the cheapest way for a service member to visit?**
If you can go on Veterans Day (11/11), that's historically ~50% off. Otherwise, select the military ticket type for the cheapest available timed slot and show your military ID. If you also qualify for NM/NV resident pricing, compare and take the lower.

**Is there a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?**
Meow Wolf's discount categories are military, children, seniors, local residents, and accessibility. There is no first responder, teacher, or nurse discount, and no separate student discount beyond those categories.
