> Scope: **Pandora jewelry** (Pandora Jewelry, LLC — pandora.net / us.pandora.net), United States. This brief is about the jewelry brand only. ## Executive Summary

Pandora jewelry offers a **10% military discount** in the United States, delivered through its "Supporting Heroes" / Student & Military program. Eligibility is verified by **ID.me**; after verifying, the shopper receives a single-use promo code valid for **60 days**, redeemable **online at pandora.net and in-store at participating Pandora retailers** (in-store is ID.me only, and participation varies by location). The offer is limited to **one code per customer per 24 hours**, **cannot be combined** with any other offer/promotion/discount, and **excludes 14K gold, Lab-Grown Diamonds (Diamonds by Pandora), and gift card/e-card purchases**. Eligible groups follow ID.me's standard military community definition (active duty, Guard/Reserve, veterans, retirees, military spouses, and immediate family/dependents). The single most important caveat: this is the **jewelry** discount (10%, ID.me) — do not confuse it with the **music service's** 20% "Pandora Premium" offer (pandora.com, SheerID).

### How Verification Works
Pandora uses **ID.me** to confirm military eligibility. You verify through ID.me (a free account is required), and on success Pandora issues a promo code. The code is single-use, limited to one per customer per 24 hours, and expires 60 days after you receive it. ID.me may periodically require re-verification of your status per its own policies; do not assume a one-time, permanent verification. For verification problems, use ID.me's help/support; for code or order issues, contact Pandora customer service. In-store redemption is available only where the store supports ID.me.

### FAQ

#### Does Pandora offer a military discount?
Yes. Pandora jewelry offers 10% off for verified members of the military community through its Supporting Heroes program, verified by ID.me.

#### How much is the Pandora military discount?
10% off eligible purchases. (Note: the unrelated Pandora **music** service offers a separate 20% "Pandora Premium" military deal — that is not the jewelry discount.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. The program covers the military community ID.me verifies: active duty, National Guard, Reserve, veterans, retirees, military spouses (including surviving spouses), and immediate family members/dependents.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify through ID.me (free account required). After verifying, Pandora gives you a single-use promo code valid for 60 days to use at checkout.

#### Does Pandora use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The Pandora **jewelry** military discount uses **ID.me**. (Students use **Student Beans**. The separate Pandora **music** service uses **SheerID** for its own offer.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes, at participating Pandora retailers that support ID.me — participation varies by location, so call ahead. Redeem the ID.me-issued code at the register.

#### What is excluded?
14K gold, Lab-Grown Diamonds (Diamonds by Pandora), and gift card/e-card purchases. It also can't be combined with other offers and isn't valid on prior purchases.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military code cannot be combined with any other offer, promotion, or discount. Compare the 10% code against the current sale price to see which is better for your item.

#### Does Pandora offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Pandora runs a 10% **student** discount via Student Beans. For first responders, teachers, nurses, government, and medical, Pandora's program officially names "Military," though ID.me's Pandora storefront lists category pages for those audiences — confirm the actual offer and amount at checkout rather than assuming 10%.

#### Is the Pandora music "Premium" military discount the same thing?
No. Pandora the music-streaming service (pandora.com) is a different company and offers its own 20%-off "Pandora Premium" subscription deal via SheerID. The jewelry discount described here is 10% via ID.me at pandora.net.
