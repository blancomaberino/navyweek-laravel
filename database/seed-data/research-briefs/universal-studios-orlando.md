# Universal Orlando Resort — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adult multi-day park-to-park admissions (used illustratively as the standard 2-park 3-day / 3-park 3-day comparison; Universal gate/online prices are dynamic — **verify live at checkout**).

## Headline finding

Universal Orlando Resort does **not** run a year-round percentage discount you claim online with an ID.me/SheerID button. Its flagship military offer is the **2026 Military Freedom Pass** — a promotional ticket sold **exclusively through Authorized Military Ticket & Travel offices (base ITT/MWR/LTS, Shades of Green, and similar outlets)**, never at Universal's front gate or online. For a flat **$220 (2-Park)** or **$255 (3-Park)** per adult, an eligible service member gets **more than a year of near-unlimited admission** (Nov 13, 2025 – Dec 18, 2026, blockout dates apply) to Universal Studios Florida + Islands of Adventure (+ Volcano Bay on the 3-Park). Against Universal's own regular multi-day prices (a 3-park 3-day is $411/adult), that's the biggest theme-park value in the market — effectively an annual-pass-grade deal at a single-visit price.

Two honest caveats: **(1)** the new **Epic Universe** park is **not** in the Freedom Pass — it's a separately priced 1-Day add-on that requires an activated Freedom Pass; **(2)** if you're a **veteran who isn't retired or 100% disabled**, or a first responder, the Freedom Pass isn't for you — your path is **GovX** (up to $100 off, buys online). Archetype: **I (attraction/travel — the channel and the pass structure matter more than a single %).**

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (all branches).
- **Reserves / National Guard** — Eligible.
- **Retired military** — Eligible.
- **U.S. Coast Guard** — Eligible.
- **Spouses of eligible members** — Eligible (and one of the six purchased tickets may be used by the spouse).
- **DOD Civilian personnel** — Eligible for the **tickets** (with qualifying DEERS ID); **not** eligible for the hotel discount.
- **100% Disabled Veterans** — Eligible.
- **Veterans (not retired, not 100% disabled)** — **Not** eligible for the Freedom Pass. Use **GovX**.
- **First responders / law enforcement / fire / EMS** — Not part of the Freedom Pass; eligible via **GovX**.
- **Not eligible for the Freedom Pass:** contractors, foreign military (including International Fellows with a CAC), NOAA, U.S. Public Health Service, DHS, divorced spouses, child dependents (as the required member), retired DOD civilians.

### How To Redeem
**Military Freedom Pass:**
1. Go to an **Authorized Military Ticket & Travel office** (base ITT/MWR/Leisure Travel Services, Shades of Green, or similar). Find locations via the list on universalorlando.com/Military.
2. Present a **valid DEERS-issued Military/DOD photo ID** and buy the 2-Park or 3-Park pass. You may buy up to **6** tickets; at least one must be for the member (or spouse).
3. At the park, the **eligible member must be present** to **exchange/activate** the promotional tickets for the whole party at the **Front Gate Ticket window, Guest Services, or a Destination Universal desk** in a Universal/partner hotel — again showing the DEERS ID.
4. Want Epic Universe? Buy the **Epic Universe 1-Day add-on** (requires the activated pass; daily pricing in the web store).

**GovX (veterans / first responders / online):**
1. Verify your status on **GovX**.
2. Buy Universal Orlando tickets online (up to $100 off); follow GovX's redemption instructions.

### How Verification Works
The Freedom Pass uses **physical, in-person verification** — a valid **DEERS-issued** Military or DOD photo ID, checked both when you buy at the base office **and** when you exchange the tickets at the park gate. There is **no ID.me or SheerID** step for the Freedom Pass. GovX, by contrast, runs its own online military/first-responder verification for the tickets it sells.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Sold only through Authorized Military Ticket & Travel offices** — not at Universal's front gate or online.
- **Epic Universe is excluded** from the Freedom Pass (paid 1-Day add-on, requires activated pass).
- **Blockout dates:** Dec 25, 2025 – Jan 4, 2026; Mar 30 – Apr 6, 2026 (both parks). Volcano Bay also blocked Oct 26 – Dec 18, 2026 (park closes for maintenance ~Oct 26, 2026).

### FAQ

#### Does Universal Orlando offer a military discount?
Yes — the **2026 Military Freedom Pass**, sold through Authorized Military Ticket & Travel offices (base ITT/MWR, Shades of Green, etc.). It's $220 (2-Park) or $255 (3-Park) per adult for more than a year of admission, not a single visit.

#### How much is the Universal Orlando Military Freedom Pass?
$220 adult / $215 child for the 2-Park (USF + Islands of Adventure); $255 adult / $250 child for the 3-Park (adds Volcano Bay). Valid Nov 13, 2025 – Dec 18, 2026, blockout dates apply.

#### Where do I buy it — can I get it online or at the gate?
Neither. The Freedom Pass is sold **only** at Authorized Military Ticket & Travel offices. It is not available at Universal's front gate or on universalorlando.com. (GovX is the online military/first-responder ticket option.)

#### Is Epic Universe included?
No. Epic Universe is **not** part of the Freedom Pass. It's a separately priced **1-Day add-on** that requires an activated Freedom Pass; daily pricing shows in the web store.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Only veterans who are **retired** or **100% disabled**. Other veterans should use **GovX** (up to $100 off, online).

#### How do I verify my military status?
With a valid **DEERS-issued** Military/DOD photo ID — shown when you buy at the base office and again when you exchange tickets at the park gate. No ID.me/SheerID for the Freedom Pass.

#### How many tickets can I buy?
Up to **six** per eligible member, and at least one must be used by the member (or spouse). The member must be present to activate the party's tickets at the gate.

#### What about hotels?
Eligible members get **40% off** the flexible rate at Cabana Bay and **up to 30% off** other Universal hotels (not available to DOD civilians) — book through the Authorized office or 1-877-801-9720.

#### Is there a first-responder discount?
Not through the Freedom Pass, but **GovX** offers Universal Orlando tickets (up to $100 off) to first responders, law enforcement, fire, and EMS.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit?
If you're eligible and can reach a base ticket office, the **Military Freedom Pass** — $220/$255 for 13+ months of admission is unbeatable. Add Epic Universe as a 1-Day only for the day you'll go, and layer the military hotel rate if you're staying over.
