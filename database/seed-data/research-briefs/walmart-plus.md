# Walmart+ (Membership) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **annual Walmart+ membership** (standard **$98/year**), compared against every discounted-membership path a service member can actually use.

> **Scope note:** This page answers one narrow question — *does the military get a discount on the Walmart+ membership subscription?* For Walmart's broader "does Walmart give a military/veteran discount on purchases?" answer (short version: no everyday product discount), see NavyWeek's separate **[Walmart Military Discount](/discount/walmart-military-discount/)** page. This brief cross-links rather than duplicates it.

## Headline finding

**There is no military- or veteran-specific discount on a Walmart+ membership.** Walmart does not publish a "Walmart+ for military" tier, and `/plus/military` does not exist as a distinct offer. The **only** way a member of the military community pays less for Walmart+ is **Walmart+ Assist** — a needs-based program that cuts the membership 50% to **$49/year (or $6.47/month)** vs. the standard **$98/year ($12.95/month)**. Veterans can qualify for Walmart+ Assist **only if** they receive the **VA "Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit"** — a needs-based pension, verified through **SheerID** — which is on Walmart's official qualifying-program list. This is **not** a broad veteran discount: standard veteran status, VA disability compensation, and military retirement pay do **not** qualify by themselves. For everyone else, the best "discount" is Walmart's own **free 30-day trial** (and occasional public $1-trial promos), which are open to all shoppers. Archetype: **everyday-low-price retailer with no dedicated military offer**, plus a **needs-based subsidy** (Walmart+ Assist) that only *some* veterans can reach.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The discounted tiers are mutually exclusive with the standard price — they are not "stacked" on it.** Walmart+ Assist ($49) and Walmart+ Student ($49) *replace* the $98 plan; you don't apply a discount code on top of a standard membership.
2. **You can't hold Assist and Student simultaneously** — you pick the one program/status you verify under. Both land at the same $49/yr.
3. **The free 30-day trial precedes a paid plan**, including Assist; it is not an extra discount layered onto the annual price.
4. **No public coupon or cashback portal applies to the membership fee** — Walmart+ is a subscription billed by Walmart; there is no verified code-stack or Rakuten-on-membership path. (Cashback portals apply to *purchases*, not the subscription.)
5. **Verification is single-gate:** SheerID confirms one qualifying program; there's no "veteran + low-income" double discount — 50% is the floor either way.

### Who Qualifies
*(For a discount on the Walmart+ membership specifically.)*
- **Active-duty military** — No membership discount for service alone. May qualify for Walmart+ Assist only via a listed assistance program (e.g., SNAP), not via service.
- **Veterans** — No discount for veteran status. Qualify for **Walmart+ Assist ($49/yr)** *only if* receiving the **VA Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit** (or another qualifying program), verified by SheerID.
- **Retirees** — No membership discount; retirement pay is not a qualifying program.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — No membership discount for service; only via a listed assistance program.
- **Military spouses / dependents** — No discount for status. **Survivors** receiving the **Survivors Pension Benefit** may qualify for Walmart+ Assist.
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not a stated military offer; **Survivors Pension Benefit** recipients can qualify for Walmart+ Assist.
- **Students (incl. military-affiliated students)** — **Walmart+ Student** offers the same $49/yr membership, verified as a college student (education-based, not military).
- **First responders / nurses / teachers** — No Walmart+ membership discount listed.

### How To Redeem Online
**There is no military-gated membership discount to redeem.** If you receive a qualifying program (e.g., the VA Veterans & Survivors Pension Benefit) and want **Walmart+ Assist**:
1. Go to **walmart.com/plus/assist**.
2. Sign into your Walmart account (or create one).
3. You're routed to **SheerID** to verify you're in a qualifying program (SheerID may request documentation; no credit check, no impact to your benefits).
4. Once verified, return to Walmart to finish signup at **$49/year or $6.47/month** — with a **free 30-day trial** to start.

### How To Redeem In Store
Walmart+ is an online membership subscription, not a register discount — there is **nothing a cashier can apply**. Membership benefits (free delivery, fuel savings, Scan & Go, etc.) attach to your account once you're enrolled, whether at the standard or Assist price.

### How Verification Works
Walmart uses **SheerID** to confirm Walmart+ Assist eligibility — **not** military service. You enter your details, SheerID checks that you're in a qualifying program (the **VA Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit** is one), and may ask for documentation. There's **no credit check and no impact to existing government benefits**. Verification lasts **one year**; Walmart re-checks eligibility annually to renew the discounted membership. There is no ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute path for a Walmart+ membership discount, because no military-specific membership discount exists.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **No military/veteran Walmart+ tier** — service, DD-214, or a VA ID gets you nothing off the membership by itself.
- **Walmart+ Assist is needs-based.** The VA path is specifically the **Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit** — a low-income pension. *(Flag: VA disability compensation, military retirement pay, and plain veteran status are NOT listed as qualifying programs.)*
- **"Not all government programs are eligible."** SheerID's screen is the final arbiter; the qualifying list can change.
- **Source discrepancy to know:** Walmart's help article lists the Veterans Pension Benefit; SheerID's public offer page and the `/plus/assist` landing page summarize as "SNAP, WIC, Medicaid & more" without naming it. If SheerID declines you, that's the governing outcome despite the help-article listing.
- **Prices are "plus applicable tax."** Terms reflect **Walmart.com (US)**.

### FAQ

#### Does Walmart+ offer a military discount?
No. There is no military or veteran discount on a Walmart+ membership, and no "Walmart+ for military" tier. The standard price is $98/year or $12.95/month for everyone. The only discounted path relevant to the military community is Walmart+ Assist.

#### How much is the Walmart+ military discount?
There isn't a military one. The relevant discount is **Walmart+ Assist**: **$49/year or $6.47/month** (50% off the $98 standard), for people who receive a qualifying government-assistance program.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a cheaper Walmart+?
Only through Walmart+ Assist, and only if they receive a qualifying program. **Veterans (and survivors) who receive the VA "Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit" qualify** at $49/year, verified by SheerID. Plain veteran status, VA disability comp, or retirement pay alone do not qualify.

#### How do I verify my military status for Walmart+?
You can't verify "military status" for a membership discount — there isn't one. For **Walmart+ Assist**, you verify your **qualifying-program** status (including the VA Pension Benefit) through **SheerID** at walmart.com/plus/assist. No credit check; verification lasts one year and renews annually.

#### Does Walmart+ use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Walmart uses **SheerID** — but only to confirm **Walmart+ Assist** eligibility (a qualifying assistance program), not military service. It does not use ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for any Walmart+ membership discount.

#### Can I combine Walmart+ Assist with a promo code or another discount?
No. Walmart+ Assist ($49) and Walmart+ Student ($49) are discounted **tiers that replace** the standard price — they aren't stacked with coupons or with each other. The free 30-day trial simply precedes whichever paid plan you choose.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to get Walmart+?
If you receive the VA Veterans & Survivors Pension Benefit (or SNAP, Medicaid, WIC, SSI, etc.), **Walmart+ Assist at $49/year** is the cheapest legitimate path. If you don't qualify, start the **free 30-day trial**, watch for public $1-trial promos, and only pay $98 if regular delivery/fuel savings make it worthwhile.

#### Does Walmart+ offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No first-responder, teacher, or nurse membership discount is published. **Walmart+ Student** does offer the same $49/year membership to verified college students — education-based, not military or first-responder.

#### Is Walmart+ Assist the same as a "veteran discount"?
No — and this is the key caveat. Walmart+ Assist is a **needs-based** program tied to low-income assistance programs. Only the subset of veterans and survivors who receive the **VA Pension Benefit** qualify. Most veterans will not qualify through the VA path.
