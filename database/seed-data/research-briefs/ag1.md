# AG1 (Athletic Greens) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one month of the standard AG1 Pouch subscription = **$79.00** (recurring, shipped every 30 days).

## Headline finding

AG1 has a **real, official military discount: 25% off a new AG1 subscription**, verified through **ID.me** and applied automatically at checkout on DrinkAG1.com. It covers the active-duty and veteran community, first responders, nurses, teachers, and medical/hospital staff. It applies **only to subscriptions** (not one-time pouches or merch) and **cannot be combined with other offers** — so a service member chooses between the 25% ongoing discount and the standard first-order "welcome kit" freebies. AG1 is a **subscription-only DTC brand** (archetype: verified-discount subscription box), so the classic max-savings levers — the Exchange, ExpertVoice, refurb, retail sale-stacking — don't exist. For anyone who stays subscribed, the **ID.me 25% is the clear winner** at ~$59.25/month ($237/year saved) versus a one-time bundle of free gifts. The main thing to debunk: coupon sites advertising "50% off" / "35% off" AG1 promo codes — AG1 does not publish public percentage codes; those are fabricated or dead.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Does not stack with any other offer.** AG1's terms: the discount "cannot be combined with other offers" — so the 25% and the first-order welcome-kit freebies are **mutually exclusive**; you pick one.
2. **Subscription only.** Applies exclusively to subscription forms of AG1 Pouch, AG1 30-count Travel Packs, AG1 Pro Travel Packs, AGZ 30-count, and AG Omega-3 (up to 3 of each per shipment). One-time purchases and merch are excluded.
3. **New subscriptions only, and it recurs.** Applies to a **new** subscription and continues on recurring shipments as long as the subscription stays active. **Not retroactive** to prior orders. **Non-transferable.**
4. **DrinkAG1.com only.** Not valid on Amazon or other third-party listings; verification happens on AG1's own checkout via ID.me.
5. **Cashback portals:** because the ID.me discount is applied at AG1's checkout, whether a cashback portal still pays on a discounted order is portal-dependent and unverified — do not assume it stacks.

### FAQ

#### Does AG1 offer a military discount?
Yes. AG1 offers **25% off a new AG1 subscription** for the verified military community, applied through ID.me at checkout on DrinkAG1.com.

#### How much is the AG1 military discount?
**25%** off subscription products — about **$59.25/month** on the standard $79 AG1 subscription, or roughly **$237 saved per year**.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. ID.me eligibility covers active duty (including Guard and Reserve), veterans, retirees, military family members over 18, and military spouses.

#### How do I verify my status?
At AG1 checkout, choose **"Verify with ID.me,"** sign into (or create) your ID.me account, and confirm your status. The discount then applies automatically.

#### Does AG1 use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**ID.me** only. There is no confirmed GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass path for AG1.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Online only, on DrinkAG1.com. AG1 has no retail stores, and the discount is not valid on Amazon or other third-party sellers.

#### What is excluded?
One-time non-subscription purchases and all merchandise. The discount also can't be combined with other offers, isn't retroactive, and is non-transferable.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. It **cannot be combined** with other offers — including the standard first-order welcome-kit freebies. You choose one or the other.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy AG1?
If you qualify and plan to stay subscribed, the **ID.me 25%** is the best deal (~$59.25/mo). If you only want to try it once, take the standard **welcome kit** on a first subscription and cancel after the first shipment.

#### Does AG1 offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or medical discount?
Yes — the **same 25% subscription discount** applies to first responders, nurses, teachers, medical providers, and hospital employees, all via ID.me.

#### Does AG1 run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Not a separate one. The 25% ID.me discount is **year-round**; AG1 promotes it more heavily on social around service holidays, but there's no observed deeper limited-time military percentage.

#### Can I buy AG1 at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
No. AG1 is direct-to-consumer only and is not carried by AAFES/NEX or offered through ExpertVoice/pro-deal channels.
