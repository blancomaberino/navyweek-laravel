# Costa Del Mar — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of current-season Costa polarized sunglasses, MSRP **$250** (e.g., Rincon/Fantail class). Tax shown at a representative **7%** because a tax-free channel (military exchange) competes.

## Headline finding

Yes — Costa Del Mar runs a **real, official 15% military/first-responder/teacher discount**, verified through **ID.me** on costadelmar.com and also offered through **GovX** and **WeSalute**. It is a flat 15% off, published on Costa's own site, and it covers the broad ID.me military community (active, Guard/Reserve, veterans, retirees, and their eligible family members). But 15% is rarely the *best* a service member can do: Costa's own **clearance runs up to 50% off**, a public **buy-one-get-one-50%-off** promo effectively beats 15% when you buy two, and **GovX pro-deal listings** sometimes price specific models below the 15% line. Costa also sells through **Navy Exchange (NEX)** and other exchanges tax-free. Archetype: **licensed-eyewear brand with an official-but-modest verified discount that a public sale routinely beats** (secondary: pro-deal/GovX brand). The honest verdict: take 15% for a specific in-season model you want at full retail, but check clearance and GovX first — they usually win.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The **military discount does not stack** with public promo codes — it is applied through a verified ID.me/GovX/WeSalute session, not a code, and Costa allows **one promo code per order**.
2. Clearance/marked-down items generally **exclude** further codes; the 15% is unlikely to apply on top of clearance (unverified that it ever does — do not assert it does).
3. **BOGO 50% off** is a standalone public promo — treat it as its own path, not something to combine with the 15%.
4. GovX pro-deal pricing is **already the discounted price** — no additional Costa code applies on GovX.
5. Exchange (NEX/AAFES) purchases are **tax-free** but do not take the ID.me 15% — they are separate channels.

### FAQ

#### Does Costa Del Mar offer a military discount?
Yes. Costa offers a **15% discount** for military, first responders, and teachers, verified through ID.me on its own site and also via GovX and WeSalute.

#### How much is the Costa military discount?
**15% off.** It's a flat rate published on Costa's ID.me exclusive page.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — Costa uses ID.me's standard military eligibility, which covers active duty, Guard/Reserve, **veterans**, retirees, spouses, eligible adult family members, and surviving spouses.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **ID.me** on costadelmar.com (or through GovX/WeSalute if you use those). Create a free account and confirm your status once.

#### Does Costa use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**ID.me** on Costa's own site, plus **GovX** and **WeSalute** as separate verified channels. There's no verified SheerID or VerifyPass Costa program.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The 15% is an **online** verified discount. For in-person buying, eligible patrons can shop Costa at **NEX/AAFES exchanges tax-free** (a separate channel, not the 15%).

#### What is excluded?
Costa doesn't publish a product exclusion list; the discount applies to "top performing gear." It **does not stack** with codes or clearance. Confirm eligible items at checkout.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Costa allows one code per order, and the military discount is applied through a verified account — it doesn't stack with public codes or clearance.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Costa?
If you're flexible on model, **clearance (up to 50% off)** usually beats the 15%. Buying two pairs? **BOGO 50% off**. Need a specific current-season frame? Take the **15%**. Check **GovX** for your exact model, and buy **tax-free at the exchange** if that's easier.

#### Does Costa offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
Yes — the same **15%** covers first responders, teachers, nurses, and some government/medical/hospital roles via ID.me.

#### Does Costa run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Costa's military value is the **evergreen 15%**. It runs public site-wide sales around major holidays that anyone can use, but no verified military-exclusive holiday markdown beyond the standing 15%.

#### Can I buy Costa at the Exchange?
Yes — Costa sunglasses are stocked at **Navy Exchange (NEX)** and other exchanges, sold **tax-free** to eligible patrons.
