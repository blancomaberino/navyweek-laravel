# Teva — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$80 pair of Teva sandals** (e.g., the Original Universal / Hurricane XLT2, Teva's core price point).

## Headline finding

**There is no Teva military or veteran discount.** ID.me's Teva page states plainly, *"We're not aware that Teva offers Military discounts,"* Teva does not run a first-responder discount, and Teva is **not on GovX**. Teva (a **Deckers** brand, sister to Hoka and UGG) publishes no ID.me/SheerID/GovX military program of any kind. So the honest best-savings path for a service member is entirely **non-military**: Teva's own **sale/outlet clearance**, the **public email-signup code**, **ID.me Shop cashback** (a shopping-portal rebate open to everyone, not a military rate), and **retailer substitutes** — chiefly **REI**, where Teva is widely stocked and REI Co-op members earn a **~10% annual dividend** on full-price purchases plus member sales. Archetype: **footwear brand with no military discount — save via sale/outlet + retailer substitutes.**
