# SEA LIFE Aquarium (Merlin Entertainments) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adult general-admission tickets to a SEA LIFE aquarium (representative walk-up adult GA ~$29.99 each, so ~$60 pre-tax for two).

## Headline finding

SEA LIFE (a Merlin Entertainments attraction brand) offers a **verified military discount redeemed online through ID.me**, not at the box office. The military ticket is available to a **broad audience — active service members, retirees, veterans, military spouses, and dependents** (US, plus Canadian military) — and typically covers **up to 4 people per transaction**, with children age 1 and under free and not counted. ID.me's storefront frames the saving as roughly **"up to 25%"**, though the exact discounted price is set per location and shown after verification. One notable location variant: **SEA LIFE at Mall of America** sells a walk-up military GA at **$18.99 + tax** at the Guest Service Desk with valid military/veteran ID. Archetype: multi-venue attraction operator that centralizes military pricing behind ID.me online verification while allowing some in-person location exceptions.
