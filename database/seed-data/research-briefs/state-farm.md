# State Farm — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 12-month personal auto insurance policy (single driver, one vehicle). Insurance "savings" here means premium discounts/programs, not a cart price.

## Headline finding

**State Farm does not offer a nationwide military or veteran discount.** Its official auto-insurance discounts page lists a dozen savings programs — Drive Safe & Save, Steer Clear, multi-policy bundling, good driver, good student, vehicle safety, anti-theft, and more — and **none of them is a military discount.** The **only** place State Farm publishes a military-specific discount is **Louisiana**, where a **"Military Personnel Discount"** appears on the state discounts page because Louisiana law requires insurers to offer one; it applies to a named insured/registered owner who is **on active duty** or an **active reservist in the Louisiana Air or Army National Guard** and who completes the state's military-discount application. (Third-party trackers put the Louisiana reduction at ~25%; State Farm's page states the discount exists but doesn't publish the percentage — treat the 25% as secondary.) So the honest answer for the ~49 other states: **no military discount**, but real savings exist through State Farm's telematics and bundling programs — and a service member should **price-check State Farm against military-focused insurers (USAA, GEICO's military program, Armed Forces Insurance)** before assuming State Farm is cheapest. Archetype: **insurance / no first-party military discount** (the honest-answer page; the value is correcting the record and routing to the real savings).

There's no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID flow — State Farm is agent-serviced. In Louisiana, verification is the **state military-discount application** plus proof of status, handled through your agent. Outside Louisiana, there's no military verification because there's no military discount.

#### Does State Farm offer a military discount?
Not nationwide. The **only** State Farm military discount is in **Louisiana** (state-mandated), for active-duty members and active Louisiana Air/Army National Guard reservists.

#### How much is the State Farm military discount?
In Louisiana, third-party trackers cite about **25%**, but State Farm's page doesn't publish the percentage. In other states there's no military discount at all.

#### Do veterans qualify for a State Farm military discount?
No — even the Louisiana discount is framed around **active duty** and **active National Guard reservists**, not veterans broadly. There's no veteran discount.

#### How do I get the Louisiana military discount?
Ask your State Farm agent and complete the **Louisiana application for military discount** with proof of military status.
