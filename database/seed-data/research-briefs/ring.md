# Ring Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Ring (Ring.com — video doorbells, cameras, and alarm systems; an Amazon company) offers a **20% "Give-Back" discount on select Ring products** for military and veterans, plus first responders, government employees, healthcare workers, teachers, and students. Eligibility is verified through **VerifyPass** (not ID.me or GovX). After verifying, the shopper receives a **one-time, single-use discount code** to enter at checkout on Ring.com; a new code can be requested no more than **once per 24 hours**. The offer is **United States only**, applies to **online orders only**, **excludes bundles and "offers" (sale/discounted items)**, **cannot be combined with other discounts** (including the 10% subscriber discount or sales events like Black Friday/Cyber Monday), and **cannot be applied retroactively**. Shipping and taxes still apply. The single most important caveat: because the code does not stack and is **"not valid during sales events,"** a Ring sale price may sometimes beat the 20% Give-Back discount — shoppers should compare.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Ring's Give-Back discount page (ring.com/give-back) and click "Get Code" / "Shop Now."
2. Sign in to VerifyPass and verify your eligibility (e.g., military/veteran status).
3. After successful verification, you'll receive a **one-time discount code**.
4. Add eligible Ring products to your cart on Ring.com. (Tip: if a code won't apply, try while signed out of your Ring account.)
5. At checkout, enter the code in the "Discount Code" field (top right).
6. The 20% discount applies to eligible items. Complete your purchase.
7. For a future purchase, return to the Give-Back page and verify again — limit one code per 24 hours.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not available. Ring's official source states the Give-Back discount is for **online purchases** of eligible products at Ring.com. No in-store or register redemption is documented. (Some third-party sites claim "participating retail locations" — Ring's own sources do not confirm in-store redemption; treat that claim as unverified.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **VerifyPass**. You start on Ring's Give-Back page, sign into VerifyPass, and confirm your status. On success you get a **single-use code** for one order. There is no permanent account-level discount: each order requires a fresh code, and you can request a new code at most **once every 24 hours** (one use per customer per code). VerifyPass notes discount codes are "subject to ID verification in our sole discretion." If verification fails, follow VerifyPass's support flow within the verification widget; for order/checkout problems, use Ring Help (support.ring.com).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Compare against Ring's current sales.** The Give-Back code can't stack and isn't valid during sales events; a sitewide sale (Ring advertises "savings up to 50%") may beat 20% on some items. Check ring.com/collections/offers before redeeming.
- **10% subscriber discount.** After buying a Ring subscription (Ring Home/Protect), a 10% discount on eligible products activates on your next login (can't be used in the same transaction as the subscription, and can't combine with the Give-Back discount). Valid in US, Canada, UK, EU, Australia, and New Zealand.
- **Free shipping and free returns** on all US orders (standing Ring policy, not status-gated).
- **Competitor comparison:** other smart-home/security brands also run verified-discount programs (commonly via ID.me, GovX, or SheerID). Worth comparing the net price on a specific device, since Ring's discount excludes bundles and sale items.

### FAQ

#### Does Ring offer a military discount?
Yes. Ring's Give-Back program gives **20% off select Ring products** to military members and veterans (and several other groups), verified through VerifyPass. Source: Ring Help "Using Ring discounts," accessed June 24, 2026.

#### How much is the Ring military discount?
**20% off** eligible Ring products. It excludes bundles and items already on sale, and can't be combined with other discounts. Source: VerifyPass Ring offers page; Ring Help, accessed June 24, 2026.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify under the "Military and Veterans" offer. Ring's own pages don't separately list spouses or dependents — eligibility for those sub-groups is determined inside the **VerifyPass** verification flow, so confirm there. Source: VerifyPass Ring offers page, accessed June 24, 2026.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to ring.com/give-back, click to get a code, and verify through **VerifyPass**. On success you get a one-time code for checkout. You can request a new code at most once per 24 hours. Source: Ring "Give-Back" page, accessed June 24, 2026.

#### Does Ring use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No — Ring uses **VerifyPass** for the Give-Back discount. (Some third-party sites list ID.me; Ring's official pages specify VerifyPass.) Source: Ring "Give-Back" page; Ring Help, accessed June 24, 2026.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. Ring documents the Give-Back discount for **online purchases on Ring.com** only — a one-time code entered at checkout. No in-store redemption is stated. Source: Ring Help "Using Ring discounts," accessed June 24, 2026.

#### What is excluded?
Bundles, items already on sale/offer, Ring subscriptions, Ring Assist+, and third-party devices. It also can't be used during sales events, can't be combined with other discounts, and can't be applied retroactively; shipping and taxes still apply. Source: Ring Help; VerifyPass terms, accessed June 24, 2026.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The Give-Back discount **cannot be combined** with other offers (including the 10% subscriber discount, Black Friday, or Cyber Monday) and is **not valid during sales events**. Source: Ring Help "Using Ring discounts," accessed June 24, 2026.

#### Does Ring offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes — the same Give-Back program gives **20% off** to first responders, government employees, healthcare/medical workers, teachers, and students, each verified through VerifyPass. Source: VerifyPass Ring offers page; Ring "Give-Back" page, accessed June 24, 2026.

#### How often can I get a discount code?
A code is single-use, and you can request a new one **no more than once every 24 hours**. Source: Ring "Give-Back" page, accessed June 24, 2026.
