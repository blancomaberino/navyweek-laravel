## Headline finding

**Titan Fitness has no military, veteran, or first-responder discount — by its own published policy.** The brand's support-center article "Military, Veteran, and First Responder Discount Policy" states it does not currently have a discount program for these groups (it says it hopes to add one in the future). The honest savings path is Titan's own aggressive public discounting: seasonal sale events (it maintains a Memorial Day sale collection; aggregator research puts Black Friday/Cyber Monday peaks around 35% off select gear), an email-signup welcome code, and the brand's published price-adjustment policy.
