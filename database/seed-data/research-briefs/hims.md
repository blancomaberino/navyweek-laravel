# Hims — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one month of a **Hims Weight Loss (compounded semaglutide GLP-1) subscription**.

## Headline finding

**As of July 2026, Hims no longer offers a military discount to new customers.** The widely publicized "Service Appreciation" program — announced September 2024 as up-to-50%-off compounded semaglutide GLP-1s for as low as **$99/month** for military, veterans, teachers, nurses, and first responders — became a **closed legacy discount effective April 10, 2025.** Per the current Hims promotion terms (effective June 2, 2026), the "Special Groups Discount" is **"no longer available to new members"**; **only users whose eligibility was verified through SheerID on or before April 10, 2025** keep the discounted pricing. So a service member signing up today **cannot** get the military rate — they pay standard pricing (a **$39 first-month / $149-per-month Weight Loss Membership**, medication if prescribed). The verifier *was* **SheerID** (never ID.me). Archetype: **telehealth subscription with a discontinued/grandfathered service-group offer** — the honest job here is to tell readers the military deal is closed to newcomers and point them to the real levers: standard promos, plan commitment, and cross-shopping other GLP-1 telehealth.

### How To Redeem (Current Reality)
There is **no active military discount to redeem** for new customers. If you're a grandfathered legacy member, the discount applies automatically at checkout on your eligible annual/bi-annual plan. New customers simply pay standard membership pricing after an online consultation.

### How Verification Worked
Hims partnered with **SheerID** to verify eligibility for the Special Groups Discount. Verification required documents like a DD214, dated ID, insurance card, official orders, or a paystub dated within 90 days (active-duty/reservist ID cards were **not** accepted, per Title 18 U.S.C. §701). This path is now closed to new members — SheerID no longer opens new military eligibility for the discount.
