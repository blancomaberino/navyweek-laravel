# George Washington's Mount Vernon — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult (12+) daytime General Admission ticket to Mount Vernon (listed ~$30) for one military visitor.

## Headline finding

Yes — Mount Vernon, the historic Virginia estate of George Washington (mountvernon.org), gives military members (and teachers) a small everyday discount of **$4 off daytime General Admission**, redeemed by verifying with **ID.me**. But the everyday discount is not the best deal for most service members. **Purple Heart recipients get in FREE year-round** (no ID.me, just self-identify at the ticket window), and on **Veterans Day (November 11) admission is FREE for all active-duty, former, and retired military** (member only — not family/guests). For anyone visiting twice or more in a year, a Mount Vernon **membership is $20 off** for military and pays for itself. Archetype: **single-venue attraction / non-profit historic site** — no GovX, no cashback portal, no exchange or pro-deal channel applies; savings come from timing the visit and from the site's own tiered military/veteran offers.

> Note on a source discrepancy: ID.me's own Mount Vernon landing page shows an inconsistent headline ("$3 Off") vs. body ("save $4 on general admission") and a stale "$14 tickets" figure that does not match Mount Vernon's current listed admission. Mount Vernon's own `id-me-discounts` / admission-discounts pages describe the everyday benefit as **$4 off**; that is the figure carried here, flagged Medium confidence pending a clean re-fetch of the brand page (see Open Questions).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The everyday ID.me discount is limited to **one ticket per ID** — it is a per-person gate discount, not a code you can multiply.
2. The **Purple Heart** free ticket and **Veterans Day** free ticket cover **daytime General Admission only** — they do **not** apply to separately ticketed special events, specialty tours, or the sightseeing cruise; those are paid separately.
3. The **Veterans Day** free ticket covers the **service member only** — "Free tickets do not extend to service members' family members or guests." Family/guests pay standard admission (or use their own ID.me discount if eligible).
4. Free-admission offers and the everyday $4 ID.me discount are alternatives, not stackable — you're already at $0 on a free day.
5. A **membership** replaces per-visit tickets rather than stacking with them; the $20 military membership discount is a one-time reduction on the membership price, which then includes a year of free daytime admission.

### Who Qualifies

- **Veterans / former & retired military** — eligible for the everyday $4 ID.me discount and Veterans Day free admission.
- **Military dependents (12+)** — eligible for the everyday $4 ID.me discount; **not** covered by the Veterans Day free-ticket offer (member only).

- **Teachers** — eligible for the same $4 ID.me discount (non-military; noted for completeness).

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Everyday $4 discount (ID.me):**
1. Go to Mount Vernon's ID.me discount / ticketing page.
2. Verify your military (or teacher) status through ID.me.
3. Apply the single-use code to your daytime General Admission ticket (one ticket per ID).

**Purple Heart free admission:**
1. Go to the ticket window at Mount Vernon.
2. Tell staff you are a Purple Heart recipient — no additional proof is requested.
3. Receive a free daytime admission ticket (excludes separately ticketed events).

**Veterans Day (Nov 11) free admission:**
1. Arrive on Veterans Day (9 a.m.–4 p.m.).
2. Pick up your free ticket on arrival (active-duty, former, or retired military).

### How Verification Works
The everyday military/teacher discount uses **ID.me**. You create or sign in to an ID.me account, verify your status once, and receive a single-use discount code ("one ticket per ID"). The **Purple Heart** and **Veterans Day** free-admission benefits are handled **in person at the ticket window** by self-identification — Mount Vernon states no additional proof is requested for Purple Heart recipients. Do not assume the ID.me verification is permanent or transferable; it is per-person and the estate controls the terms.

### FAQ

#### Does Mount Vernon offer a military discount?
Yes. Military members (and teachers) get **$4 off daytime General Admission** via ID.me. Purple Heart recipients get in free year-round, and all active-duty/former/retired military get in free on Veterans Day (Nov 11).

#### How much is the Mount Vernon military discount?
**$4 off** daytime General Admission for the everyday discount. Purple Heart recipients and eligible military on Veterans Day pay **$0**.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans and dependents 12+ qualify for the everyday $4 ID.me discount. The **Veterans Day** free ticket, however, covers the **service member only** — family and guests are not included.

#### How do I verify my military status?
For the everyday $4 discount, verify through **ID.me** and apply the single-use code (one ticket per ID). For Purple Heart or Veterans Day free admission, simply identify yourself at the ticket window.

#### Does Mount Vernon use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Mount Vernon uses **ID.me** for its everyday military and teacher discount. It does not use GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for admission. The Purple Heart and Veterans Day benefits are verified in person.

#### Can I use it online or only in person?
The ID.me discount can be applied to ticketing; the Purple Heart and Veterans Day free tickets are handled **in person** at the estate's ticket window.

#### What's excluded?
Free-admission offers (Purple Heart, Veterans Day) cover **daytime General Admission only** — separately ticketed special events, specialty tours, and cruises are excluded. The everyday discount is limited to one ticket per ID.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or membership?
No stacking: the $4 discount, free-day offers, and membership are alternatives. Coupon-site "$20 off" codes are not real ticket codes — the only real $20 is the **membership** discount.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit?
If you're a Purple Heart recipient, it's always free. Otherwise, visit on **Veterans Day (Nov 11)** for free admission, use the **ID.me $4 off** on any other day, or buy the **$20-off membership** if you'll return within the year.

#### Does Mount Vernon offer a teacher, first responder, or student discount?
Teachers get the same **$4 off via ID.me**. No dedicated first-responder or student admission discount was found on primary sources.

#### Does Mount Vernon run a Veterans Day free-admission day?
Yes — recurring annually. In 2026 it's **November 11, 9 a.m.–4 p.m.**, free for active-duty, former, and retired military (member only, not family).
