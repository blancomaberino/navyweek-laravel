# discovery+ — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 12 months of discovery+ (Ad‑Lite) — $5.99/mo × 12 = **$71.88/yr** at standard monthly pricing.

## Headline finding

**discovery+ no longer offers a military discount to new subscribers.** The program — which was 40% off the Ad‑Lite plan ($4.99 → $2.99/mo), verified through **SheerID** — was closed to new sign‑ups on **June 1, 2023**. Existing military subscribers are grandfathered and keep their discounted rate only as long as they stay continuously subscribed and remain eligible; cancel once and it cannot be reinstated. This is a *discontinued-discount* brand (archetype F that lapsed into B): there is no first‑party military route today, no GovX/exchange path (it's a streaming subscription), and streaming subs generally aren't on cashback portals. The best play for a service member now is the **annual plan** ($59.99/yr Ad‑Lite ≈ $5.00/mo, ~2 months free vs. monthly) plus the free 7‑day trial and any **carrier bundle** (e.g., Verizon +play has historically included discovery+). If the goal is simply "a streaming service with a real military discount," cross‑shop **Paramount+ (50% off any plan for life via SheerID)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The historical 40% military rate applied to Ad‑Lite only** and could **not** be combined with other promotions or applied to the Ad‑Free tier (per terms + editorial). Moot for new subs.
2. **Annual pricing and the free trial are mutually exclusive** — annual is a paid up‑front commitment; the trial is a separate new‑subscriber path.
3. **discovery+ is a direct subscription** — no GovX marketplace, no military exchange SKU, and no reliable cashback‑portal rate for the subscription itself, so there is nothing to "stack" a portal on top of.
4. **Carrier bundles are substitutes, not stacks** — a Verizon/T‑Mobile‑type perk *replaces* paying discovery+ directly; you don't layer it on the annual plan.
5. **Max is a substitute channel** — most discovery+ library content is available inside Max; paying both is redundant for many viewers.

### FAQ
#### Does discovery+ offer a military discount?
No. discovery+ closed its military discount to new subscribers on June 1, 2023. Only members who enrolled before that date keep a discounted rate.

#### How much was the discovery+ military discount?
It was 40% off the Ad‑Lite plan — $4.99/month down to $2.99/month — verified through SheerID. That rate is no longer available to new subscribers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Not for new sign‑ups. Historically the offer covered active duty, veterans, reservists, and active‑duty spouses (US and Canadian Armed Forces). Today none of these groups can newly enroll.

#### How do I verify my military status with discovery+?
You can't currently — there's no active verification flow. The old program used SheerID (name, email, date of birth, military status, branch).

#### Does discovery+ use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
It used **SheerID** for the now‑discontinued military discount. It has no GovX storefront and no ID.me or WeSalute military checkout.

#### Can I still get the $2.99 military price I saw online?
No. Coupon sites still list it, but that's the pre‑June‑2023 offer. New subscribers pay standard pricing ($5.99 Ad‑Lite / $9.99 Ad‑Free).

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to get discovery+?
The annual Ad‑Lite plan ($59.99/year, ~$5/month), or a free carrier bundle if your wireless plan includes discovery+. Use the 7‑day free trial for short‑term viewing.

#### Does discovery+ offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No current first‑responder, teacher, or nurse program is published. A student discount has been reported historically but is not confirmed on an official page as of this review.

#### Which streaming services still give a military discount?
Paramount+ offers verified military 50% off any plan for life (via SheerID); Peacock and Disney+ (through the military Exchange) also run military offers. These are the strongest current streaming options for service members.
