# Béis — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $150 Béis order (e.g., the Weekender or a mid-size roller — the free-shipping threshold and a realistic single-bag purchase).

## Headline finding

**Béis (BÉIS Travel) publishes no military or veteran discount, and there is no SheerID, ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute military offer for the brand.** A widely-shared SheerID "Active Military & Veterans — 20% off Adventure, Travel & Everyday Carries" offer is for **Osprey, not Béis** — don't confuse the two.

The genuine best path for a service member is the same as for everyone: the **Béis mobile app's 20%-off exclusive**, or the **15% off your first order** for email/SMS signup, layered on top of Béis's periodic public sales (holiday events, warehouse/last-call markdowns). Béis is also on **ID.me as cashback-for-everyone** — up to ~5% for new customers, ~3% existing (a rebate, not a discount, "as of July 15, 2026"). Archetype: **DTC brand with no military discount**, where the app/email/sale stack is the real savings engine. Ignore aggregator "Béis military discount" pages — the underlying finding is that no military-specific Béis program exists.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **No military discount exists to stack** — the base case is full retail, so every saving here is an alternative to plain retail, not a military stack.
2. **App 20% and email 15% are one-offer-per-order** — they don't combine with each other, and a single promo field at checkout means you pick one code.
3. **Sale items generally exclude additional codes** — during a public sale you usually can't also apply the 20%/15%; take whichever total is lower.
4. **ID.me cashback is a separate rebate layer** — it's paid by ID.me after purchase and can sit on top of an on-site code, but it's "up to" and not guaranteed at the max rate.
5. **Free shipping ($150+) is automatic** and independent of any code.

#### Does Béis offer a military discount?
No. Béis has no military or veteran discount and no military verification partner. The best savings — the app's 20% and a 15% first-order email offer — are open to everyone.

#### How much is the Béis military discount?
There isn't one. The closest everyday saving is the Béis app's 20% exclusive, available to all shoppers regardless of military status.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for anything special?
No Béis military tier exists for any of these groups. They can use the same public app/email/sale offers as everyone else.

#### How do I verify my military status with Béis?
You can't — there's no military program to verify for. The only ID.me link is a cashback offer open to all members, not a military discount.

#### Can I use these offers in stores or only online?

#### What is excluded?
There's no military code to exclude. The app 20% and email 15% are one-per-order and usually excluded on sale items.

#### Can I combine offers or sale items?
Generally no — you pick one code per order, and sale items usually exclude additional codes. ID.me cashback can sit on top as a separate rebate.
