# Monterey Bay Aquarium — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adult admissions (2 × $65 = $130) — the everyday unit a visiting couple compares.

## Headline finding

**Monterey Bay Aquarium does not offer a military discount — and this is unusually well-documented from primary sources.** The Aquarium's own ticketing and FAQ pages list no military rate, the Aquarium's own Navy MWR / ITT ticket office (Navy Life SW, Monterey) explicitly states *"Monterey Bay Aquarium tickets are not available through our office. The Aquarium does not offer a military discount and does not conduct in-person ticket sales,"* and Military.com's dedicated pages now read *"Monterey Bay Aquarium does not offer a military discount at this time"* and *"…does not offer a Veterans Day discount at this time."* There is also **no in-person ticket counter** — tickets are sold **online only**, so the old "show your military ID at the ticket booth for $10 off" claim is not just discontinued, it describes a purchase channel that no longer exists.

The best paths that DO save: **Museums for All** gives **free admission for up to four people** to valid **SNAP EBT / CalFresh (EBT) cardholders** — the single biggest real saving. A **membership** (free admission for a year plus member perks) wins for anyone planning two or more visits. Standard prices are **$65 adult, $50 youth (5–17), $50 senior (70+)**. Archetype: **attraction with no military discount and a hard "buy only on our site" anti-scam posture** — the important consumer-protection angle is steering readers away from fraudulent third-party "discount" tickets. Debunk the stale "$10 off adult / $5 off child at the ticket counter" claim outright.

### FAQ

#### Does Monterey Bay Aquarium offer a military discount?
No. The Aquarium's own pages, the Navy's ITT ticket office, and Military.com all confirm there is no military discount, and there's no in-person ticket counter where one could be applied.

#### Is there a $10-off military rate at the ticket booth?
No — that's outdated. The Aquarium sells tickets online only (there is no ticket booth), and both the Aquarium's Navy MWR office and Military.com state there's no military discount. Don't rely on the "$10 off adult / $5 off child" claim.

#### Do veterans, active duty, or dependents qualify for anything?
Not for a military discount. If a military family holds a SNAP EBT / CalFresh card, they qualify for free admission (up to four people) via Museums for All — the same as any eligible cardholder.

#### How do I verify my military status?
You can't — there's no military program to verify for. The only status benefit, Museums for All, is verified with a valid SNAP EBT / CalFresh card.

#### Does the Aquarium use GovX, ID.me, or a base ITT office for discounted tickets?
No. The Navy's Monterey ITT office explicitly does not sell Aquarium tickets, and there's no GovX or ID.me military ticket program. Buy on the official site only.

#### Can I get discounted tickets anywhere else?
Only through Museums for All (SNAP/EBT) or by buying a membership for repeat visits. The Aquarium warns against third-party "discount" ticket sellers as scams.

#### What are the standard ticket prices?
Adult $65, youth (5–17) $50, senior (70+) $50; children under 5 are free. Advance purchase is recommended.

#### Does the Aquarium run a Veterans Day free or discounted admission?
No — Military.com confirms it "does not offer a Veterans Day discount at this time."

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit?
If you have SNAP EBT / CalFresh, use Museums for All (free for up to four). If you'll visit twice or more, buy a membership. Otherwise, buy standard advance tickets on the official site — there's no military rate to chase.

#### Does Monterey Bay Aquarium offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No walk-up discount for those groups. The Aquarium does run free school field-trip programs (arranged through schools), but not individual professional discounts.
