# Deer Valley Resort Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Deer Valley Resort (Park City, Utah) offers a **resort-run military discount on lift tickets and season passes** — an **approximate 25% discount throughout the ski season**, with single-day military lift tickets advertised at **$189** for the 25/26 winter. Eligible groups are **active duty, reserve, honorably discharged, and retired U.S. military personnel, plus their spouse and dependents (23 and under)**. Status is verified through **SheerID** at online checkout, but every military ticket/pass is **non-transferable and requires in-person ID verification at a Deer Valley ticket window before it can be issued** — you cannot print military tickets at a kiosk, even if you buy online. Separately, the **Grand Hyatt Deer Valley "Salute to Service" program** (a partner hotel program, not the resort discount) lets service-member *lodging guests* buy tickets at the property for **up to 75% off the window rate** along with lodging, spa, and food/beverage discounts. The single most important caveat: **the 25/26 winter season has closed** as of access date, and exact 26/27 pricing is not yet published — treat the $189 figure as the most recent season's rate and the 25% as the recurring evergreen mechanic until the resort posts new-season numbers.

> Accuracy note: Coupon/aggregator pages (e.g., budgetseniors.com "20 Deer Valley military discounts," mymilitarybenefits.com) circulate inflated or vague claims. Only the two figures Deer Valley itself publishes — **"approximate 25%"** (press release) and **"$189"** military lift ticket (military page) — are treated as fact here. The 75% figure belongs to the Grand Hyatt hotel program, not to Deer Valley's own ticket discount, and applies only to guests staying at that property.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Deer Valley's military tickets & passes page and choose **Buy Lift Tickets** or **Buy Season Pass**.
2. Select your day(s) and add the military product to your cart.
3. **Apply the discount in cart at checkout** and complete **SheerID** verification of your military status.
4. Complete your purchase online.
5. **Before skiing, visit a Deer Valley ticket window** and present a current, valid military photo ID for each pass holder so the ticket/pass can be issued. (Military tickets cannot be printed at a kiosk.)

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Call or visit the Deer Valley Ticket Office (**435-645-6626**; the Nov 2025 press release also lists 435-645-6625).
2. For honorably discharged veterans buying a Military Season Pass, complete **SheerID** verification when prompted.
3. Go to a **Deer Valley ticket window** in person.
4. Present a **current, valid military photo ID** for each pass holder.
5. The window issues the ticket/pass directly to the verified, eligible guest (it cannot be picked up by anyone else).

### How Verification Works
Deer Valley uses **SheerID** to confirm military status during online checkout, and honorably discharged veterans are explicitly told SheerID verification is required before a Military Season Pass is issued. SheerID alone is not sufficient at the mountain: every military ticket and pass must be **issued in person at a Deer Valley ticket window**, where each pass holder shows a current, valid military photo ID. Tickets and passes are **non-transferable** and can only be issued to the verified eligible guest — someone else cannot pick them up. For questions or to purchase by phone, the Deer Valley Ticket Office is reachable at **435-645-6626**.

### FAQ

#### Does Deer Valley Resort offer a military discount?
Yes. Deer Valley offers an approximate 25% military discount on lift tickets and season passes, with military single-day lift tickets advertised at $189 for the 25/26 season. Eligibility is verified through SheerID, and tickets are issued in person at a ticket window.

#### How much is the Deer Valley military discount?
Deer Valley's press release describes it as an "approximate 25% discount" on lift tickets and passes throughout the season, and its military page advertises a $189 military single-day lift ticket (25/26). Exact next-season pricing isn't published yet — confirm on Deer Valley's official military page.

#### Who qualifies for the Deer Valley military discount?
Active-duty, reserve, honorably discharged, and retired U.S. military personnel, plus their spouse and dependents 23 and under. National Guard is named in the separate Grand Hyatt hotel program but not separately on Deer Valley's ticket page — confirm with the Ticket Office.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Online, you verify through SheerID and apply the discount in cart at checkout. Honorably discharged veterans should call the Ticket Office (435-645-6626) and complete SheerID verification. At the mountain, each pass holder must show a current, valid military photo ID at a ticket window.

#### Does Deer Valley use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Deer Valley's official military page specifies **SheerID** for online verification. In-person military photo ID is also required at the ticket window.

#### Can I print my military lift ticket at a kiosk?
No. Military lift tickets require in-person ID verification at a Deer Valley ticket window before they can be issued. Even if you buy online, you must stop at a ticket window.

#### Can someone else pick up my military tickets?
No. Tickets and passes are non-transferable and must be issued directly to the verified eligible guest, who presents valid ID at the window.

#### What's the difference between the Deer Valley discount and the Grand Hyatt "Salute to Service" deal?
Deer Valley's own discount (~25%) is on lift tickets and passes for eligible military and is open to all eligible service members. Grand Hyatt Deer Valley's "Salute to Service" is a separate hotel program: guests staying at that property can buy tickets up to 75% off the window rate and get lodging, spa, and food/beverage discounts.

#### Does Deer Valley offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not stated in Deer Valley's official source. The published discount covers U.S. military personnel and their immediate family; other groups are not listed.
