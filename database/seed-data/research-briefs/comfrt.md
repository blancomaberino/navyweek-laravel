# Comfrt — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two **Comfrt Minimalist Hoodies** (Comfrt's flagship oversized heavyweight hoodie) — listed at **$120 each ($240 "MSRP")** but selling at the standing sale price of **$39 each ($78)** on comfrt.com. All figures pre-tax.

## Headline finding

Comfrt (comfrt.com — the mental-health / comfort apparel brand) runs a **real, first-party discount of "15% or more off" verified through ID.me** — stated on Comfrt's own ID.me program page. It covers a wide set: **military (active duty, veterans, reservists, and their spouses/dependents), first responders, government employees, medical providers, nurses, students, and teachers**, in **both the U.S. and Canada**. Verify once with a free ID.me account and the discount applies automatically at checkout — no code. So "yes, there's a genuine military discount" is the honest headline answer.

The catch — and it's the whole story — is that **the ID.me discount "cannot be combined with sale pricing,"** and Comfrt is a **perpetual-sale, anchor-priced brand**: the flagship Minimalist Hoodie shows a **$120 list struck through to $39** (≈68% off) as its everyday price, and the site banner reads "Up to 70% Off" at all times. Because nearly every item is already sale-priced, the ID.me 15% has almost nothing to attach to — and even applied to the $120 "full price" ($102), it loses badly to the $39 public sale. **The public sale is the real deal, and the ID.me discount usually can't beat it.** The one everyday layer that *does* work on sale orders is **ComfrtClub loyalty credit-back** (free Insider = 5% back; VIP $9/yr = 15% back + $10 signup + $10 birthday) — but ComfrtClub credits also can't stack with the ID.me discount, so it's one or the other. Archetype: **C — real discount but the sale beats it**, with a strong **H — anchor-priced "myth" pricing where the everyday value is the standing sale plus the loyalty wallet**. It is **ID.me**, not SheerID or GovX — correct that record.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **ID.me discount is strictly non-combinable** — Comfrt states it "cannot be combined with other promotional offers, coupon codes, or **sale pricing**," nor previous purchases, nor gift-card purchases. It is auto-applied (no code) on *eligible full-price* items only.
2. **Sale pricing wins by default** — because the ID.me discount excludes sale items and Comfrt's everyday prices are sale prices (anchor $120 → $39), the public sale is almost always lower than any 15% path. You do not stack them; you take whichever is lower (nearly always the sale).
3. **ComfrtClub credits ≠ ID.me** — "ComfrtClub credits cannot be stacked in tandem with ID.me discounts." Pick one per order.
4. **ComfrtClub credit-back *does* work on sale orders** — it's a loyalty credit on the amount paid, not a coupon, so (unlike ID.me and welcome codes) it isn't blocked by sale pricing. That's why it's the one meaningful everyday layer for members. Credits are **store credit toward a future order**, not instant cash off the current one.
5. **Welcome codes (email 15% / SMS 10%)** are public promo codes and fall under the same "not combinable with sale pricing" exclusion — they won't stack on already-discounted items.
6. **Exclusions:** gift cards, bundles, pre-orders, shipping, and taxes are out for the ID.me discount; select products/collections may be excluded (noted on the product page).

#### Does Comfrt offer a military discount?
Yes. Comfrt offers "**15% or more off**" verified through **ID.me** for active-duty military, veterans, reservists, and their spouses/dependents (plus first responders, government employees, medical providers, nurses, students, and teachers), in the U.S. and Canada.

"**15% or more**," per Comfrt's own ID.me page. But it **excludes sale pricing**, and since Comfrt runs standing deep sales (hoodies list $120, sell ~$39), the public sale usually beats it. Coupon sites advertising "75% off military codes" are conflating the everyday sale, not a real code.

#### Do veterans, reservists, spouses, and dependents qualify?

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?

Gift cards, bundles, pre-orders, shipping, and taxes; previous purchases; and — most importantly — **sale-priced items**. Select products/collections may also be excluded (noted on the product page).

#### Does Comfrt offer a first responder, medical, nurse, teacher, or student discount?

#### Does Comfrt run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
