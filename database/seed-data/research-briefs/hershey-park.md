# Hersheypark Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Hersheypark (theme park, Hershey, PA) does **not publish a fixed military discount percentage** on its own site. Its official FAQ answer to "Do you offer a military discount?" thanks service members and directs them to the park's **online ticket offer** ("usually the best available price") and notes that **additional offers may be available seasonally on ID.me**. So the honest headline is: **no standing published % from Hersheypark directly** — the real military savings come through **verification providers and base ticket offices**, not a hersheypark.com code.

Where the actual value is: **GovX** (a military/first-responder verification marketplace) sells Hersheypark tickets year-round at meaningful savings (aggregators cite up to ~40% off); **WeSalute** lists a Hersheypark military offer (aggregators cite up to ~25% off, sometimes with a ZooAmerica perk); and **U.S. military base ticket offices** (ITT/ITR/MWR) sell discounted Hersheypark admission. Historically, Hersheypark has run a **Military Appreciation Weekend in May** giving **40% off an adult single-day admission plus free parking** with an Active/Retired DoD/Civilian Military ID — a limited-time event, labeled historical below.

The single most important caveats: (1) any specific percentage you see is from **third-party providers or historical events**, not a standing hersheypark.com figure — verify the live GovX/WeSalute/ID.me price before quoting it; (2) online, Hersheypark's own **advance-purchase ticket price is frequently the best deal for anyone** (the park says so), so a service member should always compare the military channel against the plain online price; and (3) the seasonal ID.me offer and the May Military Appreciation Weekend are **not guaranteed to be live** at any given time — check the FAQ/tickets page for the current window.

> Accuracy note: Coupon aggregators headline "up to 40% off Hersheypark military," "$20 off," and "up to 25%." Hersheypark's **own** page publishes **no percentage** — it points to the online ticket price and seasonal ID.me offers. The "40% off + free parking" figure is a **historical Military Appreciation Weekend** (May) event; the "up to 40%" and "up to 25%" year-round figures are **GovX** and **WeSalute** third-party channels. Always verify the live provider price; do not present any single percentage as Hersheypark's own standing rate.

### FAQ

#### Does Hersheypark offer a military discount?
Not as a published percentage on its own site. Hersheypark's FAQ says its **online ticket price** is usually the best deal and that **additional military offers may appear seasonally on ID.me**. The reliable year-round military savings come through **GovX, WeSalute, and military base ticket offices**.

#### How much is the Hersheypark military discount?
Hersheypark publishes no set figure. Third-party channels vary — aggregators cite **up to ~40% off via GovX** and **up to ~25% via WeSalute** (verify the live price). The historical **May Military Appreciation Weekend** gave **40% off a single-day adult ticket + free parking**.

#### Who qualifies and how do I verify?
Active and retired military (and, via GovX/WeSalute, the broader military/first-responder/veteran community). Verify through **ID.me** (seasonal, on hersheypark.com), **GovX**, or **WeSalute**, or show a **Military ID at a base ticket office**.

#### Is buying through GovX or WeSalute better than Hersheypark's own price?
Sometimes — but not always. Hersheypark says its **advance online ticket** is usually the best general price, so **compare** the military-channel price against the plain online price before buying.

#### Does Hersheypark use ID.me?
Yes, for **seasonal** military offers on hersheypark.com. Year-round discounts run through **GovX** and **WeSalute** instead.

#### When is the Hersheypark Military Appreciation Weekend?
Historically a weekend in **May**, with 40% off a single-day adult ticket and free parking for DoD/Civilian Military ID holders. Confirm the current-year dates on Hersheypark's FAQ/tickets page.

#### Can I get discounted Hersheypark tickets on base?
Yes — U.S. military base ticket offices (ITT/ITR/MWR) sell discounted Hersheypark admission through the Military Ticket Program.
