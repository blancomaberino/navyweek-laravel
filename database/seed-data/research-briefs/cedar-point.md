# Cedar Point — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult single-day Cedar Point admission (regular gate price **$105**; public online advance price **~$52** as of July 8, 2026).

## Headline finding

Cedar Point runs a **genuine, year-round military and first-responder ticket discount**: verified members pay **$49** for single-day Cedar Point admission (vs. the $105 gate price), and can buy **up to 6 tickets** for family and friends. Verification is through **ID.me**, and the discounted tickets are **online-only** and **cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales**. The single best deal of the year, though, is not this everyday price — it is **free single-day admission for active and retired military on Memorial Day weekend (May 22–25, 2026)** with a valid U.S. Military ID at the gate.

Two honest caveats drive this page. First, **the $49 military price barely beats the ~$52 public online advance price** — the discount's real edge is the ability to lock in $49 for up to six people and the free Memorial Day window, not a dramatic single-ticket saving. Second, the corporate owner changed: **Cedar Fair merged into Six Flags**, so `cedarpoint.com` now 301-redirects to `sixflags.com/cedarpoint` (verified July 8, 2026) — the offer is intact but lives on the Six Flags domain.

**Archetype:** primarily **C (real discount, but the public online price nearly matches it)**, with a strong **military-holiday calendar** (free Memorial Day admission) and **multiple parallel verification marketplaces** (ID.me direct, GOVX, WeSalute, base ITT/MWR) — closest to a theme-park variant of C + I calendar behavior.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The direct military/veteran/first-responder ticket discount cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales** (Cedar Point military-discounts terms). There is no stackable "military promo code."
2. **GOVX and WeSalute are separate marketplaces, not stacks.** You buy through them instead of the Cedar Point site; you cannot add the ID.me $49 price on top, and each sets its own prices, fees, and (strict) return terms. WeSalute sales are **final**.
3. **Free Memorial Day admission covers the verified military member only** (active/retired, valid U.S. Military ID at the gate). Friends' and family's tickets are **separate paid online purchases in advance** — they are not free.
4. **The resort hotel discount cannot be combined with any other discount, package, or offer**, is limited to select dates and a limited number of units, and requires valid Military or First Responder ID at check-in.
5. **Cross-park exclusion:** a discounted **Cedar Point** ticket is **not valid at Cedar Point Shores** waterpark, and vice versa — they are separate tickets.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty U.S. military** (all branches) — eligible; free Memorial Day admission with military ID.
- **Retired U.S. military** — eligible; free Memorial Day admission with military ID.
- **Veterans** — eligible for the year-round $49 discount via ID.me. (The *free* Memorial Day admission is stated for "active and retired members" with a valid Military ID at the gate.)
- **Reserves / National Guard** — eligible for the year-round discount via ID.me.
- **First responders** — eligible for the same discounted ticket price via ID.me.
- **Immediate family / friends** — the verified member can buy up to 6 discounted tickets online for them.
- **Teachers / nurses / students / general government** — no Cedar Point-specific offer confirmed here for these groups (nurses have a separate WeSalute route); do not assume eligibility.

### How To Redeem Online (year-round $49 discount)
1. Create or sign in to a free **ID.me** account.
2. Go to `sixflags.com/cedarpoint/military-discounts` and select your ticket(s) — Cedar Point single-day or Cedar Point Shores.
3. Complete the **ID.me** verification prompt that appears after you select your product(s).
4. Add up to **6 tickets** for family and friends and check out online (plus applicable taxes & fees).

### How To Redeem In Store / At the Gate (free Memorial Day admission)
1. Confirm you're active or retired U.S. military.
2. Visit any one day **May 22–25, 2026**.
3. Present your **valid U.S. Military ID at the front-gate turnstiles** for free single-day admission.
4. Buy any friends'/family tickets **online in advance** — the free admission covers the military member only.

### How Verification Works
Cedar Point uses **ID.me** for its online military and first-responder discount. Set up a free ID.me account, and after you choose your tickets you'll get an ID.me login prompt to confirm your status. Your ID.me verification is reusable across many retailers. For the **free Memorial Day admission**, no online verification is needed — you show a valid **U.S. Military ID at the gate**. GOVX (via a GovX ID) and WeSalute+ (paid membership) are separate marketplaces with their own verification.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Discounted tickets are **valid only on the date selected** and are **online only**.
- A discounted **Cedar Point** ticket is **not valid at Cedar Point Shores**; a Cedar Point Shores ticket is **not valid at Cedar Point**.

- All prices are **plus applicable taxes & fees**.
- **Free Memorial Day admission covers the military member only**; guests' tickets are purchased separately online.

### FAQ

#### Does Cedar Point offer a military discount?
Yes. Cedar Point offers a year-round discount for military (active and retired), veterans, reserves, National Guard, and first responders: a **$49** single-day ticket (the 2026 gate price is $105), verified through **ID.me** and purchased online. Active and retired military also get **free single-day admission on Memorial Day weekend (May 22–25, 2026)** with a valid U.S. Military ID at the gate.

#### How much is the Cedar Point military discount?
The verified military/first-responder single-day Cedar Point ticket is **$49** (plus taxes & fees), versus the $105 gate price. Cedar Point Shores waterpark tickets start **from $45**. On Memorial Day weekend, admission is **free** for active and retired military.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify for the year-round **$49** discount via ID.me. Immediate family and friends aren't verified themselves, but the verified member can buy **up to 6 discounted tickets** for them online. Note the *free* Memorial Day admission is stated for **active and retired military** members (military ID at the gate); guests pay for their tickets.

#### How do I verify my military status?
For the online $49 discount, you verify through **ID.me** — create a free account, and you'll get an ID.me prompt after selecting your tickets. For the free Memorial Day admission, you simply show a **valid U.S. Military ID at the front gate**; no online verification is required.

#### Does Cedar Point use ID.me, GOVX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Cedar Point's **own** online discount uses **ID.me**. Cedar Point tickets also appear on the **GOVX** marketplace (GovX ID verification) and through **WeSalute+** (paid membership, via Working Advantage) — but those are separate storefronts, not a discount you stack on the Cedar Point site.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or online?
The **$49 discount is online only** (via ID.me). The **free Memorial Day admission** is redeemed **in person at the gate** with a military ID. There is no walk-up register discount for the everyday price.

#### What is excluded?
The discount is valid only on the date selected, is online only, and **can't be combined with any other offer, including sales**. A Cedar Point ticket isn't valid at Cedar Point Shores (and vice versa). Prices are plus taxes and fees. The free Memorial Day admission covers the military member only.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale prices?
No. Cedar Point's terms state the military, veteran, and first-responder discount **cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales**. There's no stackable "military code," so ignore coupon-site codes claiming to layer on top.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Cedar Point tickets?
If you're active/retired military and can go **May 22–25, 2026**, it's **free** — that's the clear winner. Otherwise the **$49 ID.me** price is the reliable route, especially for a group of up to six. But compare it to the **public online advance price (~$52)**, which is nearly the same and open to your whole party; and if you have base access, price-check your **ITT/MWR office**.

#### Does Cedar Point offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
**First responders** get the same $49 discounted ticket via ID.me, and can access the resort hotel discount. **Nurses** have a separate offer via WeSalute. No Cedar Point-specific teacher, student, or general-government ticket discount is confirmed here — check ID.me for current group eligibility before assuming.

#### Does Cedar Point run a Veterans Day sale?
No. Cedar Point operates seasonally (roughly May through early November), so it's closed by Veterans Day. The brand's military moment is **Memorial Day weekend**, when active and retired military get in free.
