# UWorld — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$500 UWorld QBank subscription** (e.g. a USMLE Step 1 or NCLEX prep package).

## Headline finding

**UWorld has a modest but real military discount — 10% off new packages — but it is manual, email-verified, and stacks poorly against UWorld's own frequent public promo codes.** UWorld's terms (surfaced on its contact/support materials) require you to email **support@uworld.com** with a **DMDC SCRA Status Report or VIC card**, or use a **.mil email**, and it only applies to **new** purchases (not renewals, not retroactive). Critically, UWorld runs large public discount codes constantly — "uworld discount code" alone draws ~5,500 US searches/month, and seasonal codes routinely beat 10%. So for most buyers the honest answer is: **take the bigger public promo, not the 10% military code** — and never stack them (you can't). Archetype: **test-prep brand with a low-value, high-friction military discount overshadowed by its own aggressive public couponing.**

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military (including U.S. Army Reserve) — eligible
- Veterans of the U.S. Armed Services — eligible
- Renewals — not eligible (new purchases only)
- Spouses/dependents — not stated as eligible

### How To Redeem Online
1. Choose the new UWorld package you want (do not buy yet).
2. Email **support@uworld.com** with your DMDC SCRA Status Report or VIC card, or send from your **.mil** address, and name the product.
3. Wait for UWorld to validate and reply with a one-time 10% code.
4. Apply the code at checkout on uworld.com or the app.

### How Verification Works
UWorld verifies manually by email. Accepted proof: **DMDC SCRA Status Report, VIC card, or a .mil email address**. UWorld explicitly warns **not** to send your CAC or other military IDs (doing so violates military regulation and federal law). Allow 1–2 business days.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **New purchases only** — renewals excluded.
- **Not retroactive** — can't be applied to a past order.
- One code per order; not combinable with public promo codes.

### Best Ways To Save More
- Watch for **public seasonal promo codes** — they usually exceed 10%.
- Buy the **longest plan** you'll actually use to cut the effective monthly cost.
- Compare the live public code against the 10% military code before checkout — take the larger.
