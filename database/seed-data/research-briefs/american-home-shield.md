# American Home Shield — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ShieldGold™ home warranty at a representative **$60/month = $720/year first-year premium** (AHS pricing is quote-based and varies by ZIP, home size, and the $100 vs $125 service-call fee; ShieldGold typically runs $55–$70/mo per 2026 third-party reviews).

> **Trust & disclosure:** This is an independent editorial guide from NavyWeek. We are **not affiliated with American Home Shield** and have no partnership with the brand. AHS controls all discount terms and can change or withdraw them at any time. Every factual claim below traces to a primary source dated in the Sources list. Last reviewed July 13, 2026.

## Headline finding

American Home Shield runs a **real, official military discount: 20% off any home warranty plan in the first year for new members** (active duty, veterans, reservists, and National Guard, all branches), which then converts to **$5/month off at renewal** — verified against AHS's own pages and its June 2025 Business Wire announcement. It is **not stackable** with any other offer. The catch: AHS *also* runs an always-on public **"$150 off your first year"** new-customer offer available to everyone with no verification, which on a mid-priced plan (under ~$750/yr) slightly *beats* the 20% in year one. The military discount's real edge is the **ongoing $5/month renewal credit** — no public offer gives you anything after year one — so it wins over a 2+ year horizon and wins outright in year one on pricier ShieldPlatinum plans (where 20% > $150). AHS is a **discretionary-verification / self-run-code** archetype: it verifies via **military email at shopping.ahs.com/military**, NOT through ID.me or GovX (ID.me explicitly lists no AHS offer). Best move: on a plan you'll keep, take the military 20% for the renewal benefit; if you only want year one on a cheaper plan, the public $150-off is marginally better.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military discount cannot be combined with any other offer** and cannot be applied retroactively (AHS military page + Business Wire) — so you pick military *or* a public code, never both.
2. **AARP is the exception AHS names as combinable** — "can be combined with any other valid offer" (AHS discounts page) — but it is combinable with *public* offers, not with the military discount (which excludes all combinations).
3. **Military discount is new-members-only**; existing members can't add it. At renewal the 20% is replaced by the flat **$5/month** credit.
4. No verification-provider stacking exists because **AHS uses no third-party military portal** (no ID.me/GovX) — it's a self-run military-email flow.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **shopping.ahs.com/military**.
2. Enter your home ZIP code and get a plan quote (ShieldSilver, ShieldGold, or ShieldPlatinum).
3. **Verify your military status by submitting your military email** when prompted.
4. The 20% first-year discount applies to your new-member plan at checkout. (There is no in-store channel — AHS is sold online/by phone only.)

### How Verification Works
AHS confirms eligibility by having you **submit a military email address** through its military landing page. Unlike many retailers, AHS does **not** route this through ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute — ID.me's own shop page states it has no AHS offer. If you lack an active military email (common for veterans), contact AHS sales directly to ask what documentation (military ID / DD-214 / Veterans ID) they'll accept; AHS does not publish an alternate self-serve upload flow, so treat this step as verify-with-support rather than instant.

### FAQ

#### Does American Home Shield offer a military discount?
Yes. AHS offers **20% off any home warranty plan for the first year** to new members who are active-duty service members, veterans, reservists, or National Guard, then **$5/month off at renewal**.

#### How much is the American Home Shield military discount?
**20% off the first year**, converting to **$5 per month off** when the plan renews. On a $720/year ShieldGold plan that's about $144 off in year one plus roughly $60/year afterward.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Veterans qualify** (all branches). **Active duty, reservists, and National Guard qualify.** AHS does **not state** that spouses or dependents qualify — the eligibility is written around the service member/veteran homeowner, so don't assume family eligibility until AHS confirms it.

#### How do I verify my military status?
By **submitting your military email** at **shopping.ahs.com/military**. Veterans without an active military email should contact AHS to ask what documentation they accept.

#### Does American Home Shield use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** AHS runs its own military-email verification. ID.me's shop page explicitly states it has no AHS offer, so any site telling you to redeem via ID.me or GovX is wrong.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or online?
Online (and by phone) only — AHS has no retail stores. Redeem at shopping.ahs.com/military as a new member.

#### What is excluded?
New members only; not combinable with any other offer; not retroactive; single-family homes under 5,000 sq. ft.; coverage not available in all areas. Service-call fees and coverage caps still apply.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military discount **cannot be combined** with any other offer. The only AHS discount published as combinable is the **AARP** discount — and that combines with public offers, not with the military discount.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from American Home Shield?
If you'll keep the plan **2+ years** or you're on **ShieldPlatinum**, take the **military 20%** (the renewal credit and the larger absolute discount win). If you only care about **year one on a cheaper plan** and don't want to verify, the always-on **public "$150 off first year"** is marginally larger. Compare both live before checkout.

#### Does American Home Shield offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
AHS publishes a **military** discount and an **AARP** discount, plus multi-property and referral perks. It does **not** publish first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discounts as of this review.
