# Rivian (R1T / R1S / R2 electric trucks & SUVs) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new **2026 Rivian R1T** (a new R1 truck; Rivian sells at fixed, no-haggle prices in the roughly $70,000–$100,000 range depending on trim — exact MSRP changes by configuration, **verify live at rivian.com**).

## Headline finding

**Rivian does not publish any military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** Rivian's own Offers page (rivian.com/offers) lists financing APR deals, lease cash, and equipment/package discounts — **no military, veteran, first-responder, teacher, student, or nurse pricing appears anywhere on it**, and the same holds for the incentives/support pages. Rivian is a **direct-to-consumer, fixed-price ("no-haggle") automaker**: everyone pays the same posted price, so there is no dealer margin for a service-member discount to come out of. The genuinely best path for a military buyer is therefore the **same public levers available to everyone** — Rivian's current APR/lease offers and package discounts, plus the **referral program** (Rivian Rewards points + ~3 months free charging at no change to vehicle price), and, for the budget-minded, a **low-mileage demo vehicle**. Note the **federal $7,500 EV tax credit has expired** for vehicles acquired after Sept 30, 2025, so that is no longer a savings lever in 2026. Archetype: **no military discount — fixed-price direct-to-consumer; steer to public offers + referral + demo, and debunk aggregator "codes."**

### FAQ

#### Does Rivian offer a military discount?
No. Rivian publishes no military, veteran, or first-responder discount. It sells direct to consumers at a fixed price that's the same for everyone.

#### How much is the Rivian military discount?
There isn't one — $0. No percentage or dollar amount for military buyers appears on Rivian's Offers page.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a Rivian discount?
No military-specific pricing exists for any of these groups. They pay Rivian's standard price like all buyers.

#### How do I verify my military status with Rivian?
You can't, because there's no military program to verify for. Rivian does not use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX for a military discount.

#### Does Rivian use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. None of these verification providers is tied to a Rivian military discount, because Rivian offers no such discount.

#### Can I use a Rivian military discount in stores or online?
Neither — it doesn't exist. Rivian's public APR, lease, and package offers apply online at rivian.com.

#### What is excluded from Rivian's discounts?
Rivian's public offers (APR, lease cash, package discounts) are limited by vehicle configuration and term and change often. There's no military carve-out to be excluded from.

#### Can I combine a Rivian discount with promo codes or sale items?
Rivian doesn't issue checkout promo codes for its vehicles. A referral code (points + free charging) can be applied alongside current public offers, but it doesn't reduce the vehicle price.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Rivian?
Apply a free referral code, take whichever current APR/lease/package offer fits your build, and compare against a low-mileage demo vehicle — that's usually the lowest real price, since no military discount exists.

#### Does Rivian offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No. Rivian publishes no discount for first responders, teachers, nurses, or students.

#### Does Rivian run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Rivian has no record of a military-appreciation sale. Its incentives are general-market and rotate through the year, not tied to military holidays.
