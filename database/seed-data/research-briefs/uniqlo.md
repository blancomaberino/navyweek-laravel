## Headline finding

**UNIQLO has no military or veteran discount.** There is no military checkout on uniqlo.com, no military tier on its Membership Benefits or Coupons & Offers pages, and no UNIQLO military offer on ID.me Shop, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute. Aggregator sites advertising "**7% / 10% / 20% UNIQLO military discount**" and "**verify with ID.me**" are fabricated — none of it is confirmed by any primary source.

The honest best path is UNIQLO's own **membership coupon wallet** plus its **frequent Limited-Time Offers**. The single biggest standing coupon is the **UNIQLO App: $10 off a $75+ purchase** (~13% on a $75 cart). Because UNIQLO's rule is **one coupon per order — coupons don't combine** (verified on its FAQ), you pick one coupon, not several; the real stacking lever is layering a **cashback portal** (Rakuten ~2% as of July 10, 2026) under a *web* coupon, and buying items while they're on a Limited-Time / app-member price. Archetype: **B — no military route at all**, with a strong **H (myth brand)** flavor: the everyday value is the coupon/membership wallet, not a blanket military %. Do **not** assert any military %, code, or ID.me/SheerID/GovX verification for UNIQLO.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal terms)
1. **One coupon per order — coupons do not combine.** UNIQLO's own FAQ states only one coupon can be used per order or in-store purchase; you cannot layer the app, email, SMS, and birthday coupons. Pick the single best one for your cart.
2. **A coupon DOES apply on top of a Limited-Time / app-member sale price** (the sale is the item price, not a coupon), so the "one coupon" still lands on the marked-down subtotal — but the coupon's **minimum spend** is checked against the discounted subtotal.
3. **App coupon vs. web cashback are effectively mutually exclusive** — the $10-off-$75 coupon requires buying **in the app**, and cashback portals (Rakuten/Swagbucks) track **web browser** purchases, not in-app checkouts. To capture cashback, use a **web** coupon (email/SMS/birthday) instead.
4. **Portal cashback on a first-party UNIQLO coupon is generally portal-safe** — because the coupon is UNIQLO's own (not a third-party code pasted into the field), it usually doesn't void tracking the way a scraped "military code" would. Still verify the live rate; rates change weekly.
5. **No military/identity discount exists to stack** — there is nothing to combine a "military code" with, because UNIQLO publishes none.
