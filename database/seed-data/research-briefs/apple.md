# Apple Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Apple offers a **10% discount on Apple products and most accessories** to current and Veteran members of the US Military, National Guard, and Reserve, plus **immediate family members who reside in the same household**, through its **Veterans and Military Purchase Program** — a dedicated, year-round online storefront. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**; you verify once, then shop the special storefront. The single most important caveat: the discount is **online-only through the special storefront** — Apple states purchases made at an Apple Store location or the regular Apple Online Store are **not** eligible for this discounted pricing, and **Apple Card Monthly Installments (ACMI) cannot be used** as payment in the program storefront. The program is for **personal use only** (no resale; 18+).

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator/guide pages publish a detailed annual purchase cap (e.g., "up to 3 Macs, 3 iPads, 3 iPhones, 3 Apple Watches, 10 accessories per year"). Those specific quantities are **NOT stated on Apple's official program page or in the ID.me program terms** as of June 23, 2026. Apple's official terms state only that the program is "for personal use only," prohibits resale, and that "additional restrictions may apply." Treat the numeric per-item caps as **unverified** and do not assert them as Apple's published figures.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Apple's **Veterans and Military Purchase Program** page (apple.com/shop/browse/home/veterans_military).
2. Select **Verify with ID.me**.
3. Sign in to ID.me (or create a free account) and verify your military/Veteran status. Immediate family members in the same household verify their eligibility through the program flow as well.
4. Once verified, you're taken into Apple's special military storefront, where the **10%** discount is reflected in product pricing.
5. Add eligible products to your bag and check out. Note: **Apple Card Monthly Installments are not available** as a payment option in this storefront.

### How To Redeem In Store

To get the 10%, you must order through the verified Veterans and Military storefront online.
1. Verify with ID.me and enter the online storefront (see steps above).
2. Complete your purchase online through the storefront.
3. If you want hands-on help, you can still visit an Apple Store for setup/support, but the discount itself is applied through the online program, not at an in-store register.

Apple uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility for the Veterans and Military Purchase Program. After verifying, you shop the dedicated storefront where the 10% pricing appears. By submitting an order you certify that you've read the program terms and are an eligible person at least 18 years old. Apple does not publish on this page how often ID.me re-confirms status; ID.me may re-verify per its own policies. For help, Apple's page offers chat or 1‑800‑MY‑APPLE; ID.me issues route to ID.me support (help.id.me).

### FAQ

#### Does Apple offer a military discount?
Yes. Apple's Veterans and Military Purchase Program gives **10% off** Apple products and most accessories to current and Veteran members of the US Military, National Guard, and Reserve, plus immediate family members in the same household, verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the Apple military discount?
**10%** off Apple products and most accessories, per Apple's official program page.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans are explicitly eligible. **Immediate family members who reside in the same household** are also eligible, which covers qualifying spouses and dependents living with the service member or Veteran. Apple uses "immediate family in the same household" rather than naming spouses/dependents specifically.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify your military or Veteran status with **ID.me** from Apple's Veterans and Military Purchase Program page. You can use an existing ID.me account or create one during the process, then shop the special storefront where the discount pricing appears.

#### Does Apple use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Apple's **official** program uses **ID.me** for eligibility verification. (Apple also appears on third-party platforms like WeSalute, but Apple's own page specifies ID.me.)

#### Can I use the discount in an Apple Store?
No. The discount is **online-only** through the special storefront. Apple states that purchases at an Apple Store location or the regular Apple Online Store are not eligible for this discounted pricing.

#### What is excluded?
ZEISS Prescription Optical Inserts are excluded. Apple Card Monthly Installments can't be used as payment in the storefront. The program is personal-use only (no resale, 18+), and "additional restrictions may apply." Apple does not publish a full exclusion list.

#### Is there a limit on how much I can buy?
Apple's official terms say the program is for **personal use only** and prohibit resale, but they **do not publish specific annual quantity limits**. Numeric per-item caps cited on other sites are not confirmed on Apple's official page.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, carrier deals, or student pricing?
Apple does not state that the military discount stacks with other promotions, carrier offers, or education/student pricing. Treat it as non-stackable, and compare against Apple's Education Store or carrier deals separately.

#### Does Apple offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not under the military program. Apple has a separate Education Store, and ID.me lists separate Apple community offers (Responder, Nurse, Medical, Teacher, Government, Student, Alumni) distinct from the Veterans and Military Purchase Program.
