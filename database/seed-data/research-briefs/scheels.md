### FAQ

#### Does SCHEELS offer a military discount?
Yes. SCHEELS gives verified military shoppers 5% off their entire order, every day — not a one-time promo. It covers active-duty members, reserves and National Guard, retirees, veterans, and their dependents, verified for free through ID.me and tied to a MySCHEELS account. #### Is the SCHEELS military discount really 10% or 15%?
No. Several coupon aggregators advertise a 10%–15% "SCHEELS military discount," but SCHEELS' own program is 5% off your whole order.
