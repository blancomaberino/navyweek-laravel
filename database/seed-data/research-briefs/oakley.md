# Oakley Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Oakley runs **two separate, distinct military benefits**, and conflating them is the #1 accuracy trap on this topic:

1. **Oakley Standard Issue (OSI / "SI")** — the real military program. A free membership at **oakleysi.com** for verified U.S. military (active, veterans, retirees, National Guard, reservists), first responders (federal/local law enforcement, fire, EMS), and U.S. government personnel. Members shop a dedicated "Standard Issue" catalog at built-in **direct/member pricing** — Oakley SI prices run roughly 50% of Oakley.com retail — and the ID.me storefront advertises **"up to 50% off select styles, plus an extra 15% and free shipping."** The discount is **not** shown as a percentage off at checkout; it is baked into SI pricing. Membership is **lifetime for U.S. military (active/retired/veteran)** and **12-month renewable** for everyone else.

2. **Oakley.com consumer ID.me discount** — a separate **15% off** offer on the standard Oakley.com store, verified through **ID.me**. Per ID.me's own pages, this consumer offer currently lists **Nurses, Teachers, Medical Providers, and Hospital Employees** — **not** a "military" community. ID.me's Oakley military page explicitly states it is "not aware of Oakley offering Military community discounts" on the main Oakley.com store. So military shoppers wanting the deepest savings should use **Oakley SI**, not the Oakley.com 15% route.

**Single most important caveat:** A military member does **not** get a 15% military discount on Oakley.com via ID.me the way nurses/teachers do — their channel is Oakley Standard Issue (oakleysi.com), where pricing is already reduced. Veterans, retirees, and Guard/Reserve **are** eligible for OSI per Oakley's official qualifications page. Spouses/dependents are **not listed on Oakley's official OSI qualifications page** (aggregators claim "immediate family" — treat as unverified and flag).

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator and even Military.com pages headline "Oakley offers up to 50% military discount." That figure traces to **Oakley SI member pricing**, not a coupon code, and the "up to 50%" applies to **select styles** only. Don't present it as a flat, sitewide, automatically-applied percentage.

### FAQ

#### Does Oakley offer a military discount?
Yes — through **Oakley Standard Issue (OSI)**, a free membership at oakleysi.com for verified U.S. military, first responders, and government personnel, with built-in member/direct pricing ("up to 50% off select styles plus an extra 15% and free shipping"). The standard Oakley.com store's 15% ID.me offer is listed for nurses, teachers, and medical workers — not military — so service members should use OSI.

#### How much is the Oakley military discount?
There's no single percentage. Oakley SI prices are already discounted (roughly half of Oakley.com retail on select styles), and the program advertises "up to 50% off select styles, plus an extra 15% and free shipping." The savings are built into SI pricing, not shown as a code at checkout.

#### What's the difference between Oakley Standard Issue and the Oakley.com discount?
**Oakley Standard Issue** is a separate website (oakleysi.com) and free membership with deep, built-in pricing for military/responders/government. The **Oakley.com 15% ID.me discount** is on the regular consumer store and is currently for nurses, teachers, medical providers, and hospital employees — not military.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and Guard/Reserve qualify?
Yes. Oakley's official OSI qualifications page lists active duty, **veterans**, **retirees**, **National Guard**, **reservists**, and **Coast Guard** as eligible.

#### Do military spouses or dependents qualify?
Oakley's **official OSI qualifications page does not list spouses or dependents.** Some third-party pages claim "immediate family" qualifies, but that's unverified — check directly with Oakley SI customer care before assuming eligibility.

#### How do I verify and join Oakley Standard Issue?
Go to oakleysi.com/osiregister, verify your military/government/first-responder credentials (valid ID or .mil/.gov email), then create an oakleysi.com password. Membership is free; it's lifetime for U.S. military and renews every 12 months for others.

#### Does Oakley use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
The Oakley.com consumer discount uses **ID.me**. Oakley Standard Issue uses its own registration flow (with **ID.me** for OSI verification). Oakley's official OSI pages reference a "trusted verification service."

#### Can I use the military discount in stores?
Oakley Standard Issue is offered through the oakleysi.com website. Some Oakley retail stores may run separate in-store military discounts, but that's not confirmed on Oakley's official OSI pages — verify with your local store.

#### What's excluded from the Oakley.com 15% offer?
A $500 max, no stacking on markdowns/new launches/limited editions, and exclusions including the NFL collection, Helmets, Goggles, Physiomorphic Family, Kato, Encoder, Kaast, and Subzero. It's not cumulable with other codes, and the Meta AI glasses discount is online only.

#### Does Oakley offer a first responder, government, nurse, or teacher discount?
Yes. First responders and government personnel qualify for **Oakley Standard Issue**. Nurses, teachers, medical providers, and hospital employees get **15% off on Oakley.com via ID.me**.
