## Headline finding

**Stanley Steemer has no company-wide military or veteran discount.** Because Stanley Steemer operates as a network of **independently owned and operated franchises**, any military discount is **at the individual location's discretion** — corporate publishes no national military rate, and there is **no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or online military code.** So the honest answer is: **no guaranteed military discount; call your local branch and ask.** What *does* reliably save is Stanley Steemer's own **local coupons and promotions** (its "Specials / Find Local Coupons" page is geo-targeted by ZIP), its **email/text sign-up coupon**, and **seasonal promotions** (holidays like July 4 and Black Friday). Archetype: **franchised home-service brand with discretionary, location-by-location military pricing** — the page's job is to set that expectation honestly, tell readers exactly what to ask the local franchise, and point them to the coupons that apply to everyone. One important disambiguation: the **GovX "Stanley" military store is Stanley 1913** (the drinkware/tumbler brand), **not Stanley Steemer** — don't conflate them.
