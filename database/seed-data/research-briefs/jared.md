# Jared — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $1,000 fine-jewelry purchase (e.g., a diamond pendant or band) at jared.com / a Jared store.

## Headline finding

Jared (Jared the Galleria of Jewelry, a Signet Jewelers banner) runs a real, published **10% military discount verified through ID.me** — "Take 10% off✽. Click below to verify your military status" — redeemable **online at jared.com and in-store** via an ID.me-issued code. Eligibility is broad (Active Duty, Veterans, Retirees, Spouses, Dependents "and more," per Jared's own FAQ). The catch is a **long exclusions list** (clearance, most designer/watch brands including Tacori, TAG Heuer, Movado, custom items, and more) and a hard **"cannot be combined with any other offers"** rule — on a brand that constantly runs public coupons and promotions, so during Jared's frequent sales the public deal often beats the flat 10%. Archetype: **E (multi-group identity discount)** with a **C secondary (sales frequently beat it)**.

**Verdict logic:**
- Non-excluded item, no live promo → **verify with ID.me, take the 10%**.
- A public promotion ≥10% covers your item → **take the promo** (the 10% can't combine, and the ID.me code will likely void portal cashback anyway).
- Designer brands on the exclusion list (Tacori, TAG Heuer, etc.) → the military discount **never applies**; shop the public sale calendar.
- On-page chooser: (1) "Is your item on Jared's exclusions list?" (2) "Is a public coupon/sale live that covers it?"

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 10% cannot be combined with any other offers** — verbatim brand language. Sale prices, coupons and the military code are an either/or.
2. **The exclusions list is the real gatekeeper**: clearance, nearly all prestige watch brands, custom, trade-ins, warranties and repairs never get the 10%.
3. **Rakuten 2%** (as of July 14, 2026) rides on public orders; a non-Rakuten coupon code — including an ID.me military code — is exactly what Rakuten's terms typically use to reduce or void cashback. Don't promise "10% + cashback."
4. In-store redemption uses the same ID.me-issued code — it is a system discount, not a register-discretion one.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add items to your bag at jared.com.
2. In the cart, click the green **Verify with ID.me** button (or start at jared.com/military-discount).
3. Sign in or create a free ID.me account and verify your military status.
4. Enter the code ID.me issues at checkout; 10% comes off qualifying items.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Log in to (or create) your ID.me account and get your discount code first.
2. Bring the code to any Jared store and present it at checkout.

### How Verification Works
Jared uses **ID.me**. Verification happens in your cart (or from the discount page) and issues a single-use-style discount code for online or in-store use. Jared's FAQ links to ID.me's military-eligibility article as the authority on who passes. Verification lasts only as long as ID.me's policy for the military community — don't assume it's one-and-done forever.

### FAQ
#### Does Jared offer a military discount?
Yes — 10% off for ID.me-verified military community members, online and in-store. Exclusions apply.

#### How much is the Jared military discount?
10%. Sites advertising 20% or 25% are citing unsupported or expired promos — Jared's own page says 10%.

#### Do veterans, spouses and dependents qualify?
Yes. Jared's FAQ lists Active Duty, Veterans, Retirees, Spouses and Dependents, deferring the complete list to ID.me's military-eligibility rules.

#### Does Jared use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
ID.me only. No GovX or SheerID program was found for Jared.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Both. ID.me issues a code you can enter at jared.com checkout or present at a Jared store.

#### What's excluded?
Clearance, loose diamonds, custom items, trade-in purchases, previously owned merchandise, gift cards, repairs/warranties, and most designer/watch brands (Tacori, TAG Heuer, Movado, Breitling, Tissot and others — the full list is above).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — "cannot be combined with any other offers." If a public sale or coupon saves 10% or more on your item, use that instead.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy at Jared?
Non-excluded item with no live promo: the ID.me 10%. Item covered by a public promotion of 10%+: the promo plus ~2% Rakuten cashback on a public checkout.
