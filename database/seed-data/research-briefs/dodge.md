# Dodge — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one new **2026 Dodge Durango GT** (~$42,000 MSRP), financed or leased through a franchised Dodge dealer.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Dodge (a Stellantis brand) runs a real, official military incentive: a $500 Military Bonus Cash Allowance** on select 2026 vehicles, verified through **SheerID** (not ID.me). It is a fixed cash allowance applied at a franchised dealer, and — critically — it **stacks on top of most other public Dodge offers** (retail bonus cash, APR/lease programs, and the separate Affiliate Rewards / employee-pricing programs where eligible). Eligibility is broad on the active/retired side but narrow for veterans: honorably discharged veterans qualify **only within 12 months of their discharge date** (plus 100% disabled veterans anytime). Archetype: **automaker manufacturer-incentive** — the "discount" is a stackable factory rebate negotiated into the deal, and the real savings come from combining it with the month's public incentives and hard dealer negotiation, not from the $500 alone.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Military Bonus Cash stacks with other Dodge Brand offers** — the incentive page explicitly says you may also qualify for other offers, so the $500 layers on retail bonus cash and lease/APR programs.
2. **It does not stack with itself** — one military authorization/certificate per eligible customer per program window; you can't claim $500 twice on one deal.
3. **Affiliate Rewards vs. military bonus cash** — these are distinct Stellantis programs; whether both apply to one deal depends on the month's program rules — confirm in writing with the dealer before signing.
4. **Dealer discretion on the out-the-door price is the real lever** — the $500 is fixed; negotiation and incentive timing move thousands. Get the incentive stack itemized on the buyer's order.
5. **No consumer cashback portal or GovX path exists for new-car purchases** — ignore any "Rakuten on a Dodge" claim.

### FAQ

#### Does Dodge offer a military discount?
Yes — a **$500 Military Bonus Cash Allowance** on select 2026 vehicles (currently Durango incl. SRT, Charger Daytona BEV, Charger ICE), verified through SheerID.

#### How much is the Dodge military discount?
**$500** as a bonus cash allowance applied at the dealer — a flat amount, not a percentage.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Only **honorably discharged veterans within 12 months of discharge**, plus **100% disabled veterans** anytime. Veterans discharged longer ago don't qualify for the $500.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through **SheerID** at dodge.com's military incentive page; you receive an authorization number to present at the dealership.

**SheerID.** Not ID.me, not GovX.

#### Can I combine it with other offers?

Take the **$500 military bonus cash and stack it on the best public incentive of the month**, then negotiate the selling price hard. Timing (Memorial Day, July 4, year-end) matters more than the $500.

#### Does Dodge offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
