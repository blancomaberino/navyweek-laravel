The one time the official 30% isn't the winner: deep clearance items already marked down further than 30%, or GovX carrying a specific model below the ID.me net price. Secondary savings archetype: **GovX marketplace substitute** for shoppers who can't verify on-site.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 30% ID.me discount is a single-gate offer** applied at checkout after verification — treat it as **not combinable** with the public 15% email code or other coupons (standard one-code-per-order behavior; confirm at checkout).
2. **ID.me 30% generally does not stack on already-discounted Sale/clearance items** — compare the deeper of the two, don't expect both.
3. **GovX is a closed marketplace** — you cannot add a wileyx.com code, the ID.me 30%, or a cashback portal on top of GovX pricing. It's an either/or vs. the brand site.
4. **Prescription pricing is a separate lane** (SportRx via GovX) — the on-site 30% applies to wileyx.com orders; Rx routing through SportRx has its own (up to 15%) terms.

### FAQ

#### Does Wiley X offer a military discount?
Yes — **30% off** for military, first responders, nurses, and government employees, verified through **ID.me** on wileyx.com.

For a full-price frame, **30% via ID.me** wins. Always glance at the **Sale** page and **GovX** for that specific model — occasionally one is cut below the 30% net price.

#### Does Wiley X offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or government discount?
