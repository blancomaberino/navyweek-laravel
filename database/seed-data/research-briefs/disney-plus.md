# Disney+ Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Disney+ (the streaming service) offers a military-exclusive discount of **25% off the annual Disney+ Premium (No Ads) subscription**, redeemed exclusively through The Exchange (Army & Air Force Exchange Service) at ShopMyExchange.com — not on disneyplus.com directly. Eligibility is gated by Exchange shopping authorization (active-duty service members, retirees, Guard/Reserve, honorably discharged veterans, and other authorized Exchange patrons), not by ID.me or SheerID. The offer is for **new or eligible returning Disney+ subscribers only** (current subscribers are not eligible), applies only to the **annual Premium plan** (not monthly, not the ad-supported plan, not bundles), and the 25% discount carries through each 12-month renewal. The single most important caveat: you must have a valid ShopMyExchange.com account and be an authorized Exchange shopper to redeem — there is no general "show your military ID at checkout on disneyplus.com" path.

### Intro
Disney+ offers a genuine military discount on its streaming service — **25% off the annual Disney+ Premium (No Ads) plan** — but it works differently from most. You don't redeem it on disneyplus.com. Instead, it's an exclusive offer through **The Exchange (AAFES)** at ShopMyExchange.com, available to anyone authorized to shop there: active-duty service members, retirees, Guard and Reserve, honorably discharged veterans, and military families.

The offer is for **new or eligible returning Disney+ subscribers only** — if you already have an active Disney+ subscription, you can't apply it to your existing account. It covers only the **annual Premium (No Ads) plan**, not the monthly plan, the ad-supported plan, or any Disney Bundle. The 25% discount continues at each yearly renewal off the then-current renewal price until you cancel.

This is an independent guide from NavyWeek. We are not affiliated with Disney+ or The Exchange. Disney controls the offer and can change or end it at any time; always confirm current terms on ShopMyExchange.com before subscribing.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Confirm you are an authorized Exchange shopper and have (or create) a valid ShopMyExchange.com account.
2. Go to the Disney+ military exclusive offer page on ShopMyExchange.com and sign in to your Exchange account.
3. Select the offer and follow the prompt to sign up for Disney+ (or log in to an existing Disney+ account, if you are a new/eligible returning subscriber) and agree to the Disney+ Subscriber Agreement.
4. Provide a valid payment method (gift cards are not accepted) and complete sign-up for the annual Premium (No Ads) plan at 25% off.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not available. This is an online-only offer redeemed through ShopMyExchange.com. There is no in-store / register redemption for the Disney+ streaming discount. (Theme-park and Disney Store military discounts are separate offers and out of scope for this page.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled entirely by The Exchange's existing shopper-authorization system, not by ID.me or SheerID. To reach the offer you must have a valid, current ShopMyExchange.com account, which AAFES grants to authorized patrons (active duty, retirees, Guard/Reserve, and honorably discharged veterans who verify status through the VA's online process). Once you have an Exchange account, no additional Disney-side military verification is described. If you have trouble redeeming, the official guidance is to contact The Exchange for support (Disney+ Help Center directs military-discount issues to Exchange). OCONUS shoppers must additionally be physically on-installation using an approved on-base internet provider.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Applies only to the **annual Disney+ Premium (No Ads)** plan in the U.S. (including territories). Not available for the monthly plan, the ad-supported (Basic) plan, or any Disney Bundle.
- For **new or eligible returning subscribers only** — current Disney+ subscribers cannot redeem.
- Cannot be combined with any other offers, coupons, discounts, or promotions.
- **Gift cards cannot be used** to redeem; a valid payment method is required.
- Limited to **one discount per ShopMyExchange.com account holder**.
- Must be 18 (or age of majority in your jurisdiction). Personal, non-commercial use only; non-transferable; not redeemable for cash; will not be honored if obtained from a third party.
- Subscription auto-renews annually (monthly for Germany) at 25% off the then-current retail price until canceled.
- OCONUS: must be physically on-installation with internet from an approved on-base provider; experience varies by location (Puerto Rico, UK, Germany, Japan).
- Germany: 25% applies to monthly subscriptions rather than annual.

### FAQ

#### Does Disney+ offer a military discount?
Yes. Disney+ offers 25% off the annual Disney+ Premium (No Ads) streaming plan, redeemed exclusively through The Exchange at ShopMyExchange.com. (Source: Disney+ press release; ShopMyExchange offer page.)

#### How much is the Disney+ military discount?
25% off the annual Disney+ Premium (No Ads) subscription, and 25% off each 12-month renewal at the then-current renewal price. (Note: some coupon sites claim "10%" — that is not supported by Disney's official sources and appears incorrect.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Honorably discharged veterans qualify if they are authorized Exchange shoppers. The press release references "military families," but redemption requires the individual to have their own valid ShopMyExchange.com account, and the standard online Veterans Exchange benefit does not automatically extend online access to family members — confirm eligibility at ShopMyExchange.com.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through The Exchange. You need an authorized, current ShopMyExchange.com account; AAFES verifies military status (veterans verify via the VA process). There is no separate Disney-side military verification step.

#### Does Disney+ use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. The Disney+ streaming military offer is verified through The Exchange (AAFES) shopper authorization, not ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. (Some aggregator pages incorrectly cite ID.me/SheerID for Disney+ — this is not accurate for the streaming offer.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. It is an online-only offer redeemed at ShopMyExchange.com. There is no register/in-store redemption for the Disney+ streaming discount.

#### What is excluded?
The monthly plan, the ad-supported plan, and Disney Bundles are excluded; current Disney+ subscribers are excluded (new/eligible returning only); gift cards can't be used; it can't be combined with other offers; limited to one per Exchange account.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The offer cannot be combined with any other offers, coupons, discounts, or promotions.

#### Does Disney+ offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Not for the streaming service. The Disney+ offer is limited to authorized Exchange shoppers (military community). No first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student streaming discount is published. Some DoD civilians have limited Exchange privileges but this is not addressed by the offer terms.
