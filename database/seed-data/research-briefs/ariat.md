# Ariat Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Ariat offers a **10% military discount** on any Ariat.com order, valid **January 1, 2026 – December 31, 2026** (a calendar-year evergreen offer Ariat renews annually). Eligibility covers **active duty, reserve, veteran, and retired members of any branch of the U.S. Military** with proper credentials. To redeem, you must become a free **Ariat Insider** (account required), then verify military status through **SheerID** inside your account page; once verified, the discount applies automatically when logged in. The offer is **US orders only**, is honored in **Ariat brand stores and outlets**, and **cannot be combined with any other offer, coupon code, or sale items**, nor used on gift cards. The single most important caveat: the discount does **not** stack with sales/clearance or other promos, so during Ariat's frequent site-wide sales the sale price may beat the 10% off — compare before buying.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Ariat.com and select Join or Sign In to create or log into a free Ariat Insider account.
2. Open your My Account (Ariat Insider) page.
3. Select "Military Discount 10% — Get Verified."
4. Complete verification through SheerID (upload additional documentation if prompted).
5. Once verified and logged in, your 10% discount applies automatically to your eligible order.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify your military status through SheerID via your Ariat Insider account (verification is handled online).
2. Visit an Ariat brand store or Ariat outlet (find one at ariat.com/brandshops).
3. Present proof of your verified eligibility per store associate instructions to receive the 10% discount.

Note: Ariat's official page states it will honor the discount in Ariat brand stores but does not publish the exact in-store proof process. Confirm with the store before relying on it.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **SheerID** through your free Ariat Insider account. You select "Military Discount 10% — Get Verified" on your account page and complete the SheerID flow. Many applicants are verified instantly; if not, Ariat says you'll be contacted by email with your eligibility status, typically in under 20 minutes (Ariat also notes verification "may take a few hours"). In some cases additional documentation is required — enter your name exactly as it appears on your credential, and if your last name has changed, upload a government-issued document showing your current name. Ariat states it uses your information only to confirm eligibility, does not sell it, and that SheerID does not store uploaded documents or images. Ariat's official source does not state how often re-verification is required, so do not assume it is one-time.

### FAQ

#### Does Ariat offer a military discount?
Yes. Ariat offers a 10% military discount on any Ariat.com order for verified U.S. military members, valid for the 2026 calendar year (1/1/2026–12/31/2026).

#### How much is the Ariat military discount?
10% off any Ariat.com order. It cannot be combined with other offers, coupon codes, or sale items.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and reservists qualify?
Yes. Ariat's official terms state the discount applies to active duty, reserve, veteran, and retired members of any branch of the U.S. Military with proper credentials.

#### Do military spouses or dependents qualify?
Ariat's marketing copy says the discount is "for you and your family," but the official eligibility terms list only active duty, reserve, veteran, and retired members, and verification is based on the member's own status through SheerID. Ariat does not separately confirm spouse/dependent eligibility, so treat it as not officially stated.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Join or sign into a free Ariat Insider account, go to your My Account page, select "Military Discount 10% — Get Verified," and complete verification through SheerID. If you're not verified instantly, Ariat emails your status, usually within 20 minutes.

#### Does Ariat use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Ariat's official military discount page names **SheerID** as its verification partner. (GovX lists an Ariat storefront as a third-party marketplace, and some sources mention ID.me, but Ariat's own military-discount page does not confirm ID.me or GovX for this offer — use the SheerID flow on Ariat.com.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Ariat says it will honor the discount in Ariat brand stores and outlets. Verification is completed online through SheerID; confirm the in-store proof process with the store.

#### What is excluded?
The discount can't be combined with other offers, coupon codes, or sale items, and can't be used to buy gift cards. It's US-only, with no adjustments on prior purchases. Ariat notes "exclusions apply" without listing specific product categories.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Ariat's terms state it cannot be combined with any other offer, coupon code, or sale items.

#### Does Ariat offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Ariat offers a separate **First Responders Discount** (10%, also via SheerID) for currently employed law enforcement, paramedics, EMTs, and firefighters. Ariat does not publish a dedicated nurse, teacher, or student discount on its discount pages.
