# Staples — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 4, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** $80 school/office-supply cart (notebooks, folders, pens, printer paper, one ink cartridge), online or in-store.

## Headline finding

Staples has **no everyday military or veteran discount** — no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute checkout program exists, and ID.me's own Staples page confirms no military offer. What Staples does have is a **holiday-only history**: a 25% off in-store discount for military/veterans and immediate family around Veterans Day, run repeatedly (2022, 2024, 2025) with a valid military ID at the register. The rest of the year, a verified military shopper saves more at **Office Depot/OfficeMax (real 20% military coupon via ID.me)** or the **military exchange (tax-free school/office supplies)** than at Staples. Archetype: **H — the "myth" brand** (aggregators claim an everyday/online % that doesn't exist; the real everyday value is the Easy Rewards loyalty program), with a secondary **L** (exchange-carried category — tax-free substitute wins for eligible shoppers).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **There is no military discount to stack at Staples.** The Veterans Day 25% is in-store; cashback portals track online purchases only, so portal cashback never applies to it.
2. **Easy Rewards points redemption applies on top of coupon savings**, and points earn on qualifying purchases — but gift cards, clearance, taxes, and shipping never earn points (staples.com/lp/easyrewardsoverview). Coupon-blog transcriptions of Staples' policy say **one coupon per item** and points-redemption-on-top; treat the per-item rule as Medium confidence (not restated on a staples.com page we could verify).
3. **Cashback + coupon codes:** portal terms (Rakuten et al.) commonly reduce or void cashback when a non-portal code is applied. A Staples-issued sitewide email offer is usually portal-safe; scraped codes are not. Rates as of July 4, 2026: ~4% — recheck monthly.
4. **Price match doesn't combine with coupons or events:** Staples excludes Black Friday/Cyber Monday, clearance, and liquidation pricing from price match, and matches only identical, new, in-stock items (Amazon marketplace sellers excluded).
5. **Office Depot's 20% military coupon is one qualifying purchase** and excludes ink & toner and technology — the two biggest Staples categories. Don't imply it covers everything.
6. **Exchange purchases are a closed channel:** no Staples coupons, portals, or Easy Rewards apply; the win is tax-free pricing + exchange sales, for exchange-eligible shoppers only.

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty — eligible during Veterans Day promos (historical terms)
- Reservists / National Guard — eligible (listed as "reservists")
- Retired or disabled veterans — eligible
- Immediate family — eligible
- All other veterans — Not stated in official source (2025 coverage said "veterans and active military"; earlier terms said "retired or disabled veterans" — flag the ambiguity, advise bringing any VA/state veteran ID)
- Military spouses/dependents beyond "immediate family," first responders, teachers (as military), nurses, students — Not stated in official source for the military promo. Teachers have separate Staples programs.

### How To Redeem In Store (Veterans Day week, when offered)
1. Confirm the promo is live (typically the days surrounding November 11; check Staples' ad or ask at the register in early November).
2. Shop in any U.S. Staples store.
3. At checkout, mention the military offer and show a valid military ID (or applicable veteran ID).
4. 25% comes off qualifying in-store purchases; some items excluded — exclusion list is not published online, so ask at the register.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no online military discount at Staples. No code exists. Any site claiming an online Staples military code is wrong.

### How Verification Works
No standing verification program. During Veterans Day promos, verification is a physical military/veteran ID shown at the register — no account, no ID.me, no re-verification cycle.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Veterans Day promo: "exclusions apply" per every year's coverage; Staples has not published the exclusion list — Not stated in official source.
- Easy Rewards points: no earn on gift cards, phone cards, postage stamps, clearance, taxes, shipping, protection plans, third-party marketplace purchases.
- Price match: identical/new/in-stock items only; Amazon marketplace sellers excluded; no matching of Black Friday/Cyber Monday/clearance/liquidation prices; services excluded.

### Best Ways To Save More

- **Price match:** identical in-stock item at a qualifying competitor (including sold-and-shipped-by-Amazon) matched in store.
- **Cashback portals:** ~4% at Rakuten/TopCashback as of July 4, 2026 — online orders only.
- **Back-to-school sales (July–September):** 25¢ doorbusters and weekly freebies — the category's deepest public pricing.
- **Office Depot/OfficeMax substitute:** real 20% military coupon (ID.me online or military ID in store); excludes ink/toner and tech.
- **Military exchange:** ShopMyExchange is tax-free and open online to all honorably discharged veterans; back-to-school events historically up to 40% off select supplies.
- **Teachers:** separate Staples teacher coupons (20% off BTS 2025), Teacher Appreciation events, and Classroom Rewards.

### FAQ
#### Does Staples offer a military discount?
Not an everyday one. Staples has no military verification program and no military promo code. It has repeatedly run a 25% off in-store discount for military and veterans during Veterans Day week (confirmed 2022, 2024, 2025).

#### How much is the Staples military discount?
When offered (Veterans Day week), it has been 25% off in-store purchases with a valid military ID. Outside that window, 0%.

#### Can I use a Staples military discount online?
No. The Veterans Day discount is in-store only, and no online military discount or code exists.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Historical promo terms covered active duty, reservists, retired or disabled veterans, and immediate family. Recent coverage (2025) said "veterans and active military." Bring a military ID, VA card, or state veteran ID; terms are set fresh each year.

#### Does Staples use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute?
No for checkout verification. ID.me Shop's Staples page lists no military offer. WeSalute members can buy discounted Staples gift cards via BenefitHub (discount amount not publicly stated). There is no Staples storefront on GovX.

#### What is excluded?
Staples says only "exclusions apply" for the Veterans Day promo; the list isn't published. Ask at the register before checkout.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Staples?
During Veterans Day week: the 25% in-store discount. The rest of the year: Staples sale pricing + ~4% cashback + Easy Rewards — or skip Staples and use Office Depot's 20% military coupon or the tax-free military exchange.

#### Does Office Depot have a better military discount than Staples?
Yes, for an everyday program: Office Depot/OfficeMax offers 20% off one qualifying regular- or sale-price purchase for verified military (ID.me online, ID in store), plus a 25%-back-in-Bonus-Rewards military/teacher offer through December 26, 2026. Ink/toner and technology are excluded.

#### Does Staples offer a teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Teachers: yes — event-based coupons (20% off back-to-school 2025), Teacher Appreciation Days, and Classroom Rewards. Nurses/first responders/students: no published Staples program.

#### Does Staples run a Veterans Day sale every year?
It has in 2022, 2024, and 2025 (25% in-store). That's a pattern, not a promise — Staples announces terms each November.

#### Can I buy school supplies tax-free at the military exchange instead?
Yes, if eligible. All honorably discharged veterans can shop ShopMyExchange.com online (benefit since November 11, 2017); every purchase is sales-tax-free, and back-to-school events have offered up to 40% off select supplies.
