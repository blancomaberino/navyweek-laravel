# Sesame Place — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a family of four (2 adults + 2 children ages 2+) buying single-day tickets to Sesame Place Philadelphia — 4 × $110.99 published online one-day price = **$443.96 + tax** (gate price is higher but not published online; ticket prices are age-uniform, kids 23 months and under free).

## Headline finding

**Sesame Place has a real, generous military program — Waves of Honor**, run by parent company United Parks & Resorts across both parks (Philadelphia and San Diego). The headline benefit: **active duty, activated/drilling reservists, and National Guard get one FREE admission per year for themselves plus up to three direct dependents** (Philadelphia, via ID.me, valid through December 31, 2026). **Veterans get 50% off single-day tickets** (off the front-gate price, up to 6 tickets, online only, via ID.me) — Philadelphia's runs through December 31, 2026; San Diego's current window requires visiting by July 31, 2026. A richer limited promo (FREE admission for veterans + up to 6 dependents, and extra free dependent tickets for active duty) **expired July 5, 2026** — it must be labeled historical. Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount — the ID.me offer IS the savings path)**, with **I traits** (ticket products don't stack; the Military Season Pass is the repeat-visit play).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Ticket offers don't stack.** The free Waves of Honor admission, the veteran 50%, MWR/ITT tickets, and public sale tickets are alternative ways to buy the same admission — pick one per person.
2. **The free admission is capped**: one per year per service member, plus a maximum of three direct dependents; extra family members need another path (the 50% offer allows up to 6 tickets).
3. **Online only.** Both ID.me offers are explicitly "not available at the front gate" — showing a military ID at the gate gets you nothing; verify and buy online first.
4. **Season-pass perks do layer**: the Military Season Pass's free parking and 10–15–20% in-park discounts apply on every visit, on top of admission already being covered by the pass.
5. **No cashback-portal play**: Sesame Place tickets route through the parks' own ID.me-gated store; no verified portal cashback on ticket purchases.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Free annual admission + up to 3 direct dependents; also qualifies for the 50% offer.
- **Activated or drilling reservists / National Guard** — Same free-admission benefit as active duty.
- **Veterans (retired or honorably separated)** — 50% off single-day tickets, up to 6.
- **Military retirees** — Qualify as veterans for the 50% offer; **excluded** from the free active-duty admission.
- **Dependents** — Up to 3 free with the active-duty admission (dependent ID required); veterans' families covered via the up-to-6-ticket allowance.
- **Inactive/standby reserve, Merchant Marine, DoD civilians** — Explicitly excluded from the free-admission benefit.
- **First responders** — Separate offer exists (sesameplace.com first-responders pages); not part of Waves of Honor.

### How To Redeem Online (only channel)
1. Go to the Sesame Place military tickets page for your park (Philadelphia or San Diego).
2. Click through to the active-duty or veteran offer and verify with **ID.me**.
3. Claim the free tickets or buy the 50% tickets in the same online flow (up to the ticket cap).
4. Bring valid military/dependent ID to the park; tickets are date-bound — check blockout dates before booking.

### How Verification Works
Verification is by **ID.me** on the park's website before purchase. Active-status offers require ID reflecting active status (or dependent ID) at the gate. There is no front-gate military discount — unverified walk-ups pay the full gate price.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Free admission: once per calendar year, member + max 3 direct dependents, use by December 31, 2026 (Philadelphia offer).
- Veteran 50%: off the **front-gate** price, online only, up to 6 tickets, some blockout dates; San Diego's current window requires visiting by **July 31, 2026**.
- Offers exclude separately ticketed events; parking not included (free general parking comes with the Military Season Pass).
- The free-veteran-ticket promo (obtain by June 30, use by July 5, 2026) is **over** — treat any page still advertising it as stale.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Use the free annual admission first** if you're active duty/Guard/drilling reserve — it's the single largest benefit.
- **Price-check the 50% offer against public online sales** — advance-purchase and flash-sale prices are sometimes in the same range because 50% keys off the gate price.
- **MWR/ITT ticket offices** on base sell discounted Sesame Place tickets year-round (San Diego page confirms) — compare if you have base access.
- **Military Season Pass** for repeat visits: unlimited entry through January 3, 2027, two guest tickets, free general parking, 10% food & beverage, 15% merchandise, 20% Abby's Magic Queue.
- **Watch the early-summer window** — in 2026 the parks ran free veteran/dependent tickets Memorial Day–July 4; historically a recurring pattern.

### FAQ
#### Does Sesame Place offer a military discount?
Yes. Through Waves of Honor, active-duty service members, activated/drilling reservists, and National Guard get one free admission per year plus up to three direct dependents, and veterans get 50% off single-day tickets — both verified through ID.me, online only.

#### How much is the Sesame Place veteran discount?
50% off single-day admission, off the front-gate price, for up to six tickets. Philadelphia's offer runs January 1 – December 31, 2026; San Diego's current window requires redeeming and visiting by July 31, 2026.

#### Is Sesame Place still free for veterans?
Not currently. The 2026 free-veteran-admission promo (up to 6 dependents) required claiming tickets by June 30, 2026 and visiting by July 5, 2026 — it has ended. The standing veteran offer is 50% off.

#### Do retirees and reservists qualify?
Military retirees qualify for the veteran 50% offer but are excluded from the free active-duty admission. Activated or drilling reservists and National Guard get the free admission; inactive/standby reserve does not.

#### How do I verify — does Sesame Place use ID.me?
Yes. Both parks verify military status through ID.me on their websites. Verification and purchase happen online; there's no military discount at the front gate.

#### Can I use the military discount at the gate?
No. Both the free admission and the 50% veteran offer are explicitly online-only and "not available at the front gate."

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other ticket deals?
No. Ticket offers are mutually exclusive — free admission, the 50% offer, MWR/ITT tickets, and public sale prices are alternate ways to buy the same ticket. Season-pass perks (parking, in-park discounts) do apply on every visit.

#### Does the free admission work at both Sesame Place parks?
The Philadelphia offer is part of the SeaWorld/Busch Gardens/Sesame Place Philadelphia choice; San Diego runs its own Waves of Honor offers on its site. Claim through the specific park's military page.

#### What about first responders?
Sesame Place runs separate first-responder ticket offers (police, fire, EMS) at both parks — see the parks' first-responder pages; they're not part of Waves of Honor.

#### What's the cheapest way for a military family to visit?
Active-duty/Guard/drilling-reserve families of up to four: the free Waves of Honor admission — $0. Larger veteran families: the 50% offer (up to 6 tickets), price-checked against the day's public online sale.
