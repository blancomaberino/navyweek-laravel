# Comfort Inn — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 3-night leisure stay at a Comfort Inn, ~$120/night = **$360** before tax (illustrative; Comfort Inn is an upper-midscale Choice brand, a notch above Quality Inn).

> Comfort Inn (and its sibling Comfort Suites) is a **Choice Hotels** brand. Its military program is not brand-specific — it is the umbrella **Choice Privileges** military program and the **Government & Military Rates** that run across every Choice brand (Quality Inn, Sleep Inn, Clarion, Cambria, Econo Lodge, etc.). This brief is the Comfort-Inn-facing view of that program; the master record is `research/discounts/choice-hotels.md`.

## Headline finding

Yes — Comfort Inn honors a military discount, but only as part of **Choice Hotels'** program, and there is **no flat "Comfort Inn military discount percentage"** because hotels are rate-based. Two parts exist: (1) a **military/veteran leisure rate** you select at booking (varies by property/date, availability-capped, and — unusually — **still earns Choice Privileges points**), and (2) free **limited-time Gold Elite** status in Choice Privileges for active duty, reserve, retired, National Guard, and spouses. This is the classic **travel/loyalty archetype (I): status stacks, rates don't.** The reliable win is the **free limited-time Gold Elite status** (claim once, it layers on every future booking while active); the nightly "discount" is just the public Best Available Rate minus the military rate for your dates. **Critical correction:** the former **Lifetime** Gold status was **downgraded to "limited-time" Gold** and the **2,500 bonus points appear to have been removed** (Frequent Miler, Feb 1 2025) — do not publish "Lifetime Gold." For a given stay, price-check the military rate against **WeSalute's flat 15%** (paid membership, verified brand-specific for Comfort Inn), Pay Now & Save, and the public member rate, and book the single lowest — they do not stack.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Status stacks; rates don't.** Limited-time Gold Elite is *status*, not a rate — it layers on top of whatever rate you book (while active). The rate discounts (military leisure rate, WeSalute 15%, Pay Now & Save, member rate) are **mutually exclusive** — pick the single lowest.
2. **The military leisure rate still earns points** — unlike many discount rates that strip loyalty earning. Gold Elite adds a further points bonus. WeSalute's up-to-15% rate also still earns points.
3. **Must be a Choice Privileges member AND book direct.** The rate and your status/points only attach on choicehotels.com or the Choice app. **OTA bookings (Expedia, Booking.com) forfeit both** — the single most common way travelers lose the benefit.
4. **WeSalute up to 15% needs a paid WeSalute+ membership (~$10/mo)** and **cannot combine** with the non-refundable Pay Now & Save advance rate (stated explicitly on the WeSalute Comfort Inn offer).
5. **No military coupon code exists** — there is nothing to paste at checkout; the "discount" is a rate you select, verified at the front desk with appropriate ID.
6. **Cashback portals (Rakuten/TopCashback) are not a verified stack here.** Portal hotel cashback typically excludes member/discounted rates and often fails to track on logged-in/loyalty bookings — do not promise a cashback layer on the military rate.

### Who Qualifies

- **Veterans** — Eligible for the leisure rate and, per the current cp-military page, limited-time Gold Elite.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible for the leisure rate and limited-time Gold Elite.
- **Military retirees** — Eligible for the leisure rate and limited-time Gold Elite.

### How To Redeem Online
1. **Claim free limited-time Gold Elite first (~2 min).** Sign in to (or create) a free Choice Privileges account and add your military status / submit the military enrollment form. Do this before booking — the status layers on every stay while active.
2. **Book direct on choicehotels.com (or the app), signed in as a Choice Privileges member.** OTAs forfeit the rate and your status/points.
3. **Select the military rate.** On the property page click **"Rate,"** scroll to **"Military/Veterans,"** and book. (For official travel, choose the government/official rate instead.)

To book a military/veteran rate you must be a member and book direct; carry a military, veteran, retiree, or government ID for check-in. **WeSalute** is a **separate, paid** verification path for its up-to-15% Comfort Inn offer.

### FAQ

#### Does Comfort Inn offer a military discount?
Yes, through **Choice Hotels'** program (Comfort Inn and Comfort Suites are Choice brands). You can book a discounted military/veteran **leisure rate** (it varies by property and date and still earns Choice Privileges points) and claim free **limited-time Gold Elite** status. Because hotels are rate-based, there is no single "Comfort Inn military discount percentage."

#### How much is the Comfort Inn military discount?
There's no fixed percentage — the military/veteran rate is a discounted *rate* that varies by property and date. The only flat, verifiable percentage is **WeSalute's up to 15%**, which needs a paid membership. Ignore aggregator claims of "up to 20%" or a military coupon code — hotels don't work that way and there's nothing to paste at checkout.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Per the current Choice program: **active duty, veterans, reserve, National Guard, retired members, and spouses** can claim limited-time Gold Elite; **active/retired military, their dependents, and members of certain government/military associations** qualify for the military/veteran leisure rate. An eligible party must occupy the room.

#### How do I verify my status?
Add your military status to a (free) Choice Privileges account / submit the military enrollment form, then show **proof of service or appropriate ID at check-in**. There's no ID.me or GovX gate for the direct program.

#### Does Comfort Inn use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
The direct Choice program uses your Choice Privileges account plus front-desk ID — no ID.me/GovX/SheerID. **WeSalute** is a separate, paid membership that unlocks up to 15% at Choice brands including Comfort Inn (and it also covers nurses and medical professionals).

#### Can I use it online and in stores?
Book **online** (choicehotels.com or the app) or by phone (877-424-6423), then verify **in person at check-in**. You must book **direct** — OTA bookings forfeit the rate and status.

#### What is excluded?
There's no tax-free or coupon path; rates are availability-capped and vary by property. The rate discounts don't stack with each other, WeSalute can't combine with Pay Now & Save, and the government rate is for official travel only.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, cashback, or sale rates?
No — the rate discounts are mutually exclusive, there's no military coupon code, and cashback portals typically don't reliably track or pay on member/discounted hotel rates. The one thing that always "stacks" is your **status** (limited-time Gold Elite), which layers on any rate you book.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Comfort Inn?
Claim free limited-time Gold Elite first, then for each trip price-check the military/veteran rate, WeSalute up to 15% (if a member), the public member/sale rate, and Pay Now & Save — and book the single lowest, direct.

#### Does Comfort Inn offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not through Choice's military program directly. **WeSalute** extends its Comfort Inn offer to **nurses and medical professionals** (and families) alongside military — that's the route for medical staff. No first-responder, teacher, or student tier is documented.

#### Was there really a "Lifetime Gold" status — can I still get it?
Choice **downgraded** it. It's now **limited-time** Gold Elite, and the former **2,500 bonus points** appear to have been dropped (reported Feb 2025). Members who claimed Lifetime Gold before the change appear to keep it, but new enrollees get the limited-time version — claim it while it lasts and don't trust pages still promising "Lifetime Gold."
