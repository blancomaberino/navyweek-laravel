# Red Lobster — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$50 dine-in dinner check for two** (food only, no alcohol), pre-tax and pre-tip — a realistic Red Lobster sit-down order (e.g. two entrées off the everyday menu, shared appetizer). All math is dine-in because every verified discount here is dine-in only.

## Headline finding

Red Lobster **does** offer a real, brand-confirmed **everyday 10% military discount** — and it survived the 2024 Chapter 11 bankruptcy and reorganization under new owner Fortress (RL Investor Holdings). Red Lobster's own **November 2025 press release** states it "proudly supports military members year-round by providing an **everyday 10% discount on dine-in meals with valid military ID or proof of service**," and the brand's official X/Twitter account confirms the same 10% for "all veterans and active-duty military personnel." So the widely-repeated "10%" is **not** an aggregator fabrication here — it's verified. The catches are real, though: it's **dine-in only**, at **participating locations** (varies by restaurant — call ahead), applies to the veteran's/service member's **individual order**, and **excludes alcohol, gift cards, tax, and gratuity**. On our $50-for-two check that's about **$5 off** (or roughly **$2.50** if a location applies it only to the one qualifying person's order). The bigger everyday lever for most diners is actually the **free My Red Lobster Rewards** program, whose **new-member 20% off a $30+ order** beats the 10% on a first visit (they generally don't combine). Separately, Red Lobster runs an **annual Veterans Day free meal** (Nov 11 — historically a free appetizer/dessert in 2018, and free "Veteran's/Walt's Shrimp & Chips" in 2024 and 2025). Archetype: **J-adjacent** (in-store, participating/YMMV discount) but brand-*confirmed* rather than pure manager's-discretion, with a strong secondary **H** (My Red Lobster Rewards loyalty wallet is the bigger everyday value).

## Stacking Rules (verified where possible; restaurant norms noted)
1. **The everyday 10% is one offer per check** and applies to the **individual order** — it **excludes alcohol, gift cards, applicable taxes, and gratuity** (Red Lobster's own X terms). Confirmed exclusions; the whole-check vs. individual-order application **varies by location** — ask your server.
2. **The 10% generally will not combine with a My Red Lobster Rewards coupon** (e.g. the new-member 20%). Restaurants apply one promotional discount per check; use whichever is larger. *(Brand does not publish an explicit combine/no-combine rule for these two — treat as non-combinable and verify at the table.)*
3. **Daily Deals / Happy Hour are menu prices, not coupons.** The 10% should apply on top of a lower-priced menu item, since it's a base price rather than a stacked offer — but confirm locally, as some locations exclude promotional items.
4. **Rewards points still accrue** on most checks (1 pt/$1), so you can earn points *and* take a discount — points are the one thing that layers under everything.
5. **The Veterans Day free meal is one redemption per customer, dine-in, no substitutions**, and excludes beverage/tax/gratuity — a standalone promo, not stackable with the everyday 10% on the same item.
6. **No online military code exists** — the discount is presented in-restaurant. Ignore any "military promo code" for redlobster.com ordering.
7. **GovX discounted gift cards** are a **payment method**, so paying with one effectively discounts the *entire* bill (including alcohol/tax) and can sit under a normal check — but it's a substitute for cash, not a stack on the 10% for the same dollars. Verify the GovX offer live.

### FAQ

#### Does Red Lobster offer a military discount?
**Yes.** Red Lobster offers a **10% everyday dine-in discount** for veterans and active-duty military who show a valid military ID or proof of service — confirmed in Red Lobster's own 2025 press release and by its official social accounts. It's dine-in only and offered at participating locations.

#### How much is the Red Lobster military discount?
**10% off** the dine-in order. It excludes alcohol, gift cards, tax, and gratuity, so on a ~$50 dinner it's roughly **$2.50–$5**. A new **My Red Lobster Rewards** member's **20% off a $30+ order** is bigger on a first visit.

#### Did the military discount survive Red Lobster's bankruptcy?
**Yes.** Red Lobster exited Chapter 11 in September 2024 under new owner Fortress (RL Investor Holdings), and the everyday 10% discount was reaffirmed in the company's **November 2025** press release.

#### Do veterans, reservists, retirees, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Red Lobster explicitly names **veterans and active-duty military**. Reservists were named in past Veterans Day offers and are commonly honored; **retirees, spouses, and dependents are not spelled out** — confirm at your local restaurant.

#### How do I verify — does Red Lobster use ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute?
There's **no online verification partner** for the everyday discount — you show a **military ID or proof of service in person** at the table. Red Lobster does have a **GovX** page (likely discounted gift cards; terms gated), but that's separate from the in-restaurant 10%.

#### Can I use the discount online or only dine-in?
**Dine-in only.** There is no verified online or promo-code military discount. Ignore "Red Lobster military codes" on coupon sites.

#### What's excluded?
The 10% excludes **alcohol, gift cards, applicable taxes, and gratuity**, is limited to **participating locations**, and may apply only to the qualifying diner's **individual order**.

#### Can I combine the discount with My Red Lobster Rewards or promo codes?
Generally **no** — restaurants apply one promotional offer per check, so use whichever is larger (the 20% new-member reward usually wins on a first visit). You do still **earn Rewards points** on most checks.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at Red Lobster?
On a **first visit**, join Rewards and use the **20% off $30+**. Otherwise show ID for the **everyday 10%** and order off **Daily Deals**. On **Nov 11**, take the **free Veterans Day meal**. For a discount on the whole bill (including drinks), buy a **discounted gift card** and pay with it.

#### Does Red Lobster run a Veterans Day free meal?
**Yes, most years.** Recently (2024 and 2025) it was a **free Shrimp & Chips** on **November 11**, dine-in, one per customer, with proof of service (a free appetizer or dessert in 2018). It's **not guaranteed** every year — confirm each late October/November.

#### Does Red Lobster offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
**No published discount** for those groups was found this pass. The verified offers are the everyday **military** 10% and the annual **Veterans Day** meal.
