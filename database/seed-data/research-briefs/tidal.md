# TIDAL — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **TIDAL Individual subscription** (standard **$10.99/month**), compared against every discounted path a service member can actually use.

## Headline finding

**TIDAL no longer offers a military discount.** TIDAL **discontinued its Military and First Responder discount plans on June 10, 2024** — subscribers on those plans (formerly ~40% off, verified via SheerID/VerifyPass) were **automatically moved to the standard Individual plan at $10.99/month.** As of July 2026, TIDAL's published plans are **Individual $10.99/mo, Family $16.99/mo (up to 6 people), Student $5.49/mo, and a DJ add-on (+$9/mo)** — there is **no military, veteran, or first-responder tier, and no ID.me/SheerID/GovX military verification.** So the honest answer is: **no military discount exists.** The best *actual* savings for a service member now are (1) the **free trial** everyone gets, (2) the **Student plan at $5.49** if you're an enrolled student (many using GI Bill/tuition assistance are), (3) the **Family plan** to split cost across a household, and (4) watching for **public promos** — e.g., TIDAL has run a Cash App–linked offer (25% off the Individual plan for 12 months for eligible U.S. customers) tied to its August 3, 2026 pricing update. Archetype: **subscription that *removed* its military discount** — the page's job is to state that plainly, debunk the stale "40% off military" listings still circulating, and route readers to the paths that still save.

1. **There is no military discount to stack** — the tier was removed in 2024.

#### Does TIDAL offer a military discount?
No. TIDAL discontinued its Military and First Responder plans on **June 10, 2024**. There is no military, veteran, or first-responder tier in 2026.

#### What happened to the old TIDAL military discount?
It offered about 40% off (HiFi/HiFi Plus) via SheerID/VerifyPass, but TIDAL ended it on June 10, 2024 and moved those subscribers to the standard $10.99 Individual plan.

Individual **$10.99/mo**, Family **$16.99/mo** (up to 6), Student **$5.49/mo**, plus an optional DJ add-on (+$9). A pricing update takes effect August 3, 2026.

No — both the military and first-responder discounts were discontinued in 2024.

Yes — **Family $16.99/mo** (up to 6 people) and **Student $5.49/mo** (with student verification).
