# Capital One — Military Benefits (SCRA) & Fee-Relief Brief

**Researched:** July 19, 2026 · **Baseline "cart" for all math:** one Capital One Venture X card with a **$395** annual fee (the flagship case the "capital one military" searcher is asking about), plus a pre-service card balance for the rate-cap math.

> **This is a statutory-relief page, not a retail percent-off discount — and its core job is a DEBUNK.** Capital One has no "military discount," and — unlike American Express and Chase — it does **NOT** waive annual fees under the Military Lending Act for cards opened *during* active duty. Its entire military benefit is an unusually generous **SCRA program (4% rate cap + no fees)** that applies **only to accounts opened before your active-duty start date**. Getting the SCRA-vs-MLA distinction right is the whole page.

## Headline finding

**Capital One does not waive credit-card annual fees for cards opened while on active duty.** There is no MLA fee benefit: an active-duty servicemember who opens a Venture X today pays the full **$395/year** — the opposite of Amex and Chase, whose ranking pages train readers to expect a $0 fee. What Capital One *does* offer is one of the industry's stronger **SCRA** programs, and it's better than the law requires: on eligible accounts opened **before** your active-duty start date, Capital One caps interest at **4%** (the statute only requires 6%) and assesses **no fees except bona-fide insurance** — which in practice zeroes the annual fee — on Capital One **and Discover** credit cards, personal loans, and auto loans. Benefits are **retroactive to your active-duty start date** (or "order to" date for reservists) and run until **one year after** active duty ends, and you must request them (online, phone, mail, or branch) — they are not automatic.

Archetype: **regulated-benefit / statutory-relief brand** (same family as the American Express and Amex Platinum pages) — the "discount" is federal-law compliance, eligibility is legally defined, and the make-or-break variable is the **account-open date vs. active-duty start date**. The Capital One twist vs. Amex: relief exists on the **SCRA side only**; there is no voluntary MLA fee waiver on the during-service side.

Capital One doesn't have a military discount — and, more importantly, it doesn't do what American Express and Chase do. There is **no annual-fee waiver for cards opened while you're on active duty**. Open a $395 Venture X during your service and you'll pay the full fee every year.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty servicemembers** (all branches) — eligible on accounts opened **before** the active-duty start date.
- **Reservists / National Guard on active-duty orders** — eligible while activated; benefits and retroactivity key to the **"order to" date**, so accounts opened between activations qualify for the next activation.
- **Military spouses** — covered only on **joint** obligations with the servicemember (per 50 USC 3937). A spouse's own separately opened card is **not** covered. Spouses/dependents may submit the request on the member's behalf.
- **Veterans** — no ongoing benefit; requests accepted up to **1 year** after active duty ends (covering the active-duty period + the 1-year tail).
- **Anyone opening a new Capital One card while on active duty** — **not** eligible for fee relief; the account fails the pre-service test.

### How To Request SCRA Benefits
1. Go to **capitalone.com/military** and start the online SCRA request (fastest), or call **1-855-CAP1-MIL (1-855-227-1645)**, available 24/7.
2. One request covers **all** your Capital One and Discover accounts. (Discover-only legacy help: 1-844-DFS-4MIL / 1-844-337-4645; overseas 1-801-451-3730.)
3. By mail or in a branch, include a copy of your **active-duty orders** or a **commanding-officer letter** showing your name and SSN, active-duty start date, and branch of service. Do **not** send military or government IDs.
4. Watch for a **decision letter within 2 monthly billing cycles**. Relief applies retroactively to your active-duty start ("order to") date; retroactive adjustments may arrive as a check.
5. Benefits stay on the account until **one year after** you leave or complete active duty.
- Mail: Capital One, Attn: SCRA Request, P.O. Box 30285, Salt Lake City, UT 84130-0285.

### How Verification Works
Capital One reviews your orders or CO letter directly — there is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute step, and no code. Requests can also be initiated by a spouse, dependent, or other representative on the servicemember's behalf. Relief is time-boxed to active duty plus one year; don't present it as permanent.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Accounts opened after your active-duty start date are declined** — this is Capital One's published decline criterion and the single most important line on the page.
- Declined if the active-duty period ended **more than one year** before the request, or the account was settled/paid in full before active duty began.
- **No MLA fee waiver exists.** MLA still applies its 36% MAPR ceiling to during-service accounts, but annual fees that are "bona fide" and reasonable are excluded from that math (32 CFR 232.4(d)) — so the Venture X's $395 fee stands.
- Bona-fide insurance charges are not waived.
- Rewards, miles, and welcome offers are unaffected — this is fee/rate relief, not a rewards change.
- Capital One's 4% cap is voluntary generosity over the 6% statutory floor and could change; the statutory 6% floor cannot (for covered obligations).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Guard/Reserve: open before you activate.** Any Capital One card opened while off orders becomes SCRA-eligible for your next 30+ day activation — effectively a repeatable fee waiver for each covered period.
- **Sweep every pre-service account:** one request covers Capital One *and* Discover cards, auto loans, and personal loans — file once, cover everything, and collect retroactive adjustments.
- **The upgrade play (unverified):** shoppers report product-changing an old pre-service Capital One card into a Venture X keeps it fee-free under the existing SCRA coverage — confirm with Capital One first; you'll likely forgo the welcome bonus.
- **If you're active duty and want a $0-fee premium card now:** Capital One isn't the issuer for that — Amex and Chase waive premium-card fees on cards opened during service. See our American Express and Amex Platinum guides.

### FAQ

#### Does Capital One offer a military discount?
No retail discount. Capital One offers **SCRA relief**: a **4% interest cap** and **no fees** (except bona-fide insurance) on eligible Capital One and Discover accounts opened **before** your active-duty start date. There's no percent-off and no promo code.

#### Does Capital One waive annual fees for military?
**Only under SCRA, only on pre-service accounts.** If you opened the card before active duty and request benefits, fees stop (annual fee effectively $0) through active duty plus one year. If you opened the card **during** active duty, the fee is **not** waived — Capital One has no MLA fee-waiver policy.

#### Why doesn't Capital One waive fees like Amex and Chase?
The Military Lending Act caps during-service credit at 36% MAPR, but its rules (32 CFR 232.4(d)) let issuers exclude bona fide, reasonable annual fees from that calculation. Amex and Chase zero annual fees **voluntarily**; Capital One chooses not to.

#### Is the Venture X free for military?
Only if the account predates your active-duty start (or you product-changed a pre-service account into it — a community-reported path; confirm with Capital One). A Venture X opened while on active duty pays the full **$395** annual fee.

#### How much interest does Capital One charge under SCRA?
No more than **4%** on covered accounts — better than the 6% federal maximum. Interest above the cap is forgiven, not deferred, and adjustments are retroactive to your active-duty start date.

#### Do spouses and dependents qualify?
Spouses are covered on **joint** accounts with the servicemember. A spouse's own separately opened Capital One card is **not** covered (the SCRA covers the member's pre-service obligations and joint member-spouse obligations). Spouses and dependents can submit the SCRA request on the member's behalf.

#### How do I request Capital One SCRA benefits?
Online at capitalone.com/military, by phone at **1-855-CAP1-MIL (1-855-227-1645)** (24/7), by mail (Attn: SCRA Request, P.O. Box 30285, Salt Lake City, UT 84130-0285), or at any Capital One or Discover branch, with your orders or a commanding-officer letter. Expect a decision within 2 monthly billing cycles.

#### Does this cover Discover cards?
Yes. Capital One acquired Discover and administers one aligned SCRA program — a single request covers your Capital One **and** Discover accounts. Discover's dedicated line is 1-844-DFS-4MIL (1-844-337-4645).

#### I'm in the Guard/Reserve — can I still get the fee waived?
Yes, with timing: open the card **before** a 30+ day activation, then request SCRA once activated. Benefits key to your "order to" date and last until one year after that activation ends — and accounts opened before a *future* activation qualify for it.

#### How long do benefits last, and is there a deadline?
Benefits run from your active-duty start date until **one year after** active duty ends. Requests are declined only if made more than one year after your active-duty period ended — more generous than the SCRA's 180-day statutory floor.

#### Does Capital One use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
No. Verification is your active-duty orders or a commanding-officer letter, reviewed by Capital One directly.
