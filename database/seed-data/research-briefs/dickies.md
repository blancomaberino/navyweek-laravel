# Dickies — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$100 full-price workwear cart** (e.g., ~3 pairs of Original 874® Work Pants at $29.99 + a work tee), pre-tax, at dickies.com. Small-ticket apparel, so tax is noted only where a tax-free channel (the military exchange) competes.

## Headline finding

Dickies (dickies.com) has a **real but modest 10% discount for military, students, and first responders, verified through SheerID** — not ID.me. You confirm your status once at Dickies' SheerID landing page (verify.sheerid.com/dickies-landing/) and get **10% off your total purchase**. That's the honest everyday answer. The verified figure is **10%** — the widely repeated "20% military" (Military.com, VeteransGuide) and "25–30%" (coupon aggregators) claims are **not confirmed** by any Dickies primary source.

Two important nuances. First, Dickies' own **/pages/special-discounts** hub currently shows **only a notice that the First Responder program "is being restructured"** — the persistent live redemption path is the **SheerID** flow, which was serving the 10% offer on July 10, 2026. Second, because Dickies is a **heavily sale-driven workwear brand**, the 10% is usually **not** the best price: the public **15%-off "Join Our Crew" email signup**, frequent **clearance events** (as of July 10, 2026: *up to 50% off clearance + an extra 20% off with code `add20`*), and — for eligible trades — the **ExpertVoice Pro Deal** program routinely beat it, and none of them stack with the 10%. Archetype: **sale-driven apparel brand with a thin verified SheerID identity discount** (public codes and clearance usually win), plus a secondary **ExpertVoice pro-deal** channel. If you have exchange access, Dickies staples (874 pants, etc.) are stocked **tax-free at the Navy Exchange and AAFES** — a quietly strong path.

### FAQ

#### Does Dickies offer a military discount?
Yes. Dickies offers a **10% military discount** on dickies.com, verified through **SheerID**. Students and first responders get the same 10% (the first-responder tier is currently being restructured).

#### How much is the Dickies military discount?
**10% off your total purchase.** That's the only figure Dickies and SheerID publish — the "20%" and "25–30%" numbers on some sites are unverified or are ordinary public sale codes.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans are covered by SheerID's **Military** verification. **Spouses and dependents are not explicitly named** on Dickies' page — check whether the SheerID Military flow accepts your category before relying on it.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to **verify.sheerid.com/dickies-landing/**, choose the Military (or Student/First Responder) flow, complete **SheerID** verification, and apply the 10% at checkout.

#### Does Dickies use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**SheerID.** Despite listicles naming ID.me, the live, named verification partner for the Dickies discount is **SheerID**. There's no confirmed GovX or WeSalute route.

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?
The verified 10% is an **online** SheerID offer for dickies.com. In-store discounting isn't documented — ask the specific store. (Exchange stores are a separate, tax-free channel.)

#### What is excluded?
Treat the 10% as a **single, non-combinable** discount excluded from clearance/sale and not stackable with promo codes or the 15% signup. The First Responder tier is being restructured.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. It's one order-level discount. Because Dickies runs deep public sales, the **public clearance + `add20`** or the **15% signup** usually beats the 10% — and none of them stack, so pick the single biggest.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Dickies?
On a **full-price** cart, the SheerID **10%** (e.g., $90 on a $100 cart) — unless you're a **new subscriber** (15%, $85) or an **approved trade pro** (ExpertVoice, usually deeper). On **sale styles**, the public **clearance + `add20`** wins. With **exchange access**, buy staples **tax-free at NEX/AAFES**.

#### Does Dickies offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
**Students** get the same 10% via SheerID. A **first-responder** 10% exists but the program is being restructured. **Teacher, nurse, and government** tiers are **not** separately published by Dickies this pass — don't assume them.

#### Can I buy Dickies through ExpertVoice or at the Exchange?
Yes to both, with caveats. **ExpertVoice** offers a gated **Pro Deal** for eligible trades/industry pros (personal use only) — *not* a military benefit per se. And Dickies staples are stocked **tax-free at the Navy Exchange and AAFES** if you have exchange access.
