# Rhoback — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $88 Rhoback "Q-Zip" quarter-zip (the brand's signature item), pre-tax.

## Headline finding

Yes — Rhoback runs a **real, year-round 15% military & first-responder discount**, verified through **ID.me** at checkout on rhoback.com. Eligible groups are active-duty military, veterans, and first responders (firefighters, police, EMTs). This is the single most dependable savings path for a qualifying shopper because it applies to full-price product any day of the year, whereas Rhoback's public sitewide codes are seasonal and shallow. Rhoback fits the **DTC apparel brand with an ID.me evergreen discount** archetype: one clean official offer, no exchange presence, and a thin third-party GovX channel limited to co-branded collab items. The official offer page does not state stacking terms, so treat "combine with sale codes" as unverified.

### How Verification Works
Rhoback uses **ID.me**, a third-party identity verification provider. You sign in or create a free ID.me account and verify your military/veteran/first-responder status through their system at checkout — no need to send Rhoback photos or scans of a government ID. ID.me periodically requires re-verification per its own policies; Rhoback's page does not state a re-verification cadence.

### FAQ

#### Does Rhoback offer a military discount?
Yes. Rhoback offers a 15% discount for active-duty military, veterans, and first responders, verified through ID.me at checkout on rhoback.com.

#### How much is the Rhoback military discount?
15% off, applied to eligible full-price orders.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify. Rhoback's page lists active-duty military, veterans, and first responders; it does not specifically address military spouses or dependents, so their eligibility is unverified — check what ID.me will verify for your status.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create or sign in to a free ID.me account and verify at checkout. You don't need to send Rhoback any ID photos or scans.

Rhoback's direct military discount uses **ID.me**. GovX separately lists some co-branded Rhoback collab items for verified members, but that's a different channel.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The verified discount is documented for online checkout on rhoback.com via ID.me. In-store terms at Rhoback's Charlottesville shop aren't documented here — confirm in person.

#### What is excluded?
Rhoback's offer page does not publish an exclusions list. Assume the discount applies to full-price product and does not stack with sales/codes unless the cart shows otherwise.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Rhoback doesn't state this either way. Treat it as non-combinable — ID.me checkout discounts typically occupy the single code slot. If a public sale is deeper than 15%, take the sale.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Rhoback?
Use the ID.me 15% year-round. The only time to skip it is when a public sitewide sale beats 15%.

#### Does Rhoback offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
