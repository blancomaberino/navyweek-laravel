# PGA Tour Superstore — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$500 order** on pgatoursuperstore.com or in a store (e.g., a mid-tier driver, or a club + apparel + accessories mix).

## Headline finding

**PGA Tour Superstore is widely reported to give active and retired military a 10% in-store discount with a valid military ID — but that exact term could NOT be confirmed on any PGA Tour Superstore primary source, so treat the 10% as aggregator-reported, not verified.** The one primary source that exists is negative: **ID.me's own store page states, "We're not aware that PGA TOUR Superstore offers Military discounts,"** which directly **debunks the aggregator claim that you can get "10% off via an ID.me code"** — there is no ID.me service tier, and no GovX/SheerID/WeSalute tier surfaced either. PGA Tour Superstore's site is JavaScript-rendered and its help/pricing pages were not machine-readable during this pass, so the in-store program's exact percentage, eligibility, and exclusions remain **unverified pending a primary-source confirmation**. Separately, the real money at PGA Tour Superstore is not a service badge at all: it's the **trade-in program**, **public promo codes** (open to everyone, e.g. 15% storewide via coupon sites), and **used/pre-owned clubs** — all of which typically beat a 10% badge, and none of which require military status. Archetype: **big-box specialty retailer — modest (unconfirmed) in-store service discount, MAP-restricted catalog, and trade-in + public-promo levers that dwarf the military angle.**
