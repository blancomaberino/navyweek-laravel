# Greyhound — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$60 Greyhound base fare** (typical medium-haul one-way), before service fees.

## Headline finding

Greyhound offers a **real, current 10% military discount** — but it runs **through WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage)**, not as a free open military rate. You must carry a **WeSalute+ Card** (a **paid** premium membership) and select **"WeSalute" as the passenger type** on greyhound.com or the app, entering your unique WeSalute+ Travel Code. The discount is **10% off the base fare only** (not service fees), is **not combinable** with other Greyhound promotions or non-Greyhound connecting services, must be applied **at time of booking** (never retroactively), and requires you to **show your WeSalute+ Card when boarding**. Because WeSalute+ costs an annual fee, the 10% only nets out ahead if you ride often; for occasional riders, Greyhound's **public advance-purchase fares, Road Rewards points, and the "Deals & Promos" page** frequently beat the WeSalute math. Archetype: **I/E (identity discount gated behind a paid membership)** — the honest job is to tell readers when the paid card actually pays for itself.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Not combinable** — the WeSalute+ 10% cannot be combined with other Greyhound promotions or with connecting bus services outside Greyhound. *(wesalute.com/get-greyhound Terms)*
2. **Booking-time only, never retroactive** — you must select "WeSalute" as the passenger type and enter your Travel Code at booking; it can't be added after purchase.
3. **Base fare only** — the 10% applies to the base ticket price, **not service fees**.
4. **Paid membership required** — the 10% is a WeSalute+ benefit; the free WeSalute tier doesn't include it. Family members need their own (50%-off) WeSalute+ cards and Travel Codes.
5. **Proof at boarding** — carry your WeSalute+ Card; the driver may check it.
6. **Public codes are an either/or** — a public Greyhound promo can't stack, so compare and take the single larger discount.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty, retirees, Guard, Reserve, veterans** — Eligible when enrolled in WeSalute+.
- **Immediate family** — Eligible with their own Family WeSalute+ Card (50% off), each with a unique Travel Code.
- **Free WeSalute tier** — Does **not** include the Greyhound 10%; the paid WeSalute+ membership is required.

### How To Redeem Online / In App
1. Enroll in **WeSalute+** and get your WeSalute+ Card and unique **Travel Code**.
2. On greyhound.com or the app, enter your trip and select **"WeSalute" as the passenger type**, then choose **"Show more discounts."**
3. Enter your **WeSalute+ Travel Code** with your first and last name in Passenger Details.
4. Complete the booking — the 10% applies to the base fare at checkout (not retroactively).
5. **Show your WeSalute+ Card when boarding** the bus.

### How To Redeem At the Counter
1. Bring your **WeSalute+ Card** to the Greyhound ticket counter.
2. Request the WeSalute military discount at purchase.
3. Keep the card for boarding.

### How Verification Works
WeSalute verifies your service status once at enrollment and issues a secure WeSalute+ ID Card with a personal Travel Code. The card is a **paid premium membership**; family members can be added at 50% off with their own cards. Greyhound checks the card at boarding. There is no free open military rate.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Base fare only** — service fees are not discounted.
- **Not combinable** with other Greyhound promotions or non-Greyhound connecting services.
- **Applied at booking, never retroactively.**
- Requires a **paid WeSalute+ membership** (annual fee) — factor it into the math.
- Show your WeSalute+ Card at boarding.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Do the membership math** — 10% pays off only if your annual base-fare spend × 10% beats the WeSalute+ fee; add 50%-off family cards if the whole household rides.
- **Book advance-purchase fares** — Greyhound's early-bird public fares often beat a 10%-off walk-up.
- **Check Deals & Promos** — a live public promo may exceed 10% (you can't combine, so take the bigger one).
- **Join Road Rewards** — earn points on paid fares over time.
- **Compare carriers** — for some corridors, other bus lines or a WeSalute partner (e.g., Amtrak/other travel offers) may beat the Greyhound math.

### FAQ

#### Does Greyhound offer a military discount?
Yes — **10% off base fares** for the military community, delivered through a **WeSalute+ Card**. You select "WeSalute" as the passenger type at booking and show the card when you board.

#### How much is the Greyhound military discount?
**10% off the base ticket price** (not service fees). Because it requires a paid WeSalute+ membership, the net benefit depends on how often you ride.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family qualify?
Yes — active, retired, Guard, Reserve, and veterans qualify when enrolled in WeSalute+, and immediate family can be added at 50% off with their own cards and Travel Codes.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through **WeSalute** — enroll, get your WeSalute+ Card and Travel Code, and use it at booking and boarding.

#### Does Greyhound use GovX, ID.me, or WeSalute?
The current live mechanic is **WeSalute** (select "WeSalute" as the passenger type on greyhound.com). GovX has been referenced historically as a verification route but is not what the Greyhound fare flow exposes today.

#### Can I use it online and at the counter?
Both — book online/in-app by selecting the WeSalute passenger type and entering your Travel Code, or present your WeSalute+ Card at the ticket counter.

#### What's excluded?
Service fees, other Greyhound promotions, and connecting services outside Greyhound. The discount isn't retroactive.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale fares?
No — it's **not combinable**. If a public Greyhound promo is larger than 10%, take the promo instead (you can't stack them).

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to ride Greyhound?
If you ride often, the WeSalute+ 10% (plus family cards) is worth the membership. If you ride occasionally, **book advance-purchase public fares** and check Deals & Promos — those usually beat a 10%-off walk-up without any membership fee.

#### Does Greyhound offer a first responder or student discount?
Greyhound's documented community discount here is the WeSalute military benefit; student/first-responder fares aren't part of this offer (WeSalute serves the military community).
