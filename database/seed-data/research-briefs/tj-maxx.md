# T.J. Maxx — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$150 mixed cart** (apparel + a home item at T.J. Maxx markdown prices).

## Headline finding

**T.J. Maxx does not offer a military or veteran discount** — and the same is true across the TJX family (Marshalls, HomeGoods, HomeSense, Sierra). There is no service-member percentage, no promo code, and no military verification (ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute) on tjmaxx.tjx.com or in stores. **ID.me Shop states outright it is "not aware of TJ Maxx offering Military community discounts."** T.J. Maxx is already an off-price retailer, so the genuine savings paths are open to everyone: the **TJX Rewards credit card** (5% back in Rewards at all TJX stores; $10 certificate per 1,000 points), fresh-markdown timing, and — for online orders — the standard **free shipping on $89+** (which is a public threshold, **not** a military perk). Cash-back portals barely cover tjmaxx.tjx.com (roughly **1.5%** where listed, and often gift-card-only). Archetype: **B — no military route at all**, off-price + store card.
