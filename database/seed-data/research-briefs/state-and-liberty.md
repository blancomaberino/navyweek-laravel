# State & Liberty — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$500 apparel order** (e.g., a $515 athletic-fit stretch suit, or ~4 dress shirts) — chosen because it is also the threshold at which State & Liberty donates a dress shirt to the SEAL Future Foundation per military ID.me order.

## Headline finding

State & Liberty offers a real, **brand-published 15% military discount**, verified through **ID.me at online checkout**, for **active, reservist, veteran and retired** military personnel. It is a standing, year-round offer — not a limited-time promo. Crucially, State & Liberty **states it does not run sales** ("We do not offer sales"), and the brand is direct-to-consumer only (not sold at the Exchange, not on GovX, no meaningful cashback-portal presence). That makes the **15% ID.me military discount the genuine best-value path for a service member** — there is no sale to stack against it and no marketplace that beats it. The only competing brand offer is a bulk **dress-shirt** code (`STATEANDLIB3` / `STATEANDLIB5`), which serves a different buyer (multi-shirt orders) and cannot be combined with the ID.me discount. Archetype: **"no-sale DTC brand, ID.me-gated"** — the verified discount is the deal.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The ID.me discount is toggled on at checkout after signing in with your verified account** — it is not a typed code, so it occupies the order's discount slot. (Source: brand military page.)
2. **Only one discount per order.** The brand's bulk dress-shirt codes and the ID.me discount both act as the single order-level discount; there is no documented way to combine them. Assume ID.me does **not** stack with any brand code.
3. **There are no sales to stack against.** The brand explicitly states it does not run sitewide sales, so "sale + military code" is not a real path here.
4. **Credit-card cash-back stacks** with the ID.me discount (it's a payment-side reward, independent of the order discount) — the only legitimate stack.
5. **Gift cards are final sale** and excluded from returns; buying gift cards is not a discount path.

### FAQ

#### Does State & Liberty offer a military discount?

**15% off** your order. First responders and students get 10% through the same ID.me system.

No. It's one discount per order, it doesn't combine with the bulk dress-shirt codes, and the brand doesn't run sales. You can still earn credit-card cash back on top.
