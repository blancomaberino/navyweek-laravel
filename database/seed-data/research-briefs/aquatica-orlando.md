# Aquatica Orlando — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one active-duty family day at Aquatica Orlando — comparing a single-day adult admission (≈$79.99 online gate) against the $139 Military Silver Annual Pass.

## Headline finding

Yes — Aquatica Orlando runs a **real, official** military program through United Parks & Resorts' **Waves of Honor** platform, verified with **ID.me**. Two distinct offers stack conceptually: (1) a **50% off single-park ticket** (limit 4) for active-duty/reservist/Guard, and (2) a **Military Silver Annual Pass for $139** (regularly $234) available to **both active-duty and veterans**, with unlimited admission, no blockout dates, free general parking, ~20% in-park discounts, and 3 free guest tickets on a rolling basis. For any family visiting more than once — or even a single visit for 2+ people — the Silver Pass is the clear winner. Offers must be bought online after ID.me verification; they are **not sold at the park**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The military offers are unlocked by ID.me and bought on aquatica.com — you cannot buy them at the gate. Plan ahead.
2. The Silver Pass's free general parking and ~20% in-park discounts are **built into the pass**, not separate codes — they apply automatically on pass presentation.
3. Free guest tickets (3) are distributed on a rolling calendar and carry their own blockout dates; they are not transferable to buy-down other tickets.
4. The 50%-off single ticket carries "blackout dates may apply"; the Silver Pass has **no blockout dates** for the passholder's own admission.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty US military — eligible for both offers.
- Activated or drilling reservists / National Guard — eligible for both.
- Veterans — eligible for the $139 Silver Pass (not the 50% single ticket).
- Direct dependents/spouses — via the member's ID.me account.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
Online (only channel):
1. Create or log in to ID.me and verify military status.
2. Go to aquatica.com's military discount page and select active-duty or veteran.
3. Choose the 50% single ticket or the $139 Silver Pass; complete purchase online.
4. Spouses/dependents log in under the primary member's account for dependent tickets.

In store: Not available — the offer is not sold at the park.

### How Verification Works
United Parks uses ID.me. Create a free ID.me account, verify once (may require document upload), and eligible pricing unlocks on aquatica.com. Re-verification follows ID.me's own schedule. Support: Aquatica Guest Relations for dependent/caregiver edge cases.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- 50% single ticket: limit 4; blackout dates may apply.
- Silver Pass: no blockout dates for the passholder; free guest tickets have their own blockout dates.
- Offers must be purchased online; not available at the gate.
- Military ID pricing is per verified member; up to 6 Silver Passes per member.

### Best Ways To Save More
- Choose the Silver Pass over single tickets if visiting more than once or in a group — the math almost always favors it.
- Use the 3 free guest tickets and ~20% in-park discounts that come with the pass.
- Non-military guests in your party: check aquatica.com limited-time public offers (Fun Cards, 2-park combos).
- Bundle with other United Parks properties (SeaWorld, Busch Gardens) via Waves of Honor if visiting multiple parks.

### FAQ
#### Does Aquatica Orlando offer a military discount?
Yes — through the Waves of Honor program: 50% off single tickets for active-duty and a $139 Military Silver Annual Pass for active-duty and veterans.

#### How much is the Aquatica Orlando military discount?
50% off a single-park ticket (limit 4), or the Silver Annual Pass at $139 (regularly $234).

#### Do veterans / spouses / dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify for the $139 Silver Pass. Spouses/dependents access tickets under the primary member's ID.me account.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me at aquatica.com — create/log in, verify, and pricing unlocks.

#### Does Aquatica use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
ID.me is the authoritative verification on aquatica.com. Aquatica is also listed on GovX, but confirm pricing against aquatica.com.

#### Can I use it in stores / online?
Online only — the military pricing is not sold at the park gate.

#### What is excluded?
The 50% single ticket has blackout dates and a limit of 4; free guest tickets have their own blackout dates. Veterans cannot use the 50% single ticket.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
The pass benefits (free parking, in-park discounts, guest tickets) are built in; military pricing generally isn't stackable with separate public promo codes.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Aquatica?
For 2+ visits or a family: the $139 Silver Pass. For a solo active-duty one-off: the 50% single ticket (~$40).

#### Does Aquatica run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
The military program runs year-round; public seasonal offers appear around holidays. Verify current offers on aquatica.com.
