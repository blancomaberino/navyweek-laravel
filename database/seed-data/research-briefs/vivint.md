# Vivint — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a typical new Vivint Smart Home starter system — ~$599–$999 in equipment financed at 0% APR, plus 24/7 professional monitoring at ~$44.99/mo.

## Headline finding

Vivint does **not publish a flat percentage "military discount"** on its own site. What it actually offers military households is two distinct things: (1) a **military-family contract-flexibility policy** (special contract exceptions/benefits for deployed members — documented in a separate "Military Policy" PDF, not a price cut), and (2) an **active third-party military offer via GovX** — historically "professional installation at no extra cost plus your first four months of monitoring covered (up to ~$399 value) with promo code GOVX4," redeemed by calling Vivint's GovX line. The single best path for a service member is to **call in through the GovX offer** (verify military status on GovX, then phone Vivint) rather than checking out online, because the monitoring-credit + free-install bundle is worth more than any on-site "special." This is the alarm-industry archetype: no published percent, value delivered as install waivers + monitoring credits through a partner line, and everything is negotiated on the phone.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **GovX offer is redeemed by phone, not online checkout** — you verify military status on GovX, then call Vivint's dedicated line and give the promo code. It is not a stackable cart coupon.
2. **The military offer and the standard public promo overlap** — free install is already the public default; the military add-on is the **monitoring-months credit**. Don't assume you get "double free install."
3. **Contract flexibility ≠ discount** — the deployed-member policy changes cancellation/relocation terms, not the price. Read the Military Policy PDF.
4. **0% APR financing** applies to equipment regardless of military status and doesn't reduce total price — it spreads it.

#### Does Vivint offer a military discount?
Not as a flat percentage. It offers a GovX bundle (free install + a few months of monitoring, up to ~$399 value) and a contract-flexibility policy for deployed members.

#### How much is the Vivint military discount?
Historically up to about **$399** in value via GovX (free professional install + first ~4 months of monitoring), not a percent off equipment.

#### Do veterans and military families qualify?
Yes — veterans via GovX/ID.me verification, and deployed active-duty families under Vivint's Military Policy contract terms.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me (Vivint's site) or GovX for the GovX offer, then finalize by phone with a Vivint rep.
