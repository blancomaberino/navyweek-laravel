# Arlo — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Arlo Pro 5S 2-camera security kit (~$249.99 hardware, before tax).

## Headline finding

Arlo (the home-security camera brand — not the unrelated Arlo Hotels) offers an **official 15% military discount** on Arlo.com hardware, verified through **ID.me** at checkout. The ID.me storefront lists it as **"15% Off for Military on all full priced and sale items,"** which is unusually generous in that it explicitly includes sale-priced hardware. The one thing to know: the discount applies to **hardware only** — Arlo Secure **subscriptions are not discounted**, and community threads consistently report frustration that the ongoing cloud subscription (often the larger lifetime cost) carries no military rate. Best play: buy hardware during a sale and stack the ID.me 15% on the sale price; treat the subscription as a separate, full-price decision.
