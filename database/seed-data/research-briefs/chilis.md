> Slug: `chilis` · Researched: June 24, 2026 · Sources checked: June 24, 2026

## Executive Summary

Chili's Grill & Bar's verified military offer is a **limited-time free Veterans Day meal**, not an everyday discount. On Veterans Day (November 11, 2026), all veterans and active military members can choose a free entrée from a limited menu at participating Chili's restaurants. The offer is **dine-in only**, valid only on 11/11, allows no substitutions, cannot be combined with other offers, and excludes beverages and gratuity. Eligibility is stated on the official page as "veterans & active military members." Notably, the official Chili's page does **not** state an ID requirement and does **not** advertise any everyday/year-round military discount. Claims of a 10–20% everyday in-store military discount appear only on coupon/aggregator sites and are unverified (location discretion at most). The single most important caveat: this is a one-day free meal, not a standing discount, and terms/participation vary by location — call ahead.

### FAQ

#### Does Chili's offer a military discount?
Chili's offers a **free Veterans Day meal** on November 11 to veterans and active military members. It does not advertise a nationwide everyday military or veteran discount; any year-round in-store discount is at an individual location's discretion. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day), accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### How much is the Chili's military discount?
On Veterans Day, eligible guests get one **free entrée** from a limited menu (a 100% discount on that item; beverages and gratuity excluded). Chili's does not publish a standing percentage-off military discount. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and active military members qualify per the official page. Spouses and dependents are **not stated** as eligible for the free meal. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)

#### How do I verify my status?
The official page does not describe a verification step or ID requirement. Verification, if any, is handled in person; bringing a military ID, VA card, or DD-214 is recommended to avoid issues. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)

#### Does Chili's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. The Chili's Veterans Day offer is redeemed in person with no third-party verification service mentioned. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)

#### Can I use the offer for takeout or delivery?
No. The Veterans Day meal is **dine-in only**, available in-restaurant. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)

#### What is excluded?
Beverages and gratuity are not included; no substitutions; cannot be combined with other offers; valid only on 11/11/2026; dine-in only. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, rewards, or "3 for Me"?
No. The official terms state the Veterans Day meal "cannot be combined with other offers." (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)

#### Does Chili's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount is published on Chili's official site. (Source: not stated in official source, accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### What menu items are included in the free Veterans Day meal?
Chili's lists a limited set of entrées for the free meal, including Chili's Chicken Crispers, Chili's Chicken Bacon Ranch Quesadillas, Oldtimer® Burger with Cheese, and a soup & House Salad option. The exact list can change year to year — confirm on the official page. (Source: [Chili's Veterans Day page](https://www.chilis.com/restaurant-events/veterans-day).)
