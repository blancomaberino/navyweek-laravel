# NFL RedZone — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 2026-season worth of NFL RedZone access for a service member, via the most common delivery paths.

## Headline finding

There is **no standalone military discount on NFL RedZone as its own product** — RedZone isn't sold as a true standalone subscription; you get it bundled. The verified, primary-source military benefit that *includes* RedZone is the **NFL Sunday Ticket on YouTube military/veteran/first-responder/medical/teacher offer via ID.me**: $198 for the 2026 season (vs. a much higher public price), plus **+$42 to add NFL RedZone** (returning ID.me subscribers renew at $198, or $240 bundled with RedZone). RedZone is otherwise delivered through **NFL+ Premium** ($99.99/season or $14.99/mo, which includes RedZone) or a pay-TV package. NFL's own support documents a Military/First Responder discount activation via ID.me for NFL subscriptions, but the exact NFL+ percentage isn't published on an accessible primary page, and ID.me's Shop catalog notes it isn't aware of an NFL+ community discount — so the fully verified RedZone-inclusive military deal is the Sunday Ticket + $42 RedZone add-on. Best path: if you want the whole Sunday-afternoon experience, the ID.me Sunday Ticket bundle is the clear win; if you only want RedZone, NFL+ Premium is the simplest.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The ID.me discount attaches to NFL Sunday Ticket**, not to RedZone itself; **RedZone is a paid add-on** (+$42 one-time / +$3.50 mo) on top of the discounted Sunday Ticket. (YouTube Help.)
2. **You don't need to be a YouTube TV subscriber** to buy the discounted Sunday Ticket via Primetime Channels. (ID.me hosted page.)
3. **NFL+ Premium already includes RedZone** — you don't add it separately there; but there's no *verified* military percentage on NFL+ Premium's price.
4. **Locally broadcast and national games are excluded** from Sunday Ticket (out-of-market only); no refunds for partial seasons. (YouTube Help.)
5. **Returning ID.me subscribers** renew at $198 (or $240 bundled with RedZone); the offer auto-renews at the eligible price.
