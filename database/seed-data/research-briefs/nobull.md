# NOBULL Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

NOBULL (training footwear, apparel, and nutrition) offers verified U.S. military members **20% off every time you shop**, sitewide, through its **Community Discount Program** verified by **SheerID** — the discount applies automatically at checkout once you've verified, with no promo code needed. The same 20% program also covers medical professionals, nurses, hospital employees, first responders, teachers, students, and fitness/nutrition professionals. NOBULL also runs a **separate ID.me verification path** (on its `/pages/nobull-id-me` page) that covers the same broad audience plus government employees; for the ID.me path the discount code is generated after verification at checkout. The single most important caveat: the discount is **U.S.-only, pre-tax, applies to in-stock items only, excludes gift cards and shipping, and cannot be combined with ID.me or any other offer or discount.**

> Accuracy notes: (1) NOBULL operates **two parallel verification systems** — **SheerID** (the Community Discount Program, which NOBULL's own FAQ ties to "20% off") and **ID.me** (a distinct page with its own terms). NOBULL's FAQ explicitly says the SheerID discount "can't be combined with ID.me," confirming they are mutually exclusive paths, not the same flow. (2) The **exact ID.me percentage is not numerically stated** on NOBULL's ID.me page (its terms say "eligible % off" generically); only the **SheerID/Community path is confirmed at 20%** via NOBULL's FAQ. Treat 20% as the confirmed military figure. (3) Some search snippets reference a limited-time **"30% off military through 6/18"** promotion — this was **not confirmed on any NOBULL primary source** during research and is treated as **unverified/likely stale**; do not assert it. (4) NOBULL is direct-to-consumer (online) with no traditional retail-store network, so there is **no in-store military-discount flow** to document.

3. **Apply that code at checkout** to redeem the eligible % off (applied evenly across eligible items, pre-tax).
4. Note: the ID.me path **cannot be combined** with the SheerID community discount or any other offer.

### FAQ

#### Does NOBULL offer a military discount?
Yes. Verified U.S. military members get 20% off every time they shop, sitewide, through NOBULL's Community Discount Program verified by SheerID. NOBULL also offers a separate ID.me verification path.

#### How much is the NOBULL military discount?
20% off, per NOBULL's official FAQ for the SheerID Community Discount Program. The ID.me path advertises "eligible % off" without stating a number on NOBULL's ID.me page; treat 20% as the confirmed figure.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify — NOBULL's ID.me page explicitly lists "Service Member, Retiree, Veteran." Military spouses and dependents are not named in NOBULL's official sources, so verify through SheerID or ID.me rather than assuming.

#### How do I verify my status?
For the 20% Community Discount, go to NOBULL's Community Discount Program page and verify with SheerID — the discount then applies automatically at checkout. For the ID.me path, verify through ID.me at checkout and apply the generated code.

#### Does NOBULL use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
NOBULL uses **both SheerID** (its Community Discount Program, the path tied to "20% off") **and ID.me** (a separate page/flow). They can't be combined. NOBULL's own pages don't reference GovX or WeSalute for its first-party program. (Note: GovX lists a NOBULL government/military storefront separately; that is GovX's marketplace, not NOBULL's checkout program — verify independently.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
NOBULL has no retail stores; it's online-only. Use the discount at nobullproject.com checkout.

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards, e-gift cards, shipping & handling, out-of-stock items, and previous purchases. The discount is U.S.-only, applies pre-tax, and can't be combined with ID.me or other offers.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. NOBULL states the discount "cannot be combined with any other offers or discounts," including the ID.me path. Compare the community discount against current sale prices before checkout.

#### Does NOBULL offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, student, or government discount?
Yes. The same program covers first responders (EMT, paramedic, firefighter, law enforcement), nurses/medical professionals/hospital employees, teachers, students, and fitness/nutrition professionals via SheerID; the ID.me path additionally covers government employees (federal/state/local).
