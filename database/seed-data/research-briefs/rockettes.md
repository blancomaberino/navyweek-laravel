**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult ticket to the *Christmas Spectacular Starring the Radio City Rockettes* at Radio City Music Hall, NYC (face value typically ~$59–$179 depending on date/seat).

## Headline finding

There is a **verified military ticket channel** for the Christmas Spectacular, but **no single published percentage**. Savings come through two verification marketplaces — **ID.me Shop** (Radio City is a listed ID.me partner) and **GovX** (military/government member ticket pricing) — plus **Vet Tix** for donated/free seats to verified service members and veterans. The discount is *variable and gated*: you see the member price only after verifying, and it moves with performance date, seat zone, and inventory. Archetype: a live-entertainment property that outsources military pricing to third-party verification marketplaces rather than posting a flat rate. The single best path depends on flexibility — Vet Tix (if you land tickets) is cheapest; GovX/ID.me give predictable member pricing.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
Online (per channel):
1. **GovX** — create a free account, verify, buy Christmas Spectacular tickets at member pricing.
2. **ID.me Shop** — open the Radio City listing, verify with ID.me, follow the offer link.

In store: box-office tickets are sold at face value; military pricing is a verified-online path.

### How Verification Works
GovX and ID.me verify military/government status with a free account; Vet Tix independently verifies service members and veterans. Each provider governs re-verification and support. The show itself does not verify at the box office for a discount.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
