# Safeway — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$150 weekly grocery run** at a Safeway store.

## Headline finding

**Safeway (Albertsons) has no published, chain-wide everyday military discount.** What officially exists is a **SheerID-verified "community & profession" status program** on safeway.com — military and veterans are listed among the verifiable groups — but the benefit is unpublished "exclusive offers, Points, and deals" inside a Safeway for U account, **not a stated percentage**. The 10%-off stories circulating online are real but historical and regional: a Denver-division **10% first-Saturday-of-the-month** military discount (2020–2023, now filed as expired by the local tracker that covered it) and one-day **~10% Veterans Day** discounts in some regions and years. Archetype: **the "myth" brand (H)** — aggregators promote a flat everyday % that doesn't exist chain-wide; the real everyday value is the free **Safeway for U** loyalty program, and for eligible readers the **military commissary** beats any grocery-store discount outright.

### Intro
Does Safeway have a military discount? **Not a standing one.** Despite what coupon sites claim, Safeway publishes no everyday military percentage. What Safeway officially offers is status verification through SheerID: military members and veterans (along with students, teachers, first responders, and seniors) can verify inside a free Safeway for U account to unlock member offers and Points — with no published discount amount.

The 10% you may have heard about was real, but limited: Safeway's Denver division ran 10% off for military shoppers on the first Saturday of each month (the local deal site that tracked it now lists it as expired), and some stores have run one-day ~10% discounts on Veterans Day. Neither is a chain-wide promise. The honest everyday plays: the free for U loyalty program for everyone, and — if you have access — the military commissary, which beats any supermarket discount. ### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty / Reserve / National Guard / retirees** — Can verify via SheerID for the status program; the historical in-store 10% covered "active duty, reserve and retired" with ID.
- **Veterans** — Can verify via SheerID ("at one time have been working for the United States Armed Services," per Safeway's FAQ).
- **Military spouses / dependents** — Not stated by Safeway's FAQ; SheerID offers sometimes include family — check the verification flow.
- **First responders, teachers, students, seniors** — Separately verifiable groups in the same program.
- **Commissary access (substitute path)** — active/retired/Guard/Reserve + families; plus veterans with service-connected disabilities, Purple Heart recipients, former POWs, and registered VA caregivers (in-store, since 2020).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Create or sign in to a free **Safeway for U** account (safeway.com or the app).
2. Go to **Account → Discount programs** (safeway.com/customer-account/discount-programs) and pick your status (military/veteran).
3. Complete **SheerID** verification — basic details, plus documents if requested. Do **not** upload photos of an active-duty military ID card (federal law prohibits photocopying; use alternate documents SheerID lists).
4. Verified member offers, Points promotions, and deals appear in your for U account — clip and use like any other deal.

### How To Redeem In Store (historical promos)
1. If a Veterans Day (or other regional) military discount is running, bring **military/veteran ID**.
2. Mention the discount at checkout — historically it was applied to the final total after sales and coupons.
3. Expect exclusions: prescriptions, alcohol, tobacco, gift cards, money orders, lottery, fuel and similar.
4. Participation varies by store and year — call ahead.

### How Verification Works
Safeway uses **SheerID** inside the for U account. SheerID confirms status from basic personal information, requests documents when auto-verification fails, and permanently deletes submitted documents after verification; Safeway may retain the verification result for the discount period. In-store historical promos were simpler: show ID at the register.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No published discount amount for the status program — don't expect a flat % at checkout.
- Historical in-store %s excluded prescriptions, alcohol, tobacco, gift cards, money orders, bus passes, ski tickets, lottery, postage, and fuel (per the Denver-division terms).
- Regional divisions set their own in-store promos; nothing here is chain-wide or guaranteed.
- SheerID cannot accept uploads of active-duty military ID cards — have a DD-214, VA card, or other listed document ready.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Shop the commissary if you're eligible** — at-cost-plus-5%-surcharge pricing is the genuine military grocery discount; DeCA cites average savings around 25%.
- **Work Safeway for U hard** — clip weekly personalized deals, redeem Points for grocery or fuel discounts; it's free and open to everyone.
- **Stack manufacturer coupons on for U prices** where the coupon policy allows.
- **Government-assistance households** get FreshPass (unlimited free delivery subscription) at half price.
- **Watch November** — if your region runs a Veterans Day discount, it historically applied after all other savings, making it the one day worth timing a big shop.

### FAQ

#### Does Safeway offer a military discount?
Not a standing published one. Safeway's official offering is SheerID status verification inside a free Safeway for U account, which unlocks unpublished member offers for military and veterans. Regional 10% promos (first Saturdays in the Denver division, some Veterans Days) have existed but are historical and not chain-wide.

#### How much is the Safeway military discount?
Safeway publishes no amount. The verified-status program's offers vary; the historical regional discounts were 10%. Any site quoting a current flat "10% at Safeway" is citing an expired regional program.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Yes — Safeway's FAQ says the discount program covers anyone who is serving or "at one time" served in the U.S. Armed Services, verified through SheerID.

#### Does Safeway use ID.me or SheerID?
SheerID, inside the Safeway for U account. There is no ID.me or GovX relationship.

#### Does Safeway give a discount on Veterans Day?
Sometimes, somewhere: individual regions/stores have run ~10% off for military and veterans on November 11 (with ID; groceries only, standard exclusions). There was no verified chain-wide 2025 offer. Call your local store in early November.

#### Can I combine a military discount with coupons and sale prices?
Historically yes — the Denver-division 10% was applied "at the end of the bill, after all savings." Status-program offers combine like normal clipped deals.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a military family to buy groceries?
The commissary, if you have access (active/retired/Guard/Reserve families, plus disabled veterans, Purple Heart recipients, former POWs, and VA caregivers): at-cost-plus-5%-surcharge pricing beats any supermarket discount. Otherwise: Safeway for U deals, or a low-price substitute like Walmart/WinCo for staples.

#### Does Safeway offer senior, student, teacher, or first-responder discounts?
Those groups can verify through the same SheerID program for member offers; like the military tier, no flat percentage is published.
