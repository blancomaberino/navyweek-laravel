# Birch Aquarium at Scripps — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** **two adult admissions** to Birch Aquarium at Scripps (online adult rate **$34.95** each = **$69.90**).

## Headline finding

**Birch Aquarium at Scripps offers a real, self-serve military discount: $3 off per ticket** for active, reserve, retired service members and veterans, redeemed with **promo code `MILITARY`** when you buy online in advance (valid for **up to 6 tickets**, with a **current U.S. military ID shown at entry**). There is **no third-party verification** (no ID.me/GovX) — it's an honor-code promo confirmed at the door. Birch Aquarium does **not appear to participate in Blue Star Museums** (the free-summer-admission program) — its published military benefit is the $3-off code, not free admission. The genuinely bigger saver for eligible families is usually the **on-base ITT/MWR ticket office**, which Birch's own page says sells discounted Birch tickets, or **buying online on a select low-demand date** (Birch advertises "**save up to $20**" on some online dates/times — a discount that can beat the $3 military code outright). Archetype: **attraction with a small flat military discount + a stronger public online-pricing lever + a base-ticket-office channel**.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **aquarium.ucsd.edu** and choose **Buy Tickets**.
2. Select your date/time and add up to **6** admissions.
3. Enter promo code **`MILITARY`** at checkout for **$3 off each ticket**.
4. Bring a **current U.S. military ID** to show at entry.

*(Tip: before applying the code, check whether a "save up to $20" online date/time is lower — you can only use one.)*

### How To Redeem via Base Ticket Office
1. Visit your installation's **ITT/MWR (Information, Tickets & Travel)** office.
2. Ask for **Birch Aquarium** tickets — pricing varies by base and often beats the online military rate.
3. Purchase there; no online code needed.

### How Verification Works
There is **no third-party verification** (no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID). You apply the `MILITARY` code yourself when buying online, and staff verify a **current U.S. military ID at the entrance**. Buy in advance — the code is not applied at the gate.

#### Does Birch Aquarium offer a military discount?
Yes — **$3 off each admission** for active, reserve, retired service members and veterans, using promo code **`MILITARY`** online, valid for up to 6 tickets, with a current U.S. military ID shown at entry.

#### How much is the Birch Aquarium military discount?
**$3 off per ticket.** On the $34.95 online adult rate, that's $31.95 per adult.

#### Do veterans and families qualify?

#### How do I verify my status?
No online verification — you enter the `MILITARY` code yourself and show a **current U.S. military ID at the entrance.**

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a military family to visit?
