## Headline finding

**onX has a real, published 50% military discount** across its three apps — onX Hunt, onX Offroad, and onX Backcountry — verified through **ID.me** (current and former military), with 40% tiers for first responders, government employees, and students. Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount)** with strong **C traits** (the public sale sometimes beats the military rate — price-check before verifying).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The ID.me discount is applied at purchase, not layered** — there's no code to combine with sale prices; the ID.me checkout price is the price.
2. **No discount on active memberships**: onX support states there's no way to apply a discount to an active membership — cancel auto-renew, let it lapse, repurchase via ID.me. Plan around your season (don't lapse mid-hunt).
3. **Public sale codes (e.g. `PEAK70`) are an alternative, not a stack** — pick sale or ID.me, whichever is deeper for your app.
4. **Renewal behavior after a discounted purchase is not stated** — assume the discount is purchase-time only and re-run the ID.me flow at renewal; verify in account settings.
5. **App-store purchases**: redemption steps route through onX's website checkout (Billing tab); buying inside Apple/Google app stores may bypass the ID.me price — buy on the web.
6. **Elite pro-deals stack conceptually** — an Elite membership (bought at 50% off) unlocks partner-brand "exclusive pricing," compounding the value of choosing Elite over Premium.

### How To Redeem Online (only channel)
1. Start the **7-day free trial** if you're new — decide your app and tier first.
2. Go to the onX Hunt/Offroad/Backcountry offer via ID.me Shop (or onX's military page) and pick your membership.
3. On the checkout **Billing tab**, click **"Verify with ID.me"** and sign in; the discount applies before you pay.
4. Buy on the **website**, not inside the iOS/Android app, so the ID.me price applies.
5. **Existing members:** cancel auto-renew, let the membership expire, then repurchase through the ID.me flow — onX cannot discount an active membership.

### How Verification Works
ID.me verifies current/former military status once against your ID.me account; at onX checkout you sign into ID.me and the discount is applied to that purchase. Whether the discounted rate persists on auto-renewal is not stated — assume you'll re-verify/repurchase at each term until onX says otherwise.

### FAQ
#### Does onX offer a military discount?
Yes — 50% off onX Hunt, onX Offroad, and onX Backcountry memberships for current and former U.S. military, verified through ID.me at checkout on onX's website.

#### How much is the onX military discount?
50% off memberships. On onX Hunt that's Elite for about $50/year (list $99.99) or Premium for about $17.50/year (list $34.99). First responders, government employees, and students get 40%.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Yes — onX's offer covers "current/former military members" through ID.me's military verification.

#### Does onX use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
ID.me is the verification provider for the direct discount. A GovX brand page for onX also exists, but its offer details sit behind GovX membership — the documented path is ID.me.

#### Can I apply the discount to my existing onX membership?
No. onX support states there's no way to apply a discount to an active membership — cancel auto-renew, let it expire, then repurchase through the ID.me flow.

#### Does the military discount work on all three onX apps?
Yes — ID.me lists offers for onX Hunt, onX Offroad, and onX Backcountry (military 50%; Hunt and Backcountry confirmed at 50% explicitly).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sales?
No. It's the ID.me price or the sale price, whichever is lower. Right now (July 14, 2026) Backcountry's public `PEAK70` code at 70% off beats the military rate; Hunt has no live public sale that does.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to get onX Hunt Elite?
Absent a ≥50% public Hunt sale, the ID.me military discount: about $50/year. Start with the free 7-day trial, buy on the website through ID.me, and re-run the flow at renewal.
