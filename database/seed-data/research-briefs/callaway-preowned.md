# Callaway Pre-Owned — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one certified pre-owned driver at ~$265 (a typical CPO club purchase).

## Headline finding

**Callaway Golf Pre-Owned (CPO — callawaygolfpreowned.com) does not have its own military discount.** Callaway's real, verified **15% military discount runs on CallawayGolf.com only**, via ID.me, and is capped at $1,000 of discount per person per year — the CPO site is a separate storefront and is **not** covered. ID.me confirms it directly: "We're not aware that Callaway Golf Preowned offers Military discounts." Archetype: **B (no military route on this specific site)**, where the honest fix is a **substitute path** — the sibling Callaway 15% on *new* gear — plus CPO's own already-deep pre-owned pricing.

So the maximum-savings question splits by what you're buying:
- **Want a specific *new* Callaway/Odyssey club?** Buy it on **CallawayGolf.com with the verified 15% military discount** (ID.me), not on CPO.
- **Want the cheapest club overall (open to used)?** **CPO's condition-graded pre-owned pricing** (certified used, up to ~45–49% off in clearance) usually beats 15% off a new club outright — plus CPO's **Trade-In/Trade-Up** program lowers the net further, and its Rewards give free shipping over $199. There's no military discount to stack on CPO, so the pre-owned price *is* the deal.

Ignore aggregator "Callaway Pre-Owned military 40% off" claims — that's a conflation of ID.me *cash-back* marketing and CPO's public clearance, **not** a verified CPO military discount.
