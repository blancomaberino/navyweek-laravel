# NFL Sunday Ticket — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 2026-season NFL Sunday Ticket subscription, standalone on YouTube Primetime Channels (no existing YouTube TV base plan).

> Scope note: This brief covers **NFL Sunday Ticket**, the out-of-market *streaming* package now sold exclusively by **YouTube / YouTube TV** (tv.youtube.com/learn/nflsundayticket). It is NOT about buying tickets to attend NFL games. DirecTV's old Sunday Ticket military pricing is obsolete — DirecTV lost the rights after the 2022 season; YouTube has been the exclusive home since 2023.

## Headline finding

Yes — there is a real, **official** military/hero discount, and it is aggressive. Through **ID.me**, eligible U.S. Military Members, Veterans, First Responders, Medical Community, and Teachers can buy NFL Sunday Ticket for the 2026 season for **$198** (vs. the $480 returning-user standalone price, or the $240 new-user promo). It is a genuine brand-run offer, not a discretionary or aggregator claim. The single cheapest legitimate path, however, is the **Student Plan at $119** (SheerID-verified) — so an active-duty service member who is also an enrolled college student saves most there. There is no stacking, no cashback, and no exchange path — this is a verified-eligibility digital subscription, so the decision is simply: are you a student ($119), a hero-tier member ($198), or neither ($240 new-user promo)?

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Verified-eligibility pricing does not stack** with the new-user promo or any other discount — you pick one price at checkout based on your verification.
2. **Student vs. hero are mutually exclusive at checkout** — you buy through one flow (SheerID student *or* ID.me hero). If you qualify for both, the Student Plan is cheaper ($119 < $198).
3. **RedZone is an add-on, not a stack** — $42 on the hero plan (→ $240) or ~$10 on the student plan; it's an upcharge, not a discount.
4. **Hero pricing is sold only as a YouTube Primetime Channel** and cannot be purchased on a Brand Account; existing non-verified auto-renewals must be cancelled first, then re-purchased through the ID.me link to get $198.
5. No third-party portal (GovX/WeSalute) resells this — buying anywhere other than YouTube via ID.me/SheerID is not the official discount.

Yes. Through ID.me, U.S. military members and veterans (plus first responders, medical workers, and teachers) can buy the 2026 season for $198.
