# MGM Resorts — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **2-night leisure stay in a standard room at an MGM Las Vegas property** — **$300 pre-tax room subtotal** (2 nights × ~$150 standard rate). A daily resort fee (~$45–$55/night) and Clark County room tax (~13.38%) are added on top and are **not** discounted by any military rate. All room-rate math below is on the $300 room subtotal.

## Headline finding

MGM Resorts (mgmresorts.com) runs a real, first-party **Military & Veterans Program (MVP)**. The headline room benefit is a **10% discount off standard room rates**, bookable online on MGM's own military-offers page and honored by showing a valid military/veteran ID at check-in — **there is no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID gate**. The program also carries a **25% spa/salon discount**, ~**10% off select food & beverage**, and historically discounted show tickets, and it **auto-enrolls eligible members at MGM Rewards Pearl status** (the loyalty tier that normally takes 20,000 tier credits). Eligible groups: active duty, National Guard, Reserve, retired military, veterans, and spouses.

Archetype: **I — travel/loyalty (status stacks, rates don't)**, with a secondary **C flavor** (the modest 10% is often beaten by other paths). The honest nuance: **10% off the room is thin**, and a direct competitor — **Caesars Entertainment, which offers up to 15% off the lowest rate via ID.me** — frequently beats it property-for-property. The two genuinely valuable MGM plays are (1) the **permanent-feeling Pearl status** (free self-parking alone can outrun the 10% on a short stay) and (2) the fact that the MVP rate is a **plain online-booked rate with no verification gate**, so it can **stack with a cashback portal** (Rakuten ~3%, as of June 2026) and **card-linked offers** (e.g. Amex Offers) in a way that ID.me-gated competitor rates often can't. Do **not** repeat the aggregator "**up to 25% off**" framing as a room discount — the 25% is **spa only**; rooms are **10%**.

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **The MVP room rate is a plain online-booked rate with no verification gate** — because there's no ID.me/coupon-code step, a **cashback portal (Rakuten ~3%) generally tracks normally**, so military rate + cashback **can stack**. (Contrast: Caesars' ID.me-gated rate frequently *breaks* portal tracking.) Confirm the property is cashback-eligible — Rakuten excludes Beau Rivage, NoMad, Mirage, and all regional properties.
2. **Discounted room rates are mutually exclusive** — you book *one* rate (MVP 10%, a public promo rate, or a Rewards member offer), not several. Pick the lowest.
3. **Pearl status stacks on everything** — it's a loyalty tier, not a rate. Free self-parking, the 10% Slot Dollars bonus, and non-expiring points apply on top of whatever rate you book. This is the durable win.
4. **Card-linked offers (Amex/Capital One) stack** with any rate — they trigger on folio spend, independent of the room discount.
5. **Resort fees and room tax are never discounted** by the military rate — the 10% applies to the nightly room rate only.
6. **Book direct to keep the benefits** — third-party OTAs forfeit MGM Rewards credit, Pearl perks, and MGM's best-rate/72-hour-cancellation terms.
7. **Spa 25% / F&B 10% are separate on-property benefits**, redeemed with the MVP card — not combinable with the room rate (different transactions).

### FAQ

#### Does MGM Resorts offer a military discount?
Yes. MGM runs a first-party **Military & Veterans Program (MVP)** with **10% off standard room rates**, 25% off spa/salon, ~10% off select dining, and automatic MGM Rewards **Pearl status** for active duty, Guard, Reserve, retired military, veterans, and spouses.

#### How much is the MGM military discount?
**10% off standard room rates.** The 25% figure some sites cite is the **spa/salon** discount, not a room rate. Resort fees and room tax aren't discounted.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans and spouses are named eligible groups; verify veteran status with a VA ID, DD-214, or veteran-designated state ID. Dependents aren't separately named beyond spouses — confirm at the MGM Rewards desk.

#### How do I verify my status?
**In person** — show a valid military or veteran ID at check-in (for the room rate) or at an MGM Rewards desk (for the MVP card and Pearl status).

#### Does MGM use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** MGM verifies with a government/military ID in person. Online verification via ID.me is **Caesars'** mechanic, not MGM's — don't assume it for MGM.

#### Can I use it online or only in person?
You **book the discounted room rate online** on MGM's military-offers page, then **verify your ID at check-in**. The MVP card, spa, and dining benefits are handled on property.

#### What's excluded?
Resort fees and room tax aren't discounted, discounted rates don't stack with each other, and the launch-era show percentages are historical. Book direct to keep Rewards/Pearl perks.

#### Can I combine it with cashback or a credit-card offer?
Yes — because MGM's rate has no ID.me/coupon gate, a **cashback portal (Rakuten ~3%)** and a **card-linked offer (Amex/Capital One)** can generally stack with it on a direct booking. You can't stack two *room rates*, though.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book MGM?
Book the **MVP 10% rate direct**, route it through a **cashback portal**, and load a **card-linked offer** — roughly $262 on our $300 room baseline before fees/tax, plus free self-parking from Pearl. But **price-check Caesars' up-to-15% ID.me rate** first; it often wins on the room alone.

#### Does MGM run a Veterans Day or seasonal military promo?
MGM has posted enhanced, **time-boxed** military room windows (community reports cite summer and early-November windows), but depth is unverified. The base 10% appears year-round; re-check the official page ~2 weeks before Veterans Day.
