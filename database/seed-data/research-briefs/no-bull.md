# NOBULL — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of full-price NOBULL Trainers at ~$129 (the everyday unit shoppers compare).

## Headline finding

**NOBULL offers a strong, official 20% military discount, verified through SheerID (its "Community Discount Program") and also available via ID.me** — for active-duty, veterans, reservists, retirees, and (per GovX's listing) military family members. The same 20% program also covers medical professionals/nurses/hospital staff, first responders, students/faculty, teachers, and fitness/nutrition professionals. It applies **on full-price, in-stock items only, pre-tax**, and it **cannot be combined with ID.me or any other offer.** Archetype: **E (multi-group identity discount via SheerID)** with a secondary **C (frequent deep sales that the 20% can't touch).**

The maximum-savings nuance: NOBULL runs regular sale/outlet events (frequently 30–50% off), and because the 20% is full-price-only and non-combinable, **during those sales the public clearance price beats the 20% military discount** — the verified shopper should buy the sale item at public price rather than pay full price minus 20%. GovX also lists NOBULL, but it's the **same 20%** through a different account, not a bigger deal or a stack. Ignore aggregator "NOBULL 30–50% military code" pages — the verified military term is a flat **20% on full-price items.**

### FAQ

#### Does NOBULL offer a military discount?
Yes — a 20% military discount on full-price, in-stock items, verified through SheerID, ID.me, or GovX.

#### How much is the NOBULL military discount?
20% off full-price items. It doesn't apply to sale/outlet/clearance items and can't be combined with other offers.

#### Do veterans, reservists, and family members qualify?
Active duty, veterans, reservists, and retirees qualify; NOBULL's GovX storefront also lists military family members.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through SheerID on NOBULL's Community Discount Program page, or via ID.me, or via a free GovX account.

#### Does NOBULL use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
All three. SheerID powers the Community Discount Program; ID.me and GovX are alternative routes to the same 20%.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
The discount is redeemed online at nobullproject.com and in the app.

#### What is excluded?
Sale/outlet/clearance items, gift cards, e-gift cards, shipping & handling, and previous purchases. The 20% is pre-tax and full-price only.

#### Can I combine the 20% with a sale or promo code?
No. It's full-price only and can't be combined with ID.me or other offers. During NOBULL's 30–50% sales, buy the public sale price instead — it usually beats full-price-minus-20%.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from NOBULL?
On full-price current releases, the verified **20%**. When a 30–50% sale/outlet event is live, buy the **public sale price** — it beats the 20% and they don't stack. If you qualify as a fitness/nutrition pro, check that tier for a bigger discount.
