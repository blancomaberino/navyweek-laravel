# Six Flags Great America (Gurnee, IL) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **adult single-day admission** to Six Flags Great America (Gurnee, Illinois), gate price **$90**.

> **Scope note:** This brief is the **Gurnee, Illinois** park (sixflags.com/greatamerica). Do **not** confuse it with **California's Great America** (Santa Clara, CA — sixflags.com/cagreatamerica), a separate park with its own pricing and Military Days. For the chain-wide picture and other parks, see NavyWeek's [Six Flags Military Discount](/discount/six-flags-military-discount/) brief; this page cross-links rather than duplicates it.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Six Flags Great America offers a real, verified military discount, and one weekend a year it's free.** Year-round, **active and retired U.S. military and first responders** buy a discounted single-day ticket **online only** after verifying through **ID.me**: the park's **Military & First Responder ticket is $45** vs. a **$90 gate** price (Hurricane Harbor waterpark tickets **$29**), and an eligible buyer can purchase **up to 6 tickets** for family and friends. Around **Memorial Day weekend (Fri May 22 – Mon May 25, 2026)**, **all active and retired military get free single-day admission** by showing a valid U.S. Military ID at Guest Services — the best deal of the year when your visit lines up. The one hard rule: the military discount **can't be combined with any other offer, including sales**, and is **never sold at the front gate** (except the free-admission appreciation days). Archetype: **regional theme park with an ID.me online discount plus a free Memorial-Day appreciation window** — the play is *go free on Memorial Day weekend if you can; otherwise weigh the $45 ticket against a season pass.*

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military/First Responder discounts cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales.** The $45 ticket is not stackable with public promos or coupon codes.
2. **Online-only** — the discounted ticket isn't sold at the front gate; only the free Memorial-Day admission is redeemed in person.
3. **The free Memorial-Day admission is for the eligible member** (active/retired, military ID) — additional family tickets must be **purchased online in advance**, not comped at the gate.
4. **Season passes/memberships are a substitute, not a stack** — you can't add the military discount to a pass.
5. **GovX is a separate marketplace** — no Six Flags code or portal layers on top of it.

### FAQ

#### Does Six Flags Great America offer a military discount?
Yes. Active and retired military and first responders get a $45 single-day ticket (gate $90) verified through ID.me and bought online, plus free admission on Memorial Day weekend with a military ID at Guest Services.

#### How much is the Six Flags Great America military discount?
The Military & First Responder single-day ticket is **$45** (versus a $90 gate price); Hurricane Harbor tickets are **$29**. On Memorial Day weekend (May 22–25, 2026), admission is **free** for active and retired military.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
The park's page names "active & retired" military and first responders; immediate family are covered through the verified member's purchase (up to 6 tickets). Separated veterans and Guard/Reserve aren't itemized on this park's page — confirm through the ID.me flow.

#### How do I verify my military status — does Six Flags use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Six Flags Great America uses **ID.me** for the online discount. GovX resells tickets as a separate marketplace. SheerID is not used here.

#### Can I use the discount at the front gate?
No — the discounted ticket is **online only**. The only in-person exception is **free Memorial-Day admission** (May 22–25, 2026), redeemed with a military ID at Guest Services.

#### Can I combine it with a sale or promo code?
No. Military and First Responder discounts **cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales**. If a public promo is cheaper on your date, buy that instead.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit?
If your trip is Memorial Day weekend, **go free**. Otherwise buy the **$45 ID.me ticket** (or a **season pass** if you'll visit 2+ times), and compare a live public promo since the two can't be combined.

#### Is this the same as California's Great America?
No. This is the **Gurnee, Illinois** park. California's Great America (Santa Clara) is a separate park with its own military pricing and Military Days.
