# Omaha's Henry Doorly Zoo & Aquarium — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 2 military adults + 2 children (ages 3–11), one summer-season (May 1–Sep 30) day visit.

## Headline finding

Omaha's Henry Doorly Zoo & Aquarium offers a **year-round discounted military admission price** published directly on its own ticketing page — roughly **10% off** the standard adult and child gate rates, in every season. On top of that, the zoo has a long-running tradition of **free gate admission on Veterans Day** (Nov 11) for all active/retired military, veterans, and their immediate families. The zoo is also listed on **ID.me Shop**, but the substantive, primary-source benefit is the on-site military ticket pricing plus the Veterans Day free day. The single best play for a military family: buy discounted military tickets year-round, and go free on Veterans Day if the visit can flex to mid-November.

1. **Military pricing is a discrete ticket type**, not a code — you select military tickets at purchase; expect to show valid military ID at the gate. It does not "stack" with other coupons.
2. **Veterans Day free admission is gate-only** — it covers admission, not paid add-ons (rides, special exhibits). (Zoo announcement.)
3. **Membership vs. military tickets** are mutually exclusive for a given visit — choose whichever is cheaper for your visit frequency.
4. **ID.me** may be the online verification path; in person, military ID at the gate is the practical proof.
