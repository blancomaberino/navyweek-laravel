# Lids — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one New Era 59FIFTY fitted cap — **$41.99** (typical Lids.com licensed 59FIFTY price). Illustrative sales tax at 7% ≈ $2.94 unless noted.

## Headline finding

Lids runs a **real, first-party 15% military discount** (and a matching **15% first-responder** offer), verified through **ID.me**: you verify on the Lids ID.me offer page and receive a **unique promo code** to enter at checkout. That's a legitimate everyday discount — but on a hat retailer whose own **Access Pass Premium loyalty tier ($10/year) gives 20% off online**, the loyalty program can *out-save the military discount* on full-price gear, and Access Pass Premium is available to anyone. Add frequent public sale/clearance and a 2% Rakuten cashback layer, and the honest verdict is situational rather than "just use 15%." Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount via ID.me)** with a strong **H-adjacent loyalty-wallet** twist (the paid loyalty tier is the sleeper deal).

Yes — **15% off** for the military community, verified through **ID.me**. You get a unique code to use at lids.com checkout.

#### How much is the Lids military discount?
**15%.** First responders also get 15%. It's delivered as a one-time-style promo code after ID.me verification.

#### Do veterans and family members qualify?

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Lids?
