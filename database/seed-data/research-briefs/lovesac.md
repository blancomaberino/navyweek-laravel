**Researched:** July 7, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$3,000 Sactionals seating order** (illustrative — Sactionals are configure-to-order; verify your build's price live at lovesac.com). A $500 accessories cart is used as a labeled sub-example where the stacking rules differ.

## Headline finding

Lovesac runs a standing **"Heroes" extra 5% discount** on lovesac.com and in showrooms, verified through **SheerID** as a single-use code. The brand's own eligibility wording is **"active or retired U.S. Service Members and their registered dependents"** plus active First Responders, Healthcare Workers, Teachers, and Veterinary Professionals — **non-retiree veterans are not named**, an important nuance most pages gloss over. The honest math: Lovesac runs near-constant deep sitewide sales (a **40%-off-seating "Couch Goals" sale is live July 7–12, 2026**), so the public sale does almost all the saving; the 5% Heroes code is a *topper* that stacks with some tiers (explicitly combinable with the current 35%-off accessories tier) but **not** with the 40%-off seating tier. Lovesac has also historically **boosted the military discount itself to 25–40% around Veterans Day and May**, which is when the military route genuinely leads. Archetype: **C (real discount, but the sale usually beats/dwarfs it)** — secondary: **D (partially combinable by design: "applied to the subtotal after all other applicable discounts")**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **Heroes is designed to stack — up to a cap.** The brand's own terms: the 5% "is applied to the subtotal **after all other applicable discounts**," but "No customer shall be permitted to combine Heroes discounts to exceed 35% off any Lovesac product." That cap sentence is ambiguous (the live promo simultaneously allows Heroes + 35% = 38.25% compounded) — quote it, don't paraphrase it into a rule. *(lovesac.com/heroes, accessed July 7, 2026)*
2. **Current sale (July 7–12, 2026):** Heroes 5% and Refer-a-Friend **can each combine with the 35%-off accessories tier**; the **40%-off seating tier combines with nothing** ("cannot be combined with Heroes, Refer a Friend, or any other discounts"). *(Couch Goals fine print, lovesac.com, accessed July 7, 2026)*
3. **Heroes + Refer-a-Friend together: unverified.** The referral program's legal terms say rewards are "not combinable with other promotions unless explicitly stated." Aggregators (ReferWise, referralcodes.com) quote older offer terms saying the referral reward "is combinable with Military Discount" — the referral microsite hosting those terms did not resolve this pass, so **treat as unverified**; test at checkout.
4. **Heroes + financing: yes.** Both the Heroes terms and the current sale terms state combinability with Lovesac Credit Card promotional financing (0% APR, 24 equal monthly payments; purchase APR 34.99% after, accounts as of July 31, 2025). Note the financing fine print: "Discounts may result in the qualifying purchase amount not being satisfied."
5. **Heroes code + cashback portal: assume void.** The SheerID code is an off-portal single-use code — exactly the kind Rakuten's and TopCashback's standard terms use to reduce or void cashback. Lovesac-specific portal exclusion lists were not fetchable this pass; at ~2% (as of July 7, 2026) the cashback is worth far less than the 5% code anyway. Pick the code.
6. **Clearance combines with nothing** — excluded from Heroes, from the sitewide sale, and from referral rewards.
7. **One promo code field, one referral reward per transaction** — plan the order around whichever single code wins.

### FAQ

#### Does Lovesac offer a military discount?
Yes — an extra **5% off** through the Heroes program, verified with **SheerID**, usable online at lovesac.com or in showrooms (US only).

#### How much is the Lovesac military discount?
**5%** standing. Lovesac has historically run time-boxed boosts — 25% around Veterans Day 2022 and a 40% heroes window in fall 2025 — but 5% is the everyday rate. Claims of an evergreen "40% military discount" are aggregator spin on those limited windows or on public sales.

#### Do veterans qualify for the Lovesac Heroes discount?
The brand's wording is "**active or retired** U.S. Service Members and their registered dependents." Military **retirees** clearly qualify; separated non-retiree veterans are **not named** — try SheerID verification, but the terms don't promise it.

#### Do military spouses and dependents qualify?
**Registered dependents** are named. Spouses aren't listed separately — a spouse registered as a dependent fits the stated wording.

#### Does Lovesac use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**SheerID only.** ID.me Shop lists Lovesac but only with affiliate links to the same offers; there is no GovX storefront and no WeSalute tier.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — the single-use SheerID code works in Lovesac showrooms and at lovesac.com.

#### What is excluded?
Clearance, floor models, the Let Me Save You 25 Years book, the Secret Ingredients Hardware Kit, Sactionals Shoes/Feet/Clamps, and Shrink Kits — plus anything a specific sale's fine print carves out.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Sometimes — that's the Lovesac twist. The 5% is "applied to the subtotal after all other applicable discounts," and the July 7–12, 2026 sale explicitly lets Heroes combine with its **35%** accessories tier — but its **40%** seating tier excludes Heroes entirely. Read the live sale's fine print each time.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Lovesac?
Buy seating during a 40%-class sitewide sale (they recur constantly), add the Heroes 5% only on tiers that allow it, and watch Veterans Day/May for boosted 25–40% military windows. The 5% alone is the fallback, not the strategy.

#### Does Lovesac offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
First responders, healthcare workers, teachers, and veterinary professionals get the same extra 5% via SheerID. No student or government-employee program is published.

#### Does Lovesac run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Historically yes around Veterans Day: 25% for military in November 2022 and a 40% heroes window ending November 6, 2025 (editorial listings). Treat these as history, not a promise.

#### Can I buy Lovesac at the Exchange or through GovX or ExpertVoice?
No evidence for any of the three: no GovX storefront, no ExpertVoice store, and no AAFES/NEX stocking found as of July 7, 2026.
