# Finish Line Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Finish Line offers a **10% military discount on in-store purchases only** to active, veteran, and retired U.S. military personnel and members of the Reserves and National Guard, plus their spouses, domestic partners, and dependents. There is **no online discount and no third-party verification provider** — eligibility is confirmed at the register by presenting a valid U.S. military ID (or other accepted proof) in person. The single most important caveat: the discount is **in-store only, capped at one discount per eligible customer per day**, cannot be used online, cannot be stacked with other coupons/offers (though it may be combined with one valid STATUS Reward), and is not valid on gift cards. Aggregator claims of a "20% off online via ID.me" offer are **false** — ID.me's own site states Finish Line does not use ID.me for military verification.

### How To Redeem Online
The Finish Line military discount **cannot be used online**. There is no military promo code, and the discount is not eligible for FinishLine.com or app purchases. (Source: FinishLine.com FAQ — "The Military discount offer is not eligible for online purchases.")

If you want to shop online and still save, see "Best Ways To Save More" below for the official, non-military options Finish Line offers to everyone.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop in person at a Finish Line store.
2. At checkout, tell the cashier you'd like to use the military discount.
3. Present a valid form of accepted ID in your own name (the eligible customer named on the ID must be the one making the purchase). Accepted forms reported include: U.S. Military Services Privilege & Identification Card, Uniformed Services ID Card, DD214, VFW membership card, WeSalute/Veterans Advantage VetRewards Card, VA Veteran ID Card, and American Legion membership card.
4. The cashier applies 10% off your eligible purchase. You may also redeem one valid STATUS Reward in the same transaction, but no other coupon or offer can be combined.

### How Verification Works
Verification is done **in person at the register** — there is no online account, no ID.me/SheerID/GovX flow, and no pre-enrollment. You prove eligibility each time by showing a valid military ID or another accepted proof of service in your name. Because it is checked per visit, there is no "verify once" convenience; bring acceptable ID every time. WeSalute+ members may present their WeSalute+ ID card as proof (per WeSalute's offer page). For issues, ask the store associate or Finish Line customer care; Finish Line — not any third party — controls eligibility decisions.

### FAQ

#### Does Finish Line offer a military discount?
Yes. Finish Line offers a 10% discount on in-store purchases to eligible U.S. military members, veterans, retirees, Reserves/National Guard, and their spouses, domestic partners, and dependents. (FinishLine.com FAQ; corporate press release.)

#### How much is the Finish Line military discount?
10% off, and it applies to in-store purchases only. (FinishLine.com FAQ.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Veterans and retirees qualify, and the discount extends to the eligible member's spouse, domestic partner, and dependents. Federal civilian employees and people who work on a base but never served do not qualify. (FinishLine.com FAQ.)

#### How do I verify my military status at Finish Line?
There's no online verification. You show a valid military ID (or other accepted proof such as a DD214, VA Veteran ID Card, VFW or American Legion membership card, or WeSalute+ ID card) to the cashier in store, and the ID must be in your name. (FinishLine.com FAQ; WeSalute offer page.)

#### Does Finish Line use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No third-party service verifies the discount at checkout. ID.me's own site states Finish Line does not use ID.me for military verification. WeSalute lists the offer and lets WeSalute+ members use their WeSalute+ ID card as in-store proof, but the discount itself is Finish Line's in-store program, not a WeSalute-gated online deal. (shop.id.me/discounts/finish-line/military; WeSalute offer page.)

#### Can I use the Finish Line military discount online?
No. The military discount is not eligible for online or in-app purchases — in store only. (FinishLine.com FAQ.)

#### What is excluded from the Finish Line military discount?
Gift cards are excluded, it can't be combined with other coupons or offers (one STATUS Reward is allowed), it's limited to one discount per customer per day, and it doesn't apply online. (FinishLine.com FAQ.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
You cannot combine it with other coupons, promo codes, or offers. The only allowed combination is one valid Finish Line STATUS Reward. Whether it applies on already-reduced sale items is not separately stated in the official source. (FinishLine.com FAQ.)

#### Does Finish Line offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Finish Line's only discount we can verify against a primary source is the in-store military discount. Claims of first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discounts appear on coupon aggregators but are not confirmed by Finish Line's own site, and ID.me indicates Finish Line does not run these through ID.me. Treat such offers as unverified until Finish Line publishes them. (Aggregator claims flagged Low confidence; FinishLine.com FAQ silent.)
