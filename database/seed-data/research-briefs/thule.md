## Headline finding

Thule (a **Thule Group AB** brand) has a **real, official 15% discount on thule.com** for **military, first responders, and students**, verified through **SheerID**. You select your group, verify online (instant), and get a one-time code applied at checkout. Two honest caveats: (1) the code is **not combinable** with any other offer and excludes select/limited items, and (2) the deepest real savings on Thule gear usually come **through retailers** — an **REI Co-op member 20%-off-one-full-price-item coupon** (when live) beats the 15%, and REI/Backcountry run periodic Thule sales that thule.com does not. Note the offer has **shrunk from 30% (2023 launch) to 15%** today. ## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The SheerID 15% is a single code and is NOT combinable** with any other offer, cannot apply to previous purchases, is valid only on **select SKUs**, and Thule reserves the right to exclude items — it's an either/or vs. any other discount. Capture the exact terms at live checkout.
2. **It does not "stack" on a Thule sale** — Thule rarely runs its own sales, and where an item is already price-reduced or excluded, the code won't layer on.
3. **REI/Backcountry are separate retailers** — their sale price or member coupon is a **substitute** for the thule.com route, never a stack on top of the SheerID code.
4. **Thule Pro** codes are for approved industry members only: **final sale**, spare parts under $75 excluded, and **cannot be combined with sale items** — no public-offer stacking.
5. **Cashback (Rakuten)** — not verified this pass; standard rule applies: an identity-gated (SheerID) checkout usually **breaks cashback tracking**, so treat "SheerID code + cashback" as **not stacking** until proven. Verify the live rate and tracking before relying on it.

### FAQ

#### Does Thule offer a military discount?
Yes. Thule offers a **15% discount** on thule.com for active-duty military, veterans, first responders, and students, verified through **SheerID** at checkout.

#### How much is the Thule military discount?
**15% off** eligible items on thule.com. It launched at 30% in 2023 but the current verified figure is 15%. It's not combinable with any other offer and applies to select items only.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — Thule's discount runs through **SheerID's military category**, which typically covers active-duty, veterans, reserve/retirees and immediate family. Confirm your specific status at the SheerID form.

#### How do I verify my status?

#### Does Thule use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?

#### Can I use the discount in stores or only online?
The verified 15% is an **online (thule.com)** SheerID offer. In-store redemption at Thule retail partners isn't documented on primary sources — ask the specific store.

#### What is excluded?
The 15% code can't apply to previous purchases, **can't be combined with any other offer**, is valid on **select SKUs**, and Thule may exclude limited-availability items.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale items?
No. The SheerID 15% is a single, non-combinable code. Thule rarely runs its own sales, so there's usually nothing to stack it on — for a deeper deal, compare a retailer like REI instead.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Thule?
Use the **SheerID 15%** on thule.com for the easy route. But if an **REI Co-op member 20%-off-one-full-price-item** coupon is live on the same rack or box, buy it at REI — 20% beats 15%, and the two don't combine. On this MAP-protected line, one of those two is almost always your best price.

#### Does Thule offer a first responder or student discount?
Yes — the same **15%** extends to **first responders and students** through SheerID. Outdoor-industry professionals may qualify separately for **Thule Pro** (application-based, deeper but final-sale pricing).

#### Does Thule run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No extra military-holiday promo was documented this pass; the 15% SheerID offer runs year-round. Watch for retailer (REI/Backcountry) Thule sales around those windows instead.
