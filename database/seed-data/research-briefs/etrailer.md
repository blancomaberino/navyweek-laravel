# etrailer — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ≈$300 towing order (e.g., a weight-distribution hitch or a bike/cargo rack) at etrailer.com.

## Headline finding

etrailer.com does **not** offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount, and there is no ID.me/SheerID/GovX verification gate. etrailer's stated position is that it already tries to give **every** customer the lowest possible price at all times, so it does not layer a separate military discount on top. The real savings paths are etrailer's own **public deals/clearance page**, **price matching / low everyday pricing**, occasional **public promo/free-shipping codes**, and — for towing hardware sold under recognizable brands (Curt, Reese, Thule, B&W, etc.) — checking whether **another retailer or the Exchange** carries the same part cheaper or tax-free. Treat coupon-site "etrailer military discount up to 50% off" claims as **fabricated**; etrailer publicly states it has no military program.
