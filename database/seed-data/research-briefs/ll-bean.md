# L.L.Bean Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

L.L.Bean offers a **10% military discount** on select merchandise to active duty, veteran, retired, and reservist personnel of the U.S. Armed Forces **and their families** (defined as someone living in the same household as an Active Duty, Reservist, or Military Retiree). Eligibility is verified through **SheerID** on L.L.Bean's own offer page; once verified you receive a unique promo code valid for **five (5) transactions**, redeemable online at llbean.com, by phone, and at retail stores and Outlets. The single most important caveat: the discount **excludes a very long list of third-party brands** (Yeti, Hoka, Birkenstock, Smartwool, Darn Tough, Garmin, etc.) plus firearms, gift cards, repairs, monogramming, and Outdoor Discovery Programs — so it functionally applies mainly to L.L.Bean's own-label products. As verified June 23, 2026, the offer terms state it is **valid until Monday, August 24, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET** — treat as a dated/renewable promotion, not strictly evergreen, and re-verify before relying on it.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to L.L.Bean's Military Discount page and click "Verify Your Eligibility."
2. Complete SheerID verification (enter your info; upload government-issued documentation if prompted).
3. Receive your unique promo code (valid for 5 uses).
4. Add eligible items to your shopping bag at llbean.com.
5. Enter the promo code during checkout; the 10% comes off eligible (own-brand) merchandise.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify through SheerID first (online) to get your code/confirmation email.
2. Shop at an L.L.Bean retail store or Outlet.
3. At the register, present your email (with the offer/code) to the cashier.
4. The 10% applies to eligible merchandise; note "select products at our retail stores" may be excluded.

### Other Channels
- **By phone:** Call L.L.Bean (800-441-5713) and provide your promo code to the phone representative.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **SheerID**, not L.L.Bean directly. You submit basic personal info and, if not instantly verified, upload official government-issued documentation showing your first name, last name, branch of service, and current military status. SheerID matches the document against your form entry, so enter your name exactly as it appears on the document. If your last name changed since the document was issued, upload an additional government ID showing your current name. Once verified, you get a code valid for **five uses**; re-verify after five uses or if you lose the code. Eligible individuals may receive **up to 3 verification codes every 30 days**. L.L.Bean reserves the right to alter these terms at any time.

### FAQ

#### Does L.L.Bean offer a military discount?
Yes. L.L.Bean offers 10% off select merchandise for active duty, veteran, retired, and reservist U.S. Armed Forces members and their household families, verified through SheerID.

#### How much is the L.L.Bean military discount?
10% off select (primarily own-brand) merchandise.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Veterans qualify, and family members living in the same household as an Active Duty member, Reservist, or Military Retiree qualify. Dependents may be asked to prove they are a registered dependent.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through SheerID on L.L.Bean's military offer page. You enter basic info and, if not instantly verified, upload government-issued documentation showing your name, branch, and current military status.

#### Does L.L.Bean use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
SheerID. L.L.Bean does not use ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for this offer (per the official page).

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes — at L.L.Bean retail stores and Outlets. Verify through SheerID first, then present your offer email to the cashier. Some select store products may be excluded.

#### What is excluded?
A large list of third-party brands (Yeti, Hoka, Birkenstock, Darn Tough, Garmin, Smartwool, and many more), plus firearms, gift cards, repairs, monogramming/engraving/alterations, shipping, taxes, Outdoor Discovery Programs, and the National Parks annual pass. It mainly applies to L.L.Bean's own-label products.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
It cannot be combined with other promotional offers or discounts, and is not valid with employee discounts or on previous purchases. (The terms do not separately address clearance — assume no stacking; confirm at checkout.)

#### How many times can I use the discount?
Each verification code is valid for five (5) transactions. You can get up to 3 codes every 30 days, and re-verify after using all five or if you lose your code.

#### Does L.L.Bean offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes — separate 10% SheerID-verified offers exist for First Responders, Medical Professionals, and Students & Teachers. A specific government-employee discount is not stated in the official source.
