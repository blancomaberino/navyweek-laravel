# Ikon Pass — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult 26/27 Ikon Pass (full pass), public list price ~$1,329 (early-bird tiers vary through the season).

## Headline finding

Ikon Pass runs a **real, official military discount** on its season passes. There are two verified channels: (1) **online at IkonPass.com**, where eligible buyers verify through **SheerID** and receive a unique discount code that drops the pass to **Military Base Pricing**, and (2) **on-base purchasing** through military installation ITT/MWR/Leisure Travel offices, which Ikon supplies at the same military price. Reported Military Base Pricing for 26/27 is roughly **$799 for the full Ikon Pass and ~$519 for the Ikon Base Pass** (versus public list prices well over $1,000), plus discounted Session and select local passes such as the Crystal Mountain Military Season Pass (~$539). The single best path is buying the pass you'll actually use through the SheerID flow or on base — the savings are hundreds of dollars, larger than any sale or cashback route. Eligibility clearly covers **active duty and retired** service members plus **adult dependents**; **separated veterans' eligibility through the online SheerID flow is not clearly confirmed** and must be treated as flagged.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military discounts cannot be combined** with other discounts or promo codes — one discount per pass (Ikon terms).
2. The military benefit is a **specific price tier / unique code**, not a percentage you layer on a sale price; you get the military price *instead of* the public price, whichever program you use.
3. **Eligible products only** — Ikon Pass, Base Pass, Session Pass, and select locals. Add-ons and some products are excluded.
4. A **credit card** used at checkout can still earn its normal rewards on the discounted price (card rewards are independent of Ikon's terms).

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty US military — eligible (SheerID / military ID).
- Retired military (typically 20+ years) — eligible.
- Reserve / National Guard — eligible per Ikon summaries.
- Adult dependents / spouses — eligible with dependent ID.
- Separated veterans (non-retired) — **flagged**: online SheerID eligibility not clearly confirmed; verify directly or buy on base.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
Online:
1. Go to the Ikon Pass military/discounts page.
2. Verify your status through SheerID.
3. Receive your unique Ikon discount code.
4. Apply it at checkout on an eligible pass (Ikon, Base, Session, select locals).

On base:
1. Visit your installation's ITT / MWR / Leisure Travel office.
2. Present valid military or dependent ID.
3. Purchase the Ikon military-priced pass in person.

### How Verification Works
Online eligibility is confirmed by **SheerID**, Ikon's verification partner. You may be asked to upload documentation if instant verification fails; SheerID runs an Ikon help center for issues. Verification is per purchase/season.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Military discounts **cannot be combined** with other offers.
- Only **eligible products** qualify (Ikon Pass, Base Pass, Session Pass, select locals) — not every product.
- Pricing is per season and can change; confirm the current tier before buying.
- Veterans (non-retired) eligibility online is **not clearly stated** — flagged.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Buy early** at spring launch — both public and military tiers are lowest then.
- **Choose the right product** — Base Pass or a local military pass can beat the full pass if it fits your skiing.
- **On-base ITT/MWR** — same military pricing with in-person help.
- **Credit-card rewards** on the discounted price (independent of Ikon's terms).
- Compare against **Epic Pass military pricing** (~$190 active/retired, ~$614 veteran historically) — for active/retired skiers Epic's military day-pass/pass pricing can be dramatically lower, though resort access differs.

### FAQ
#### Does Ikon Pass offer a military discount?
Yes — Military Base Pricing on eligible season passes, verified via SheerID online or sold on base.

#### How much is the Ikon Pass military discount?
Reported 26/27 Military Base Pricing is ~$799 for the full Ikon Pass and ~$519 for the Ikon Base Pass — hundreds below list.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Active duty, retired, reserve/guard, and adult dependents qualify. Separated (non-retired) veterans' online eligibility is not clearly confirmed — verify directly.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **SheerID** on the Ikon site, or with a valid military/dependent ID at an on-base ITT/MWR office.

#### Does Ikon Pass use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**SheerID** is the confirmed online provider. GovX has listed Ikon government/military offers, but the primary official path is SheerID.

#### Can I use it online and on base?
Yes — both. Online via SheerID code; on base via ITT/MWR.

#### What's excluded?
Non-eligible products, and any other discount or promo code (military pricing can't be stacked).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — military discounts can't be combined.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy an Ikon Pass?
Buy the pass you'll actually use at Military Base Pricing (via SheerID or on base). If you ski mostly one resort, that resort's local military pass may be cheaper.

#### Can I buy Ikon at the Exchange or on base?
Yes — many installation ITT/MWR/Leisure Travel offices sell Ikon at military pricing.
