# Hawaiian Airlines — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one round-trip economy ticket + checked bags for an active-duty traveler (the everyday case a service member compares).

## Headline finding

**Hawaiian Airlines' own military benefit is a checked-bag fee waiver, not a fare percentage.** For active-duty members (and qualified dependents) traveling **on official orders**, Hawaiian waives fees on the **first 5 checked bags** (up to 70 lb / 80 linear in each); on **personal/leisure travel**, active-duty members get **2 free checked bags** (up to 50 lb / 62 linear in each). It's verified in person at airport check-in with a **valid military ID and proof of orders** — there's no online military-fare portal and no everyday percentage off the ticket price from Hawaiian directly.

The only **fare percentage** is external: **5% off Alaska/Hawaiian economy fares via WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage) **VetRewards**, a *paid* membership — year-round, no blackout dates, applied to up to six people on one booking. Weigh that 5% against WeSalute's annual membership fee before assuming it's a win.

**Critical timing context:** Alaska Airlines has acquired Hawaiian. The **HawaiianMiles program merged into Atmos Rewards on October 1, 2025**, and **Hawaiian's operation folds fully into Alaska's system around April 22, 2026** — Hawaiian flight numbers disappear and bookings move to Alaska. Expect Hawaiian's military policy to converge with Alaska's; re-verify before every trip. Archetype: **travel brand** — a baggage/benefit + paid-membership-fare-% layer, no direct everyday fare discount, plus loyalty status that stacks on any fare.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The baggage-fee waiver layers on any fare** — it's a benefit, not a fare code, so it applies regardless of ticket price (on orders vs. personal sets the bag count).
2. **VetRewards 5% is a fare discount via a paid third-party membership** — it reduces the fare itself; it does not add bags. Its true value is 5% **minus** the WeSalute membership fee.
3. **Atmos Rewards status and points stack on top of any fare or discount** — loyalty always layers; the discounted fare rates themselves don't combine with each other.
4. **No GovX / exchange / cashback-portal path** applies to Hawaiian airfare.
5. **Verification differs by benefit:** the bag waiver is checked in person at the airport (ID + orders); VetRewards is gated by WeSalute membership at booking.

### Intro
Hawaiian Airlines does **not** publish a percentage off airfare for military travelers. What it offers directly is a **checked-bag fee waiver**: active-duty members (and qualified dependents) traveling **on official orders** get their **first 5 bags free** (up to 70 lb each), and on **personal/leisure travel** active-duty members get **2 free bags** (up to 50 lb each). ### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty on official orders** (+ qualified dependents) — **5 free checked bags** (70 lb / 80 lin in each).
- **Active-duty on personal/leisure travel** (+ qualified dependents) — **2 free checked bags** (50 lb / 62 lin in each).
- **Veterans / retirees** — the fare **5% via WeSalute VetRewards** is open to eligible members; the on-orders bag waiver is specific to those actually traveling on orders. Confirm current retiree bag policy at check-in during the Alaska transition.
- **Dependents** — qualified dependents share the bag waiver when traveling under the eligible member's benefit.

### How To Redeem — Baggage Waiver (in person)
1. At airport check-in, tell the agent you're military and traveling on orders (or active-duty personal travel).
2. Present a **valid military ID** and, for the 5-bag on-orders allowance, **proof of official travel orders**.
3. The eligible checked bags are waived at the counter — this is not an online process.

### How To Redeem — VetRewards 5% (online, paid membership)
1. Hold an active **WeSalute (VetRewards)** membership.
2. Book Alaska/Hawaiian economy through the WeSalute discount channel; the 5% applies to up to six travelers on the booking, no blackout dates.
3. Weigh the 5% saved against the annual membership fee before relying on it.

### How Verification Works
Hawaiian's **bag waiver** is verified **in person** by a gate/check-in agent checking your military ID (and orders for the 5-bag allowance) — nothing is verified online, and no everyday fare % is gated by ID.me/GovX/SheerID. The **5% fare** is gated by your **paid WeSalute membership**, not by Hawaiian.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The **5-bag** allowance requires **traveling on orders** with **proof of orders**; personal travel is **2 bags**.
- Weight/size caps apply (70 lb on orders / 50 lb personal; linear-inch limits).
- **VetRewards 5% is economy only** and requires a **paid** WeSalute membership; business class isn't eligible.
- Hawaiian is transitioning into **Alaska Airlines** (ops cutover ~April 22, 2026); policies, flight numbers, and the loyalty program (**Atmos Rewards**) are changing — verify current terms before travel.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Bag-fee waiver** — the biggest concrete military value, especially the 5-bag on-orders allowance.
- **Atmos Rewards** — free to join; points and status layer on every booking (replaces HawaiianMiles).
- **VetRewards 5% (WeSalute)** — only if your annual Alaska/Hawaiian spend clears the membership fee.
- **Public fare sales + travel-rewards cards** — a live fare sale frequently beats a 5% membership discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Hawaiian Airlines offer a military discount?
Not as a fare percentage. Hawaiian waives **checked-bag fees** for active-duty members and qualified dependents — 5 bags on official orders, 2 bags on personal travel — verified with a military ID (and orders) at the airport. A separate **5% economy fare** discount exists through a paid WeSalute VetRewards membership.

#### How much is the Hawaiian Airlines military discount?
There's no direct fare percentage from Hawaiian. The value is the **bag-fee waiver** (often $100+ round trip on orders). The only fare **percentage** is **5%** via a paid WeSalute VetRewards membership.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and dependents qualify?
Qualified dependents share the bag waiver when traveling under the eligible active-duty member. Veterans can use the **VetRewards 5%** via WeSalute. The 5-free-bag allowance specifically applies to those **traveling on orders** — confirm retiree bag policy at check-in during the Alaska transition.

#### How do I verify my military status?
For the bag waiver, in person at airport check-in with a **military ID and proof of orders**. For the 5% fare, your **WeSalute membership** is the gate. Hawaiian doesn't use ID.me/GovX/SheerID for a fare discount.

#### Does Hawaiian Airlines use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Only **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage), and only for the external **5% VetRewards** fare discount. Hawaiian's own bag waiver is checked manually at the airport.

#### Can I use it online or only at the airport?
The **bag waiver is claimed at the airport** (in person). The **VetRewards 5%** is booked online through WeSalute.

#### What is excluded?
The 5-bag allowance needs **orders**; personal travel is 2 bags. VetRewards 5% is **economy only** and needs a paid membership. Weight/size caps apply.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Hawaiian?
Use the **bag-fee waiver** (huge if you're on orders), join **Atmos Rewards** for points/status, and book on **public fare sales** — only add paid **VetRewards 5%** if you fly enough to beat the membership fee.

#### Is Hawaiian Airlines becoming Alaska Airlines?
Effectively yes — Alaska acquired Hawaiian; HawaiianMiles became **Atmos Rewards** (Oct 1, 2025) and Hawaiian's operation folds into Alaska's system around **April 22, 2026**. Re-verify military policy before booking.
