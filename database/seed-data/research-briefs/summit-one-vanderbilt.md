# SUMMIT One Vanderbilt — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult SUMMIT Experience (general-admission observation-deck ticket), gate price **$44** + $3 online processing fee.

## Headline finding

Yes — SUMMIT One Vanderbilt runs a genuine, official, and unusually generous military offer, and it is the best deal by a wide margin. The venue gives a **complimentary SUMMIT Experience admission ticket to every active-duty and retired member of the U.S. Armed Forces**, plus **25% off for up to 4 guests** in the military family. That free ticket beats every third-party path (ID.me ~11%, GovX member pricing, reseller sites ~19%). The one catch is the redemption mechanic: it is **not** an instant online code — you request it by sending a message through SUMMIT's **Contact Us form and selecting "Military" as the Reason for Inquiry**, then present military ID. Archetype: **single-venue attraction / experiential ticket** — one official comp offer, several third-party resale/portal discounts orbiting it, and no stacking.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The comp ticket and the 25% guest discount are a single SUMMIT-issued offer redeemed through the Contact Us form — they are **not** combinable with online promo codes, resident pricing discounts, or third-party resale tickets.
2. The **ID.me** offer and the **GovX** offer are separate third-party paths; using one means you are **not** getting the free SUMMIT ticket. They do not stack with the official comp offer.
3. No published mechanism stacks a reseller ticket (FunEx etc.) with the military comp — resellers sell their own inventory at their own price.

### How To Redeem
1. Go to summitov.com and open the **Contact Us** form.
2. Select **"Military"** as the Reason for Inquiry.
3. Send your message requesting the complimentary Experience ticket (and note guests, up to 4, for the 25% discount).
4. Follow SUMMIT's instructions and bring valid military ID to the venue.

### How Verification Works
SUMMIT handles military verification manually through its Contact Us form and on-site ID check — it does **not** use an instant ID.me/SheerID/GovX code for the free ticket. (ID.me and GovX are separate third-party discount paths, not the comp offer.) Request ahead of your visit; same-day requests may not be honored in time.

- The comp ticket covers the **SUMMIT Experience** (general admission); upgrades like SUMMIT Ascent, Ultimate SUMMIT, and Premium are not stated to be free — verify at request.
- The offer must be redeemed by the active-duty or retired service member (source does not confirm guest-only or dependent-only redemption).

### FAQ

#### Does SUMMIT One Vanderbilt offer a military discount?
Yes. Active-duty and retired U.S. military members get a **free SUMMIT Experience ticket**, plus 25% off for up to 4 guests.

#### How much is the SUMMIT One Vanderbilt military discount?
The service member's SUMMIT Experience admission is **complimentary (free)**; guests get **25% off**, up to 4.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Retired veterans qualify for the free ticket. Guests/family get 25% off. SUMMIT states the offer "must be redeemed by active-duty military members or retired veterans," so a spouse/dependent redeeming alone isn't confirmed.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Send a message through SUMMIT's **Contact Us form**, choose "Military" as the Reason for Inquiry, and bring valid military ID.

#### Does SUMMIT use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not for the free ticket — that's arranged directly via the Contact Us form. Separately, **ID.me Shop** lists ~11% off and **GovX** lists a member offer, but choosing those means forgoing the free comp ticket.

#### Can I use it online or only in person?
The free-ticket request is made online via the Contact Us form and finalized with on-site ID; it is not an instant checkout promo code.

#### What is excluded?
Upgrades (Ascent, Ultimate, Premium) aren't confirmed free; the offer doesn't combine with promo codes, resident pricing, or resale tickets.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The comp offer is standalone and does not stack.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy?
Request the **free comp ticket** via the Contact Us form — it's $0, which beats every third-party discount.

#### Does SUMMIT offer a first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not directly, but **ID.me Shop** lists ~11% off for first responders, nurses, teachers, students, government, and medical providers.
