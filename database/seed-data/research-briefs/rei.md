# REI Co-op Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**REI does not offer a traditional military or veteran discount.** REI's own help center answer ("Does REI offer a military discount?") and ID.me both confirm there is no military/veteran percentage-off program — REI does not partner with ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for a service-member discount, and there is no military price on the REI Co-op membership. Instead, REI's saving model is the same for everyone: a **$30 lifetime Co-op membership** that returns an **annual Co-op Member Reward (typically ~10% back, but not guaranteed)** on eligible full-price purchases, plus member coupons, member sales (the May Anniversary Sale, with 20% off one full-price item and 30% off one Outlet item), free U.S. standard shipping, and **Re/Supply** used gear (the year-round replacement for the old in-store Garage Sales). The single most important caveat for a NavyWeek page: **widely circulated claims of a "$20 military membership" and a "10–20% military/corporate bulk discount" appear only on aggregator sites — neither is stated on any REI primary page, so neither may be presented as fact.**

> Accuracy note: Multiple military-discount aggregators (operationmilitarykids.org, usmilitary.com, honestmos.com) state that REI gives military members a **$20 lifetime membership (20% off the $30 fee)** and that REI Corporate/Group Sales gives **10–20% off bulk military unit purchases**. **Neither claim is confirmed on rei.com.** REI's official membership page lists a flat **$30** membership for everyone with no military tier, and REI's Corporate & Group Sales page did not load any published discount terms (JS-rendered help-center stub). Treat both as **unverified / do not publish as fact** until confirmed directly with REI.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no military discount to apply online. To use the savings REI does offer:
1. Buy a **$30 Co-op membership** at rei.com/membership (one-time, lifetime).
2. Sign in to your REI account when you shop so eligible full-price purchases accrue toward your annual Co-op Member Reward.
3. Watch your email/account for member coupons and member sale events, and apply them at checkout.
4. Each March, REI issues your Co-op Member Reward for the prior year's eligible purchases — redeem it online, in store, or in the app.

### How To Redeem In Store
There is no military discount at the register. To use member savings in store:
1. Buy or look up your **Co-op membership** ($30, one-time).
2. Give your member number at checkout so the purchase counts toward your annual reward.
3. Shop the **Re/Supply** used-gear section for lower prices on gently used gear.
4. Redeem your annual Co-op Member Reward and any member coupons at the register.

### FAQ

#### Does REI offer a military discount?
No. REI does not offer a military or veteran discount. REI's help center and ID.me both confirm there is no military discount program. REI's savings come from its $30 lifetime Co-op membership and the annual Co-op Member Reward, which anyone can get.

#### How much is the REI military discount?
There isn't one. REI publishes no military percentage-off or flat discount. Some coupon sites claim a "$20 military membership" or "10–20% military bulk discount," but REI does not state either on its official pages — treat those claims as unverified.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for an REI discount?
No — REI has no military discount for any group, including veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, or dependents. They can, however, buy the same $30 lifetime membership anyone can buy.

#### Does REI use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. REI does not run a military verification program with any of these. ID.me's own REI page states it is "not aware that REI offers Military discounts."

#### Can I use a military discount in REI stores?
No — there is no military discount to use in store or online. Member savings (the annual reward, member coupons, Re/Supply used gear) apply equally to all members.

#### Is the REI membership cheaper for military members?
Not according to REI. The membership is a flat $30 one-time fee for everyone on rei.com/membership; no military price is published. The "$20 military membership" you may see online is an unverified aggregator claim.

#### What's the best way for a service member or veteran to save at REI?
Buy the $30 lifetime Co-op membership, shop full-price eligible items signed in to earn your ~10% annual Co-op Member Reward, use member coupons and member sales, and check Re/Supply for used gear. Separately, veterans and Gold Star families can get a free federal America the Beautiful Military Lifetime Pass for national parks (a government program, not REI).

#### Does REI offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount?
No. REI does not publish a discount for first responders, medical workers, teachers, government employees, or students. The Co-op membership is the same offer for everyone.

#### What happened to REI Garage Sales?
REI replaced in-store Garage Sales with **Re/Supply**, where members can shop and trade in gently used gear year-round, online and in many stores.
