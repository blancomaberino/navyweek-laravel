# Texas Roadhouse Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Texas Roadhouse's only military benefit confirmed on its **own official website** is the annual **Veterans Day free meal** for veterans and active military — its Community Impact page states: "on Veterans Day, we're honored to offer free meals to veterans and active military members across the country." The widely repeated **everyday 10% military discount is NOT confirmed on any Texas Roadhouse official page**; it appears only on aggregators and military-deal blogs, which uniformly add that, because Texas Roadhouse restaurants are independently operated, the everyday discount is **location/franchise-dependent and not guaranteed**. Treat the everyday 10% as **unverified and location-dependent — call your local restaurant first**. The single most important caveat: do not assert a standing everyday percentage as a brand-wide fact; only the Veterans Day free meal is company-confirmed, and even its detailed terms (entrée list, redemption window) come from secondary sources rather than a fetchable official press release.

> Accuracy note: The "**10% everyday military discount**" repeated across coupon and military-deal sites could **not be verified against texasroadhouse.com** as of June 23, 2026. The official site confirms only the Veterans Day free meal. Because Texas Roadhouse locations are independently operated, any everyday discount is set by the individual restaurant — present it as possible-but-unconfirmed, never as a guaranteed brand policy.

### How To Redeem In Store
**Veterans Day free meal (Nov 11):**
1. Visit a participating Texas Roadhouse on Veterans Day.
2. Show proof of service (per secondary sources: U.S. Uniformed Services/military ID, VA card, veteran ID, or discharge papers/DD-214).
3. Receive a free-meal voucher (distributed during set hours, commonly cited as ~11 a.m.–2 p.m.).
4. Redeem the voucher for one of the listed entrées with two sides and a non-alcoholic beverage — that day or later, in-restaurant or carry-out, until the voucher's printed expiration (commonly cited as ~May 30 of the following year).

**Everyday discount (unconfirmed / location-dependent):**
1. **Call your local Texas Roadhouse first** to ask whether they offer an everyday military discount and what it is.
2. If offered, tell your server you'd like the military discount and present a valid military/veteran ID.
3. Confirm what's included (e.g., food only, alcohol excluded) before paying — terms are set by the location, not published by the brand.
