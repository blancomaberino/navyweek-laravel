# The Children's Place — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic $100 back-to-school apparel cart (regular-price kids' clothing, pre-tax, shipped in the US).

## Headline finding

The Children's Place has **no official, everyday military or veteran discount**. ID.me — the verification provider most kids-apparel shoppers would expect — explicitly states it is "**not aware of The Children's Place offering Military community discounts**," and the brand runs no military page, no SheerID/GovX/WeSalute program, and lists no military offer in its own coupon terms. What *has* existed is a **discretionary, in-store-only holiday gesture** (a Veterans Day "show your military ID, get 25% off" in-store event in past years, and Exchange-channel "Month of the Military Child" promos), which was inconsistently honored at the register. The honest best path for a military family is the same as for any shopper: **stack a public promo code on The Children's Place's frequent deep sales (uniforms 50% off, clearance up to 70% off, 30% off for text/email signup), plus My Place Rewards points and PLACE Bucks** — this routinely beats any 25% holiday one-off. Archetype: **H (the "myth" brand — no everyday % exists; a loyalty wallet + holiday one-offs is the reality)**, secondary **B (no first-party or GovX military route)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **One web code per online order.** Percentage-off and dollar-off coupons cannot be combined with each other.
2. **Coupons do NOT stack on a site-wide percentage-off event** (in-store or online) — during those events the sale *is* the discount; use My Place Rewards points, not a code.
3. **What a code *can* combine with:** My Place Rewards Certificates, My Place Rewards points Discounts (MPR, up to $50/day), and a free-shipping promo. MPR Certificates/Discounts apply first, then the % code.
4. **PLACE Bucks are their own lane:** redeem up to 12 per order, but they cannot be combined with a percentage-off or dollar-off coupon or a site-wide % event.
5. **No military code exists to stack** — the only military-flavored savings were discretionary in-store holiday events, which would not combine with a coupon anyway.
6. **Cash-back caveat:** ID.me/Rakuten cash back generally tracks on ordinary online orders, but can be reduced/voided by third-party coupon codes — use the brand's *own* published code and confirm the portal's terms before assuming the stack.

#### Does The Children's Place offer a military discount?

#### How much is The Children's Place military discount?

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?

#### Does The Children's Place run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
