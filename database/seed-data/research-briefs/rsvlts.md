# RSVLTS — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $140 order (two $70 KUNUFLEX short-sleeve shirts), pre-tax.

## Executive Summary / Headline finding

**RSVLTS runs a real, official first-party military discount: 25% off sitewide, 365 days a year, for active-duty and veteran members of the U.S. Armed Forces**, redeemed with a unique code you receive after verifying through RSVLTS's "ID system" (VerifyPass). This is a genuine standing offer stated in RSVLTS's own words on rsvlts.com/pages/military-discount (verified July 8, 2026), not an aggregator claim.

The honest catch that this page must lead with: **25% is the same headline rate RSVLTS gives any new email subscriber.** So a service member's true advantage from the military program is not a bigger number — it is that the 25% is *repeatable and standing* (a fresh code for every purchase, all year), whereas the newsletter welcome code is a one-time, first-order deal. For a first-time RSVLTS buyer, the email welcome code reaches the same price with no verification; for repeat buyers, the verified military discount is the durable 25%.

**Archetype:** verified-community flat discount (flat % gated behind a verification provider), with a secondary "loss-leader parity" trait — the verified rate matches the public newsletter offer. **Best path:** the 25% military code for repeat/verified buyers; the email welcome code for a one-time first order; referral/$10 only helps on very small carts. Only one code applies per order — nothing stacks code-on-code.

> Debunk line: coupon aggregators advertise RSVLTS "military" savings of "up to 75% off," "60% off," "40% off," and assorted one-word codes. **None of these is a verified RSVLTS military rate.** The only official military figure is **25% off**, and the only legitimate way to get the code is through RSVLTS's ID/VerifyPass verification flow — codes are unique, one-time, and non-shareable. Third-party "codes" promising more are not RSVLTS military offers.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **One promo code per order.** RSVLTS states discount codes "cannot be combined." The military code, an email welcome code, and any referral reward all occupy the single code slot — you pick one. (rsvlts.com/pages/military-discount, Jul 8, 2026)
2. **Military codes are unique, one-time, and non-retroactive.** Each purchase needs a fresh code from the ID/VerifyPass system; limit 1 per customer per 24 hours; codes cannot be applied to past orders; sharing/harvesting codes leads to a permanent ban.
3. **Code-on-markdown is not confirmed.** Whether the 25% applies on already-reduced/sale items is not stated; "some exclusions apply," and high-demand licensed/collab drops are the usual apparel exclusions — treat as possibly excluded until confirmed at checkout.
4. **Cashback portals are a separate rebate** paid by the portal, not RSVLTS, so they generally layer on top of an RSVLTS code — but confirm the portal's terms don't void the rebate when a code is used. Rate varies and must carry an "as of" date.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to RSVLTS's military discount page (rsvlts.com/pages/military-discount) and choose **Access Military Discount**.

3. Receive your **unique, one-time** discount code.
4. Apply it at checkout on rsvlts.com. It can't be combined with other codes and can't be applied to past orders.
5. For a future order, return and pull a **fresh code** (limit 1 per 24 hours; codes are non-shareable). Allow an extra 5–7 business days for shipping.

### FAQ

#### Does RSVLTS offer a military discount?
Yes. RSVLTS offers an official **25% off sitewide** discount, 365 days a year, for active-duty and veteran members of the U.S. Armed Forces, per its own military discount page (verified July 8, 2026).

#### How much is the RSVLTS military discount?
**25% off sitewide.** That is the only official RSVLTS military figure. Coupon sites advertising 40–75% "military" savings are not stating a real RSVLTS military rate.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify. RSVLTS names only **active-duty and veterans** — it does not publish eligibility for spouses, dependents, Guard/Reserve, or retirees, so don't assume those groups are covered until VerifyPass confirms your status.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through RSVLTS's **ID system (VerifyPass)**. You must receive your code through verification — "no exceptions" — and each purchase needs a fresh, one-time code.

#### Does RSVLTS use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or VerifyPass?
**VerifyPass.** RSVLTS's offer is hosted through VerifyPass (called the "ID system" on-site). We found no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID flow for RSVLTS.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or only online?
Online at rsvlts.com. RSVLTS is primarily direct-to-consumer; no in-store military discount is documented.

#### What is excluded?
RSVLTS says only "some exclusions apply" without listing them. The code can't be combined with other codes, can't be applied to past orders, and high-demand licensed/collab drops are the likely exclusions — confirm at checkout.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No code-on-code stacking — **one code per order**. RSVLTS does not confirm whether the 25% applies on already-marked-down items, so don't count on it stacking with sale prices.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from RSVLTS?
For repeat buyers, the standing **25% military code**. For a first order, the **email welcome code** reaches roughly the same price without verifying. On a tiny cart, a ~$10 referral credit can beat 25%. You can also layer a cashback portal on top of whichever code you use.

#### Does RSVLTS offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not that we could verify. As of July 8, 2026, RSVLTS publishes a military/veteran discount only — no first responder, healthcare, teacher, or student offer.
