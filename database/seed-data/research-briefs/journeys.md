# Journeys Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Journeys offers a **10% off military discount**, delivered as a **one-time-use code** that is unlocked by verifying military status through **ID.me**. Per Journeys' own help page, members verify in-store by scanning a QR code (staff-assisted) and verifying through ID.me on the spot; once a code is generated it can be used **in Journeys stores across the U.S. (including Puerto Rico) and online at checkout**. Members can generate **up to 3 codes per month**, and **each code is one-time use and expires 7 days after it's created**. Eligibility follows ID.me's standard military community (active duty, Guard/Reserves, veterans, retirees, spouses, surviving spouses, and dependents). The single most important caveat: codes are issued and supported by ID.me — **Journeys cannot reissue or troubleshoot a code**, and any code problem must go through help.id.me.

### Who Qualifies
Per ID.me's military eligibility (the verification provider Journeys uses):
- **Active duty** — eligible (includes National Guard and Reserves)
- **Veterans** — eligible (discharged/released under conditions other than dishonorable, under 20 years' cumulative service)
- **Retirees** — eligible (entitled to retirement/retainer pay; e.g., 20+ years or 100% medically retired)
- **National Guard / Reserves** — eligible
- **Military spouses** — eligible (spouse of a service member, veteran, or retiree)
- **Surviving spouses** — eligible
- **Dependents / military family** — eligible (immediate family member 18+ of a service member, veteran, or retiree)
- **First responders / medical workers / teachers / government employees / students** — Not stated for this offer (Journeys runs a separate student program; see "Best Ways To Save More")
- **Explicitly excluded** (per ID.me) — civilian employees, veterans with less-than-honorable discharges, non-U.S. military

### How To Redeem Online
1. Verify your military status through **ID.me** to generate your one-time-use code.
2. Add your items to the cart at journeys.com and proceed to checkout.
3. Enter the generated code at checkout to apply 10% off.
4. Complete the order within **7 days** of the code being created (codes expire after 7 days).

> Note: Journeys' help page nudges members toward the in-store flow for "the smoothest experience," but explicitly confirms the code can be used online at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit any Journeys store in the U.S. (including Puerto Rico).
2. Ask an associate for the military discount; they'll help you **scan a QR code**.
3. **Verify your status through ID.me** on the spot.
4. Receive your **10% one-time-use code** and apply it to your purchase.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**, a third-party identity-verification service. In-store, an associate helps you scan a QR code that launches the ID.me flow; you can also verify through ID.me directly. ID.me confirms military status using a `.mil` email or by uploading qualifying documentation (per ID.me's standard process). Each successful verification can produce **up to 3 codes per month**; **each code is one-time use and expires 7 days after creation**. Because codes are generated through ID.me, **Journeys cannot send a replacement or fix a broken code** — code issues go to **help.id.me**. The Journeys page does not state how often you must re-verify with ID.me beyond the per-code/monthly mechanics above.
