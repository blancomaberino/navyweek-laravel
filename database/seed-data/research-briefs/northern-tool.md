# Northern Tool + Equipment — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$400 in-store tool/equipment purchase (e.g., a generator or air compressor), where the 10% military discount = $40.

## Headline finding

Northern Tool + Equipment offers a **genuine 10% military discount — but in-store only.** It's available every day at any Northern Tool store to **active duty, military retirees, and veterans** who present a **valid military ID at the register**. It is **capped at a maximum discount of $500**, **excludes Stihl products and gift cards**, and **cannot be combined with any other offers**. The critical, widely-mis-stated detail: there is **no online military discount** — Northern Tool is **not** an ID.me partner (ID.me confirms "we're not aware that Northern Tool offers Military discounts"), and Military.com lists redemption as **in-store only**. Aggregator claims of an online "unique military code," ID.me/SheerID verification, or blanket **family/spouse/dependent** eligibility are **not supported** by primary sources — don't assert them. So for online buyers, the honest best path is Northern Tool's **public promo codes** (e.g., $20-off-$100 style offers) and email/clearance deals, which anyone can use — since the 10% military discount doesn't work online and doesn't combine with those codes anyway. Archetype: **real in-store discount, non-combinable, physical-ID-verified** (with an online gap that public codes fill).
