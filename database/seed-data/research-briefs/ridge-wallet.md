**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $95 aluminum Ridge Wallet (representative current price for the base aluminum model on ridge.com; ridge.com blocked automated reads — verify the exact SKU price at build). ## Headline finding

Ridge (ridge.com — the minimalist wallet/ring/accessory DTC brand, **not** Ridge Global tools or Ridge Outdoors/footwear) offers a **real, first-party 20% military discount for active duty, veterans, and military family members, verified through GovX** at checkout — confirmed by Ridge's own X/Twitter account and a standing GovX storefront. Separately, Ridge runs an **ID.me** program for **first responders, nurses/medical providers, teachers, and government employees** (percentage not first-party-confirmed this session — reported as low as 5%, older sources say 15%; verify at checkout). The single most important thing to know: Ridge discounts hard and often — **Memorial Day up to 40% off, Black Friday up to 47% off, a permanent Outlet, and an email welcome offer** — and the identity discount **does not stack** with public codes or sale pricing (one code per order). So during any 25%+ sale the **public price beats the 20% military discount**, and you take the sale, not the GovX code. Archetype: **DTC accessory brand + multi-provider identity discount where the public sale frequently wins** (brand-archetype "sale-beats-the-discount, non-stacking identity tier").

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The identity discount (GovX or ID.me) does not combine with other promo codes or sale pricing** — Ridge accepts **one code per order** (per Ridge FAQ snippet + standard checkout). Military % vs. public sale is either/or.
2. **Because public sales run 40–47%, the sale frequently beats the 20% GovX discount** — and you can't have both. During a real sale, take the public price.
3. **GovX has two forms:** (a) GovX ID verification via the green button at **ridge.com** checkout, and (b) the **govx.com storefront** (a closed members marketplace). On the govx.com storefront you can't add a ridge.com code or a cashback portal on top.
4. **Cashback portals (Rakuten, ID.me Shop) void or reduce on coupon-code orders and identity-gated checkouts** — pairing "GovX 20% + Rakuten" is unreliable; a portal is only safe on a plain no-code order.
5. **ID.me Shop cashback ≠ Ridge's identity discount** — different programs; don't imply the cashback is "the military discount."

### How Verification Works
Ridge uses **GovX** (for military) and **ID.me** (for first responders, nurses/medical, teachers, and government). Both place a green "verify" button in the ridge.com checkout. You verify your status once with the provider (name, service/role details, sometimes a document upload), and the provider gates the discounted price. Re-verification frequency isn't published; both providers commonly re-check periodically. Verification is no more permanent than the provider states.

No — one code per order, and the identity discount isn't combinable with sales. During a 25%+ sale, the public price beats the 20%, so buy the sale instead.

#### Does Ridge offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
Yes — through **ID.me** (first responders, nurses/medical providers, teachers, government employees). Verify the exact percentage at checkout.

#### Does Ridge run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
The 20% GovX military discount is year-round. Memorial Day brings a public sale up to 40% off (open to everyone), which often beats the 20%.
