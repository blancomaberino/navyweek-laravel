# Vessel (Golf Bags) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$325 Vessel stand bag (e.g. a Player/VLS-class carry bag — Vessel's core premium unit).

## Headline finding

**Vessel offers a genuine, clean, first-party military discount: 20% off sitewide, verified through ID.me at checkout.** Eligibility is broad — **active-duty service members, retirees, veterans, military spouses, and military family members** — and the discount applies to your entire order. This is the rare "the official discount actually is the best deal" case: Vessel is a premium direct-to-consumer brand that rarely runs deep public sales and isn't meaningfully undercut on a cashback portal for verified buyers, so the **20% ID.me discount is the strongest broadly-available path** for a service member.

The one rule that matters: the discount **cannot be combined with other promotions, discount codes, or coupons.** Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount)** — a real brand-run discount verified via ID.me, with no coupon/cashback stacking game and no public code that beats it. Ignore aggregator pages claiming "30% off Vessel military codes" — the verified offer is **20% via ID.me**, and Vessel's own help center describes an email-verification fallback that issues a **unique** one-time code.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add your Vessel bag to cart and go to checkout.
2. Under the Order Summary, click **"Verify with ID.me."**
3. Complete ID.me verification of your military status.
4. The **20%** is applied to your order total automatically.
5. *(Fallback)* If auto-verify fails, email your proof of service to Vessel support; they'll send a **unique code** to use at checkout.

### How Verification Works
Vessel uses **ID.me** as its primary verification at checkout. If that doesn't auto-verify, Vessel's help center accepts a **DD-214**, a **veteran designation on your driver's license/state ID**, a **`.mil` email**, or **active-duty orders/LES** — or third-party verification via ID.me/SheerID — and then emails a **unique one-time discount code**. Verification is per the provider's process; the emailed code is single-use.
