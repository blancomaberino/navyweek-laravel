# Progressive (Auto Insurance) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> **YMYL / insurance note:** Insurance terms are state-regulated and change frequently. Every numeric and eligibility claim below is tied to a primary or regulatory source. Where the only support is a coupon/aggregator site, it is flagged **Low** and must not be presented as a confirmed Progressive offer. Accessed: **June 23, 2026.**

## Executive Summary

Progressive does **not** advertise a nationwide military or veteran auto-insurance discount. Its official auto discounts page lists no military, veteran, or first-responder discount of any kind (verified June 23, 2026). The only "Progressive military discount" that exists is a **state-mandated 25% discount on auto liability premiums in Louisiana**, which **every** insurer licensed in Louisiana must give to qualifying residents — it is a Louisiana law (La. R.S. 22:1482, as amended by Act 173 of 2024, effective Jan 1, 2025), not a Progressive program. Eligibility is Louisiana-resident active-duty members, military reservists, and Louisiana Army/Air National Guard; the discount is claimed by submitting the state's "Louisiana Application Form for Military Discount" plus documentation (e.g., military orders) to your agent/insurer. Veterans and retirees are **not** entitled to the 25% mandate; the law instead offers insurers a *tax credit* if they choose to extend the discount to retirees/disabled veterans (50%+ rating) — extension is optional, and Progressive has not published whether it does so. Outside Louisiana, Progressive's relevant military support is operational, not a price discount: comprehensive-only/stored-vehicle handling during deployment and not penalizing deployment-related coverage lapses (both **aggregator-sourced, unverified** against Progressive's own pages). **Single most important caveat: there is no Progressive military discount anywhere except Louisiana, and even there it is a legal mandate applied by all insurers, not a Progressive perk.**

Progressive does not offer a nationwide military or veteran discount on car insurance. ### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Eligible in Louisiana (LA resident, buys insurance in LA, meets residency rules). Not stated to qualify for any discount in other states.
- **Veterans** — Not covered by the Louisiana 25% mandate. May qualify only if an insurer voluntarily extends it (tax-credit provision); Progressive's participation is not published.
- **Retirees** — Same as veterans: outside the mandate; optional extension only.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Reservists and Louisiana Army/Air National Guard are covered by the Louisiana mandate. (Out-of-state Guard/Reserve: not stated.)
- **Military spouses** — Per military.com, the Louisiana discount can extend to a spouse and dependent children on the same policy. Confirm with insurer. (Medium confidence.)
- **Dependents** — Same as spouses (on-policy dependents), per military.com. (Medium confidence.)
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star** — Not stated in any source.
- **First responders / medical / teachers / government / students** — Not stated; Progressive lists no such discounts on its auto discounts page.

### How To Redeem (Louisiana residents)
1. Confirm you meet Louisiana's requirements: LA resident, full-time active duty / reservist / LA National Guard, and insurance bought in Louisiana.
2. Get the **"Louisiana Application Form for Military Discount"** from the Louisiana Department of Insurance (ldi.la.gov/military) or your agent.
3. Gather required proof — e.g., a copy of military orders (active duty) or a commander's letter on unit letterhead (National Guard).
4. Submit the completed, signed form and documentation to your Progressive agent or to Progressive directly, and keep a copy.
5. Ask Progressive to apply the 25% liability-premium discount to your policy.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
Not applicable. This is an insurance policy adjustment, not a retail discount. There is no online promo code, no in-store register step, and no ID.me/GovX/SheerID verification flow. It is handled through a Progressive agent or Progressive's policy service using the Louisiana state form.

### How Verification Works
There is **no third-party verification provider** (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute). Verification is documentary and state-defined: complete the Louisiana Application Form for Military Discount and provide supporting documentation (military orders for active duty; a commander's letter for National Guard), submitted to your agent or insurer. This is per Louisiana Department of Insurance Bulletin 2023-04. Confirm the current form and proof requirements with the LDI or Progressive — requirements have been revised more than once.

### FAQ

#### Does Progressive offer a military discount?
Not nationally. Progressive's official auto discounts page lists no military or veteran discount (verified June 23, 2026). The only "Progressive military discount" is a Louisiana state-mandated 25% liability discount that all insurers in Louisiana must provide.

#### How much is the Progressive military discount?
There is no set nationwide amount because there is no nationwide discount. In Louisiana, the statutory discount is 25% off auto **liability** premium, mandated for every insurer by La. R.S. 22:1482 (Act 173 of 2024).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees are **not** covered by Louisiana's 25% mandate (an insurer may extend it voluntarily; Progressive hasn't published whether it does). For active members in Louisiana, the discount can include a spouse and dependent children on the same policy (per military.com — confirm with your insurer).

#### How do I verify my military status?
For the Louisiana discount, you submit the state's "Louisiana Application Form for Military Discount" plus proof (military orders for active duty; commander's letter for National Guard) to your agent or Progressive. There is no app-based verification.

#### Does Progressive use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. We found no evidence Progressive uses any third-party military-verification service for auto insurance. The Louisiana discount uses a state form and documents instead.

#### Can I get the discount in states other than Louisiana?
No. The discount exists only in Louisiana because it's required by Louisiana law. No Progressive source indicates a military discount in any other state.

#### What's excluded?
The 25% is limited to Louisiana residents and (per regulatory summaries) to liability premium. Veterans/retirees aren't entitled to it under the mandate. Non-Louisiana drivers get no military discount.

#### Can I combine it with other Progressive discounts?
The Louisiana statutory discount is separate from Progressive's standard discounts (bundling, multi-car, safe driver, etc.). How it interacts with other discounts is not detailed in official sources — confirm with Progressive. (Not stated.)

#### Does Progressive offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Progressive lists no first-responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount on its auto discounts page. It does offer a **good student** and **distant student** discount (these are not military-related). Verified June 23, 2026.

#### Does Progressive help deployed service members?
Aggregator sources say Progressive offers comprehensive-only coverage for stored vehicles during deployment and won't penalize deployment-related lapses for the continuous-insurance discount. We could not confirm this on Progressive's own site — verify directly before relying on it.
