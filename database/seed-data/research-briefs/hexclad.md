HexClad runs an evergreen "HexClad Heroes" program offering a **10% discount** to US Military Service Members, Veterans, Police, Firefighters, and EMTs — explicitly including current and Veteran members of the US Military, National Guard, and Reserve, plus their immediate family members. ### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to HexClad's Heroes page (hexclad.com/pages/hexclad-heroes).
2. Click "Verify with ID.me" and complete the ID.me verification for your eligible group (Military or First Responder).
3. After successful verification, ID.me provides a unique 10% off discount code.
4. Shop hexclad.com and add items to your cart; the 10% discount is automatically applied at checkout. (If it does not appear, apply the code ID.me provided in the discount-code field.)
5. Note: only one discount per order — you cannot combine it with another sale or promo.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by ID.me. You click "Verify with ID.me" from the HexClad Heroes page, sign in to (or create) an ID.me account, and confirm your military/first-responder status. On success, ID.me issues a unique single-use-style 10% off code that auto-applies at checkout. ID.me accounts and their group verifications are managed by ID.me, which may periodically require re-verification per its own policies — HexClad's page does not state a HexClad-specific re-verification frequency. For verification problems, use ID.me's help center (help.id.me); for order/discount questions, use HexClad's Help Center (support.hexclad.com).
