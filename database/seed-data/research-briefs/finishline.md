# Finish Line — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **~$150 in-store sneaker purchase** at a Finish Line store.

> **Scope note:** NavyWeek also has a sibling brief at `research/discounts/finish-line.md`. This brief (slug `finishline`) is the maximum-savings-format version of the same brand; cross-link the two and keep a single canonical published page rather than shipping duplicate URLs.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Finish Line offers a 10% military discount, but in-store only.** If you serve or have served in any branch of the U.S. military — active, veteran, retired, Reserves, or National Guard — you (plus your spouse, domestic partner, and dependents) get **10% off in-store purchases** by showing a valid military/veteran ID at the register. There is **no online discount and no third-party verification** — Finish Line does **not** use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID (ID.me's own page confirms it). The critical caveats: **one discount per eligible customer per day**, **not stackable** with other coupons (though **one STATUS Reward** may be used in the same transaction), and **not valid on gift cards**. Because there's no online military discount, the best *online* path for a service member is a completely different play: **sale + a public promo code + a cashback portal + STATUS loyalty**. Archetype: **mall athletic retailer with an in-store-only discretionary-adjacent military discount** whose online savings come from stacking public offers, not status.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible; show a valid military ID in store.
- **Veterans / retired** — Eligible; DD214, VA Veteran ID Card, VFW or American Legion card accepted.
- **Reserves / National Guard** — Eligible.
- **Spouse / domestic partner / dependents** — Eligible when the named eligible customer presents ID.
- **Federal civilians / on-base workers** — **Not** eligible.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop a **Finish Line store** and bring your purchase to the register.
2. Present a valid **military/veteran ID** (Uniformed Services ID, DD214, VA Veteran ID Card, VFW/American Legion card, WeSalute VetRewards Card, etc.).
3. The associate applies **10% off** (one discount per eligible customer per day).
4. You may also redeem **one STATUS Reward** in the same transaction. Not valid on gift cards.

### How To Buy Online (no military discount)
There's no military discount online. To save: catch a **sale**, apply a **working public promo code**, and route through a **cashback portal** (verify the portal allows the code so your cashback isn't voided). Join **STATUS** for points and member offers.

### How Verification Works
Finish Line verifies **in person** at the register — there is **no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute** integration for the discount. Because it's checked each visit, there's no "verify once, reuse" convenience; bring an accepted ID every time.

### FAQ

#### Does Finish Line offer a military discount?
Yes — **10% off in-store purchases** for active, veteran, and retired U.S. military, Reserves, and National Guard (plus spouse, domestic partner, dependents). It does **not** apply online.

#### How much is the Finish Line military discount?
**10%**, in stores only. On a ~$150 purchase that's about $15 off.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes — veterans and retirees qualify (DD214, VA Veteran ID Card, VFW/American Legion card accepted), and so do spouses, domestic partners, and dependents when the eligible customer presents ID.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person at the register with a valid military/veteran ID — there's no online verification.

#### Does Finish Line use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. ID.me's own page confirms **"Finish Line does not use ID.me for Military verification."** A WeSalute (Veterans Advantage) VetRewards Card is one *accepted ID* at the register, but it isn't an online verification path.

#### Can I use it online?
No — the military discount is **in-store only**.

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards, and any online/app purchase. It can't be stacked with other coupons (only one STATUS Reward).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Only **one STATUS Reward** may combine in store; no other coupon stacks. Online, there's no military discount, so use public sale + code + cashback instead.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Finish Line?
In store: **10% military + a STATUS Reward**. Online: a **sale + public code + cashback**, which often beats 10%.

#### Does Finish Line offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No separate first-responder, teacher, or nurse program is published; the in-store discount is military/veteran only.
