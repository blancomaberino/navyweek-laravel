# Walmart Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Walmart does not offer an everyday military or veteran discount** on its products — no percentage off, no flat amount, no gated coupon for active duty, veterans, or spouses, in store or online. Walmart's public stance is "Everyday Low Prices for everyone," and none of its official consumer pages publish a standing military discount. What Walmart *does* offer the military community is indirect: (1) **Walmart+ Assist**, a 50%-off Walmart+ membership ($49/yr or $6.47/mo vs. the standard $98/yr) available to recipients of qualifying government-assistance programs — and **"Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit" is on Walmart's official list of qualifying programs**, verified through **SheerID**; and (2) seasonal **Veterans Day "deals"**, which on Walmart's own site are general public sales pages, not a verified members-only discount. The single most important caveat: the only veteran-specific path Walmart officially documents is Walmart+ Assist *for veterans who receive the VA Pension Benefit* — a needs-based pension, not all veterans — so this is **not** a broad veteran discount. The widely-repeated "Walmart gives veterans 10% off on Veterans Day via SheerID" claim is **not confirmed on any current Walmart-owned page** and should not be stated as fact.

> Accuracy note: Aggregator and "military discount" blog pages frequently claim a **10% Walmart Veterans Day discount verified by SheerID**. As of June 23, 2026, **no Walmart.com or corporate.walmart.com page confirms a 10% Veterans Day discount or any SheerID-gated Veterans Day offer.** Walmart's "Veterans Day Deals" / "Veterans Day Sales" pages are ordinary product-sale landing pages ("Shop for Veterans Day Deals at Walmart.com. Save money. Live better") open to all shoppers. Treat the 10% figure as **unverified**. The only SheerID use Walmart documents is for Walmart+ Assist eligibility.

### How Verification Works
Walmart uses **SheerID** to verify eligibility for **Walmart+ Assist** — not for any product discount. You enter your information, SheerID confirms you're in a qualifying program (the VA **Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit** is one), and you may be asked for documentation. Verification lasts **one year**; Walmart re-checks eligibility annually to renew the discounted membership. There is no ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute flow on Walmart's consumer site for a military discount, because no such discount exists.

### FAQ

#### Does Walmart offer a military discount?
No. Walmart does not offer an everyday military or veteran discount, in store or online. Its policy is "Everyday Low Prices" for all customers. No Walmart consumer page publishes a military discount as of June 2026.

#### How much is the Walmart military discount?
There isn't one. Walmart publishes no military or veteran percentage off. The only military-relevant savings is Walmart+ Assist (50% off a Walmart+ membership) for those receiving a qualifying government program such as the VA Veterans & Survivors Pension Benefit.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for anything?
Not for a product discount. Veterans (and survivors) who receive the VA **Veterans and Survivors Pension Benefit** can qualify for **Walmart+ Assist** at $49/year, verified through SheerID. Standard veteran status, VA disability comp, or military retirement pay alone is not listed as a qualifying program.

#### Does Walmart give a 10% discount on Veterans Day?
Not according to Walmart's own pages. Walmart runs "Veterans Day Deals/Sales," but those are general sale pages open to every shopper — not a verified, military-only 10% discount. The widely-shared "10% via SheerID" claim is unverified on official sources.

#### How do I verify my military status with Walmart?
For a discount, you can't — there's nothing to verify. For **Walmart+ Assist**, you verify your qualifying-program status (including the VA Pension Benefit) through **SheerID** at walmart.com/plus/assist; verification lasts one year and renews annually.

#### Does Walmart use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Walmart uses **SheerID** — but only to verify eligibility for **Walmart+ Assist**, not for a military product discount. It does not use ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for any customer military offer.

#### Can I use a military discount in Walmart stores?
No. Cashiers cannot apply a military or veteran discount. Walmart has no register-level military discount program.

#### What about the Walmart 10% discount I've heard about?
That 10% is the **Walmart associate (employee) discount card** on general merchandise and produce — an employment benefit after 90 days on the job. It is not available to customers and is unrelated to military status.

#### Does Walmart offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No first-responder, teacher, or nurse discount is published. **Walmart+ Student** offers the same 50%-off membership ($49/yr) to verified college students — but that's education-based, not military or first-responder.
