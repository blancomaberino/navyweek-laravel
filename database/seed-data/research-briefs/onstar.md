# OnStar — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 12 months of a standalone OnStar Connected Services subscription (plan prices vary by tier — see note; treated as ~$300–$600/year).

## Headline finding

There is **no published, standalone OnStar subscription military discount**. OnStar does not advertise a percentage off its Connected Services plans (Remote Access / Premium) for veterans or active-duty members, and its own plans page shows no ID.me/military verification path. What *does* exist is **GM's Military Appreciation vehicle-purchase program** (gmmilitarydiscount.com / gmmilitaryappreciation.com) — a discount on **buying or leasing a new GM vehicle**, a different product from an OnStar subscription — which historically bundles complimentary OnStar trial periods and has separate OnStar MPP program rules. Aggregator claims of "up to 20% off OnStar for the life of the subscription" appear to conflate this with the **GM College Discount** (a student program that does discount OnStar plans), and are **not** a verified military benefit. Honest answer: for a standalone OnStar subscription, the real levers are the **complimentary trial that ships with a new GM vehicle**, **retention/promo pricing**, and **choosing the lowest tier that covers your need** — not a military code.

### Who Qualifies
- For the **GM vehicle** program: Active Duty, Reservists, National Guard, Retirees, Veterans within 3 years of discharge, plus spouses/designated household members (U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard).
- For a **standalone OnStar subscription**: no military eligibility group applies — there's no such discount.

### How To Redeem (what actually exists)
1. **Buying a GM vehicle?** Visit gmmilitarydiscount.com / gmmilitaryappreciation.com, confirm eligibility, get your authorization number, and present it at a GM dealer.
2. **Managing a subscription?** Choose the lowest OnStar tier that meets your need; ask about annual prepay or current promos; use the complimentary trial that came with your vehicle.

### How Verification Works
The **GM vehicle** military program verifies eligibility through GM's military discount portal (authorization number presented at the dealer). There is **no military verification on OnStar subscription checkout** because there's no subscription discount.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The GM Military Appreciation discount is a **vehicle-purchase** incentive; it does **not** reduce OnStar subscription fees.
- GM's OnStar Military Appreciation content historically **does not apply to data plans**.
- Complimentary OnStar trials are tied to **eligible new vehicles**, not existing subscriptions.

### Best Ways To Save More
- Take the **complimentary OnStar trial** included with a new GM vehicle.
- **Downgrade to the lowest tier** (e.g., Remote Access) if you don't need in-vehicle data/nav.
- Ask about **promo/retention pricing** or **annual prepay** when renewing.
- If purchasing a car, use the **GM Military Appreciation** vehicle discount — the meaningful dollars are on the vehicle, not OnStar.

### FAQ
#### Does OnStar offer a military discount?
Not on standalone subscriptions. GM offers a **Military Appreciation vehicle-purchase** discount, which is a different product.

#### Is there a percentage off OnStar plans for veterans?
No verified standalone percentage exists. Claims of "20% off OnStar for life" for military appear to conflate the **GM College (student)** discount and are unverified for military.

#### Do veterans and retirees qualify for the GM vehicle discount?
Yes — the GM Military Appreciation program covers Active Duty, Reserve/Guard, Retirees, and Veterans within 3 years of discharge, plus spouses/household members.

#### How do I verify my status?
For the vehicle program, through GM's military discount portal (gmmilitarydiscount.com). There is no verification for subscriptions because there is no subscription discount.

#### Does OnStar use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No — none of these apply to OnStar subscription checkout.

#### Can I get free OnStar as a veteran?
Not via a military benefit. You can get a **complimentary trial** with an eligible new GM vehicle, available to any buyer.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to use OnStar?
Use the complimentary new-vehicle trial, keep the lowest tier that meets your need, and ask for promo/retention pricing at renewal.

#### Does the GM discount lower my monthly OnStar bill?
No. It reduces the price of a **new vehicle**, not your OnStar subscription.
