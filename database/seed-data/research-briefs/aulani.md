# Aulani, a Disney Resort & Spa — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 4-night stay in a Standard View hotel room at Aulani in the late-summer window (~$599/night rack = **$2,396 room subtotal**, before Hawaii taxes ~17.9% and the $45/night resort fee, which apply on every path).

## Headline finding

Yes — Disney's Aulani resort in Ko Olina, Hawaiʻi runs a **real, official military room-rate discount**, refreshed every season, of **up to 25–35% off** select rooms and villas for stays of **2 or more consecutive nights**. It is booked directly with Disney (online or by phone) and verified **in person with a valid U.S. military ID at check-in** — there is **no ID.me / SheerID / GovX gate**. Eligibility is **active or retired** service members (all branches incl. Guard, Reserve, Coast Guard, Space Force, PHS and NOAA Commissioned Corps) and their spouses; ordinary **veterans who are not retired do not qualify**, and neither do DoD civilians. This is a **standalone Aulani offer — NOT the Disney Armed Forces Salute** (that program covers Disney World / Disneyland park tickets and Salute room rates, and does not apply at Aulani). Best-savings verdict: for a **standard hotel room**, the military rate is the best readily-available path (30% off in the current late-summer window); for **studio/1-bedroom villa** stays, **renting DVC points** through a broker frequently beats the military percentage. Archetype: **Travel/hospitality seasonal-offer brand** with a dedicated, recurring military rate.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **No stacking, period.** Disney's terms state the military offer is "applied once per reservation and is not valid in combination with any other hotel or villa discount or offer." You pick one discount — military OR public OR Visa OR AP — never two.
2. **DVC point rental is a different booking channel, not a stack** — you either book a discounted cash rate through Disney, or you book a villa via rented points. You can't apply a Disney % discount on top of a points reservation.
3. **The discount is conditional on ID at check-in.** If the eligible member/spouse isn't present with a valid military ID and staying in the room, Disney rebills the full non-discounted rate — effectively "unstacking" the discount after the fact.
4. Resort fee (~$45/night), Hawaii TAT/GET taxes (~17.9%), and parking are charged on top of every path and are **not** discounted by the military rate.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** (all branches) — eligible.
- **Retired military** — eligible.
- **National Guard & Reservists** — eligible.
- **U.S. Coast Guard, U.S. Space Force** — eligible.
- **Commissioned Corps of PHS and NOAA** — eligible.
- **Spouses** — eligible, if presenting a valid & active U.S. military ID in the service member's absence; member or spouse must stay in the room.
- **Veterans who separated but did NOT retire** — not eligible per the terms.
- **DoD civilians / contractors** — not listed; not eligible.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Book (online or phone):**
1. Go to disneyaulani.com and open the current military room offer, or call **(866) 443-4763** (7am–9pm PT).
2. Choose a stay of **2+ consecutive nights** inside the active discount window; select an eligible room/villa (suites and 3-BR Grand Villas excluded).
3. Book — no status upload is required at booking. Limit two rooms/villas per reservation.

**At check-in:**
1. The eligible **active or retired member (or spouse)** presents a valid U.S. military ID.
2. That person must be staying in a room on the reservation. If no valid ID is presented, Disney charges the full non-discounted rate for the stay.

### How Verification Works
Verification is **manual and in-person**: a valid military ID (CAC, retiree ID, or eligible dependent/spouse ID) shown at the front desk. There is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute account involved, and no code to enter online. Because the check happens at check-in, an ineligible booking can be re-priced to rack rate on arrival — so only book it if you can present a qualifying ID.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- 2+ consecutive nights required; length-of-stay rules affect eligibility and cancelling nights can void the discount.
- "Up to" %: the headline rate is a ceiling and varies by room type and date; not all rooms/dates get the max.
- Suites and 3-bedroom Grand Villas are excluded; allocation is limited and can sell out.
- Not combinable with any other room/villa discount or offer; one discount per reservation.
- Resort fee (~$45/night), Hawaii taxes (~17.9%), and parking are **not** discounted.
- Peak-summer weeks (roughly mid-June to early August) usually fall outside the military windows.

### FAQ
#### Does Aulani offer a military discount?
Yes. Disney's Aulani Resort runs a seasonal military room-rate discount of up to 25–35% off select rooms and villas for stays of 2+ nights, for eligible active and retired service members.

#### How much is the Aulani military discount?
It varies by season: up to 25% (winter 2026), up to 30% (spring and late-summer 2026), and up to 35% (fall/holiday 2026). The "up to" figure is a ceiling that depends on room type and dates.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Spouses qualify (with a valid active military ID in the member's absence). Active and retired members of all branches qualify. A **veteran who did not retire does not qualify**, and DoD civilians are not covered. The eligible member or spouse must stay in the room.

#### How do I verify my status?
You show a valid U.S. military ID **in person at check-in** — there's no online verification step and no code.

#### Does Aulani use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Aulani verifies with a physical military ID at the front desk; none of those third-party providers are used for this offer.

#### Can I use it online or only in stores?
You book online at disneyaulani.com or by phone; the ID is checked at check-in. It's a room reservation, not a retail purchase.

#### What is excluded?
Suites and 3-bedroom Grand Villas, non-consecutive stays, stays under 2 nights, and most peak-summer dates. Resort fees, taxes, and parking aren't discounted, and it can't be combined with other offers.

#### Can I combine it with other discounts or promo codes?
No. Disney applies the military rate once per reservation and it can't be combined with any other room/villa discount or offer.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to stay at Aulani?
For a standard hotel room, the military rate (30% now) is the best easy path. For a studio or 1-bedroom villa, **renting DVC points** through a broker usually saves more than the military percentage.

#### Is the Aulani military discount the same as the Disney Armed Forces Salute?
No. The Armed Forces Salute is a Walt Disney World / Disneyland ticket-and-room program and does not apply at Aulani. Aulani has its own standalone military room offer.

#### Does the discount include park tickets?
No — Aulani has no theme parks. It's a resort-only room discount; there are no bundled park tickets like at Disney World.
