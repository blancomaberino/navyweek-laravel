# Guitar Center Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Guitar Center offers active-duty service members and veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces **10% off a single item of $199 or more, capped at a $500 maximum discount**. Eligibility is verified through **SheerID** via a "verify your military ID" flow on Guitar Center's military offer page; on success the shopper receives a unique promo code to apply at checkout. The offer can be redeemed online, in store, or by phone (866-498-7882). The single most important caveat: the official page displays the offer with a **"Now thru Nov. 11"** date window, so this may be a Veterans Day–period promotion rather than a guaranteed year-round program — the exact percentage and availability should be re-verified before publishing, especially outside the November window (verified June 23, 2026). Eligibility is limited to active duty and veterans only; reservists, retirees, spouses, dependents, and first responders are not named in the official offer.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Guitar Center's military offer page (guitarcenter.com/pages/Military-Discount.gc).
2. Click to verify your military ID; complete the SheerID verification.
3. Receive your unique promo code.
4. Add a qualifying single item of $199+ to your cart.
5. Apply the promo code at checkout (discount is 10%, up to $500 max).

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify your military status through Guitar Center's SheerID flow and obtain your unique promo code (the offer is claimable in-store, online, or by phone).
2. Bring your qualifying item ($199+) to the register.
3. Provide your promo code so the associate can apply the 10% discount (up to $500 max).
4. If anything doesn't apply, call 866-498-7882 for support.

*Note: The official page lists in-store as a redemption channel and points to the same verify-and-code flow; the exact in-store proof process (e.g., showing the code vs. a military ID) is not spelled out step-by-step on the page. Confirm locally.*

### How Verification Works
Guitar Center uses **SheerID** to confirm military status. You click to verify your military ID through a SheerID flow on Guitar Center's offer page; on successful verification you get a unique promo code to apply at checkout. Account creation requirements and re-verification frequency are not stated on the official page. SheerID verifications are typically one-time per offer/code, but Guitar Center does not publish a re-verification cadence — do not promise "verify once forever." If automatic verification fails, the page directs shoppers to claim by phone at 866-498-7882.

#### How much is the Guitar Center military discount?
10% off one qualifying item priced $199 or more, capped at a $500 maximum discount. (Source: Guitar Center Military Discount page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

Veterans qualify, along with active-duty members of the U.S. Armed Forces. Military spouses and dependents are not named on the official offer page, so their eligibility is not confirmed. (Source: Guitar Center Military Discount page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. The offer can be claimed in store, online, or by phone (866-498-7882). You verify through the same flow and apply your promo code. (Source: Guitar Center Military Discount page, accessed June 23, 2026.)
