# Batteries Plus — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ≈$120 order (e.g., a car battery or phone-screen repair) at Batteries Plus.

## Headline finding

Batteries Plus (Batteries Plus Bulbs) does **not** publish an official, company-wide military or veteran discount. ID.me explicitly states it is "not aware that Batteries Plus offers Military discounts," and there is no ID.me/SheerID/GovX storefront gate. What genuinely saves money are Batteries Plus's own **public coupons/promotions**, the **My Plus Rewards** loyalty program, occasional **WeSalute (Veterans Advantage) member offers** (a benefit of paid WeSalute membership, not a Batteries Plus program), and — because Batteries Plus is a franchise — **individual store manager discretion**. Treat any coupon-site "15% off military" claim as unverified. The honest answer: **no official military discount published**; stack a public coupon with My Plus Rewards for the real best price.
