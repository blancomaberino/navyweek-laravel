## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **Member store pricing does not stack with portal cashback — treat as no-stack.** Rakuten and TopCashback track purchases on **geappliances.com**; the member store runs on a different domain (**myapstore.com**). No source supports cashback on member-store orders, and TopCashback explicitly voids on codes/paths it doesn't list. Don't promise this stack.
2. **Public sale + portal cashback DOES work.** Both Rakuten and TopCashback listed GE's July 4th sale deals as cashback-eligible on July 7, 2026 — the portal % applies to the sale price. This is the reliable stack.
3. **Portal exclusions:** Rakuten excludes filters and parts/accessories; TopCashback excludes filters, Kona Pellet, installation services, and any coupon code it doesn't display. The public codes STRIPES26 (filters 10%) and STARS26 (parts 20%) are for excluded categories anyway.
4. **Member store internal stacking is unpublished.** Whether member pricing combines with GE's public rebates (Innovation Rebate, FIT Guarantee) is **not stated** anywhere — flagged as an open question, never assert it.
5. **Big-box military discounts are a dead end for appliances.** Lowe's 10% (ID.me): "can't be applied to major appliances." Home Depot's 10% is "on full-priced, non-appliance merchandise." Neither touches a GE fridge.
6. **GovX is a closed substitute** — no portal, no codes on top; compare its net price only.
7. **Exchange = tax-free + free major-appliance shipping**, but no additional 10% on appliances. Eligibility per channel, precisely: all honorably discharged veterans can shop the exchange **online** (Veterans Online Shopping Benefit, 2017); in-store privileges are limited to active duty, retirees, dependents, and the 2021-expansion groups (service-connected disabled veterans, Purple Heart recipients, former POWs, caregivers). Never imply every veteran can shop on base.

### FAQ
#### Does GE offer a military discount?
Yes — GE Appliances (the Haier-owned appliance maker) offers verified military members exclusive pricing through a private online store. You verify once with ID.me, then shop the GE Appliances Store where the discount is applied automatically to appliances $99 and up.

#### How much is the GE military discount?
GE doesn't publish a percentage — anywhere. Pricing is set per item inside the member store. Shoppers have reported roughly 20% off list on some models, but that's not a guaranteed rate. Ignore coupon sites claiming a flat "10–25%" or "40% off" military discount; GE states no such number.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
GE's own page says "members of our U.S. military" without itemizing. Military.com's offer page lists active duty, retirees, veterans, Reserve/National Guard, and dependents. ID.me's verification determines final eligibility.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me, from geappliances.com/military-discount. It's a one-time verification; afterward you just sign in to the GE Appliances Store.

#### Does GE Appliances use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
ID.me is the verification provider for the official program. GovX hosts a separate GE Appliances storefront, and WeSalute lists an offer that routes to the same member store. SheerID is not part of this program.

#### Can I use it in stores?
No. The program is online-only, through the private GE Appliances Store. It doesn't apply at Home Depot, Lowe's, or Best Buy — and note that both Home Depot's and Lowe's own 10% military discounts explicitly exclude major appliances.

#### What is excluded?
Monogram® appliances, and anything under $99. The discount also can't be combined with cashback portals, which only track regular geappliances.com purchases.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
There are no codes — member pricing applies automatically. GE hasn't published whether member pricing combines with its public rebates. The alternative stack is buying a public sale price on geappliances.com plus portal cashback (Rakuten 10% / TopCashback 8% as of July 7, 2026), which often beats the member store during big sales.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from GE Appliances?
If your model is in a GE public sale: the sale price plus cashback usually wins. If it's full price: the ID.me member store. If you're a veteran in a high-tax state buying big-ticket: price the tax-free Home Depot storefront on shopmyexchange.com — no sales tax and free appliance delivery.

#### Does GE offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Yes — parallel programs with the same ID.me verification, same private store, same $99 floor, and same Monogram exclusion, via dedicated pages for first responders, healthcare professionals, and teachers & students.

#### Does GE run a Veterans Day sale?
GE maintains a Veterans Day deals page, but it promotes the same year-round ID.me member benefit rather than a dated extra discount. Its public holiday sales (July 4th, Black Friday, Memorial Day) are where the dated markdowns happen.

#### Can I buy GE Appliances at the Exchange?
Yes, via the Home Depot storefront on shopmyexchange.com — tax-free with free major-appliance shipping. All honorably discharged veterans can shop the exchange online (Veterans Online Shopping Benefit); in-store base access is narrower.
