# Dell Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Dell runs a **Heroes Purchase Program** that gives verified military members, veterans, and retirees **up to 10% extra off** eligible PCs, electronics, and accessories on **Dell.com only**. Eligibility is verified through **SheerID** from inside your Dell account's membership section after you create a Dell account and register for **Dell Rewards**; once verified, the discount applies automatically in your cart at checkout. The single most important caveat: the official page says "**Save up to 10% extra**," so 10% is the **maximum**, not a guaranteed flat rate, and the discount is capped at **two eligible products per transaction and two transactions per year**, with broad exclusions (warranties, gift cards, clearance, limited-quantity deals, and "some PCs, electronics, and accessories"). Dell's official page lists only **active-duty, veterans, and retirees** as eligible — it does **not** state that Reserve/National Guard, spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families qualify.

> Accuracy notes:
> - Many aggregators present a **flat "10% off."** Dell's own Heroes page says "Save up to **10% extra**" — treat 10% as a ceiling that varies by product, not a guaranteed rate on every item.
> - Aggregators (and some AI summaries) claim **Reserve/National Guard members, spouses, dependents, and Gold Star families** are eligible. Dell's official Heroes FAQ does **not** state this — it lists only active-duty, veterans, and retirees. Flagged as unverified.
> - Verification provider is **SheerID** per Dell's official Heroes page. A separate **Dell store also exists on ID.me Shop** (shop.id.me/stores/508-dell), but Dell's own program page routes verification through SheerID. Do not assert ID.me as the official Dell.com checkout provider.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Dell.com and **create a Dell account** (or sign in to an existing one).
2. **Register for Dell Rewards** (free) during account creation or in your account.
3. In your account's **membership section**, select the **military discount program** and click **Verify**.
4. Complete the **SheerID** verification form; upload documents if SheerID requests them.
5. Once verified, shop eligible products — the discount is **automatically applied in your cart at checkout** (no code needed).

### How To Redeem In Store
Dell's military discount is **online only**. There is **no in-store redemption** — the Heroes program is valid only for purchases made directly on Dell.com. (Dell does not operate consumer retail stores for this purpose, and the discount cannot be used at third-party retailers that sell Dell products.)

### How Verification Works
Dell uses **SheerID**, a third-party verification service, to confirm your active-duty, veteran, or retiree status. You start verification from your **Dell account's membership section**, not at general checkout: open the military discount program, click **Verify**, and complete the SheerID form, uploading documents if requested. Verification is usually **almost instant**; if SheerID needs to review documents manually, it typically takes about **20 minutes** in the U.S. For verification problems (pending or failed), Dell directs you to **SheerID's Help Center**. For discount or purchase issues on Dell.com after you're verified, Dell directs you to its **sales team via Contact Us**.

### FAQ

#### Does Dell offer a military discount?
Yes. Through its Heroes Purchase Program, Dell gives verified military members, veterans, and retirees up to 10% extra off eligible PCs, electronics, and accessories on Dell.com.

#### How much is the Dell military discount?
"Up to 10% extra." Dell's official page frames it as a maximum, so the exact savings vary by product. It's capped at two eligible products per transaction and two transactions per year.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify, along with active-duty members. Dell's official page does **not** list spouses, dependents, Reserve/National Guard, or Gold Star families as eligible, so don't assume they qualify — verify directly through SheerID.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a Dell account, register for Dell Rewards, then go to your account's membership section, select the military discount program, and complete the SheerID verification form. Verification is usually almost instant.

#### Does Dell use SheerID, ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute?
Dell's official Heroes program verifies through **SheerID**. (A separate Dell store exists on ID.me Shop, but Dell's own program page routes verification through SheerID.)

#### Can I use the Dell military discount in stores?
No. The discount is valid only for purchases made directly on Dell.com — there's no in-store or third-party-retailer redemption.

#### What is excluded?
Warranties, gift cards, clearance, limited-quantity deals, certain order codes, and some PCs, electronics, and accessories are excluded. The discount is also limited to two items per order and two orders per year.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
It can combine with select Dell offers but **cannot** be stacked with other Dell coupons. Sometimes a seasonal sale price beats the discount, so compare before checking out.

#### Does Dell offer a first responder, nurse, medical, or student discount?
Yes — the same Heroes program covers first responders (police, firefighters, EMTs, search-and-rescue) and medical workers (doctors, nurses, healthcare workers), each verified separately via SheerID. Dell also runs separate Student and Senior discount programs.
