# Marcus Theatres — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult evening general-admission movie ticket (typical Marcus box-office adult evening ≈ $12–14 before any discount).

## Headline finding

Yes — Marcus Theatres runs a genuine, standing military discount, and it's one of the simplest in the cinema space: **active and retired military can see a movie for $8.50 anytime** by presenting a **valid military ID at the box office**. Separately, up to **25% off box-office price** is available on **electronically delivered / mailed tickets** through Marcus's third-party ticket partners (surfaced via **ID.me** and **WeSalute**, fulfilled through Working Advantage). The box-office $8.50 is the everyday winner for spontaneous single tickets; the ~25%-off electronic tickets win for premium formats or when you're buying ahead for a group. Both are participating-location offers and both exclude the premium-format surcharges (UltraScreen, SuperScreen, 3D, 4DX, ScreenX).

**Electronic / mailed tickets (up to 25% off):**

### FAQ
#### Does Marcus Theatres offer a military discount?
Yes — $8.50 general-admission tickets anytime for active and retired military at the box office with a valid military ID, plus up to 25% off electronic/mailed tickets.

#### How much is the Marcus Theatres military discount?
$8.50 per general-admission ticket at participating box offices. Electronic/mailed tickets are up to 25% off box-office price.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
The box-office rate specifies "active and retired military." The electronic-ticket path via **WeSalute+** extends to veterans, Guard/Reserve, and immediate family. Confirm veteran acceptance at your local box office.

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a valid military ID at the box office for $8.50. For electronic tickets, verify via **ID.me** or subscribe to **WeSalute+**.

#### Does Marcus use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The box-office rate uses a **physical ID check**. Electronic tickets are surfaced through **ID.me** and **WeSalute** (fulfilled via Working Advantage); **GovX** lists Marcus tickets too.

#### Can I use it in stores/online?
The $8.50 rate is **in person at the box office**. The up-to-25%-off rate is **online** electronic/mailed tickets.

#### What is excluded?
Premium formats (UltraScreen/SuperScreen/3D/4DX/ScreenX) carry surcharges; participating locations only; electronic sales final.

#### Can I combine it with a value day or promo code?
No — military pricing doesn't stack with public value days. Buy whichever single price is lowest.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to see a movie?
Standard show: **$8.50 box office**. Premium/group/advance: **electronic tickets up to 25% off**. If a public **Value Tuesday** beats $8.50, take that.
