# Universal Studios Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Universal runs **separate, structurally different military programs at its two U.S. resorts**, and the page must keep them apart. **Universal Orlando Resort** offers the **2026 Military Freedom Pass** — a year-plus theme-park pass priced at **$220 (2-Park)** or **$255 (3-Park, adds Volcano Bay)**, valid **November 13, 2025 through December 18, 2026** with limited blockout dates, sold **only at Military Base ITT/MWR ticket offices (not at the Universal front gate or website)**, plus discounted add-on Epic Universe 1-day tickets and up to 40% off select hotels. **Universal Studios Hollywood** has no "Freedom Pass"; instead it offers **military pricing on day tickets and select passes** purchased through **base MWR/ITT offices** or **online via ID.me verification**. The single most important caveat: the Orlando offer is a **limited-time annually-renewed promotional ticket with hard dates, blockouts, ITT-only purchase, and per-ID purchase limits** — none of those terms transfer to Hollywood, and the exact Hollywood dollar discount is **not published on Universal's official site** (it varies by purchase location).

> Accuracy note: Aggregator and forum pages cite a Hollywood "gate" military discount as anywhere from **$3 to $4 off**, and quote varying MWR ticket prices. Universal's official Hollywood page does not publish a specific dollar figure, so NavyWeek should not assert one. The Orlando $220/$255 figures and dates below ARE confirmed by Universal's official November 13, 2025 press release.

The Orlando offer is a **dated, annually-renewed promotion** with blockout dates and purchase limits, so confirm current terms before you travel.

### How To Redeem In Store
**Universal Orlando — 2026 Military Freedom Pass:**
1. Bring a valid **DEERS-issued military Photo ID** to a participating base **ITT/MWR ticket office** (or order from an authorized office like Patrick SFB ITT or Shades of Green if you lack a local base).
2. Purchase the 2-Park ($220) or 3-Park ($255) pass — up to **6 per valid military ID**.
3. You receive **vouchers** (not active tickets). Save your receipt and photograph the vouchers.
4. At Universal Orlando, **activate** the vouchers at any park ticket booth or resort-hotel ticket counter; the military member or spouse must be present with DEERS ID, and one ticket must be used by the member/spouse.
5. The member/spouse must also show DEERS ID at the **first park entry**; after activation, passes are tied to each person (facial recognition at entry).

**Universal Studios Hollywood:** Buy military-priced tickets in advance at a base **MWR/ITT office**, then present them at the park.

### How Verification Works
- **Orlando:** Verification is **in person via DEERS-issued military Photo ID** at the base ITT/MWR office at purchase, again at voucher activation, and at first park use. No third-party online provider is used for the Freedom Pass — it is sold only through military ticket offices.
- **Hollywood:** Online purchases use **ID.me** to verify the Military community (re-confirmation per ID.me's own policies); in-person purchases use base MWR/ITT credentials. ID.me account creation is required for the online route. Support for ID.me issues: help.id.me.

### FAQ

#### Does Universal Studios offer a military discount?
Yes — but it differs by resort. Universal Orlando Resort sells the **2026 Military Freedom Pass** ($220 2-Park / $255 3-Park) through base ITT/MWR offices. Universal Studios Hollywood offers **military pricing on tickets and select passes** via base MWR/ITT or online with ID.me.

#### How much is the Universal Orlando military discount?
The 2026 Military Freedom Pass is **$220 for 2 parks** (Universal Studios Florida + Islands of Adventure) and **$255 for 3 parks** (adds Volcano Bay), valid Nov 13, 2025 through Dec 18, 2026 with limited blockout dates.

#### How much is the Universal Studios Hollywood military discount?
Universal does not publish a specific dollar amount on its official site — the savings depend on where you buy (base MWR/ITT typically offers deeper discounts than the gate). Verify the current price through your base ticket office or the ID.me-gated checkout.

#### Where do I buy the Military Freedom Pass?
Only at participating **base ITT/MWR ticket offices** (or authorized offices like Patrick SFB ITT or Shades of Green). It **cannot** be bought at the Universal Orlando front gate or on Universal's website. You'll need a valid DEERS-issued military photo ID.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Spouses of eligible members qualify (and can use the benefit without the member present, with DEERS ID). Active duty, retired, Guard, Reserve, Coast Guard, and active DoD civilians qualify. Universal Orlando's press release also names **100% disabled veterans and/or their spouses**. General veterans without a qualifying DoD/DEERS ID aren't broadly named for the Orlando ITT program; check ID.me for Hollywood's online eligibility.

#### Does Universal use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Universal Studios Hollywood uses **ID.me** for online military verification. Universal Orlando's Military Freedom Pass is verified **in person via DEERS military ID** at base ticket offices, not through an online provider. (Universal Studios Hollywood also appears on GovX as a third-party seller.)

#### Are there blockout dates?
Yes, for the Orlando Military Freedom Pass: Dec 25, 2025 – Jan 4, 2026 and Mar 30, 2026 – Apr 6, 2026 for all parks, plus Oct 26 – Dec 18, 2026 for Volcano Bay (3-Park only).

#### Can I use the pass at Epic Universe?
Epic Universe isn't included in the base pass. With an **activated** Military Freedom Pass you can buy specially priced 1-Day Epic Universe add-on tickets (limit 1/day/pass, 6 per transaction).

#### Does Universal offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
At **Universal Studios Hollywood**, ID.me lists separate communities (nurse, responder, teacher, government, medical, student, alumni) that may have offers. These are not part of the Orlando Military Freedom Pass.
