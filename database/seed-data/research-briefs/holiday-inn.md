# Holiday Inn — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **1-night leisure stay** at a Holiday Inn / Holiday Inn Express whose **Best Flexible Rate is $150/night** (pre-tax; a representative mid-market IHG nightly rate). All effective prices are pre-tax unless noted.

## Headline finding

Holiday Inn — an **IHG (InterContinental Hotels Group)** brand — participates in **two real, IHG-run booking rates** you can select on **ihg.com**: a **Military Appreciation (leisure) Rate** and a **Government Rate**. The Military Appreciation rate is genuine but **variable** — IHG publishes only a floor of **"minimum 5% off the Best Flexible Rate,"** with the actual discount varying by hotel, date, and availability (properties often run 10–15%). It's for **leisure travel**, open to **US and Canadian active duty, veterans, retired military, and family/dependents with valid ID**, requires **military or veteran ID at check-in**, **earns IHG One Rewards points**, and **cannot be combined with any other offer, discount, or promotion**. The separate **Government Rate** is for **official government/military travel** (federal/state/local employees, military, contractors, state universities/healthcare) and is priced **at or under the GSA per-diem cap**, verified with an **official ID or work orders/orders** at check-in. As with every hotel program, the honest travel-loyalty truth applies: **rates don't stack, but status and points do** — so the real long-game prize is a **free IHG One Rewards account** (book direct, earn points/status on every stay), and on any given night you should **price-check the military rate against the Best Flexible, AAA, senior, member, and advance-purchase rates and book whichever is lowest** — the military rate is frequently *not* the cheapest. **Archetype: I (travel/loyalty — status stacks, rates don't).**

## Stacking Rules (verified, IHG terms)
1. **Rates are mutually exclusive.** The military, government, AAA, senior, member, and advance-purchase rates each state they **"cannot be used in conjunction with any other offer, discount or promotion."** You choose one rate per booking — you cannot layer the military rate onto a sale rate or a promo code.
2. **Points and status DO layer on top.** Both the military and government rates **"qualify for IHG One Rewards points,"** and qualifying nights count toward status — so the loyalty benefit stacks on whichever paid rate you book. This is the travel-archetype rule: **status stacks, rates don't.**
3. **Book direct to earn.** IHG One Rewards points and member rates require booking through IHG (ihg.com / app) or IHG channels; third-party/OTA bookings generally forfeit points and elite benefits.
4. **Cancellation is not guaranteed flexible.** The military rate's deposit/cancellation follows **the individual hotel's policy** — it may be less flexible than the standard Best Flexible Rate. Advance Purchase is **final/non-refundable**.
5. **No coupon-code stack.** There is no IHG "military promo code"; the discount is a bookable rate, so scraped coupon codes do not apply to it.
6. **Blackout dates and participating-hotel limits** apply to every one of these rates, and **IHG Army Hotels are excluded** from the military leisure rate.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible for the Military Appreciation (leisure) rate with valid military ID.
- **Veterans** — Eligible with a valid veteran ID.
- **Retired military** — Eligible with valid ID.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Covered under active-duty/military ID (verify your ID is accepted at check-in).
- **Military spouses / dependents / family** — Eligible with a **valid dependent ID**; the reservation must be in the **eligible ID holder's name**, and that person must **check in** (a dependent can't book the room for a non-eligible person).
- **Government employees & contractors** (federal/state/county/local, CRCs, state universities/healthcare) — Eligible for the **Government Rate** on **official travel** with official ID/orders.
- **Not eligible for the leisure military rate:** the general public; also note the leisure rate is **not available at IHG Army Hotels**.

### How To Redeem — Military Appreciation (leisure) Rate, online
1. Go to **ihg.com** (or the IHG One Rewards app) and enter your hotel, dates, and guests.
2. On the rate/room selection screen, choose the **Government/Military** rate and select **"Military Appreciation Leisure Rate."**
3. **Compare it** to the Best Flexible, AAA, senior, member, and Advance Purchase rates shown — book whichever is lowest for your dates.
4. Complete the booking (sign in to your free IHG One Rewards account first, so the stay earns points).
5. At check-in, present a **valid military or veteran ID** (or dependent ID if you're a family member). Without accepted ID the hotel can deny the rate.

### How To Redeem — Government Rate (official travel), online or phone
1. On **ihg.com**, select the **Government** rate at room selection (or call **1-877-424-2449**).
2. Confirm the rate is **at or under your GSA per-diem** for the destination.
3. At check-in, present your **official government ID and/or travel orders** (or use your government travel card).

#### How much is the Holiday Inn military discount?
IHG publishes only a **floor of 5% off the Best Flexible Rate**; the actual discount **varies by hotel, date, and availability**, and many properties run **10–15%**. There is no single fixed percentage — the exact number appears at booking.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — **veterans, retired military, and family members/dependents with valid ID** all qualify for the leisure rate. A dependent must book **in their own name** and **check in themselves**.

#### Can I use the military rate for any trip, or only leisure?
The **Military Appreciation Rate is for leisure travel only**. For **official government or military travel**, use the separate **Government Rate**, which is priced at or under the federal per diem.

#### Does the government rate match the per diem?
The Government Rate is set as a **percentage off the Best Flex Rate, at or under the GSA per-diem cap** for that location. It's intended for **official travel** and requires **orders or a government travel card / official ID** at check-in. Exact pricing varies by property.

#### Can I combine the military rate with promo codes, AAA, or IHG points?

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Holiday Inn?

Then earn points by booking direct through IHG One Rewards.

#### Is the military discount available at every Holiday Inn?
No — it's **participating hotels only**, with **blackout dates**, and it is **not available at IHG Army Hotels**. Availability and the exact discount vary by property.

#### Does Holiday Inn run a special Veterans Day or Memorial Day rate?
