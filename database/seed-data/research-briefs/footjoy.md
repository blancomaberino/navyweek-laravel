# FootJoy — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of **FootJoy Pro/SL golf shoes, $175** (footjoy.com list price).

## Headline finding

**FootJoy does not offer a military or veteran discount.** There is no military program on footjoy.com, and **ID.me's own FootJoy page states "We're not aware that FootJoy offers Military discounts"** — no ID.me, no GovX, no SheerID path. FootJoy (an **Acushnet** brand, alongside Titleist) also publishes no first-responder discount. So for a service member the real savings come from **channels that have nothing to do with military status**: FootJoy's **Semi-Annual Sale** and outlet, a **working public promo code**, the free **FJ Insider** program (free shipping), **ID.me Shop cashback (up to ~2.5%)**, and — often the biggest lever — **buying a competing golf-shoe brand that *does* give a military discount** (e.g., PUMA, Callaway, and others via ID.me) or checking the **base exchange** (tax-free). Archetype: **premium single-brand retailer with NO military discount** — the honest answer is "no," and the value is in the sale calendar and competitor substitutes.
