# Norton (antivirus / Norton 360 with LifeLock) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Brand:** Norton (Gen Digital) · norton.com / us.norton.com · **Baseline cart for all math:** one year of **Norton 360 with LifeLock Select** — public new-customer intro **$99.99 for the first year** (renews $189.99/yr), the plan most shoppers searching "Norton military discount" are pricing.

## Headline finding

**Norton does not publish a direct military or veteran discount on norton.com.** Norton's own pages advertise only two self-serve savings: a **multi-subscription discount** ($10 off each annual subscription from the second one onward, shown on the "student discount" URL) and standard **new-customer first-year intro pricing** (e.g. Norton 360 with LifeLock Select at **$99.99 the first year, 47% off** the $189.99 renewal). No page on us.norton.com states a military, veteran, or first-responder rate, and a longstanding Norton Community thread notes the company **discontinued its old military-discount/free-renewal program** years ago. Every "Norton military discount" that circulates online is delivered through a **third-party channel** — **GovX** ("up to 45% off Norton 360 with LifeLock"), **ID.me Shop** (cash back), or **WeSalute** (behind a paid membership). The honest headline: *Norton has no direct military discount; the cheapest path for almost everyone is Norton's own public first-year intro price (already ~47% off), and the only genuine "extra" savings for service members is stacking a cash-back portal (ID.me) on top of that public price.* Archetype: **no direct military discount — public intro price is the real deal; third parties resell it or add cash back.**
