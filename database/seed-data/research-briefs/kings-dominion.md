# Kings Dominion — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult single-day summer 2026 admission to Kings Dominion (Doswell, VA), reference gate price $90.

## Headline finding

Yes — Kings Dominion runs an official, **year-round** military/veteran/first-responder discount: a **$40 single-day ticket** (reference gate $90), verified through **ID.me**, plus a **2026 Military Gold Pass at $89** (unlimited visits, free parking). Eligible buyers can add up to six friend/family tickets online. The park is now operated by **Six Flags** (Cedar Fair and Six Flags merged in July 2024); the old `kingsdominion.com/military-discounts` URL 301-redirects to `sixflags.com/kingsdominion/military-discounts`, which is the authoritative offer page.

The honest catch: the military single-day price is **not always the cheapest single visit**. As of July 8, 2026 the park is running a **public Flash Sale ticket at $35** (through July 31, 2026, no verification needed) that beats the $40 military price — and the military discount **cannot be combined with sales**. The single best path for a repeat visitor is the **$89 Military Gold Pass** (cheaper than the regular $105 Gold Pass and worth it after ~2 visits). Archetype: **ticketed theme park** — an ID.me-gated year-round rate, event-based free-admission windows (Memorial Day "Military Days"), a GovX marketplace channel, and the real value concentrated in the discounted annual pass rather than the single-day ticket.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military/veteran/first-responder discount cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales** — official terms, verbatim. So you choose the military ticket *or* a public sale ticket, whichever is cheaper that day.
2. **Discount is online-only** ("all offers must be redeemed online") except the Memorial Day free-admission window, which is redeemed **in person at the front-gate turnstiles** with a physical U.S. Military ID.
3. **ID.me gates the year-round online discount**; the Memorial Day free day is gated by **physical military ID at the gate**, not ID.me. Different mechanics for different offers.
4. **Up to 6 friend/family tickets** can be bought at the discounted rate under one eligible ID.me account.
5. The **Military Gold Pass ($89)** is itself the discounted product — it is not further combinable with single-day promos.

### Who Qualifies

- **Retired military** — eligible.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online (year-round $40 ticket or $89 Gold Pass):**
1. Go to the Kings Dominion military discounts page (sixflags.com/kingsdominion/military-discounts).
2. Select the military single-day ticket or the 2026 Military Gold Pass; add up to 6 tickets if buying for family/friends.
3. Complete the **ID.me** login prompt to verify eligibility.
4. Pay online and receive digital tickets — all offers must be redeemed online.

**In store (Memorial Day "Military Days" free admission — historical/annual):**
1. During the published Memorial Day weekend window (May 22–25 in 2026), go to the front-gate turnstiles.
2. Present your valid U.S. Military ID for free single-day admission (one day within the window).
3. Buy any friend/family tickets online in advance.

### How Verification Works
The year-round online discount is gated by **ID.me**: after you select your ticket or pass, an ID.me login prompt confirms your military/veteran/first-responder status before checkout. If you don't have an ID.me account you'll create one once and reuse it. The Memorial Day free-admission offer is verified differently — by a **physical U.S. Military ID checked at the gate**, not ID.me. GovX is an alternative marketplace channel that uses its own verification if you prefer it over ID.me.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The military/veteran/first-responder discount **cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales** (official terms).
- Year-round discount is **online only** — "all offers must be redeemed online."
- Up to **6** discounted friend/family tickets per eligible account.
- The $90 figure is a **gate reference price**; the realistic public online price is lower ($45, or less during sales).
- Taxes and fees apply on top of listed prices.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Compare against the current public sale.** If a flash sale (e.g. $35 through July 31, 2026) is below $40, buy the public ticket — it's cheaper and needs no verification.
- **Get the $89 Military Gold Pass** if you'll visit twice or more — it undercuts the regular ~$105 Gold Pass and adds free parking and Halloween Haunt.
- **Use the Memorial Day "Military Days" free-admission window** if you can time a visit there (active/retired military).
- **Buy online, never at the gate** — the gate price roughly doubles the advance online price.
- **GovX** is a fallback verification channel if ID.me gives you trouble.

### FAQ

#### Does Kings Dominion offer a military discount?
Yes. Kings Dominion offers a year-round $40 single-day ticket and an $89 Military Gold Pass to active and retired military, veterans, first responders, and their immediate families, verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the Kings Dominion military discount?
The military single-day ticket is $40 online (the park references a $90 gate price). The 2026 Military Gold Pass is $89 plus fees and covers unlimited visits, free parking, and Halloween Haunt.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify. Immediate family members can be included — an eligible buyer can purchase up to 6 discounted tickets for friends and family online under one ID.me-verified account.

#### How do I verify my status?
For the year-round online discount, you verify through **ID.me** — a login prompt appears after you select your ticket or pass. For the Memorial Day free-admission day, you show a **physical U.S. Military ID at the gate**.

#### Does Kings Dominion use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
The official year-round online discount uses **ID.me**. **GovX** also sells Kings Dominion military/government tickets as a separate marketplace channel. SheerID, WeSalute, and VerifyPass are not used for this offer.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The year-round discount is **online only**. The exception is the Memorial Day "Military Days" free-admission offer, which is redeemed **in person at the front-gate turnstiles** with a military ID.

#### What is excluded?
The military discount **cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales**. It's online-only, capped at 6 friend/family tickets, and taxes/fees apply.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The discount can't be stacked with sales or other offers. Because of this, when a public sale price drops below $40, the public sale ticket is the cheaper choice.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Kings Dominion?
If you're visiting once, buy whichever is cheaper that day — the public flash sale (e.g. $35 through July 31, 2026) or the $40 ID.me military ticket. If you'll visit twice or more in 2026, the $89 Military Gold Pass is the best value. And if you can time a Memorial Day weekend visit, active/retired military get in free.

#### Does Kings Dominion run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Kings Dominion runs "Military Days" over Memorial Day weekend, giving active and retired military free single-day admission with a valid U.S. Military ID (May 22–25 in 2026). Dates vary year to year — verify before you go.
