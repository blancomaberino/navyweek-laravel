# Six Flags Fiesta Texas — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one single-day adult admission (front-gate reference price ~$90).

## Headline finding

**Six Flags Fiesta Texas (San Antonio) has a real, generous military discount — but only online, verified through ID.me.** Military (active & retired) and First Responders can buy a single-day ticket for **$35 online** (front-gate price ~$90) — roughly **61% off** — plus tax and fees, and can add **up to 6 discounted tickets for family and friends**. Separately, the park runs annual **Military Appreciation Days** (historically Memorial Day weekend) with **FREE park admission** for active/retired/veterans/reserve/National Guard on presentation of a valid military ID.

The single best path is situational: **visiting during Memorial Day weekend → the free-admission Military Appreciation Days win outright**; **any other day → the $35 ID.me ticket** (which also beats most public online single-day deals). If you'll visit **2+ times a year**, price a **Season Pass** instead. Archetype: **ticketed-venue online military discount (ID.me) + recurring free-admission appreciation window.** The discount is **not available at the front gate** on ordinary days and **can't be combined** with other offers.
