# Monster Jam — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** four general-seating Monster Jam tickets at ~$40 face each = **$160 face (plus per-ticket fees)** for a family of four.

## Headline finding

There is **no single brand-wide Monster Jam "military program" with a fixed percentage.** Monster Jam is a touring live event; tickets are sold per date through the host venue's ticketing platform (AXS, Ticketmaster, or the venue box office), so the military deal is **fragmented and varies by date and venue.** Three real, verified paths exist: (1) a **venue/box-office military rate redeemed with an ITT-office passcode** — documented at up to **$10 off per ticket plus fee savings** at the Broadmoor World Arena (Colorado Springs) example; (2) **GovX**, the verified military/first-responder ticket marketplace, which lists Monster Jam and prices the discount into the seats after you log in; and (3) **ID.me Shop**, which lists a Monster Jam "military discount and/or cash back offer." A fourth path — **Veteran Tickets Foundation (VetTix)** — periodically offers free or deeply discounted Monster Jam seats to verified service members and veterans, subject to availability. Archetype: **third-party-verified event/ticketing brand** — no owned discount, savings routed through GovX/ID.me/VetTix and the box office, with heavy YMMV by venue. Best move: **check GovX and your base ITT office first for your specific date**, and watch VetTix for free seats.

> **Debunk line:** Aggregators claim a flat "Monster Jam military discount" (some say "up to 40%" via ID.me, "up to 50%" via GovX). Those percentages are **the marketplaces' site-wide maximums across all brands, not a confirmed Monster Jam rate** — do not present them as Monster Jam's number. What is actually verified: a venue box-office rate (up to $10/ticket at the Broadmoor example), a GovX listing whose amount is only shown after verified login, and an ID.me "discount and/or cash back" listing with no published figure. The public "VAAXMJ – save up to 25%" code seen on Monster Jam social posts is a **limited-time public promo (anyone), not a military offer.**

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military/box-office rate does not stack with public promo codes.** The ITT passcode discount is coded directly into the seat price on AXS; you pick one price type, not both (broadmoorworldarena.com).
2. **GovX and ID.me pricing is standalone** — you buy through their marketplace at the shown verified price; Monster Jam/Ticketmaster public codes are not entered on top.
3. **VetTix free/discounted tickets are a separate allotment**, not combinable with any paid discount.
4. **Day-of-event purchases forfeit the discount** — the discounted inventory is not sold the day of the show (broadmoorworldarena.com).

Monster Jam does not run one nationwide military discount with a set percentage. ### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty / Guard / Reserve** — eligible via ITT office, GovX, ID.me, and VetTix.
- **Veterans** — eligible via GovX, ID.me, and VetTix; box-office rates vary by venue.
- **Military spouses/dependents** — often can buy through a sponsor's ITT office; GovX/ID.me eligibility follows their program rules.
- **First responders / law enforcement** — eligible on **GovX** (not a Monster Jam-specific military benefit).

### How To Redeem Online / In Store

**Via base ITT/MWR office (best on fees):**
1. Contact your installation's ITT (Information, Tickets & Travel) or MWR office and ask about Monster Jam for your city/date.
2. Buy directly from the office, or get the AXS/venue **passcode**.
3. Open the venue's ticketing link on **personal wi-fi or cellular data** (gov/military networks block it).
4. Enter the passcode; the discount auto-codes into your selected seats. Buy **before** event day.

**Via GovX:**
1. Create a free GovX account and verify military/first-responder status.
2. Find Monster Jam under GovX event/motorsport tickets; select your city/date; the verified price is shown at checkout.

**Via ID.me:**
1. Verify your military status with ID.me.
2. Open the Monster Jam Tickets offer on ID.me Shop and follow the offer/cash-back link.

**Via VetTix:**
1. Register at vettix.org and verify service/veteran status.
2. Watch for Monster Jam listings in your area; claim free/discounted tickets while available.

### How Verification Works
Each channel verifies separately: **ITT/MWR** checks your military ID in person; **GovX** verifies through its own account system (military, veteran, first responder, law enforcement); **ID.me** verifies your status once and reuses it across partner offers; **VetTix** verifies service/veteran status at signup. None of these is a permanent Monster Jam entitlement — offers appear per event and can lapse.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **No brand-wide guaranteed rate** — availability and amount vary by venue, date, and platform.
- Discounted tickets are **not sold the day of the event**; buy in advance.
- Ticketing links **do not load on government/military networks** — use personal wi-fi/data.
- Discounts typically **don't stack** with public promo codes; premium seats, pit parties, and VIP upgrades may be excluded.
- GovX first-responder eligibility is **not** a Monster Jam-specific military benefit; don't conflate the two.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Check your base ITT/MWR office first** — often the deepest cut plus fee savings.
- **Compare GovX/ID.me vs. any live public code** — sometimes a public "save up to 25%" promo beats the verified price.
- **Watch VetTix** for free Monster Jam seats in your city.
- **Kids-ticket & free pit-party deals** are public and family-friendly — stack the family math there.
- **Buy early** — best seats and discount inventory go before event day.

### FAQ

#### Does Monster Jam offer a military discount?
There's no single nationwide Monster Jam military program, but verified service members can save several ways: a base ITT/MWR box-office rate, GovX, ID.me Shop, and free seats via VetTix. Availability varies by city and date.

#### How much is the Monster Jam military discount?
It's not a fixed percentage. One arena example documents **up to $10 off per ticket plus lower fees** through the base ITT passcode; GovX and ID.me price the discount into the tickets after you verify, so the amount depends on the event.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify through GovX, ID.me, and VetTix. Spouses/dependents can usually buy through a sponsor's ITT office; GovX/ID.me follow their own eligibility rules.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person with a military ID at an ITT/MWR office, or online through GovX, ID.me, or VetTix account verification.

#### Does Monster Jam use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
Monster Jam tickets appear on **GovX** and **ID.me Shop**, and free/discounted seats show on **VetTix**. There's no confirmed SheerID/WeSalute/VerifyPass Monster Jam military offer.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Both: buy in person at a base ITT office or box office, or online via a venue passcode link, GovX, ID.me, or VetTix. Just not on the day of the show, and not over a government network.

#### What is excluded?
Day-of-event purchases, premium/pit-party/VIP upgrades (often), and combining with public promo codes. Amounts and availability aren't guaranteed at every venue.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — you choose one discounted price type. The military/box-office rate and public codes don't stack.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Monster Jam tickets?
If you can reach a base ITT/MWR office, that's usually cheapest (rate plus fee savings). Otherwise compare GovX/ID.me against any live public code, and check VetTix for free seats first.

#### Does Monster Jam offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
First responders and law enforcement can buy through **GovX**. ID.me Shop lists offers for broader groups (nurses, teachers), but the Monster Jam amount is event-specific and not published in advance.
