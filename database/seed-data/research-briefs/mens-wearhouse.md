# Men's Wearhouse Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Men's Wearhouse does **not** run a verified everyday, year-round military discount. What is documented from a primary source is a **seasonal Military Appreciation Week**: a **25% in-store discount** for **active military service members, veterans, and their family members** with a **verified military ID**, offered across Men's Wearhouse, Jos. A. Bank, and Moores stores (all Tailored Brands). The verified instance ran **June 29 – July 6, 2024** (Tailored Brands' first-ever Military Appreciation Week, announced via a Men's Wearhouse/Tailored Brands press release). Around **Veterans Day**, the brand has separately run a **20% in-store** military event. There is **no ID.me path** — Men's Wearhouse is listed on ID.me as **not currently** offering a military discount there — and the discount is applied **in store** with a qualifying ID.

The single most important caveats: (1) these are **limited-time events**, not an always-on discount — the exact percentage and dates change by promotion, so a page must be framed around "watch for Military Appreciation Week / Veterans Day" rather than a standing rate; (2) the verified 25% offer **excludes rental items** (a core Men's Wearhouse category — tuxedo/suit rentals); and (3) redemption is **in store**, not online. For a shopper who needs a suit today outside a promo window, the honest best-savings paths are Men's Wearhouse's own frequent sales/clearance, the Perfect Fit rewards program, and outlet pricing — not a military code.

> Accuracy note: Coupon/aggregator sites advertise "up to 40% off," "25%," "20%," and even "60% OFF" Men's Wearhouse military discounts, sometimes implying a permanent code. The **verified** figures are a **25%** in-store Military Appreciation Week (June 29–July 6, 2024, verified military ID, excludes rentals) and a **20%** Veterans Day in-store event. Men's Wearhouse **does not** offer the discount through ID.me. Treat any always-on percentage, promo code, or "online military discount" as unverified.

#### Does Men's Wearhouse offer a military discount?
Yes, but **seasonally**, not year-round. The documented offer is a **25% in-store** Military Appreciation Week discount (with a verified military ID), plus a separate **~20% in-store** Veterans Day event. There's no verified everyday rate.

#### How much is the Men's Wearhouse military discount?
Up to **25% in store** during Military Appreciation Week; about **20% in store** around Veterans Day. The exact percentage and dates change by event.

#### Who qualifies?
Active service members, veterans, and their family members, with a verified military/veteran ID (Veterans Health ID, VIC, Uniformed Services ID, CAC, or a state ID with a veteran designation).

#### Can I use it online?
No verified online military discount exists — the offer is applied **in store** with a qualifying ID. Men's Wearhouse is **not** listed on ID.me for this.

#### Does the discount cover tuxedo/suit rentals?
No — the 25% Military Appreciation Week discount **excludes rental items**. Plan rentals around the brand's regular rental pricing/promos.

#### When does Men's Wearhouse run its military discount?
Historically around **late June/early July (Military Appreciation Week)** and **Veterans Day**. Watch for those windows; there's no standing military rate.

#### Does it apply at Jos. A. Bank too?
Yes — the event spans Tailored Brands banners: **Men's Wearhouse, Jos. A. Bank, and Moores**.
