# SureFire — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$300 SureFire illumination purchase (a premium handheld or weapon light on surefire.com).

## Headline finding

SureFire publishes a real **20% military discount for personal purchases — but it's phone-order only**: the brand's own Military Sales page says to **call 800-828-8809 (Mon–Fri 8–5) and request the military discount**. There is no online ID.me/SheerID checkout gate on surefire.com. The same page routes buyers to **GovX.com** ("a selection of products… for current and former military, first responders and law enforcement"), and SureFire also maintains an **ExpertVoice brand store** — both are membership-gated substitutes whose pricing isn't public. Archetype: **E (real brand-run discount, unusual phone mechanic)** with a **K secondary (pro-deal channels exist and may run deeper)**. surefire.com is not on Rakuten, so there is no cashback layer to manage.

### How To Redeem (Phone)
1. Pick your products on surefire.com (note SKUs and prices).
2. Call **800-828-8809**, Monday–Friday 8–5, and **request the military discount** for a personal purchase.

### How To Redeem (GovX)
1. Create a free GovX account and verify (current/former military, first responders, LE).

### How Verification Works
For the 20%, verification happens **on the phone call** — SureFire doesn't publish the exact documents required, so have proof of service ready. GovX and ExpertVoice each run their own one-time platform verification.

### FAQ
#### Does SureFire offer a military discount?
Yes — 20% off personal purchases. You get it by calling 800-828-8809 (M–F 8–5) and requesting the military discount; there's no online code.

#### How much is the SureFire military discount?
20%, per SureFire's own Military Sales page.

#### Is there a SureFire military discount code?
No. The discount is applied on phone orders after validation. Any site selling a "SureFire military promo code" is not describing a real program.

#### Do veterans qualify?
The brand's page doesn't enumerate groups for the phone discount, but it points "current and former military" to GovX, and veterans can verify with GovX. Ask about veteran eligibility when you call.

#### Does SureFire use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No ID.me or SheerID gate exists on surefire.com. GovX carries a selection of SureFire products for its verified members, and SureFire maintains an ExpertVoice brand store for qualified pros.

#### Can I combine it with sales or other discounts?
There's nothing to combine — no public codes exist, GovX is a closed marketplace, and ExpertVoice deals are platform-only. Buy through the single cheapest channel.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy SureFire?
Benchmark the phone-order 20% (e.g., $240 on a $300 light), then price-check the same SKU on GovX and (if eligible) ExpertVoice, and take the lowest.

#### Can my unit or agency buy at a discount?
Yes — that's a separate program: military/federal agency inquiries go through the form on SureFire's Military Sales page, and law enforcement has a dedicated sales channel.
