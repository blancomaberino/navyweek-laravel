# dbrand — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$40 dbrand phone skin + Grip case order** (typical single-device order; skins run ~$14.95–$24.95, the Grip case ~$49.95, so ~$40 is a representative real cart before tax/shipping).

## Headline finding

**dbrand publishes no military, veteran, student, or first-responder discount — and does not run a traditional "enter a coupon code" program at all.** None of dbrand's own pages (the support/FAQ hub, the Product Questions FAQ, or the Terms of Use) mention a military/veteran/student/first-responder discount, a verification partner (ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, VerifyPass), or a promo-code field. dbrand is the **"anti-discount / irreverent-brand" archetype**: it deliberately does not offer identity-gated discounts, and the "codes" that coupon aggregators list for it (most infamously **`GFY`**) are dbrand's own tongue-in-cheek marketing gag, not a service-member benefit. The honest headline for a service member: **there is no dbrand military discount to claim.** The only real savings are dbrand's **public, everyone-gets-them promotions** — sitewide sales (Black Friday / seasonal), first-order/newsletter offers when live, and dbrand's own low-cost "Grab Bag" mystery products — none of which require or reward military status. Aggregator "30%/35%/50% military" claims are fabricated.
