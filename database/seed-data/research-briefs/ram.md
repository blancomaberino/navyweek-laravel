# Ram Trucks — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new 2026 Ram 1500 (a ~$45,000–$60,000 vehicle purchase or lease).

## Headline finding

Ram Trucks runs a real, official **$500 Military Bonus Cash allowance** on select new 2026 Ram-brand vehicles, verified through **SheerID** and redeemed at a franchised dealer (not online, not a percentage). It is a **flat $500 stackable dealer incentive**, not a discount code — it layers on top of national/regional incentives, dealer discounts, and financing offers. The single best strategy is to **stack the $500 on whatever public incentive is live** at purchase time; deployed personnel should compare the separate Military AutoSource overseas program. Archetype: **auto-incentive** (a manufacturer bonus-cash program, not a retail % discount).

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. The $500 Military Bonus Cash is **additive to other Ram incentives** — the FAQ explicitly says you "may also qualify for other offers." It is not an either/or with national bonus cash the way a retail coupon would be. (Source: ramtrucks.com military-incentive FAQ.)
2. It is **dealer-redeemed only** via a SheerID authorization number — there is no online checkout code. (Source: same.)
3. Final pricing, tax, title, registration and any dealer discount are negotiated at the dealer; "pricing and offers may change at any time without notification." (Source: ramtrucks.com footer disclosure.)
4. Not stated in official source: whether the $500 can combine with employee/affiliate ("Friends & Family"/supplier) pricing — confirm with the dealer.

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty — eligible
- Active Reserve — eligible
- Retired military / retired military reserve — eligible

- Honorably discharged veteran within 12 months of discharge — eligible

- General veterans past the 12-month window, other dependents — Not stated as eligible for the $500 program

### How To Redeem
1. Go to ramtrucks.com/incentives/military-incentive.html.
2. Click "Get Active/Retired Military Authorization Number" (or the Disabled Military button) — this opens SheerID.
3. Verify your status; SheerID issues an authorization number.
4. Bring the number to a franchised US Ram dealer at the time of purchase or lease of an eligible 2026 model.
5. Ask the dealer to apply the $500 on top of any current national/regional/dealer incentives.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **SheerID** via a Stellantis offer flow (offers.sheerid.com/stellantis/military?brand=ram). You may be asked to provide qualifying documentation to confirm status. There are two entry points — one for active/retired military and one for disabled military. No more permanent than the source states: the authorization number is tied to the purchase.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Valid only on the specific 2026 models listed (verify the current list).
- US franchised-dealer purchase/lease only; not an online code.
- Documentation may be required.
- Pricing/offers change without notice.
- Not stated whether it combines with employee/supplier/affiliate pricing — confirm with the dealer.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Stack the public incentive.** Ram's seasonal national bonus cash / APR / lease events are usually far larger than $500 — time your purchase to them and add the $500.

### FAQ
#### Does Ram offer a military discount?
Yes — $500 Military Bonus Cash toward select new 2026 Ram-brand vehicles, verified via SheerID and redeemed at a franchised US dealer.

#### How much is the Ram military discount?
A flat **$500** allowance (not a percentage). It stacks on other Ram incentives.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Active, Active Reserve, retired military/reserve, 100% Disabled Veterans, and honorably discharged veterans within 12 months of discharge qualify. Spouses of deceased eligible military may also receive it. Veterans past the 12-month window and other dependents are not listed as eligible for the $500.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through SheerID, from the Ram military-incentive page — you receive an authorization number to present at the dealer.

#### Does Ram use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**SheerID** (via the Stellantis offer flow).

#### Can I use it online or only in store?
In-dealer only. It's a dealer-redeemed authorization number, not an online checkout code.

#### What is excluded?
Any vehicle not on the current eligible-2026 list; there's no percentage component to exclude, but the models covered change by model year.

#### Can I combine it with other offers?
Yes — the FAQ says you may also qualify for other Ram offers, so the $500 is additive to national/dealer incentives. Combining with employee/supplier pricing isn't addressed — ask the dealer.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Ram?
Claim the $500, then stack it on the largest live public incentive/APR/lease deal — timing the public offer matters more than the $500 itself. Overseas, compare Military AutoSource.

#### What if I'm stationed overseas?
Use Ram's partner **Military AutoSource** for tailored overseas pricing and benefits.
