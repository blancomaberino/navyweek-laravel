# AT&T Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

AT&T offers its **AT&T Appreciation Savings** program — a recurring per-line discount of **up to 20%** on eligible AT&T Unlimited postpaid wireless plans for active-duty military, veterans, reservists/National Guard, retired veterans, and qualifying family members. The exact percentage depends on the plan tier: **20% off AT&T Premium 2.0, 15% off AT&T Extra 2.0, and 10% off AT&T Value 2.0** (or an older qualifying unlimited plan). Eligibility is confirmed through AT&T's own military verification form at att.com/verification/military (or in an AT&T retail store) by uploading proof such as a military ID, a recent pay stub, a DD214/NGB 22, or a veteran-designated driver's license — and the discount applies after AutoPay and paperless-billing discounts, starting within three bills. The single most important caveat: AT&T's own pages **do not name a third-party verification provider** (aggregators inconsistently claim SheerID or ID.me; neither is stated by AT&T), and the discount **only applies to the newer AT&T 2.0 unlimited plans or older unlimited plans**, not to every AT&T plan.

> Accuracy note: Many coupon/aggregator and editorial pages still advertise a **25% AT&T military discount**. That figure is **outdated/inaccurate** for the current program. AT&T's official military-discount page and support article state **"up to 20%"**, with the per-plan breakdown of 20% / 15% / 10%, as of June 23, 2026. Use 20% as the headline maximum, never 25%.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (KM1436361; military verification form).
- **Veterans (honorable)** — Eligible; proof via DD214 or veteran-designated driver's license (KM1436361).
- **Retirees / retired veterans** — Eligible ("a retired veteran") (KM1436361).
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible ("full- or part-time military reserve employee"; NGB 22/22A accepted) (KM1436361).
- **Military spouses (active-duty)** — Eligible; must show a pay stub <90 days old plus proof of marriage (KM1436361).
- **Surviving spouses of active-duty** — Eligible (KM1436361).
- **Dependents / family members** — Eligible if a family member of a qualified military person who is the primary owner of the AT&T Wireless account (KM1436361).

- **First responders (active)** — Not under this program; AT&T directs them to FirstNet and Family (separate program).

- **Students / government employees / nurses** — Not under the military program; see AT&T Signature/verification hub for other discounts.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to AT&T's military verification form at **att.com/verification/military**.
2. Enter your details (service status, branch of service, name, date of birth, discharge date, email) and whether you currently have AT&T Wireless.
3. Submit to check eligibility. You may be approved immediately, or asked to upload a document (pay stub <90 days, DD214/NGB 22, or veteran-designated license).
4. Once verified, ensure you're on a qualifying plan (AT&T Premium 2.0, Extra 2.0, Value 2.0, or older unlimited).
5. The per-line discount applies to your account, starting within three bills.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit an AT&T retail store (att.com/stores to locate one).
2. Tell the representative you want to sign up for AT&T Appreciation Savings (military/veteran).
3. Present your proof of eligibility (military ID, pay stub <90 days, DD214/NGB 22, retired military ID, or veteran-designated driver's license; spouses also bring proof of marriage).
4. Confirm or switch to a qualifying unlimited plan (Premium/Extra/Value 2.0 or older unlimited).
5. The discount is applied to your account and appears within three bills.

### How Verification Works
AT&T verifies eligibility through its **own military verification form** at att.com/verification/military (or in store). You enter your service details and, if not auto-approved, upload a supporting document — a current pay stub (<90 days), a DD214 or NGB 22/22A, or a veteran-designated driver's license. AT&T's pages **do not name a third-party verification vendor** for this program; some third-party sites claim SheerID or ID.me, but AT&T itself does not state this, so we don't assert a provider. For help, AT&T lists **855-830-7190** to speak to an expert. AT&T does not state on these pages how often re-verification is required, so treat verification as account-based and confirm with AT&T if your status or plan changes.

### FAQ

#### Does AT&T offer a military discount?
Yes. Through its AT&T Appreciation Savings program, AT&T offers up to 20% off eligible AT&T Unlimited wireless plans for military members, veterans, and their families.

#### How much is the AT&T military discount?
Up to 20%, by plan: 20% off AT&T Premium 2.0, 15% off Extra 2.0, and 10% off Value 2.0 (per line). Older qualifying unlimited plans also count. (Note: pages advertising a 25% AT&T military discount are out of date — the current maximum is 20%.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes. Veterans (with an honorable discharge, shown via DD214 or a veteran-designated driver's license), active-duty spouses and surviving spouses (with proof of marriage and a recent pay stub), and family members of the qualifying military account owner are all eligible.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Complete AT&T's verification form at att.com/verification/military, or sign up in an AT&T store. You may be approved instantly or asked to upload a document — a pay stub under 90 days old, a DD214/NGB 22, or a veteran-designated driver's license.

#### Does AT&T use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
AT&T's official pages do not name a third-party verification provider for this program — verification runs through AT&T's own military form or in-store proof. Some third-party sites claim SheerID or ID.me, but AT&T does not confirm this, so treat the provider as unstated.

#### Can I get the discount in an AT&T store?
Yes. Bring your proof of eligibility to an AT&T retail store and ask to enroll in AT&T Appreciation Savings; the discount is applied to a qualifying unlimited plan.

#### Which plans qualify?
You must be on AT&T Premium 2.0, AT&T Extra 2.0, AT&T Value 2.0, or an older unlimited plan. Non-unlimited and prepaid plans are not listed as eligible.

#### Can I combine it with other discounts?
The military discount applies on top of AT&T's AutoPay and paperless-billing discounts, and military wireless customers can separately get 20% off AT&T Fiber when bundling. Taxes and fees are extra, and other restrictions apply.

#### Does AT&T offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Active first responders and healthcare workers are served by AT&T's separate FirstNet and Family program. Teachers and retired responders qualify under AT&T Appreciation Savings (same structure as military). Students, government employees, and others should check AT&T's verification/Signature hub.
