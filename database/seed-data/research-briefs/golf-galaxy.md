**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $500 online order (e.g., a new driver or a wedge-and-balls package) at golfgalaxy.com.

## Headline finding

**Golf Galaxy has no published military or veteran discount.** ID.me states plainly that it is **not aware of Golf Galaxy offering military discounts**, and there is **no ID.me/GovX/SheerID military checkout**. Golf Galaxy is a **DICK's Sporting Goods brand**, and DICK's likewise has **no chain-wide military discount** — only a **discretionary, manager's-discretion ~10% in-store** offer that some locations grant (never guaranteed, clearance/sale excluded). Archetype: **B (no first-party military route) blended with J (discretionary, manager's-discretion in-store at the parent chain)** — the page's whole value is correcting the "10% military discount" myth and pointing to what actually saves.
