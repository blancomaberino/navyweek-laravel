# Six Flags Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Six Flags offers discounted single-day admission tickets to U.S. military personnel (active and retired), veterans, first responders, and their immediate family members, available year-round. The discount is **redeemed online only** (not at the front gate) and requires verification through **ID.me** — after selecting a Military & First Responder product, the buyer is prompted to log in to ID.me to confirm eligibility. Eligible buyers can purchase up to 6 tickets for family and friends. Pricing is **set per park and not a fixed percentage** — examples verified June 24, 2026: Six Flags Magic Mountain Military & First Responder single-day from $50 (gate $110); Six Flags Great Adventure from $39 (gate $90). Some parks also offer a discounted Military Gold Season Pass and select free-admission Military Appreciation days (e.g., California's Great America gives active/retired members free single-day admission Memorial Day Weekend, May 22–25, 2026, redeemed in-person with a valid U.S. Military ID). The single most important caveat: **Military, Veteran, and First Responder discounts cannot be combined with any other offers, including sales.**

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to your specific park's Military & First Responders ticket page on sixflags.com (each park has its own page and pricing).
2. Select the Military & First Responder ticket, waterpark ticket, or Military season pass for your park.
3. Choose your visit date (single-day tickets are valid only on the date selected).
4. When prompted, log in to ID.me to verify your military, veteran, or first responder status.
5. Complete checkout. You can include up to 6 tickets total for family and friends.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Standard discounted tickets are not sold at the front gate — buy online before you arrive.
2. For select free-admission Military Appreciation days only (e.g., California's Great America, Memorial Day Weekend May 22–25, 2026): bring a valid U.S. Military ID and present it at Guest Services for free single-day admission.
3. Additional tickets for friends and family on those days must still be purchased online in advance.

### How Verification Works
Six Flags uses **ID.me** to verify eligibility. After you select your Military & First Responder product(s) online, you receive a login prompt from ID.me to confirm your status before completing the purchase. An ID.me account is required ("you must be registered with ID.me"). The official pages do not state how often re-verification is required or what specific documents ID.me will request — ID.me manages its own verification and renewal process. For free in-person appreciation days, verification is by presenting a valid U.S. Military ID at Guest Services. If automatic verification fails, follow ID.me's support process; the Six Flags pages do not publish a separate support path.

### FAQ

#### Does Six Flags offer a military discount?
Yes. Six Flags offers discounted single-day admission to U.S. military personnel (active and retired), veterans, first responders, and their immediate family members, redeemed online with ID.me verification. (Source: park Military Discounts pages, accessed June 24, 2026.)

#### How much is the Six Flags military discount?
There is no single nationwide percentage — each park sets its own discounted price. Verified examples (June 24, 2026): Magic Mountain Military & First Responder single-day from $50 (gate $110); Great Adventure from $39 (gate $90). Check your specific park's page for its current price.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify and are named explicitly. Immediate family members are covered through the verified member's purchase (up to 6 tickets total). The official pages do not describe spouses or dependents verifying independently as the primary ID.me holder.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through ID.me. After selecting your Military & First Responder product online, you'll get an ID.me login prompt to verify before checkout. You must have an ID.me account.

#### Does Six Flags use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Six Flags uses **ID.me**. The official military discount pages name ID.me as the verification provider; GovX, SheerID, and WeSalute are not referenced.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or at the gate?
No. All military offers must be redeemed online and are not available at the front gate. The only in-person exception is select free-admission Military Appreciation days (e.g., CA Great America Memorial Day Weekend), where you show a valid U.S. Military ID at Guest Services.

#### What is excluded?
The discount can't be combined with any other offer, including sales. Available products and pricing change periodically, single-day tickets are valid only on the selected date, and you can buy a maximum of 6 tickets per purchase.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Military, Veteran, and First Responder discounts cannot be combined with any other offers, including sales.

#### Does Six Flags offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders qualify under the same military/first-responder program (ID.me verified). The official military pages do not state a separate discount for teachers, nurses, government employees, or students.

#### Are tickets free for veterans?
Not year-round — the year-round offer is a discount, not free admission. Some parks run limited free-admission appreciation days for active and retired military (e.g., CA Great America, Memorial Day Weekend 2026); availability varies by park and year, so check your local park.
