# Allegiant Air — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one domestic round-trip on Allegiant for an active-duty family of three, checking **2 bags per person each way plus one carry-on each** — the fee load Allegiant's military program waives (Allegiant a-la-carte bag fees vary dynamically by route/date; figures below are illustrative and labeled).

## Headline finding

Allegiant does **not** publish a percentage-off fare discount for the military. Its program — **Allegiant Honors** (allegiantair.com/military-discount) — is a **fee-waiver and benefits package**, not a cheaper ticket. Verified active-duty, reserve, veteran travelers and eligible family on the same reservation get **up to 2 free checked bags each (under 99 lbs), no oversized-bag fees, 1 free carry-on, 1 free personal item, 1 free in-cabin pet, and priority boarding** — plus, for active/reserve members with official orders, **no change/cancellation fees and a full refund (fees included) if orders cancel the trip**. Because Allegiant is an ultra-low-cost carrier that charges a-la-carte for nearly everything, the **bag-fee waiver alone is the real money** — frequently worth more than a single-digit fare percentage would be. Verification is **at the airport, agent-checked, with a valid military ID** — there is no online ID.me/SheerID/GovX code. Archetype: **I (travel — perks, not a rate)**, with the twist that the "discount" is fee elimination on an unbundled fare.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Benefits attach to the reservation, not a code** — eligible family members must be **on the same itinerary** as the service member/veteran to get the waivers. *(allegiantair.com/military-discount)*

3. **Domestic flights only** — the benefits apply to domestic itineraries.
4. **Orders relief requires documentation** — the no-fee change/cancel and full-fee refund apply to active/reserve members with **official orders/directives**; keep supporting paperwork.

6. **No cross-channel stack** — there is no GovX/WeSalute/ID.me code to layer; the program is Allegiant's own airport-verified benefit set.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (all branches incl. Space Force & Coast Guard).
- **National Guard / Reserve** — Eligible.
- **Veterans** — Eligible (Veteran ID card or driver's license with veteran designation).
- **Service academies, PHS & NOAA Commissioned Corps** — Named as eligible.
- **Spouse / domestic partner** — Eligible on the same reservation (DD Form 1173/1173-1).
- **Children / dependents** — Eligible on the same reservation; under age 10 need no ID.

### How To Redeem (at the airport)
1. Book your domestic Allegiant flight as normal — **don't prepay for bags** you expect to be waived.
2. At the airport, present a valid military ID to the Allegiant agent.
3. The agent verifies your ID and adds eligible free services (bags, pet, boarding pass) to your reservation.
4. Family members must be on the **same itinerary** to receive the benefits.
5. Already prepaid for eligible items? Contact Allegiant Customer Care **after** ID verification to receive a credit for those fees.

### How Verification Works
Verification happens **in person at the airport** — an Allegiant agent checks your CAC, Uniformed Services ID, DD Form (2, 214, or 2765), Veteran ID card, or veteran-designated driver's license. Spouses use DD Form 1173/1173-1 with the sponsor on the same reservation. There is no online military account, code, or third-party verifier (no ID.me/SheerID/GovX).

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Domestic flights only.**
- Benefits are **airport-applied**; there is no online military fare or code.
- Orders-based fee waivers and refunds require **supporting documentation** (orders/directives).
- Eligible family must travel on the **same reservation**.
- Bag-fee amounts are dynamic — the value of the waiver depends on the live fee at booking.

### Best Ways To Save More

### FAQ

#### Does Allegiant offer a military discount?
Yes, but it's a **benefits and fee-waiver program (Allegiant Honors)**, not a percentage off airfare. Eligible military travelers get free checked bags, a free carry-on and personal item, a free in-cabin pet, priority boarding, and orders-based change/refund relief on domestic flights.

#### How much is the Allegiant military discount?
There is **no published fare percentage**. The value comes from waived bag and add-on fees (which vary by route and date) and, for active/reserve members with orders, waived change/cancel fees and full refunds. On a bags-heavy family trip that can total hundreds of dollars.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — veterans (with a Veteran ID card or veteran-designated license) qualify, and a spouse/domestic partner (DD Form 1173/1173-1) plus children/dependents qualify when traveling on the **same reservation**. Children under 10 need no ID.

#### How do I verify my military status with Allegiant?
At the airport. An agent checks your military ID and adds the free services to your booking. There's no online verification step.

#### Does Allegiant use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Allegiant Honors is verified **in person at the airport** with a military ID — not through any third-party online verifier or code.

#### Can I use the benefit online or only at the airport?
It's applied **at the airport**. If you already paid for eligible items online, contact Customer Care after ID verification to get those fees credited.

#### What's excluded?
International itineraries (benefits are domestic-only), and anything requiring documentation you can't produce (orders for the change/refund relief). Bag-fee value depends on the live fee.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or fare sales?
There's no military code to combine. You book the public fare (including any public sale price) and the agent applies the fee waivers on top — the benefits attach to the reservation.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to fly Allegiant?
Book the lowest public base fare, **don't prepay for bags**, put the whole eligible family on one reservation, and let the airport agent waive the bag/carry-on/pet fees. For active/reserve members, the orders refund makes booking early lower-risk.

#### Does Allegiant offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No first-responder/teacher/nurse/student program is published; Allegiant Honors is a military/veteran benefit.
