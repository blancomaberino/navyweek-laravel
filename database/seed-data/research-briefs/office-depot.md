# Office Depot & OfficeMax Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Office Depot / OfficeMax offers a verified **everyday 20% off** qualifying **regular-price** in-store purchases for the military community, redeemable at Office Depot and OfficeMax stores nationwide. Eligibility is broad: **veterans, active-duty and retired military, reservists, and their dependents**, verified in store by presenting a **valid military ID, military dependent ID, or a form of veteran ID** at the time of purchase. This is the company's own program (announced by The ODP Corporation / Office Depot's corporate newsroom and hosted at officedepot.com/l/rewards/military). Online, Office Depot verifies military status through **ID.me** and runs a separate **Teacher/Military Exclusive** offer of **25% back in Bonus Rewards** on qualifying purchases at officedepot.com.

The single most important caveats: (1) the flat **20%** is **in-store, on regular-price items** — it does not apply to sale/clearance items or to many excluded categories (technology, ink/toner and gift cards are commonly excluded at office-supply retailers; Office Depot's page defines "qualifying" purchases, so treat "20% off everything" as false); (2) the **online** benefit is structured as **25% back in Bonus Rewards** (store credit toward a future purchase), **not** 25% off at checkout — a materially different mechanic; and (3) around **Veterans Day**, Office Depot has historically boosted the in-store discount to **25% off** and extended it to **sale-priced** items for a few days (Nov 9–11 in 2024) — a limited-time promo, labeled historical below.

> Accuracy note: Coupon aggregators list "20% off Office Depot military" and sometimes a flat "25% off." The verified everyday in-store figure is **20% off regular-price** purchases; the **25%** figures are either the **online Bonus-Rewards** mechanic (25% *back*, not off) or the **historical Veterans Day** in-store boost. Any "military promo code" from a coupon site is not the program — the in-store discount is applied at the register with ID, and the online path uses ID.me.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Confirm you qualify (veteran, active-duty, retired, reservist, or dependent).
2. Bring a **valid military ID, military dependent ID, or form of veteran ID** (e.g., Veterans Health ID Card, VIC, Uniformed Services ID, CAC, or a state ID with a veteran designation).
3. Shop **regular-price** items at any Office Depot or OfficeMax store.
4. Present your ID **at the register**; the cashier applies **20% off** qualifying regular-price items.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to officedepot.com and start the **Military verification** flow (officedepot.com/l/rewards/military).
2. Verify your military status through **ID.me**.
3. Shop qualifying items; the **Teacher/Military Exclusive** applies **25% back in Bonus Rewards** (store credit for a future purchase) — not a 25% price cut at checkout.
4. Redeem accrued Bonus Rewards on a later order.

### How Verification Works
Office Depot uses **two different paths**. In store, a cashier checks your **physical military/dependent/veteran ID** at checkout — no app required. Online, officedepot.com routes verification through **ID.me**; once verified, the online offer is delivered as **25% back in Bonus Rewards**. ID.me's own re-verification cadence governs how often you re-confirm online; Office Depot doesn't publish a separate frequency.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The everyday **20%** is **in-store and on regular-price items** — it does **not** apply to sale/clearance in normal periods.
- The discount applies to **"qualifying"** purchases only. Office-supply retailers typically exclude **technology, ink & toner, gift cards, and select brands**; Office Depot's page defines the exact list — **confirm before claiming "20% off everything."** (This exclusion detail did not render on the JS-shell officedepot.com page — flag.)
- **Online is not 20% (or 25%) off** — it's **25% back in Bonus Rewards** (store credit). Different mechanic; state it clearly.
- **Veterans Day** boosts (25% off, incl. sale items, ~Nov 9–11) are **limited-time** — don't present them as everyday.
- Whether the military discount **stacks** with coupons, Rewards, or other promos: **not stated in official source** — treat as non-stacking unless confirmed.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **In store, buy regular-price at 20% off**; online, use the **25%-back Bonus Rewards** on categories you'll re-buy (paper, everyday supplies) so the store credit actually gets used.
- **Time big/tech buys to Veterans Day**, when the in-store discount has historically risen to 25% and covered sale items — often the best window for excluded-category or clearance purchases.
- **Stack an Office Depot Rewards / OfficeMax Rewards** account for percentage-back on print, ink recycling ($ per cartridge), and everyday purchases — separate from the military discount and usable even where the 20% is excluded.
- **Compare Staples' and Amazon's** prices on technology/ink, which are commonly excluded from the 20% — a competitor may beat a "discounted" Office Depot tech price that never qualified.
- **Business buyers:** ODP Business Solutions contract pricing can beat retail on bulk supplies — a different lever from the consumer military discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Office Depot offer a military discount?
Yes — **20% off** qualifying **regular-price** purchases in Office Depot and OfficeMax stores nationwide, for veterans, active-duty and retired military, reservists, and their dependents.

#### How much is the Office Depot military discount?
**20%** off qualifying regular-price items in store. Online, the military/teacher offer is **25% back in Bonus Rewards** (store credit toward a future order), not 25% off at checkout.

#### Who qualifies?
Veterans, active-duty and retired military, reservists, and their dependents. Teachers share the online offer via a separate Teacher/Military program.

#### How do I verify — in store vs online?
In store, show a **military ID, military dependent ID, or form of veteran ID** at the register. Online, verify through **ID.me** on officedepot.com.

#### Is the discount valid online?
The online benefit is **25% back in Bonus Rewards**, not a straight discount — you earn store credit to spend later. The **20% off** is the in-store benefit.

#### What's excluded?
The 20% applies to **qualifying regular-price** items only — not sale/clearance in normal periods, and typically not categories like technology, ink/toner, or gift cards. Check the current terms for the exact exclusion list.

#### Does Office Depot use ID.me?
Yes — for **online** military verification. In store, a cashier checks your physical ID instead.

#### Does the discount go up around Veterans Day?
Historically yes — in 2024 it rose to **25% off** qualifying regular and sale-priced items in stores for **Nov 9–11**. That's a limited-time promo, not the everyday rate.
