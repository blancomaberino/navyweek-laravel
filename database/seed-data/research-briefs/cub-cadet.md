# Cub Cadet — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new Cub Cadet PRO Z 500-series zero-turn mower purchased from an independent dealer (≈$6,000).

## Headline finding

Cub Cadet does **not** offer a point-of-sale military discount. It runs an official **Heroes Program** rebate (administered by Stanley Black & Decker at sbdheroprogram.com): a **one-time $300 rebate** on select ZTX/ZTXS and PRO Z HW 300 / PRO Z 100 mowers, or a **$500 rebate** on PRO Z 500/700/900 and PRO X series, for verified military **and** first responders who buy from a local Cub Cadet Independent Dealer. It is a **mail-in/online rebate after purchase**, not an instant discount, and is limited to premium PRO/commercial-grade equipment — homeowner riding mowers and push mowers do not qualify. For the 2025–26 program period the rebate window is Oct 1, 2025 – Sept 30, 2026, with claims due within 30 days of purchase.

### Who Qualifies
- US Military — active, reserve, retired, or veteran (all branches, Guard, Reserve).
- First responders — Fire, Law Enforcement, Paramedic, EMT.
- Proof: DD-214 (SSN covered), VA ID card, veteran-designated driver's license, or departmental ID. CAC/military ID not accepted.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
1. Buy a qualifying new Cub Cadet PRO product from a local Cub Cadet Independent Dealer during the program period.
2. Register the product within 10 days of purchase.
3. Within 30 days of purchase, log into sbdheroprogram.com and submit the invoice, serial number, dealer info, and proof of service.
4. Wait for approval; the rebate is mailed/issued after processing.

### How Verification Works
Verification happens at claim time via the sbdheroprogram.com portal — you upload proof of service (DD-214, VA ID, veteran license, or departmental ID). There is no storefront ID.me/SheerID gate; the rebate is validated against your uploaded documents and dealer invoice.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Only qualifying PRO Z / PRO X / ZTX / ZTXS / PRO Z HW/100 models — homeowner riding and push mowers excluded.
- Must buy from a Cub Cadet Independent Dealer; one rebate per customer per invoice.
- Not combinable with any other offer, demo, or promotion.
- Claim within 30 days; register within 10 days; program period Oct 1, 2025 – Sept 30, 2026.

### Best Ways To Save More
- File the Heroes rebate on any qualifying PRO purchase — it's the single biggest military-specific saving.
- Negotiate dealer price first; the rebate stacks on top of whatever price you agree (but not on top of other Cub Cadet promotions).
- For homeowner models, watch cubcadet.com seasonal offers and financing.
- Check ID.me's Cub Cadet listing for any online cashback on direct purchases (homeowner models).

### FAQ
#### Does Cub Cadet offer a military discount?
Not an instant one. It offers the Heroes Program rebate — $300 or $500 back after purchase for verified military and first responders on qualifying PRO models.

#### How much is the Cub Cadet military discount?
$300 on ZTX/ZTXS and PRO Z HW 300 / PRO Z 100 models; $500 on PRO Z 500/700/900 and PRO X series.

#### Do veterans / spouses / dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify (active, reserve, retired, veteran). The program is for the service member/first responder; spouses/dependents are not listed as separately eligible.

#### How do I verify my status?
Upload proof of service (DD-214 with SSN covered, VA ID card, veteran-designated driver's license, or departmental ID) at sbdheroprogram.com. Military CAC cards are not accepted.

#### Does Cub Cadet use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No — the rebate is verified through the sbdheroprogram.com claim portal, not a storefront ID.me/SheerID gate. Cub Cadet is separately listed on ID.me for general cashback.

#### Can I use it in stores / online?
You buy the qualifying product from a Cub Cadet Independent Dealer, then file the rebate online. It is not an instant in-store or online-checkout discount.

#### What is excluded?
Homeowner riding mowers, push mowers, and non-PRO models; big-box/non-dealer purchases; combining with other offers.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the Heroes rebate cannot be combined with any other offer, demo, or promotion.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Cub Cadet?
Buy a qualifying PRO model from a dealer, negotiate the price, then file the $500 (or $300) Heroes rebate within 30 days.

#### Does Cub Cadet offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Yes for first responders (Fire, LE, EMT, Paramedic) — same Heroes Program. No listed teacher/nurse/student program.
