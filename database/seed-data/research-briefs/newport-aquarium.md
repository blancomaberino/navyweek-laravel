# Newport Aquarium — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adult 1-Day General Admission tickets (dynamic "plan-ahead" pricing; adult often ~$34.99, as low as $25.99).

## Headline finding

Newport Aquarium (Newport, KY — part of Herschend/attractions) offers a real, year-round **$5-off-per-ticket military discount**, verified through **ID.me** online with a **valid military ID shown at entry**. It covers active-duty, former military, veterans, reservists, spouses, and dependents. On top of that, during its annual **Military Appreciation Week**, active service members get **FREE admission** (and a free appreciation gift), with family members at $5 off. Because admission uses dynamic plan-ahead pricing, the biggest lever is often **booking the lowest-demand date/time in advance** — and for families, the **Kroger** channel (up to $8 off) or a **membership** (2 free months on Gold/Platinum) can beat the $5 military discount. The $5 does not combine with other offers, so you pick the single best path per visit.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The military $5 (and Military Appreciation Week free admission) **"may not be combined with other offers"** — one offer per ticket.
2. **Dynamic plan-ahead pricing is not a "discount"** — it's the base price, so booking an off-peak slot early lowers the price the $5 comes off of.
3. **Kroger, AAA, AARP, educator, and employer** discounts are separate channels; you choose one per ticket, not a stack.
4. **Membership** is its own program (unlimited visits) — not stacked with single-visit discounts but often cheaper for repeat visitors.
5. Family/friends without a military ID cannot use the military benefit ("cannot be made to family members or friends who don't have a military ID"), though eligible family members get the $5-off family rate.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online:**
1. Verify your military status through ID.me.

### How Verification Works
Status is verified online via ID.me, and a valid military ID must be presented with your tickets at the time of entry. The military benefit cannot be extended to friends/family who lack a military ID (though eligible family members get the $5-off family rate). Questions: 1-800-406-3474.
