# Space Needle (Seattle) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult general-admission Space Needle ticket (standard adult GA is posted as **$35.00–$55.00** depending on date/time), pre-tax.

## Headline finding

The Space Needle offers a **military rate available only at the Box Office (admissions office)** — you must show ID in person, and per the official FAQ the discount applies to **anyone in the party with an ID**. It is **not available online**; there's no ID.me/GovX flow, and the Space Needle **does not publish the military price or percentage**. Standard adult GA runs $35–$55 by date/time; visitor reports put the walk-up military rate lower (reports of roughly ~$44, and older reports of ~$24 for active duty), but these are unverified and pricing is dynamic. Archetype: single-attraction operator with a discretionary in-person military rate rather than a published verified-online program. Best path: buy at the Box Office with military ID if visiting once; consider a Seattle **CityPASS/C3** bundle if hitting multiple attractions.
