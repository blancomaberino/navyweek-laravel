# Caraway — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $395 Caraway Cookware Set (the flagship non-toxic ceramic 7-piece — the everyday unit most readers compare).

## Headline finding

**Caraway does not publish a native military discount on carawayhome.com, but a verified 10% military discount exists through Caraway's partner MilitaryBridge — and, importantly, it stacks "on top of most current Caraway sales."** That stackability is the whole story: Caraway runs frequent site-wide sales (often 15–20%+ off sets), and the MilitaryBridge 10% layers on top of the already-reduced price, which usually makes it the single best path for a service member. Eligibility is **military members, veterans, and their families**; you redeem via the MilitaryBridge link plus a promo code (an "mb2026"-style code has been used in their 2026 campaigns).

Two other routes exist but are weaker: **ID.me** offers Caraway **cashback for everyone** (up to ~6% for community/new members, ~3% existing, "as of July 15, 2026") — a rebate, not a checkout discount — and **GOVX** lists Caraway for verified military/government members at gated member pricing. Archetype: **DTC cookware brand with no native military page, served through editorial/affiliate partners (MilitaryBridge) and marketplaces (ID.me, GOVX)**, where the make-or-break fact is that the partner 10% **stacks on sales**. Note: the MilitaryBridge 10% is an affiliate-partner offer, not a carawayhome.com-published term — treat it as a working partner code (medium confidence), and confirm at checkout.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
