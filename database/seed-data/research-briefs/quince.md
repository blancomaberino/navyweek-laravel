# Quince Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Quince offers **10% off your first purchase** to new customers who verify as U.S. military, first responders, nurses, or educators through **ID.me**. Verification is online-only via ID.me on Quince's dedicated page; on success Quince issues a **one-time discount code** that auto-applies at checkout. The offer requires a **$40 minimum order**, is for **new customers only (one use per customer)**, is valid only on **regularly priced products (excludes gift cards)**, **cannot be combined** with any other promotion, and ships to **U.S. addresses only** (Quince is an online-only retailer — no physical stores). The single most important caveat: this is a **first-purchase-only** discount, not an everyday/ongoing military discount. Veterans are eligible via ID.me's military verification, though Quince's own page does not enumerate sub-groups — it links ID.me's military eligibility criteria, which include active duty, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, and dependents.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Quince's service & education discounts page (quince.com/discounts/service-education) and click "Get Verified Now."
2. Sign in to or create a free ID.me account and complete verification for your group (military, first responder, nurse, or teacher).
3. Copy or write down the one-time discount code ID.me/Quince provides (keep it handy just in case).
4. Use the link in the ID.me pop-up to return to quince.com, then add your items to your shopping bag ($40 minimum).
5. At checkout, confirm the code auto-applied; if not, paste it into the discount code field and hit apply.
6. Complete checkout (U.S. shipping address required).

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Quince is an online-only retailer and operates no physical stores; the discount is redeemed online at checkout. (Some third-party aggregators claim in-store or mobile-app redemption — this is not confirmed by Quince's official source.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**, a third-party identity/affiliation service. You create or sign in to a free ID.me account and prove your group affiliation (e.g., military service is checked against records or a .mil email; spouses/dependents upload documents). On success, you receive a **one-time code** for 10% off your first Quince purchase. ID.me, not Quince, controls your ID.me account and communications. For verification problems, contact **ID.me Support: 866-775-4363, 8 AM–midnight ET, 7 days a week**. Quince's page does not state how often you must re-verify; because this is a one-time, first-purchase code, ongoing re-verification is not applicable.

### FAQ

#### Does Quince offer a military discount?
Yes. Quince offers 10% off your first purchase to new customers who verify as U.S. military (as well as first responders, nurses, and educators) through ID.me. (Source: Quince service-education page, accessed Jun 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the Quince military discount?
10% off, applied to your first purchase, with a $40 minimum order value. It's a one-time, new-customer offer rather than an ongoing everyday discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify through ID.me's military verification. ID.me's military group also covers active duty, retirees, Guard/Reserve, military spouses, and immediate dependents. Quince's own page does not list these sub-groups; it links ID.me's eligibility criteria, so eligibility for spouses/dependents is governed by ID.me.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to quince.com/discounts/service-education, click "Get Verified Now," and complete ID.me verification. You'll get a one-time code that auto-applies at checkout.

#### Does Quince use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Quince uses **ID.me**. There is no indication in the official source of GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. Quince is an online-only retailer with no physical stores; the discount applies at online checkout on quince.com. (Aggregator claims of in-store/app redemption are not confirmed by Quince.)

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards and non-regularly-priced (sale) items are excluded. The offer can't be combined with other promotions, requires a $40 minimum, is new-customers-only, and ships to U.S. addresses only (no Guam/APO/FPO/DPO).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The discount applies only to regularly priced items and cannot be combined with any other promotion or discount.

#### Does Quince offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders, nurses, and teachers/educators qualify for the same 10%-off-first-purchase offer via ID.me. Government employees and students are not listed as eligible groups in Quince's official source.

#### Is the Quince military discount ongoing or one-time?
It is one-time — valid on your first purchase only, one use per customer. It is not a recurring everyday military discount.
