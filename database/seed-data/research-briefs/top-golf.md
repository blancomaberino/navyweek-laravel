# Topgolf — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **2-hour weekday-evening bay of game play at a ~$52/hour peak rate = ~$104 in game play**, pre-tax (food, beverage, and gratuity excluded — Topgolf charges per **bay per hour**, up to 6 players share a bay). Rates vary widely by venue/day/time (national Q2-2026 range roughly **$27–$64/hr** weekday, up to **~$100/hr** weekend evenings), so treat $104 as one realistic illustrative booking, not a fixed price.

## Headline finding

Topgolf has a **real, first-party military discount** — the **Heroes Program**, **10% off game play** (and 10% off Event Game Play & Room Rental), **presented/sponsored by GovX**. It covers **military (active duty & veterans), police, fire & EMS, healthcare workers, and teachers**, with **valid ID required** at the venue. That's the honest everyday answer and it's stated on Topgolf's own promo page. But the counterintuitive truth for this brand: the **public "Half-Off Golf" promo (50% off game play, Monday–Thursday, booked online/app)** almost always **beats the 10% Heroes discount** — and the two **can't combine** ("only one discount may be applied per group/bay"). So a verified service member playing on a weekday should usually **skip their own 10% and take the 50%**.

The one thing that genuinely **stacks** is the **GovX gift-card bonus**: a **$75 Topgolf gift card comes with a $20 bonus game-play gift card ($95 value)**, and GovX/Topgolf explicitly say that card **can be combined with the 10% Heroes discount** — because a gift card is a *payment method*, not a coupon. Layer the discounted card under whatever game-play discount applies and you get the best net cash outlay. Archetype: **C — real discount, but the public promo usually beats it** (non-combinable game-play % on a brand that runs deep standing promos), with a **GovX-sponsored identity-program** flavor (A/E) and a **Veterans Day holiday spike** (H). Do **not** repeat the aggregator "up to 25%" everyday claim (25% is **Las Vegas-only**, with a Nevada ID) or any "ID.me" verification — the named partner is **GovX**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + partner terms)
1. **Heroes 10% is strictly non-combinable** — Topgolf's own terms: *"Cannot be used in conjunction with any other coupon, offer, or promotion. Only one discount may be applied per group/bay."* It will **not** stack with Half-Off Golf, Beat-the-Peak, birthday, or any promo. You pick **one** discount per bay.
2. **The public Half-Off (50%) usually wins over the 10%** — because they're mutually exclusive and 50% > 10%, a verified guest playing Mon–Thu should **skip their Heroes discount** and book the public half-off rate.
3. **The GovX gift-card bonus is the one real stack** — a gift card is a **payment method, not a discount**, and GovX/Topgolf state the $95-for-$75 card *"can be combined with the Heroes Program discount of 10% off gameplay."* Being payment, it also sits under a Half-Off booking. (Verify the bonus is currently live before relying on it.)
4. **Food & beverage are never discounted** — all game-play discounts (Heroes and Half-Off) apply to **game play only**, not F&B or arcade.
5. **Format exclusions** — the Heroes discount is **not valid at Topgolf Swing Suite or Toptracer Range** locations and doesn't apply to the reservation **deposit**; Half-Off excludes **parties/events**, Swing Suite, Toptracer, and arcade.
6. **The Heroes Membership 20%** is a separate discount on **select monthly memberships**, not on a single walk-in booking.
7. **TOPGOLF15 ($15 off first GovX order $50+)** applies to a **GovX.com marketplace order**, not to a Topgolf-venue booking — don't conflate it with the venue discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Topgolf offer a military discount?
Yes. Topgolf's **Heroes Program** gives **10% off game play** to active-duty military and veterans (plus police, fire, EMS, healthcare workers, and teachers), presented by GovX, with valid ID.

#### How much is the Topgolf military discount?
**10% off game play** nationwide (game play only — not food or drink). In **Las Vegas**, it's **25%** with a valid Nevada ID. Coupon sites advertising a flat "25%" everywhere are misreading the Vegas-only term.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
**Veterans** qualify (Topgolf names "military (active duty and veterans)"). **Spouses and dependents are not separately named** in the Heroes terms — verify at the venue or with GovX before assuming coverage.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify through the **GovX**-sponsored Heroes Program and/or present a **valid photo ID** at the venue (Military ID, Veteran ID, DD214, or first-responder/healthcare/teacher credential).

#### Does Topgolf use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The named partner is **GovX** (presenting sponsor), plus at-venue ID checks. There is **no confirmed ID.me, SheerID, or WeSalute** route — treat those aggregator claims as unverified.

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?
The 10% is applied **at the venue on game play**. The GovX gift-card bonus is bought **online at GovX.com** and then spent at the venue.

#### What is excluded?
Food, beverage, and arcade are excluded; the discount isn't valid at **Swing Suite/Toptracer Range** and doesn't apply to the deposit. It **can't be combined** with any other coupon, offer, or promotion — only one discount per bay.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the Heroes 10% is non-combinable. Notably, the **public Half-Off Golf (50%, Mon–Thu)** can't be stacked with it either, so you pick one. The **only** thing that layers in is a **GovX gift card** (a payment method), which GovX says can combine with the 10%.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to play Topgolf?
Book **Half-Off Golf (50%) Monday–Thursday** online/app — it beats the military 10% roughly fivefold and the two don't combine. Use the **Heroes 10%** for weekend/peak play and for events/room rentals, and pay with a **discounted GovX gift card** to squeeze out more.

#### Does Topgolf offer a first responder, healthcare, or teacher discount?
Yes — the same **10% Heroes discount** extends to police, fire, EMS, healthcare workers, and teachers, with valid ID.

#### Does Topgolf run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day promotion?
Historically yes — Topgolf sponsors **Patriot Golf Day** on **Nov 11** (free Heroes Membership with ID, a $25 game-play code for existing members) and additional Patriot Golf Days around Memorial Day / July 4. Verify each year; these aren't guaranteed to repeat.
