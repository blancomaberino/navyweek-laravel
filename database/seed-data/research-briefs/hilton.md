# Hilton Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Hilton offers a **Military Family Rate** — a discounted leisure-stay rate at participating hotels across its portfolio for active-duty, reserve, retired military, veterans, and their families. The offer is booked directly on Hilton.com (or by phone) under the "Special Rates → Military Rate" option, or via rate plan code **MFR / PGMFR1**, and requires a **valid military ID shown at check-in**. Hilton's own offer page describes the benefit as "exclusive savings" and states "rates will vary by hotel and dates" — it does **not** prominently publish a fixed percentage in the page's readable copy; the widely-cited "up to 10% off" figure comes from aggregators/editorial and one offer-page snippet, and is flagged below as not independently confirmed verbatim on the rendered official page. Separately, Hilton also offers **US government/military per diem rates** (for official travel, with government ID) and a distinct **Hilton All-Inclusive** military offer that *does* use **ID.me** verification. The single most important caveat: the Military Family Rate is for **leisure stays only** (not official duty travel), is subject to availability, and generally cannot be combined with other promotions.

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty — eligible (valid military ID required)
- Veterans — eligible per offer wording ("veterans … and their families"); confirm acceptable ID with the hotel (DD-214 commonly referenced by third parties, not stated as the required document on Hilton's page)
- Retirees — eligible (retired service members)
- Reserve / National Guard — eligible (reserve service members)
- Military spouses — eligible (spouses and families)
- Dependents — eligible (families)
- Surviving spouses / Gold Star families — Not stated in official source
- First responders — Not stated for the Military Family Rate (Hilton All-Inclusive ID.me offer references a "Military and First Responder" category)
- Medical workers — Not stated for the Military Family Rate (Hilton All-Inclusive ID.me storefront lists nurses/others separately)
- Teachers — Not stated in official source
- Government employees — Eligible for government per diem rates (official travel), with valid government ID — separate from the Military Family Rate
- Students — Not stated in official source
- Explicitly excluded — Official government/military *duty* travel is excluded from the Military Family Rate (use per diem rates instead)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Hilton.com or open the Hilton Honors app and enter your destination and dates.
2. Open "Special Rates" and select the Military Rate option (rate plan code MFR / PGMFR1).
3. Review available rates (pricing varies by hotel and dates) and complete your booking.
4. Bring your valid military ID and present it at check-in.

### How To Redeem In Store (At The Hotel / By Phone)
1. To book by phone, call +1-800-HONORS (445-8667) and request the "Military Family Rate" plan (code MFR / PGMFR1).
2. At check-in, present your current, valid military identification card.
3. The hotel applies the booked military rate; rates do not include taxes, gratuities, incidentals, or resort fees where applicable.

### How Verification Works
For the **Military Family Rate**, Hilton does not use an online verification partner — eligibility is confirmed by showing a valid military ID at check-in. No special account is required to book, though a Hilton Honors account is needed to earn points. For Hilton's **government per diem rates**, you show valid government identification at check-in. Hilton's separate **Hilton All-Inclusive** military offer is verified online through **ID.me** before booking. If a hotel cannot honor the rate at check-in (e.g., ID issue), contact the front desk or Hilton Honors support; specific re-verification frequency is not stated on Hilton's page.

### FAQ

#### Does Hilton offer a military discount?
Yes. Hilton offers a Military Family Rate for discounted leisure stays for active-duty, reserve, and retired military, veterans, and their families, plus separate government/military per diem rates for official travel.

#### How much is the Hilton military discount?
Hilton's official page describes "exclusive savings" and says rates vary by hotel and dates rather than stating a fixed percentage. Many third-party sources cite "up to 10% off," but Hilton's rendered offer page does not clearly confirm that number — check the actual rate on Hilton.com for your stay.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. The offer covers active-duty, reserve, and retired service members, veterans, and their spouses and families, with a valid military ID required at check-in.

#### How do I verify my military status?
For the Military Family Rate, you show a valid military ID at check-in — there is no online verification step to book. Hilton's separate Hilton All-Inclusive offer uses ID.me.

#### Does Hilton use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For the standard Military Family Rate, no online verification provider is used. ID.me is used for Hilton's separate Hilton All-Inclusive resort offer (which also lists other audiences such as nurses/first responders).

#### Can I use the discount in stays/hotels?
Yes. Book the Military Rate online or by phone, then present your valid military ID at check-in at participating hotels.

#### What is excluded?
Official duty travel (use per diem rates instead), group bookings, existing reservations, and combining with other promotions are excluded; the rate is subject to availability and excludes taxes, resort fees, and incidentals.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other offers?
No. The military rate generally may not be combined with other select promotions, discounts, or offers, and does not apply to existing reservations.

#### Does Hilton offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Hilton offers government per diem rates for government employees on official travel. First-responder and nurse savings appear only on Hilton's separate Hilton All-Inclusive (ID.me) offer; teacher and student discounts are not stated in Hilton's official sources.
