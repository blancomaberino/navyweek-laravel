# Fubo — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** first two months of **Fubo Pro** at the everyday rate (~$84.99/mo; prices are volatile — see note), the tier the verification offers are written against.

## Headline finding

**Fubo does not run an ongoing (permanent) military or veteran discount.** What exists are **promotional, first-month(s)-only** offers gated behind identity verification, and they are open to a **broad "Heroes" group — not just military.** The one Fubo publishes on its own site is the **ID.me Heroes offer: "$30 off your first two months of Fubo"** for military, government workers, first responders, nurses, teachers, hospital workers, and medical providers (verified via ID.me). A separate **WeSalute** offer gives **25% off the first month of Fubo Pro** (~$21.25). Both revert to full price after the intro window. This is archetype **F (time-boxed subscription discount)** — but weaker than a rate lock: it's an intro credit, not a standing % off.

The honest best path for most verified shoppers is the **ID.me Heroes offer** (largest verified intro credit, on the plan you'll likely keep), stacked with Fubo's **free trial**. The widely-repeated aggregator claim of a **"$10 off every month forever via GovX"** could **not** be confirmed against a GovX primary source and is treated here as **unverified — do not publish it as fact.**

### FAQ

#### Does Fubo offer a military discount?
Not an ongoing one. Fubo does not run a permanent military or veteran rate and says it "does not guarantee an ongoing military discount." It offers a **promotional intro credit** through ID.me — its "Heroes" offer of **$30 off your first two months of Fubo** — available to military and several other groups.

#### How much is the Fubo military discount?
Fubo's ID.me "Heroes" page states **"$30 off your first two months of Fubo."** Fubo's own deals page has also described it as $30 off each of months 1 and 2 (up to $60), so confirm the exact amount at checkout. Separately, WeSalute offers **25% off your first month of Fubo Pro** (about $21.25). Both are intro-only.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify through both ID.me and WeSalute. **Immediate family members** qualify through **WeSalute**, which includes families in its eligibility; confirm family/spouse eligibility inside the ID.me flow for Fubo's own offer.

#### How do I verify my status?
For Fubo's own offer, verify with **ID.me** during sign-up. For the WeSalute offer, verify through your **WeSalute** membership. Each is a one-time verification that unlocks a one-time intro discount.

#### Does Fubo use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Fubo's **own** offer uses **ID.me** (the "Heroes" discount). **WeSalute** runs a separate 25%-off-first-month Fubo Pro offer. Coupon sites also mention **GovX**, but we could not confirm a specific ongoing GovX value against a GovX primary source — treat those claims cautiously and verify at signup.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Online only. Fubo is a streaming service, so all discounts are applied at fubo.tv checkout — there is no in-store option.

#### What is excluded, and is it permanent?
It is **not permanent** — the discount applies only to the first month or two, then billing reverts to the regular plan price. Only one intro offer applies per account, and taxes/fees apply on top.

#### Can I combine the military discount with promo codes or the free trial?
You can start the **free trial** and then have an intro discount apply to your first paid month(s). But the verified offers do **not** stack with each other or with a separate public promo code — Fubo applies one intro discount per account.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Fubo?
Start the **free trial**, then use the **ID.me Heroes offer** ($30 off first two months) on Fubo Pro if you qualify. If you'd rather not verify, Fubo's **public first-month promo** is usually within a few dollars. Don't count on any "ongoing $10/month" claim — it isn't verified.

#### Does Fubo offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
Yes — Fubo's ID.me "Heroes" offer explicitly covers **first responders, teachers, nurses, government workers, hospital workers, and medical providers**, on the same "$30 off your first two months" terms as military.

#### Does Fubo run a Veterans Day or holiday sale?
Fubo runs **public** holiday promotions (e.g., "$30 off" plan deals ran in the 2025 holiday season) open to everyone, not a distinct military event. Watch fubo.tv around November for public seasonal pricing.
