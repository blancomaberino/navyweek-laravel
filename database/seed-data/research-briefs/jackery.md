# Jackery — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Jackery Explorer 1000 v2 portable power station at a ~$799 list price (the everyday mid-tier unit most readers compare).

## Headline finding

**Jackery has a real, first-party 15% military/veteran discount, verified through ID.me, on jackery.com.** It covers active-duty military, veterans, retirees, spouses and dependents, and extends to first responders, nurses/medical providers, and teachers. It applies to most products but **excludes special collaborations**, and it **cannot be combined** with other promotions during a promo period. Archetype: **C (real identity discount, but the sale usually beats it) with E (multi-tier ID.me discount) secondary.**

The catch that makes this page worth more than a coupon page: **Jackery runs deep, frequent sitewide sales** (Prime Day, Black Friday/Cyber Monday, seasonal events) that routinely hit **30–50% off** — far past the 15% identity discount, which those sales don't stack with. So the honest verdict is situational: **when a 30%+ sale is live, a verified shopper should take the public sale price, not their 15%.** The 15% wins mainly on full-price units between sale windows. Ignore coupon-aggregator "Jackery military promo codes" — the only verified military route is the ID.me 15% on Jackery's own site.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 15% "cannot be combined"** with other promotions during a promo period (Jackery promo page) — so it won't stack on a sitewide sale or another code.
2. Because the 15% is non-combinable and **public sales run deep (30–50%)**, the sale is frequently the better single path; you pick one, not both.
3. **Special collaborations are excluded** from the 15% entirely.
4. ID.me-gated checkout is a separate verified flow; a *public* newsletter code is the one most likely to be usable on ordinary orders, but not on top of the military code.
5. A Jackery *reseller's* GovX/military offer (e.g., Outbound Power) is governed by that reseller, not Jackery — don't assume Jackery's 15% terms apply there.

### FAQ

#### Does Jackery offer a military discount?
Yes — a 15% discount for active-duty military, veterans, retirees, and military spouses/dependents, verified through ID.me on jackery.com. The same 15% also covers first responders, nurses/medical providers, and teachers.

#### How much is the Jackery military discount?
15% off eligible products. It excludes special collaborations and can't be combined with other promotions.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Jackery's discount covers active duty, veterans, retirees, and their spouses/dependents, all verified via ID.me.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me — create a free account, verify once, and get your code. Jackery also runs a parallel SheerID-based service-discount page.

#### Does Jackery use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
ID.me is the primary partner; a SheerID-based "Service Discount" page also exists. Jackery corporate doesn't run a GovX military store — any GovX offer you see is from an individual Jackery reseller, not Jackery.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
Online at jackery.com (US site). That's where the ID.me verification and code apply.

#### What is excluded?
Special collaborations are excluded, and the 15% can't be combined with other promotions.

#### Can I combine the 15% with a sale or promo code?
No — it can't be combined. And since Jackery's public sales often reach 30–50%, during a big sale the public price usually beats the 15%. Pick whichever is lower.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Jackery?
If a 30%+ sitewide sale is live, take the public sale price. Between sales, use your ID.me 15%. Also check official refurbished/outlet stock.

#### Does Jackery offer a first responder, nurse, or teacher discount?
Yes — the same 15%, each verified through ID.me.
