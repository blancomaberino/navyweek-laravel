# NordVPN — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one NordVPN **2-year Basic plan**, ~$94 upfront pre-tax (~$3.49/mo intro pricing as of July 14, 2026, per current pricing roundups; NordVPN's own pricing page is bot-walled — spot-check the live price at build time).

## Headline finding

NordVPN runs a real, official **military discount — an extra 15% on top of the current promo (marketed as "Extra 15% + 60% OFF on a 2-year plan")** — verified through **GoCertify** (secure.gocertify.me), not ID.me or SheerID. But it is emphatically **not the best deal right now**: **Rakuten is paying 98% cashback on new NordVPN sign-ups (as of July 14, 2026)**, which reduces a ~$94 two-year Basic plan to nearly free after payout — and Rakuten's terms **void cashback if you use a non-Rakuten coupon code**, so the military code and the cashback are **mutually exclusive**. Archetype: **C (real discount, but another path crushes it)**, with an **F** overlay — all NordVPN pricing is intro-term pricing that renews at a much higher rate.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military code + Rakuten cashback do NOT stack.** Rakuten's NordVPN terms void cashback on any non-Rakuten coupon code, and the GoCertify military code is exactly that. Pick one.
2. **The military 15% DOES stack on the public promo price** — that's the offer's own framing ("Extra 15% + 60% OFF"), so it's a discount on the already-promo 2-year price, not on list.
3. **Rakuten cashback is new-customers / first-sign-up only, one per member**, and on Plus/Complete/Prime bundles pays only on the **Basic-plan portion** — buying Basic maximizes the effective rate.
4. **All paths hit the renewal cliff.** Intro pricing (with or without the 15%) applies to the first term; renewal is at the then-current higher rate. No verified path discounts renewals except re-running the military verification (re-usability at renewal not stated — flagged below).

### FAQ
#### Does NordVPN offer a military discount?
Yes — an official extra 15% off, on top of the current public promotion, verified through GoCertify.
#### How much is the NordVPN military discount?
An extra 15% applied to the already-discounted promo price — marketed as "extra 15% + 60% off a 2-year plan." It is not a standalone 75–83% code, despite what coupon sites claim.
#### Do veterans qualify?
The offer's headline says "Veterans and Military Personnel," but GoCertify's accepted documents list only active armed-forces and Guard/Reserve IDs. Try verifying; don't count on it. 
#### Does NordVPN use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No — verification is through **GoCertify**. Separately, ID.me Shop lists NordVPN cashback offers for ID.me members (rate shown after login).
#### Can I combine the military discount with Rakuten cashback?
No. Rakuten's terms void cashback when you use a non-Rakuten coupon code. Pick whichever is worth more (as of July 14, 2026, the 98% cashback wins decisively for new customers).
#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to get NordVPN?
New customer while the elevated Rakuten rate is live: buy the 2-year Basic plan through Rakuten with no codes — roughly $2 net after cashback on a ~$94 plan. Otherwise, the military 15%.
#### Does the discount apply to renewals?
Not stated. Intro pricing ends after the first term, and cashback is first-sign-up-only; re-running military verification at renewal is unconfirmed.
