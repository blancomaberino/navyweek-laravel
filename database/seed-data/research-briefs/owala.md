# Owala Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Owala (insulated water bottles, tumblers, and drinkware) offers a **one-time 10% off discount** to verified military members and veterans, redeemed online at owalalife.com. Eligibility status is confirmed through **VerifyPass** (not ID.me, GovX, or SheerID); after verifying, the shopper receives a **single-use discount code** by email to enter at checkout. The discount **cannot be combined with other offers** and is **one-time use only**. The single most important caveat: this is a one-time code, not a standing/repeatable discount, and the official Owala page does not state a per-account renewal cadence. (Source: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount, accessed June 23, 2026.)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Owala's military discount page (owalalife.com/pages/military-discount).
2. Complete the form / verification through VerifyPass to confirm your military or veteran status.
3. Receive your unique, single-use discount code by email.
4. Add your Owala items to your cart at owalalife.com and proceed to checkout.
5. Enter the code in the discount/promo field before paying. The 10% off applies to the order (cannot be combined with other offers).

### How Verification Works
Owala uses **VerifyPass** to confirm eligibility. You submit your information through VerifyPass on Owala's military discount page; once your military/veteran status is verified, Owala emails you a **unique, single-use discount code** to apply at checkout. Account creation specifics, accepted documents, and re-verification cadence are **not detailed on Owala's own page**. If automatic verification fails, the standard path is VerifyPass support (help@verifypass.com) or Owala customer service — but the official Owala page does not spell out a failure/appeal process, so do not promise one. Do not describe verification as permanent or "verify once" — the source does not state how long verification or codes remain valid.

### FAQ

#### Does Owala offer a military discount?
Yes. Owala offers a one-time 10% off discount to verified military members and veterans, redeemed online at owalalife.com. (Source: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the Owala military discount?
It is 10% off, applied via a single-use code, and it cannot be combined with other offers. (Source: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify — Owala's page states "verification of veteran status required," and Owala's VerifyPass offer is labeled "Military and Veterans." Spouses and dependents are **not stated** as eligible in the official source. (Sources: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount; verifypass.com/offers/website/owalalife.com.)

#### How do I verify my status?
You verify through VerifyPass on Owala's military discount page. After your status is confirmed, Owala emails you a unique single-use code to use at checkout. (Sources: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount; verifypass.com/offers/website/owalalife.com.)

#### Does Owala use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Owala uses **VerifyPass** for its own military discount, not ID.me, GovX, or SheerID. (A separate "30% off Owala drinkware" offer seen on Keurig is run through WeSalute by that retailer — that is not Owala's own program.) (Source: verifypass.com/offers/website/owalalife.com.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Owala's military discount is documented as an online, code-based offer at owalalife.com. An in-store register process is not stated in the official source. (Source: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount.)

#### What is excluded?
The official page states the 10% code is one-time use and cannot be combined with other offers. Specific product, collection, gift-card, or sale exclusions are not stated in the official source. (Source: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Owala states the discount "cannot be combined with other offers." (Source: owalalife.com/pages/military-discount.)

#### Does Owala offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Owala offers a separate healthcare/medical worker discount (one-time 15% off) and separate first responder verification offers through VerifyPass. A teacher, government, or student discount is **not confirmed on an official Owala page** in this research. (Sources: owalalife.com/pages/medical-nurses-discount; verifypass.com/offers/website/owalalife.com.)
