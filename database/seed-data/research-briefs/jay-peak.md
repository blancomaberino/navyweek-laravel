# Jay Peak Resort — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult weekend/holiday day of skiing (2025–26 advance online adult lift ticket = **$119**; window/day-of = **$139**), plus a full-season scenario.

## Headline finding

Jay Peak runs one of the strongest military ski offers in the East, but it is built around **active-duty**, not veterans. **US active-duty members and their dependents (spouse + children 18 & under) get a full-season pass for $199 + tax** — a standout when a single weekend window ticket is $139. Active-duty also get **50% off Pump House indoor waterpark day access, 50% off ski/snowboard rentals, and 10% off lodging**. **Canadian active-duty and veterans** get **50% off day lift tickets** (member only, not friends/family). Redemption is **in person at Tramside Guest Services** with military ID (and, for the US season pass, orders showing a Vermont base assignment) — not an online code. US **veterans** are not clearly covered on the day-ticket line in the official terms, so the honest answer for a US veteran is: the published program is active-duty-first; check Ski Vermont's separate military days and call the resort before counting on a veteran rate.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. The military season pass and Canadian 50% day ticket are **standalone rates** — treat them as the final price, not a code stacked on a public ticket.
2. Season pass has **blackout pickup** around **Christmas and Presidents' Week** — plan around it.
3. **In-person verification is mandatory** at Tramside Guest Services; there is no online military code. US applicants need orders showing a **Vermont base assignment** plus military + Vermont ID.
4. Rentals/waterpark/lodging discounts attach to the **active-duty** benefit and are redeemed at each respective location.
5. Passes are **non-transferable and photo-verified at the lift** — sharing triggers a **$200 fine** and permanent disqualification.

### FAQ
#### Does Jay Peak offer a military discount?
Yes. US active-duty and dependents get a $199 season pass plus 50% off rentals/waterpark and 10% off lodging; Canadian active-duty and veterans get 50% off day lift tickets.

#### How much is the Jay Peak military discount?
The headline is the **$199 + tax** US active-duty season pass. Canadian military get **50% off** day tickets. Rentals and waterpark are 50% off; lodging 10% off.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
US **dependents** (spouse + kids ≤18) qualify with the active-duty member. **Canadian veterans** qualify for 50% day tickets. **US veterans** without active-duty status are not named for a Jay Peak day rate — use public advance pricing and Ski Vermont military days.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person at **Tramside Guest Services** with military ID (US: plus orders showing a Vermont base assignment and a Vermont ID). No online code.

#### Does Jay Peak use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No — verification is **in-person document check**, not a third-party online verifier.

#### Can I use it online or only in person?
In person only for the military rate. The public ticket can be bought online in advance (saves $20 vs the window).

#### What is excluded?
Season-pass pickup is blacked out around Christmas & Presidents' Week; the Canadian day-ticket rate is member-only; passes are non-transferable.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy?
US active-duty who'll ski 2+ days: the **$199 season pass**. Canadian military: **50% off day tickets**. US veteran: **public advance online ticket + Ski Vermont military days**.

#### Does Jay Peak run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day deal?
Jay Peak participates in **Ski Vermont's** statewide military deals; watch skivermont.com around those holidays.
