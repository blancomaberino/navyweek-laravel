# Casio — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $99 G-SHOCK GA-2100 ("CasiOak").

## Headline finding

Casio America runs an **official military/first-responder/healthcare discount: 10% off G-SHOCK and BABY-G**, verified through **ID.me**, redeemable only on Casio's own US sites (casio.com/us and gshock.casio.com/us). It is a real, brand-run program. Eligibility covers military, first responders, and medical/healthcare providers; the coupon is single-use, **one per customer per 30 days**, and excludes sale/outlet items, anything priced over $999.99, and does not apply to shipping or tax. A **second, separate channel** — **GovX** (and Casio's "Project Peacekeeper" pro-deal program) — offers military/government pricing on G-Shock. Best path depends on the watch: for standard G-Shock/Baby-G at full price, the ID.me 10% is the clean win; for tactical/pro buyers, compare GovX pricing.
