**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** an **$80 apparel cart** (Brandy Melville's items run ~$15–$40, so an order like two tops + a bottom). Illustrative sales tax at 7% ≈ $6 unless noted.

## Headline finding

Brandy Melville publishes **no military or veteran discount**. Its official customer-service/FAQ page (us.brandymelville.com) carries no military program, no ID.me / SheerID / GovX military checkout, no loyalty or rewards program, and no student/first-responder tiers — the words "military," "veteran," "loyalty," "rewards," and "student" appear nowhere on it. Brandy Melville is famously minimalist on discounts: it has **no standing sale section**, issues essentially **no public promo codes** (SimplyCodes has tracked zero working codes in ~2 years), and it is **not on Rakuten** (the store page 404s), so there's no cashback layer either. The only real savings are timing the brand's rare **Black Friday / Cyber Monday flash deals** (historically up to ~30% off) and accepting that **all discounted items are final sale**. This is an honest advisory page: **the discount doesn't exist** — and the aggregator "BRANDY 30% off / BRANDY25 40% off military codes" are fabricated. Archetype: **B (no military route at all)** — no first-party discount and no GovX substitute.
