# On (On Running / On Cloud) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

On (the Swiss running brand behind the "Cloud" shoes, sold at on.com) offers a **community discount verified through ID.me** for military members, plus nurses, medical providers, first responders, teachers, and students. On's own discount page deliberately does **not** publish a percentage ("Discount value on offer is subject to change. Please refer to id.me"), but the primary ID.me Shop store page lists the current value as **15% off for U.S. and Canadian Military**. Verification is through **ID.me only**, you receive a **single-use code** to enter at checkout, and the offer is **online-only — it cannot be used in On stores**. The single most important caveat: the code is **capped to orders of $575 USD / $800 CAD or less (pre-discount)**, is **valid 30 days** after verification, excludes Limited Edition / Last Season products and gift cards, and cannot be combined with other promotions.

> Accuracy note: Some coupon/aggregator pages list On's military discount as **10%** (and a few claim "up to 50%"). The only figure confirmed on a primary source as of June 23, 2026 is **15%**, shown on the ID.me Shop On store page. On's own page publishes **no percentage** and points users to ID.me for the current value — so treat the percentage as ID.me-driven and subject to change, and never assert a number On itself does not display.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to On's Community Discounts page and click the **ID.me verification link**.
2. Create or sign in to **ID.me** and verify your community (e.g., Military).
3. Receive your **single-use discount code** (valid 30 days).
4. Log in to your account on **On.com or the On app** and add eligible items (keep the order at or below **$575 USD / $800 CAD** before discount).
5. Enter the code at checkout to apply the discount.

### How To Redeem In Store
On does **not** offer this discount in stores. Per On's official page: "The single-use code is valid only on On.com or the On app." There is no in-store redemption path.

### How Verification Works
On uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You click the verification link on On's discount page, create a free ID.me account (or sign in), and verify your community — for military, that means providing your branch, status, and service details and, if prompted, uploading proof such as a military ID or DD-214. Once verified, ID.me issues a **single-use code valid for 30 days**. You may request up to **two single-use codes per calendar month**. The value shown is set via ID.me and can change; On's page itself does not publish a percentage. For verification problems, On directs users to the ID.me Help Center (help.id.me).

### FAQ

#### Does On (On Cloud) offer a military discount?
Yes. On offers a community discount to verified military members through ID.me, redeemable online at On.com or the On app. On's own page doesn't print a percentage; ID.me currently lists 15% off for U.S. and Canadian military.

#### How much is the On military discount?
On's official page does not publish a fixed percentage and says the value is "subject to change," pointing shoppers to ID.me. As of June 23, 2026, ID.me Shop lists **15% off** for military. (Older coupon pages citing 10% appear outdated; ignore "up to 50%" claims — those are aggregator inflation.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify through the ID.me Military community. On does not state on its official page whether military spouses or dependents are eligible, so check directly through ID.me.

#### How do I verify my status?
Click the ID.me verification link on On's discount page, create or sign in to ID.me, verify your community, and you'll receive a single-use code to enter at checkout.

#### Does On use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
On uses **ID.me**. Its official discount page and verification flow run entirely through ID.me.

#### Can I use the discount in On stores?
No. On states the single-use code is valid only on On.com or the On app — it cannot be used in stores.

#### What is excluded?
Limited Edition Collections, Last Season products, and gift cards. The code also can't be applied to shipping, can't be combined with other promotions, and only works on orders of $575 USD / $800 CAD or less before the discount.

#### How long is my code valid and how often can I get one?
Each code is single-use and valid for 30 days after verification. You can request up to two codes per calendar month through ID.me.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. On states the discount cannot be combined with other promotions, and Last Season (sale) products are excluded.

#### Does On offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Yes — the same ID.me community program covers nurses, medical providers (U.S.), first responders, teachers, and students, each verified as a separate community through ID.me.
