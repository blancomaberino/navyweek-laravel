# San Diego Zoo Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

The San Diego Zoo and San Diego Zoo Safari Park (San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance) offer **free complimentary admission to U.S. Active Duty military, every day of the year, at both parks** — no ticket or reservation required; service members just present a valid Common Access Card (CAC) or Uniformed Services ID Card at the gate. **U.S. Active Duty military spouses and eligible immediate dependents** receive **10% off a 1-Day Pass** at both parks (shown at a ticket window; they do not need to be accompanied by the service member). The single most important caveat: **the parks do NOT offer a discount to retirees or veterans** — those groups are directed to their installation's MWR ticket office. The free/discount offers cover Active Duty only (plus the listed Reserve/Guard/cadet components), do not cover companions' admission, do not include Safari Park parking, and are not valid for special ticketed events or programs. (Source: San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance Help Center/FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

The San Diego Zoo and San Diego Zoo Safari Park give **free admission to U.S. Active Duty military, every day of the year, at both parks** — no ticket or reservation needed. Just show a valid Common Access Card (CAC) or Uniformed Services ID Card at the gate. **Active Duty spouses and eligible immediate dependents** get **10% off a 1-Day Pass** at any ticket window, and they don't have to be with the service member to use it.

There is an important limit: **the parks do not offer a discount to retirees or veterans.** If you're retired or a veteran, the Zoo directs you to the MWR (Morale, Welfare & Recreation) ticket office at a local military installation, which may offer discounted package deals.

This is an independent guide to help military families plan a visit; it is not affiliated with the San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance. The Zoo sets and can change these terms at any time, so confirm details on the official Help Center before you go.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard)** — Yes, free admission with CAC/Uniformed Services ID.
- **Reserve / National Guard (Ready Reserve, National Guard, Reservists in training)** — Yes, included in the Active Duty free-admission group per the official list.
- **Service-academy cadets and U.S. Merchant Marine Academy cadets** — Yes, included per the official list.
- **Active duty spouses** — Yes, but only the 10%-off-1-Day-Pass benefit (not free admission). No need to be accompanied by the service member.
- **Eligible immediate dependents** — Yes, 10% off 1-Day Pass with valid dependent ID.
- **Retirees** — No park discount; directed to MWR ticket office.
- **Veterans** — No park discount; directed to MWR ticket office.
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not stated in official source.
- **First responders** — Not stated in official source (no first-responder offer listed).
- **Medical workers** — Not stated in official source.
- **Teachers** — Not stated in official source.
- **Government employees** — Not stated in official source.
- **Students** — Not a military category, but the Zoo separately offers students 18+ a 10% discount off the 1-Day Pass with valid photo ID and proof of enrollment.
- **Civilians / foreign military** — Explicitly excluded.

### How To Redeem In Store (at the park)
**Active Duty (free admission):**
1. Go directly to the entry turnstile at the San Diego Zoo or Safari Park — no ticket or reservation needed for yourself.
2. Present your valid Common Access Card (CAC) or Uniformed Services ID Card.
3. Enter free. Note: if you cannot present a valid active-duty ID, you are not eligible for free entry and must buy non-refundable admission (no retroactive refunds).
4. Buy tickets/memberships separately for any accompanying guests ages 3 and older.

**Active Duty spouse / eligible immediate dependent (10% off 1-Day Pass):**
1. Go to any ticket window at the Zoo or Safari Park.
2. Show your valid dependent ID card.
3. Receive 10% off the 1-Day Pass. You do not need to be accompanied by the active-duty service member.

### How Verification Works
Verification is **in person and ID-based** — there is no third-party verification provider (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute) and no online account requirement for the military benefit. Active Duty service members present a CAC or Uniformed Services ID Card at the gate; spouses and eligible dependents present a valid dependent ID card at a ticket window. Verification happens at the point of entry/purchase each visit. If an active-duty member cannot present a valid ID for any reason, free entry is denied and admission must be purchased (non-refundable, no retroactive refunds). For retiree/veteran offers, the support path is the MWR ticket office on a local military installation.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Retirees and veterans get no discount at the parks** — directed to MWR ticket offices for any package deals.
- Free admission covers the **active-duty service member only**; accompanying guests ages 3 and older must have a ticket or membership.
- **Safari Park parking is not included.**
- **Not valid for special ticketed events or programs.**

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Retirees & veterans:** contact your installation's **MWR / ITT ticket office** — the Zoo partners with local-base MWR departments on discounted package deals for retirees, veterans, and others.

### FAQ

#### Does San Diego Zoo offer a military discount?
Yes. U.S. Active Duty military get free admission every day of the year at both the San Diego Zoo and the Safari Park, and Active Duty spouses/eligible dependents get 10% off a 1-Day Pass. Retirees and veterans do not get a discount at the parks. (Source: SDZWA Help Center, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the San Diego Zoo military discount?
For U.S. Active Duty military it's 100% — free admission. For their spouses and eligible immediate dependents it's 10% off the 1-Day Pass. There is no published discount for retirees or veterans at the parks.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees do **not** get a park discount (the Zoo refers them to MWR ticket offices). Active Duty spouses and eligible immediate dependents do qualify — for 10% off a 1-Day Pass, shown at a ticket window, even if the service member isn't with them.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person. Active Duty present a Common Access Card (CAC) or Uniformed Services ID Card at the gate; spouses/dependents present a valid dependent ID card at a ticket window. There is no online verification.

#### Does San Diego Zoo use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. The military benefit is verified by showing a military/dependent ID in person — no third-party verification service and no online account are involved.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / at the gate?
Yes — the military benefit is in-person only. Active Duty enter free at the turnstile; spouses/dependents get 10% off at a ticket window. It is not available online or by phone.

#### What's excluded?
Companions ages 3+ need their own ticket/membership; Safari Park parking isn't included; the benefit isn't valid for special ticketed events or programs; and civilians, foreign military, retirees, and veterans aren't eligible.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Not stated for the military free admission. The Zoo's other named discounts (AAA, airline/cruise, travel agent) state they can't be combined with other offers, but the military free-admission terms don't address combining. Confirm at the ticket window.

#### Is the active-duty free admission available every day?
Yes — it's valid every day of the year ("Visit as often as you wish!"), except it's not valid for special ticketed events or programs.

#### Does San Diego Zoo offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount is listed. Students 18+ separately get 10% off the 1-Day Pass with valid photo ID and proof of enrollment.
