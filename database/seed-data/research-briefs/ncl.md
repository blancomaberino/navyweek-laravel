# Norwegian Cruise Line (NCL) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Norwegian Cruise Line (NCL) runs the **Military Appreciation Program**, which gives validated members **10% off the cruise fare portion of any cruise**, all year long (evergreen). Eligibility covers active members, veterans, spouses, and surviving spouses of nine U.S. uniformed services branches (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Space Force, National Guard, NOAA Corps, U.S. Public Health Service), each verified through **ID.me**. Verification is a one-time step tied to a free My NCL account; after validating, discounted prices appear automatically at checkout when logged in, and NCL states you "won't ever need to verify again." The single most important caveat: you must validate **before booking** to receive the discount, and the 10% applies only to the cruise fare — not taxes, fees, or onboard charges. (A widely repeated "20% off select cruises" claim appears on aggregator/search snippets but is **not** confirmed in NCL's own FAQ copy verified here — treat it as unverified.)

### Intro
Norwegian Cruise Line's Military Appreciation Program gives eligible service members and their families **10% off the cruise fare portion of any cruise**, available year-round. It covers active-duty members, veterans, spouses, and surviving spouses of nine U.S. uniformed services branches, with status verified through ID.me.

To use it, create a free My NCL account, verify once through ID.me, and your discounted price will show automatically at checkout whenever you're logged in. You can also book through a travel advisor. NCL says you only need to verify once — you won't have to re-verify for future bookings.

This is an independent guide to help you understand and use the offer. We are not affiliated with Norwegian Cruise Line, and NCL sets and can change the terms at any time. Always confirm current details at ncl.com/military before booking.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to ncl.com/military.
2. Log in or register for a free My NCL account.
3. Verify your military status through ID.me (a one-time verification).
4. Once verified, search for cruises at ncl.com while logged in — the prices shown at checkout will reflect your 10% discount.
5. Complete your booking. (You won't need to verify again for future bookings.)

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable — Norwegian Cruise Line is booked online or by phone/travel advisor, not in physical retail stores. To book with assistance, verify via ID.me first, then contact an NCL travel advisor or NCL by phone (1-866-234-7350, per ncl.com site footer) to complete the booking with the discount applied.

### How Verification Works
NCL uses **ID.me**, a third-party verification service. You must create a free My NCL account and verify your military status through ID.me **before booking** to receive the discount. Verification is described by NCL as a one-time step: "You won't ever need to verify again." After verifying and while logged in, your 10% discount appears automatically in checkout pricing. If you have trouble enrolling, NCL directs guests to its support path (see NCL FAQ "What should I do if I have issues enrolling for the military discount?"). Note: ID.me itself may have its own account and re-authentication requirements; this brief reflects only what NCL's FAQ states about NCL-side verification.

### FAQ

#### Does Norwegian Cruise Line offer a military discount?
Yes. NCL's Military Appreciation Program gives validated members 10% off the cruise fare portion of any cruise, year-round. (Source: NCL "What is the military discount" FAQ.)

#### How much is the NCL military discount?
10% off the cruise fare portion of any cruise. It is combinable with Norwegian's Free at Sea promotion and with other available promotions, though those are subject to change. (Source: NCL "How much do I save" FAQ.) A "20% off select cruises" figure circulates on third-party sites but is not confirmed in NCL's official FAQ copy.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or surviving spouses qualify?
Yes. Eligibility includes active members, veterans, spouses, and surviving spouses of the covered U.S. uniformed services branches, each verified through ID.me. (Source: NCL "Who is eligible" and "I am a veteran" FAQs.) Dependents and Gold Star families are not named specifically in the official source.

#### Which military branches are covered?
Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Space Force, National Guard, NOAA Corps, and U.S. Public Health Service. (Source: NCL "Who is eligible" FAQ.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
Create a free My NCL account, then verify through ID.me before booking. It's a one-time verification; NCL says you won't need to verify again. Discounted prices then show at checkout when you're logged in. (Source: NCL "How do I apply" FAQ.)

#### Does NCL use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
NCL uses **ID.me**. (Source: NCL "How do I apply" FAQ.) GovX, SheerID, and WeSalute are not used for this program per the verified sources.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
There are no NCL retail stores. Book online at ncl.com while logged in to your verified My NCL account, or through a travel advisor / NCL by phone. (Source: NCL "How do I apply" FAQ.)

#### What is excluded?
The discount applies to the cruise fare portion only (not taxes, fees, or onboard charges, per NCL's wording). You must validate before booking, and the discount cannot be given to non-eligible friends. Caps and full exclusion lists are not detailed in the official FAQ. (Source: NCL "What is the military discount" / "How much do I save" FAQs.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other offers?
NCL states the military discount is in addition to Free at Sea and is "combinable with other promotions that are available, but subject to change." (Source: NCL "How much do I save" FAQ.)

#### Does NCL offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
NCL publicizes a separate Teacher Cruise Discount and a First Responder Cruise Discount (listed in NCL's site navigation). Nurse, government, and student programs are not stated in the verified military sources. Verify those separately on ncl.com.
