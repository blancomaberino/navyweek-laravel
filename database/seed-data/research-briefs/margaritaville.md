## Headline finding

**Margaritaville is a licensing/franchise brand, so there is no single brand-wide military discount.** Each business line runs its own program, and the terms are genuinely different by property and channel — so the aggregator line "Margaritaville gives 25% off to military" is misleading. Here is what is actually verified:

- **Resorts & hotels (margaritavilleresorts.com):** a per-**property** "Heroes" rate, ranging from **10% up to 35% off the room rate** (some properties add a small resort credit), verified by showing a valid **military / veteran / first-responder / government ID at check-in** (no verification partner — it's in-person). Deepest confirmed this pass: **35% at Fort Myers Beach** (code **HERO**); 30% at Kansas City and Lake Tahoe; 15% (+$75 credit) at Hollywood Beach; 10% at several. Non-combinable; applies to room rate only (resort fees and taxes are extra). Some properties also publish a **government per-diem rate** (e.g. Lake Conroe **$128/night**, code **PERDIEM**) for official gov/military travel.
- **Margaritaville at Sea (cruises):** the standout — the **"Heroes Sail Free"** program, verified through **GovX**, gives a **free interior cabin for the first and second guest** (you pay only taxes, fees, and port expenses), or an **extra 20% off** when free cabins aren't available. Broad eligibility (military, veterans, first responders, medical, educators, government). This is by far the highest-dollar benefit in the Margaritaville universe.
- **Margaritaville Run Orlando:** **20% off** race registration via **GovX**.
- **Margaritaville Restaurants / LandShark Bar & Grill:** a **GovX** storefront exists (offer terms gated behind GovX login — treat exact value as unconfirmed); individual restaurants have run **Veterans Day** free-meal or ~15% offers (location-specific, historical).
- **Retail / apparel (margaritaville.com store):** **no military discount confirmed** this pass.

Archetype: dominant **I — Travel/loyalty (rates vary by property; never publish one flat %)**, with a strong secondary **A — GovX identity discount** (the cruise, Run, and restaurants route entirely through GovX) and a dash of **H/J — the "myth" flat percentage** the aggregators invent. **Best single play:** if you're eligible and flexible, **the Heroes Sail Free cruise is the biggest win by far** (a whole cabin free). For a land stay, **book the specific property's Heroes rate and check the % — it's 10–35% depending on location**, and a government per-diem rate can beat it for official travel.

### FAQ

#### Does Margaritaville offer a military discount?
Yes, but not one single discount — Margaritaville is a licensed brand, so each business runs its own. Resorts offer a per-property **Heroes rate (10–35% off the room)**, the **cruise** offers a **free interior cabin** ("Heroes Sail Free") via GovX, and **Margaritaville Run Orlando** gives **20% off** via GovX.

#### How much is the Margaritaville military discount?
It depends on the channel. Resort Heroes rates run **10% to 35% off the room rate** by property (35% at Fort Myers Beach, 30% at Kansas City and Lake Tahoe, 15% at Hollywood Beach, 10% at several). The cruise gives a **free interior cabin** or **+20%**. The Run is **20%**. There is **no flat brand-wide 25%** — that aggregator claim is false.

#### Do veterans, first responders, and government employees qualify?
Generally yes, though it varies. Most resort Heroes pages name military, veterans, first responders, and government IDs; a few (e.g. South Padre) name active-duty and first responders only. The cruise and Run cover military, veterans, first responders, medical, educators, and government via GovX.

#### How do I verify my status?
At **resorts**, you verify **in person** — book the rate and show a valid ID at check-in. For the **cruise, Run, and restaurants**, you verify through a free **GovX** account.

#### Does Margaritaville use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The confirmed partner is **GovX** (cruise, Run, restaurants). Resorts verify by **in-person ID**, no online partner. There is **no confirmed ID.me, SheerID, or WeSalute** route — treat those aggregator claims as unverified.

#### Can I use it online or only in person?
Resort Heroes rates are booked online but **verified in person at check-in**. The cruise, Run, and restaurant offers are redeemed **online through GovX**.

#### What is excluded?
Resort rates are non-combinable and cover the **room rate only** — resort fees and taxes are extra, with possible blackout dates, minimum stays, and group-size limits. The cruise free cabin is interior-only, capacity-controlled, and not for solo travelers (taxes/fees/port extra).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Every resort rate is non-combinable, and the Heroes rate and public promo codes are mutually exclusive — pick whichever is lower. The cruise's +20% applies "on top of any sales available online" per Margaritaville at Sea, but taxes/fees are always extra.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to enjoy Margaritaville?
