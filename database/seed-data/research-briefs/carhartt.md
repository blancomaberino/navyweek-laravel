# Carhartt Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Carhartt offers a **15% discount on apparel and accessories** to verified military members, first responders, nurses, and medical providers. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**, both online (a button at checkout) and in store (show your validated ID.me eligibility card at the register). The offer is evergreen — Carhartt's own page states your ID.me account does not expire, so the discount can be reused on future orders. The single most important caveat: the discount covers **apparel and accessories only**, and Carhartt/ID.me note "additional restrictions may apply" without publishing a full exclusion list, so it should not be assumed to apply to every product or to stack with other promo codes and sales.

> Accuracy note: Many coupon/aggregator pages still advertise a **25% Carhartt military discount**. That figure is **outdated** — Carhartt reduced the discount to 15%. Only 15% is confirmed on Carhartt's official page and on the ID.me store page as of June 23, 2026.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add Carhartt apparel or accessories to your cart and go to checkout.
2. Select the **ID.me** button at checkout.
3. Sign in to ID.me (or create an account) and verify your eligible community.
4. Once validated, the **15%** discount applies to your current order.
5. Your ID.me account is saved for future Carhartt orders.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Create and verify an **ID.me** account in advance (military, first responder, nurse, or medical provider).
2. Pull up your **validated ID.me eligibility card** (in the ID.me app/account).
3. Show it at checkout.
4. The associate applies the **15%** discount to your purchase.

### How Verification Works
Carhartt uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You create a free ID.me account and verify your status in the relevant community (Military, First Responder, Nurse, or Medical Provider). Online, you click the ID.me button during checkout; in store, you present your validated ID.me eligibility card. Carhartt states your ID.me account does not expire, so you can reuse the discount on later orders — though ID.me may ask you to re-confirm your identity or status at sign-in per its own policies. For verification problems, Carhartt directs shoppers to ID.me support (help.id.me).
