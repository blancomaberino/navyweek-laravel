# Sam's Club Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Sam's Club does **not** offer an everyday product discount for military shoppers. Instead it runs a **new-member sign-up offer**: verified military members can join a one-year **Club membership for $15** (plus **$5 in Sam's Cash**) or a **Plus membership for $50** (plus **$5 in Sam's Cash**), versus the standard $50 Club / $110 Plus annual fees. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me** at dedicated Sam's Club hosted pages that samsclub.com links to directly. The offer is **new members only** — explicitly not valid for renewals, current members, anyone who held a Sam's Club membership within the prior 24 months, or Walmart/Sam's Club associates. The single most important caveat: this is a **promotional membership price, not a discount on merchandise**, the price/structure changes periodically, and it auto-renews at the then-current rate unless you cancel.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregators (and Sam's Club's own older help-center copy) still describe this as **"60% off — join for $20"** for Club and **"$60"** for Plus, plus a **"$5 in-club gift card."** As of June 23, 2026 the **current, live** Sam's Club–linked ID.me pages state **$15 Club + $5 Sam's Cash** and **$50 Plus + $5 Sam's Cash**. The dollar figures and the "Sam's Cash vs. gift card" wording have changed over time, so only the hosted-page figures are treated as current here; the older numbers are flagged as stale.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Sam's Club's military membership page (samsclub.com/content/military-membership), which routes to the ID.me-verified join page.
2. Choose your tier: **Club** ($15 + $5 Sam's Cash) or **Plus** ($50 + $5 Sam's Cash, via the Plus link).
3. Click **Verify with ID.me** and sign in or create a free ID.me account.
4. Verify your military status (ID.me may ask for service documentation).
5. Once verified, complete the new-member signup; the Instant Savings price applies at checkout (pre-tax).
6. Your $5 in Sam's Cash posts per the offer terms; membership is valid one year from issue and auto-renews unless cancelled.

### How To Redeem In Store
Sam's Club presents this as an **online, ID.me-verified new-member offer**. The official join flow is online. In club, proof of service for membership purposes can include an active/retired DoD military ID; a service-organization card (American Legion, VFW, DAV) with photo ID; or a CAC or VA medical benefits card — but the discounted-price offer documented here is redeemed through the online ID.me link, not as an in-store percentage off. *(If you join in club, confirm with the membership desk whether the same promotional price is honored — not stated on the official online offer page.)*

### How Verification Works
Sam's Club uses **ID.me** to confirm military status for the online membership offer. You create a free ID.me account and verify in the Military community; ID.me may request service documentation. After verification you're routed back to Sam's Club to complete the new membership at the offer price. Verification is tied to ID.me's own re-confirmation policies — don't assume one-time verification is permanent. For membership or verification problems, Sam's Club support is 888-746-7726, and ID.me support is help.id.me.

### FAQ

#### Does Sam's Club offer a military discount?
Not on merchandise. Sam's Club offers verified military members a **discounted new membership** — currently a one-year Club membership for $15 plus $5 in Sam's Cash, or Plus for $50 plus $5 in Sam's Cash — verified through ID.me. It does not give a percentage off the products you buy.

#### How much is the Sam's Club military membership offer?
As of June 2026, the Sam's Club–linked ID.me pages show **Club: $15 + $5 in Sam's Cash** and **Plus: $50 + $5 in Sam's Cash** for new members. (Older copy advertising "$20 / 60% off" Club and "$60" Plus is out of date.) Prices can change.

#### Do veterans and military spouses qualify?
Yes. Sam's Club's help center lists active and retired military, veterans, and spouses (and civilian military employees) as eligible. Non-spouse dependents and surviving spouses are not specifically stated.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verification is done online through **ID.me** on the Sam's Club military join page. Create a free ID.me account, verify your military status, and you're routed back to complete the membership at the offer price.

#### Does Sam's Club use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The official Sam's Club military membership page uses **ID.me**. (Sam's Club also appears on the GovX marketplace, but the official join flow specifies ID.me.)

#### Can current members or renewing members use this offer?
No. It's for **new members only** — not valid for renewals, current members, anyone who was a Sam's Club member within the prior 24 months, or Walmart/Sam's Club associates.

#### Is the discounted membership a one-time price?
The offer applies to your initial new-member signup. Membership **auto-renews** each year at the then-current standard rate unless you cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

#### What is the $5 in Sam's Cash?
The current offer pages list **$5 in Sam's Cash** as a bonus for joining via the offer. The exact redemption mechanic has changed over time (older copy described a $5 in-club credit redeemable within 30 days), so confirm the current terms at signup.

#### Does Sam's Club offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount?
Sam's Club runs **separate** discounted new-member membership offers for First Responders, Government Employees, Seniors (50+), and SNAP/EBT recipients via ID.me/its help center. These are distinct from the military offer; check each program's own page for current pricing.
