# Spectrum (Charter Spectrum) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Spectrum (Charter Communications) does not offer a standing military, veteran, or senior discount on its internet, TV, or mobile service.** Spectrum maintains a dedicated "military" landing page (spectrum.com/choose-spectrum/military), but that page markets standard new-customer promotional pricing — it does **not** state any percentage or dollar discount tied to military or veteran status, and it requires no military verification (no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID at the consumer-service level). What Spectrum *does* offer service members are two genuine, officially documented benefits tied to the **Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA)**: (1) the ability to **suspend Spectrum Mobile service** with proof of deployment 90+ days or a permanent change of duty station to a no-coverage area, with no service fees during suspension; and (2) the ability to **cancel home services and reserve your phone number for up to 3 years** via the SCRA Disconnection Request Form at spectrum.net/Militaryleave. Separately, low-income veterans/seniors may qualify for **Spectrum Internet Assist** ($25/mo, or $15/mo with an added qualifier) if they participate in NSLP/CEP or receive SSI — but this is income/program-based, not a military discount. Aggregator and "ID.me Shop" listings advertising "up to 40% off for military/nurses/teachers" are tied to **third-party Spectrum Authorized Retailers**, not Charter Spectrum directly; treat those as unverified promotional pricing, not an official military discount. The single most important caveat: **there is no verified military/veteran price cut from Spectrum itself — the real benefits are SCRA suspension/cancellation rights and (income-gated) Internet Assist.**

### How To Redeem Online
There is no military discount to apply online. To use the actual military benefits:

**Suspend Spectrum Mobile (deployment):**
1. Confirm you have proof of deployment for 90+ days, or a permanent change of duty station to an area with no Spectrum/roaming coverage.
2. Contact Spectrum to request active-military suspension (see spectrum.net/support/mobile/suspend-spectrum-mobile-service-for-active-military).
3. Provide your documentation; no service fees apply during the suspension.
4. To resume within 39 months, contact Spectrum; you keep the same number.

**Cancel home service & reserve your number (SCRA):**
1. Go to spectrum.net/Militaryleave.
2. Complete the SCRA Disconnection Request Form (online or by mail).
3. Submit your relocation order or deployment letter.
4. Settle any outstanding charges/taxes/fees prior to cancellation.
5. Reactivate the reserved number within 3 years; reconnection/activation fees are waived (install/equipment charges still apply).

### How To Redeem In Store
Spectrum stores can direct you to SCRA processing, but the SCRA Disconnection Request is handled via the online form (spectrum.net/Militaryleave) or by phone, with documentation. There is no in-store military discount to present, because no such discount exists. Bring your orders/deployment letter for any SCRA request.

### How Verification Works
Spectrum does **not** use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or any third-party military-status verifier for a consumer discount on its own service — because there is no such discount to gate. For SCRA benefits, "verification" means submitting your **military orders or deployment letter** (relocation/deployment 90+ days) directly to Spectrum via the SCRA Disconnection Request Form or to customer service. No standing online account verification is required beyond your normal Spectrum account. Note: "Spectrum Authorized Retailer" listings on ID.me Shop are operated by **third-party resellers**, not Charter Spectrum; any "verified" discount there is the retailer's offer, not an official Spectrum military discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Spectrum offer a military discount?
No. Spectrum (Charter) does not publish a military, veteran, or senior discount on internet, TV, or mobile. Its "military" landing page markets standard plans and new-customer promos, not a status-based price cut.

#### How much is the Spectrum military discount?
There is no Spectrum military discount, so there is no percentage or dollar amount. Any "up to 40% off military" figure you see comes from third-party authorized retailers (via ID.me Shop), not from Charter Spectrum directly.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
No Spectrum military discount exists for any of these groups. Veterans may qualify for the income-based Spectrum Internet Assist plan only if they meet program criteria (SSI, NSLP/CEP). Spouses and dependents are not addressed.

#### How do I verify my military status with Spectrum?
You don't, for a discount — there's nothing to verify. For SCRA benefits (mobile suspension, cancellation, number reservation), you submit your military orders or deployment letter to Spectrum directly, not through an online verifier.

#### Does Spectrum use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not for a consumer military discount on Spectrum's own service. Listings under "Spectrum Authorized Retailer" on ID.me Shop are run by third-party resellers, not Charter Spectrum.

#### Can active-duty members suspend or cancel Spectrum service when deployed?
Yes. Under the SCRA, you can suspend Spectrum Mobile (proof of deployment 90+ days or PCS to a no-coverage area; no service fees) and cancel home service while reserving your phone number for up to 3 years. Use the SCRA Disconnection Request Form at spectrum.net/Militaryleave.

#### What benefits do military customers actually get from Spectrum?
Two SCRA benefits: (1) Spectrum Mobile suspension during qualifying deployment with no service fees and same number on reinstatement; (2) home-service cancellation with phone-number reservation up to 3 years and waived reconnection/activation fees on return.

#### Can I combine a military discount with promo codes or sale prices?
There is no military discount to combine. Standard new-customer promotional pricing applies to everyone who qualifies as a new customer, regardless of military status.

#### Does Spectrum offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Not as an official Spectrum-direct discount. Some third-party "Spectrum Authorized Retailer" offers on ID.me Shop target these groups, but those are reseller promotions, not Charter Spectrum's terms. Spectrum markets student-oriented internet plans, but not a verified student status discount.

#### Is there a low-cost Spectrum internet option for low-income veterans or seniors?
Yes — Spectrum Internet Assist: $25/mo (up to 30/4 Mbps), or $15/mo with an added qualifier, for households on NSLP, CEP of NSLP, or SSI (including seniors 65+ on SSI). It's income/program-based, not a military discount.
