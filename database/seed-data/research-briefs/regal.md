## Executive Summary

Regal Cinemas offers military and veteran savings on movie tickets, but **not through a single ID.me code** — it works differently from a typical retailer. **Online**, savings flow through two partners: **WeSalute+** (a paid membership, formerly Veterans Advantage; redeemed via its entertainment partner **Working Advantage**) for **up to 30% off** box-office price, and **GovX** (free GovX ID verification) for discounted tickets. **In person**, a separate military discount is offered **at the box office only**, with a valid **Military ID, Veteran ID, or DD214** — the amount and availability **vary by location**. WeSalute serves military, veterans, retirees, reserve/Guard, and immediate family; GovX also verifies **first responders**. WeSalute / Working Advantage tickets are pre-purchased and **all sales are final** (non-refundable, non-exchangeable). The free Regal Crown Club, Value Tuesdays, and member concession deals are separate loyalty perks — not the military discount.

### Intro
Regal Cinemas is one of the most-searched movie-ticket military discounts — but it works differently from a typical retailer. There is no single ID.me code at checkout. Instead, the online discount runs through two partners (**WeSalute+** and **GovX**), while a separate discount is available in person at the box office with a valid military or veteran ID.

This independent guide focuses on the online routes service members and veterans ask about most: how to get **up to 30% off** Regal tickets through WeSalute+ (redeemed via Working Advantage) and how to buy discounted tickets through GovX — plus the in-person box-office option, who qualifies for each, and the fine print to watch. We link straight to Regal's official program and its partners so you can confirm the current terms before you buy.

The headline savings — up to 30% off box-office price — come from WeSalute+, a paid membership (formerly Veterans Advantage). Members order discounted Regal tickets through Working Advantage and receive them as e-tickets or mailed tickets; because these are pre-purchased partner tickets, all sales are final. GovX is the second online route: after a free GovX ID verification, eligible military, veterans, and first responders can buy discounted Regal Cinemas tickets directly from GovX — the better fit if you want free verification or you qualify as a first responder. Separately, many Regal theatres honor a military discount at the box office with a valid Military ID, Veteran ID, or DD214; this in-person discount is set locally, so the amount and availability vary by theatre.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (all branches), via WeSalute+ or GovX online, or at the box office with valid ID.
- **Veterans / military retirees** — Eligible with a verifiable service history.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible (current and former).
- **Immediate military family** — Eligible through a WeSalute+ membership.
- **First responders** — Eligible **online via GovX only** (police, fire, EMT/paramedic); not at the box office.
- **Box office (in person)** — Military and veterans only — present a valid Military ID, Veteran ID, or DD214.

### How To Redeem Online
1. **Pick your route.** Regal does not sell its military discount directly on regmovies.com. Online savings come from two partners: WeSalute+ (best for the up-to-30%-off rate and for including family) and GovX (free verification, also covers first responders).
2. **WeSalute+ via Working Advantage.** Join WeSalute+ (paid; formerly Veterans Advantage), then open Working Advantage and order discounted Regal tickets — up to 30% off box-office price — delivered as e-tickets or mailed tickets. All ticket sales are final.
3. **GovX tickets.** Create a free GovX account, verify your military, veteran, or first-responder status with a GovX ID, then buy discounted Regal Cinemas tickets from the Regal venue page on GovX.
4. **Redeem at the theatre.** Use the partner-issued ticket or voucher to enter your show, following the redemption instructions provided with your order.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit your local Regal box office — the in-person military discount is offered at the box office only, not in advance online.
2. Present a valid **Military ID, Veteran ID, or DD214** at the time of purchase. This route is for military and veterans only.
3. The amount and availability vary by location, so ask your Regal whether the discount is offered and on which showtimes before you buy.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- There is no single ID.me program for Regal — online savings run through WeSalute+ (paid membership) and GovX, not a Regal checkout code.
- WeSalute+ is a paid membership tier; factor the membership cost into your savings.
- Tickets bought through WeSalute / Working Advantage are final — all sales are non-refundable and non-exchangeable.
- The box-office discount amount, eligibility, and availability vary by theatre and can change without notice.
- Partner discounts generally cannot be combined with other Regal promotions, and premium formats or special events may be excluded.
- The free Regal Crown Club, Value Tuesdays, and member concession deals are separate loyalty perks — not the military discount.

### FAQ

#### Does Regal offer a military discount?
Yes. Regal Cinemas offers military and veteran savings, but not through a single ID.me code. Online, you can save **up to 30% off** ticket prices through WeSalute+ (redeemed via its partner Working Advantage) or buy discounted tickets through GovX after free verification. Separately, many Regal theatres give a military discount at the box office with a valid military or veteran ID. Confirm the current terms on Regal's official page.

#### How do I get the Regal military discount online?
Use one of Regal's ticket partners. WeSalute+ members order discounted Regal tickets — up to 30% off — through Working Advantage, delivered as e-tickets or mailed tickets (all sales final). Or create a free GovX account, verify your status with a GovX ID, and buy discounted Regal Cinemas tickets on GovX. Regal does not apply the military discount directly at checkout on regmovies.com.

#### How much is the Regal military discount?
The best-known rate is **up to 30% off** the box-office price through WeSalute+ and Working Advantage. GovX also sells Regal tickets below standard pricing. The in-person box-office discount is set by each theatre, so the amount varies by location — check your local Regal for its current offer.

#### Do veterans get the Regal discount, or only active duty?
Veterans qualify. WeSalute+ and GovX both serve active-duty members, veterans, retirees, and reserve and National Guard personnel, and WeSalute can extend savings to immediate family members. The box-office discount is for military and veterans who present a valid Military ID, Veteran ID, or DD214.

#### Does Regal use ID.me or GovX for its military discount?
Regal does not run an ID.me program. Its online military savings are verified through **GovX** (free GovX ID) or through a paid **WeSalute+** membership redeemed at Working Advantage. In person, the box office verifies eligibility with a physical military or veteran ID rather than an online provider.

#### Does Regal offer a first responder discount?
Yes, but only online through **GovX** — not at the box office. First responders such as police officers, firefighters, EMTs, and paramedics can create a free GovX account, verify their status with a GovX ID, and buy discounted Regal Cinemas tickets. Regal's in-person box-office discount is limited to military and veterans.

#### Are Regal partner tickets refundable?
No. Tickets purchased through WeSalute / Working Advantage are final — all sales are non-refundable and non-exchangeable — so only buy what you plan to use. Always review the redemption and refund terms shown by the partner before you check out.
