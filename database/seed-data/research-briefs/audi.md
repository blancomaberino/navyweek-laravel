# Audi — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new 2026 Audi Q5 financed or leased at a U.S. dealer (~$46,000 MSRP as a representative "select" eligible model).

## Headline finding

Yes — Audi runs a real, official, brand-published military offer: the **Audi Military Select Program**, a **$1,000 bonus** toward the purchase or lease of a select new, unused MY25/MY26 Audi, for the current offer period **January 6, 2026 – January 4, 2027**. Eligibility is broad (active duty, veterans, retirees, National Guard/Reserve, spouses, surviving spouses, and immediate family) and is verified through **ID.me**, which issues a 6-character authorization code you bring to a participating dealer. Audi's archetype here is a **manufacturer incentive/bonus-cash brand** (like an auto OEM), not a retail % discount: the $1,000 is a fixed bonus layered onto whatever price you negotiate. The single best path depends on where you are stationed: **CONUS buyers** should treat the $1,000 as a stackable bonus on top of publicly advertised lease/APR/customer-cash incentives (which frequently dwarf $1,000), while anyone **stationed/deployed OCONUS** should compare against the **Military AutoSource (MAS)** overseas program, whose tax-free (VAT-free) factory-direct pricing and price protection can beat a stateside deal by far more than $1,000.

### FAQ

#### Does Audi offer a military discount?
Yes. The **Audi Military Select Program** gives eligible military members a **$1,000 bonus** toward the purchase or lease of a select new, unused 2025/2026 Audi, verified through ID.me. It's bonus cash, not a percentage off.

#### How much is the Audi military discount?
**$1,000**, applied to your purchase or lease. It cannot be taken as cash.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — veterans, retirees, National Guard/Reserve, military spouses, immediate family in the same household, and surviving spouses all qualify, along with active duty, NOAA Corps, and U.S. Public Health Service.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **ID.me** on Audi's Military Select page. After verification you get a 6-character authorization code to present at the dealer.

#### Does Audi use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
Audi uses **ID.me**. GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, and VerifyPass are not used for this program, and none of them sell cars.

#### Can I use it in stores / online?
It's redeemed at a **participating U.S. Audi dealership** — you verify online via ID.me, then bring the code to the dealer to complete a purchase or lease.

#### What is excluded?
**R and RS models and the MY26 Q3** are excluded (MY25 Q3 is eligible). The bonus isn't redeemable for cash and can't be combined with the Dealer Employee, Innovator, Corporate Sales, or VIP & Diplomat programs.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Audi's terms only bar four named programs (employee/corporate/VIP). The $1,000 is not listed as incompatible with public lease/APR/customer-cash offers, so in practice it layers on top — but confirm the specific stack with your dealer, since an individual advertised offer can carry its own "not combinable" clause.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy an Audi?
**Stationed overseas?** Price **Military AutoSource** first — tax-free factory-direct pricing often beats a stateside deal by well over $1,000. **Stateside?** Verify on ID.me, take the **$1,000 bonus**, and **stack it on the best public incentive** for that model and month. The military bonus alone is rarely the biggest saving.

#### Does Audi offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not under this program — the Audi Military Select bonus is military-eligibility-based. ID.me runs separate discount programs for other groups, but Audi's vehicle offer here is for the military community.

#### Does Audi run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No separate Audi *military-specific* holiday promo is published — the $1,000 military bonus runs year-round on a rolling annual cycle. Save more by timing your purchase to public seasonal/year-end incentives, which aren't military-gated.

#### Can I buy an Audi at the Exchange or overseas?
Not through AAFES/NEX (they don't sell new cars), but yes overseas through **Military AutoSource**, the factory-authorized Audi distributor for U.S. personnel serving abroad, with tax-free factory-direct pricing.
