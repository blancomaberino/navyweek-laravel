## Headline finding

Virgin Voyages runs a real, official, published **Military & First Responders Rate: 5%, 10%, or 15% off the retail voyage fare** (level varies by sailing and demand), redeemed with the Access Key **SERVICERATE** at checkout and verified by **showing a physical military/first-responder ID at terminal check-in** — no ID.me or SheerID step. The rare and valuable part: **it's explicitly combinable with select public promotions** (70% Off 2nd Sailor, Free Balcony Upgrade, promotional Sailor Loot onboard credit), so the best path is to stack SERVICERATE **on top of** a big public sale, not to choose between them. Archetype: **D (combinable discount — stacking is the strategy)**, secondary **I (travel: level varies by sailing; reduced-fare programs are the either/or wall)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **SERVICERATE stacks with select public promos — confirmed in writing on both sides.** The military offer's own terms say it's "combinable with select public promotional offers," and the 70% Off 2nd Sailor, Free Balcony Upgrade, and June Sailor Loot terms each name the "Military and First Responders Rates Offer" in their combinable lists (virginvoyages.com/promotional-terms-and-conditions, accessed July 7, 2026). Combinability lists change per promo — check the live promo's T&C each time.
2. **The wall is reduced-fare programs:** Voyage Vacation Program, Casino Player Program, First Mate Rates, Interline Rates, any reduced-fare Access Key (including Lock It In GTY rates), Tour Operators, and Incentive/Contracted/Groups programs are all ineligible — either/or, compute both totals.
3. **Cabin gate:** the military rate applies only to balcony-and-below categories (Sea Terrace variants, Sea View, Social Insider, Insider). Suites never get it.
4. **Fare tiers:** Base, Essential, and Premium fare bookings are eligible (as of October 7, 2025 per the terms).
5. **No cashback layer exists** — Virgin Voyages isn't on Rakuten/TopCashback (checked July 7, 2026), so there's no portal to void and no reason to avoid the code.
6. **Onboard-value layers stack independently of fare promos:** Refer a Sailor $150 bar tab (new sailors, non-Lock-It-In cabins) and promotional Sailor Loot OBC ride on top of the fare math.
7. **Port fees/taxes are never discounted** on any path.

### FAQ
#### Does Virgin Voyages offer a military discount?
Yes — an official Military & First Responders Rate of 5%, 10%, or 15% off the retail voyage fare on select sailings, for US and Canadian military (active, veterans, spouses, surviving spouses) and first responders. Terms updated June 11, 2026.

#### How much is the Virgin Voyages military discount?
5%, 10%, or 15% off the voyage fare. The level is set per sailing "dependent on various market factors" — the rate page groups current voyages into 5%, 10%, and 15% lists.

#### What is the Virgin Voyages military code?
SERVICERATE. It's an Access Key you enter at checkout (or your travel agent adds manually). It is not applied automatically, and no other "military code" exists.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans, spouses, and surviving spouses all qualify in their own right. Dependents beyond spouses are not mentioned in the terms.

#### How do I verify my status — does Virgin Voyages use ID.me or SheerID?
Neither. There's no online verification: you book with the code, then bring your physical military or first-responder ID (plus passport) to the terminal at check-in, where proof may be requested. The "Virgin Voyages" store on ID.me Shop is actually MemberDeals, a third-party ticket/travel seller — not Virgin's verification.

#### Can I combine it with sales or promo codes?
Yes — this is the standout. The rate is combinable with select public promos, and Virgin's own terms for "70% Off 2nd Sailor," the Free Balcony Upgrade, and Sailor Loot OBC offers each list the military rate as combinable. It won't combine with casino, interline, travel-agent, or reduced-fare (Lock It In) rates, tour operators, or group programs.

#### What cabins are excluded?
Suites (RockStar and Mega RockStar Quarters) and reduced-rate Lock It In categories. Eligible: Sea Terrace variants, Sea View, Social Insider, Insider.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Virgin Voyages?
Book direct during a public sale window and add SERVICERATE on top. On a $2,000 two-person fare in the current 70%-off-2nd-sailor window, the stack lands around $1,105 (≈45% off) versus $1,700 with the military rate alone — plus any onboard-credit promo running.

#### Does Virgin Voyages offer first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discounts?
First responders (firefighters, EMTs, paramedics, police) get the same 5–15% rate. There is no teacher, nurse, government, or student rate on the Virgin Voyages site.

#### Does Virgin Voyages run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
The military rate itself is year-round. Virgin runs public seasonal sales (Memorial Day, July 4, Black Friday "$99 or 80% off 2nd sailor") — historically these are the windows to stack the military rate into, not separate military-only events.
