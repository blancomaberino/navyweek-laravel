# Zenni Optical — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **~$50 prescription order** (e.g., a mid-tier frame plus standard single-vision lenses) on zennioptical.com.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Zenni Optical offers a 10% military discount on all purchases, verified through ID.me.** Active military, veterans, reservists, and military spouses click the **Military** ID button at checkout and verify via **ID.me** to take 10% off. Zenni runs an identical **10% first-responder / medical / nurse** program (police, firefighters, EMTs, nurses, other medical personnel) through the same ID.me flow. The catch that decides the whole page: the 10% **cannot be combined with any other code or offer and overrides all other discounts** — and Zenni's baseline prices are already rock-bottom (frames from **$6.95**), so on a deeply-discounted or clearance order the public sale price frequently beats "10% off full price," and you must choose one. Zenni uses **ID.me only** — **not GovX** — for status verification. Archetype: **ultra-budget DTC eyewear with a real but non-stacking single-provider discount**, where the smartest move is often Zenni's own low prices rather than the 10%.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **The 10% never stacks.** Zenni states applying the military discount **overrides all other available discounts** and **cannot be combined** with other promo codes or offers — this is the make-or-break fact.
2. **Sale vs. 10% is an either/or.** Because the 10% overrides sale pricing, on a deep sale the public price can beat the discount; compare before verifying.
3. **Gift cards are excluded** from the discount.
4. **ID.me is the sole gate** — one verified status (Military *or* Responder *or* Medical *or* Nurse) per order; there's no double-status stack.
5. **Cashback via the ID.me Shop extension** is a separate mechanic (not a Zenni code); confirm it tracks on your specific order rather than assuming it stacks.
