# Schlitterbahn — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one single-day adult admission to **Schlitterbahn New Braunfels, TX**, measured against every discount path.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Schlitterbahn offers a real military discount, but the two Texas parks now run different programs under different operators.** **Schlitterbahn New Braunfels** (operated by **Six Flags**, on sixflags.com) sells **discounted military/veteran/first-responder tickets starting at ~$35**, gated by **ID.me**, **online only**, for the member **plus up to 6 friends/family**, not combinable with other sales — plus **free parking**. **Schlitterbahn Galveston** (operated separately via **enchantedparks.com**) offers **10% off the online ticket price at the gate**, verified by showing a **military ID / DD-214 / veteran-designated license / work badge at the ticket booth** — a smaller, in-person discount. Archetype: **regional waterpark with a genuine ID.me-gated online military ticket** (New Braunfels) alongside a **thinner in-person 10% counter discount** (Galveston) — the discount is legitimate and usually the best price, but watch for public seasonal sales that the military rate can't be combined with.

5. **Season passes are separate math** — for repeat visits, a pass usually beats repeat single-day military tickets.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — both parks.
- **Retired military** — both parks.
- **Veterans** — both parks (Galveston accepts DD-214 / veteran-designated license).
- **First responders** — both parks.
- **Immediate family** — covered with the member; New Braunfels allows up to 6 tickets total.

### How To Redeem — New Braunfels (online, ID.me)
1. Go to **sixflags.com/schlitterbahnnewbraunfels/military-discounts**.
2. Select the military/veteran/first-responder ticket(s) — up to **6**.
3. Complete the **ID.me** login/verification prompt.
4. Buy online (from ~$35 each) and bring your confirmation; **free parking** included.

### How To Redeem — Galveston (at the booth)
1. Go to the **ticket booth** at Schlitterbahn Galveston.
2. Present a **non-expired Military ID, DD-214, veteran-designated driver's license, or work ID/badge**.
3. Receive **10% off the online ticket price** for you and immediate family.

### How Verification Works
**New Braunfels** verifies through **ID.me** at online checkout — you register/log in with ID.me and it confirms your status before you can buy the discounted ticket. **Galveston** verifies **in person** at the ticket booth by inspecting a military ID, DD-214, veteran-designated license, or work badge. Providers and terms can change; confirm on the official pages.

### Exclusions And Fine Print

- **New Braunfels is online-only** (ID.me); **Galveston is booth-only** (in person).
- New Braunfels covers **up to 6 tickets**; Galveston covers **immediate family** presenting with the member.
- The two parks are **separate operators** with **separate programs** — don't assume one park's terms apply to the other.

### FAQ

#### Does Schlitterbahn offer a military discount?
Yes. **New Braunfels** sells discounted military/veteran/first-responder tickets (from ~$35, online via ID.me, up to 6 guests, free parking). **Galveston** gives **10% off** the online price at the gate with ID.

#### How much is the Schlitterbahn military discount?
New Braunfels tickets start around **$35**; Galveston is **10% off** the online ticket price. Neither combines with public sales.

#### Do veterans and first responders qualify?
Yes — both parks cover active/retired military, veterans, and first responders, plus immediate family.

#### How do I verify my status?
New Braunfels uses **ID.me** at online checkout. Galveston checks a **military ID, DD-214, veteran-designated license, or work badge** at the ticket booth.

#### Does Schlitterbahn use ID.me or GovX?
**New Braunfels uses ID.me.** Galveston verifies **in person** at the booth (no ID.me). Schlitterbahn tickets also appear on **GovX** for some venues — compare if you're a member.

#### Can I use the discount online or at the gate?
New Braunfels is **online only**; Galveston is **at the ticket booth**.

#### Can I combine it with a promo code or sale?
No — the military discount is **not combinable** with other offers or sales at either park. Compare and pick the cheaper.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit Schlitterbahn?
At New Braunfels, buy the **~$35 ID.me military ticket** online (with free parking) unless a deeper public sale is live. At Galveston, take **10% at the booth** unless a public online advance sale already beats it.
