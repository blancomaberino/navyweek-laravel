# 7 Brew Coffee — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one medium 7 Brew drink (~$5.50, in-store/drive-thru only — 7 Brew has no e-commerce cart).

## Headline finding

7 Brew Coffee has **no standing, year-round military or veteran discount**. What 7 Brew runs is an **annual Veterans Day (Nov 11) in-store promotion whose terms change every year** — official 7 Brew accounts posted **25% off** in 2024 and **50% off any drink for veterans** in 2025 (and a "100% off small" free-drink offer in an earlier year). It is redeemed in person at the drive-thru with a **valid military/veteran ID**, is not advertised as available online, and is not tied to any third-party verifier (no ID.me / GovX / SheerID / WeSalute partnership was found). The single best everyday savings path is 7 Brew's own free **Rewards** program (rewards.7brew.com / the 7 Brew app), not a military discount. Archetype: **discretionary once-a-year Veterans Day promo**, decided per year and often per-location — never promise a specific future percentage.
