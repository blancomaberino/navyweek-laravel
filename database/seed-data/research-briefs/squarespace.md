# Squarespace — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** first-year cost of one new **annual** Squarespace website plan (call it **P**; all savings expressed as % of P because the discounts are percentage-based and the plan tier the reader picks sets the dollar amount).

## Headline finding

**Squarespace does not offer a military or veteran discount.** There is no military/veteran checkout, no ID.me / SheerID / GovX / WeSalute / VerifyPass military verification, and no military language anywhere in Squarespace's official student-discount, coupons, or pricing pages (verified July 18, 2026). The widely-scraped "Squarespace Military Discount — 50% OFF" headlines on coupon sites are a **conflation of Squarespace's student discount** (a real, separate 50%-off program verified through Student Beans) and are unverified as a military offer. Brand archetype: **B — no military route at all** (no first-party military discount and no GovX marketplace storefront, because Squarespace is a SaaS subscription, not a resold product). The genuinely best path for a service member depends on one question: **are you a currently-enrolled full-time college student?** If yes, the **50% student discount** (annual plans, via Student Beans) is by far the biggest saving. If no, the best available deal is a **public promo code** — a first-purchase **10% code (GIMME10)** or the recurring **~20%-off** limited-time code — applied to an **annual** plan (annual billing itself is already ~28–36% cheaper than monthly).

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **No military discount exists, so nothing "stacks" onto one.** Do not trust any scraped "military code."
2. **Squarespace codes do not combine with each other.** Student, GIMME10, and the 20% seasonal code are **mutually exclusive** — the student terms say the discount "cannot be combined with other promo codes," and the /coupons terms say each code cannot be used "in combination with other offers." Pick the single largest one you qualify for.
3. **Discounts hit the first payment only.** Student and public codes apply to the **first** payment; the site **renews at full price**. There is no permanent military/veteran rate to protect.
4. **Annual-only for the big discounts.** The student 50% and the code discounts are for **annual** website plans, not monthly, Domains-only, Acuity, Mobile Start, or reactivated sites.
5. **Cashback portals are not a reliable Squarespace path** — Squarespace subscriptions are not a dependable Rakuten/TopCashback category, and code-gated checkouts typically void portal tracking anyway; treat any listed rate as unverified and time-variant.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty / Veterans / Retirees / Reserve / Guard / Spouses / Dependents / Surviving spouses (as military)** — **Not eligible** for any military discount; none exists.
- **Currently-enrolled full-time college student** (which many service members and veterans are) — **Eligible** for the 50% student discount via Student Beans (annual plans; not alumni, faculty, staff, or high-school students).
- **First responders / medical / teachers / government** — **Not stated** in official sources; no dedicated program found.

### How To Redeem Online (student discount — the closest real path)
1. Start a Squarespace trial site at squarespace.com.
2. Register and verify your student status at Student Beans.
3. Copy the single-use offer code (or use the direct link) from the Student Beans portal.
4. Choose an **annual** website plan and paste the code into the **Promo code** field at checkout.

### How To Redeem Online (public code — if not a student)
1. Check squarespace.com/coupons for the current live code (GIMME10 for 10%, or a seasonal ~20% code).
2. Select an **annual** website plan.
3. Enter the code in the **Promo code** field before paying. It applies to the first payment only.

### How Verification Works
There is **no military verification** at Squarespace. For the student discount, verification is handled by **Student Beans** (account required); Squarespace issues a single-use code good for one purchase within 12 months. No re-verification maintains a discount, because the discount only applies to the first payment and the plan renews at full price.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No military/veteran offer of any kind.
- Student and public codes apply to the **first payment only**; sites **renew at full price**.
- **Annual plans only** for the student 50% and code discounts; excludes Squarespace Domains, Acuity Scheduling, monthly plans, Mobile Start, and reactivated sites.
- Codes **cannot be combined** with each other or other offers; not valid on previous purchases, upgrades, or In-App Pay.
- Seasonal codes are time-boxed (enter by the stated expiration).

### FAQ

#### Does Squarespace offer a military discount?
No. As of July 18, 2026, Squarespace publishes no military or veteran discount and uses no military verification partner (ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, VerifyPass).

#### How much is the Squarespace military discount?
There isn't one. The "50% off" military claims on coupon sites are a mislabeled version of Squarespace's **student** discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
Not as military. They can qualify for the **student** discount if currently enrolled full-time at an eligible college, or use the public promo codes anyone can use.

#### How do I verify my military status with Squarespace?
You can't — there is no military verification. The only identity-verified discount is the **student** offer through Student Beans.

#### Does Squarespace use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
Not for a military discount. Squarespace uses **Student Beans** for its student discount. (GovX does not resell Squarespace; forum mentions are merchants adding GovX verification to their own Squarespace stores.)

#### Can I use a discount in stores or online?
Squarespace is online-only (a SaaS subscription). All discounts apply at squarespace.com checkout, on annual plans, in the Promo-code field.

#### What is excluded?
Monthly plans, Squarespace Domains, Acuity Scheduling, Mobile Start, reactivated sites, renewals, upgrades, previous purchases, and In-App Pay. Codes don't combine.

#### Can I combine a military or student code with other promo codes?
No. Squarespace codes are mutually exclusive — use the single largest one you qualify for.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Squarespace?
If you're an enrolled full-time student: the **Student Beans 50%** off the first annual year. Otherwise: the largest live public code (**~20%** seasonal, else **GIMME10 10%**) on an **annual** plan.

#### Does Squarespace offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Only a **student** discount (50%, via Student Beans, annual plans) is documented. First responder, teacher, nurse, and government programs are **not stated** in official sources.

#### Does Squarespace run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No military holiday sale is on record. Squarespace runs generic seasonal first-purchase codes not tied to military holidays.
