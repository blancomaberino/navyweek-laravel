# Pirates Voyage Dinner & Show — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 2 adult + 1 child (age 3–9) standard-seating tickets to the Myrtle Beach show — roughly $150 before tax (exact price varies by show date/time and is only shown inside the booking flow).

## Headline finding

Pirates Voyage **does** offer an official military rate, confirmed on the brand's own FAQ: *"Yes! We are proud to offer a special military rate as a thank-you for your service."* The catch is the mechanic — **it cannot be booked online**; you must **call the box office of your location** and (per third-party listings and standard box-office practice) present a valid military ID. The brand does **not publish the dollar amount** of the discount anywhere on its own site. Third-party destination sites report roughly **$10 off each adult ticket and ~$4 off each child ticket**, plus a **$5-off first-responder adult** rate at the Pigeon Forge location — but none of those figures is confirmed by Pirates Voyage itself, so treat them as directional, not fact. This is the classic **ticketed-attraction / dinner-show archetype**: a real but phone-gated discount, no ID.me/SheerID/GovX verification, and a SERP flooded with third-party resellers (Tripster, Groupon, Westgate, Ticketing for Less) whose "deals" generally cannot be stacked with the military rate. For a service member, the single best move is a phone call to the box office; the military rate almost always beats the small ($2-off-type) public coupons floating around.

### How To Redeem
1. Choose your location and show date/time.
2. Call that location's box office directly (numbers above) — the military rate is **not** available on the website or in the online booking flow.
3. Ask for the current military rate and confirm eligibility (active/veteran/first responder) and how many discounted tickets you can buy.
4. Bring your valid military ID to will-call / the box office.

### How Verification Works
There is no online verification partner. Eligibility is checked the old-fashioned way — a person at the box office reviews your military ID when you book by phone and/or pick up tickets. Nothing about the rate is tied to an ID.me or SheerID account, so there's no re-verification cycle; you simply show ID each time.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Military rate **cannot be booked online** (brand FAQ).
- The brand publishes **no exact discount amount**; reported figures vary by source and should be confirmed by phone.
- Child tickets cover ages up to 9; children 2 and under are free on an adult's lap sharing the meal (a child ticket is required if you need a high chair).

### FAQ

#### Does Pirates Voyage offer a military discount?
Yes. The brand's FAQ confirms a "special military rate as a thank-you for your service." It must be booked by phone with the box office, not online.

#### How much is the Pirates Voyage military discount?
Pirates Voyage does not publish the amount. Third-party destination sites report roughly $10 off each adult ticket and about $4 off each child ticket, but these figures vary by source and location — confirm the current rate when you call.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The brand says "military" without defining the groups. Veteran, spouse, and dependent eligibility isn't stated on the site, so call the box office to confirm before you go.

#### How do I verify my status?
There's no online verification. You book by phone and present a valid military ID at the box office / will-call.

#### Does Pirates Voyage use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. The military rate is a manual box-office discount with no third-party verification provider.

#### Can I use the military discount online?
No. The FAQ states the military rate cannot be booked online — you must call the location directly.

#### What's excluded?
There's no published amount or code, the rate can't be combined with online coupons or reseller deals, and pricing varies by location, show time, and seating tier.

#### Can I combine it with a coupon or reseller deal?
No. Because the rate is phone-only, it can't be stacked with any online code or third-party reseller (Tripster, Groupon, Westgate) package — you pick one path.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy?
Call the box office and ask for the military rate on standard seating at an off-peak show. That's typically cheaper than any public coupon.

#### Does Pirates Voyage offer a first responder discount?
A $5-off adult first-responder rate is reported at the Pigeon Forge location by a destination-marketing site, but it's not confirmed on the brand's own pages. Confirm by phone.

#### Does Pirates Voyage run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No special military-holiday promotion is documented; the year-round phone military rate is the mechanism.
