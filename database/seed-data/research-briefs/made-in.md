# Made In (Made In Cookware) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **~$150 order** (a single skillet or a small starter set), the smallest order that clears Made In's typical free-shipping / welcome-offer thresholds.

## Headline finding

**Made In Cookware does not publish an official military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** There is **no GovX, ID.me, or SheerID military program** on madeincookware.com — its "Discount Eligible" collection is a generic **sale** landing page, and its only *named* verified-pricing programs are business/trade (Industry/Restaurant Pricing, Corporate Gifting, Design Trade), not military. Aggregators claim a "standing military discount — reach out to customer service," but that is **unverified** and not stated anywhere on Made In's own site; treat it as false until Made In publishes it. So the honest answer is: **no military discount exists.** The genuine best-savings paths for a service member are Made In's **New Customer Welcome Offer** (email signup for a first-order discount), its frequent **seasonal / "The Seconds" (factory-second) sales** (which run much deeper than any typical military %), and **retailer substitutes** — notably the **military Exchange (AAFES/NEX) and GovX-style cookware alternatives** if a comparable brand carries a real military deal. Archetype: **premium DTC brand with no military program**, where public sales and factory-seconds beat what a military discount would have been anyway.
