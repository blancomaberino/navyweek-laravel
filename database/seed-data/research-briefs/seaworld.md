# SeaWorld — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **family of 4** (service member/veteran + 3 guests), **one-day SeaWorld Orlando admission**. Representative online single-day price **~$110/ticket = $440 pre-tax** (SeaWorld Orlando gate price is **$170.10 each, tax & fees included**; online dynamic pricing runs roughly **$65–$110**).

## Headline finding

SeaWorld's **Waves of Honor** program (run by parent **United Parks & Resorts**, formerly SeaWorld Entertainment) is one of the strongest military offers in the theme-park industry, and it is real and first-party — verified through **ID.me** (a.k.a. "Troop ID") on the park's own site, not an aggregator. **Active-duty U.S. military, activated/drilling reservists, and National Guard get one free single-day admission per year for themselves plus up to three direct dependents** — so a serving member's family of 4 can visit **free** (effective **$0** on the $440 baseline), valid through **December 31, 2026**. Active members can also buy **up to 6 additional tickets at 50% off**. **Veterans and retirees** don't get year-round free admission, but SeaWorld runs an **annual "Military Appreciation Month" (May) free-admission window** for veterans + up to 3 guests (in 2026: register **through May 10**, visit **by June 30** — that national window has closed as of today; **SeaWorld San Diego's** veteran free-admission runs **through July 31, 2026**). Year-round, veterans/retirees get **50% off single-day tickets (up to 6)** and a **Military Silver Annual Pass at $161** (reg. $279, SeaWorld Orlando). Verification is one-time via ID.me; **you must show a valid U.S. military ID / proof of service at the gate.** Archetype: **E (multi-tier identity discount via ID.me — the identity offer *is* the savings path; no coupon/cashback stacking game)**, with a **seasonal veteran-window** element (F flavor). There is no meaningful GovX/cashback/sale-stack angle here — the free/deep first-party offer dominates.

## Stacking Rules (verified, first-party terms)
1. **Each Waves of Honor offer is a standalone entitlement — it does not "stack" with anything.** There is no coupon code, no cashback portal, and no public sale to layer; the free/discounted ticket *is* the price.
2. **Free active-duty admission and the 50%-off guest tickets are separate buckets**, not combinable into "more free" tickets — the comp covers the member + up to 3 dependents; extra people are 50%-off guest tickets (member must be present).
3. **The Military Silver Pass is either/or vs. single-day tickets** — you buy the pass *or* day tickets, not both toward one visit. The pass includes 3 free guest tickets separately.
4. **Veteran free-admission window and the 50%-off/Silver Pass are alternatives**, not a stack — during MAM the veteran + 3 guests are free; outside it, use 50% off or the pass.
5. **Discovery Cove and Aquatica are excluded from the year-round free comp** (Discovery Cove has its own separate discounted military rate).
6. **No third-party marketplace beats this.** GovX/cashback/discount-gift-card plays are irrelevant vs. a free or 50%-off first-party ticket; do not manufacture a "stack."

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Free admission (self + up to 3 dependents), once/year; 50% off up to 6 guest tickets; Silver Pass.
- **Activated or drilling reservists & National Guard** — Same as active duty for the free comp.
- **Direct dependents** — Covered by the active-duty free comp (up to 3); dependents can even visit **without** the service member once status is ID.me-verified.
- **Veterans & retirees** — Free admission during the **May Military Appreciation Month** window (self + up to 3 guests); year-round 50% off (up to 6) and the $161 Military Silver Pass.
- **Military spouses** — Access veteran-tier offers by logging in under the veteran's ID.me account.
- **DoD civilians** — **Not eligible** for the year-round active-duty free comp.
- **Non-U.S. military** — **Not eligible**; Waves of Honor is U.S. military only.

### How To Redeem — Active Duty (online)
1. Go to the SeaWorld **Waves of Honor / military-discount** page for your park (seaworld.com).
2. Click to **log in or create a free ID.me account** and verify your active-duty status.
3. Select your **one free admission** plus up to **3 direct-dependent** tickets (dependents can print their own).
4. (Optional) Add up to **6 guest tickets at 50% off** — you must be present with guests.
5. Print/download tickets; **bring a valid U.S. military ID to the front gate.** Redeem by **December 31, 2026**.

### How To Redeem — Veterans & Retirees (online)
1. During the **May Military Appreciation Month window**, register via **ID.me** for **free** admission for yourself + up to 3 guests (watch the annual register-by / visit-by dates; San Diego's veteran window runs through **July 31, 2026**).
2. Outside the free window, buy **50% off single-day tickets (up to 6)** or a **Military Silver Annual Pass ($161)** — both online only via ID.me, not sold at the park.
3. Bring a **valid military ID / proof of service** to the gate; the veteran must be present with guests.

### How Verification Works
SeaWorld uses **ID.me** (its military product is branded "Troop ID") to confirm you're a member of the military community — active duty, veteran, retiree, spouse, or family member. You create a **free ID.me account once**, verify your status, and then access the offers on seaworld.com. Verification is online; **a physical U.S. military ID or proof of service is still required at the park gate.** SeaWorld/United Parks can re-verify eligibility and can change or end any offer at any time. Base **MWR/ITT** ticket offices are an alternate distribution channel.

- Year-round free comp is **active-duty/reservist/Guard only** — **retirees and DoD civilians don't get the free comp** (retirees use veteran-tier offers).

- Silver Pass and veteran offers are **online only — "not available for purchase at the park."**
- 50%-off guest tickets and veteran guest tickets require the **service member/veteran to be present**; excludes separately-ticketed events (e.g., Howl-O-Scream).
- **U.S. military only.** Offers expire **December 31, 2026** (comp/discounted single-day); dates and prices vary by park.

### FAQ

#### Does SeaWorld offer a military discount?
Yes — one of the best in the industry. Through **Waves of Honor**, **active-duty U.S. military, activated/drilling reservists, and National Guard get one free admission per year for themselves plus up to three direct dependents**, verified through **ID.me**. Veterans get a free May window plus year-round discounts.

#### How much is the SeaWorld military discount?
For active duty it's **free admission** (self + up to 3 dependents) once a year, plus **50% off up to 6 guest tickets**. Veterans get **free admission during the May Military Appreciation Month window**, and year-round **50% off single-day tickets** or a **$161 Military Silver Annual Pass** (regularly $279).

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes, in different ways. **Dependents** are covered by the active-duty free comp (up to 3). **Veterans and retirees** get the **May free-admission window**, 50% off tickets, and the Silver Pass. **Spouses** log in under the veteran's ID.me account. **DoD civilians do not** get the free active-duty comp.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a free **ID.me** account (branded "Troop ID" for military), verify once on the SeaWorld military page, then **show a valid U.S. military ID or proof of service at the gate**.

#### Does SeaWorld use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
SeaWorld's pages use **ID.me / Troop ID**. Some third-party summaries mention GovX at certain parks — follow whichever provider the specific park's page shows, but ID.me is what the official SeaWorld pages cite.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or only online?
Register and buy **online** via ID.me — the Silver Pass and veteran offers are "not available for purchase at the park." Base **MWR/ITT** offices also distribute tickets. You still show your **military ID at the gate**.

#### What is excluded?
The year-round free comp is **active-duty only** (not retirees/DoD civilians) and covers SeaWorld/Busch Gardens/Sesame Place — **Discovery Cove and Aquatica are excluded** from the free comp. The veteran free admission is **seasonal (May)**, online only, and excludes separately-ticketed events.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale prices?
No — each Waves of Honor offer is **standalone**. There's no code, cashback, or public sale to stack; the free or 50%-off ticket already *is* the deal.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit SeaWorld?
**Active duty:** take the **free** admission for you + 3 dependents — a family of four visits for **$0**. **Veterans:** aim for the **May free window**; otherwise 50% off single-day, or the **$161 unlimited pass** if you'll visit twice or more.

#### Does SeaWorld run a Veterans Day sale?
SeaWorld's veteran **free-admission** event is tied to **Military Appreciation Month in May**, not Veterans Day. We found no documented November Veterans Day freebie — plan around the May window.
