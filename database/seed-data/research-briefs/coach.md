# Coach Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Coach (the handbag and apparel brand at coach.com, owned by Tapestry, Inc.) does not offer an official, published military or veteran discount.** Coach's own promotions page lists only seasonal sales (up to 50% off select styles), the free Coach Insider rewards program, and email/SMS sign-up offers — no military, veteran, or first-responder program. ID.me, the largest military-verification provider, **explicitly states it is "not aware of COACH offering Military community discounts."** The savings paths that do exist for service members are indirect: WeSalute and GOVX list Coach as a partner offering **cash back or third-party deals** (not a coach.com checkout discount), and individual Coach Outlet retail stores occasionally run short-term in-store military offers (e.g., a Tanger Outlets "extra 10% off with military ID" promo) that are store-specific and not a standing coach.com program. The single most important caveat: **any "20% Coach military discount" figure on coupon/aggregator sites is unverified and not supported by any primary Coach source** — treat it as false until Coach publishes otherwise.

> Accuracy note: Aggregators (MilitaryDiscountcode, WorthEPenny, Knoji) advertise a "20% Coach military discount." No Coach-owned page, ID.me, SheerID, GOVX, or WeSalute confirms a 20% coach.com military discount as of June 23, 2026. The page should state plainly that there is **no official Coach military discount** and route service members to the legitimate alternatives below.
