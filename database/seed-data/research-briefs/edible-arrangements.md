## Headline finding

**Edible Arrangements does NOT offer a military or veteran discount — and this is confirmed on Edible's own website, not just inferred.** Edible's Special Offers FAQ states plainly: *"No, Edible Arrangements® does not offer a military discount at this time."* Its own blog echoes it for veterans: *"No, Edible Arrangements® does not currently offer a veteran discount."* There is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass military gate, and the old `edible.com/military-discount` URL now dead-ends on a 404 error page. So any coupon site advertising a "30% off Edible Arrangements military code" is fabricated.

The honest best path for a service member is the same as for everyone: the **currently live public code `BLOOM10` (10% off)** stacked on nothing, plus a cashback portal on top where it tracks (**Rakuten shows 2% cash back as of July 18, 2026**), and **Edible Rewards** loyalty for repeat gifting (free dipped-fruit box every third order + a birthday freebie). If a military discount is a must-have on fresh-fruit gifts, a **competitor substitute wins**: sister-category brands **FruitBouquets.com and 1-800-Flowers do publish military offers** (via WeSalute / promo codes). Archetype: **no-military-discount gifting/food brand** — the page's value is debunking the fake codes and routing readers to the real savings. One nuance to watch: Edible stores are independently owned franchises, so a local store *could* honor an in-store courtesy, but nothing corporate or published backs it — treat as YMMV only.
