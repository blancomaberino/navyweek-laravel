# Audible — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 12 months of **Audible Premium Plus** (1 credit/month), the standard credit membership — list price $14.95/month = **$179.40/year**.

## Headline finding

**Audible has no military or veteran discount.** There is no active-duty, veteran, retiree, Guard/Reserve, spouse, or dependent price on any Audible plan, and Audible runs **no military offer through ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute.** Audible's own membership page lists only standard plans plus a Prime-member promo — no military tier — and Military.com's Audible listing states outright that "Audible may not offer a military discount at this time." The "10%," "25%," and "30% military discount" figures scattered across coupon and military-info sites are **fabricated or copied from another brand**: the real ID.me military offer those pages cite belongs to **Audiobooks.com** (a separate competitor, id.me store #9777/#808), *not* Audible. This is a myth-brand pattern (archetype H) layered on a no-military-route brand (archetype B). The genuine best paths for a service member are the same ones open to everyone: the **Prime-member offer (3 months free + $20 Audible credit)**, the **annual Premium Plus plan (~17% cheaper than paying monthly)**, and **free library audiobooks (Libby/Hoopla, including base/MWR libraries)**.

1. **No military discount exists, so there is nothing military to stack.** Any page telling you to "stack an Audible military code with Prime" is describing a code that does not exist.
