# United Airlines Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

United Airlines does offer airfare discounts to the military — and, unusually, it runs **two separate 5% discounts** plus a set of policy perks. (1) United's **own Active-Duty Military Leisure Discount** gives **active-duty service members 5% off** select United/United Express-operated flights, verified inside the member's **MileagePlus profile** (no third-party code) — active duty **only**, U.S. Army/Navy/Air Force/Marines/Space Force/Coast Guard. (2) The separate **WeSalute+ discount** gives **5% off united.com fares** to a much broader group — active duty, retirees, veterans, Guard/Reserve and immediate family — but requires a **paid WeSalute+ membership** and a personal **Travel Code**. On top of the fare discounts, United extends **policy perks** worth real money to active military: **up to 5 free checked bags** "depending on your type of travel," **pre-boarding** with the priority group, **United Club access when space is available**, and a **military pet program** for PCS moves between **Honolulu (HNL) and Guam (GUM)**. A separate **United Vacations** offer gives active-duty and veteran personnel **up to $150 off** the hotel portion of flight-and-hotel packages by email request. The single most important caveat: **veterans and family members do not get United's own 5% leisure discount** — that benefit is **active-duty only**; veterans' path to a fare discount is the **paid WeSalute+ membership**.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator pages collapse United's offers into one "5% military discount via Veterans Advantage/WeSalute." United's own page shows the **5% leisure discount is a United-run, active-duty-only benefit verified in MileagePlus** — distinct from the **WeSalute+ 5%** (paid membership, broader eligibility). "Veterans Advantage" is the **former name** of WeSalute and no longer appears on United's page. Verified June 23, 2026.

### Intro
United Airlines actually gives the military a fare discount — two of them, in fact. If you're **active duty**, you can get **5% off select United flights** by verifying your status right inside your free **MileagePlus** profile; there's no third-party code and no membership fee. If you're a **veteran, retiree, Guard/Reserve member, or immediate family**, your route to **5% off united.com fares** is a paid **WeSalute+** membership, which gives you a personal Travel Code to enter at booking.

Beyond the fare discounts, active military travelers get **policy perks** worth real money: **up to five free checked bags** (depending on your type of travel), **pre-boarding** with United's priority group, **United Club access when space allows**, and help moving pets on PCS orders between **Honolulu and Guam**. There's also a separate **United Vacations** offer — **up to $150 off** the hotel portion of a flight-and-hotel package — for active-duty and veteran personnel.

This is an independent guide to help you use these benefits. We're not affiliated with United Airlines, and United controls the terms and can change them at any time. Always confirm details on United's official military benefits page before you book.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active duty** — Eligible for **everything**: United's own 5% leisure discount, the WeSalute+ 5% (if a paid member), free checked bags, pre-boarding, United Club access, and the pet program.
- **Veterans** — **Not** eligible for United's own 5% leisure discount (active-duty only). Eligible for the **WeSalute+ 5%** with a paid membership and for the **United Vacations** package discount.
- **Retirees** — Same as veterans: not eligible for the United-run 5% leisure discount; eligible via WeSalute+.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible for United's airport benefits (bags/boarding/lounge) when **on active orders**; eligible for the WeSalute+ 5% as members. United's own 5% leisure discount is framed as active-duty only.
- **Military spouses / immediate family** — Eligible for the **WeSalute+ 5%** (WeSalute lists immediate family — mother, father, sister, brother, son, daughter); United Vacations covers traveling companions on the same reservation. United's own page does not separately state spouse/dependent eligibility for the 5% leisure discount.
- **Dependents** — Covered under the free-checked-bag benefit per WeSalute's partner page (up to 3 free bags, 70 lbs each, personal travel); **not stated numerically on United's own page**.
- **Service academy cadets / midshipmen; USPHS & NOAA Commissioned Officer Corps** — Listed as eligible for United benefits.
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not stated in official source.
- **First responders / medical workers / teachers / government employees / students** — Not listed among United's military benefits.

### How To Redeem Online (Active-Duty 5% Leisure Discount)
1. Enroll in **MileagePlus** (United's free loyalty program) if you don't already have an account.
2. **Verify your active-duty status in your MileagePlus profile.**
3. Search flights on **united.com** or the United app while signed in; eligible flights are **marked with a discount applied**.
4. The active-duty MileagePlus account holder must be on the itinerary; you can book up to **8 tickets per itinerary**.

### How To Redeem Online (WeSalute+ 5%)
1. Enroll in **WeSalute+** (paid membership; verifies military/veteran status and issues a WeSalute+ ID Card).
2. Find your personal **Travel Code** on your WeSalute+ ID Card or in your WeSalute+ account.
3. Book on **united.com** or the United app and enter your **Travel Code**; the 5% applies to the published base fare on eligible fare classes (Economy through United First; Basic Economy "N" excluded).
4. Optionally enroll in MileagePlus to **earn miles** on the discounted fare (the two are combinable).

### How To Redeem In Store / At the Airport (Bags, Boarding, Lounge)
1. At **check-in**, present your **government-issued military ID**; if traveling **on orders**, also show your **official travel orders**.
2. The agent applies your free-bag allowance — **up to five checked bags depending on your type of travel** (per United; partner sources state 5 on orders / 3 personal incl. dependents, 70 lbs each).
3. At the gate, board with United's **pre-boarding group**.
4. At a **United Club**, present your ID for entry **when space is available** (per United Club access rules).

### How Verification Works
For United's **own 5% leisure discount**, there is **no third-party verifier and no code** — you **verify active-duty status inside your free MileagePlus profile**, and eligible flights then show the discount applied at search. United's page does not name ID.me or SheerID for this. For the **WeSalute+ 5%**, verification is handled by **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage) through a **paid membership**; you receive a WeSalute+ ID Card with a personal **Travel Code** to enter at booking, and that membership re-bills per WeSalute's own terms. For the **airport perks** (bags, boarding, lounge), you simply show your **military ID** (and **travel orders** if on orders) at the counter and gate — no online verification.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Basic Economy** ("N" class) is **not eligible** for either 5% discount; these fares carry heavy restrictions (no changes/refunds, no seat selection, one personal item only).
- Discounts apply to the **published base fare** and **do not** cover government-imposed taxes/fees or carrier-imposed international surcharges.
- **Not combinable** with other travel discount certificates, discounted fares, award (MileagePlus) flights, coupons, special promotional fares, or United Vacations discounts; applied **at time of booking only**, not retroactively.
- Flights must be **fully operated by United / United Express** for the discount to apply.
- **Active-duty 5%:** flight must **originate in the U.S., Guam, Puerto Rico, U.S. Virgin Islands or Canada**; the active-duty MileagePlus holder must be on the itinerary; up to 8 tickets/itinerary.
- **WeSalute+ 5%:** valid from U.S./Canada to the 50 states, Canada, Mexico, Central America, Caribbean and Europe; **excludes travel booked from U.S. territories** and is **not valid for travel to or originating in Guam**; up to 8 travelers per itinerary; the **limited-time code** issued by Jan 31, 2026 expires for booking after **June 30, 2026**.
- **United Vacations:** up to **$150 per reservation** (3% off the **hotel portion** only); requires **minimum $1,000 spend and a 3-night stay**, air-and-hotel packages to international or Hawaii destinations; **not** combinable with a price match or another promo code; not valid on multi-stop bookings.
- Free-bag **numeric split** (5 on orders vs. 3 personal incl. dependents, 70 lbs each) is from WeSalute's partner page and military.com; **United's own page states only "up to five … depending on your type of travel"** and links out to its checked-bags page. *(Flag: confirm the exact count for your travel type at check-in.)*

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Active duty: use United's free MileagePlus verification first** — it's a real 5% with no membership fee, so there's no reason to pay for WeSalute+ unless you want the broader cross-brand benefits.
- **Veterans/family: weigh the WeSalute+ membership cost** against how often you fly — 5% off base fare can pay for itself, and the same membership unlocks other travel/retail discounts.
- **Stack miles** — both 5% discounts are combinable with **earning MileagePlus miles**, so enroll in MileagePlus regardless.
- **Travel on orders for the biggest bag savings** — the on-orders allowance (up to 5 bags) beats personal-travel limits; keep orders handy at check-in.
- **United Vacations for trips with hotels** — active-duty/veteran personnel can take up to $150 off the hotel portion and still earn MileagePlus bonus miles.
- **Competitor comparison:** WeSalute also lists **American Airlines Vacations (10% land portion)**, **JetBlue**, **Avis/Budget (up to 25%)** and others — useful internal-link targets when comparing military travel savings.

### FAQ

#### Does United Airlines offer a military discount?
Yes. United offers active-duty members **5% off** select flights (verified free in your MileagePlus profile), and a separate **5% off** to a broader group — veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve and family — through a paid **WeSalute+** membership. Active military also get free checked bags, pre-boarding and United Club access.

#### How much is the United Airlines military discount?
**5%** off the published base fare in both programs. United's own discount is **active-duty only**; the WeSalute+ version covers veterans and family but requires a paid membership. Neither discount applies to taxes, fees, surcharges, or Basic Economy fares.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve and immediate family qualify for the **WeSalute+ 5%** (paid membership) and the **United Vacations** package discount. United's **own** 5% leisure discount is framed as **active-duty only**. Dependents are covered under the free-checked-bag benefit per WeSalute's partner page.

#### How do I verify my military status?
For United's own 5%, **verify active-duty status in your MileagePlus profile** — no code needed. For the WeSalute+ 5%, your status is verified by **WeSalute** when you join, and you book with a personal **Travel Code**. For bags, boarding and the lounge, just show your **military ID** (and **orders** if applicable) at the airport.

#### Does United use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
United's own active-duty 5% is verified **inside MileagePlus** — United's page does not name ID.me or SheerID. The broader military fare discount uses **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage), which requires a paid membership.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / at the airport?
The 5% discounts are **online** (united.com / United app). The **airport benefits** — free checked bags, pre-boarding, and United Club access when space allows — are redeemed in person with your military ID (and travel orders if on orders).

#### How many free checked bags do military members get?
United's official page says **"up to five checked bags, depending on your type of travel."** Partner sources (WeSalute, military.com) describe this as **up to 5 bags when traveling on orders** and **up to 3 bags (including dependents) on personal travel**, each up to **70 lbs**. Confirm your exact allowance at check-in.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, sale fares, or award flights?
No. Neither 5% discount is combinable with other discount certificates, discounted/promotional fares, coupons, MileagePlus award flights, or United Vacations discounts. You **can** still earn MileagePlus miles on the discounted fare.

#### Does United Airlines offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, government, or student discount?
United's military benefits page lists only **military** programs (with academy cadets, USPHS/NOAA officers, and Reserves/Guard on orders included). First responder, nurse, teacher, government and student discounts are **not listed** among United's offers.
