# SeatGeek Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

SeatGeek does offer a military discount, but it is unusual: there is no public code, no fixed published percentage, and no third-party verification provider. Per SeatGeek's own Help Center, eligible **active-duty service members and U.S. military veterans** may qualify for a discount on their **first SeatGeek purchase** by requesting it through SeatGeek's **support chat** (type "Military discount") and following the instructions to verify eligibility. SeatGeek does **not** state the discount amount, does **not** list spouses/dependents/reserve/Guard as eligible, and notes the discount is **one-time use**, **may apply only to eligible events and ticket listings**, and that **verification may be required**. The single most important caveat: any specific percentage you see online (commonly "5% off, up to $20," or "up to 20% via ID.me") comes only from coupon aggregators and is **not confirmed by SeatGeek** — ID.me's own SeatGeek page explicitly states it is not aware of a SeatGeek military discount. Treat the amount as unpublished.
