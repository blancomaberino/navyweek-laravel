# Dave & Buster's — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$60 dine-in food & non-alcoholic-beverage tab** (a typical shared table for a small group; game play and alcohol are billed separately and are **not** eligible for the food discount).

## Headline finding

Dave & Buster's has a **real, brand-published year-round military discount: 15% off food and non-alcoholic beverages** with a **valid Military/First Responder ID**, stated on Dave & Buster's own "Specials" page. It covers the ID holder **plus up to 4 guests**, at **participating locations**. There is **no verification app** — you show a physical ID to the server in the restaurant; Dave & Buster's does **not** use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. That's the honest everyday answer.

Two important limits shape the real math. First, the discount is **food-and-non-alcoholic-drinks only** — it **never touches game play** (the Power Cards / chips), gift cards, or alcohol, and it is **not combinable** with other offers or special-event packages. Since game play is usually the biggest line on a D&B visit, the best *total* savings usually come from stacking your food discount with the **public game-play specials** you'd use anyway — **half-price games every Sunday & Wednesday**, online Power Card deals, and the free **D&B Rewards** program (bonus game chips) — not from the 15% alone. Second, the **richest single event is the annual Veterans Day (Nov 11) offer**: historically a **free entrée (up to ~$20) plus a free $10 Power Card** — worth far more than the everyday 15%, but it runs one day a year and is a promo the brand re-declares each fall.

Archetype: a **restaurant/entertainment venue in-store ID discount** — closest to **H (holiday promo + free loyalty wallet)** with a **G flavor** (year-round in-store discount, no online/verification-partner path). It does not map cleanly to the retail archetypes; there is **no GovX, ID.me, cashback-portal, exchange, refurb, or pro-deal path** here. Do **not** repeat the aggregator claims of "**20% off food AND game play**," a **Monday free $10 Power Card**, or military **promo codes** (VETS15, POWER10, EATDRINKPLAY20, etc.) — none of those is confirmed by Dave & Buster's.

## Stacking Rules (verified against Dave & Buster's terms)
1. **The 15% is a single, non-combinable food discount** — Dave & Buster's states it is "**not valid with any other offers or Special Events packages**." You get one food offer per check; if a public special (e.g. $1 wings) is cheaper for your order, use that instead.
2. **The 15% applies only to food + non-alcoholic beverages** — it **never** discounts **game play, Power Cards/chips, gift cards, or alcohol**, and excludes tax and gratuity.
3. **Game-play specials are a separate category** — **half-price games (Sun/Wed)** and online Power Card bonus deals apply to game play, which the food discount doesn't cover, so you can benefit from both in one visit (the food-discount non-combinable clause governs the *food* portion). Confirm at your location, since terms are set per participating store.
4. **D&B Rewards is free and additive** — joining and earning bonus game chips is not an "offer" that conflicts with the ID discount; it layers on normal spend. Member-only *food* promos, however, are "other offers" and won't combine with the 15% — pick the lower.
5. **No online code stacks** — there is no military promo code; anyone advertising one (VETS15, POWER10, etc.) is fabricating it.
6. **Participating locations only** — franchised/participating stores can vary the terms; verify with your specific D&B.
