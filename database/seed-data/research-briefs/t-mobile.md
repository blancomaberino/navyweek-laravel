# T-Mobile Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

T-Mobile offers **dedicated military "Savings" versions of its current Experience plans** to verified military members, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, and Gold Star families. On the official plans page (June 23, 2026), the rates are **Experience More w/ Military Savings at $160/mo for 4 lines ($40/line)** and **Experience Beyond w/ Military Savings at $220/mo for 4 lines ($55/line)** — prices that exclude AutoPay and other discounts. T-Mobile markets these as saving military families **$720/year on 4 lines vs. regular Experience plans**. Eligibility is verified through **T-Mobile's own in-house verification** (via the T-Life app, T-Mobile.com, in store, or by phone) — **not** ID.me, SheerID, or GovX. The single most important caveat: **you must verify the account holder's military status within 45 days of activation or the plan reverts to a non-discount Magenta plan at up to +$20/line per month**, and the military member generally must be the **primary account holder**. Spouses/immediate family **without** Gold Star status are explicitly **not** independently eligible.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator and even older news pages still advertise stale T-Mobile military terms — "$25/line for 4 lines," "40% off Go5G," or the "Go5G Military / Magenta Military" plan names. As of June 23, 2026 the official plans page sells **Experience-tier** plans (Experience More / Experience Beyond) "w/ Military Savings," not Go5G. T-Mobile's own **verification support page still uses the legacy "Magenta Military" plan name** in its signup instructions — a known inconsistency between T-Mobile's plans page and its support page; treat the **plans page pricing as authoritative** and flag the Magenta/Go5G/$25 figures as not currently confirmed on the plans page.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (all branches: Air Force, Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard).
- **Veterans** — Eligible (all branches; documentation such as DD214 required).
- **Retirees** — Eligible (all branches).
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible (Air/Army/Navy/Marine reserves and Air/Army National Guard).
- **Gold Star families** — Eligible.
- **Military spouses / dependents (non–Gold Star)** — **Not** independently eligible per T-Mobile. (A deployed active-duty member's spouse/family may *verify on the member's behalf* with extra documentation, but the military member must still be on the account.)
- **Surviving spouses without Gold Star status** — Not eligible.
- **Civilian military / DoD employees & contractors** — Explicitly not eligible.
- **Cadets / recruits without documentation** — Not eligible.
- **First responders** — Eligible for a *separate* First Responder plan (not this military offer).
- **Teachers / Students / Government employees** — Not listed for the military offer.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Visit T-Mobile's military plans page and choose **Experience More w/ Military Savings** or **Experience Beyond w/ Military Savings**.
2. Select your number of lines (online checkout caps the number of lines/devices per Military transaction).
3. Complete activation as the **primary account holder** (military member), with a government-issued ID ready.
4. Within **45 days**, log in to T-Mobile.com, choose **Profile > Military Verification**, select your status, submit the form (and documentation if prompted).
5. You'll get a text confirmation within ~48 hours, or an email within ~24 hours after documents are reviewed.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit a T-Mobile store (or call **1-800-T-MOBILE**) to activate a Military plan as the primary account holder.
2. Bring a government-issued ID (State ID, Driver's License, or US Passport).
3. Staff can verify your status on the spot in many cases; bring military documentation (e.g., DD214, LES, Orders) in case it's needed.
4. Existing customers: contact T-Mobile to switch the line to the Military plan, then verify within 45 days.

### How Verification Works
T-Mobile runs verification **in-house** — there is no third-party provider like ID.me on the military plan. You verify through the **T-Life app** (Manage/Account > Settings gear > Profile > Military Verification) or on **T-Mobile.com** (Profile > Military Verification), or in store/by phone. If your credentials can't be auto-verified, T-Mobile asks you to upload documentation appropriate to your status (Active Duty, Reservist, Veteran, Retiree, or Gold Star). The military member must be the **primary account holder** (deployed active-duty exception noted above), and a name can only be used once for validation. You have **45 days from activation** to complete verification or the plan reverts to Magenta at up to **+$20/line/month**. Submitted documents are used solely for verification and destroyed afterward.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **45-day verification deadline:** Verify the account holder's military status within 45 days or the plan becomes Magenta at up to **+$20/line per month**; the account must maintain a valid military line.
- **Primary account holder rule:** The military member must be the primary account holder (deployed active-duty exception with extra documentation).
- **Not independently eligible:** Spouses/immediate family without Gold Star status; civilian military/DoD employees and contractors; undocumented cadets/recruits.

Monthly Regulatory Programs & Telco Recovery Fee (~$4.49/voice line) plus federal/local surcharges and taxes apply; one-time $35/line device connection charge. *(Exact AutoPay discount amount is rendered dynamically — confirm at checkout.)*

### FAQ

#### Does T-Mobile offer a military discount?
Yes. T-Mobile offers military "Savings" versions of its Experience plans to verified active duty, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, and Gold Star families — for example, Experience More w/ Military Savings at $160/mo for 4 lines as of June 2026.

#### How much is the T-Mobile military discount?
On the official plans page (June 23, 2026): Experience More w/ Military Savings is **$160/mo for 4 lines ($40/line)** and Experience Beyond w/ Military Savings is **$220/mo for 4 lines ($55/line)**, before AutoPay and taxes/fees. T-Mobile markets this as about **$720/year** in savings on 4 lines. (Older "$25/line" and "40% off Go5G" figures circulating online are out of date.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify (with documentation like a DD214). **Spouses and dependents without Gold Star status do not independently qualify** — the military member must be the primary account holder. Gold Star family members do qualify.

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify in the T-Life app (Profile > Military Verification), on T-Mobile.com (Profile > Military Verification), in store, or by phone. You have **45 days from activation**, and may need to upload documentation. Confirmation typically arrives within 24–48 hours.

#### Does T-Mobile use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. T-Mobile verifies military status **in-house** through its own app/website and stores — not through ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Activate a Military plan at a T-Mobile store (or via 1-800-T-MOBILE) with a government-issued ID; staff can often verify on the spot.

#### What is excluded / what's the catch?
You must verify the account holder's military status within **45 days** or the plan reverts to Magenta at up to **+$20/line per month**. The military member must be the primary account holder. Spouses/family without Gold Star status, civilian/DoD employees, contractors, and undocumented cadets/recruits are not eligible. Listed prices exclude AutoPay, taxes, and fees.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other discounts?
The Military Savings discount is built into the plan price; AutoPay is applied separately. T-Mobile doesn't state it stacks with unrelated discounts, and you choose one savings track (Military, First Responder, or 55+) per account. Device/trade-in promos are separate.

#### Does T-Mobile offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
T-Mobile offers a **separate First Responder** plan (Experience plans w/ First Responder Savings, plus Essentials First Responder). It does **not** publish teacher, nurse, government-employee, or student plans on the military page.
