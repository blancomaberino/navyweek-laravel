# LEGO Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Scope: This brief covers the **LEGO toy brand** — the official LEGO Shop (shop.lego.com / lego.com/en-us) and LEGO Brand Retail Stores in the United States. It does **not** cover **LEGOLAND Parks**, **LEGOLAND Discovery Centers / LEGO Discovery Centers**, or LEGO Certified Stores, which are run by Merlin Entertainments and have their own, separate military ticket discounts. Aggregator and search results constantly conflate the two — keep them strictly separate.

## Executive Summary

**LEGO (the toy brand) does not offer a dedicated military or veteran discount** on its official Shop or in LEGO Brand Retail Stores, as of June 23, 2026. The verification provider ID.me states plainly on its LEGO page that it is "not aware of LEGO offering Military community discounts" — and the same applies to first responders, nurses, teachers, and government per ID.me's other LEGO category pages. LEGO's own Offers & Sale page describes its savings as the free **LEGO Insiders** rewards program, free standard shipping on orders over $35, and rotating themed promotions (free gifts, bonus points) — none of which are military-gated. LEGO does run a verified-community offer ecosystem through **Beans iD / Student Beans** (linked from LEGO.com's footer as "Student & exclusive offers"), and that platform exposes a "military" verification tab; however, the offers it surfaces appear to be LEGO's general promotions (e.g., free gift over a spend threshold, free shipping over $35) rather than a military-specific percentage off. **No verified, military-specific discount percentage exists from a primary source.** Several coupon aggregators claim a flat % off (e.g., 20%) or "show your military ID at the register" — none of this is confirmed by LEGO or by a verification provider, and it directly contradicts ID.me; treat all such claims as **unverified / Low confidence**.

> Accuracy note: Aggregators (military-discount.com, worthepenny, grabon, militarymarkdown, beansid.com) variously state LEGO gives "20% off," a discount to "active duty, reserve, retired, spouses and dependent children," or that you can "show a valid military ID at the cash register." **None of these claims are supported by any LEGO official page or by a verification provider.** The only primary-source statements available are (a) ID.me: no military community discount, and (b) LEGO.com: savings via Insiders, free shipping, and themed promos. Do not publish any military percentage, eligibility list, or in-store register mechanic for LEGO as fact.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no military discount to redeem online. To save at LEGO.com without military status:
1. Create a free **LEGO Insiders** account and log in before checkout to earn points and have any Insiders price applied automatically.
2. Make sure your merchandise subtotal is over **$35** to get free standard shipping (contiguous U.S.).
3. Watch LEGO's **Offers & Sale** page for themed promotions (free gifts, bonus points) and apply any LEGO Insiders promo code in the "Promo Code / Discount Code" box at checkout.
4. If you're a verified community member (e.g., student), use LEGO's "Student & exclusive offers" link (footer) to verify through Beans iD and unlock any applicable code.

### How To Redeem In Store
There is no military discount to redeem in a LEGO Brand Retail Store. To save in store:
1. Show proof of a valid **LEGO Insiders** membership at the till so any Insiders price/points apply.
2. Ask staff about current in-store promotions and free-gift-with-purchase events.
3. Note: ignore third-party claims that you can "show a military ID at the register" for a LEGO discount — this is **not** confirmed by LEGO and there is no published military offer.
