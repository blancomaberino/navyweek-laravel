# Georgia Aquarium Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Georgia Aquarium (Atlanta, GA) runs several distinct military programs rather than a single flat ticket discount. Two are concretely confirmed by primary sources: (1) **20% off Individual and Family Memberships** for all current and former U.S. Armed Forces members, and (2) **Military Appreciation Days** — select dates each year offering **free admission to current and former U.S. service members plus 30% off general admission for accompanying family members**. The Aquarium also states that "military discounts are available daily" and that military guests can buy discounted admission online or in person with a valid Military ID (and via local ITT/MWR base ticket offices), but it does **not publish a specific everyday-ticket percentage**. Verification is by valid Military ID at the Aquarium or online via **ID.me**. The most important caveat: the discount cannot be combined with other discounts/promotions, all guests must be on the same order, and the headline "free admission / 30% family" applies only on designated Military Appreciation Days, not every day.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the Georgia Aquarium Military Appreciation Days page or the Military Membership page on georgiaaquarium.org.
2. Verify your government-issued Military ID through ID.me (creating an ID.me account takes about 5–10 minutes and can be reused for future visits).
3. Once verified, the military pricing is applied to your order on georgiaaquarium.org or the Georgia Aquarium mobile app.
4. Add all members of your party to the same order (required to receive the discount), then check out.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Bring your valid Military ID to Georgia Aquarium (225 Baker Street NW, Atlanta, GA).
2. Present the Military ID at the ticket window (or, for a Military Membership, when you arrive to redeem it).
3. Ensure everyone in your party is on the same order to receive the discount.
4. Alternatively, purchase discounted admission in advance through your local base ITT (Information, Tickets & Travel) or MWR (Morale, Welfare and Recreation) office — contact them for availability and current offers.

### How Verification Works
Georgia Aquarium verifies eligibility two ways: in person with a valid government-issued Military ID, or online through **ID.me**, a third-party verification platform. The Aquarium notes that setting up an ID.me account takes about 5–10 minutes and the account can be reused for future Aquarium visits and at other businesses that accept ID.me. For Military Memberships, you will still be asked to show your Military ID when you arrive to redeem. The official source does not state how often ID.me re-verification is required for this offer; treat ID.me's own account/re-verification rules as the authority. For verification problems, ID.me provides its own support, and the Aquarium's call center is (404) 581-4000.

### FAQ

#### Does Georgia Aquarium offer a military discount?
Yes. Georgia Aquarium offers 20% off select Memberships for current and former U.S. Armed Forces members, free admission for service members plus 30% off family general admission on Military Appreciation Days, and states that discounted military admission is available daily (online or in person with a Military ID).

#### How much is the Georgia Aquarium military discount?
Memberships are 20% off (Individual or Family). On Military Appreciation Days, service-member admission is free and accompanying family get 30% off general admission. For everyday single-day tickets, the Aquarium confirms a discount is available but does not publish a specific percentage on its site.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify — the offer covers "current and former members of the United States Armed Forces." Family members qualify for the 30% accompanying-family rate on Military Appreciation Days. The official source does not separately spell out spouse/dependent eligibility outside that family rate.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Present a valid government-issued Military ID at the Aquarium, or verify online through ID.me before you buy. ID.me setup takes about 5–10 minutes and can be reused.

#### Does Georgia Aquarium use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
The Aquarium's official Military Appreciation Days page names **ID.me** as its online verification platform. Georgia Aquarium also appears on GovX, but the Aquarium's own pages direct online verification to ID.me; treat GovX listings as secondary.

#### Can I use the discount in person at the Aquarium?
Yes. Bring a valid Military ID to the ticket window. You can also buy discounted admission in advance through your local base ITT/MWR office.

#### What is excluded?
The discount is not valid with other discounts, promotions, or offers, and everyone in your party must be on the same order. Military Appreciation Days pricing applies only on select dates. Memberships are limited to 2 Individual or 1 Family.

#### Can I combine it with other promo codes or sale offers?
No. Official terms state it is "not valid with other discounts, promotions, or offers."

#### When are Georgia Aquarium's Military Appreciation Days?
They fall on select service-member-focused dates each year. The dates published for 2026 were May 16 and May 25, 2026 (both now past as of June 23, 2026). Check the official Military Appreciation Days page for newly announced dates.

#### Does Georgia Aquarium offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not stated in the official source for those groups. The published programs target current and former U.S. Armed Forces members (and Gold Star/wounded-warrior initiatives via Military Salute).
