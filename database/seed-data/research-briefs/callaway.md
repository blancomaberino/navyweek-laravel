# Callaway Golf Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Callaway Golf offers a **15% military discount** on the entire order at CallawayGolf.com (gift cards excluded). It is available to **active-duty, veteran, reserve, and retired** members of the U.S. military, verified through **ID.me / TroopID** on the Secure Checkout page. There is **no promo code** — once ID.me verification succeeds, the 15% is applied automatically. The single most important caveat: the discount **cannot be combined with other promotional codes**, "some exclusions may apply," and there is an **annual limit of $1,000 per person**. The offer is documented as an online/checkout discount on CallawayGolf.com; Callaway's official pages do not state an in-store discount. Note: Callaway also runs a **separate, parallel 15% First Responder discount** (also via ID.me) on its own dedicated page.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add eligible Callaway items to your cart at CallawayGolf.com (gift cards do not qualify).
2. Proceed to the Secure Checkout page.
3. When prompted, verify your military eligibility with ID.me / TroopID (log in or create an ID.me account and complete verification).
4. Once your credential is verified, the 15% discount is applied to your order automatically — no promo code needed.

### How To Redeem In Store
Callaway's official military discount pages describe an online checkout discount only and do not state an in-store military discount. Not stated in official source. If you want to shop in person, confirm directly with the retail location before relying on a discount.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me** (Callaway also refers to it as "ID.me/TroopID") on the Secure Checkout page. You log into or create an ID.me account and verify your military status; once the credential is verified, the 15% is applied immediately. Callaway's pages do not state how often ID.me re-verification is required or whether it persists across visits — ID.me generally manages credential status on its side, so re-verification frequency is governed by ID.me, not stated by Callaway. For verification problems, ID.me support is the path for credential issues; Callaway customer service (1-877-723-5218) handles order/discount-application questions.

### FAQ

#### Does Callaway Golf offer a military discount?
Yes. Callaway offers 15% off your entire order at CallawayGolf.com (gift cards excluded) to active-duty, veteran, reserve, and retired U.S. military members, verified through ID.me at checkout. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))

#### How much is the Callaway military discount?
15% off your entire order at CallawayGolf.com, excluding gift cards, with an annual limit of $1,000 in discounts per person. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify, along with active-duty, reserve, and retired members. Callaway's page names only those four groups; it does not list military spouses or dependents, so eligibility for them is not stated in the official source. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))

#### How do I verify my military status?
On the Secure Checkout page, verify your eligibility with ID.me / TroopID. Once your credential is verified, the 15% is applied to your order automatically. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))

#### Does Callaway use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Callaway's official program uses **ID.me** for verification at checkout. ID.me independently lists Callaway's 15% military offer. (GovX and WeSalute list Callaway on their marketplaces, but Callaway's own pages direct verification to ID.me.) ([Callaway](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program); [ID.me Shop](https://shop.id.me/stores/4325-callaway-golf))

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Callaway's official pages describe an online checkout discount on CallawayGolf.com and do not state an in-store military discount. If shopping in person, confirm with the retailer first. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards are excluded, the discount can't be combined with other promo codes, "some exclusions may apply" (specific SKUs aren't listed), and total discounts are capped at $1,000 per person per year. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military discount cannot be combined with other promotional codes. Callaway does not state whether it applies on top of sale-priced items beyond the no-other-codes rule. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))

#### Does Callaway offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Callaway runs a separate 15% First Responder discount (via ID.me). Teacher, nurse, government, and student discounts are not stated on Callaway's official site. ([First Responder program](https://www.callawaygolf.com/first-responder-discount-program))

#### Is there a limit on how much I can save?
Yes — the annual discount limit is $1,000 per person. ([Source](https://www.callawaygolf.com/military-discount-program))
