# HP Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

HP (HP Inc., hp.com) runs an active military and veteran discount through its **HP Frontline Heroes Store**, an affinity store gated by **ID.me** verification. HP's own page advertises "discounts up to 40% everyday savings" plus free shipping; HP's ID.me Shop listing separately advertises "Save up to 60% off plus free shipping for Military." Eligibility per HP's official terms is open to "military and veterans and their families, first responders, nurses, doctors, medical providers, and hospital staff, who reside in the United States" — a valid ID.me account with one of those affiliations is required. The discount is **online only at hp.com** (not in physical stores or third-party sellers), cannot be combined with other offers unless explicitly stated, and is subject to per-calendar-year purchase caps (2 PCs, 2 tablets, 10 accessories, 2 printers, 12 supplies). Single most important caveat: the headline savings are an "up to" range that varies by product and over time — there is no fixed flat percentage applied to every item, and ink/toner have been excluded from program discounts since March 1, 2023.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the HP Frontline Heroes Store at hp.com/us-en/shop/cv/hp-frontline-heroes.
2. Click "Verify with ID.me" and sign in or create an ID.me account; verify your military/eligible-group status.
3. Complete the HP store registration to link your verified status to your HP account.
4. Shop qualifying products on the Frontline Heroes Store; member pricing displays on eligible items.

### How Verification Works
HP uses **ID.me** as its verification partner. You verify your eligibility (e.g., military status) through ID.me once; this requires creating or signing in to an ID.me account. After verifying, you complete HP store registration to associate the status with your hp.com account. HP's page states returning members can skip the ID.me step and simply sign in. ID.me may re-verify status periodically per its own policies (HP's page does not specify a re-verification cadence — do not promise "verify once forever"). For verification problems, HP directs users to ID.me's help center (help.id.me); HP reserves the right to suspend or revoke participation for fraud or rule violations.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Online only:** valid only at hp.com; not in stores or via third-party sellers.
- **Not combinable:** Frontline Heroes offers "may not be combined with other products offers, discounts, coupons or program benefits unless explicitly stated," and are separate from the HP Loyalty/Referral program.
- **Ink & toner excluded** from program discounts effective March 1, 2023.
- **Excluded from qualifying spend:** free gift promotions, sales tax, state fees, shipping/delivery charges, personalized products purchased on store.hp.com, discounts, HP Rewards redemptions, gift card redemptions, and other excluded fees.
- **Purchase caps (per calendar year):** 2 PCs, 2 Tablets, 10 Accessories (incl. CarePacks), 2 Printers, 12 Supplies; exceeding can cause expulsion.
- **No resale:** for end-user customers only; not for businesses/resellers; contract and volume customers not eligible.
- **Returns/cancellations** reimbursed at the discounted dollar value paid, not list price.
- **HP discretion:** HP may modify, suspend, or terminate the program or any user's participation at any time, for any reason or no reason.

### FAQ

#### Does HP offer a military discount?
Yes. HP runs the HP Frontline Heroes Store on hp.com, which offers special pricing for the military community (and healthcare workers and first responders), verified through ID.me. (Source: HP Frontline Heroes Store)

#### How much is the HP military discount?
HP advertises "up to 40% everyday savings" on its Frontline Heroes page; HP's ID.me Shop listing advertises "up to 60% off plus free shipping for Military." These are "up to" ranges — actual savings vary by product and change over time. HP does not publish a single fixed percentage applied to every item. (Sources: HP Frontline Heroes Store; ID.me Shop – HP)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes. HP's terms cover "military and veterans and their families." ID.me's military verification category includes active duty, National Guard, Reserves, veterans, retirees, military spouses, and immediate military family members. (Sources: HP Frontline Heroes Store; ID.me military eligibility)

#### How do I verify my status?
Verify through ID.me from the HP Frontline Heroes Store, then complete HP store registration. ID.me can confirm military status using an existing USAA login or by matching your service information against its databases. (Sources: HP Frontline Heroes Store; ID.me)

#### Does HP use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
HP uses **ID.me** for the Frontline Heroes program. (Source: HP Frontline Heroes Store)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. HP states participation is "valid only at hp.com." There is no in-store or third-party-seller redemption. (Source: HP Frontline Heroes Store)

#### What is excluded?
Ink and toner (since March 1, 2023), gift cards, HP Rewards redemptions, personalized products, plus sales tax, fees, and shipping; offers are not combinable with other HP discounts. Annual purchase caps apply. (Source: HP Frontline Heroes Store)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No, unless HP explicitly says so. Frontline Heroes offers "may not be combined with other products offers, discounts, coupons or program benefits unless explicitly stated." (Source: HP Frontline Heroes Store)

#### Does HP offer a first responder, healthcare, teacher, government, or student discount?
First responders and healthcare workers (nurses, doctors/medical providers, hospital staff) are included in the Frontline Heroes program. Teachers, government employees, and students are not part of Frontline Heroes; HP runs separate education and other affinity programs. (Source: HP Frontline Heroes Store)
