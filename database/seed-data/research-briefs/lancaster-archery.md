## Headline finding

**Yes — Lancaster Archery Supply gives a real 10% military discount** on almost everything at lancasterarchery.com, verified once through **VerifyPass**. Eligible: **active-duty, reservist, veteran, and retired** US military personnel. After a one-time verification, the discount applies automatically to future orders while you're logged into the same email. It's a genuine account-level everyday discount (better than a rebate or a per-order code), but it's **capped by sale/manufacturer rules**: many products are **MAP-restricted or already on sale** and are "exempt from further discounts," and gift cards, donations, return coverage, and labor are excluded. So on hot new-release gear the 10% often can't apply, while on regular-priced accessories it's the cleanest win. Archetype: **specialty pro-retailer with a solid verified everyday discount that's gated by manufacturer MAP pricing.**
