# Disney Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Disney runs an official **Disney Military Salute** program (often called the Disney Armed Forces Salute) offering deeply discounted **theme park tickets** and **resort hotel room rates** at both Walt Disney World (Florida) and Disneyland (California) for **2026**. The headline tickets: at Walt Disney World, a **6-Day Park Hopper for $449 + tax** (and a new **Disney Celebrates America Military Salute Ticket — all-year access for $499 + tax**, marking America's 250th); at Disneyland, a **3-Day Park Hopper for $314** or **4-Day for $369**. Room discounts run **15%–30% off** at WDW (and "great rates" at Disneyland) depending on resort tier and dates. Eligibility is narrow and specific: **active or retired U.S. military** (including National Guard, Reserve, Coast Guard, Space Force, PHS and NOAA Commissioned Corps), **plus spouses who hold a valid military ID** — not both the member and spouse. The single most important caveat: **ordinary (non-retired) veterans are NOT eligible** for the Salute tickets/rooms; eligibility is tied to a valid active/retired U.S. military ID (a DD-214 alone does not qualify). Each eligible member or spouse may buy up to **6 tickets** total, one of which the member/spouse must use, and tickets must be **activated in person with military ID**. Separately, **Disney+** offers **25% off an annual Premium subscription** through The Exchange (ShopMyExchange.com) — a different program with different (broader, including honorably discharged veterans) eligibility.

> Accuracy note: Ticket prices, blockout dates, and room-discount percentages are **set per calendar year and change annually**. All figures below are confirmed from Disney's official 2026 offer pages as of June 23, 2026. Disney can pause, change, or end these offers without notice — re-verify before each annual refresh.

### Intro
Disney offers one of the most valuable military discounts in travel through its **Disney Military Salute** program (also called the Disney Armed Forces Salute). For **2026**, eligible service members can buy specially priced multi-day **theme park tickets** and book **discounted resort hotel rooms** at both Walt Disney World in Florida and Disneyland in California. At Walt Disney World, that means a **6-Day Park Hopper for $449 plus tax** — and, for America's 250th anniversary, a new **all-year Disney Celebrates America Military Salute Ticket for $499 plus tax**. At Disneyland, tickets start at a **3-Day Park Hopper for $314**. Room discounts run **up to 30% off** at Walt Disney World resorts.

Eligibility is specific. The Salute tickets and rooms are for **active or retired U.S. military** — including National Guard, Reserve, Coast Guard, Space Force, and the PHS/NOAA Commissioned Corps — and **spouses who carry a valid military ID** (the member or the spouse, not both). Ordinary, non-retired **veterans do not qualify** for these tickets/rooms based on a DD-214 alone. You buy through a U.S. military base ticket office, Shades of Green Resort, GOVX (Disneyland), or a Disney park ticket window, and you must **activate the ticket in person with your military ID**.

This is an independent guide to help you use the offer. We're not affiliated with Disney, and Disney controls the terms and can change, pause, or end them at any time — including the annual price and date resets. Always confirm current details on Disney's official offer pages before you buy.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (all branches, incl. Space Force).
- **Retirees** — Eligible (retired U.S. military, incl. retired Guard/Reserve).
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible (active or retired members).
- **Coast Guard, Space Force, PHS Commissioned Corps, NOAA Commissioned Corps** — Eligible (named explicitly on Disney's pages).
- **Military spouses** — Eligible if they present a valid and active U.S. military ID. The member OR spouse may purchase — not both. (For rooms, the spouse can use the offer "in the absence of" the member.)
- **Veterans (non-retired, honorably discharged)** — **Not eligible** for Salute tickets/rooms based on a DD-214. *(Note: a **100% disabled veteran with the official 100% DAVPRM ID card** issued by DEERS/RAPIDS holds a qualifying military ID and is generally treated as eligible — flag: stated by reputable specialist sources/planDisney, not enumerated as a separate group in Disney's offer terms. A VHIC, paperwork, or benefits letter is not accepted. Verify before claiming.)*
- **Dependents (other than spouse)** — Not separately listed as purchasers; the eligible member/spouse buys tickets that family and friends may then use within the offer terms.
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not stated in Disney's official 2026 offer terms. *(Flag: third-party sources reference un-remarried widow(er)s with a qualifying ID; confirm with the base ticket office or Shades of Green.)*
- **Disney+ (separate program)** — Broader: active service members, military families, **and honorably discharged veterans** authorized to shop at The Exchange (ShopMyExchange.com) qualify for 25% off annual Disney+ Premium.
- **First responders / nurses / teachers / government employees / students** — Not eligible for a dedicated Disney parks discount (no such public Disney program).

### How To Redeem Online
1. **Disneyland tickets:** Go to **GOVX.com**, verify your military status, and purchase the 2026 Disney Military Promotional Park Hopper ticket; or use ShopMyExchange/base office. (WDW Salute tickets are not sold on the public Disney site — see in-person steps.)
2. **Rooms:** Call Disney to book — **WDW (407) 939-7830**; **Disneyland (866) 309-9737** — and request the 2026 U.S. Military room offer.
3. **Disney+:** Log in to **ShopMyExchange.com**, find the Disney+ Military Exclusive Offer, click **Authenticate Now**, and you're redirected to Disney+ with the 25% annual-Premium discount applied.
4. Make required **theme park reservations** immediately after buying any park ticket — they're limited.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit a participating **U.S. military base ticket office**, the **Shades of Green Resort** ticket office (at WDW), or a **Disney park ticket window**.
2. Present a **valid U.S. military ID** (the eligible member or spouse must purchase).
3. Buy up to **6 Salute tickets** (one must be used by the member/spouse); no tax if bought at a base office or Shades of Green.
4. **Activate** the tickets in person with your military ID at a Disney park ticket window (WDW) or at the Main Entrance turnstile (Disneyland) on first use.
5. Link tickets in the My Disney Experience / Disneyland app and make **park reservations**.

### How Verification Works
There is no online ID.me/SheerID flow for the parks Salute offer. Verification is done **in person with a physical, valid U.S. military ID** at the point of purchase and again at activation/first use. Tickets are sold only through authorized military channels — base ticket offices, Shades of Green Resort, Disney park ticket windows, and (for Disneyland) **GOVX.com**, which verifies military status online. The eligible member or spouse must be the purchaser, and at least one of the tickets must be used by that member or spouse. For **Disney+**, verification runs through **The Exchange (ShopMyExchange.com)**, which authenticates your authorized-shopper status before redirecting you to Disney+. Re-verification: military ID is checked at each purchase and at activation; the Disney+ discount continues on annual renewals while you remain an eligible Exchange account holder.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Non-retired veterans are not eligible** for the Salute ticket/room offers; eligibility is tied to a valid active/retired U.S. military ID, not a DD-214.
- **Park tickets cover entry only** — no parking, and no discounts on dining, merchandise, or separately priced activities/events.
- **All tickets are nontransferable and nonrefundable**; each ticket must be used by the same person on all its days; may not be resold or commercially transferred.
- **Cannot be combined** with other ticket discounts or promotions. (WDW room offer can combine only with the 2026 Kids Dining Plan offer; otherwise rooms can't be combined with other discounts.)
- **Room exclusions (WDW):** Cabins at Copper Creek Villas & Cabins; Bungalows, 1-/2-Bedroom Villas and Penthouses at Disney's Polynesian Villas & Bungalows; Suites at Moderate/Deluxe/Deluxe Villa resorts; Club Level; and 3-Bedroom Grand Villas. **Room exclusions (Disneyland):** select Concierge rooms, Signature/Premium suites, and 2-/3-Bedroom Villas. Valid military ID required at check-in or the non-discounted rate is charged; member/spouse must stay in the room.
- **Blockout dates (WDW tickets):** March 29 – April 11, 2026 and November 22 – 28, 2026. **Park reservations required** and limited; availability not guaranteed.
- **Caps:** 6 Salute tickets max per member/spouse (5 with Annual Pass/Magic Key); WDW Memory Maker limited to 2 per member/spouse; Disneyland rooms limited to 2 per reservation.
- **Disney+:** 25% applies to the **annual Premium (No Ads)** plan only — not monthly plans, not the Disney Bundle; one discount per ShopMyExchange account; subscriber must be 18+.
- **Annual reset:** prices, percentages, blockout dates, and validity windows are set per calendar year — treat all figures as 2026-specific.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Buy at a base ticket office or Shades of Green** — no sales tax is added (tax applies at Disney park ticket windows), and individual base offices may charge **less** than Disney's published prices.
- **Shades of Green Resort** (a DoD-operated Armed Forces Recreation Center at WDW) offers military lodging plus a ticket office — often the best one-stop for WDW military travelers.
- **Disney Celebrates America Military Salute Ticket** — at $499 + tax for all-year WDW access (through Dec 18, 2026), this beats buying multiple multi-day tickets if you'll visit more than ~once.
- **Stack the room + ticket offers** — book a discounted military room and add discounted Salute tickets for the biggest total savings; at WDW you can also add the **2026 Kids Dining Plan** offer.
- **Disney+ 25% via The Exchange** — separate from the parks; an easy year-round saving for eligible Exchange shoppers and veterans.
- **GOVX** — convenient online purchase route for Disneyland military tickets if you can't reach a base office.

### FAQ

#### Does Disney offer a military discount?
Yes. Disney runs the **Disney Military Salute** program with discounted theme park tickets and resort rooms at both Walt Disney World and Disneyland for eligible U.S. service members. Disney+ separately offers 25% off an annual Premium subscription through The Exchange.

#### How much is the Disney military discount?
For 2026, Walt Disney World offers a 6-Day Park Hopper for **$449 + tax** (4-Day $409, 5-Day $429), plus an all-year "Disney Celebrates America" ticket for **$499 + tax**. Disneyland offers a 3-Day Park Hopper for **$314** and 4-Day for **$369**. Room discounts run **15%–30%** at WDW depending on resort and dates.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Spouses** with a valid, active U.S. military ID qualify (the member or spouse — not both). **Retired** military qualify. **Non-retired veterans do not qualify** for the Salute tickets/rooms on a DD-214 alone, though a 100% disabled veteran with the official 100% DAVPRM ID card holds a qualifying ID. The eligible member/spouse buys the tickets, which family and friends may then use within the offer rules.

#### How do I verify my status and where do I buy?
Buy in person with a **valid U.S. military ID** at a U.S. military base ticket office, the **Shades of Green Resort** ticket office, or a Disney park ticket window. **Disneyland** tickets are also available online via **GOVX.com**. Rooms are booked by phone (WDW 407-939-7830; Disneyland 866-309-9737).

#### Does Disney use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For the parks Salute offer, Disney verifies with a **physical military ID** at purchase and activation — not ID.me or SheerID. **GOVX** is an authorized online seller for **Disneyland** military tickets. **Disney+** verifies through **The Exchange (ShopMyExchange.com)**.

#### How many tickets can I buy, and do I have to activate them?
Up to **6 Salute tickets** per eligible member or spouse (5 if you hold an Annual Pass/Magic Key), and **one of those tickets must be used by the member or spouse**. Tickets are **not valid until activated in person with your military ID** at a Disney ticket window (WDW) or at the Main Entrance turnstile (Disneyland).

#### What's excluded?
Tickets cover park entry only — not parking, dining, or merchandise. Tickets are nonrefundable/nontransferable and can't be combined with other discounts. Certain suites, villas, club-level, and specialty rooms are excluded from the room offer. WDW ticket blockout dates are March 29–April 11 and November 22–28, 2026.

#### Does Disney+ have a military discount?
Yes — **25% off an annual Disney+ Premium (No Ads) subscription** via **ShopMyExchange.com** for eligible Exchange shoppers, including active members, military families, and **honorably discharged veterans**. It's not valid on monthly plans or the Disney Bundle and is limited to one discount per Exchange account.

#### Does Disney offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Disney does not publish a dedicated first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount for park tickets or rooms. The military Salute program and the Disney+ Exchange offer are the established official military/veteran channels.
