**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one first box / starter box (fresh-food subscription; first-box price is the discounted lever). Assume a ~$100 first box at full trial price for illustration.

## Headline finding

The Farmer's Dog offers a **genuine, verified military discount: 70% off your first box** (some channels add "**plus a free welcome kit**"), redeemed after verifying military status through **ID.me** or **WeSalute** (a Farmer's Dog verification partner since 2025); the offer is also surfaced via **GovX**. That 70% is **meaningfully deeper than the standard public new-customer offer** (currently shown as ~20% off the first box on the public site, historically up to ~50–60%). Key caveat: it's a **first-box-only** discount on a **subscription** — ongoing boxes bill at regular price, so the real decision is trial economics, not everyday savings.
