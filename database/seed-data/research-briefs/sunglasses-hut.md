## Headline finding

**Sunglass Hut has a real, published 15% military discount**, verified through **ID.me**, and it is genuinely one of the better everyday paths — but it is narrower than aggregators imply. It covers **Active Duty, Retirees, Veterans, Military Spouses, and Military Family Members** (U.S. only), applies **online at sunglasshut.com** at the cart page, and **excludes a long list of premium/luxury brands** (Chanel, Dior, Prada, Cartier, Maui Jim, Persol, Moncler, Meta-branded smart glasses, and more), plus prescription lenses, markdowns, and new launches. Because Sunglass Hut is a **full-price fashion-eyewear retailer that rarely deep-discounts** its core Ray-Ban/Oakley lines, the 15% military discount is often the best available deal on an in-season, non-excluded pair. Archetype: **fashion retailer with a solid mid-tier ID.me military discount and heavy brand-level exclusions** — the exclusions are the story, not the percentage.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The 15% does not stack** with other promo codes, markdowns, new launches, limited editions, or gift-card purchases — brand terms are explicit.
2. **On a multi-item order, the discount applies to the cheapest eligible item**, not the whole cart — a material limit for bundles.
3. **Excluded brands never qualify**, so the 15% can't be "recovered" by trying at the register — the exclusion is brand-level, not channel-level.
4. **Cashback + military code is unreliable** — ID.me-gated checkouts frequently break portal tracking; treat any Rakuten/TopCashback stack as YMMV and never assume it holds.
5. **Prescription sunglasses are excluded** — the discount is on the sunglasses, not the Rx lens work.
