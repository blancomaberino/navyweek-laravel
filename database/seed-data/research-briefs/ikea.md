# IKEA Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

IKEA U.S. does **not** offer a standing, everyday, nationwide military or veteran discount. What IKEA runs instead are **store-specific, limited-time** military/veteran promotions — typically **10–15% off in-store purchases**, most often clustered around Veterans Day (November) or run as local store events. The clearest officially-documented current example is IKEA Norfolk's "Capture the Flag" promotion: a **15% military discount, May 1–31, 2026, verified through SheerID**, in-store only, for U.S. military families (a parallel NATO offer gives a gift-card-back deal). Across all of these offers the mechanics are consistent: **in-store only (not valid online)**, **must scan an IKEA Family membership number** (free to join) **and show proof of military/veteran status at the register**, applied before tax/shipping, and excluded from services, gift cards, the IKEA Restaurant/Bistro, and credit-card payments. Veterans, active-duty members, and (at least at Norfolk) **spouses** qualify; eligibility otherwise varies by store and is not centrally published. The single most important caveat: **there is no guaranteed offer at your local IKEA on any given day** — shoppers must check their specific store's events page or the official "Capture the Flag" article, since these promotions are local and time-boxed. IKEA Family membership (free) is the everyday savings path available to everyone, including the military community.

### How To Redeem Online
Military/veteran IKEA discounts in the U.S. are **in-store only**. The store PDFs state plainly: "Not valid online." There is no online redemption path for the military discount.
- Exception: IKEA Norfolk's "Capture the Flag" promotion allows **remote planning and purchase managed by the store's RCMP team** by phone (+1-410-657-1267), with orders placed up to 120 days in advance — but this is a store-managed sales process, not standard ikea.com checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Confirm your local store currently has a military/veteran offer — check that store's events page on IKEA-USA.com or the official "Capture the Flag" article.
2. Join IKEA Family (free) if you haven't, and have your membership number ready.
3. For SheerID-verified offers (e.g., Norfolk), complete SheerID military verification per the store's instructions before/at purchase.
4. Shop in store. At checkout, have the cashier **scan your IKEA Family member number** and the offer.
5. **Show proof of eligibility** — military or veteran ID, or other proof of status (spouses: show proof of the service member's eligibility where the offer allows).
6. The discount is applied **before tax, shipping, and handling**, proportionally across eligible items (shown on your receipt).

### How Verification Works
For the IKEA Norfolk "Capture the Flag" offer, verification is handled through **SheerID**. For typical in-store store-event offers, there is no third-party portal — you simply **present a valid military/veteran ID (or proof of status) at the register** along with your scanned IKEA Family number. An **IKEA Family membership is required** to receive the discount (it's free to join). No source reviewed indicates IKEA uses ID.me or GovX for these offers. Because each promotion is store- and date-specific, re-verification is effectively **per offer / per purchase** — there is no persistent "verified once" military status across IKEA. If verification or the discount fails, contact the specific store (Norfolk uses a dedicated RCMP phone line) or IKEA customer service.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
Summarized from official IKEA store-offer terms (Renton and Norfolk PDFs):
- **In-store only** — not valid online or on previous purchases.
- Must **scan IKEA Family member number** and show proof of eligibility.
- **Not valid on services**: delivery, installation, assembly, Click & Collect, kitchen planning, or other services.
- **Not valid on** IKEA Gift Cards, payment of an IKEA credit card, the IKEA Kitchen Event offer, food offers, or in the IKEA Restaurant, Bistro, or Swedish Food Market.
- Discount **applied before tax, shipping, and handling**; applied proportionally across items.
- On returns, **only the net (discounted) purchase price** shown on the receipt is refunded.
- "No cash value… Void if altered, copied, transferred, sold or prohibited. Other restrictions may apply. See store for details."
- **Stacking with sales/promo codes**: Not stated in official source — terms exclude specific event offers (e.g., Kitchen Event) but do not comprehensively address stacking; treat as store discretion.
- **Caps**: Not uniformly stated; some offers reference a "one-time purchase." Confirm per offer.

### FAQ

#### Does IKEA offer a military discount?
Not as a standing, nationwide program. IKEA U.S. runs **store-specific, limited-time** military and veteran offers (commonly 10–15% off in store), such as IKEA Norfolk's "Capture the Flag" 15% offer running May 1–31, 2026. Check your local store's events page for current availability.

#### How much is the IKEA military discount?
When offered, it has typically been **10% to 15% off in-store purchases**, depending on the store and promotion. There is no single published nationwide rate because there is no nationwide program.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans and active-duty members qualify for the documented offers. **Spouses** qualify at IKEA Norfolk by showing proof of the service member's eligibility. Other dependents, retirees, and Reserve/Guard are **not stated** in IKEA's published offer terms — confirm with the specific store.

#### How do I verify my status?
For the Norfolk offer, verification is via **SheerID**. For most in-store store-event offers, you **show a valid military or veteran ID (or proof of status) at the register** and scan your IKEA Family number. A free IKEA Family membership is required.

#### Does IKEA use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
The Norfolk "Capture the Flag" offer uses **SheerID**. No reviewed IKEA source indicates ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for military offers. Most in-store offers rely on showing ID at the register rather than a third-party portal.

#### Can I use the IKEA military discount online?
No. IKEA's military/veteran offers are **in-store only** — the official terms state "Not valid online." The Norfolk program offers a store-managed remote/phone purchase path, but that is not standard ikea.com checkout.

#### What is excluded?
Services (delivery, assembly, Click & Collect, kitchen planning), gift cards, IKEA credit-card payments, the IKEA Kitchen Event offer, food offers, and the Restaurant/Bistro/Swedish Food Market. The discount applies before tax, shipping, and handling.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Official terms exclude certain specific event offers (like the Kitchen Event) but **do not clearly address stacking with general sales or promo codes** — treat combinability as store discretion and confirm at the register.

#### Does IKEA offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No first-responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount is documented in IKEA's official sources. IKEA has separately promoted a **student** offer (15% off a one-time purchase, verified via SheerID) — confirm current status on IKEA's site.

#### Do I need an IKEA Family membership?
Yes. The store offers require you to **scan an IKEA Family member number** to receive the discount. IKEA Family is free to join.
