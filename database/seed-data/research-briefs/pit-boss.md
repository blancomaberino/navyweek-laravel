# Pit Boss Grills — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $599 Pit Boss pellet grill (e.g. a Pro/Sportsman-tier pellet grill at MSRP on pitboss-grills.com), pre-tax.

## Headline finding

Pit Boss runs a **genuine, published 25% "Heroes" discount** for active military, verified veterans, and first responders (US and Canada). It is redeemed on **pitboss-grills.com only** after verifying through **ID.me or GovX**, which issues a unique single-use code. This is one of the strongest first-party discounts in the grill category — 25% off DTC-priced grills, griddles, and smokers beats almost any sale-stack. The one real limit: it does **not** apply to already-discounted or retailer-exclusive items and can't be combined with other offers, so on a deep clearance item a public sale can occasionally win. Archetype: manufacturer-DTC brand that funds a fixed verified-audience discount to pull buyers off big-box retail onto its own store.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **25% applies only to non-discounted items** on pitboss-grills.com — already-reduced or retailer-exclusive SKUs are excluded.
2. **Not combinable** with other offers/codes — the Heroes code stands alone.
3. **DTC-only** — the code cannot be used at Walmart, Lowe's, Academy, Costco, or other retailers that also sell Pit Boss.
4. Verification via **either** ID.me or GovX yields the same 25%; you don't need both.
