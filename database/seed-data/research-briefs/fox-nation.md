# Fox Nation — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 4, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one year of Fox Nation on the standard annual plan — **$71.88/year** (standard monthly is $8.99/mo = $107.88/yr). Prices exclude any applicable state tax; no tax-free channel exists for this brand.

## Headline finding

Fox Nation gives **all active-duty military and veterans their first year FREE**, verified through **SheerID** on Fox's own offer page (try.nation.foxnews.com/military) — live and redeemable as of July 4, 2026, though Fox labels it "limited time only." After the free year, the subscription **auto-renews at 50% off the then-current annual rate** (≈ $35.94/yr at today's $71.88 list) using the payment method you must supply at signup; cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the charge. Existing subscribers can redeem on their current account. This is a classic **Archetype F** brand (time-boxed subscription offer with sibling offers): the free year beats every other path outright, and the sibling offers (first responders free year, students free with .edu) cover most other readers.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **Military + First Responder offers do not combine.** Fox states both offers "may not be combined. Subscribers eligible for both offers may only redeem one offer." ([source](https://try.nation.foxnews.com/military))
2. **The military plan is its own checkout plan** — it replaces, and cannot be layered on, intro promos like the $17.76 annual deal. It's either/or by design (you check out with the "Military plan").
3. **Nothing else exists to stack:** no promo codes are involved, no cashback portal lists Fox Nation (as of July 4, 2026), and there is no gift-card or exchange channel for this subscription.
4. **The renewal discount is part of the offer, not a stack:** after the free year, renewals bill automatically at 50% off then-current annual rates for additional one-year periods unless you cancel ≥24 hours before the renewal date ([WeSalute T&C](https://www.wesalute.com/offer/49226-fox-nation?g=military); Fox FAQ).
5. **No military offer is published for FOX One or the FOX One + Fox Nation bundle** — Not stated in official source. Verified members who want FOX One pay public bundle pricing.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty service members — eligible (official source)
- Veterans (any branch, served at any time) — eligible (official source)
- National Guard / Reserve — eligible per SheerID's "served or currently serving" standard; Fox's page does not list components separately
- Military spouses / dependents — **not stated in official source**
- Active first responders (fire, EMS, law enforcement incl. federal LE) — eligible via the separate first-responder free-year offer
- Students with a .edu email — eligible via the separate student offer (new accounts only)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to try.nation.foxnews.com/military and click **Start Now** (existing subscribers: click **Sign In** instead — you keep your account).
2. Create your Fox Nation account (or sign in).
3. Complete the SheerID verification form — enter your name exactly as it appears on any document you might upload.
4. If not instantly verified, upload official documentation showing your full name, branch, and discharge date or current affiliation (e.g., DD-214). **Do not upload a photo of your military ID — SheerID cannot legally process it.**
5. Check out with the Military plan and add a payment method (not charged during the free year).

### How Verification Works
SheerID first attempts an instant database check. If that fails, you upload supporting documents; SheerID compares them against your form entries. Verification support runs through SheerID's information form (linked in Fox's FAQ). Fox does not state a re-verification cadence for renewals — not stated in official source.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Payment method required at signup even though year 1 is free.
- Auto-renews annually at 50% off then-current rates; cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date (My Account → Cancel Plan) to avoid the charge. Subscription fees are non-refundable.
- Cannot combine with the first-responder offer — one free year per person.
- Applies to Fox Nation standalone only; no military offer is published for FOX One ($19.99/mo) or the FOX One + Fox Nation bundle ($24.99/mo).
- A Fox Nation subscription does not include full live FOX News (that still requires a TV provider or FOX One).

### Best Ways To Save More

### FAQ
#### Does Fox Nation offer a military discount?
Yes — better: a completely free first year for active military and veterans, verified via SheerID, then 50% off annual renewals.
#### Is Fox Nation free for veterans?
Yes, the first year is free for all veterans and active-duty members; a payment method goes on file for the (discounted) renewal.
#### How do I verify my military status with Fox Nation?
Through SheerID at try.nation.foxnews.com/military — instant database check, with document upload as fallback. Never upload a military ID photo.
#### Do military spouses or dependents qualify?
Fox's official terms cover only those who served or are serving; spouse/dependent eligibility is not stated in the official source.
#### I already subscribe — can I still get the free year?
Yes. Sign in through the offer page, verify, and check out with the Military plan on your existing account.
#### What happens after the free year?
It auto-renews at 50% off the then-current annual rate (≈ $35.94/yr today). Fox emails a reminder first; cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date if you don't want to be charged.
#### Does Fox Nation use ID.me or GovX?
No — verification is SheerID. GovX lists Fox Nation but routes to the same offer; WeSalute members get the identical free year.
#### Can I combine the military offer with promo codes or other offers?
No. It can't be combined with the first-responder free year or public intro promos — and no promo code exists for the military offer.
#### What's the cheapest way to get Fox Nation if I'm not military?
The seasonal annual promos (e.g., $17.76 for year one in the July 2026 America-250 window), the first-responder free year, or the free student offer with a .edu email.
#### Does the free year include FOX One or live FOX News?
No. It covers Fox Nation only; FOX One and full live FOX News are separate paid products with no published military offer.
