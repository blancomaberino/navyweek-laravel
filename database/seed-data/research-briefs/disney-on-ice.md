# Disney On Ice — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one family 4-pack of Disney On Ice tickets at ~$30 face value per seat ($120 face), bought online through Ticketmaster (adds roughly $12–$18/ticket in service + facility fees). All effective prices below are **illustrative** — real fees vary heavily by venue, show, seat, and date.

## Headline finding

Disney On Ice's **own website publishes no military or veteran discount** — its FAQ and Special Offers pages say nothing about military pricing, GovX, or ID.me (verified July 13, 2026). Disney On Ice is a **Feld Entertainment** touring production; tickets sell through Ticketmaster and arena box offices. A military offer **does exist**, but only through third-party/authorized channels: the **GovX** marketplace, **ID.me Shop**, per-show **FEVO military-offer pages** (gofevo.com), and some **venue box offices** that honor a military ID. There is **no fixed national percentage** — the benefit varies by show and market and is historically small (a 2019 Military.com listing cited "up to $4 off per ticket plus reduced service fees"). This is archetype **A (GovX-only / no first-party discount)** with a **J (box-office discretion)** streak. The genuinely best move for most families is usually **buying select-seat tickets directly at the box office to skip Ticketmaster fees**, and/or applying a widely-reported public code (e.g. FS24 ≈ $5/ticket) — the military route's real edge is the **service-fee reduction**, not a large per-ticket cut.

**Verdict logic:**
- **Can get to the box office →** buy select seats there; avoiding Ticketmaster fees usually beats every online path, including the military offer.
- **Buying online, want lowest per-ticket price →** try a widely-reported public code (FS24 ≈ $5/tkt); it often matches or beats the small military per-ticket cut.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military offers are substitutes, not stacks.** A GovX/FEVO/ID.me military price is its own transaction; you cannot also apply a public promo code (FS24, MCD, AAA) on top. Choose whichever nets lower.
2. **Box-office discounts and Ticketmaster codes don't combine** — a box-office military/select-seat price is separate from online promo codes.
3. **Group rates (20+) are separate** from individual military offers; you don't stack a military discount onto a group order.
4. **Discounted tickets aren't sold day-of** through the discount channels — the box office is the only day-of seller, at standard pricing (per disneyonice.com FAQ).

- **Veterans** — historically listed as eligible; GovX/ID.me verify veteran status online.
- **Retirees, Reserve/National Guard** — historically listed as eligible.

- *Eligibility is stated per offer — confirm the specific show's terms at checkout; it is not a permanent, guaranteed national program.*

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online (GovX):**
1. Create/log in to a free GovX account and verify your status.
2. Search Disney On Ice on GovX; select your city/show.
3. Follow the offer link (often a FEVO page) to pick seats at the military price.

**Online (ID.me):**
1. Sign in / create a free ID.me account and verify military status.
2. Open the Disney On Ice offer in ID.me Shop and click through to redeem — confirm the exact discount/cash back before purchase.

**In store (box office):**
1. Go to the arena box office before show day (discount tickets aren't sold day-of).
2. Present a Military ID / Veteran ID / DD214 and ask for any military or discounted select-seat rate.
3. Buying at the box office also avoids Ticketmaster online service fees.

### How Verification Works
GovX and ID.me both verify military/veteran status through a free account (document or database check) before showing a redemption link. Box-office redemption uses a physical ID (Military ID, Veteran ID, or DD214) at the counter. Neither is a permanent entitlement to a set price — each offer is show- and market-specific and can end without notice.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No first-party Disney On Ice military discount is published; offers come only through GovX, ID.me, FEVO, or the box office.
- Discounted tickets are not sold on the day of the event (box office sells day-of at standard prices).
- Military offers don't combine with public promo codes or group rates.
- Exact discount amount, eligible groups, and availability vary by show and market — verify at checkout.
- Any "25% off" or flat "military %" from coupon sites is unverified.

### FAQ

#### Does Disney On Ice offer a military discount?
Not through its own website. Disney On Ice (a Feld Entertainment show) publishes no first-party military discount on its FAQ or Special Offers pages (checked July 13, 2026). Military offers exist through GovX, ID.me Shop, per-city FEVO links, and some arena box offices.

#### How much is the Disney On Ice military discount?
There's no fixed national amount — it varies by show and market. A 2019 Military.com listing cited "up to $4 off per ticket plus reduced service fees." Treat any flat percentage from coupon sites as unverified; confirm the exact figure at checkout.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Historically yes — Active Duty, Retirees, Veterans, Reserve/National Guard, and Dependents were all listed as eligible on a 2019 military-rate order, and GovX/ID.me verify veteran status online. Eligibility is stated per offer, so confirm for your specific show.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online through a free GovX or ID.me account (document/database check), or in person with a Military ID / Veteran ID / DD214 at the arena box office.

#### Does Disney On Ice use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
GovX and ID.me both list Disney On Ice military offers. Feld also runs per-market military-offer pages on FEVO (gofevo.com). SheerID, WeSalute, and VerifyPass are not confirmed for Disney On Ice.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Both. Online via GovX or ID.me; in person at the arena box office (not on the day of the event through the discount channels).

#### What is excluded?
Day-of discount purchases, stacking with public promo codes or group rates, and any unstated show/market. Exact terms vary per offer.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Military offers are standalone — you pick either the military price or a public code (FS24, MCD, AAA), whichever is lower, not both.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Disney On Ice tickets?
Usually **buying select seats at the box office** to avoid Ticketmaster fees, and/or a public $5/ticket code. Use the GovX/FEVO military offer when buying online, mainly for its service-fee reduction on multi-ticket orders.

#### Does Disney On Ice offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
GovX includes first responders and law enforcement on its Disney On Ice storefront. Teacher/nurse/student programs are not confirmed for Disney On Ice.

#### Does Disney On Ice run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No verified national holiday sale. The military benefit is delivered as ongoing per-market GovX/FEVO offers rather than a dated holiday promotion.
