# Mountain High Resort — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult peak-Saturday day lift ticket at Mountain High (Wrightwood, CA).

## Headline finding

Yes — Mountain High runs a **standing, official military discount**: a flat **$99 per day** (or **$59 per night**, 5pm–10pm when available) for active, veteran, retired, and reserve service members. It's redeemed **in person at the VIP Window at the West Resort** by showing a Department ID or Military ID — no online provider, no reservation, one ticket per ID. Because Mountain High uses **dynamic pricing** (adult "Anytime Flex" runs $99–$159 online and $10 more at the resort window), the flat $99 military rate is a real win on **peak/holiday days** (saves roughly $60–$70 vs. a ~$169 walk-up) but only breaks even on cheap early/late-season days when the public online rate already sits near $99. The best path for most service members is the **official $99 VIP-window rate on a busy day**; the main alternative is **GovX**, which lets you buy a verified-military ticket online in advance (useful for veterans who no longer carry a physical military ID, since the resort window asks for one). Archetype: **experience/ticketed-venue with a discretionary flat military rate**, layered over dynamic ticket pricing.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The **$99 military rate is a flat substitute price, not a percentage** — it does **not** stack with promo codes, group rates, or online advance deals. You pick one path.
2. It's **one ticket per valid ID** at the VIP window — you can't buy discounted tickets for a whole non-military group off one ID.
3. **GovX and the resort's own VIP-window rate are separate channels** — you buy through one or the other, not both; GovX pricing is set by GovX and may differ from the $99 window rate.
4. Night skiing ($59 military / $69–$89 public) is availability-dependent ("when available") — confirm night operations before driving up.

### How To Redeem
**In store (VIP Window):**
1. Go to the **VIP Window at the West Resort** — no reservation needed.
2. Show your **Department ID or Military ID**.
3. Buy your $99 day (or $59 night) ticket — one per valid ID.

**Online (GovX):**
1. Create a free **GovX** account and verify your military/veteran/government/first-responder status.
2. Open the **Mountain High** listing on GovX and buy the ticket (GovX price + any service fee).

### How Verification Works
The resort verifies **in person** — a staff member checks your Department ID or Military ID at the VIP Window. There is no account, no ID.me/SheerID, and no re-verification schedule. **GovX** verification is a one-time online check tied to your GovX account (military, veteran, first responder, healthcare, or government). Neither channel is more permanent than the source states.

### FAQ

#### Does Mountain High offer a military discount?
Yes. Active, veteran, retired, and reserve service members ski or ride for a flat **$99/day** or **$59/night** (5pm–10pm when available).

#### How much is the Mountain High military discount?
It's a flat rate, not a percentage: **$99 per day**, **$59 per night**. On a busy day that's roughly $60–$70 below the ~$169 walk-up window price; on a bargain day the public online rate can already match $99.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and reservists qualify?
Yes — the resort lists "Active, Veteran, Retired, Reserve." Spouses/dependents are not mentioned; ask at the window.

#### How do I verify my status?
In person at the **VIP Window, West Resort** — show a **Department ID or Military ID**. Online, use **GovX** verification.

#### Does Mountain High use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not for its own window rate — that's an **in-person ID check**. **GovX** sells Mountain High tickets to verified military/government/first responders online.

#### Can I use it online or only in stores?
The resort's $99/$59 rate is **in-person only** at the VIP window. For an online purchase, buy through **GovX**.

#### What's excluded?
No stacking with codes or group rates; one ticket per ID; night rate only "when available."

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. It's a flat substitute price and doesn't stack with promo codes, advance online deals, or group rates.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Mountain High?
On a **peak/holiday day**, the **$99 VIP-window military rate** — it's well below the dynamic walk-up price. On a **low-demand day**, compare it to the public online "Anytime Flex," which can already be ~$99.

#### Does Mountain High offer first-responder, teacher, nurse, or government discounts?
Not through its own window rate (that's military-only), but **GovX** — which sells Mountain High tickets — verifies first responders, nurses/healthcare, teachers, and government employees.

#### Does Mountain High run a Military Appreciation Day?
It has historically (2019 saw a $42 military day with live music and demos). That's a **past** special, not a standing promise — the evergreen benefit is the $99/$59 rate.
