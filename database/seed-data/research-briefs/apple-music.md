# Apple Music — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** Apple Music Individual plan at **$10.99/mo** (US; **verify live**).

## Headline finding

Apple Music has **no standing military or veteran discount** on the subscription. The widely-cited "4 free months of Apple Music for military and veterans" was a **limited-time 2021 promotion (verified via ID.me, expired August 1, 2021)** — it is **not a current offer**. Apple's own military page ("Veterans and Military Store") is a **hardware** purchase program (roughly 10% off Macs/iPads for verified service members via Apple's Veterans and Military Purchase Program) and does **not** discount Apple Music streaming. The genuine ongoing discount on the Apple Music *subscription* is the **Student plan: $5.99/mo (vs $10.99 Individual) for up to 48 months, with Apple TV+ included**, verified through **UNiDAYS / SheerID** — available to enrolled college students, which many junior service members and veterans-using-GI-Bill qualify for. So the honest headline: *there is no military discount on Apple Music; if you're an enrolled student, the Student plan nearly halves the price; otherwise the Apple One bundle or a carrier plan (some include Apple Music free) is the real lever.* Archetype: **documented "no military discount" — student plan + bundles are the actual savings.**
