# Michaels Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Michaels (Michaels Stores, the U.S. arts-and-crafts retailer) offers an everyday military discount of **15% off your entire purchase, including sale items**, valid **both online and in store**. Eligibility is stated by Michaels as military members and their immediate family. The discount is tied to a **Michaels Rewards** account: members verify their military status once (military verification on Michaels is handled by **SheerID**) and the discount then applies automatically whenever they sign in or give their Rewards phone number at checkout. New customers can also show a valid military ID in store to receive the discount for up to three initial purchases while verification completes. The single most important caveat is the long exclusion list (clearance, custom framing, gift cards, and most craft-technology machines such as Cricut, Silhouette, and Glowforge are excluded), plus a limit of one military discount per product and one per day.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Michaels' military discount page (michaels.com/military-discount).
2. Log in to your Michaels Rewards account, or create a free account.
3. Complete the military verification prompt (powered by SheerID); most verifications are instant.
4. Once verified, shop michaels.com signed in to your account — the 15% discount applies automatically to eligible items at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Sign in to the Michaels app or have your Michaels Rewards phone number ready.
2. At checkout, sign in or provide your Rewards phone number so the discount applies to eligible items.
3. If you are not yet verified, show a valid military ID at the register. Michaels states a valid military ID allows the discount for up to three (3) initial purchases prior to verification completion, and that in-store ID presentation syncs your status to your Rewards account within 24 hours.

### How Verification Works
Michaels verifies military status through **SheerID**, integrated into the Michaels Rewards program. You log in or create a Michaels Rewards account and follow the verification prompt; most verifications complete instantly, though in rare cases additional documentation may be requested. After verification, the discount is attached to your Rewards account and applies automatically online and in store. In store, you can also show a valid military ID to use the discount immediately (for up to three initial purchases) while your account verification syncs (Michaels states within 24 hours). Michaels does not publicly state how often re-verification is required; SheerID programs commonly re-verify periodically, so do not assume it is permanent.

### FAQ

#### Does Michaels offer a military discount?
Yes. Michaels offers an everyday 15% military discount on your entire purchase, including sale items, usable online and in store, through the Michaels Rewards program.

#### How much is the Michaels military discount?
15% off your entire purchase, including sale items, on eligible products. Many categories are excluded (see exclusions).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Michaels states the offer is for "military members and their immediate family." It does not separately list veterans, spouses, or dependents on its page — eligibility for each is determined through Michaels' verification (SheerID). Confirm your specific status during verification.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Create or sign in to a free Michaels Rewards account and complete the verification prompt (powered by SheerID); most verifications are instant. In store, you can show a valid military ID to use the discount right away.

#### Does Michaels use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Michaels uses **SheerID** for military status verification, integrated into Michaels Rewards. (WeSalute, military.com, and similar sites list the offer but are not the verification provider.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes (U.S.). Sign in or give your Michaels Rewards phone number at checkout, or show a valid military ID. Note the Canada version of this offer is in-store only with different terms.

#### What is excluded?
Clearance, custom framing, gift cards, classes/events, cut-to-order fabric, balloons, third-party-seller items, and most craft-technology machines (Cricut, Silhouette, Glowforge, Brother, Creality, 3Doodler) plus select branded goods (Lego, Pokémon cards, and more). See the full list above.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Sale items are included in the 15% discount. Michaels limits it to one military discount per product and one per day; it does not extend to excluded items, and Michaels does not publish broad stacking with other coupons.

#### Does Michaels offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Michaels offers a **teacher** discount (15%) and a **senior** discount (10%, age 55+), both via Michaels Rewards. A standing first responder, nurse, government, or student discount is not stated in Michaels' official source.
