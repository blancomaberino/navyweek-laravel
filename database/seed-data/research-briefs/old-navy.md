# Old Navy Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Old Navy offers a **10% military discount on in-store purchases**, available to active, retired, and reserve U.S. military personnel and their family members. Verification is done **at the register by showing a valid, current military ID** — there is **no third-party provider** (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX) and **no online discount**: the offer is explicitly **not valid at Oldnavy.com**. The single most important caveat: **Old Navy does not publish this discount on its own website.** The 10% in-store offer is documented only through military-discount directories (military.com, Veteran.com), and **participation can vary by store** — Gap Inc. brand locations are told to honor it, but shoppers should confirm at their local store. The discount cannot be combined with other offers, does not apply to gift cards, and gives no adjustments on prior purchases.

> Accuracy note: Several aggregators imply you can "verify online through ID.me" for an Old Navy military discount. That is **incorrect**. ID.me's own Old Navy page states: *"We're not aware that Old Navy offers Military discounts."* As of June 23, 2026, the only documented offer is the **10% in-store** discount redeemed by showing a physical military ID — there is no verified online military discount channel. Coupon sites advertising "30% off military" or "online military codes" are **not supported by any primary or brand source** and should be treated as false.

### FAQ

Yes — a 10% discount on in-store purchases for active, retired, and reserve military and their family members with a valid military ID. It is not offered online. Note that Old Navy does not publish this discount on its website, and participation can vary by store, so confirm locally.

#### How much is the Old Navy military discount?
10% off, in store only. (Ignore coupon sites advertising "30% military" codes — those are not supported by Old Navy or any verification provider.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Family members — including spouses and children — qualify with a valid military ID. Veterans are listed as eligible by military-benefit directories, but a *current* military ID is generally required, which many non-retired veterans don't have. Confirm at your store.

#### How do I verify my status?
You present a valid, current military ID at the register. There's no app, code, or online account.

No. Old Navy does not use any third-party verification provider for its military discount. ID.me's own page confirms it is "not aware that Old Navy offers Military discounts," and there is no online military discount.

#### Can I use the discount online at Oldnavy.com?
No. The military discount is in store only and is explicitly not valid at Oldnavy.com. For online savings, use Old Navy promo codes, rewards free shipping, and sales.

#### What is excluded?
It can't be applied to gift cards, can't be combined with other offers, and isn't adjusted on past purchases. Whether it works on sale/clearance versus regular-price items varies by store.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — it can't be combined with other offers or discounts. Whether it applies to already-reduced sale/clearance items depends on the store.

#### Does Old Navy offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
