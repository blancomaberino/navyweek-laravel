# Sun Outdoors — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one short-term stay — 4 nights in an RV site or vacation rental at a participating Sun Outdoors resort, retail ~$100/night → **~$400 short-term booking** (plus a secondary look at a seasonal/annual stay).

## Headline finding

**Sun Outdoors (Sun RV Resorts, part of Sun Communities) has a real, straightforward military discount — no third-party verification portal required.** With promo code **SALUTE**, current and former military of the **U.S. and Canadian** armed forces get **20% off select short-term RV stays, tent sites, and vacation rentals**, **or $200 off** a new **seasonal or annual** stay. You book online with the code (or call for seasonal/annual) and simply **present proof of military service at check-in** — there is no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID gate. The catch is in the fine print: it's **not combinable** with any other offer, promotion, or weekly/monthly pricing; **site-type restrictions and blackout dates vary by resort**; a minimum number of nights (up to 14) applies; the $200 seasonal/annual credit is a **one-time, non-refundable** credit for **new** long-term guests only; and the whole promotion **expires December 31, 2026**. Archetype: **campground/RV-resort chain with a self-serve code-based military discount (manual proof at check-in), no verification provider.** Best path: for most trips the 20% SALUTE code is the winner, but always price-check it against Sun's own weekly/monthly rates and any public seasonal promo, since SALUTE can't stack.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **SALUTE cannot be combined with any other offer, promotion, discount, or weekly/monthly pricing** — it is strictly a single-offer code (official terms).
2. **20% (short-term) and $200 (seasonal/annual) are separate offers** for different stay types — you use whichever fits the booking, not both on one reservation.
3. **The $200 seasonal/annual credit is one-time and non-refundable**, for **new** long-term guests only.
4. **Blackout dates and site-type restrictions vary by resort** — the code may be unavailable on peak dates or premium sites at a given location.
5. **No verification-provider or cashback-portal stack** — booking is direct with the code and manual proof at check-in; no ID.me/GovX layer and no verified Rakuten/portal path on Sun bookings.
