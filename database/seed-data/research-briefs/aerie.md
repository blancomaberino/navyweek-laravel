# Aerie (American Eagle) — Military & Veteran Discount Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $80 Aerie apparel/intimates order.

## Headline finding

**Aerie has no official, brand-published military or veteran discount.** Aerie is a brand of American Eagle Outfitters (AEO) and shares ae.com; AEO's own "Promotion and Discount Information" page lists **no military offer**, and **ID.me confirms** it is "not aware of American Eagle Outfitters offering Military community discounts." There is **no online military code** for aerie.com and **no ID.me/GovX/SheerID** military verification. The only military offer with any outside corroboration is a **store-level 10% off with a military ID at the register**, documented on mall-landlord listings — **not** an AEO corporate policy, and store-dependent. The real savings are Aerie's frequent, deep public sales plus **Real Rewards** and the AEO credit card. Archetype: **no official route (B) + discretionary in-store (J)** — the page's job is to correct the "Aerie military discount" myth and route shoppers to the sale-stack.

### FAQ

#### Does Aerie offer a military discount?
No official one. Aerie (part of American Eagle Outfitters) publishes **no military discount**, and ID.me confirms it's not aware of any AEO military offer. The only outside-corroborated offer is a **store-level 10% with a military ID**, which is store-managed and not guaranteed. #### How much is the Aerie military discount?
There's no official percentage. Third-party listings report **10% off in store with a military ID**; AEO doesn't publish this, so treat it as store-reported and unverified. Ignore aggregator "up to 25%" claims — those aren't real.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
AEO publishes no eligibility list. Aggregators claim active duty, veterans, and Reserve/Guard for the store-level offer, but **spouses and dependents aren't stated** anywhere. Eligibility is effectively decided at the store.

#### How do I verify my military status?
There's **no online verification** — Aerie/AEO isn't on ID.me, and there's no SheerID/GovX military program. The only verification is **showing a physical military ID in store**.

#### Does Aerie use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No brand-run discount through any of them.** ID.me says it's not aware of an AEO military discount. GovX may resell AEO goods (not a percentage discount; its page is JS-rendered). WeSalute lists a BenefitHub gift-card deal behind a **paid** membership — not an Aerie discount.

#### Can I use a military discount online at aerie.com?
**No.** There's no online military discount or code. Any military-ID discount is in store only and store-managed.

#### What's actually the cheapest way to buy Aerie for a service member?
Use Aerie's **public sales + promo codes** (often 30–40% off), which beat the store-level 10%, plus **Real Rewards** and the **AEO card**. These are open to everyone and don't require any military verification.

#### Does Aerie run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day military sale?
No dedicated AEO/Aerie **military** event was found. Aerie runs public sales in those windows, open to all shoppers — not a military-specific offer.

#### Does American Eagle (the parent) have a separate military discount from Aerie?
No — AE and Aerie share ae.com and the same policy: **no official military discount** online; any in-store military-ID discount is store-managed and unverified by corporate.
