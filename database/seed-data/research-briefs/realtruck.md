## Headline finding

**RealTruck (realtruck.com) publishes no military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** There is no military checkout field, no verification partner, and no military page on its site. ID.me states plainly on RealTruck's store page, **"We're not aware that RealTruck offers Military discounts"** — meaning no active ID.me program — and RealTruck has **no GovX storefront, no SheerID, and no WeSalute** offer either. So the honest answer is: *no discount to verify.* Ignore the coupon sites that claim a "$100 off military discount" or a "military discount at checkout" — neither exists.

Because RealTruck is the **world's largest truck-accessory company and actually manufactures the brands it sells** (BAK, Extang, Retrax, UnderCover, TruXedo, Husky, Lund, and more), its flagship products are **MAP-protected** — the same price ($1,149.99 for the BAKFlip MX4) essentially everywhere, including Amazon and third-party retailers. That shapes the real savings game: the best *reliable* everyday move on a full-price MAP item is a **cashback portal** (TopCashback was **4%** as of July 10, 2026, ahead of Rakuten's 2%), and the deepest wins come from **RealTruck's own frequent public sitewide sales** (open to everyone, no verification). A service member who wants a *real* military discount on a truck accessory has to go to a **substitute retailer that runs a GovX/ID.me program** — e.g. **DECKED (~10% via GovX ID)** on its own storage products or **4 Wheel Parts (~5% on non-sale items)** for off-road parts — but those programs generally **exclude MAP house brands like BAK/Extang**, so they rarely help on this exact cover.
