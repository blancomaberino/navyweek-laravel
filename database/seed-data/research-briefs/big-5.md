# Big 5 Sporting Goods — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $100 in-store purchase (apparel, footwear, or gear — the everyday unit most readers compare).

## Headline finding

**Big 5 Sporting Goods offers a brand-published 10% military discount off your entire in-store purchase**, and it's one of the broadest in retail on eligibility: **active duty, reserves, retirees, veterans, spouses, and dependent children** all qualify. You show a **valid military ID — or veteran status printed on your driver's license** — at the register. It's **in-store only** (not online), **excludes gift cards and licenses** (e.g., hunting/fishing licenses), and **can't be combined with other coupons** or applied to prior purchases.

Because it can't stack with coupons, the real decision is simple: on any given item, take **whichever is lower — the 10% military discount or Big 5's weekly sale/coupon price.** Big 5 runs aggressive **weekly ad sales and email/app coupons**, so during a strong sale the public price often beats the flat 10%. Archetype: **brick-and-mortar sporting-goods retailer with a clean, in-store flat military discount** that competes with (rather than stacks on) an active weekly-sale calendar. This is a rare "yes, it's real, and it's simple" case — no false-percentage debunking needed beyond noting the discount is in-store only.

Yes — 10% off your entire in-store purchase for active duty, reserves, retirees, veterans, spouses, and dependent children, with a valid military ID or veteran status on your driver's license.

#### How much is the Big 5 military discount?
A flat 10% off your entire purchase, in store.

In person at the register — show a valid military ID or a driver's license with veteran status. No online verification or account is needed.
