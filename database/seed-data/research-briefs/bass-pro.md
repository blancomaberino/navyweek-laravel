## Headline finding

Bass Pro Shops runs a real, official, everyday military discount — the **Legendary Salute**: **10% off most merchandise and 5% off firearms & ammunition**, online, in store, and by phone, at both Bass Pro Shops and Cabela's (one program, two banners, both run by BPS Direct, L.L.C.). The rare and valuable part: **Bass Pro's own FAQ says the discount "can be used on top of other promotions or on select sale items"** — so the winning play is sale price + 10%, not either/or. This is a **combinable-discount brand (Archetype D)**, with a secondary **exchange-carried angle (Archetype L)**: since September 2024, AAFES's ShopMyExchange.com sells Bass Pro house brands tax-free at prices that already include the 10% Legendary Salute discount.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

1. **Military % + sales/promos: YES (official).** Bass Pro's Legendary Salute FAQ: "The Legendary Salute military discount can be used on top of other promotions or on select sale items." Caveat the word **"select"** — not every sale item qualifies; some categories (boats, ATVs, alcohol, gift cards, licenses/services) are excluded outright.
2. **Military % + cashback portal: PLAUSIBLE, UNCONFIRMED.** The discount is account-level (no coupon code), so TopCashback's "non-TCB coupon code voids cashback" clause isn't obviously triggered — but neither portal states that SheerID-verified orders track. Flag as "may not track"; TopCashback pays nothing on firearms, ammo, or gift cards regardless.
3. **Military % + CLUB card points: effectively yes** (points accrue on the payment; Bass Pro states no exclusion). Community reports also describe cardholder promos combining with the 10% — treat those as unverified (see Community-Reported).
4. **Exchange path: no double-dip.** ShopMyExchange prices already include the 10% Legendary Salute; you can't apply it again. The win there is **no sales tax** (+ any MILITARY STAR promo shown on the listing).
5. **Gift cards: excluded from the discount as a product** (can't buy gift cards at 10% off). Paying with a gift card is not restricted in the official terms; discounted third-party gift cards are a marginal extra layer — reseller risk applies, and portal cashback on gift-card-paid orders varies by portal terms.
6. **Firearms/ammo: 5%, not 10%,** and cashback portals exclude those categories entirely.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Sign in to (or create) your basspro.com or cabelas.com account.
2. On the Legendary Salute page, verify your military status (military flow) — LEO/firefighter and wildlife-agent flows have separate "Verify Now" buttons; wildlife agents currently must use the in-store route.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop any U.S. Bass Pro Shops or Cabela's store.
2. At checkout, show proof of service: military ID card (active **or expired**), state-issued ID listing veteran status, or Form DD-214. LEO/firefighter/EMT/wildlife agents: show current employment ID.
3. The register applies 10% (5% on firearms/ammo).

### How To Redeem By Phone
1. Complete online verification first (Step 2 above).
2. Call 1-800-227-7776 (Bass Pro) or 1-800-237-4444 (Cabela's) to place the order with your discount.

### How Verification Works
Online verification runs through your Bass Pro/Cabela's store account; SheerID's shopper catalog lists the offer, indicating SheerID powers the check (name, DOB, branch/agency; document upload if instant verification fails). Bass Pro doesn't publish a re-verification schedule — shoppers report military status persists on the account while first responders re-verify monthly, but that's unofficial. In store, no enrollment is needed; just show qualifying ID. Support: Bass Pro customer service, 1-800-227-7776.

### FAQ

#### Does Bass Pro Shops offer a military discount?
Yes — the everyday Legendary Salute: 10% off most merchandise and 5% off firearms and ammunition, online, in store, and by phone. Cabela's runs the identical program.

#### How much is the Bass Pro military discount?
10% on eligible merchandise; 5% on firearms and ammunition.

#### Is Cabela's military discount the same as Bass Pro's?
Yes — same Legendary Salute program, same terms and exclusions, verified the same way; the two banners are one company (BPS Direct, L.L.C.).

#### Do veterans qualify? What ID works in store?
Yes. Stores accept a military ID card (active **or expired**), a state-issued ID with veteran status, or Form DD-214.

#### Do military spouses or dependents qualify?
Bass Pro's official pages don't say. Some third-party sites claim spouses can verify separately — unconfirmed; check with Bass Pro before counting on it.

#### Does Bass Pro use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX?
Verification happens inside your basspro.com/cabelas.com account; SheerID's own shopper site lists the Bass Pro offer, indicating SheerID powers it. Bass Pro does not use ID.me, and there is no Bass Pro storefront on GovX.

#### Can I combine the military discount with sales or promo codes?
Bass Pro's FAQ says the discount "can be used on top of other promotions or on select sale items." That's the real money move — stack it on Classic-sale or Bargain Cave prices. Note "select": not every sale item qualifies.

#### What is excluded?
Boats/trailers/motors (canoes and kayaks are fine), ATVs/UTVs, tractors, related services, alcohol, gift cards, and non-merchandise services like hunting licenses and Gear Guard. Firearms and ammo get 5% instead of 10%.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Bass Pro?
Wait for the item to hit a sale (Classics, Bargain Cave, holiday events), stack the 10% on the sale price, click through a cashback portal, and pay with the CLUB card if you have one. On a $150 cart with a 30% sale that's roughly $94.50 versus $150 — about 37% off. If you're exchange-eligible and buying Bass Pro house brands at full price, ShopMyExchange sells them tax-free at 10% off MSRP.

#### Can I buy Bass Pro gear at the military exchange?
Yes — since September 2024, ShopMyExchange.com (AAFES) carries Bass Pro house brands (Bass Pro Shops, Cabela's, RedHead, World Wide Sportsman, Ascend and more) tax-free, with the 10% Legendary Salute already in the price. Exchange online shopping is open to active duty, retirees, dependents, DoD civilians, and honorably discharged veterans (Veterans Online Shopping Benefit); in-store exchange access is narrower.

#### Does Bass Pro offer a first responder discount?
Yes — the same Legendary Salute 10%/5% covers law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs, and fish & wildlife agents (wildlife agents verify in store only).

#### Does Bass Pro run a Veterans Day sale?
Historically yes — most recently a 10% window around November 8–11 for veterans and active duty. Now that the everyday rate is 10%, the holiday window mostly matters for walk-in convenience. Never assume a future promo.
