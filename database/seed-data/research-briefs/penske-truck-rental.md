# Penske Truck Rental Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Penske Truck Rental offers a **15% military discount** on truck rentals with promo code **MILITARY**, available to **active-duty service members and veterans** — you simply show a **military or veteran ID at pickup**. Some promotional periods push the savings higher, **up to 20% off**. If you carry a **WeSalute+** membership (formerly Veterans Advantage), Penske honors the discount that way too: **book online with your WeSalute+ ID and present the card at pickup**. Either path is **reserved online** — the WeSalute+ rate in particular **cannot be applied by phone, chat, or at the counter**. For a PCS, Penske also runs a **dedicated military move call center at 1-844-4TROOPS (1-844-487-6677)** staffed by associates with a personal military connection, useful for a personally procured move (PPM/DITY). The discount applies to **time and mileage on the base rental rate**, not to taxes, fees, or optional coverage, and it **cannot be combined** with other promotions or coupons.
