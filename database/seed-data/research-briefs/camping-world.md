# Camping World — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $400 retail accessories/gear cart at Camping World (parts, camping gear, RV accessories — not an RV, boat, or service). The big-ticket RV path (Hero Program) is covered separately because its discount is a fixed dollar amount, not a percentage.

## Headline finding

Yes — Camping World runs a **real, official, evergreen military benefit**, split into two programs. (1) The **Hero Program** gives active duty, veterans, and first responders a fixed dollar discount off the advertised price of a **new RV**, tiered by RV type from **$300 (small trailer) up to $2,000 (Class A diesel)**. (2) A separate **5% off** applies to select retail items at Camping World locations, year-round, for the same groups. Verification is **in person with a physical ID document** (DD-214, VHIC, CAC, state veteran-designated license, USID, or first-responder ID) — Camping World does **not** use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID for this. Archetype: **big-ticket dealer + retail loyalty hybrid** — the headline "up to $2,000" is a new-RV incentive, while the everyday retail win is small (5%). The single biggest retail lever is not the military discount at all: a **Good Sam membership (~$30–$39/yr) gives 10% off most retail**, and Camping World's own guidance says Good Sam savings **stack on top of** the military 5%, so an in-store service member who joins Good Sam gets roughly **15% off retail** — beating the 5% alone.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Good Sam 10% + military 5% stack in store** — Camping World's own RVers guide states Good Sam savings "stack on top of your military discount." (Medium confidence: brand blog, not the terms page; verify at register.)
2. **The military discount does not apply to RVs, boats, service, or gift cards** (reported by military sources; the Hero Program's *new-RV* discount is the separate RV benefit). Do not expect the 5% on an RV — the RV lever is the fixed-dollar Hero discount.
3. **No verified military promo code** and no confirmation the 5% combines with site-wide coupon codes or clearance/sale items — aggregators note it "often cannot be combined with other deals or sale items." Treat coupon-code stacking as unverified.
4. **Cashback portals** track online campingworld.com orders and are independent of the in-store military ID discount; they cannot be earned on an in-store purchase.
5. **Verification is per-purchase in store** (present certificate + physical ID), not a saved account status.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Go to rv.campingworld.com/hero-program and enter your info to receive your **Hero Discount Certificate** by email.
2. Bring the certificate **and** an accepted physical ID (DD-214, VA Health ID / VHIC, Active Duty CAC, state veteran-designated driver's license, Uniformed Services ID / USID, or First Responder ID).
3. Present both **at the register at time of purchase**. RV buyers: negotiate the advertised price first, then apply the Hero certificate.
4. Stack a Good Sam membership for 10% off most retail, on top of the 5% military discount.

### How To Redeem Online
Camping World's official Hero page describes **in-store redemption with a physical ID**; a clearly stated online 5% path is **not confirmed** on that page. For online gear orders, the reliable levers are a **Good Sam membership** and a **cashback portal** (Rakuten 3% baseline; some portals higher). Confirm any online military discount with Camping World support before counting on it.

### How Verification Works
Camping World verifies **in person**: you request a Hero Discount Certificate online, then show it with one accepted physical ID document at the register each time you buy. There is **no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID account** and no saved digital status — bring the document. Accepted documents are listed above.

### FAQ
#### Does Camping World offer a military discount?
Yes. Active duty, veterans, retirees, Reserve/National Guard, and first responders get a fixed discount off a **new RV** through the Hero Program ($300–$2,000 by RV type) plus **5% off select retail items** year-round.

#### How much is the Camping World military discount?
New RVs: $300 (small trailer) up to $2,000 (Class A diesel), by RV type. Retail: 5% off select items. A Good Sam membership adds 10% off retail that stacks with the military 5%.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans with honorable service qualify (verify with DD-214, VHIC, or a veteran-designated license). Retirees and Reserve/Guard qualify for the 5% retail discount. **Spouses and dependents are not listed** on the official page — verify in store before assuming.

#### How do I verify my status?
Request a Hero Discount Certificate online, then present it with a physical ID (DD-214, VA Health ID, CAC, veteran-designated license, USID, or first-responder ID) **at the register**.

#### Does Camping World use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. The Hero Program uses **in-store verification with a physical ID document**, not a third-party online verifier.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The discount is documented for **in-store** use with a physical ID. A stated online 5% path is **not confirmed** on the official page — for online gear, use Good Sam plus a cashback portal instead.

#### What is excluded?
The 5% retail discount reportedly does not apply to RVs, boats, service, or gift cards, and may not combine with sale/clearance items (aggregator-reported — verify in store).

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
There's **no verified military promo code**, and stacking with sale/coupon codes isn't guaranteed. What is confirmed: **Good Sam's 10% retail stacks with the military 5%** in store, per Camping World's own guide.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Camping World?
New RV: the Hero Program. Gear in store: Good Sam membership + military 5% (~15% off). Gear online: Good Sam + the best cashback portal that day.

#### Does Camping World offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
First responders (police, firefighters, EMTs, paramedics) qualify for the **same Hero Program and 5% retail**. No teacher, nurse, or student discount is stated.

#### Does Camping World run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
The military benefit is **evergreen** — it doesn't spike for a holiday. Camping World does run **public** Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, and Black Friday RV/gear sales open to everyone, which RV buyers can layer the Hero certificate onto.

#### Can I buy Camping World products at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
No. Camping World merchandise isn't sold through AAFES/NEX exchanges, GovX, or ExpertVoice — the levers are the in-store military discount, Good Sam, and cashback portals.
