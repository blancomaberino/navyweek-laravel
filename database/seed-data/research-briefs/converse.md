# Converse — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of Chuck Taylor All Stars at full price (illustrative ≈ $65; verify live).

## Headline finding

Converse (a Nike-owned brand) publishes a clear, first-party military discount: **15% off** for all active, reservist, veteran, and retired US Military personnel, **plus the spouses and dependents of active personnel**. It's stated verbatim on Converse's own military-discount landing page, verified through **SheerID** (not ID.me or GovX), and valid on Converse.com and at US Converse retail stores. The honest headline: *the 15% is real and generous on eligibility (veterans and dependents included), but Converse is a promo-heavy brand — a live sitewide sale or the "15% off + free shipping" email signup can match or beat the military rate, and discounts generally don't stack.* This is a **standard retail-verification archetype (SheerID gate, single-discount)**. Best move: verify once via SheerID, then compare the 15% against whatever sale/clearance is live before checkout.

### How To Redeem
1. Go to Converse's military-discount page and click **Verify Your Details** (converse.com/military-verification).
2. Complete **SheerID** verification — enter your name exactly as it appears on your military document.
3. If prompted, upload government-issued documentation showing first name, last name, branch of service, and current military status.
4. Once verified, the **15% discount** applies in cart. You may need to re-verify on future visits.
5. Before paying, compare the 15% against any live sale — take whichever is lower.

### How Verification Works
Converse uses **SheerID**. If you're not instantly verified, SheerID compares your form entries against uploaded documents, so match your name and status to your paperwork. Documents are reviewed in order received, and you'll get an email result — usually within about 20 minutes. SheerID states it doesn't sell, rent, or store uploaded documents. Converse does **not** use ID.me or GovX for this discount.
