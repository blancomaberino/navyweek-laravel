# Away — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Away **Carry-On — $275 MSRP** (representative polycarbonate suitcase), pre-tax.

## Headline finding

Away offers a **real, official 10% off sitewide** military/veteran discount, verified through **ID.me** and applied automatically in checkout. Away's own ID.me landing page states it plainly: *"To thank you for your dedication, we're offering veterans, active military, medical personnel, teachers, and students 10% off sitewide."* Eligible military groups are broad — *"Active duty, veterans, retirees, spouses, dependents, and more."* The catch that makes this page useful: the 10% **"cannot be combined with any other promotion or discount code,"** and Away runs **deep sitewide sales (20–25%, biggest at Black Friday)** several times a year. So the counterintuitive verdict is that during a 20%+ Away sale, a verified shopper should **skip their own 10%** and buy the public sale price (which a cashback portal can layer under, since it needs no code). Archetype: a genuine **ID.me identity discount** that behaves like **C — the sale usually beats it** during major sale windows. Note precisely: **first responders (police/fire/EMT) are NOT listed** among Away's eligible groups; the medical tier is nurses and medical providers.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 10% cannot be combined** with any other promotion or discount code — Away states this explicitly. So military-10% + sale, or military-10% + welcome code, is **not** allowed.
2. **The public sale beats the 10% and excludes it** — during a 25% event, the sale price is lower and you cannot add your 10% on top; you choose one.
3. **Cashback vs. the 10% is an either/or** — the ID.me-verified checkout is identity-gated, which typically breaks Rakuten/portal tracking. A **no-code public sale** keeps portal tracking intact, so *sale + cashback* is the portal-safe combo, not *military code + cashback*.
4. **No GovX layer** — there's no Away GovX storefront to price against; GovX is only relevant for substitute luggage brands.
5. **Gift cards and prior purchases are excluded** from the discount (standard terms).

### FAQ

#### Does Away offer a military discount?
Yes. Away gives **10% off sitewide** to veterans, active military, medical personnel, teachers, and students, verified through **ID.me** and applied automatically at checkout.

#### How much is the Away military discount?
A flat **10% off**, sitewide. It is not a typed coupon code — it applies once you verify with ID.me.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Away's military tier covers "active duty, veterans, retirees, spouses, dependents, and more" (ID.me's full military eligibility list governs).

#### How do I verify my status?
Use the **Verify with ID.me** button on Away's Hero Discounts page (awaytravel.com/pages/herodiscounts), complete ID.me verification, then shop — the 10% is applied in checkout.

#### Does Away use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Away uses **ID.me**. There is **no Away storefront on GovX**, and no SheerID/WeSalute military path for Away.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or only online?
The 10% ID.me discount is an **online** offer for US customers on awaytravel.com.

#### What's excluded?
It **can't be combined** with any other promotion or discount code, and it excludes gift cards and prior purchases.

#### Can I combine it with a sale or promo code?
No. It's non-combinable. And because Away's sitewide sales run 20–25%, during a big sale the **public price is lower than your 10%** — buy the sale price instead (you can add a cashback portal, since no code is required).

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Away?
On a regular-price day, the **ID.me 10%**. During a **20%+ sitewide sale** (especially Black Friday), the **public sale price plus a cashback portal** — the two never combine, so pick whichever is live.

#### Do first responders, nurses, teachers, or students get the Away discount?
**Nurses/medical providers, teachers, and students: yes** (10% via ID.me). **First responders (police/fire/EMT): not listed** as an eligible group at Away.

#### Does Away run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
We found no Away military-specific holiday promo — the 10% is evergreen. Away's deepest public discounting is at **Black Friday/Cyber Week** (25% in 2025).
