# TRUEWERK — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** **$200 workwear cart** (e.g., one T2 WerkPant + one B1 breathable work shirt + socks), pre-tax, direct at truewerk.com.

## Headline finding

TRUEWERK runs a real, official, published **15% discount via ID.me** for **military (active, veterans, Guard/Reserve, retirees, spouses, families), first responders, government employees, teachers, and students** — one of the broader eligibility lists in workwear. The twist: TRUEWERK's public **email/SMS welcome offer is also 15% off your first order**, so on a first purchase the military discount adds nothing over what anyone can get — its real value is on **every order after the first**, since welcome codes are one-time. Nothing stacks with anything at TRUEWERK (promos, rewards points, and the ID.me discount are all mutually exclusive). Archetype: **E (multi-audience identity discount; the identity discount basically is the savings path)**, with a small cashback sideline (Rakuten ~2–3%).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Nothing combines at TRUEWERK.** The help center is explicit: identity discounts "cannot be combined with any other offer or promotion," and promo codes and rewards points can't share a transaction. One lever per order.
2. **Military 15% + sale price:** the discount "auto-applies to qualified products" — the help center doesn't state whether marked-down items qualify; treat sale-item applicability as **not stated** and let checkout decide.
3. **Rakuten ~2–3% + any coupon code:** standard Rakuten rules — non-Rakuten codes risk voiding cashback; at 2–3% the portal is a rounding error, never the strategy.
4. **Rewards points bank between orders** — earn on a code order, redeem on a later code-free order.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add items to your cart at truewerk.com.
2. At checkout, click the green **ID.me** button (if you don't see it, select "Check out as guest" — the button appears at the top of checkout).
3. Sign in or create an ID.me account and verify your group.
4. The 15% applies automatically to qualified products. (No in-store channel — TRUEWERK is direct-to-consumer online.)

### How Verification Works
ID.me verifies your status once against your ID.me account; the discount then auto-applies at checkout when you're signed in through the ID.me button. Re-verification frequency is not stated. Note: loyalty points earned while checking out as a guest take ~40 days to post to your account.

- Applies to "qualified products" — whether sale/clearance items qualify is not stated; check at checkout.
