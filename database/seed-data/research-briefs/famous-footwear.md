# Famous Footwear Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Famous Footwear offers an everyday **10% military discount**, valid both **online and in store** in the **U.S. only**. Online, eligible shoppers verify through **SheerID** and receive a unique one-time promo code by email; in store, shoppers show valid military ID at checkout. The online offer is open to a broad group — **active duty, reservists/National Guard, veterans/retirees, military spouses, dependents, and families including Gold Star families** — but the **in-store offer is narrower per the official FAQ** (active duty, reservist/National Guard, veteran/retiree). The single most important caveat: the discount **cannot be stacked or combined with any other promotions or offers** (it is combinable only with Rewards Cash), and a long list of brands (Nike, Birkenstock, Crocs, Brooks, Dr. Martens, etc.) is excluded. Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program, accessed June 24, 2026.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the Famous Footwear Discount Program page (famousfootwear.com/discount-program) and click the verification button.
2. Complete the **SheerID** verification form; SheerID may ask you to upload documentation proving eligibility.
3. After you're verified, a **unique 10% promo code** is shown and emailed to you (from the address noted on the page; check junk mail if you don't see it).
4. Shop famousfootwear.com and enter the promo code in the promo code box at checkout, before purchasing.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop at a U.S. Famous Footwear store.
2. At checkout, present valid military identification — an active, reserve, or retired government-issued military ID card; VA/VIC card; driver's license with a "V" designation; or other military identification.
3. The associate applies the 10% discount before you pay.

### How Verification Works
Online verification is handled by **SheerID**, a third-party service that confirms military status on Famous Footwear's behalf. You may be asked to provide basic personal information and upload supporting documentation. Verification typically takes a few minutes, after which you receive a single-use promo code (codes are tied to the recipient and are non-transferable). If your code doesn't arrive or doesn't work, check spam and contact Famous Footwear Customer Service at **1-888-869-1053**. Note: online codes expire at 11:59 PM CST on December 31 of the year issued, and you may receive up to five codes per 30 days — so re-verification is required for ongoing use rather than being a one-time, permanent benefit. In store, verification is done at the register each time by showing ID.

### FAQ

#### Does Famous Footwear offer a military discount?
Yes. Famous Footwear offers an everyday 10% military discount, valid online and in store in the U.S. (Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program, accessed June 24, 2026.)

#### How much is the Famous Footwear military discount?
It is 10% off one purchase. (Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans and retirees qualify both online and in store. For the **online** offer, military spouses, dependents, and families including Gold Star families also qualify. The official **in-store** FAQ lists only active duty, reservist/National Guard, and veteran/retiree. (Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program.)

#### How do I verify my status?
Online, you verify through SheerID on the discount-program page and receive a unique code by email. In store, you show valid military ID at the register. (Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program.)

#### Does Famous Footwear use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Famous Footwear uses **SheerID** for online military verification. (A Famous Footwear listing also appears on ID.me Shop, but the brand's own discount-program page directs online verification to SheerID.) (Sources: famousfootwear.com/discount-program; shop.sheerid.com/offers/10-off-for-military-2.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Show valid military identification (active/reserve/retired military ID, VA/VIC card, driver's license with "V" designation, or other military ID) at checkout. (Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program.)

#### What is excluded?
Many brands are excluded (including Nike, Birkenstock, Crocs, Brooks, Dr. Martens, Vans, and more — see full list), along with gift cards and previously purchased merchandise. The discount also can't be combined with other promotions. (Source: famousfootwear.com/exclusions.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The discount cannot be stacked or combined with any other promotions or offers; it is combinable only with Reward Cash. (Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program.)

#### Does Famous Footwear offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Not stated in the official source. Famous Footwear's published discount program covers military members only; no first-responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount was found on official pages as of June 24, 2026.

#### How many times can I use the online code, and does it expire?
Online, you may receive up to five discount codes per person per 30 days, and each code expires at 11:59 PM CST on December 31 of the year it was issued. Each code is single-use and non-transferable. (Source: famousfootwear.com/discount-program.)
