# Dave's Hot Chicken — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one in-store visit — a Dave's #1 combo (2 sliders, fries, drink), ~$11.99 before tax.

## Headline finding

Dave's Hot Chicken publishes **no national military or veteran discount**. There is no offer on the brand's website, no dedicated military page, no ID.me / SheerID / GovX / WeSalute partnership, and no military tier in the Frequent Fryer rewards app. Because Dave's is a franchise-heavy chain, an *individual* location can choose to give a courtesy discount at the register, but that is local, discretionary, and unverifiable from any primary source — treat it as "ask, don't count on it." The genuine best repeatable savings path for a service member is the **free Frequent Fryer rewards app** (10 points per $1, free drink on first order, free food at 300/600/1,200-point tiers) — order direct (not DoorDash/Uber Eats) so points count. This is the classic **fast-food franchise / loyalty-app archetype**: no chain-wide discount, franchisee discretion at best, and the app is the real value.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **Rewards are single-use per order:** one reward redeemed per order, once per day — you cannot stack two Frequent Fryer rewards on the same order.

3. **No published military discount exists to stack with anything** — so there is no military-discount-plus-rewards combination to verify. Any franchise-level courtesy discount is off-menu and its stackability with app rewards is undefined (ask the store).
