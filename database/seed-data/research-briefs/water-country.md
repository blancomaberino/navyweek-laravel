# Water Country USA — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **single-day admission** to Water Country USA (Williamsburg, VA) for an active-duty family, and separately a **12-month membership** for repeat visitors who also go to Busch Gardens Williamsburg.

## Headline finding

**Water Country USA (a United Parks / Busch Gardens Williamsburg property) is included in the free "Waves of Honor" military program — active-duty service members get one FREE single-day ticket plus up to three FREE tickets for direct dependents each year — and beyond that, active duty can buy up to six discounted single-day tickets (reported up to ~40% off) and discounted 1-Park/2-Park annual memberships.** Verification is through **ID.me** online or a **base ITT** office. Unlike its Tampa sibling Adventure Island, **Water Country USA is a valid Waves of Honor park**. Veterans/retirees get discounted (not free) admission. Archetype: **United Parks water park fully inside Waves of Honor — free single-day for active-duty families, deep membership value for repeat visitors.**

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. Waves of Honor free admission is **once per year** per eligible person and requires **online registration** (print tickets) — verified via ID.me; the **spouse logs in under the active-duty member's account** to claim dependent tickets.
2. Free/discount tickets are **admission only** — they exclude **separately ticketed events** (e.g. Howl-O-Scream, Christmas Town at Busch Gardens) and aren't valid at non-participating parks.
3. Discounted single-day tickets (up to 6) are valid **one year from purchase**; multi-day tickets ~6 months.
4. Buy through **one channel** — ID.me online **or** base ITT — not both on the same ticket.
5. **Veterans/retirees** get a **discount, not the free Waves of Honor ticket** — don't conflate the two.

### FAQ
#### Does Water Country USA offer a military discount?
Yes — active-duty get **free** single-day admission (self + up to 3 dependents) via Waves of Honor, plus discounted tickets and memberships; veterans get a discount.

#### How much is the Water Country USA military discount?
Active-duty single-day admission is **free** (up to 4 people/year); extra single-day tickets are reported up to ~40% off; memberships run ~$140 (1-Park) / ~$159 (2-Park).

#### Do veterans, retirees, and dependents qualify?
Direct dependents get the free active-duty tickets; **veterans and retirees get a discount, not free** admission.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **ID.me** online, or your military ID at a base ITT office.

#### Does Water Country USA use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**ID.me** (through the Waves of Honor / United Parks military program); base ITT is the in-person alternative.

#### Can I use it online and at the gate?
Register and print the free tickets **online** first; they aren't issued fresh at the gate. Memberships/discounted tickets are bought online (ID.me) or at base ITT.

#### What is excluded?
Separately ticketed events (Howl-O-Scream, Christmas Town) and non-participating parks; free admission is once per year within the dated window.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — use one channel per ticket; the military offer isn't stacked with public promos on the same admission.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to visit?
Active-duty families should use the **free Waves of Honor tickets** first; for repeat or two-park visits, the **membership** wins.

#### Does Water Country USA run a Veterans Day or summer military offer?
Veterans free/discounted admission offers have run in dated windows (historical); the active-duty Waves of Honor free ticket runs through the annual season window.
