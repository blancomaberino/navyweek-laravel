# Rawlings — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$200 Rawlings ball glove** (mid-range Heart of the Hide / Liberty Advanced class) on rawlings.com.

## Headline finding

**Rawlings does not offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** There is no military discount page on rawlings.com, no ID.me/GovX/SheerID verification, and ID.me confirms it is "not aware that Rawlings Gear offers Military discounts." Rawlings honors service symbolically (e.g., a Veterans Day REV1X ball post) but publishes no service-member price. So the honest best path is entirely **public**: the **newsletter signup code**, **seasonal/outlet sales**, **ID.me Shop cashback** (a rebate, not a military discount), and — for tax savings — **buying Rawlings through a military exchange** where it's stocked. A competitor note matters: **Wilson (Rawlings' chief rival) does run a 15% military discount**, so a service member choosing between comparable gloves can effectively "get a military discount" by buying the Wilson equivalent. Archetype: **no-military-discount manufacturer where savings come from sales, cashback, the exchange, and a discount-carrying competitor.**
