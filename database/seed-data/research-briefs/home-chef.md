# Home Chef — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a standard **4-meals × 2-servings weekly box ≈ $80 + shipping** (~$9.99/serving list), measured over the **first box** and over **a year of weekly boxes** — subscription math needs both views.

## Headline finding

Home Chef (Kroger-owned) runs a real, official **Heroes program via ID.me**: **50% off your first box (up to $60), 10% off all future orders for life, free shipping on the first box, and a free item ("free dessert") for life** — for military, first responders, doctors, nurses, hospital employees, and teachers. The unusual part is the **10% lifetime discount**: most meal-kit intro deals die after the first month, so for ongoing subscribers the Heroes program beats Home Chef's public "18 Free Meals" intro offer over any horizon past a couple of months. Archetype: **E (identity discount IS the path) with an F overlay** — the fork is Heroes vs. the public new-customer promo, and they're either/or.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + program terms)
1. **Heroes vs the public intro offer is either/or.** Home Chef doesn't state they combine, and one promotional price applies per signup — treat as mutually exclusive (flagged as inference from standard terms; Home Chef publishes no stacking language for Heroes).
2. **WeSalute's version explicitly does not combine**: "May not be combined with other offers," one per household, new customers only, and the % "does not apply to any delivery charge."
3. **The 10% is a lifetime rate on future orders** — it layers on your ongoing subscription pricing, which is the rare durable stack in meal kits.
4. **Retail (Kroger shelf) kits sit outside all of it** — no verification, no discount.

### How To Redeem Online
**New customers**
1. Go to homechef.com/heroes and start signup.
2. Verify your status with ID.me when prompted in the signup flow.
3. The 50% first-box discount (up to $60) and free first-box shipping apply; pick your meals.
4. The 10% + free item apply automatically to subsequent boxes.

**Existing customers**
1. Sign in and open the **payment page** of your account.
2. Click the ID.me verification option and verify.
3. The ongoing Heroes benefits attach to your future orders (the 50% first-box piece is new-customers-only).

### How Verification Works
ID.me confirms your group during signup (or from the account payment page). Home Chef doesn't state a re-verification cadence; the discount is described as applying to future orders once verified ("lifetime"). Verification support runs through ID.me.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- First-box 50% is **capped at $60** and (per Military.com) **new customers only**.
- The 10% applies to orders, not delivery charges (explicit in the WeSalute variant; Home Chef's own page doesn't address delivery fees — flagged).
- One promotional signup offer per account — don't expect Heroes to stack on 18-Free-Meals.
- Meal availability and some offers vary by state/delivery zone.
- Skipping/canceling: standard Home Chef weekly-deadline rules apply (edit meals by the weekly cutoff).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Existing customers: verify anyway** — the payment-page ID.me route adds 10% + the free item without being a new customer.
- **Trial shoppers: compare the public 18-Free-Meals screen** against the Heroes screen before picking a lane.

### FAQ
#### Does Home Chef offer a military discount?
Yes — the Heroes program: 50% off your first box (up to $60), 10% off all future orders, free shipping on the first box, and a free item for life, verified via ID.me.
#### Do veterans, Guard/Reserve, and dependents qualify?
Yes — Military.com's listing of the program names active duty, veterans, retirees, Reserve/National Guard, and dependents.
#### Does Home Chef use ID.me or SheerID?
The official Heroes page names ID.me. (Some listings mention SheerID; follow the official page.)
#### Can existing customers get the discount?
Yes — verify with ID.me on your account's payment page to attach the ongoing 10% and free item. The 50% first-box piece is for new customers.
#### Can I combine it with the 18 Free Meals offer or promo codes?
Treat them as either/or — Home Chef applies one signup promotion, and the WeSalute variant of this offer is explicitly non-combinable.
#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to use Home Chef?
Long-term subscribers: Heroes, hands down — it matches the intro offers up front and keeps paying 10% forever. Trying it for a month then canceling: compare the public 18-Free-Meals structure first.
#### Is there a nurse, teacher, or first responder discount?
Yes — the same Heroes program covers doctors, nurses, hospital employees, first responders, and teachers.
#### Does the discount work on Home Chef kits sold in Kroger stores?
No — retail shelf kits are a separate channel with no verification or Heroes pricing.
