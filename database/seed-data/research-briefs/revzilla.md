# RevZilla — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$500 full-price motorcycle-gear cart from non-excluded brands** (e.g., a $350 textile jacket + $150 gloves — helmets deliberately excluded because most helmet brands sit on RevZilla's exclusion list).

## Headline finding

**RevZilla runs a real, official military & first-responder discount, verified in-cart through GovX ID — but it never publishes the percentage.** Eligibility is "Active Duty or Retired U.S. Police, Military, Fire, and EMS Members" (note: *retired*, not all veterans), the discount applies automatically at checkout after verification, it **stacks with nothing**, and an exclusion list of 70+ brands (Alpinestars, Dainese, REV'IT!, Shoei, Arai, AGV, HJC, Shark, all tires and wheels) carves out much of the premium catalog. Aggregators quote 5% (some claim 10–25%) — unverified. Because the discount is full-price-only and non-combinable, **closeout/sale pricing plus RevZilla's public 10% email sign-up code frequently beats it**, and the paid **RPM membership (5% back + 10% off 70+ brands)** is the bigger lever for regular buyers. Archetype: **C (real discount the sale often beats)** with an **E** flavor (gated amount, GovX ID mechanic).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military/first-responder discount stacks with nothing.** RevZilla: "This discount may not be stacked with any other promotion, code and/or offer." That includes the 10% sign-up code, closeout pricing, and RPM's 10%-off brands — pick ONE path per order.
2. **Excluded brands kill the discount silently** — 70+ brands (most premium helmets, all tires/wheels) simply won't discount at checkout. Build the cart, verify, and read the total before paying.
3. **RPM benefits apply order-over-order** (5% back is a rebate on future spend, not an instant discount) — compare "instant military %" vs "10% off RPM brands + 5% back" per cart.
4. **Cycle Gear's in-store discount is the family's deepest** (20% exclusive brands) but in-store only with ID; the 5% online rate matches RevZilla's reported number.
5. **Cashback portals:** no RevZilla portal rate was verified this session — don't promise a portal layer; the GovX ID-gated checkout is exactly the kind that breaks tracking. (Flag for monthly re-check.)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Build your cart at revzilla.com (check your brands against the exclusion list first).
2. In the cart, click the **GovX ID** banner and verify your status (one-time; free).
3. The discount applies automatically at checkout — **read the order total** to see your actual percentage and confirm your items weren't excluded.
4. Don't enter a promo code — it will conflict with the discount rather than stack.

### How Verification Works
GovX ID is a free verification account (govx.com/t/govx-id). You verify once with service documentation; RevZilla then recognizes the verified account at checkout on future orders. If verification fails, GovX ID support handles documentation review.

### Exclusions And Fine Print

- No stacking with any promotion, code, or offer.
- Amount not published; whatever your checkout shows is the offer.
- Full-price items are the practical target — sale/closeout items already carry a better public price the discount won't beat or join.

### FAQ
#### Does RevZilla offer a military discount?
Yes — an official discount for active-duty or retired U.S. military, police, fire, and EMS, verified via GovX ID in your cart and applied automatically at checkout.
#### How much is the RevZilla military discount?
RevZilla doesn't publish the amount anywhere on its site. Shopper reports and aggregators suggest about 5% on full-price items, but the only reliable number is the one your verified cart shows.
#### Do veterans qualify for the RevZilla military discount?
RevZilla's page says "Active Duty or Retired." Veterans who separated without retiring aren't stated as eligible — GovX ID verification may still process, but don't count on the discount until your cart confirms it.
#### Does RevZilla use ID.me or GovX?
GovX ID. ID.me lists no RevZilla military offer.
#### Can I stack it with promo codes or sale prices?
No. RevZilla states the discount "may not be stacked with any other promotion, code and/or offer" — and it effectively applies to full-price items from non-excluded brands.
#### What brands are excluded?
70+ brands, including Alpinestars, Dainese, REV'IT!, most major helmet makers (Shoei, Arai, AGV, HJC, Shark), and all tires and wheels.
#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy at RevZilla?
First order: the public 10% sign-up code. After that: closeout pricing when your item hits it, or RPM membership if you buy gear a few times a year. Use the military discount for full-price, non-excluded, non-first orders.
#### Is there a first responder discount at RevZilla?
Yes — the same program covers active or retired police, fire, and EMS under the same terms.
#### Does Cycle Gear have a better military discount?
In-store, yes: 20% off Cycle Gear's exclusive brands and 10% off third-party brands with valid ID (online it's 5%). Same GovX ID mechanic, same no-stacking rule, similar exclusions.
