# Avis Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Avis offers a military and veteran car rental discount of **up to 35% off "Pay Now" rates**, delivered exclusively through enrollment in **WeSalute+** (the paid premium tier of WeSalute, founded as Veterans Advantage). Eligible groups are U.S. military veterans, active-duty service members, National Guard & Reservists, retirees, and their immediate family members. There is no public Avis "military rate" you can claim simply by showing a military ID at the counter — the savings require a WeSalute+ membership, which assigns the member an Avis Worldwide Discount (AWD) number entered at booking. The single most important caveat: the headline "up to 35%" applies only to Avis **Pay Now** rates and only to time-and-mileage charges (not taxes, fees, or optional products), car group X is excluded, and the offer cannot be combined with any other AWD number. First responders and government employees who are not eligible for WeSalute can instead access Avis rates through **GOVX** (separate channel, GovX ID verification). A separate WeSalute offer page also documents a limited-time enhancement (free Avis Preferred Plus upgrade, $15 off $175+ on 3+ day rentals) **valid until June 30, 2026**.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Confirm eligibility and enroll in WeSalute+ at wesalute.com (paid membership). The basic free WeSalute tier and WeSalute+ paid tier differ — confirm which tier unlocks the Avis rate during sign-up.
2. Obtain your assigned Avis Worldwide Discount (AWD) number from your WeSalute account.
3. Go to Avis.com and select your pick-up location and rental dates.
4. Enter your AWD number in the discount/AWD field when booking (choose a "Pay Now" rate to get the up-to-35% savings).
5. Review the vehicle list — the discounted prices appear on the next page. Note that car group X and some rates may not be eligible.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Enroll in WeSalute+ and obtain your assigned Avis AWD number before you travel.
2. Make a reservation that includes your AWD number (recommended; some rates and locations require an advance reservation).
3. At the Avis counter, present your reservation; counter and phone redemption are supported per WeSalute. Bring a valid driver's license and meet Avis age/driver/credit requirements.
4. Confirm the discount is reflected before signing; the discount applies to time-and-mileage charges only, not taxes, fees, or optional products.

### How Verification Works
Eligibility is verified by WeSalute, not Avis. Members enroll in WeSalute+ (a paid membership; pricing and tiers are set by WeSalute and change periodically). WeSalute issues a secure WeSalute+ ID and assigns the member an Avis AWD number used at booking. WeSalute states membership applies to active duty, retirees, veterans, current or former guard & reserve, and immediate family members. This is a membership program — eligibility is tied to maintaining an active WeSalute+ membership, not a one-time check. For first responders/government who use GOVX instead, verification is via GovX ID (instant online status verification without uploading documents).

### FAQ

#### Does Avis offer a military discount?
Yes. Avis offers a military and veteran discount of up to 35% off "Pay Now" rates, delivered through WeSalute+ (founded as Veterans Advantage). [Avis WeSalute partner page]

#### How much is the Avis military discount?
Up to 35% off Avis "Pay Now" rates, applied to the time-and-mileage portion of the rental only. "Up to 35%" is a maximum — actual savings vary by location, dates, and car group. [Avis WeSalute partner page]

#### Do veterans, spouses, and family members qualify?
Yes. The offer covers U.S. veterans, active-duty, National Guard & Reservists, retirees, and their immediate family members who enroll in WeSalute+. [Avis WeSalute partner page]

#### How do I verify my military status for the Avis discount?
You verify through WeSalute by enrolling in WeSalute+. WeSalute issues a secure WeSalute+ ID and assigns you an Avis AWD number to use at booking. [WeSalute Avis offer page]

#### Does Avis use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For its primary military/veteran rate, Avis uses **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage). First responders and government employees can separately use **GOVX** (GovX ID). We did not find an Avis ID.me or SheerID military flow on Avis's own pages. [Avis WeSalute partner page; GOVX Avis page]

#### Is there an Avis military AWD code I can just type in?
No verified public code. The AWD number is assigned to you when you enroll in WeSalute+; it is personal to your membership. Codes circulated on coupon sites (e.g., "T765700") are not confirmed on Avis's or WeSalute's own pages — treat them as unverified. [Avis WeSalute partner page]

#### Can I use the discount in stores / at the counter?
Yes. WeSalute states the Avis discount can be used worldwide at the counter, online, and by phone, using your assigned AWD number; an advance reservation may be required at some locations. [WeSalute Avis offer page; Avis WeSalute partner page]

#### What is excluded?
Car group X; taxes and recovery/facility fees; optional products like LDW and refueling; blackout/holiday dates; non-participating locations. The discount does not apply to taxes and fees. [Avis WeSalute partner page]

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other offers?
Avis's own terms say it may not be combined with any other AWD number, promotion, or offer. WeSalute's page says it can combine with public Avis promotions but not private corporate codes. Because these differ, verify combinability at booking and treat the Avis terms as authoritative. [Avis WeSalute partner page; WeSalute Avis offer page]

#### Does Avis offer a first responder, nurse, or government discount?
First responders, law enforcement, and government employees can access Avis rates via GOVX (GovX ID). Nurses and medical professionals can access an Avis offer through WeSalute's nurse/medical service groups. [GOVX Avis page; WeSalute Avis offer page]
