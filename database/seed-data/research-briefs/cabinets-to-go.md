# Cabinets To Go Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

**Researched:** July 16, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$6,000 in-store cabinet order** (a typical mid-size kitchen).

## Executive Summary

Cabinets To Go offers a genuinely generous **35% off all cabinet collections** for **military, first responders, and educators** — but it is an **in-store-only** discount, applied manually by a sales representative after you show proof, with **no online checkout path and no ID.me/GovX/SheerID verification**. Eligible military groups are **Active Duty and Veterans** (spouses and dependents are not listed). The discount **cannot be combined with any other promotion or discount**.

The counterintuitive finding a coupon page misses: because Cabinets To Go runs a near-constant tiered **"Buy More, Save More"** public sale (currently 20%→40% by spend) plus deeper 45–60% markdowns on featured collections, the 35% military discount is **not always the best price**. For a typical kitchen order (roughly **under ~$10,000**), the military 35% wins. But on a **very large order ($15,001+)** the public sale reaches **40%** and beats it — and it requires no verification. So the honest advice is situational: verify and take 35% on normal carts; on a very large project or a deeply-discounted featured collection, compare the current public sale first, because you can't stack the two.

Brand archetype: **C — "real discount, but the sale can beat it"**, with a secondary **in-store-only service-counter** flavor (no tax-free kicker; cabinets aren't sold through the military exchanges).

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The military 35% cannot be combined with any other promotion or discount** — brand's exact wording. This includes the "Buy More, Save More" sale, featured-collection markdowns, and any promo code. You pick **one** path.

3. **Financing (Wells Fargo Cabinets To Go card) is not a discount** and is a separate decision — special-APR terms, not a percentage off. Nothing in the source says financing voids the military discount, but confirm at the showroom.
4. The **30-Day Price Match** is a separate guarantee; treat as situational, not a stack on top of the 35%.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Visit a Cabinets To Go showroom (this discount is in-store only).
2. Tell your sales representative you're claiming the military / first responder / educator discount.
3. Provide proof: **military email address, service records, or military ID** (first responders: license/state certificate, ID card, or paystub; educators: teacher's license, paystub, state certificate, business card, or school ID).
4. The 35% is applied to your cabinet order. Remember it can't be combined with any other promotion — so compare it against the current public sale first if your order is very large.

### How To Redeem Online
- There is **no online military discount**. Cabinets To Go's military/first-responder/educator offer is in-store only, applied by a sales rep. Online shoppers use the public sales and codes instead (which the military discount can't be combined with anyway).

### How Verification Works
Cabinets To Go uses **no third-party verification portal** — not ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. You verify **in person** by showing one accepted proof to the sales representative. Because it's applied manually at the showroom, practices may vary slightly by location; bring physical proof.

- In-store only; no online redemption.

- Covers "all cabinet collections"; the offer language doesn't extend to countertops or installation labor — confirm scope on your specific order.

### FAQ

#### Does Cabinets To Go offer a military discount?
Yes — **35% off all cabinet collections, in store**, for active-duty military and veterans (also first responders and educators). You show proof to a sales rep; there's no online code.

#### How much is the Cabinets To Go military discount?
**35% off** cabinet collections. It's one of the more generous home-improvement military discounts, but it's in-store only and can't be combined with other offers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify (along with active duty). The official page lists only "Active Duty, Veterans" for military — **spouses and dependents are not listed**, so ask your local showroom rather than assuming.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person, by showing one of: a **military email address, service records, or a military ID**. There's no online verification step.

#### Does Cabinets To Go use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Verification is manual, done by the sales representative in the showroom. Aggregator results pairing "Cabinets To Go" with GovX belong to a different retailer.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
**In stores only.** Cabinets To Go's military discount has no online checkout path.

#### What is excluded?
It covers cabinet collections and **cannot be combined with any other promotion or discount**. The offer language doesn't cover countertops or install labor — confirm your order's scope.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or the sale?
**No.** Both the military 35% and the public "Buy More, Save More" sale state they can't be combined with other offers — you pick one.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Cabinets To Go?
For a typical kitchen (roughly under $10,000), the **military 35%** is the best price. On a **very large order ($15,001+)**, the public sale hits **40%** and beats 35% with no verification needed. If your cabinets are in a **45–60%-off featured collection**, that public markdown wins. Because none of these stack, compare the current sale tier against 35% before you commit.

#### Does Cabinets To Go offer a first responder, teacher, or nurse discount?
First responders (police, fire, EMT) and educators (teachers, administrators, school counselors, librarians) get the **same 35%**. Nurses/healthcare and students are not listed.

#### Does Cabinets To Go run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day military sale?
The 35% military discount is **year-round**, not a holiday promo. Cabinets To Go does run big public seasonal sales (Memorial Day, Labor Day, Black Friday), but those are for everyone and can't be combined with the military discount.
