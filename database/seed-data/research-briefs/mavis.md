# Mavis — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one installed set of 4 mid-range tires at **$800 pre-tax, out-the-door**, on a brand with an active manufacturer rebate (Continental "Save $160" = $110 mail-in + $50 online-exclusive, valid 7/1/26–8/31/26).

## Headline finding

Mavis runs a **real, official, everyday 10% military/veteran discount** — 10% off the total sales price *before tax*, in-store only, at every Mavis, NTB, and Tire Kingdom location (same terms across all three sibling banners). It is **non-combinable**: it "cannot be combined with any other coupons, vouchers, or special offers," and price-match and warranty transactions are excluded. That non-combinable rule is the whole story. Because Mavis constantly runs **manufacturer tire rebates worth $100–$160** and printed **service coupons ($30 off oil, up to $80 off brakes)** that are usually larger than 10%, the official military discount is frequently **not** the best deal — the shopper often saves more by taking the rebate/coupon path and forfeiting the 10%. Archetype **G (in-store service discount where a live coupon beats the military %)**, minus Firestone's tax-free perk and WeSalute tier — Mavis has neither. Eligibility is stated only as active duty, reservists, and veterans; **spouses, dependents, and first responders are not mentioned**, so don't assert them.

**Verdict logic:**
- **No rebate on your tire →** take the flat **10% military discount** in-store ($720). It's the biggest guaranteed lever.
- **A manufacturer rebate ≥ ~$90 is running →** the **rebate path ($640) beats the flat 10% ($720)**; skip the military discount unless the store will let the *manufacturer mail-in* stack on top.
- **Ask the counter one question:** *"Can I take the 10% military discount AND still send in the manufacturer's mail-in rebate?"* If yes, that's the max ($610). If it's an either/or, **take whichever single number is bigger** — usually the rebate.
- **On services (oil/brakes/alignment):** the printed **service coupon almost always beats 10%** (a $30-off oil coupon > 10% of a ~$90 oil change). Use the coupon, not the military discount.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **The military discount does NOT combine** with any Mavis/NTB/Tire Kingdom coupon, voucher, promo code, or "special offer." Verified verbatim on all three banner pages. This is the make-or-break fact.
2. **Price-match and warranty transactions are excluded** entirely — no 10% on a price-matched quote or a warranty replacement.
3. **In-store only.** Buying online forfeits the military discount completely — and the fat manufacturer "$50 online-exclusive" rebate lives online, so you cannot get both the in-store 10% AND the online-exclusive rebate on the same purchase.
4. **Open question — manufacturer mail-in rebates:** Mavis's non-combinable rule names "coupons, vouchers, or special offers." A **manufacturer's mail-in rebate** is arguably a separate program (paid by the tire maker, not Mavis). The brand page does **not** explicitly address it. Shoppers should ask; treat any "yes, they stack" as store-level discretion, unverified.
5. **No verification-portal breakage risk** (no ID.me/GovX), because redemption is a manual ID check at the register — but that also means **no online military route exists at all**.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Choose your tires or service in-store (the discount does not apply online).
2. At checkout, tell the store manager you qualify for the Active Military/Veteran discount.
3. Present valid military identification.
4. 10% comes off your pre-tax subtotal. You cannot also apply a Mavis coupon or promo code on the same ticket.

### How To Redeem Online
Not available. Mavis states the military discount "is not applicable for online purchases." If you want the online-exclusive portion of a manufacturer rebate, you'll buy online and forgo the 10%.

### How Verification Works
There is no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute step. Verification is a **manual ID check at the register** — the store manager confirms your military ID in person each visit. Because it's manual and in-store, there's no online account and no online redemption path.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- In-store only; not valid online.
- Cannot be combined with any other coupon, voucher, promo code, or special offer.
- Price-match transactions and warranty transactions are excluded.
- Applied to the pre-tax subtotal (not a sales-tax exemption).
- Eligibility groups beyond active duty / reservists / veterans are not stated by Mavis.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Compare 10% vs. the current manufacturer rebate.** Mavis routinely runs $100–$160 tire rebates (e.g., Continental $160, Dunlop $150, Bridgestone $130). If the rebate beats 10% of your cart, take the rebate — you can't have both online.
- **Ask if the manufacturer *mail-in* rebate can ride along with the in-store 10%.** Mavis's rule bars *Mavis* coupons; a tire maker's mail-in rebate may be treated separately (store discretion, not guaranteed).
- **For services, use the printed coupon, not the 10%.** $30 off an oil change or up to $80 off brakes usually beats 10%.
- **Check a base Exchange tire center** (AAFES/NEX car-care) if you're near one — Exchange pricing is **tax-free**, a real substitute on a big installed-tire ticket.
- **Don't buy online expecting the military discount** — it's forfeited; online only makes sense to capture an online-exclusive rebate.

### FAQ

#### Does Mavis offer a military discount?
Yes. Mavis gives 10% off the pre-tax total to active duty military, reservists, and veterans, in-store, with military ID. The same offer runs at NTB and Tire Kingdom.

#### How much is the Mavis military discount?
10% off your total sales price before tax, on all products and services.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify (with military ID). Mavis names only active duty, reservists, and veterans — **spouses and dependents are not mentioned**, so don't assume they're covered; ask at the store.

#### How do I verify my status?
There's no online portal. You show valid military identification to the store manager at checkout each visit.

#### Does Mavis use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Verification is a manual ID check in-store; there is no third-party verification partner and no online military route.

#### Can I use the military discount online?
No. Mavis states it "is not applicable for online purchases." It's in-store only.

#### What is excluded?
Price-match transactions and warranty transactions are excluded, and the discount can't be combined with any coupon, voucher, or special offer.

#### Can I combine it with a promo code, sale, or manufacturer rebate?
Not with Mavis coupons, promo codes, or special offers — those are explicitly non-combinable. A tire manufacturer's mail-in rebate *may* be treated separately (store discretion, unverified). The online-exclusive part of a rebate can't be combined because the military discount is in-store only.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy tires from Mavis?
Compare two numbers: (1) 10% off your cart, and (2) the current manufacturer rebate/service coupon. Take the bigger one — on tires with a $100–$160 rebate running, the rebate usually wins. Best case is if the counter lets the manufacturer mail-in ride on top of the 10% (ask; not guaranteed).

#### Does Mavis offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No such program is published. Mavis's discount page addresses military/veteran (and mentions senior citizens for ID purposes) only.

#### Does Mavis run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Mavis promotes its everyday 10% around Veterans Day alongside public tire deals, but there's no evidence of an *extra* veteran-only markdown. Public seasonal sales run at Memorial Day/Black Friday but are non-combinable with the military discount.
