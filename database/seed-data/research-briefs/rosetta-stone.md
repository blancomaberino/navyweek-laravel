# Rosetta Stone — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Rosetta Stone Lifetime "Unlimited Languages" subscription** (all ~25 languages, one-time payment) — Rosetta Stone's flagship product, regularly listed **$299–$399** and on near-constant public sale at **~$149–$199** in 2026.

## Headline finding

Rosetta Stone's *military* story has two parts, and the honest answer is different from what most coupon pages say.

**1) The paid consumer discount is small and easily beaten.** Rosetta Stone advertises a modest **10% military discount** (military.com lists it for Active Duty and Military Retirees, U.S., online, verification required), with **WeSalute** as its official military-verification partner since 2024. But Rosetta Stone runs **near-constant public sales** on its Lifetime Unlimited plan (down to ~$149–$199 from a $299–$399 list), so a 10% code off list is marginal and frequently **matched or beaten by an everyday public promo** — and WeSalute's own headline military offer (**Lifetime Unlimited for $149, no renewal fee**) is roughly the *same price the general public pays during a normal sale.* Archetype: **subscription/digital brand whose public sale usually wins** (like Babbel), with a token verified discount layered on top.

**2) The famous "free Rosetta Stone through the military" is now mostly historical.** The direct Army–Rosetta Stone contract expired around 2011; DoD MWR/base libraries offered it for years afterward. **Today, most branches' free digital-library language benefit is Transparent Language Online (Navy/USMC/USCG MWR) or Mango — not Rosetta Stone.** Military OneSource's current MWR digital-library page lists no Rosetta Stone. Free Rosetta Stone access still exists at *some* installations (base library/education center/unit funding) but is now the exception, not a DoD-wide benefit — **verify with your local MWR/library before assuming it's free.**

Rosetta Stone is owned by **IXL Learning** (acquired March 2021). **Best move:** check your base library/MWR for any *free* language platform first (often Transparent Language, sometimes still Rosetta Stone); if you're buying Rosetta Stone, take the **Lifetime Unlimited public sale (~$149–$199)** — the 10% military code is marginal and the WeSalute $149 lifetime is about the same as the public deal.
