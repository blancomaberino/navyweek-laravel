# TravisMathew — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$200 apparel order** (e.g., two ~$95 polos) on travismathew.com.

## Headline finding

**TravisMathew publishes no on-site military discount and no ID.me/SheerID verification on travismathew.com.** Its own policy page is blunt: "TravisMathew doesn't offer discount coupons or codes," and the only code it names is a **one-time 20% off your first online order for newsletter signup.** ID.me confirms it is "not aware that TravisMathew offers Military discounts." The **one verified military-specific path is the GovX marketplace** (govx.com/m/2104/travis-mathew) — a **separate storefront** where verified military/first-responders buy TravisMathew items at member pricing; TravisMathew does **not** publish that exact percentage publicly (it's member-gated). So for a **first-time buyer, the public 20% newsletter code usually beats GovX;** for repeat buyers, GovX or a seasonal sale is the play. Archetype: **premium apparel brand with NO official service discount, where the best move is code-stacking/sale-timing on the brand site, with GovX as a separate marketplace option.**
