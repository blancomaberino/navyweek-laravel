# Peloton Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Peloton runs a "Special Pricing" program that gives verified Military members discounts on both Peloton equipment and the Peloton App. On its own Military offer page, Peloton lists the eligible military community as "Active military personnel, veterans, reservists, and registered dependents." Verified members get the Peloton App at reduced rates — App One at $9.99/mo (vs. $12.99) or $99/year, and App+ at $19.99/mo (vs. $24.99) or $199/year — and discounted equipment (Cross Training Bike, Bike+, Tread, Tread+, Row+, and Refurbished Original Bike/Bike+). Peloton states equipment "discount amounts vary per-package and per-product" and does not publish a single fixed military equipment percentage on the offer page; the program landing page advertises "up to $600 off equipment" across special-pricing groups. Eligibility is confirmed through a verification partner before checkout, the offer is US-only, cannot be combined with other offers/referral codes/discounts, and there is one discount per eligible Product or App. The single most important caveat: the exact equipment dollar/percentage savings is not stated per-product in official copy, and the current military verification provider is ambiguous (see Open Questions) — Peloton's page says "our verification partner" without naming it, while ID.me's own brand page states Peloton does not use ID.me for Military verification.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Peloton's Special Pricing page (onepeloton.com/offers/specialpricing) and select the Military group.
2. Choose the equipment package or App membership offer you want.
3. Verify your status by entering qualifying information, which is checked by Peloton's verification partner.
4. Create or sign in to your Peloton account; once verified, the special pricing applies automatically in your cart at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Peloton's special pricing is verified and redeemed online at onepeloton.com. No in-store/register redemption process is stated in the official source.

### How Verification Works
Per Peloton's offer page, you enter qualifying information that is "checked by our verification partner" before the discount unlocks; once verified, pricing applies automatically in your cart. Peloton's page does not name the partner in the copy reviewed. Note: ID.me's own Peloton brand page states that Peloton does not use ID.me for Military verification and that military members "verify their status directly with Peloton," while some editorial coverage reports Peloton moved special-pricing verification from SheerID to ID.me. Because the primary sources conflict, treat the specific provider, account requirements, and re-verification frequency as unconfirmed and direct readers to Peloton's offer page and support to verify. Do not state a re-verification cadence (e.g., "verify once" or "3x per year") as fact — it is not confirmed in Peloton's primary copy.

### FAQ

#### Does Peloton offer a military discount?
Yes. Peloton's "Special Pricing" program provides verified military members with discounts on Peloton equipment and the Peloton App. (Source: Peloton Military offer page.)

#### How much is the Peloton military discount?
For the App, verified members pay $9.99/mo for App One (vs. $12.99) or $99/year, and $19.99/mo for App+ (vs. $24.99) or $199/year. For equipment, Peloton says "discount amounts vary per-package and per-product" and does not publish a fixed military percentage; the program landing page advertises "up to $600 off equipment" across all special-pricing groups. (Source: Peloton Military and Special Pricing pages.)

#### Do veterans, reservists, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Peloton lists the eligible military community as "active military personnel, veterans, reservists, and registered dependents." Spouses and retirees are not separately named in the current offer copy; spouse coverage would depend on dependent registration. (Source: Peloton Special Pricing landing page.)

#### How do I verify my military status?
You enter qualifying information on Peloton's offer page, which is checked by Peloton's verification partner; once verified, pricing applies in your cart. Peloton's offer copy does not name the partner. (Source: Peloton Military offer page.)

#### Does Peloton use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
This is currently unclear from primary sources. Peloton's page references "our verification partner" without naming it. ID.me's own Peloton page states Peloton does not use ID.me for Military verification and that military members verify directly with Peloton, while some editorial reports say Peloton moved from SheerID to ID.me. Verify on Peloton's offer page before relying on a specific provider. (Sources: Peloton Military offer page; ID.me Peloton Military page.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
The military special pricing is verified and redeemed online at onepeloton.com. No in-store redemption process is stated in the official source. (Source: Peloton Military offer page.)

#### What is excluded?
The discount cannot be combined with other offers, referral codes, or discounts; one discount per eligible Product or App; All-Access Membership is separate; offer is US-only, while supplies last, and void where prohibited. (Source: Peloton Military offer page.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Peloton states the offer "cannot be combined with other offers, referral codes, or other discounts." (Source: Peloton Military offer page.)

#### Does Peloton offer a first responder, healthcare, teacher, government, or student discount?
Yes — Peloton runs separate special-pricing groups for healthcare workers, first responders, educators, veterinarians, government workers, and students (students for the App offer only). Each has its own offer page. (Source: Peloton Special Pricing landing page.)

#### Does the military equipment price change if I return it?
Yes. Peloton states that if a Product purchased with the offer is returned for a refund, you are refunded the cost of the Product "less the discount amount." (Source: Peloton Military offer page.)
