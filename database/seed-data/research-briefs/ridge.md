# Ridge (Ridge Wallet / The Ridge) — Military & Veteran Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $95 aluminum Ridge Wallet (ridge.com).

## Scope note (important — two different "Ridge" brands)
This brief covers **The Ridge / Ridge Wallet at ridge.com** (wallets, rings, keycases, travel, tech accessories). It is **not** "Ridge Outdoors / Ridge Footwear" (ridgefootwear.com), a separate tactical-boot company that runs its own **10%** GovX discount. Do not conflate the two — they have different percentages and different sites. If a page ever cites 10%, that's the footwear brand, not the wallet.

## Headline finding

The Ridge (ridge.com) offers an official **20% military & family discount through GovX**: active duty, veterans, and military family members verify with **GovX ID** and get a single-use code to use at ridge.com checkout. The Ridge confirms the 20% directly (its own X/Twitter account and the GovX brand storefront). It also sells an **officially licensed U.S. Military Collection** (U.S. Army / U.S. Navy wallets and KeyCases) and donates a percentage of those sales to **Folds of Honor**. Archetype: **A/K-adjacent (verification-gated brand discount via GovX)** — GovX here is the *verification rail for a first-party 20%*, not a separate marketplace. The main competing path is Ridge's own frequent sales (kits and outlet run 25–39% off), which usually **can't** be combined with the 20% code — so during a deep sale, compare rather than stack.
