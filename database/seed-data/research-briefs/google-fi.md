# Google Fi Wireless — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Unlimited Standard** line = **$50/month** (Google Fi's mid-tier single-line unlimited plan, formerly "Simply Unlimited"; taxes/fees not included, and there is no tax-free channel for a wireless service, so all figures are pre-tax service price).

## Headline finding

**Google Fi Wireless has no standing military or veteran discount on its phone plans.** Active duty, Guard, Reserve, veterans, retirees, and dependents all pay the same published price as everyone else — $20/mo + $10/GB (Flexible), $35 (Unlimited Essentials), $50 (Unlimited Standard), or $65 (Unlimited Premium) per line. There is no "military plan," no ID.me/GovX/SheerID service discount, and no promo code that cuts the monthly bill for being a service member. This is **archetype B — no military route on the product**, with a dash of **archetype H (the "myth" brand)**: coupon aggregators fabricate a "$20 military service credit via ID.me" that does not exist for Fi service (it's a garbled version of the separate **Google Store hardware** discount).

What Google Fi *does* give military members is genuinely useful but is **not a price cut**: an **OCONUS international-usage exception**. Fi normally suspends international data if you're abroad too long, but **active-duty military and State Department employees stationed abroad** can verify through **SheerID** and use Fi overseas for **up to 9 months per verification** (re-verify to continue), and the exception extends to everyone on their group plan. That's the real military value proposition — keep your U.S. number and data working while deployed/PCS'd abroad — and it's why Fi is popular with OCONUS troops despite having no discount. The best actual dollar savings for a new service member are the **public $60 referral credit** (not military-specific), **picking a lower plan tier**, and, if you're buying the phone itself, the **Google Store's real 10% ID.me military discount on hardware** (a separate business from Fi service). If a monthly *price* discount is the priority, the honest answer is that **T-Mobile, Verizon, and AT&T do run military plan discounts and Fi does not** — that's the true competitive comparison.

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **There is no military service discount to stack** — the only military-specific Fi program is the OCONUS exception, which is a *usage policy exception*, not a discount, so "stacking" doesn't apply to it. It layers on any plan tier at no cost.
2. **The $60 referral credit is service-only** — "applied toward monthly service costs only… cannot be applied to device purchase or financing." It's a one-time new-activation credit, not combinable with itself repeatedly on one account (cap $600/yr across referrals).
3. **The Google Store 10% (ID.me) is a hardware discount on a different Google business** — it does **not** touch your Fi monthly bill. You can own both (buy the Pixel at 10% off *and* run it on Fi), but they are separate transactions, not a stack on the plan.
4. **OCONUS exception ≠ free international calling** — it preserves *data* roaming; "international calling rates still apply." Don't conflate.
5. **No cashback/portal layer exists** — recurring wireless service is not a Rakuten/TopCashback-eligible purchase; don't imply "Fi + Rakuten."

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — No plan discount. **Eligible for the OCONUS international-usage exception** (SheerID) if stationed/deployed abroad.
- **State Department employees abroad** — Same OCONUS exception via SheerID.
- **Group-plan members of a verified account holder** — Covered by that account's OCONUS exception.
- **Veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, dependents** — **No plan discount.** Not named for the OCONUS exception (which is scoped to active-duty & State Dept abroad); verify your specific status at goo.gle/fi-sheerid.
- **Buying a Pixel/Nest?** — Military (and first responders, nurses, teachers, government, students) get **10% off Google Store hardware via ID.me** — separate from Fi service.

### How To Redeem — OCONUS International Exception (active-duty & State Dept abroad)
1. Go to **goo.gle/fi-sheerid** (Google Fi's government-employment verification via SheerID).
2. Upload **government-issued documents** showing your full name, branch (Military or State Department), and proof you work in another country. **Do not upload a photo of your military ID** — Google's page explicitly says not to.
3. Once SheerID verifies, use Fi's international data **up to 9 months** without suspension; **re-verify** to continue. International *calling* rates still apply.

### How To Redeem — $60 Referral Credit (public, any new signup)
1. Get a friend's Fi referral code (or find one; both parties benefit).
2. Activate a **full-service** Fi plan with the code — data-only SIMs and new group-plan members don't qualify.
3. Keep service active **30 days** and pay your first bill; you and your friend each get a **$60 credit** toward service (not device). Cap: $600/yr in referral credits.

### How To Redeem — Google Store 10% (hardware, via ID.me)
1. At the **Google Store**, verify with **ID.me** (military/first responder/nurse/teacher/government/student).
2. Get **~10% off eligible hardware** (Pixel phones, Nest, etc.). This does **not** discount Fi service — it's a separate purchase you then activate on Fi.

### How Verification Works
Two different providers for two different things: **SheerID** verifies government/military employment for the **OCONUS usage exception** on Fi service (a policy exception, not a discount). **ID.me** verifies military status for the **Google Store hardware** discount (a different Google business). There is **no verification path that produces a discount on your Fi monthly bill**, because no such discount exists. Google can change or end any of these programs at any time.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **No military plan discount** — the central fact. Ignore any "$20 credit" or "ID.me Fi discount" claim.
- **OCONUS exception is data-only** — international calling rates still apply; it lasts up to 9 months then requires re-verification; it's scoped to active-duty military & State Dept employees stationed abroad.
- **Referral credit is service-only** — can't be applied to device purchase/financing; new full-service activation required; $600/yr cap.
- **Google Store 10% is hardware-only** — verify the exact percentage and eligible items at checkout; it never touches Fi service pricing.

### FAQ

#### Does Google Fi offer a military discount?
**No.** Google Fi Wireless has no military or veteran discount on its phone plans. Everyone pays the published price. Its military-specific benefit is a usage exception for using Fi abroad, not a price cut.

#### How much is the Google Fi military discount?
There isn't one. Any "$20 military credit" or percentage-off you see on coupon sites is not real for Fi service — the "$20" is just Fi's Flexible base plan price, and the "10% ID.me" figure belongs to the separate **Google Store** hardware discount.

#### Do veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, or dependents qualify for anything?
Not for a plan discount. The only military-scoped Fi program — the OCONUS international-usage exception — is written for **active-duty military and State Department employees stationed abroad** (and their group-plan members). Verify your status at goo.gle/fi-sheerid.

#### Does Google Fi use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
For the **OCONUS usage exception**, Fi uses **SheerID** (not ID.me). The **Google Store** (hardware, a different product) uses **ID.me** for a 10% discount. Neither produces a discount on your Fi monthly service bill.

#### What is the Google Fi military benefit if it's not a discount?
The **OCONUS international-usage exception**: after SheerID verification, active-duty and State Dept members stationed abroad can use Fi's international data for **up to 9 months** without suspension (re-verify to continue), covering everyone on their group plan. International calling rates still apply.

#### Can I combine a military status with promo codes or credits?
There's no military service discount to combine. The public **$60 referral** credit (service-only, new activations) and the **Google Store 10%** (hardware only) are separate levers anyone eligible can use.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to use Google Fi?
Pick the **right plan tier** (Essentials $35 or Flexible $20 + $10/GB if you're light-data), use a **$60 referral** at signup, and buy your **phone through Google Store with ID.me** for ~10% off. If you're deploying, verify the **OCONUS exception**. If a lower bill outright is the goal, compare **T-Mobile/Verizon/AT&T military plans**.

#### Does Google Store offer a military discount?
Yes — a separate business from Fi. Google **Store** gives military members (and first responders, nurses, teachers, government, students) about **10% off eligible hardware** (Pixel, Nest) via **ID.me**. That discount does not apply to Fi wireless service.

#### Does Google Fi run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No military-specific plan promo was documented this pass. Fi runs occasional **public** device/plan promotions (e.g., around Black Friday) open to everyone, not military-gated. Re-check before each holiday.
