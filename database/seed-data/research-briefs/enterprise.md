# Enterprise Rent-A-Car Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Enterprise Rent-A-Car runs **two distinct military/government programs**, and the difference matters. For everyday personal trips, **Government Leisure Travel** gives a **5% discount off base rates** to current U.S. federal employees, active-duty members, retired federal employees, and veterans — no military-association membership required. Separately, **Official Government Travel** provides **specific contracted rates** (plus unlimited mileage in the continental US/Canada, damage and liability protection, and a full tank of gas) for federal renters traveling on official government business or temporary duty, who must show official orders or a government-issued credit card at the counter. The single most important caveat: the publicly stated leisure discount is **5% off base rates only** — it is not a flat "military discount" on the total, it does not apply to taxes/fees/surcharges, and Enterprise's official pages do not publish a specific leisure discount/coupon code, so the exact online redemption mechanic (which corporate/discount number to enter) is **not stated on the official source** and should be confirmed at booking.

> Accuracy note: Several aggregator pages claim the Enterprise military discount is obtained "through ID.me" or via a "Veterans Advantage 5% off" code, and one lists a "USAA MEMBER PROGRAM" corporate number. **None of these mechanics is stated on Enterprise's own military/government discount pages** as the route for the general military/veteran leisure discount. Enterprise's official copy says current federal employees, active-duty, retirees and veterans are eligible with **no association membership required**, and separately notes USAA members receive added benefits. Treat ID.me / Veterans Advantage / specific corporate codes as **unverified** against the primary source as of June 23, 2026.

### How Verification Works
For the **leisure** discount, Enterprise does **not** require membership in any military-related association and does **not** state use of a third-party verifier (ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute) on its own pages. Instead, "military or government identification may be asked at the time of pick-up." For **Official Government Travel**, eligibility is confirmed at the counter by showing **official orders or a government-issued credit card**, and renters must hold a valid driver's license or military ID and be 18+. Enterprise is a participating vendor under the U.S. Government Car Rental Agreement, which governs the official rates. If verification or rate questions arise, use Enterprise's Contact Us / Help & FAQs, or ask the branch directly.

### FAQ

#### Does Enterprise offer a military discount?
Yes. Enterprise offers a 5% discount off base rates for Government Leisure (personal) travel to active-duty members, veterans, retired federal employees, and current U.S. federal employees, plus separate contracted rates for Official Government Travel. No military-association membership is required.

#### How much is the Enterprise military discount?
For Government Leisure Travel, it's **5% off base rates**. For Official Government Travel, Enterprise provides specific contracted government rates rather than a fixed percentage.

#### What's the difference between Government Leisure and Official Travel?
Government Leisure Travel is off-duty/personal travel (5% off base rates). Official Government Travel is for official government business or temporary duty and uses contracted rates with extra benefits, requiring official orders or a government credit card at pickup.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans are explicitly named as eligible for the leisure discounted rates. Enterprise's official page does **not** state whether military spouses or dependents qualify — confirm directly with Enterprise.

#### How do I verify my status?
For the leisure discount, no association membership is needed, but a military or government ID may be requested at pickup. For Official Government Travel, show your official orders or a government-issued credit card at the counter.

#### Does Enterprise use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Enterprise's official military/government pages do **not** state use of any third-party verification service for this program. Claims that the discount runs "through ID.me" or "Veterans Advantage" appear on third-party sites and are not confirmed by Enterprise's own pages.

#### Can I use the discount in stores / at the branch?
Yes. All Enterprise locations worldwide accept the military/government discounts. Present a military/government ID (leisure) or official orders / government credit card (official travel) as applicable.

#### What is excluded?
The leisure discount is 5% off **base rates** only — not taxes, fees, or surcharges. Enterprise doesn't publish a full exclusion list or a specific leisure promo code, and stacking with other offers isn't stated, so confirm your rate at booking.

#### Does Enterprise offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not as part of this program. Enterprise's military/government discount pages cover federal employees, active-duty, retirees, and veterans only. First responders, teachers, nurses, and students are not listed.
