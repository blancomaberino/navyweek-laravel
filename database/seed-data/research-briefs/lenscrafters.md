# LensCrafters — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **complete pair of prescription eyeglasses at LensCrafters — $400 pre-tax** (a mid-range designer frame ~$250 + single-vision lenses with anti-reflective coating ~$150). This reflects LensCrafters' designer-heavy inventory; their entry "Great Value Package" complete pair starts at $99, and their own guidance puts a typical order around $100–$200+, so $400 is a realistic mid-tier ticket that also exposes the sale-vs-discount fork clearly.

## Headline finding

LensCrafters (a Luxottica / EssilorLuxottica brand) has a **real military discount: 15% off eyewear and 10% off contact lenses, verified through ID.me** — that's the exact figure shown on LensCrafters' ID.me store page. It is the honest everyday answer, but for this brand it is **usually not the best deal**. LensCrafters runs **deep, near-permanent public sales** (currently **40% off prescription glasses**, **50% off lenses**, second-pair 50% off), and the 15% military discount **cannot be combined with any other offer or with vision insurance** — so on almost any real cart, a public sale or your vision benefit beats the 15%, and you have to pick one.

The single most important fact for a military shopper is the **insurance fork**: TRICARE-eligible families with FEDVIP vision coverage, or anyone with EyeMed/VSP, will almost always pay less by **running the purchase through insurance than by taking the 15% discount** — and the two don't stack. **Active-duty service members generally get prescription eyewear free through military optometry (MTF)** and shouldn't pay LensCrafters retail at all. Archetype: **C — real discount, but the sale (or here, insurance) usually beats it**, with a secondary **E — ID.me multi-community identity discount** (LensCrafters also lists military, government, teacher, nurse and first-responder tiers through ID.me, with no public-code or cashback game to stack). Ignore the coupon sites advertising "50% / 60% off lenses" or "20–35% off frames" as *military* offers — those are the public lens sale, the AAA member deal, or fabrications. The only verified military figure is **15% eyewear / 10% contacts via ID.me**.

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **The 15% military discount does NOT combine with vision insurance** — this is the category-defining fork. You either run the order through TRICARE FEDVIP / EyeMed / VSP **or** take the discount, never both. For anyone with real coverage, insurance almost always wins.
2. **The 15% does NOT combine with LensCrafters' public sales or other offers.** The current 40%-off-glasses / 50%-off-lenses promos and the 15% are **mutually exclusive** — pick the lower price (the sale usually is).
3. **The AAA member discount is also non-combinable** with insurance or other offers, and **excludes premium brands** (Ray-Ban, Prada, Oakley, Coach). It's a separate either/or, not a stack.
4. **ID.me is a single identity verification**, applied online at checkout ("Verify with ID.me"); it is one order-level discount, not a code you layer on other codes.
5. **Cashback is not a reliable stack** — no confirmed LensCrafters portal rate this pass, and identity checkouts typically break tracking. Don't rely on "ID.me + portal."
6. **Contacts vs. eyewear are separate lines** — the military tier is 15% on eyewear, 10% on contact lenses; verify each at checkout.
