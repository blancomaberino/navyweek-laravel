# Chrysler — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one new eligible Chrysler (e.g. a ~$40,000–$60,000 Pacifica or Voyager minivan purchase or lease).

## Headline finding

Chrysler runs a real, official **$500 Military Bonus Cash allowance** on select new 2027 and 2026 Chrysler-brand vehicles, verified through **SheerID** (the Stellantis military verification flow) and redeemed at a franchised dealer. It is a **flat $500 stackable dealer incentive** — not a percentage, not an online code. The right play is to **claim the $500 and stack it on whatever public national incentive is live**, since the seasonal public offer (national bonus cash / APR / lease deal) is usually worth far more than $500. Archetype: **auto-incentive** (manufacturer bonus cash, not a retail % discount). Same structure, provider, and $500 amount as sibling Stellantis brands (Jeep, Ram, Dodge) — only the eligible model list differs.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. The $500 Military Bonus Cash is **additive to other Chrysler incentives** — the FAQ says you "may also qualify for other offers from the Chrysler Brand." (Source: chrysler.com military-incentive FAQ.)
2. **Dealer-redeemed only** via a SheerID authorization number; no online checkout code. (Source: same.)
3. Final pricing, tax, title, registration and dealer discount are negotiated at the dealer; "pricing and offers may change at any time without notification." (Source: chrysler.com footer disclosure.)
4. Not stated in official source: whether the $500 combines with employee/affiliate/supplier pricing — confirm with the dealer.

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty — eligible
- Active Reserve — eligible
- Retired military / retired military reserve — eligible
- 100% Disabled Veteran — eligible (separate "Disabled Military" verification button)
- Honorably discharged veteran within 12 months of discharge — eligible
- Spouse of deceased eligible military — eligible per FAQ
- General veterans past the 12-month window, other dependents — Not stated as eligible for the $500 program

### How To Redeem
1. Go to chrysler.com/incentives/military-incentive.html.
2. Click "Get Active/Retired Military Authorization Number" (or the "Get Disabled Military Authorization Number" button) — opens the SheerID (Stellantis Military) flow.
3. Verify your status; SheerID issues an authorization number.
4. Bring the number to a franchised US Chrysler dealer when you buy or lease an eligible model (Pacifica / Voyager).
5. Ask the dealer to apply the $500 on top of any current national/regional/dealer incentives.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **SheerID** via the Stellantis offer flow (offers.sheerid.com/stellantis/military?brand=chrysler; the page is titled "Stellantis - Military"). You may be asked to provide qualifying documentation. There are two entry points — Active/Retired Military and Disabled Military. The authorization number is tied to the purchase.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Valid only on the specific listed models (currently 2027 Pacifica ICE; 2026 Pacifica ICE, Pacifica Hybrid, Voyager). Verify the current list.
- US franchised-dealer purchase/lease only; not an online code.
- Documentation may be required.
- Pricing/offers change without notice.
- Not stated whether it combines with employee/supplier/affiliate pricing — confirm with the dealer.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Stack the public incentive.** Chrysler's seasonal national bonus cash / APR / lease events are usually far larger than $500 — time your purchase to them and add the $500.
- **Compare Military AutoSource** if stationed overseas.
- **Negotiate the dealer discount** separately; the $500 is additive.
- Cross-shop Jeep, Ram, and Dodge — same $500 Stellantis program on their own model lists (useful if you're open to another Stellantis brand).

### FAQ
#### Does Chrysler offer a military discount?
Yes — $500 Military Bonus Cash toward select new 2027/2026 Chrysler-brand vehicles (Pacifica and Voyager), verified via SheerID and redeemed at a franchised US dealer.

#### How much is the Chrysler military discount?
A flat **$500** allowance (not a percentage). It stacks on other Chrysler incentives.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Active, Active Reserve, retired military/reserve, 100% Disabled Veterans, and honorably discharged veterans within 12 months of discharge qualify. Spouses of deceased eligible military may also receive it. Veterans past the 12-month window and other dependents are not listed.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through SheerID, from the Chrysler military-incentive page — you get an authorization number to present at the dealer.

#### Does Chrysler use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
**SheerID** (via the Stellantis offer flow).

#### Can I use it online or only in store?
In-dealer only — a dealer-redeemed authorization number, not an online checkout code.

#### What is excluded?
Any vehicle not on the current eligible list. The list is narrow (Pacifica / Voyager) and rolls with model year.

#### Can I combine it with other offers?
Yes — the FAQ says you may also qualify for other Chrysler offers, so the $500 is additive to national/dealer incentives. Employee/supplier pricing overlap isn't addressed — ask the dealer.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Chrysler?
Claim the $500, then stack it on the largest live public incentive/APR/lease deal — timing the public offer matters more than the $500. Overseas, compare Military AutoSource.

#### What if I'm stationed overseas?
Use Chrysler's partner **Military AutoSource** for tailored overseas pricing and benefits.
