# Netflix Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Netflix does not offer a military or veteran discount.** There is no service-member, veteran, first-responder, student, or teacher rate, and Netflix does not use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute to verify and discount any plan. Netflix's only published prices are its standard public tiers — **Standard with ads $8.99/mo, Standard $19.99/mo, Premium $26.99/mo** (US, confirmed on help.netflix.com). The genuine ways for a military household to save are: (1) choose the **Standard with ads** plan ($8.99), the cheapest tier; and (2) get Netflix **bundled at no extra cost through an eligible carrier package** — most notably **T-Mobile "Netflix On Us,"** which Netflix's own help center documents and which is available on T-Mobile's Military plans. The single most important caveat: any "Netflix military discount — 15% off via SheerID/ID.me" claim you see on coupon or benefits-aggregator sites is **fabricated and unverified** — no such program exists on any Netflix primary source.

> Accuracy note: Aggregator pages (perksofduty, togetherprice, worthepenny and others) variously claim Netflix gives "15% off for military via SheerID," promo codes, or USAA/Exchange/VetTix partnerships. **None of these are confirmed on netflix.com or help.netflix.com, and Netflix publishes no discounted plan.** Treat every such figure as invented. As of June 23, 2026 the only verified prices are the three public tiers above.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no military discount to redeem. To subscribe at the lowest standalone price:
1. Go to **netflix.com/signup**.
2. Choose the **Standard with ads** plan ($8.99/month), the cheapest tier.
3. Create your account and enter payment.
4. Change plans anytime at netflix.com/account.

To get Netflix free through a T-Mobile bundle:
1. Confirm your T-Mobile plan is eligible for **Netflix On Us** (T-Mobile sets eligibility; many plans including Military plans qualify).
2. Open the **T-Life app**, go to **Benefits > Entertainment**, select the **Netflix** tile.
3. Choose a Netflix service and start activation.
4. Link an existing Netflix account or create a new one to your T-Mobile package.

No. Netflix does not offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount. Everyone pays the same published prices.

There isn't one. The cheapest Netflix plan is Standard with ads at $8.99/month — the same price for everyone.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a Netflix discount?
No. Netflix has no discount for any military-affiliated group, including veterans, spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families.

#### How do I verify my military status with Netflix?
You can't, because there's no military offer to verify. Netflix does not use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute.

No. Netflix does not partner with any military-verification service for discounts. Any coupon site claiming "verify with SheerID for 15% off Netflix" is inaccurate.
