# Xfinity (Comcast) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Xfinity's current military offer is **not a cash discount or prepaid card** — it is a bundle of membership perks for verified service members and military families who are (or become) Xfinity Internet customers. On its official offer page (xfinity.com/learn/deals/military, last modified June 11, 2026), Xfinity lists: **one free Xfinity Mobile "Mobile Select" line for 2 years** (included with Xfinity Internet), an **instant upgrade to Diamond**, the top Xfinity membership tier (which includes **Peacock Premium at no extra cost**), and **NOW TV free for 1 year** (125+ live channels). Eligibility is verified after you sign in at xfinity.com/military. Eligible groups are broad: active/reserve, retired, veterans, reservists, Gold Star families, and household spouse/family members. The single most important caveat: **these benefits require an Xfinity Internet subscription** — this is a benefits package layered onto paid service, not a standalone percentage discount, and the older "$50 prepaid card" offer that still circulates on aggregator sites is no longer the offer.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator and editorial pages (Operation Military Kids, WorthEPenny, etc.) still advertise a **$50 Visa prepaid card** as the "Xfinity military discount." That framing is **outdated**. Xfinity's current official military page describes a perks bundle (free Mobile line for 2 years, Diamond membership/Peacock, NOW TV for 1 year), not a prepaid card. Only the perks bundle is confirmed on Xfinity's official page as of June 23, 2026.

### FAQ

#### Does Xfinity offer a military discount?
Yes, but it's a perks bundle rather than a percentage off. Verified military members and families with Xfinity Internet can get a free Xfinity Mobile line for 2 years, an instant Diamond membership upgrade (with Peacock Premium included), and NOW TV free for 1 year.

#### How much is the Xfinity military discount?
There's no flat percentage or dollar amount. The value is in the included benefits: a free Mobile Select line (2 years), Diamond membership plus Peacock Premium, and a free year of NOW TV. Older pages advertising a "$50 prepaid card" are out of date.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes. Xfinity lists veterans, retirees, active/reserve members, reservists, Gold Star families, and spouse/family members in the household of someone who qualifies.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Sign in at xfinity.com/military with your Xfinity ID and confirm your status. Verification is processed through SheerID; for status questions, Xfinity lists 1-833-231-6446.

#### Does Xfinity use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Per Xfinity's support documentation, military verification is handled through **SheerID**. (Comcast/Xfinity also appears on the WeSalute marketplace, but the official offer routes verification through the xfinity.com/military sign-in flow.)

#### Do I need to be an Xfinity Internet customer?
Yes. The military perks are built around Xfinity Internet — the free Mobile Select line is included with Internet, and Xfinity Mobile requires Xfinity Internet. If you're not a member, you can join at xfinity.com or 1-800-XFINITY, then verify.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
The offer is verified and redeemed online through your Xfinity account at xfinity.com/military. You can become a member in an Xfinity Store or by phone, but the military verification itself is an online/account step. Xfinity's military page does not describe an in-store redemption flow.

#### What's excluded or limited?
The benefits require Xfinity Internet. Mobile Select data may be slowed after 50 GB during congestion; NOW TV taxes are extra and pricing can change; managing an existing Peacock subscription may be required. The free Mobile line is 2 years and NOW TV is 1 year.

#### Does Xfinity offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Xfinity's military page covers military service members and their families only. It does not state a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount.
