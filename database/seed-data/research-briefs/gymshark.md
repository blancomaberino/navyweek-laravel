# Gymshark Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Gymshark offers a **10% military/veteran discount in the United States exclusively through WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage), its verification partner since 2019. Eligible groups are active-duty military, veterans, retirees, current/former Guard & Reserve, and immediate family members. After verifying through WeSalute, members shop a private Gymshark.com store and the 10% applies at online checkout (it can be combined with public sales and free standard shipping on $75+ orders, but cannot be applied retroactively). Gymshark's own US support page lists **only WeSalute** for military — it does **not** list ID.me or SheerID, and ID.me's own shop page states it is not aware that Gymshark offers a military or first-responder discount. The single most important caveat: widely circulated aggregator claims about a "Military and First Responder Discount" verified via "ID.me or SheerID at checkout" are **not confirmed by any primary source** and appear to be inaccurate for the US store; treat them as unverified. Gymshark separately offers a verified **student** discount (12% via Student Beans and via UNiDAYS in the US).

- Veterans — eligible (WeSalute)
- Retirees — eligible (WeSalute)
- Reserve / National Guard (current or former) — eligible (WeSalute)
- Immediate family members — eligible (WeSalute; "immediate family member")

- Dependents — eligible if they fall under "immediate family member" (not separately itemized)
- Surviving spouses / Gold Star families — Not stated in official source

- Students — Eligible via separate program (12% via Student Beans / UNiDAYS), not the military offer

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Gymshark's official Discounts page and select the WeSalute (military) option, or go directly to WeSalute's Gymshark offer.
2. Sign in to or create a WeSalute account and verify your military/veteran/family status.
3. Once verified, open Gymshark's private WeSalute store / apply your member offer.
4. Add items to your bag and go to checkout.
5. Confirm the 10% discount is reflected before paying — it cannot be applied after the order is placed.

### How To Redeem In Store
Gymshark's official US Discounts page and the WeSalute offer page describe the 10% as an **online checkout** benefit ("Discount can only be applied at online checkout and is not available retroactively"). In-store/app redemption is claimed by some aggregators but is **not confirmed by a primary source**, so do not rely on it. If shopping a Gymshark retail location, ask staff whether the WeSalute discount can be applied; treat any in-store discount as unverified until confirmed by Gymshark.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **WeSalute** (formerly Veterans Advantage), Gymshark's military verification partner since 2019. You create/sign in to a WeSalute account and confirm eligibility (active duty, veteran, retiree, current/former Guard & Reserve, or immediate family member). WeSalute then unlocks access to Gymshark's private member store where the 10% applies at checkout. Gymshark's pages do not state the exact re-verification cadence; one aggregator cites a 12-month renewal, which is **not confirmed** by Gymshark or WeSalute primary pages. For verification problems, use WeSalute's member support and Gymshark Customer Support.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Only **one discount code per order** — codes can't be stacked (e.g., military + Student Beans + athlete code together). (Gymshark Support)
- Discounts can't be applied retroactively; enter/confirm before completing purchase. (Gymshark Support; WeSalute)
- Codes aren't valid on **e-gift cards**. (Gymshark Support)
- Refunds on discounted orders reflect the **discounted** amount paid, with the discount split evenly across items. (Gymshark Support)
- WeSalute offer states 10% **can** be combined with public sales and free standard shipping on $75+ orders. (WeSalute) — note this is sale pricing, not stacking a second code.
- Whether the 10% applies to clearance/outlet specifically is **not explicitly stated**; the WeSalute page references "site-wide" and "public sales." Flag as partially unverified.
- "All\*" products in the student copy carries an asterisk on Gymshark's page; specific category exclusions for the military offer are **not enumerated** in the primary sources.

### FAQ

#### Does Gymshark offer a military discount?
Yes. In the US, Gymshark offers a 10% military/veteran discount through WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage), its verification partner since 2019. (Gymshark Support; WeSalute)

#### How much is the Gymshark military discount?
10% off, available year-round in the US. It can be combined with public sales and free standard shipping on $75+ orders. (WeSalute)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify, as do active duty, retirees, current/former Guard & Reserve, and "immediate family members." WeSalute's wording covers immediate family; it does not separately itemize spouses or dependents, and it does not mention Gold Star/surviving spouses. (WeSalute)

#### How do I verify my status?
Through WeSalute — create or sign in to a WeSalute account, verify your eligibility, then shop Gymshark's private member store where 10% applies at checkout. (Gymshark Support; WeSalute)

#### Does Gymshark use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
For the US military discount, Gymshark's official Discounts page lists **WeSalute** only. ID.me's own shop page states it is not aware that Gymshark offers a military or first-responder discount via ID.me. Claims that Gymshark verifies military/first responders via "ID.me or SheerID at checkout" appear on coupon/aggregator sites but are **not confirmed by any primary source**. (Gymshark Support; ID.me Shop)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
The official sources describe the 10% as an online-checkout benefit that can't be applied retroactively. In-store redemption is claimed by some third-party sites but is not confirmed by Gymshark — ask in-store staff and don't assume it applies. (WeSalute; Gymshark Support)

#### What is excluded?
Only one discount code per order (no stacking codes); no use on e-gift cards; not applied after an order is placed. Refunds reflect the discounted price paid. Specific product-category exclusions for the military offer aren't enumerated in the primary sources. (Gymshark Support)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
You can shop public **sale** prices with the WeSalute 10% per WeSalute's offer page, but you can't combine multiple discount **codes** — Gymshark allows only one code per order. (WeSalute; Gymshark Support)

#### Does Gymshark offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Students: yes — 12% via Student Beans or UNiDAYS (US). First responders / teachers / nurses / government: **not** offered via Gymshark's US Discounts page (and ID.me states it is not aware of first-responder or government Gymshark discounts). In the UK only, emergency services, NHS, and armed forces can get 10% via Blue Light Card. (Gymshark Support; ID.me Shop)
