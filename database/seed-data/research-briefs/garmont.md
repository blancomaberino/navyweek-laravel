**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of Garmont T8-class tactical boots (list price roughly $180–$220; exact MSRP not verified this session).

## Headline finding

**Garmont Tactical (garmonttactical.com) does not publish an official military or veteran discount on its own store.** Its own site carries only a "We support Veterans" acknowledgment plus public promos (a boot-bundle deal and add-on socks), and **ID.me explicitly states it is "not aware that Garmont Tactical offers Military discounts"** — so there is no ID.me/SheerID identity-verified discount at the brand's checkout as of July 18, 2026. The one verified professional savings path is **GovX**, which operates a Garmont storefront (e.g., the T8 Defense LE boot) at member-only pricing for verified military/government/first-responder shoppers. **Archetype: primarily "no official discount" + secondary GovX marketplace (pro/agency channel).** Best path depends on the item: **GovX member pricing** for a single boot if you can beat retail there, or the brand's own **public "Buy 1 T8, get 35% off Nemesis G-DRY" bundle** if you're buying two pairs. Ignore any coupon-site "Garmont military promo code" — none is verified.
