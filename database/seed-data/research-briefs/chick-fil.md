# Chick-fil-A Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Chick-fil-A does **not** offer a chain-wide military or veteran discount. Because every Chick-fil-A restaurant is independently owned and operated by a local franchise Owner-Operator, any discount, free meal, or Veterans Day offer is entirely at the discretion of the individual operator and varies by location — Chick-fil-A, Inc. confirms this directly on its own support pages, which also state plainly that the company "do[es] not offer discounts or promo codes." Many locations near military installations do choose to offer a standing discount (commonly cited as ~10% off) or free meals on Veterans Day / Military Appreciation events, but none of this is guaranteed and Chick-fil-A publishes no official percentage. Separately, Chick-fil-A uses **ID.me** to verify "Community Helper" status (military, teachers, first responders, nurses) for Chick-fil-A One members so they "might have an opportunity to be recognized and/or rewarded" — but the company does not promise any specific reward for verifying. The single most important caveat: **there is no national offer to redeem; verify your status in the app/ID.me and contact your local restaurant to confirm what, if anything, it offers.**

### Intro
Chick-fil-A does not have a national military or veteran discount. Every Chick-fil-A restaurant is independently owned and operated by a local franchise Owner-Operator, and Chick-fil-A, Inc. confirms that any discount or free-meal offer is "at the discretion of the Owner-Operator and vary[ies] by location." The company also states on its own support site that it does not offer discounts or promo codes. So whether you can save depends entirely on the specific restaurant you visit.

What Chick-fil-A *does* do nationally is let military members verify their status through ID.me as part of its "Community Helper" program (which also covers teachers, first responders and nurses). Verifying gives you "an opportunity to be recognized and/or rewarded" — the company does not promise a fixed discount for it. Many restaurants near military bases also choose to honor service members with a standing discount or with free meals around Veterans Day and at Military Appreciation events.

### How To Redeem Online
1. There is no national online military discount to apply. Chick-fil-A states it does not offer discounts or promo codes, and online ordering does not surface a chain-wide military price.
2. To capture any reward tied to verified status, sign in to your Chick-fil-A One account in the Chick-fil-A app or on chick-fil-a.com.
3. Verify your Community Helper (military) status through ID.me at chick-fil-a.com/community-helper-verify. You will be redirected to ID.me to complete verification.
4. Watch the app for any offers; specific rewards for verified status are not guaranteed and are not published by Chick-fil-A.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Before you go, check your local restaurant's page in the Chick-fil-A app or its social media, or call it, to confirm whether it offers a military/veteran discount or event.
2. If a discount or free-meal offer exists, bring valid proof of service (e.g., a military or veteran ID). Requirements are set by the operator — Chick-fil-A publishes none.
3. At the register, ask for the local military/veteran offer before paying and present your ID.
4. On Veterans Day or at a Military Appreciation event, follow the participating location's instructions for that event.

### How Verification Works
Chick-fil-A uses **ID.me** to identify and "from time-to-time honor" Chick-fil-A One members who belong to Community Helper groups — military, teachers, first responders and nurses. You start at chick-fil-a.com/community-helper-verify and are redirected to ID.me to validate your status; a Chick-fil-A One account is required to link the verification. Chick-fil-A states this gives members "an opportunity to be recognized and/or rewarded for their service" — it does **not** promise a specific or permanent discount, and it does not state a re-verification frequency. ID.me sets its own document/re-verification requirements. For help, Chick-fil-A CARES is at 1-866-232-2040 (Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM–10:00 PM ET) or via chick-fil-a.com/contact-cares.

### FAQ

#### Does Chick-fil-A offer a military discount?
Not company-wide. Chick-fil-A confirms that any discount is "at the discretion of the Owner-Operator and vary[ies] by location." Some restaurants offer one; many do not.

#### How much is the Chick-fil-A military discount?
Chick-fil-A does not publish a percentage. Editorial sites commonly cite ~10% at participating locations, but that figure is not confirmed by Chick-fil-A and varies by restaurant.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Chick-fil-A only names "military" as a Community Helper group for ID.me verification and does not break out veterans, spouses, or dependents. Whether any of these groups get an in-store offer is up to the local operator.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through ID.me at chick-fil-a.com/community-helper-verify. You sign in with Chick-fil-A One and are redirected to ID.me to complete verification.

#### Does Chick-fil-A use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
ID.me. Chick-fil-A uses ID.me for Community Helper verification. (WeSalute lists Chick-fil-A on its site, but Chick-fil-A's own verification provider is ID.me.)

#### Can I use a military discount in stores?
Only if your specific local restaurant offers one. Confirm with the restaurant via the app, its social media, or by calling before you order, and bring valid proof of service.

#### What is excluded?
There are no national terms. Each operator sets its own offer, eligibility, proof requirements, and limits. Chick-fil-A, Inc. itself states it does not offer discounts or promo codes.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Not applicable nationally — Chick-fil-A does not offer promo codes, and any local offer's stacking rules are set by the operator.

#### Does Chick-fil-A offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
First responders, teachers, and nurses can verify through ID.me as Community Helper groups, the same as military — but, as with military, any actual discount is operator-dependent. Students are not named as a Community Helper group.

#### Does Chick-fil-A give free food to veterans on Veterans Day?
Some locations do, through free meals or Military Appreciation events, but it is not guaranteed everywhere. Check your local restaurant near Nov. 11.
