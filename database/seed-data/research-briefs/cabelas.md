## Executive Summary

Cabela's runs an everyday military and first-responder discount called **Legendary Salute**. Per Cabela's own help center, verified customers receive **5% off firearms and ammunition and 10% off all other eligible merchandise** (exclusions apply). Eligible groups named by the official source are **military personnel, law enforcement officers (LEOs), firefighters, emergency medical technicians (EMTs), and fish & wildlife agents**. To redeem online or by phone, you create/log in to a cabelas.com account and complete an eligibility verification through the Legendary Salute page; in stores you show a current ID proving your military status or current first-responder employment. Wildlife agents cannot verify online — that group's discount is in-store only. The single most important caveat: the official help page does **not** publish the program's fine print (exclusions, stacking rules, spouse/dependent eligibility, re-verification cadence), so those points must be treated as "not stated in official source" rather than asserted from coupon sites. Cabela's and Bass Pro Shops share one identical program.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Cabela's Legendary Salute page (cabelas.com/b/legendary-salute).
2. Create a cabelas.com account or log in to your existing one.
3. Start the eligibility verification from the Legendary Salute page and submit your verification information.
4. Once verified, your account reflects your eligibility; follow the on-screen instructions for how the discount applies to your order.
5. Place your order while logged in to the verified account. (Note: fish & wildlife agents cannot verify online — use the in-store method.)

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop in any Cabela's retail store.
2. At checkout, show a current ID that verifies your military status, or your current employment as an LEO, firefighter, EMT, or fish & wildlife agent.
3. The associate applies the discount (5% firearms/ammo, 10% other eligible merchandise) to your purchase.

### How Verification Works
Online, Cabela's requires you to be logged in to a cabelas.com account and to submit verification information through the Legendary Salute page; once verified, your account reflects your eligibility for the discount. Cabela's official help page does **not** name the verification provider or state the re-verification frequency, so we do not claim "verify once and never again." Several reputable military-focused outlets report the provider is **SheerID**; treat that as likely but not officially confirmed. For wildlife agents, online verification is not available — the discount is applied to retail purchases only. If automatic verification fails, contact Cabela's customer service (help.cabelas.com → Contact Us) for the support path.

### FAQ

#### Does Cabela's offer a military discount?
Yes. Cabela's offers an everyday "Legendary Salute" discount: 5% off firearms and ammunition and 10% off all other eligible merchandise for verified military and first-responder customers (exclusions apply).

#### How much is the Cabela's military discount?
Per Cabela's help center, it is **10% off most eligible merchandise and 5% off firearms and ammunition**. Cabela's notes exclusions apply but does not publish the full exclusion list on that page.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The official page lists "military personnel" along with LEOs, firefighters, EMTs, and fish & wildlife agents. It does not separately spell out veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, or dependents. In stores you must show "a current ID that verifies your military status." Spouse and dependent eligibility is not stated in the official source — verify your specific status during the verification step.

#### How do I verify my status?
Online: create or log in to a cabelas.com account, then submit your verification information through the Legendary Salute page. In store: show a current qualifying ID at checkout. Wildlife agents must verify in store (online verification isn't available for that group).

#### Does Cabela's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Cabela's official help page does not name a provider. Reputable military-focused outlets report the online check is handled by **SheerID**. We flag this as likely but not officially confirmed by Cabela's.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Show a current ID verifying your military status or your current employment as an LEO, firefighter, EMT, or fish & wildlife agent at the register.

#### What is excluded?
Cabela's states "Exclusions Apply" but does not publish the specific exclusions on the military-discount page. Firearms and ammunition are discounted at the lower 5% rate. Check the Legendary Salute page or ask in store for current exclusions.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Not stated in Cabela's official source. We can't confirm whether the discount stacks with sales, clearance, or promo codes — confirm at checkout or with a store associate.

#### Does Cabela's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders are covered: LEOs, firefighters, and EMTs qualify for Legendary Salute. Teachers, nurses (non-EMT), government employees, and students are not listed on the official military-discount page.

#### Is the Cabela's discount the same as Bass Pro Shops?
Yes. Cabela's and Bass Pro Shops are operated by BPS Direct, L.L.C. and share the identical Legendary Salute program and terms.
