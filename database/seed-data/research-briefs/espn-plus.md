# ESPN+ — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **annual ESPN+ subscription** (the former ESPN+ tier, now billed as **ESPN Select**, ~**$119.99/year** / ~$11.99/mo), with a secondary look at **MLB.TV** (all-teams annual, regular ~$149.99) — the one product sold through ESPN that *does* carry a verified military discount.

## Headline finding

**ESPN+ has no military or veteran discount — and, as of the August 2025 direct-to-consumer relaunch, "ESPN+" is no longer even a live plan.** The old ESPN+ content tier is now sold as **ESPN Select** (with a larger **ESPN Unlimited** tier above it), and none of the ESPN streaming plans offer a service-member rate. ID.me, the verification provider most readers try first, states plainly: **"We are not aware of ESPN+ offering Military community discounts."** No verified ESPN/ESPN+ military offer exists at GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass. The coupon-site claims of **"50% off ESPN+ for military with code ESPNHALF"** and **"40% off military"** are aggregator inventions — `ESPNHALF`, where it works at all, is a general (non-military) promo, not a service-member program. Do not repeat them as fact.

**Archetype: digital subscription with no native discount.** The only genuine military discount reachable *through ESPN* is on **MLB.TV**, which ESPN began distributing in 2026 — new MLB.TV subscribers now register in the ESPN app, and MLB.TV carries an official **up to 35% off** military discount verified through **GovX** (and historically SheerID). For ESPN+/ESPN Select itself, the only real savings levers are **annual billing** (vs monthly) and the **Disney Bundle** (Disney+/Hulu/ESPN).
