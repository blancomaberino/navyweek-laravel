# Nine Line Apparel — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $100 apparel cart of regular-priced (non-sale) items, shipped to a US address.

## Headline finding

Nine Line Apparel — a veteran-owned brand — runs a clear, official **20% off (non-sale items) military and first-responder discount verified through ID.me**, confirmed on Nine Line's own site: *"ID.Me Military discounts available at checkout. Powered by ID.ME."* Eligible groups extend beyond military to first responders, medical/nurses, and teachers. It's a strong, simple offer; the main catch is that it applies to **non-sale items only** and can't be combined with other codes. Nine Line also lists a GovX "pro-deal" style channel and runs an email-signup 10%-off plus a points-based rewards program. For most eligible shoppers, verifying with ID.me for 20% off is the best single path; the best-value move is applying the 20% to already-marked-down "on sale" logic carefully (it won't stack on sale items).

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **20% applies to non-sale items only** — it does not stack on already-discounted/clearance items. (Nine Line FAQ.)
2. **"Some discounts cannot be combined"** — in general you use one code per order; don't expect to stack ID.me with the email code. (Nine Line FAQ.)
3. **Limited editions, collaborations, and new launches** may be excluded from discounts — check the product page. (Community/directory-reported; verify per item.)
4. **Free shipping over $70 is automatic** and independent of any code.
