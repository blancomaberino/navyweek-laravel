# Outback Steakhouse — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $60 dine-in check for two (food + non-alcoholic drinks, before tax).

## Headline finding

Outback Steakhouse runs a **genuine, official, everyday 10% "Heroes Discount"** — 10% off the entire check, all day every day, for military veterans and servicemembers, police, firefighters, first responders, nurses, doctors and medical staff (with valid ID). It's published on Outback's own site and excludes alcohol and tax. This is a **dine-in / call-in only** offer — it does **not** work through online ordering. The single most valuable path is not the everyday 10% but the **Veterans Day free-meal event** Outback has run every November (a full free entrée in recent years, ~$15–20 value). Archetype: **in-store service discount (H/G hybrid)** — a real everyday % plus a much bigger annual holiday giveaway.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. Heroes Discount **cannot** combine with any Dine Rewards™ redemption — it's an either/or; choose whichever saves more on that check.
2. It **cannot** combine with the AARP membership discount (both are 10%; no double-dip).
3. It does **not** apply to catering orders.
4. **Alcohol and tax are always excluded** from the 10%.
5. **One discount per table** — a group can't split into multiple discounted checks at one table.
6. Not available via **online ordering** — call the restaurant, present ID, and pay in person.

### How To Redeem In Store / By Phone

### FAQ

#### Does Outback Steakhouse offer a military discount?
Yes. Outback publishes an everyday **10% "Heroes Discount"** off the entire check for military veterans and servicemembers (plus first responders and medical staff) with a valid ID. It excludes alcohol and tax and works dine-in or by phone only. (Source: [Outback Heroes Discount](https://www.outback.com/offers/military-mates), accessed July 6, 2026.)

#### How much is the Outback military discount?
**10% off** your entire guest check, excluding alcohol and applicable taxes, all day every day. Around Veterans Day, Outback has historically offered a **free meal** instead, which is worth far more for one visit.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify with an accepted ID (DD214, VIC, Veterans Organization card, etc.). Outback's terms also extend the 10% to **Military Personnel and their immediate family members** who hold one of the accepted forms of ID.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Just show a valid ID to staff — no online account. Accepted IDs include Uniformed Services/Retired ID, current LES, VFW/American Legion card, DD214, Veteran ID Card, or a photo in/wearing U.S. uniform.

#### Does Outback use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Outback verifies in person by ID. (WeSalute lists an Outback marketplace entry, but the actual 10% discount is Outback's own in-restaurant offer, not a WeSalute-gated one.)

#### Can I use it online?
**No.** The discount is not available via online ordering — you must dine in or call the restaurant, present ID, and pay in person.

#### What is excluded?
Alcohol and tax. It's also limited to **one discount per table** and can't combine with Dine Rewards, AARP, or catering.

#### Can I combine it with Dine Rewards, AARP, or a coupon?
**No.** It won't stack with a Dine Rewards™ redemption, the AARP® discount, or catering. Choose whichever single offer saves you the most on that check.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to eat at Outback?
Everyday: the **10% Heroes Discount**. If you've banked a Dine Rewards reward (e.g. 50% off an entrée), compare — on an entrée-dominant check it can beat 10%. Around **Veterans Day**, the free-meal event wins outright.

#### Does Outback offer a first responder, nurse, or teacher discount?
First responders (police, firefighters), nurses, doctors, and medical staff get the **same 10% Heroes Discount** with a corresponding service or medical ID. No separate teacher/student program is published.

#### Does Outback do a Veterans Day free meal?
Historically yes — a free entrée or Bloomin' Onion on/around Nov 10–11 for military ID-holders, dine-in at participating locations. Terms change yearly; confirm the current-year offer close to the date.
