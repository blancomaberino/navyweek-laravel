# Field Museum (Chicago) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult Chicago-resident Basic (All-Access) admission (~$30–45 range depending on package) at the Field Museum.

## Headline finding

The Field Museum gives **free general admission** to active-duty military personnel, veterans, and Illinois State Gold Star ID cardholders, with **discounted admission for up to six military family members**. It is redeemed **in person at the ticket counter** by showing a valid military status ID (or Illinois Gold Star Family ID) — there is no online military checkout. On top of that, the Field Museum is a **Blue Star Museums** participant, giving active-duty service members and up to five family members free general admission during the summer program window (May 16–September 7, 2026). So a veteran gets free general admission year-round; an active-duty family gets the whole family in free during the summer.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military — free general admission.
- Veterans — free general admission.
- Illinois State Gold Star ID cardholders — free general admission.
- Military family members — discounted, up to six people.
- Active-duty + up to 5 family (summer) — free via Blue Star Museums.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online:** No military rate online. Active-duty and veterans should redeem in person; family companions can buy standard tickets online if preferred.
**In store:** 1) Go to the main ticket counter. 2) Present a valid military status ID (or Illinois Gold Star Family ID). 3) Receive complimentary general admission; add up to six discounted family tickets. 4) In summer, ask for the Blue Star Museums benefit with your CAC/DD Form 1173.

### How Verification Works
Verification is a simple in-person ID check at the counter — employee ID, military status ID, or Illinois State ID with Gold Star Family status. Blue Star Museums requires a DoD-issued ID (CAC, DD Form 1173/1173-1, or Next Gen USID).

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The free benefit covers **general admission**; special exhibitions and All-Access packages may still carry a fee unless stated.
- No advance online purchase for the military discount.
- Family discount capped at six total people.
- Blue Star Museums limited to the summer window and active-duty households (+ up to 5 family).

### Best Ways To Save More
- Veterans/active-duty: use the free counter admission year-round.
- Active-duty families: plan a summer visit for Blue Star Museums (whole family free).
- Illinois residents: check the museum's free-resident days for non-eligible companions.
- Consider membership or CityPASS only if visiting frequently or bundling multiple attractions.

### FAQ

#### Does the Field Museum offer a military discount?
Yes — free general admission for active-duty military, veterans, and Illinois Gold Star ID cardholders, plus discounts for up to six family members.

#### How much is the Field Museum military discount?
General admission is free (100% off) for the eligible service member/veteran; family members get a reduced rate.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify for free general admission. Family members (up to six) get discounted admission; in summer, Blue Star Museums covers active-duty plus up to five family free.

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a valid military status ID (or Illinois Gold Star Family ID) at the ticket counter — it's an in-person check, not an online service.

#### Does the Field Museum use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
No. Verification is an in-person ID check at the counter.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
In person only. There is no online military admission rate.

#### What is excluded?
The free benefit is for general admission; special exhibitions and All-Access upgrades may cost extra.

#### Can I combine it with other offers?
The military benefit covers general admission; you'd pay separately for special-exhibit upgrades. Blue Star (summer) already includes the family.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a military family to visit?
Visit during the Blue Star Museums window (May 16–Sep 7, 2026) for free entry for the active-duty member and up to five family; otherwise the service member/veteran is free and family members pay the discounted rate.

#### Does the Field Museum run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day offer?
Year-round free veteran admission already covers Veterans Day; Blue Star Museums opens around Armed Forces Day / Memorial Day for active-duty families.
