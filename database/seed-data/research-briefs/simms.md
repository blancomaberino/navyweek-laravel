## Headline finding

Simms Fishing Products offers a **military and first-responder discount verified through ID.me at checkout** on simmsfishing.com, but the **exact percentage is not published** — it's revealed only after ID.me verification, and eligibility is framed around **active-duty military and first responders** (broader groups like veterans/family are not spelled out in Simms' policy). For many service members the bigger savings lever is not the ID.me consumer discount but **ExpertVoice** (Simms runs a brand community there for verified pros, military, and first responders, with pro-level pricing) and **GovX**. Archetype: premium fishing-apparel brand that routes discounts through gated verification/pro platforms rather than posting a flat public military rate. Best path depends on access: if you qualify for ExpertVoice/pro pricing, that usually beats the ID.me consumer discount; otherwise ID.me at checkout, stacked on seasonal sales where allowed.

### FAQ
#### Does Simms offer a military discount?
Yes — a military and first-responder discount verified through ID.me at checkout, plus ExpertVoice pro access.

#### How much is the Simms military discount?
Simms doesn't publish the percentage; it's shown after ID.me verification. Aggregator "10–25%" or "50%" claims are unverified.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Simms' policy specifies active-duty military and first responders; veterans/family aren't stated. Veterans may find access via ExpertVoice or GovX.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me at checkout for the consumer discount; separately via ExpertVoice for pro pricing.

#### Does Simms use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
ID.me (checkout) and ExpertVoice; Simms gear also appears in GovX's fishing category. Not SheerID/WeSalute/VerifyPass.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
Online on simmsfishing.com (ID.me) and via ExpertVoice; there's no first-party in-store discount channel.

#### What is excluded?
Closeout/sale items generally don't take the verified discount; the percentage and veteran/family eligibility aren't published.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the verified discount doesn't stack on sale items, and ExpertVoice/ID.me are separate channels.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Simms?
ExpertVoice pro pricing if you're approved; otherwise ID.me at checkout — but check the public closeout/BOGO price on that SKU first.

#### Does Simms offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders are included with military via ID.me; government/military members can also use GovX. Teacher/nurse/student tiers aren't published.
