### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Backcountry's ID.me Program page (backcountry.com/sc/id-me-program) or start a Backcountry order.
2. Add eligible full-price items to your cart.
3. Select the **ID.me ("Verify with ID.me")** button — it most often appears at checkout.
4. Sign in to your ID.me account, or create one for free if you don't have one.
5. Complete military verification (enter service details and/or your .mil email, or upload an acceptable military document).
6. Once verified, the 20% discount is applied to eligible items — no promo code needed.

### How Verification Works
Backcountry uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You verify once by signing into (or creating) a free ID.me account and providing military credentials — typically a .mil email address you confirm, or an uploaded military document. After verification is accepted, you authorize ID.me to share your status with Backcountry and the discount unlocks. ID.me controls re-verification: military status can require periodic re-verification, and you can re-verify or change your military sub-group through your ID.me Wallet. ID.me Support handles verification issues; for problems applying the discount at checkout, contact Backcountry directly.

### FAQ

#### Does Backcountry offer a military discount?
Yes. Backcountry offers 20% off select full-price purchases to verified active-duty military, veterans, military family members, and government employees, through ID.me.

#### How much is the Backcountry military discount?
20% off select full-price purchases, per Backcountry's ID.me Program page. It does not apply to all items and cannot be combined with other offers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — Backcountry's page names veterans and military family members. ID.me's military eligibility rules (which govern verification) also cover spouses, dependents over 18, retirees, reservists/National Guard, and surviving spouses.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through ID.me: sign in or create a free account, then confirm your status with a .mil email or an acceptable military document. Once verified, the discount unlocks at checkout — no code needed.

#### Does Backcountry use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Backcountry's own ID.me Program page and ID.me Shop both confirm **ID.me**. Some aggregators mention GovX or SheerID, but the primary sources point to ID.me; treat GovX/SheerID claims as unverified for Backcountry.com.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Backcountry's official page describes the offer for Backcountry.com only and does not confirm in-store redemption. Aggregator claims about "participating retail locations" are unverified — ask Backcountry support before relying on in-store use.

#### What is excluded?
Select full-price items only; the discount can't combine with other promo codes, coupons, or sitewide promotions; and "some brand exclusions apply." Backcountry doesn't publish the excluded-brand list on the program page.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Backcountry states it cannot be combined with other promotional codes, coupons, or sitewide promotions. It applies to full-price items, so sale/clearance items generally won't qualify.

#### Does Backcountry offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
Yes. Backcountry's "community of heroes" program extends the same 20% to healthcare workers, first responders, educators, and government employees — all verified through ID.me. A dedicated student discount is not stated on the program page.

#### How often do I need to re-verify?
You verify once through ID.me, but ID.me controls re-verification and may require it periodically. You can re-verify or change your military sub-group anytime in your ID.me Wallet.
