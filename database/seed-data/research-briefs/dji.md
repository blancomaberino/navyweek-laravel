# DJI — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one DJI consumer drone at ~$1,099 MSRP (e.g., DJI Air 3) — the mid-to-big-ticket case where a percentage discount and tax actually move the needle.

## Headline finding

**DJI runs no first-party military or veteran discount.** There is no military checkout, no ID.me/SheerID gate, and no always-on "military code" on store.dji.com — DJI's own store surfaces only a 1% DJI Credit reward, a 5%-off newsletter code for everyone, official refurbished, and seasonal sales. The one genuine military-verified route is the **GovX storefront** (govx.com/site/brands/961/dji), which sells DJI gear at member pricing to military, veterans, first responders, and government employees — but GovX is a **substitute** for buying direct, not a stack, and on current-season DJI (a MAP-controlled brand) it's frequently near-retail. The bigger money truth: DJI discounts deeply and often on its own — **Spring Sale up to ~35%, Summer Sale up to ~50%** — and sells **official refurbished at up to ~20% off with the same 1-year warranty as new**, so the public sale or refurb usually beats whatever GovX shows. Archetype: **A (GovX-only, no first-party discount)** with a strong **C secondary (the public sale/refurb usually beats the "military" route).** Aggregator claims of a "20% DJI military discount" are **fabricated** — no such program exists.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **There is no military discount to stack** — the question of "military code + sale" is moot on DJI's own store.
2. **GovX is a closed marketplace** — you cannot add a DJI newsletter code, DJI Credit, or a cashback portal on top of a GovX order; compare its net price as a standalone substitute, and factor GovX shipping (shoppers report shipping can erase the savings on small items).
3. **DJI's own codes are single-use and generally don't combine** — the 5% newsletter welcome code is one-time and typically won't stack with a seasonal sale; pick the lower price.
4. **DJI Credit (1%) layers** on direct DJI-store purchases (it's a reward, not a coupon) but is trivial versus a seasonal sale.
5. **Refurbished vs. sale are mutually exclusive** — refurb units are already discounted; you're choosing one channel or the other.
6. **Cashback portals** generally void on coupon-code/identity-gated checkouts and DJI rarely appears with meaningful rates — do not assume a portal stacks on a DJI code or on GovX.

### FAQ

#### Does DJI offer a military discount?
No. DJI runs no first-party military or veteran discount on its store — no military checkout and no verification partner. The only military-verified route is the separate **GovX marketplace**.

#### How much is the DJI military discount?
There isn't one from DJI. Coupon-site claims of "10–20% DJI military discount" are **fabricated**. GovX offers member pricing that varies by item and is often close to retail on current-season gear.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify for anything?
Veterans (and first responders and government employees) can shop the **GovX** DJI storefront. DJI itself offers nothing military-specific. Spouse/dependent eligibility follows GovX's account rules — verify at GovX.

#### How do I verify my status?
DJI has no verification. For the GovX route, create a free GovX account and verify your military/first-responder/government status there.

#### Does DJI use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
DJI's own store uses **none** of these. GovX operates its own separate marketplace with its own verification; ID.me has no DJI store listing.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
There's no DJI military discount in either channel. GovX is online-only. DJI's public sales and refurbished are online at store.dji.com; Best Buy and other retailers carry DJI in stores.

#### What is excluded?
Everything, in the sense that no military program exists. On GovX, orders are closed (no stacking) and shipping/returns differ. DJI's newsletter code is single-use and won't stack with sales.

#### Can I combine a discount with promo codes or sale items?
There's no military discount to combine. DJI's newsletter code generally won't stack with a seasonal sale (pick the lower price); DJI Credit (1%) layers as a reward on direct orders.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from DJI?
Usually the **public seasonal sale** (Spring up to ~35%, Summer up to ~50%) or **Official Refurbished** (up to ~20% off, 1-yr warranty) — both beat the GovX marketplace on most items and need no verification. Price-check GovX only as a fallback.

#### Does DJI offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No first-party consumer discount for any of these today. First responders and government employees can use **GovX**. DJI historically offered ~10% for students/educators; there's no verified everyday student discount now — watch the newsletter and seasonal sales.

#### Can I buy DJI at the Exchange (AAFES/NEX)?
DJI carriage at the military exchanges was not confirmed as of July 14, 2026 — don't assume a tax-free exchange price. Check shopmyexchange.com / mynavyexchange.com directly.
