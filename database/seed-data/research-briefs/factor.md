# Factor (Factor_ / Factor75) — Military & First-Responder Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a first Factor box (mid plan, e.g., 8 meals ≈ $110 list) plus a note on the first-year total across boxes.

## Headline finding

Factor (Factor_, formerly Factor75, a HelloFresh brand) runs an official **Hero Discount Program**: verified military/veterans and first responders (plus medical providers, nurses, hospital employees, teachers) get **55% off the first box + free shipping, and 15% off every box for the next year (the next 51 boxes)**. Verify through **ID.me or GovX**. It is **not combinable** with other promotions or gift cards, and it's for new customers (or accounts deactivated 1+ day before redemption). Archetype: **F (time-boxed subscription discount)** — the big first-box % and the 12-month/380-day tail are the story, and there's a cliff when it ends. Note: some sources (including Military.com, sourced from an older campaign) still quote "50% off first box + 10% off next 12 boxes" — Factor's own hero-discount page now states **55% / 15% / 51 boxes**; treat that as authoritative.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand terms)
1. **Hero discount does NOT combine** with any other promotion or with gift cards (Factor terms). It's an either/or vs. public welcome codes — pick the better one.
2. **New-customer / reactivation gate:** valid for new customers or accounts deactivated more than 1 day before redemption, with a qualifying auto-renewing subscription. Existing active subscribers can't apply it mid-subscription.
3. **Excludes** premiums, meal upgrades (add-ons), and taxes. The 15% applies to eligible boxes only.
4. **Time-boxed:** the whole offer expires **380 days after purchase**; after that you revert to standard pricing (the cliff — state it clearly on the page).
5. **Free shipping applies to the first box** per the terms wording; later boxes follow standard shipping.

### FAQ

#### Does Factor offer a military discount?
Yes. Factor's Hero Discount Program gives verified military and veterans 55% off the first box plus free shipping, and 15% off the next 51 boxes over about a year.

#### How much is the Factor military discount?
55% off your first box (with free shipping) and 15% off each of the next 51 boxes. Older listings saying "50% + 10%" are out of date; Factor's own page states 55/15/51.

#### Do first responders, nurses, and teachers qualify?
Yes. The same Hero program covers first responders, medical providers, nurses, hospital employees, and teachers, alongside military and veterans.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through ID.me or GovX on Factor's Hero Discounts page. Once verified, the discount applies automatically at checkout for new or reactivated accounts.

#### Does Factor use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**ID.me and GovX** — you pick one. Factor's Hero page does not use SheerID.

#### Can I use it if I'm already a subscriber?
No. It's for new customers or accounts deactivated more than 1 day before redemption. Existing active subscribers can't apply it mid-subscription.

#### What is excluded, and does it stack?
It doesn't stack with any other promotion or with gift cards, and it excludes premiums, meal upgrades, and taxes. It's an either/or against public welcome codes.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to order Factor?
If you'll keep the subscription past the first box, the Hero discount (55% + 15%-off year + free shipping) is the best deal. If you only want one box or aren't verified, compare it to the public first-box code and pick the lower one.

#### Is the discount permanent?
No — it runs for 380 days from purchase, then you revert to standard pricing. Plan around that cliff.
