# Wayfair Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**Wayfair does not offer a military or veteran discount.** As of June 23, 2026, there is no standing percentage-off military/veteran discount, no military promo code, and no ID.me/SheerID/GovX verification flow that unlocks a price reduction for service members. The verification provider ID.me states plainly on its Wayfair page: *"We are not aware of Wayfair offering Military community discounts."* Wayfair's own "Military Community at Wayfair" page is **not** a discount page — it is a charity/cause-marketing page about Wayfair's partnership with Homes For Our Troops (>$1M contributed; 300th adapted home completed). The single most important caveat: aggregator sites advertising a "Wayfair 10% military discount" or "Wayfair first responder discount up to 50% off" are not supported by any primary source and should be treated as false/expired. The legitimate way for military shoppers (and everyone) to save at Wayfair is the paid **Wayfair Rewards** program ($29/year: 5% back in rewards + free shipping) plus Wayfair's frequent sitewide sales (Way Day, seasonal clearance up to ~70% off).

### FAQ

#### Does Wayfair offer a military discount?
No. As of June 23, 2026, Wayfair does not offer a military or veteran discount. ID.me, a common verification partner, states on its Wayfair page that it is "not aware of Wayfair offering Military community discounts."

#### How much is the Wayfair military discount?
There is no Wayfair military discount, so there is no percentage or dollar amount. Aggregator claims of "10% off" or "up to 50% off for first responders" are not supported by any Wayfair or ID.me primary source.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a Wayfair discount?
No — because no military discount exists, no group (active duty, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families) qualifies for a status-based price.

#### How do I verify my military status with Wayfair?
You can't, because there is nothing to verify for a discount. Wayfair does not run a military verification flow through ID.me or any other provider.

#### Does Wayfair use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
ID.me lists Wayfair as a store, but it shows no military discount to unlock and explicitly says it is "not aware" of one. There is no published GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute military offer for Wayfair.

#### Can I use a Wayfair military discount in stores?
No. Wayfair is primarily an online retailer and there is no military discount to use in any channel.

#### What is excluded from the Wayfair military discount?
Nothing is "excluded" because the discount does not exist. Be cautious: coupon sites advertising military or first responder codes for Wayfair are unverified and typically don't work.

#### Can I combine a military discount with Wayfair promo codes or sale items?
There is no military discount to combine. To save, stack a sale event with Wayfair Rewards (5% back + free shipping) where the terms allow.

#### Does Wayfair offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No verified discount for any of these groups is published. ID.me lists these as questions on its Wayfair page but provides no active offer for them. Wayfair's public sales and Wayfair Rewards are open to everyone.
