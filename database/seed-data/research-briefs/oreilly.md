# O'Reilly Auto Parts — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 11, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$150 in-store parts basket (e.g., a car battery + a set of brake pads/rotors), regularly priced, taxable.

## Headline finding

O'Reilly Auto Parts runs a **genuine, officially published military discount: 10% off most regularly priced items, in-store only, with valid military/veteran ID presented at the register.** There is no verification provider (no ID.me/GovX/SheerID) — it is an at-the-counter ID check, and it is available "only at participating store locations." The discount **cannot be combined** with other promotions, coupons, or special offers, and it **excludes** motor oil, antifreeze, sale/clearance items, special orders, appearance chemicals, outside-purchase items, and all online orders. Archetype: a **real, published, non-combinable in-store identity discount (archetype G-adjacent)** — the costly trap is buying online (where the discount does not exist), so an eligible shopper who buys in-store at full price is the winner. The best-total-savings move is simply: buy the regularly-priced parts **in store**, show ID for the 10%, and let your **O'Rewards** loyalty points accrue in the background; online, the discount is gone, so the online path relies on O'Rewards + a modest cashback portal instead. AutoZone and Advance Auto Parts both offer the same 10% in-store deal, so O'Reilly is at parity with its two main competitors — no cross-shop advantage either way.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military 10% does NOT combine** with any other promotion, promotional coupon, or special offer (O'Reilly military-discount page, explicit). It's an either/or vs. coupons and vs. price match.
2. **Military 10% excludes sale/clearance** items entirely — so during a markdown, the sale price stands alone and the 10% cannot be added.
3. **Military discount is in-store only** — buying online forfeits it completely. There is no code, no online toggle.
4. **O'Rewards points earning** is a loyalty accrual, not a "coupon"; O'Reilly's FAQ says reward *dollars* "can often be combined with other eligible promotions," but it does **not** confirm the military discount as an eligible combinable promotion — so treat "earn points on a military-discounted purchase" as plausible/unverified, and treat "redeem a $5 O'Rewards reward on the same transaction as the military discount" as **likely blocked** (the military terms exclude combining with other offers). Verify at the register.
5. **Price match** is its own path and, like coupons, cannot be stacked with the military 10% — pick the lower of (competitor-matched price) vs (10% off O'Reilly's price).
6. **Cashback portals (Rakuten/etc.) apply to online orders only** — and online has no military discount, so there is no "military + Rakuten" stack to chase in the first place.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Bring an accepted ID: DoD ID, Uniformed Services ID, Veteran ID Card, veteran-designated state ID/driver's license, or DD214.
2. Shop regularly-priced items (avoid oil, antifreeze, appearance chemicals, sale/clearance, special orders).
3. **Before the sale is rung up**, tell the parts associate you're claiming the military discount and present your ID.
4. The 10% is applied at the register. ID must be shown for each transaction.

### How To Redeem Online
- **You can't.** O'Reilly states online orders do not qualify for the military discount. Online, use O'Rewards and any current public promo instead — there is no military code for oreillyauto.com.

### How Verification Works
There is **no third-party verification provider** — no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. Verification is a manual, in-person ID check by an O'Reilly associate at the counter, repeated each transaction. No account is required for the discount (though a free O'Rewards account is worth having for points). If an associate is unsure, ask a manager and reference the store's military-discount policy.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Excluded items: **motor oil, antifreeze, appearance chemicals, sale/clearance items, special orders, outside-purchase items, and all online orders.**
- **Cannot be combined** with other promotions, promotional coupons, or special offers.
- Available **only at participating store locations** — not guaranteed at every store.

- Surviving-spouse/Gold Star eligibility is **not specified** in O'Reilly's official terms — flagged; confirm in store.

### FAQ

#### Does O'Reilly Auto Parts offer a military discount?
Yes. O'Reilly offers a **10% in-store discount** on most regularly priced items to eligible military members, veterans, and qualifying family, with valid ID.

#### How much is the O'Reilly military discount?
**10% off** most regularly priced items, in store only. It excludes motor oil, antifreeze, appearance chemicals, sale/clearance, special orders, and online purchases.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — active duty, reserve, retired, and honorably discharged veterans qualify, along with immediate family members who hold a DoD dependent ID (DD form 1173 / 1173-1). Surviving-spouse/Gold Star eligibility isn't specified in O'Reilly's official terms.

#### How do I verify my status?
Present a valid ID in person at the register: DoD ID, Uniformed Services ID, Veteran ID Card, veteran-designated state ID/driver's license, or DD214. ID is required for each transaction.

#### Does O'Reilly use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. There is no third-party verification. It's a manual in-person ID check at the counter, and it's in-store only.

#### Can I use the military discount online?
No. O'Reilly states online orders do not qualify. There is no military code for oreillyauto.com — any "20% off military code" from a coupon site is not a real O'Reilly military benefit.

#### What's excluded?
Motor oil, antifreeze, appearance chemicals, sale/clearance items, special orders, outside-purchase items, and all online orders. The discount also can't be combined with other coupons or promotions.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, sale items, or O'Rewards?
No coupons or promotions stack with the 10%, and it doesn't apply to sale/clearance. You can still **earn** O'Rewards points on purchases, but redeeming a $5 O'Rewards reward on the same transaction as the military discount is likely blocked by the "no combining" rule — verify at the register.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from O'Reilly?
Buy regularly-priced parts **in store**, show ID for the 10%, and let O'Rewards points accrue. If you must buy online, the discount is unavailable — use O'Rewards + a current site-wide promo + a cashback portal.

#### Does O'Reilly offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Not that O'Reilly publishes. The only identity discount confirmed is the military discount.

#### Does O'Reilly run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day military sale?
No documented military-specific holiday uplift — the 10% is a year-round standing benefit. O'Reilly runs public seasonal sales open to everyone (which the military discount can't be stacked on).

#### Is AutoZone or Advance Auto Parts a better deal for military?
They're at parity — AutoZone and Advance Auto Parts both offer 10% in-store military discounts with valid ID. Shop wherever the part is cheaper or in stock.
