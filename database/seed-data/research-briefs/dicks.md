# DICK'S Sporting Goods Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

**DICK'S Sporting Goods does not offer an official, company-wide military or veteran discount.** This is the single most important finding, and it contradicts dozens of coupon and "military discount" aggregator pages. DICK'S does run an **ID.me discount program — but it is for nurses, medical providers, and hospital employees only** (10% off one eligible product, online at DICKS.com or in the DICK'S App, via a single-use code refreshed roughly twice a month). DICK'S brands this as "Community MVPs." **Military, veterans, retirees, Guard/Reserve, spouses, and dependents are not listed as eligible communities for that program,** and ID.me's own page for DICK'S states plainly: "We are not aware of DICK'S Sporting Goods offering Military community discounts." Some individual stores reportedly grant a ~10% in-store discount to service members at the manager's discretion, but that is **not an official DICK'S policy**, is not published by DICK'S, and cannot be relied upon — it varies by store and is not verifiable against any primary source.

> Accuracy note: Many aggregators (operationmilitarykids, usmilitary.com, themilitarywallet, worthepenny, etc.) state or imply DICK'S "gives service members and veterans a 10% military discount." **No DICK'S primary source confirms a military discount.** The only DICK'S-published ID.me offer is for nurses/medical/hospital communities. Treat any "DICK'S military discount" claim as unverified store-by-store manager discretion, not company policy.

### FAQ

#### Does DICK'S Sporting Goods offer a military discount?
No. DICK'S does not offer an official, company-wide military or veteran discount. Its ID.me "Community MVPs" program is for nurses, medical providers, and hospital employees only, and ID.me's own DICK'S page states it is "not aware of DICK'S Sporting Goods offering Military community discounts."

#### How much is the DICK'S military discount?
There isn't one. The only published DICK'S ID.me discount is 10% off one eligible product for nurses/medical/hospital employees. Reports of a ~10% in-store military discount are at individual store managers' discretion and are not official.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
No — military, veterans, spouses, and dependents are not eligible communities for the DICK'S ID.me program, and its codes are non-transferable.

#### Does DICK'S use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
DICK'S uses **ID.me** — but only for nurses, medical providers, and hospital employees, not for military.

#### Can I get a military discount in store at DICK'S?
Not officially. The ID.me discount is online-only, and DICK'S publishes no in-store military discount. Some shoppers report a manager-discretion ~10%, but it's unofficial and not guaranteed — ask the store manager and bring military ID if you want to try.

#### What is excluded from the ID.me discount?
It applies to select eligible products only, one item per single-use code, and cannot be combined with other offers or discounts. Additional restrictions may apply and each code expires within two weeks.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The ID.me code cannot be combined with other offers or discounts.

#### Does DICK'S offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Only nurses, medical providers, and hospital employees have a published DICK'S ID.me discount. First responders, teachers, students, and government employees are not listed as eligible communities for the DICK'S program.

#### What's the best way for a military shopper to save at DICK'S?
Join the free ScoreCard program, use the Best Price Guarantee, grab the $20-off-$100 text sign-up offer, and shop seasonal sales. For an actual verified military discount, compare retailers that do offer one (e.g., Under Armour, Nike, YETI).
