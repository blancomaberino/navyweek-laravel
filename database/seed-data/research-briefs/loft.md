# LOFT — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $150 full-price LOFT apparel order (two full-price tops), before tax.

## Headline finding

**LOFT does not offer a standing military or veteran discount**, and neither does its sister brand **Ann Taylor**. There is no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass military gate at LOFT checkout. What LOFT *does* have is a year-round **"LOFT Loves Teachers" 15% educator discount** (not a military benefit) and very frequent public promotions — LOFT is a **promo-driven mall-apparel brand** that runs sitewide markdowns (often "50% off everything + extra 15%") almost continuously. For a service member, the honest maximum-savings path is **a public sitewide sale stacked with the email/SMS sign-up code and the LOFT credit-card 20%-off-first-purchase**, not a military discount.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. LOFT typically allows **one promo code per order**; the flagship "50% off everything + extra 15%" is a single bundled promo, not two separate stackable codes.
2. **LOFT Loves Teachers 15%** applies to **full-price merchandise** and generally does not combine with a deeper sitewide markdown — take whichever is larger.
3. New-subscriber (email/SMS) and **LOFT credit-card first-purchase 20%** are onboarding offers, usually one-time and subject to their own exclusions.
4. StyleRewards loyalty points accrue on top of sale purchases.

#### Does LOFT offer a military discount?
No. LOFT has no standing military or veteran discount, and neither does Ann Taylor.

#### How much is the LOFT military discount?
There isn't one. The verified savings paths are public sales plus subscriber/card offers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for anything?
Not through a military program. They can use the same public sales everyone gets.

#### How do I verify my status?
No military verification exists at LOFT. (The teacher program verifies teacher ID, not military status.)
