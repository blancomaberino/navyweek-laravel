# Omaha Steaks — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$150 Omaha Steaks gift/family package order (the typical unit shoppers compare).

## Headline finding

**Omaha Steaks does have a real, official military discount, but it's small: a flat $10 off any order of $79 or more, verified through ID.me** — for active-duty, veterans, retirees, reservists, military spouses and dependents. It applies on omahasteaks.com, the mobile app, and participating retail locations, and it **cannot be combined with any other offer or discount.**

The honest catch: Omaha Steaks is a **perpetual-sale** brand. Its packages are almost always marked 40–70% off list, and it runs stackable public promo codes (e.g. "$30 off" / free-shipping deals) plus a free Steakhouse Rewards program. Because the $10 military credit can't be combined with those public deals, on most carts **the public sale price + a public promo code beats the $10 military discount outright** — so the verified shopper should compare both totals and take the lower, not reflexively use the military code. Archetype: **C (real discount, but the sale usually beats it)** with a secondary **E (ID.me identity discount)**.

Separately, on Veterans Day (Nov 11) Omaha Steaks has historically run a richer **10% sitewide + free shipping on select packages** offer with a troop ID — a better military deal than the everyday $10, but date-boxed. Ignore coupon-aggregator "Omaha Steaks military 20%/50% off" pages — the only verified everyday military term is **$10 off $79+**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The $10 military discount "cannot be combined with any other offers or discounts"** (Omaha Steaks military terms) — so it will not stack with a public sale price, a promo code, or the ID.me 10%/free-ship offer. It's an either/or.
2. **Public package sale prices and public promo codes DO combine with each other** (Omaha Steaks routinely stacks a % code and free shipping on an already-marked-down package) — this everyone-path is why the sale usually beats the military credit.
3. **The ID.me "Extra 10% off + Free Shipping $139+" is its own offer**, not a stack on top of the $10 military credit — pick one.
4. **Veterans Day 10% + free shipping is a standalone dated promo**, not stackable with the everyday $10.
5. No Omaha Steaks GovX storefront and no military-exchange grocery channel — those paths don't exist here.
