## Headline finding

**Anytime Fitness officially confirms it offers a military discount — but it is franchise-discretionary, not centrally published.** Anytime Fitness's own FAQ says plainly: *"Do you offer student, senior, or military discounts? We do! … However, it is up to each location to opt-in to offer the discount. It's always best to check with your local club directly to learn what discounts are available near you."* Because each club is **independently owned and operated**, there is **no corporate-set military percentage or dollar amount** — the discount, if any, and whether joining/key-fob fees are waived, are decided club-by-club. So the honest headline: *Anytime Fitness confirms a military discount exists, but the amount is set by your local franchise and isn't published nationally — you must call your specific club to learn the rate, and you should also weigh HSA/FSA eligibility and off-base alternatives (base gyms are free) before signing.* Archetype: **franchise-discretionary — brand confirms it exists, but the number lives at the local club.**

### FAQ

#### Does Anytime Fitness offer a military discount?
Yes — Anytime Fitness's FAQ confirms it offers military (and student/senior) discounts. But it's **opt-in per club**, so the amount varies and isn't published nationally. Call your local club to confirm.

#### How much is the Anytime Fitness military discount?
There's **no set national amount** — each independently owned club decides. The "10–25%" figures online are third-party generalizations, not a corporate rate. Ask your specific club.

#### How do I get the military discount?
Contact your local Anytime Fitness, ask if they offer a military discount, and bring **proof of service** (military ID or discharge paperwork) when you sign up. Also ask them to waive the joining/key-fob fees.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Usually yes, with discharge paperwork or a veteran ID — but it's club-dependent, so confirm locally.

#### Does Anytime Fitness use ID.me or GovX?
No central program. Verification is done **at the club** with your military ID or discharge paperwork.

#### Can I use my HSA or FSA for an Anytime Fitness membership?
Possibly. You may qualify with a **Letter of Medical Necessity** (Anytime Fitness points to a Dr. B $15 virtual consult), then submit receipts to your HSA/FSA administrator.

#### What happens to my discount if I move (PCS)?
Memberships transfer with no transfer fee for relocations over 10 miles, but **rates can change** at the new club — reconfirm the military discount there.
