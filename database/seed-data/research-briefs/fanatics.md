# Fanatics Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Fanatics.com offers a **15% off all orders** military and first responder discount, verified through **ID.me** (Troop ID for military, First Responder ID for first responders). It is an evergreen, year-round offer good on most products sold on Fanatics.com **except gift cards**, and it includes **free U.S. shipping** (including P.O. Boxes, FPO and APO addresses) when the discount is applied. Eligible military groups are active duty, National Guard, veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents; first responders (police, sheriff, state trooper, corrections, federal officers, firefighters, EMT, paramedic — active or retired) and their spouses and immediate families also qualify. The single most important caveat: **the discount cannot be combined with any other promotional codes**, and you verify online via the ID.me widget at checkout — there is no in-store program because Fanatics is an online-only retailer. (Source: Fanatics Military & Veterans Discount page and FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

### How To Redeem Online
1. Shop Fanatics.com as you normally would and add eligible items to your cart.
2. Proceed to the checkout page.
3. Click the **Troop ID** button (military) or **First Responder ID** button (first responders) in the ID.me widget.
4. Sign in to or create your ID.me account and verify your military or first responder status.
5. Once verified, ID.me passes the discount back automatically — your 15% off and free U.S. shipping apply to the cart. (Cannot be combined with other promo codes; not valid on gift cards.)

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Fanatics is an online retailer with no physical stores; the discount is redeemed only at Fanatics.com checkout via the ID.me widget. (Source: Fanatics FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by **ID.me**, a third-party identity-verification service. At checkout you click Troop ID or First Responder ID and verify through ID.me, which requires creating or signing in to an ID.me account (email and password you choose). After verification, ID.me passes the discount to your cart automatically. Fanatics does not state how long a verification lasts or whether re-verification is required per order; ID.me may periodically re-verify group status per its own policies — do not assume a one-time, permanent verification. For verification problems, Fanatics directs shoppers to ID.me Support, available 24/7. (Fanatics lists two different ID.me support numbers across its pages — 844-729-2323 on the FAQ and (866) 775-4363 on the discount page; treat the number as subject to change and use whichever appears on the live page / inside the ID.me flow.)

### FAQ

#### Does Fanatics offer a military discount?
Yes. Fanatics.com offers 15% off all orders to the military community — active duty, National Guard, veterans, retirees, spouses and dependents — verified through ID.me. (Source: Fanatics Military & Veterans Discount page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How much is the Fanatics military discount?
It is 15% off all orders on most products (gift cards excluded), plus free U.S. shipping when applied. (Source: Fanatics Military & Veterans Discount page and FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Fanatics' eligible Troop ID groups are Active Duty, National Guard, Veterans, Retirees, Spouses, and Dependents. (Source: Fanatics FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### How do I verify my status?
At checkout, click the Troop ID button (military) or First Responder ID button (first responders) in the ID.me widget, then verify through your ID.me account. The discount is applied automatically once verified. (Source: Fanatics FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does Fanatics use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Fanatics' official pages name **ID.me** (Troop ID / First Responder ID) as the verification method. Some coupon aggregators reference GovX or SheerID, but those are not confirmed on Fanatics' own military-discount pages — treat ID.me as the authoritative path. (Source: Fanatics Military & Veterans Discount page; ID.me Fanatics storefront, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No — Fanatics is online-only. The discount is redeemed at Fanatics.com checkout via the ID.me widget; there are no physical Fanatics stores for in-person redemption. (Source: Fanatics FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### What is excluded?
Gift cards are excluded, and the discount cannot be combined with any other promotional codes. The offer is for personal use and cannot be shared with non-eligible friends. (Source: Fanatics FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The military/first responder discount cannot be combined with other promotional codes. It does apply to "all orders" on most products, but you cannot stack it with a separate coupon. (Source: Fanatics Military & Veterans Discount page, accessed June 23, 2026.)

#### Does Fanatics offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Fanatics offers a **first responder** discount (same 15%, via ID.me First Responder ID) covering police, sheriffs, state troopers, corrections officers, federal officers, firefighters (career & volunteer), EMTs, and paramedics — active or retired — plus spouses and immediate families. Teacher, nurse, government, and student discounts are **not stated** on Fanatics' official military/first-responder pages. (Source: Fanatics FAQ, accessed June 23, 2026.)
