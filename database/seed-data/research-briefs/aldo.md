# ALDO — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~$120 pair of ALDO shoes (a new-arrival, full-price style).

## Headline finding

ALDO offers a genuine, official **20% military discount** on new-arrival (full-price) merchandise, verified through **SheerID** (and surfaced via **ID.me** as well), valid **online and in store**. Online you verify once, receive a **single-use promo code**, and enter it at checkout; in store you present valid military/veteran ID. The catch is scope: the 20% applies to **new arrivals only** and **cannot be combined** with other offers, loyalty rewards, sale items, select limited-edition collaborations, shoe-care products, or gift cards. So the real decision is 20%-off-full-price vs ALDO's frequent public sales (often 40–60% off) — on marked-down styles a sale beats the military code, but on a just-dropped full-price pair the 20% is the best available. This is the fashion-retail archetype: solid SheerID-verified percentage, locked to full-price/new-arrival inventory, non-stackable.

- On-page chooser: "Is the item full-price/new-arrival?" → 20% military code. "Is it already marked down?" → the sale is your better price.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Non-combinable** — the 20% cannot be used with other offers, promo codes, or ALDO loyalty rewards.
2. **Sale items excluded** — the code only applies to full-price new arrivals; on discounted styles the sale price stands alone.
3. **Category exclusions** — select limited-edition collaborations, shoe-care products, and gift cards don't qualify.
4. **Single-use online code** — you verify via SheerID and get a one-time code; it's not a reusable coupon.
5. **Cashback portals** (ID.me Shop / Rakuten-style) attach to the transaction, so they can add a small % on a sale purchase, but not alongside the exclusive military code.

### Who Qualifies

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to ALDO's military-discount page and verify through **SheerID** (or via ID.me).
2. Receive your **unique single-use promo code**.
3. Add qualifying **new-arrival** item(s) to your bag.
4. Enter the code at checkout to take 20% off.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Bring a valid military or veteran ID.
2. Present it at checkout and ask for the 20% military discount.
3. Applies to full-price new arrivals only (same exclusions as online).

### How Verification Works
ALDO uses **SheerID** to instantly confirm military status; the offer is also surfaced through **ID.me Shop**. After verification you get a one-time code (online) — there's no permanent account discount, so re-verify for future purchases as required.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Applies to full-price **new arrivals** only; sale items excluded.
- Cannot be combined with other offers, promo codes, or loyalty rewards.
- Excludes select limited-edition collaborations, shoe-care products, and gift-card purchases.
- Online code is single-use.
