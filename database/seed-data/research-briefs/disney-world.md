# Walt Disney World Resort Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Walt Disney World Resort (Florida) runs an annual "Disney Military Salute" promotion offering two distinct deals to U.S. military members: (1) deeply discounted, flat-priced multi-day theme park tickets, and (2) tiered percentage discounts on rooms at select Disney Resorts Collection hotels. For 2026 the ticket offer prices a 4-Day Salute ticket from $409 (Park Hopper) up to $529 for the new "Disney Celebrates America Military Salute Ticket" with Park Hopper Plus — all flat prices, not a percentage off. Room discounts run 15%–30% off depending on resort tier and season. Eligibility is limited to active or retired U.S. military (including National Guard, Reservists, Coast Guard, Space Force, PHS and NOAA Commissioned Corps) and their spouses with a valid, active U.S. Military ID; tickets are valid January 1–December 18, 2026, require advance park reservations, carry blockout dates, and are capped at 6 tickets per eligible member or spouse. The single most important caveat: these are seasonal, time-limited promotional offers with strict eligibility and activation rules — they are not a general public discount and not a flat percentage, and Disney verifies military ID at purchase, at first ticket activation, and at hotel check-in.

### Intro
Walt Disney World Resort in Florida offers a seasonal "Disney Military Salute" each year with two separate deals for U.S. service members: deeply discounted multi-day theme park tickets at flat promotional prices, and percentage discounts (15%–30%) on rooms at many Disney Resorts Collection hotels. These are not everyday discounts and not a single percentage off — they are limited-time offers with specific dates, blockout periods, and purchase limits.

Eligibility is restricted to active or retired U.S. military (including National Guard, Reservists, Coast Guard, Space Force, and the PHS and NOAA Commissioned Corps), plus their spouses, who can present a valid and active U.S. Military ID. Disney checks military ID in person — when you buy, when you first activate a ticket, and at hotel check-in.

### How To Redeem Online
Disney does not sell these Salute tickets through a self-serve online checkout. For hotels:
1. Review eligible resorts and savings on Disney's official 2026 Military Hotel Discount page.
2. Call Disney at (407) 939-7830 to check availability and book a room with the offer.
3. Present a valid, active U.S. Military ID at check-in; the military member or spouse must stay in the room.

### How To Redeem In Store
For Salute theme park tickets:
1. Visit a participating U.S. military base ticket office, the Shades of Green Resort ticket office, or a Walt Disney World theme park ticket window.
2. Present a valid, active U.S. Military ID (Eligible Service Member or spouse) to purchase. No tax if bought at a base office or Shades of Green; tax added at park ticket windows.
3. Activate the ticket at a Walt Disney World theme park ticket window — tickets are not valid until activated by the Eligible Service Member or spouse.
4. Make advance theme park reservations immediately after purchase (required; limited availability).

### How Verification Works
Disney does not use a third-party verification service (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute) for this promotion. Verification is in person via a valid and active U.S. Military ID. The ID is checked at the point of purchase, again when the ticket is first activated at a park ticket window (which the Eligible Service Member or spouse must do), and at hotel check-in for the room offer. There is no online account flow; for booking help or questions, Disney's reservation line is (407) 939-5277 (general) or (407) 939-7830 (military room offer).

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Tickets are valid for park entry only — no parking and no discounts on dining, merchandise, or other offerings.
- Valid Jan 1–Dec 18, 2026; tickets expire and cannot be used after Dec 18, 2026.
- Blockout dates: March 29–April 11, 2026 and November 22–28, 2026.
- Advance theme park reservations required for every visit date.
- Max 6 Salute tickets per Eligible Service Member or spouse (5 if they hold a valid Annual Pass); 1 of the 6 must be used by the member or spouse.
- Each ticket must be used by the same person on all days; tickets are nontransferable, nonrefundable, and cannot be resold.
- Cannot be combined with other discounts or promotions (hotel offer exception: combinable only with the 2026 Kids Dining Plan Offer).
- Hotel offer: limited room allocation; valid military ID required at check-in and the member/spouse must stay in the room; excludes certain room types (e.g., Copper Creek Cabins; Polynesian Bungalows/Villas; suites at Moderate/Deluxe/Deluxe Villa resorts; Club Level; 3-Bedroom Grand Villas); additional per-adult charges may apply for more than 2 adults per room.
- "100% disabled veteran" eligibility is NOT stated on Disney's official 2026 pages — do not assert it.

### Best Ways To Save More
- Add the specially-priced Memory Maker (Disney PhotoPass) for $98 (max 2 per eligible member/spouse), purchasable only at WDW park ticket windows by eligible members/spouses.
- Buy tickets at a base ticket office or Shades of Green to avoid sales tax (tickets bought at park windows add tax). Actual base prices may be lower than Disney's published prices.
- Hotel offer can be combined with the 2026 Kids Free Dining Plan Offer (kids ages 3–9) when booking a qualifying Walt Disney Travel Company package.
- Shades of Green (the Armed Forces Recreation Center on WDW property) offers its own military-only resort and ticket pricing — worth comparing separately.
- Non-military savings open to all (for comparison): seasonal Disney room offers (e.g., "Save up to 30% on rooms" general public offer) and Florida Resident ticket offers.

### FAQ

#### Does Walt Disney World offer a military discount?
Yes. Walt Disney World runs an annual Disney Military Salute promotion with discounted multi-day theme park tickets and percentage savings on select Disney Resort hotel rooms. For 2026 the offer covers visits and stays January 1–December 18, 2026.

#### How much is the Walt Disney World military discount?
Tickets are flat promotional prices, not a percentage: for 2026, 4-Day from $409, 5-Day from $429, 6-Day from $449 (Park Hopper), with Park Hopper Plus adding $30. The new Disney Celebrates America Military Salute Ticket is $499 (Hopper) or $529 (Hopper Plus). Hotel rooms are discounted 15%–30% depending on resort and season.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Spouses qualify if they present a valid and active U.S. Military ID. Active and retired members qualify. Disney's official eligibility lists "active or retired" military and specific Commissioned Corps — it does not name general veterans who are neither active nor retired. Dependents/children are not listed as independent buyers, but eligible members/spouses may buy tickets for use by family and friends.

#### How do I verify my military status?
In person, with a valid and active U.S. Military ID. Disney checks it at purchase, at first ticket activation at a park ticket window, and at hotel check-in. There is no online verification.

#### Does Walt Disney World use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Disney's military offers are verified by U.S. Military ID in person, not through any third-party verification service.

#### Can I buy these tickets in advance, and where?
Yes — at participating U.S. military base ticket offices, the Shades of Green Resort ticket office, or Walt Disney World theme park ticket windows. Tickets bought at a base office or Shades of Green are tax-free; those bought at park windows add tax.

#### What is excluded?
Tickets cover park entry only (no parking, dining, or merchandise discounts), can't be combined with other discounts, are nonrefundable and nontransferable, and have blockout dates (March 29–April 11 and November 22–28, 2026). Park reservations are required, and there's a 6-ticket cap per eligible member or spouse.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or other offers?
No for tickets. The hotel room offer cannot be combined with other discounts either, with one exception: it can be combined with the 2026 Kids Dining Plan Offer.

#### Does Walt Disney World offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
The Disney Military Salute is military-only. Disney does not publish a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount under this promotion. Florida Resident offers and general seasonal room offers are available to broader audiences.
