# DSW (Designer Shoe Warehouse) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

DSW (Designer Shoe Warehouse) offers a **10% military discount, valid online only**, redeemed by verifying military status through **BeansiD (Student Beans)** and entering the resulting one-time code at dsw.com checkout. Eligible groups, per DSW's verification provider and Military.com, are **active duty, military retirees, veterans, Reserve/National Guard, and dependents**. The single most important caveat: the offer is **not valid in DSW U.S. stores, in DSW Canada, or on www.dsw.ca** — it is an online-U.S.-only, one-time-use, limit-one-per-customer code, and brand exclusions apply (see dsw.com/exclusions). DSW does not publish an in-store military discount. DSW also runs parallel 10% BeansiD offers for first responders, teachers, and healthcare workers.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to DSW's special group discount page at dsw.com (dsw.com/special-group-discount; the dsw.com/military-discount path also routes here).
2. Select the Military / Veteran discount option to launch the BeansiD (Student Beans) verification widget.
3. Create a free BeansiD account (or log in) and verify your military status.
4. Receive your one-time DSW discount code.
5. Add eligible items to your cart at dsw.com and enter the code at checkout to take 10% off (limit 1 per customer, per transaction).

### How To Redeem In Store
DSW's military discount is **not valid at DSW U.S. stores**. There is no in-store military redemption path published. In-store shoppers should rely on DSW's VIP Rewards program and posted sales instead. (Source: [BeansiD DSW Military](https://beansid.com/us/dsw/military) — "Not valid at DSW US stores, DSW Canada, or www.dsw.ca.")

### How Verification Works
DSW verifies military status through **BeansiD (Student Beans, operated by Pion)**. From DSW's special group discount page, selecting the military offer launches a Student Beans widget. You create or log into a free BeansiD account and prove eligibility; once verified, BeansiD issues a one-time DSW code to use at checkout. The code is single-use and limited to one per customer. DSW's terms are subject to change without notice. For help, DSW directs shoppers to the shoephoria! Center at 1.866.DSW.SHOES or customerservice@dsw.com. (How often BeansiD requires re-verification for a new code is not stated in the official source.)

### FAQ

#### Does DSW offer a military discount?
Yes. DSW offers 10% off for verified military shoppers, valid online only at dsw.com, through BeansiD (Student Beans) verification. (Source: BeansiD; Military.com)

#### How much is the DSW military discount?
10% off a qualifying online purchase, one-time use, limit one per customer per transaction. (Source: BeansiD)

#### Do veterans, retirees, Reserve/National Guard, and dependents qualify?
Yes. Military.com lists the DSW offer as available to active duty, military retirees, veterans, Reserve/National Guard, and dependents. Military spouses are not separately named (likely covered under dependents — verify in the BeansiD flow). (Source: Military.com)

#### How do I verify my military status for DSW?
Go to DSW's special group discount page, choose the military offer, and complete BeansiD (Student Beans) verification with a free account. Once verified you get a one-time code for checkout. (Source: BeansiD; DSW page)

#### Does DSW use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
DSW's official military offer is verified through **BeansiD (Student Beans)**, not ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute. Some third-party listings reference ID.me, but DSW's own special group discount page routes through the Student Beans widget. (Source: BeansiD; DSW page markup)

#### Can I use the DSW military discount in stores?
No. The discount is online-only and explicitly "Not valid at DSW US stores, DSW Canada, or www.dsw.ca." (Source: BeansiD)

#### What is excluded from the DSW military discount?
Brand exclusions apply (see dsw.com/exclusions), it's one-time use, limited to one per customer per transaction, and other restrictions may apply. Full terms are at dsw.com/en/us/legal/web-terms. (Source: BeansiD)

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale items?
The official source does not state that the code stacks with other promo codes, sale, or clearance pricing, and it is limited to one-time/one-per-customer use. Treat it as non-stacking unless DSW confirms otherwise at checkout. (Source: BeansiD — not stated)

#### Does DSW offer a first responder, teacher, nurse/healthcare, government, or student discount?
DSW offers separate 10% online BeansiD discounts for first responders, teachers, and healthcare workers under the same terms. A specific government-employee or student military-style discount is not stated in the official source. (Source: BeansiD first-responder/teacher/healthcare pages)
