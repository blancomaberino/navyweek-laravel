# Frigidaire — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $1,199 Frigidaire refrigerator bought on frigidaire.com (appliance path); secondary $50 water-filter cart for the parts path.

## Headline finding

Frigidaire runs a **real, year-round military/hero discount, verified through SheerID (not ID.me, not GovX)** — but it comes in **two separate tiers that people constantly confuse**: **10% off home appliances** for military and first responders, and a much stronger **25% off parts, accessories, and filters purchased on Frigidaire.com** for military, first responders, and teachers. The 25% parts tier is the standout — it is the single best way to buy Frigidaire water filters and OEM parts anywhere. For a whole appliance, the honest answer is that the **10% code often loses to a big-box retailer sale** (Lowe's, Home Depot, Best Buy routinely mark Frigidaire units down more than 10%), so the verified shopper should price-check before burning the one-time code. Archetype: **E — multi-tier identity discount via SheerID** (no coupon/cashback stacking game), with a **secondary C flavor** on appliances (a deep public sale frequently beats the 10%).

> **Accuracy notes.** (1) **ID.me does not offer Frigidaire** — ID.me's own store page states "We're not aware that Frigidaire offers Military discounts." Any "verify with ID.me" claim for Frigidaire is wrong; the provider is **SheerID**. (2) Coupon aggregators blur the two tiers into a single "25% off Frigidaire military" — that is false: **25% is parts/filters only and explicitly excludes appliances**; appliances are **10%**. (3) frigidaire.com is a fully client-rendered SPA that would not render for direct scraping on the access date; the load-bearing confirmation therefore comes from **SheerID (the verification provider that hosts the offer) and Frigidaire's own SheerID subdomain (frigidaire.sheerid.com, "Rewarding our Heroes")**, corroborated by military.com and mymilitarybenefits. Re-verify exact tier terms on frigidaire.com/en/discounts before major changes.

### FAQ
#### Does Frigidaire offer a military discount?
Yes. Verified military members get **10% off appliances** and **25% off parts, accessories, and filters** on Frigidaire.com, through SheerID.

#### How much is the Frigidaire military discount?
**10% on home appliances** and **25% on parts/accessories/filters** (Frigidaire.com only). They're separate offers, not a combined 35%.

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — active duty, reserve/Guard, veterans, retirees, and families of active personnel are listed as eligible for the appliance offer; verify current groups at checkout via SheerID.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **SheerID** on Frigidaire's discounts page. You get a one-time-use code on approval.

#### Does Frigidaire use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**SheerID.** Frigidaire is **not** on ID.me or GovX — ID.me itself says it isn't aware of a Frigidaire military offer.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
It's redeemed at **frigidaire.com** checkout; some sources also mention the Frigidaire app and participating retail locations. The code is single-use.

#### What is excluded?
The 25% tier excludes **appliances** and any purchase not made on Frigidaire.com. Codes are one-time use; the filter-subscription bonus applies to the first order only.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, sales, or cashback?
Not confirmed — treat the SheerID code as **non-stackable**. Portals like Rakuten commonly void cashback on coupon/verified checkouts, so don't rely on stacking.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Frigidaire?
For **parts/filters**, the 25% SheerID code. For an **appliance**, compare the 10% code against a deeper retailer sale or an open-box unit — the public price often wins.

#### Does Frigidaire offer a first responder or teacher discount?
Yes — first responders get the same 10% appliances / 25% parts tiers; **teachers** get the **25% parts/accessories/filters** tier (US only).
