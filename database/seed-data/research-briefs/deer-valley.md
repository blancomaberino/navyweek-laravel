# Deer Valley Resort — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **adult single-day winter lift ticket** at Deer Valley Resort (Park City, Utah). Percentages are the durable output; window rates float by date.

> **Scope note:** Deer Valley's own **resort discount** (this brief) is separate from the **Grand Hyatt Deer Valley "Salute to Service"** hotel program — see NavyWeek's [Grand Hyatt Deer Valley](/discount/grand-hyatt-deer-valley/) brief. If you're staying at the Grand Hyatt, that program's *up to 75% off the window rate* on tickets beats the resort's own ~25%, so cross-check both.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Deer Valley Resort runs its own military discount on lift tickets and passes: roughly 25% off throughout the ski season**, with a **military single-day winter lift ticket published at $189** and discounted season passes (e.g., a military **Summer Access Pass at $247**, full-day bike ticket **$42.75–$46.50**). Eligibility is broad — **active, reserve, retired, and honorably discharged U.S. military, plus their spouse and dependents 23 and under**. You verify through **SheerID** online, but the rule that trips people up is that **every military ticket and pass must be issued in person at a Deer Valley ticket window** with a valid military photo ID — you **cannot** print one at a kiosk, even if you bought online. The bigger-savings move for anyone booking lodging: staying at **Grand Hyatt Deer Valley** unlocks tickets at **up to 75% off the window rate** — far past the resort's own ~25%. Archetype: **ski resort with a self-run ~25% military rate + a deeper hotel-partner ticket channel** — the play is *stay at the Grand Hyatt for up-to-75%-off tickets if you're booking lodging; otherwise use the resort's ~25% SheerID rate, picked up at a window.*

Tickets/passes are **non-transferable** — someone else can't pick them up.

### FAQ

#### Does Deer Valley Resort offer a military discount?
Yes — roughly 25% off lift tickets and passes throughout the season, with a military single-day winter lift ticket published at $189. Eligibility is verified through SheerID, and tickets are issued in person at a ticket window.

#### How much is the Deer Valley military discount?
About **25% off** lift tickets and passes; the military page publishes a **$189** winter day ticket, a **$247** Summer Access Pass, and **$42.75–$46.50** summer bike tickets. Confirm current-season rates before buying.

#### Who qualifies?
Active, reserve, retired, and honorably discharged U.S. military, plus their spouse and dependents 23 and under. National Guard is named only in the Grand Hyatt program — confirm with the Ticket Office for the resort discount.

#### How do I verify — does Deer Valley use SheerID, ID.me, or GovX?
Deer Valley uses **SheerID** online. You also must show a valid military photo ID in person at a ticket window before the ticket/pass is issued.

#### Can I print my military lift ticket at a kiosk?
No. Military tickets require in-person ID verification at a Deer Valley ticket window before they're issued — even if you bought online.

#### What's the difference between the Deer Valley discount and Grand Hyatt "Salute to Service"?
Deer Valley's own ~25% is on lift tickets/passes for eligible military. Grand Hyatt Deer Valley's "Salute to Service" is a separate hotel program: guests staying there buy tickets up to 75% off the window rate and get lodging/spa/F&B discounts.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to ski Deer Valley?
If you're booking lodging, **stay at the Grand Hyatt** and buy tickets there (up to 75% off the window rate). Otherwise use the resort's **~25% SheerID rate** ($189 winter day ticket), and price a Military Season Pass if you'll ski several days.

#### Does Deer Valley run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
The ~25% military rate is a **season-long standing discount**, not a holiday promo; the Deer Valley + Grand Hyatt Salute to Service partnership was highlighted around Veterans Day 2025. Re-check before each season.
