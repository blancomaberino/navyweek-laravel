# Cirque du Soleil — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one adult ticket to a Las Vegas resident show at ~$120 face value (e.g., a mid-tier *Mystère* / *"O"* category seat).

## Headline finding

Cirque du Soleil has **no single, flat, publicly published "military discount percentage"** on cirquedusoleil.com — the deals pages there ("save up to 20/25/30%") are general public promotions open to everyone, not a military offer. The real military savings come through two verified channels: **(1) the Las Vegas box office**, where **active military** (and children under 12) can get **35% up to 50% off select resident shows based on availability** — box-office/phone only, never online, not combinable; and **(2) GovX Tickets** (govx.com), a free military/first-responder/government marketplace where verified members access discounted Cirque tickets online for both touring and Las Vegas resident shows. Cirque behaves as a **discretionary / box-office-gated** brand: the deepest deal (Vegas box office) is availability-based and active-duty-oriented, while GovX is the dependable, broadly-eligible online path (veterans and family included). Best move: **if you're active duty and in Las Vegas, call the box office**; **everyone else (veterans, family, touring-show cities) uses GovX**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Box-office military rate does not stack.** Treasure Island states child and active-military discounts "may not be combined with any other offer." One discount only.
2. **Box-office military rate is box-office/phone only** — "currently not available online." You cannot apply it on cirquedusoleil.com checkout or the app.
3. **GovX pricing is the member price** — it is not a code layered on a public sale; you don't stack it with cirquedusoleil.com promos (different seller). Savings are baked into the GovX-listed price.
4. **Public cirquedusoleil.com "up to X%" promos are not military discounts** and are not additive to either military channel — they are the fallback everyone already gets.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — eligible for the deep Las Vegas box-office rate (35–50% off select shows, availability-based) and GovX.
- **Veterans** — verified through GovX (and ID.me); the primary source for the Vegas box-office rate specifies "active military," so veterans should rely on GovX/ID.me rather than assuming the box-office deep rate.
- **Military spouses / dependents / family** — eligible through GovX per GovX's eligibility rules.
- **First responders, government employees, teachers** — eligible through GovX (not a "military" discount, but the same marketplace).
- **Children under 12** — eligible for the Las Vegas box-office child rate alongside an adult purchase.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Online (GovX — the reliable path):**
1. Go to govx.com and create a free GovX ID; verify your military/veteran/first-responder/government status (real-time database, email, or document upload).
2. Open the Cirque du Soleil store or the specific show listing on GovX.
3. Select seats — the member price is already discounted; check out.

**Las Vegas box office (active military, deepest rate):**
1. Call the Cirque Las Vegas box office at **1-702-894-7722** (or Treasure Island at **1-866-712-9308** for *Mystère*), or visit any Las Vegas Cirque box office in person.
2. Ask for the active-military rate for your show and date; availability is limited and seat categories vary.
3. Present a valid military ID; pay the discounted rate (not combinable with other offers). This rate is **not available online**.

### How Verification Works
- **GovX ID** is free and one-time: it checks DoD/government databases or an uploaded document; approved documents are deleted after approval. Once verified, discounted pricing shows automatically.
- **Box office** verification is a **live military ID check** at the window or a phone booking tied to picking up with ID.
- **ID.me** verification (if you use the ID.me Shop tile) is a separate free account; confirm the actual offer price before buying, since the tile's "up to 30%" is not a guaranteed rate.
- No channel makes verification more permanent than the source states; re-verify if your account lapses.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Las Vegas box-office military/child rates are **based on availability**, **not combinable** with any other offer, and **not available online**.
- Cirque's public "up to 20–30% off" deals pages are general promotions, not a military discount — don't expect them to stack with a military rate.
- GovX savings vary by show, seat, and date; there is no single guaranteed percentage.
- Nevada Live Entertainment Tax and venue/service fees apply on top of ticket price at all channels.
- Veterans' access to the *deep* Vegas box-office rate isn't confirmed by the primary source (which says "active military") — flagged as unverified; use GovX for veteran pricing.

### FAQ
#### Does Cirque du Soleil offer a military discount?
Yes, but not as a flat online code. Active military can get roughly 35–50% off select Las Vegas resident shows at the box office (availability-based), and verified military/veteran/family members can buy discounted tickets online through GovX.

#### How much is the Cirque du Soleil military discount?
There's no single published rate. The Las Vegas box office lists "35% up to 50%" for active military and kids under 12 based on availability; GovX savings vary by show and date (commonly around 20%).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Through GovX, yes — GovX verifies active military, veterans, family members, first responders, and government employees. The Las Vegas box-office deep rate is specified for "active military," so veterans should use GovX (or ID.me) rather than count on the box-office 35–50%.

#### How do I verify my status?
GovX: free GovX ID (database/email/document). Box office: show a valid military ID at the window or when picking up a phone order. ID.me: free ID.me account on the ID.me Shop tile.

#### Does Cirque du Soleil use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Cirque appears on both **GovX** (the main online military ticket channel) and **ID.me Shop**. The Las Vegas box-office rate is verified by an in-person military ID check, not a third-party provider. No SheerID/WeSalute military program is confirmed.

#### Can I use it in stores (box office) or online?
The deep Las Vegas rate is **box office / phone only** — not online. GovX is **online only**. They're separate channels; pick based on where you are and your status.

#### What is excluded?
Box-office military/child rates are availability-based, not combinable with other offers, and unavailable online. Fees and Nevada entertainment tax apply everywhere. Public "up to X%" promos are not a military discount.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. The box-office military rate can't be combined with any other offer, and GovX's member price isn't stacked on top of a public Cirque sale (different seller).

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Cirque du Soleil?
If you're active duty and can reach the Las Vegas box office, that 35–50% rate (when available) is the cheapest. Otherwise, GovX is the reliable best online price; compare it against Cirque's own advance-purchase public deal for your exact show and seat.

#### Does Cirque du Soleil offer a first responder, teacher, or government discount?
Yes, via GovX — first responders, government employees, and teachers verify the same way military members do and access the same discounted ticket marketplace.

#### Can I buy Cirque du Soleil tickets at the Exchange (AAFES/NEX)?
No. Cirque tickets aren't sold through the military exchange, so there's no tax-free exchange path — GovX and the box office are the military channels.
