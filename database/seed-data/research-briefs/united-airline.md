# United Airlines — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one round-trip domestic United economy ticket at roughly **$300 total** (~$250 published base fare + ~$50 government taxes/fees), traveling with **2 checked bags**.

## Headline finding

United Airlines' only currently **published, first-party military fare discount is a 5% "Active-Duty Military Leisure Discount"** on select United / United Express-operated flights, self-verified inside a free MileagePlus profile — and it is **active-duty only** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Space Force, Coast Guard). The 5% applies to the **published base fare only**, excludes taxes/fees, Basic Economy, and promotional fares, and **cannot be combined** with other discounts or United Vacations. For most service members the **free checked-bag benefit is worth far more than the 5%**: up to **3 free bags for personal travel** and **5 free bags (up to 100 lbs each) on military orders**, extended to dependents on the same reservation. **Veterans and retirees who are not on active duty currently have no verified first-party United fare discount** — the WeSalute United 5% offer that aggregators still cite now redirects to WeSalute's general travel-booking portal, whose terms state credits "cannot be earned or redeemed on flights," so it should be treated as discontinued, not asserted as live. Airlines do not sell fares through GovX and airfare booked direct is not covered by cashback portals, so the honest best paths are: the free bags, the 5% if active duty, United Vacations for flight+hotel packages, miles/loyalty, and a United MileagePlus Chase card for non-active-duty flyers who want a free bag and boarding.

**Verdict logic:**
- **Active duty, checking bags →** book on united.com signed into your verified MileagePlus profile: you get the 5% *and* free bags automatically. The bags are the real money.
- **Active duty on orders →** the 5-bag / 100-lb allowance is the standout benefit; make sure the whole trip is United/United Express (partner segments void it).
- **Veteran / retiree / family (not active) →** no first-party fare discount right now; your best levers are flexible-date pricing, MileagePlus miles, a United Chase card for a free bag, and United Vacations for packages.
- **One-line verdict:** *Active duty → verify in MileagePlus and let the free bags + 5% apply. Not active duty → there's no United veteran fare discount today; use miles, a co-brand card, or United Vacations.*
- **On-page chooser:** "Are you currently active-duty (or on active orders)?" → Yes = 5% + free bags path; No = card/miles/United Vacations path. "Are you checking bags?" → Yes = the free-bag benefit is your biggest saver.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The 5% does NOT stack.** United states it "cannot be combined with other travel discount certificates, discounted fares, or United Vacations discounts," and special promotional and Basic Economy fares are ineligible. Pick the 5% *or* a promo fare, whichever is lower. (Source: united.com military page.)
2. **The 5% and the free-bag benefit are independent** and both apply — the discount is a fare reduction; the bag waiver is a separate active-military baggage policy. Active duty booking leisure travel effectively gets both.
3. **Bag benefit dies on partner segments.** If any part of the itinerary is on another airline (including Star Alliance partners), the military bag benefits do not apply. Keep the whole trip United/United Express.
4. **No cashback / GovX layer exists for direct airfare.** Airlines aren't GovX merchants, and cashback portals generally exclude airline tickets booked on the carrier's site — there is nothing to stack there, so don't imply "military code + Rakuten."
5. **United Vacations discount is its own channel** (emailed one-time code) and is not combinable with a price match or another code.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty U.S. military (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Space Force, Coast Guard)** — eligible for the 5% discount and all benefits. (Source: united.com military page)
- **Service-academy cadets/midshipmen (Air Force Academy, West Point, Naval Academy, Coast Guard Academy)** — eligible for benefits. (Source: united.com military page)
- **USPHS & NOAA Commissioned Officers Corps** — eligible for benefits. (Source: united.com military page)
- **Military Reserves / National Guard — on active orders** — eligible. (Source: united.com military page)
- **Recently discharged (within 7 days of scheduled travel)** — eligible. (Source: united.com military page)
- **Dependents of active-duty personnel** — share the checked-bag benefits when on the same reservation (or traveling alone with orders). (Source: united.com checked-bags)
- **Veterans (not active) and retirees** — **Not** eligible for the 5% or the active-military benefits; eligible for the United Vacations package discount. (Source: united.com military page; United Vacations)
- **First responders, medical workers, teachers, government employees, students** — Not stated in official source (no dedicated United discount).

### How To Redeem Online (Active-Duty 5%)
1. Sign in to (or [enroll in](https://www.united.com/en/us/account/join-now/about-me)) a free **MileagePlus** account.
2. In your **MileagePlus profile**, verify your active-duty military status (branch/details).
3. Search flights on united.com or the United app **while signed in** — eligible flights show the discount applied to the base fare.
4. The active-duty account holder must be on the itinerary; you can book up to **9 passengers** per itinerary.

### How To Redeem In Store (Airport — bags/boarding/club)
1. **Free checked bags:** At the counter, present a valid **military ID** (and orders, if traveling on orders) — up to 3 bags free for personal travel, 5 bags free on orders.
2. **Priority boarding:** Active military board with United's pre-boarding group at the gate.
3. **United Club:** Enter with a valid military ID when space is available.

### How Verification Works
The 5% is verified through your **MileagePlus profile** (no third-party verifier or code). United's FAQ notes that supporting documents may be requested and re-verification can be required periodically; consult United's [Military Leisure Discount Verification FAQs](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/company/company-info/military-verification.html) for the current re-verification cadence (the exact interval was not fully rendered at access time — flagged for re-check). Airport benefits (bags, boarding, club) are verified in person with a **military ID**; benefits require the trip to be fully United/United Express operated.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- 5% applies to the **published base fare only** — not government taxes, fees, or carrier-imposed international surcharges. (Source: united.com military page)
- **Basic Economy** and **special promotional fares** are not eligible for the 5%. (Source: united.com military page)
- Flight must be **fully operated by United/United Express** and originate in the U.S., Guam, Puerto Rico, USVI or Canada. (Source: united.com military page)
- 5% **cannot be combined** with other travel discount certificates, discounted fares, or United Vacations discounts. (Source: united.com military page)
- **Checked-bag benefits do not apply** if any segment is on another airline, including Star Alliance partners. (Source: united.com checked-bags)
- Military pet-transport help is limited to **HNL↔GUM on current orders**. (Source: united.com military page)
- United Vacations discount: reported ~$150 off hotel portion, $1,000 minimum / 3-night, one-time emailed code, not combinable with a price match — **verify current terms on the United Vacations page before publishing exact figures** (primary page did not render at access time).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Free checked bags** — the single biggest saver; nearly always beats the 5% in dollar terms (up to 5 bags / 100 lbs on orders).
- **MileagePlus miles + fare sales** — public sales aren't military-gated but are available to everyone; combine with miles.
- **United MileagePlus Chase co-brand card** — free first checked bag (cardholder + companion), priority boarding, and inflight credit; useful for **veterans/retirees** who don't qualify for the active-military perks.
- **United Vacations** — active-duty and veteran package discount for flight+hotel trips.
- **Competitor context:** Delta, American, Alaska, and Southwest also waive checked-bag fees for active-duty on orders; Alaska/JetBlue historically offered a broader WeSalute 5%. No major U.S. carrier publishes a flat everyday veteran fare discount, so United is competitive on active-duty benefits but offers little to non-active-duty veterans.

### FAQ

#### Does United Airlines offer a military discount?
Yes — a **5% Active-Duty Military Leisure Discount** on select United/United Express flights, verified in a free MileagePlus profile, plus free checked bags, priority boarding, and United Club access (space available). The 5% is active-duty only. (Source: united.com military page)

#### How much is the United Airlines military discount?
**5% off the published base fare** (not taxes/fees), which is modest on a typical domestic fare. The higher-value benefit for most is **free checked bags** — up to 3 for personal travel and 5 (100 lbs each) on military orders. (Source: united.com military page; checked-bags)

#### Do veterans, retirees, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Dependents** share the free-bag benefits on the same reservation. **Veterans and retirees not on active duty do not qualify** for the 5% or the active-military benefits, but can use the United Vacations package discount. (Source: united.com military page; United Vacations)

#### How do I verify my military status?
Self-verify active-duty status inside your **MileagePlus profile**; show a **military ID** at the airport for bags, boarding, and club access. No ID.me, GovX, or SheerID is used for the 5%. (Source: united.com military page)

#### Does United use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No — the 5% is verified directly through **MileagePlus**, with no third-party verifier or code. A former **WeSalute** United offer for veterans/family appears **discontinued** (its pages now redirect to WeSalute's general travel portal). (Source: united.com military page; wesalute.com)

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
The 5% is applied **online** (united.com / app) when signed into a verified MileagePlus account. Bags, boarding, and club access are handled **at the airport** with a military ID. (Source: united.com military page)

#### What is excluded?
The 5% excludes taxes/fees, Basic Economy, promotional fares, and non-United-operated flights, and doesn't combine with other discounts. Bag benefits are void if any segment is on another airline. (Source: united.com military page; checked-bags)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale fares?
No. United says the 5% can't be combined with other discounts, discounted fares, or United Vacations, and promotional/Basic Economy fares are ineligible — choose the 5% or the sale fare, whichever is lower. The free-bag benefit still applies alongside whichever fare you book. (Source: united.com military page)

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to fly United?
If you check bags, the **free checked-bag benefit** is your biggest saver; stack it with the 5% if you're active duty booking leisure travel. Keep the whole itinerary on United/United Express so the bag benefit holds. (Source: united.com checked-bags)

#### Does United offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Not stated in United's official source — the published program covers active U.S. military. (Source: united.com military page)
