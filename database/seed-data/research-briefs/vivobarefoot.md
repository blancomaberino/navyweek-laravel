# Vivobarefoot — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one pair of Vivobarefoot everyday shoes at **$160** (Primus Lite-class pair on vivobarefoot.com/us).

## Headline finding

Vivobarefoot has a real military discount: **15% off for military personnel and veterans**, run inside its **"Key Worker" / essential-worker program** and verified by **Gocertify** (not ID.me/SheerID/GovX) — the same program also covers healthcare workers and emergency responders. It's **not combinable** with other codes or offers. The counter-play is Vivobarefoot's own **ReVivo refurbished channel (up to ~40–50% off RRP)** and seasonal sales, which beat 15% outright — and a healthy ~4–5% cashback-portal rate layers on public purchases. Archetype: **E (identity discount is the main path)** with a strong refurb secondary (**C** traits during sales).
