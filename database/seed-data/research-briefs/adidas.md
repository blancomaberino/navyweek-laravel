# adidas Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

adidas offers a **30% discount** to verified military members, first responders, teachers, nurses, medical providers, hospital employees, and seniors 65+, redeemable **online, in store, and at factory outlets** as part of its "Home Team Heroes" program. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me** (not SheerID — aggregators conflict on this). The military community is broadly defined by adidas to include active duty, veterans, retirees, spouses, and dependents. The single most important caveat: a **long list of products is excluded** (select Gazelle/Samba/Campus/SL 72/Spezial, 4D, select Ultraboost, select Y-3, Pharrell, Disney, Human Made, limited-edition Originals, select NMD/Stan Smith/Superstar/adizero, Final Sale, gift cards, and Confirmed App purchases), and there are **monthly redemption caps** (online max 2/month; in-store and factory outlet max 1/month each). The exact **factory-outlet percentage is not published** on adidas's official page — third-party sites claim 15% or 20%, but adidas itself does not state it, so it should not be asserted.

> Accuracy note: Several aggregators and even some military-focused sites describe this offer as verified by **SheerID**, cite a **$250 savings cap / "one use per customer" / expiry 12/31/2026**, or say you can show a **military ID at the register**. None of those are stated on adidas's official discount page. adidas's page specifies **ID.me**, monthly (not per-customer/lifetime) caps, and an **ID.me in-store verification link** (not a physical military ID) for in-store redemption. Treat the SheerID/$250/expiry/military-ID claims as **unverified** as of June 23, 2026.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add your items to the bag and go to checkout.
2. Under **"Use a promo code,"** select **"Verify with ID.me."**
3. Log into your adidas account; a new tab opens to log in with your **ID.me** credentials.
4. Once verified, the **30%** discount is automatically applied to your bag.
5. Limit: **max 2 discounts per month** online.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify in store via adidas's ID.me link: `hosted-pages.id.me/offers/adidas-in-store-cs`.
2. Receive your **unique ID.me discount code**.
3. Use the code at checkout to save.
4. Limit: **max 1 discount per month** in store.

### How To Redeem At A Factory Outlet
1. Verify via adidas's factory-outlet ID.me link: `hosted-pages.id.me/offers/adidas-in-store-fo`.
2. Receive your **unique ID.me discount code**.
3. Use the code at checkout. (Outlet discount percentage is not published by adidas.)
4. Limit: **max 1 discount per month** at the factory outlet.

### How Verification Works
adidas uses **ID.me** to confirm eligibility. You create a free ID.me account and verify your community (Military, First Responder, Teacher, Nurse, Medical Provider, Hospital Employee, or Senior 65+). Online, you click **"Verify with ID.me"** at checkout and the discount applies automatically. For stores and factory outlets, you verify through adidas's ID.me hosted pages and receive a unique code to use at the register. adidas does not publish a re-verification cadence; ID.me may ask you to re-verify if your community status expires. For verification problems, adidas directs shoppers to ID.me support (help.id.me).

### FAQ

#### Does adidas offer a military discount?
Yes. adidas offers 30% off to verified military members (and other "Home Team Heroes" groups) online, in store, and at factory outlets, verified through ID.me.

#### How much is the adidas military discount?
30% off online and in store. adidas does not publish the factory-outlet percentage on its discount page (third-party sites claim 15% or 20%, which we can't confirm).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes. adidas lists active duty, veterans, retirees, **spouses**, and **dependents** under its Military community.

#### How do I verify my status?
Create a free **ID.me** account and verify your community. Online, choose "Verify with ID.me" at checkout; for stores and factory outlets, verify through adidas's ID.me link and use the unique code at the register.

#### Does adidas use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
adidas's official discount page uses **ID.me**. (Despite some third-party claims of SheerID, the official page specifies ID.me.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Verify through adidas's in-store ID.me link to get a unique code, then use it at checkout (max 1 discount per month in store; 1/month at factory outlets; 2/month online).

#### What is excluded?
Many styles are excluded — including select Gazelle, Samba, Campus, SL 72, Spezial, 4D, select Ultraboost, select Y-3, Pharrell, Disney, Human Made, limited-edition Originals, select NMD/Stan Smith/Superstar/adizero, Final Sale items, gift cards, and Confirmed App purchases. The list is "not limited to" those, so check before you buy.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
adidas applies the discount as a single ID.me-verified offer and doesn't state it stacks with other promo codes; Final Sale items are excluded. Assume it can't be combined.

#### Does adidas offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — the same 30% applies to first responders, teachers, nurses, medical providers, hospital employees, and seniors 65+, each verified through ID.me. Students have a **separate** adidas Student Discount program.
