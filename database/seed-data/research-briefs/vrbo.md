# Vrbo — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $1,000 week-long vacation rental (7 nights, ~$143/night, before taxes/fees) on vrbo.com. Effective prices exclude taxes and cleaning/service fees except where noted.

## Headline finding

**Vrbo has no platform-wide military or veteran discount.** Vrbo (an Expedia Group marketplace) does not run a first-party military program — no company-wide code, no ID.me/SheerID identity discount applied by Vrbo itself, no automatic percentage off. Pricing is set by **individual hosts**, so the only "military discount" that exists on Vrbo is one a host chooses to give — typically **5–15%**, granted by the host editing your quote after you message them and (often) show a military ID. It is discretionary, per-listing, and never guaranteed. Archetype: **no-first-party-discount marketplace + travel-loyalty** — the savings come from (1) asking hosts, (2) Vrbo's **One Key** loyalty (earn 1–2% OneKeyCash once you hit Silver/Gold), (3) **ID.me Shop cashback** (a third-party portal, ~5.75% back capped at $50 — this is ID.me's rebate, NOT a Vrbo discount), and (4) structural levers hosts already offer: **weekly/monthly length-of-stay discounts and off-season booking**, which routinely beat any 10% ask. For travelers who want a guaranteed military rate, a hotel booked at the government/military rate (or a military-focused rental site like Crewdogs) is often the more reliable path.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **There is no Vrbo military code to stack** — the platform grants no first-party military discount, so any "military code + X" claim is moot.
2. **A host-set military discount and One Key don't conflict** — the host lowers your quote; OneKeyCash is earned/redeemed by Vrbo on eligible bookings independently. But **OneKeyCash can't be redeemed on Pay Later or package bookings, some vacation rentals, or damage deposits**, so you can't assume you'll spend it on any given stay.
3. **ID.me Shop cashback is a portal, not a Vrbo discount** — you must click through ID.me → Vrbo and complete a standard online booking for it to track. **Negotiating a custom quote or moving off-platform with the host can break portal tracking**, and cashback excludes reservations under ~$20 plus refer-a-friend/listing renewals. Treat ID.me cashback and a host's custom military rate as *either/or* on the same booking, not a guaranteed stack.
4. **Length-of-stay (weekly/monthly) and off-season pricing are structural**, applied by the host's own settings — they're the most reliable savings and apply to everyone, no verification.
5. **One Key status stacks, rates don't (travel norm)** — holding Gold/Platinum earns 2% on every eligible booking, but you can't combine two competing negotiated rates on one stay.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Getting a host military rate (online, before booking):**
1. Open the listing and use "Ask owner a question" / send an inquiry.
2. Mention you're military/veteran and ask if they offer a discount for your dates.
3. If yes, the host sends a revised quote (they edit the price — e.g. $100/night → $85 at 15%).
4. Book from that custom quote. Be ready to show a military/veteran ID if asked.

**ID.me Shop cashback (verified members):**
1. Sign in / verify at shop.id.me and open the Vrbo offer.
2. Click through to Vrbo from ID.me so the visit tracks.
3. Complete a standard reservation (min ~$20; excludes some offers).
4. Cashback (~5.75%, up to $50) posts to your ID.me wallet after the stay clears.

**One Key (automatic):**
1. Join One Key (free) and sign in when booking.
2. Earn OneKeyCash on eligible bookings (Silver 1%, Gold/Platinum 2%).
3. At checkout, "Apply OneKeyCash" on eligible Pay Now rentals.

### How Verification Works
Vrbo runs **no military verification** — there's nothing to verify because Vrbo grants no discount. If a **host** offers a military rate, they set their own proof requirement (commonly a photo of a military/veteran ID). For **ID.me Shop cashback**, you verify your status once with **ID.me**, then click through to Vrbo; the cashback is a third-party rebate to your ID.me wallet, not a discount Vrbo applies. Do not expect an ID.me or SheerID button at Vrbo checkout.

### FAQ

#### Does Vrbo offer a military discount?
Not as a company. Vrbo is a marketplace and sets no platform-wide military discount. The only military discount on Vrbo is one an individual host chooses to give — typically 5–15% if you ask before booking.

#### How much is the Vrbo military discount?
There's no fixed amount because Vrbo doesn't run the program. Hosts who offer one usually give 5–15% off the nightly total, at their discretion.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Whatever the individual host decides. Vrbo sets no eligibility rules. A host may extend a discount to veterans, spouses, or dependents — ask, and be ready to show ID.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Vrbo has no verification because it grants no discount. A host may ask to see a military/veteran ID. Separately, ID.me Shop verifies your status to give cashback (not a Vrbo discount).

#### Does Vrbo use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Vrbo does not apply an ID.me/SheerID/GovX/WeSalute military discount at checkout. ID.me Shop *lists* Vrbo, but only for third-party cashback paid to your ID.me wallet.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
Vrbo is online-only. Any host military rate is arranged by messaging the host before booking; ID.me cashback requires clicking through from shop.id.me.

#### What is excluded?
OneKeyCash is excluded from Pay Later, package bookings, some rentals, and deposits. ID.me cashback excludes sub-$20 reservations, refer-a-friend, and listing renewals. There's no Vrbo military discount to exclude anything from.

#### Can I combine a host discount with cashback or One Key?
One Key OneKeyCash accrues separately and generally coexists with a host rate. But a negotiated custom quote can break ID.me cashback tracking, so treat a host military rate and ID.me cashback as either/or on the same booking.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book Vrbo?
Book a full week for the host's length-of-stay discount, choose off-season dates, message the host for a military rate, and let One Key accrue — that combination beats chasing a nonexistent platform code. Use ID.me cashback on bookings you don't negotiate.

#### Does Vrbo offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not directly. As with military, any per-listing discount is host discretion. ID.me Shop lists Vrbo cashback for nurses, first responders, teachers, government employees, and students.

#### Does Vrbo run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No military-specific sale. Vrbo and Expedia run public seasonal travel promotions open to everyone, not gated to service members.

#### Is there a better option than Vrbo for a guaranteed military rate?
Yes — hotels publish real government/military rates you can book directly, and military-focused rental sites (e.g. Crewdogs) cater to service members. Vrbo can't guarantee a military rate.
