# National Aquarium (Baltimore) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

The National Aquarium in Baltimore (aqua.org) publishes a clear, everyday military/veteran discount: **special year-round, on-site ticket pricing of $39.95** for guests with a valid active military ID or veteran ID, versus the standard adult (ages 21–69) admission of **$49.95** — a **$10 saving (~20% off the adult rate)**. The offer is redeemed **in person at the box office only**; the Aquarium does not publish an online military price and does not name an online verification provider (no ID.me/GovX/SheerID on its own page). Eligible IDs are spelled out precisely: active military show a military ID; veterans show a DoD ID card, a Veteran Health Identification Card, a Veteran ID Card, or a veteran designation on a state-issued driver's license/ID. Dependents eligible for adult admission also get the $39.95 rate with the servicemember's ID. Separately, the Aquarium runs a **consignment ticket program** on military bases nationwide (buy through your local MWR/ITT office).

Two important caveats. (1) The $39.95 rate is an **adult (21–69)** price. Youth (5–20) and seniors (70+) **already pay $39.95** as the standard rate, and children 4 and under are free — so the military discount is really a benefit for the 21–69 adult ticket, not an additional cut for those other ages. (2) **Blue Star Museums:** the National Aquarium does **not** advertise Blue Star Museums participation on its own site and does not appear among confirmed participants; it offers its own year-round military price instead. Blue Star (free summer admission for active-duty families) is a separate NEA program — verify participation each season on the official arts.gov map before relying on it.

### Who Qualifies
- Active duty — eligible (present military ID at the box office)
- Dependents — eligible for the $39.95 rate when eligible for adult admission, using the servicemember's military ID
- Veterans — eligible with a DoD ID card, Veteran Health Identification Card, Veteran ID Card, or veteran designation on a state-issued driver's license/ID
- Retirees — eligible (retired members are referenced for the base consignment program; a retiree military ID satisfies the box-office check)
- Reserve / National Guard — referenced for the consignment program; a valid military ID satisfies the box-office check
- Military spouses — Not separately stated; covered only insofar as a dependent is eligible for adult admission with the servicemember's ID
- First responders — Not stated in the official source
- Teachers / nurses / medical workers / government employees / students — Not stated in the official source (note: a separate "Maryland Mornings" timed offer gives $5 off military but is open to general adults too)

### How To Redeem In Person
1. Go to the box office at the National Aquarium (501 East Pratt Street, Baltimore, MD) on the day of your visit.
2. Present your valid ID: active military show a military ID; veterans show a DoD ID card, Veteran Health Identification Card, Veteran ID Card, or a veteran designation on a state driver's license/ID.
3. The $39.95 adult (ages 21–69) rate is applied. Dependents eligible for adult admission receive the same rate with the servicemember's ID.
4. Children 4 and under are always free; youth (5–20) and seniors (70+) pay the standard $39.95.

### How To Redeem Through Your Base (Consignment Program)
1. Contact your local MWR (Morale, Welfare and Recreation) or ITT (Information, Tickets & Travel) office.
2. Ask about National Aquarium consignment tickets — these are sold on military bases nationwide.
3. Compare the base rate against the $39.95 box-office price; buy whichever is lower for your party.

### How Verification Works
Verification is entirely **in person at the box office** — you show a valid military or veteran ID at the time of purchase. The National Aquarium's page does **not** name an online verification provider (no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID), and it does not publish an online/advance military price. Treat any aggregator claim of an "online military code" or "verify with ID.me for the Aquarium" as unverified. The accepted veteran ID types are listed explicitly on the Special Offers page.

### FAQ

#### Does the National Aquarium in Baltimore offer a military discount?
Yes. It offers special year-round, on-site ticket pricing of $39.95 for guests with a valid active military ID or veteran ID, versus the standard $49.95 adult admission.

#### How much is the National Aquarium military discount?
The military/veteran rate is $39.95 for adult admission (ages 21–69), about $10 off the standard $49.95 adult ticket. Youth (5–20) and seniors (70+) already pay $39.95, and children 4 and under are free.

#### Do veterans, retirees, dependents, and Guard/Reserve qualify?
Veterans qualify with a DoD ID card, Veteran Health Identification Card, Veteran ID Card, or a veteran designation on a state driver's license/ID. Active military and their dependents (eligible for adult admission) qualify with a military ID. The consignment program references active duty, reserve, and retired members — a valid military ID satisfies the box-office check.

#### How do I verify my military status — is there an ID.me code?
Verification is in person at the box office by showing a valid military or veteran ID. The Aquarium's page does not name ID.me, GovX, or SheerID and does not publish an online military price. Ignore aggregator claims of an online military code for this venue.

#### Can I buy the military ticket online?
The official source describes on-site box-office pricing only. There is no published online military rate. You can, however, buy through your base MWR/ITT office via the consignment program.

#### Does the National Aquarium participate in Blue Star Museums?
The Aquarium does not advertise Blue Star Museums participation, and it is not confirmed among 2026 participants; it offers its own year-round military rate instead. Blue Star (free summer admission for active-duty families) is a separate NEA program — check the current list at arts.gov/BlueStarMuseums before relying on it.

#### Can I combine the military discount with other offers?
The official military section does not address combining offers. Treat it as not combinable unless the box office confirms otherwise — do not assume stacking with Maryland Mornings or other promotions.

#### Does the National Aquarium offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Not stated in the official source. The published military rate covers military/veteran guests; a separate "Maryland Mornings" timed offer gives modest early-entry savings but is open to general adults, not a first-responder/teacher program.
