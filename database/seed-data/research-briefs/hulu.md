## Executive Summary

Yes — Hulu offers a military discount, but **only through The Exchange (ShopMyExchange/AAFES)**, not directly on Hulu.com and not via ID.me or SheerID. Verified Exchange-authorized shoppers get **25% off the monthly price of Hulu (With Ads)** when they sign up for a monthly subscription billed directly by Hulu. Eligibility is gated by your **ShopMyExchange.com account**, which covers active-duty service members, retirees, honorably discharged veterans, Guard/Reserve, and (for active-duty/retiree households) authorized dependents. The single most important caveat: the discount applies **only to Hulu (With Ads), only in the US, only when billed by Hulu** (not Apple/Roku/Amazon), and it **cannot be combined with free trials, other offers, or the Disney Bundle** — switching plans after redemption may forfeit the discounted price.

### Who Qualifies
Eligibility is determined by **Exchange (ShopMyExchange) authorization**, not by Hulu directly:
- **Active-duty military** — Eligible (Exchange-authorized).
- **Military retirees** — Eligible (Exchange-authorized).
- **Honorably discharged veterans** — Eligible via the Veterans Online Shopping Benefit (Exchange-authorized; VA-verified). *Note: the veteran online benefit is generally for the veteran's own account; it does not by itself extend Exchange access to the veteran's spouse/dependents.*
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Eligible (Exchange-authorized).
- **Military spouses / dependents** — Eligible **only** if they hold their own Exchange shopping privileges (typically authorized dependents of active-duty or retiree households with a military ID). Not automatically covered for veteran-only households. *(Verify your Exchange status.)*
- **Surviving spouses / Gold Star families** — Not separately stated on the Hulu offer; depends on Exchange eligibility — verify with The Exchange.
- **First responders / nurses / teachers / students / government employees** — **Not eligible** for this offer; it is Exchange-authorized military shoppers only.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Make sure you have a **valid, current ShopMyExchange.com account** (set one up / verify eligibility at shopmyexchange.com if you don't).
2. Go to the **official Exchange Hulu offer page** (shopmyexchange.com/hulu/6679749) and select the Hulu (With Ads) military offer.
3. **Sign up for a new Hulu account or log in to an existing one** through the offer flow — billing must be **directly through Hulu** (not Apple, Roku, or Amazon).
4. Choose the **monthly Hulu (With Ads)** plan and add a valid payment method; agree to the Hulu Subscriber Agreement.
5. The **25% discount** applies and your subscription auto-renews monthly at 25% off until you cancel.

### How To Redeem In Store

You must already be an Exchange-authorized shopper — active duty, retiree, honorably discharged veteran (verified through the VA's online shopping benefit), Guard/Reserve, or an authorized dependent with Exchange privileges. You then access the Hulu offer through the Exchange and complete signup on a Hulu-billed account. ID.me's own Hulu page confirms Hulu has a military discount but notes verification happens "directly with Hulu," which in practice means through the Exchange gateway. For Exchange-eligibility problems, use ShopMyExchange/AAFES support; for billing or subscription issues, contact Hulu Customer Service.

### FAQ

#### Does Hulu offer a military discount?
Yes. Hulu offers **25% off Hulu (With Ads)** for verified military shoppers — but only through **The Exchange (ShopMyExchange)**, not directly on Hulu.com.

#### How much is the Hulu military discount?
**25% off** the monthly price of Hulu (With Ads). The Exchange states "25% off the then-current regular monthly price," so it's a percentage off Hulu's current base price rather than a fixed dollar amount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Honorably discharged **veterans** qualify if they have Exchange (ShopMyExchange) shopping privileges via the Veterans Online Shopping Benefit. **Spouses/dependents** qualify only if they hold their own Exchange privileges (typically authorized dependents of active-duty or retiree households). Verify your status with The Exchange.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through your **Exchange / ShopMyExchange.com account** — not ID.me or SheerID. You must be an Exchange-authorized shopper, then access the Hulu offer through the Exchange and sign up on a Hulu-billed account.

#### Does Hulu use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** ID.me's own page confirms Hulu does not use ID.me for this discount. Verification is through **The Exchange**. Claims that Hulu verifies via SheerID are not supported by any primary source.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No. It's an **online-only subscription** offer billed by Hulu; there's no in-store redemption.

#### What is excluded?
It applies to **Hulu (With Ads) only**, **US only**, and **only when billed by Hulu**. It can't be combined with a free trial, other offers, or the **Disney Bundle**, and it's limited to **one per Exchange account**.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, a free trial, or the Disney Bundle?
No. The official terms state it cannot be combined with a Hulu (With Ads) free trial or any other offers, coupons, discounts, promotions, or pricing, **including the Disney Bundle**.

#### Does Hulu offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
This offer is for **Exchange-authorized military shoppers only**. There is no first-responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount confirmed for Hulu via primary sources. (Students may separately qualify for unrelated Hulu student/Disney+ student pricing — that is not part of this military offer.)
