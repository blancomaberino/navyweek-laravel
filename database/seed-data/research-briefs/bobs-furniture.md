# Bob's Discount Furniture — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $1,000 furniture order (e.g., a sofa), the threshold at which Bob's advertises free delivery.

## Headline finding

Bob's Discount Furniture has **no published, corporate-level military or veteran customer discount**. Its official site (mybobs.com) and blog foreground **charitable and community support** for the military — partnerships with **Operation Homefront, the USO, and K9s For Warriors** — not a percentage-off program for shoppers. The claims of "10% off for military" that circulate on coupon aggregators (worthepenny, militarymodelling, militarymarkdown) are **not verified against any official Bob's source**. There are, however, real signals of **discretionary, store-level discounts**: a Bob's-branded Facebook post states "We proudly offer discounts for Veterans, police, and first responders — reach out to [your local store]." Treat that as manager's-discretion, not a guaranteed policy. The single best savings path for a service member is Bob's own machinery: **sales/clearance + the "Bob's-O-Pedic"/financing offers + price-match**, and simply **asking the store manager** whether they'll extend a military courtesy discount on the day.
