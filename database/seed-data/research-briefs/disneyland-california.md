## Headline finding

**Yes — Disneyland Resort runs official "Disney Military Promotional Tickets" for 2026** (its Disneyland version of the Armed Forces Salute). Rather than a percentage off, Disney sets **flat promotional prices**: a **1-Day 1-Park** ticket at **$104 adult / $98 child**, a **3-Day Park Hopper** at **$314** (or **$416** with Lightning Lane Multi Pass), and a **4-Day Park Hopper** at **$369** (or **$505** with Lightning Lane Multi Pass). The single best path is simply **buying the Salute ticket itself** — it's well below standard multi-day gate pricing and is sold at the **same price** whether you buy at a **participating U.S. military base ticket office (ITT/LTS)** or **online via GovX**, so the "decision" is convenience, not price (base offices can occasionally price *below* the advertised rate). The make-or-break caveat is **eligibility**: this is **NOT an all-veterans discount** — it's for **active OR retired** U.S. military (plus Guard, Reservists, Coast Guard, Space Force, PHS & NOAA commissioned corps) and a **spouse may buy in the member's place (not both)**. A veteran who separated without retiring **does not qualify**. Archetype: **high-value gated travel benefit** — flat pricing, strict per-person caps, no stacking, valid military ID required to buy *and* use.

## Stacking Rules (verified, Disney terms)
1. **The military ticket cannot be combined** with other ticket discounts or promotions — it *is* the discounted price, not a code on top.
2. **Per-member caps are strict:** max **6** 3-Day/4-Day tickets per eligible member or spouse (max **5** for Magic Key passholders), and **at least one must be used by the member or spouse**.
3. **Valid military ID is required to purchase and to use** — the eligible member/spouse must be present per the terms; tickets can't be sold/transferred commercially.
4. **Park reservations still apply** — a valid ticket **plus** a park reservation is required for the same date/park.
5. **No cashback portal, promo code, or ID.me** applies — this is a gated, in-person/GovX-verified benefit.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty U.S. military** — Eligible (all branches + Space Force).
- **Retired U.S. military** — Eligible.
- **National Guard / Reservists** — Eligible.
- **U.S. Coast Guard / PHS / NOAA commissioned corps** — Eligible.
- **Spouse** — May purchase **in the member's place** (not both the member and spouse).
- **Veterans who separated without retiring** — **Not eligible** (this is not an all-veterans discount).
- **Dependents / other family** — Not the purchasing party; travel with the eligible member/spouse per the terms.

### How To Buy And Use
1. Confirm eligibility (**active or retired**, or the spouse buying in their place).
2. Buy at a **participating U.S. military base ticket office (ITT/LTS)** — often the best price — **or online via GovX** (GovX verifies your military status).
3. Bring your **valid military ID** — required to purchase **and** to use the tickets.
4. Make a **park reservation** for each date/park (required alongside a valid ticket).
5. Observe the caps: 3-Day/4-Day max **6** per member/spouse (5 for Magic Key holders); **at least one** must be used by the member or spouse.

### How Verification Works
Verification is **in person** (base ticket office checks your military ID) or **through GovX** online (GovX confirms military status before sale). **Valid military ID is required both to buy and to use** the tickets. There is **no ID.me** path for this offer. Tickets are non-transferable and can't be resold commercially.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Not combinable** with other ticket discounts or promotions.
- **Active/retired (or spouse) only** — separated-without-retiring veterans don't qualify.
- **Caps:** 3-Day/4-Day max 6 per member/spouse (5 for Magic Key passholders); 1+ must be used by the member/spouse.
- **Park reservation required** in addition to a valid ticket.
- **Buy/use windows:** 1-Day through Dec 30, 2027; 3-Day/4-Day valid Jan 1–Dec 31, 2026 (buy through Dec 27, 2026).
- Separate **hotel** offer exists (book by phone; no published %).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Buy at a base ITT/LTS office** — may price **below** GovX's advertised rate.
- **Choose Park Hopper multi-day** for the biggest gap vs. standard gate pricing.
- **Skip Lightning Lane Multi Pass** unless you value it — the base PH ticket is much cheaper.
- **Separate hotel military discount** — call (866) 309-9737 for select-room rates.
- **Combine with general Disneyland savings** (off-peak dates, packing food, etc.) — the ticket itself can't be discounted further.

### FAQ

#### Does Disneyland offer a military discount?
Yes — **Disney Military Promotional Tickets** for 2026, at flat prices (1-Day $104/$98; 3-Day Park Hopper $314; 4-Day $369), for eligible service members.

#### How much is the Disneyland military ticket?
Flat pricing, not a percentage: **1-Day 1-Park $104 adult / $98 child; 3-Day Park Hopper $314; 4-Day Park Hopper $369** (Lightning Lane Multi Pass versions are $416 and $505).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
**Active or retired** U.S. military qualify, and a **spouse** may buy in the member's place (not both). **Veterans who separated without retiring do not qualify.** Family travels with the eligible member/spouse.

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a **valid military ID** in person at a base ticket office, or verify through **GovX** online. ID is required to purchase **and** to use the tickets.

#### Does Disneyland use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**GovX** for online sales; **in-person military ID** at base ticket offices. **Not ID.me or SheerID.**

#### Where do I buy the tickets — base MWR/ITT or the gate?
At a **participating base ticket office (ITT/LTS)** or **online via GovX** — **not** at the Disneyland gate at this price. Base offices may beat the advertised price.

#### What's excluded, and can I combine it with other discounts?
It **can't be combined** with other ticket discounts/promotions; caps apply (max 6 multi-day tickets per member/spouse, 5 for Magic Key holders), and a park reservation is required.

#### Is there a Disneyland military hotel discount too?
Yes — a **separate** offer for select Disneyland Resort hotel rooms (no published %); book by phone at **(866) 309-9737**. See our Disneyland hotel guide.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to do Disneyland?
Buy the **3-Day/4-Day Park Hopper Military Ticket at a base ITT office** (often below GovX's advertised price), skip Lightning Lane Multi Pass unless you value it, and add the separate hotel discount.
