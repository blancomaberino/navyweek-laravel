# Dillard's — Military & Veteran Discount Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $200 apparel/home order at Dillard's.

## Headline finding

**Dillard's has no everyday, year-round military or veteran discount** — none published on dillards.com and ID.me confirms Dillard's does not offer a military community discount. The only military offer Dillard's actually runs is a **limited "Hero Appreciation Days" event**: **20% off in-store purchases** for a **couple of days each year (historically early December, e.g. Dec 4–5)** for **active military, police, and firefighters and their immediate families** — and notably this event does **not** include most veterans (it's aimed at *active* service). Outside that window, the best savings are Dillard's own clearance, coupons, and rewards program. Archetype: **no everyday route (B) + a time-boxed holiday event (H)** — the page's value is correcting the "Dillard's military discount" myth and pinning down the real Hero Appreciation window.

### FAQ

#### Does Dillard's offer a military discount?
Not as an everyday program — ID.me confirms Dillard's has no military community discount. Dillard's does run a limited **Hero Appreciation Days** event (**20% off in-store**, historically early December) for **active military, police, and firefighters and their families**. (Sources: [ID.me – Dillard's](https://shop.id.me/discounts/dillard-s/military); [Dillard's on X](https://x.com/Dillards/status/1864338877101822142); accessed July 6, 2026.)

#### How much is the Dillard's military discount?
There's no standing percentage. During Hero Appreciation Days, Dillard's has offered **20% off in-store**. Outside that event, there is no military discount.

#### Do veterans qualify?
Generally **no** for the standard Hero Appreciation event — its published wording names **active** military plus police and firefighters and their immediate families, not separated/retired veterans. Confirm with your store, since terms can vary by year.

#### How do I verify my military status at Dillard's?
Only during Hero Appreciation Days, by **showing a valid active ID** in store. Dillard's uses no ID.me, GovX, or SheerID program.

#### Does Dillard's use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**No.** ID.me explicitly states it's not aware of any Dillard's military discount, and there's no GovX or SheerID program.

#### Can I get a Dillard's military discount online?
**No.** There's no online military discount or code; the Hero Appreciation event is in-store only.

#### When is Dillard's Hero Appreciation Days?
Historically **early December** (2024 was Dec 4–5). Dates shift year to year — confirm with your local store or Dillard's social channels in late November.

#### What's the cheapest way to shop Dillard's for a service member?
If you're active service/first responder in the event window, use **Hero Appreciation 20%** in store. Otherwise, **clearance and public sales** (often deeper than 20%) plus the **Dillard's Card/Rewards** are your best savings.

#### Does Dillard's offer a first responder, nurse, or teacher discount?
Police and firefighters are included in the **Hero Appreciation Days** event. No separate everyday nurse, teacher, or student program is published.
