# Mavis Discount Tire — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$600 four-tire replacement with install** (four mid-range tires + mounting/balancing/valve stems), the most common big-ticket Mavis visit.

## Headline finding

Mavis Discount Tire runs a **clear, official, chain-wide military discount: 10% off, applied to the sale price before tax, in-store only, for all active-duty military, reservists, and veterans with valid military ID**. This is published on Mavis's own site (mavis.com), which is unusually explicit for a tire chain — the terms, exclusions, and eligibility are stated by the brand itself. The discount is **not available online** and **cannot be combined** with other coupons, vouchers, or offers, and **price-match and warranty transactions are excluded**. Because it can't stack, the real decision is *military 10% vs. the best current Mavis coupon* (Mavis constantly runs "buy 3 get 1," rebate, and oil-change deals). Archetype: *in-store, ID-verified, non-stackable service discount* — simple and honest; the optimization is choosing the single best offer per visit and layering a tire-brand mail-in rebate, which lives outside Mavis's coupon rule.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Non-combinable.** The military/veteran 10% **cannot be combined** with any coupon, voucher, or special offer — it's one discount per transaction, so it's always *military 10% OR the coupon*, never both.
2. **Applied before tax** — the 10% comes off the pre-tax subtotal, so the tax you pay also drops slightly.
3. **In-store only** — no online application; you must present a valid military ID and tell the manager at checkout.
4. **Excluded transactions:** price-match and warranty work don't get the discount.
5. **Manufacturer mail-in rebates** (Michelin, Goodyear, etc.) are the tire brand's promotion, not a Mavis coupon — they can be claimed alongside your in-store discount. This is the only legitimate "stack."

### FAQ
#### Does Mavis Discount Tire offer a military discount?
Yes — 10% off, applied before tax, in-store, for active-duty military, reservists, and veterans with a valid military ID, per Mavis's own site.

#### How much is the Mavis military discount?
10% off the pre-tax total. On a $600 four-tire install that's about $60 off before tax.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans, active-duty, and reservists qualify. Mavis's page doesn't name spouses or dependents — confirm in-store.

#### How do I verify my status?
Show a valid military ID in-store and tell the store manager you qualify before checkout. There's no online verification.

#### Does Mavis use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No — verification is manual, with a military ID at the store. Mavis isn't sold through GovX or the Exchange.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
In-store only. Mavis explicitly excludes online purchases from the military discount.

#### What is excluded?
Online orders, combining with other coupons/vouchers/offers, and price-match and warranty transactions.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. It's one offer per transaction — take the military 10% OR the coupon, whichever is larger. A manufacturer mail-in rebate is separate and can be added.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy tires at Mavis?
Compare the 10% to the best live Mavis coupon, use the larger, and claim any tire-brand mail-in rebate on top. Check NTB/Tire Kingdom if no Mavis is nearby.

#### Does Mavis offer a first responder, senior, or student discount?
Mavis's sibling brands (NTB, Tire Kingdom) publish veteran/first-responder pages; senior-discount language appears on some Mavis materials. Confirm current terms in-store.
