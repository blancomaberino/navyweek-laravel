# Valvoline Instant Oil Change Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Valvoline Instant Oil Change (VIOC) offers a **15% off** in-store military discount on your service invoice. Per the brand's own military page (vioc.com/military), the only stated requirement is to **show a military ID** at a participating Valvoline Instant Oil Change location; the discount is applied at the register. The brand's official page does **not** enumerate which military subgroups qualify (active duty, veteran, retiree, reserve, spouse, etc.) — it states only "Show your military ID," so the precise eligible-group breakdown is **not specified by the primary source** (third-party sites assert active duty/veterans/retirees, but that is unverified against VIOC's own copy). The single most important caveat: the offer **excludes battery replacement and state inspection, cannot be combined with any other offer/discount (including fleet), and is good only at participating locations** — VIOC operates a mix of company-owned and franchise stores, so participation and any additional terms can vary by location. This is an **in-store, point-of-sale** discount; it is not an online code. (Note: ID.me lists VIOC for *cash back*, which is a separate affiliate program, not the in-store 15% discount.)

### FAQ

#### Does Valvoline Instant Oil Change offer a military discount?
Yes. Valvoline Instant Oil Change offers 15% off your service invoice when you show a military ID at a participating location, per its official military page (vioc.com/military).

#### How much is the Valvoline military discount?
15% off your invoice, applied in store. It excludes battery replacement and state inspection and can't be combined with other offers (including fleet).

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Valvoline's official military page only says to "show your military ID" and does not list which subgroups qualify. Whether veterans, spouses, or dependents are covered is not specified by Valvoline — confirm with your local store before your visit.

#### How do I verify my status?
For the in-store discount, just show a valid military ID to the technician at the register. There's no app or account to set up for the 15% offer.

#### Does Valvoline use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not for the in-store 15% discount — that's verified by showing your military ID in person. ID.me separately lists Valvoline Instant Oil Change for *cash back*, which is a different program with its own terms. WeSalute and other sites also list Valvoline, but those are third-party listings, not the brand's in-store mechanism.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes — the military discount is in-store only. There's no online promo code for it.

#### What is excluded?
Battery replacement and state inspection are excluded. The discount also can't be combined with any other offer or discount, including fleet, and is good only at participating locations.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Valvoline's terms state the military discount cannot be combined with any other offer or discount (including fleet). Compare it against any local coupon and use whichever saves you more.

#### Does Valvoline offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Valvoline's military page covers the military discount only. First-responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student pricing is not stated on that page and would be at individual-location discretion if offered — confirm directly with your local store.

#### Is the Valvoline military discount available at every location?
No — it's good only at participating locations. Valvoline Instant Oil Change includes company-owned and franchise stores, so call ahead to confirm participation and any local terms.
