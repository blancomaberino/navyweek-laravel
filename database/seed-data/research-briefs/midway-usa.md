# MidwayUSA — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$200 shooting/optics order** on midwayusa.com (e.g. a mid-tier riflescope or a mixed optics-and-accessories cart), pre-tax. Firearms and ammunition are called out separately because they are routinely excluded from coupons, cashback, and shipping promos.

## Headline finding

**MidwayUSA does not offer a military or veteran discount — and says so plainly on its own site.** Its official Help answer is that it does **not** offer a military discount because founder **Larry Potterfield is an Air Force veteran but believes the best strategy is to charge every customer the same low price.** There is **no verification partner** (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute storefront confirmed on primary sources), so any coupon-site "MidwayUSA military code" or "ID.me military discount" is unverified and should be treated as false. The real savings for a service member come from paths open to *everyone*: the free **MidwayUSA Rewards** loyalty program, **email/text sign-up** coupons, frequent **Sale & Clearance** (up to ~75% on select gear), holiday events (a public **Veterans Day sale, up to ~60% off**, in Nov 2025 — open to all shoppers, not military-gated), and a **cashback portal** layer on non-excluded categories. Archetype: **B (no military route at all)** — no first-party discount and no GovX marketplace — with a secondary **"myth to debunk / loyalty-wallet"** flavor (H): the everyday value is the Rewards program plus sale timing, not any blanket military %.

> Disambiguation: **MidwayUSA** (midwayusa.com, the Columbia, Missouri shooting/hunting retailer) is unrelated to the **USS Midway Museum** (midway.org, San Diego), which *does* offer free/discounted admission to active-duty military. Don't conflate the two — coupon aggregators sometimes mix them.
