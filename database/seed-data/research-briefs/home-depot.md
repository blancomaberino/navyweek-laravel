# The Home Depot Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

The Home Depot offers a **10% everyday military discount** to **U.S. veterans, active-duty service members, and their spouses** on **full-priced, non-appliance merchandise**, in stores and online. Eligibility is verified once through the Home Depot app/website (using third-party verifier **SheerID**), and your verified status is saved to your Home Depot account; in store you present a **virtual ID QR code** in the app at the register, and online the discount applies automatically at checkout when you're logged in. The single most important caveat: aggregators (military.com, The Military Wallet) widely report a **$400 maximum annual discount** (i.e., on roughly the first $4,000 of eligible spend per calendar year) plus a broad exclusion list (appliances, gift cards, clearance/value-priced items, commodities like lumber, labor/installation, delivery, tool rental) — **but The Home Depot's own readable pages do not confirm the $400 cap or the itemized exclusions**, because the official Military Discount FAQ at homedepot.com/c/military is JavaScript-rendered and did not return its terms to our fetch. Treat the cap and the detailed exclusion list as **strongly-reported-but-officially-unconfirmed** and flag them on the page.

> Accuracy note: The discount is **everyday/year-round** for veterans, active duty, and spouses — this was expanded from a holidays-only veteran discount in **December 2021**. Older pages that say veterans only get the discount on patriotic holidays (Memorial Day, July 4th, Veterans Day) are **outdated**. Also distinct: a separate **Military Exchange tax-free program** (via ShopMyExchange.com / myNavyExchange.com) covers appliances and most online products tax-free — that is a different benefit from the 10% homedepot.com discount and reaches a broader audience (retirees, dependents, etc.).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to homedepot.com (or open the Home Depot app) and **log in** to your Home Depot account (or create one).
2. Visit the **Military Discount** page (homedepot.com/military/discount) and select **"Verify Military Status."**
3. Complete verification through **SheerID** (name, branch of service, and possibly supporting documentation); this is typically quick.
4. Once verified, your military status is saved to your account.
5. Shop eligible items; while you're **logged in**, the **10%** discount applies automatically at checkout. You can ship to home or pick up in store.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Verify your military status in advance (see online steps) so it's saved to your Home Depot account.
2. Install the **Home Depot app** and log in on your phone.
3. In the app, open your account profile and find the **"Military Discount Program"** section.
4. Tap **"Scan Virtual ID"** to display your **QR code**. (Screenshots or printouts of the QR code do not work — it must be live in the app.)
5. Show the QR code at the register; the associate applies the **10%** discount to eligible items.

### How Verification Works
The Home Depot verifies military eligibility through **SheerID** (a third-party verification service) during a one-time enrollment in the Home Depot app or on homedepot.com. You sign in to (or create) a Home Depot account, choose "Verify Military Status," and submit your details; SheerID confirms eligibility and may request supporting documents. Your verified status is then stored in your Home Depot account, so you don't re-verify on every visit. For in-store use you present a **virtual ID QR code** generated live in the app. Spouses enroll separately under their own account using a dependent ID and the service member's information. Note: you can **no longer** simply show a DD-214 or a physical military/veteran/dependent ID at the register — the app-based virtual ID (or being logged in online) is required. For help, Home Depot customer service is 1-800-466-3337. *(Provider name and mechanics are sourced from military-benefit publications; Home Depot's own FAQ page is JavaScript-rendered and not directly readable — confirm at homedepot.com/c/military.)*

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Appliances are excluded** from the 10% discount (confirmed, Home Depot IR release). Eligible military shoppers can instead buy many appliances **tax-free** via the Military Exchange program (ShopMyExchange.com / myNavyExchange.com).
- Discount applies to **full-priced** merchandise (confirmed) — so generally **not** clearance or already-reduced items.
- **Commonly reported but NOT confirmed on Home Depot's own readable page (flag):** a **$400 maximum annual discount** that resets each calendar year (≈ first $4,000 of eligible spend); plus exclusions for **gift cards, freight/delivery, installation and other labor, value-priced/clearance items, tool rental fees, commodity products (lumber, wire, building materials), and manufacturer-restricted goods.**
- **Personal use only (reported):** no commercial credit card, no job number on the order; not applied retroactively; generally non-stackable with other discounts (e.g., Pro/contractor discount), though it may combine with a storewide deal.
- **Region:** United States (homedepot.com). Home Depot Canada/Mexico are separate sites with separate terms.

### FAQ

#### Does The Home Depot offer a military discount?
Yes. The Home Depot offers a **10% everyday military discount** to U.S. veterans, active-duty service members, and their spouses on full-priced, non-appliance merchandise, in stores and online.

#### How much is The Home Depot military discount?
**10% off** eligible (full-priced, non-appliance) items. Independent military-benefit sites also report a **$400 maximum annual discount** per calendar year, but that cap is not confirmed on Home Depot's own readable terms page — verify before relying on it.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans, active-duty members, and spouses qualify for the 10% discount (Home Depot official). Non-married/non-spousal dependents are reported **not** eligible for the 10% discount, though dependents and retirees can qualify for the separate **Military Exchange tax-free** program.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Log in to (or create) a Home Depot account, go to the Military Discount page, and choose "Verify Military Status." Verification runs through **SheerID** and your status is saved to your account. In store, show the **virtual ID QR code** in the Home Depot app.

#### Does The Home Depot use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Home Depot's enrollment flow is reported to use **SheerID** as the third-party verifier. (Home Depot's own FAQ page is not publicly machine-readable, so confirm on homedepot.com/c/military.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Verify once, then open the Home Depot app at checkout, go to the Military Discount Program section, and scan your **virtual ID QR code**. Screenshots and printouts won't work, and a paper DD-214 or physical ID is no longer accepted at the register.

#### What is excluded?
**Appliances** are excluded (confirmed) and the discount applies to **full-priced** items only. Other commonly reported exclusions — gift cards, clearance/value-priced items, lumber and other commodities, labor/installation, delivery, and tool rental — are widely cited but not confirmed on Home Depot's own readable page.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
It's generally reported as **not stackable** with other discounts (like a Pro/contractor discount) and applies to full-priced items, so it usually won't combine with clearance or sale prices. It may sometimes combine with a storewide deal — ask in store or check the current terms.

#### Is the discount only on holidays?
No — not anymore. Since **December 2021**, the 10% discount is available **every day, year-round** to veterans, active duty, and spouses. Pages claiming veterans only get it on patriotic holidays are outdated.

#### Are appliances discounted?
Not under the standard 10% (appliances are excluded). Eligible military shoppers can buy many appliances **tax-free** through the Military Exchange program at ShopMyExchange.com or myNavyExchange.com.
