# Paramount+ Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Paramount+ offers verified service members a **50% discount on any plan for the life of the subscription** (until cancellation), redeemed online through **SheerID** verification. The official SheerID offer page states the deal is **"available to active military, veterans & their spouses"** — so on the primary source, only those three groups are confirmed, even though aggregators list a broader set (retirees, Guard, Reserve, dependents). After verifying through SheerID you sign up (or, if already subscribed, cancel and re-subscribe) through the offer link, and the discount applies for as long as you keep the plan. The single most important caveat: this is **streaming-only (US), online-only, non-stackable**, and Paramount+ controls the price the 50% is taken against — so the dollar amount moves whenever Paramount+ changes base prices (most recently raised in January 2026).

> Accuracy note: Several aggregator/blog pages assert that **retirees, National Guard, Reservists, and dependents** qualify, and some claim a **12-month re-verification requirement**. Neither the eligibility expansion beyond "active military, veterans & spouses" nor the 12-month re-verification clause is confirmed on the primary SheerID offer page as of June 23, 2026 — both are flagged below as not stated in the primary source. SheerID's standard practice does allow brands to require periodic re-verification, but Paramount+'s offer page does not publish that term, so it should not be asserted as fact.

### FAQ

#### Does Paramount+ offer a military discount?
Yes. Paramount+ offers 50% off any plan for the life of the subscription (until cancellation) to verified service members, redeemed online through SheerID.

#### How much is the Paramount+ military discount?
50% off any plan. The dollar amount depends on the current plan price — for example, with the discount the Essential plan has run around $4/month and Premium around $6.50/month, but those figures move whenever Paramount+ changes base pricing.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The official SheerID offer states it's available to **active military, veterans, and their spouses**. Other dependents, retirees, and Guard/Reserve are not confirmed on the official offer — run your details through SheerID to see if you qualify.

#### How do I verify my status?
Go to Paramount+'s SheerID verification link, enter your details, and upload documentation (military ID, DD-214, or `.mil` email) if SheerID requests it. Once verified, sign up for Paramount+ through the offer link.

#### Does Paramount+ use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Paramount+ uses **SheerID** for military verification — not ID.me. (Paramount+ may also appear on ID.me Shop listings, but the official military offer runs through SheerID.)

#### Can I use the discount if I'm already a subscriber?
Reportedly you must cancel your current subscription and re-subscribe through the SheerID military offer link so the discount attaches to the new subscription. Confirm the exact steps in your Paramount+ account, as this isn't spelled out on the official offer page.

#### Does the discount expire or need re-verifying every year?
The official offer says it lasts "until cancellation" with no stated annual re-verification. Some third-party pages mention a 12-month re-verification step, but that isn't confirmed on Paramount+'s official offer — keep the subscription active and watch your account for any re-verification request.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, free trials, or a Walmart+ bundle?
The offer doesn't state that it stacks with other promos, free trials, or bundles. Assume it can't be combined unless the offer says otherwise.

#### Does Paramount+ offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
There's no first-responder or nurse Paramount+ offer on SheerID. Paramount+ does have separate SheerID **teacher** and **student** offers (50%), which are different from the military discount.
