## Headline finding

**Yes — the Amex Gold is effectively free for active-duty servicemembers and their spouses**, exactly like the Platinum: under the **MLA**, American Express adjusts the annual fee to **$0 on personal cards opened while you are a covered borrower** (active duty; Guard/Reserve on 30+ day orders; spouses/dependents). It is **automatic** — Amex checks Department of Defense records at application; no code, no enrollment. If you held the Gold **before** going on active duty, the **SCRA** applies instead: a 6% interest cap whose definition of "interest" includes **annual membership fees, late fees, and returned-payment fees** — fee waived/refunded for the active-duty period, but you **must request it** (online, or 1-866-391-1460) while on active duty or within 180 days after.

The Gold-specific math is unusually good: the card carries **up to $424/year in statement credits** ($120 Uber Cash + $120 dining + $100 Resy + $84 Dunkin', all enrollment-required) against a fee that MLA sets to $0 — so a covered borrower who uses even part of the credits comes out ahead in cash, before counting points. The community's favorite angle — **DECA commissaries (including many OCONUS locations) often coding as "U.S. supermarkets" and earning 4x** — is real as a pattern of member datapoints but is **not published by Amex** and varies by location; this page treats it as labeled signal, never as fact. The honest limits: **veterans and retirees not on active duty get no ongoing waiver** — outside the 180-day SCRA window the Gold costs the full $325 — and **business cards** don't get the MLA $0-fee adjustment.

Archetype: **regulated-benefit / statutory-relief brand.** The "discount" is compliance with federal law; eligibility is legally defined, not a marketing choice; the make-or-break variable is the **account-open date** (before vs. during active duty → SCRA vs. MLA).

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **SCRA and MLA never stack on one account** — the account-open date picks exactly one. SCRA covers accounts opened before active duty; MLA covers accounts opened while a covered borrower. *(Amex SCRA & MLA FAQs)*
2. **MLA is automatic; SCRA must be requested.** Never tell readers SCRA self-applies. *(Amex SCRA & MLA FAQs)*
3. **The fee waiver does not reduce the card's earnings** — welcome offer, Membership Rewards, the 4x/3x/5x rates, and all statement credits work normally (credits still require enrollment).
4. **The waiver covers the fee, not spending** — there's no percent-off at merchants and no code; nothing to stack with portals or promos.
5. **Business cards get no MLA fee adjustment** (consumer credit only); SCRA's rate cap can still reach a pre-service business/corporate account.
6. **Per-account coverage:** each covered borrower's own personal cards are covered — which is why two spouses can hold two fee-waived Golds, and why one member can hold Gold + Platinum + Delta Reserve etc., all at $0, while covered.
7. **Earn caps still apply at $0 fee:** 4x U.S. supermarkets stops at $25,000/calendar year and 4x restaurants at $50,000/calendar year (then 1x) — the waiver doesn't lift them.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty members** of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard (and activated Space Force personnel under the Armed Forces umbrella) — MLA if the account opens during service; SCRA if it predates service.
- **National Guard / Reserve on active orders of more than 30 days** — MLA covered borrowers while activated.
- **Military spouses and dependents** (DEERS-listed; more than half support) — MLA $0-fee relief on their **own** accounts.
- **Veterans / military retirees not on active duty** — **no ongoing waiver**; the SCRA claim window closes 180 days after leaving active duty.
- **Business cards** — excluded from the MLA fee adjustment.

### How To Redeem — MLA (new card while serving)
1. Apply for the **personal** Gold Card on americanexpress.com as normal — there is no special military application page or code.
2. Amex screens the application against Department of Defense MLA records automatically. (Check your own status first at mla.dmdc.osd.mil if unsure.)
3. If you're a covered borrower, the annual fee is **adjusted to $0 automatically** as soon as the account is opened. Your Card Member Agreement (Part 1) will identify you as a covered borrower.
4. **Enroll in the credits** — Uber Cash, dining, Resy, and Dunkin' credits all require enrollment even on a fee-waived card.

### How To Redeem — SCRA (card opened before active duty)
1. Log in → Account Services → **"Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA) Benefits"** / Document Center (fastest), or call **1-866-391-1460** (7am–11:30pm, 7 days), fax 623-444-3000, or mail Attn: Servicemembers Civil Relief Act, PO Box 981535, El Paso, TX 79998-1535.
2. Provide military documentation: active-duty orders, change-of-station orders, a DD-214, or a commanding-officer letter.
3. Amex advises your status **within 2 billing cycles**; relief covers the 6% cap plus fees (annual, late, returned-payment) for the active-duty period.
4. File **while on active duty or within 180 days** of completing it.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No retail discount, coupon, or code exists — the benefit is the fee waiver itself.
- One relief per account, decided by the open date; they never stack.
- Veterans/retirees outside the 180-day window: standard $325 terms.
- Business cards: no MLA fee adjustment.
- The 4x earn caps ($25,000/yr U.S. supermarkets; $50,000/yr restaurants) apply regardless of the fee waiver.
- Commissary purchases earning 4x is **not an Amex-published rule** — many bases code as U.S. supermarkets, some don't; treat it as a nice surprise, not a guarantee.
- Amex can change the fee, earn rates, and credit lineup at any time (it did in July 2024 and April 2026).

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Both spouses apply separately** — each covered borrower gets their own $0-fee Gold, welcome offer, and full credit lineup; don't settle for authorized-user status.
- **Hold Gold + Platinum together** — MLA applies per personal card, so the classic setup is Platinum ($0, travel/lounges) + Gold ($0, 4x food/groceries). "Which card first" is really "which welcome offer first."
- **Enroll in and use the credits** — up to $424/yr (Uber $120, dining $120, Resy $100, Dunkin' $84) on a card that costs you $0.
- **Grocery-run strategy:** the 4x U.S.-supermarket rate covers up to $25,000/yr — and many commissaries (stateside and overseas) have been reported to code as U.S. supermarkets, though not all do.
- **Take it overseas:** no foreign transaction fees make the Gold a solid OCONUS PCS/deployment card.
- **File SCRA on old accounts** — pre-service accounts can yield historical fee refunds.
- **Compare issuers:** Chase, USAA, Navy Federal, PenFed, and Citi also honor SCRA/MLA; Amex stands out for applying $0 to its premium annual-fee cards.
