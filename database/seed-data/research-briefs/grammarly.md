Ignore the aggregator claims of "25%," "40%," or "60% off" — the verified, current number straight from Grammarly's own support documentation and SheerID is **50%**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Promo offers cannot be combined with other offers** and cannot apply to previous purchases — so no coupon-code stacking on top of the 50%.
2. **First seat only.** Additional seats on the same plan bill at the standard rate; the discount does not cover a whole team.
3. **Not valid through the App Store or Google Play** — you must upgrade via the SheerID promo link / web, not an in-app purchase.
4. **One subscription period only.** At renewal the plan reverts to $144/year unless a new offer is available.

### How To Redeem Online
