# LEGOLAND Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

> Scope: U.S. LEGOLAND Resorts — LEGOLAND California (Carlsbad, CA), LEGOLAND Florida (Winter Haven, FL), and LEGOLAND New York (Goshen, NY). LEGOLAND Discovery Centers (indoor attractions) are a separate product line and are out of scope for this brief except where noted. Accessed June 23, 2026.

## Executive Summary

All three U.S. LEGOLAND Resorts offer a military discount on theme-park tickets and hotel rooms for U.S. Military, Veterans, and their families, but the structure differs by park. **LEGOLAND California** publishes the clearest number: **"Save up to 35% off tickets"** on 1-Day LEGOLAND tickets and SEA LIFE Hopper tickets, plus up to 10% off hotel rooms. **LEGOLAND New York** offers a **1-Day ticket + 2nd Day Free** plus up to 10% off the hotel. **LEGOLAND Florida** advertises "special pricing" / "heavily discounted" tickets (no exact ticket percentage is published online) plus a limited-time **20% off hotel rooms** (stay any day now–Sept 3, 2026). Discounted tickets are purchased in advance — online through LEGOLAND's military offer pages (verified via ID.me) or through a local on-base ITT/military ticket office with a valid military ID. The single most important caveat: the military discount **cannot be combined** with any other offer, discount, or promotion, and (Florida) does **not** apply to annual passes — only 1- or 2-Day or Multi-Park tickets.

### Intro
All three U.S. LEGOLAND Resorts — California, Florida, and New York — give U.S. military members, veterans, and their families a discount on theme-park tickets and hotel stays. The exact savings depend on which park you visit: LEGOLAND California advertises up to 35% off tickets, LEGOLAND New York offers a 1-Day ticket with a free second day, and LEGOLAND Florida offers special military pricing on tickets plus a limited-time 20% off hotel rooms.

You buy discounted tickets in advance — either online through each park's military offer page (your status is verified online through ID.me) or at an on-base ITT / military ticket office using your valid military ID. Bring a valid Military/Department of Defense ID, or a government-issued ID showing veteran status, to present at the park.

This is an independent guide to help military families navigate LEGOLAND's published offers. It is not affiliated with LEGOLAND or the LEGO Group. LEGOLAND sets the terms and can change them at any time — always confirm current pricing on the official park page before you buy.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty service members — eligible (CA & NY T&C; FL).
- Retirees — eligible (CA & NY T&C).
- Veterans — eligible (all three parks).
- Reserve / National Guard — Not separately stated in official source (covered implicitly under "military" in marketing; not itemized in the eligible-categories list).
- Military spouses — eligible (CA & NY T&C; FL FAQ).
- Dependents / military family members — eligible (CA & NY T&C; FL FAQ).
- Surviving spouses / Gold Star families — Not stated in official source.
- First responders — Not confirmed on the official military offer pages' terms; marketed separately by LEGOLAND New York (flag as unverified; see Open Questions).
- Medical workers, teachers, government employees, students — Not stated on the military offer pages. (LEGOLAND's ID.me Shop store pages reference discounts for "Military, Nurses, & More," but specific non-military terms are not published on the park military pages — do not assert.)
- Canadian Military — eligible at LEGOLAND New York (NY T&C).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to the official military offer page for your park (legoland.com/california, /florida, or /new-york → Deals → Military Offers).
2. Select the ticket offer (e.g., "Buy Now" / "Ticket Offers").
3. Verify your military affiliation through ID.me when prompted.
4. Choose your ticket type (1-Day, 2-Day, Multi-Park, or hotel package as offered) and date.
5. Complete payment (full prepayment is required for hotel bookings).
6. Bring your valid Military/DoD ID — or government ID showing veteran status — to present at the park entrance/hotel check-in.

### How To Redeem In Store / On Base
1. Visit your local on-base ITT (Information, Tickets & Travel) or military ticket office.
2. Present your valid military ID.
3. Purchase the discounted LEGOLAND tickets there (these offices sometimes carry deeply discounted bundles, including offers with a second day free).
4. Bring the tickets and your ID to the park.

### How Verification Works
For online purchases, LEGOLAND verifies military status through **ID.me**, a third-party verification service. You sign in or create an ID.me account and confirm your military affiliation; once verified, you can access the discounted pricing. For in-person redemption and at hotel check-in, you must present a current, valid **Military/Department of Defense ID** or a **government-issued ID indicating veteran status** — LEGOLAND states that inability to provide this ID can result in being charged the higher (non-discounted) rate. ID.me verification status is governed by ID.me's own re-verification policies; LEGOLAND does not publish how long a verification lasts, so do not assume it is permanent. Support is via each park's Help Center (florida-support / california-support / newyork-support.legoland.com).

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Cannot be combined with any other offers, discounts, or promotions (all three parks).
- Florida: military discount applies to 1- or 2-Day LEGOLAND Florida Theme Park-only tickets or Multi-Park tickets — **not** annual/season passes.
- Hotel offers: not applicable to suites (FL); "not applicable to all room or theme types" (CA); subject to availability with limited rooms allocated per date; full prepayment required.
- Hotel free daily breakfast is valid only for guests on the reservation and not on day of arrival/check-in (CA & NY).
- Parking and resort fees are charged separately at the hotel (CA & NY).
- FL 20% hotel offer is limited-time: stay any day now through September 3, 2026.
- Prices, times, and schedules subject to change without notice; LEGOLAND reserves all rights and interpretation.

### Best Ways To Save More
- On-base ITT/military ticket offices sometimes carry deeper bundles than the website (LEGOLAND Florida explicitly points veterans/non-active military/families there for "deeply discounted" tickets, some including a second day free).
- Watch for LEGOLAND's seasonal free-admission events: in recent years (e.g., Oct–Nov around Veterans Day) LEGOLAND Florida and New York have run free-admission promotions for military and first responders — these are limited-time and announced each year, so verify current-year terms before planning. (Editorial/seasonal context — not an evergreen guarantee.)
- Other LEGOLAND savings open to everyone: Florida & Georgia resident offers, "Book 2 Nights, Get 1 Free," hotel + ticket packages, and annual-pass holder hotel rates.
- Competitor comparison: SeaWorld/Busch Gardens parks and many regional theme parks offer their own military programs (and the DoD "Waves of Honor" free-admission program covers SeaWorld parks); compare per-visit value against LEGOLAND's per-ticket discount before buying.

### FAQ

#### Does LEGOLAND offer a military discount?
Yes. All three U.S. LEGOLAND Resorts (California, Florida, New York) offer military discounts on theme-park tickets and hotel stays for U.S. military, veterans, and their families.

#### How much is the LEGOLAND military discount?
It varies by park. LEGOLAND California advertises "up to 35% off" tickets and up to 10% off hotel rooms. LEGOLAND New York offers a 1-Day ticket plus a free second day, and up to 10% off the hotel. LEGOLAND Florida advertises "special" / "heavily discounted" ticket pricing (no exact percentage is published on its site) and a limited-time 20% off hotel rooms.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes. LEGOLAND's terms list active service members, retirees, veterans, military spouses, and military family members/dependents as eligible.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Online, LEGOLAND verifies your status through ID.me during checkout. In person, you must show a valid Military/Department of Defense ID or a government-issued ID indicating veteran status.

#### Does LEGOLAND use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
LEGOLAND uses ID.me for online verification on its own military offer pages, and LEGOLAND Parks are listed on the ID.me Shop. LEGOLAND tickets are also separately available to military/government members through GovX. The official park pages route through ID.me.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or on base?
You can buy discounted LEGOLAND tickets at an on-base ITT or military ticket office by showing your military ID. The park discount itself is redeemed at the gate/hotel by presenting valid ID.

#### What is excluded?
The military discount cannot be combined with other offers or promotions. At LEGOLAND Florida it does not apply to annual/season passes (only 1- or 2-Day or Multi-Park tickets). Hotel offers exclude suites/select room types and are subject to availability.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. LEGOLAND states the military discount cannot be combined with any other offers, discounts, or promotions.

#### Does LEGOLAND offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
LEGOLAND New York markets discounts for first responders, and LEGOLAND's ID.me Shop pages reference offers for "Military, Nurses, & More." However, specific terms for first-responder, nurse, teacher, or student discounts are not detailed on the park military offer pages, so confirm directly with the park or via ID.me before relying on them.
