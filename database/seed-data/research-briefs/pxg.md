# PXG Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

PXG (Parsons Xtreme Golf) runs an evergreen "PXG for Heroes" program that gives **20% off equipment and 20% off apparel and accessories** to verified members of the U.S. military (active duty and veterans), first responders, law enforcement, teachers, and nurses. Eligibility is verified through **ID.me**; once verified, you log into your PXG Heroes account and the discount is applied automatically to eligible items in your cart, both online at pxg.com and at PXG retail locations nationwide. The single most important caveat: the offer applies to **select** equipment and apparel ("some exclusions may apply"), and older PXG/aggregator references to "up to 50% off" reflect a **2022 promotional blog post**, not the current published 20% evergreen offer — do not state any figure above 20% as current.

PXG (Parsons Xtreme Golf) offers a 20% military and veteran discount through its "PXG for Heroes" program. Verified active-duty service members and veterans get 20% off select PXG golf equipment and 20% off select apparel and accessories — applied automatically once you sign in to your verified Heroes account. ### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to PXG's "PXG for Heroes" page (pxg.com/pages/heroes-program) and click "Verify with ID.me."
2. Sign in to ID.me or create an account, then select your group (Military or First Responder) and complete verification with the requested documents.
3. After verification, log in to your PXG account at pxg.com.
4. Add eligible clubs, apparel, or accessories to your cart — the 20% Heroes discount is applied automatically to eligible items.
5. Check out. (The site shows a persistent "You're saving 20%" banner once you're verified and signed in.)

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Complete ID.me verification for the PXG for Heroes program (steps above) before or at the time of your visit.
2. Visit a PXG retail/fitting location (200+ nationwide; use pxg.com locations finder).
3. Sign in to / confirm your verified PXG Heroes account so staff can apply Heroes pricing to eligible equipment, apparel, and accessories.

(Note: PXG states Heroes pricing is available "online and in PXG retail store locations nationwide" but does not publish a step-by-step in-store register process; confirm the exact in-store method with the location.)

### How Verification Works
PXG uses ID.me to verify military, veteran, first responder, teacher, and nurse status. You create or sign in to an ID.me account, select your group, and submit the documents ID.me requests for your category (e.g., military ID, EMT/paramedic or firefighter certification, law-enforcement ID, retired ID/pension statement, etc.). Once ID.me confirms your status, your PXG Heroes account unlocks the discount. PXG/ID.me do not publish how often re-verification is required; ID.me credentials can expire and require periodic re-verification, so do not assume it is permanent. For help, PXG directs users to email HELP@ID.ME or call 1.844.PLAY.PXG (1.844.752.9794), 7 days a week, 6a–6p MST.

### FAQ

#### Does PXG offer a military discount?
Yes. Through the "PXG for Heroes" program, verified active-duty military and veterans get 20% off select PXG equipment and 20% off select apparel and accessories.

#### How much is the PXG military discount?
20% off equipment and 20% off apparel and accessories on select items, per PXG's official Heroes page and ID.me. Older "up to 50% off" claims come from a 2022 PXG promotional blog post and do not reflect the current published evergreen offer.

#### Do veterans qualify for the PXG discount?
Yes. PXG lists "Active Duty - Veterans" as eligible, and ID.me covers "U.S. Military Members." Eligibility for spouses, dependents, Reserve/National Guard, and Gold Star families is not stated in the official source — check the ID.me military verification flow.

#### How do I verify my military status for PXG?
Click "Verify with ID.me" on PXG's Heroes page, sign in or create an ID.me account, select your group, and submit the requested documents. Once verified, sign in to your PXG account and the discount applies automatically to eligible items.

#### Does PXG use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
PXG uses **ID.me** for Heroes verification. There is no published PXG offer through GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute.

#### Can I use the PXG military discount in stores?
Yes. PXG states Heroes pricing is available online and at PXG retail locations nationwide. Verify with ID.me first and confirm the exact in-store process with your location.

#### What is excluded from the PXG Heroes discount?
The discount applies to "select" equipment and apparel, and ID.me notes "some exclusions may apply." PXG does not publish a specific list of excluded products or collections.

#### Can I combine the Heroes discount with promo codes or sale items?
Not stated in PXG's official source. Whether the 20% stacks with sale/clearance items, promo codes, or gift cards is not published — assume it may not stack and confirm at checkout.

#### Does PXG offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
First responders (law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs/paramedics, 911 dispatchers), teachers, and nurses all qualify for the same 20% via PXG for Heroes/ID.me. No active government-employee or student PXG discount is published.
