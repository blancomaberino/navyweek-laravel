# Houston Zoo — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one family day visit — **2 adults + 2 children (ages 3–12)** general admission, benchmarked against Houston Zoo flex pricing.

## Headline finding

Houston Zoo runs a strong, **official year-round military discount: 50% off general admission for U.S. veterans and active military, covering up to five guests (2 adults + 3 children)**. You verify your status through **SheerID** on the Houston Zoo site to receive a discount code, or present a valid military ID at the gate — but a **reservation is required** (the Zoo sells tickets online only, no on-site sales), and you **must show proof of military service** when presenting your ticket for admission. For a military family, this 50%-off path is clearly the best available; the only competing consideration is a Houston Zoo **membership** if you visit multiple times a year.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The military discount applies to **general admission** for **up to 5 guests** — larger parties pay standard price for additional guests.
2. **Reservation is mandatory** — tickets are online-only; the discount is realized via a code (SheerID) or gate verification, not walk-up.
3. Children (3–12) and seniors (65+) already receive an automatic **20% off** at checkout; the 50% military rate is the deeper discount for eligible guests within the 5-guest cap.
4. Military, student, and Lone Star discounts are **separate programs** — you pick the best one, not a stack.

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
Online (recommended):
1. Go to the Houston Zoo prices & discounts / tickets page.
2. Complete **SheerID** military verification to receive your discount code.
3. Reserve your date/time and apply the code (up to 5 guests).
4. Bring your reservation confirmation and military proof to the gate.

At the gate:
1. Reserve online (required — no on-site sales).
2. Present valid **military ID** and your reservation for the 50% rate/verification at admission.

### How Verification Works
Verification is via **SheerID** online (issues a one-time discount code) or by presenting a **valid military ID** at the gate. Either way, you must show proof of military service when presenting your ticket for admission. There is no ID.me or GovX integration reported for the Zoo.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Covers **up to 5 guests** (2 adults + 3 children); additional guests pay standard price.
- **Reservation required** — online-only ticketing, no walk-up sales.
- Applies to **general admission**; special events (e.g., Houston Zoo Lights) may price separately (flagged: confirm event applicability).
- Proof of service **must be shown** at admission or the discount can be refused.
