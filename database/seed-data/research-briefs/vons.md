# Vons — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$150 weekly grocery order** at Vons (the everyday basket most readers compare).

## Headline finding

**Vons (an Albertsons Companies banner) runs a SheerID-verified "community & profession" program that includes Military — but Vons does not publish a guaranteed flat military percentage.** The official Vons and Albertsons FAQ pages confirm you verify through **SheerID** as Military (also Teachers, Students, First Responders, Seniors, and Government-Assistance recipients), after which **exclusive offers are unlocked** — but neither page states a standing "10% off every order." The widely repeated **"Vons 10% military discount" is not corporate-published policy**: it traces to a temporary 2013 government-shutdown regional promotion and to SheerID/aggregator marketing copy, not a durable Vons rule. Treat any flat 10% as unverified.

So the honest answer: **verifying unlocks personalized member offers loaded to your Albertsons for U account**, whose value varies — not a guaranteed percentage. The path that actually saves the most is open to everyone: the free **Albertsons for U** loyalty program (personalized deals, digital coupons, points → grocery/gas rewards, weekly ad). Archetype: **grocery loyalty / SheerID community-verification program** — the "discount" is really *loyalty-account offer targeting*, not a register percentage. One eligibility note that matters: **SheerID does not accept active-duty/reservist military ID card uploads** — verify with other documentation.

### FAQ

#### Does Vons offer a military discount?
Vons has a **SheerID-verified military program** that unlocks member offers in your Albertsons for U account — but it does **not** publish a guaranteed flat percentage. Verify as Military through SheerID to access whatever targeted offers are live.

#### How much is the Vons military discount?
There's no published standing amount. The often-quoted "10%" is not current corporate policy (it traces to a 2013 regional promo). The benefit is access to personalized member offers, which vary.

#### Do veterans and family members qualify?
The program covers people currently or formerly in the U.S. Armed Services, verified via SheerID. There's no separate published military-family grocery discount — use for U.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Through **SheerID** on Vons' community-and-profession page. SheerID won't accept an active-duty/reservist ID card upload, so use another proof of service.

#### Does Vons use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
**SheerID** only. There's no ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute military program with Vons.

#### Can I use it online or in stores?
Yes — once verified, offers apply on vons.com, in the Vons app, and at participating stores through your for U account.

#### What is excluded?
Grocery promos typically exclude alcohol, tobacco, gift cards, lottery, postage, and pharmacy. And there's no guaranteed percentage to apply outside live member offers.

#### Can I combine offers?
For U digital coupons and personalized deals generally stack with each other (one clip per item). Military verification adds targeted offers to the same account rather than a fixed percentage.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to shop Vons?
Use free **Albertsons for U** (coupons + points + gas rewards) every trip, and verify via SheerID for any extra targeted offers. If you have base access, buy staples at the **commissary**, which is usually cheaper than any supermarket.

#### Does Vons offer a first responder, teacher, student, or senior discount?
Those are separate SheerID audiences in the same community program — verify to unlock member offers, same as military.
