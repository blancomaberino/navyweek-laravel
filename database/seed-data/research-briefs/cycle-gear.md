# Cycle Gear — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **SEDICI Garda Waterproof Jacket — $249.99** pre-tax (a Cycle Gear *exclusive/house brand* item; price on cyclegear.com). Chosen deliberately because a house-brand item is where Cycle Gear's discount is strongest and where the in-store vs. online gap is largest.

## Headline finding

Cycle Gear (cyclegear.com) has a **real, brand-published military/first-responder discount, verified through GovX** — stated on Cycle Gear's own "Police / Military / Fire / EMS Discount" page. But the number depends entirely on **where** and **what** you buy, and the gap is huge:

- **In-store, with ID: 20% off Cycle Gear's exclusive/house brands** (SEDICI, BILT, Street & Steel, etc.) and **10% off third-party brands.**
- **Online: only 5% off**, applied to your account after a one-time GovX verification.
- **~100 premium brands are excluded from any discount** (Alpinestars, Shoei, Arai, Bell, Dainese, REV'IT!, Klim, Icon, plus all tires and wheels).

So the single best path is **walk into a store and buy a house brand at 20% off** ($199.99 on the $249.99 baseline). Online, the discount is weak (5%) and doesn't stack with anything Cycle Gear runs — so for online orders the real game is a **Rakuten cashback layer** or the **$39.99/yr RPM loyalty membership** (5% rewards cash + $15 welcome), which are the levers that actually move the price. Archetype: **A — GovX-partner identity discount**, with a strong secondary **loyalty-and-exclusions** flavor (a tiered discount gutted by a 100-brand exclusion list, wrapped around the RPM rewards program). Do **not** repeat the aggregator claim of a **"20% off online via SheerID"** code — Cycle Gear uses **GovX**, and online is **5%**, not 20%.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal terms)
1. **The military discount is strictly single** — Cycle Gear states "Discounts may not be stacked with any other discount, code and/or offer." That means it **won't combine** with RPM Cash, promo codes, clearance/sale prices, or a second offer.
2. **In-store tiers don't transfer online** — the 20%/10% figures are explicitly **"In-Store With ID."** Online is a flat **5%**, full stop.
3. **~100 excluded brands + all tires/wheels get 0%** at every tier — verify your specific item isn't on the exclusion list before counting on any discount.
4. **RPM membership vs. the military discount = pick one** — RPM's 5% rewards cash is "any other discount/offer," so it can't ride on top of the military discount on the same order. RPM shines for repeat buyers and on **excluded** items where the military discount is $0.
5. **Third-party cashback portals (Rakuten, ID.me Shop) are rebates, not Cycle Gear "discounts/codes/offers"** — they track the amount you actually pay at cyclegear.com, so a GovX **account-level** 5% + Rakuten 2% can co-exist. This is the one legitimate online "stack." Confirm the Rakuten click tracked (a gated/identity checkout can occasionally break portal tracking).
6. **RPM Cash is shared across RevZilla, J&P Cycles & Cycle Gear** — earn on one, spend on another; but it still can't stack with the military discount on the earning order.

### FAQ

#### Does Cycle Gear offer a military discount?
Yes — verified through **GovX**, for active-duty or retired **police, military, fire & EMS**. It's **20% off exclusive/house brands and 10% off third-party brands in-store with ID**, and a flat **5% off online orders**.

#### How much is the Cycle Gear military discount?
**20% off house brands / 10% off third-party — in-store only.** **Online it's 5%.** The "20% off online" figure some coupon sites cite is not real — that's the in-store house-brand rate.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The brand page names "**Active Duty or Retired** Police, Military, Fire & EMS." Honorably discharged **veterans** typically still verify via GovX. **Spouses and dependents are not named** — don't assume; verify your category at GovX.

#### How do I verify my status?
Add items to your cart at cyclegear.com, click the **GovX ID** banner, and complete the free one-time verification. The discount then attaches to your account. In-store, just show valid ID.

#### Does Cycle Gear use GovX, ID.me, or SheerID?
**GovX.** There is **no confirmed SheerID or ID.me path for the discount** — the "verify with SheerID for 20% off online" claim on coupon sites is fabricated. (ID.me Shop is a separate cashback site, not Cycle Gear's verifier.)

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Both — but the rate differs: **20%/10% in-store**, **5% online**. The higher in-store tiers do not apply to online orders.

#### What is excluded?
Roughly **100 premium brands plus all tires and wheels** (Alpinestars, Shoei, Arai, Bell, Dainese, REV'IT!, and more). The discount also **can't combine** with promo codes, sale prices, or RPM rewards.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, sales, or RPM?
No — Cycle Gear says discounts "may not be stacked with any other discount, code and/or offer," so it won't layer on RPM, a promo code, or a clearance price. The one legitimate online layer is a **third-party cashback portal** (Rakuten ~2%), which rebates on the amount you pay.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Cycle Gear?
**Buy house-brand gear in-store at 20% off** (about **$199.99 on the $249.99 SEDICI Garda jacket**). If you must buy online, stack **GovX 5% + Rakuten ~2%** (≈$232.74), or join **RPM** if you shop often. On excluded premium brands, the military discount is $0 — use RPM or a public sale.

#### Does Cycle Gear offer a first-responder discount?
Yes — the same program covers **active or retired police, firefighters, and EMS**, at the same tiers (20%/10% in-store, 5% online), verified through GovX.

#### Does Cycle Gear run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Cycle Gear posts a "Veteran's Day Deals" page in November, but this pass it showed general/RPM promotions, not an added military percentage. Compare any public sale against the standing 20% in-store on house brands — they don't stack.
