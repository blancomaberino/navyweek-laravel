## Executive Summary

**Rogue Fitness does not offer a standing, everyday percentage-off military or veteran discount.** This is confirmed by Rogue's own help-center article and a public statement from Rogue's official account ("We do not offer a military discount"). Rogue's stated position is that it keeps prices "as low as possible" for everyone because such a large share of its sales go to military members and first responders. What Rogue *does* offer to the military community: (1) **occasional military/veteran-exclusive sales, historically on/around Veterans Day**, accessible only to logged-in, verified veterans or active U.S. military; (2) an **APO/FPO/DPO shipping store** (rogueapo.com) so deployed service members can receive equipment; and (3) charitable giving tied to certain products. Verification is done **"in your account"** on roguefitness.com — Rogue does **not** publicly name a verification provider (GovX, SheerID, and ID.me are **not** stated as policy on any Rogue page). No discount percentage or dollar amount is published. **Caveat / most important caution:** widely circulated aggregator claims of a "10%–25% Rogue military discount via GovX/SheerID with a unique checkout code" are **unverified and contradicted by Rogue's official sources** — do not state any percentage as fact.
