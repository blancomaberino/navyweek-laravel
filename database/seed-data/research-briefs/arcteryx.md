# Arc'teryx — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one Arc'teryx Beta jacket — **~$450 MSRP** (representative Gore-Tex shell), pre-tax.

## Headline finding

Arc'teryx (an **Amer Sports** brand) offers **no military or veteran discount** — there is no percentage, no promo code, and no ID.me / SheerID / GovX military checkout on arcteryx.com. Its professional-purchase program, **Arc'teryx PRO (formerly LEAF)**, is for occupational "mountain professionals," and its own **PRO Program FAQ explicitly states that "Law enforcement and military personnel are not eligible for a PRO Purchase membership due to system limitations"** (the brand says it's "working towards a future solution"). A Reddit user's 2025 veteran PRO application was **denied**, consistent with that policy. The **LEAF line** (leaf.arcteryx.com) is a purchase-restricted *product* line built for armed forces / law enforcement — **not a discount** — sold largely through authorized tactical dealers. So the honest maximum-savings paths are open to everyone: the **Arc'teryx Outlet** (outlet.arcteryx.com, roughly 30–40% off past-season), **ReBIRD Resale** (refurbished/used gear, often ~30% under new), the **Trade-In** credit program (20% of original price), and email/seasonal offers. Archetype: **B (no military route at all)**, with a professional-use caveat and a strong secondary of **outlet/refurb**.

### FAQ

#### Does Arc'teryx offer a military discount?
No. Arc'teryx has no military or veteran discount and no military verification (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX). Its own PRO FAQ states military and law enforcement are **not eligible** for the PRO Purchase program currently.

#### What about the LEAF line — isn't that a military discount?
No. **LEAF** (now unified with PRO) is a purchase-restricted *product* line for armed forces and law enforcement, sold through Arc'teryx and authorized tactical dealers. It's product access, **not** a price discount.

#### Can I get the Arc'teryx PRO deal as a service member?
Not right now. PRO is an occupational program for mountain professionals, and Arc'teryx says military/LE aren't eligible for a PRO Purchase membership "due to system limitations." A 2025 veteran application was reportedly denied.

#### Does Arc'teryx use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. There's no military verification partner or storefront for Arc'teryx.

#### How do veterans or first responders save at Arc'teryx?
Through the same public channels as everyone: the **Outlet**, **ReBIRD Resale** (refurb/used), **Trade-In** credit, and seasonal sales.

#### What's actually the cheapest way to buy Arc'teryx?
The **Outlet** (past-season, ~30–40% off) or **ReBIRD Resale** (inspected used/refurbished, ~30% under new). Both beat full price by more than any discount you were hoping for.

#### Does Arc'teryx run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
We found no military-specific holiday promotion. Arc'teryx rarely discounts current-season gear; the Outlet and ReBIRD are the steady value channels.

#### Will military become eligible for PRO later?
Arc'teryx says it is "working towards a future solution" to the system limitation — but there is no confirmed date or program. Don't count on it until it's live.
