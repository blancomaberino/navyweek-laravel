# American Girl — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $115 American Girl 18-inch doll (representative core-line item).

## Headline finding

American Girl offers a **real, official military discount: 15% off**, published on its own FAQ page and valid **through 11:59 p.m. Central Time on 12/31/2026**. Per the FAQ's exact wording it is for **"all active or retired members of the US Armed Forces"** — redeemable **in store by showing a military ID to the cashier**, or **online by entering a promotion code** at checkout. It's valid only at American Girl US retail locations and americangirl.com, excludes a substantial list of collections/services, and cannot be combined with other offers. Note two things the primary FAQ does **not** say: it does not explicitly name veterans (non-retired separated), spouses, or dependents, and it does **not** state a "$60 maximum discount" (that cap appears only on aggregators — treat as unverified). American Girl also has a genuine **tax-free exchange path**: the Navy Exchange (NEX), plus select AAFES and MCX locations, carry curated American Girl assortments.

## Stacking Rules (verified, from the FAQ)
1. The 15% **cannot be combined** with other offers or promotion codes — one discount only.
2. **Limit one offer per household.**
3. No refunds/adjustments on previous purchases, returns/exchanges, or in-progress unshipped orders.
4. Discount does **not** apply to shipping/processing or sales tax (those are charged in full).
5. Exchange (NEX/AAFES/MCX) is a **separate channel** — you don't stack the AG code there; the benefit there is tax-free pricing.

### FAQ
#### Does American Girl offer a military discount?
Yes — an official 15% off eligible purchases, valid through 12/31/2026 at US stores and americangirl.com.

#### How much is the American Girl military discount?
15% off eligible items. American Girl's FAQ does not list a dollar cap; a "$60 maximum" appears only on third-party sites and is unverified.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
The FAQ specifies "active or retired members of the US Armed Forces" and does not explicitly name veterans, spouses, or dependents — confirm with American Girl before assuming.

#### How do I verify my status?
In store, show a military ID to the cashier. Online, enter the appropriate promotion code at checkout.

#### Does American Girl use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No third-party verifier is named in the FAQ; in-store uses a military ID and online uses a promotion code.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — both: military ID in store, promotion code online at americangirl.com.

#### What is excluded?
Disney Collection, Modern Eras, Create Your Own, Ultimate Collections, Doll Care Center, salon experiences, parties/dining/food, event tickets, gift cards, gift wrap, shipping/processing, and sales tax, among others.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — it can't be combined with other offers or promotion codes, and it's limited to one per household.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy American Girl?
For eligible core items, the 15% is the best lever. For excluded items (or to skip sales tax), buy at the Navy Exchange or select AAFES/MCX locations.

#### Can I buy American Girl at the Exchange?
Yes — the Navy Exchange carries a curated assortment at ~a dozen locations, and select AAFES and MCX locations sell the 14.5-inch dolls; exchange purchases are sales-tax-free.
