# SiriusXM — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline for all math:** one **Streaming Platinum (All Access, app-only) subscription** at the **$10.99/mo** full-price rate, compared against the military rate and public new-subscriber promos.

## Headline finding

**SiriusXM has a real, official military discount: 25% off the full-price rate for the life of the subscription.** It's open to veterans, retirees, active-duty, reserve, and their **spouses and dependents**, verified through **SheerID** at siriusxm.com/military25. On the $10.99/mo Streaming Platinum plan that's roughly **$8.24/mo, and it never expires** — unlike a promo rate, it re-applies (25% off) even as SiriusXM raises standard rates (as it did Feb 24, 2026). The catch for the maximum-savings analysis: SiriusXM runs aggressive **new-subscriber promos** (often ~$5/mo for 12 months, or the standard streaming plan discounted the first year) that beat 25% off in year one — but those expire and snap to full price, where the military 25% keeps saving. Archetype: **subscription service with a genuine lifetime loyalty discount that competes with, and usually loses year-one to, its own churn-promos but wins long-term.**

**Verdict logic:**
- **Long-term subscriber (2+ years)** → the **military 25%-for-life** wins because promos expire to full price and the military rate keeps re-applying (even after rate hikes).
- **Brand-new subscriber optimizing year one** → a live **new-subscriber promo** (~$5/mo for 12 months) usually beats 25% off in the first year — then switch/re-shop when it expires.
- **Enrolled student** → the **student plan (~$4/mo)** is cheapest of all, but education-gated, not military.
- On-page chooser: *"Are you a brand-new subscriber and is a promo live?"* Yes = take the promo for year one; No / renewing = military 25% for life.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand terms)
1. **The military 25% does not combine with any other offer** — SiriusXM states the offer "cannot be combined with any other." You pick the military rate *or* a promo, not both.
2. **It's an account-level rate, not a coupon code** — applied after SheerID verification; there's no code to stack at checkout.
3. **It survives rate increases** — after the Feb 24, 2026 rate change, military subscribers are billed 25% off the *then-current* full price (a rare, valuable "discount off a moving number").
4. **No cashback-portal path** — SiriusXM is a direct-billed subscription; portals don't track the recurring fee.
