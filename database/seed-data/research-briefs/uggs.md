# UGG — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **UGG Classic Ultra Mini Boot for Women** — **$170 MSRP**, pre-tax (UGG's evergreen flagship boot; the price shown on ugg.com and matched at Foot Locker, Nordstrom, and Dick's).

## Headline finding

UGG (ugg.com, a Deckers brand) offers a **real, brand-run 10% "Community Heroes" discount**, verified through **SheerID** — good for **10% off full-priced styles**. The eligible groups are **military (active duty & veterans), first responders, healthcare/medical workers, and teachers** (UGG also runs a separate **10% student** offer). You verify once through SheerID and the 10% applies on ugg.com. That's the honest everyday answer and the best *standing* route for a full-price pair.

The catch: the 10% is **full-priced-only and non-combinable** — UGG's own terms are "one code per order," so it won't stack with anything. Because UGG runs **deep seasonal sales** (up to ~60% off past-season styles around Black Friday/Cyber Monday, plus periodic "UGG Closet" clearance events), the public price frequently beats the 10% on non-flagship styles — but note the *evergreen Classics* (Ultra Mini, Classic Mini) rarely discount, so on the baseline boot the 10% usually is the best deal. Two things can beat the Community Heroes 10%: the **UGG Rewards new-member 15%-off-first-order** offer (deeper, but one-time and non-stacking), and, historically, the **Veterans Day** promo where UGG has extended **10% off *all* orders including sale items** (the one time the full-price restriction lifts). UGG is also **stocked at the military exchanges (AAFES / Navy Exchange), tax-free** — a real secondary path on excluded or sale styles. Archetype: **E — multi-tier identity discount via SheerID (little stacking/cashback game)**, with a secondary **L (exchange-carried, tax-free)** and a **C** flavor (deep seasonal sales beat the 10% on non-Classics). Do **not** repeat the aggregator claims of "up to 25% military," "ID.me verification," or "GovX military discount" — none is confirmed; **ID.me itself states UGG does not use ID.me**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + provider terms)
1. **One code per order.** UGG does not allow stacking codes — the SheerID 10% is a **single order-level discount** that won't combine with any other promo, welcome code, or sale price. Confirm at checkout.
2. **Full-priced only.** The Community Heroes 10% is **excluded on sale/clearance items** — so on a discounted style you either take the sale price or (in the Veterans Day window) the 10%-incl-sale exception, never both.
3. **Rewards 15% welcome vs. the 10% are mutually exclusive** — one code per order means you pick the lower. The 15% is one-time (first purchase).
4. **Rakuten cashback is portal-fragile.** Rakuten's UGG terms: "Use of coupon/promotional codes **not listed on Rakuten** may void Cash Back." A SheerID-issued discount is exactly such an unlisted code, so **"SheerID 10% + Rakuten 2%" should not be assumed to stack** — treat cashback as an *alternative* to the code, not an add-on. (Rate 2% as of Jul 10, 2026.)
5. **Exchange is a closed channel** — no SheerID code, no cashback portal, no UGG promo stacks on an AAFES/NEX purchase; the win there is **tax-free** (+ possible MILITARY STAR card perks), not a code.
6. **Veterans Day (historical) is the one exception** where 10% has applied to **all orders including sale items** — verify each year; don't promise it.

#### How much is the UGG military discount?

#### What is excluded?
**Sale/clearance items** are excluded (full-priced only), and it's **one code per order** — no stacking. The historical **Veterans Day** promo is the exception that has included sale items.

#### Can I combine the discount with promo codes or sale items?
No — UGG allows **one code per order** and the 10% is full-price-only. Because the two don't combine, compare the 10% against the current sale or the 15% Rewards welcome and take the single lowest price.
