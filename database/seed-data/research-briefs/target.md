# Target Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Target's military benefit is **not an everyday discount** — it's a **limited-window Target Circle offer** delivered during twice-yearly Military Appreciation campaigns (historically around the Fourth of July and Veterans Day). For the current 2026 summer event, Target's own corporate announcement confirms **20% off one qualifying storewide purchase from June 21–July 4, 2026** for verified military members, veterans, and their families who are Target Circle members. The standard cadence has historically been **10% off**; the 20% figure is a confirmed, higher one-time summer 2026 offer. Eligibility verification is handled by **SheerID** (named on Target's own help page), and per Target's help article the discount covers **active or veteran service members plus one dependent**. The single most important caveat: this is a **time-boxed, single-use Circle Bonus** that loads into a verified account during a live campaign — it is **not** an everyday 10% off, and exclusions apply (the full current exclusion list is held inside the Target Circle account/offer terms, not published openly on the public landing page).

> Accuracy note: Many aggregator pages frame Target's military discount as a standing "10% off." That is misleading. The discount is only live during Target's twice-yearly Military Appreciation windows, and the amount has varied (10% historically; **20% for June 21–July 4, 2026** per Target's own June 2, 2026 press release). Specific excluded brands/products (e.g., Apple, PlayStation consoles) circulate on coupon and editorial sites and reflect prior-year terms — they are **not confirmed on Target's current public official pages** and are flagged below as unverified.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Join **Target Circle** (free) at Target.com or in the Target app if you're not already a member.
2. Verify your military status **before the event starts**: in the Target app, go to **My Target → Settings (wheel) → Military verification** under "Your discounts"; on Target.com, go to **Account → Settings → Verify status → Military**. Submit the verification form (processed by SheerID).
3. Once a campaign is live and you're verified, the **Circle Bonus** loads into your account automatically (allow up to 1 hour after verifying).
4. Add qualifying items to your cart and check out signed in to your Target Circle account — the discount applies to your qualifying purchase.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Have a **Target Circle** account with **verified military status** (verify in advance via the Target app or Target.com).
2. At checkout, **scan your Target Circle barcode** in the Wallet tab of the Target app (or provide the phone number tied to your Circle account).
3. The active Military Appreciation **Circle Bonus** applies to your qualifying purchase during the event window.

### How Verification Works
Target verifies military eligibility through **SheerID**. You submit a one-time verification form (name, branch, current affiliation, discharge date if applicable) plus an accepted government document through your Target Circle account in the Target app or on Target.com. Once SheerID approves you, your status is stored to your Circle account, and the Military Appreciation discount auto-loads as a Circle Bonus whenever a live campaign is running. If verification fails (often a name mismatch or typo), correct your details to match your documents exactly and resubmit; for SheerID-side issues use SheerID's help center, and for missing bonuses after a successful verification, contact Target's Help Center.

### FAQ

#### Does Target offer a military discount?
Yes, but it's **not an everyday discount**. Target runs a **Military Appreciation** discount during limited windows (historically twice a year, around the Fourth of July and Veterans Day) through Target Circle. For summer 2026, it's **20% off one qualifying purchase from June 21–July 4**.

#### How much is the Target military discount?
For the current summer 2026 event, **20% off one qualifying storewide purchase** (June 21–July 4). In past years the offer was **10%**. The amount is set per campaign — always check the live offer.

#### Is the Target military discount available year-round?
No. It only loads into your account when a Military Appreciation campaign is live. Outside those windows there is no active military discount.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Yes — veterans qualify, and Target's help page states the discount covers active or veteran service members **and one dependent**. The 2026 summer offer extends to "military families."

#### How do I verify my military status?
Join Target Circle (free), then submit a verification form in the Target app (My Target → Settings → Military verification) or on Target.com (Account → Settings → Verify status → Military). Verification is handled by SheerID.

#### Does Target use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**SheerID.** Target's own help page states it partners with SheerID for military eligibility verification.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Verify in advance, then scan your Target Circle barcode in the Target app Wallet (or give your Circle phone number) at checkout during the event.

#### What is excluded?
The offer applies to "one qualifying storewide purchase," and exclusions apply. Target keeps the authoritative exclusion list inside the Circle offer terms in your account — check there. (Earlier years excluded items like Apple and game consoles; those reflect prior terms and aren't confirmed for the current offer.)

#### Can I combine it with sales or other Circle deals?
The 2026 window overlaps Target Circle Deal Days, so already-discounted items may qualify, but Target doesn't state that the military Bonus stacks with other offers. Assume no extra stacking unless the offer terms say otherwise.

#### Does Target offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
There's no Target first-responder/nurse discount. Target does offer a **Student & Teacher Appreciation** discount — over 50% off an annual Target Circle 360 membership for verified students and teachers — which is separate from the military offer.
