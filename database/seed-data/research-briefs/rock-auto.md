# RockAuto — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$300 parts order** (e.g. brake rotors + pads + hardware for one axle) shipped from rockauto.com.

## Headline finding

**RockAuto has no military or veteran discount — and no everyday discount of any kind for any group.** ID.me states plainly that it is "not aware that RockAuto offers Military discounts," and RockAuto's own promotions page lists only manufacturer instant-savings promos and closeout inventory, never an identity-based discount. This is the **"no military route" archetype (B)** with a strong **"myth brand" (H)** overlay: coupon sites advertise "35% off RockAuto military" codes that do not exist. The honest savings path is RockAuto's already-low base pricing + Wholesaler Closeouts + the manufacturer instant-savings promos on the promo page; a service member who wants an actual military discount on parts should use **O'Reilly (10% in-store)** or **Advance Auto Parts (10% in-store)** instead and price-compare against RockAuto's total with shipping.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)

4. **The retailer substitutes don't stack either.** O'Reilly's and Advance's 10% military discounts are in-store only and exclude sale items — they replace, not stack on, promotional pricing.

### FAQ

#### Does RockAuto offer a military discount?
No. RockAuto has no military or veteran discount, and ID.me — the largest military-verification provider — says it is "not aware that RockAuto offers Military discounts." RockAuto offers no identity-based discounts to any group.

#### Is there a RockAuto military coupon code?
No. Sites advertising "35% off RockAuto military codes" are fabricating them. RockAuto issues only targeted codes (a shopper-reported ~5% emailed to past customers, plus car-club codes), entered in the "How did you hear about us" box — there is no public code and no military code.

#### Why is RockAuto still cheap without a discount?
RockAuto's stated model is low base prices with shipping charged at actual cost per warehouse ("Shipping is never free… we think it makes more sense to set part prices low"). Its everyday prices often beat parts-store retail even after a competitor's 10% discount — but always compare totals including shipping.

#### Does RockAuto use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. RockAuto has no verification partner and no GovX storefront.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy auto parts?
If you can wait for shipping: RockAuto's base price or Closeouts, from a single warehouse. If you need it today or the part ships expensive: O'Reilly or Advance Auto Parts in-store with the 10% military discount, then compare that total against RockAuto's delivered price.

#### Do O'Reilly or Advance Auto Parts have military discounts?
Yes — both give 10% off in-store to verified military and veterans (O'Reilly: most retail-priced items, participating stores, ID each visit; Advance: regular-price items, excludes online/sale/gift cards). Neither applies online.

#### Does RockAuto run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No such promo is documented on RockAuto's own pages in any year. Its only recurring price cuts are rotating manufacturer instant-savings promos and closeout clearance.

#### Does RockAuto offer a first responder, senior, or student discount?
No. RockAuto offers no identity-based discounts to any group.
