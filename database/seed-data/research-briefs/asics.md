# ASICS Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

ASICS runs a verified-community discount program offering **up to 25% off full-priced footwear and up to 30% off full-priced apparel** to military service members and veterans (alongside medical professionals, first responders, teachers, students, and employees of select gyms). To use it you must be a free **OneASICS™** member and verify your status through **SheerID**. The discount works **both online at ASICS.com and at ASICS U.S. retail stores**, but online and in-store eligibility are verified separately. The single most important caveat: the discount **applies only to full-priced merchandise** (never sale/clearance), **cannot be combined with other offers**, is **capped at two purchases per month / five full-priced items per purchase**, and **expires after one year**, requiring re-verification.

> Accuracy notes: (1) Several coupon/aggregator pages advertise **"40% off"** for ASICS — that figure is **not supported by ASICS's current official source** and appears to trace to an older, time-limited 2020 healthcare-worker promotion. Only **up to 25% footwear / up to 30% apparel** is confirmed on ASICS's own pages as of June 23, 2026. (2) Some aggregators state ASICS verifies via **ID.me** and that in-store shoppers simply "show a military ID." ASICS's official pages name **only SheerID** as the verification provider and describe in-store redemption via an **emailed QR code**, not a flashed military ID. Do not assert ID.me or "show your military ID" as fact.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Join or log in to **OneASICS™** (free) at ASICS.com.
2. Go to the ASICS Discount Programs page and select your discount group (Military).
3. Choose **online** verification and verify with **SheerID** (you may be asked for a work/school-issued email or documentation).
4. Once approved, your discount appears in your OneASICS Member Dashboard and your shopping bag — no code needed.
5. Add full-priced qualifying items, then click **Apply** in your bag at checkout.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Join or log in to **OneASICS™** and select your discount group (Military).
2. Choose **in-store** verification and verify with **SheerID** (in-store is verified separately from online).
3. Once approved, you receive a **QR code by email**.
4. At an ASICS U.S. retail store, show the QR code to the associate.
5. The associate scans it and applies your discount to eligible full-priced items at checkout.

### How Verification Works
ASICS uses **SheerID** to confirm eligibility, and the discount is tied to your free **OneASICS™** account. You select your community (Military), then verify online and/or in store — the two channels are verified separately. SheerID may ask for a work/school-issued email address or supporting documentation; verification is usually instant but can take longer if a document review is needed. Approval is communicated by email. **Your eligibility lasts one year**, after which you re-verify with SheerID to renew. Verification is not guaranteed, and you may be denied. For issues, work through your OneASICS account and the SheerID prompt; ASICS customer service can assist with account questions.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Full-priced, in-stock items only.** The discount does not apply to sale or reduced-price merchandise.
- **Not combinable** with other offers, coupons, discounts, or benefits — except the New OneASICS Members 10%-off-first-purchase offer, or as ASICS otherwise determines.
- **Caps:** up to 2 purchases per month; the discount applies to a maximum of 5 full-priced items per purchase (if an order has more, it applies to the 5 lowest-priced eligible items). Unused monthly discounts do not roll over.
- **No bulk/resale:** purchases must be for the verified participant's personal use; bulk orders (5+ of the same SKU) and resale are prohibited and can trigger account suspension/order cancellation.
- **Excluded from discount:** prior purchases, gift cards, gift certificates, taxes, shipping/handling. Taxes and shipping are calculated after the discount.
- **Returns/warranty:** any credit uses the discounted price paid, not full price.
- ASICS maintains a "Styles Excluded from Discounts" list; specific excluded styles are not enumerated here — confirm at checkout. *(Flag: the itemized style-exclusion list was not captured; verify the current list on asics.com before claiming any specific product is or isn't covered.)*
- **Region:** US and Puerto Rico residents only. Void where prohibited.

### FAQ

#### Does ASICS offer a military discount?
Yes. ASICS offers verified military service members and veterans up to 25% off full-priced footwear and up to 30% off full-priced apparel, online and in U.S. retail stores, through its OneASICS + SheerID discount program.

#### How much is the ASICS military discount?
Up to 25% off full-priced footwear and up to 30% off full-priced apparel; the exact amount varies by community and is shown in your OneASICS account. (Coupon sites advertising "40% off" are not supported by ASICS's current official terms.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify — ASICS explicitly lists "military service members and veterans." ASICS's official pages do not confirm eligibility for military spouses or dependents, so check directly through SheerID rather than assuming.

#### How do I verify my status?
Join free OneASICS, select the Military discount group, and verify with SheerID. You may be asked for a work/school-issued email or documentation. Online and in-store eligibility are verified separately.

#### Does ASICS use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
ASICS's official pages name **SheerID** only. We don't confirm ID.me, GovX, or WeSalute for ASICS, despite some third-party claims.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
Yes. Verify the in-store option with SheerID, then show the QR code ASICS emails you to the store associate, who scans it at checkout.

#### What is excluded?
Sale/clearance items, prior purchases, gift cards, taxes, and shipping. It's full-priced items only, capped at 2 purchases/month and 5 full-priced items per purchase, and can't be combined with other offers.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — it can't be combined with other offers or applied to sale items. The one exception ASICS allows is the new-member 10%-off-first-purchase offer.

#### How long does the ASICS discount last?
Your eligibility is valid for one year; after that you re-verify with SheerID to renew.

#### Does ASICS offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — the same program covers medical professionals, first responders, teachers, students, and employees of select gyms (Orangetheory, Crunch, LA Fitness), each verified as its own community through SheerID.
