# Stetson — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $150 Stetson hat (mid-range straw/felt western) at stetson.com.

## Headline finding

**Stetson publishes no military discount.** The stetson.com FAQ and the full Stetson Help Center contain zero mention of a military, veteran, or first-responder program (read in full July 14, 2026); Stetson has no store on ID.me Shop and no GovX brand storefront. Aggregators (SemperSave, WorthEPenny) claim a "10% for active duty via ID.me" — with the tell-tale hedge "the company does not specify who is eligible in their policy" — which no primary source confirms. This is a **no-military-route brand (archetype B) with a "myth" overlay (H/J)**. The genuine best military path is a **retailer substitute**: Boot Barn, an authorized Stetson retailer, gives verified military and first responders **10% off full-priced styles every day, online and in-store, via ID.me**. On stetson.com itself, the savings lever is the end-of-season sale (a 40%-off EOS collection and an extra-25%-off-sale-items collection were live July 14, 2026).
