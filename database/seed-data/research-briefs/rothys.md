# Rothy's — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $155 pair of Rothy's flats (e.g. The Point), pre-tax, shipped to a US address.

## Headline finding

Rothy's runs an **official, evergreen 20% military discount** — good for **one pair of shoes**, verified through **SheerID** (not ID.me), valid on rothys.com and in Rothy's own retail stores. It is a genuine brand program, not a discretionary or aggregator claim. For an eligible service member buying a current-season pair, the best path is the **SheerID 20% code layered with a cashback portal** (Rakuten ~2% as of July 13, 2026): about **$121.50 on the $155 baseline**. The one exception: if your exact size/style sits in Rothy's continuously-rotating **Final Few** clearance (up to ~50% off), that markdown can beat 20% — but it can't be combined with the military code, so buy whichever nets lower. Archetype: **DTC brand with a real SheerID audience discount + near-zero sitewide sales** — the discount is the deal, and stacking is tightly restricted by Rothy's own "may not be combined" terms.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military 20% code stands alone.** Rothy's terms: *"may not be combined with any other offers, promotions, or discounts."* It will not stack with welcome, referral, or Final Few clearance.
2. **One pair only.** The 20% applies to a single pair of shoes per the offer — not a whole cart of multiple pairs.
3. **Single-use code.** SheerID issues a unique, single-use code after verification; it isn't a reusable account perk.
4. **Cashback is the one legitimate layer.** Rakuten/ID.me Shop cashback is a rebate paid by the portal, not a discount deducted at Rothy's checkout, so it generally rides on top of the SheerID code. It is **not guaranteed** — Rothy's can exclude discounted/coded orders from commission, and rates change. Treat the ~2% as a bonus, not a certainty. (Medium confidence.)
5. **Welcome and referral don't stack with each other** or with the military code; referral codes issued on/after Jan 17, 2024 also can't be stacked with each other.

### FAQ

#### Does Rothy's offer a military discount?
Yes. Rothy's offers 20% off one pair of shoes to military members, verified through SheerID, valid online and in Rothy's stores.

#### How much is the Rothy's military discount?
20% off a single pair of shoes.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes — active duty, reservists/National Guard, veterans/retirees, military spouses/dependents, and Gold Star families all qualify.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through SheerID at rothys.com/pages/discount. After verification you get a unique single-use 20% code.

#### Does Rothy's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
Rothy's uses **SheerID**. It does **not** run its military discount through ID.me — ID.me's own page says it's "not aware that Rothy's offers Military discounts" (ID.me Shop only provides cashback via affiliate links). Rothy's is not on GovX.

#### Can I use it in stores and online?
Yes — the 20% is valid on rothys.com and in Rothy's retail stores.

#### What is excluded?
It's limited to one pair and can't be combined with any other offer, promotion, or discount.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No. Rothy's terms state the offer "may not be combined with any other offers, promotions, or discounts," so it won't stack with welcome, referral, or Final Few clearance. A third-party cashback portal is the only realistic extra layer.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Rothy's?
For a current-season pair: the SheerID 20% code plus a cashback portal (~$121.50 on a $155 pair). If your exact size/style is in Final Few clearance, that can be cheaper — but you can't use both.

#### Does Rothy's offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Yes — the same 20% extends to first responders, licensed healthcare workers, teachers/faculty/staff, and enrolled students, each verified through SheerID.

#### Does Rothy's run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No documented military-specific holiday promo beyond the standing 20%. Rothy's rarely runs sitewide sales, so the evergreen discount is usually your best guaranteed price.

#### Can I buy Rothy's at the Exchange or through ExpertVoice?
