# Urban Air — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one walk-up visit for a family of four — 4 Ultimate/Platinum attraction wristbands at a representative ~$32/person park = **~$128 before tax** (prices vary widely by franchise; see note).

## Headline finding

Urban Air Adventure Park is a **franchise chain** (an Unleashed Brands company), and the military discount is set and honored **at the individual park level, not by corporate.** Many parks give a **flat 10% off attraction tickets, year-round, for anyone who shows a Military ID at the front desk** — this is well-documented across individual park social accounts and a 2021 corporate tweet, but it is **not published on urbanair.com's national offers page**, is **not guaranteed at every location**, and is **in-park only** (no verified online code). Verification is a physical **Military ID at the register — NOT SheerID or ID.me**, despite coupon sites claiming otherwise. The archetype is **J (unofficial / franchise-discretion)** with a strong **H (aggregator-invented central program)** overlay: sites like WorthEPenny fabricate a "10% via SheerID unique code at urbanair.com" flow that does not exist. **Best-savings reality:** the 10% is small and can't be booked online, so a one-time visitor usually saves *more* by buying **discounted advance/off-peak tickets online** (or a Groupon) than by using the walk-up military 10%; a family that visits twice a month or more saves far more with a **membership ($16.99–$24.99/mo)** than with any per-visit discount. Always call your specific park to confirm the military rate before you go.

**Verdict logic:**
- One-time visit, planning ahead → **buy discounted advance tickets online** (or a Groupon); usually cheaper than the walk-up 10%.
- One-time visit, walking up, park honors military → **show your Military ID for 10%** (small but free).
- Visiting during late May / Veterans Day / a park appreciation week → **ask about the event promo** (can hit 50% off) — far bigger than 10%.
- Two-plus visits a month → **buy a membership**, not tickets.
- On-page chooser: *"Are you visiting once, or repeatedly?"* → once = advance online / promo / event; repeatedly = membership. Then: *"Paying walk-up with no code?"* → show Military ID for 10%.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. The **military 10% is an in-park, at-the-register discount** on walk-up tickets — you cannot apply it to an online booking, so it does **not** combine with online promo codes, advance-purchase pricing, or Groupon (those are separate online transactions).
2. **Membership vs. discount:** membership is a subscription price, not a ticket; the military 10% does not apply to it. Choose one path, not both.
3. **Franchise variance is the make-or-break fact:** because each park sets its own prices, tickets, and whether it even honors a military rate, no stack can be promised chain-wide. Confirm with your location.
4. **No portal stack:** with no GovX/ID.me/Rakuten presence, there is no cashback or marketplace layer to add.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military — eligible at most participating parks (show Military ID).
- Veterans — eligible at parks that advertise "year-round" and "veterans" (e.g. Aurora, Appleton, St. Charles); confirm locally.
- Military family/dependents — not consistently stated; ask your park.
- First responders — offered at some parks (e.g. St. Charles appreciation events), not chain-wide.
- Teachers / nurses / students / government — no chain-wide program found; ask locally.

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Confirm by phone that your specific Urban Air park honors a military discount and what the rate is.
2. Go to the park and bring a valid Military ID.
3. Buy attraction tickets at the front desk and present your ID to get the discount applied.
4. There is no online military code — do not expect the discount on urbanair.com or the app.

### How To Save Online (usually the better one-time deal)
1. On urbanair.com, select your park and check advance/off-peak (weekday) ticket pricing — often below walk-up.
2. Check the park's current public promo code (note: "coupon codes may not be valid at all locations").
3. Check Groupon for your specific park.
4. Compare that online total to walk-up-minus-10% and take whichever is lower.

### How Verification Works
There is no third-party verification provider. Eligibility is checked by a park employee looking at your Military ID when you buy tickets in person. Claims that you verify through SheerID for an online code are inaccurate.

### FAQ

#### Does Urban Air offer a military discount?
Not as a single national program. Many individual Urban Air parks offer roughly 10% off attraction tickets for military with a valid ID shown in person, but it's set park by park and isn't published on urbanair.com — confirm with your location.

#### How much is the Urban Air military discount?
Where offered, it's commonly 10% off attraction tickets. Some parks run deeper, time-boxed military-appreciation events (St. Charles ran 50% off Platinum tickets for a week in June 2025). Aggregator claims of "up to 85%" or "25%" military rates are not credible.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Several parks explicitly extend the year-round 10% to veterans. Spouse/dependent eligibility isn't consistently stated — ask your park.

#### How do I verify my status?
By showing a physical Military ID at the front desk when you buy tickets. There's no online verification for this discount.

#### Does Urban Air use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Coupon sites claiming a SheerID online code for Urban Air are inaccurate; verification is an in-person Military ID check. Urban Air is not on GovX or ID.me.

#### Can I use the military discount online?
No — it's an in-park, at-the-register discount. For online purchases, use advance/off-peak pricing, a public promo code, or Groupon instead.

#### What is excluded?
It typically won't combine with online promo codes, advance-purchase deals, Groupon, or membership pricing, and availability/terms vary by franchise.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale tickets?
No. It's a separate, in-park discount. Because you can't apply it online, it can't stack with an online code or advance-ticket price — pick whichever is cheaper.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy Urban Air tickets?
For one visit: compare discounted advance/off-peak online tickets and Groupon against walk-up-minus-10%, and take the lowest. During a park appreciation event: use that (can be 50% off). If you visit twice a month or more: buy a membership.

#### Does Urban Air offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Some parks run first responder appreciation offers (e.g. St. Charles). No chain-wide first responder, teacher, nurse, or student program was found — ask your local park.

#### Does Urban Air run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Individual parks stay open and restate the everyday 10% on Veterans Day; some run deeper appreciation events around Memorial Day / Military Appreciation Month. These are location-specific and vary year to year.
