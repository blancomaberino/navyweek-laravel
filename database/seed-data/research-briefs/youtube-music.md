# YouTube Music (Google) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** 12 months of YouTube Premium (which includes YouTube Music Premium) at the $15.99/mo individual rate = $191.88/year.

## Headline finding

There is **no military or veteran discount** on YouTube Music Premium or on YouTube Premium (which bundles YouTube Music). Service members and veterans pay standard rates. The military-discount search intent belongs to a **different Google product — YouTube TV**, which does run an ID.me military/first-responder/medical/teacher discount, and NFL Sunday Ticket has its own ID.me offer. For YouTube Music/Premium itself, the genuine savings levers are the **student plan ($8.99/mo, verified by SheerID)**, the **family plan ($26.99/mo for up to 6, ~$4.50/person)**, and the **annual individual plan ($159.99/yr, ~$13.33/mo)**. An honest "no discount, here's what actually saves" page is the right build.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. YouTube plan tiers **do not stack** — you're on exactly one (individual, family, student, or Lite).
2. The **student** rate requires ongoing SheerID re-verification; since Jan 2026 it reverts to the full $15.99 individual rate after the 4-year cap or a failed re-verification.
3. **Family** requires all members in the same household (Google enforces this).
4. **YouTube TV's** ID.me discount is a separate product entirely and does not apply to Music/Premium.

### FAQ

#### Does YouTube Music offer a military discount?
No. Neither YouTube Music Premium nor YouTube Premium has a military or veteran rate.

#### How much is the YouTube Music military discount?
There isn't one — military members pay the standard price.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
No military-based discount exists for this product.

#### How do I verify military status?
You can't for Premium/Music — there's no military tier. The student plan uses SheerID; YouTube TV's separate offer uses ID.me.

#### Does YouTube use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
SheerID for the student plan. ID.me applies only to the **separate** YouTube TV product, not Music/Premium.

#### What's the cheapest way to get YouTube Music/Premium?
Student plan ($8.99) if eligible; otherwise a family-plan split, then the annual plan.

#### Is the YouTube TV military discount the same as YouTube Premium?
No — YouTube TV (live TV) is a different product with its own ID.me military discount. It does not cover YouTube Music/Premium.

#### Can I combine plans or codes?
No. You're on a single plan; tiers don't stack.

#### Does YouTube offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount on Music/Premium?
Only a student discount (SheerID). No responder/teacher/nurse tier on Music/Premium.
