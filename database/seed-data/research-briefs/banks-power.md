# Banks Power — Military & First Responder Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one $1,200 Banks Power performance order (e.g., a Ram/Duramax exhaust or intake system).

## Headline finding

Banks Power (Gale Banks Engineering, bankspower.com) runs an **official military and first responder discount program**, verified through **VerifyPass**. There are two verified offers: a **Military Discount** for **Active Duty, Veterans & Retirees**, and a **First Responder & Government Discount** for **Police/Fire/EMT** and **Federal & Local government employees**. You verify once via VerifyPass on the discount page; once approved, a discount code is issued for use at checkout. **The exact discount percentage is not published on Banks' terms page or the VerifyPass offer page** — it's revealed after verification — so any specific number (aggregators claim "10%," "10–25%," "25%") is **unverified**; do not assert one. The program is US-only, one use per customer, cannot be combined with other discount codes, and is not applied retroactively.
