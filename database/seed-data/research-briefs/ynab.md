# YNAB (You Need A Budget) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one year of YNAB (the $109 annual subscription).

## Headline finding

**YNAB does not offer a military, veteran, or active-duty discount.** As of July 18, 2026, YNAB's own pricing page, its "Ways to Get YNAB for Free" blog post, its referral page, and its support center list every discount and free path YNAB runs — and none of them is military. The genuine best path for a service member is the same as for any new user: start the **34-day free trial (no credit card)**, then subscribe to the **annual plan at $109/year** ($9.08/mo) instead of $14.99/mo monthly. If the service member — or a spouse/dependent — is a **currently enrolled college student**, they qualify for YNAB's **free 365-day (one-year) subscription**, which is by far the highest-value legitimate path. YNAB is a pure digital SaaS subscription (archetype: *subscription software, no military program*), so there is no GovX, ID.me/SheerID military verification, military exchange channel, cashback portal, or pro-deal to stack.

> **Note on the "free year for active duty" rumor:** A free *year* offer at YNAB is documented only for **college students**, not for active-duty military. I found **no** YNAB primary source (pricing page, blog, support, referral page) offering a free year — or any discount — to active-duty military or veterans as such. Do not publish a "free year for military" claim.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military — no discount.** YNAB publishes no military offer.
- **Veterans — no discount.** Same.
- **Military spouses / dependents — no military discount**, but if they are enrolled college students they qualify for the free-year College Program.
- **College students (any, including service members using tuition assistance/GI Bill on active enrollment) — free 365-day subscription** with proof of current enrollment.

### How To Redeem (there is no military path)
**Standard (everyone, including military):**
1. Go to ynab.com and start the **34-day free trial** — no credit card required when you sign up directly.
2. Before the trial ends, subscribe to the **annual plan ($109/year)** rather than monthly ($14.99/mo).
3. Optional: get a **referral link** from a current YNAB subscriber so you both get a free month.

**College Program (if you or a household member is enrolled):**
1. Go to ynab.com/college.
2. Submit proof of current enrollment (student ID, transcript, or tuition statement).
3. Receive a **free 365-day subscription**.

### How Verification Works
YNAB does **not** run military verification (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, WeSalute, or VerifyPass military gate). The only verification YNAB performs is **college enrollment** for its free-year student program, submitted directly to YNAB support with a student ID, transcript, or tuition statement.

### FAQ

#### Does YNAB offer a military discount?
No. As of July 18, 2026, YNAB publishes no military, veteran, or active-duty discount on its pricing page, its "ways to get YNAB for free" list, its referral page, or its support center.

#### How much is the YNAB military discount?
There is none. Military members pay the standard price: **$109/year** or **$14.99/month**.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
Not as military. There is no veteran, spouse, or dependent military rate. A spouse or dependent who is a **currently enrolled college student** qualifies for YNAB's free one-year College Program.

#### How do I verify my military status with YNAB?
You don't — YNAB has no military verification. It only verifies **college enrollment** for its free-year student program.

#### Does YNAB use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No, not for a military discount. YNAB uses none of these for military eligibility.

#### Is there a free year of YNAB for active-duty military?
No verified free-year offer exists for active-duty military. The **free 365-day** offer applies to **college students** (proof of enrollment), not to military status.

#### What is actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy YNAB?
If you or a household member is an enrolled college student: the **College Program (one year free)**. Otherwise: the **34-day free trial**, then the **$109 annual plan**, plus a **referral** for a free month.

#### Can I combine YNAB promo codes with a discount?
YNAB does not issue general public promo codes, so aggregator "50% off" or "military" codes are unverified and typically don't work. The legitimate savers are the trial, the college year-free program, and referrals.

#### Does YNAB offer a student, teacher, nurse, or first-responder discount?
Only a **student** offer: a free 365-day subscription for currently enrolled college students. No teacher, nurse, or first-responder program is published.
