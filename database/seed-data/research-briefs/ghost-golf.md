## Headline finding

Ghost Golf runs a **real, official military discount** verified through **GovX**. The brand maintains a dedicated `ghostgolf.com/pages/military-discount` page, and the GovX brand listing shows a discount of **up to 20% off** for active-duty, veteran, and first-responder shoppers (immediate family included per the brand's own summary). You verify once through GovX, receive a **single-use code**, and apply it at checkout on ghostgolf.com. The catch: it typically **won't stack** with public promo codes, so the play is to verify and then apply **whichever runs deeper** — the GovX rate or the current sitewide code.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **GovX codes are single-use and generally do not stack** with other promo codes — most Shopify stores restrict one discount code per order.
2. **Compare, don't combine** — check the GovX-verified rate against the current public code and apply whichever is deeper.
3. Sale-priced items may or may not accept the code — confirm at checkout, since exclusions on clearance are common.
