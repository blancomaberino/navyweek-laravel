Separately, Norton runs **public first-year promotions of roughly 37–52% off** on LifeLock plans that **anyone** can take — these frequently rival or beat gated military pricing in year one. Archetype: **GovX-gated pricing on a subscription that also has an aggressive public first-year promo — verify GovX vs. the public promo, and watch the year-2 renewal.** The honest verdict: check the **GovX** Norton/LifeLock price against the current **public first-year deal**, buy the lower, and set a renewal reminder because both jump at renewal.
