# Uber — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$25 UberX ride** (pre-tip; Uber's booking fee, government surcharges, tolls and taxes are billed on top and are excluded from every promo per Uber's terms). A parallel **$30 Uber Eats order** is used for the delivery-side notes.

## Headline finding

**Uber has no standing military or veteran discount on rides or Uber Eats.** There is no active "military rate," no verified military promo code, and no ID.me/SheerID rider-fare discount — Uber's own promo page and Help Center describe only general new-rider offers and the paid Uber One membership, with **no military-specific pricing** anywhere. The "UberMILITARY" name that aggregators cite is a **2014-era veteran/military-family *driver* onboarding and recognition program** (plus a one-day Veterans Day 2014 rider promo that waived Uber's fee through the end of that year) — it is **not** a current rider discount. What *does* exist for the military community: (1) a small, **not-military-exclusive** ID.me Shop **cash-back** of *up to $2.50 per rider trip* available to every verified ID.me group; (2) the **VHA-Uber Health Connect** program, under which **beneficiary-travel-eligible veterans get free rides to VA medical appointments** — a VA benefit arranged by VA staff, not an Uber consumer discount; and (3) the genuine everyday saver, **Uber One** ($9.99/mo or $96/yr: 6% back on rides, $0 Eats delivery fee + up to 10% off, surge savings). Archetype: **B (no first-party military route)** + **H (myth brand — aggregators invent a flat "military %" that doesn't exist; the real value is the membership/loyalty wallet, not a discount).**

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The new-rider promo does not stack** — RIDEUBER2026FT is "cannot be combined with other promotions," one trip, personal profile, 14-day expiry, and excludes fees/tolls/tips.
2. **Uber One credits are earned, not stacked as a discount** — 6% back posts as Uber One credit for a *future* ride/order; it doesn't reduce the current fare and can't be combined into a bigger single-ride cut.
3. **ID.me Shop cash-back is a rebate, not a fare code** — it comes from ID.me (activate via the ID.me Shop link before booking). It is *not* a military-exclusive discount and Uber applies no code at checkout; identity/app-gated tracking can break, so treat it as best-effort, not guaranteed to layer on top of a promo.
4. **VUHC free rides are VA-booked** — arranged by VA staff through Uber Health; a veteran can't self-apply a consumer promo to them, and they only cover approved VA-appointment travel.
5. **Uber Cash / gift-card value stacks under any of the above** — loading discounted Uber Cash lowers what you pay regardless of promo, since it's the funding source, not a competing discount.
6. **No GovX, WeSalute, or cashback-portal rideshare route exists** — Uber rides/Eats are not sold through GovX or Rakuten-style portals, so there's no marketplace-substitute or portal-stack to compute.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty military** — No Uber rider discount. Eligible for the general ID.me Shop cash-back like everyone verified; save via Uber One / new-rider promo.
- **Veterans** — No Uber rider discount. May qualify for **free VA rides (VUHC)** if beneficiary-travel eligible; otherwise Uber One / promos.
- **Military spouses & families** — No dedicated discount; general savings paths only.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — No dedicated discount; general savings paths only.
- **First responders / nurses / teachers / students / government** — No Uber rider discount; all can use the same general ID.me Shop cash-back (up to $2.50/trip). The cash-back is **not** military-exclusive.
- **BT-eligible veterans (SC ≥30%, SC-condition travel, VA pension, or income under the max pension rate)** — May receive **free** VUHC rides to VA appointments, arranged by VA staff.

### How To Save — New Riders (public promo)
1. In the Uber app, open **Account → Wallet → Add Promo Code**.
2. Enter the current public code (e.g. **RIDEUBER2026FT**, as of July 10, 2026 — codes rotate).
3. Get **30% off your first ride, up to $8** — one trip, personal profile, expires 14 days after activation, excludes fees/tolls/tips, not combinable. This is a general offer, **not** a military discount.

### How To Save — Frequent Riders (Uber One)
1. In the app, go to **Uber One** and start the **4-week free trial** (eligible first-time members) or subscribe at **$9.99/month** or **$96/year**.
2. Earn **6% back** in Uber One credits on eligible rides and get **automatic surge savings**.
3. On Uber Eats, get **$0 delivery fee** and **up to 10% off** eligible orders. Cancel anytime.

### How To Save — ID.me Shop Cash-Back
1. Verify once with **ID.me** (any eligible group) and open the **Uber store on shop.id.me**.
2. Activate the offer and use the linked path before booking to earn **up to $2.50 cash back** per rider trip. It's a rebate, not a checkout discount, and is available to all verified groups.

### Veterans: Free VA Rides (VHA-Uber Health Connect)

2. If you're **beneficiary-travel eligible** (service-connected rating ≥30%, traveling for an SC condition, receiving VA pension, or income under the max annual pension rate) and ambulatory with a common-carrier BT consult, ask your **VA Beneficiary Travel point of contact**.

### FAQ

#### Does Uber offer a military discount?
No. Uber has no military or veteran discount on rides or Uber Eats — no military rate, no verified military promo code, and no ID.me/SheerID fare discount.

#### What is "UberMILITARY," then?
It's a **2014 program to recruit and recognize veteran, service-member and military-spouse *drivers*** (50,000 signed up by 2016), plus a one-day Veterans Day 2014 rider promo that has long expired. It is not a current rider discount.

#### Is there an Uber "$10 + 15% off" military promo code via SheerID?
No — that claim on coupon sites is **fabricated**. Uber issues no military code and uses no SheerID fare-discount checkout for riders.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents get a discount?
Not from Uber directly. Veterans who are VA beneficiary-travel eligible may get **free** rides to VA medical appointments through the VA's VHA-Uber Health Connect program — a VA benefit, not an Uber discount.

#### Does Uber use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for a discount?
Only ID.me, and only for (1) a small **general** Shop cash-back (up to $2.50/trip, open to all verified groups) and (2) veteran **driver** affiliation badges. There's no GovX, WeSalute, or SheerID rider discount.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to use Uber?
New users: the public **30%-off-first-ride** promo (up to $8). Regulars: **Uber One** ($9.99/mo or $96/yr) for 6% back, $0 Eats delivery, and surge savings. Veterans going to VA care: the VA's free-ride program if BT-eligible.

#### How much is Uber One and is it worth it?
$9.99/month or $96/year, with a 4-week free trial for eligible first-timers. It gives 6% back on rides, $0 Eats delivery fee plus up to 10% off, and surge savings — worth it if you ride or order often enough to cover the fee.

#### Can I get free Uber rides to VA appointments?
Possibly. If you're beneficiary-travel eligible (service-connected ≥30%, SC-condition travel, VA pension, or income under the max pension rate) and ambulatory with a BT consult, VA staff can arrange free Uber Health rides. Contact your VA Beneficiary Travel point of contact.

#### Does Uber run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day rider discount?
Not currently. The only documented military rider promo was a one-day Veterans Day 2014 event, long expired. Don't count on a holiday rider discount; check the app each November.

#### Does the ID.me cash-back only apply to military?
No. The Uber ID.me Shop cash-back (up to $2.50/trip) is open to every verified group — military, first responders, nurses, teachers, students, and government — it is not a military-exclusive offer.
