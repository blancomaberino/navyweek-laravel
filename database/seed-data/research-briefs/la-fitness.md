# LA Fitness Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

LA Fitness (Fitness International, LLC) does **not** publish a standalone, year-round military or veteran discount. As of June 24, 2026, its official website shows no military/veteran offer page, no enrolled verification provider (ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute) for a military discount, and ID.me's own brand page states it is "not aware that LA Fitness offers First Responders discounts." Three real, source-backed savings paths exist, none of them a simple "X% off for veterans": (1) **WeSalute+ (formerly Veterans Advantage)** — paid membership whose **GlobalFit** wellness benefit lists LA Fitness among participating clubs with member rates on dues, available only through GlobalFit and not to anyone who held an LA Fitness membership in the prior 12 months; (2) **LA Fitness Corporate Wellness Program** — discounted corporate rates available to employees of organizations (including government agencies) that set up an account, not military-specific; and (3) a **seasonal Veterans Day free-access promotion** that LA Fitness's own X account ran for Nov 11-14, 2021 (free club use for veterans, active military, and their families/friends with ID) — historical and not confirmed for 2026. The single most important caveat: LA Fitness memberships and fees are negotiated club-by-club, so any "military rate," waived initiation fee, or promo varies by location and must be confirmed with the local club; do not assume a fixed national discount.

### How To Redeem Online (WeSalute+ / GlobalFit path)
1. Join or log in to **WeSalute+** at wesalute.com (paid subscription; verify your service status during signup).
2. Access the **GlobalFit** wellness benefit from your WeSalute+ account.
3. Browse participating clubs through GlobalFit and select LA Fitness; member rates are available **only through GlobalFit**, online or by phone — not at the club desk and not to the general public.
4. Complete enrollment through GlobalFit. Note the 12-month prior-member restriction below.

### How To Redeem In Store (local club / Veterans Day promo)
1. Find your local club at lafitness.com → Locations.
2. For any **local military courtesy** (e.g., reduced or waived initiation fee), call or visit and ask directly; bring military ID, DD-214, or veteran status card. Terms are set per club and are not guaranteed.
3. During a **Veterans Day free-access promotion** (when LA Fitness runs one), simply show your ID at the club for free access on the promo dates. Watch LA Fitness's official channels each November to confirm whether the promo is running.

### How Verification Works
LA Fitness does not operate its own military verification flow and is not listed as a partner of ID.me for a military or first-responder discount. For the WeSalute+ path, **WeSalute** verifies your military/service eligibility as part of its (paid) membership, and the gym rate is then unlocked through GlobalFit. For any local club offer or a Veterans Day promo, the club itself checks ID at the point of signup or entry (military ID, DD-214, or veteran ID, per the club). There is no published "verify once" national LA Fitness military credential.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **WeSalute+/GlobalFit:** Health-club rates are **not available to current or recent past members within 12 months** of canceling a prior membership. Rates are available **only through GlobalFit** (online/phone), not through the clubs or to the general public. "Some restrictions apply." (WeSalute terms.)
- **Veterans Day promo:** Documented for Nov 11-14, 2021 only; dates, participation, and whether it runs at all in a given year are not guaranteed. Treat as seasonal, not evergreen.
- **Local club offers:** LA Fitness states offers may not be available at all locations and are not combinable with other offers or rates; advertised rates can change without notice. Initiation fees, dues, and any service courtesy vary by club.
- **First responder $39.99:** Not confirmed by any primary source and contradicted by ID.me. Do not rely on it without club confirmation.
- Caps, stacking with promo codes, gift-card rules specific to a military offer: **Not stated in official source.**

### FAQ

#### Does LA Fitness offer a military discount?
Not as a standalone, advertised program. LA Fitness's website shows no military/veteran discount and no military verification partner. Service members can save through WeSalute+ (GlobalFit), an employer's Corporate Wellness Program, occasional Veterans Day free-access promotions, or by negotiating directly with a local club. (Sources: lafitness.com; shop.id.me; wesalute.com — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### How much is the LA Fitness military discount?
There is no published percentage or flat military discount from LA Fitness itself. WeSalute+ offers "member rates" on dues through GlobalFit but does not publish a fixed percentage on its public page. Any local initiation-fee waiver or rate is set club-by-club. (Source: wesalute.com — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
For WeSalute+, eligibility covers active duty, retirees, veterans, current/former Guard & Reserve, and immediate family — so spouses and dependents can qualify through that paid membership. LA Fitness's 2021 Veterans Day promo extended free access to veterans, active military, and their "families and friends." (Sources: wesalute.com; LA Fitness X post — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### How do I verify my status?
LA Fitness has no national military verification flow. WeSalute verifies your service status as part of its membership; for a local club courtesy or a Veterans Day promo, bring a military ID, DD-214, or veteran ID and the club verifies on the spot. (Sources: wesalute.com; lafitness.com — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### Does LA Fitness use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
LA Fitness does not use ID.me for a military/first-responder discount — ID.me's own page says it is "not aware" of one. The only verification-service link is indirect: WeSalute+ subscribers can reach LA Fitness rates through the GlobalFit benefit. (Sources: shop.id.me; wesalute.com — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### Can I use the discount in stores (at the club)?
WeSalute+ GlobalFit rates are available **only through GlobalFit** (online/phone), not at the club desk. A Veterans Day free-access promo is redeemed in-club by showing ID. Local fee courtesies are arranged in person at the club. (Sources: wesalute.com; LA Fitness X post — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### What is excluded?
WeSalute+/GlobalFit gym rates are not available to anyone who held an LA Fitness membership within the prior 12 months, and are not offered to the general public or through the clubs directly. Local offers are not combinable with other rates and vary by club. (Sources: wesalute.com; lafitness.com — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
LA Fitness states its offers are generally not available in combination with other offers or rates. There is no published policy on stacking a military offer with promo codes. Confirm with the club or GlobalFit. (Source: lafitness.com — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)

#### Does LA Fitness offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No verified first-responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount exists from LA Fitness; ID.me reports no first-responder partnership, and aggregator "$39.99 first responder" claims are unverified. Government employees aren't offered a military discount but may access the Corporate Wellness Program through their agency. (Sources: shop.id.me; lafitness.com/pages/corporatesignup.aspx — accessed Jun 24, 2026.)
