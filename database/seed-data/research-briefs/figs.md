# FIGS Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

FIGS offers a **15% military discount** on most products to U.S. service members, verified through **ID.me** at online checkout. The offer is evergreen and explicitly covers active-duty, veteran, and retired individuals with a U.S. military service record active from 9/30/1985, plus USAA members. It is **U.S. only**, applies to most (not all) products, **cannot be stacked** with other codes or used on items already discounted during a promotion, and **is not available in the FIGS mobile app** — only the online store at checkout. The single most important caveat: when a site-wide sale is running, the ID.me option may not appear, and the military discount cannot be combined with sale pricing.

### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military — eligible (U.S. service record active from 9/30/1985), per FIGS Military page.
- Veterans — eligible (U.S. service record active from 9/30/1985), per FIGS Military page.
- Retired military — eligible, per FIGS Military page.
- USAA members — eligible, per FIGS Military page and ID.me restrictions.
- Reserve / National Guard — Not stated in official source (eligibility runs through ID.me's military group verification).
- Military spouses — Not stated in official source.
- Dependents — Not stated in official source.
- Surviving spouses / Gold Star families — Not stated in official source.
- First responders — Not eligible. FIGS states only students and military are eligible at this time.
- Teachers — Not eligible. FIGS states only students and military are eligible at this time.
- Medical workers / nurses — No dedicated medical/nurse discount; eligible only if they separately qualify as military or student. (ID.me lists a "nurse" question for FIGS but FIGS's own help center limits discounts to students and military.)
- Government employees — Not eligible (only students and military, per FIGS).
- Students — Eligible under a separate 15% student program (ID.me in the U.S.; Student Beans internationally), not the military offer.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Add eligible items to your cart and proceed to checkout on the FIGS website.
2. On desktop, find the order summary sidebar on the right; on mobile web, tap "Show order summary" at the top of checkout.
3. Select "Students and Military" from the options listed under the items in your cart.
4. In the ID.me window, choose "Military," then sign in or sign up with ID.me and follow the prompts to verify.
5. Return to checkout — the 15% discount applies automatically to eligible items. If it doesn't show, refresh the page.
6. Place the order using the same email address you used to verify with ID.me.

### How To Redeem In Store
FIGS is primarily a direct-to-consumer online retailer. The military discount is documented only for the online store checkout via ID.me. In-store redemption is not stated in any official source; FIGS's community/store locations are not described as discount-redemption points for the military offer. Treat in-store redemption as unconfirmed and direct shoppers to the online store.

### How Verification Works
Verification is handled by ID.me, FIGS's verification partner. At online checkout you select "Students and Military," then sign in or create an ID.me account and complete ID.me's military verification. An ID.me account is required. FIGS does not publish a re-verification schedule; ID.me governs how long a verification stays valid and may require re-verification on its own terms, so do not promise "verify once forever." If automatic verification fails, FIGS directs shoppers to ID.me's military qualification page, and FIGS support is reachable by phone/text at 424-500-8209 or toll-free 1-888-462-1901 (5am–8pm PST, Mon–Fri) and via chat/contact form in the Help Center.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- The military discount cannot be stacked with other codes and cannot be used on items already discounted during a promotional period.
- Items not eligible for any discount or promotion: Text/Icon/Icon & Text Embroidery, Gift Cards, Gift Packaging, FIGS Face Masks & Face Shields, Sale items, New Balance Shoes, Eko CORE 500 Digital Stethoscope, and STAR WARS x FIGS.
- During an active site-wide promotion, the ID.me checkout option may not appear; the discount can be used once that sale ends.
- Some final-sale items (e.g., Compression Socks) may accept one discount per order; eligibility is indicated at checkout.
- The discount is U.S. only and is not available in the FIGS mobile app.
- Order must be placed with the same email used to verify with ID.me.
- Annual/per-order spending cap: Not stated in official source.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Student program** — separate 15% discount for students via ID.me (U.S.) or Student Beans (international).
- **Sample Sale / site-wide sales** — FIGS periodically runs sales (e.g., Sample Sale up to 40% off). These don't stack with the military discount, so compare which is the better single price for your cart.
- **Refer a Friend** — FIGS runs a referral program (see the Refer a Friend page) for additional savings.
- **Note for medical buyers** — FIGS rarely deep-discounts core scrubs, so the standing 15% military discount is meaningful on full-price scrub sets, tops, pants, lab coats, and underscrubs.

### FAQ

#### Does FIGS offer a military discount?
Yes. FIGS offers 15% off most products for U.S. service members, verified through ID.me at online checkout.

#### How much is the FIGS military discount?
15% off most products. The same 15% applies to FIGS's separate student discount.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and USAA members qualify?
Yes. FIGS states the discount applies to USAA members and active, veteran, and retired individuals with a U.S. military service record active from 9/30/1985. It is for U.S. service members only.

#### Do military spouses or dependents qualify?
Not stated in FIGS's official sources. Eligibility is determined by ID.me's military verification; spouses and dependents are not named on FIGS's military page.

#### How do I verify my military status?
At online checkout, select "Students and Military," choose "Military," and verify through ID.me. You'll need an ID.me account, and you should place the order with the same email you used to verify.

#### Does FIGS use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
FIGS uses ID.me for the U.S. military and student discount. (Its international student discount uses Student Beans.)

#### Can I use the military discount in the FIGS app or in stores?
No. The discount works only on the FIGS online store at checkout. FIGS confirms it is not available in the mobile app, and in-store redemption is not documented.

#### What is excluded from the FIGS military discount?
Sale items, embroidery, gift cards, gift packaging, FIGS face masks & shields, New Balance shoes, the Eko CORE 500 stethoscope, and STAR WARS x FIGS. It also can't be combined with other codes or promotion-period pricing.

#### Can I combine the military discount with promo codes or sale items?
No. It can't be stacked with other codes or applied to items already discounted during a promotion. Some final-sale items (e.g., Compression Socks) may take one discount per order, shown at checkout.

#### Does FIGS offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or government discount?
No. FIGS states that only students and military personnel are eligible for discounts at this time. There is no dedicated nurse, first responder, teacher, or government discount.
