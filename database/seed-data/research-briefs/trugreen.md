# TruGreen — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one annual residential lawn-care plan (~$500/year — actual price varies by lawn size and region; verify with a TruGreen quote).

## Headline finding

TruGreen offers a genuine, **year-round 10% military discount** on **annual residential plans**, verified through **ID.me**. It covers **active-duty, reservist, National Guard, retiree, and veteran** personnel, applies to TruGreen's core plans (Lawn, TruDefense Mosquito, TruBarrier Perimeter Pest, Flea/Tick Outdoor Nuisance, and TruShrub Tree & Shrub), and — importantly — the discount **continues in subsequent years** and applies to all non-discounted applications. You verify at ID.me, get a **single-use promo code with no expiration**, then call TruGreen to apply it to your account. TruGreen made this a permanent year-round benefit in November 2024 (the launch promo also included 50% off the first service). The discount **can't be combined** with other offers, so the decision is *military 10% + your annual prepay* vs. *TruGreen's frequent public new-customer promos* — whichever is deeper for your yard.
