# Marriott (Marriott Bonvoy) Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Marriott runs two distinct, officially documented military-related savings programs, and the difference matters because they have different audiences and mechanics. (1) The **Government & Military rate** on Marriott.com — a special negotiated/per-diem-style hotel rate selectable under "Special Rates → Government & Military" (or by entering corporate rate code `GOV`). Per Marriott's own help content, it is aimed at **active-duty military, reservists, and certain federal government employees**, requires a **valid ID at check-in**, and is **not a fixed percentage** — the savings vary by hotel, date, and availability. Eligibility and availability vary by property and region. (2) The **Military and First Responder Appreciation Rate** at Marriott Vacation Club, Westin Vacation Club, and Sheraton Vacation Club resorts (deals.marriott.com) — a **fixed 15% off flexible rates**, valid for **U.S. Active, Reserve, Retired Military, National Guard, and First Responders**, booked online or by phone with **promo code XYD**, requiring valid military or first-responder ID at check-in. The single most important caveat: Marriott does **not** publish a flat percentage discount for its standard hotel Government & Military rate, and a valid ID at check-in is mandatory — without it, the rate reverts to the market/best-available rate.

Marriott offers special rates to the military community, but it works differently than a single coupon code. For everyday hotel stays, Marriott.com has a **Government & Military rate** you select under "Special Rates" (or by entering corporate code `GOV`). ### Who Qualifies
- Active-duty military (DoD, Army, Navy, Marines, Air Force, Coast Guard) — eligible for hotel Government & Military rate and Vacation Club 15% rate.
- Reservists / active reserve members — eligible (both programs reference reserve).
- National Guard — eligible for the Vacation Club 15% rate (stated in T&Cs); for the standard hotel Gov/Mil rate, not separately stated — confirm Rate Details per hotel.
- Retired military — eligible for the Vacation Club 15% rate (stated). Not stated for the standard hotel Gov/Mil rate.
- Veterans (separated, non-retired) — **Not stated in official source** for either program; the Vacation Club T&Cs name "Active, Reserve and Retired Military, National Guard," not "veterans" broadly. Treat veteran eligibility as unconfirmed.
- First responders — eligible for the Vacation Club 15% rate (stated). Some individual properties also extend "Salute to Heroes" rates to firefighters, law enforcement, and healthcare workers; varies by property.
- Federal government employees — certain federal personnel eligible for the Government rate; eligibility can vary by hotel/region.
- Military spouses / dependents — **Not stated in official source.** (Aggregators claim dependents with a valid dependent ID may book if traveling/checking in themselves; not confirmed on a Marriott primary page — flagged Low.)
- Surviving spouses / Gold Star families — Not stated in official source.
- Teachers / nurses / students — Not stated in official source.

### How To Redeem Online (Marriott.com hotel Government & Military rate)
1. Go to Marriott.com and enter your destination and dates.
2. Open the **Special Rates** menu and select **Government & Military** (or enter corporate/promo code `GOV`).
3. Search and review available rates; check **Rate Details** to confirm eligibility for that specific hotel.
4. Book your reservation.
5. Present your valid government/military ID at check-in. (Federal per-diem bookings can be pre-qualified if booked with a U.S. Federal Government-issued credit card.)

### How To Redeem Online (Vacation Club 15% Military & First Responder rate)
1. Go to deals.marriott.com/militaryfirstresponderrate.
2. Reserve online with promotional code **XYD**, or call **1-800-228-9290** (U.S.) and quote code XYD.
3. Advance reservations are required; a limited number of rooms are available.
4. Present valid military or first-responder ID at check-in.

### How To Redeem In Store / At Check-In
1. Arrive at the property for your reservation booked under the Government & Military or Military & First Responder rate.
2. Present a valid ID at the front desk — e.g., U.S. Military ID card, Common Access Card (CAC), federal civilian personnel badge/ID, or valid first-responder ID (for the Vacation Club rate).
3. If you cannot present required ID, the rate reverts to the market/best-available rate.

### How Verification Works
Marriott does **not** use a third-party verification service (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute gate) for these direct rates. You book the rate yourself and verify by **presenting a valid ID at check-in**. For U.S. Federal Government Per Diem rates, acceptable IDs include a U.S. Military ID card, a Common Access Card (CAC), or a federal civilian personnel badge/ID; Marriott can pre-qualify a Federal Government Rate reservation (waiving the ID check) if you book with a U.S. Federal Government-issued credit card. Because there is no account-based verification, there is no annual re-verification — but ID must be shown for every qualifying stay. If a rate doesn't appear or eligibility is unclear, check the hotel's Rate Details or contact Marriott customer support / the property directly.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Government & Military hotel rate:** not a published flat percentage; rate, eligibility, and availability vary by hotel, date, and region. Valid ID required at check-in.
- **Vacation Club 15% rate (per deals.marriott.com T&Cs):** valid for U.S. Active, Reserve, Retired Military, National Guard, and First Responders; valid ID at check-in required or rate increases to market rate; **limit 2 rooms per valid ID per night**; late arrival or early departure forfeits the special rate; at check-out, rate reverts to best available; reservations cannot be assigned/transferred to third parties; limited rooms available; tax additional; does not apply to groups of 10+ rooms; **cannot be combined with any other promotions**; blackout dates may apply; advance reservations required; other restrictions apply; rates per room, per night, based on availability.
- Veteran (non-retired separated) eligibility, spouse/dependent eligibility, and stacking with Bonvoy points/elite benefits are **not stated in the official sources** reviewed — do not assert.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Join Marriott Bonvoy** (free): member rates are typically lower than standard rates and stays earn points — compare the member rate against the Government & Military rate, since the cheaper one isn't always the special rate.
- **Compare both Marriott offers:** for a Vacation Club property the 15% XYD rate may beat the per-diem rate; for a standard hotel the Gov/Mil rate or the Bonvoy member rate may win. Always check Rate Details.
- **Federal per-diem travelers:** booking with a U.S. Federal Government credit card can pre-qualify the rate and streamline check-in.
- **Property "Salute to Heroes" pages:** some individual Marriott hotels publish their own appreciation rates for military, first responders, and healthcare workers — check the specific property's Experiences page.
- **Competitor comparison:** Hilton, IHG, Wyndham, and Choice Hotels also offer government/military rates; veterans who don't qualify for Marriott's named programs may find broader veteran-inclusive offers elsewhere — verify each chain's current terms.

### FAQ

#### Does Marriott offer a military discount?
Yes. Marriott offers a Government & Military special rate on Marriott.com (selectable under Special Rates or via code `GOV`) and a separate 15% Military and First Responder Appreciation Rate at participating Marriott, Westin, and Sheraton Vacation Club resorts (code XYD).

#### How much is the Marriott military discount?
For standard hotels, Marriott does not publish a fixed percentage — the Government & Military rate is a negotiated/per-diem-style rate that varies by hotel, date, and availability. The Vacation Club Military & First Responder rate is a stated 15% off flexible rates.

#### Do veterans qualify for the Marriott military discount?
The Vacation Club 15% rate names "Active, Reserve and Retired Military, National Guard and First Responders." Broad "veteran" (separated, non-retired) eligibility is not stated in Marriott's official sources for either program, so we don't assert it — confirm with the property before booking.

#### Do military spouses or dependents qualify?
Not stated in Marriott's official sources. Some third-party pages claim dependents with a valid dependent ID may book if they travel/check in themselves, but this isn't confirmed on a Marriott primary page — verify directly with Marriott.

#### How do I verify my military status?
There's no online verification service. You book the rate yourself and present a valid government/military or first-responder ID at check-in. Federal per-diem reservations can be pre-qualified by booking with a U.S. Federal Government-issued credit card.

#### Does Marriott use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Not for these direct rates — verification is by ID at check-in. (WeSalute and other sites may list separate member-only Marriott offers, but those are third-party programs, not the direct Marriott.com Government & Military rate.)

#### Can I use the discount when I book by phone?
Yes for the Vacation Club rate — call 1-800-228-9290 (U.S.) and quote promo code XYD. The standard hotel Government & Military rate is selected online via the Special Rates menu or code GOV.

#### What is excluded?
For the Vacation Club rate: it can't be combined with other promotions, is limited to 2 rooms per ID per night, excludes groups of 10+ rooms, has possible blackout dates, requires advance reservations and valid ID at check-in, and tax is additional. Without valid ID, the rate reverts to market/best-available.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, points, or sale rates?
The Vacation Club rate explicitly cannot be combined with other promotions. Stacking the hotel Government & Military rate with other offers or Bonvoy point redemptions is not addressed in the official sources — don't assume it stacks.

#### Does Marriott offer a first responder discount?
Yes — first responders are explicitly eligible for the 15% Vacation Club Military and First Responder Appreciation Rate (code XYD), and some individual properties extend "Salute to Heroes" rates to first responders and healthcare workers.
