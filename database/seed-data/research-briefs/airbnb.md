# Airbnb Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Airbnb does **not** offer a platform-wide military, veteran, or first-responder discount. There is no Airbnb promo code, no membership tier, and no third-party verification partnership (no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute) that grants service members a fixed percentage off bookings. Airbnb's own Help Center states plainly that "Airbnb does not currently offer any loyalty discounts" ([airbnb.com/help/article/128](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/128), accessed June 24, 2026), and ID.me — a verification provider Airbnb does not use — confirms "We're not aware that Airbnb offers Military discounts" ([shop.id.me/discounts/airbnb/military](https://shop.id.me/discounts/airbnb/military), accessed June 24, 2026). Airbnb is a marketplace, not a hotel chain: pricing is set by independent hosts. The only paths to a military rate are (1) **individual hosts** who voluntarily list or message a military discount, applied through Airbnb's official "special offer" tool, and (2) Airbnb's official length-of-stay discounts (weekly/monthly), which are open to everyone, not military-specific. The single most important caveat: any "Airbnb military discount" code on a coupon site is not real — Airbnb issues no such code.

### How To Redeem Online
There is no Airbnb military promo code. To pursue a host-level discount:
1. Open the listing and read the description — some hosts state a military/first-responder discount and their conditions there.
2. Before booking, click **Contact host** / send a message and ask whether they offer a military discount and what proof they need.
3. If the host agrees, they send you a **special offer** with a custom price in the message thread.
4. Open **Messages**, review the offer, and click **Book now** (you have 24 hours before it expires).
5. Click **Reserve**, review payment info, then **Confirm and pay**. Note the special offer covers the nightly rate, cleaning fee, and extra-guest fees — not taxes, the Airbnb service fee, or any security deposit.

### How To Redeem In Store
Not applicable. Airbnb is an online/app-only marketplace with no physical stores or register.

### How Verification Works
Airbnb operates no military verification. It does not use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute for any military discount, and ID.me itself states it is "not aware that Airbnb offers Military discounts." If an individual host chooses to give a military discount, that host sets their own evidence rules (for example, asking you to mention your service in a message); this varies host to host and Airbnb does not standardize or guarantee it.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- No Airbnb-issued military discount exists, so no platform terms apply.
- For a host **special offer**: the price covers the nightly rate, cleaning fee, and extra-guest fees, but **does not include** taxes, the Airbnb service fee, or a security deposit ([Help article 128](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/128)).
- A special offer expires after **24 hours**, and changing dates or guest count may invalidate it ([Help article 128](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/128)).
- Weekly/monthly host discounts are not military-specific and are set at each host's discretion ([Help article 1233](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1233)).
- Any third-party "Airbnb military promo code" should be treated as unverified — Airbnb issues none. Flagged: aggregator-only claims of fixed percentages are **Low** confidence and are not asserted here.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Ask the host directly.** Some hosts offer a military/first-responder discount via a special offer; messaging before booking is the only way to trigger it ([Help article 128](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/128)).
- **Book longer stays.** Hosts can set weekly (7+ nights) and monthly (28+ nights) discounts; discounts of 10%+ show in search results, and Airbnb sometimes adds extra monthly-stay savings it funds itself ([Help article 1233](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1233)).
- **Shift dates / book early** to catch lower nightly rates (host-set pricing varies by demand).
- **Compare verified alternatives.** Some hotel chains (e.g., Hilton, Marriott, Wyndham) and military-focused booking platforms do run verified military rates; if a guaranteed military discount matters more than a specific listing, those may save more.

### FAQ

#### Does Airbnb offer a military discount?
No. Airbnb has no platform-wide military or veteran discount. Its Help Center states it "does not currently offer any loyalty discounts." Some individual hosts offer one voluntarily.

#### How much is the Airbnb military discount?
There is no set Airbnb military discount, so there is no official percentage. Any amount you see is set by an individual host, not by Airbnb. Coupon sites that advertise a fixed "Airbnb military discount" code are not reliable — Airbnb issues no such code.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Airbnb defines no eligibility because it has no program. Whether veterans, spouses, or dependents get a discount depends entirely on the individual host you book with.

#### How do I verify my military status with Airbnb?
You can't — Airbnb has no military verification process. If a host offers a discount, that host decides what (if any) proof they want; this is informal and not standardized.

#### Does Airbnb use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
No. Airbnb is not listed as a partner of any of these for a military discount. ID.me explicitly says it is "not aware that Airbnb offers Military discounts."

#### Can I use a military discount in Airbnb stores?
Airbnb has no physical stores. Bookings happen only on the website or app, and any host discount is applied online through a special offer.

#### What's excluded from a host's special-offer price?
A host special offer covers the nightly rate, cleaning fee, and extra-guest fees. It does not include taxes, the Airbnb service fee, or a security deposit.

#### Can I combine a host discount with promo codes or other offers?
Airbnb applies only one discount or promotion per reservation. A host's special offer or a weekly/monthly discount generally cannot be stacked with another Airbnb promotion on the same booking.

#### Does Airbnb offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
No. Airbnb offers no occupation-based discount for any of these groups at the platform level. As with military, individual hosts may choose to offer one.

#### Is there any official Airbnb program that helps service members or veterans?
Airbnb's nonprofit, Airbnb.org, runs emergency and temporary-stay programs (for example, for people displaced by disasters), but this is humanitarian housing assistance, not a consumer military discount. Confirm current programs at airbnb.org.
