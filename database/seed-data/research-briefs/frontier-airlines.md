# Frontier Airlines — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one round-trip Frontier itinerary (representative ~$79 base fare) for one active-duty traveler carrying **1 carry-on + 1 checked bag each way** (the bags, not the fare, are where the military benefit lands on an ultra-low-cost carrier). Bag fees are dynamic/route-priced on Frontier; figures below use representative at-booking prices and are labeled as such. Effective prices exclude government taxes/fees except where noted.

## Headline finding

Frontier Airlines has **no year-round military or veteran fare discount** — its own FAQ states plainly that "Frontier Airlines does not offer military discounts" on fares. What it offers instead is a genuine, valuable **military baggage waiver**: active-duty U.S. military (and accompanying spouse/children on the same itinerary) get **1 free personal item, 1 free carry-on, and 2 free checked bags — at up to 70 lb each — with oversize/overweight fees waived**, on presentation of a CAC or Armed Forces ID at the airport. On a bag-fee-driven ULCC where a single checked bag can run $50–$100+, that waiver is often worth more than a modest fare discount would be. Separately, Frontier runs a **recurring seasonal Veterans Day fare promo** ("VETS" promo code, historically 50%–up to 100% off *base fares only*) — a limited-time public/military promotion, **not** an evergreen discount. This is the airline **bag-waiver archetype**: the win is free/heavier bags for active duty on any ticket, not a percentage off the seat. Veterans (separated, not active) and retirees get the promo when it runs, but **not** the standing bag waiver.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The bag waiver is fare-agnostic** — it applies to "all types of tickets," so it layers on any fare, including a VETS-promo fare. The waiver affects **bags**, the promo affects the **base fare**; they don't compete.
2. **The waiver is not addable online** — bags must be **checked at the airport counter** with ID presented; if you pre-pay bags online you're paying standard fees. (Confirm current mechanic at check-in — Frontier states benefits are "eligibility-based exceptions.")
3. **VETS promo is base-fare only** — never discounts government taxes or carrier-imposed fees, and carries round-trip, advance-purchase, blackout, and eligible-travel-day restrictions that change each run.
4. **No GovX/cashback-portal stack on fares** — Frontier fares aren't a GovX-marketplace or Rakuten-style path here; the military value is the bag waiver, not a portal rebate.
5. **Family eligibility is conditional** — spouse/children get the bag waiver **only** when the active-duty member is present and on the **same itinerary**; there is no standalone dependent or veteran bag waiver.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty U.S. military** — eligible for the bag waiver (CAC or Armed Forces ID)
- **Reserve / National Guard** — ID card lists reserve/guard affiliation as valid for the bag benefit; confirm current acceptance at the counter
- **Accompanying spouse / children** — eligible for the bag waiver **only** when the active-duty member is present and on the same itinerary
- **Military retirees** — eligible for the VETS fare promo when it runs; the standing bag waiver is written around **active** duty (verify retiree bag acceptance at counter)
- **Veterans (separated)** — eligible for the VETS promo in runs that include veterans; **not** for the standing active-duty bag waiver
- **First responders / teachers / nurses / students** — no Frontier program found

### How To Redeem Online / In Store
**Bag waiver (at the airport):**
1. Book your Frontier fare as normal — do **not** pre-pay for bags online (the waiver isn't addable online).
2. At the airport, go to the Frontier check-in/bag-drop counter.
3. Present your valid/unexpired **CAC** or **Armed Forces of the U.S. ID card**.
4. The agent waives fees for up to **2 checked bags (70 lb each)**, your carry-on, and personal item. Accompanying spouse/children on the same itinerary are covered while you're present.

**VETS fare promo (online, when live):**
1. Go to flyfrontier.com during the promo window (typically Veterans Day season).
2. Search an eligible **round-trip** itinerary on qualifying travel days.
3. Enter promo code **VETS**; the discount applies to the **base fare only** (taxes/fees still apply). Mind blackout dates and advance-purchase rules.

### How Verification Works
There is no third-party portal (no ID.me/SheerID/GovX) for the bag waiver — verification is **in person at the airport** by presenting a valid CAC or Armed Forces of the U.S. ID card. Frontier calls the waivers "eligibility-based exceptions": if the ID/eligibility requirements aren't met, standard bag fees apply. The VETS promo is a self-applied online code with its own eligibility terms per run.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- Bag waiver is for **active** U.S. military; family only when the member is present on the same itinerary.
- All bags must still comply with Frontier's size/weight acceptance limits (waiver raises the checked-bag limit to 70 lb and waives oversize/overweight fees on the 2 free bags).
- Bag waiver must be claimed **at the airport** — pre-paying bags online forfeits it.
- VETS promo: **base fare only**, never taxes or carrier-imposed fees; round-trip and advance purchase required; blackout dates and eligible-travel-day limits apply; terms change each run.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Active duty: always use the bag waiver** — on a ULCC it's the single biggest saving (~$100–$180+ round trip vs. paying for a carry-on and checked bag).
- **Buy any non-waived bags at booking, never at the gate** — Frontier's gate bag prices are far higher than at-booking.
- **Watch the Veterans Day VETS promo** — the only Frontier fare discount military travelers get; stack it with the bag waiver if you're active duty.
- **Frontier Miles / Discount Den membership / GoWild pass** — loyalty programs open to everyone, unrelated to military status, can beat a base fare on frequent flying.
- **Compare other carriers' military bag policies** — Delta, American, United, Alaska, and Southwest each publish their own active-duty free-bag rules (often more generous on orders); price the whole trip, not just the fare.

### FAQ

#### Does Frontier offer a military discount?
Not on fares year-round — Frontier's help center states it "does not offer military discounts" on airfare. Active-duty members do get a **baggage waiver** (free carry-on, free personal item, and 2 free checked bags up to 70 lb with oversize/overweight fees waived), and Frontier runs a seasonal Veterans Day "VETS" fare promo.

#### How much is the Frontier military discount?
There's no standing percentage off fares. The value is in **waived bag fees** for active duty (often worth $100–$180+ round trip on a ULCC). The Veterans Day VETS promo has historically been 50% up to 100% off **base fares only**.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Spouses and children qualify for the **bag waiver only** when the active-duty member is present and on the same itinerary. Separated veterans do **not** get the standing bag waiver, but can use the VETS fare promo in runs that include veterans. Retirees get the promo; verify retiree bag acceptance at the counter.

#### How do I verify my status?
At the airport counter, by presenting a valid/unexpired **CAC** or **Armed Forces of the U.S. ID card**. There's no online verification portal for the bag benefit.

#### Does Frontier use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
No. The bag waiver is verified in person with military ID at the airport; the VETS fare promo is a self-applied online code. No third-party identity provider is involved.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
The **bag waiver is airport-only** — don't pre-pay bags online or you forfeit it. The **VETS promo is online** at flyfrontier.com during its window.

#### What is excluded?
The bag waiver excludes bags beyond acceptance limits and non-eligible travelers. The VETS promo excludes government taxes and carrier-imposed fees, and carries round-trip, advance-purchase, blackout, and eligible-travel-day restrictions.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale fares?
Yes for the two military benefits: the **bag waiver applies to any fare**, including a VETS-promo fare or a sale fare, because it discounts bags, not the base fare.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to fly Frontier?
If you're active duty: book the lowest fare and use the **bag waiver** at the airport (biggest saving). During Veterans Day season, add the **VETS** promo to the base fare. Buy any non-waived bags at booking, not the gate.

#### Does Frontier offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
No dedicated Frontier program for those groups was found.

#### Does Frontier run a Veterans Day sale?
Yes, historically — the **VETS** promo around Veterans Day (e.g., 50% off base fares in 2024; a separate run up to 100% off select travel days). It's seasonal and terms change each year; it is not an evergreen discount.
