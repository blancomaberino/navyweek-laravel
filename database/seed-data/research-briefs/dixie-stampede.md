# Dolly Parton's Stampede (formerly Dixie Stampede) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** two adult + two child tickets to the Pigeon Forge, TN show (Regular Experience, adult $69.99 / child $34.99 = **$209.96 + tax**).

## Headline finding

Dolly Parton's Stampede (the dinner-attraction show in Pigeon Forge, TN and Branson, MO; **rebranded from "Dixie Stampede" in 2018**; operated by Herschend, the same company behind Dollywood) **does offer a military rate**, but it is a **box-office / phone-only discretionary rate with no published amount**. The official FAQ confirms the rate exists and states it **cannot be booked online** — you must call the location directly (Pigeon Forge (865) 453-4400 or Branson (417) 336-3000). There is **no online verification provider** for this offer (no ID.me, SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute checkout path), and Stampede does **not publish a dollar amount or percentage**. Coupon aggregators claim "$10 off adult / $4 off child," but that figure is **not confirmed by any official source** and should be treated as unverified. Archetype: **local attraction / experiential venue with a discretionary box-office military rate** — the discount lives at the register, not on the website. The single best move: **call the box office, confirm the current rate and eligibility, and compare it against public online-advance and third-party reseller prices**, since Stampede's own web fares and package deals sometimes beat a modest phone discount.
