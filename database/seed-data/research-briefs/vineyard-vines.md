You verify once through SheerID (takes a few hours) and get a **one-time-use 15% code by email**; in stores you just show a military ID at checkout. It's valid on vineyardvines.com, at Vineyard Vines retail stores, and by phone (800.892.4982) — but **not at outlet stores**. That's the honest everyday answer, and for a **full-price** item it's the single best standing route. The same 15% runs as a multi-tier SheerID program: **first responders, healthcare workers, teachers, and students** each get 15% too.

The catch is that Vineyard Vines is a **frequent-sale preppy apparel brand**, and the 15% is **non-combinable and full-price-only** (merchandise only, no future price adjustment). Do **not** repeat the aggregator claims of "20–50% off military codes," a "20% welcome offer," or "GovX / ID.me verification" — the verified facts are **15% via SheerID** and a **10% email signup**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, brand + portal terms)
1. **The SheerID 15% is a single one-time code**, "valid for 15% off … limit of 1 code … every 30 days," and **cannot be combined with any other offer except a gift-with-purchase promotion** (Vineyard Vines states this verbatim). One code per order.
2. **It's full-price / merchandise only** — "applies to merchandise only" (not shipping, taxes, or packaging) and purchases are "not eligible for future price adjustment." In practice this means it **does not stack on sale/clearance** items, so a deep public sale and the 15% are mutually exclusive — pick the lower.
3. **The 10% email signup and the SheerID 15% won't combine** — they're both order-level offers; you use one.
4. **Rakuten 2% (as of Jul 10, 2026) tracks on no-code orders.** Using a SheerID or promo code typically **voids or reduces** portal cashback, so don't assume "15% code + Rakuten 2%." A plain sale purchase with **no code** is the portal-safe way to add the 2%.
5. **Outlet stores are excluded** from the discount entirely — the code only works on vineyardvines.com, at full-line retail stores, and by phone.
