# AutoZone Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

AutoZone offers a **10% discount on in-store purchases** to active and retired members of the U.S. Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Space Force, Army National Guard, Coast Guard, and Air National Guard, **and their dependents**. This is a **published corporate program** — AutoZone hosts an official Military Discount Program landing page stating the terms — not merely store-manager discretion. There is **no third-party verifier** (no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX gate): you simply tell an AutoZoner you qualify and show a military ID or other accepted official document, and they apply the discount at the register. The single most important caveat: **the discount is in-store only — it does NOT apply to AutoZone.com or the AutoZone App** — and documentation may be requested for each purchase.

> Accuracy note: Some aggregator pages frame AutoZone's military discount as informal or "manager's discretion." That is **outdated/incorrect** — AutoZone publishes the 10% program on its own site (autozone.com/lp/military-discounts). Separately, some pages list AutoZone "first responder," "nurse," or "online" military discounts; AutoZone's official page does **not** state any of those, so they are flagged as unverified below.

### How To Redeem Online
AutoZone's military discount **cannot be redeemed online.** The official page states it "does not apply to purchases on AutoZone.com or through the AutoZone App." For online savings, use AutoZone's standard promo codes/coupons and AutoZone Rewards instead (see Best Ways To Save More).

### How To Redeem In Store
1. Shop in person at any U.S. AutoZone store.
2. Before the AutoZoner finalizes your order, tell them you qualify for the military discount.
3. Show a valid military ID or another accepted official document (DoD ID, Uniformed Services ID, Veteran ID Card, Veteran Health ID Card, veteran-designated state ID/driver's license, DD214, or DoD dependent ID / DD form 1173 or 1173-1).
4. The AutoZoner applies **10%** off your eligible items.
5. Expect to show documentation again on future visits — it may be requested for each purchase.

### How Verification Works
There is **no third-party verification service** and **no account to create**. AutoZone uses simple in-store proof: you state that you qualify and present a military ID or one of the accepted official documents to an AutoZoner, who applies the discount at the point of sale. AutoZone notes documentation may be requested for each purchase or discount application, so carry acceptable ID whenever you plan to use it. (A WeSalute+ secure military ID may be accepted as proof in some cases — WeSalute lists AutoZone and suggests showing the WeSalute+ card — but AutoZone's own page does not name WeSalute, so don't promise it; bring a primary government/military document.)

### FAQ

#### Does AutoZone offer a military discount?
Yes. AutoZone has an official Military Discount Program: 10% off in-store purchases for active and retired service members (all U.S. branches, Guard, and Coast Guard) and their dependents.

#### How much is the AutoZone military discount?
10% off, applied to eligible in-store purchases.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and dependents qualify?
Yes. The program covers active-duty and retired members, and dependents are explicitly eligible. Accepted proof for veterans includes the Veteran ID Card, Veteran Health ID Card, a veteran-designated state ID or driver's license, or a DD214.

#### How do I verify my status?
There's no app or third-party service. In store, tell an AutoZoner you qualify and show a military ID or another accepted official document; they apply the discount at the register.

#### Does AutoZone use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
AutoZone's official military discount uses **none of these** — it's verified in store by showing your ID. (GovX sells discounted AutoZone gift cards, and WeSalute lists the offer, but neither is the verification route on AutoZone's own page.)

#### Can I use the AutoZone military discount online?
No. AutoZone states the discount does not apply to AutoZone.com or the AutoZone App — it's in-store only. For online savings, use AutoZone Rewards or current promo codes.

#### What documents does AutoZone accept?
A valid DoD ID, Uniformed Services ID, Veteran ID Card, Veteran Health ID Card, a state ID/driver's license designating veteran status, a DD214, or a DoD dependent ID (DD form 1173 or 1173-1). The list is "not limited to" these.

#### What is excluded?
The discount is in-store only, and "some restrictions may apply due to product availability or special pricing." AutoZone doesn't publish a full exclusion list, so confirm at the register.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes, sales, or AutoZone Rewards?
AutoZone doesn't state whether the military discount stacks with coupons, sale pricing, or Rewards. Don't assume it stacks — confirm in store.

#### Does AutoZone offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
AutoZone's official military discount page does not mention first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discounts. Some third-party sites claim a first-responder discount, but it isn't confirmed on AutoZone's official page — check with your local store.
