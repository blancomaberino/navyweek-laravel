## Executive Summary

JD Sports (US) does **not** publish a military or veteran discount on its own website (jdsports.com), and its third-party verification partner ID.me **explicitly states it is not aware that JD Sports offers a Military discount** — ID.me only provides cash back (Retro up to 1.5%, Sale Items up to 1.0%) for ID.me members. Separately, multiple military-discount aggregators (MilitaryBridge, SemperSave) report a **10% in-store-only** military discount with detailed eligibility/ID terms, and an outlet operator (Tanger) confirms at least one JD Sports store running a "Military Offer 10% off purchase — must show military ID" promotion. Because no JD Sports primary source confirms the 10% figure, the eligible groups, or the ID list, the discount must be presented as **store-/location-dependent and unverified**: shoppers should ask the specific JD Sports store whether it offers a military discount and bring a valid military ID. The single most important caveat: there is **no confirmed online military discount** at jdsports.com, and the 10% terms are aggregator-sourced, not brand-confirmed.

Because terms are not brand-published, treat these as best-effort steps:

4. Never email or hand over a photocopy of a CAC or government ID; present it in person only.

### FAQ

#### Does JD Sports offer a military discount?
JD Sports (US) does not advertise one on its website, and ID.me states it is not aware of a JD Sports military discount. Some directories report a 10% in-store discount, and at least one outlet store runs a "10% off, show military ID" promotion — but it is store-dependent and not brand-confirmed. Ask your local store.

#### How much is the JD Sports military discount?
Reportedly 10% off in-store, per military-discount aggregators and one outlet-store listing. JD Sports has not published this figure, so it should be treated as unconfirmed and may vary by location.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
Aggregators list active duty, veterans, retirees, Reserve/Guard, spouses/partners, and dependents as eligible. None of this is confirmed by JD Sports. Confirm with the specific store.

#### How do I verify my military status at JD Sports?
There is no online verification for a JD Sports military discount. Any in-store discount is verified by showing a valid military or veteran ID to the cashier at a participating store.

#### Does JD Sports use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
JD Sports (US) is on ID.me Shop for **cash back only** — ID.me explicitly says it is not aware of a JD Sports military discount. No SheerID, GovX, or WeSalute military discount is documented.

#### Can I use a JD Sports military discount online?
No confirmed online military discount exists at jdsports.com. ID.me members can earn cash back online, but that is not a military discount.

#### Is the JD Sports military discount the same as the UK Blue Light Card offer?
No. JD Sports UK runs a separate emergency-services/military scheme (e.g., Blue Light Card) on jdsports.co.uk. That does not apply to US stores or jdsports.com.

#### What's excluded from JD Sports discounts?
JD Sports has not published exclusions for a military offer. Its general US coupon exclusions cover select Nike, Jordan, adidas/Yeezy, Timberland 6-inch boots, Brooks, and others, plus gift cards.

#### Does JD Sports offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No such discounts are documented. ID.me's responder, medical, nurse, teacher, government, and student pages for JD Sports all show no offer.

#### Are there better military discounts for the same sneakers?
Yes. Foot Locker offers a documented 10% military discount online and in-store via SheerID, and Finish Line (owned by JD Sports) reports a 10% in-store military discount — both better-documented than JD Sports' own.
