### FAQ

#### Does Sony offer a military discount?
Yes — on **electronics.sony.com**, verified through **ID.me**, as part of Sony's "Special Group Offers" program. The listed value is **up to 10% off** for military and veterans.

#### How much is the Sony military discount?
**Up to 10%** on eligible items, per Sony's ID.me partner page. It's an "up to" discount applied per item, so some products are excluded. (Some coupon sites say 15%, but that isn't confirmed on Sony's or ID.me's primary listing.)

#### Do veterans qualify or only active duty?
Both — the offer is for the military community, and ID.me extends military verification to veterans, reserves, National Guard, and retirees. Verify your status in ID.me.

#### Does the Sony discount work on PlayStation?
No. The discount is for Sony electronics (TVs, cameras, headphones, audio). **PlayStation Direct** — consoles, games, and PlayStation Plus — has **no official military discount**.

#### Does Sony use ID.me or GovX?
Sony's own store discount uses **ID.me**. There's a separate GovX "PlayStation Store" marketplace listing, but that's a different channel and not Sony's electronics offer.

#### Can I stack the military discount with a sale?
Yes, on eligible items the group discount applies **on top of the current sale price**. It generally **cannot** be combined with other promo codes or coupons.

#### How do I verify my military status?
Select **"Verify with ID.me"** at checkout on electronics.sony.com, sign in or create a free ID.me account, and confirm your service status. You re-verify each transaction.

#### Is there a nurse, teacher, first-responder, or student discount?
Yes — Sony's ID.me program lists parallel **up-to-10%** offers for nurses, first responders, teachers, medical providers, and students.

#### What's the cheapest way for a service member to buy Sony?
Verify with ID.me and buy an **eligible item while it's on sale** so the 10% stacks on the reduced price. For PlayStation, there's no military rate — buy during seasonal sales or with discounted gift cards.

#### Does Sony Rewards give military points?
No. The legacy **Sony Rewards program closed December 31, 2024**. Today's loyalty is the PlayStation Visa card (5x at PlayStation/Sony Store) and the free PlayStation Stars program — neither is military-specific.
