# Kings Island — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **adult single-day general-admission ticket to Kings Island (Mason, OH), 2026 season** — **$90 gate price** (the park's stated non-discounted single-day rate). All effective prices are the base ticket price; Kings Island adds a per-order processing fee (~$9.99/transaction) and any applicable fees on top of every online path, so they cancel out of the ranking except where noted.

> This is **Kings Island (Mason, Ohio)** — a Six Flags / former Cedar Fair park now hosted at **sixflags.com/kingsisland** (the old visitkingsisland.com URLs 301-redirect there). It is **not** Kings Dominion (Doswell, VA), which runs a separate program at kingsdominion.com.

## Headline finding

Kings Island has a **real, first-party military/first-responder discount: a flat $45 single-day ticket, year-round, verified through ID.me**, and you can buy **up to 6 tickets** at that price for family and friends. Eligible groups (per Kings Island's own page) are **active military, retired military, veterans, and first responders, plus their immediate family**. It is **online-only** and **cannot be combined with any other offer, including sales**. That is the honest everyday answer, and ID.me is the confirmed verification partner — not GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute (though GovX separately *resells* Kings Island tickets as a marketplace).

The catch — and the whole point of this page — is that **$45 is also roughly the public online price**. Kings Island's regular "good-any-day" online single-day ticket has sold for **$45 base** in 2026 (versus $90 at the gate), and third parties (Tiqets $49, Sam's Club ~$46, Groupon $49) sit in the same band. So the year-round military ticket is best understood as a **stable $45 floor** that wins only when the public price has climbed above it — not a deep cut. The genuinely valuable military perks are the **free-admission events**: **free single-day admission on Memorial Day weekend (Fri May 22 – Mon May 25, 2026)** for anyone with a valid U.S. Military ID, and Kings Island's historical **Community Appreciation Days** (free admission for first responders, teachers, and healthcare workers on select late-summer days). **Archetype: E (multi-tier ID.me identity discount, no stacking/cashback game)** with a strong **C flavor** (the public price routinely matches the "discount"), plus a theme-park **free-admission appreciation** play that is the real prize. Ignore aggregator pages that frame the ID.me offer as a generic "$5 off" or imply stackable coupon codes — the verified mechanic is a **flat $45 ticket**, non-combinable.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **The military/first-responder ticket does not combine with anything** — Kings Island states it "cannot be combined with any other offers, including sales." No promo code, season-pass discount, or public sale stacks on the $45 ticket.
2. **The free Memorial Day admission is gate-redeemed with a military ID** — it is not a stackable coupon; family/friends tickets are a **separate online purchase** at the discounted rate.
3. **ID.me is a gated checkout** — cashback portals commonly stop tracking identity-verified orders, so treat "military ticket + Rakuten" as **not** stacking; use a portal only on an ordinary public online order if at all.
4. **GovX is a closed resale marketplace** — its member price is the price; you cannot add a Kings Island code or a portal on top.
5. **Season passes are a separate product** with no confirmed military discount — you don't stack the single-day military rate onto a pass.
6. **Base ITT/MWR / AFTA tickets are a separate military channel** — bought through the base office, not layered on the ID.me offer.

#### Does Kings Island offer a military discount?
Yes. Kings Island offers a **$45 single-day ticket year-round** for active and retired military, veterans, and first responders (plus up to 6 family/friends tickets), verified through **ID.me** — and **free admission on Memorial Day weekend** with a valid U.S. Military ID.

#### How much is the Kings Island military discount?
The year-round military/first-responder ticket is **$45** (plus fees) for a single day. On **Memorial Day weekend (May 22–25, 2026)**, admission is **free** with a valid U.S. Military ID.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Veterans qualify for the **$45 ticket via ID.me**. Immediate family is covered by the "up to 6 tickets" allowance. For the **free** Memorial Day entry, you must show a **valid U.S. Military ID** at the gate — a veteran without one should use the $45 online ticket instead.

#### Can I use the discount in stores or only online?
The **year-round $45 ticket is online only**. The **free Memorial Day admission** is redeemed **in person at the gate** with a military ID.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — it's a single, non-combinable price. Because $45 is often the same as the public online price, just compare: if a public sale, employer program, or base ITT ticket is cheaper, use that instead.

#### Does Kings Island offer a first responder, teacher, or healthcare discount?
