# Priceline — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **2-night mid-range hotel stay**, room subtotal **$320 pre-tax** ($160/night). Hotel taxes & occupancy fees (~15%) apply on top of every retail/OTA/military-channel row alike, so the comparison is on the pre-tax room subtotal. ## Headline finding

**Priceline (an online travel agency) does NOT offer its own military or veteran discount.** Priceline's help center answers the question directly, and ID.me confirms verbatim: *"We're not aware that Priceline offers Military discounts."* There is no ID.me/SheerID military checkout on priceline.com. What Priceline *does* do is **power American Forces Travel (AFT)** — the U.S. Department of Defense's official leisure-travel booking site — which offers eligible members **up to 40–60% off** hotels and cruises. That is the single best Priceline-related military path, but it is a **DoD MWR benefit gated by DoD eligibility verification**, not a discount you get on priceline.com. For everyone, Priceline's real everyday savings come from its **free VIP program** (four trip-based tiers, Express Deal coupons 5%→10%, "member deals" up to 50% off), **Express Deals** (opaque, unsold-inventory rates up to ~40–60% off), and comparison shopping — and for many stays, **booking the hotel's own government/military per-diem rate direct** beats any OTA because it stays refundable and earns hotel loyalty points/status. Archetype: **B (no first-party military route on its own site)** with the genuine military value living in a **separate DoD channel (American Forces Travel)**; secondary **I (travel/loyalty — status/coupons stack, opaque rates don't)** via Priceline VIP, plus a **WeSalute-negotiated private car-rental engine** (up to 40% off cars, paid membership).

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + channel terms)
1. **No Priceline military discount exists to stack** — the question is which *channel* wins, not how to layer a military code.
2. **American Forces Travel is a separate, closed DoD site** — you can't add a Priceline coupon, VIP tier perk, or (typically) a cashback portal on top; its price is its price. Payment via MILITARY STAR card is a perk, not a discount stack.
3. **VIP Express Deal coupons (5–10%)** apply to **Express Deal** bookings when signed in; they don't combine with the Best Price Guarantee refund and generally aren't stackable with other promo codes on the same booking.
4. **Best Price Guarantee excludes special/discounted fares** — "military, student, group … coupon, loyalty, or rewards" fares are **not** eligible for a price match, so an AFT or military-rate booking can't be BPG-matched.
5. **Cashback portals track standard, non-opaque, non-coupon bookings** — Express Deals, coupon-code orders, and gift-card payments commonly void portal tracking. Verify the live rate and terms before relying on it.
6. **WeSalute+ car engine is a closed private-rate channel** — no Priceline coupon or public deal stacks inside it (though WeSalute lets you compare it against Priceline's standard rate and pick the lower).

### FAQ

#### Does Priceline offer a military discount?
**No.** Priceline's own help center and ID.me both confirm there is no Priceline military or veteran discount at priceline.com. The real military value is **American Forces Travel**, the DoD's official leisure-travel site that Priceline powers.

#### How much is the Priceline military discount?
There is no Priceline military discount. Through **American Forces Travel**, DoD-eligible members can save **up to 40–60% off** hotels and **up to 80% off** cruises — but that's a DoD benefit, not a priceline.com rate.

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify for anything?
Not on priceline.com. For **American Forces Travel**, veterans with an **honorable or general-under-honorable-conditions discharge** (since July 16, 2021), plus eligible spouses and family with an ID card, qualify.

#### How do I verify my military status?
priceline.com has no military verification. **American Forces Travel** verifies through a **DoD record check** — last name, last 4 of SSN, and date of birth — not ID.me or SheerID.

#### Does Priceline use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Priceline uses **none** of these for a military discount (it has none). **WeSalute** independently runs a private Priceline **car-rental** engine (up to 40% off) for paid WeSalute+ members. American Forces Travel uses a **DoD** eligibility check.

#### Can I get a military rate in the Priceline app or only on American Forces Travel?
The military rates are on **americanforcestravel.com**, a separate DoD site. The regular Priceline app/site has no military rate — only its public VIP and Express Deals savings.

#### What is excluded?
Everything military at priceline.com (none exists). On American Forces Travel, standard booking rules apply. Priceline's **Best Price Guarantee explicitly excludes military, student, group, coupon, and loyalty fares** from price matching, and Express Deals are non-refundable.

#### Can I combine a military rate with Priceline coupons or cashback?
No. American Forces Travel is a separate closed DoD site — Priceline coupons, VIP perks, and cashback portals don't stack on it. On priceline.com, Express Deal coupons apply to Express Deals but not on top of a military/special fare.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to book travel?
If you're **DoD-eligible, book on American Forces Travel** (up to 40–60% off). Otherwise, sign into **free Priceline VIP**, compare **Express Deals** against **booking the hotel's own military/per-diem rate direct** (which stays refundable and earns points), and check a cashback portal on standard bookings.

#### Does Priceline offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No. Priceline publishes no first-responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount, and American Forces Travel is limited to the DoD community. Use the public VIP/Express Deals savings.

#### Does Priceline run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day military sale?
No Priceline.com military-specific holiday promo was documented this pass. American Forces Travel runs year-round for eligible members; Priceline's public seasonal sales are open to everyone. Re-check before each holiday.
