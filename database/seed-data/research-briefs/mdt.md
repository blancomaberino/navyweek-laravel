### FAQ
#### Does MDT offer a military discount?
Yes — 10% off for military and law enforcement. Email support@mdttac.com and they'll send you a discount code.

#### How much is the MDT military discount?
10% off your purchase, per MDT's help center (last updated March 4, 2026). Claims of 25–30% military discounts on coupon sites are not supported by MDT.

#### Do veterans qualify for the MDT discount?
MDT's published policy just says "Military and Law Enforcement" without defining the groups. Veterans should email support@mdttac.com and ask — the policy doesn't exclude anyone, but it doesn't promise either.

#### How do I verify my status with MDT?
By email. MDT doesn't use ID.me, SheerID, or GovX — support issues the code directly and will tell you what proof they need.

#### Can I use the MDT military discount in stores?
MDT's policy covers the emailed code, redeemed at mdttac.com checkout. In-store use isn't addressed in the published policy.

#### Can I combine the military discount with sales or promo codes?
Not stated by MDT, and checkout codes typically don't stack. During the annual Black Friday sale, compare the sale price against your 10% code and use whichever is lower — don't count on both.

#### When does MDT go on sale?
Once a year: Black Friday. MDT says everything on the site gets discounted at varying levels, plus factory seconds and overstock are listed.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy an MDT chassis?
Most of the year: the emailed 10% code (≈$405 on a $449.99 ORYX). In late November: whichever is lower of Black Friday pricing/factory seconds or the code. Also spot-check Brownells/OpticsPlanet promos on your SKU.

#### Does MDT offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Law enforcement is covered alongside military. Fire/EMS, teachers, nurses, and students have no published MDT program.

#### Does MDT sell on GovX or through the Exchange?
No GovX storefront or exchange listing was found as of July 14, 2026.
