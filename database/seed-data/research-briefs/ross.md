# Ross Dress for Less — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$50 in-store apparel cart** at Ross Dress for Less (pre-tax; representative small everyday off-price basket).

## Headline finding

**Ross Dress for Less does NOT offer a military or veteran discount** — not in-store, and there is no online store at all. This is confirmed by Ross's own Contact Us / FAQ (which documents its programs and states merchandise "cannot be purchased online") and by **ID.me**, whose Ross page states plainly: *"We're not aware that Ross offers Military discounts."* The only structured discount Ross runs is the **55+ "Every Tuesday" senior discount: 10% off in-store purchases on Tuesdays for customers age 55 or older** — that is an **age-based** program, not a military one, and most active-duty shoppers won't qualify. The genuine savings at Ross come from its **off-price business model itself** (deep-discount closeouts/overstock already 20–60% below department-store prices), disciplined **clearance/markdown timing**, and — for those 55+ — stacking a Tuesday visit. Any "Ross 10% military discount, year-round" you see on coupon/aggregator sites is **fabricated or a conflation of the senior program** — debunk it. Archetype: **B (no military route at all)** with a **J (unofficial / individual-store manager's-discretion)** tail — a manager *may* knock a little off if asked, but it is never guaranteed and not a program.

### FAQ

#### Does Ross Dress for Less offer a military discount?
**No.** Ross does not offer a military or veteran discount. Its customer-service FAQ lists no military program, and ID.me states it is "not aware that Ross offers Military discounts." There is also no online store where a military code could apply.

#### Is there a "Ross 10% military discount" I've seen online?
That claim is **not accurate.** The only 10% Ross runs is its **age-55+ Tuesday senior discount**, which some websites appear to have mislabeled as "military." There is no verified military discount, code, or online checkout at Ross.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents get a discount at Ross?
**No** — there is no veteran, spouse, or dependent military discount. They would qualify for the 55+ Tuesday discount only if they meet the age requirement.

#### Does Ross have a senior discount, and how does it work?
**Yes.** Customers **age 55 or older** get **10% off in-store on Tuesdays** ("Every Tuesday Club"). Sign up at the Customer Service Desk, tell the cashier before paying, and show ID if asked. Some brands/items are excluded.

#### Does Ross use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** Ross has no military-verification partner because it has no military discount, and it has no online store or marketplace storefront.

#### Can I use a Ross discount online?
**No.** Ross **does not sell merchandise online** — all shopping is in physical stores. Any "Ross online promo code" is invalid.

#### Will a Ross store give me a discount if I show my military ID?
There's **no guarantee.** Ross has no policy for it. A few individual stores may offer a small courtesy at a manager's discretion, but it's informal and not something to count on.

#### Does Ross run a Veterans Day or Military Appreciation Month sale?
Ross has **no documented military-specific sale.** It has publicly recognized Military Appreciation Month as a thank-you, but that's acknowledgment, not a discount. General seasonal markdowns are open to all shoppers.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to shop Ross?
Ross's already-low off-price tags are the deal — there's no military discount to add. If you're 55+, shop Tuesdays for 10% off. If you specifically want a *military* discount, buy the item at a retailer that offers one (many verify via ID.me) or tax-free at your exchange.

#### Where can military shoppers get a real discount on similar items?
Brands like Nike, Under Armour, Columbia, and Merrell offer verified 10–20% military discounts via ID.me; the military exchanges sell comparable apparel and home goods **tax-free**. These are guaranteed routes Ross can't match on the "military" angle.
