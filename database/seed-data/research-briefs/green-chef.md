# Green Chef — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one first Green Chef box — a 3-recipes / 2-servings plan at a **~$100 full box price** (pre-discount, before shipping), the plan most new subscribers start on.

## Headline finding

Green Chef (a HelloFresh-owned organic meal-kit brand) runs a **real, official military discount, delivered through identity verification (ID.me, historically also SheerID)** rather than a public code. As accessed on July 20, 2026, the ID.me storefront states the offer as **55% off your first box, then 15% off the next 51 boxes** for verified military members. Note that this is a *new-customer, auto-renewing-subscription* promotion — its headline percentage tracks Green Chef's general new-customer promo and **moves up and down over time** (aggregators variously claim 60%, 65%, and 70%; those are inflated or stale). The single best path for a first order is the **verified military offer stacked with free shipping on box one** when Green Chef is running that; the discount does not help existing subscribers beyond the 15% ongoing tail. Archetype: *meal-kit acquisition discount* — the number is a customer-acquisition lever, not a fixed veteran benefit.

**Verdict logic:**
- **New customer + verifiable military status →** ID.me military offer (55% + 15% tail); check whether a public promo momentarily beats it and pick the higher first-box number.
- **New customer, can't verify →** public new-customer promo + a cashback portal (Rakuten ~$15 flat).
- **Existing/returning subscriber →** the discount rarely applies; pause-and-return or a win-back email offer is usually your only lever.
- **One-line chooser:** *"Are you a new customer? → verify with ID.me and compare its first-box price to today's public promo, take the lower. Staying long-term? → the 15% military tail is the reason to verify."*

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **One promotion per order.** The military offer **cannot be combined** with other Green Chef promo codes — you pick the single best first-box deal, not a stack of codes.
2. **Cashback portals (Rakuten) sit outside the code** and generally still pay on a subscription order — that's the only legitimate "stack" (code OR public promo, *plus* portal cashback). Confirm the portal's current rate and hold-time; meal-kit cashback is frequently a flat dollar bounty, not a percentage.
3. **New customers only** for the headline percentage; the 15% is the recurring tail, not a first-box booster.
4. **Auto-renewing subscription required** to unlock the promo — you must start (and can later cancel) a subscription; it is not a one-off box discount.

#### How much is the Green Chef military discount?
As accessed July 20, 2026, 55% off box one and 15% off the following 51 boxes. This headline number tracks Green Chef's rotating new-customer promo and can be higher or lower on any given day — the figure at verification is what governs.
