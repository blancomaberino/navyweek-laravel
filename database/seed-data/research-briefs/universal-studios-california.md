# Universal Studios Hollywood — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **adult 1-Day General Admission** ticket to Universal Studios Hollywood (date-priced; roughly **$109–$189** at the gate depending on visit date).

> **Scope note:** This brief covers **Universal Studios Hollywood** (California) only. Universal runs a **structurally different** program at **Universal Orlando Resort** (the ITT-only *2026 Military Freedom Pass*, $220/$255) — see NavyWeek's [Universal Studios](/discount/universal-studios-military-discount/) and [Universal Orlando](/discount/universal-orlando-military-discount/) briefs. Do not conflate the two: none of Orlando's Freedom Pass terms apply in California, and vice-versa.

## Headline finding

**Yes — Universal Studios Hollywood has a real, officially published military discount**, with two channels that don't stack: an **online discount via ID.me** and a usually-deeper **on-base MWR/ITT** channel. Online (after ID.me verification) the published savings are modest and flat: **$4 per person off a 1-Day General Admission, $4 off Universal Express, and $20 per pass off the California Neighbor Pass or Silver Annual Pass**, plus savings on Halloween Horror Nights and Fan Fest Nights. The genuinely better deal for most eligible members is buying **through a base MWR/ITT ticket office**, where Universal sells discounted **1-Day General Admission and a 12-Month Military Pass** at prices that typically beat the $4 online cut. Eligibility is broad — **Active Duty, National Guard, Reservist, eligible Veteran, or Retired member** of any U.S. branch — but the discount is **never sold at the park ticket booth**. Archetype: **theme park with a verified online micro-discount plus a deeper closed on-base channel** — the honest advice is *skip the $4 online cut and price the MWR/ITT ticket first.*

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Military pricing is not combinable with other offers or promo codes** — the online military ticket is a single, ID.me-gated price, not a code you layer on a promo ticket.
2. **The online and on-base channels are alternatives, not a stack** — you buy through one or the other.
3. **No cashback portal applies** — the ID.me identity gate breaks portal (Rakuten/TopCashback) tracking, and admission tickets aren't a portal-eligible category here.
4. **GovX is a separate third-party marketplace**, not a stack on the Universal site — compare its net price independently.
5. **A valid military or U.S. Government ID is required** in addition to ID.me verification; the discount is tied to the verified person.

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to **universalstudioshollywood.com/military**.

### How Verification Works
Online, Universal Studios Hollywood uses **ID.me** — you're redirected to ID.me (a third-party service governed by its own terms) to confirm you're Active Duty, Guard, Reserve, an eligible Veteran, or Retired before the military price appears. On base, MWR/ITT offices verify your military/U.S. Government ID directly. Either way, a **valid military or U.S. Government ID is required**, and the discount is not available at the park ticket booth.

### FAQ

#### Does Universal Studios Hollywood offer a military discount?
Yes. Verify with ID.me online to save $4 per person on 1-Day General Admission (and Universal Express) or $20 per pass on the California Neighbor or Silver Annual Pass. Base MWR/ITT offices sell discounted admission and a 12-Month Military Pass, usually a deeper deal.

#### How much is the Universal Studios Hollywood military discount?
Online it's a flat **$4 per person off 1-Day GA and Express**, and **$20 per pass** off the Neighbor or Silver Annual Pass. On-base MWR/ITT pricing is set by each office and is typically deeper.

#### Do veterans and retirees qualify?
Yes — Universal lists "**eligible Veteran**" and "**Retired member**" among qualifying groups for the online ID.me discount, alongside Active Duty, Guard, and Reserve. On-base purchase depends on your base shopping privileges.

#### How do I verify my status — does Universal use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID?
Universal Studios Hollywood uses **ID.me** for the online military discount. GovX lists Universal as a third-party marketplace seller (separate). The park does not use SheerID for this offer.

#### Can I use it at the ticket booth?
No. The military discount is **not available at the Universal Studios Hollywood ticket booth** — you must buy online (ID.me) or on base.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy?
If you can shop at a **base MWR/ITT office**, that's usually the deepest ticket price (and the only 12-Month Military Pass source). Otherwise verify online with ID.me — but compare Universal's public multi-day promo tickets, which sometimes beat the $4 military cut.

#### Does Universal Studios Hollywood offer a first responder, teacher, or nurse discount?
The official Hollywood military page covers military groups only. Any first-responder/teacher/nurse offer would appear separately on ID.me — verify there.

#### Is there a Universal Orlando military deal too?
Yes, but it's completely different — Orlando's ITT-only **2026 Military Freedom Pass** ($220 2-park / $255 3-park). See our Universal Orlando brief; none of its terms apply in California.
