# Fleet Feet — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 20, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **$140 pair of full-price running shoes** (the core Fleet Feet purchase after a gait fitting).

## Headline finding

Fleet Feet — the specialty run-shoe retailer built on in-store gait fittings and a franchise-heavy footprint — offers a **10% military (and law-enforcement) discount on full-priced items, redeemed in-store with a valid ID**. This is confirmed on an official Fleet Feet store page (Augusta franchise's "Military & Law Enforcement Personal Rewards Program"). Because Fleet Feet is a **franchise system, the program is administered store-by-store**: the 10% in-store benefit is common but each location sets its own participation, and there is **no single chain-wide corporate military-discount terms page**. An online 10% path via **SheerID** is widely reported by directories but is **not confirmed on Fleet Feet's own corporate site** — treat it as store/promo-dependent, not a guaranteed national online offer. Archetype: *local specialty retailer, in-store ID discount* — the real value is 10% off full price at the register, and the bigger savings lever is Fleet Feet's own rewards/loyalty program plus outlet/clearance shoes.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Not combinable in-store.** The military/LE 10% **cannot be combined with other discounts** — it applies to full-priced items only, so it never stacks on sale merchandise.
2. **Full-price only.** Sale/clearance shoes are excluded from the 10% — meaning a clearance price and the military discount are mutually exclusive; compare the two totals.
3. **Franchise discretion.** Participation, ID types accepted, and any online option are set per store — confirm with your local Fleet Feet before relying on it.
4. **Rewards points** generally accrue regardless and are the one loyalty lever that layers onto a purchase.

### FAQ
#### Does Fleet Feet offer a military discount?
Yes — 10% off full-priced items for military and law-enforcement personnel, redeemed in-store with a valid ID, per Fleet Feet's own store pages.

#### How much is the Fleet Feet military discount?
10% off full-priced merchandise. On a $140 shoe that's $14 off ($126). It doesn't apply to sale items.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify?
It varies by franchise. Active-duty and law enforcement are consistently covered; veteran, spouse, and dependent eligibility differs by store — confirm with your local Fleet Feet.

#### How do I verify my status?
In-store, show a valid Military, EMS, or Peace Officer ID. Any online path uses SheerID, but that isn't confirmed on Fleet Feet's corporate site.

#### Does Fleet Feet use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, WeSalute, or VerifyPass?
In-store is manual ID verification. Online, directories reference SheerID; Fleet Feet is not sold through GovX or the Exchange.

#### Can I use it in stores or online?
In-store is confirmed. Online is store/promo-dependent and not corporately guaranteed.

#### What is excluded?
Sale and clearance items, and any stacking with other discounts.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
No — the 10% applies only to full-priced items and can't be combined. Compare it against the clearance price instead.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Fleet Feet?
If you'll take a last-season colorway, clearance usually beats the 10%. For the newest model, use the in-store 10% with your ID and join Rewards for points.

#### Does Fleet Feet offer a first responder, teacher, nurse, or student discount?
Law enforcement/EMS are covered by the same in-store program. Fleet Feet has also run student discounts; teacher/nurse offers vary by store and season.
