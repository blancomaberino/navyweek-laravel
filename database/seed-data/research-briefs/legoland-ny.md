# LEGOLAND New York — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 18, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **family of four visiting LEGOLAND New York (Goshen, NY)** — measured two ways: (a) a **single day** of admission, and (b) a **12-month annual pass** for a family that will return, since the pass math is what actually moves the needle for a LEGOLAND-shaped attraction.

## Headline finding

**LEGOLAND New York does have official military discounts, and they run through two separate channels — verify carefully before you assume a flat number.** Park **ticket** discounts are gated through **ID.me**, where LEGOLAND New York Resort lists **military members "save up to 35%"** (and nurses, first responders, teachers, government employees, and medical providers "save up to 20%"). The **LEGOLAND Hotel** offer is separate: **"up to 25% off" room rates** for U.S. Military, Veterans, and their families. The **best total value for a returning family is almost always the annual pass, not a single-day ticket** — **GovX** (the military marketplace) advertises **"up to 40% on Annual Passes and Daily Tickets," annual passes "starting at $109,"** a **"2nd Day Free"** ticket promotion, and **up to 10% off the LEGOLAND NY Hotel**. Archetype: **theme-park / Merlin attraction — real, published military pricing, but the discount is a *tier* ("up to X%") not a fixed number, and it is delivered through third-party verification (ID.me) and a competing marketplace (GovX) rather than a single at-gate price.** Because the offers are "up to" tiers on variable-price date tickets, the exact dollar saving depends on your visit date; confirm the live price in your ID.me or GovX cart before buying.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The hotel military rate is explicitly non-combinable** — "cannot be combined with any [other offers]," requires full prepayment, and may carry a 2-night minimum on select dates. It does **not** stack with a ticket discount into one code; tickets and hotel are priced separately.
2. **ID.me ticket offers are own-the-offer, not stack-on-top** — you verify through ID.me and buy the discounted ticket; there is no verified path to add a public promo code on top of the ID.me military price.
3. **GovX is a closed marketplace** — you cannot layer a cashback portal, a LEGOLAND promo code, or an ID.me discount onto a GovX order. Treat it as a *substitute* channel and compare its net price to the ID.me price.
4. **ID.me vs GovX is either/or** — pick the cheaper cart for your dates; you can't run a ticket through both.
5. **Cashback portals are not a reliable stack here** — identity-gated (ID.me) and closed-marketplace (GovX) checkouts typically break portal tracking, so don't count on Rakuten-style cashback on top.
