# Trijicon — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Trijicon RMR® Type 2 RM06 3.25 MOA Adjustable LED red dot sight** — **$779 MSRP on trijicon.com**, ~**$569–$599 typical street price** at major optics retailers (the flagship pistol red dot; the model most "trijicon military discount" searchers are pricing).

## Headline finding

Trijicon (Wixom, MI) does **not publish a flat military-discount percentage** and has **no military verification at its own checkout**. Its official "Military / Law Enforcement / Government Programs" page instead routes qualifying shoppers to two **pro-purchase programs — ExpertVoice and Guidefitter** — for "special pricing on select optics," and offers a unit-level **Test & Evaluation (T&E)** program to military/LE agencies (not individuals). No exact percentage is stated anywhere on Trijicon's site. Aggregators' confident "**10%–25% military discount at trijicon.com**" claim is **unverified and misleading** — there is no such storefront discount. The genuine savings routes are the **gated ExpertVoice/Guidefitter MIL/LE pro price**, the **GovX** marketplace, and — critically — Trijicon's **periodic public $100 factory rebate** on RMR LED models plus **open street pricing** (~$569–$599) that on a hot commodity like the RMR frequently **matches or beats the gated pro price with no verification at all**. Archetype: **K (pro-deal brand — ExpertVoice/Guidefitter crush any public "military %")** + secondary **A (GovX marketplace as a substitute, no first-party checkout discount)**.

### How To Redeem — Guidefitter (pro program)
1. Create a **Guidefitter** account and apply as an Insider under your category (Military, First Responder, etc.).
2. Verify eligibility — active/reserve/Guard: current **LES** (≤2 months, PII can be redacted) or a **@mail.mil** email; veterans: confirm **Honorable / General-under-Honorable** discharge. Verification typically clears in **under 3 business days**.
3. Once verified, opt into the **Trijicon Pro Program** and shop the gated MIL/LE pricing on eligible optics (personal-use; acceptance into the brand program isn't guaranteed).

### How To Redeem — ExpertVoice (pro program)
1. Create an **ExpertVoice** account and verify your affiliation with a partner organization (military/LE among them).
2. Join the **Trijicon** brand store; complete short product quizzes to unlock top-tier pricing.
3. Buy at the pro price (personal-use; no public stacking).

### How To Redeem — GovX (marketplace)
1. Register **free** at GovX.com and verify your status (military, veteran, LE, fire, EMS, healthcare, or government).
2. Open the **Trijicon** storefront and buy at member pricing (closed marketplace; nothing else stacks).

### FAQ

#### Does Trijicon offer a military discount?
Not as a flat percentage at its own checkout. Trijicon's official page routes military and law enforcement to **ExpertVoice** and **Guidefitter** pro programs for special pricing on *select* optics, and it lists **GovX** as another verified route. There is **no "trijicon.com military discount code."**

#### How much is the Trijicon military discount?
Trijicon **publishes no percentage.** The MIL/LE pro pricing on ExpertVoice/Guidefitter is gated behind verification and varies by SKU. Coupon-site claims of "10%–25% off at trijicon.com" are unverified — and on popular items like the RMR, the open street price often matches the pro price anyway.

#### Do veterans, reservists, and National Guard qualify?
Yes. Guidefitter verifies veterans (Honorable / General-under-Honorable discharge) and active/reserve/Guard members (via LES or a @mail.mil email); ExpertVoice and GovX also cover these groups. Spouse/dependent eligibility isn't stated for the Trijicon program — verify.

#### How do I verify my status?
On **Guidefitter**, submit a current LES (≤2 months) or use a @mail.mil email; veterans confirm discharge status — usually verified in under 3 business days. **ExpertVoice** verifies partner-org affiliation. **GovX** runs its own free check. Verification never happens at Trijicon's own checkout.

#### Does Trijicon use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or ExpertVoice?
Trijicon uses **ExpertVoice** and **Guidefitter** for its MIL/LE pro pricing, and it's also carried on **GovX**. It does **not** use ID.me or SheerID at its own checkout (there's no military checkout there).

#### Can I use it in stores or only online?
The pro-program and GovX pricing are **online** (ExpertVoice, Guidefitter, GovX). Trijicon's direct site sells at MSRP; individual local dealers set their own prices and may or may not honor any program.

#### What is excluded?
There's no first-party discount to exclude items from. The pro programs cover **select optics** only and are personal-use; the **$100 RMR rebate excludes** Green Dot, Dual Illuminated, RMR HD, RMRcc, and RCR models. T&E is agency-only.

#### Can I combine the pro price with sales, codes, or rebates?
No. ExpertVoice/Guidefitter/GovX prices are closed and don't stack with retailer sales, public rebates, or cashback. The one stackable lever is the **$100 factory RMR rebate on a normal retail purchase** of an eligible LED model.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy a Trijicon RMR?
Buy an **eligible RMR LED model at open street price and file the $100 factory rebate during a rebate window** (~$469–$499 net) — a public offer open to everyone. Outside a rebate window, verify free on Guidefitter/ExpertVoice and GovX, then buy whichever price actually beats open street on your exact SKU.

#### Does Trijicon offer a first responder or law enforcement discount?
Yes — **Guidefitter** verifies LEO/retired LEO, Fire, EMS/EMT/Paramedic/RN, 911 dispatch, and Search & Rescue for the Trijicon Pro Program, and **GovX** covers the same groups. Agencies also have direct T&E and authorized-distributor channels.

#### Does Trijicon run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
No dedicated military-holiday discount was documented this pass. The recurring public lever is Trijicon's periodic **$100 RMR / $50 MRO factory rebate** (spring and fall windows) — open to all buyers. Watch MillionthRMR.com / Trijicon's promotions page.
