# Vortex Optics Military & Veteran Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Vortex Optics runs a standing military and law enforcement discount program — not a public consumer coupon, but an application/verification-gated "Vortex Discount Program." Eligible members get **40% off MSRP** on Vortex products (the only discount figure Vortex/its partners publish; confirmed in the official Scheels-channel block on Vortex's own page and on the co-branded Scheels page). Eligibility covers **current or retired US Military and Law Enforcement**, and — per the Scheels channel — **Service Members, Retirees, Veterans, and First Responders (Firefighters, Police Officers, EMTs, 911 Dispatchers)**. There is no single Vortex storefront discount; instead Vortex routes shoppers to four separate verified programs: **ExpertVoice**, **GovX**, **Scheels (via ID.me)**, and **AAFES (shopmyexchange.com)**. Each requires registering and verifying status with that provider before discounted pricing appears; there is no walk-in/public code. The single most important caveat: this is an **application-based, verification-gated pro-deal**, and the 40% figure is explicitly published only for the Scheels channel — Vortex does not publish a uniform percentage across ExpertVoice/GovX/AAFES on its own page, so the discount amount can vary by channel and by product.

### How To Redeem Online
ExpertVoice (Vortex's primary military/LE channel):
1. Go to Vortex's Military & Law Enforcement page and select ExpertVoice.
2. Sign up and follow the prompts; create your profile (include your shipping address).
3. Request to join your team (military/law enforcement).
4. Within 24–72 hours you'll get an email — click the link to verify your account.
5. Shop Vortex through ExpertVoice; verified pricing applies.

GovX:
1. Create a free GovX ID account and verify your affiliation.
2. Log in with your GovX ID wherever you see the GovX logo.
3. Shop the Vortex offer; you're automatically approved for discounts you've earned.

Scheels channel:
1. Log into your Scheels.com account.
2. Verify your Military/LEO status through ID.me (Military or First Responder).
3. Shop Vortex optics and accessories at 40% off MSRP.

AAFES:
1. Go to shopmyexchange.com and validate shopping eligibility.
2. Set up username/password and complete account creation.
3. Log in to purchase; military-exclusive, tax-free pricing applies.

### How Verification Works
Verification depends on the channel you choose:
- ExpertVoice: Create a profile and request to join the military/law enforcement team; account verification typically completes in 24–72 hours via an emailed link.
- GovX: Verify affiliation when creating a free GovX ID; that ID is reused across GovX partner offers.
- Scheels: Verify Military/LEO status through ID.me.
- AAFES: Validate shopping eligibility during account creation at shopmyexchange.com.

Account creation is required for all channels. Re-verification frequency is not stated by Vortex; verification providers (e.g., ID.me, GovX) may re-verify periodically per their own policies. For help, Vortex lists email info@vortexoptics.com and phone 1-800-486-7839 (department/agency inquiries: govsales@vortexoptics.com).

#### Do veterans, retirees, and first responders qualify?
Veterans and retirees are listed as eligible. First responders — firefighters, police officers, EMTs, and 911 dispatchers — are eligible through the Scheels channel. Source: Scheels + Vortex Discount Program page, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### How do I verify my status?
You verify through whichever program you choose: ExpertVoice (profile + team request, 24–72 hour email verification), GovX (free GovX ID), Scheels (ID.me), or AAFES (eligibility check at shopmyexchange.com). Account creation is required. Source: Vortex Military & Law Enforcement page, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Does Vortex use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
Vortex uses GovX (GovX ID) and ID.me (through the Scheels channel), plus ExpertVoice and AAFES. SheerID and WeSalute are not listed. Source: Vortex Military & Law Enforcement page, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Can I use the discount in stores?
No general in-store Vortex discount is stated. The program runs online through verified partner programs. Confirm any in-store option directly with the retailer. Source: Vortex Military & Law Enforcement page, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### What is excluded?
GovX requires purchases be for personal use, not resale. Other product/category exclusions, gift-card rules, and stacking limits are not stated by Vortex and are governed by each partner program's terms. Sources: GovX terms (via search excerpt); Vortex Military & Law Enforcement page, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or sale items?
Not stated by Vortex. Program (member) pricing is generally not combinable with other public promotions; confirm in each program's terms. Source: Vortex Military & Law Enforcement page, accessed June 23, 2026.

#### Does Vortex offer a teacher, nurse, government, or student discount?
Vortex's published program targets military and law enforcement (and first responders via Scheels). Teachers, nurses, and students are not stated as eligible.
