# Knott's Berry Farm — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **adult 1-Day general admission** to Knott's Berry Farm, Buena Park, CA — **gate price $110**; realistic public online-advance price runs **~$65–$79** (dynamic/date-based pricing). All comparisons are per single adult ticket.

## Headline finding

Knott's Berry Farm (operated by Six Flags Entertainment; discount page now lives at **sixflags.com/knotts/military-discounts**) has a **real, brand-run military/veteran/first-responder ticket discount, verified through ID.me** — discounted single-day admission (**from ~$57** this pass, vs. a $110 gate) with up to **6 tickets** for family and friends, redeemed **online only** and **not combinable** with any other offer or sale. That's the honest everyday answer and the route that works any operating day.

But the everyday ID.me ticket is **not** the deepest deal. Knott's runs two **calendar-driven appreciation programs that beat it**: (1) **Military Tribute Days** (Mondays–Thursdays in November and early December) at **$25 per person** for the service member and **up to 5 guests**, and (2) **Memorial Day weekend free admission** for active/retired/veteran military (May 23–25, 2026), with up to 6 guests at a reduced rate. First responders get their own **Fire & Law Tribute Days** at $25. Archetype: **dominant E — multi-tier ID.me identity discount** (brand-run, no coupon/cashback stacking game), with a strong **secondary H — calendar/event-driven appreciation days** where the real value lives. GovX also sells Knott's military/government tickets as an alternative marketplace (price gated behind a free login). Do **not** repeat aggregator "military promo code" claims — the discount is ID.me-gated, with no public code, and it never stacks with a sale.

## Stacking Rules (verified as far as sources allow)
1. **The ID.me military/veteran/first-responder ticket is a single, non-combinable discount** — Knott's states it "cannot be combined with any other offers, including sales." No public promo code stacks on it.
2. **Military Tribute Days ($25) and Fire & Law Tribute Days ($25) are standalone event programs** — you buy the event ticket; it is not layered on top of the everyday discount.
3. **Memorial Day free admission is a standalone appreciation offer** — the member is free; guest tickets are a separate reduced rate, not a stack.
4. **GovX is a separate marketplace** — its member price is the price; no knotts.com code or cashback stacks on top, and it is not the same as the on-site ID.me route.
5. **Tribute Days eligibility requires the service member present** — "Spouses and children are not eligible to purchase these tickets without the military member present."
6. **All offers are online-redeemed** for the year-round discount ("All offers must be redeemed online"); Tribute Days additionally allows park ticket booths / participating Military Ticket Offices.
