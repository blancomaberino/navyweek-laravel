# MLB.TV — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 12, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one MLB.TV Yearly (all-teams) subscription for the 2026 season (regular price $149.99).

## Headline finding

MLB.TV runs a **real, generous military discount: up to 35% off** the all-teams yearly subscription, verified and sold **through GovX** (there is an official MLB landing page at `mlb.com/live-stream-games/subscribe/military`, but the purchase and verification happen on GovX). On the full $149.99 regular price that lands the all-teams package near **$97.50** — a genuine ~$52 saving early in the season. **But the official discount is frequently not the best deal:** MLB drops the *public* seasonal price as the year progresses, and by July 12, 2026 the public all-teams price was already **$94.99** — below the military price. Because the GovX military price is a separate, non-combinable purchase channel pegged to the regular price, the calendar can beat it. **Verdict: buy through GovX early in the season (Feb–Apr) when the public price is full; from about mid-season on, price-check the public MLB.TV page — the in-season drop usually wins.** Archetype: **C (real discount, but the sale beats it)** on a **subscription (F)**, brokered through **GovX (A)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The military discount does not stack with MLB's public in-season price drops or ESPN Unlimited subscriber pricing.** GovX is a separate purchase channel; you either buy through GovX at the military price *or* buy direct at the public price — never both.
2. **The GovX 35% is off the regular price ($149.99), not the current promotional price.** As MLB steps the public seasonal price down through the year, the fixed military price can be undercut by simply buying direct — so the discount is time-sensitive, not always the floor.
3. **Only the yearly packages (all-teams and single-team) are in the military offer.** The $29.99 monthly plan is not discounted through GovX.
4. **Verification is per GovX account, not per purchase forever** — GovX may re-verify eligibility; the discount is not a permanent locked-in rate on renewal.
