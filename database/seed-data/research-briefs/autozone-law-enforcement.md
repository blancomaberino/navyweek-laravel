**Researched:** July 23, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** $150 in-store parts cart (a typical front-brake job: pads + rotors).

**Scope note:** This brief covers the **law enforcement / first responder** audience only. The military program is covered by the existing `research/discounts/autozone.md` brief (verified June 23, 2026; official page re-verified unchanged July 23, 2026).

## Headline finding

**AutoZone has no official law-enforcement or first-responder discount.** The company's only published identity discount is its Military Discount Program (10% in-store for active/retired military and dependents) — the official page names no law-enforcement, police, fire, EMS, nurse, or first-responder group, and no other autozone.com page publishes one. The best *verified* LE-specific path is **GovX: 5% off AutoZone gift cards** (GovX verifies federal/state/local law enforcement), usable online or in store and layerable under AutoZone's public promo codes. During sitewide promos (code SUMMERDEALS, 20% off $100+ ship-to-home, live at access), the public promo beats everything else outright. Archetype: **A (GovX-only — no first-party discount)** with secondary **J (community-reported in-store discretion, YMMV)**.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **Gift cards are a payment method, not a coupon** — a GovX-bought AutoZone gift card can pay for an order that also uses a public promo code. (GovX states the cards work online or in-store; AutoZone publishes no restriction on paying promo orders with gift cards. Flag: AutoZone's gift-card terms don't address it explicitly either way — stated as "no published restriction," not as a guarantee.)
2. **The military 10% does NOT extend to LE** — do not present the military program as reachable for first responders; eligibility is military ID / DoD documents only.
3. **GovX is a closed channel for the gift cards** — the 5% is GovX member pricing; no further code applies on the GovX purchase itself.
4. **SUMMERDEALS-type codes are ship-to-home online codes** — they do not apply in store, where the LE reader has no discount at all; that asymmetry is the page's key practical advice.

### FAQ

#### Does AutoZone offer a law enforcement discount?
No. AutoZone's only published identity discount is its military program (10% in-store for active/retired military and dependents). No official AutoZone page offers a discount to police or other first responders.

#### Does AutoZone offer a first responder discount?
No — not for firefighters, EMS, dispatchers, or corrections either. Claims of a "10% first responder discount" on coupon sites aren't confirmed by any AutoZone source.

#### Can police officers use the AutoZone military discount?
No. The military program's accepted documents are military IDs and DoD paperwork (DoD ID, Veteran ID Card, DD214, dependent ID). A police badge or department ID isn't on the list. If you're also a veteran, use your veteran documentation.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a cop to buy from AutoZone?
Verified LE members can buy AutoZone gift cards at 5% off through GovX and spend them online or in store. When AutoZone runs a public sitewide code (at our last check: 20% off ship-to-home orders over $100), that public deal beats the gift-card savings — and you can pay a promo order with the discounted gift cards.

#### Does AutoZone use ID.me, GovX, or SheerID for first responders?
AutoZone itself uses none of them — there's no first-responder program to verify for. GovX's role is separate: it sells discounted AutoZone gift cards to its verified members (military, LE, fire, EMS, and other public-safety groups).

#### Do some AutoZone stores give first responders a discount anyway?
Shoppers occasionally report a store manager extending the 10% as a courtesy at the register. That's individual store discretion, not policy — it isn't published anywhere, so don't count on it.

#### Does AutoZone run a First Responders Day promotion?
We found no history of one. AutoZone's promotions calendar is public sitewide codes, Rewards, rebates, and clearance — all open to everyone.

#### Is there a nurse, teacher, or government-employee discount?
No. None is stated on any official AutoZone page.
