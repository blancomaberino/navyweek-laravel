# NBA League Pass — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 13, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one full 2025–26-season subscription to NBA League Pass (standard tier) — full price $16.99/mo ($203.88 over 12 months) or $149.99 for the annual plan.

## Headline finding

Yes — the NBA runs a **real, official military discount on NBA League Pass: 30% off 12 months of League Pass *Monthly*, gated behind GovX verification.** But two honest caveats decide the page: (1) it is a **limited-time seasonal promo, not a permanent rate** — the most recent run **expired April 12, 2026**, and as of this research date (July 13, 2026, NBA offseason) there is **no active military code to redeem**; expect it to return for the 2026–27 season (~October 2026). (2) The military rate is **not the cheapest path.** The NBA's own **early-season annual promo (~$109.99, "over 40% off")** and the **student discount (40% off via UNiDAYS)** both beat the 30% military monthly rate over a full season. The military deal only wins when you want **month-to-month flexibility** (cancel after the playoffs) or you missed the early-bird annual window and aren't a student. Archetype: **F — time-boxed subscription discount with a bigger sibling offer** (the student 40%), with a strong **C flavor** (the public sale beats the military rate). This is an independent guide; NavyWeek is not affiliated with the NBA or GovX, and the NBA can change these terms at any time.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + portal terms)
1. **The GovX military code does not stack** with anything. It is a single-use code, applies **only to League Pass Monthly**, and "cannot be utilized or upgraded on any other subscription type" — so it can't be applied to the annual plan, Premium, Team Pass, or NBA TV.
2. **Military vs. student vs. early-bird annual are mutually exclusive** — one promotional path per subscription. You pick the lowest effective price; you never combine them.
3. **No cashback layer.** NBA.com subscriptions aren't reliably tracked by cashback portals, and a promo-code checkout would break tracking regardless. Treat all paths as substitutes, not stacks.
4. **No exchange / pro-deal path.** League Pass is a digital subscription; it is not sold tax-free at AAFES/NEX and has no ExpertVoice-style pro program.

### FAQ

#### Does NBA League Pass offer a military discount?
Yes — 30% off 12 months of NBA League Pass Monthly, verified through GovX. It's a seasonal promo, not a permanent rate; the most recent run expired April 12, 2026.

#### How much is the NBA League Pass military discount?
30% off the League Pass Monthly rate. On the $16.99/mo standard tier that's about **$11.89/mo (~$142.73 for 12 months)**.

#### Do veterans, retirees, and reservists qualify?
Yes. The GovX offer covers active duty, veterans, retirees, and reserve/National Guard, plus first responders, medical professionals, government employees, and educators. Spouse/dependent eligibility isn't stated in the terms.

#### How do I verify my status?
Through **GovX** — verify on the GovX NBA League Pass offer page, then receive a single-use promo code to enter on NBA.com.

#### Does NBA League Pass use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or UNiDAYS?
The **military** offer uses **GovX**. The **student** offer uses **UNiDAYS** (historically SheerID). ID.me is not the League Pass military verifier.

#### Can I use the military discount on the annual plan or Premium?
No. The code applies **only to League Pass Monthly** and "cannot be utilized or upgraded on any other subscription type."

#### Can I combine it with promo codes or the student discount?
No. It's a single-use code with no stacking. You pick one path — military, student, or the public early-bird annual price.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy NBA League Pass?
If you're a student, the **40% student plan (~$10.19/mo)** is cheapest. If not, the **early-season annual promo (~$109.99)** usually beats the 30% military rate. Use the GovX military monthly deal mainly for cancel-anytime flexibility.

#### Does NBA League Pass offer a first responder, teacher, or student discount?
Yes — first responders, medical professionals, government workers, and educators are eligible for the same 30% GovX offer, and enrolled students get a separate 40% plan via UNiDAYS.

#### Does the NBA run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day League Pass sale?
There's no separate holiday League Pass military sale distinct from the season-long GovX offer; the GovX 30% deal is the vehicle, tied to the season rather than a specific holiday.
