# Holland America Line — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 14, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one 7-day Alaska cruise, interior/oceanview stateroom for two, **$1,798 total fare** (mirrors the Princess Cruises brief baseline for cross-line comparison). Onboard credit (OBC) is folio spending money, not a fare cut, so "effective price" = fare minus benefit value.

## Headline finding

Holland America Line's military benefit is real, official, and modest: a **US$100 onboard credit per stateroom** on new bookings for **U.S. and Canadian active-duty, retired, and veteran** military guests, verified through **SheerID** ("Military Appreciation Offer," part of HAL's Community Appreciation program). It is **not a fare discount** — there is no Holland America military rate or percentage — and the credit is capped at **one per stateroom** (unlike sister line Princess, which grants $50–$250 **per qualified passenger** and states its OBC is combinable). Archetype: **I (travel/loyalty)** — the benefit layers onto whatever fare you book, so the winning play is the cheapest public/sale fare + verification before booking; secondary **flag**: HAL's offer terms don't state combinability with other OBC promos, so treat stacking beyond the fare itself as unconfirmed.

## Stacking Rules (verified, both brand + program terms)
1. **The OBC layers on the fare, not against other credits.** HAL's terms confirm the credit applies to a new booking at whatever fare you book; they do **not** state whether it combines with other promotional OBC (Have It All perks, casino offers, shareholder credit). Treat multi-OBC stacking as unconfirmed — Princess states combinability, HAL does not.
2. **One credit per stateroom, period.** Two veterans sharing a HAL stateroom still get $100 total (Princess would give each their own credit).
3. **The OBC can't pay casino charges, gratuities, or specified fees**, expires at cruise end, and has no cash value — spend it aboard or lose it.
4. **New bookings only.** Verify before you book; the benefit is applied to the reservation, not retro-fitted automatically to existing bookings (per HAL's "book a new cruise… ensure the verified benefit is applied" flow).
5. **Shareholder OBC has its own gate** (100+ CCL shares, Stockperks claim ≥3 weeks out, one per stateroom, no casino/gratuities) — its terms are Carnival Corp's, not HAL's military terms.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-duty (US/Canada)** — Eligible.
- **Retired military (US/Canada)** — Eligible.
- **Veterans (US/Canada)** — Eligible.
- **Spouses / dependents** — Not stated as independently eligible; the verified member must be on the booking.
- **Reserve / National Guard** — Not explicitly stated on the offer page; SheerID's military check typically covers them, but confirm during verification.
- **Non-US/Canada military** — Not eligible via this program.
- **Healthcare workers, first responders, teachers, AARP members** — Eligible for their own identical $100 OBC under Community Appreciation (one credit per stateroom).

### How To Redeem Online
1. Go to Holland America's Military Appreciation Offer page and click "Verify Your Status" (SheerID form).
2. Complete verification as active, retired, or veteran US/Canadian military.
3. Book a new cruise on an eligible sailing (directly or through your agent) and make sure the verified benefit is attached to the reservation.
4. Check your booking confirmation for the $100 onboard-credit line; spend it onboard before the cruise ends.

### How Verification Works
Holland America uses **SheerID**, not ID.me. You submit your status through HAL's secure verification form; once approved, the benefit can be applied to a new booking. Verify **before** booking — HAL's FAQ recommends it, and the offer is new-bookings-only. HAL does not state how long a verification remains valid, so re-verify if prompted on a later booking.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- One $100 credit **per stateroom**, regardless of how many qualified members share it.
- OBC is **non-refundable, non-transferable, no cash value**, expires at cruise end, and can't be used in the casino or on gratuities/specified fees.
- Select sailings; new bookings only; offer "may be modified or withdrawn without prior notice."
- No percentage-off military fare exists — ignore "HAL military rate" marketing.
- Combinability with other OBC promotions is not stated — confirm on your reservation.

### FAQ
#### Does Holland America offer a military discount?
Yes — a US$100 onboard credit per stateroom on new bookings for verified U.S. and Canadian active-duty, retired, and veteran military. It's spending money aboard the ship, not a reduced fare.

#### How much is the Holland America military discount?
$100 in onboard credit per stateroom, once per booking. There is no percentage off the cruise fare.

#### Do veterans qualify? Spouses?
Veterans qualify (US and Canada), alongside active-duty and retired members. Spouses and dependents aren't stated as independently eligible — the verified service member should be on the booking.

#### How do I verify — does Holland America use ID.me or SheerID?
SheerID. Use the "Verify Your Status" link on Holland America's Military Appreciation Offer page, then book a new cruise with the benefit applied.

#### Can I combine the military onboard credit with other offers?
HAL's terms don't say. The credit applies to a new booking at any eligible fare, but combinability with other onboard-credit promotions (including the Carnival Corp shareholder credit) is not stated — confirm on your reservation before counting on a double-post.

#### What can't the onboard credit be used for?
Casino play, gratuities, and other specified fees. It expires when the cruise ends and has no cash value.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to cruise Holland America?
Book the cheapest public/sale fare for your dates, with the $100 military OBC verified and attached — and add the shareholder credit if you own 100+ CCL shares. Never pick a pricier fare just to use the military offer; it attaches to cheap fares too.

#### Does Holland America offer first responder, teacher, or healthcare discounts?
Yes — the same $100 onboard credit per stateroom for healthcare workers/first responders/medical professionals and for active or retired teachers, plus AARP members, under its Community Appreciation Cruise Offers.

#### Is Princess or Carnival better for military cruisers?
Princess (same parent company) pays $50–$250 onboard credit **per qualified passenger** on every cruise and states it's combinable with other OBC offers — richer than HAL's $100 per stateroom, especially for two-veteran cabins. Carnival runs actual military fares. See our Princess and Carnival guides.
