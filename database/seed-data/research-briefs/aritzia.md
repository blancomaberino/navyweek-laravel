# Aritzia — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** a **$200 apparel cart** (e.g., one Babaton/Wilfred sweater + a tee). Illustrative sales tax at 7% ≈ $14 unless noted.

## Headline finding

Aritzia publishes **no military or veteran discount** — there is no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX military checkout on aritzia.com, and no in-store military program. It also runs **essentially no public promo codes** (SimplyCodes tracks zero official codes released in ~two years) and has been **removed from Rakuten** ("the store you're looking for is no longer available on Rakuten"), so the usual cashback layer is gone too. Aritzia's model is deliberately full-price: markdowns are funneled into a permanent **Sale** area (its in-house labels), and anything **50%+ off is final sale**. The only structured savings is the free, invite-based **Clientele** loyalty program (spend-triggered events save ~10–50%). So this is an honest advisory page: **the discount doesn't exist**, and the real playbook is Sale + Clientele + newsletter timing + accepting final-sale terms. Archetype: **B (no military route at all)** — no first-party discount and no GovX substitute.
