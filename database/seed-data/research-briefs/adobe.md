# Adobe (Creative Cloud / Acrobat) — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 6, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one year of **Creative Cloud** (Adobe's own "Creative Cloud Pro" individual plan is listed at **$69.99/mo** regular, i.e. ~$839.88/yr; intro offers apply — **verify live**).

## Headline finding

**Adobe does not publish a direct military or veteran discount on its own site.** Adobe's official Special Offers page (adobe.com/products/special-offers.html) lists intro pricing, a **students-and-teachers education discount (over 71% off — pay $19.99/mo the first year)**, business plans, and an Adobe Stock trial — **but no military/veteran offer**. The only "Adobe military discount" that exists is delivered **through a paid third-party membership, WeSalute (formerly Veterans Advantage)**, which advertises **15% off Creative Cloud All Apps** and **20% off Adobe Premiere Pro** to its members — a benefit that requires a **WeSalute+ paid membership**, not an Adobe-direct entitlement. So the honest headline: *Adobe has no self-serve military discount; the real cheapest path for most eligible service members and their families is Adobe's own education discount (71% off) if any household member is a student or teacher — otherwise the WeSalute 15% requires paying for membership first, and Adobe's public intro promos often beat it outright.* Archetype: **no direct military discount — steer to the education channel and honest promo math.**

### FAQ

#### Does Adobe offer a military discount?
Not directly. Adobe's Special Offers page lists **no military or veteran discount**. The only "Adobe military discount" is a **WeSalute+ (paid membership)** benefit — 15% off Creative Cloud, 20% off Premiere Pro.

#### What's the cheapest legitimate way to get Adobe Creative Cloud?
If anyone in your household is a **student or teacher**, Adobe's **education discount (over 71% off, ~$19.99/mo year one)** is by far the cheapest — and it isn't a military program, so it's widely usable.

#### Is the WeSalute Adobe discount worth it?
Only if you already pay for **WeSalute+** for its travel/other perks. The 15% off Creative Cloud is real, but you must factor the annual membership dues — often Adobe's own promos save more with no membership.

#### Does Adobe use ID.me or GovX for military verification?

#### Is there a 30% Adobe Acrobat military discount?
That claim circulates online but **could not be confirmed on Adobe.com**. Don't rely on it until verified.

#### Do veterans qualify for an Adobe discount?
Not through Adobe directly. Veterans can access the **WeSalute+** Adobe offers (paid membership), or use Adobe's education price if they're currently a student.
