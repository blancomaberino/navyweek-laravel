# Cavender's — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 10, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one ~**$200** Western outfit — a pair of house-brand/non-excluded Western boots (~$160) + a pair of Wrangler jeans (~$40), pre-tax. (If the boots are Ariat, Lucchese, Old Gringo, Stetson or Resistol, note they are on Cavender's **promotional-exclusion** list and no discount touches them — see below.)

## Headline finding

Cavender's (cavenders.com; Cavender Stores L.P., Tyler, TX — Western wear & boots) publishes **no official, verifiable military or veteran discount** on its own site, and there is **no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX military route** for the brand (ID.me and its government page both state, in Cavender's own listing, "We're not aware that Cavenders offers Military discounts" / "…Government Employees discounts"; no GovX storefront exists — `govx.com/m/cavenders` redirects to the GovX home page). Cavender's **"Thank You Veterans" page is a charity donation promotion, not a shopper discount** — on Veterans Day the company donates ~2% of sales to the Lone Survivor Foundation. The only military-specific signal is a **third-party Tanger Outlets store listing** advertising "**10% off** every in-store purchase for active-duty & veterans who join My Cavender's" (ends Dec 31) — that offer is **not published on any Cavender's-owned channel**, appears **outlet/location-specific**, and reads as **store-level/discretionary**, so we treat it as an unverified signal, not a corporate program. The honest best path for a service member is the same one everyone gets: Cavender's is a **near-constant discounter** ("markdowns up to 50% off" clearance runs continuously), plus the **free My Cavender's Rewards** program (points, member offers, free shipping, receipt-free returns) and occasional **manufacturer mail-in rebates**. Archetype: **B (no first-party military route and no GovX)** with a strong **J/H flavor** (aggregators fabricate a flat "military %" and fake codes; a possibly-discretionary in-store offer some outlet locations grant).

### FAQ

#### Does Cavender's offer a military discount?
**No — not an official one.** Cavender's publishes no military or veteran discount online, and there's no ID.me, SheerID, or GovX route. ID.me's Cavender's page says, "We're not aware that Cavenders offers Military discounts." Some **outlet stores** may extend a discretionary 10% in-store courtesy, but it's unverified and not company-wide.

#### How much is the Cavender's military discount?
There is **no published amount**. A third-party outlet listing advertises "10% off in-store" for active-duty and veterans who join My Cavender's, but Cavender's does not confirm this on its own site — don't rely on it without checking your store.

#### Is the "Thank You Veterans" page a discount?
No. It's a **charity campaign** — on Veterans Day, Cavender's donates about 2% of that day's sales to the Lone Survivor Foundation. It's not a price break for shoppers.

#### Do veterans, spouses, or dependents qualify for a discount?
There's **no program to qualify for.** Any in-store courtesy is at a location's discretion with a valid military/veteran ID. For a guaranteed saving, use clearance and the free rewards program.

#### Does Cavender's use ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute?
**No.** ID.me and SheerID have no Cavender's military offer, and there is no Cavender's GovX storefront (`govx.com/m/cavenders` redirects to the GovX home page).

#### Can I use a military discount online or only in store?
Neither is guaranteed. **Online:** no military discount or verification exists. **In store:** only a possible discretionary courtesy at some (likely outlet) locations — ask first.

#### What's excluded from Cavender's discounts?
Per Cavender's Promotional Exclusions, these **brands never discount**: 7 For All Mankind, Ariat, Charlie 1 Horse, Lucchese, Old Gringo, Resistol, Stetson, Timberland PRO, Turtlebox, Yeti. Also excluded: Gift Cards and Wrangler Rigid styles. Promotions can't be combined unless stated.

#### Are those "SOLIS10" / "30% off military" / "80% off" codes real?
No. Those are **aggregator/influencer codes**, not military offers, and aren't verified on any Cavender's channel. Ignore fabricated "military codes."

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy from Cavender's?
Shop **Clearance (up to 50% off)** and join **My Cavender's Rewards** (free) for points, member offers and free shipping. If you want a specific national brand, check whether **that brand** offers its own military discount (ID.me/GovX) — that often beats anything at Cavender's.

#### Does Cavender's run a Veterans Day or Memorial Day sale?
Cavender's runs near-constant public sales and clearance year-round, and does a Veterans Day **charity** donation — but no verified **military-specific** discount around those holidays. Watch for standard site-wide sale events.

#### Does Cavender's offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
No published discount for those groups either (ID.me's government page confirms none). Use clearance and rewards, or the specific brand's own program.
