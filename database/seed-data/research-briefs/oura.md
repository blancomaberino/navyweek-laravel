**Researched:** July 8, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one **Oura Ring 4 (Horizon, Silver) — $349** + Oura Membership **$5.99/mo ($69.99/yr)**. (Ring price from ouraring.com Ring 4 PDP; membership from Oura's subscription terms.)

## Headline finding

There is **no standing ID.me military discount** on Oura, and there is **no permanent SheerID military offer** either. Oura's evergreen ID.me "preferred pricing" is **10% off Oura Ring 4 and Oura Ring 4 Ceramic** and is explicitly scoped to **students, educators, first responders, and healthcare professionals — military is not on that list.** Oura routes the **military community to GOVX**, where the dedicated offer is **up to 25% off the Horizon and Heritage (Ring 4) styles** — a substantially better deal than the ID.me 10%, and the single best standing path for a verified service member. Every one of these discounts applies to the **ring hardware only, not the $5.99/mo membership**, and Oura's own rules say **discounts can't be combined or stacked** (one per order) and that a better sitewide sale (Black Friday/Cyber Monday) overrides them. A limited **SheerID summer promo (20% off one purchase for verified military/veterans/families) ran June 21–July 4, 2026 and has ended.** Archetype: **A/E hybrid** — no first-party military checkout (military lives on GOVX), alongside a multi-tier ID.me identity discount for adjacent groups.

## Stacking Rules (verified)
1. **Nothing stacks.** Oura states plainly: "Discounts can't be combined or stacked, and only one discount can be applied to an order." GOVX, ID.me, and referral offers are mutually exclusive.
2. **A better public sale overrides your discount** — "Site-wide promotions… will override other discounts if they offer a better price." During Black Friday, the sale may beat GOVX's 25%; take whichever is lower.
3. **Discounts are ring-only.** "All partner Oura Ring discounts apply only to Oura Ring 4 models unless otherwise specified." The **$5.99/mo membership is never discounted** by these programs.
4. **HSA/FSA is not a stack, it's a payment method** — it applies pre-tax dollars to the ring and membership regardless of which discount you used, so it effectively layers under any path.
5. **GOVX is a closed marketplace** — no external codes, no cashback portal, no ID.me on top of a GOVX order.

### FAQ

#### Does Oura offer a military discount?
Yes — through **GOVX**, where eligible service members get **up to 25% off the Ring 4** (Horizon/Heritage). Oura's own ID.me 10% preferred pricing does **not** include military.

#### How much is the Oura military discount?
**Up to 25% off** the Ring 4 via GOVX — roughly **$87 off** a $349 Ring 4 Horizon. The ID.me 10% (~$35 off) is for first responders, healthcare, educators, and students, not military.

#### Does the discount cover the Oura membership?
No. Every Oura discount applies to the **ring only**. The $5.99/mo ($69.99/yr) membership is separate — but it's **HSA/FSA eligible**, which is the best way to reduce it.

#### Does Oura use ID.me, GOVX, or SheerID?
All three appear in Oura's ecosystem: **GOVX** for military/first-responder/government, **ID.me** for first responders/healthcare/educators/students (10%), and a **limited SheerID** military promo that ran June 21–July 4, 2026 (now ended).

#### Do veterans, spouses, and dependents qualify?
Yes via GOVX: honorably discharged veterans, plus **military spouses and dependents ages 18–24**, are listed as eligible groups.

#### Can I stack the military discount with a sale or code?
No. Oura states discounts "can't be combined or stacked," and a better sitewide sale automatically overrides your discount.

#### What's actually the cheapest way for a service member to buy an Oura Ring?
**GOVX (up to 25% off the Ring 4)**, then pay with an **HSA/FSA** to also take pre-tax dollars off the membership. During Black Friday, compare the public sale — it may beat GOVX.

#### Does Oura offer a first responder, nurse, teacher, or student discount?
Yes — **10% off Ring 4 / Ring 4 Ceramic via ID.me** for first responders, healthcare professionals, educators, and students. First responders can also use GOVX.
