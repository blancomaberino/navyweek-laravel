# Honda Military Appreciation Offer — Discount Page Brief

## Executive Summary

Honda runs a national **Honda Military Appreciation Offer**: **$500** toward a new, untitled **2025-or-newer Honda** automobile, applied to the down payment (purchase) or cap-cost reduction (lease), when the vehicle is **financed or leased through Honda Financial Services (HFS)** and the buyer receives HFS credit approval. Eligibility is broad for an automaker: **active-duty U.S. military and Ready Reserve, veterans within 2 years of separation, retirees, spouses of any of those groups, and Gold Star family members**. Verification is document-based at the dealership (LES/DFAS forms, DD-214, Military ID + spouse's LES, or Gold Star documentation) — there is **no ID.me/SheerID/online code**. The offer text is Honda Financial Services' own program language (surfaced verbatim on Honda dealer pages), valid **through March 31, 2027** unless HFS terminates it earlier.

The single most important caveats: (1) it's **$500 flat, not a percentage**, and it **requires HFS financing/leasing** — a cash buyer or someone financing elsewhere doesn't get it; (2) **only one** $500 certificate applies per vehicle; (3) it **stacks with all other HFS programs EXCEPT the Zero Due at Signing Lease program** — this is the make-or-break stacking rule and it's stated explicitly. For most service members the bigger savings lever is negotiating the vehicle price and comparing HFS's rate against outside financing (e.g., a military-friendly credit union), then layering the $500 on top where allowed.

> Accuracy note: Individual Honda dealers phrase the offer differently ("$500 off," "military discount," sometimes bundling it with a College Graduate bonus). The **program itself is Honda Financial Services'**, national, and $500 — not a dealer-specific percentage. Some pages show an older window ("valid through 3/31/2026"); the current verified HFS text reads **through March 31, 2027**. Treat any "% off MSRP for military" claim as **not** part of this program.

### Who Qualifies
- **Active-Duty U.S. Military** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, National Guard, Coast Guard) **and Active Reserve** — Eligible.
- **Ready Reserve** (Inactive National Guard, Individual Ready Reserve, Selected Reserve) — Eligible.
- **Veterans within 2 years of separation** from active service — Eligible.
- **Retirees** (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, National Guard, Coast Guard, Active Reserve) — Eligible.
- **Spouses** of active-duty, Ready Reserve, veterans, and retirees — Eligible (with Military ID + spouse's LES).
- **Gold Star family members** (including widows, parents, next of kin) — Eligible (with DD-1300, DD-3, or other official documentation).
- **Veterans separated more than 2 years** — **Not eligible** (the window is 2 years from separation).
- **First responders / nurses / teachers** — Not part of this program (some dealers run a separate First Responder/Teacher offer; verify locally).
- **Students / recent grads** — Not under the military offer, but Honda's separate **College Graduate Bonus** ($500) may apply.

### How To Redeem
1. Confirm you're in an eligible group and gather your verification documents (LES/DFAS form, DD-214/DFAS RAS, Military ID + spouse's LES, or Gold Star paperwork).
2. Choose a **new, untitled 2025-or-newer Honda** at a participating Honda dealer.
3. Apply to **finance or lease through Honda Financial Services** and get credit approval.
4. Present your documents at the dealership; the dealer applies the **$500 Military Appreciation Certificate** — to your down payment (purchase) or cap-cost reduction (lease).
5. Confirm any other HFS program you're using does **not** conflict (the $500 combines with all HFS programs **except Zero Due at Signing Lease**).

### How Verification Works
There's no ID.me or online verification for this offer — it's **document-based at the dealership**. Active-duty and Ready Reserve members show an **LES or DFAS confirmation**. Veterans show a **DFAS RAS form, DD-214, or Certificate of Release/Discharge**. Spouses show a **Military ID plus their spouse's LES** (the dealer keeps only a copy of the LES). Gold Star family members show a **DD-1300, completed DD-3 application, or other official documentation**. Final approval always runs through **HFS credit approval**.

### Exclusions And Fine Print
- **Requires HFS financing or leasing** and credit approval — a cash purchase or outside financing doesn't qualify.
- **New, untitled 2025-or-newer Hondas only** — not Honda Certified Pre-Owned or used vehicles.
- **One** $500 Military Appreciation Certificate per vehicle.
- **Does not combine with the Zero Due at Signing Lease program**; otherwise combinable with other HFS programs.
- **$500 is flat**, not a percentage — the value is fixed regardless of vehicle price.
- During the HFS contract/lease, you may **not take the vehicle outside the U.S.** without HFS's prior written consent, and must notify HFS of address changes.
- Offer **valid through March 31, 2027**, and may be terminated earlier by HFS. Some dealer pages still show an older 3/31/2026 window — the current HFS text reads 2027.

### Best Ways To Save More
- **Negotiate the vehicle price first.** The $500 is fixed; the biggest dollars are in the sale price and the money factor/APR. Lock those before layering the certificate.
- **Compare HFS financing vs. a military-friendly credit union** (Navy Federal, PenFed, USAA-affiliated lenders). If an outside loan beats HFS's rate by more than the $500 is worth over the term, outside financing can win — but note the $500 **requires** HFS, so run both totals.
- **Stack allowed HFS programs.** The certificate combines with other HFS offers except Zero Due at Signing Lease — ask the dealer to show the deal both with and without each program.
- **Time it with model-year changeover / national Honda incentives**, when APR and lease support are richest; the $500 rides on top where combinable.
- **Grad in the household?** The separate **$500 College Graduate Bonus** may apply to a different eligible buyer — but only one certificate/bonus per vehicle, so plan which buyer is on the contract.
- **Trade-in and tax:** in some states the trade-in reduces taxable price more than $500 would — a separate lever worth optimizing.

### FAQ

#### Does Honda offer a military discount?
Yes — the **Honda Military Appreciation Offer**: **$500** toward a new 2025-or-newer Honda, applied to your down payment (purchase) or cap cost (lease), when you finance or lease through Honda Financial Services.

#### How much is the Honda military discount?
A flat **$500** per vehicle — not a percentage off MSRP.

#### Who qualifies for the Honda military offer?
Active-duty military and Active Reserve, Ready Reserve, veterans within **2 years** of separation, retirees, the **spouses** of all those groups, and **Gold Star family** members.

#### Do I have to finance through Honda to get it?
Yes. The $500 requires **financing or leasing through Honda Financial Services** with credit approval. A cash deal or outside loan doesn't qualify.

#### How do I prove my military status?
With documents at the dealer: an **LES or DFAS form** (active/reserve), **DD-214 / DFAS RAS** (veterans), **Military ID + your spouse's LES** (spouses), or **DD-1300 / DD-3** (Gold Star). There's no ID.me or online code.

#### Can I combine the $500 with other Honda offers?
Yes — it combines with other Honda Financial Services programs, **except the Zero Due at Signing Lease program**. Only one $500 Military Appreciation Certificate applies per vehicle.

#### Does it work on used or Certified Pre-Owned Hondas?
No. The offer is for **new, untitled 2025-or-newer** Hondas.

#### How long is the offer good for?
Through **March 31, 2027**, unless Honda Financial Services ends it earlier.

#### Are veterans who separated years ago eligible?
Only veterans **within 2 years of separation** qualify under this offer. Retirees qualify separately.
