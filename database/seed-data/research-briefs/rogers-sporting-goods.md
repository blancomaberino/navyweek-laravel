# Rogers Sporting Goods — Military Discount & Maximum-Savings Brief

**Researched:** July 15, 2026 · **Baseline cart for all math:** one realistic **$300 waterfowl/hunting gear cart** (e.g. waders, decoys, and men's clothing — non-firearm, non-ammo so promotions can apply) shipped to a standard US address.

- **Brand:** Rogers Sporting Goods (rogerssportinggoods.com) — family-owned waterfowl, hunting, shooting, and outdoor gear retailer (founded 1980), sells firearms and ammunition.
- **Slug:** `rogers-sporting-goods`
- **Audience:** US military + veterans (checked first responders too)
- **Archetype:** No-official-discount specialty retailer (dominant) + loyalty/promo-code outdoor retailer (secondary). There is no military program; savings come from the free Rogers Rewards loyalty program, sale/clearance stacking, and free-shipping thresholds — with firearms/ammo carved out of most promotions.

## Headline finding

**Rogers Sporting Goods does not offer a military, veteran, or first-responder discount.** There is no ID.me, GovX, SheerID, or WeSalute integration on rogerssportinggoods.com, no military line on its FAQ or checkout, and no published military percentage anywhere in its own materials (checked July 15, 2026). Aggregator pages claiming "up to 80% off military," a "30% military code OUTDOORLIMITS," or an in-store military-ID discount are **unverified and contradicted by the brand's own site** — treat them as fabricated. The genuine best-savings path for a service member is the same one available to everyone: join the **free Rogers Rewards** loyalty program (earn $1 = 1 point; 50 points = $1, i.e. ~2% back), stack it on **clearance/sale items**, and clear the **free-shipping-over-$99** threshold — noting that promo codes are typically rejected on **firearms and regulated ammunition**, the exclusion pattern standard for this category.
